Re: A Quick Question About How YOU Moderate
My philosophy is pretty simple: The reason a person becomes a moderator of a forum is because they are a member of that forum. They are one of them. They are not some outside authoritarian figure who rides a high horse and lives in a castle in the sky. They are not of some elite status that gives them a right to stomp on the peons. They are regular users who have been given extra permissions so they can help keep the forum clean. Because of that, my first duty is to gain the posters' respect by being one of them - posting actively, helping people, and when needed moving a thread. You can't scare someone into respecting you by threatening them with a ban every time they fuck up. That's why nobody likes cops.

In the past I've asked moderators to post more often in their forums because when we were trying to find inactive mods, we'd go by posts not moderator activity. So of course, after demodding them I get an email, "Hey, why did you demod me? I close threads there every day" Well, then you're not really doing your job, because moderating is more than just closing and moving threads.

Sam, Euda, and Zok pretty much said what I was going to put here, so I won't go into that.

Anyway, in all of the forums I've moderated since I started, I really just tried to make the forum more enjoyable for the rest of the regulars. Seems like common sense, but unfortunately you wouldn't be surprised to see how many people forget that, and make it their mission to eliminate "bad" posters whenever they might slip up. I don't think anyone should be banned unless there aren't any other options. If I had been banned when I started posting, just for being a moron (which I certainly was) and irritating people (which I certainly did), it's possible that I'd be Alduin right now. :p

Closing borderline threads has never made much sense to me, because it's not something that most people dislike. Only a few nitpicky people will complain about these threads. Others see nothing wrong with it, and will start good discussions. So why close them? The whole point of being a moderator is to make the forum more enjoyable, and I don't see how posting in a forum full of locked threads and having to question every post you make is fun.

Most people only really want to see spam and obviously off-topic threads removed, so that's generally all I remove. What's the point in moderating a forum if you're just pissing (good) people off?
