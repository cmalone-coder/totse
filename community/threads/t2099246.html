This sound about right for the dose i took? Experience(shrooms)
Well haha for my first trip i managed to enter with a bang so to speak. I had a half ounce to myself(~15grams). Gave away about 3(11g) then took about 1g twice on two separate times(7-9g's left). I sat down my 3rd time messing with them and managed to eat my whole stash of them, I dont know what put the fire underneath my ass but as i started to feel the come up i started munching away at everything I had. I originally measured out like 3g's, but then managed to slam down the rest.

So effects i witnessed. I was eating at the time and watching Sanford and son(yea funny shit). As they started to do work on me. I noticed as if the picture from my TV(LCD TV) was just a random floating image, like It didnt appear to me that the imagine was coming from a TV, almost like watching a painting in full motion. I remember going down to dip a cheese stick in the pizza sauce that it seemed as if my arms were tools being held by something else. Like the fact they were coming from my body, as that of saying your hands holding a screwdriver. I cant really explain it but it was some explanation i got caught up in.

I finished up eating then had the urge to have some type of contact with my friends(anxiety from being close to peaking i guess?). Fired up my 360 and surprising half of my good friends were online. Joined a game with my buddy in Halo3. I was talking random jarble according to him, only thing i remember is talking nonsense and his friend asking WTF is wrong with this kid. I found it amusing. As the gameplay started I fucking couldnt distinguish between what team i was on and every time a certain color was pertinent on the screen thats what color was creating a aura/halo around my field of vision. Also random objects in my side vision made me glance over several times cause it looked like someone was sitting watching me play. Really fucked me over and after 2 games I quit. I had klonopins on hand, HONESTLY, i didn't think i needed them but i came to the conclusion that fuck i gotta get descent sleep tonight for work, so i popped 2.

I thought fuck so much for that idea of 360, I looked up at my ceiling(which is painted like the sky, blues with whitish blue clouds). One of the clouds started swirling then it morphed into a leprechaun, smiled, then gave me a thumbs up. I laughed and out loud im said HAHA ohhhh shit I gotta move. Went up on my computer and the monitor brought out patterns in my vision like hell. Almost like the mortar shells from fireworks exploding in my vision. Words on the screen(i was on AIM) that weren't in my direct center of vision appeared as foreign/morphing characters. I was often backspacing like mad as the wrong letters were seemingly to appear when i hit the right button on the keyboard. I closed my eyes for like 5 seconds and it was like i was in a virtual reality zone flying a plane, was weird. Where my computer sits in the corner, and often in the corner like 2 feet away it seemed like there was a portal or something similar to that appearing there. There was just some presence there and It made me feel like i was in a parallel universe at that time. That idea really stuck in my head, since ive seen alot of shows/cartoons/games where a parallel universe is involved and everything is like opposite of what it really is. I felt like i was seeing everything from the opposite end of reality. I dont know if that makes sense, but thats the vibe i had going on.

Finished up on my computer, headed out in the living room, and the walls appeared pink(actually are painted tan) and the furniture in the room looked like it was moved, and the room as a whole looked way bigger. I headed out past my mom and went downstairs to grab some clothes. Heading down the steps it felt like i was in someone else's house, my basement looked foreign. I grabbed my clothes from the laundry, headed back thru the living room, stopped, looked at the clock, like a whole 6-7 minutes went by it was around 8:31 when i was on the computer, from the time i was on there, left and came back only like 6 mins went by. It didnt bother me as i was enjoying myself. Took a shower, and I noticed the mirror, im like OOOO here we go. Walked in front of the mirror staring myself in the face. Eyes looked like that of a alien, barely could see the color of my eyes because of the dime sized pupils. Face expanded in and out, almost looked like as i breathed in and out that my cheeks were following that rhythm. The shower was great and i felt like i was cleansed both physically and mentally, like i just underwent some ceremony.

As soon as i was finishing toweling off immediate nausea overwhelmed me and i just let it go, blew chunks in the toilet. Didn't change the Intensity of the trip at all. By this time i felt like I was done, headed out to the living room, talked to my dad, who DIDNT seem to me like my dad, but obviously seeing him it still clicked in my head that he was infact a authority figure above me. Yes both my parents were home when i did this, WASNT A THREAT TO ME AT ALL, nor did it affect my trip at all. I proceeded to lay down on the couch enjoying the visuals. Closing my eyes was like entering a new realm, it seemed as if the darkness was a whole new area, the volume of the darkness filled out like i was in a room of darkness. Almost like i was out in a dark field staring over 100 yards of darkness. It felt great, at that moment i felt Cleansed. Thats all i can describe the trip as being cleansed, my head felt clearer the next day and I felt upbeat.

Does that sound like a worthy experience according to the amount i took. Had b+ strain by the way(minus-ing the stuff i couldnt recall). Just trying to judge a worthy dose for my next run.
