how salvia kicked my collective ass
When I was received a gram of 5x salvia, I wasn't entirely sure what to expect, on any level despite extensive reading. I had read on erowid and even seen videos of the effects being so powerful that the user literally falls over and becomes entirely immobile for the entire trip, being transported mentally to a new dimension. Yet I've also heard first-hand accounts of people saying that the effects were much milder than most claimed and that they mostly laughed hysterically and were still able to walk without much difficulty. I've also heard by some that there is even a "reverse tolerance" and that most don't even get trip their first time because they smoked it incorrectly, didn't inhale enough or didn't hold it in long enough.

Doubting that I would be able to hold the smoke in long enough, especially after hearing how bad it tastes, smells and the fact that I would be using a torch lighter without a bong (using a standard pipe), I packed my bowl to the top and crammed as much as possible into it. One fourth of a gram would probably be a conservative estimate for the amount I was able to fit in.

I finally lit up in my backyard, sitting in a lawn chair in the shade. The first few hits I took, I coughed out immediately. Probably around the third, and largest hit I was able to hold in. For how long? I don't know. This is the point where time became meaningless, unfortunately dragging much of my memory of the next ten to fifteen minutes with it.

Not long after I took that final hit I for some reason decided to go back inside. This is not only surprising in that I was able to walk around the narrow passageway around my pool without falling in, but that I have no memory of doing this. All I remember about this is almost feeling a part of my backyard. It and I were alive, not in any animalistic sense but in one that transcends explanation. While everything was in constant change and perhaps what could be described as 'motion', everything was resisting me. It felt as if it were a futile and even lethal struggle to move beyond my 'place' in the backyard, but for some reason I felt it was necessary. The best I can describe this was that I felt as if I were an inanimate object, such as a chair (although I didn't feel like a specific object), trying to move with my own will and accord. I felt like I were breaking the laws of physics and the universe was expending its own wrath trying to force me back.

After I was inside my house, the hallucinations stopped. I was at a moment of clarity and decided to go upstairs and put up my pipe. After getting upstairs, I noticed I had a burnt hole in my shirt that went all the way through to my pants (although I didn't have any burn marks on my body or pain from it, and obviously no memory of this happening). At this point I decided to change my clothes and go ahead and wash out my pipe with some water. After this, I decided to lie down for a while because I just wasn't feeling normal.

This is where things get fuzzy again. Reflecting on the whole experience, I'm not entirely sure if at this point I was 'merely' thinking about the hallucinations I had coming inside or if these were entirely new hallucinations, or perhaps that I was thinking about what happened outside and the memory itself triggered new hallucinations, which is probably what happened since I'm guessing it took no longer than three or four minutes to do all the above, meaning the trip was probably not completely over at the time.

All I remember for certain is that I again felt as if I were a part of the inanimate world. This time there was this cracking type noise and everything was being pulled apart and simultaneously spinning in a non-periodic way. I was running, or rather escaping-- I didn't really have a body at this point--and when I thought I had escaped around a 'corner' the splitting and the cracking noise began again. After this hallucination, or perhaps memory of one, faded I realized this splitting was actually caused by my own movement (and reflecting, at this point, I think it was actually each breath I took). After this realization I wondered, what happened to this being that was trying to escape my own movement? Had I killed it? Had I killed myself in another 'reality' in order to live in this one? This was the reality I was trying to escape to or was I trying to escape from it? It was all surreal and still is even thinking about it.

When I finally came back completely to sanity, and thought about the trip, I assumed I had decided to go back inside because I had probably thought I wasn't going to feel anything-- since I had assumed I would be completely immobile. Although after considering the burns through my clothes, I think it is also possible that I subconsciously stood up as a reaction to the burns and just began walking because, well, I was tripping, and only happened to end up inside and not stumbling into my pool and drowning (or even on fire from due to dropping the pipe) by pure luck.

I always try to draw insight from trips, yet surprisingly have had trouble finding any from this one, despite what most people say about salvia trips being naturally insightful. After further thought though, I think I can draw from this experience that humans and perhaps all animals thrive on the illusion that we have control, that we have free will and that we are separate entities from the universe, when in fact we are none of these things. The universe will resist all this, not because it is angry or evil, but that is just how things are. Nothing can change that. And I think this is what made this trip the most terrifying experience in my life, because of the impersonality in the dynamic, ever changing atmosphere that I experienced. For, if this resistance to change and control happened due to anger, or spite there would at least be something to relate to, some justification for the illusion of my individual existence.

My only recommendation for anyone who wants to take salvia, is to not worry about what will happen mentally, salvia will kick your ass for trying to guess where it wants to take you, but to worry about what will happen physically. If you are completely against having a trip sitter (which I still don't think is necessary) at least smoke over a table, so when you do drop the pipe you won't burn yourself or catch yourself on fire, and keep all hands and feet in the vehicle at all times-- no standing or walking.
