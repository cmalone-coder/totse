Re: Ideas for night op?
For night ops you really don't need to take too much stuff with you. Wear dark, comfortable clothing that works as a suitable camouflage for the environment. Wear good shoes for climbing and stealth, (wrestling shoes work excellent) carry a backpack, and bring some light tools. Never keep any ID on you, don't carry much if any money. Try not to keep any valuable personal items on you unless you really need them, you don't want anything to get broken or lost.

If you do bring a lot of equipment, make sure you have a plan. It's always a good idea to travel light in case you need to make a quick get away.

Also, don't wear a helmet. That's retarded. Wear a beanie type hat if it worries you, a baggy hoodie is good also. Don't wear all black clothing either. Wear casual clothing that is dark (preferable midnight blue) and comfortable, but not suspicious in the event you are seen.

Hope that helps.
