There are no completely foolproof methods of contraception and it's entirely possible to fall pregnant while using the pill (or a condom, for that matter). However, if she's using it correctly (which shouldn't be taken for granted), the risk is very low. If you're worried or if it would be absolutely disastrous for her to fall pregnant, it's safest to double up, but you should be completely fine having sex without the condom.
