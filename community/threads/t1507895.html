How do we feed these desires or alternatively, how do we keep them at bay? And is either course of action healthy? This is the dilemma, and it's a problem that's been bothering me for some time now.

First let's look at your normal everyday male or female, masturbating to images of an adult member of the opposite sex. Many people would consider this healthy, right? So then let's consider your not quite so normal everyday male or female, who enjoys masturbating to slightly more risque pictures, pictures of men and women of the opposite sex participating in degrading acts of submission and domination. Pictures of people tied up, being beaten, spanked, tortured, and raped, all staged of course. Is this normal? Why or why not? Or a man who masturbates to children dressed provocatively. Perhaps someone who is aroused by the thought of intercourse with animals? Or maybe a man who enjoys candid snapshots of teenagers on the toilet?

Our base urges, these primal instincts, are almost uncontrollable. If we don't find an outlet for them they threaten to consume us, or in many extreme cases to consume others through our frustration. Is it not better for someone to release these urges on their own, in private, rather than let them build up so they can manifest themselves negatively in other aspects of their life, oftentimes violently? Would you rather your neighbor jerk off to pictures of a corpse or would you rather having him fondling your freshly buried grandmother? Would you rather have your Aunt Claudia molesting your cousin Billy or fingering herself to images of naked schoolboys on the internet?

When I was a child I thought people who liked these things were hermits, monsters living in shacks in the woods. In reality these people are everywhere, in fact, these people are you and me. I've realized that to some extent we're all tainted, that we all get off on kinky things, some more extreme then others. Missionary in the dark carries no mystery, there is no taboo there. It doesn't get us off anymore, and as such we've begun searching for new alternatives, new ways to "keep it hard". No one is pure, we all carry dark desires inside of us. On some level we are all tainted, at least to some extent, and there is no escaping it, it's just a matter of how well we hide it. It's a fun little game we play isn't it? Hide your failings, hide your arousals, tuck it all away and hope no one finds out.

It seems to be in our nature, a man wants to dominate, to defile, to conquer. A woman wants to submit, to be defiled, to be an object. But somewhere along the way these lines got blurred. A myriad of fetishes cropped up, and within those fetishes were offshoots, and then offshoots of offshoots. First there were guys who liked legs, then guys who liked legs in stockings, then guys who liked feet, then guys who liked feet in heels, then guys who liked feet in patent leather shoes and white socks with frilly lace, etc., etc., you get the idea. Nowadays you don't just have someone who's aroused by children, you have someone who's aroused by children in specific attire, in a particular age group. You don't have someone who enjoys cartoon pornography, you have someone who likes cartoon pornography from a specific region drawn in a specific style, and then you have someone who only gets off to the bloody and violent form of that particular style.

Where does what arouses us become part of who we are as a person? Does someone who is aroused by these "unconventional" forms of erotica immediately become a danger to society, a social outcast fit only for the aforementioned solitary mountain retreat? Is masturbating to a picture of an 18 year old any worse than masturbating to a picture of a 14 year old? Both people are being exploited, perhaps to different extents, but the person masturbating plays no part in the material. They didn't create it, they just happened upon it and were aroused by it. If someone masturbates to a picture of a child being abused, do they become part of the crime? Is it their fault what is being portrayed happened? You could argue that if there wasn't a demand for such material then it wouldn't have happened, but is that really the case? What about someone who merely fantasizes about these things, who uses no objectionable material whatsoever, only the impure thoughts that run wild within their imagination? Is that a crime? If so, how do we police a thougt crime?

There's a variety of reasons why someone could be turned on by any one thing, and the mind is often times too complex to pinpoint any exact reason why someone turns to these fetishes. It could have to do with their upbringing, with their biology, or even with some childhood trauma. But is it wrong for people to try and relieve themselves, to do whatever it takes, without hurting someone else of course, to release these urges in the safest manner possible, in the privacy of their own home? If it is wrong, what can be done, what kind of punishment does someone deserve for simply being the way they are, for becoming the person they've become?
