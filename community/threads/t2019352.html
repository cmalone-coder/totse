On a certain annoyance of speaking English..
This may sound like a rather strange topic, but when you prounounce the "tt" In the middle of a word- such as "Better"- Do you say:

"Bedder"
If you're American you'll probably do this- (Pronounce your T's as D's.) Yet i do this on some occasions and i am English.

The original "Better"
If you're an upperclass Brit you'll probably do this- It generally sounds quite posh.

And of course, the "Be'er"
True English slang, most classes of the average English person will do this; Being of general loutish type accents, that is.

Yes, as said previously- This is a strange topic but curiously interesting- It also says alot about yourself.. Plus as i have OCD, i get obsessed at times with which one i say, which is fucking annoying.
