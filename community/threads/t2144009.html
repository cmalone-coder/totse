Wax can normally be removed by chilling the solution and thus forcing it out. What is important is the amount of solvent used in extraction, this is so rarely mentioned and yet can have a massive effect on the outcome. Know each solvents properties and exactly why that particular one is being used at that particular time (sequence).

PSE doesn't readily disolve in acetone even if it's at room temp and not been dried (unless of course the acetone is from a majorly crap source). ALWAYS keep the acetone that filters off and evap it to see what it has removed. In fact keep everything and label at the time neatly and in a manner that you will understand.
