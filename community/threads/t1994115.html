Re: Being a car salesman
Dude, if you're on comission and there's only one of that place. Advertise for them, get them lot's of business. Take out ads in craigslist, posters everywhere, get them a little known if you can, it will do you good. Like the above poster said, go into details about positive features, those positive features build the car in the clients mind. Always be as 'happy' as you can, that means putting a fake smile on all the time. Get a litle personal with your clients, not like touching anyones tits or anything, but like talk to them, as if you would talk to a friend almost. If you're on 25% commission, do your best to get you a lot of customers, because the more customers= the more chance of you getting a sale.
