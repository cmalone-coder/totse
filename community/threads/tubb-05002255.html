Re: Music
I'm going to list some here. They are all from the 60's. If you are willing to go back that far in musical time you will find that they are most exellent to listen to while tripping [remember: acid rock and psychedilic were terms given to music that was meant to be heard while tripping and/or music that helped recreate the psychedilic experience]. I've had countless hours of tripping pleasure from all of these. [Since Floyd goes without saying and has already been mentioned, I won't list it here].

Vanilla Fudge: Their first album and Renaissance are the best two, though any of their records are good to trip to.

Electric Prunes: Mass in F-Minor

Spooky Tooth: Ceremony, The Last Puff [this has a great psychedilic version of 'I'm the Walrus'. It can also be found on 'Best of Spooky Tooth]

Iron Butterfly: Inna-Godda-Da-Vida [The cliche acid song]

Argent: Nexus

The Rolling Stones: Their Satanic Majesties Request ['2000 Light Years from Home' and 'She's a Rainbow' are the best off this album]

The Doors: Anything by the Doors is good to trip to, but the first two albums and 'Absolutly Live' stand out.

Hendrix: This should also go without saying

Mothers of Invention: Any of the early recordings [this was Frank Zappa's first band].

Harry Partch: [though this guy isn't really 60's, let alone rock, he is never the less very good to trip to, though kind of hard to find.]

Dukes of Stratosphere: The 'Dukes' are really the members of XTC who did a couple of records under this name, but they really got down what is was that made psychedilic music psychedilic: They do it better than many 60's groups did.

King Crimson: First two albums are the best

Black Sabbath: Though not technically psychedilic, their first two albums are great while tripping


There are many more, but these are the best examples. If you decide to be bold enought to try any and decide you like, Ill be happy to offer up more.
