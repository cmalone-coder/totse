first rave. most amazing night of my life
me and 2 friends went to this rave in austin last night. it was the most amazing experience of my life.

we got very lucky and found a guy selling strait up MDMA .15 a pop. this was the second time i ever rolled, and let me tell you these 2 pills i took on an empty stomach made me feel NOTHING like what i took the first time. i KNOW that was good e.

it was a very small pill with a motorola imprint on it. the pill was perfectly shaped looked like a smarty. we bought a total of 8 of these motorolas, some white and some blue but the same pill. i had no idea what i was in for.

the music dominated everything, i was constantly moving if i was in the warehouse, there was no stopping it.
i began with c walking because thats all i really knew how to do then it just evolved into my feet shuffling in perfect rhythm with the beat while my body just moved along with it. this was the first time i ever danced. it was amazing

drove home at around 4 in the morning. still rolling balls. a 45 minute trip down 35 was the craziest part of my night. the lights and blinkers were flashing all over while my mind just went on and on and on. it was badass
prolly wasnt too smart. but i made it home no problem.

if any of you have never taken good e and never been to a rave i highly suggest doing both. it was pure bliss
