Re: Drugs in Maui
Looking for weed? Depends where you are staying, lurk the beaches in kihei, ask the surfers for weed and the local (read: brown skinned) surfers for weed and possibly meth. Normal rules apply, do not give anyone money and expect them to return with drugs. Do not make demands, you are on their turf and the wrong set of locals will beat the shit out of you and take your money, especially if its meth you are looking for as the tweakers are more prone to shady shit like that. Don't say "you know where i can get some ice/meth?" Meth is called Batu (bah-too) or Tutu (too-too), it sounds stupid but they will immediately know what you are talking about. As for weed, "buds" is universal among locals, ex: Ho cuz, you know where I can pick up buds?. Simply saying "you know where I can pick up?" is also a widely accepted way of saying you are looking for weed.

Beaches in lahaina are applicable as well but usually harbor more tourists than locals. If someone is surfing chances are they live on maui. Your best bet are young (18-28) generally brown skinned surfing males. If you are mobile, Paia town is a guaranteed place to score bud, your strategy here will be the opposite however. In paia look for the hippies, generally they are white, and dirty/poor looking, but very nice people. They always have weed or can get it easily and can sometimes hook up other uncommon things from ecstasy (sometimes fake), to lsd, to mushrooms (which grow wildly in the cow pastures following a rain). Meth can be found almost exclusively through the locals in the lower income parts of town and to a (possibly much) lesser extent the beaches. These are also the areas you do not want to be in alone and especially at night. If you are adventurous you can try shoulder tapping in places like Happy Valley in wailuku but it's probably safer to try the beaches during the day.

Use your head before you approach anyone, you are equally as likely to be turned down as you are to be hooked up. Prices are high on maui, 1 gram of weed is $20, eighth is $60 and quarters are $120. Meth prices can vary. You are not going to be able to haggle. Say the wrong shit and the locals will just take your money, beat the fuck out of you, and call you a fuckin haole. Start at around noon, if you don't score by the time the sun sets give up or you risk getting fucked with.

Happy hunting.
