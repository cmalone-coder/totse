Actually...

An opioid is a SYNTHETIC/SEMI-SYNTHETIC OPIATE; for example, SYNTHETIC OPIOID = Methadone, Hydromorphone, Oxymorphone, etc, etc.; SEMI-SYNTHETIC OPIOID = Oxycodone, Hydrocodone, Demerol, etc, etc. something that may have been derived from Thebaine, Codeine and/or Morphine at first, but then synthesized to be stronger and more potent analgesically and euphorically :)

And an OPIATE would be a natural narcotic analgesic pain killer derived naturally from usually Opium (Morphine, Codeine, Thebaine, etc.) ONLY; for example... Codeine, Morphine, Heroin, Opium, etc, etc.

Nothing at all to do with receptors...
