We were all new once.

check out www.shroomery.org if you have any time because that truely is one of the best sites to help you along the way.

your going to need to build an incubator for the incubation process:

-two containers that fit into each other, like smaller rubbermaids.
-fishtank heater
-thermometer

basically, you put the fishtank heater on the bottom of one of the containers.
set to around 78 or so
fill the bottom with water so it completely covers the heater
fit in the other container into the other one, so it is floating in the water on the other one.

you put your jars (assuming PF-Tek) in the one that is floating on top of the heated water, and cover that container.

This is your incubator.


for your Fruiting chamber, its simple as well:

-container with a clear top
-perlite
-polyfill
-small CFL or flouro light
-hand mister


You take your container, drill SEVERAL holes around the sides. Fill these holes with the polyfill so contams can't enter your FC.

Fill bottom of container, at least 4 inches or so of damp perlite.

this gives your container the humidity

mist the fuck out of that perlite several times a day and give your cakes fresh air exchange.

I used a fish tank bubbler to provide constant air exchange.


like I said this is a basic PF Tek set up

go to the shroomery and lurk and search and read and you'll be on your way to success.
