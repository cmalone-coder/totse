If one travels very fast, as in at relativist speeds, it is proven (no longer just a theory)that time on board the craft will pass more slowly. One could travel 'round the galaxy at near light speed, seem to be gone only a year or so, but return to earth 1000 years later. There is also the deep freeze. If all your bodily functions were suspended for a period, you could 'wake up' years later, with no memory of time passing in between. Of course it is easy to see that there is no undoing suspended animation.
