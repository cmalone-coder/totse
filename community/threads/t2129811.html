Re: lsd and shrooms question
I strongly prefer LSD to mushrooms.

Mushrooms often make me feel kind of crazy. Like, cross chatter in my head and shit like that. Also the trips on mushrooms tend to be VERY confusing and disorienting.

In contrast, on LSD I seem to be much more in control over my trip. There are just as many "WTF" moments on LSD as mushrooms - if not more. There are some serious mental hallucinations (that is -- things that happen in your head that will not be so sure of) that you will likely be thinking about over the next week or two.

Keep in mind, LSD is a very strong psychedelic. Rather than a drug, it would be more appropriate to call it a tool. There's a whole hallway of locked doors in your mind, and LSD is the key to unlock them. It is up to you to open them.

On a strong LSD trip, realize that you may experience things and see things about yourself that you may not want or be ready to see.

If you have any skeletons buried in your closet, expect to be staring them in the eye.

Don't let that scare you though, a strong LSD trip is one of the most beautiful things someone can experience.

This 'drug' can and will change your life and perspective.

Enjoy. Free your mind, and your ass will follow.

Trip safe.
