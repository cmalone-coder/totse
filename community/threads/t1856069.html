I can help you a little on the ghetto part. Anything over 100C is really pushing it with the pickle jar or any other non lab glass container. Any time you have to use a non lab glass jar, make sure you have a "aluminum sleeve" as I like to call it. You make it (of course, thats what all the tweakin is for) by yanking out a big ole piece of aluminum foil and start smoothly folding it down until you have a square (perfect square). When you sit your jar in question down in the center of the square, it is just large enough os that you are able to pull the sides of the foil up to about the halfway point.

From here on in you must rely on your tweaker instincts to help you decide how to fold, smooth, and crimp that baby into a beautifully hand crafted aluminum bowl, conforming exactly to the shape of the jar.

I know a guy, who just the other day was boilin a little pseudo pm in a very large motts applesauce jar. These are well known (to the guy anyway) to be very reliable (as much as jars can be called reliable). He has several of these shape conforming, heat insulating sleeves next to his heating area.

That thick pm on the bottom of the jar really can hold a lot of heat(thats why its important to stir it a lot when doing the straight to E). Well, he was fucking off or something and was reminded of the job at hand when he heard that dreaded, but distinctive ping or maybe you prefer the word clink. It automatically strikes fear in the heart of anyone who has suffered substantial loss from this demons ability to take the entire bottom off of a jar that is filled with hot liquid that not only represents a days worth of driving all over town, but is flooding hot electrical connections with extremely volitile solvents.

When he realized that the jar was surrounded by an aluminum sleeve, he regained his composure, positioned his back up jar, prayed to the meth gods for steady hands, and slowly picked the pinged jar from the (ghetto) hot plate. He forgot that meth gods require incantations and do not know how to interpret prayer. Half way up, the bottom fell out and swoosh, the sleeve was full of pm/elixir slurry. He picked up the sleeve and poured the contents into back up jar, rinsed it out into new jar and lost maybe a quarter gram of theoretical pseudo that splashed on his arms. He WILL remember incantations next time, but the sleeve completely contained the contents.

You know, all I really wanted to do was emphasize the importance of having a good safety protocol in place, especially when using ghetto equipment. Aluminum sleeves are worth their weight in gold the very first time you need it. If the result of the mishap would have been a fire that brought on the police and fire departments, the value instantly soars to priceless.
