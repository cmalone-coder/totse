The 6th Circuit Psychedelic Circus (OR: The D&D Mushroom Adventure)
Count Rizzo has been cooking up a splendid idea into the outer edges of the cosmos, or rather, he's been pondering ideas. Never a good sign, right?

Well, well...Over the years there have been many psychonaughts, yes? People have traveled inside their...minds? Spirits...to...incredible places of perception. Learned great truths. Got really, really high. Etc.

And so we share our knowledge, what we learned, how it happened, what we brought back from the trip. Although in the end, it's only a finger pointing to the moon.

But let's cut the nonsense because I've written far too much nonsense in my life and gotten away with most of it which makes life all the more splendid when you realize nothing is true and everything is permitted.

Or was it everything is true and nothing is permitted? Damned, if you get THAT one wrong.

Let me try to outline what I'm trying to say then...

Psychedelics.

Imagination.

Adventure.

Psyche.

Self-realization.

6th/7th circuit tinkering.

Big Fun.

H'okay, so now that I got the general gist of it out, this is what I'm planning to do, and why:

Sometime this weekend, most likely Saturday night, two companions and I will be ingesting 2-cb, mushrooms, LSD, potent cannabis, and Lord only knows what else will spring up. By the time the first of the drugs hit, we will be entirely different people and we will know it and be in (somewhat) control. After that, we will go on a journey, an adventure, travel many miles...who knows?

What? What do you mean?

Well, in searching for the Philosophers Stone, I have come to the conclusion that psychedelics in and of themselves will NOT take you to Where You Want To Go. No, for that you need Imagination, Creativity, and possibly bodily appendages like guts or balls or what have you. So my thought process goes, "When were you most creative and imaginative?"

Why! When I was not but half my size, if less! Of course, that child like imagination, all the horror stories or fantasies and illusions and what have you. So why not put oneself INTO that mindset deliberately to create a conscious cognitive dissonance.

Put simply:

Create a new "self". A character. It can be in any kind of vein you want, but it has to be self-sustaining and consistent. For example, you could pretend that you are a Greek God and are meddling in the affairs of mortals. Or maybe you're a treasure hunter, looking for phat l00t in the Egyptian Pyramids...Or just some adventurer. A wizard. A priest. A king. WHATEVER.

Now. Add together psychedelics, and a few friends that are willing to go along with this and you've basically created yr own private universe.

But, you say, it isn't REAL.

A-ha!

Obviously it is not real, you just made it up. But - what of yr so called REAL self?

And then, that is where it begins.

I can not tell you what happens after that, for it is for each man to experience. What is reality, what is the self? What is imagination, creativity, etc? Who is the creator and who is the created? The Big Questions.

So while this is by no means a completely novel idea, it still has a fairly stable back bone for creating a supremely enlightening experience which others can tamper with and use to their own needs.

How weird can you get?


ps: if none of this makes sense, or is completely and totally unreadable, I'll re-write it for yr reading ple-a-sure. Until then, let us collaborate, corroborate, masticate, and digest some psychedelic ideas.
