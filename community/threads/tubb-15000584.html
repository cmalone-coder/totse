The Bible is a world away from infallable

quote:

...for all I know leprechauns might exist...


The most intellegent thing you can do when people come at you spouting wild, unproveable, unsubstantiated claims is to go ahead and automatically assume they don't exist until proven otherwise.

Like free will. If God is omnicient, then there is no free will cause' we are stuck fulfilling his omnicience.

The very ability to know what humans will do to a 100% accuracy in the future means there are no truly random, unknown factors which could affect the decisions humans make and screw up the prediction. In other words, if anything can know everything there is to know about the universe, it operates completely deterministically. And when everything is determined, free will is a complete illusion.

I am still going to live my life as though free will is not an illusion. You can't just go off what people tell you, proof is needed.

Talk is cheap, let's see some results validating these claims.
