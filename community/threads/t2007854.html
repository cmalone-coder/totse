Holy shit! Tagamet HB extends and improves a weed high!
I swear, every drug user needs to have a box of these babies laying around, it's hard to find a class of drugs they DON'T improve...

That's right. According to THIS chart, we see that the substrate tetrahydrocannabinol(THC) is metabolized by enzyme CYP2C9.

We look further down, to the inhibitor section, and find that Cimetidine (Tagamet HB) is a significant inhibitor of this enzyme.

DAMN. This means that, when a large amount of Tagamet(600mg-800mg [3-4 pills]) is ingested ~45 minutes before smoking, the weed high SHOULD last significantly longer, and you should get higher as well.

While I haven't explicitly tested this yet, Some of you may remember a thread I had put together a while back describing a method of weed potentiation with large amounts of cocoa powder.

I had fantastic results, and got SUPER baked and for a longer period of time with just a bit of bud, but none of the people who tried experienced the same results.

Now I realize, while the anandamide and cannabinoid inhibitors present in cocoa may have helped a bit, I had taken 400mg of Tagamet HB an hour or two earlier. THIS must have been what caused such pronounced effects.

I hadn't put the two together until now.

This should be ESPECIALLY useful for pot brownies, or any other method of oral marijuana. This would completely get rid of or at least temporarily postpone(depending on Tagamet's length of action) the first pass metabolism. This means it would take SIGNIFICANTLY less weed to get baked, as first pass metabolism destroys some of THC.

(First pass metabolism only takes place when eaten, though)

I have no facts supporting this, but assuming I haven't made any logic errors, a mildly tolerant person could get super baked from a .3 gram pot brownie. (made with good shit)

So, before I go and take massive amounts of Tagamet in an attempt to make a smoked weed high last like 7 hours, do you guys see any immediate faults in my plans here?

Also, are there any known health complications of taking upwards of 1 gram of Cimetidine?
