1baddude, who do you want in a foxhole next to you? The guy that did the pussy training you would want the military to do, with a drill sergeant that acted like his personal trainer to ensure he could bench press x man more pounds, or run x distance farther?

Or do you want the guy who was constantly pushed to both his physical and psychological limits everyday, for months on end, to the point of breaking down, and then had to go further? The guy with the mental fortitude or the guy who lifted weights?

You're right, military training isn't about building better bodies. It is about building better and more resilient minds, and sorting out the rest that can't hack it. That is why the military doesn't give a damn how well you shoot when you join. Practically anyone can be trained to shoot a rifle, and shoot it really well given the proper amount of training. Same with raw strength.

However, the military needs both of these things in combat, but realizes that without the proper mental discipline both are useless. That is why SEAL training is designed to weed out the mentally weak. Same with all special operations training.

That is why minutemen, who could probably shoot and move a hell of a lot better than their British opposition, ran in droves. They had the skills for war, they just didn't have the military training to stand up to the horrors of war.
