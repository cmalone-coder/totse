Re: Recently bought a laptop of unknown origin
If it's in the MBR, and you're in a Linux LiveCD/LiveDVD, just login as root (should be easy -- there should be a default password they tell you, or no password at all). Now, execute "dd if=/dev/zero of=/dev/hda bs=512 count=1". Congrats, you've just killed the MBR on the first hard drive (if it's normal ATA, if SATA or SCSI it'd be a different name than /dev/hda).
