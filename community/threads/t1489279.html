I'm not so sure asking about a more open relationship is a good idea in this case. They've been together too long, and she obviously counts on him being there in a monogamous capacity.

You're not going to avoid hurting her, and you likely won't avoid hurting her badly. If you're only away for school, you'll see her again, so you're probably be better off telling her exactly how you feel. It's hard to feel close to someone who's so far away, and if your only contact is through computer and phone, tell her it's too hard for you to keep doing. Allow for the possibility of getting back together when you see her again, and unless you need to avoid all contact, tell her you still want to be her friend and know what's going on with her. Then, when you meet again, see how it goes.
