Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Re: Ball Mills
for gods sake just make it!!!! my ball mill costed me exactly $7. yup you heard me $7 and about 5 hours of planning. (as my design was very different) ok heres what you do. we need a motor, so... we either A. pick up a dryer from a junkyard or the motor, or any motor that is AC for that matter. OR B&gt; wait until someone throws a dryer out around your neighborhood (trust me i thought it would take months for someone to throw one out then withina week and a 4 block vicinity of my house i found one!) it should spin at about 1725 RPM but its not that important. So you've got your motor and belt how much did you spend? (0) ok now we need a jar well 4 inch PVC pipe is the minimum so ask around if anyone has it. I got my pipe just lying around the garage. ok now just buy 1 endcap and 1

4 to 2 inch reducer with threaded interior and matching plug that screws on and off of it. ok now put the jar together with PVC primer and cement your jar should be anywhere from about 14 to 5 inches long depending on how much you mill. Now we have a jar a belt and a motor that you were smart enough to hook up with a switch. now we just need a stupid roller to attach the belt to and voila El cheapo premo performento ball mill completed. im sure someone will give you links so im not gonna bother posting em less you ask nice.
