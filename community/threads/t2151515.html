Any amount of Marijuana under 50 grams in NJ is automatically a misdemeanor. If for some reason you have under an ounce and there is reason to believe you are selling it (several different bags, a scale, lots of money, etc) then that is another misdemeanor. In New Jersey "intent to sell" doesn't necessarily have to do with how much weed you have, since under an ounce is a misdemeanor and over an ounce is a felony. Now, that is not to say that if you get caught with a pound and no scales or bags you won't get intent to sell... but that is irrelevant.

Being in the park after hours is probably just a citation, and being in the park with a controlled substance (I'm guessing) is another misdemeanor.

What kind of park is this!?!?

Here in NY we have this thing for young offenders called "youth court" where you are tried by your peers and upon completion your record remains clean and you're slapped with community service. If you don't have that option in Jersey I suggest you lawyer up. Either way all I see in your future is fines and community service.

Well you are a juvenile so the only fines will be court costs. What will happen is you will go to court and either plead guilty to the charge or go to trial. Then idk how it works in NJ but in Nebraska you and a parent meet with a probation officer and discuss what happened and they will ask about your life and see if you are a good kid or a total fuck up. Then you will go back to court, probably get 6 months to a year probation. You will also most likely have to attend outpatient treatment, which costs anywhere from $700 - $1000 dollars. Some community service is also possible. You will meet with your PO every 2 weeks or once a month to take drug tests also. After you do all that shit you will be off probation, and I don't think your record as a juvenile carries over to adult so you should be good.

Edit: I forgot you may get a "diversion" program which isn't with the courts but its the same thing that youth court stuff what that other person brought up. It would be similar to probation except the charges are formally dropped after you complete it.
