Re: meth > MDP2P?
MDP-2-P is Methylenedioxy phenyl-2- propanone, it is a precursor to MDMA, and is the last precursor to this fine chemical so looking to buy it will get you bagged and tagged.

It cannot be made simply or directly from meth nor can meth be simply and directly from it. I'm not saying it can't be done, I'm just saying that there is no reason to do it as there are much more practical ways to obtain either, yield and expense would make this a silly thing to do.

Unless you mistook your words and meant P2P or phenyl-2-propanone. This is a direct precursor to meth and can also be made from meth itself (who would want to go backwards I know not)

Bottom line is that meth and MDMA chemistry are related by a similar structure but they may as well be in different universes because of that Methylenedioxy group.
