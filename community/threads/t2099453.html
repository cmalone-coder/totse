Also Fedor has a lot more fights under his belt as an MMA fighter. Some would say "Well Couture's amateur Greco-Roman background makes up for that."

Well no it doesn't, as Fedor has also been competing at a very high level (and often being at a champion) since 1997 and is still doing it even while competing in MMA! And also I GUARANTEE that his amateur judo and sambo experiences were much more rigorous than Couture's wrestling as Russian amateur athletes compete WAY more than Americas. Boxing is a good example.

Most amateur boxers in the USA who turn pro after competing through their teens until they first try for an Olympic/Pan-American/International Championships win, have a minimum of 50 fights, with usually not too much more than 100 - though of course kids who start really young like 9 years old might have 200. Many curent pro stars have even more, Oscar de la Hoya had somewhere around the vicinity of 250, Shane Mosley had almost 300, though it varies wildly as current BEST in the world fighter Floyd Mayweather Jr. had less than 100! In Russia though, its not unusual for a guy to have 500 by the time he's in his early 20s. And people who compete in Sambo and Judo fight even MORE often than boxers.

So just keep all that in mind!
