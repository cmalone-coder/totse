Entheogen [and other] reviews
I'd like to hear about your experiences with different botanicals. I'm not talking about "legal alternatives", I mean different plants and herbs that have [or are said to have] psychoactive properties. Any you've shown enough interest in to actually try.

I'll start. I've had a shitload of different ones, I'll try to remember them all..


Amanitas - I've never personally tried them, but friends have and didn't get much effects from them. However, they were a low grade, so I'm assuming that could be a major factor in that. I'd like to try a high grade at some point.

Betel Nut - I got a few packs of the "chew" from IAS a while back. I didn't get any effects from them, but they do have a pleasant taste. I imagine a lot more might have SOME effect..

HBWR - Tried a few times, all bunk. I have some more I'm waiting to get from a friend, very hopeful about this time.

Calea [Dream Herb] - Ordering some very soon, heard it works quite well. I'd like to hear experiences about this one, and the other "dream herbs", silene capensis and entada rheedii.

Kava - Excellent for relaxating.

Kratom - Very opioid-ish. My only experience was when I got some free from a promotion on the internet. I took more than I should have, and wound up very sick the next day. But I'd do it again in a smaller dose. Or maybe an extract. Very strong compared to what I thought it would be like.

Blue Lotus - Got a gram of 10x for free from IAS. Tried smoking it [should've made a tea]. Didn't get much from it, but now I know tea is a better way to take Lotus. Would try again the right way.

Salvia - I don't really need to put this one down, it's too popular to share any new info. Very effective, trippy as fuck.

Kanna - Burns like FUCK when insufflating. Definitely felt something, but the pain overpowered it. I don't want to try it again.

Valerian root - Good for sleeping.

Damiana - Good in tea, bad to smoke. Didn't get effects either way.



There's a million others, I want to hear personal experiences, recommendations, whatever you want to share! :)
