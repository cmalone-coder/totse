Arrive at the shopping mall and select your target. Have your friend wait outside around the stores corner with the RC car control, and have him place the RC car 20 meters away from the exit of the store.

Calmly walk up to a selected item (make sure is in clear view of the exit) you would like to steal and stick a Velcro patch on it. Turn around and walk away, making sure no one saw what you just did. Walk outside the store, turn around to face your item. You will be standing outside the detectors. Pull out your jumpline gun, and fire it at your item, making sure the 2 Velcro patches connect. Now, hit the jumpline gun button and watch as the item sores towards you.

Hit the release button on your jumpline gun to disconnect the item from your gun. Signal to your friend and have him race the car off. Have him drive up a specially prepared ramp that leads into your car. You both should be able to reach your car in time. Drive away and don't look back.
