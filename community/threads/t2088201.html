In scientific studies, the term 'overdose' is in reference to the LD-50. The LD-50 of a substance is the dose at which 50% of the experimental animals (people) die.

The LD50 value for rats by inhalation of THC is 42 mg/kg of body weight ... There has never been a documented fatality from marijuana or THC overdose.

Thanks wikipedia! (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahydrocannabinol)

This basically translates to: it is impossible to overdose on cannabis. You would have to inhale somewhere around 1500 pounds of pot with about 12% THC content ... within roughly 15 minutes ... to officially overdose. To die.

'Overdose' might be used commonly to refer to taking too much in the community of whoever wrote your definition, and among you and your friends ... but that is not the accepted definition.

Edit - Oh, and since this thread is about eating pot ... it would take much, much more to OD by eating it. The above was for smoking.
