This just occured to me. All you athiests (of which I am one), imagine the situation. One day, God appears in front of you. You arn't drunk, on drugs, just normal. He utterly proves his existance to you, and says that unless you change your sinful ways to follow him, you'll burn in hell for all eternity.

Do you convert to Christianity? Would you give up all your earthly vices in order to avoid damnation?

Personally, I like to think that I wouldn't, most because my ethics don't gell with Christian values. I support free abortions, assisted suicide, casual drug use, casual sex, whatever. Dropping all those would make me feel like too much of a hypocrite, just abandoning them on the spot like that. To be honest though, I'd probably go for a deathbed conversion...see if that would work to avoid getting my ass toasted...

So, what about you lot? Hmm. I suppose this could also pass for religious non-christians out there as well as for athiests.
