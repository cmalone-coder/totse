Re: Bible Predictions-coinscidence? i dont think so
It's important to note, Bakdoor wizard, that that whole series of events probably didn't happen that way. There is no record - and keep in mind, the Romans were insane about keeping records - of Pontius Pilate (the "Roman judge guy") or any of his predecessors releasing a prisoner as a "Passover Tradition."

It's also highly unlikely that Pilate would be involved at all if Jesus was the religious rebel the Bible makes him out to be. More likely, he was preaching political rebellion against Rome. And if he was doing that, there's no way he'd be released, regardless of what the assembled Jews had to say.

Even the phrasing of the whole episode shows signs of tampering. In particular, Pilate claims to "wash my hands of this." At the time, washing one's hand to atone for a murder was not a Roman custom, and certainly no atonement would be required for the execution of a rebel in any case. This was in fact a custom relatively unique to the Essenes, a group living at Qumran along the Dead Sea.

One does not need to read the Bible to know its falsehood; a healthy familiarity with history can handle that task quite nicely. I'd be happy to share some more of these historical innaccuracies if you like, but given the tone you've manifested, you appear to prefer joyous ignorance.
