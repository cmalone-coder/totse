don't bother trying to figure out what the trip will be like, because once you do it its going to be nothing at all like what you thought it would be in the first place...

dont do it with someone just trying to get fucked up and see shit, try to do it with someone with a good open mindset and not really depressed if you can... just take a couple of hits, and as the girl who introduced me to this wonderful drug said "just let the lovin take hold".. my first trip was simply beautiful, more of a mindfuck than visual, i felt insane but held onto the thought of "everything will be normal by sunrise".... on erowid i believe it says it causes a mood lift, that was an understatement that i didn't expect to be so drastic... opiates cause a mood lift, acid took the happiest i had ever felt up until that point and multiplied it by a few thousand times.... i felt so happy for once, i laughed because it felt so good to laugh... then i laughed at myself for laughing at nothing other than it feeling good, and it was one insane pattern of happy and laughing...

go for a long walk, or if it feels like the right thing to do jog... it can be pretty stimulating... i prefer to do acid on a nice clear night so i can see the hundreds of thousands of stars that litter the sky like god had puked diamonds across it. watching the street lights sway and the shadows moving around as if they were alive. walking down the street at night for me has the feeling of walking into a beautiful and perfect painting...

like i said before my first trip was a mind fuck like i had never experienced before. thousands upon thousands would race through my head, one revelation about myself, those around me, and the universe. I would close my eyes for what felt like hours of thoughts only to open them again and see that only a few minutes had passed....

Yea shrooms would have been an easier psychedelic to handle for me at around 2 grams, which i found to be pretty nice. heavily visual but not to much of a mind fucking... but i never regretted that first trip, it was intense and one of the most powerful experiences of my life, but i learned alot about myself that night that i wouldn't give up for the world... so my advice is find someone to do it with, take a couple of hits and have a nice trip...

i took 1 and a half hits my first time, but it was some pretty good acid according to some people i know who had taken the same hits...
