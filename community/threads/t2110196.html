Re: Cooking up crack
Uh since no one gave a reliable method, it's simple:

Take spoon, put amount of coke you want to turn into crack along with a third of the amount of baking soda. Add enough water to turn the coke/soda mix cloudy and dissolved. Heat from the bottom with a lighter gently, boiling until it forms a solid mass and there is no more liquid. Add more water to the spoon and heat again from the bottom, the hardened white mix will slowly disappear in the fresh water leaving a yellowish oily "glob" floating around.

Drag the glob out of the remaining liquid and allow to dry.
