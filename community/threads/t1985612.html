So ye' say yr self-confident now, do ye'? Welllll then, just how confident are you? Are you only confident in certain clothes, with certain hairstyles, etc? Do you look good sometimes, but not all the time?

People who radiate confidence are attractive, but how can you truly be confident of everything? You're too fat, too skinny, too wrinkly, too flat, not enough muscle, you slouch too much, etc. We all have our "flaws".

But do you acknowledge them as such? Because if you do, you're falling prey to it. If you can truly believe with 100% of your heart that there is nothing wrong with you, then it will be true. You can't do this with the mindset that you're better than anyone else though. They could all be this beautiful, they just need to smile more!

If you think other people have flaws, then you acknowledge that humans can have flaws, thus keeping yourself open to this. But if you act as innocent as a little kid and recognize that the physical, material world is full of flaws, and that the world of the spirit is beautiful and flawless, then you will be in pure bliss.

Nothing can harm you, no one can touch you, it doesn't matter if so-and-so cares if you look good because you know that they go to home and cry themselves to sleep and starve themselves and use drugs to cover up for their own insecurities. But not you though, you have fun doing ANYTHING, being ANYWHERE, with ANYONE. Because you act in the spirit of absolute innocent confidence!

So put on the weirdest, stupidest looking clothes you can find. Smear makeup horribly all over your body. Stuff pillows in yr shirt. Wear really baggy clothes. Now, go in front of a mirror and realize that there is absolutely nothing wrong with the way that you look. You're fantastic! Beautiful baby, beautiful. Give me a kiss, hun.
