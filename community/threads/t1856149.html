Re: methanol/ethanol/water vaporization points
Plan 1 will definately not work. All 3 components must freeze together at the same temperature.

Plan 2 might sort of work if you have a good fractioning distallation column, but will not produce pure ethanol because the azeotrope mixture will distil off the other unwanted substances vapors. You could probably get it to 95% maybe!

Boilint Pt. Methanol = 64.7 C

Boiling Pt. Ethanol = 78.3 C
