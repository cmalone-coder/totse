Granted, there would certainly be people in various high-rises that could spot the thief, but...if this person had a mask and a good escape route...it'd be quite easy.

Late at night downtown, you can spot a car coming from a mile away...there are plenty of alleyways and such...

Anyways, has anyone ever done this? I'm not planning it of course, but I'd be interested in some stories.
