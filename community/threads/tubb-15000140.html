You know that your love and affection for him as well as his for you is impervious to cancer and anything else so fragile as the physical realm.

Whatever the two of you feel for one another will not die unless you choose to kill it or let it waste away. I still say Hi to Puddintame everytime I pass his grave out there at the end of the driveway where he used to sit and watch the world go by and jump down off the post to greet me the minute I pulled in the driveway.
Sometimes when I am reminded of him and how much I miss his being there to be "just a part of whatever is going on" I say aloud, "I miss you Puddintame" and I do and it somehow warms my heart to speak it because I feel all the love and hours and years that he was there right with me, and I know that is true and I love him just as much. It would be an insult to his furry loyalness to suppose that he was not also capable of transcending the physical if he wished.

Anyway it occured to me that day in the plane that they were all waiting there in queque for their turn to cross the sky as an ever changing playful, lazy, silly, whatever cloud. It was such a clear and totally novel thought that I didn't even take it as something I'd imagined, but more like something I was thankfully lucky enough to have witnessed and realized.
I'm sorry that you and your dog will have to miss each other.
In a way the closer you are to a soul while you are both together in life, the less far away they seem after death.
Another thing is that I have found pets have a wonderfully understanding and forgiving nature about them, they seem just as interested in your love and affection not being negated by death as they did in seeking it out whether or not they always got it in life. sometimes I think they are most proud to be yours when your feelings show so strongly in death. Just cause they're not right there doesn't mean that there not going "alllright! allright! he likes me he really likes me! haugh haugh drool haugh, stuff like that, cats aren't always so openly gluttonous about their affection for affection, then again I guess some dogs aren't either. In any event in such an uncertain thing as existence and all that we do not understand or have proof to believe in
don't close the door on the continued reciprocation of love and whatever you call it when someone wants to be near you just because.....That would be messed up! You can try checking the clouds, I hope that if you do he'll be there, just being himself.

I like animals so much better than people sometimes, alot...
