Basing with lye
A while ago I had a dream in which, while railing my way through a couple of grams of bolivias finest I got an urge for a couple of hits of the hard stuff, and being an "infrequent flyer", I did not have soda or ammonia handy, never mind ether. What I did have was some caustic soda from a soapmaking kit, and after slapping down the little voice that warns me of dumb ideas with potentially disasterous results I threw a couple matchheads of the lye into a test tube and dissolved it, the mixture was slightly warm and quite slipprery indicating high ph. I dumped in a few rails worth of blow and swirled the mix in some hot tapwater. At first I thought nothing was happening, as no visible reaction was occuring, the tube held just a few tiny white dots, and something that distorted the water a little. I cooled the mix and swirled it around a few times and lo and behold a few small white balls appeared, looking for all the world like styrofoam nuggets. I rinsed them well, and dumped them out onto a coffee filter to dry them. I hesitated before smoking any, going so far as to crush a tiny piece in my mouth to see if I could could taste anything nasty.

The first hit was great, it dissolved into the ash instantly and I exhaled a cloud of very tasty smoke much larger than I anticipated. After a second larger hit is when things got a little wierd, I felt very flushed and when I looked in the mirror I was indeed quite red. I cant say if any of the further symptoms were because of the drug or the abject paranoia I felt, but things got unpleasant fast, causing a rare event, I
put away the rest of the blow and after several cigarettes, whiskies,and hot showers I went to sleep.

So my questions are;
a; has anyone tried this?
b; do you think there was residual lye in what I smoked,and could it have caused the flushing?
c; could the lye have changed the structure of the cocaine a little?, it did react off the hcl ion without causing any release of gas which I found strange.
thanks and cheers
co

Lye doesn't boil easily at all. In fact, I think you'd have to thermally reduce it to metal to boil it... which doesn't happen very easily at all.

Quote:
Originally Posted by culinaryoverlord
and could it have caused the flushing?
You realize that the core nucleus of cocaine is hydromethyleniacin methyl ester?

Not, mind you, that anyone really knows what that does...

Quote:
Originally Posted by culinaryoverlord
c; could the lye have changed the structure of the cocaine a little?
Yes, there's a risk that it lysed the pseudopeptide bond, creating sodium benzoate, and leaving you smoking ecogonovine.

This risk is slightly lower since I believe it's an organic-base-alcohol/acid ester, which is really fucking wierd, and also slightly lower since you heated briefly rather than in prolonged fashion... but, anything is possible.

If your coke was pharmeceutical, I'd say "evap the water"... but, well... hard to find shit in cut, even in good coke.

Quote:
Originally Posted by culinaryoverlord
it did react off the hcl ion without causing any release of gas which I found strange.
NaOH + HCl -> NaCl + H2O.

Fairly normal.
