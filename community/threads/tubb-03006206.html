start by teaching him a "leave it" and a "take it" command.

You do this by starting kneeling with him in front of him your hand in his collar. hold a treat in your other hand and keep a good hold on it, so he can't get it. say "leave it" (you can also use the commands "mine, yours") and as soon as he hesitates for even the slightest second say with enthusiasm "good leave it, "take it" and give it to him. *NOTE- THE FOOD MUST ALWAYS COME FROM YOUR HAND! NOT THE FLOOR OR ANYTHING.

Keep doing this until he's looking away, and even moving back a bit. If he sits or something else great!

after this a bit, you stay kneeling and then drop the food on the ground *NOTE- HE CANNOT GET THIS, MAKE SURE IT'S FAR ENOUGH AWAY. Wait till he hesitates, etc, like for holding it. always pick the food up from the ground and give it from your hand!

Keep this up for a week, then email me for progression.

Re: Training Dogs
quote:
Originally posted by Chunkmiester:
Its annoying because they don't let you see what they do, you have to wait outside whilst they "work their magic".


I would be very skepticle of this. If they are true professionals then there would be no reason why you can't be present! There are dog behaviourists you can go to. But for a high energy dog that wont do much.

Dogs wont completely tire with just physical activity, they need to be mentally stimulated as well. This can tire them out more then the previous. A few short training sessions throught the day, 5-15 mins is all it takes. After each training session play with your dog for equal amount of time. Not only does this reinforce that training is a good thing (Hey, I do what my owner wants and get treats that way, PLUS I get to play afterwards! OH BOY!!) They will become more attentive for training sessions.

Also you can teach a "settle" command. Do this by first teaching a good "down" (lie down, keep it to one word. it's a lot easier to say "down" in a jam, then it is to say "lie down". Same goes with sit!. Also, if you have a dog that jumps up. Use the command "off" instead of down, otherwise you'll be using one command for multiple behaviours!)..anyway..

When your dog is in a down (you can do this while watching tv or something) just pet him. keep it relaxing. If he keeps wanting to get up, then have his dinner rations there, and reward him every few seconds for staying down. Slowly work on getting him on his side. This is important for the settle. As soon as he starts going on his side more reliably, you can add the command "settle", or "relax", anything you want to use.

When this is reliable, you can use it whenever he's getting too pumped.

Also with a completly hiperactive dog, and nothing works, get his glands and thyroid checked. It could be a bigger problem!
