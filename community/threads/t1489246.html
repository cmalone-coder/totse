The chemical name for the injection is Depo-Provera.

I was on it for 9 months, myself, and didn't really like it. I'd been on the pill for a while and wanted to try to the injection, since the pill (at the time) was about $25 per month and the injection was $40 for three months.

Anyway, with the injection, you kind of flat-line emotionally. Never happy, never sad, and never aroused either. It eventaully made me quite depressed.

So I finally asked myself - what's the point of making myself infertile if I never wanna have sex??? And I went back on the pill.

As far as other side effects go - some women bleed for a long time (like a month straight) when they start. For me - I never got my period the entire time I was on the injections. THAT was the best damn part of the whole thing.
