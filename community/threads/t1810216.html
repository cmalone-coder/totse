No, I'm not being sacreligious, because I don't think there really is one. But I was thinking about the Bible, and how it describes God. Well, humans create their own reality through perception, we gave ourselves free will to do what we want, nobody can take it from us. Bad actions create negative feelings and emotions for us, like sending ourselves to hell, and vice-versa. I know there are points in the Bible that say things like god forbids you to eat the meat of cloven animals, but I'm not going into that, just touching off on the basics all people know. Faith in God is faith in ourselves, we pray, but we ourselves make our prayers come true through our own action or the actions of others. I just think it's interesting how the major monotheistic religions can be interpreted through an allegory such as this one.
