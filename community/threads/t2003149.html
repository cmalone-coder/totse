Forums being indexed by google?
I am not sure if anybody has said something about this before, so apologies if they have.

But doesn't anybody else have a problem with google indexing all of our posts now? And every single post we have EVER made being in the archive, which is also indexed in google?

Perhaps I am just being a little paranoid, but one of the things I used to like about totse was the fact you could post on the forums and they wouldn't be indexed in google, so somebody couldn't do a search and come up with everything you had posted from google. If anybody wanted to search the forums the search would have to originate from totse.com forum search engine.

There are a lot of problems with this.

People often post silly admissions to crimes in several forums on totse - bad ideas, bb, bltc, labtips, ect. and we all know that police do their investigations through a simple google search to try to find people involved, ect. in crimes. A lot of the time the people have no involvement, they are just trying to act 'cool' on the forums, nonetheless this just brings unnecessary heat to the boards.

You may say 'good, that means that only the guilty have something to worry about'... but that kind of mentality contradicts the freedoms of totse. It is like saying CCTV cameras aren't a problem if you are a normal, law abiding citizen.

For all the paranoid; if they delete their posts on totse it won't do any good, google has already indexed and cached their posts.

What about all the posts with personal details in them? Often things will not be picked up by a mod in time and somehow will slip through. In the past these posts would be gone in a few weeks so it didn't really matter, now they will stay in the archives forever.

What about legal implications for things that are posted? I could go in to a range of hypothetical situations that would result in legal problems for both totse and individual posters. I don't like the idea that there is now a permanent, searchable archive of this.

Totse is hardly a "safehaven for visionaries and paranoids" at the moment.

I wouldn't have a problem with this on any other forum but totse, I just don't think it is a very good idea for a forum of this nature, especially the archive of older posts.
