Re: Partying in Europe
You are going to be a lot poorer by the end of your holiday, as traveling around Europe is often more expensive than the cost you might think because public transport (where it exists) and general cost of living is high. Going to Ibiza is certainly a fun time if you have a real passion for dance music/clubs etc but there's nothing there which mainland Spain hasn't got.

You may want to re think the Berlin - Stuttgart - Amsterdam leg of your trip as you will be doubling back on yourself.

As far as drinking/partying goes you should have no problems with getting age checked in Spain, Germany and Netherlands. If you have a fake ID then dope in NL too. You may have some problems in Northern Ireland but I suppose you could always fake ID your way in.

Stay clear of England/Scandinavia as you all your cash will go down the drain there.

If you want to party attempt to learn a bit of the local lingo.

I live a bit further east than the places you want go to but rest assured that I know Europe fucking well and think your trip is okay. Also, unless you're staying with friends throughout you will need a bona fide 18+ ID to book accommodation. Good luck
