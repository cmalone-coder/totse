Re: Banks in america
Quote:
Originally Posted by Haircut Shoes
I have a really DUMB question, please don't mind me. If a bank was to close with all my funds in it, what would happen to my money? Would they send me a check or something?
Chances are that you might not even know about it, initially. As long as you are within the insured limit (which is $100,000 per beneficiary, with a few other loopholes), then you're fine. They won't send you a check. You'll keep the same account information, but it will be under the new name of whatever bank took over your current bank or whatever resulted from the government seizure. Everything will still work the same way, you can still go to the same branches, and you can probably still use the same checks, even with the old bank name, for a while.
