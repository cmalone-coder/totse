This works:

Ratio for nano rp/i

Pseudo(ephedrine)=1

RP(really clean mbrp)=0.6 (soso clean)=1.0

Iodine (from 2%tincture)=1.5

decent small rb boiling flask (500ml)

heating mantle would be nice, but oil bath on hot plate will do.

Swigotta is quite fond of his mantles, controllers, and boiling flasks (rb=round bottom). My wife picked them up for him at Christmas time. He holds them quite dear and says he could not do without them. The mantles were a steal at 40 bucks (for both) and the rb boiling flasks are rare 500ml. single neck 10/?, I forget.......but they are very small necked flasks. They are absolutely perfect for performing nanos with balloon condensers.

large balloon (the kind with a rubberband attached for kids to bounce it on their hand)

twist ties and don't forget DH2O, the D means distilled, NO SUBSTITUTES.



Procedure

Make sure that your vessel (whatever it is that you plan to cook in) is as clean and dry as you are able to get it.

Add RP to flask followed by pseudo(ephedrine) both chopped up (rp ground up finely).

The rp is important to be ground up as finely as possible, the pseudo is not.



Add DH2O, amount depends on who you ask. You wanna go dry, use .5 ml. per gram of Iodine. You wanna go long and wet, use 1.0ml. per gram of Iodine.

Yes, I said per gram of iodine. Iodine is the primary controlling factor in the production of HI. Many cooks prefer to add the iodine in increments to insure even production of HI. These little modifications to the method will be added in as you become more comfortable and knowledgable of the reaction. There are many subtle nuiances that you will discover that will help you successfully achieve better quality and more complete reactions in the future. For now Keep It Simple Stupid.

Oh, yeah. There is really no such thing as the short, hot cook. I just added that in for those who advocate that kind of thing. After some experience under your belt, you can decide for yourself, but for now, know that the rection requires water and time to complete. Excessive heat causes side reactions that may get you high, but doesn't come from meth. Swigotta has received criticism for mixing all the reactants together. The actual reduction of the pseudo is done with 57% or higher HI or hydrioic acid. There are those who say thatit is necessary to make the hydriotic acid first, then add the pseudo for it to reduce. My point is, "If it is necessary to add more rp and possibly more iodine (depending on your personal preferences) why go through the trouble of making the HI in a separate step? Just to make sure the % is high enough? or there is enough water? I don't know why I'm even adding this, I don't really know the answer to these two questions.

I do know this: It is both possible and practical to add all the ingredients to the reaction at the same time. For a first timer, it is almost necessary. Sorry for the long interuption. I'll get back to the procedure now.



Mix.

Get your balloon ready and add the Iodine (dry iodine EXTRACTED and very DRY) to the mix.

If done properly you should get a rxn producing white smoke relatively soon, so get that balloon attached. Secure the balloon with twist ties tightened tightly around balloon and flask.

Cook it slowly (if long, slow is chosen) with only a few bubbles on surface of liquid, for about 12 to 30 hrs. Longer the better. If short is chosen, bubbles should cover the surface, not too vigorous, and cook for at least 3-6 hrs.

The smoke can be brown, red, yellow, as long as you get some kind of smoking reaction within about 45 seconds after adding and mixing (by slapping the flask against the side of your hand) in the iodine. Swigotta has increased the temp since he started to the area closer to 100C. He does this slowly over a period of 4 hours. If he has any trouble with the balloon expanding too fully, he simply removes the flask from the heat for am =minute or two, and loosens the ties on the balloon. Then he lets some air out of the balloon. Then he resecures everything and puts it back on the heat immediately. This keeps the balloon from breaking so easily. This is also a good time tojudge the amount of water you may want to add or iodine an even rp. I put it in here anyway, but that comes later.
