I did it in 2005. Best summer of my life.

I plan on taking weed, alcohol, oxy, e, shrooms, nitrous, poppers, poppies, and any of the 2c_ compounds if I can find some.

Acid. Acid acid acid acid acid acid acid.

What I want to know is which drugs can I use in combination, which I should stay away from mixing with, and roughly how long I should wait in between certain drugs to get the most out of everything.

I wouldn't mess with anything like benzos, coke, meth, etc. You don't need to build an addiction on this trip.

I highly recommend getting a hold of LSD, MDMA, Ketamine, and DMT. They're all very easy to conceal in a car. I open up the bag part of a cd case, where the disc holder and the back are connected. You can stuff a good deal of stuff in each case. Then just put the case back together and there ya go. Make sure you have a whole bunch of cds with you, though.

And how do you go about getting these drugs?

Music festivals. Mark it on your calendar and try to go to Summer Camp, All Good, Rothbury, and Gathering of the Vibes. If you can, end this roadtrip at Burning Man. You'll find any drug you could possibly want, and that's no exaggeration. The drugs are cheap, and most importantly the drugs are good. Done correctly, this summer will be the best one of your life and change you permanently and positively profound.

As said above, the law enforcement is very lax at music festivals. It's very easy to spot out a narc, and to be honest I've never seen any undercovers and I never saw anyone getting busted. A big reason for this is because a lot of festivals are WAY far out in the boonies. All Good is in the middle of nowhere in West Virginia. As far as you can see all you can see are mountains. The closest town is about 20 minutes away and has a population of 800, and they support the fest (economic burst.) All you have to worry about is leaving. The cops are swarming like angry bees in a 100 mile radius. They know they can't do anything AT the fest, so they do 99% of the busts on the highways around.

Drive safe. Stick to the EXACT speed limit, and wear your seat beats. Make sure you have no taillight problems and that your tags aren't expired. Don't even throw a cigarette butt out the window. Some douchebag cops might consider that 'littering' just to get a reason to pull you over. I didn't carry weed on me, just bought and smoked at the fests. If they want to search, ask them why. You don't have to give consent. Also, make up some bullshit excuse like you have to be at work tomorrow afternoon and you'd like to get home as quickly as you can.
