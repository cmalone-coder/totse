Environmental Web
Useful weblinks for environmental knowledge.:cool: This is not for specific articles. Only post links to the homepage of environmentally oriented sites.

http://www.realclimate.org/
RealClimate is a commentary site on climate science by working climate scientists for the interested public and journalists.

http://www.crisisgroup.org/home/index.cfm?l=1
International Crisis Group. "An independent, non-profit, non-governmental organisation, with some 130 staff members on five continents, working through field-based analysis and high-level advocacy to prevent and resolve deadly conflict."

http://www.wri.org/
World Resources Institute. An environmental think tank which engages in research of environmental problems and discovering solutions to them.

http://nrdc.org/
National Resource Defense Council. Initiates lawsuits to force companies, agencies, etc to comply with environmental regulations.

http://www.environmentaldefense.org/home.cfm
Another org. that initiates lawsuits. "We find innovative, practical ways to solve the most urgent environmental problems."

http://www.nwf.org/
National Wildlife Federation

http://www.epa.gov/
Environmental Protection Agency

http://www.ciesin.columbia.edu/
Center for International Earth Science Information Network at Columbia University. "works at the intersection of the social, natural, and information sciences, and specializes in on-line data and information management, spatial data integration and training, and interdisciplinary research related to human interactions in the environment."

http://www.ucsusa.org/
Union of Concerned Scientists. "UCS stands out among nonprofit organizations as the reliable source for independent scientific analysis."

http://www.ipcc-data.org/ddc_about.html
Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change.

http://www.enn.com/
Environmental News Network. Provides current information on environmental news.

http://www.eere.energy.gov/
US Department of Energy--Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy.

http://sierraclub.org/
Non-profit organization. "The Club is America's oldest, largest and most influential grassroots environmental organization."

http://www.noaa.gov/
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. "A federal agency focused on the condition of the oceans and the atmosphere."
