LSD and Me: What the hell happened?
Monday at around noon a few friends and I dropped, one of them for the first time. I took 4 hit, most I've done, and proceeded to trip pretty well. We were all having a good time sitting around playing with bouncy balls and Super Smash Bros. We decided to go on a bike ride to the park. Once there I remember lights being very bright, I figured this was just because of my pupils being so dilated. All of a sudden everything was white and I could feel the acid very strongly, figured I was peaking and went to go sit down on a bench with my friends. The next thing I remember is being on a gurney with paramedics around. According to my friend I turned blue and all my muscles tensed up completely and he had to give me some sort of CPR after which I coughed up a bunch of shit. I'm pretty sure they didn't give me any kind of anti-psychotic but they did give me some sort of depressant. My discharge papers say that I had Tachycardia which can be caused by excessive stimulants but I had one cigarette that day and the acid. What the hell happened and what should I be concerned about? I went to my doctor yesterday and they took a blood test so hopefully I'll find out if my heart is about to give out or something but all the doctors told me was it was probably made in someone's toilet!

Totse, what the hell happened to me?
