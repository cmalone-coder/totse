here is some more info on orange gak1 and orange gakk2 from ware over at CH2. thanks again ware & CH2!!!

The color signs to look for concerning orange gaaks will be found in the polar layer when basing.

OrangeII gaak will turn the basified solution a rootbeer color.

OrangeI gaak will turn the basified solution bluish at around pH 13 then purple above pH 13.

Upon settling after ~24hrs, it will be noticed that the surface emulsion contains more color.

It's best to remove the colored scum(retain for disection) before proceeding or add 1ml or 2ml h2o2 to break UP and oxidize the emulsion. The h2o2 works best after salting the basified solution.

Re-add a dual NP and re-base solution to push the goodies UP.

When working UP gaaked post-rxn goodies, Ibee has had best results when using dual solvents.

The ultimate NP solution would be a 50/50 mixture of ether and naptha and this leaves room for a trisolvent concoction by adding a few ml tetra to the whole mess.

As long as toluene or xylene are not in the picture, a squirt or two of tetra into the polar layer may disable the encapsulators and help shove the gaak to the bottom and away from the amine.
