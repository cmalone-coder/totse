Re: 1% or 3.1% iodine make crystals?
Hello shooter

Tweekaneere, how about if we tweek those figures just a little. You want to use the minimum amount of H2O2 as possible to achieve the maximum amount of iodine available.

Now I don't claim to know the chemistry behind all this bullshit becuse I don't. What I do know is that too much H2O2 will cause the iodine to become sticky and wet looking when you are trying to dry it in the final filters. When you get the amount just right, you should get about a gram, per ounce of 2% tincture used, of silvery grey large flakey crystals that require less rinsing (although a half gallon is pretty good. Swigotta hates to skimp on the rinsing). They will also dry easier, leaving very little blue or black on the final filters. The resultant iodine, after being squeezed out with all the pressure you can muster, (stepping on it works good)should look like a dry grey rock. Break this up with a plastic or glass pointey object before you "bell jar" it in the freezer for a couple of hours. Then, when put in a jar and stored inside another jar with epsom salt, it will stay dry and crumbly for a while. I don't know how long, but so far a couple of months is for sure.

Damn ramblin on again.

Think about these numbers.

For that 7%, try doin it the Geez way. Add the H2O2 slowly, over a period of five minutes while stirring. Use 2 pints of H2O2 instead of three. I don't know about this personally as I don't get to do the 7% thing, but Geez is a very reliable old fart and he is quite confident that you will achieve better results with these simple modifications.

The reason 1% won't work is because of all the alcohol. Some part of the iodine or all of it or some shit dissolves in alcohol. Since it is an alcohol tincture, very low concentrations cannot overcome the solvent effect of the alcohol and therefore cannot fall out of the solution. Also, lower %'s ned more washing.

2% calculations-

Muritic acid -25% of total iodine tincture volume.

H2O2 -50% tincture volume

DH2O -200% tincture volume

Use even more distilled water to make sure they are very clean. They won't be as easy as that 7% was though.

One more thing. Swigotta likes to put th iodine crystals in the freezer during the oxidation stage (after you finish adding the peroxide) and leaving it for 4-6 hours. It wshes easier this way.

Oh, and don't try to evap off that alcohol unless you are prepared for the iodine to sublime. Subliming it is the best way, but I am not that patient and also won't comment on things not tried.
