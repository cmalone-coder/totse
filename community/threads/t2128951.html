I think you might be misinterpreting what he meant (not that I'd know, but I sort of doubt that that's what a person would mean by that). He may have thought you meant "could you have had as good of a time on that specific night without x?" in which case his answer isn't that shitty. I mean, "best night of my life" is a pretty good evaluation. People often have "good nights" but are wary of saying it was the best of their life, because "best" implies that it was outstanding/ head and shoulders above anything in recent memory.

One of the good things about x is it can take something that would normally only be "good" and make it that much better. All he could have meant is that if he didn't take x he wouldn't have had as good of a night as he did. I don't think it necessarily means he's incapable of having a "best night of his life" without being on x, just that it may have made it that much better in this particular case.

I've had a "best night of my life" partially due to the influence of MDMA, before. I don't think that's screwed me up or made me set the bar too high, though. I still think I'm capable of having an awesome time without it... just on that one occasion the MDMA just happened to make something that was already good that much better.
