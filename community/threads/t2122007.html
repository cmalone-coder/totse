Re: what acid did for you, in the long run
The first time I dropped acid was my introduction into psychedelia, and one of the first times I looked at myself objectively. I remember being disappointed, sitting on my couch watching the door change sizes and kind of glow, and thinking, "It's just another drug." disappointedly after all of the wonderful visions and revelations I'd had during the night. I changed my mind the next morning, and felt an immense motivational boost for a few days.

I did a much higher dose a week or two later, and it blew my head open. I was in a calm, perfect state for a long time, and got re-dosed that night with a couple friends at my house and watched the electric visuals for a few hours.

I'd comment on how these changed me more, but I think most of what they did was push me down the psychedelic path a little and got me away from the pharmecuticals and hard shit I had been playing with. All I'd ever really wanted to do was acid and mushrooms anyways, but all I could find was pot.

I ended up doing a lot of mescaline over the course of six months in the form of San Pedro cactus, usually eaten raw. I ended up tripping three or four times a week for a month or two. I came out of it with a few mental peculiarities that straightened themselves out.

I haven't touched anything hard in about four months, for lack of desire, and I'm not a judgmental know-it-all prick anymore. I resumed my studies of mysticism and meditation, and am actually making progress. I'm not angry and cynical and spiteful anymore, but I still have self-confidence and the ability to defend myself. I'm happy for once too, and getting ready to get into college.

I guess acid more started the chain than anything...

I want to do acid, but with shrooms and ketamine (So many fucking times) I've realized what they did to me.

They let me take a step back and look at the painting.
Then to swim through each brush stroke.

After that it was up to me to do what I did with the perspectives I experienced during the trips. I had many spiritual experiences and I got to re-experience the unfiltered mind of my inner child.
I had been a lucid dreamer for years and was very much into nature since a tiny child and I think that helped transition me into these perspectives.

I think the biggest changes took place in between the trips and in a sense they were much more powerful emotionally and spiritually.
Like sitting at the edge of the river near my house and all of a sudden being nothing but everything, knowing nothing was different from what I would have previously called "me" and "the frog" on "the rock". I was never the same after that.
There were a few times like this, one of them I got laughed at for the tears, of happiness, on my face in a science classroom when I was looking at bacterium in a microscope. Nothing could ever be more beautiful, then that one little living thing but the point was again that nothing could be more beautiful because everything is the same. I never gave an explanation for the tears, it didn't bug me.

Now I glow at the sign of any life, from grass and trees to the bacterium in that microscope. I'm much less depressed on an ongoing basis, no longer take my anti-depressants or lithium and am doing great (Though I am on Adderall), and I stay away from pretty much every drug other then dissociative and psychedelic drugs.

Everything life changing really was just me experiencing things while sober and being able to link those feeling to the similar feelings on the drugs.

In summary: Because of my own doing, I am now much more spiritual, happy, aware, thoughtful, appreciative of all forms of life and can now see the big picture anytime I want really (Does not = experience the big picture, that's rare).
