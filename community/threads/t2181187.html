How is the a/c set up in your building/room? Is there a unit in each room, or a central unit in the building with vents running to each room, or what?

Remember, in order to ventilate a room you have to have air flow out and air flow in.

I think the best thing to do is put a fan in the window, blowing out and well sealed, and leave the door unblocked.
That way, the fan will create a negative pressure in the room relative to the rest of the building which will cause air to be sucked through the cracks in the door (& other crevasses) and make it impossible for the smokey air to leave the room through anywhere but out the window, unless it's windy outside and air is being forced into the window.

i dunno if it was mentioned, but one thing i found that helps, even if you're going to plug the door cracks, is to figure out which way the air is moving. hold a lighter next to the door crack and see which way the flame blows. the reason this is good to do, even if you seal the door, is if you miss a spot, if the air is blowing out, it'll still go through that hole. if it's blowing in, you wouldn't have to be AS careful when sealing the door.

in terms of how to seal it, magnetic strips is a good idea, something i've though of doing myself as well. rather than putting a tarp over the entire door, maybe cut it in a large upside down U shape and just put it around the frame. maybe use both magnets and velcro on the door and just magnets on the doorframe. that way the door can still be opened and the tarp stays on the door, but it can also all be removed.
