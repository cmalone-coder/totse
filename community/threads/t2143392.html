You might be immensely surprised by the type and amount of valuables in a church. You're not going to find gold, cash, and jewels. But you probably will find electronics.

I've done IT work at a couple of local churches and these places had massive digital projectors, high-end keyboards to replace their pipe organs (especially so in newer churches that never had pipe organs to begin with), audio and mike equipment, modern computers, camcorders and digital cameras lying around, etc. And churches that run "youth center" programs are likely to have one or two game consoles lying around, probably TV's, and in one case I saw a church with a Street Fighter arcade cabinet...
