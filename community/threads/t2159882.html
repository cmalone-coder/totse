Re: Quiting Weed
any help on how to stay sober?
I recommend you don't bother "quiting" weed as such, just cut back to start. As other posters in this thread said weed isn't addictive, and it isn't, any more than WoW is. What you've got to do is buy a half O (or whatever amount you feel will last you a month at the pace you want) and make it last a month. If it doesn't just don't buy any more. Repeat for month 2, 3, etc, until you feel like doing something different.

Don't buy any other weed, just that half O. For special occasions take out a joint, for day to day type shit just smoke a small bowl (invest in a pipe or something if you don't have one) if you're bored. I don't know how much you're smoking but I cut back from 1/8-1/4 per day to a joint or two tops. Quiting cold turkey is gay, and ultimately futile unless you've got hobbies or some shit.
