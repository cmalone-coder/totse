The petrodollar and The Iranian threat?
Iran has been trading in Euros with China since 2003 and has announced it will resume world oil trade in Euros very soon.

How do you think this will undermine the petrodollar and the US oil markets?

It is no secret that shortly before Iraq was named as a target it announced its oil trade will be resumed in Euros instead of dollars, meaning that the US would need to sell items to Europe in exchange for Euros to buy its oil as the US banks can nor print Euros.

Now we have a system where the US is able to buy from other nations, selling in Dollars and it can print its own currency, known as the petro dollar cycle, in the end the dollars get back to the US.

Also, how do you think ever since the Saudi deal that the world has coped with world oil trade, prior to this deal prices were as low as $2 a barrel and now look what we have.

Do you think it is a coincidence that Iraq, and now Iran have both been in the spot light a lot more, they seem to wedge Iran into every threat we hear now.......now that they have announced (like Iraq before) that they will sell in PetroEuro and not PetroDollar?
