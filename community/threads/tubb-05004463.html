One of the best things to do is to make a vaporizer. You won't be inhaling smoke, just the THC.

Get a lightbulb. Take the metal piece off. Put the bud inside the lightbulb. Get an empty 2-liter bottle. Poke a hole in it and put in a pen or something to suck through. Put the bulb in the top of the bottle where the soda comes out. Now, hold the lighter under the bulb heating the glass. And suck through the straw. This will allow you to suck in the invisible vapors.

This method also produces no smell so you can do it in your room.
