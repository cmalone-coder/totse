MDMA thank you for the apology.

This setup will work for vac distillation, however the aspirator will work for most vac needs but it won't build up enough vacuum to distill your ketone. You need a mechanical pump of some sort.

Another note, while using a graham condenser in a distillation setup you must setup for downwards distillation otherwise the condenser will block itself and cause sputtering and backing up, if not something worse.
