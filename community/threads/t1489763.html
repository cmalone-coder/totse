Just because she blindly followed after him for however long doesn't mean he's obligated to keep his eyes on one place.

His journal is private. Your mother should have never opened it in the first place. Raping his mind is just as bad as him using her. Fact is, he felt unsure of what he should do. And has been said, it's impossible to gauge a man based on one page he writes in his journal. Perhaps his day was extra emotional...or down, or something.

Throw out the journal entry, because as much as it affects the emotions, its accuracy is up for debate. Instead, focus on what he has actually done to her.

That's your best bet.
