yeah meta knows what he's talking about. fuck using methadone to quit, methadone isn't a short term program, all it is, is a legal means, a substitution to your current problem. instead of buying illegal drugs for your fix, you hit the clinic. methadone is still an opiate and still a source of addiction/withdrawls so unless you are just tired of the daily hustle to get off ill and are okay with being an addict, then fuck using methadone.

people who go onto methadone end up using more of that shit than they did of heroin/oxy/whatever. i have a friend who was using maybe a quarter gram of china dope a day ($50ish) and now he's on like 150mg of methadone a day. he's got more opiates in his system daily than he did as a common street junky, methadone programs disgust me. you are no better than you were when you decided to clean yourself up.

heroin withdrawls are much much more tolerable and manageable than that of methadone or many other pharms.

here's what you ought to do. go buy whatever amount of black you can afford. if you are IVing it, then i guess continue to IV but if you smoke it, then don't progress any further into IVing. wait as late into the day as you can, then do the smallest amount you can to get off dope sick. most of the time you'll end up doing it way earlier than you need or way more than you need, but if you are sincerely trying to clean your act up then be a minimalist.

after you do your tiny dose to stay off ill, hide the shit for the rest of the day. that night about an hour before you're going to sleep do another dose and again just enogh to stay off ill.

next day, wake up, wait as late into the day as you can before you decide to fix up again and do yet again a minimal dose. wait until bed time, repeat process.

do this for like 3-4 days then eventually cut out the daily dose and make it only your sleeping dose. after another 3-4 days cut your sleeping dose in half to where you are doing a spec of dope. after a day or two of that cut everything out and you should be gravy. the worst will be a day of mild withdrawls, maybe 2 but it'll be so mild you'll think you're catching a cold.

the key is to consistently ween, don't get caught up in the same size doses daily or you're not helping yourself. and i know how hard it is to only use when you feel the dope sickness coming on and i know how hard it is to only do a dose big enough to get off ill. the mind of a junky is universal, we've all been through these struggles and the only thing that will get you through to the other side, the successful side is inner strength and will power. it never hurts to have a support system though as well for your moments of weakness.

so in short-- ween, only use small doses 1-2 times daily to stay away from being sick and then once to sleep. minimalist doses. and fuck methadone!

edit as a quick reference for going cold turkey or not tapering properly this is what you can expect-

Black Tar withdrawls=
day 1. hell- cold sweats, chills, goosebumps, the whole nine yards. no sleep for sure
day 2. same thing
day 3. a lesser extent of those and you'll probably catch some sleep
day 4. starting to feel better
day 5. feeling either normal or almost normal
day 6. feeling completely normal.

basically 2-4 days of withdrawls.

Methadone withdrawls-
about 2 weeks of absolute hell followed by another week of mild recovery. so about 20+ days of getting back to normal.

methadone is absolute hell honestly. if you feel you need to use something other than tar, dillys or done to get yourself back on track, consult a doctor, try getting subutex or suboxone. they will only give you a script for a month (it's a short term program unlike methadone), so you have to follow their instructions otherwise you will withdrawl. the way they have subuxone treatment set up is it's so gradual over a month or so that by the time your script ends you were hardly taking it anymore so you don't even notice.
