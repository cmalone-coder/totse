My advice is this: don't ask on totse.
Everything you've been told thus far sucks, aside Psionicist telling you to buy picks and learn to use them.

- Thermite will ruin anything useful inside the safe if you use enough to burn through. If you use too little, it'll just weld it shut making it worse. Furthermore, it takes a shitload of it to burn through a lock so you can't just "put a little in the lock". Not to mention the massive light show it puts on for everyone to see. Beginner's advice: never take anyone who touts thermite seriously. It's an instant kewl alert.

- Napalm doesn't burn metal. Even if you somehow managed to fill an entire building with it so it produced enough heat to actually... ah hell you can tell where this is going. Just no.

- You don't need a black light to see fucking pencil lead. You tap some graphite powder onto a keypad, shift it around, then gently blow it off to reveal the used keys in the combination. Depending on the safe brand, you can determine how many keys to punch in.

- You don't pick master locks with soda cans. You make shims from the soda cans and attempt to release the shackle with them. Completely different art.

Nobody serious about B&E is going to be around &TOTSE because it's full of kewls, cops, and regular old idiots. The few that are able to help you have long left, and if they do happened to come back to browse, they'll probably feel that helping you is a waste of time. So go elsewhere for your B&E info, like roguesci.org at the very least.
