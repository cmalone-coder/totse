Re: Benzos VS Opiates
In a very simple nutshell, without going into the Cl- channels, dopamine systems, glutamate and what-not....

Benzos are basically anything that ends in "pam" or "am" in the generic name.
Midazolam, Clonazepam (Klonopin), Alprazolam (Xanax)

Benzos act on the GABA receptors, much like alcohol.
They make you feel like everything is "ok", and some make you feel drunk. Blackouts are common at high doses. And these should not be mixed with alcohol, as this may result in death.... though if done right can be fun.

Opiates, from "semi, fully and non-natural" sources typically have the "-ine or -one" ending
Morphine, Codeine (Leen aka purple drank), Diacetylmorphine (heroin), Acetyldihydrocodeine, Hydrocodone (Vicodin) Oxycontin (Oxycodone)

Opiates mimic natural body "painkillers" called endorphins, by doing so they act on the same receptors as the endorphins and thus release pleasurable signals in the dopaminic system.

Fun Facts:
1) W/D from Opiates hurt because your body slows/stops its production of natural pain killer (endorphins).

2) During an opiate high your pupils will become constricted.

3) During the opiate high you will have trouble taking a piss.

4) Benzos come in a assortment of "flavors" primarily due to differentiating binding affinities on subtreceptors found on the GABA-A receptors.

5) As opposed to opiate W/D, which cant in itself kill you, Benzo W/D can.

6) Benzo W/D can result in seizures, extreme nervousness, and the feeling that cats are scratching at the insides of your soul.... you cant find relaxation or peace.

*W/D means withdrawal


Final Note of Safety:
Dont mix any of the 3 together unless you know EXACTLY what you are doing: OPIATES, BENZOS, ALCOHOL (and other downers).
