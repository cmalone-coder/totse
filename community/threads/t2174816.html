MAO inhibitors do cause fatal reactions with various drugs. Meth and MDMA are among those. Jeez, it's really not much to expect for people to know this, you should be expected to know that much.... DON'T take E with an MAOI! it will create a massive surge of serotonin (essentially an untreatable and persisting form of serotonin syndrome) that will raise body temperature (serotonin regulates/increases body temp) causing your organs to break down and for you to die!

Similar deal with meth, only dopamine is involved instead. The high levels of dopamine cause a spike in blood pressure, due to MAO inhibition, that could quite easily kill someone.

MAOI's are srs bsness. Deadly interractions seem quite the common affair when dealing with them. That's one reason why MAOI's aren't used that often in treating depression or other commonplace things. They can be quite effective, there are just too many potential side effects for ordinary people to bother understanding all the considerations.

Think: the way MAOI's work is by inhibiting the breakdown of neurochemicals. In treating depression, we feel better because certain 'feel good' chemicals are kept around in the brain longer than normal, and so naturally there is an associated moodlift with taking these drugs. MAO is an enzyme that breaks down our neurotransmitters in order to be eliminated from the synapse. MAOI's inhibit this enzyme, and thus inhibit the natural elimination of the brain's neurotransmitters. Cocaine and MDMA cause surges in certain neurotransmitters.... So if you have a huge influx of neurotransmitters, and no way to eliminate them... really it just sounds like a fatal combination.
