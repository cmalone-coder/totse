Store Returns for $Cash$
This I think would be a useful posting to have for reference purposes as well as collaborative efforts.

No one wants store fucking credit. Whether you bought something, stole something, or are broke and just want to return your damn own shit then I salute you and SWIM is here to help. I will try to update this particular posting with the most pertinent. Keep in mind your age when doing returns. This shit is damn near difficult if you are say under 16. For large money returns under 20 would be pushing it but that's just one person's opinion.

Some basic rules to get you started:
1) Receipts paid in cash are gold. Pick them up. Find them. Acquire them. Make them. Cash receipts = Cash in Hand. Credit card receipts only work if it's your card so don't really bother with them.

2) You are the boss. The employee is there to serve you. Don't volunteer information to them because it just makes a story into a tale. If they cannot help you then their manager can.

3) Teamwork does work. Having a friend to return something for a cut isn't a bad idea. Just don't use the same person often and try to make sure any people you use don't know each other.

4) You can return ANYTHING with a receipt. It's all how you act and play the role but I promise you SWIM has returned shit that's just mind blowing.




So you are broke and you have no money? That sucks First thing I would do in a situation like that is look around your house! Yes this is small fries for money but sometimes you need a pack of smokes or a case of beer. Most stores will payout cash if it is under 10$ in value and anything over that amount lands you store credit. These items here are especially good for the younger crowd getting funds for whatever.
Think any household item with a UPC on it (the barcode scanner thingy) and think price is right... How much is that dish washer liquid worth that I used about 3/4 of? Oh it's still worth about 7$ cash... Half eaten food etc it really doesn't fucking matter but obviously you would want to be tactile if you plan on doing business with the store in the future.

So what about those brand new things that you got as uh... gifts from uh... Aunt Mildew. So you have new shit to work with but no receipt? That's ok! A good story can sometimes get you the cash but it is quite rare. Check the price before you bring it in too... Why would you return something when it is CURRENTLY on sale and wasn't before? You will typically be asked when you purchased it and "it was a gift" is always the best policy. This will land you store credit but being polite can tend to land you cash or at least the max amount for the credit.

Now with credit in hand you still need cash... This is where you need receipts. You purchase an item that you have a receipt for. It doesn't necessarily need to be from the same store but it does have to be the same exact item. You can match the SKU (usually found on receipt) or the UPC.


I will touch on more later and edit this. There are some things in relation to acquiring the new merch etc but that's more theft related where this is a somewhat legitimate practice for changing store credit to cash. If people are more interested in a "Returns for Profit" then SWIM can go that route...
