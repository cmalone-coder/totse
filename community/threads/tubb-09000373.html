Ghost-like beings.
A friend of mine whom I have known for nearly three years may have a very interesting ability.

She says that the majority of people have a being, like a cross between a ghost and a guardian angel, that follows them around, and tries to look out for them. She says that when some people, often children, die, they latch onto someone that reminds them of a loved one, and stay with them for very long periods of time.

I can point out a person, and my friend can say right away whether they have one of these things, and if so, why they are attached to this person, and what they were like when they were alive. They move around like people do. According to my friend, I have a little boy following me around, who died young, and he is attached to me because I look like his older brother who he was very close to.
This is where it gets weirder. One time about 2 years ago me and several of my friends were out the back of a guys house smoking bud, when I saw something odd. I saw an old man in a dark grey or black suit standing against the wall behind this girl. I assumed I was just imagining it, and didnt tell anyone about it. About 6 months later, I asked my friend if this girl had one of these things, and she said "Yes, its an old man in a black tux, she reminds him of his granddaughter." Unless it was a coincidence, maybe when in altered states of mind 'normal' people can see them too.

I assure you that I am making up none of this, and that I have complete faith in my friend telling the truth. What I would like to know is whether anyone else either sees these things, or knows someone who does, or even has heard of them before. Thanks.

(Edit: If you have never heard of anything like this, dont hesitate to post that.)
