This is a simple case of the Rule of 13's. If your conscious mind notices a number a few times, your subconscious continues to look for it.

ex. "I saw the number 13 everywhere. My toaster has a 13 in it's serial number, I live on 01300 Anystreet, I just bought 13 cases of beer."

Basically the Rule of 13's is like self-fulfilling prophecies. You begin to look for something consciously or subconsciously, and you WILL find it.
