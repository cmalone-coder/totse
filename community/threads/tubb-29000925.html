Re: xplain something.
A carbine is basically a short rifle.

Bolt action refers to the type of gun that has a manually operated handle in line with the barrel. To load another round you move the bolt itself back, the old round pops out, a new round comes out of the magazine. Then when you push the bolt forward, the new round gets chambered and the bolt gets locked into place. You can usually tell these types of guns by the handle sticking out from the side of the gun, typically just above the firing hand.

The max effective range varies depending on the gun and ammo. There's guns that would kill out past 1000 yards easily, then there's some that wouldn't do it past 50 yards.

about the bolt action

in my opinion bolt action is no longer any more acurate than a high quality lever or pump action weapon. with that said there are several reasons snipers use bolt action.

first and foremost when a semi auto is used the round is automaticly ejected from the chamber. this is not a great feature when your trying to hide. it may seem insignificant to most people but to a trained counter sniper an enemy can be spoted when the shinny brass ejects.

secondly your weapon must be in tip top shape. bolt action rifles are by far the easiest to maintain and take down for cleaning. they are also inherently compact and durable because of the bolt design.

ergonomics also plays a role in the choice of bolt action. a bolt gun can be fired comfortably standing, sitting, or lying. a pump or lever gun cannot because it is far to dificult to chamber another round in the prone position.

also as it was mentioned above a bolt gun can chamber a more potent round without as much reliability concerns that other actions would face. they also retain accuracy longer than a semi auto because the slight shock of a round cycling in a semi auto may alter the scopes settings (even if it is ever so slightly, it adds up)

the list goes on and on why bolt is the superior action for any weapon not requiring sustained fire.
