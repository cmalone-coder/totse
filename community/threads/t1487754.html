2. To search a specific site, you just put "site:totse.com" after your keywords. For example, if you are searching for the NSA, you would say "NSA site:.gov" This will search only the .gov domain.

Here are some specific sites you should start with:

access.gpo.gov (example "NSA site:access.gpo.gov")

house.gov

senate.gov

3. Once you find things that are revelent, simply go to www.totse.com and click on the "Submissions" which is the last button on the left hand side, or the URL is: http://www.totse.com/bin/submit/submit.cgi

4. Paste the stuff you find into the forms, and hit submit! That's really it.

5. You can email me at:

jcstanton at totse dot com(anti-spam email address, I am sure you can figure out how to translate it into a real email address)
