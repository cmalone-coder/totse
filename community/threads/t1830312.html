Re: Making my Own flash/black powder
Rule of thumb...mesurements are always by weight and not by volume unless sepecifically stated. Also, since your new, start out with small batches.

An easy method of mixing flash is mesuring out a 7:3 ratio of KClO4 and Al, putting it in a large ziplock bag, spraying it with static gaurd, and shaking until uniform.

As for the Blackpowder, the general formula is 75% KNO3, 15% charcoal, and 5% sulfer, by weight. For best methods, ball mill these chemicals together for around 3-24 hours, or use the CIA percepitation method.

Re: Making my Own flash/black powder
You really shouldnt even be messing with flash powder OR M-80s, since you have very little knowledge of anything involving pyrotechnics. You really should start out making smoke bombs, and black powder salutes.

Before you mix the aluminum and KClO4 (potassium perchlorate) mix in a few grams of aluminum before mixing it all in, and press the lumps out. Its a lot faster, cheaper, and easier than a screen. Then you cam mix all them chemicals together.

Im against the diaper and baggie method. I dont trust that static stuff, so I just put the chemicals in a bowl, and mix them with a 3 foot long wooden dowel. If you plan on mixing with the baggie or diaper method, mix no more than 100 grams of flash at a time, and if you are mixing with at least a 3 foot long pole, you can go ahead and mix a MAXIMUM of 200 grams. Either way, static guard is a good idea, and leather gloves, long sleeve cotton shirts, and sunglasses are a must, since 1/8 teaspoon of flash powder is enough to damage your eyes, even a couple of feet away.

Re: Making my Own flash/black powder
When people warn you about not making salutes for your first project - they don't think youre incapable of mixing two chemicals together and gluing it together in a tube.

They're concerned about you not treating flash powder (a VERY SENSITIVE LE) with respect.

With flash, there is very little room for error, and by starting 'small' with black powder, you can learn proper safety procedure in mixing chemicals.

I believe that if you treat flash with respect, you can get away with it being one of your first projects.

ALWAYS use non-sparking materials when weighing or mixing flash.

Wear cotton clothing and either ground yourself or liberally spray yourself with static guard.



If your potassium perchlorate came chunky,

(like from the cheap bastards at UN that already gouged your eyes out with their prices)

press it through a kitchen sieve SEPARATELY, or just break the larger chunks with a plastic/wooden dowel.

The SAFEST way to mix flash is with the diaper method.

(If you're really concerned about safety, there's no reason to take a risk and use the easy way out with baggie mixing - no matter how small the risk.)

Weigh out your KClO4 and Al powder, and place them on a large sheet of newspaper.

Raise the corners and let the flash 'tumble' and mix until uniform.

You SHOULD spend about 10 minutes to fully mix the two, but I usually spend around 5 minutes mixing after I got the hang of the technique.

As long as you put safety as your #1 priority, salutes can be a very rewarding investment

The problem with mixing with a wooden dowel isn't a static risk-it's the same reason why you don't mill flash- the friction.

For the diaper method, just dump all of both chemicals in the middle of the sheet of paper.

Take two corners and center the powder so it doesn't come off the edge.

Take another corner up and let the powder roll down, ad infinitum.
