This sounds possibly like bad advice, but if you can handle something like this, you have one solid fucking relationship:

Sit down and talk with her about her past, ask her WHY she was with these people. Ask her WHY she chose somebody like you as opposed to the usual. Explain to her your fears in the relationship. If both partners know the fears that are there, they can BOTH work towards eliminating them.

You're afraid you're going to be hardworking then come home to see her fucking the pool boy, or some equivalent? You need to tell her about your insecurity, and explain why you have it, and why you want to rid yourself of it (from the sounds of it, you think she's a great person, so include that in your explanation if you follow this advice).

Opening up about your insecurities in a relationship to your partner is one of the hardest things to do, and can be extremely destructive to a lot of relationships, though the ones it destroys are the ones that are doomed to failure. If you two can work around insecurities and eliminate them, your relationship is going to be a great deal stronger. If you let them fester and just try to ignore them, there's going to be an unsaid aggression that will ultimately tear apart the relationship...maybe a week later, maybe a few months, maybe two decades...but the lack of communicating your fears and desires is a gauranteed way to cripple love and eventually shoot it in the face.
