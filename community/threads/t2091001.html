Puke. It's really the only way to get rid of it.

But if you want to stall it, and hope it will pass, these things always have helped me a bit:

- Lay down. But make sure you're propped up a little bit, I find that if you lay down flat, it just makes it worse.

- Talk to someone. If you are in silence, you end up thinking about how shitty you feel, and we all know what that leads to. :(

- Bread, bread, and more bread.

- Get comfortable. I know it's hard to get comfy when you have the spins, but god damn is it worth is when you finally get into that comfy spot.

- Despite what people say, DO not smoke weed after getting drunk. I live by 'grass before beer, in the clear; beer before grass, on your ass'

I know, everyone is different and these things might not help at all, but they're just some random suggestions.
