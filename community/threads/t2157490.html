The answer to tolerance and withdrawl? (all drugs)
So, for those who remember the Phenylalanine thread a while back, there was a lot of good info inside it. One item I just recently remembered was that magnesium was an important part of the formulation because it increased dopamine receptor sensitivity and prevented said receptors from cooking (excitotoxicity). Well, something caused me to remember this recently (crackhead tried to rob me) and so I decided to run a google on the benefits of good 'ol Mg++. It turned up this:

http://www.john-libbey-eurotext.fr/en/revues/bio_rech/mrh/e-docs/00/04/3B/7E/article.md

A basic summary would be to say that this is a compilation of a crapload of studies that say that INCREASING DIETARY MAGNESIUM PREVENTS TOLERANCE AND HELPS WITHDRAWL FOR ALMOST EVERY DRUG OUT THERE. Now, I'm a lazy paranoid fuck who doesn't do hard drugs (the ones that cause tolerance and withdrawl) on any regular basis, but I happen to know that a lot of you, here at BLTC, do. So, I just figured I'd put this out there.
