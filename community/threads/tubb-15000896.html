Re: Questions to ask your Priest.
Regarding the ball dropping and God sending us on our way and all that...

You're assuming the doctrine of Determinism in some sense, that our actions are governed entirely by chemical and physical factors. If you believe the Libertarianist stance, then it is impossible to predict someone. You can predict how they will likely act, but even if you analyze every atom of their body and put it in a giant calculating machine, you cannot predict them perfectly.

If that is true, then God knowing how you will act is impossible, not because he is not omniscient, but because you possess the (as some people have put it) godlike ability to make your own choices. This doesn't mean God can't know the future however. Crucial events may already be pre-determined in such a way that even with free choice, you can't back out of it rationally. Or God may intervene and force an event to happen because it is part of His divine plan.
