Re: Funeral Behavior
I disagree with everyone else on this one.

BEFORE the funeral you need to somehow get word to him through a third party that you want to attend. If he is receptive to you being there, then attend and let whatever chips fall where they may. If he does NOT wish you to be there, respect the wishes that it is HIS father and let him have his day of grief with his family. No matter how close you were to the person that died, he has rank over you in such a matter. Going in you honestly don't know how his feelings are towards you. If he still does have a grudge and you would be the last person he would want to see, you could just make a very bad day worse. Whatever your past grudge is and no matter who was right or wrong, it is completely irrelevant in regards to this. Don't just show up and let him "deal with it". When someone just lost a loved one there is no "right" or "wrong" way to feel or act. Respect his wishes and base your attendance/demeanor on whatever info the third party gives you....BEFORE the day of the funeral.
