first time with E
I just tried it for the first time last night, and I thought i'd share the story.

I was hanging out with a friend, trying to find weed at 3 AM, and we asked some random kid in a 7/11. He didn't have any weed, but sold me 3 rolls. I got his plate# in case he ripped me off. I took one at around 3:15, and walked around for a while until it started to kick in. I started feeling happy and energized, and my friend said I was breathing really weird. We started walking back to my house (about an hour after i'd taken it), and I was starting to shake and clench my jaw. I was getting a little worried then, but not seriously. We stopped at a gas station to get a gatorade, I know that you should drink plenty while on E. I walked into the gas station, and I thought it was filled with a white mist or fog. My friend said that meant I was rolling really hard. The shaking had stopped, and I wasn't feeling at all worried now. We called a cab to go to another friend's house, and I drank another gatorade while we waited for it. The whole ride over I was feeling fucked up, not really aware of where I was or what I was doing, I just knew that I liked it. When we got there I gave one of them to my other friend, and the kid who was with me earlier started to drink. (he can't have E because of a heart problem). I was just sitting there smoking cigarettes and talking to the other kid who had taken the E. I was gradually feeling less fucked up, like I could concentrate again, but I was still happy and relaxed, but I had plenty of energy. I walked to the store with the other kid to get something for them to drink, and I was just feeling happy and energetic, but not really weird anymore. Around 6:30 I took a cab back home. I wasn't really tired, but I went to bed and fell asleep fairly quickly. I woke up at about 2:30 today and I'm not feeling any bad after effects, but my thoughts are maybe a little less coherent than is normal for me. I still have one left for tonight too.
