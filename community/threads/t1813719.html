How in this day and age can so many people follow religion so....blindly? I mean there is no proof of God what so ever, granted there's also no proof that there isn't a God. But that doesn't mean that by default there must be a God.

Noone has seen or heard from this supposidly, kind, all loving, all powerful entity, for the last few thousand years. So what makes you think that one exists? What makes you follow so blindy?
