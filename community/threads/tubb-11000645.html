Re: How do you start an ISP
quote:
I uncapped my cable modem and had a T3 connection for a while, but then the company caught me and slapped my wrist.
That just proves you don't know what you are talking about. A cable connection and a T3 are hightly different connections, regardless of similar bandwidths. T3 connections run over twisted pair using AMI (Alternative Mark Inversion) coding where Cable runs over coax using CDMA (Collision Detection Multiple Access) encoding. Besides all that, T3's are multiplexed differently than cable.

If you think your 450 could route all the data running down a major backbone then your are not looking at the whole picture. Another note. Even if you upgrade to gigabit NIC, your PCI bus is slower than fiber at full burst. More data than just that of 8 computers is running down a backbone.

Cisco 12000 series routers (made for ISPs and other large corps) are designed for this kind of load and costs a shitload. If a 450Mhz 32Mb RAM PC could route all that data then why would ANYONE bother spending the money on a high power router.

I have a linux router myself for my cable connection. Its a 120Mhz CYRIX 20Mb RAM and it works just great for my 4 computers. Even though its a good system, I know it wont route data for even 100 computers very effectively.

And I do agree, LINUX ROCKS.
