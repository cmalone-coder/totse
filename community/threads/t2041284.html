What is a personal info violation?
Do not post messages with credit card numbers, calling card numbers, passwords to private computer systems, other user's real names and personal info (including pictures), or unsolicited phone numbers.

So someone recently asked me a few questions about what constitutes personal information on the forums. In some of the instances he described, I had some difficulty deciding whether or not it would be a violation of personal information being shared. Because I had difficulty deciding, I figured that I wasn't alone. So I'm going to shed some light on the gray areas and we'll decide once and for all what is and isn't a violation. The following is a list of factors that go into my decision when dealing with a possible personal info violation. In some of them I posted what I would do in such an instance. If you do something different, please quote me and tell us how you would handle the situation. There are no wrong answers, I'm just trying to figure out what page everyone is on before we start turning pages. Ya know? And if you think there's a situation or factor that I did not cover, please post here!

For the following I'm going to call the victim XYZ and the offender ABC.

The vitality of the info: I would usually say that posting just a first name is not a bannable offense. Certainly something you should edit out (should XYZ request it) and you should probably give ABC some sort of warning about it if it didn't look malicious.

The parrot effect: Someone releases personal info into a thread. The user is not well liked on the boards by a substantial number of people. These people all take it upon themselves to spread the info as well. My general rule of thumb is that if they do it within the original thread, only the original poster should be punished (unless they repost it after it's been removed from the original post). If they spawn their own thread or invade other threads to share the info, they should be punished as well.

Intent of the poster: If ABC did it with the intent of releasing the user's info (ie: ABC was trying to spread XYZ's name) then he should be infracted and/or banned. Any malicious attempt (regardless of how minor the personal info) should not go unnoticed or unpunished.

Compounded personal info: I'll use myself in this example. I don't mind sharing my first name with people. I also tell people the general area that I live in (central ohio). But if someone were to tell totse what university I went to, I would be very angry. Anyone who had read my other posts would be able to figure out who I am in real life with relative ease now. With ONLY the knowledge of what school I go to, you would have a 1 in 4000 shot or so (not quite a bannable offence since you can't identify me in real life by what school I go to). With the knowledge of my first name however, you've got a 1 in 3 shot at figuring out who I am.
-This one I'm still not sure what to do with. Technically I should not be giving out any info if I don't want to be identified in real life. But at the same time, I was very careful of what info I did release - someone else fucked it up for me.

Common knowledge: XYZ posts his own full name in a thread. Months later, ABC starts calling ABC by his full name in all of his threads (his intent is to spread ABC's name). In this situation I would have to say that it's just XYZ's fault. Regardless of how burried he thought the thread was, it's his fault for posting it in the first place.

Mistrusted friend: I've seen this happen MANY times. ABC befriends XYZ on the boards and eventually on some sort of chat medium (AIM, IRC, MSN, or one of those). ABC gets XYZ to send him pictures of himself. ABC posts the said pictures on the BBS. - For this I'd say that ABC just needs to be punished. That's a clear violation because the pictures were never posted on the BBS or on the photo gallery. If they were, it would be free game.
