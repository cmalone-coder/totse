Acid.
Surprisingly i live in Ohio too.

Two hits of acid should be enough for a intense trip. Those "bad trips" are basically people not willing to go with the flow of the trip. Eventually you will peak and let go of whatever you've been holding on to and it will be like a wave of emotion slamming into you. Suddenly you are remembering, feeling and seeing things you thought you buried in your conscience. The oncoming visuals and time perception along with the anxious feeling is great. I suggest doing it with a few other people who are ALSO on it aswell.

I've only done it during the spring and summer times so i guess you might have to limit how much you are outside. But we are supposed to be getting a heat wave today or something. I would suggest doing it around 5-6 pm on a Friday. Maybe a little earlier because you would want to start feeling it before the sun sets. Just don't worry too much about the 'feelings' that will be involved because they will come on and you can't really stop them. Expect to not sleep for 10+ hours. Which is why i suggest doing it on a Friday because i don't think walking into school after a long night of tripping is much fun. Anyways, happy tripping. Even if it takes months, don't stop looking for it.
