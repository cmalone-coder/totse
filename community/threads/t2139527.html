Re: Acid tolerance
Most prominent effect my psychedelic (ab)use had on me last summer was a noticeable psychosis in the fall. Just a general disconnection from reality and severely distorted thought patterns. There was a lot of 'cross chatter' going on in my head too. Kind of like how you see in cartoons with the good conscious and the bad conscious and they start arguing.

I also had relatively severe HPPD (Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder). Basically recurring visual distortions that would come on without much of a warning. Not like a full blown flashback, but seeing minor distortion in objects and seeing a lot of what I dubbed "shadow rats." I'd see these dark shadow figures out of the corner of my eye, and when I'd look at them they'd scurry away much like a rat would. HPPD would kick into high gear whenever I got really stoned.

Other than those just some minor mental occurrences throughout the next couple months. I kind of isolated myself for a while because I was having a bit of "Syd Barret" syndrome. Like I said, I was disconnected from reality for a bit. My mind needed some recovery and that wasn't going to happen with outside stimuli like friends.
