Gigers work is more often than not, biomechanical, which... as it says... a mixture of living and nonliving things, meshed together. I can't honestly recall a single Giger image where there were gears, cogs, and hydraulic cylinders. Most of it is usually tubing, along with a sheet of steel, with the odd occasional protrusion and anemic woman modeled from Li (he loved her so very much, so much in fact he wore bread loaf's as shoes).

Even in Birth Machine, there aren't cogs or any real mechanical things, maybe a spring or two. Gimme a few hours to get home and I'll post something along the lines of it.
