In Retrospect, and Future Musings
In retrospect, 2008 didn't yield very many truly memorable games, did it? Every decent game that came out had some recurrent flaw in it that just made it half-decent; nothing special, nothing that's really going in the hall of fame. No sir, 2008 wasn't a particularly great year, especially with the letdowns of Haze, GTA4, and Army of Two.

So hopefully next year will churn out some real competitor's for 2009's Game of the Year. It seems that they're starting off the year well with Skate 2, Prototype, and Resident Evil 5. Then there's the projected releases of Killzone 2, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2, God of War 3, Heavy Rain, Postal III (for the 360!), and Saboteur. We've got a hell of a year ahead of us.


So what do you think about this year?
What are you really excited about for '09?
