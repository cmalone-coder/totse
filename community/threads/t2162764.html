One time a friend of mine just gave me an armful of fresh branches from a huge plant he robbed, and I wanted to dry and cure the weed correctly, of course. But, I had these huge marijuana branches, and wanted to at least get stoned to deal with trimming them all down.
I tried a couple of methods.
I didn't dare try to microwave, because that just seems fucking retarded.
I tried using my oven, setting it to the lowest possible heat, and hanging some small buds on the top rack, I was kind of worried about burning it and making my whole house smell like weed, so I only put it in for less than ten minute intervals, and it worked well enough for me to toast a couple bowls and get stoned.

I tried drying it under the light I have over my snake's tank, which was a bit too hot, and burned the weed a bit.

The best way, I found, for drying weed quick, is to put a couple buds in a paper bag and let it sit. I had a couple of those silica gel packets, so I put one in each bag to sop up some extra moisture.

Thanks for all the input. I ended up not going the tobacco route. Here's what I did instead:
