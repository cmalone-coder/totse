Yeah, last winter my father's dogs had puppies a month before Christmas. 8 If I remember correctly, once you have all of them registered and immunized, it's much easier to sell them, around Christmas he sold most for 800$ each I think (though two were stolen, and one ran away ) Immunization isn't much money, about $50 each? Another thing, pet stores will buy them, just a a much lower price (probably half, mabey closer to 300$).

Then again there are things like the health of dogs, the cost of food, and puppies have to be a certain age before you can sell them (About a month old), which means you have to be able to care for nearly a dozen dogs for that long.

Note: This is New England, cost/prices may vary, also, they were sold near Christmas time. The dogs I'm referring to are purebreed German Shepards. You don't need a breeders liscense or something like that to sell a litter, but to actually make a business from it, (not money on the side) I can't say, you should check rules and regulations for where you live.
