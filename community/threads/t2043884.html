zx diet pepsi is the thing you mentioned about the left and right brains connecting, freud's theroy?

No. If you're talking about Freud's Seduction Theory, that has more to do with later-in-life social problems being caused by the untimely introduction of sex into a child's world. I think that it was finally denounced by Freud himself, but I can't remember. It was touched upon in a Criminology class I took.

Anyway, the theory I'm talking about is touched on in the Continual-Activation Theory by Jie Zhang. It has to do with short-term memory being transferred into long-term memory. During this process, parts of the brain that usually remain separate (not just the Right and Left brain, because they communicate quite often) are communicating with each other. The transfer of memory that occurs is hypothesized to cause a sort of "scanning" of old neurons, resulting in a random firing of past experiences. This, is what we call dreams.

However, Zhang (and others) says that there may actually be a touch of consciousness during REM (explaining phenomena like lucid dreams) which may cause repressed memories and desires to appear in our dreams.

Hope that explanation clears things up.
