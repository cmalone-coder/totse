Recent Events and Pending Changes.
I'm sure you've all had your share of panic attacks, worries, concerns, bitching, etc over the past few days or weeks. I know many of the problems totse has been experiencing have been attributed to me, which may or may not be unfounded. However, regardless of cause, it is clear that there were and are issues that need to be addressed. To that end, the administration have just spent a considerable amount of time discussing the status quo and our actions from this point out.

This post is to notify all users of totse about impending changes, so that you have at least some advance notice of what is about to happen, and some explanation when it does. Note, in part many ideas here have been our own, but we DO take to heart what has been said to us in the past, so many changes are based on actual requests.

Obviously, emails from our administration mailing list have been leaked by one or possibly more moderators. They were posted all over the boards. What bothered us most was not the content, because most things there are things you eventually find out about anyway - my post about merchandise, for example, was only a couple of days before a similar post here in news of the temple. What bothered us most was that our 'circle of trust' (yeah, I'm quoting Meet the Parents, what of it? :P) was broken. That meant there were serious problems with some of our staff. We've reacted and dealt with this in many ways, some public, some very much private. Whatever your argument with our actions, remember, we act for the good of ALL.

We have decided that there are moderators here that need to be removed, for various reasons. In some cases, this may outrage you. In others, many of you may be pleased. Note, in some cases those that are being removed are our friends, however, we act for the good of totse, not for our own personal motivations. Please do not question why certain mods are removed, nor petition for their return. Some mods are absentee and obviously not doing their jobs, so it should be clear to you why they are going. In other cases, it will be less clear, but just as necessary, in our opinion.

To those moderators who may be outraged at their removal - We thank you for your hard work on totse in the past, but we believe it is time for us and you to move on. If you wish to remain as a poster here, you will be welcomed. If not, thank you once again, and have a nice life.

We have also decided that several forums need to be combined, changed around, renamed. This is an ongoing thing, you may find new forums in new places. No forum is completely disappearing, it is simply evolving, as everything does. We may also add new forums, if the need arises.

If you have an urge to be a moderator, we ARE looking for new staff, however, please do not ASK to be a moderator. Post intelligently and wisely in the forums you like, and you will be noticed, believe me.

If you would like a new forum, again, please wait and see. I have paid attention to ALL suggestions I have ever read, and we will consider all the options open to us.

Regarding new software, updating text files, merchandise, etc. Be patient. Behave yourselves, and we WILL do everything we can to improve, update, and repair or replace what needs to be done. Asking for features, asking for anything, simply takes time away from us actually DOING things to improve the site while we answer your queries.

Finally. I would like to extend an amnesty to banned users. IF you believe you are capable of following our rules, IF you truly want what is BEST for this site, email me - update@estot.com, listing your banned username (in the case of those with multiple accounts, ONE and ONLY ONE will be unbanned) and WHY you want to return. I will consider your request, but I WILL keep an eye on you, and this is the ONLY time I will be this lenient. If you misbehave again, by spamming, posting page stretching threads, blank threads, or in other ways breaking the rules, you will be gone for good. I'm hoping that doing this will allow us to have the freedom to open registration again, and give you no reason to make multiple backup accounts. I want to extend freedom to ALL. But not freedom to abuse this site. Freedom to help us bring this site back to what it once was. Reform, and be forgiven.

Wintermute, Zok and Acidmelt.
