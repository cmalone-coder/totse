Hot Cocoa Recipes
The winter season has come and I always make tons of hot chocolate in the month of December. I just love it. I have seen many different ways of making it the most common with sugar, milk and baking cocoa. Share your favorite recipe.

My usual is:
3 tablespoons of baking cocoa
2 tablespoons of sugar
1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
1/2 tablespoon of condensed milk
1/2 cup of Half and Half
2 cups of milk
To make it I just mix the half and half, condensed milk and milk in a saucepan, heat it to a good temperature, not boiling though. And then throw in the rest of the stuff. Top with whip cream.

Re: Hot Cocoa Recipes
1/2 cup sugar
1/3 cup HERSHEY'S Cocoa
1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
4 cups milk
4-8 oz. peppermint schnapps (1-2 oz per drink)

Combine sugar, cocoa, and cinnamon in large saucepan. Gradually stir in milk; heat stirring occasionally, to serving temperature. Pour into mug or cup. top with schnapps. Serve immediately.

Makes 4 servings.

Re: Hot Cocoa Recipes
Hot cocoa is cheap. The way to go if you have the time and a little bit of money is hot chocolate.

Easy as Hell recipe:

milk
ghirardelli 60% chocolate
vanilla extract


take a pot or saucepan and heat as much milk as you need (I measure by pouring the cold milk until it is 7/8 of the way filling the mug, then dumping into pan.) take the bar of ghirardelli and break it in half. 1/2 bar of chocolate per mug. Heat the milk, add the vanilla (1 drop per mug), and add the chocolate. Tend to till no chocolate chunks. Pour and add marshmallows or whip cream. ENJOY!

Caution: do not put your milk on high heat, it can scorch the pan when you add the chocolate.
