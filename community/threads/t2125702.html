Pssh. Marquis on TLC. ;)

As for cleanup, depends on what's in it. Presuming you can do an a/b, your main likely contaminants would probably be b-iodomethamphetamine, unreacted ephedrine, and if from plant sources, the n,n-dimethyl analog of all the products...

reflux in NaOH in np should convert the iodometh into ephedrine, at which point, when starting from cold pills, you'd only have two products to seperate. In normal-phase chromatography, the ephedrine should be the first to elute. Speaking of chromatography, I've heard a few rumors that methamphetamine and several of its near analogues fluoresce under blacklight without degredation, which should make it more than easy as shit - load silica gel, flip on lights, isolate each individual alkaloid.

The n,n-dimethyl analog should be seperable by acidification fractionation, or by its more hydrophobic nature, or by a shitload of things. Repeated recrystallizations should be able to eventually exploit its hydrophobicity, for instance.
