Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Pyrotechnic Compositions

Chemicals:

· Potassium Perchlorate (KCIO4)

· Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)

· Aluminum Powder (Al)

· Sulfur (S)

· Charcoal Air-Float (C)

· Sugar (Sucrose)

· Dextrin

Compositions:

· Flash Powder 1 – The common Flash Powder mixture (More Al = louder)

o Potassium Perchlorate (KCIO4)……… 70%

o Aluminum Powder (Al)…………… 30%

· Flash Powder 2 – Almost the same effect as the first mixture (A little louder)

o Potassium Perchlorate (KCIO4)……… 50%

o Aluminum Powder (Al)…………… 23%

o Sulfur (S)……………………… 27%

· Flash Powder 3 – Almost the same effect as the first mixture (A little brighter)

o Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)……… 50%

o Aluminum Powder (Al)……… 50%

· Flash Powder 4 – Relatively safe composition. Burns with a brilliant white light in an open tube, or when unconfined. When well confined, it produces a loud, low pitched report and a short but intense flash.

o Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)……… 50%

o Aluminum Powder (Al)……… 20%

o Sulfur (S)………………… 30%

· Smoke Composition 1 – Is most successful when prepared by melting the sugar and potassium nitrate together on low heat, but this requires good stirring, and there is a risk of accidental ignition. The molten mixture can be poured in cardboard containers and a fuse inserted while the mixture solidifies. Can also be mixed together and poured into a tube (without melting it).

o Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)……… 60%

o Sugar (Sucrose)……………… 40%

· Sparkler Composition 1 – A common sparkler mixture.

o Potassium Perchlorate (KCIO4)……… 60% or 50%

o Aluminum Powder (Al)…………… 30% or 35%

o Dextrin………………………… 10% or 15%

· Black Powder – Two methods of preparation exist, the precipitation or CIA method, and the ball milling method. The ball milling method produces slightly superior results. This produces Meal Powder.

o Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)……… 75%

o Charcoal Air-Float (C)……… 15%

o Sulfur (S)………………… 10%

Just to avoid any more confusion about flash powder compositions,

you can stoichiometrically find the exact amounts needed for perfect oxidation.

Adding a higher ratio of aluminum just makes a brighter flash.

3 KClO4 + 8 Al -----|&gt; 3 KCl + 4 Al2O3

1 mole of KClO4 = 138.55

138.55 x 3 = 415.65

Therefore, 3 moles of KClO4 = 415.65

1 mole of Al = 26.98

26.98 x 8 = 215.84

Therefore, 8 moles of Al = 215.84

415.65 [3 moles of KClO4] + 215.84 [8 moles of Al] = 631.49.

(215.84 / 631.49) x 100= 34.17948% (Al)

(415.65 / 631.49) x 100= 65.82052% (KClO4)

So perfectly oxidised flash should be a ratio of approx. 34.2/65.8 of Al/KClO4 respectively.
