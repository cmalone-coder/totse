Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Just to avoid any more confusion about flash powder compositions,

you can stoichiometrically find the exact amounts needed for perfect oxidation.

Adding a higher ratio of aluminum just makes a brighter flash.

3 KClO4 + 8 Al -----|&gt; 3 KCl + 4 Al2O3

1 mole of KClO4 = 138.55

138.55 x 3 = 415.65

Therefore, 3 moles of KClO4 = 415.65

1 mole of Al = 26.98

26.98 x 8 = 215.84

Therefore, 8 moles of Al = 215.84

415.65 [3 moles of KClO4] + 215.84 [8 moles of Al] = 631.49.

(215.84 / 631.49) x 100= 34.17948% (Al)

(415.65 / 631.49) x 100= 65.82052% (KClO4)

So perfectly oxidised flash should be a ratio of approx. 34.2/65.8 of Al/KClO4 respectively.
