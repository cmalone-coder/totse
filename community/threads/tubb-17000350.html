Re: Piracy: what the hell?
I have pirated games, and I'll probably do it again in the future. I have a real simple rule of thumb- I'll only nab games that I won't pay for myself anyway. I only have so much money, and I want to make sure to the best of my ability that the games it goes towards are the ones it's best spent on. (On the rare occasion that a game I get turns out to be phenomenal, I'll generally go buy it. Usually, they're a nice diversion, but that's it (ie. Shogun).)

As much as I'm for supporting good developers (no matter how much I may not be a fan of FPS games, Max Payne was amazingly done), and as asinine as some of the laws towards copy protection being suggested are, the companies themselves need to take better steps to protect their shit too. There will always be thieves. Remedy (or GOD or whoever) got trashed on Max Payne, but part of the problem was theirs- it was single-player only (MAJOR turnoff for a lot of people), which in turn led to them not being able to have an effective copy protection- so they didn't have any at all. They were asking to get screwed, and they did.

There are companies such as Blizzard and Black Isle who have been successful because their games are the absolute bomb. It can be done. The BG games don't even have copy protection, but they still had a shitload of sales. There's no doubt piracy hurts, but having a good product makes more of a difference.. maybe I'm being overly optimistic, but my hope is that some companies figure out that you can make money by putting out something that's high-quality, enough so that if the good-but-not-incredible games of the world (ie. Arcanum, Max Payne) wind up diminishing in number, they'll be replaced by better quality games, even if the number released overall decreases.
