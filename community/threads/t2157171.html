Think how much work it would be to remove a sea of pink insulation from an entire attic JUST TO GAIN VISIBILITY to the framework...then from there what's the person gonna do...even if they got a tip that it was hidden in a 2x4 in your attic they could collapse the whole roof on themselves (etc.) if they even think about cutting up and fucking around with load bearing 2x4's.

AND REMEMBER, the best way to get into a wall to stash something is from the attic, if you're going to stash in a wall, access it from the attic, if done otherwise you'll more than likely end up with an obvious entrance sitting on your drywall.

Roofing structures vary a HUGE amount from house to house, old houses will have rafters, newer ones won't, but if you don't have rafters there are a ridiculous amount of ways to frame it, just go up there and check it out - if you're really paranoid just build a secret room up there that can only be accessed from a secret spot in your ceiling.
