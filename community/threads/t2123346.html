Anyone used DMSO?
It's a rather unknown and untested method for drug delivery, so there won't be much information on what drugs work best with DMSO or in what concentrations of DMSO/drug. Only experimentation will be effective to know more about DMSO as a delivery system since the solute/solvent/cell membrane interactions are unpredictable. I'm planning to begin experimenting with DMSO as a vehicle for amphetamine salts (Adderall IR). I don't expect it to make the amphetamines ridiculously more potent, but I should get an idea of what concentrations are safe for the skin. My real goal, however, is to test various tryptamine compounds.

Anyone have personal experience with it?

Re: Anyone used DMSO?
Alright, so first test went pretty well actually. After deciding that I did not want to simply crush up an adderall and get the dye and binders in my bloodstream, I did an a/b extraction with 4 10mg generic blue IR pills to get some white crystalline amphetamine HCl. I used just enough solution of 85% DMSO to dissolve the crystals/powder and quite reluctantly dropped the foul oyster-smelling solution under my tongue. Within a few seconds I could feel a powerful rush and A LOT of euphoria. My body felt weightless and it was quite enjoyable. I can't really compare it to other amphetamines, but it was definitely a lot more powerful than parachuting 80mg of adderall with no tolerance. The high only lasted for a little over an hour and the crash was pretty bad.

This test was pretty much just to test the whole principal of DMSO drug delivery for myself and I would say that it certainly is a comparable method to shooting something, but without the hassle of needles and gives more versatility. Later on I will try a psilocybin extraction and see if i can get a stronger trip out of less material by tweaking the DMSO concentrations. I could try DMT instead, which would be faster and easier to do, but I want to test out an alcohol-based extraction of psilocybin.
