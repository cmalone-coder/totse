I dont know about the stealing of the tips or whatever, but minimum wage only applies to non tipped employees. Employers are allowed to consider your "estimated tips" as counting towards your tips. Get a job waiting tables elsewhere. I waited tables for a long time and pretty much never saw a dime on an actual paycheck, but would leave a shift on a friday night easily with 3-400 dollars for a few hours of work.

So you kind of have to claim an amount at the end of your shift around that number. When I clocked out every shift I was given my sales total for the shift and I would just claim 12% of that as my tips and the taxes on that tip amount were taken out of my paycheck (which is why it never amounted to shit)
