Life Sciences Manifesto
HTML version with supportive links at: http://lifesciences.secureid.org/

The Life Sciences Initiative promotes a world view based on scientific understanding. Considering the constant and proliferate mass-deaths due to suicide, the religious tension and bigotry that justifies current wars, the rise of anti-pleasure politics and continuous social injustice based on outmoded religious dogma and irrational politics, it is time for a scientific and life-affirming world-view. This world view is based on 8 Essential Realities: the Atom, the Cell, the Body, the Self, Reputation, Group, Norms and Laws.(1) In the short term Life Sciences will promote a way living that recognises the 8 Essential Realities, through teaching, counselling, activism and celebration. From these 8 Essential Realities, guiding principles and projects are being developed.

the Cell

Traditional marriage practices are reducing human genetic potential, not to mention increasing genetic diseases. Making it safe and easy to have a sex-affirming lifestyle, even in repressive environments, should be a priority. The desire for free love, for more than one lover, otherwise known as polyamory, is in the genes. Free love is not infidelity, but natural. Free love and pansexual acceptance would make for a sex-affirming lifestyle and species. We can be free from the domination of traditional ideas that are not necessary in the modern world.

Safe sex is a priority now that 40 million people worldwide have AIDS and it will kill 70 million people over the next 20 years unless the rich nations of the world step up their efforts to curb the disease.

the Body

Saving women from forced marriages and increasing the status of women, so they can control their own sexuality, has got to be a priority. Worldwide, women are still subject to oppression. Woman still have to face forced marriages, the slavery of illegal prostitution, segregation from many activities like work, education and religion, and genital mutilation. One in five woman are raped, tortured, beaten and assaulted. Women are emotionally tormented, and exploited.

the Cell and the Body

The enormous waste of human resources has got to be stopped. Health, education, and basic needs must be available to all. Not to mention avoiding wars and stopping the other destructions of humans. Escape from our current condition of mass-death, destruction and rapine waste is imperative if we and our children are to survive. Population growth is not the problem, the distribution of wealth is. People are needed for industrialization of space, basic research, etc. Given space colonization, we need a population doubling every seven years, to fill available living areas. This means more sex, more children, and better support and emancipation for women and children. As things are set up now, the most capable and gifted members of society are having the fewest children.

the Self

Given the need for research and industrialization, meaningful roles for the population can be developed. So, high esteem can be a general condition, not only for the 500 richest persons on the planet.

Reputation

"Reputation is an externalized self. In the simplest case, it is merely the memories of experiences with that person within a social group. Reputation is of social value because it allows prediction of and, to a certain extent, control of individuals' behavior. In modern societies, the concept of a "legal person" has been developed. This concept can associate certain objects, accomplishments, obligations, and rights with a given physical person. Computerization has led to a vast expansion in the amount of personal data that can be acquired, stored, and mobilized in decision making. One of the earliest applications was the "credit rating," permitting the transfer of goods and services without immediate payment, thus formalizing a form of trust. Detailed information about people is now collected routinely, permitting one to know what products they use, how they spend their time, whether they have health problems, and so on."(2)

Reputation development means having the same security for soft qualities that we now only have for financial transactions. Online privacy and online security are the only ways to maintain a reliable reputation.

Group

"The group is crucial to survival of the individual, both physically and as a social being, that is, as a body and as a self."(3) Therefore, a software system that can work in a networked environment, permitting spontaneous formation of groups is essential. Recent developments in mobile phone technology should facilitate networked social encounters away from the desktop.

Support for group Norms and Laws via a storage system, etc. should be developed. This includes the question of minority group rights, that is, the social, economic, and political rights under the UN Charter, which the US has never endorsed. Scientific communication can be a basis for governance using computer-network based democracy. "A democratic system must incorporate both protections for individuals and mechanisms for coordination. Access to information is crucial for democratic decision making. If people withhold information, however, then even free distribution of available information will not ensure democracy. One problem is that people may be punished if they speak in opposition to power holders. More often, persons censor themselves because they fear that speaking out will damage their career possibilities. Finally, people may prefer to avoid the duplication of effort that occurs when many people write comments simultaneously in response to an event or statement. These problems of protection and coordination must be solved before direct democracy is feasible with large populations."(4)

the Atom

Basic social and scientific research should be supported on a grand scale, so we have a chance of surviving contact with advanced alien civilizations (the likely hood of which is high - one chance in 50,000 per year. Also, if we have been sent any message by our "Creator", it would have to be embedded in the fundamental physical parameters of our Universe. For example, it has been suggested that the endless sequence, pi, could encode a message.

The Future

In the long term we - as a species - have no choice but to aim to survive a Gamma Ray Burst. Space is our safe haven where we can avoid catastrophic galactic events like this. Supporting space colonization and space travel in general will free us from the constant threat of being wiped out by a single Earth destroying event like a meteor strike, the Sun heating up or global environmental disaster. Space offers untold opportunities, resources and discoveries.

Although I've only really studies anthropology and evolution in a passing manner, and am certainly not an expert, I do remember the point being driven home that altruism is not a dominant behavior, existing only in some rare situations. By altruism I mean one organism helping another of the same species when this action has either no effect or is harmful to the altruistic organism. Sure, animals will help one another hunt, but only because their survival depends on it as well, and they will share in the result. Animals, even in anthropoid circles and wolfpacks, will not, for example, gather food or kill a prey animal, and then give it all away to a group member (unless that member has a direct bearing on the first animal's genetic survival, ie is its offspring, mate, or potential mate).

And while dominance displays do not always mean death for either combatant, they most certainly do mean severe limitation on the loser's ability to mate in all circumstances, not to mention those animals where excess males are expelled from the group to fend for themselves. Every textbook and lecture I've ever seen about reproduction in group societies makes the clear point that, contrary to your statement, those males in dominant positions actually do mate far more frequently than non-dominant males, both through their displays against the latter as well as females choosing to mate with dominant males more often than non-dominant.

In larger groups, as you mention, non-dominance does not mean that an animal will NEVER mate, it just means that he won't be the one doing it with every female possible at each cycle of estrus. And the fact remains that in anthropoids (and other animals that live in social groups with alpha male-dominant structures, such as lions), infanticide is very common upon the ascendance of a new alpha male to the dominant position. This is because animals are never concerned with the survival of the species, they are concerned about the survival of their own genetic material. Species survival and evolution is only a by-product of this selfish pattern.

Traits that are selected for are not selected from their enhancing survival of the species, again contrary to a statement you make. Selection is based on the survival of the individual with a certain trait, and if individuals with this trait survive then the trait is passed on to a population. Altruism that does not directly influence the individual's reproductive success therefore does not help select for the trait that causes the altruism.

Of course, that's just the school of thought I learned under, and there are many more. My professor was Dr. Don Symons, Emeritus at the University of California (his research focus is on primate social behavior and human evolutionary psychology---pretty much everythig we're talking about here). I'm obviously a reflection of my teacher, especially on a subject I have little other experience in, so...

Despite all this, I agree with the last part of your post, which makes theoretical arguments based on evolutionary psychology a little suspect when they're applied to modern human behavior. Probably the only place we're still controlled by our instincts is in sexual psychology, and if you've ever been to a bar on friday night, you know that members of the same species are not at all interested in helping eachother reproduce unless "she's got a friend."
