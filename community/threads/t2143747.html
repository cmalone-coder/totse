Weirdest dream ever.
It was actually a series of dreams, although, in most of them I couldnt really tell if it was a dream or it was true,

goes like this:

I was alone at home. My father was away for a week. Anyway, one day I receive a message on my cellphone saying this:

-I am -(I dont remember the name it said)-, I came here to save you.-

The message started with a "hello" and my name. I was freaked out. who was this guy, how did he know my name and my cellphone number. Also, the message had no origin (reply to/ sender) number. It didnt even say -private-.

Next thing I know, is Im sitting on my bed and its about 10 in the morning.
I check my cellphone for any strange message in bold letters from an unknown sender and find nothing, but the rest of the messages were there in the same order as in my dream.

Forgotten about that, about a week later, I am driving in my car and lose my exit, so I have to take a long road around to find a return to the place Im going to. I find it and head back, when I suddenly receive a message from this unknown sender and in bold letters. It reads:

-I know you do not still believe in me, but give me a second and I will tell you what car you are driving.-

Shit! What the Fuck, this guy again and how did he know I was driving. Was he spying on me or what?

Everything goes withe then and the next thing I see is my wall, and I sitting on my bed again. Check my cellphone and it was 10 in the morning as well.

There is more to this, but Ill be telling you later after I hear some of your thoughts. This post is already too big for my likes.
