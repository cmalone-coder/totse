Re: Does the magic bag really work? And what anti-theft device is this?
I did some scientific testing with a "magic bag" a few months back (and got banned for life from Kmart as a result!). It does work, if constructed properly. I used it with success on a coil antenna tag (sticker with the foil spiral inside), a dual strip tag (the puffy white rectangular sticky things), and an ink tag (the ones pinned to clothing).

The basic operating principle behind each of these devices is more or less the same. I've never seen a theft tag with a battery in it, but I would imagine the battery serves only to light a little LED or somesuch to scare off the potential thieves. Using a battery to power a transmitter for the alarms to detect would be wasteful, since there are at least three other passive systems that would work just as well. Besides, the store would have to constantly replace the batteries in their stuff on a regular basis, which would cost more than the merchandise that the store would lose to shoplifters anyway.

~Zero
