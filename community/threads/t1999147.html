You may say yes, but what if an Albert Einstien was in the group sacrificed. And if you say yes, then you must be all for stem cell research from aborted fetuses. ( I am for the research to save lives if you wonder).

Discuss- is it right for a leader to sacrifice a few to save many.
Also- does this justify abortion and stem cell research (although this can be compleatly unrelated)

Quote:
Originally Posted by fallinghouse
Two children are dying, both need organ transplants (they need different organs to each other). Is it right to kill a man, then use his organs so that both the children may live?
The problem with this is that, for one, it disrupts society. If people knew that going into a hospital meant they had a chance of having their organs stolen, they would avoid hospitals. Soon the organ seeking doctors would form roaming mobs, and people would need to barricade themselves in their homes and buy weapons for protection.

Alternately, preservation of life is not the greatest good. No one has the obligation to preserve the lives of others at all costs. Everyone dies; their lives are their own responsibility. People do, however, have the obligation to respect the rights of others. You can't hold the belief that you have a right to your life and possessions if you deny that of others. As sad as two children dying for lack of organs is, it is not wrong to let it happen. It is wrong to take someones life without their consent.
