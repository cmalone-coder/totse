I thought no one got high their first time?

I sure as hell got high my first time. Don't think you're going to get high the first hit, but it is possible to get high your first time. Notice I said possible. There's no guarantee, and you won't know until after you've already smoked.

Don't worry about smoking a specific amount, just smoke until you don't want to feel any higher. Or in the case that you don't get high the first time, until you feel like stopping and trying again later. Be sure to allow for the coming up and peak, you will feel a little bit higher a few minutes after you've already stopped smoking.

The best way to smoke is all up to your personal preference. However, I'd recommend you try a cheap method for your first time so you don't invest a lot of money in a piece and discover you don't actually like smoking weed. Joints are cheap, and gravity bongs are pretty cheap too. Blunts are cheap, but don't try to roll a blunt your first time, it's way too hard for a beginner, and paper is much more forgiving than blunt wraps are. Understand that gravity bongs are one of the most intense smoking methods, much more intense than a joint would be. Waterfall bongs are hard to operate by yourself, a bucket bong would be easier if you're smoking alone.

Yes, you will be sober enough to be around people at 12:30, but that doesn't mean you'll be completely sober. You're going to feel an afterglow, especially if it's your first time. You'd probably benefit from alloting more than three hours, it will give you more time to enjoy the coming down instead of worrying about acting normal.

Deodorant and gum will do the trick, but don't forget eye drops.
