Nicotine I can't beleive I'm saying this...
Well, here's how it goes... something like 60+ hours ago(I stopped counting) I quit a 7 year smoking habit about a pack and a half a day.. cold turkey.. now at first it wasn't so bad but after about 6 hours the irritation started to begin.. big deal... some irritation im always irritated anyway so it didn't bother me much... now fast foward to yesterday when I started getting the mega hot flashes and hallucination, yes i said hallucinations, all day I was fucking convinced there was a black flying disk following me around(haha, lame I know), now here I was going to quit smoking thinking nicotine withdrawls would be a big joke compared to coming off other drugs but now I'm begining to think nicotine withdrawls are worse than fucking coke withdrawls. I mean hallucinations, I've never heard about nicotine hallucinations mentioned before, but yet, here just a few minutes ago I saw the flying black disk again which reminded me of yesterdays hallucinations and thus inspired this thread.

I mean I knew quitting smoking would be fucking hard but seeing black things flying around in the air is just a tad bit creepy and very unexpected. It feels almost the same as coke withdrawls(believe it or not) minus the paranoid/"brain fuck" feeling and im actually sweating morenow than I have before coming off hard coke binges.

Another thing that's bugging me is my teeth are hurting like a motherfuck and I can't stop chewing on things and I can't stop eating.. I've had a toothpick or a wooden skewer in my mouth for the last 3 days now.

Jesus, maybe I should have a cigarette before I start seeing pink elephants and shit.

This is the kind of stuff I guess they don't advertise on those quit smoking commercials and campaigns.

SIDE EFFECTS CAN INCLUDE FLYING BLACK DISKS FOLLOWING YOU EVER WHERE YOU GO AND YOU'LL TEETH JUST LIKE YOU DID WHEN YOU WERE 8 MONTHS OLD.

blargh I'm so irritated I could punch a hole in my computer screen right now.
