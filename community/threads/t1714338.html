hmm, what I've heard is that a lot of the radar detectors can't detect the very short bursts used by the newer police radar/laser systems. Similarly, they use the technique you descibed, firing only at cars they suspect to be speeding, and for a short time, not just firing stray radar waves all along the roadway. also, does anyone know if these systems are illegal in any way? or maybe just in some areas?

All in all, the price is high for a system which I wouldn't place a lot of trust in.

Best thing to do is to speed with a pack of cars, cops can't easily turn out and pull you over, its harder to clock individual cars(you may be able to fight it in court, too), and you're less likely to be singled out. Try not to be the last one in the pack or speed on a road without other cars around you, this is when you get caught, every time.

Additionally, look in the distance for cars slamming on their brakes for no apparent reason. This has worked for me many times. People almost always react this way to cops they see along the road, radar out or not.

Just play it smart and you'll be fine, I speed all the time, like everyone or usually more. (crossing fingers) No tickets yet.
