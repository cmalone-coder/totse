*sigh*....tapering is much easier said than done.


It does depend on your will power and it really helps to have a good friend help you - one who will be strong enough to not give in and give you something before it's time, but one who can see if you're really in enough discomfort to give if needed. And above all one who will put up with it all to help you.

I smoked heroin for 15 years. I quit twice. Both times by tapering off.

The first time I had a friend help me. I picked up so many grams and made her a chart. Each time she gave me some to smoke, it weighed a little less than the one before it and the time between got longer and longer [and if she wasn't able to be around when it was time for the next dose, she would weigh it out ahead of time, hide it, then phone in the location to me when it was time. And no, I didn't go searching for it. If I didn't have at least that much self control the whole thing would have been pointless].

It took around 2 weeks and by the last foil I really didn't need it, but smoked it anyway. I would have stayed off too but for the fact that the pain was still there. But mostly it was that I stayed in the the same area I had been living in and still saw many of the same people and so within months I was back on it.

The second time I did it by myself. Due to a fire in my apartment I had to move so I decided to move further north - 60 miles north - where I couldn't get he unless I drove to Oakland. I bought 8 grams I think it was, and what I did was smoke as I normally would. But I would not quite kill off the foil - I would leave some on there - and saved the foils. When I got to the last 2 grams I started to taper off. Then, when the H was all gone, I would switch to the foils.

I would give myself a time when I would allow myself the next foil and then I would go as far past that time as I could; waiting until I got to the point where I didn't want to feel that bad anymore. By the time I was on the last foil I was once again free of the addiction.

Within 6 months I was able to find a Dr who would give me real pain meds. So the pain was no longer an issue and neither was being around the place and people I did heroin with [yes I ended that sentence with a preposition] so It was easy, so to speak - to stay off.

Of course, one thing about being on - and addicted to - morphine is that I could mess with heroin and not worry about getting addicted again since I was already addicted to opiates. So for a while I would go to Oakland and get 2 grams or so, every three or 4 months because there was still - and always will be - that mental pull that heroin has on me. But about 3 years ago that dropped off enough where I no longer went to Oakland. And I haven't touched it since.

Though when I do have to go through Oakland I still feel that pull and so I try my best to stay away from there.
