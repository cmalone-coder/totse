CoD5 Beta
So I got a key from one of the various internet friends I have, and went over to a friends house and we got the CoD5 beta. The level cap was 11, and there were only 3 maps. Anyone who doubts this game: Don't. It is purely awesome, not sure how well the vehicles will work out in the end, but it is still a great game. There are four factions:
Russia
Nazi Germany
Japan
and the US.
Russia has the sickle and hammer for a team symbol, nazis proudly wave the swastika around, Japan has the symbol of the rising sun. The US have some sort of symbol for a faction of their army, as far as I can tell. I've not seen it before. The guns seem fairly balanced, there are no sniper rifles, you just put a sniper scope on a normal rifle. Ask any questions you want, and I will try to answer them.
