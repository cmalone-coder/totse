sorry but there is no way to justify taking 16 grams of apap. If it is suggest that no more than 4 gram be consumed in one day I would follow that considering the bottle suggests something like no m,ore than 2 grams. Weight isn't everything, either, because I remember an acquaintance's suicide attempt, wherein she took over 14g of APAP, a few temazepam, and a few shots; she lived, no liver failure (probably some temp. damage), and at a mere 115-125 pounds of weight. Clearly a lot also has to do with your previous liver condition (hers was probably pretty virginal), and also the specific toxicity of APAP to each individual, which could vary wildly about a fixed average.

I'm only pointing out that while it won't be a fun ride, and you might wish for death to escape it, 16g isn't an automatic, definite inducer of liver-failure, nor will it neccessarily harm it permanently. I know it could, everyone should, but lets not make this out to be arsenic, cyanide, or vapourized mercury. It's a very hit-and-miss suicide method, at best. Most of the time you just end up in a hospital, extremely sick, or wake up, not dead, but extremely sick, and with potential (and likely temp., livers regen. exceedingly well with good nutrition and overall health) liver damage, and realize (hopefully), that you took a fucked up dose of APAP and should avoid that in the future. It's a rare event (relative to more toxic agents), usually protracted (death comes slowly as a result of this chemical's acute toxicity), and tremendously painful, to die of APAP-induced liver failure or other possible overdose related damage. It can take a couple weeks, and some people don't even know something is terribly wrong with their livers for days (it keeps trying to regenerate, unless so damaged that its cells remain permeated with acetaminophen); suddenly and rapidly succumbing to the toxic effects on the liver even a week or more after initial time of overdose.

Even if he survives, he should check in with an MD. He could be sick without knowing it for a while, not feeling too bad, but organ-scorched on the inside.

But it's not intrinsically assumable that death will occur, nor probable that an individual will succumb to APAP toxicity at these kinds of mild-moderate dosages, barring other stressors and/or variables of course. A healthy, large liver, possessing DNA specifying enzymes maximizing resistance to APAP toxicity, in an otherwise fit and nutritionally secure body, could well pull through with no immediately apparent and/or severe damage, and little, if any, permanent loss of liver function or induction of long-term noticable symptoms at all. Not recommended, but not a death sentence as a naturally assumable/unavoidable proceeding course of events.
