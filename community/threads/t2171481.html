I imagine that was a troll, but for future reference, PCP is handily very soluble in water, whereas THC & cannabinoids in general are not; some people even 'water cure' their bud.

Its therefore a simple matter of soaking the bud in a glass of water for half an hour or whatever, and drying it out again. Evaporate the water, or reduce its volume, and you've scored some free PCP, along with your pot. If it had PCP in it, it probably means that the pot is of really low quality though, and there's probably only a tiny amount of PCP in/on there. Most people know its generally really not worth it to the dealer to lace his or her products with cuts s/he could sell anyway.
