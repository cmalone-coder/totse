Re: So I have a virus...
The first step in eliminating a virus is to determine which one you have. I'm assuming its a Rogue, so I'll detail the steps I took to eliminate another Rogue Trojan, a real pain in the ass, Virtumonde:

Get an AV. Download SuperAntiSpyware and HiJackThis.

Disable your internet connection.

For the time being, disable Spybot's Tea Timer function. It may interfere with scans.

Run HijackThis and eliminate harmful/suspicious hidden processes. These will most likely be attaching themselves to RunDll32.

Run a full system scan with your AV.

Do a full system scan with SAS, remove harmful files. Set SAS to do a quick scan on startup. Reboot immediately. Allow SAS to run, elimintating files that could not be deleted on initial scan.

If you've noticed a serious decrease in your internet speed, you can run winsockfix in attempt to boost it back up.

If you've got Virtumonde, there's a chance you might have to edit your registry files. DO NOT edit your registry until you've researched exactly how to do it.

EDIT: There is also a tool called VundoFix, an AV/AntiSpyware program that is specifically designed to kill Virtumonde. Its got varying results.
