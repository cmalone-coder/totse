Re: ephedrineHCL, 25mg per pill, bottle of a 1000
yep

used them myself

grind them up to fine dust with a coffee bean grinder

spoon the pill mass into a beaker

for every inch of pill mass add 2 inches of dry mineral turpentine100%,,then seal airtight and leave for 24 hours..

pour mineral turpentine through filter

chuck turps

spoon any pill mass in filter back into beaker

make sure pill mass is dry

pour in same ratio of dry freezing acetone.

stir for 3 mins,,

allow to sit for 5 mins

pour through filter and spoon pill mass back into beaker

chuck acetone

repeat acetone wash

after 2nd wash allow to dry fully

add 600ml of isopropyl,,or methanol,,or methylated spirits,,

stir for 3 mins

allow to sit for 5

pour through filter

save metho,,(we are presuming your using methanol)

spoon pill mass back into beaker

add 800 ml of metho

same procedure

add 1 litre of metho

same procedure

saving the metho each time

now add all the metho to one beaker

evaporate on hot plate at 50c

when first scale on surface of metho appears

allow to cool

place in freezer for 6 hours

pour of metho

theres your ephedrine

evap metho again till scale appears

freezer

etc

keep doing this till no more eph comes out

good luck
