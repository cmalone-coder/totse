True. He has a point, though. The mind is a result of a functioning brain. The network of reactions between the firing neurons gives rise to the mind. Once the neurons stop firing, the mind ceases to exist. No "soul" remains afterwards, unless there is some sort of echo of the interactions which formed the mind originally (since it is the interactions which form the mind, not actually the electrical impulses themselves, another means to cause the system to continue functioning with the same interactions would cause the mind to continue to exist).
