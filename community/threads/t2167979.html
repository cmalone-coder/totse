Re: I'm a pirate.
500 hp on low boost and pump gas... i call bs.. or bigass turbos...

by low boost I mean ~19psi.

(edit: with a well tuned timing map, it might make around 580WHP at 19psi. It all depends. It's never been anything but street tuned, so I only have guesstimations based on other cars with the same turbos and engine, power:weight ratios of cars I've raced + beaten, etc.)

High boost would be 30+.

It has: twin T3/T04B hybrid Garrett turbos with .48 A/R exhaust housings on a custom mandrel bent equal length T3/T4 header set with real TiAL 38mm wastegates.

I opted for the stage 3 wheel, so the only difference between my set and the ~800AWHP capable set was the .63 a/r exhaust, which I can swap out any time.

"The 5 of the top 10 Quickest ¼ mile ET 3000gt/stealth cars in the USA are running Dynamic Racing DR-800-up kits. That speaks for it’s self to the level of performance delivered by these kits."

That was before Matt Monett (owner of dynamic racing) made his DR-1300 kit and ran an 8.8 @ 166. On a stock block 3000GT with DR racing heads.

It has a custom fueling set up, with an aeromotive A1000 pump, custom sump system, -8 AN "True Bore" silicone coated hose leading to the Y, individual -6AN true bore feeds into the -6AN size custom fuel rail, 780CC injectors (69% duty cycle at 18psi of boost, and still plenty of fuel pressure overhead), dual -6AN from the pump feeds the second Y, goes through the aeromotive fuel pressure regulator, and back to the fuel tank via another -6AN line.
For tuning I'm running an AEM EMS which is a complete standalone engine management system, with an AEM dual UEGO wideband controller.

It has a custom PPE front mount intercooler kit, with an HKS super sequential "show off" valve, and an AEM intake air temp sensor, post intercooler.

I have a quaife limited slip differential in a rebuilt stock transmission. I'll upload pics of what I did to my original transmission in a few minutes.
