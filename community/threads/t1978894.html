Re: Whats wrong with me?
Anti-depressants can and do stuff around with your sex drive (as well as most of your emotions..) if you are on the wrong dosage, or the wrong medication. They tend to get rid of the "peak emotions" - instead of getting depressed, you may get a little sad. Instead of feeling ecstacy, you feel happy. Instead of feeling horney, you feel, well, not much.

Antidepressants need to be regularly reviewed until the right one and the right dose for you is found. And you need to find the right balance between feeling and not feeling emotions.

My advice? Give them time to take effect (at least 2 weeks or so), and if things don't improve, talk to your doctor about lowering the dosage, or changing the medication.

In my case, my wife got pretty severe post-natal depression after our second child. We tried different things, until she found the right one and the right dose, and she is gradually lowering that one now. We found:

* being on the pill lowers your libido,

* constantly worrying that you should be horney definitely kills the feelings,

* exercise more, eat better and keep busy,

* and don't worry so much.

You'll find if you don't think about sex, the feelings will creep up on you when you least expect it...
