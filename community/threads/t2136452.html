Your medicine cabinet
Re: some questions about xanax xr 2mg
I notice when I don't have it I feel nervous and almost scared

This could be for several reasons.

One, it could be because you do have a legitimate anxiety condition, in which case feeling nervous/scared without the anxiolytic is more or less normal.

Two, you're developing a physical tolerance to the alprazolam, and what you're experiencing is a taste of withdrawal along the lines of rebound anxiety. In my opinion, from a medical standpoint, I don't consider Xanax useful when administered BID etc. for general anxiety purposes because the relatively short length of it's duration inevitably leads to not only more frequent dosing (not uncommon for Xanax patients to require 4x daily dosing), but more periods, albeit short ones, of rebound anxiety between these doses.

For panic attacks, or for PRN use in general, especially related to social anxiety, I don't think there is anything better than Xanax. Tolerance tends to build quickly with repeated and frequent use (ie. daily) but if used only on a true as-needed basis, and sparingly, that same dose will likely be effective for the patient who uses it responsibly for an indefinite amount of time. I've known people who have been prescribed to Xanax for 5+ years who have never needed a dose increment because of the fact that they rarely use more than a few tablets in a week.

You'll get better control of your anxiety by taking that XR as-is; it's the only proper way Xanax is effective as an all-day anxiety treatment, IMO.

Tolerance is still going to raw-dog you though.

If you feel you do need all-day anxiety relief I recommend giving Klonopin a shot before settling for the Xanax. Most doctors are fairly open to this especially if they prescribed the alprazolam first.
