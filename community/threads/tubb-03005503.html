My grampa died
Bill Twiss. He was my best friend. My BEST friend. He had a long hard life."<don't say anything or there will be consiquences!" He lived through war. He expected no gratitude from others. He was a hero of heros. He told me this:

"it might be a bit fucked up but this is the basic info."
We were in german territory, and our communications were cut off. Every man we sent out to find the wire was killed. One after the other. We were trying to keep quiet, but my friend's hands were blown clean off from a grenade. I had to shoot him. He was screaming.
I then took my turn to try to get contact with the other "groups" of men. I crawled through my friend's bodies. They were like hamburger meat. They had tank track marks on their bodies. I threw up many times. Everything was on fire. We were trapped in a town.
I managed to get out of the town, and my buddies said to forget about them. I didn't want them to die, so after I got contact with our HQ I returned, with more men. We heard a *whoosh* noise and a man five feet away from me was blown in half. He screamed like crazy. I had to shoot him too. His guts were all over the place.
We ran for cover, and found my trapped group. I brought them with me to safety.

He got vascular dementia in his old age. When it first started, he realized he was going crazy. He asked my older cousin Danny to shoot him. After many years of this, he couldn't mantain hiself very well. My family helped him out for a while, but it was too much stress for all of us.

He was transfered to a few different places, but his final place was in the VA hospital. My sister Aislyn and my mother Allison went to visit him. He was very very weak. The last words he said to my sister were:
Bill: Are you married yet?
Aislyn: No
Bill: They're all a bunch of fags!

my sister thought that was funny. A day later he died. He went in his sleep.

I was at the wake, and was the last person in there. My final words were:

Fuck. I love you dumcough.

Then a tear went down my face, down the side of my nose, on my cheek. I walked fast out the door and into the street. I watched the moon. I though about how much I'll miss him.

He was the best friend I ever had. A good man. I love him, and alway will. He did many more things but I don't feel up to writing them. I just got back from his wake. I'm crying writing this.

I will love him forever. My best friend, Bill Twiss.
