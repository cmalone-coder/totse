The Drip
Any of you who have snorted drugs have probably experienced the drip - when the excess of the substance you snorted runs down your sinuses into your throat.

Some people really enjoy it but i think it tastes like shit, and besides, if i wanted to eat the drugs i would've just eaten them. Even though most drugs that are effective intranasally are effective orally aswell, i prefer the rush that insufflation provides and would rather that all of the drug be absorbed in my sinuses.

So how can one avoid the drip? Should they do smaller lines? Or give more time between doses? Does the drip even contain a significant amount of psychoactive or is it mostly pill binders/impurities?

Thanks in advance.
