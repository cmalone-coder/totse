coke per sitting?
how much coke do you guys normally do in a sitting? This thread has no real informational value but I'm just curious. Please try to answer in grams and not in amount of lines (can vary too much). Also, please state how experienced you are and how often you do it. Oh, and by 'per sitting' I don't mean at the end of the night, I mean when you do it at that moment. I know lots of people (including myself) do a few lines, then do a few more when the buzz goes down.

Some of my friends like to do a gram or more straight (per person). they each get a gram, chop it up and do the whole gram in x amount of lines.

I personally don't like that. I've done it once and it made my stomach really burn afterward and it just wasn't a pleasant experience coming down.

I've done coke 14 or 15 times, so I'd say I'm familiar with the drug. I like to do just a bit. Maybe a .3 or .4 of a gram. It's just enough for 5-6 small lines that go up my nostrils really fast and it gives me a comfortable high.
