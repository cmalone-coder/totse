Here's the best solution to this problem that I've found (it does work, I promise, and I have the exact same problem)

(1) go to sleep tonight, but set an alarm to get up at whatever time is a reasonable normal time to get up everyday. So, I'd pick like, 8.30 or 9 or whatever, depending on if you're a layabout like me or not.

Get up then. It requires willpower, but you have to do it, and keep active enough all day so that you don't fall asleep.

Finally, when it gets to half an hour before your desired bedtime, turn on something you can listen to, music, tv, radio whatever. Something you can concentrate on.

Because this requires less brainpower or awakeness than lying there thinking your own thoughts around and around in your head, you actually fall to sleep faster, its almost like self hypnosis. It's best if you just shut your eyes, lie comfortably, and listen to whats going on, and fall asleep.

plus, because you're already tired, you have that added boost. Try to get up at the correct time again the next day. Keep going to bed at the same time, and getting up at the same time. In a week, you shouldn't be having any problems being sleepy at the right time, and the problem with your overactive brain should be solved by the music or whatever.
