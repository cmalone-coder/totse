smoking weed with idiots.... share your stories
Why does all this bullshit happen to me? What have I done to deserve this shit? Fuck, I meet this guy in one of my classes and we started talking about random shit. You know, small talk. Anyway we somehow ended up on the conversations about drugs and he asked me if I smoked weed. I said yes and he asked me if I wanted to smoke with him some time. I said sure and we continued talking.

Well today I had the class with him and he asked me if I was free to smoke after class. I was gonna go meet another friend and just hang out until my next class but I went to smoke instead.

So we're at a park near the campus and we're smoking and he starts saying shit like "i need to sit down" so we sit on a bench, and we're making small talk about the hot girls in our class and then he starts with the "fuck, i'm too high bro. im too high". So I tell him to stop and he says "no man, i gotta keep up with you"

Right then I knew this guy was full of shit. So I tell him, "man, it's not a competition, we're just relaxing. you don't gotta smoke if you don't" The following is 90% word-for-word, no joke.

"No, man, I gotta, you're doing it with ease bro. I gotta"

"no, you don't"

So it's my turn with the joint, and my next puff was gonna be it. I haven't smoked in at least a week, and this was some pretty good weed. I won't lie, I was thinking I had too much too. But I controlled it and relaxed.

he continues, "man how much weed you smoke man, fuck I gotta lie down on the bench"

So I'm standing near him while he's lying down on the bench.

So I ask him if he's alright and he says "man i knew i shouldn't have smoked with you. why did you even ask me to come?"

That was it, I never asked him to come, I didn't need to hear this shit. He was ruining my buzz.

I thanked him for smoking me out and made up an excuse I had to hand in an assignment at 4 and I forgot to do it.

Fuck that, I didn't need that shit. Fucking guy is 19 and acted like a bitch. I should have told him to grow a sac.


It's safe to say I won't be talking to him anymore.
