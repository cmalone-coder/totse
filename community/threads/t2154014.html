It apparently isn't designed to catch me out, but rather, so the recruiters can work out what kind of person I am, and whether I can be trusted with handling important information.

I'm basically wondering, will admitting to having smoked marijuana in the past put me at a serious disadvantage? And if not, what would be the best way of phrasing my experiences with drugs?
