Re: A ? for you chemists.....
Add d-tartaric acid to racemic meth freebase in methanol. d-methamphetamine d-tartrate is marginally soluable in methanol, so cooling and concentration of the alcohol will precipitate pure d-meth d-tartrate, which can be filtered and worked up to obtain d-meth HCl. The left over alcohol contains l-methamphetamine d-tartrate.
