The brutal reality of life......
So I'm in this really odd mood today probably brought on by drinking a little last night. And I was in Costco today, and looking at all the middle age lost souls and their consumption hunger after walking through the parking lot full of SUVs and minivans. I came up with a theory that I've brushed before:

In the universe as a system maximum entropy or disorder is desired. It is the most stable state of affairs. So if left to it's own devices a system (like our universe)will seek out this state in the fastest manner available. That is where life comes in.....

Life is natures way of disipating energy and creating disorder in the fastest way possible. Think about this: Higher life forms consume energy at an increasing rate compared to other earlier/less sophisticated organsms. Procreation/the pursuit of happiness/ any other concievable purpose of life reason fits with accelerating energy consumption as these goals are pursued.

Questions: Why do we like to use power in frivolous ways like fireworks/auto racing/art/ any number of human pursuits.

As humans we are at the top of the ladder, we use an amazing amount of energy and are good at the job nature intended us for.

Hmm this means that there will be a finishing point where the system reaches its stable configuration, then there must be some sort of re-cycling of the universe in some type of big crunch/big bang deal.

Hmm this is what happens when I sit back and think about the big picture and my thoughts of energy as universal "currency". But re-examining it this effect only occurs on a planetary/soon to be star system level.

More foods for thought.....

Anyone have any comments on my theory, flaws, counter theories, anything?

I don't think it's a good idea to give up ones life and live in the bush like an animal or some hippy.

Barely related point: I do however for the sake of our children think it would be smart to reduce consumption of petroleum.
