Morphine plugging
So I got over the whole stigma thing, cos my oral tolerance was getting way too high (150mgs to get a nod), and that took about an hour and a half to reach peak effects.

Prepared 2 30mg MS tabs by crushing them into powder, dissolving into sterile saline (I doubt the purity of the water matters when plugging, but better to be safer), drawing up though an oral syringe, and up it went. Not too painful or anything, it was just weird cos stuff was going into my asshole, rather than exiting it. It only took a couple of seconds to get it all in, so I took out the syringe, wrapped it up securely for disposal, and lied on my stomach to let the solution absorb. 5 minutes later, I was nodding the fuck out, and the high lasted for about 4-5 hours.

Considering I went from 150mgs + 1.5hrs to nod, to 60mgs and just 5 mins before nodding (the next day as well, so a tolerance break was not a factor), I would definitely recommend plugging for those who want to maximise their morphine, but don't want to shoot. I also had qualms about plugging, but didn't want to waste any more like I previously was, so I thought 'fuck it', and by god was it strong. I almost even felt a rush, the kind of thing you'd imagine IV to be (obviously nowhere near as close).

Infinitely better than eating, snorting or parachuting. It's definitely my new ROA of choice for morphine, at least until I can get opes with a higher oral bioavailability.
