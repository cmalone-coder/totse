Re: If you believe in an afterlife
quote:Originally posted by dearestnight_falcon:

So... are you saying that you're an athiest, or that you will be spending eternity gardening?

Heh. Neither really. I don't like the term "atheist" but sometimes I tell people I am one because it's easier for them to understand. As I've noted here before, I consider myself a "religious atheist." All of the magic and mystery of the universe can't be boxed into neat and tiny categories, and I don't believe my beliefs can be either. You could say I'm comfortable with contradictions.

As someone else mentioned, energy can neither be created nor destroyed. I simply feel my energy will take another form. If I was to be buried, I expect my body would disintegrate and I would be food for worms or fertilizer for flowers. (This is paraphrased from a scene in the movie Dead Poet's Society, to give credit where credit is due.) Other people may think this is depressing, but I think it's beautiful. It's the cycle of life and I feel that is something sacred/religious/spiritual/whatever you want to call it. I think concepts like "reincarnation" are metaphors that explain this natural process.

Of course, there may be something "more." I really don't know. Maybe I'll end up in the Christian Heaven/Hell, maybe I'll be literally reincarnated as another human or an insect, or maybe I'll be skipping across marshmallow clouds with unicorns and green dragons. I think these are all nice, fanciful stories. There's certainly nothing wrong with that - I like fanciful stories - I just don't see any reason to interpret them in a literal sense.

Maybe I'll be sent to Hell for an aversion to literality. Who knows?
