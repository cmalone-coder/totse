Re: Adam Corolla's take on the fun in Detroit
I know there is other issues than this, but the last thing I judge a car on is the interior. Maybe I've always been a spartan.... although I can certainly appreciate nice interiors.... but I couldn't care less about the aesthetics inside if I like the car itself. The only things I really look for is a good dash cluster with an informative set of gauges (Fucking hate missing tachometers, automatic or not), shifter placement, pedal/steering placement and driving position. Bad seats don't really bother me, a resiliency I'm sure to lose as I age. Frankly sometimes I get a kick out of a stupid fugly interior (like the one in my 86' mercury...RIP) with a huge left-to-right scrolling speedo, strange maroon upholstery, a couch as a front seat and a dash mounted shifter.

Granted I really lean towards angular, 'race' style set ups. I really enjoy a bunch of gauges and readouts on operation (not rice, only if the gauge actually applies/is important or you use it, and I HATE pillar gauges and big stupid dash ones), and I like switches and levers.... but only if they actually function.

Of course, I know what is being said about the american interiors... and I have to agree despite my personal indifference... but they need to re-think their whole business plan before they get around to making nice interiors!
