Re: Vegetables Ahoy!
i LOVE vegetables. with the weather getting cooler (it is here at least), i'm getting back into roasting vegetables. sweet potatoes, capsicum, red onion, baby eggplant and zucchini, with some whole unpeeled garlic, drizzled with olive oil, S&P, perhaps a sprig of rosemary then into a hot oven until it's all deliciously roasted and caramelised on the edges. sometimes i slice haloumi cheese at this point and put it on top then back in the oven to brown it.

they're a really good way of getting vegies into your diet, go well with a heap of things. if you want something a little bit more basic, you might want to invest in a steamer, it's a really quick easy way to cook vegies and retain their goodness. i always like broccoli, can be tossed into a pasta with some garlic/olive oil/sliced toasted almonds, or just served with a grilled meat.

spinach is also really good (and versatile). very healthy, barely needs cooking - mixing it with something else that's hot wilts it enough. or just toss it into a salad.

oh! get some corn on the cob! it's not only tasty, it's fun to eat! cook it in the husk, on the bbq. mmm... i haven't had that since summer...

there's probably no need to cook all the vegies and keep them in your fridge, cooked. it's easy enough to cook enougheach time you need them, if they even need cooking.
