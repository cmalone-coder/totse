Re: Shopping for new knife
I have a few suggestions, actually. And why do you like the liner lock? It's probably the weakest of the locks out there, and the hardest to get right for knife in the CRKT price range.

Cold Steel Voyager: Lockback, AUS-8 steel, Zytel scales. Decent.

Camillus EDC in 154CM: Framelock, great 154CM steel, stainless handles. Looks cool, too. IMO best knife for the money. Don't know if it comes with serrations, though.

Anything from Outdoor Edge: All of the knives are great, and are in the same price range as the CRKTs. The Impulse is awesome.

Kershaw Speed-assist knives are pretty good.

Spyderco: Endura or Delica with FRN scales and VG-10 steel. Lockbacks. Incredible knives.

You could save up a bit more and get a Griptilian from Benchmade, which is on a whole different level from the knives above. It has the AXIS locking mechanism, too.

Oh, and one more. The FL1 or FL2 from S&W. It's another framelocker, *but* it's a 3.75" blade, with the same designer as the EDC and the Paragee.

Of course, the M16's a sweet knife, too. But for me, it just seemed like one of those stepping stones onto getting a better knife.


peace out.
