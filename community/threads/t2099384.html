Yes I do believe in it, and I found her as well as married her. What we are really discussing is a "spiritual" connection of sorts.....throwing out any religious connotation behind those words. The reason I KNOW we are connected moreseo than just physically, emotionally and legally........is because we are oftentimes way too deep inside the other's minds and hearts. Silly example to make my point......

Imagine you have been with your girl for a year or more. One day you are home from work and waiting for her to come home. The thought of dinner hits you, and for whatever reason you think....."mmmmm, lets do Mexican". Neither one of you have had that for over 6 months, and you yourself haven't even thought about Mexican food for perhaps several weeks. She walks in the door and 2 minutes into the conversation she says....."What do you want to do about dinner, I was thinking Mexican". WTF?????? How the hell does anyone explain that? I could understand if we were both watching TV at the same time, and some commercial came on and it rubbed our brains the same way at the exact moment.

Shit like that happens all the fuckin time to me and my wife. And it ain't just dinner ideas......it could be calling an old loved one we haven't talked to in ages, an idea for the evening, a thought of sadness or elation, some horny thought we both have in bed. I have zero logical and rational explanation at "why" our minds/hearts are so intertwined with such frequency....so I just have to fall back on that mysterious "soul mate" answer.
