I think that you are going to need at least 50g as well. Here is why:

1 MG seed should contain 1 ug (microgram) of LSA. LSA is only 5-10% as potent as LSD, meaning you will probably have to take 10-20 times as much LSA to get the same results. LSD doses should be somewhere in the 60-200 ug range. This means that you would probably take 600-4000 ug of LSA. One MG seed weighs approx. .1 g, or there are 10 seeds in 1 g. You are going to want to take between 60-400 g of MG seeds.

I have tripped MG seeds before, and I don't think I have taken near enough yet (most I've done is 75 g). Actually, I just finished extracting the LSA from 150 g of MG seeds today. I am preparing to trip off of that tonight, and I still think that it is going to be too low of a dose.


AS FOR THE TRIP ITSELF:

If I was you, I would definitely do the extraction. They are TERRIBLE. The seeds will make you sick, more than likely. Extracting the LSA should help with the nausea and leg cramps (which can be unbearable).

I have not tripped very hard off of them yet (and I have NEVER done less than 45 g...). In all honesty I have been fairly disappointed, but I have never tried the extraction in such a great amount. I believe I have low dosed everytime. For this reason, I can't really tell you what to expect.

I can tell you this, though: I would get more seeds if I was you. You can get them directly from Crosman Seeds, they are the company that does not put anything on the seeds. They sell them by the LB. or 1/2 LB. (224 g-448 g).
http://www.crosmanseed.com/specialblue.htm

Since I have posted so darn much already, I figure I may as well tell you the best way to extract, from what I have found:

1. Grind up 150 g MG seeds in pepper or coffee grinder...
2. Soak them for 2 days in 150 mL petroleum ether (I use Naptha (Zippo fluid))...
3. Filter the liquid from the seeds...
4. Discard liquid, allow mush to dry...
5. DO NOT PROCEED UNTIL MUSH IS COMPLETELY DRY. This may take a few days. There should be ABSOLUTELY NO smell of the naptha (unless you want to get sick and/or die).
6. Soak the mush in 120 mL ethanol for 2 days (use the highest proof alcohol you can, Everclear is by far the best)...
7. Filter and save liquid, labeling it "1"...
8. Resoak mush in 120 mL ethanol for 2 days...
9. Filter and save liquid, discard the mush...
10. Add liquid "1" to the new liquid...
11. Pour all the liquid into a cookie tray and allow it to evaporate...
12. When the liquid has evaporated, a yellow gum remains. Scrape it up and put it in capsules. ENJOY!

If you want, you can skip steps 11 and 12 and just drink the liquid, but it'll be a decent amount of Everclear, and I think it is MORE than worth it to wait an extra day to swallow some capsules instead of Everclear.

I hope all this info helps a little. Oh, and maybe you should check out Hawaiin Baby Woodrose Seeds. They are supposed to contain a lot more LSA than MG seeds.
