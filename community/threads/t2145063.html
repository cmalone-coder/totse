You are confusing yourself because you have absolutely no idea what BPD is like. Suicidal tendencies and promiscuity are more common in women who are borderline. I have BPD and I have only had a few suicidal episodes in the past 7 years.


Borderline women can be promiscuous because they believe they are worthless and deserve to be exploited, hence their pursuit of sex. If they are abused, it only reasserts their belief and that can be paradoxically comforting.
Then again, there are borderline women who hate sex due to past mistreatment.

I do constantly think about suicide but I don't advertise that to friends, again, it's the women who are more likely to seek attention through self harm. Self harm is not entirely for attention though, it temporarily gets rid of the emptiness.
I prefer shopping though.
It's not fair to say that I'm exempt from this though, I am rather self-centred and I tend to seek attention quite a lot of the time.

BPD feels like you are completely devoid of any purpose or direction and are just daydreaming your way through life, it can become completely unbearable, which is why we consider suicide at times and engage in endorphin-releasing activities, such as cutting or listening to loud music.

I also think in extremes, something is either good or bad, or I don't give a fuck about it. The problem is, my beliefs are constantly changing along with my mood, so I am completely erratic and self-contradictory in conversation.
