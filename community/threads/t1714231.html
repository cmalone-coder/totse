Video game scam.
First off, im not sure if this is already a well known scam. I did this after last christmas, and am just realizing its scam-ness. Anyway, hope at least someone likes this.

First off, get a game. Either buy it or do this next time you get one as a gift. Try to get the receipt, as it makes this easier. It may not work if you steal the game, because when they scan it it might reveal that it isnt paid for.

Next, open the box so you dont damage it. Take the cd key out (you can figure out how).Then burn a copy of the game for yourself. Then put the cd's and such back in. If it has any free give-aways, go ahead and take those too. then put the essentials (excluding cd key) back in (remember to put the original cd's in. They will probably be unhappy if you try to return a bootleg ).

Next, take the game back to the store and return it. If they ask why, tell them it diddnt include the cd key. They will likely give you cash back, but if not you will have to settle for store credit, most likely in the form of a gift card.

Now you have an option. You can either take that cash and spend it on whatever you want (within price range ) or you could buy another game with it and repeat the process. If you repeat, you should definately go when another person is working at the returns counter. I would wait a while between returns though, they will be less suspicous.

Or you could just steal a CD key and download a copy of the game, but thats a hassle.

Anyway, i hope i have helped someone get a few free games.

Re: Video game scam.
Yes this has been discussed before; however, there are two significant problems with this idea.

1) Many new games have, at least somewhat sophisticated, security schemes (SafeDisc, LaserLok, DiscGuard, SecuROM, SafeCast, ProtectCD, and many more) Thus, one would need specific programs/know-how to bypass the security measures in order to copy the CD

2) Most stores only accept opened CDs/games/software/DVDs for an exchange, not a refund (yes, even with a receipt). Contact your particular store, or glance through a short compendium of return policies here: http://www.totse.com/bbs/Forum7/HTML/004766.html
