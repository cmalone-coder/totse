Re: Car hopping tonight, tips from anyone?
breaking glass is loud, but its the sound of glass falling on itself/ground combined with initial impact that is responsible for the noise. Loud wind should be alright for covering you, best bet though is to duct tape the window and then smash it with your elbow/kick it in. Should reduce some of the shattering sound. To maximize the amount you're going to pull in, case the area first and scope out your targets. Test doors first (of course) before you go around making a bunch of noise smashing shit. I don't steal anything but if it was me, I'd cruise the area, find isolated cars and check the doors on em making notes of which ones are safest to hit. I saw the guy pull out 4 packs of smokes, itrip, bunch of power tools and some cds.

Re: Car hopping tonight, tips from anyone?
I'm no expert but my tips:

-Don't go under the influence

-Don't car hop for too long

-If you get really good loot, like a laptop, then call it a night

-If you see something nice in a car but it's not unlocked, break the window, grab it, and call it a night. Don't push your luck

-Don't car hop the same neighborhoods regularly

-Don't go with a friend unless you absolutely trust them

-Be prepared for alarms to go off even if the door is unlocked. It happened to me 3 times in one night.

-When checking visors, flip it back up slowly. A lot of times that's where the garage openers are and you can accidentally open it.

-Don't fucking get caught. If you're being chased by someone do everything you can to get away.

-People will kill you for breaking into their car, so be careful.

Good luck
