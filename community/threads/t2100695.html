Re: Do you eat your shrooms fresh, or dried?
Dry, because thats my favorite way to eat them.

I use a coffee grinder to powder 4-7 grams, then I mix that in with a tall glass off fruit juice and chug. It doesn't taste bad at all and you don't have to chew on some nasty ass mushrooms (I hate eating any mushrooms for some reason). It only takes about 10 seconds to consume your desired amount of shrooms.

Also, the come up is WAY quicker, and IMO it helps get rid of that awkward feeling you get 30-40 minutes after munching. It also makes the peak and entire experience more intense.

However it does make the trip end sooner... unless you potentate upon the peak. Recently I tried doing a small line of ketamine just as the peak was ending, and it prolonged it for an hour. Nitrous is also good for making the peak last longer.

I dunno man, i've had some strange, horrible, amazing, profound, mind blowing, spiritual..... crazy experiences on mushrooms.

In 2 1/2 years or so, I took them.... 9 or 10 times? That included a break of 14 months or so when I didn't have any, and then the past summer when I had 75g dried which i'd acquired, and had some nuts times with friends, on.

I have dosed from like 2g odd to almost 7g (mixed with 100mg-ish of MDMA) and with many drugs, I feel I "progress" quite fast. Mushrooms have seriously affected my mindset and outlook on life... sure, it's changed normally, but boy, they have opened my mind more to my position in the world, universe, society.... my potentials, my failures and bad experiences.... all sorts of stuff. I have tripped in my bed room and listened to a strings orchestra album on repeat for 6 hours, whilst sliding down the side of my bed and focusing totally on the visuals and the mindset (my first trip), run 2-3 miles across a city in 40 minutes scared pretty shitless, sat in sunshine and felt euphoria truly beyond almost everything with my best friend, laughed at a kid falling over on a tricycle (:D), walked along a main road tripping, sat on this stone (http://www.megalithic.co.uk/a558/a312/gallery/England/Gloucestershire/Threecounties.JPG) starting to peak, ridden over a forest of red, beautiful, amazing leaves in autumn on a bike, as they crunched, crackled and radiated energy that left me grinning and laughing uncontrollably. I have biked down a huge fucking grassy hill tripping my tits off, and down a road with cars, weaving all over the place in the dark whilst stopping to comment with a friend about how the "road looks like a dinosaur's neck...", I have smoked salvia and done nitrous and popped into crazy dimensions (voids?) when on shrooms, I have learnt nothing is real, and yet everything is, I have despaired, I have been reborn, I have had moments in job interviews where a sonic wooshhhhh just embraces me, and the mushroom voice yells "NOTHING IS REAL, REALLY" and internally I crack up and have to remind myself that life is srs bsns......

Oh, i've seen the clouds part, and reform, and swirl into amazing shapes, i've seen every colour i've ever seen in my life radiating out of the leaves of a tree whilst lying in the dirt on my back, I have been Shpongle'd, I have read William Blake and felt moved to tears, I have run around a cow field and watched a friend yelling at cows, I have felt that golden energy that tells you to truly live life to its fullest, and got lost in dark woods after running into a private birthday party for 40-50 year olds, yelling loudly and with pupils the size of saucers. I've got separated from a friend on the 7g trip, coming up on MDMA, and wandered around trying to work out why he went running into a bramble bush.... I have questioned the very existence of my name, and how it defines me... Oh, and i've beaten up a tree.....

Haha, seriously, this is just a list, and i'm not going off on one huge description of any single trip - shrooms themselves are the trip really, and I feel like I have learnt my lessons. Taking them again is pushing something which I cannot explain, and taking me into the darker emotions of shrooms, which hung over my later trips. I think i've "messed up" some areas of my mind at points with shrooms, i've also gained profound joy and hope through them..... they're a mixed bag, and one that seriously respect, but I need no more. I need a loooooong break from shrooming, I know i'll feel it if i'm ready to trip again. Until then.... we shall see.

I guess after an Indian deity, fresh out of an Alex Grey picture envelops your very being as you close your eyes, beckons with a "hand" which turns into a foetus, looping through the circle of life and then imploding in a ball of energy...... it takes a bit to top it. Psychedelics are a tool to learn from - I have learnt I no longer need them at this stage in my life, instead, I need to address what they have already taught me, some lessons which are still sinking in, after 2 1/2 years, and shit, there's a potential lifetime ahead.

I've rambled so much here, my experiences aint been out of the norm, or even that exciting according to some, but damn, they've shaped me! Writing this post has put things into perspective a little more, even....

Push your luck with mushrooms and they can kick your ass, there is something they unleash within your emotions which really controls the trip. I'm not going to ramble on about the "Mushroom spirit" or any of that crap, but there is a definite vibe at the heart of the trip, which ebbs and flows as the trip progresses. I've ridden the waves anyway, and now i'm going to live my life in this crazy, wonderful world. At any moment, everything can feel like a dream, a delusion, a blindfold..... i've had the "nothing is real!" thoughts, and through that..... it's shown me how real everything truly is (it doesn't last though.... but shit, nothing's been lost! ;)).

Sorry for this ramble! :)
