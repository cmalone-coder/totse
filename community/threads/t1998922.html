Milkshakes
it has come to my attention that the concept of milkshakes is foreign to most people, that is why im going to share the knowledge i have with "yall"
stuff you will need:
a cooking pot (large)
a glass (drinking)
a blunt
a pen tube
icey water

steps:
roll the blunt with the pen tube inside the innerleaf, so you have a blunt with a hollow tube coming out of the back, resembling a cigarette in a fancy holder that you used to see in the twenties.

next, you put a pot of icey water in the freezer with the glass in it. let it sit for a half hour, or till the glass is ice cold.

now that your class is cold, spark the blunt and put your mouth over the cherry as if you were going to blow a shotty. now put the end of the pen tube (connected to the blunt) and place it in the bottom of the glass. now blow a shotty into the glass.
you will see that the smoke has stuck to the glass, you are now ready to tilt the glass back and and sip the smoke right out of the glass.... just like drinking a milk shake.
