Jamiroquai I have tried your method before and found it to not rid the impurities entirely. Many cuts are very water soluble. I was toying with solubility curves before I went straight edge and have found better results for myself.

How do I do it? Well first I make sure all my product is powderized. then I take some dionized water (or distilled water) and bring it to about 160 degrees ferrenheit. Then I quickly take the baby syringe and suck the hot water and poor it into the meth powder. I only use just enough water so the meth dissolves and no extra water. Then I place the meth/water solution in a ice bath. An ice bath is a jar or bucket or whatever filled with mostly ice and then filled with water. I then place the glass jar filled with the meth/water in the ice/water bath and let the meth/water solution get as cold as can be (32 ferrenheit prefably). Next I throw a starter crystal (a pure shard of meth) inside the meth/water solution. Instantly a bunch of crystals will form. Then I quickly throw the meth/water/crystal solution in a buchner funnel w/filter and vacuum aspirator (pump). The pure crystals will sit on top the filer. Let the left over solution evaporate for a while and repeat steps with ice bath.
