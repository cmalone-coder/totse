PO box vs private mailbox for questionable packages?
my friend has a PO box he's been using for receiving some highly questionable packages.

so far everything's been fine, but recently he's been getting extremely sketched out about it, reading about PO box busts, having them reseal the package, put it in there, and bust them as they come pick it up.

so, he was wondering, would a private mailbox, specifically one at the UPS store or similar locations, be safer than a PO box at the post office? these private boxes are open 24/7, and i beleive his PO box isn't (not 100% on that). this would mean being able to come in at night, make sure there's no strange cars in the parking lot, etc, and as long as the package fits in his box, he wouldn't have to sign the UPS store log for packages that don't fit.

all the packages are (of course) coming through USPS, and a controlled delivery is not one of his major worries as he doesn't intend to sign for anything.

my main questions are:

1. does the UPS store have the right/ability to search your package without a warrant just because you're using their mailbox service? do the same rules that apply to USPS apply here, since USPS would still be transporting the package?

2. are these personal mailboxes generally safer (obviously nothing is 100% safe) than PO boxes?

3. does anyone know if busts happen more often at these UPS-store-like boxes, or PO boxes? keep in mind my friend is not sending or receiving any extremely large quantities. the most physical weight he would be receiving would be about a QP of vegetable matter, double vaccuum sealed and covered in about an entire roll of packaging tape, and large packages like this wouldn't be coming as often. mostly letters/small packages.

4. would a UPS store box be best for maximum privacy, or one by a smaller, locally owned private mail box provider?

my friend plans to open it up on the premise of an ebay business, using the business name as the addressee. he seems to think that this would make frequent receipt/sending of packages less noticable. obviously he would most likely still have to provide ID, but the PO box he currently has is in his real name (seems like a very bad idea IMO, he's not quite sure how to get around it though since most places in his area seem to require ID)

please keep in mind he has done a good amount of research on this subject, and is just looking for some extra input. any helpful feedback will be appreciated.
