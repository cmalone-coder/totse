Re: Oven dried epsom salt?
hi kahim,,,tryps advice is always A1 and ive never found it lacking--yet sometimes he forgets that most here dont have a chem education and doesnt get down to explaining it in basic terms--or set out procedures for the novice---so here goes---get about a kilo of epsom salts--spread it evenly on a baking pan/dish and place in oven on about 250c for around 3 hours---allow to cool then take out of oven and tap out of baking pan/dish--crumble into knuckle size pieces and store in a airtight container---how much you use is a personal choice on the amount of solvent to be cleaned to the water content you fear is in there---but about a small handfull a litre ive found to be sufficient--just drop it in the solvent and reseal solvent lid---swirl around for 5 minutes and then let sit for a couple of hours--your solvent is now anhydrous and ready to use---some filter it before use for fear of epsom salts contaminating there pill mass---goodluck

Remember the purpose of the salt. Epsoms salt molecules can hold several water molecules (I can't remember right now, maybe seven), but you need to make sure its as fully dehydrated as you possibly can to insure adequate efficiency.
