He is here, as He has been since the beginning of time.
He is benevolent, rewarding the Good and disappointing the Evil in the world. His tools of Purity and Wellwishing are the Gifts he carries; the Punishment and Reprimand he doles out are in Coal.
Many doubt his presence, but yet they are rewarded, for He requires not that the Good acknowlege his presence.

The Blasmephers of Evil denouce Him and portray Him as merely a figment of a man's imagination, but we, the enlightened, bask in His omnipresent existance, as it inspires us to a greater good. They say it is impossible for one man to deliver gifts to billions of people; how fooler they are, for He is powerful, and works in mysterious ways that are not of our concern.

Bewarned, Blasmephers, for He is making His list of the Naughty and the Nice, checking it twice, and before you know it, he will be coming to town. Seek redemption at His hands and be saved - it is not too late, for He is one of benevolent soul.

Santa Claus: hear His name, and accept Him as your saviour.
