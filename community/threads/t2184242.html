Angel caught on camera
http://news.ninemsn.com.au/world/703983/hospital-angel-caught-on-camera/?rss=yes

A mother believes a visit from an angel saved her sick daughter's life  and she has the photos to prove it.

Chelsea Banton, 14, was fighting for her life after a bout of pneumonia, a collapsed lung and subsequent infections saw her breathing with the aid of a ventilator at Charlotte Presbyterian Hospital.

As her family gathered to say their final goodbyes and mother Colleen faced the decision on removing her life support, hospital staff noticed a mysterious bright light outside her room.

"On the monitor, there was this bright light," the Daily Mail reported Mrs Banton as saying.

"And I looked at it and I said, 'Oh my goodness! It looks like an angel!''

When nurses then took off her oxygen mask, Chelsea's vitals returned to normal.

A fortnight later, she returned home in time for her 15th birthday.

"The nurse practitioner who saw the image in the monitor said, 'I've worked here 15 years, and I've never seen anything like it'," Mrs Banton said.

Chelsea's mother captured the baffling light on the CCTV monitor with a digital camera.

"If [people] doubt it, that's fine ... but I know what I saw, and the picture's untouched," Mrs Banton said.

"I didn't make it up.

'I look at things differently than I used to, because I know God is in control."

Amazingly, it wasn't the first time Chelsea had escaped a brush with death.

Doctors reportedly gave her 36 hours to live after she was born five weeks prematurely, but she recovered to lead a relatively normal childhood.

"I'm learning that every day she's alive is a miracle," Mrs Banton said.

What's interesting about this photo is that one would assume the anomaly in question is simply light which has refracted from a metal or smooth surface, especially with the setting having a long, marble-like hallway, and many solid and smooth surfaces close by. However, when one looks a little further anyways, we find a few interesting conflicts with the initial assumption. For one, a death was occurring very near where this image appeared. Coincidence? Maybe. For two, if we take a close look at the inverted image, we see that at the very bottom of the anomaly, there is some straight-cut lines, but I do not see anything within the close vicinity which might have caused this cut-off. There is no square or sided objects between the anomaly and the available light source, all looks clear in all directions, yet we have an image of light which is cut off at the bottom. Thirdly, the angle of the image appears to be leaning slightly to the left, which would make sense if an entity did in fact just exit through that wall, heading for the hallway, coming from what looks to be a room on the other side of that wall. The apparent speed and direction of the image looks almost freeze-framed into two parts, as if the camera could not capture the entire movement in one shot, but produced two images in sequence on the same shot. And lastly, the image appears to be roughly in the shape of a body, with a smaller roundish shape on the top, and wider features at the middle - hardly evidence, but certainly coincidental, considering there appear to be no objects in the immediate vicinity which would cause light to make this multi-part shape. In cases like these, we can never rule out spook, but we can simply keep them in the back of our minds to compare with other similar captures.
