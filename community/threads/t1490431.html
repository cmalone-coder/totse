Re: Engagement Rings (what to spend)
Tradition used to dictate that a man would save up an amount of six months' salary to buy an engagement ring.

Of course, this was before some 'temporary wartime measures' like income tax. So we're not sure what they mean, exactly. 'Could therefore be three months' gross, six months' net, six months' gross, or twelve months' net.

I guess that they figured you really, really, really cared for the lady in question. Or that if you the sort of guy who was living from paycheque to paycheque, that she'd get bored after waiting 10 years for you to put aside that much cash and surrender her affections to another suitor. Fathers 'looking out for' their daughters and all that.

If you consider, nowadays, that some courtships only last for six months (and some even less), then this would seem a little unreasonable. After all, with what could you offer to pay for dinner (or pay your rent, for that matter) if you are impoverished by saving for a ring?

So modern wisdom, if you can believe in such a thing, says that one, two, or three months' salary (gross or net -- your pick) is acceptable.

This can range from $1000 for minimum-wage earners, to over $25000 for doctors, lawyers, engineers, and other really capital-intensive employees.
