Re: Mescaline.
Assuming of course that you intend to extract caffeine from tea...

Start reading up on:

acid base extraction theory
solubility of salts and freebases in polar and non polar solvents
solubility
polarity

Organic chemistry in general, just read everything and anything.

Start small - screwing up with 100g is a lot less costly, and I've found it more difficult when working with large quantities. Also the quantities of reagents you have to work with obviously goes down, thus saving your ass if you do something really stupid.

Make sure to recrystallize, and don't skimp on paying more for a better quality non polar... evaporating lighter fluid will not do!

For now, I would advise following a tek, one that looks comprehensive. Follow measurements, don't swap reagents unless you're absolutely sure, and write down everything you do! Try to label containers; guessing the difference between 15% NaOH and 33% HCl is not entertaining for you or your extraction.

These are just my thoughts, written down rather quickly, so take from them what you will. Good luck.
