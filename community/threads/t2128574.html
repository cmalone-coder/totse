Re: doing CID alone tonite? yay or nay?
I've kind of had that before, but I wouldn't describe it as bad so much as difficult. It definitely wasn't fun, but it's not always about that. I've described some of what happened to my friends and they said that they went through some of the same things I did during a bad trip.

But you sound pretty confident and have some experience, so I think you'll be fine. If things get rough, just remember to not judge yourself. Also, the most grounding thought that I can think of (if it goes bad simply for being too intense) is to remind yourself that you took drugs. I've heard advice to write down your name, what you took, when you took it and when you should be sober.

I've never tried it, but the theory is that seeing your own handwriting, and especially that information, will remind you that you're just on drugs and that you will come down. I do not, however, advise trying to abort a trip at all. If you have issues, work through them. That's what psychedelics are really for.

You sound like you'll be fine, though. The number one thing that stops bad trips is confidence in yourself to not have one.
