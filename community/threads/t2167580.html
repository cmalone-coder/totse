Re: Weird laptop problem - Broken power button?
Well you should probably stop trying to turn it on to start with. Since tea is sticky, you can't expect all the residue to evaporate like water. If you feel confident enough, dismantle, inspect and clean it. There are plenty of instructional videos on youtube for dismantling various models, and if it happens to be a dell, service manuals are available for free on the dell website. Generally all you need is one or two screwdrivers, and maybe some clean cloths and canned air. It's really not too difficult to do once you've seen how it's done.

When you take it apart, make sure you remove the battery and do not plug the battery back in until you're sure each component is fully dry and clean again, it's worth waiting an extra day or two just to be sure all the moisture has gone away. If you're still having similar problems with it then post back here or something.
