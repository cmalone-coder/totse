A Closer Look at the Exact Benefits of Marijuana
An old and possibly tired subject for most of you, I bet, I still would like to discuss the side of marijuana for self-development/improvement, and question weather it is useful in this area.

Joan Bello, author of The Benefits of Marijuana, says that the "regular" (daily) use of marijuana leads to the individual to realize and develop their true values, in other words, cut the crap of what you think you'd like to do, or what others are telling to do, and pick what you truly want to do and systematically work towards it.

Based on your experience, would you say this is true?

She also discusses the possibilities of a higher consciousness, centered on the "now". I agree, there may be a possibility of this, however, I only experience this "higher consciousness" when I'm high and it goes away when I'm not, which creates two separate modes of thinking conflicting with each other, being less productive than if I were either high all the time or not high at all.

From what I've experienced I can draw two conclusions,

1) A regular use of marijuana is only positive in helping one figure out their values but not in helping them realize them.

2) It can be interesting on what knowledge can be learned from a separate perspective of being high but the only way to get the true value out of it would be to record ones thoughts in the state and realize them when sober.

Your thoughts?
