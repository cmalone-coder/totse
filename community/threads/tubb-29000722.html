Re: Finally got that dang USC!!
Poop: You did indeed mis-state in the calico thread. No big deal. Special Weapons makes a lower for both the USC and the SL8. Overall I've heard nothing but crap about SW when it comes to their clones (the SW3 and the SW5) but people have been putting thousands of rounds through their conversions with no problems. With both conversions you need to dremel out a small (and I mean SMALL) amount of material from the inside of the reciever. The USC conversions run flawlessly with factory m3 grease gun magazines (not para-ordinance) while the SL8 requires some tinkering to work. Only certain brand mags, ya need to upgrade the spring and use the 'green follower.' After tinkering around a bit, they also run perfectly. Not too sure about the 40 rounders though... The gas piston works pretty quickly. I think the weight of the extra 10 cartriges might be a weak point and cause some stovepiping
Oh, you wanted some pics. I havent gotten the m3 conversion but it looks pretty tactical as is :-)
http://phido.org/guns/noahusc/index.htm

I've put around 1300 rounds through the rifle and it only had 2 FTEs. And that was within the 1st 50 shots. Aside from that it runs perfectly. Next step is the conversion so I can cut off that damn PC 'thumbhole.' With the conversion, it has enough US made parts to get around the silly import laws.

If anybody wants a lightweight, practical, carbine-accurate home defense piece, I highly recommend picking one up.

My $.02
