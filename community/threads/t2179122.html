Desperate Bicycles -- #1 British punk band of the '70s

The Kids -- another great Belgian band. "Do You Love the Nazis" is a classic
Negative Approach -- token early hardcore pick
Crime -- too rock to be respected as punk, far too shitty to be respected as rock, played San Quentin in cop uniforms (http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1EnZi8C_VYE), primitive as fuck, zero musical talent but rocked anyway
Minutemen -- true Gs, greatest thing to ever come out of SST
Black Randy & the Metrosquad -- 3rd greatest punk album title ever: "Pass the Dust, I Think I'm Bowie." This band was led by an obese gay white man who was convinced he was black
Teengenerate -- beasts from the East. These crazy Japs actually knew how to rock, unlike 99% of punk bands from the '90s
Suburban Lawns -- Frank Zappa's favorite punk band. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32-LHS2-LxU
Art Phag -- most primitive band ever. The singer from this band is an insane hick and built a rollercoaster in his backyard. They also invented grunge (along with the Scientists) but no one wants to acknowledge this
Theoretical Girls -- the only No Wave band worth listening to

Elvis Hitler (last 4 are psychobilly type of stuff)
