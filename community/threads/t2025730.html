"Man's Consciousness is gained through what, he, man, does about the knowledge of what he is, in relation to that from which he came, and toward which he is going. Hence a man's analysis and
understanding of himself - it is as well to know from whence he came as to know whither he is going." - . . .We find ourselves, being born, living, learning, growing old, and passing on on this hunk of junk we call earth. All the time desperately searching for a greater purpose. A purpose to justify our existence, to put meaning to the seemingly random patterns that life follows. The greater majority of the populace will come to hold dear to one idea/belief/faith or another, all
the time they ignore the somewhat silent, but ever persistent feeling that something does not quite gel. That what they have come to call their 'personal salvation', does not quite stack up with personal life experiences, and leaves much unexplained. The true nature of the Universe, the Absolute and Perfect Truth floats unobserved mostly, showing its beauty throughout key moments in ones life, ever reminding that we should never settle for less. To come to rest upon a peticular idea, or faith, or belief that SOMEONE else has laid down for you, is defeating the purpose of existence. All around us lies the evidence of the Truth, we must be open to it, shedding the skeptical thought patterns bourne to us from birth. Take a moment, this moment, to find harmony in nature, in being in nature, and by being a part, one with nature. Overcome fear of actualization of our True meaning, not to be afraid to break the mold, seeking realization of
what eternity and the eternal life truly mean. Putting aside egos and individualism, to perceive our lives not from the physical standpoint, but to observe, to realize, to witness the entirety of the universe from the spiritual aspect. The Weave - The Force - the whatever token term you define it by, the 'natural' laws that govern the universe -
motion/energy/vibration/magnetism/gravity - separate light from itself and you have darkness, separate darkness from itself and you have... Without light there could be no dark and without dark there could be no light, but without ether you could have neither. This interconnecting force, the master pattern for all of existence flows from one central 'creator' and connects all life within the endless bounds of space, one to another, more than that, connects everything back to the 'creator'. Without the understanding of this 'bond', or understanding the immediate cause of manifest creation, one has no hope of ever obtaining an abstract view of nature. You will find that understanding Eternity, and our role within it, to be completely impossible, and garnering Absolute Truth will continue to elude. Life, this existence in a physical realm, is but a series of brief and fleeting moments. These moments in this time are shared by a multitude of individuals, who are intertwined one to another. Rather directly or indirectly, through friends, family, community, or even the seemingly limitless stolid acquaintances, meetings unnoticed and unacknowledged, and taken for
granted. Among the countless strangers, lost in our passing each and ever day, all are connected, all share one singular bond. We can only hope that we happenstance to meet those with whom we share a similar disposition, that they would be among the few who remain True. This is not to say that we snub the rest of humanity, merely that we should find those few, in whom we have absolute trust, and share the Eternal Truth. From the many failed, and faulty connections we inevitably find ourselves tangled within, we wish to find but a handful of spirits. That a True and Genuine bond would arise. Count
yourself fortunate to have found such compassionate spirits whence you come by them. Interaction with said spirits, who by their similarities, and likewise differences, enables us to explore who
we truly are and how we relate within the 'Weave'. We come to understand, the barest of understandings mind you, that these relations bring forth light upon the reality of eternity that
we are ALL embodied within, the Truth. Within the first seconds of melding with a being of perceived unknowing, our spirit has examined, processed, and determined rather a further intimate
melding of our bond would result in exotic bliss, or perpetual misery. Rather or not on one of many different levels of connection, that there is the compatibility required to make a more melding of existence. If the seeds planted from this first chance encounter perchance take root, that two spirits feel compelled to further explore the depths of their Weaves intertwined, within a blink of an eye, two becomes one - one becomes two and an Eternal pattern has been formed.
Flowing forth through the farthest and darkest reaches of the Universe, forever connected and familiarity one to another. By these bonds we come to appreciate the delicate nature, and initial
understanding of the Eternal weave. We start to grasp bonds made on a mental, an emotional, and on a spiritual realm. And when these bonds form we get the curious feeling that this physical
nature is not the entirety of our existence. From this standpoint of realizing a spiritual connection, shall move onward with the Weave.
The same connection you find yourself sharing with those closest to you, you share with countless others, from what you would define as sentient beings, unto the seemingly inanimate objects propagated throughout this planet we call Earth, even beyond. The Weave, The Force, Creator, and Author of existence -GOD-. All of nature, the universe, and all creation, seen and unseen, stem from one central source. Reality as we know it, is a manifestation of the Creator,
as we understand it, fulfilling His Purpose. Obliging His Will to Create, to construct, to LOVE. In its most basic definition, Creation is Love. From this Creator flows the essential laws, and components for all of existence, all of creation was drawn from, and created from His Being. All we see, All we know, is from God, and all is upon is journey back to God. All of creation emanating from God, logic and reason tells us, that to come from God, that we are in fact part of God, and thus we come to the foundation of the Weave. All of Creation has a direct and uninterpretable bond to our Creator. This energy touches
all of Creation, the seen, the unseen, all of nature from humans to animals , from plants to the microscopic, the planets, the stars and everything in between. From what we see in this physical realm, through all the realms hidden from our meager understanding. Everything was culled from the very essence of God, His Energy thus manifesting itself by taking form of existence. Creating an unimaginable 'Weave' of beings, connected one to another, and having an effect one to another. Rather you believe or not, you are intertwined and woven within this weave, held together by energy in its most basic definition, Love. This in itself holds the very threads of
existence together, the absolute need of our Creator to shed His Love upon His Creation. His plan for all of existence to share in the absolute bliss that perfect and true love holds, and that not one single atom be separated from it. Our sole purpose is to awaken, to realize Eternity, to shine personal illumination upon God within us. To see that God is in everything we see, is within everything we feel, and is the author of and coinhabitates within existence. To acknowledge union with our loving Creator is our
destiny and His Divine Will. We begin to see that these myriad of connections we make with fellow spirits, rather they be real, or fake, permanent or in passing, to a person or to the houseplant that you pay devotion to, that all is connected, woven together in a Weave of energy/love, and our interactions has a lasting and noticeable effect upon the Weave as a whole. Common misinformation has the majority of the populace believing, we are nothing but a mass of individuals, evolved entirely from one-cell organisms, aeons ago, that we have no purpose, that no Divine will guides us, and worst
of all that there is nothing that connects us together. In later chapters we shall delve into more detail behind the actual foundations, and workings of the weave, and the ins and outs of
eternal existence Realize this, beyond what we comprehend as reality, what we observe through our physical senses, nature around us, and nature within us, these things that we define as reality, are in
actuality an illusion. This existence we find ourselves embodied within, brings a host oflimitations, one of which is the viewing of reality for what it truly is. Through motions and vibrations, energy and gravity, a painting is being drawn for our physical beings to observe.
Understanding the true nature, and coming to Absolute Truth, brings us to the immense, infinite, unbelievable truth about nature, existence, eternity, and the paths we tread along the way to
our Eternal home, within the loving grasp of our Creator. There is nothing, but God, nothing can exist outside of God, reality is but an illusion of physical senses, there is nothing that really
exists, other than God and the manifestation, the reflection, the shadow of -GOD-. The Author of the Weave, being God, or whatever you may choose to call the orchestrating force behind creation, provides the blueprint for Creation, the foundation and the structure of the Weave, as well as His Divine Will, and Eternal plan for ALL of us to return unto HIM. Most
humans have the preconception of a God being all loving, this is true beyond the scope of all imagination. Holding to a concept as well of Hell, or Shadow, or outer darkness, eternal suffering beyond all comprehension. This concept could not be more of a fable. God being apart of all of us, and all of us being part of God, it would be unrealistic to think he would suffer a part of Him to spend Eternity in Damnation. Women have a better understanding of this, no matter
how evil a child of theirs may be, no matter what wrong they commit, could any mother stand to see her child suffer just one day, let alone an eternity of suffering. Time was created by humans to better control the environment they found themselves in, there is no time in Eternity. If you can grasp that exists neither a beginning nor an end, once manifested, there is no matter in existence that can be removed. You can not truly and completely destroy matter derived from energy, you may change its basic foundation, but its energy
still remains. Energy is eternal, indefinite, infinite, and all of creation is formed from it. In the same way that lunar cycles effect the tides, and climates upon the earth, so does our bond. Through the weave, our Creator is thus effected by our choices and actions, and as
such all of existence is effected as well. Through the Weave we feel and are felt, we are affected by others, be it their negative or positive, from action, to speech, even the inner most thoughts has an effect on all of existence. Through the Weave all who are connected to the Weave, have an effect on each other, and the eternal damnation on just one spirit, well you can only imagine the effect this would have on Creation.
