Online gaming.
Does anyone do this exclusively? I've been playing video games since before I can remember, but I've never taken to online gaming in any of its many forms.

MMORPG I consider to be the 'hardcore' end of online gaming; yet, I've never even seen the appeal in playing games like Quake 3 online, or any other FPS for that matter. Sure, I own the capabilities to play online - a 360, PS2, PC, and a fast Internet connection - but have never felt compelled to get on this growing phenomena. Instead, if I were going to play video games with someone else, I find more joy in playing with my 'real-life' friends. Of course you can play with your friends over an Internet connection, but still.

I was just wondering if anyone plays games online exclusively, or even as a preference. Maybe you're into WoW, or Halo 3 (online multiplayer) for example? If you do like online gaming, please state the game[s] you play online.
