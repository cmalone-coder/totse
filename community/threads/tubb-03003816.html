I always see the age of consent as more of a guideline. However, they can lead to the victimisation of those over the age of consent with slightly younger partners. Is there really a great difference between the average 17 year old and an 18 year old?

If age of consent laws are to work effectively, they need to be in stages. I don't see too much trouble in a 16 year old and a 14 year old having sexual relations, provided they both consent and know what they are doing. If it was a 25 year old and a 14 year old then the case would need to be examined because it would seem like the older partner was taking advantage.

Cases could and often are left to the discretion of the police and courts, but this leads to inevitable discrepancies between cases. The solution, therefore would be to have a flexible law which takes into account age differences.
