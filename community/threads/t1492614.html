An experiment in the desperation of males
So here I am, alone on a Monday night. It's 11, a bit too late to really do anything useful... what to do, what to do.

Awhile back my ex signed up on a dating site. Before then I had always considered dating sites to be for the middle-aged, the pathetic, the unattractive, the losers... my ex is none of these. It piqued my interests, not enough to sign up though... fuck-off, I'll get my men the old-fashioned way, thanks!

But tonight, born of sheer boredom, I did it... I created a fake profile. Really all I wanted to do was see what the fuss was all about, why SO many people are joining these things these days! I figured I'd check back sometime this week and see if my profile had gotten any messages... well, I just went in to edit something on the profile, and to my shock, a message had already arrived!

... this has wierded me out. I did create a fake profile, but the thing is, I didn't spice things up, I didn't even post a sexy picture of some random model chick... in fact I was brutally honest. I described myself as a "bit of a train wreck" and used the words "slightly damaged goods"... hell, I even admitted to being a single mum of a 1-month-old son... some guy is still so desperate that he replies to someone like that? What... the... fuck.

So, I take a look at his profile, figuring there must be something horribly wrong with this guy, maybe a deformed face? Nope, nothing wrong, seems pretty average to me! This is where it gets really wierd, I shrug and return to my profile's inbox... in the space of 23 minutes there are now 9 messages from 9 different guys...

Umm, is there a shortage of single women that I am unaware of?

Something about all this reminds me of Groucho Marx's famous quote "I wouldn't join an exclusive club that would have me as a member". Self-effacing has spiraled down into self-deprecating.

Nobody's perfect, and a lot of people just aren't in that "happy average" that can manage to summon some social skills without overthinking things and sabotaging themselves.

Think about it. When you venture out into the social arena, you're exposing yourself to a very limited social milieu. The odds of meeting someone unique and interesting is slim.

Dating services aren't anything new; in the 70s-early 90s, it was all done in independent newspaper classifieds. Before that, the "personals" section of the major newpaper classifieds and through matchmaking services (which still exist). Before that, it was mostly done through busy-body females who always knew someone who had a son/daughter/nephew/cousin/niece that was "perfect for you". Matchmaking is really the "old fashioned way".

Anyways, try not to be so amazed if you find someone through non-mediated means. A lot of people would do well to adjust their attitudes and not be so judgemental.
