When I used to mod T&T I would always edit out links to illegally distributed copyrighted material. Of course when I was modding there, torrents weren't very popular at all. Today I would continue to do the same thing, though. If someone asks for or posts a website where they can get illegal downloads, I would leave it. That's my stance. I know a lot of other mods have a pretty strict policy as well. Totse isn't a warez site nor is it a torrent search engine.
