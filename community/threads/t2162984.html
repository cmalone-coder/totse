Re: Whole "FIELD" of morning glories
The morning glory flowers (note spelling) last about 4 days.

Like
The first day they are a spiraly pointed green flower.
The second day they change to white with a green base
The third day they open up & display their colourfull flower.
The forth day they are wrinkled & wilted. Also if you touch the flower it will wilt almost immediately.

Then the flower falls off & leaves the green base to it, inside this is where the seeds are, little rock hard chunks.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morning_glory

So to get the seeds you will have to do it every second day or such.
Lovely flowers though...
