Not really potassium nitrate?
Years ago, I used to buy stump remover to use the potassium nitrate in flares and black powder - never had any problems. Recently the need for a potassium nitrate flare has arisen again, so I bought more stump remover. After trying to use it, though, I'm not sure it's the same stuff.

It looks and feels like the same white poder. Water soluble, as it should be. It didn't seem quite right, though. It smelled differently after being added to the caramelized sugar. It didn't seem to mix as easily. After it had set and cooled I tried to burn some, but it would only blacken and smoulder a bit over the heat of a lighter. As it smoulders, it seems to "grow" a bit the way those lame snake fireworks do. I tried making it a second time, but got the same results. I'm wondering if maybe the manufacturers of this stump remover have switched to a different oxidizer? Or is there any other theory that could possibly explain how I fucked up a simple potassium nitrate flare?

If anyone is interested, I've included a short video of some of the failed batch smouldering. I used a blow torch because it makes the stuff "grow" faster. http://s54.yousendit.com/d.aspx?id=16CE1OEHR0ERH1F7H7CELEDPQQ
