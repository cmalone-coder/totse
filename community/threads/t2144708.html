How to economize your weed so you get the most bang for your buck.

A lot of people suggest that if you refrain from smoking weed for a bit, your tolerance builds up, and you'll get more high. Smoke 1 week, off 1 week, smoke more, etc.

However, I've discovered, after smoking weed for 3 weeks straight (1/2 times a day) that you don't need to refrain from smoking weed to get the best high, just mix up the different strands. I remember the first time I tried white widow, I was already pretty fucked on alcohol and shitty weed, but I still got mind-blowing high because it was a strain I'd never done before and it was crazy.

So buy 1 brand from the dealer this week, then move on to another brand, preferably a very different one (go hydro one week, cess another, skunk another, etc).

That seems to be working for me. I got some crazy piny shit with chunt this weekend, and after 2 weeks of hydro, this piny stuff really adds a new flavor to it.
