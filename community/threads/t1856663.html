Re: more questions about meth :)
1-Yes. This is a minor pathway in the HI reduction in which P is present. PI3 (or any phosphorus halide) is great for halogenating any alcohol, and will work on pfed. In fact, PCl3 or PCl5 is used to halogenate pfed when doing a Pd/C reduction.

2-HI first gives H+ and I-. The H+ protonates the alcohol (OH) and gives OH2+, which the charge migrates to the carbon and the OH2 splits off as H2O. The positive carbon scoops up the negative iodine, and becomes iodoephedrine (OH replaced with I).

Then the HI reacts once more, this time the I from the iodoephedrine and the I from HI reacts and gives I2. The left over hydrogen saturates the carbon, and gives you meth.

3- 2P + 3I2-----&gt;2PI3

PI3 + 3H2O----&gt;3HI + 1H3PO3

1H3PO3 + 1I2 + 1H2O-----&gt;1H3PO4 + 2HI

H3PO3=phosphorous acid H3PO4=Phosphoric acid

4-Yes.
