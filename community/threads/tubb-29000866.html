I agree with Mack that some of the modern MMA/NHB fighters today would just annihilate Bruce. Bruce as far as I know never fought in the ring professionally, although I have heard of him being challenged by various fighters, but I have heard varying stories on the outcomes.

The one thing that everyone agrees upon is that he started the "modern" martial arts movement. Bruce Lee's theory on martial arts is as follows IMHO:

1. Crosstrain in as many martial arts as possible.
2. Extract from what you study that is most useful to YOU individually, and create your own fighting style per se, and discard what is useless.
3. The individual is more important then the system.

and some other points that I can't think of right now.

He would put as "Have your style as no style" and "your form as no form". I think he was a philosophy major, it really shows in his writing.
