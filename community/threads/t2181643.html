Re: Drugs they dont test for
A) Drug testing thread.

B) It depends on what you were arrested for and what they decide to test you for. You can be tested for almost anything but they probably wont test you for everything.

You can be sure you'll be tested for the NIDA 5. so thats:
-Cannabis and THC products
-Methamphetamines (and some other amps)
-Cocaine
-Opiates that have common metabloites with morphine (heroin, morphine, opium including pods and seeds, and codeine)
-PCP

Those are almost certain to be tested for. If you were arrested with mushrooms they may check for those too. If you were arrested for drinking they may check for that. You get the idea..

Basically they could test you for anything. Try and find out what you're being tested for if you can without raising suspicion.

Check the DT thread for more help.

Yeah, actually they do just test you for a "select few drugs". It is only cost effect to screen for so many, as it costs additional money for every one added onto the test, and some are not common enough, or going to have long-lasting metabolites that will show up.

Hence, the formation of the NIDA 5. Yes, this is sometimes expanded now, but they are not going to scan for every specific type of molecule and metabolite. Just the ones deemed cost effective and likely to hit a positive if one is indeed using drugs.
