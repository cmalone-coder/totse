In my posting and perusing of totse I notice that some forums have a decidedly more chilled out or helpful than others. What I am curious it why? Is it the nature of the subject matter of some of them, or is it the people?

Least Hostile

Printed Matter: I suspect this is because the topic of books doesn't attract so many children, plus there is no politics and really, no wrong answers; only different opinions.

Travellers Travails: Same Reason as Printed Matter

BLTC: This may seem like an odd choice but generally, even if you get called a fucking moron you will still get the info you ask for. Probably because drugs are a dangerous business and at least a handful of the forum members will look out for less experienced users.


Most Hostile

Everything else I frequent to is mostly in the ego or art sections, plus Rock and Roll, and they all are somewhere in between. I cannot speak for any other forum.
