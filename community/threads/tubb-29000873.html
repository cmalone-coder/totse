Generally anything made by heckler and koch kicks ass.

This is a long barrel .45 (5.87"), overall length is close to 10"

.45's perform best when shot out of 4.5" + barrels,

The improvements over it's little brother the HK USP TACTICAL (one step down from the Mark 23) would be,

The slide rails are throughout the frame, instead of the slide tabs on other USP's.

The decocker is seperate from the safety and when decocked it doesn't SLAM down, thus creating noise.

The finish on the gun is supposively a lot more tolerant to extreme conditions.

On average the MARK 23 alone sells for about $1600.00 to $2000.00 US dollars, tweleve round magazines go for about $70-100.00 US dollars each.
