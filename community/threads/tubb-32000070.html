Look, fries and donuts are carbohydrate, which is broken down into a very easy form for the body to use;- GLUCOSE. First, the body converts enough carbohydrates into glucose to use for energy. Then some are stored in the muscles as glycogen. The rest is sent to the liver for storage, also as glycogen. Then the surplus is transformed by the liver into fat to be deposited in fatty tissues around the body.

Overall, the body can hold about a half day�s worth of energy in the form of glycogen that must be converted first back to glucose. The blood system holds about an hour�s supply of glucose.

The problem comes when you eat excess carbohydrate over an extended period which gets laid down as fat, and are not active enough to use up your glucose and glycogen reserves. The body WILL NOT turn to stored fat as a fuel source if theres glucose/glycogen available (or alcohol come to that). Its that simple. Hope that clears things up a little for you tribalmunkey
