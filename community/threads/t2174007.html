The hardest thing for you in this situation will be to separate your wants and desires as an individual from what you want for her.

I get the sense, from your original post and this little extra bit above, that you're really uncomfortable in this relationship now that the stakes have escalated. If you really love this young woman enough, maybe your thoughts would probably be to stick it out with her instead of weighing your options. But you're right -- at your age you really can't be expected to know what you want out of life -- or romance, for that matter.

Do the decent thing: Schedule a visit to the guidance counselor at your school, and mention that she's having this trouble. Maybe they can help her out, but a suicidal young woman is probably beyond your ability to treat. You can help her by being there, but I've heard it said that some of the worst relationships are founded on a desire to help rather than to share, so my advice would also be to ease yourself out of the relationship as best as you can manage.

If you don't want her to know that it was you who let the school in on the secret, that's fine -- you can remain anonymous if you pen a short little note to the principal and let him deal with delegating it to the guidance counselor(s). But there's also the chance that as her boyfriend, you might know more about how serious the situation is than they might be able to uncover. You can probably be of most help to her by approaching the school staff in person and keeping them apprised of anything they should know.

If it salves your conscience, remember that in some jurisdictions you'd be compelled to report her to some kind of authority if she was in danger of harming herself, and privacy be damned.
