Do shit you would normally do but it'll be better because you're on shrooms.

I ALWAYS make an effort to listen to music while tripping. If you play an instrument, try making some sweet new riffs. Being creative is great on mushrooms I find. Drawing, writing, music, whatever suits you.

Nature walks are neat too. Bring a camera along so you can take pictures of things that trip you out and remember the trip later.

Re: About to do some shrooms, looking for ideas
do whatever you think is fun. remember to have trip toys, and other things to indulge your senses.

things i use:

sight: trippy posters, visualizers (usually my CEV/OEVs blow any visualizer out of the water), blank walls, pretty much anything that looks cool.

smell: flowers, scents, the little E toys that you twist and smell super bomb, get creative. tripping with girls is fun because a lot of the time have super bomb smelling perfume on.

touch: females. for. the. mother. fucking. win. also, fuzzy pillows, cold things, vibrating things (lol) and apparently vicks vapo-rub is awesome, but i have yet to get some.

taste: candy, for sure. on shrooms i usually don't feel like eating things, so that's where candy comes in, but friday i ate the bombest burrito of my life on 2C-I, and i like eating actual food on candy(e: wtf? i meant RCs and cid) too. if you feel nauseous though, stick to candy or nothing. don't want to barf up your precious drugs

my second favorite (besides sight): SOUND. music. you NEED music for any indulgence, you need music even when you AREN'T tripping. depends on your music taste, but on shrooms, i like shpongle, infected mushroom, hallucinogen, 1200 mics all that tripppy asss shitt. on friday i listened to MSI on 2C-I and it was fucking amazing. on RCs and cid, i like realllly good electronic stuff like pendulum, 1200 mics, etc, more than trippy shaman-esque type music.

tool is ALWAYS fucking amazing.

forgot to add, i just made a queens of the stoneage playlist, with all their good songs from their discography. it's gonna be AWESOME next time i trip. i gotta do this with MSI too, cuz when i was kholing i kept having to get up to change the music, which was extreeemeellyyy difficult.

listened to demon days (gorillaz album) the first time i kholed. needless to say, it was fucking amazing. gotta try that one out if you haven't heard it, its awesome.

this reminds me...listening to MSI on 2C-I, i discovered something. fuck mindless self indulgence, at least when i do drugs, mindful self-indulgence is my plan (can't think of a better word for that. theme? code? idk)

feel free to add to my list of trip toys, i looove new things to trip with, and i know i didn't include anywhere near everything i use.
