Re: Mushrooms are lame
Ahh... sorry you had such a lame experience with shrooms man. They are really an awesome drug and I wouldn't give up on them so quickly.

I've had experiences like you have described above, and this usually means the shrooms were not so good ones. But then I've had experiences were I have had full blown hallucinations were I see a 3D galaxy of Aztec symbols floating around in my eyelids.

So don't give up on them just quite yet.

As far as DXM goes, I personally don't like it. I feel like it closes my mind and doesn't expand it. I consider it more of a dissociative than a psychedelic. For me it's like being drunk with some minor hallucinations, and I'm not a big fan of alcohol.

As far as a good dose goes, I've never taken Zicam max, (I've tried normal robo cough syrup and robo gels) make sure you read up on doses at http://www.erowid.org/chemicals/dxm/dxm_dose.shtml

Good luck, be safe.
