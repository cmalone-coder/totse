It sounds like he is talking about taking a spore print. You put the fresh cap on a piece of white paper and a ring of spores is left after a while. Cubensis spores are a dark sort of blue. But unless you look at the spores under a microscope, the fact that they are dark blue doesn't mean they are safe. Magic mushrooms also bruise blue due to the oxidation of the active ingredients. This is also not a indicator of safety as there are poisonous mushrooms that do the same thing
