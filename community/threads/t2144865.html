Smartest Fucking Mouse. Evar.
A few days ago I discovered there was a mouse in my apartment. I got out the trap, put some cheese in it, and expected to see a dead mouse a couple hours later. To my surprise, I came back and saw that the food was gone. I figured that I must have accidentally shaken the food off while setting it down. So, I reloaded the trap, and this time double-checked that it was baited. I go to bed, and the next day the food is gone, and the trap is still locked and loaded.

Now I know that I'm being fucked with. So, I load the trap with food in several places, figuring the mouse will be more likely to set it off. But alas, all of the food is somehow gone.

The next time I put cheese underneath the little button, figuring that the mouse HAS to bump the button (and thus set off the trap) in order to reach the piece. But no! All of the food disappears, and the trap hasn't even been set off.

So, at this point I'm basically feeding the mouse like a fucking pet. I decide I'm going to have to get a little more creative, so I take a needle and thread, and tie the bait to the trap. Now the mouse will set it off when it tries to pull it away. I even snapped my finger while testing to make sure it would work.

I leave the house for the evening, and I just got back now. That motherfucker. I'm not lying at all, there is no exaggeration in this story whatsoever. I came home and saw that the trap somehow was wedged underneath a door, about 6 inches from where I placed it. The trap hadn't even been sprung until it reached the door, at which point it was loaded against the door. It's a bit complicated to describe, but basically the mouse pushed this trap several inches without setting it off, got the food, and reset the trap against a closed door. It's unbelievable.

Now I'm somewhat scared, because if this smartass has figured out that I'm trying to kill it...it might come after me next.

Anyone else had any amazing encounters like this?
