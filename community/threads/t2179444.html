The most simple way.
1. buy spores
2. buy a growbag online
3. inject the spores
4. put in a dark place around 75-80 degrees (the growbags dont need as high heat because they generate around 5 degrees heat themselfs and you do not want the temperature over 85 degrees or else the mycelium will die)
