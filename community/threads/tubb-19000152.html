Re: Rail Guns
The theory of a rail gun anyhow is to basicaly use the magnetic field of a wire coiled made to look like a spring. Current is ran through the coil and a magnetic field is generated using the the left handed coil method: Use your left hand and place your fingers pointing in the direction of the current. The end your thumb points to is the north end of the magnetic field. The field flows from the south end to the north end. So basically your thumb will point in the direction the "gun" will fire. You have to realize there are other factors involved in making such a gun. I would suggest reading up on things like flux density, teslas, and webers, before you go balls out with this.

The book i used to the get information is:
"Electrical Fundamentals for Technicians" by Robert L. Shrader. Most electronics books you can find go into all of the stuff you should need at least in theory to make a rail gun. I think the main problem is you will never be able to generate enough power to fire a projectile at large velocities.
