Intercooler for 360?
I was reading through the xbox support forums, and it seems in the past few days several more people are having the same problem with their consoles as I have with mine (still waiting for the box to arrive to send to microsoft for repair).

Anyway, one guy puts forth a theory about excess heat damaging the av port on the back of the xbox 360. I know mine used to get really hot, but I never thought much of it since it never seemed to cause any problems. Now I'm considering investing the $20 or so in a cooling system. the one that seems to be the most widely used & recommended is the Nyko Intercooler. I was wondering if any of you guys had any experience with this, or had any relevant thoughts.

It seems that a $20 investment would be well worth the hassle of another dead 360. Too bad microsoft doesn't include the necessary cooling equipment out of the box. Sad, sad business.
