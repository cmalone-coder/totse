7 - 7.5 grams of APAP is about the lethal dose. Of course it will be different for different people. But I wouldn't go past 4 grams either - though if you do I wouldn't panic....too much.

About 10 years ago I didn't know that APAP was hard - very hard on the liver. I kept a supply of Vics on hand in case I ever had trouble getting heroin [I was addicted to it at that time].

At one point, the price got ridiculously cheap - $220 for 12 grams. And so I let my habit go up to where I was doing almost 3 grams a day.

Well the bubble not only burst one day - it fucking exploded. I was unable to find any heroin anywhere! And not just me, nobody in Oakland could find it [you could tell by just looking at people in East Oakland that they were going through W/D]. It was about 4 days before I could find any and it was like $80 a gram at that point [it finally came down to, I think, it was $40 a gram].

But before I found any I used my Vic reserve. And since I had a huge habit, it took a lot of vics to keep me from being sick. So I just kept popping them and popping them. I did a total of about 65 Vics over a 2 1/2 day period.

By the end of that third day I was feeling very sick - and not dope sick either. I was puking up everything I tried to put in my stomach - including water. This reminded me of when I had hepatitis from years of drinking [I was given 5 years to live if I didn't stop drinking when I was 27].

So I looked up Vics and APAP and my heart sank when I saw that I had likely taken a lethal dose.

I called the local ER [Big county hospital in Oakland]. You can imagine how long the wait usually was there...about 8 to 10 hours. When I went to the desk I told them what I had done [I told them I had injured my neck again as explanation for my taking so many]. Instead of making me wait, they took me right in. Which, while good that I didn't have to wait, only made my anxiety that much worse. I was convinced I had killed myself.

Well they shot me up with some sorta counter acting thingy and made me drink that charcoal shit - that's some nasty shit, let me tell you!. And they did blood work on me.

The 1/2 hour I had to wait for those test results were some of the longest of my life.

Finally the Dr came back with a big smile on his face. He said: I don't know how you managed it, but you are going to be ok.

I can't begin to tell you the range of emotions that flowed through me.

Ok, well I kinda made this longer than I had planed on. Blame it on the meth: I had some tonight for the first time this year - theoretically...With some hypothetical tar to take the edge off of it.
