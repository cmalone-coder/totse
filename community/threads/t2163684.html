Re: It's Been Awhile, Questions About Poppy Tea
Are you trying to grow your poppies or just buy the pods? If you're just buying them, then go find them on ebay like that guy said. If you're growing them, you need the somniferum seeds, known in culinary circles as "poppy seeds". You can use the supermarket ones if you want, or you can get someone to give you some better ones. Try the folks at www.poppies.org. There's a trading forum there, but you need 30 posts or something to get in. Anyway, if you're going to just use the supermarket ones, which should work OK, get a few pounds of them and scatter them on some freshly excavated dirt (i.e loose, little or nothing growing on it). Plant a few different fields so you don't lose everything when one gets bulldozed. You can do this any time from now until March, but now is better because your seeds will have a head start when Spring rolls around (they won't grow until Spring; they'll overwinter). If you follow those simple directions, you should have a few hundred/thousand plants by the time you're ready to harvest, depending on how hardcore you are. If you're making tea, cut your pods (with the stems) when they turn hard and bluish and then dry 'em. If you're making opium, score them seven days after the petals drop, and continue doing that for as long as they leak white stuff. I'd grow them if I were you. It's way cheap. That whole operation will cost you $20.


EDIT: and yes, ebay is the way to go for pods. Don't dare go to IAS. In fact, you should never go there again.

EDIT2: As for preparation, grind up your pods with a coffee grinder and make tea like you normally would, but steep for ~30 min.

Re: It's Been Awhile, Questions About Poppy Tea
Glad to help
There's no blanket dose. They're like mushrooms in that way. The only way to find your dose is trial and error, because pods aren't all the same potency. If you have pretty big pods, like golfballs or bigger, start with 4-6 and work from there. If you buy them from ebay, get the big ones. Look for Giganthemums. Try the ones from ebay and grow them if you like them. They really are easy, and it's fun to scout out good spots to sow and watch them grow. I keep a bag of seeds in my car in case I see a good place when I'm out and about.
