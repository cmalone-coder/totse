how to live a month off 20 dollers
in my many years being a poor ass bachelor ive learned how to eat like a king with minimal cashflow.

things u need:

20 dollers

transportation(optional)

a sams club membership

first of all this is a rough template. prices may very a bit.
ahh sams club how i love u. sams club has we poor people like to call bulk. u can go buy a cube of 30 packs of ramen noodles for 4 dollers. i dont know about u but 1 cube of ramen dosnt cut it for me. i almost always eat 2 at a time so thats 2 cubes a day for 30 days. for a total of 8 dollers. now were down to 12 dollers.
so theres your food for a month but ramen every day can be annoying at best. so we want alittle variety. next is one of my favs. the gas station hot dog. almost everywhere in the US ive been has had the 2 for 99c deal. they also give free condiments with hot dog purchases. so just stuff your pockets with catsup, mustard, relish ect ect. that can be done once a week or even twice a week for another 8 bucks which leaves us with 4 dollers. next is the drink trick. go to your nearest fast food place that has a drink station in the lobby. next go up and buy a small or med. drink. this will run u from a doller to a buck 50. so now your down to 2.50 cents. this allows u unlimited refils. just take the cup back to the fast food place when u want drink. they cant do anything about it, i used to work at mcdonalds and i asked my manager if there was anything we could do if someone asked for a cup for water(which mind u also works u wont have to spend the money but they often give u shit small cups) and then got soda. she said no cept ask them to leave. and ill tell u this no one gave a shit. they were to busy taking orders and making food. u could even get a collection of cups from different resturants and go to each of them a different day. so were down to 2 dollers and 50 cents. this is candy money. go to a doller store or around here its called deals. nothing in the store is over a doller. go in there and get a shit load of candy for that 2 bucks. which leaves u with 50 cents. what to do with this 50 cents. well simple, go to a crowded place and buy a news paper. cept dont just take 1 news paper take the whole lot of them. sell them for a discount of 25 cents to the crowd. say theres 10 papers, 25c a peice is 2.50 cents. which can be more candy money or what ever u want to put it twards. good luck

bonus material

heres a dirty trick i learned while walking around my town. dunken donuts cant keep there donuts over night. at the end of the day they have to take there stock and ditch them in the dumpster. all u have to do is wait in the dumpster and have a garbage bag at the ready. this will get u POUNDS of old donuts. but there not bad donuts there just aged a bit. u can talk to the guy and pay him a fiver for the extra donuts. also at gas stations there are donuts that late at night they have to eather give away or throw away. be nice ask the cashier what there going to do with thouse donuts. or be a dick and complain that this donut is old and crusty why should i have to pay for it.
and bakeries do the same thing donut stores do. they throw out so much damn bread its a crying shame. literaly garbage bags full of good bread.

and there u have it how to eat like a king for a month with only 20 bucks.

ABOUT FOOD.

I camped out in the remote mountains for months at a time, maybe made it into a town for supplies once a month at most.

Bread was something that would not keep SO,
I got a receipe for American Indian Fry Bread from an Am. Indian woman. (find it on the web)

FLOUR is a very inexpensive commodity and even free at food give away programs. With a bag of flour and some baking powder, a little bit of sugar and cooking oil (vegetable oil) I would make a 2-3 day supply of fry bread in a frying pan and put it in a plastic bag and have fresh bread for a month or more. You can make it sweet like doughnuts or even add things to it like wild berries and stuff.

Another option is to use sourdough and just keep a fermentation process going.

Rice is an excellent commodity.

Navajo Fry Bread

Serving Size : 8

4 cups All-purpose flour
1 tablespoon Double-acting baking powder
1 teaspoon Salt
1 1/2 cups Water
1 cup Vegetable shortening
In a bowl whisk together the flour, the baking powder, and the salt, stir in the water, and knead the mixture on a floured surface until it forms a soft but not sticky dough. Let the dough stand, covered with a kitchen towel, for 15 minutes. Pull off egg-size pieces of the dough and oat and stretch them into 1/4-inch thick rounds. Poke a hole with a finger through the center of each round so that the breads will fry evenly. In a large heavy skillet heat the shortening over moderately high heat until it is hot but not smoking, in it fry the rounds, 1 at a time, for 2 minutes on each side, or until they are golden, and transfer the breads as they are fried to paper towels to drain.
Source: Gourmet Magazine : April 1993 : Pg. 193-194
