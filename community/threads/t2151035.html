I basically try to stay away from pills. They're expensive and addictive and most of them are overrated. I used to like methadone til I got sick of it, withdrawals were a bitch. I got up to 80mgs which would get me high, real high, for 4 or 5 hours. But my connect would only sell 10s for 5 bux a piece. Its a pretty straight street price, but it was basically 40 bucks to get high. Thats like smoking a whole bag of good pot by yourself in one sitting. Economically it makes no sense.

Couple years back I had a decent OC connection. I dabbled with that for a bit mainly insufflation and oral use. I realized that OC is the biggest rip off and most overrated drug ever. I could snort anywhere from 40-80mgs and maybe get a real nice rush for about 20 minutes then it tapered of to a nice opiate buzz plateau for an hour or two. Orally it was better, not as intense but the plateau was decent. I banged em once, that was pretty good, but I'll never do it again.

Valium and any benzo for that matter is something that I might do once in a blue moon. I find them to be nothing great at all. Maybe a valium before smoking a bowl is alright, but then again nothing beats plain old reefer by itself anytime anywhere.

Currently I take suboxone daily. It keeps me stable, I do want to get off of it someday though.
