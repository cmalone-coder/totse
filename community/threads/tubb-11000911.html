Re: mp3 player?
If you're a music purist, there is no choice. Go MiniDisc.

For most, though, it's not that easy.

MDs are like futuristic CDs. They can hold 80min of damn fine quality sound, they're cheaper than flash or harddrive equivalents, and they're small, well-designed, and virtually shockproof. You can get MDs for about $1.50 a disc. Yes, it's more than CD-Rs, but MDs are rewritable. It's a much simpler rewrite than RWs, too. With recent enhancements to the technology, you can compress the music. At the 2x compression, the sound is comparable to your average MP3. At 4x, it just plain sucks. The NetMD system has great potential, but the software absolutely blows. MDs are very good for recording, as well. It's easier to swap MDs with a friend than upload/download between two MP3 players.

A HDD jukebox has its own pros, too. You never have to swap discs. Transfers can be much faster. Some of them double as portable hard drives. They're generally heavier and bulkier than an MD unit, though. They never have battery life as good as MD. The huge variations between brands can make it risky, and each has different upload/download procedures.

So, it depends on the type of person you are. I think MD rocks ass.
