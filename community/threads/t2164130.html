Re: A few concerns.
Nothing you listed, testosterone or estrogen themselves promote height growth, it's actually certain hormones that stimulate growth plates that enable you to grow. However, estrogen closes growth plates and those with more estrogen (women) tend to be shorter than men. So the theory goes that by using AIs (Aromatase Inhibitors) you can slow down the closure of your growth plates and thereby lengthen your time to grow taller.

Look into Letrozole and Arimidex.
Otherwise, just get your hands on some growth hormone.
