Re: HBW seeds
If you have a couple of days first, this works well:

Try a low dose, maybe 2 or 3 seeds. Just enough to get you feeling the boundries, a bit of lucidity, slight ADD. Maybe a little bit of sweating but that's not so bad depending on the climate. Maybe more maybe less, whatever.

Now if this works out well for you, take a high dose the next day. 8+ seeds. That's a low start, especially if you're worried about tolerance. If you don't think like this idea from the start don't bother trying.

Don't tire yourself out from the first one. Do something relaxing and prepare yourself from the show. The next day shouldn't really be on your mind. Just do what you do and let hope do the rest.

Seeds are cheap, so there shouldn't be a problem with upping the dose the next day.


Otherwise, the trip is light but forceful (5-7 seeds.) Nothing to be afraid of but your own shadow. I kid, fuckin' robotic children.

Anyway, the nausea can be easily managed with a little bit of bread. The problem with this is that it can significantly delay the onset, as well as diluting the intensity of the trip. If you meditate (or concentrate) at all you can mediate a significant portion of the nausea.

Your best bet is to lemonparty or do a real extract. Not a particularly hard process. Waiting is a bitch, so learn to fly.

Re: HBW seeds
Its pretty interesting to take those seeds and go see a psychedelic rock band. I know, cliche, but I tried it anyways and it was such an amazing experience that afterwards I felt like I was walking on deep, thick, clouds... still a few hours away from the peak... and it was really obvious to the people around me. XD

I don't recommend trying to roll a joint while on it, in the event that you decide to throw a bit of pot into your experience. Have a joint ready before you start to trip, otherwise you'll spend the rest of eternity trying to roll it and then suddenly you'll be sober.

I tried 10 seeds my first time and, to be honest, it was actually too intense at certain moments.

I'd say those seeds were almost, if not just as strong, as the two blotters I took the first and only time I tried acid. Though, I had an easier time with the acid. I'd say the LSA seeds are like acid but with a pinch of shroomy volatility in the sense that they sometimes pull you along and other times you just go somewhere.... the oddness at the cost of some of the depth of LSD.

The nausea from those seeds is a very funny thing. I found that I was only nauseous while chewing them up and then the nausea would go away and come back in trippy waves depending on how hard I focused on it. XD
I usually just sip some fresh water and that seems to cure it. However... if you decide to just eat the seeds then I strongly recommend NOT BURPING as that brings the taste and the nausea back in full force.
Otherwise, happy tripping and make sure you have some music with you in case you need it to pacify your mind a little... preferably (my personal preference) something that could possibly be considered funny (in a simple way, not so much like a stand up comedy act) or just something easy to listen to.
