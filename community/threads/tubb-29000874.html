The pistol calibers I currently own are,

9MM, .357SIG, .40S&W and .45ACP

The calibers i frequently shoot at the range are, 9MM, .357SIG and .45ACP

The caliber pistol i frequently carry for personal protection are, 9MM and .357SIG

I've shot plenty of other calibers but i seem to always come back to my 9MM at the end of the day.

Federal 9MM 124 +P+ JHP mimics the charecteristics of the .40S&W, plus you get less felt recoil, less muzzle flip, less flash (dependant on load).

Generally it's a all around performer, the .357SIG tends to have a edge over the 9MM when it comes to penatration. However i've seen plenty of tests where the .357SIG has failed to realiably expand. Whereas if the 9MM has enough velocity behind it, it'll reliably expand to .50-.60" (from a .355" bullet).

My Springfield can shoot .45 super. It was worked over by Ace customs. The reason it's not very popular is that the gun needs to be customized to shoot it reliably and the switch back and forth between .45 super and .45 acp usually involves a recoil spring swap. They say that changing recoil springs isn't necessary, but the one they put in was a little too strong for .45 acp to cycle reliably. I use a 18 lb spring for .45 acp and the one for .45 super is several pounds stronger.
