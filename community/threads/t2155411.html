help me fix a hole in my Honda CX500's exhaust
I'm not sure what the component is called, but it's this big manifold-lookin piece between the headers and the mufflers, and I don't know why they made it out of anything different than the rest of the exhaust system, but the entire thing is heavily rusted, including a nice quarter-sized hole right in the middle (hence why i'm posting).

I'm thinking that somehow fastening a piece of plate steel to it will be my best (if not only) way to maybe fix it. First I thought maybe use self-tapping screws or something, but the metal is probably to thin from the rust to have that hold up well. Now I'm considering JB Weld (it's epoxy, but with steel-based resin so its much tougher). The stuff has a max working temperature of about 600 F ... Do you guys know how hot it gets around there? If it's within that range, I was actually considering coating the whole manifold (or whatever its called) with a layer of it to prevent further rusting. It's almost totally outa sight, and flat grey epoxy wouldn't look any worse than heavy rust. Opinions?

I know a hole there's effect on performance is probably negligable, but it sounds trashy enough that I'd like to try and fix it.


so ya... main question here is how hot do you think my CX500's exhuast system gets to just before the mufflers?

thanks a lot
-Jon
