drugs that blew your expectations when you tried them for the first time
Is it me or did I have the wrong image of how getting high feels like, back when I was a 14 year old aspiring druggie?

I remember being 14 years old and witnessing the fucked up debate about the legality of weed, the "addicted to weed" shockumentaries, and hearing all those wannabe stoner posers brag about how fucked up they got last night on t3h kr0nic. I thought, if this drug is causing so much debate and shit all over the world, then getting high must be the sweetest thing ever.

Needless to say, when I finally got around to actually trying it, I was sortof surprised, yet disappointed. I remember thinking to myself "is this it?" I was expecting to get an awesome euphoric feeling dwarfing that of alcohol, yet the effects were alot more gentle than I thought they would be. Feeling happy, laughing at everything, and music sounding somewhat better hardly seemed worth all the socio-political debate, let alone the bragging about how fucked up you got last night smoking weed in the couch while watching reruns of The Fresh Prince of Bel-air.

Recently I've had the same thing with MDMA. After reading the reports on erowid I was convinced I would be utterly blown away, that I would be laden with physical energy, that I would not be able to stop dancing, that I would feel so emotionally open that it would become impossible to tell lies, that it would be "BETTER THAN SEX!!11!1!" (well, getting touched did feel sortof better than sex, but not in the way it's commonly presented). It's as if people try to make Ecstasy not seem like a "drug" at all, but as a special raver pill that can only be properly appreciated when wearing pink baggy pants and listening to music produced by drum machines and synthesizers, as a substance that is somehow in a substantially different league than alcohol or coke.

I thought MDMA felt alot more "druglike" than those easily impressed erowid-writers said it would. I felt more open and emotionally satisfied than I usually do, but in my mind I was fully aware that I was experiencing a very subjective perception of the world, and that alot of the things I was saying would end up haunting me the day after. I didn't really felt that stimulated either. I could sit down on a couch and cuddle with people easily without feeling the need to move. It also felt a bit trippy, with slight visuals (tracers and shimmering edges), and I got a feeling not unlike standing on the edge of a tall cliff, looking down. But the bottom line is that X is a drug like any other, it just gets you inebriated, high, buzzing, fucked up! There's no need to venerate it like it's the blood of Christ, as some people tend to do.

I wonder if there are any other drugs out there that will probably not live up to their name. I know coke doesn't. Same for heroin, unless you inject it.
