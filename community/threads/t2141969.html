It might not work for a fifteen year old anyway, or for most of the dumb asses I've seen attempt to use it. A drunk asshole screaming that he pleads the fifth over and over again typically doesn't go over well. Pretty funny from the third perspective though.

Which isn't to say it doesn't work, but I find it's much easier to just not look sketched out and be as honest as possible unless either a)you're not doing anything illegal, or b)it's something you'll actually end up getting charged with. I don't even bother to put my shit away anymore if a cop rolls by while I'm smoking at the park or something. I don't carry pieces I give a real fuck about anywhere, or all of my stash at once so I generally end up having to walk a mile back to my house, grab my bong, and smoke there.

The Key Points: If you're going to use the legal system against them, actually know what you're talking about. Totse doesn't count, go look it up and make sure you understand it. Preferably, try to get some experience in using it while you have nothing to lose, not only is it safer, but if they do manage to search your jacket after twenty minutes of arguing with you, and find nothing, their faces are rather humorous. Be assertive about it, but don't be a dick and you'll get a lot farther. Oh, and don't carry shit you care about around.
