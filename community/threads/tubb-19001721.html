Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Stupid, but amusing.
One of the perks of being a moderator is that I can post stupid things and everyone will take me seriously.

I was recently introduced to this little plaything by a friend of mine. Find a wide necked glass jar (we used a pickle jar) and fill it with gasoline. Take a stroll down to your local dollar store and get a five pack of disposable cigarette lighters. Drop all five lighers into the gas jar, light the surface of the gasoline, and step back.

The gasoline will eat through the plastic on the lighters, eventually getting one or more of them to vent. Gasoline and butane spews, and a fireball is formed - Sometimes multiple fireballs.

And if you did it like we did, a driveway is set on fire... But that's not the point.

Useless, but what do you expect for a buck?

~Zero
