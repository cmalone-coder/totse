Australian Burgers
Just curious as to how many people from other countries have tried a real Australian burger and their opinions of them.

Recipe:
Bread roll
Beef Steak
Chopped Onion
Tomato sauce
Sliced Beetroot
Cheddar
Sliced pineapple
Sliced Tomato
Grated Carrot

Optional: egg, tomato, bacon

Cook steak to desired rarity.

Half way through the steak cooking throw the pineapple slice and chopped onion on.

Rip bun apart with your bare hands. (Like a real man ) Butter and toast it.

Put on roll in this order. Steak, Cheese, Pineapple, Onion, Lettuce, Beetroot, Carrot.

Put sauce inside of the top half of the bun and put on top.





Very filling, tasty, and ads a lot of vitamins.
