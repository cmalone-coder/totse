Flint Flash
I have posted this a while back, but im sure some of the newer posters havnt seen it yet. Its somthing to try when your out of supplies or have nothing to do.

You dont have to be wasteful with that flint on your magnesium firestarter. Powder it with a file very slowly or it will spark and set your whole pile up as it burns well withough oxidiser. Once your pile starts building up contain it and start a new pile, in case it does spark you don't ruin the whole batch.

Once you get a measureable amount, add some oxidiser 7 oxidiser/3 flint And you have some extremely strong flash. Its just a novelty though as its very sensitive.

If you light the pile w/o oxidiser it still flashes. Like a weak flash powder. But adding the oxidiser makes it incredible.
