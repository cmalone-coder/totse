Re: best mushrooms, your thoughts
All Psilocybin Cubensis strains, contrary to the different "strain names" that are attached because of different genetic mutations, are still Psilocybin Cubensis. Potency will fluctuate depending on your growing methods. If you're just looking for a small head stash just to get your feet in the water, then the BRF/Vermiculite "PF Tek" is the way to go. However, if you're looking for higher yields, increased potency, and a little more work, you may want to find some more advanced growing techniques.

It ain't easy, let me forewarn you, but it's not rocket science either.

www.shroomery.org
www.mycotopia.net

There are two good places to start. They both have vendor sources from which you can order your supplies.

P.S. Don't buy a grow kit, they're a waste of money for something that you can go to Wal*Mart and construct yourself.

Have fun in this rewarding hobby.
