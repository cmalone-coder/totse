The entirety of your experiences with drugs.
Drugs that I have done and the age I did them:

Weed/12
Dimenhydrinate/12
Alcohol/14
DXM/14 (from low to abnormally high doses)
Ecstasy/14
Speed/14
Mushrooms/14
Oxycodone/14
Benzos/15
LSD/15 (from low to moderately high doses)
Ketamine/15

When you actually step back and think about it, the concept of a "drug" and "different consciousnesses is quite amazing. I often find myself wondering what it would be like to go through life with out experiencing a high at all.

Drugs have made me a very different person than what I imagine I would be like having not done them. They've opened my mind to so many new ideas and extravagances that are simply not attainable without the act of being thrown into new realities. I mean, I'm sure if I had stayed clean up to this point, I would still be just as intelligent as I am, but I would not be as open-minded.

I've never had a problem with people doing drugs, even from the very first time I learned what they are. In fact it aroused my curiosity. I now understand that as much as I don't want to accept it, they do have negative effects as well. Its like the scene in the matrix. Once you take the blue pill (so to speak), there is no going back... ever. Doors open that can never be closed again.

From my experience, drugs as a whole are a double-edged sword. I don't do drugs just for the fun of being high, its for exploration. We are, for whatever reason, thrown into this twisted life, and must make so many decisions and must overcome difficult obstacles throughout. As hard as it is, I don't use drugs to escape from that. I feel that using drugs (psychedelics mainly) simply give you a different perspective on life (sounds like hippy babble, but it IS true) and how you can approach different situations and handle them correctly, whatever that means for you.

If I had a choice now to go back, though it makes me intensely curious on how I would be without them, I would not tell myself to stay away from drugs. I thoroughly enjoy the person I've become and how expanded my mind is. I know I've got a looong way to go before my psychedelic exploration is complete, but I've had a brilliant time so far. Thank you, whoever is up there, for bringing such a thing as drugs into existence to give us a more interesting life...

Start by listing all the drugs you've done and at what age, and then tell of how drugs have affected you as a person, the negative and the positive. Conclude with what you would do if you had the choice to go back.
