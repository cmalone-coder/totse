Deadlifts: The King of All Lifts...
For those of you who don't know dead lifts are a sure fire way to make you a real man. They are extremely taxing on the body and mind because of the amount of weight required. Post your max lift, why you do/don't like the lift, your height/weight, and if it's benefited you.

I usually do about 215 for reps right now. I maxed out at 305lbs. Keep in mind I'm a 5'10 150lb vegan guy at about 6% body fat, so if you're not lifting more than me you better start.

This is my favorite lift of all time. I can't imagine starting a back workout without this lift. I change up all the other lifts in my workout every couple of weeks between t-bar rows, bent over rows, machine cable rows, dumbell rows, and whatever other back exercise I might feel like is necessary.

I hardly ever see any guys in the gym even touching the dead lift rack. If they do it's for another lift, or for extremely light lifting. I'm the only guy I've ever see throw a few 45's on each side.
