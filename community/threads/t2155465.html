Re: Decorating an apartment for acid trips
How about a window, or better yet, just GO FUCKING OUTSIDE!
Tripping looses its spirituality, if you do what you are purposing.

Also remember:
If you change the inside of your living quarters "for tripping", you will end up altering and impeding your consciousness for further progression. Inbeading yourself in such a physical state, via the non chemical means, is something i strongly advise against.

The alteration of your direct living space, is no less an alteration of your mind. Mind "expansion", at times achieved via psychedelics, contrary to what you mind think, begins way before you actually ingest the substance and continues far after the substance passes.

In summery:
Trip outside in nature(Especially with your geographical location). Dont make your primary living space centered, inclined toward tripping via glam, fiz and pop, you are suggesting. In my recommendation; leave your space grounded, educational, and introspective.

A few natural/handmade/quality rugs are always a great addition though.
