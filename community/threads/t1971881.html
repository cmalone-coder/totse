Ok, basically, I make shitloads of money selling DVDs at my dorm. The trick to this is to buy a DVD, and then put all the necessary software on a CD and have them pay to copy the DVD. Since my dorm never goes to Fry's or anywhere like that, I can sell DVDs and blank DVDs at exorbitant prices ($6 per blank DVD and $1 to rent a DVD) and people will pay. The trick is to look like you have it all working out. Make a list of all the DVDs you can get access to and pass it around, maybe put a 1-second image of your name at the start of the menu screen. You can do this using DVD-Shrink or DVD Decrypter, which I think are better than anything else. They're free. Also, I can get you a hacked version of Super DVD Ripper, which rips and encodes DVDs quickly and painlessly. Good luck!
