Oral oxycodone is 1.5 times more potent than oral hydrocodone in terms of analgesia. This means that 20mg of oxy eaten is equivalent to 30mg of hydro eaten.

However, your ROA matters a lot with oxy. Even though intranasal admiinistation has a lower bioavailability than oral, it feels stronger because all of the drug hits you at once. This results in a higher peak plasma concentration (I believe), but it doesn't last as long.

Also, all known equivalencies are in terms of analgesia (pain relief). So there are other variables, like euphoria, for which we dont know the equivalency.

This, obviously, makes calaculating equivalency more diffuclt. So, instead of taking the time to figure out the exact number I'll just tell you this:

Blow 10mg. If, after a half hour, you arnt where you want to be, then blow 5mg more. Wait another half an hour, and do the rest if you want.
