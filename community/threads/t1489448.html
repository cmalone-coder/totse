Re: Is Pornography Cheating?
As with most relationship things, this isn't just an easy yes/no answer...

If you're sneaking around watching porn behind your girlfriend's back, when you KNOW she's uncomfortable about pornography, then I say you're being unfaithful... it's not cheating by definition... but you are deceiving her and it involves sexual stimulation by another woman.

If your girlfriend is comfortable with porn, and enjoys watching it with you, then it can be REALLY enjoyable. My guy and I rented some naughty videos, and when we weren't laughing at how pathetic the "acting" was we were fucking like bunnies.

It's a judgement call, and it all depends on what your girl is comfortable with. If you're having a problem with your girl it might be because she feels left out... jealous of the attention you're giving these porno girls when she's not around... yeah that might sound silly to a guy (or it might not) but it's something you kinda have to accept. Maybe if you involve her in your porn viewing it could turn into something really fun, instead of something she feels threatened by.

Sneaking, no matter what or who it involves, is always a bad idea when it comes to relationships. If you can't be perfectly open and honest with your partner about anything and still have their acceptance then you're probably going to end up pretty miserable together. You and your partner should accept each other, but you should also make sure that you're doing what you can to make yourself easily acceptable... look out for their needs, while making sure they are meeting yours.
