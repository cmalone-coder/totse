I could also see it getting infected easily depending on your sterilization techniques.

What you do in your home is a crude version of a cautery pen (a soldering iron looking device). The actual devices are constantly heated and the artist usually has enough practice to maintain even pressure. This is actually one of the least popular kinds of branding.

What I have done, and what is very popular in DIY and just regular shops is to do strike branding. This is where a single shape is heated and then applied evenly to the area. Although, I did notice that with my brand and with others I've seen that are DIY, the lines are rarely even. This leads me to do "touch-ups" later on in the year to correct the fainter lines.

For infections I do the following. Firstly, anything that brands my body needs to be THOROUGHLY disinfected using rubbing alcohol, that should go without saying. Secondly, the first few days/weeks I pay very close attention to the brand and clean it two to three times a day, or every time I wash my hands since it's on my forearm.
