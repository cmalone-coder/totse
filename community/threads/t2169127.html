Great Disappointments--sequels that suck
ITT, we bitch about really piss-poor sequels. Sure, sequels rarely come up to the original (exceptions including The Godfather II and Bride of Frankenstein)--but some are so poorly done, such obvious attempts at making a quick cheap buck, it is disgusting.

Take Lake Placid II--please. I loved the first one, waited eagerly for the sequel to filter down from the new release section to the 99 cent section at BB--and it was awful. Wooden acting, totally cliched characters, gratuitous soft-cord porn, mediocre FX--what a piece of poop.

So--what sequels do you love to hate?
