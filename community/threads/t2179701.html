I was born dyslexic... but fortunate to be able to draw really cool, with a great imagination. If you sit at your computer - use what you have available, your paint program. Become skilled in using that pencil directed with your mouse - it's not easy, but you also don't have to "draw" conventional... you can be surreal ~ I am left handed, I never did relocate my mouse to the left side of my computer... a few years ago, I was sacked with a crashed computer... I spent many days using my paint program... switching the hands, switches the brain als!! Left handers are right brained - *shrug* no this is not all about me - it is actual scientific facts... I had made some really cool pics in paint... then a "geek" friend of mine was trying to get my computer up and running - he deleted all my paint projects...... have fun, be real ~ stay cool!
