I think my mom smoked my weed.
Seriously, anyone ever had this happen to them?

I feel like I need to share this story it's not so long but lol a bit with me I haven't posted in a while. :D

Ok so, here I am, it's probably 12AM I'm stoned and on the computer probably browsing porn and lurking totse, and after a while I get tired, take my pipe, lighter and weed baggie to my room. Now I am quite diligent in hiding my paraphernalia as in I hid it in places that are:

1) Out of plain sight.
2) not easily reachable(as in needing to get a small ladder to get to it.
3) where the smell might be contained a little if I hide the stuff right away(My closet smells like weed smoke, but I've been clean for about 2 weeks so I guess the smell is starting to dissipate.

Ok so the only thing I admit MAYBE didn't hide that great was the weed baggie which I placed next to the T.V. under a bunch of index cards (which isn't also immideately in sight because if you enter my room and look to the left the tv is facing diagonally to the right so the baggie was behind the tv's left).

After puting everything in it's place I went to bed since the next day I had to go to my classes(COLLAGE LAWL). And when I return home at about 7pm and go directly to my room, oh the horror, I find that my room has been "tidied up"(Which I admit is not something I would have expected but I didn't take into account that my mother had just recently retired and I guess with her having litle to do probably decided to do something I'd rather put off until later as a favor or whatever train of thought she was following but at this point my face is blue and I'm sweating cold).

So I panic, I jump inside the closet and start looking fo rmy things, everything is where it used to be the only thing gone was my baggie and I guess a few rolling papers I put under the baggie. Now I just panic, sit on my bed and start watching tv waiting for some kind of "confrontation" as in "we need to talk I think you know what I mean".

But the thing is that to this day, nothing has happened, I haven't been "Talked to" nor have my parents said anything which scare me because I know deep down these two are anti-durgs. My moms roman catholic and my dads a protestant but perhaps their religions aren't the thing here.

Has anyone have this happen to them?
