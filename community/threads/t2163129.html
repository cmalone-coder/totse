Re: Is it better to just eat kratom?
First and for most: you have really shitty Kratom.

Kratom is good, but it does matter, significantly, where you get it from.

Making a tea, and having it stand for some time, prior to filtering out the particles is the best option.
I, myself, quite enjoy the earthy taste of kratom tea.
I find that it is significantly better to filter the fine powdered insoluble "tea" matter.

But understand: Kratom, is not analogous to "opiates". Its not something that reinforces "stinginess"(for the lack of a better word) in its use.
Its about the Quality.
If you are looking for an opiate high, you will be sorely disappointed.
But if you approach the herb, without prejudice expectations of its action, it is quite respectable.
Dont bitch because it doesn't feel like an opiate, its a novel and deserving herb, entirely on its own.

It is true Malaysian and Bali are the types which feel the most Opiate-ish, but other types are also quite desirable in my opinion.

Yes, kratom is a "bad drug", but it is a "good tea" with novel side effects.
And with average kratom it should not take more then ~6 gram to achieve desirable effects. Taking more, will not make the buzz stronger, but just get you sick.

You are doing this for a cheep buzz, and kratom is a bad candidate for this opinion.
But at least, give good quality kratom a try, before you formulate your opinion on the substance.

Yes kratom is soluble in water. If making tea take your time, do two strains, and simmer it as opposed to a heavy boil. Something acidic may be added for flavor and may aid the process.
