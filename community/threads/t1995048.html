Okay. First of all, you don't need the hydrogen. Chlorine gas bubbled through water will evolve oxygen and hydrochloric acid in the presence of sunlight...

Quote:
Originally Posted by JoePedo
Congratulations. Table salt is your new best friend.
WTF is the chemical formula of table salt, again?

Take a buttload of table salt. Cover it in white vinegar. Bring to a boil.

...take a big horkin' whiff. Tell me what it smells like.

Yes. It just so happens that HCl gas has a much, much lower boiling point than, oh, say, acetic acid. By far. Distill that shit through some table salt, and you should have decently pure anhydrous HCl gas.

Or, if you felt like electrolyzing table salt, you'd have a jar of sodium hydroxide solution and a jar of HCl solution.

The answer to "how do I get some acid" is always :

1. Find salt.

2. Liberate acid.

3. Cleanup.

...or electrolysis... but for fuck's sake, do things the traditional way before you detonate an H2 bomb in the middle of a solution of hydrochloric acid, k? Splatter's a bitch.

Re: Impure HCl
I'm guessing u r in the UK. Your local DIY should almost definately stock 'Brick Acid' - ask them for it - if they ask, which they won't (as it is a commonly used item) your brother asked you to pick it up to clean up some 'excess' mortar on the brickwork or something or you have some extreme deposits of limescale in a toilet. Search for brick acid and u will have as many uses as you need. A lot of those toilet chems have detergents and other shit in (non-ionic surfactants etc). They may also stock something called, 'Spirits of Salts' which is approx 34-36% HCl acid which is around the usual highest strength one will find HCl (Aqueous) in. At this concentration (34%) it is fuming when you remove the lid.

I have found both of these items in very small DIY stores - in small towns, even. Brick acid, particularly, is extremely widely available.
