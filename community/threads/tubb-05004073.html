A guy I know once did somewhere in excess of 50 hits. He and his friend were both on 4 hits when it happenned. Let's call them P and Q. P had a vial. P and Q had just finished smoking in a parking lot off the main drag of their shit town when they saw some guys they knew drive by. P and Q really wanted to catch up to these guys, and the guys really did not want to be caught. P&Q pursued the guys and they turned onto a side road. On this side road which was icy and had a 30 mph speed limit, P&Q had reached around 60 or 70 mph when flashing blue and red lights appeared behind them. P lost his shit. He knew that there was still smoke smell in the car and that the car owner Q still had weed in the car and that he was carrying a vial of acid. P&Q were both tripping pretty hard and P was sure they were going to jail. While Q took evasive manouvers and tried to lose the cop, P grabbed the vial out of his pocket and started emptying it into his mouth. The vial (a sweetbreath container) was mostly empty when P realized what a bad idea that was. He squirted out what was left onto the floor. Around this time Q managed to lose the cop in a really bad part of town. They made it back to Q's apartment and put in "Fear and Loathing." P was still feeling a little paranoid. Around the time Hunter S. Thompson said, "I knew it, you're a federal agent!" P snapped. He was convinced that the weed that Q has supplied was in some way tainted so as to mark him as a drug suspect. He thought the kool-aid they were drinking was to serve the same purpose. He thought that all of his friends were part of an "Operation Candy" (don't ask) task force to find drug dealers and that the night was an elaborate ruse to bring him in on narcotics charges. He said, "I think you and your roomates are federal agents." Q gave him a few xanaxes. P then demanded to be driven either to his home which was in a different jurisdiction or to a friend's house. Q finally agreed to return him to his friend's house. When P arrived he interrupted a movie and said something along the lines of, "Oh God, I'm freaking out. I need to watch CNN." At the time he thought that the Feds had tapped into the cable line and were broadcasting propaganda. Luckily P's friend is a fucking great guy and understood. P layed on the couch for a while and watched CNN to be sure that the cable wasn't tapped and being piped in off some sort of recording, because they can't really fake the news that well. After a while P got tired and went to sleep. He wasn't quite right for a few months. He tore apart his room at home looking for bugs and peaked out through the blinds in his window anytime he heard a car drive by.
