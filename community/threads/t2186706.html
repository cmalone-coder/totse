I've gone over the logistics of this ten times over. You'd also need to take out your own insurance, I'm assuming the company that owns the laser tag arena wouldn't want a rave happening under their liability. Then you have to consider the lighting. For it to be a kickass rave you need a lightshow projector. You also need a DJ, but you could probably get someone to do that for free, but then you don't always know if the DJ has their own stuff to play a show, so there's more possible costs. Then you have to think about your turn out and advertising.

Also, unless you find a way to keep it hush hush and still have people turn out for it, you'll have to work with the police, making sure you kick out absolutely anyone seen taking/dealing drugs out in the open.
