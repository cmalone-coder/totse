Tell her the Concerta gives you tremors. Thats what I told my doc for a ritalin-->amp switch, but your side effect explanation might work but might also get the response of "This girl gets too wired from stimulants" (making her want to try a different nonstimulant approach with you). If you tell her tremors though she wont necessarily think its b/c youre too stimulated (esp. if you tell her you concentrated a little better), tremors are more common with methylpenidate containing drugs (Ritalin, Concerta) but people can get tremors form amps also. Dont ask for anything that is a controlled drug by name EVER b/c this is a huge red flag for drug seeking.

She probably wont give you strattera or something else that takes a while to start working, especially if you explain the seriousness of your situation at school. Emphasize the difficulty you have during quizzes and tests and essays (I assume its true if you find it hard to concentrate in normal classes).

You wont get Desoxyn but I think you already know that ("yeah methamphetamine helped me concentrate real well when I was younger...).

There's an amino acid supplement called tyrosine (L-tyrosine on the bottle) and it comes in 500 mg capsules. When I was having trouble with classes and tests in the past it helped me out a lot. Its a precursor in your brain to dopamine, which is one of the most important brain neurotransmitters related to concentration. Tyrosine also helps restore the dopamine thats drained away with stimulant use. Take 4 capsules per day, 2 twice a day in between meals to help absorbtion. Also, b/c its something your body has in it naturally it shouldnt cause any side effects, less than stimulants anyway.
