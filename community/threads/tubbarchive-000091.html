Hey, I live in the Loop. All the streets are nicely numbered and in a neat grid (with every street going due East / West or North / South). As long as you get your cardinal directions right, you're in excellent shape.

Get off at Jackson then walk straight East on Jackson to Michigan Ave (its not far, only 4 blocks or something like that). When you reach Michigan, look to your right (South) and you'll see it. The station is below street level, there's an entrance down in to the tunnel on Michigan Avenue. Can't miss it. Hope you enjoy the city.

I live in Chicago too...it's the easiest city I know of as far as navigation. It's on a grid, just know the cross-streets and you pretty much will just have to go in straight lines.

Van Buren...I believe that's 400 S (double check that). If so, just get off at Jackson, walk over to Van Buren, and take a bus till you get to your east-west destination.
