I like Boston's core as well, but as of right now their only decent big man is Jefferson. He needs someone next to him(which is Oden or Durant would have been great picks because Oden is a pure post player and Durant is versatile enough to play the four).

I also don't see them getting much trade value out of the number five pick. They could, but I just think they'd be better off getting a young stud and building for two or three years down the road. If they were one player away from being good, then they should trade the pick, but they need a lot of help(and a new coach) before they'll be good again.

It's also hard to trade picks straight up for players because first rounders only make around three million a year, which mean you can only take back someone making around five million(I think that's about the maximum), which is around the league average.

They would have to package the pick with a current player or two to make the trade work, but I'm not sure Boston has anyone that other teams will want(Pacers for instance since I know there's talk of moving Jermaine O'Neal). It's just very difficult to trade players for picks.
