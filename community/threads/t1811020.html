mine happened a couple weeks ago. i was hanging with a friend of mine in her room and we she was studying, while i was chilling on the couch. i was just thinking about past memories and i felt pretty happy. about 20 mins later she was ready to go to class and so i walked her and on the way i had this intense feeling of calm and oneness with everything around me, and it was like my vision sharpened to this amazing clarity. i don't remember what i was saying to her (but i'm sure it was profound, haha). i remember it was this incredible feeling and i felt like i could lose myself in it. it was this incredible peace and security with everything around me. amazing. i didn't know what it was exacty, and still don't.

although, i'm in a college class called "women and religion" and in one of the books, Karen Armstrong's "A History of God" it described what the prophets called "experiencing the divine." it was exactly what i had felt.

i don't know what triggered it, or what my brain was doing at the time. but it sure was interesting.
