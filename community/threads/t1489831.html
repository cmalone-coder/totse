Bisexual Rant
I date people because I like the person. Genitalia is sort of secondary to me. If someone is intelligent, nice to look at, interesting, and makes me laugh I couldn't care less if they're a man, woman, transexual, transvestite, hermaphordite, whatever.

And no one ever asked me if I was gay, growing up. No one ever called me gay, growing up. It never crossed my mind.

I had my first kiss when I was thirteen, with a boy. But, in my freshman year of high school, when my best girly friend kissed me I wasn't shocked. For years earlier I'd had another close friend, who was a girl, who I found very attractive but I knew she didn't like girls so I never made a move.

This girl was beautiful, and since we were already friends I knew I liked her personality, and she wanted me. So, when she asked me to be her girlfriend I said "yes" without even thinking of the fact that suddenly I was going to be Gay. Gay with a capitol "G". A Gay Lesbian. Now, I'd be "the lesbian" to all my straight friends and whenever they introduced me to someone they'd introduce me as

"Hi, this is ****. She's gay."

There was a small clique of gay kids in the (San Francisco in the early 90's) school and her and I definitely didn't fit in there. We wore make up, shaved our legs, and loved to flirt with guys.

But, now I suddenly had to choose. People wanted to know: Was this a permanent thing? No, it was a relationship. Was it a phase? No, I wasn't going to grow out of liking cool, good looking, people. How do you just stop liking guys? I didn't. Are you going to date guys, too? Not while I'm with my girlfriend. So, are you going to start using words like "dyke" and "fag" all the time because it's ironic? Uh... no.

Bottom line is: not all of us define ourselves by our sexuality.

Cheers,
