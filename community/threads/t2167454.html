On the other hand, something else to keep in mind is that once enough people decide to invest in gold and silver, China and Russia will realize just how much gold and silver are/will be worth here, and they WILL be dicks about it; selling a shitload of gold, killing it's value as well as screwing over everyone who bought gold. Why would they be such dicks, you ask? Well, they will probably not even realize they're crashing the market, they will just see high values on the gold in the backs of their closets.

So, you have two choices: Securely lose money slowly in a bank, or take the risky path and keep your worth by buying gold/silver, all the while hoping Murphy's Law does not kick in.

That said, I would definitely buy gold and silver, while closely monitoring their prices, in case they start to drop.
