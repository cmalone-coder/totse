Why did Crocop lose?
I was genuinely stunned by Crocop's loss against Gonzaga.
I had him down as one of the best fighters in MMA, but from the fight report he got his ass handed to him.
In an interview today he appears to be frustrated by UFC's glitz and glamour, in Pride he would stay in his hotel room the day of the fight, turn up to the match and go home afterwards, at UFC he has to do weigh in posing lots of pre-match interviews e.t.c and this could have thrown him psychologically, furthermore there was a huge amount of pressure on him for the fight.

Psychological factors seem to be a reason but it really isn't an excuse for a professional fighter of his calibre.

Speaking of underestimating things... a lot of fans OVERESTIMATE Mirko. He's a dominant MMA fighter, but his style and fight plan have holes in them, he does have several losses in MMA and in kickboxing.

also Gonzaga is five years younger than him, and seemed to be in better condition.

Mirko keeps his left so low as well, he's practically asking for a big strike from that side. He wasn't being active enough standing up. Still he NEVER should have got caught with a kick like that. It was completely his fault, very poor move. If you look at the reply as well, he just wasn't prepared in his defense to deal with high kicks. He flinched when he saw it coming instead of trying to weave way from it, block it, catch it, etc etc. Maybe his mental game was suffering? Perhaps he was distracted.
