Re: Testing Homemade Alchohol Purity
I have tested "moonshine" before and here is how I do it.

1. The blue flame method is a good test but not an exact science. The purer the alcohol the cleaner and bluer the flame. NOT ALL IMPURITIES WILL BURN! use caution.

2. The light test. Hold the alcohol up to the light in a clear jar or glass. (IF Your Alcohol is "white"). Check for sediment and objects floating. Also shake it with a lid on it and you shoud see clear tiny bubbles rising to the top.

3. The Buddy test is the best and most accurate. Give some to a friend and let them drink it first. Obseve carefully, if they do not die, go insane, become paralyzed, or go blind then it should be ok to drink.

4. The self test. This is also a good test. Drink a SMALL amount. (2 or 3 ounces) Wait one hour. See above. If none of the effects are noted then it should be alright in a limited quantity. Do not over indulge the first time. Wait a day if no adverse effects are noted then PARTY ON !!!!

Always use caution when drinking anything homemade or from an unknown source. (It could be pee and Pepsi).
