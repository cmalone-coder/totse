Re: caught smoking weed in park by police
It was less than an ounce so that would make it a misdemeanor. Whether or not you do time is based upon your past. If you have no record and are still in school than I would say have your attorney ask about a diversion program but NOT boot camp. A typical program means a meeting about once a week for 6 weeks to discuss your drug use and some community service. If the program they offer sounds good, than take it. If you aren't in school and have a prior, plead not guilty jury trail. Another problem you will probably face is distribution. They will probably try to use their MANS unit to milk you for info and will threaten you with being charged for felony distribution if you don't set up your dealer. If you or your friends are interrogated by them at any time refuse to talk without a lawyer present, no matter what they say demand a lawyer. If it comes to that, go out and get your own attorney, don't rely on a public defender. Get a local one, like a former DA, judge, or mayor, someone who knows people in your district. Remember, if you don't get a descent plea bargain, for any reason, plead not guilty jury trial.
