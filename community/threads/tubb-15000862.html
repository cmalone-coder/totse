Should I accept that what I've discovered is true - and wait for something to take me out? Right now, I'm looking at Taoism, which appears to me to be the best, for many reasons. Can anyone please help me? Is my choice right? Should I go forward...or stop?

Re: Help me....
The whole thing about life is that it is a matter of belief. You say you are an atheist but that just means you don't believe in a god as a personage. You still have beliefs and these beliefs form your "god" for you, if you admit it or not. Maybe that doesn't fit your definition of god but nonetheless an argument can be made for it.

Like say your belief is that things just happen by chance, which is a common scientific belief used in evolution, for instance. Then that means you use this belief to explain why things happen. If this is your absolute belief, your only one, then this is your only "god".

But probably you have other absolute beliefs, such as the speed of light in a vacuum is such and such a number and doesn't vary. These other beliefs can also be seen as your other "gods".

So it ends up that most people have multiple gods and they basically worship them by having faith in them. So you might say they aare pantheists instead of atheists or christians or whatever.

And it turns out that there is a system of belief that does away with these multiple gods and replaces them with one god or belief that encompasses all the others.

This belief is that all questions have more than one correct answer. This idea is contradictory and seems illogical to all other ways of thinking that can be devised. And it is the only idea that is unique and cannot be explained by any other idea.

Any other "religion" that a person searches for besides this belief will eventually not be able to answer all questions and will lead to disappointment. Taoism, for instance, was very good at describing the fruits of the belief that I alluded to above, but Taoism may have fallen short in not being able to say where these observations came from. Otherwise Taoism probably would have succinctly stated this belief, in some form. (Or maybe it did state this somewhere. I haven't studied it in depth.)

You are not without religion or god(s). That is just your viewpoint on the matter. Try this one out.
