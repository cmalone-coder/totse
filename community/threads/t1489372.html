I've known couples that fought just as passionately as they loved, their arguments were absolutely OVER the top, but when they were peacefull (which was most of the time) they were very loving and happy together.

Some personalities just clash like that, it doesn't mean that you shouldn't try to have a relationship with someone just because you get into rampaging fights, as long as it's ONLY an argument... if these fights are ruining your life and making you so miserable that you don't want to be with this guy then the tempestuous relationship isn't for you. If you can handle a yelling match every once in awhile and it doesn't effect the way you love each other afterward then stick with it.

I don't like arguing, yet my fiance and I get into arguments over pretty stupid things. We're both fairly stubborn, so that only makes matters worse. At the end of arguments we snuggle and make up, and usually have sex... and I don't think poorly of him or love him any less because of it. I wouldn't leave him over an argument.

If you do marry you'll have to really work on calming yourselves, you can't fight like that in front of your children, it's not healthy for them. You as an adult might be able to handle a heated argument, but realize that your future kids shouldn't be expected to.
