The not being a "true quad" deal, just means that the chip is simply two dual core dies, instead a a single quad core die. It's really no big deal.

I also use a q6600, and I do suggest going quad. Games are starting to take advantage of quads, and I think my quad core will last me longer than a dual core.

As for that board, do you plan to go crossfire? Are you going to over clock? If no to both of those, you could save a lot of money by buying a cheaper board. However, if you don't mind spending that much, go right ahead; it's a good board.
