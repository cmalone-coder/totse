Hey Canadian drugy, You germinated way to early unless you live in the southern hemisphere,or grow indoors. Marijuana is a annual plant, meaning it grows, produces seed and dies in the span of less than a year. Marijuana starts to flower when the amount of sunlight or artificial light reaches 12 hours or less a day. This facilitates the production of pollen by the males, which in turn is spread by the wind to the females which produce the seed.
