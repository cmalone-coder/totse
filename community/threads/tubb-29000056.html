I must disagree on most of you people's view on knives.
If someone with a knife knows what there doing they can get to you and deliver a disabling blow before someone with a gun has the time to lift the gun and shot. If you are trained to use a knife (I prefer aardvark butterflies) you can draw and defend yourself far faster than if you just happen to carry a gun.

Also a knife can do so much more damage. A bullet has a strait trajectory that will enter the body in a strait line. Yes guns can serious harm you but once the bullet is inside your body it often goes in so fast the exothermic heat from the bullet will cauterize the wound and the bullet if it stays in place which it usually does plugged the wound.

I knife in the hands of someone who knows what they're doing will never attack in a strait line. There are the 9 parallel planes of attack and all of those include a slicing action which harms the victim far more than a strait entry. Also the knife wielder will try the wiggle the blade as much as possible to slice as many internal organs as possible.

A knife demands skill. A gun demands nothing. I prefer to know what I'm doing.

There is also the legal standpoint to take into account. A knife in most states is viewed as a second class weapon so you will get off so much easier if you harmed someone with a knife than a gun.

All in all I like knives. I think that IF you know how to use them they are far more effective. if you don't....stick with the gun.
