I want love
I want love.

I want somebody. I want somebody who can love me without so much ambiguity and without letting others get in the way; someone who can devote herself to me (this doesn't mean "let Infrared consume all your time or he'll get jealous", it means "devote yourself to Infrared, don't string other guys along, be good to him"), someone who really does love me, someone I can really love; someone who's sincere to me and open and can cry on my shoulder, someone who can feel as if everything is right in the world when she's in my arms, not someone who hides things from me or is insincere to me, usually in the guise of "I knew it would hurt you, so I didn't tell you".

Admittedly, I want somebody who can look up to me, and yet who also has confidence enough in herself to see herself on my very same level; not someone who tells me, "I don't deserve you" or "How can you love someone like me". I want somebody who I mean the world to.

What makes me feel I even deserve someone who meets these highly-basic characteristics (all the things I want could never be worded)? Because I just do. I'm a man who has confidence in himself and can devote himself to someone; who will give anything just to see his love smile for a day, even if it causes him some pain. This devotion has gotten me hurt many times in the past; yes, it is in check, but it's still as passionate as ever. My love is a pure one; it's one that she can come home to and be wrapped in at any time, it's one that isn't ambiguous and insincere.

I want love, and I want to give love. I have a lot of love to give, even after giving lots of love and, though having it return, still having not found what I'm looking for, apparently. All the things that I want I can give back, and more.

But loving me or being loved by me doesn't have to be some convoluted thing. While I want someone who can challenge me on intellectual levels and who isn't my lapdog, I also want someone who is willing to listen to me. I'm just a 6'2", 190-lb. man who likes rock music, can work well with his hands, figure out anything mechanical, snipe a gallon milkjug from 800 yards away with iron sights. Who can apply many of his hand-related skills in bed, not to mention.

I want someone. I want to want someone. I have a lot to give, and I want to give it, before it's too late. I want to find someone. I want to find someone who loves me and who I can give all my love to. Is she out there somewhere? I wonder when I will find out?

/rant. No replies necessary. I didn't know where else to put it.

-Infrared
