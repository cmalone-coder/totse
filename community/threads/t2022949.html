Re: chinatown
You can expect to get pretty much anything you want for pretty much whatever you want to pay for it. For the more illegal shit, like a fake ID or self-defense weapons or whatever, you'll have to ask around, as they generally don't put that kind of shit on display at the tables. If you're not an idiot, you'll know to keep small incriments of money in various pockets, preferably $10 in each. This way, when the person tells you that something costs like $20, you can be like, "Alright cool." and pull out a 10 and be like, "Shit this is all I have," and since everything in Chinatown is stolen (ESPECIALLY the things they have on the random tables against the curb..Pipes, bongs, etc) they'll give it to you if you're persistent. Don't let them take you for any more than you want to pay. Start leaving if they refuse to give you your price, and they will give in.

I'm moving to Chinatown in 2 months. Mulberry St. Between Canal and Hester :-)
