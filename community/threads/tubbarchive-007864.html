following was posted by Chemethist:

The Straight to bee 3 by WhoMe is a valuable extraction write-up that i am proud to sponsor on this board.

With modified ratios and other such tweaks in the write-up, The chemethist consistently comes away with 80% yields after turning the freebase back to a salt (HCL).

For every 800ml of white vinegar, add 280g of salt, and 50ml of Muriatic Acid.

Shake rattle and roll till its well mixed then let sit ofr a few hours. Filter the clear brine and keep in a seperate bottle.

Now it doesnt matter what pills you use, whether its 48ct. white 60s or 20ct. 120s always use 100ml of brine.

For those than cant count here is the run down on pills to brine ratio...

100ml of Brine per...

96 30mg pills
48 60mg pills
20 120mg pills
10 240mg pills

Always crush the pills to a fine powder. then mix with brine and boil for at least 20 minutes.

Filter this through 1 coffee filter and 1 cotton ball, this should give a clear solution. If the solution isnt clear, then bee prepared to do some extra cleaning after this extraction.

Base with sodium carbonate till ph 7.

Add plenty of naptha to this, and start heating.

Use a 40% Sodium Hydroxide solution, add the solution until the bottom is full of fluffy white substance. This is your freebase.

Boil until the fluffy white freebase has dissapeared and dissolved into the naptha layer. Collect the naptha and put in freezer.

Now wait a few hours and you return you should find all of the freebase at the bottom of the jar. Filter this and wash with a little fresh naptha. Then with a ice-cold water wash, and then a hexane wash. Do not skip these steps.

Collect/weigh.


You should get at least 70% yields. Those of you that really strive for every little bit will get 80-85% yields.
