Ah, the old identity over time nugget.

My view is that you being X's Girlfriend or, Y's son is merely another aspect of your own identity.

Along the same lines, there runs an argument that George Orwell and Eric Blair were not the same person (George Orwell was the pen name of Eric Blair) because Eric Blair was not known as an author, and George Orwell was not known as the little guy who enjoyed a pint down the pub in his wee english village. However, this is bollocks. Being George Orwell is merely a quality of Eric Blair - George Orwell's existence, (as long as we assume the existence of a George Orwell to necessitate the existence of a figure with all the qualites of George Orwell as we know him) is contingent on the existence of Eric Blair. Hence, your existence as X's girlfriend or Y's son is contingent on your own existence. So you're always you.
