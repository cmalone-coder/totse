i tried nutmeg, i give it a nuetral almost negative rating
lately ive had a bit of action on totse planning for the big last friday, i started a thread about the best courage drug and then prepared, i got some alcohol and my precious valium to help me through this day
the thread on what happened on friday is here http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=2160649

but i thought if the plan failed (which it didnt but wasnt a complete success) i may as well trial something at the same time, so while buying the alcohol i bought a preground bag of 25g of nutmeg.

on friday at 12:30 i decided to try it, i made myself a cup of coffee as normal and poured the entire packet into it, it was terrible, i couldnt slam it down so i had to sip it, it was thick and stuck inside your mouth like curdled milk would, as soon as i drank that i had a shower and it made me feel a little sick.

i set off for a long walk and had a good old time, i talked to myself in my head as anyone does (rite?) and laughed at myself for going the wrong way, wasnt much different except i was in a happier mood, at the end of the walk i took a valium and drank some booze before doing what i went to do.

during the day i was on and off, different moods every so often and very light nasua, on my way home i was very irritible and that went quickly to happy many times, even when i was a little happy i still felt on the bad side and wanted the high to go away, wanted to feel normal again.

around 8 hours into it things finally got better, a very mild marijuana feel came on and off, i remember laughing histerically at something for ten minutes and then after realising i completely forgot what i was laughing at, laughed even harder. but later on that night the feeling of me wanting to be normal again kept coming back.

the next day was much being slightly happy and then being slightly slow, it cut out half way through the next day. later on in that night i took 300 mg of DXM which usually lasts 4-5 hours, it took twice as long to set in and 2 hours into it i had a coffee to wake up and the high completely dissapeared, so maybe it was the coffee or nutmeg doesnt agree with DXM.

thats my story and i would like to note that im lucky enough not to be immune to codeine like i feared. i had 300mg's of it earlier today and felt rather good from it for an hour or two, right before i felt tired as shit and have been taking mini sleeps all night, 5 hours in all the effects have completely gone, whats some peoples experience's with codeine.
