True Friendship
He's got a steady girlfriend and a good job. He wants to buy a bag, but I know he's not a smoker. So I figure somethings up, depression or something or maybe just an excuse to come over. We've been friends for as long as I can remember but I've only made a few apperances in his scene in the past few years. Birthdays and so on. But what the fuck does it matter why, it's twenty five in my pocket. I'm a businessman in a business I hate, the only business I know.

He comes over and installs a couple computer games for me and gives me a few burn CDs and we knock around the punching bag for a bit. Then to business. Of course I tactfully avoid any issues deep and/or meaningful and/or connected in any way to his sudden desire to smoke. I'm not the kind of friend for that shit, not to him anyway. So he gets out his money and he's a dollar and five cents short. No problems, you can pay us back later. Don't make me break your legs for it. I mull his bag, and roll it into joints for him. Top quality service with a smile and comical banter. Then he leaves.

He's a true friend. He enjoys my company. But there's no room for friends to me anymore. I'll be ready for a social visit when his stash is dry. And that is as close to true friendship as I'll see.
