Re: Disrhythmic Anthems
There is nothing to get; as I said, it was an excercise where I let the words pick themselves. I was going for the overall way it sounds - without the words having any meaning.

Like you might hear a foreign song and like the way it sounds desppite not knowing what the heck it says.

Sometimes I use the vocals [or writen word] as an instrument of it's own, to blend in with the others to make the whole.

This time it was merely free form, off the top of my head stuff: I kinda just blanked my mind [no jokes, please folks ;-) ] and let 'what felt right' float to the top and that's what I wrote.

I have done other stuff that took more effort.

THere was one I did a while back that I sat listening to for hours, 'feeling' different words and combinations of words to get something that just came together 'comfortably well'.

Though it wouldn't neccesarrily seem so to hear it, if you do listen to it you'll find that it does seem to work and flow and seem to be 'just the right words' [or not ].

I'll put it up on my MP3.com page.

Of course, I expect I'll get many many ore people deriding it. That's cool; I don't write for anybody but myself [and my daughter, now a days]. And if they do like it, well, natch, that's cool too.
