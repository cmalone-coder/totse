The uneasy feeling could have been from an underlying negative mindset that you may not have noticed until you were tripping. Also could have been caused by any number of thought patterns, usually negative ones.

As far as xanax goes, that's the kind of stuff that's good to have on hand should the trip start getting too overwhelming or unpleasant. Don't take it before the trip because it WILL dampen the effects, that's why you save it for when/if it becomes a little too much to handle. A xanax and a beer should calm things right down for you.

Anyway, on to the second part. If I were you and those were the chemicals I had access to I would pick up some acid, MDMA, and weed. Acid trips are generally very unlikely to become unpleasant when combined with MDMA (Candyflip), and of course, smoke weed at the peak.

If that's the route you go, take the acid and wait until you're near the peak, then get the MDMA (if it's crystal) and dab it behind your lip or under your tongue. In no time at all you'll be frying pretty nice. Once you're peaking on both go ahead and smoke a few cones :)

Re: Uncomfortable on LSD
You didn't take enough.

That could very well be.

A lot of the time when you're tripping below the point of breakthrough it can be an uncomfortable feeling. The point of breakthrough (in my opinion) as far as LSD goes is the point past just seeing morphing objects and enhanced colors and trails. There's a level of dosage with acid that will start giving you very intense visual/auditory/mental hallucinations, prominent closed eye visuals, and in many a 'spiritual' thought process.

Tripping below that point, as I've said many times, is like mentally blue balling yourself. It's like there's so much to see and it's just out of your grasp.It's very common for there to be discomfort or unease during the come up period of the trip, and I suppose if the dose isn't high enough that feeling could stick around for the duration of the trip.

Try taking more next time, but make sure you have the IDEAL setting and mindset.
