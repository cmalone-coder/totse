Re: HK USP 9mm or Norinco CZ Knock off?
Are you shooting IPSC? How long?

If you're looking for a CZ look at the SP-01, it's made for production in IPSC IIRC and from my research is probably one of the best choices out there. As for the HK, i don't like the trigger a great deal either and it seems people either love or hate the mag release.

If you want something in New/Great condition you'll pay $1100-$1600 for most guns, The best suggestion i can give is to keep an eye and ear out at your pistol club (Have a chat with the president too!) because people often sell their shit to upgrade/shoot a different division w/e and you can pick up some good bargins, last time i was there someone was selling a G17, holster, 6x 10rnd mags, mag pouches, belt, and 200 or so rounds of 9mm for $1000 (might of been $1100? either way it was cheap), unfortunatly i still don't have my Cat H licence .

oh BTW, second hand glocks from guys at the club are a good idea because unless they have another glock they won't need the holster or mag pouches which saves you even more, where as i they're selling say a CZ-75 to upgrade to an open Tangfoilo or whatever they are called, the holster are often the same which means you'll have to buy those new and seperate .

Re: HK USP 9mm or Norinco CZ Knock off?
I honestly wouldn't go with the Norinco, if you want a CZ design, you should probably either get an original one or a Tanfoglio, they're both relatively fairly priced.

I'm from a country with high gun prices too, and both CZ and Tanfoglio are quite inexpensive when compared with other makers, unless of course you go for the uber customized Tanglio Limited models, those are quite pricey.

I have a Tanfoglio Force and it works great, okay I have it in .22 but it's identical to her bigger cousin, so really I'm sure you'd love the Tanfoglio, they're easy to customize and they have great customer service too.

Regarding Norinco, my range was given one of their 1911 clones for free by the Italian Norinco importer, I swear after barely six months of regular use, the barrel had lost the rifling(well not totally but it was visibly fucked), the gun was all loosy and the firing pin broke.

Also the gun looked really bad, the finish was awful, I've seen it next to some guy's Colt and the Norinco looked fake, like on of those cheap toy guns that shoot pellets, it also came in a carboard box, no kidding, it didn't even have a plastic case.

Seriously you get what you pay for, Chinese products are generally speaking shit, they use low quality raw materials, cheap unskilled workforce, they try to cut the costs in every step of the production process, it's inevitable that the final product will be bad.

The only thing I would buy from Norinco is maybe one of their former arsenal SKS, but definately not one of their copies, just buy a good quality second hand pistol, really.

It's also unethical if you ask me, Western firms invest quite a great deal of resources in R&D, behind a new product that comes out there's a lot of effort to bring something new to the customer, and it's fair that the customers should buy an original product so to compensate the company for their innovative and creative work.

It's the gamble factor that I don't like.
