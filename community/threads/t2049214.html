Re: best way to aproach an alone girl
Go in, follow these steps, see where it takes you:

1) Approach with a big smile and good body language. Head up, back straight, chest out, and remember to take up a lot of space, as this "proves your ego".

2) Go in with something interesting, something that will get her talking. A good starter for anything is, "Hey, can I get your opinion about *insert something interesting here*?" If you can cold read or want to google that, it may set you apart and help establish rapport and/or an early emotional connection.

3) Always give yourself a time constraint. She doesn't have a reason to keep talking to you, and if she doesn't like you, she'll want you to leave. By telling her that "I can only talk for a minute, then I have to get to *something here*" you allow her to relax and here you out without wondering "when is this guy gonna leave?".

4) Keep your distance, don't get in her personal space. Make early eye contact and hold it as long as you can without feeling that you're "staring". Ensure that you speak loudly and slowly enough to be heard and understood; women can make a real connection through voice and eye contact.

5) Make her laugh, and try to distinguish yourself from the horde of other guys. Just be your best self.

So, that's how I would do it. I'm curious as to how this advice will be recieved.
