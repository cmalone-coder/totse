I go up to raves like every single weekend. Every single saturday night, someone at an events does too much of something. It's not a fun thing to see someone start to wobble around, fall down, and start having a seizure. It's breath-taking, but in a bad way.

I have wondered the same thing as the OP, but I don't really want to ever have to go through what those people are dealing with.

Now, I'm not being a hypocrite at all here, but I do have a decent opinion on the topic. All you need to remember is this word: MODERATION. Don't see "how fucked up" you can get by taking more than you KNOW you can handle to see what the experience is like. When I want to roll, I take one or two pills, 5 being the most i've ever done. Another problem is people buying the drugs are the most oblivious to what they're actually putting in their bodies. Most people into E don't care what kind of cut is in their pills. They're just e pills........

Now, I do know that there are people out there (such as myself) who actually do take their own health and safety into regard and DO test their pills, or at least get them from a trusted source who isn't going to pull any bullshit or tell you misleading information.
That's not the majority though.

People are going to abuse every substance out there, that's a given, and there will ALWAYS be people pushing shit past it's limits. You can't say much against that. BUT, you can help people get educated so they have better information, leading to better judgements (hopefully). This is the case with me. I go to raves, and my main purpose for being there is to sell beans. I do get SO much pleasure from them, but when it comes down to it, it's business. Whenever I sell anyone there anything, I sit down with them and talk to them. Tell them what's in the pills. Tell them how the roll will be. Most importantly, I tell every single person to be safe. You should all do the same.

Oh and btw, with pure MDMA, the roll should last a good 4-5 hours (sometimes longer), and you should be able to sleep that night and eat fine the next day. You'll still be a little drained, but you won't have nearly as bad a comedown as you would if you take pills with unknown substances in them.
