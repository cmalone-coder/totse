Re: THE REAL WAY TO MAKE ACID?
ergotamine reflux in inert atmosphere with hydrazine to give dl-isolysergic acid hydrazide. Then with either di(p-toluyl)-l-tartaric acid or l-norephedrine to give d-isolysergic acid. Then make the azide with sodium NO2 to give d-isolysergic acid azide. Then mix this with diethylamine to give a mixture of d-isolysergic acid diethylamide and d-lysergic acid diethylamide. Seporate these two compounds on a column of aluminium oxide. Mix with d-tartaric acid to give the water soluable d-LSD-d-tartrate.
All these steps are done in darkness and in an inert atmosphere. You will require approximately $10,000 for equipment and chemicals. Plus you will need a whole lot of practical organic chemistry knowledge. If you do not have said you will most likely blow yourself up or burn the building down. You are working with organic solvents every step of the way.
