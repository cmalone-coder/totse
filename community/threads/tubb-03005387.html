First of all, flasks are not something that come 'long' like a tube [like you would use for a bong]. They do however, make great water pipes [best to use a 1 or 2L side arm filter flask. I described the one I made - dubbed 'the Gas Chamber' - in a thread in BLTC].

Still, not worth getting busted for as you can buy one easily [I know a bought one isn't as sweet as the stolen fruits. Still...]

Second of all, HCI.

There is no such chemical as HCI.

There is HCL [Hydrochloric acid], which is of some use to a cooker, though not allthat much since it is not hard to get [I can get concentrated HCL by the gallons and nobody would bat an eye].

Definitely not worth the risk of getting busted.

Of course, you may have been referring to Hydriodic acid, which is HI [not HCI].

Hydriodic acid is of value to cookers, as it is a restricted item. I sincerely doubt, however, that you would find any in a high school chem. lab.

Something that you might find in a high school chem. lab, which a cooker might want, is Iodine crystals.

Still, not worth the risk of getting busted, IMO.
