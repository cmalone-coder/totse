Re: absinthe oil
Just so yall aren't misinformed. Absinthe is much more than just a high proof alcohol and wormwood. Generally its a distilled maceration of wormwood, hyssop, anise/star anise, fennel, mint, and alcohol. Anise is a big ingredient that people seem to forget, its a big part of the flavor. There are also other things, some people like putting ginger in theres, its pretty good. People seem to forget that its not just a drug, its a drink, and although you may get some effect from the thujone, its not really worth drinking badly prepared absinthe.

Ok, because I really like absinthe, I'll list some more popular ingredients, and my opinions on them. I'm sure you don't care, but I'd like to type a little extra.

coriander seeds: These are really good, and add an excellent peppery flavor.

mint: Often used in the coloring process, but, although it doesn't add any true mint flavor, you can tell its there. I don't like too much mint in mine, because it has a tendency to make the think taste like listerine.

citrus fruits: sometimes the peels from things like oranges, lemons and limes are added. The flavor is barely noticable. I'd say orange and lime are the best, lemons don't really balance out with the rest of the flavors.

Of course, generally absinthe tastes very similiar to shit (well, how I'd imagine shit tastes). However, its its prepared properly (even the bottled stuff isn't really), although its just as bitter, the flavors are more apparent, and you can actually enjoy drinking it. Of course, with most liquors, its one of those "acquired taste" things.

One tablespoon angelica root
One teaspoon hyssop
One half teaspoon coriander seeds
One quarter teaspoon caraway seeds
One pinch cardomon pods
One tablespoon fennel or anise seeds
750 ml. 100 proof vodka
