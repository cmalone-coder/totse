My experience and insight w/e w/ 2C-E
About a month or two ago I recieved 500mg of 2c-e from an email source.

I recieved a miligram scale from ebay.

I've been taking 2c-e ..2x-3x a week I would say and I sold the rest of it off. I handed ALOT out to people, also.

Here is what I have to say about this drug, alot of it conflicts what is talked about on the internet.

Effects:
Trails, mad mad trails. I can see trails up to 2 days after taking the drug, easily.
Breathing walls, this only happens when you look for it, unless you do above 20mg.
Heavy body load. Sometimes I think this is amazing, other times I feel this is REALLY uncomfortable, it is really based on the enviornment.
Clenched teeth, feels like I'm tweaking
Lack of sleep sometimes but best sleep other times..weird..again enviornmental.
Most beautiful hallucenations over any drug, period. 10000x better then LSD and mushrooms. I can not explain how beautiful this drug is in words.. very difficult.
Thoughts aren't really that different..but I trip alot even when I'm sober so I don't know how this would effect other people. "Normal" people I've given this too say they philosophize like crazy on this drug, and one kid even told me it has changed his life. Personally, doesn't change much at all for me. Makes me a bit moar paranoid, though.

Things I have noticed:
Enviornmental is HUGE for this drug! My hardest trip I had was off 10mg. I've taking 30mg at one time, and about 80mg over the course of 2 days but the one day that I did 10mg was my largest trip, and that was (I'm sure) because of enviornmental factors. If I am in a boring room, it can literally take up to 3 hours to fully kick in. Even then it is not that interesting. If I am in a really pretty/trippy room, it can probally take an hour tops to kick in fully. After I leave the pretty room, even if I go to a boring room, that boring room is still fully intense and now pretty!

There is NO TOLERANCE! Who ever says this is really fucking stupid. The time I tripped harder off 10mg was after the 80mg/2day deal. Everyone I have talked to claim that there is no tolerance. People on the internet are fucking stupid.

There is a certain chain of events that occur when I take this drug:
Take the drug
Body load
Trails
Trip

Sometimes the body load leaves, other times it doesn't.

WILL EDIT MOAR I HAVE DINNER!
[ediz]

Taking ketamine before this drug pretty much nulifies the effects. I hadn't taken ethyl (2c-e) for about a week (not like it matters because tolerance to this drug isnt real), I did a good bit of K, then dropped 30mg of ethyl and it felt like..5-7mg of it. My girl reported the same thing, but she dropped 20 instead.

This drug is BANK. I made my money back about 3 times, and I still tripped AT LEAST 30 or so times.. and I gave a shit load out.

I did get it for much cheaper because the vendor fucked up and apologized, though. He turned scam and now I have no source as near as him, next one is about 180$ a gram but still, I know I could make 3-5x my money back and still have enough to trip mad if I wanted to.

MG scales are NOT that unnaccurate! I got the shitty one off of ebay for about 35 total. I put the 5g weight that comes w/ it and the scale always came to 5.002-5.005.


HMMM I can't really think of anything else. I have a lot of shit to say but can't put it out there. Any questions you can ask I have ALOT of insight about this.
