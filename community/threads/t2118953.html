Well I don't have any extra reasons for legalizing heroin, but I can give you a counter to one of the more common reasons people give against legalizing it. That argument, which is used against legalizing heroin for pain patients or even terminally ill patients, is: They are already in great pain [or dying], why add to their troubles by getting them addicted in addition to everything else?

If heroin were legalized medically, it would be for people who have conditions that are going to be with them for the rest of their life. If it's going to last their life, then they'll be in pain the rest of their life. If they're in pain the rest of their life, they'll need the heroin for the rest of their life. If they're already going to be using it for the rest of their lives, then why even be concerned about addiction since it would be redundant anyway. So the point is moot.

And for those fuckers whose concern is only that the pain patients would be getting high, anyone who has needed pain killers - correctly - knows that if you take the proper dose, you don't get high; that it goes to the pain before it goes to the head, so to speak.

In short, the reason all drugs should be legal and controled is because when you make drugs illegal you create a black market for them, in every instance. Our country eventually realised that prohibition doesn't work and made alcohol legal again (just like they should do with drugs.) Then, over night, Al Capone and his gangsters were put out of business because honestly, if you can go buy your brew from the store up the street, why would you go buy it from Johnny on the street corner?

See what I mean? That is a MUCH better arguement than any of the others mentioned.
