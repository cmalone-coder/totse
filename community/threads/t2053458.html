Re: Getting the guy
What if the conversation dies right after the, "How are ya?" Do I have to think of another question or do I just leave it?

You should try to keep it going (and so should he, of course). Of course, if his reaction is really negative, then best to leave it.

Basically, start with commenting on something, best if it's an open question as opposed to a "yes/no" one. If absolutely nothing comes to my mind, use a simple "How're you / what're you up to". In fact, "What're you up to" is probably a better option, because it's more likely to get the conversation somewhere. If you ask "How're you" and he just answers "fine", then you'll have to struggle. If he replies with "fine, you?", then you'll have to speak a bit more, e.g. ("I'm alright. Spent the whole night working on.. "), and hope that he picks up on it.

Hope that helps.
