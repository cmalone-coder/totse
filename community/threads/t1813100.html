Well, if you want to be a full Jew, then youd have to go through an Orthodox synagogue. Contact a Rabbi and ask him about it. They would require you to have a Bris(circumsision). It also could take up to 3 years. Its a long process, and its probably not very easy.

If you wanted to convert to Reform Judaism, the process would probably be shorter, and Im not sure if they require a Bris(Although they would probably have at least a ceremonial one). The problem with this path though is that you could not emigrate to Israel, since the Chief Rabbis do not consider Reform convert to be converted through Halackah, and therefor not real Jewish Converts.

I would recomend Orthodox Judaism anyhow, because it has a lot more cultural value, and also the Rabbi doesnt preach at you like a Reform Rabbi would.

As always though, a Rabbi would be happy to meet with you and discuss the process, and Im sure that he would be more helpful than I. I would recomend seeking a Orthodox and Reform Rabbi in you area to talk to.
