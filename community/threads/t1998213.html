Understanding Metal Gear Solid
Don't read this if you have not completed Metal Gear Solid 1 or 2. This thread will most probably spoil the entire series for you.

I, like many others am a massive fan of the Metal Gear Solid series, but some parts of the storyline confuse me a bit.

I'm going to ask some questions about both MGS1 and MGS2 (I've completed both many times, but want to understand them more before going onto MGS3), if anyone knows the answers please reply. Also, any additional info would be great!

1) What happens to Meryl after MGS1? After you save her in MGS1, you don't hear anything about her at all afterwards. Or is it just a 'use your imagination' thing?

2) Why won't Liquid Snake die? You kill him multiple times, but he still lives. In the game he states 'as long as you live, I shall live' (or something), and that just doesn't make sense.

3) Who is Revolver Ocelot? Is he a member of/working for the Patriots?

4) I thought (maybe I assumed) Foxhound and Next-Generation Special Forces were the good guys, why do they become/are they terrorists?

5) Where is MGS3 set? I believe it's set before MGS1 and MGS2. Is it the 'Outer Heaven' story that can be read about in the MGS1 book, or something completely different?

6) What the hell is going on with MGS2? The storyline confuses the hell out of me. Can someone please try to explain it to me? So I can then ask about anything that doesn't make sense to me.

7) Does Richard Ames work for the Patriots? When I read one of the Shadow Moses articles on the MGS2 'Special' section, I can't remember if it said Richard Ames was a member of the Patriots or not; but he seemed to have some "power".

8) Why did Ocelot choose to have Liquid Snake's arm? Out of all the fucking arms he could have had, why did he chose Liquid's? Is there any point in that? Also, why does Liquid have control over Ocelot when he likes? It makes no sense.

9) What other MGS games are there, and do they relate in anyway to the main MGS1, 2 and 3 storyline? Basically, would it matter or not if I didn't play them/would you recommend playing them.

I can't think of any other questions right now. I'll add if one pops into my head.

Thanks for any replies.
