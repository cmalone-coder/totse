Tips for Hitmen
Never do a job without the proper info, equiptment or payment. Always demand payment up-front, in several small installments, and be sure to dispose of your employer (if he is not one of the criminal fraternity) after the job. If he's a civilian and he's caught out, he'll drop you in it to save himself. NEVER go without gloves, a hat and some form of facial disguise. If you do fuck up, you don't want people knowing what you look like. Wear tall shoes and stuff your clothes with cushions to disguise your height and weight. Never deal under your real name. If your employer demands results within a certain timeframe, demand that he provides you with the neccesary information to complete the job; the quarry's daily routine, their car registration, address, usual hangouts etc. Never accept a job from young people of highschool age, and try not to get involved in street-gang warfare. If you are caught during a job, dispose of as much evidence as you can before you are taken into custody (getting rid of weapons is advisable. A good tactic is to carry a knife as well as your rifle/pistol, and if you are certain to be caught, dispose of all disguises and firearms and make yourself available for arrest. This will make you look like a two-bit kid. Once arrested, you can turn on the waterworks, spill your guts on your employer and hopefully escape a full sentance). Time your job to the second, and have a series of cues. If you miss even one cue, abort the mission. Above all, ALWAYSUSE A SILENCER FOR FIREARMS!!, and if you are using a knife as a primary weapon, make sure it is small enough to be concealed and disposed of easily (down a drain or something), and make sure that it is long enough to strike vital organs. A man stabbed in the heart or brain dies instantly. A man who is bleeding to death does not, and will rouse some sort of alarm before passing away.
