Making RDX based Platic Explosives
I don't take any responsibility written, spoken, or implied in any way for what anyone does with this info. Making any explosives is dangerous and illegal. I suggest further reading for understanding the chemistry, handling, and legal use of explosives.

Ok guys so many are asking how to make something. Though I am a fan of using premade explosives for safety reasons, I'm posting this due to so many repeated questions.

You will need Hexamine, nitric acid, sulfuric acid, ice, salt,distilled water, Baking soda.

Hexamine. Hexamine can be obtained in three manners. 1. From a chem supply 2. From Hexamine Fuel tablets found at surplus stores, camping stores, and gun shows 3. It can be easily synthesized.

If fuel tablets are bought you don't have pure hexamine. You have hexamine mixed with parraffin. To separate it out crush the wax and mix the "powder" with acetone diluted with 35% by weight alcohol. This will remove the hexamine. Filter out the solution and evaporate the remaining liquid, the white solid remaining is hexamine.

Hexamine is easily synthesized. It's a simple process of mixing Spirits of Ammonia, household clear cloudless unscented Ammonia, with formaldahyde. Formaldahyde can be purchased at some pharmacies or ordered from pharmacies or chem suppliers.
The reaction is:
4 NH3(aq) + 6HCHO ---> (CH2)6N4 + 6H20
Evaporate the liquid to get the solid hexamine.

Now the hard part, trinitration of hexamine.

The nitric acid should be of the red fuming variety, the sulfuric acid should be white fuming.

Place a quar jar in an ice bath, a mixture of ice, salt, and just enough wate to make the ice float and have water between each cube. Weigh out 300g of Nitric acid and 90g of Sulfuric acid. Place the Nitric acid in the jar first and let it cool to under 30�F. Now slowly add the Sulfuric acid, about 30g at a time. After each addition mix the solution and allow it to cool. We now have what is called mixed acid, probably the most vital ingredient to explosive manufacturing.

Now pour off the solution into 3 separate containers, 130g each. Chill one container to under 30�F. To this add 55.73g of hexamine.

This is the reaction in whole:
6HN03.H2SO4+2(CH2)6N4-->2(CH2)6N4(NO3)3+3H2
+H2SO4

The sulufuric acid acts as a catalyst, hence it is not used up.

Add the hexamine around 10-11g at a time, stir the mixture slowly, it will start to warm up, as it does allow it cool and stir again. White crystals will start to appear. Filter these out and add to the second container of acid and repeat the mixing. Do this again with ther third container of acid.

After the final filtering wash the crystals with a dilute mixture of baking soda and distilled water, then again with just distilled water. You now have fresh RDX Crystals. These are heat and friction sensitive.

To use them add them to a 1/2 as much by volume Petroleum Jelly. This makes the explosive more stable and safer to handle, a cap is needed for detonation.

There you have it folks.
