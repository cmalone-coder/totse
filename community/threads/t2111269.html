Effect of demonizing drugs
I was thinking the other day about the effects that demonizing drugs has on our society. I mean, when we were in tribal settings, if a certain plant or flower had psychotropic effects it would be revered as sacred and injested with responsibility and respect. It seems like this also has lead to the very increased perception of drugs as party enhancers. Many people seem to revere drugs as only tools for getting fucked up and acting crazy.
