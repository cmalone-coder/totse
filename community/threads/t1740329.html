i have two stories worth mentioning that i can remember off the top of my head.

1. just after i was born and my parents put me to bed id start crying. this might seem normal, but they would put me there after i was already asleep and id wake up within 5 minutes screaming. this went on for a few weeks atleast. well one night after my dad got me to go to sleep in his arms he put me to bed and of course i started crying. he went in to check on me and he said he "felt like someone was there". when he looked over by my crib he noticed that the wall above me looked like it was breathing.. he called my mother in and she saw it too, without my father telling her about it. i think my mother yelled something like "get out of my house" and it just sort of faded out. i never cried in that room again.

2. when i was 4 or 5 i was over my grandmas house playing in one of those little cars you can ride around in. i went to go say hi to my grandpa who had ms and couldnt move. right after i said hi to him i heard something go "hehehehehe". i know it wasnt my grandpa because 1. it was much louder than he could talk and just didnt sound like him and 2. he was in the middle of a sentence when i heard it. it was really "evil"(?) sounding and freaked me out. i looked over to where it was comming from and there was this black *thing* sitting under my grandmas night table. it was never there before and i just knew something wasnt right. flame me if you want nephtys but i know what i saw heard and felt. so anyway i run out of the room screaming and go tell my dad and grandma. they go back into the room and look where i told them i saw it but they didnt find anything. i went back in with them and nothing was there.

i made a little clay model of what it looked like and ill post the pic later. it was very strange looking and maybe 2 feet long by 6 inches tall and 7-8 inches wide.

Behind the boat house
I grew up in a funeral home. I have millions of ghost stories. But, I'll just say a few better ones:

1. This was more like a "mass hallucination". My family had just sat down to dinner when we all heard a strangers voice say "Hey." We all looked, at the same time, to the end of the table and saw there was a large, black, guy with short dreds sitting at the end of the table. We looked back at each other and he was gone.

2. I don't know what this was, but my old bedroom was upstairs from the actual prep room and there was a door that led to the stairs down to it. Anyways, one night I heard a crash in the prep room. This is a really bad sign, because there should be anyone/thing in there that's alive. So, I took a nail gun and went down there and found one of the dead bodies, face down beside the prep table. Who the fuck knows how that happened, but whatever it was, it wasn't kosher.

3. I was once walking to the bus stop for school and I noticed this woman walk out from behind our house. I figured she was just lost, since we lived on the site of a cemetary and I didn't think anything of it until she seemed to be coming straight for me. I figured it was in my head and I just stopped at the bus stop and pretended not to notice. She came and stood beside me and was staring at me.

I asked her "Can I help you with something? I live in The Gallery." That's what we call the funeral home, but she didn't say anything at first and I continued "I'm just going to school."

"I feel strange." She then said and she sounded wierd, may be almost like she had a bubble in her throat but it was all wierd and echoey. I thought I was hearing things because it was windy or something and I just told her to go in the house, but suddenly it was like she had never been there at all.

4. Also there was a time, when I was about thirteen, when wierd shit started happening, but no one ever saw a ghost. Just things would happen, like my mum stepped on a piece of glass and the glass was like big and it vanished in her hand. I was attacked mysteriously by a bottle of shampoo once. Stuff like that. But, it went away.

Cheers,

Lost

You Look Adopted
 
One time back in England, me and some friends decided to go to an old insane asylum. This insane asylum looked like a very large manor and was secluded and pretty far from actual human activity. So anyway, we got in through one of the windows.

Well inside this asylum they had those large tubs full of water that were used for placing the patient in and shocking them. The water was still in the tub but it was all murky and green. Well we were just looking around the room and we heard a splash. We looked back at the tub and the water was crystal clear and there were all these ripples and waves in the water like something had just moved. Also some of the water was on the floor. Well the clearness of the water only lasted for about a second because it was back to being murky and green. But my friend Kyle said he swore he saw something in the water. So we dared James to stick his hand in the water and fish out whatever it was. Well, he didn't stick his hand in but he used this iron pipe which was very thin to swish the water around.

He pulled up what looked like a straight jacket and from inside the jacket, swam out a VERY large red bellyed snake. It scared the SHIT out of us. So we ran out of that room and we were gonna go back hom because we were freaking out because that place had a spooky vibe.

When we started going back to our window, one of the doors down the hall slammed OPEN, with so much force it echoed through the halls, and we heard static and gurgling, like the kind you hear when you blow bubbles in the bathtub.

So we went home and did some research on it and found out that one method they used for patients was to put a snake into the water with them, and electrocute the patient in hopes of the evil energy flowing out into the snake.

Now you guys, this place was closed down for A WHILE. Like way over 20 years. Why a snake was still in that water, I don't know.

And the gurgling and door slamming was the scariest thing that has ever happened. I'm gonna go cry now since I had to re-live that just now.
