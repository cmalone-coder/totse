Man is a profoundly social being and derives enormous satisfaction from feeling himself at one with the other members of his group. War strengthens all the ties that bind the individual to the group and heightens his sense of group solidarity to the pitch of intoxication.
The barbarian and the unconscious sadist are strong within us. So strong that even in peace time news paper proprietors find it worth there while to devote a large proportion of there space to the description of crimes. In war time, half the mornings paper deal with slaughter and most of the rest consist of incitements to hatred on the one hand and to group solidarity on the other.
The first condition of war is that the population of the planet should be divided into organized groups and that each individual should be conscious of his groups separateness from and superiority to all other groups.
The patriot is allowed to indulge, with good conscious in both vanity and hatred. Vanity in regard to his own group and hatred in regard to all other groups.
