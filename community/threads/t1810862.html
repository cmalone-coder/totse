I have just formed my own religion. It doesn't have many principles, as I greatly detest rules and any person commanding another.

Basically, you follow your conscience and do what feels right. Also, pacifism is preferred.

Self-control in eating, conversations, etc., is also recommended.

There is an afterlife, but I have no clue as to what it is like or who will be there, but it is most likely much the same as this life.

The following are the basic principles of this newly born religion:

"What goes around, comes around." -Essentially, if you are a good person, you will have good fortune; if you are mean and crude, you will have bad luck.

"Live your own life." -Do not tell others what to do, unless asked for help. Do not control people unless they submit to your power. Do not grow over-zealous in your ruling, however humble.



Finally, the dieties...

There aren't any.

Well, there are, but you choose them yourself. I, myself, worship John Lennon, Albert Hoffman, and a variety of my favorite foods and drinks.



That's it! Any followers?
