It depends, really.

There are the good hacks who enhance certain features, without giving you any advantage against opponents. In starcraft, for example, that would be advLoader. It allows you to see your APM (actions per minutes), allows you to see everything the players do when you're observer in a game, autosaves the replays, alerts you when someone is mineral hacking and plenty of the other things.

Then there are the bad ones, intended to allow cheating, such as the obvlion hack for Starcraft. Maphacks, mineral hack, drop hack, etc. And since blizzard won't do shit about it, battle.net is now crowded with cheaters. Most of the times I'll log on, I'm like 99% sure to get at least one game some guy using a hack (from oblivion). I also get often dropped, which is also very frustating.

At least, there are the private servers such as ICCup who offers you to use an anti-hack and tells you whether a player has his anti-hack on or off, allowing you to chose whether or not you want to play against him.
