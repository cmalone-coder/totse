There is a very cool message board I frequently read called abovetopsecret.com. If you have never been or heard of this, I highly suggest you check it out, if you are into conspiracy and that type of thing.

Anyway, I came across a thread, about a mountain on the border of germany and austria called Untersberg. I highly suggest you take 2 minutes and at least read the first post in this thread - http://tinyurl.com/6e8stp . To sum it up as quickly as possible, this mountain has hundreds of tunnels going through it, and into the earth, DEEP (The last mission that went in 2008 went down 1056 meters and had to turn around, so the how deep it goes is still a mystery). Hitler had an obsession with this mountain, even built a house with high quality telescopes, just to observe this mountain. There are HUNDREDS, if not thousands, of stories of people going there and seeing gnomes, dwarfs, fairies, time warps, etc etc. This area also has extreme magnetic anomaly's. (sp?) There are stories of people going in, and coming back days (weeks maybe?) later to find people at there funeral, but had only been in the cave a few hours. Another of three people who in 1987 went into the cave, never to be seen for 3 months, there car still found parked outside. 3 months later there family got a call and they were on a ship in the middle of Alexandria, Egypt, with no recollection of the past 3 months and no idea how they got there (kinda like no recollection of a hardcore DMT trip) Just weird EXTREME time distortion etc.

Does anyone see a link between this and DMT? (Elves, Time distortion, Mystical creatures, Extraterrestrials)

I found this HIGHLY interesting, and thought maybe some of you might like to read about this, and share some thoughts on the subject.

If you are skeptical, please just read through the thread I posted. On that site I believe its usually looked down upon to talk about drugs when talking about paranormal things and such, for obvious reasons. When I first read the thread, DMT is the FIRST thing that came to mind. On the second page, someone else did make that connection also and suggested it might have something to do with magnetic anomaly's doing something with the pineal gland, making you trip, getting time distortions, seeing elves, etc (This is not my theory, but an interesting one none the less, I just found the connection between this and DMT).

Thoughts? Comments? Tin foil hatz? Lulz.

"D.R. Hill and M.A. Persinger at Laurentian University have conducted related research with magnetic fields instead of sound waves. (Remember that electromagnetic “wave-like” phenomena may be much more akin to mechanical waves than some scientists have been willing to admit.) By applying magnetic
plates to the temples of their subjects (they call them “experients,” with blatant disregard to homonymy), they were able to obtain a fascinating set of phenomenological reports. The most curious thing about these testimonies is that they are almost identical to those from clinical studies with DMT (n,n-dimethyltryptamine), a powerful endogenous psychedelic that is secreted from the pineal organ – the crown, the supposed seat of the soul – in psychoactive amounts at birth, death, and periods of incredible stress). The details were too close to be dismissed; visions of hyperdimensional but mundane neighboring realities, washes of intense anxiety, and the feeling or seeing of nonhuman sentient entities were common to both. Their resultant hypothesis was that these fields were actually triggering a release of DMT by the brain by simulating (or inducing) the correlated brain activity.
