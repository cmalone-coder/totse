Re: Immune to Marijuana?
If the bong you made is shit chances are you're not inhaling smoke, just air. Make sure there are no leaks where the stem goes into the bottle and where the bowl meets the stem. Use ductape if you have to.

Make sure you're inhaling too. Inhaling a small breath after taking a hit helps 'force' the smoke into your lungs.

It might also help to smoke w/ somebody who's done it before. That way they can tell you if you're doing something wrong.

Have fun. The first time is always a memorable one.
