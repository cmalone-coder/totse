IODINE PRODUCTION
Extract all the soluble part of kelp by water, and crystallize the soda by evaporation; to the mother liquor add sulphuric acid in excess, and boil the liquid , then strain it to separate some sulphur, and mix the filtered liquor with as much manganese as there was sulphuric acid: on applying heat, the iodine sublimes in the form of greyish-black scales, with a metallic luster. The boiling is done in a glas still with a very short still head, and connected with two or three globular glass recivers. Care must be taken to watch the process, and prevent the neck of the still becoming choked with condensed iodine.
