I'm agnostic, and one of the main reasons why I come to this forum is to gain wisdom. I do not consider my beliefs to be a religion, because a religion is a set of personal or institutionalized attitudes, beliefs, and practices relating to or manifesting faithful devotion to an acknowledged ultimate reality or deity. My set of attitudes, beliefs, and practices, while personal, are in no way institutionalized nor do they manifest any sort of faith or devotion to a deity or "ultimate reality". I hold personal value, by my own terms, in honesty, integrity, justice, compassion, and improvement in myself and everything around me. I have no intention of coverting people, only in familiarizing myself with as many different view points as possible, and hopefully in the process others will learn to respect other view points as well.

Using my own judgement, I have personally chosen for myself to follow certain moral guidelines, such as spreading kindness, not murdering or stealing, being generous to others, understanding other view points, etc., many of which have been held in high regard at one point or another in the Bible.
