Re: I'm travelling to one of three places, give me your opinion
Been to Rome and Venice, got a cousin who works in Japan.

Strike Venice off there because you'll be bored shitless after two days.

Rome's ok. A lot to see and do so long as you like your history and art.

However if you're prepared to take some culture shock I'd definately choose Japan.

I really wouldn't recommend a tour group. I went on a tour once and it was ok but just so rushed. You'll be sharing the tour with a bunch of people you probably wont like all with conflicting interests. About the only thing I liked about the tour was the ease with which they organised travel, accomodation and visas. Since you're going to one destination though it's really not that hard to organise yourself and plan an itinerary based on what you like.
