Mellow_Fellow

Re: How much are shrooms?
Shrooms vary between free, and very expensive. There is no set price. It depends who you're buying them from...

In Canada, it seems people do regularly buy them though, so what, you're looking at splashing out like up to 30bucks for 1/8? Personally, for your first trip I wouldn't take a whole 3.5g of cubensis, BUT i would take more than 1/2 that. Around 2.2-2.5g is a good amount to take, if they're average potency, and do it with someone you know well and can trust.

Don't trip with big groups of people, especially not first time - get outside AWAY from the hectic bullshit of every day life and shrooms are way more rewarding...

Don't smoke weed to calm yourself down on shrooms. It doesn't work. It intensifies the trip, and is unpredictable. Weed affects different people in many different ways... personally, I don't like smoking weed when tripping at all, I think it clouds the trip and makes it confusing and more negative.

In the end, you'll find stuff out yourself really. No, you wont see monsters eating orange juice turning into lamp-posts or whatever.... but you can get some very strong visuals at higher doses. I've taken up to 7g of shrooms before, and it's..... not where the "average" person needs to be. Anything about 4.5g is to me, a very strong trip, and you should be damn careful you're in the right mindset etc.

Be careful and use common sense basically, especially if picking them in the wild, you MUST know what you're doing...

As for buying them online, sure it's possible, and theoretically I may have done so before and had some very interesting times. I would not do this again, however. Enough is enough with shrooms. They can be pretty damn cheap if you buy them online though....
