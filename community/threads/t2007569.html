hmm, your girlfriend will probably take that as a crush unless you are very specific about it being for the family.
a lot of hospitals don't like flowers on the wards; risk of infection and all that. and if he's banged up or has any bad wounds, he'll want to stay away from things like flowers (my mum received flowers after an operation but was told to keep them out of her room for at least three weeks).

something like a fruit basket or gourment basket could be nicer and your girl would probably be less bothered by it. send it to the house though. go to the florist, ask if they do fruit/gourmet baskets, or if they'll gift wrap food you bring in and deliver it.
it's an increasinly popular gesture, and lots of bigger florists in the uk are becoming more accpeting of it(especially if you life in a very "ethnic" area).
