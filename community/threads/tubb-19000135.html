Non lethal weapons though, I think for use in self defense, are a joke. The only thing that I've read about really working well and always turning a bad guy away is pepper foam. And lastly as one more benefit most contain a UV dye so if the person does evade police, they can later pick him up and look for the dye that is not visible without a UV light source, the dye will stay on for days depending on how often your assailant bathes or sweats.

While I'm on it, I highly do not recommend using less than lethal ammo. Unless you are fully trained in it's use, are a great shot, and KNOW that you are using it at all times. Bean Bag rounds, Zytel Buck shot, Wooden bullets, rubber bullets, etc can all kill at close ranges and if impacted in certain spots. If you forget you have lethal ammo in you gun and use it as non lethal be prepared for one heck of a legal battle. If you mistakenly have less than lethal ammo in you gun rather than slugs, you may lose the fight if defending against a crack head or what have you.
