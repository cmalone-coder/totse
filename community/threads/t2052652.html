Organising my life
Well recently I have been tossing up the idea of a 'change' or a 'break' to my regular day to day life. I feel as if I am stuck in a rut, and if I continue going the way I am, I may end up failing university, distancing myself from my family...etc. I do like my course and my family so don't get me wrong in that respect. It's hard for me to explain why I feel I need this break, I just know that I do (trust me on that).

A little about me - I go to university in Melbourne, Australia (RMIT) and study IT, this is my first year. I have a girlfriend of 3.5 years who I am totally in love with. I live at home with my parents and always have.

On the top of my life changing list was to travel. A working holiday (probably in Britain) feels almost exactly what I need/want. It will give me a break have my normal life and I will feel like I have achieved something. My plan was to go for the first semester of next year.

Now the only problem with this plan (and the biggest) is my girlfriend. In my head I had imagined her loving the idea to come with me when I made the proposition, however this is far from what happened. She is too comfortable in her life and I can't force her to come with me. Now this screws up my entire plans as I don't think there is any way I could go for 6 months without her (ohh soppy i know). Everyone has told me the whole "she will be here when you get back", though I don't doubt that. I feel that if I went alone, my whole trip would be ruined by the effect her absence will have on me.

Of course she is not selfish and is urging me to go, she does not want to hold me back. I am in quite a dilemma. I have considered other options as a break but none really stand out for me except travel.

So ladies and gentlemen of S&A, I now ask for your thoughts, suggestions and guidance. What should I do when I am unhappy as I am, but the only way to change it will likely result in me being unhappy as well?
