Amsterdam weed was just as expected, fire beyond belief, but other thc products were not up to expectations. The weed was grown very well, almost always in soil with organic nutes. Best tasting and smelling weed was found here.

Cali blew my mind, Hash, kief, oil and eatibles... so much to pick from so little time... even street weed dealers had few things on the menu. Strain wise there were usually 4-7 strains to pick from, and it was rather expensive, but for same quality as BC weed, cost was ~45$ per 3.5g not too bad.
