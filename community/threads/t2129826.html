Re: Fishing tips?
Well I fish 6 days of the week, and I could give you some help if you gave me a general area of where you are. In Michigan the trout streams are slowly getting less and less productive this time of year; and I have never been a catfish angler.

But if you are after trout are you fishing streams and rivers? Or are you fishing out on a lake/large body of water? Are you using fly fishing gear or just spin-casting gear with a light(4 to 6lbs) monofilament leader? If you use more then 8 pound test line you aren't going to catch many trout(except for lake trout in deep reservoirs), they have excellent eyesight, and can see a swivel and large test line. You can always use a slip bobber with light test line, and on the terminal end just tie a fly fishing lure(if you want it to sink use a wet fly, if you want it stay on the surface use a dry fly).

Also if you wish for more information e-mail me at alwaysasleep3@gmail.com

I am always there for someone in need of fishing help or information.

When going after cats , I have always had MUCH better luck using a sliding sinker, like a bullet or egg sinker. Use that above a swivel and a 20 inch or so leader and your chicken livers or nightcrawlers.
In rivers, I also like to float a stick bobber above a large leader. Enough to float the bait right above the bottom.
The first fish of any decent size that I caught was a 13 pound channel and I was using a 4ft rod. I fell in the river landing it lol.

Re: Fishing tips?
I don't fish for trout but can give you some tips for cat fishing.

Take a coffee can and punch holes in it. throw in anything pretty much. Dog food, chicken liver and blood, dead bait fish, etc. The day before you plan to fish, go to your spot and tie it off and throw it in to chum the water. While you are there, take some crappie nibbles, small pieces of worms, whatever and catch the local small bait fish. Leave them on the bank over night to get them nice and nasty for the next day's fishing.

When you come back, catch a few more bait fish. Then start mixing it up. Try them live, then try them alive but slit their stomachs open, then try cut bat and the ones you left out the day before. Wrap your chicken liver in pieces of panty hose and dip it in the blood. It will help keep your bait on when you cast, and keep smaller fish from stripping you hook.

Take at least two poles for yourself. I prefer taking at least three and an ultra light for bait fishing. Put One on float and one on bottom. Test how they are biting.

As far as what time of the day. It's fishing, everyone has their preferred method and you will hear about it, constently, over and over. Dusk is the best time, no it's dawn, day time on float, no no night time on bottom. Personally, I have hit them at all times with different methods. Just keep testing different set ups and different baits and different depths. I have gone all day without so much as a nibble and then I have literally cast out and the float went under and never came up, put the hook right in the cat's mouth.

Have fun for me man, haven't been out fishing yet this year. Oh and pics if you land a good one.

Re: Fishing tips?
Yeah check your local fishing laws because chumming isn't legal everywhere. I used chum a few times and got caught, but I knew the local CO and got out of the ticket. It is a useful tactic for catching cats though.

Catfish have the uncanny ability to tell if the bait isn't from the local waters, so be sure to catch your bait where you are planning on fishing. Catch a few panfish and cut them up, use what you would eat for bait on the hook(s), and the rest for chum if legal in your area.
