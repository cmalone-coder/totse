It was rainy, foggy, and dark in the middle of the woods with two others tripping with me and one sober. I was trying to be a badass and snorted 25mg of t7 while the other two did 20mgs. Not the brightest idea in the world either. I started to vomit profusely like I always do after snorting that shit and the vertigo and visuals hit me like a speeding train. I'm bent over holding my legs and I felt like I'm right side up spinning around and around. I mumble that I can't do this in front of them and the sober guy walked them back to one of the tripper's places. This left me alone in the foggy dark on my first dose of 25mgs and my first dose outside.

I get ahold of the trip eventually and start trying to walk down the nature path back to my house. Then, I got hit with a head trip like no other. I became the fucking fog and watched my stupid drugged up ass hobble down the path. It went to a blinding light and a fucking demon appeared calling me into his void of nothing. I whited out, but kept on my feet for an unknown amount of time. I see the demon standing in front of his portal of brightness and then faded into the darkness and mist. I have no idea what happened in that time frame.

I get back to dragging myself to the concrete bridge which turned into a fucking tree root and the sidewalk going up hill became the trunk. It was like one bizzare treehouse. I then saw this little creature with a leaf on its head. It kind of looked like a pokemon in truth. The closer I got the more I could tell it wasn't real, but more of a digital esque out line of a natural thing plastered onto a man-made base.

I finally got to the top and was so happy to be almost home. I got up into the housing and my vision just shattered. I mean it was like someone took a hammer to my glasses. It rearranged the shattered pieces and it took me forever to figure out what was what and which way I needed to go. I must of looked like a fucking idiot with my head tilted towards the sky and to the left, but it was the only way I could attempt to see where I was headed.

Now my house is in my line of sight after what feels like an eternity, I run to it like I was returning home from war or something else that could scared me out of my mind. I get to the front door and get my key out. This was hell in a whole new fashion. I played with the key and trying to fit it into the lock. It took me a while, but I eventually got it. I turned the key and the door wouldn't budge. I turn it the other way to make sure I didn't fuck up and it still wouldn't budge. Okay, maybe the dead bolt was locked, I thought. Well, at that point in time I tried to pull my key out and unlock the dead bolt, but I couldn't get my key out.

From there on out I didn't say a word, but it was like spouses fighting one another on what they should do. I kept turning my key back and forth, giving up, walking away, and then coming back to repeat the cycle for about an hour. I finally got the fucker out to my relief, but I was too afraid to attempt it again. So I sat on the bench out side my house until my sister left for school that morning to her surprise.

All that time on the porch, I down talked myself into quitting drug and becoming a "decent" person. I went all whiney bitch and spewed my guts and secrets to my mother. Got yelled at, waited until everyone left, and smoked some weed laughing at my stupidity.

That was more than I meant to write... fucking insomnia...

TL;DR ~ 2c-t-7 is one mother fucking hell of a drug that should not be toyed with or snorted for that matter because it burns like no other.
