You can't really change who you are without a lot of work.

You need to:
* Work out what it is you are lacking exactly,
* Work out what your current strengths are,
* and work out what it is you need to improve.

Then, go out and work out ways to improve what you are missing.

If you are shy, get a job that forces you to talk to people and try to sell them something. If your group of friends are a weakness, get a new group. For every perceived weakness, there must be someone or something you are comparing yourself to to know you are lacking.

In my case, I was very shy when I was 17/18. I worked at a junk store at the time. I started being the spruiker out on the street trying to get people into the store. From there, I started DJ'ing parties, then weddings. Then I started MC'ing weddings. It took about 4 years or so, but now I can talk to anyone, anywhere about whatever I like.

In your case, you think you need "game". What I think you lneed is to lose your shyness, and not be afraid to make a bit of a fool of yourself from time to time. You can read all sorts of books and internet sites, but in the end, they all basically say the same thing - go out and talk to women. Lose your fear, and you'll be 10 times better. Once you've lost your fear, then you can work on your sense of humour, your sexyness, and any other appealing traits you need.
