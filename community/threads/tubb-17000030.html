Re: Tomb Raider
I beat all the weapons, there's a certain way to do it that makes it very easy.
Ruby:
First you have to get mime materia, you should get at least 2 of them, but three is optimal. Then you should get W-Summon, with Hades and Knights of the Round. When you start the fight, 2 of your guys should be dead, otherwise he'll kill two randomly before sticking his hands into the ground, which is the only time you can hurt him. The fastest way to revive them is with Phoenix. Hades will keep him paralyzed, and Knights of the Round is just the most expedient way to do it. You need to breed a golden Chocobo to get mime or Knights of the Round, and W-Summon is in the battle arena on the Golden Saucer.
Emerald:
First you may want to get the Underwater materia, to get rid of the counter, although if you do it right, you should be able to beat him well before the counter runs down. Emerald is much more simple. Again, you need Knights of the Round, at least two stars, and at least 2 mimes, but this time you should have Phoenix linked with final attack materia, so when he does Air Tam Storm, which deals 9999 to all your party members, they all get brought back to life. Thats why
you need KotR on 2 stars, so that after doing that you can go back to miming it. If you do it right, he should only get Air Tam Storm in once, and then die soon after.

Oh, and you should have enough HP Plus materia so that all of your characters have 9999 life. And dont use ultimate weapons, cause youre not attacking them, and they give a lot of experience to materia.
