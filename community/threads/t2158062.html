Suboxone, or Buprenorphine for that matter, is only 10% bioavailable when swallowed orally (Because of a VERY HIGH first pass metabolic reaction), instead of the higher 55-70% bioavailability you would have gotten by sublingual or insufflated administration. But with Suboxone, less is more, as in, 2mg would get you feeling even better than say 8mg even with a tolerance; unless one has steady plasma levels of the drug in their blood already, i.e. on Suboxone Maintenance Therapy.

So to answer your question, as said above, YES you did reduce it's effectiveness 5-7 fold, but you will still get something out of it, just not nearly as much.

Next time either try 2mg insufflated (Snorted) or 4mg under the tongue for a great long-lasting high. It's a hate it or love it feeling though, so be fore-warned!

Take care and be safe!

PS: If your body is physically dependent on opiates currently, then taking Suboxone will induce precipitated withdrawals (Full blown withdrawals) and taking Suboxone with any other opiate is just asking for an awful time and it's a waste. Suboxone will compete for the mu receptors and win just about every time (Blocking the high if taken before another opiate/oid) and if taken after, will literally rip out and partially replace the receptor sites once occupied by the "contin" so you are lucky you just swallowed it in one way. Just save an 8mg and split it into 4 equal pieces for a couple rainy/dry days.
