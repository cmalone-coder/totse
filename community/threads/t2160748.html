In the name of Science...
I've decided to smoke .3g of stems in a sobe waterfall bong. The stems are about 1-9 months old, and were found between my matress and the wall (I have a little shelf that I often throw stems on, and they eventually fall down into the crack).

Having no coffee grinder and finding scissors useless for the task, I used an old fashioned blender to grind up the stems. It did a decent job.

http://i35.tinypic.com/6xum2g.jpg

I loaded a bowl, and let the chamber fill with, thick, dark yellow smoke. I suppose having the stems out in the open must have dried them out.

I managed to inhale most of the smoke before I gagged, and a nasty, acrid taste filled me mouth. I quickly inhaled the rest, held the smoke for 5 seconds and exhaled, once again gagging on the taste. Before I could finish exhaling, a thick stream of vomit forced its way past the smoke and out of my throat.

Overall I got a slight buzz, not even comparable to one or two decent tokes off a dank joint which lasted around 30 minutes.

Overall: Failure.

Re: In the name of Science...
this is what i did with my surplus of stems which was by far less than what you have.


I took all of them and cut them into tiny pieces with scissors, then i placed all of them into a shot glass and then i filled it 2/3 with rubbing alcohol. then i let it sit for a while(a couple hours) then i took the back of a small screw driver and i basically smashed the stems in the alcohol solution repeatedly for about 15 minutes. then it turned dark green/brown. then i drained it onto a plate and i strained the stems as best i could until every last drop of alcohol was on the plate. I then set the plate out in the 90 degree july sunlight and two hours later it had completely evaporated leaving a hash like substance, i put it into a ball and smoked it.


i got high, no headache, and it was cool. but i did not get blazed...more like mellow. i did still get the munchies and i am(was) a heavy smoker. try it. you won't be disappointed.
