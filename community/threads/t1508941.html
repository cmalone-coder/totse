Re: I got the perfect girl and I'm bummy
Obviously there's something about you that turns her on.

Don't try and figure out what it is! Because:

1) If you let yourself adopt that mentality, what you're really doing is giving in to self doubt. This is counterproductive! It cannot end with anything positive.

2) If you start to rationally objectify the things about you that she likes, let's say you did figure it out, what has it achieved? Two things. One, you'll have solved a mystery. And two, you'll now be self-conscious about the very things that she likes about you. If this happens, those qualities will cease to be spontaneous and genuine (if you doubt yourself, see #1), and you might end up destroying the very reason why she likes you in the first place. Is that worth the satisfaction of solving a pointless mystery?

You want to keep her? You're going to have to train your mind to work differently. This is in fact easier than you'd think, it just takes dedication. So depends how much you want to keep her.

It's very simple: the best way to change your mentality is to pretend you're already the way you want to be. In the beginning you'll feel silly, many people just give up because it seems pointless. But this works - the longer you keep pretending you are the way you want to be, the easier it gets to pretend. And the better you get at pretending, the more like that you become in reality, until eventually you are genuinely that way and no longer have to pretend.

EVERYTHING is in your mind.

Now what you've got to make an effort at pretending is this: being secure, the exact opposite of clingy.

Imagine you've always had girls like her in the past. This doesn't mean being arrogant, in fact you're trying to REMOVE things from your mentality instead of ADDING anything fake - confidence is about not doubting yourself, it's not about possessing delusional self belief.

If you really want this bad enough, spend a little time every day imagining how your life might have gone if some details were different, specifically your luck with girls. Every day imagine up a little movie in your head about an imaginary relationship with some stunning girl who was madly in love with you. Imagine little "scenes" about how she might have acted, and you might have acted.

Do that for a few days and you'll start to get an idea of the kind of person you COULD have become if life had been different. The only reason you AREN'T that person is purely down to fortune and bad choices you made, nothing more. Therefore you contain within you the potential to be that person, it's obvious when you think about it.

So try pretending you actually are that version of you. You're NOT trying to pretend you're someone else, you're trying to pretend you're the best possible version of yourself. The imagined scenarios from a fictional past are just stage props to help you adjust mentally.

This shit works. I recommend giving it a try if you really care about her.

People rarely have a problem that is so fundamentally engrained in their mind that it can't be changed through willpower and intelligent methods. That's the fucking truth.
