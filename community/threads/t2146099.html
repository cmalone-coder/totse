Its easy to make tea the easiest way to make a good brew is to take your pods and crush them to a powder and put them in a pot or jar. Now boil enough water to cover the pods plus half (make sure it fits in container with pods) Now add boiling water to pods and let steep for 30-45 min. Strain and drink. A second brew can be done for any extras but not really needed. I have some pods comming my way too I cant wait.
