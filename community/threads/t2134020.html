Is this one of the most universally unattractive traits?
Okay, as we all know, there is a dizzying array of things certain types of men and women find attractive, particularly in regard to personality and social status. However, no matter what socioeconomic group, race, religion, creed, etc. someone belongs to it seems there is one trait everyone finds attractive.

That is, they want a potential partner to be demonstratively attractive to others in a romantic way. In short, people always love to feel that their partner has options, and is thus showing discrimination through being with themselves, and not going out with any of those others.

I also thought of what might happen if a person who had no romantic options managed to go out with someone with many options. It seems the person with no options would be essentially "powerless" in the relationship. If their partner left them today, they would have literally nowhere else to turn to. This seems to be an extremely unattractive trait, at the very least it is subconsciously unattractive. I bring this up as I am almost certain this happened to me once when I was younger, and it was much more difficult to get over the relationship because of my complete lack of other romantic companionship at the time. A friend of mine and I discussed this and he agreed it is vitally important, and he often likes to keep low-key dominance in a relationship by subtly reminding his girl she could be replaced at a moment's notice.

I would just like to know your thoughts on this matter Totse, and comment on whether you have ever consciously thought about this before asking someone out or evaluating their or your own dating potential.
