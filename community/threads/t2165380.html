Zenwalk is a slackware derivative and will install *by default* under 2GG.
Zenwalk is simple, won't get in your way, no duplicated features, no unneeded shit that runs in the background, fast and eye-candy out of the box.

Here is the very brief manual:
http://manual.zenwalk.org/en/

You can download it here:
http://www.zenwalk.org/modules/tinyc...index.php?id=1
(You can use google for third party mirrors)

You will need to burn it using the *image/iso* option, at max 4x (speed).

EDIT: And of course Abiword is installed by default.

I stopped checking this forum for new threads on my 'high priority' totse forums list. 'nazi mods' being the reason.

If you literally _only_ want word processing, then build a gentoo system with only the kernel, various needed libraries, and openoffice (with minimal use flags). You can get even less if you use TeX and EMACS. Use this howto for how to do it. However, you don't need to use busybox, and you should NOT use uclibc.

Once you have that, make an image of the root FS and dd it onto the EEE in BackTrack. Configure the bootloader, and you're in business.
