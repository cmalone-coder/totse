Re: Left 4 Dead: X-Box 360
Dead Rising is a great game. The amount of zombies on screen is impressive. The graphics are great, especially considering all of the items and characters on the screen. The variety in weapons. The unlockables...the game is just awesome. I love how there is an actual reason to go for the hard achievements, and how there are like 4 different endings depending on how you play.

Its just a fantastic game that a lot of people didn't like because of the save system. Personally, I never felt there was a lack of save spots or that there needed to be anymore. Once you learn the layout of the mall, you know where the save spots are, and usually they're not far from the hard boss fights. I felt that the save system actually added some challenge to the game as well. Quicksaving has always been a fault for games IMO, because I end up saving every three steps and it kind of takes the fun away.

Speaking of saves, and this might be going off on a tanget, but I think the GTA series starting with 3 has the best dying/saving system. Personally, I'm not a huge fan of the game over screen, but sometimes it can add challenge (Dead Rising, Mario). I like in GTA that you respawn at a hospital or cop station, and you can keep playing without having to reload. Sure, you lose some money or guns, but its not the end of the world. I really hate having to reload my game every time I die. It breaks the flow.
