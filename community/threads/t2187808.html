Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Easy Napalm experiment
Take a quart of gasoline and pour into a large decanter. Dip in pieces of styrofoam, which should dissolve quickly, until the mixture takes on the consistency of jelly. There you have it, your very own napalm! It burns for a very long time! Try it on wood, glass, metal, and stone. It is almost as good as thermite for melting through or simply disintegrating objects.
