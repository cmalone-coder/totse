Take a quart of gasoline and pour into a large decanter. Dip in pieces of styrofoam, which should dissolve quickly, until the mixture takes on the consistency of jelly. There you have it, your very own napalm! It burns for a very long time! Try it on wood, glass, metal, and stone. It is almost as good as thermite for melting through or simply disintegrating objects.
