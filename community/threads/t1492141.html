Three days later she breaks up with me.

Three hours later she tracks me down and asks me to take her back, that she realized that she didn't want anyone but me blah blah blah and that Andy had helped her ocme to this conclusion. Against my better judgement, I took her back. Two weeks later I find out that she had been "seeing" Andy for a while. I tell her that she can't see him any more, which I don't think was too unreasonable, although I should have just ended it then and there. She talks to him about it blah blah blah and stops seeing him, whatever.

The relationship goes South, of course. She says that she can't trust me because I got stoned with my drummer, who is a girl, and hung out with her in my father's car in the middle of the night (which doesn't look too good, I know) where we just sat around being cold and stoned. She of course needs more attention from me to get over Andy but I had grown distant from her (I wonder why...dur), but didn't quite know why. I wanted to be close to her and love her. Only after months did I figure out what the hell was really going on.

Wellp, after all of that bullshit, my question is: did she cheat on me?

I know that it may sound like a stupid question to ask, but after 6 months I am still not sure about it. It feels like she did, but I just don't know.
