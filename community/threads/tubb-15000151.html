Re: Zen
An excellent book about Zen is called "Zen Speaks", under $15 at Barnes & Noble. You'll know it when you see it.. it looks like a bunch of comic strips. The book's designed for people who don't know much about it; with the tales are basic explanations of what they mean. The reason I say it's aimed towards people who don't know much about Zen is because the tales and koans (Zen riddles) related in the book teach more if the reader draws their own conclusions, but someone new to Zen can easily get frustrated if they read a bunch of things that don't make sense. The explanations serve as a pointer in the right direction.

You guys are right- Zen is all about living your everyday life. But how many people actually take joy in that life? When the average person is hungry, they rarely eat; they consume food while pondering the other goings-on in their lives. It's not often that they sincerely enjoy eating; the problems of what has been, and what's to come, instead of the reality of the present, take precedence. And how many people are happy that way?

Suz, as much as Zen isn't found in words, I would be super happy to talk about it with ya.. it's like a key to life for me, but it's also the one thing that no one else I know has really gotten into (which has helped in some ways).. it is head fuck kinda stuff till you start to understand it more. A lot of it makes sense intellectually at first, and you'll be able to feel the truth in some things right off, but the process of learning in Zen never stops.
