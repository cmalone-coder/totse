Your stomach has a mucus lining to protect it from 'disgestion' (gastric acid ranges Ph1-3); however, your throat does not have any kind of defence against acid (and is why you can experience 'Heartburn' when a little stomach acid manages to 'escape' the stomach) so, I am sure you'd experience a painful burning of the throat that just would not be pleasant at all if HCl was drunk.
