Re: Is she playing mind games?
Psych: long distance relationships DO work... they really do.

It's just that the actual transition between a long-distance relationship to a live-in or local relationship is very very brutal.

You're just not expecting it. All you can think about is how you can't wait to be together, you're so busy thinking about how great it's gonna be that you don't prepare yourself for how difficult it really is to make that kind of relationship change.

With long distance relationships, as much as it feels as though your partner is a part of your life, they really aren't. They take up no more time than say, a hobby. You know them, but you don't know the 24/7/365 them. They're reachable by phone, internet, the mail... but as much as they are there in your life, they aren't really there. They're not there when you wake up, when you go to sleep, at almost every meal. You don't stumble over their shoes on your way to the toilet. Their nasty little habits don't effect you in the slightest bit. It isn't to say that all of these things are easily adjusted to over a period of time, but if you're not ready for it then you are fucked. If you're rushing into it full steam ahead then you're going to end up in a big wreck, broken hearts all around.

When you make the move from the convenience of a long distance relationship to the three-ring circus that is a 'normal' relationship it's just... it's overwhelming.

Normal relationships have the upper hand because they become accustomed to each other over a much longer period of time. Long-distance relationships develop very well emotionally, but there is a very small basis of reality. You tend to use your imagination alot. You wonder "What would it be like if I were there right now" and you imagine it, but you don't imagine shit like "What if I wanted to play on my PC for 5 hours and she got bitchy and ended up walking out and didn't come home for 2 hours"... you just don't imagine that kind of thing, but it happens to everyone.

This guy just isn't prepping himself very well, and I can't completely blame him because I didn't prepare myself either.

It never dawned on me that it would be just as difficult getting used to sharing a life with someone I had known long-distance for a few years, than it would be getting used to sharing my life with a perfect stranger, but it was! Boy was it...
