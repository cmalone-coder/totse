How well would this synth work?
Simply, if SWIM were to follow this hacked

together synth, how well would the end re-

sult be?

Thanks.

-----------------------

To a 500ml flask add in the following order:

50 ml of D-H20

5 g of Red Phosphorus

11 g of Iodine

Allow the mixture to react for 20 mins, add light heat if needed.

Add 2.7 g of Ephedrine

Fit flask with a reflux condenser.

Fit the reflux condenser with a length of tubing, and attach a balloon to the end of the tubing.

Reflux for 30 hours, then remove from heat.

Let sit for 12 hours, Dilute with twice the

volume of D-H20.

Workup as normal.

-----------------------

Re: How well would this synth work?
That depends on how long you are willing to go. Some do, some don't. Swigotta is cautious, so he holds back about 20% until the last I2 goes in. If NSleepy is correct, you will still achieve adequate HI% even if you add all of the water.

Your goal is to get enough DH2O in the reaction to sustain the loop, while making sure you don't add too much to bog it down and require the blast furnace of &gt;100C. From a country boys point of view, "Ya gotta fanagle it a little at first til ya git the feel fer it."

Bottom line, hold of on the last 20% DH2O until 2/3 of the chosen time has expired and add it when you add the last of the I2. Then, document your observations and take it from there.

As I said before, everybody's technique is different and subtleties make a difference in the outcome. Little things that some take for granted are obvious to everyone, many times are not.
