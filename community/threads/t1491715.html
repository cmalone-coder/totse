Girls (all girls here) DON'T treat a vaginal discharge, itchy painful vagina or fever with cloudy urine with "natural" treatments such as cranberry juice, unless your doc gives you the go ahead - which they most likely WON'T. They might tell you to take cranberry juice IN ADDITION TO some antibiotic.

Treat your vagina like the Queen of the World, cuz it's the only one you've got
