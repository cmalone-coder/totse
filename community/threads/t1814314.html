Come on guys, yes satanism is just a dressed up atheist/secular humanest philosophy, but that dressing is of great psychological importance. We choose the symbology of "Satan" because we were raised Xian and want to reject that. And to intellectually reject something in favor of another philosophy is one thing, but to fully accept that on all levels is quite a different thing. So we commit acts of "blasphemy" in order to gain full psychological acceptance of the ramifications of rejection. If you can spit on a cross, have sex while eating communion wine and wafers, and "worship" a golden cow, that shows that you've gained some level of freedom from the ideology that was ingrained into you throughout your childhood.

This is why the modern black mass can be about any subject, a rejection of any socially imposed standards, rather than just the Catholic Mass.

So modern Satanism is "just" an dressed up atheist/secular humanest philosophy, but the tools associated with that dressing have been quite helpful in many people's personal development.
