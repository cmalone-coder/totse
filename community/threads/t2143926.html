My Trip to Rothbury [Pics!] [LONG]
I know I've been back for like 3 weeks but I still haven't made the thread and I have nothing to do. So this is the story of the best week I have ever had.


So, we started driving from New York at like 4pm and made it Rothbury Michigan at like 5am an passed out. We had some fun along the way, we got window chalk and wrote on my car "ROTHBURY!" and my friend's cell number. A few people that were headed there called us, and one girl wanted phone sex... Some girl randomly flashed us her tits too (unrelated to the phone sex bitch). So we slept in a Walmart parking lot and woke up 8am and drove to the actual festival.

Upon arriving, we got our spot in the field situated, met our neighbors, and smoked a joint. After I finished setting up my tent, some guy comes over and wanted to sell me acid. It was awesome because the kind he had were 'Jerry Faces' which was Jerry Garcia's face like all tripped out with the faded color and him looking at you. I think it sold pretty good because Phil Lesh was playing and I saw around 40,000 Dead Heads

On the way back from Dave Matthews I made a pretty good gank. Usually the Vendor stands are like closed down at night and you can get in them and steal soda.
