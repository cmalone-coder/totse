The sipping chicken contraption whatever... wouldn't work forever because of air friction and eventual evaporation.


The sipping chicken thing works because the alcohol in the bulb is kept at such a low presure that it boils at room temperature. The vapor pressure forces the fluid from the bottom bulb to the top bulb. When it becomes overbalanced it tips over. This allows the fluid collected in the top a return path to the bottom bulb. At this point the vapor pressure in the top bulb is the same as the bottom bulb. The evaporation of the water on the 'beak' provides the cooling for the vapor in the top bulb to condense, providing enough of an inbalance for the fluid to be pushed up to the top bulb.

Esentialy, the energy of evaporation is converted to mechanical energy. This is impractical on a large scale, due to the fact that if the tube is too long, the presure imbalance is too much for the vapor pressure to overcome, and that much evaporation can only occour so rapidly on such a small object.
