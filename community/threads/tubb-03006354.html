I have a theory that Deja Vu is a situation where the mind's perception of time "skips" like a scratched record, and what happens in the future is recorded (faintly) in the memory. Then, when the end of the skipped period is reached, the actual event is recorded again in the memory, producing the response "Wait a minute, didn't that just happen before?" or a variant of that response, sometimes with colorful obscenities.

Myself, sometimes I'll think about a particular situation, event, person or object, and it will become a part of my immediate future, usually within a day or two. The problem is my mind is constantly active, producing myriads of different trains of thought all at once. (I have some of the symptoms of ADD, Bi-Polar, Paranoid Schizophrenia, and High Grade Autism all at once, which makes it very hard for me to meditate using conventional means, namely the acheivement of clarity in thought) Ergo, it's very difficult for me to tell when something I'm thinking about a lot is just in my head or lies somewhere (somewhen?) in my immediate future.

I also have a theory that sixth and higher senses and paranormal talents are present in people with uncommon and abnormal neural connection patterns in the frontal lobes of the brain. By stimulating the development of additional connections, through mediation, guided thought imagery, certain drugs, electro-magnetic stimulus to the brain tissue, or anything else that stimulates the creation of new neuron connections, these abilities might be developed and strengthened, just like doing physical exercises to develop muscle mass and tone, endurance, and the like.
