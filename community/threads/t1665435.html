Soda Can Stash
ok... for anyone who wants to make a soda stash can here it is. I thought of this idea when i saw an ad for one. Take a full can and carefully cut off the silver top. Iused a thin hack-saw but a razor blade would be better. Next, after i dried the can out... I epoxied the lid of a plastic spice jar to the underside of the can lid that we just cut off. Next, i filled the bottom of the can with wax. With the wax still wet, i screwed the spice bottle together and set it in the wax. At this point it appears as a normal soda can. (put a book ontop so the jar doest float causing the lid to rise. When the wax dried i unscrewd the lid and filled the can with water around the spice jar; ontop of that i poured more hot wax. Drill a small hole in this wax and drain out some water so it sloshes around. Silicone sealent hels keep this from leaking. There you have it good luck.
