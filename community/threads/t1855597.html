HI is one of the most powerful acids known to man. In fact, it is so strong that it has been deemed "No Commercial Use".

It will eat through stainless steel. SWIL just takes a small pocket knife, and sticks that into the rxn. He allows some HI to get on the blade, and then watches to see how much the blade smokes. The more, the better, usually.
