Re: I think Im crazy
it's normal to be a bit sketched if you really like some... mostly because it's normal to be insecure.

Part of the cheating thing is probably that you really like her and when people are very interested in someone they tend to think that they aren't worthy of that person's love. When things are working out well, it makes you feel amazing, and you think about how good that is when you're not around them and you hope that they aren't doing something to hurt you. Kind of a... too good to be true mentality, I suppose.

In addition to that, it's obvious that you have some sense of abandonment/ some trust issues. Could be from relationships with parents or friends or romantically in the past, but either way a little bit of these feelings is perfectly normal.

People lose people and break up and move away for whatever reason, and you can't always control the outcome. Some people cheat, it's true, but you must learn to recognize your jealous or paranoid feelings and calm yourself down. Don't give in to musings about what she could possibly be doing because ultimately you will know. Cheaters don't get away with it for very long. Most people in general are bad liars :p So take some time to breath and think about the good times that you have together. The only time that someone was dishonest to me ( we were in an open relationship but I told him I needed to know everything so I didn't think he was hiding from me, and he couldn't handle being truthful about his exploits for fear that he would lose me) I kept texts and messages from him to remind me that it was obvious he cared. Even at the times that I was really afraid that it all had been for naught, I read the things he had written about his feelings an I knew they were genuine. In the situation, some people would have been too overpowered by jealousy to see the real picture: People sometimes cheat, but that doesn't mean that everyone is vulnerable... think about your girls loyalty to you. Even if some guy kissed her or something... don't you think she would be honest with you and talk to you about the problem? Don't you think that people know she has someone and respect that? Guys tend to give other guys very little credit with that, but I know from a girls perspective that it's not so easy to cheat as just picking a guy out of a crowd and doing something wrong. Even though I personally don't like monogamous relationships because I kiss my friends and do crazy shit, I have had them for 3 years, 10 months, 19 months at a time.
