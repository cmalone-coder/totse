Cosmetically or mechanically?

Cosmetically, though it kind of depends. The 930 is of course Turbo charged and made between 275-300 HP or so. It's heavy, and has a 4 speed manual, until the 1989 model (the final year for the 930) when they finally used a 5 speed. It's quite quick, but the 2.7 RS, conversely, was extremely light, much lighter than the 930. The 2.7 made 215 HP or so, though the RSR model which was essentially the racing version of the car made 280 HP with the same engine increased to a 2.8L. Though I've heard of production RS's being uprated to the 2.8L, I wouldn't use it as an example. The RS used a 5 speed.

On longer, wider, sweeping roads with lots of straight stretches, the 930 would walk away from the RS once the turbo spools up and lets loose all holy hell. On a more technical bit of road made up entirely of corners and crests, the agility of the RS would probably harrass the 930, if not out-pace it. Of course, the RSR would ruin the Turbos' shit..
