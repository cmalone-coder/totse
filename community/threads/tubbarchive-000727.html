Hewo, I agree that $1 Million is too small if this injury will affect you for the rest of your life. However, you need to keep at least 90% of your settlement money out of stocks completely. You are NOT looking for capital appreciation. You need to live off this money for ther rest of your life so your risk tolerance is low.

I would also avoid starting a business or anything else with any risk. You might live for another 80 years and if you lose this money and can't work it will be a slow and painful 80 years.

My advice would be a mutual fund that invests in bonds. There are funds that invest in "US Government Income" or GNMA's which will give you a higher return than CD's but they invest in seurities that are backed by the full faith and redit of the US Government. That means that there is almost no risk of default, unless the government is overthrown in which case your money is worthless anyway. These funds pay a monthly dividend. Right now, they are paying about 3.5%- 4.5% yields depending on the fund. CD's are paying about the same right now, so maybe put some in CD's and some in a bond fund.

Let's say you get 4% on a million that's $40,000 a year. Rates will fluctuate and you have to pay income tax on it so take only what you need. Remeber, even though rates on these investments fluctuate, they don't flutuate as much as returns in the stok market. You need as much consistency in your returns as possible to help you budget. Diversification is ok, as long as you diversify among investments with low risk.

DON'T let anyone talk you into putting it into an annuity. You need this money now, not when you retire. Annuities pay very big commissions and some advisors will try to steer you that way.

I wish you the best in your recovery. God bless you and remember, don't get greedy!
