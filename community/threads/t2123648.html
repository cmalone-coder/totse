Whoa whoa, hold up.

Ok.

CWE's don't hit you 'better', they hit you faster. And it's not because there's just something special about a CWE, it's because the pills are already in liquid form and so are much easier for the walls of the stomach an GI tract to suck up.

You only have 1500mg of APAP in there, so there is NO need for a CWE. (4000mg of APAP is the generally accepted safe limit, but it's not horrible to go over it)

With a CWE, you invariably lose SOME of your opiate along with the filtered APAP, and you only have 22.5mg to start out with, so you want to make damn sure you get it all. Therefore, I recommend dissolving the pills into some lukewarm water, and chugging that all at once. That will make for a high bioavailibilty, and a quick onset.

The .5 of xanax is a great idea, although I personally would eat it. There has been a large amount of evidence suggesting that insufflating benzos does in fact work, however, in my own personal experience I seem to get more effect from eating them. And I have tried insufflation of klonopin, xanax, and valium, and found that for each, oral administration on an empty stomach seemed to be most efficient.

Also, I highly recommend you pop 25mg of benadryl and take a sip of robitussin before you leave. SMALL amounts of benadryl and DXM (from robitussin. we're talking like 30mg, tops) make the opiate experience SO MUCH BETTER!

I find that they significantly increase the euphoria as well as get rid of that annoying itch.
