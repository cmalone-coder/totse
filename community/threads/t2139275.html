I once lived in an apartment that I always felt something was there with me. It wasn't creepy or scary though. But there was one weird thing that happened and really shook me up. At the time I didn't have a car and I rode the bus and walked everywhere. That morning I was going to catch the bus to work because it was really cold out and there was a little snow on the ground. I looked out the window to see how icy it looked so I'd know what kind of shoes to wear. Under my window in the snow (it was only about half an inch) was a track or trail, like something had been dragged through the front yard. I just thought it was maybe a cat or another animal taking her baby to a warmer spot and went on with eating my cereal.

Once I got outside and started walking to the bus stop, I realized it wasn't a normal track in the snow. It was a continuous line that was about one inch wide and it had a uniform lip that ran along either side of the track. There was no wavering in the line, like something walking while dragging something. And there were no footprints or pawprints.

The line ran along my route, going completely sideways into a deep ditch and even going under the top of a birdbath that was propped against the base. It moved near the road and away from it, but stayed steady the whole time. When I got to the spot where I cross the road, it continued up a little ways, but once I crossed I saw that it had also crossed the road and went all the way to the bus stop. At that point, it went into a spiral right by the sign and ended.

It freaked me the fuck out. I still can't think of what it would have been. If anyone has any suggestions, I'd love to hear them. Not many things in this world would make me happier than to know what the fuck that was.
