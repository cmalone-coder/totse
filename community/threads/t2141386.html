Re: People uglier/prettier when high?
On every psychedelic I've done including weed, this is what I've found.

Weed:
People look exactly like they really do, things you find unattractive come to your attention and vice versa, you just notice them more.
One example, with one of my exes, I saw her as beautiful but not someone I would want to date (this is when we were dating). I left her later.

On shrooms I've found the same thing only there are also some hallucinations added in sometimes.
Some of them seem meaningless to me about the person and have to do with other things but sometimes
it seems like they were exaggerations of good and bad attributes of the girl.

With my last ex, I noticed this consistently under the influence of K.
I could look at my hands and see how they were disgusting, "what the fuck are fingers? Bones, muscle, tendons, etc.. wtf..."
I could look around the room and see my friends as strange species, humans. Lanky, strange, self-destructive.
Though I could see the beauty in all of their lives also, I saw tiny details that were amazing, like a movement to the sound of their words.
With my girlfriend at the time, it was so strange. She shined in front of me, I could see things I found a little unattractive but they would not stick in my mind like with the others, the positives of her whole being from body to mind just poured into my senses, I sat with a spirit of pure being.
I believe this has to do with how much I loved her, my emotions were stronger and manifested in hallucinations also.

I think the way you look and feel about someone while you're high (noticing things, bad or good) is really just an amplification of what you already felt and saw before. However I've noticed seeing these things can cause real changes in how you see people later on.
