Re: Concentrating hydrogen peroxide (H2O2).
Ok hydrogen peroxide in its natural state is a liquid, not solid (crystals). Second water boils at 100*C and h202 at 150*C. The freezing point for h2o is 0*c and for h202 is I think -.4*c. Very close, but the temp should stop at 0 until all of the water is frozen and then start to climb up and start freezing h202.

So for example if we wanted 30ml of 30% h202 we would measure off 30ml of water and pour it into the container and then mark the water line. Then we would spill that out and add 300ml of 3% h202 (we want it 10x stronger so we add 10x as much) and pop it in the freezer. Then you would every once in a while remove ice from the solution with tongs or whatever and keep doing this until the volume of the liquid reachs that mark you amde earlier. Bam you have some 30% h202 now.
