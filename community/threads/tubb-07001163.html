Re: Secuirty at the malls
Quite the contrary, I think. According to my suspicions, most malls have a central security room manned by a few people that watch all the cameas, and the larger stores (Sears, Macy's, &c) have their own security setups.

For the most part the cameras are to scare shoplifters and catch employee theft. The bulk of the shoplifters caught are flat out seen by an employee or set off the alarms on their way out of a store.

If you are seen by a camera, the security operators have to radio the mall rent-a-cops (goons) with your location and description, whereupon they dogpile you and detain you until the police arrive. The thing with security goons is they seldom carry real weapons and can easily be dispensed with. Translation: Kick them in the balls. Problem solved...

You only get caught because you're afraid.
