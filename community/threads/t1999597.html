The 'Official' ABORTION Thread (POLL)
As most of the regulars know threads here which touch on abortion tend to quickly develop into soapbox rants for both camps. This means that they are some of the few threads that i police heavily(ie edit).

For ages now I have been promising an abortion thread where everyone may soapbox to their hearts content without editing.

I almost posted this a while back but then we had a spate of 'Fuck I'm pregnant!' threads and I felt that it wasn't tactful or helpful.

So here it is , with a poll.

HOWEVER please remember that this a public forum. If you've been (as I have) in the more criminal element of either camp or perhaps if you've had an abortion without your partner's knowledge then do please exercise caution.

Also, emotive as it is, lets try and be nice to each other huh?

Anyway I'll start by proclaiming my views, which most of you know already I guess:

I am Pro Life. Very much so a lot. I believe that abortion for any reason, except in cases when a clear and present medical danger to the mother exists or the child can be shown to be so deformed as not capable of life, is murder and that the abortioner and abortionee should be tried accordingly (inculding the Death Penalty if they live somewhere like Texas).

I do believe that ECP's are not only morally justifiably but should be avaible free OTC to anyone at any age.

I believe there is a justified case for the use of terminal force in the fight against abortion and that any doctors, nurses,staff or 'clients' killed , say in the bombing of a clinic, can be fairly judged 'to have brought their deaths upon themselves'.

NB in the poll any reference to 'time limit' should be taken to mean the time limit where you are. We can discuss the exact point at which you feel the limit should be in the thread.

'Validation' means the process a woman needs to go through to get an abortion in most countries- formal counselling/doctors agreement etc.
