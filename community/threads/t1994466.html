OdayJuarez

Re: Fake Id Please Help
How I imitate things in photoshop is by taking a scan, making that the background layer, and then tracing over them on layers above it until everything matches up. Then I delete the background layer. Then I make prints and alter colors as necessary for the printer. I recreate what I can, and only use the scans for what I can't otherwise duplicate. The scans will always look a bit blurry in comparison to the real thing. It's inevitable. Better to imitate clearly than duplicate blurry.

All the little details like line thickness, contrast, brightness, color match, and correct font styles/sizes are what make the difference. If you normal the font when it should be crisp, the difference will jump at you. If you can tell the difference without close study, the imitation isn't worth much.

I don't think this would work for fake ids though. They seem too high tech.
