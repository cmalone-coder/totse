Re: Favorite Series appriciation thread
Great thread. I've been meaning to do something like this myself.

1.) Deadwood - The G.O.A.T. One of the most ambitious shows ever produced, with by far the most star studded cast and complex scripts. Every single aspect of this show is leagues ahead of anything that came before it, and anything that's been done since.

2.) The Twilight Zone - Really, is any explanation needed?

3.) The Larry Sanders Show - To quote tommy shales (famous washington post tv critic featured in an episode of LSS) "Bitterly, brutally, blatantly hilarious." Taking cues from Fernwood 2 Night it was the inspiration, influence, and spiritual predecessor to Curb, and left a significant mark on virtually every quality sitcom that's come since. Plus, it was a launching point for jeff tambor, one of my comedy idols.

4.) Twin Peaks - Another show that doesn't need much explaining, there will never be anything like peaks. Bizarre, terrifying, and hilarious, it introduced us to Special Agent Dale Cooper, one of tv's greatest characters.

5.) Curb Your Enthusiasm - An R-rated Seinfeld featuring the comedy stylings of the great larry david. Why didn't I think of that?

6.) The Sopranos - Considered by many to be the Citizen Kane of tv. One of the deepest and most complex shows you'll find, for some reason it's commonly overlooked by lots of people.

7.) The Wire - The best cop show ever. Not a big surprise, since there hasn't been a cop show with 2/3 as much money thrown at it.

8.)Batman the Animated Series - BTAS absolutely owned me in the 90's. The best work from bruce timm and co., I've never met a male 16-25 that didn't absolutely love this show.

9.) Homicide - The predecessor to the Wire, this was one of those rare properties where I actually read the book first. Featuring the great andre braugher as frank pembleton, another one of tv's greatest characters (maybe that should have it's own thread) it was the grittiest show since Hill Street Blues, and one of the first shows to use the city it's filmed in (baltimore) as a character itself.

10.) The Venture Bros. - In my opinion, Team Venture is the best thing since the Simpsons. Unbelievable art, characters design and writing, I can't get enough of this fucking show. Bring on the goddamn merchandising!

edit: Honorable Mentions: the original Star Trek, Hill Street Blues, Ren & Stimpy, The Tick, Arrested Development,
