Okay, here's a start. When God created us, first of all, it wasn't so we could live a single lifetime--an infinitesimal period, make the mistakes we would inevitably make, then hope for the best. People couldn't figure this out, kept saying things like "The Lord works in mysterious ways."

The purpose of life--why we were created in the first place--is so that God himself could create and experience who he is on a physical level through all of us, lifetime after lifetime.

Our purpose is to create ourselves--that is, decide who and what we wish to be in accordance with our greatest, grandest, and most noble conception of ourself. In a larger sense, our purpose is to experience ourselves as all of it. We are evolving. We are becoming.

Becoming what? Beats me. We haven't gotten there yet, and we cannot know what we may become until we become it. But the journey is the joy--and as soon as we get "there", as soon as we create the next highest idea of who we are, we will create a grander thought, a higher idea, and continue the joy forever.

Speaking personally, it has been a hell of a lot of fun for me so far.

As far as numer four goes, can you? Probably. In everyday reality, pretty much anyone can kill anyone else--assuming that they are willing to accept the consequences.

But I see no point to this. For one thing, at the highest level, you can't kill anyone--we are all immortal beings--life is eternal. A sinlge lifetime in the context of eternity is less than a single leaf in a great forest.

For another thing, what you posit is impossible. We all create our own reality--the only thing in life that limits you, will ever limit you, can ever limit you, are your own doubts and fears.

There are no villains, no victims. We are the architect of all our own fortunes--and misfortunes.
