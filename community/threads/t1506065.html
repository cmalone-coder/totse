Daddy issues (Long, sorry)
My dad and I have had a strained relationship ever since he divorced my mom.

When I was in fifth grade, I spent the night at my friend's house. Her mom was going to take me home, but my mom called and said to meet at a hotel where they were holding an AA convention. I didn't think anything of it, since both my parents were active in AA, but when we got there my mom had a room.

When my friend left my mom broke down and told me that she'd just found out that my dad had been having an affair with her best friend. Apparently, the night after my dad told her (I heard the fight, but wasn't too worried because they only fought once every few years... seemed healthy, you know? To relieve some built up tension), he took my brother over to the best friend's house. The best friend, Susie, has five kids that my brother and I grew up with. My dad kissed her right in front of my brother, who was only 5 or 6 at the time.

So we stayed at my aunt's house for a few days, and my mom wouldn't let me see my dad. I refused to talk to him, until one time I went home and he started crying and apologized. Regardless of what he did, he's my dad, so of course I accepted the apology.

I lived with my mom for about a year after they divorced, but after awhile she couldn't pay the house payment and was really struggling with being a single mom. So my brother and I moved in with my dad. At first things were great, but after awhile my dad and I started fighting a LOT. Sometimes, if my mom couldn't talk, I'd call Susie. I did grow up around their family, and she was close to my dad... so I assumed she understood.

About a year later, everybody was fighting and I was sitting there getting really annoyed. When Susie called her middle daughter a bitch, my defensiveness and protectiveness over my friend kicked in. I called her a witch (ha, I know...) and went to comfort Molly. Susie turned the whole thing on me and said I embarassed her in front of her neighbors and all that. This marked the end of any relationship I could have ever had with her.

Over the years my dad and I continued to have pretty serious arguements. My dad is 6'4 and gets VERY frustrated very easily, so it's quite scary when he gets mad. I was no picnic either, I'm sure I was an awful kid. Very defiant. One time Susie asked me if I was still angry about how my parents split up... this was about a year after. I said yeah, a little bit... So she and my dad sent me to a shrink.

My dad, brother, and I moved in with Susie and four of her kids at the beginning of my freshman year. They got married shortly before we moved in. This is when everything really went to shit. I started dating a guy they didn't approve of, shopping at Hot Topic (because I wasn't allowed to wear clothes the "cool" kids were wearing, so I was trying to rebel I guess), and being openly defiant and rude all the time. I snuck out almost daily, but overall I was a good kid. Didn't do well in school, but I didn't drink, do drugs, or have sex. I was 14... Not into those things yet.

Strike one: I had this cat named Ledo. I'd had him since first grade. Around seventh grade, he started puking everywhere. The vet said he was fine, and there was no problem with him if we didn't mind the mess. When we moved in with Susie, Ledo disappeared. I later found out Susie and my dad had put him to sleep... I didn't get to say goodbye to my kitty, and they both lied about it. They didn't bother asking my mom to take him.

Strike two: Susie started telling Molly's (stepsister, two years younger than me) friends' parents that I had "a dark side" and to keep their kids away from me. She also called me a devil child because my dad and I had many, many fights about me going to church/bible classes. I've never been interested in religion, and I didn't appreciate him suddenly converting then telling me I had to do the same. I got kicked out of classes because I told the teacher I had no reason to be there.

Strike three: (Sophomore year) I said something rude to Susie. I don't remember what it was, but I do remember that what I said wasn't rude, it was just the way I said it, and hearing from about six other adults that it was rude, but nothing to get especially excited over. I stayed at my mom's that night, because when my dad came to pick my brother and I up he told me it'd be best if I didn't come home. He didn't bring clothes for school the next day or offer my mom gas money to get me to school. I didn't even have clothes to sleep in there... it was a small apartment, and generally if I wanted to spend the night, I just brought clothes.

My mom didn't take me to school the next day, and of course my dad threw a fit. She took me to his house later, and I called my boyfriend and we talked for awhile. My dad and Susie came in and said to hang up the phone right now. I got indignant and dropped the phone, and the cord made it snap in the general direction of my dad... about three feet away. They said I was being disrespectful (that was their favorite word for awhile) and that I needed to be taken to the Huckleberry House, which is a shelter in Columbus for runaways and delinquents. I was terrified at the thought... the worst thing I'd done was get suspended, and that was over a year before this. I tried to go downstairs to call my mom so she could pick me up, but my dad threw me onto my bed and they yelled at me. I got out and ran to get the phone, but Susie ran into the kitchen and grabbed it from me. I said something, then called her a "fucking bitch" and ran over to my friend's house. He gave me a ride to my boyfriend's house, where my mom picked me up. I didn't talk to my dad for about six months after that.

When I was allowed back in the house, it was sort of tense. He made me apologize to his wife, which I did because I wanted to start over with my dad. I had no interest in making things okay with his wife, but I did anyway to make him happy.

Basically since I've moved out I've gained confidence, become much more secure with myself, and was able to get out of an unhealthy relationship with that guy the parents hated, because I was no longer dependant on guys. Since then, my dad and I have a pretty good relationship. We can talk about almost anything, we share the same tastes in music, art, books, and movies, and we can have a good time.

But after I turned 18, his wife started going back to her old bitchy ways. She's a hyperchondriac, she has severe emotional issues (seems that way from the mood swings and amount of meds in the cabinet), and my mom told me Susie used to offer her pills and stuff when they were friends. Her kids can't stand her half the time, half the town we live in hates her, but she still has my dad by the balls. And recently, I've only been allowed at his house if she says it's okay.

Now that my dad has no legal responsibility over me, she's trying to cut me out again. I have no problem staying away from their house, but it hurts when he says "let me see if that's alright" if I want to come over for a little while. He divorced my mom because she was too controlling, but that took him 13 years. He's almost divorced Susie at least four times... and he caught her cheating once, but still they haven't broken up.

I managed to grow up to be a decent kid. Too much free time the summer after sophomore year led to drug use and sleeping with a 23 year old, but nothing really bad came out of that. But I do have issues because of this... for example, I find it hard to trust people. Especially best friends and boyfriends. Usually I can ignore it, but occasionally it pops up... It didn't help that said bad boyfriend cheated on me, then only a couple months after we broke up my best friend dated him. I told her to go for it, but at the time I was stoned off my ass and would have agreed to anything. I didn't think she actually would. I'm over that now, and we're friends again.

I don't know how to get over these issues though. I want to trust people, and I want to talk to my dad about it. I'm done trying to split him and his wife up, but it's getting ridiculous again and if I don't say anything, I feel like we're going to have another huge fight... and if that wasn't bad enough for my mental health, he's paying for college. I don't have enough money saved to cover all of it, and my mom can't pay for it.

Short version: My dad has a bitchy controlling wife that hates me, and because of the way they treated me and the way my parents split up, I have trust issues and I want to find a way to resolve those, and also talk to my dad about the fact that she has him by the balls again and I don't appreciate being second to his wife.
