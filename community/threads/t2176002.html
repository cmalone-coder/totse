valium works best for me, doc's won't prescribe!!!
Hello...I started using lorazepams regularly (legally prescribed) about a year ago due to terrible anxiety/panic attacks. From there I went to 1mg/Xanax XR per day. Got that upgraded to 1.5mg/day instants. At that dose my anxiety was finally under control. I never had to increase my dose, in fact sometimes I would voluntarily decrease it. Months later I thought I didn't need it anymore and I was tired of NEEDING it, so I tried to taper. Everyone but doctors seems to know you can't taper off xanax. I had my psyc switch me to klonopin, which I got worked back up to 1.5/day. The benefits of switching are great in that I don't wake up shaking and craving a xanax and I can forget to take a dose and be OK. You miss one dose of xanax and you can't function...want to die...too short acting. Even the XR's are not much more forgiving.

During my time on xanax, I was a heroin/oxycontin addict so I had no reason to abuse it because I was already high enough. I've been off opiates 100% for six months now (except suboxone...afraid of relapse) and all of the sudden I love benzos WAY too much. I can get 1mg IR xanax virtually anytime from a friend, hell he practically gives me a couple everytime we hang out for the hell of it. He also gets the same klonopins that I do. Keep in mind I already get 90/month anyway...

I've also started to love uppers (adderall, dex, ritalin) the past couple months. The problem is I end up taking around 8 klonopins/xanax/whatever everytime I take uppers because I am sensitive to the side effects (nervous, can't sleep/eat).

Lately I have been taking so many it's just insane! I just love the calming feeling so much, after years and years of dealing with anxiety. I am also going through a TERRIBLE time right now.... so overwhelmed and stressed. I'm stopping the uppers because they make me take too many downers.

Usually by the end of the month I'm back down to 3 or 4 k-pins a day, which almost does the trick. The problem with klonopin is that when I do get panic attacks, those damn things take a good hour at least to feel! It is NOT a good breakthrough anxiety drug.

I've tried diazepam several times and it is by far my favorite of them all. It possesses the quick acting effects of xanax, and the long acting effects of klonopin, even longer! It makes me feel GREAT all day, and on LOWER DOSES! Supposedly, .5mg clonazepam=10 mg diazepam, but I feel 3 valium 10's MUCH more than 3 Klonopin .5's. I think it has something to do with valium's insane half life and sedating properties...not sure. I just think I would do better on 30mg/valium rather than 1.5mg klonopin since it last longer, provides relief of panic attacks, and to be honest, I don't feel the need to take more all day! I can dose ONCE daily!

Valium is an old school med that is hard to get prescribed to these days. I've only ever known 3 people who got them. I think doctors are afraid of high numbers, regardless of actual potency, due to fear of loosing their licenses. For example, 1.5mg does sound like less than 30mg. I've tried to get valium from 2 different doctors, and they both refused. I recently switched klonopin doc's again, and he gladly gave it to me. I want him to switch me to valium.

Anyone out there have experience getting switched to valium? I really think it would positively influence my life. Any suggestions of what to say to my doctor?

Also, has anyone tried those "orally disintegrating" Klonopin wafers? Do they kick in any faster? Any help would be great.

Well you're right, Klonopin is much less effective as a PRN/anxiety agent than Xanax is. In fact, I really don't think there is a better benzo on the market for rapid relief of spontaneous panic than Xanax. Klonopin is longer-acting, obviously, and therefore, in my experience, much more effective as a treatment for something like Generalized Anxiety Disorder where the symptoms are not dependent on external variables such as they are in Panic Disorder or even Social Anxiety disorder.

You say that Valium works better than Klonopin, but those two are a lot more similar than either of them and Xanax are. In fact, Valium is even longer acting than Klonopin is, with, in my experience, an even poorer effect on sudden onsets of anxiety. Different people process different benzos differently, of course, but then again, what kind of anxiety do you have, exactly? Is it a constant, daily issue, or more centered around panic attacks? Do you notice triggers with your anxiety? Variables that make it worse?
