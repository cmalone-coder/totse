I grew up in a house hold where my parents were hippies and dont believe in a God. I guess you could say it is pantheism. I never saw anything of a higher power or supernatural being so I guess you could say I was an atheist. I was into drugs and alcohol and fell into a bout of severe depression where I didn't want to live anymore. So I decided to take my own life by jumping off of a building. 67 feet to be exact. I should have died and in fact I was dead for about an hour and a half before I woke up on the ground with one last yell for help. After two weeks or so I can start to remember what was going on and that was about when I knew that something had saved me because I knew that I shouldnt be alive. Some of my friends gave me a Bible to read which meant basically nothing ot me at the time because I still didnt really believe. I started to go to church to see what it was all about and as I was there or reading the Bible I would get a little streak of light just beyond my periferal vision in my left eye. I didnt think anything of it at the time and quit going to church and focused more on my rehab. and stuff like that and for about 2 months I didnt see it anymore. Then i started to get depressed again and since my friends told me to turn to the Bible in times like that I did. It didnt help so I turned to prayer and just asked the Lord to to help me and to guide me and to tell me where to turn. I continued to talk to Him and told him that I dont care about myself anymore and that I was going to help other people and out of nowhere I just blurted out that I was a Christian. Then as soon as I laid my head down on the pillow that little certain streak of light returned as if to say that I was in the right place with God. Since then I got baptized and became a Christian and a lot of the time when I read the Bible, pray, attend church, or just act in a Christ-like way towards people it returns as if to reassure me that He is there and Im doing the right thing. You might say that its always there so how can you give credit to God? Well, its not there when I get angry at someone, or acting in certain ways that arent Christ-like. I dont know what this means to you but to me, its a sign that God does exist and that he does love me. To me this is my evidence to there being a God and that is why I believe in a God.
