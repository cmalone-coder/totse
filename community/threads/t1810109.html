Then i started to get depressed again and since my friends told me to turn to the Bible in times like that I did. It didnt help so I turned to prayer and just asked the Lord to to help me and to guide me and to tell me where to turn. I continued to talk to Him and told him that I dont care about myself anymore and that I was going to help other people and out of nowhere I just blurted out that I was a Christian. Then as soon as I laid my head down on the pillow that little certain streak of light returned as if to say that I was in the right place with God. Since then I got baptized and became a Christian and a lot of the time when I read the Bible, pray, attend church, or just act in a Christ-like way towards people it returns as if to reassure me that He is there and Im doing the right thing. You might say that its always there so how can you give credit to God? Well, its not there when I get angry at someone, or acting in certain ways that arent Christ-like. I dont know what this means to you but to me, its a sign that God does exist and that he does love me. To me this is my evidence to there being a God and that is why I believe in a God.
