List of Actual experiences at Wonderland (Toronto, Ontario):
-Each visit was +6 hours-

Weed (Repeated): All the "business" and the colours and sounds are pretty neat. The "chill" rides like slow "ride through some themed area" were really fun. Food was godly. Rollercoasters and fast and Insane, "dizzy" rides were either a huge blast and full of pumped euphoria and rushing sensations or sickening and could be terrifying. Those moving chair/3D-glasses rides were totally mind blowing and I went on them repeatedly, totally involved in the "world" the creators wanted you to feel like you were immersed in. When we needed to re-dose, far edges of the grass in the water park, no one seems to notice. Always bring Axe and breath mints or something similar, smelling it not good.

Amps: "I' AM ON LIFE!" (Screamed on the fall of a coaster). So much to do while doing so much, so many thoughts to fill an overflowing mind. So euphoric and having a great time! I felt like you can't get much more spun then at an amusement park. DRINK A LOT YOU CRAZY FUCKER! Rollercoasters were insane (Sometimes for the best, some for the worst), you're (feeling like) your going a million miles per hour faster then normally on a coaster. "Dizzy" rides made me feel sicker then normal and I avoided them after two so I can't say with too much accuracy what they're all like. The "slow themed" rides were interesting but made me feel like getting out of my seat to do other things. Again those Moving/3D-glasses ride had the same feelings as just weed but intensified and accompanied by huge rushing sensations.
Happily wandering around a "World (Felt like it)" spun with everyone oblivious is a sweet feeling. Fell asleep in the car ride home avoiding a crash (Took a therapeutic dose of benzos at that point), woke up at home and was ready to drink and have a party.

Weed (Repeated) + Amps: Paranoia got the best of me about three times, make sure you can handle that, I have major anxiety disorder so maybe it was worse for me. The "Amusement world" turns into a different type of "world" with ego exaggerations for everything from clown faces to the meaning of the "themed rides". Not a bad thing but I'm sure it could freak you out, did for me once or twice. Rollercoasters were AMAZING and fun, heart rate increased noticeably to the point where it caused the third paranoia attack for me after the third or so ride. These "attacks" lasted 30 min tops. I still enjoyed food and made sure to drink a lot. My sense of Being (Yes, I'm very spiritual) was very noticeable this day for some reason and added to my blissful adventure. "themed rides" were now enjoyable but there was no way I could handle any more then the one "dizzy" ride I went on. Amp crashed was handled by weed just before the ride home.

Benzos: Oh... My...God... I suggest a High dose (All my experiences were high doses so make up your own mind however). I'm floating through a blur of sounds, lights, people, I'm almost dreaming. Rollercoasters were a whole new experience. I could see everything without the "To fast to notice what's going on" feeling. I could see everything to where the coaster was on the track to being able to focus on people on the ground while upside down. I think I could focus this well because of the rush from the coaster 'cause I couldn't focus That well while I was wandering. Themed rides were pretty damn relaxing and enjoyable. Care Free to the maximum usage of those word. A Great experience and a wonderful choice if you have fears of coasters or anxiety in situations with lots of people, eliminated my Bro's deathly afraid feelings about them (coaster/people) and he went on every one. All in all a great way to spend a day.

Weed + Benzos: Amplifies the good feelings of both and removes the negatives of both (The weed more so like paranoia). Other then that just mix my description of weed and my description of benzos.

Drugs and amusement parks go together like baking a cake from scratch with sugar, eggs, and flour.

HOWEVER-WARNING: Do not, repeat (really think about this), DO NOT do ANYTHING to draw attention to the fact hat you are not sober. Try your best not to smell, use Visine, wear sunglasses (Also do that anyways since your eyes will REALLY hurt if it's sunny), don't be falling all over the place (Benzos) and definitely don't talk about what your on with your firneds unless your talking very quietly and no one is around. Most Parks have undercovers.
