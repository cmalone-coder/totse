Maybe one of you can help me, Problem i developed with smoking reefer.
If your going to flame just keep it to yourself. I dont care if you think its retarded but your not the only dbag that has had problems.

Alright, well i'll start from the beginning. I first tried/started smoking reefer in January of 2004. At first getting high was pleasurable and fun. I remember smoking quite a bit to and watching people throw up when they smoked with my group of friends. In December i was diagnosed with depression (worst thing that ever happned to me in my life) and was put on Prozac. After i was put on prozac i decided to not smoke for a while to see if i could straighten things out.
Then my friends birthday came up and we decided to smoke, i still remember a lot from that night. At first i was just starting to feel it like normal but then it just kept getting more and more intense. Before long i found myself lying on the couch more stoned then i have ever been in my life. I couldnt talk and when people talked to me i felt distant and paranoid. I thought maybe it was just from not smoking for a while but about a month later i started smoking every day again but this time it was different then before and i kept getting those intense feelings of distance and paranoia.

Well eventually i quit the prozac because it turned me into a zombie and my life ran itself. But i continued to smoke marijuana because i thought i still enjoyed it. I started smoking by myself more and more because smoking with people kept getting harder and harder to do because of paranioa/anxiety. O and i should add i started developing reverse tolerance and even while not high i had paranioa/anxiety that progessively got worse.

A year later i dropped out of school, started living with my sister, and doing all the hard drugs i could find in the small college town. I kept smoking pot because i thought it might be different from the last time i got high (even though it never did, and this is the definition of insanity). Even after doing a line of coke i would try and smoke marijuana but all it would do is instantly bring me down into the pits of hell i so deperatly tried to escape by DOING drugs.

After 6 months of living like this i went to rehab and i came to the conclusion that i smoked reefer to get "rid" of my paranioa/anxiety because when i came down i would be glad i wasnt high anymore.

Anyway I rarely smoke nowdays (probably about once every 4 months on average, and the tiniest amout from swag gets me super blazed) and my anxiety/paranoia has decresed dramatically.

One last note, everyone in my imediate family smoked marijuana like i did then burned out on it and they dont like it anymore (my sister still drinks like a mule, smokes 2 packs of cigs a day, still does coke, pops oxy/hydrocodine a lot, but never smokes ganja anymore).

Alright Thanks for reading and maybe actually caring to help some random soul. This was just a short overview on the subject as i could tell stories for pages.

And my question is, Why cant i enjoy marijuana like everyone else? Am i the only one who's effects felt by ganja are Paranoia, Anxiety, Distant outlook, Light Headed, Racing Heart, Racing Thoughts, Extreme Fatigue, and Depression?
