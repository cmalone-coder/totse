You know, shit happens. No one's perfect and this is more common than one might think.

Cheating, "slips in judgment", occur in a lot of relationships, but that doesn't have to mean the end. You work through it and with good reason, you're in love! Some people might say love doesn't do that, but I say fuck all that. People aren't the essences of love, we are just human, we are subject to human behavior and faults. That doesn't mean you shouldn't try.

There's nothing you do or say to take the hurt away from her. The best thing you can do is entertain her when she asks you questions. You might want to get angry, but she'll want to know and your answers (I assume) will comfort her.

Try not to beat yourself up about it, it was just a case in 3 years. You're by no means Satan. If you look at the big picture, you see how this fits into your life. This single event doesn't define you, she knows that (not consciously) which is why she'll stick around (unless she's totally neurotic/your her first serious relationship-sometimes they don't realize what they have).

Whatever the case may be, good luck. And.. you'll live.
