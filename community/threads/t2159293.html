I'll be honest with you; I've never taken it. I do, however, know a good deal of people who have, and am familiar about it's basic mechanisms of action.

From what I've heard/read, it's supposed to be ideal for what you mentioned because it blocks the effects of stimulation on the adrenergic receptors; as in, it helps to stop any kind of symptoms that could be caused by an increase in adrenaline (basically everything you mentioned). It used to be used for hypertension pretty often but it seems to be less favored for that now and more often these days used by doctors for the reasons we're talking about here.

Wiki says a lot of people use it for "stage fright", which makes sense.

If you truly feel your anxiety is mostly physical, as you said, test-running some propanolol and recording your perceived effects sounds like a fairly good idea. Just make sure you only use a reasonably therapeutic dose and, if you can, do it when you're already anxious about something and/or are experiencing those negative physical symptoms.
