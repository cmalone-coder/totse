Dying In Your Dreams
I've been told that it's impossible to die in your dreams, however I have died a total of four times in my dreams. I can remember the moments before death, dying, and then hovering over my body. Once I actually saw the white light.

The first time I was riding a horse and fell off and broke my neck. Instantly, I was looking down at my body and I remember saying to myself

"This isn't so bad."

The second time I was in a car accident where my family had left the car and it exploded. Again, I remember the heat and the sting and then I remember standing with my family and watching myself burning in the car, but my family couldn't see me. At that moment I actually remembered the other death dream. Then is became an astroprojectile dream where I flew up to Heaven, but I never made it. A voice told me I wasn't allowed into Heaven.

The third time I drowned and was at the bottom of a pool with several sharks, but the sharks weren't eating me. I remember choking and then feeling very empty and saying to myself

"I'm fine with this."

And the fourth time I was shot in the head. It felt like I was punched in the back of the head and then I fell over, on the concrete, and gasped a little before I died.

Is this normal? Can you really die in your dreams? And, if you can, is it really dying? I mean, did I actually die and come back?

Cheers,
