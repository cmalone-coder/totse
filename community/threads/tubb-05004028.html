School on drugs
If you're a kid, there's a good chance you've done it. If you're an adult, you might have when you were young. What this topic is about though, is

What drugs have you been to school on (or done in school), and which gave you the best (or worst) experieces.


I've been to school drunk, stoned, tweaked, and sedated. Minor opiates (hydrocodone, etc.) are alright. They make you relaxed, mellow, so you don't really have to worry much. Being drunk is fun if you know how to control it (like I can). Just as long as nobody smells your breath. Being stoned is ok as long as you don't have to talk much during your first couple classes, and you can eat and sleep throughout the rest. Going to school tweaked, however, is one of the worst feelings in the world for me. I was able to do my work better, and I could engage in classroom discussions, but every time I tried to sit back and relax when nothing was going on, I'd always get fidgety, start flinching, and get really really paranoid. I don't ever want to have to do that again.
