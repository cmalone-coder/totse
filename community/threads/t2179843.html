psych 'experiments' every month or two or so could be very beneficial.

as far as different species go, there is a bit of controversy surrounding that.

now, cubes are cubes, however there are differences in potency.

one of the most potent mushrooms are psilocybe azurescens. these contain baeocystin as well as psilocin and psilocybin. they contain up to 1.8% psilocybin, 0.5% psilocin, and 0.4% baeocystin.

another potent mushroom is panaeolus cyanescens. these contain psilocybin, serotonin, and urea. (psilocybin being the psychoactive portion)

so... some people say they are pretty much the same, other people, including expirienced mushroom growers and trippers, say that different species produce different highs. some say that pan cyans produce a much cleaner high, and therefore that is their favorite.

but it all depends on the person. I can't really say if you would notice a difference, and if you did, which would be best. that is for you to decide.

Basically all psychadelic mushrooms fall into one of two categories: those containing psilocybin, and amanita muscarias.

Amanita muscarias are those ones you usually see drawn in children's books. They're legal. But they are not a very enjoyable high and, from what i've heard, mostly just cause nausea and disorientation

What you want is mushrooms that contain psilocybin. There are several species of these and some people contend that different strains have distinctly different trips. i personally think that this is just placebo effect, but a lot of the people who disagree with me know way more about shrooms that I do, so i could very well be wrong. If anything i would liken the differences between mushroom types to the idfferences between different strains of weed.

Really though, unless you want to take the time and effort to grow your own, you're basially stuck with whatever type you friendly neighborhood drug dealer has anyway.
