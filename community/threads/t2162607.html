since you've helped me in other threads [although you probably dont remember]...

I've had my betta for about 6 months now in a 5 gallon tank. quite spacious for him and the lid is open. Since you plan on leaving the lid open, i supposed it'd be fine since there would still be air circulating above the water. Now, if you closed it, the only air left would be the one between the cap and where the water starts, and the betta is sure to gobble it up. Bettas dont need oxygen pumps to survive. As you mentioned, they rise to the surface to catch air, which is where the bubbles they make come from. So yea, if you leave the cap open it should be fine, but either way, dont be cheap and get them a 5 dollar bowl. Even a pickle jar'll do

Re: Betta Fish question
Those "oxygen tablets" are for extending the amount of time fish have in the bag when you buy them from the store, not for long term use as a means of putting O2 in the water. You need to keep the cap off so at least some gas exchange can occur. You'll also want to change the water in the bottle 2-3x a week, otherwise your betta will be floating and not swimming.
