As a fellow Totsean with bad teeth, I can tell you this. If you can bear the pain for a while, and keep the inflammation under control, the nerve will die. This will render the offending tooth painless.

Being poor, I have had to do this many times. My first line of defense is ibuprofen. I take a prescription dose every six hours, which is 800 milligrams. Buy large bottles of ibuprofen � 500 tablets at 200 milligrams each � and commence taking four of these every six hours without fail. At first, you will notice your ears ringing, and you will bleed heavily if you are cut. But after a few months of this threapy, these side effects will subside. Remember to take 800 milligrams every six hours without fail, or your pain will return with a vengeance.

The ibuprofen will reduce or eliminate the swelling in the offending tooth. The swelling is what causes the pain. During this time, eat soft foods, away from the bad tooth. Avoid crunchy or hard foods, and eat small bites that can easily be chewed on the side opposite the bad tooth. You might also notice pieces of the dying tooth breaking off, which can be very creepy. For the most part, ibuprofen will take care of the pain, unless you bite down hard on the offending tooth.

There are times, however, when ibuprofen won't do anything for you. When the pain is really bad, have a supply of Darvocet-N, Lortab, or Vicodin, on hand. I have an open-ended prescription for Darvocet-N from my doctor for arthritis pain, but use it almost exclusively for tooth pain. Go see your doctor, tell him that you have arthritis, and need something for pain. He will most likely write a script for Darvocet-N, which you can take two of every four hours. Try not to take too much of it, as Darvocet-N contains acetaminophen (Tylenol), which can and will damage your liver.

When you sleep, do it sitting up, with no pressure on the side of your face where the bad tooth is. Learn to meditate. This will help you concentrate on relieving your pain. The most important thing is remembering to take 800 milligrams of ibuprofen every six hours.

If I made more money, and wasn't such a pussy about going to the dentist, I wouldn't have had to invent this elaborate scheme for controlling my tooth pain. If you can do it, go have the tooth extracted. It's a hell of a lot easier than doing what I just described. If you do neither, your only alternative is to live in constant agony.
