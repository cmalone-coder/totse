I did that once actually. Not necessarily on purpose, but I did it, it ended up being more of a dulled emotional state towards the entire world and humanity in general though; not really just an emotionless state towards some shitty girl.

I'm not sure it's possible at all for you to do it though...just because my situation was really unique.

As a kid I was really...we'll say chronically nervous, always really anxious, that kind of thing. Now my father happens to be a psychiatrist, so he took it into his hands to change that. I learned mental tricks to dull anxiety, physical pressure points that stimulate relaxation, and even got to the point where we used hypnotism to dull my emotions in general. I got "fixed" as one might call it, and became the laid back slacker I am today - without a care in the world; however I can honestly say that nothing makes me nervous at all now, I can count 2, perhaps 3 times in my entire life that I've been actually angry. I cry perhaps once every 2 or 3 years, and so forth. The one difference was that I was more than capable of finding myself hating people or things, though I kept it to myself.

Now, then this broad stumbled into my life and I got all caught up in the concept of love, or perhaps it was lust, and anyways, she kept "mind-raping" me (or so I liked to phrase it), and I kept coming back like a love-struck puppy.

Anyways, eventually I got tired of it and got to thinking: the only reason this was happening was because, as a kid, when I did those mental tricks and physical things and hypnotism, it was to dull emotions I had AS a child (ie- NOT hate or love). So I went about reliving my youth as best I could, and entirely dulled my sense of love in general. I now think of love as something that requires far more time and effort than it's worth, and thanks to this have no reason to be so infatuated with any femme fatale.
