Oxycontin shaver
I have seen many pill heads just using a credit card or lighter and dollar bill to crush pills. I have also noticed a lot of threads here about oxy and assume that a lot of you use this method too ( I could be wrong!). Especially in lower mg pills, this way is a bitch since the powder clumps together and takes forever to get a fine powder; you may also trap oxycodone in the time release matrix.
Here is my solution: use a hose clamp. First, you need to just straighten the hose clamp out and remove the pill coating.
Next, just rub the pill up and down the rough side of the clamp like you are grating cheese. And, vuala, you have a nice fine powder to parachute and/or sniff.
Just thought that I'd throw that out there.
