My point is: when some one comes to this board and is contemplating suicide, don't tell them how they feel. They know how they feel. Suicide is a last resort, when no other option can be seen. Sympathize with those people that have mentally eroded to such an extent that they see no other option but to end their lives.

Lastly suicidal people are not 'weak'. They are ill. It can be biological or environmental. Not every one is a product of a comfortable home, a loving family and financial security.
