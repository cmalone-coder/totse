Re: Ruger 10/22 Vs M38 Vs Savage .22
get a 10/22, Hogue Overmolded stock, and a high quality barrel, such as a Tactical Solutions. With a decent scope mount, you're looking at around $450, but it'll be seriously accurate.

The Savage, for all of 100 bucks, will give you that accuracy, but its ugly and mine is prone to jamming when it gets really hot. I suppose you're talking about the MKII, right? I haven't tried the 64 autoloader.

The M38 is fun, but I would start with the M91/30 if given a choice... Interordnance often has M38s, M44s, and M91/30s for around sixty bucks.
