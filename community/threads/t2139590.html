Do you remember what sobriety feels like?
I've been doing drugs for three years. Mostly psychedelics and opiates. I always find some way to get fucked up, and while I don't normally abuse hardcore drugs, I sometimes go farther than I'd expected. But the thing is that over time, all the non-hardcore drugs can really add up. I remember last year I stopped using everything for about 5 months, and it was only after three months that I realized that my mind was normal again.

Only on acid does that happen to me. It's refreshing. How about you guys? Is this a documented thing or is it all in my head? I believe it would be called "burning out."

Re: Do you remember what sobriety feels like?
besides for getting drunk 2 times a week and using salvia yesterday, I haven't used any psychoactives aside from nicotine or caffeine since 4/20. Being sober is a state of mind that one should learn to appreciate. I used drugs to cover up my pain previously and I have really straightened my life around since I stopped, mentally anyway (financially, physically, etc, I was great before). I'm not saying drugs make you stupid, because my grades my first semester of college were much better when I was fucked up all the time, but my anxiety has decreased and I feel generally happier. Being stoned all the time creates an imbalance in your brain's chemistry, with both dopamine and seratonin levels, and it is better appreciated getting stoned every once in a while to fully appreciate the excess seratonin and dopamine rather than use all the time just so you have the high levels and not feel depressed...JMO.
