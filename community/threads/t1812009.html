Re: What is Evil?
Well how about this;

Let's say that evil is the direct intention of doing great harm, with only the harmful aspects in mind.

For example, let's say there was a certain person in the world that I hardly knew. There's a lot of them, so bear with me. Okay, let's say this person walks in front of me. It would not really give me any personal satisfaction, or any perticular remorse, for that matter, to...I don't know, reach over and shoot them in the head. (This is assuming people don't have problems walking in front of people who have guns in their hands.) But, if I did, and didn't care at all, the act of killing this person for no reason, I think, would be evil.

So, to me, and I think others can agree, evil is the act of causing great harm, with only the thought of how much harm one can cause on a meaningless target. The fact also remains that the person doing this act of cruelty,(is that different then evil?) is not doing so out of an act of hatred, or any particular bias towards said meaningless target. And an evil person, is one who repeatedly does things like this, with no intention to stop. Maybe they actually enjoy it, or try to advocate this evil onto others.

Oh, and by the way, the devil is not evil.

-Omnigast
