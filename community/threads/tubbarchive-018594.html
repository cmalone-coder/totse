Re: Picking Lockers....
Master got wise to that scam.

At the hardware store we used to call up Master to get lost combos and keys for people who owned the locks (as in, brought us the lock not attached to anything, but needed a way to open it). While they used to just hand out measurements or combos to anybody official sounding with the lock's serial, now they ask for faxed ID and a notarized statement that you are the actual owner of the lock.

It turned into such a pain in the ass for us that we resorted to learning how to crack the combo locks and writing down the combo for people because it was easier and faster.
