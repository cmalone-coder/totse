Tips For Increasing Yields.....
Not getting 80% of the E back as Meth by weight?

It is not surprising, but the reasons are not what you would expect.

First of all, generally, the stuff is being produced in the reaction, it is the recovery and cleaning phases that are killing the good yields.

To recover an additional 15%-30% of your product takes some time, and a lot of work, it is not quick.

Another problem is it is lost in various different solutions making it confusing and complicated as to what was what, especially if you have saved everything including the ephedrine powder on through to the final product.

One way to get a 5% to 20% of your shit back is simply keep your lye water and water wash solutions, put them in a big jug, blast them with more NaOH get it up to pH13 - pH14, add toluene shake it very well, let it sit for 24 hours, go back pull off the toluene and any whitish emulsion layer. Forget the brown emulsions(too nasty). Wash this three(3) times with small amounts of d-water, about 1/4 the volume of the Toulene solution. Smaller multiple washes are more efficient than larger washes.

Add HCl (don't gas) you will be surprised to find quite a bit. It is not the cleanest but if you save it after a few batches you will have a substantial amount that is worth the effort to clean up.

Add to the above all Acetone washes after evaporating the acetone and your looking at another 5% to 25% depending on the brand of acetone you use, most acetone contains water and some contains benzene, and other chemicals, warm acetone can dissolve large amounts of your product.

How often to you treat the speed with acetone while still warm?

Plenty of times, if your like me.

Another thing about acetone is it will dissolve away Meth-HI salt, which is always present in varying amounts.

A final source of easily recovered lost product will be in the alcohol left over from recrystalization, it is saturated with meth, and full of toxins also. evaporate the alcohol and add it to the pile of recovered speed.

These three sources is where you can make a 50% return into an 80% return.

Filters of all types suck up dope like a sponge, wash them out in alcohol and discard them , evapoate the alcohol.

After you have collected enough of these spare bits and pieces, maybe from several batches, then clean them up.

Grind them to a powder,

wash with cold acetone

Recrystallize with a dry alcohol like methanol or a good denatured. Some denatured is full of everything under the sun MEK, Benzene, Acetone , and water, this is no good for your purposes.

The simplest method is to boil a little methanol or denatured or dried isopropynol in a vision ware bowl, with a fan blowing on it to prevent fire. Pour in your go-go retun to a boil. Add enough alcohol to dissolve all the speed. If the mixture appears cloudy consider adding more alcohol and filtering it first.

Once everything has dissolved, boil the alcohol off until

A skin begins to form on the surface.

Cover the bowl and place it in the freezer for ten to twelve hours. The longer in the freezer the better and the bigger will be your crystals. It is hard to tell just by looking at the surface of the crystals, in the bowl, but once you pull them out and dry them you will definately tell the beneficial effect, that longer times have upon this process. Since the alcohol does not freeze it continues to digest and reform the crystals of meth, this purifies them and grows them larger.

Quickly break up the chunks and place in a filter, vacuum filters are the best because less time involved.

As the solution warms up it will dissolve more dope, so you want to filter it and dry it as as quickly as possible.

Place the damp crystals on a sheet of paper to help draw out more of the fluids as quick as you can. Then let the crystals air dry to evaporate away any residual solvents for an hour or more. This will have cleaned out the toxins, and recovered a large pile of lost dope.

I told you it took a while and is somewhat labor intensive, but if you consider the difficulty in obtaining E, you may find that the 15% - 30% extra return is worth the effort.

Then again you might say screw it and cook up another batch.

Since it takes 2-3 days to complete the process, this is not for everyone.
