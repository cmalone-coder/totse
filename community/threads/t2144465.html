I've had my fling with psychedelics and designers already, some were good experiences and some were bad. I think boomer's (shrooms) were the most "natural" feeling in regards of the high. You would feel the shit digesting and the inside of your body starts to feel warm and almost gassy. Then the peak starts to hit and if the mood and environment is right it just flows. Man I've had some seriously good times with old buddies tripping driving around the city and country roads (with a DD of coarse!) exploring parks and trails. Young restless minds running a hundred miles per hour as enlightening thoughts just pour into my head. Walking along the trails vibeing with nature and the company of your good friends who are also thwacked out of there skull just talking for hours about everything. The come down is a little ridged but nothing a good bowl and an apple can't fix.

While on the other hand LSD and XTC and 5-MEO-AMT were to synthetic and tweaky for me. I could feel the chemicals just fucking up my head, but I'll tell you what nothing is like LSD. It is truly in a realm of it's own. Sure there's stronger stuff out there but man LSD just fucking trips the shit outta ya. I Remember my friend putting in the old Aeon Flux cartoons from Peter Chung and I couldn't handle it. I know it sounds pussy but I've watched those episodes sober and it really had some deep meanings in them. So when I watched it fucked up I could slightly understand it being that I watched them before but man it was just to whacked out for me. But anyways the come down is horrible and I feel like a scum bag for a couple days afterwards.

These days my brain can't handle that shit anymore and I feel that I've taken enough already from the experiences that I had. Maybe if an old friend had a quad of killer boomer's than I would on a special occasion. Perhaps go to like a secluded public trail or a house that doesn't have weirdos in it.
