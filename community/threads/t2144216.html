Re: Do you always throw up when mixing weed with alchohol?
Not always by a long shot, but on certain occasions and in certain situations yeah.

If you're borderline double vision drunk, you're probably going to want to take it easy on the ganj, or avoid it all together. Because hitting it will almost guarantee you will throw up.

However, if you're not that drunk, you should be fine, since normally weed actually helps you with nausea. But I have found that the more weed you smoke, the less alcohol it takes to get to a good level of fucked up-ness. It seems kind of obvious to me but some people don't think about it.

So just generally, if you're smoking and drinking, smoke less and drink less than if you were going to do just one of the two. The keyword here is synergy.
