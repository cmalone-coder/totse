Open carry?
I found this topic over at the firing line and was wondering what people's opinions are here.
http://www.thefiringline.com/forums/showthread.php?s=2518fb825d066f1ee1419fcb73aa7099&threadid=94624

There are very good points made on both sides. Some say that it goes along way to desensitizing the public to responsible firearm ownership and others say that it only sets you up for trouble.

I carry my .45 springfield open in a paddle holster when I'm on the way to go shoot. If I have to stop somewhere to get gas, food, etc. I leave it on just for convinience. Holsters are a pain in the ass sometimes to get off and on properly. Other than that I leave it in the car or at home.

Read the link to firing line before you jump to conclusions in either direstion. There is some good shit there.

New Mexico rescinded their concealed carry law about a month ago. The reason was the Albuquerque mayor didn't want concealed permits in his town. Unfortunately the state constitution has a provision in it prohibiting the state from making laws that don't give the cities the final say in situations like that. I was a sheriffs reserve deputy and could carry concealed legally, but I quit that when I tore up my knee. It's kinda hard to carry concealed in the desert with a big gun in the summer time, so I didn't very often.

I'm going to carry open for a few days everywhere I go, just as an experiment to see people's reactions. My neighborhood has alot of shady people and speed freaks. Alot fo shit gets stolen, but because I practice my draws and weapon retention drills with a friend in my front yard, my house never gets touched. It's a great deterent to crime.
