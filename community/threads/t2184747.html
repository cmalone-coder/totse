Obvious attempts at trolling notwithstanding, it simply makes sense to let even the most shocking and vile images onto Totse. Without this guiding principal, half (or perhaps more) of us could very well end up in jail for publishing things on here that go against the wills and whims of the authorities. Furthermore, all of this vast amount of information could be taken down simply because most of it is radical and subversive.

Just think about how many articles you have read that graphically describe the construction of high explosives, the synthesis of illegal drugs, the hacking of protected software, and, in this section, the undesirable sexual practices of certain people. That could all disappear in an instant if we allow a small group of people to control the voices of the rest. Therefore, it makes sense that anything should be fair ground on this site's forums, unless it is a deliberate attempt to disrupt the site itself (trolling).

And if you are so concerned about others' opinions of what you view while exercising your Constitutional rights, just hit the minimize button when they come bye and do not use them as an excuse for why YOU are uncomfortable viewing such material.
