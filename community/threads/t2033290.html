Class B is where Marijuana belongs. If you go over the list of Class C drugs, they are all drugs with widely accepted medical uses and are generally marketed in at least some western countries for these uses; however, these drugs have some potential to be abused in ways other than prescribed and are thereby regulated. Class B drugs generally do not have any widespread medical uses, and despite the push to get marijuana distributed medically, the truth simply is, as stated by the FDA on April 20, 2006, that "marijuana has a very high potential for abuse, no currently accepted medical use, and a lack of accepted safety for use under medical supervision." This is not the government restating the hypocracy of the 50s, but countering claims of marijuana's complete and undeniable safety, backed up by large amounts of sound evidence. It continues, "there is currently sound evidence that smoked marijuana is harmful"
http://www.fda.gov/bbs/topics/NEWS/2006/NEW01362.html

I understand that Class B is a UK designation, but evidence gathered here in the United States can still be used to refute it.
