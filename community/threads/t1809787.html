Christian heaven
I'm not actually christian, but I had this weird thought about the christian idea of heaven.

So if we assume the following christian beliefs:

God created us all

God didn't create us evil

God gave us free will which is why people are evil

Heaven is a completely perfect place with total love and no sins or hatred whatsoever

So when we get to heaven, do we still have free will?

If we do then it means heaven would be far from perfect and there'd at least be some sin. Or it could mean that God only lets people who are totally pure, follow the bible 100% and never do or think bad things ever. Which must mean hell must be pretty crowded right now. Or it could mean when we get to heaven god just totally erases our free will and turns us into mindless drones, which would just suck...
