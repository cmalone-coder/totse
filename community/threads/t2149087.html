Re: Cleaning a multi-chambered bong (Help)
I've found the best way to clean a gross bong is this...

1. Dump out all bong water/chunks/whatever you can get out with warm water. Rinse it around and get as much out as you can with warm water.

2. Get a bottle of Rubbing alcohol and dump it into the bong. Add regular table salt, or white rice into the bong, and shake like a mofo.

3. After shaking for several minutes you should see everything lift off the side of the chambers, and rinse it out with warm water until the alcohol smell is gone.

4. Fill your bong with cold water, and add a couple shots of mouthwash to the bong, let sit for a few hours or overnight, depending on how bad the bong is.

5. Rinse out bong, and refill with water. :D

You should now have a practically spotless bong, that has a slightly minty smell from the mouthwash. The smell should fade quickly, than it should just smell like your average bong.
