I do not know much about Satanism, but I do know a little about Alchemy. It is closely intertwined with a Christian philosophy of the soul, spirit, and body; of the cycle of rebirth; and of corruption versus purification. It also borrowed ideas from Greek theology, not necessarily as a belief in Greek gods and such but as archetypal characters that fit in with the alchemist's mystical views of nature.

The symbols were mostly for hiding the alchemist's secrets from the ordinary man. After all, they thought they were dabbling with divine methods of creation -- the powers that God used to create the world. It certainly wouldn't be good to let such information fall into the wrong hands. They went to great lengths to put all their work into code, which is one of the things that has hampered modern studies of ancient alchemy.

I'm not sure if they had some deeper meaning behind the symbols, though. For most of them, I suspect the answer is "no". Also, there are some materials that have several symbols that represent them.
