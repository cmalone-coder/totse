Acid-Best Possible First Trip
So I finally found acid last night on bicycle day. I took it at 10pm and played geometry wars until I started really feeling it (I broke 1 million on acid). When the time came that I felt I should be tripping balls everthing became more clear and interesting. After at least three tries, we made it out of my room.

Walking around a city on acid is amazing. Everything was so perfect; I could feel the happiness in my skin. I spent about an hour or two walking around and looking at things while writing down my thoughts. Flowers were amazing, trees were amazing, life was amazing. The best part of it all was standing right next to a train, just feeling the train move along beside me.

I thought about some crazy shit too: the 60's, why acid is illegal (it shouldnt be), and even trains, lol. I wrote some stuff in a book while I was tripping to descibe how I was viewing the world:

-normal is astounding
-visualizers are like acid for potheads, real life is the visualizer for ppl on acid.
-Everything is an adventure
-the future is our creation
-to really see everything, time must stop to evaluate each detail
and finally
-acid is like glasses that let you see more clearly what you can already see, but didnt know it.

My point is this- everyone should try acid at least once, it is a compleate mind opener. I hope everyone can have as possitive a time as I did.
