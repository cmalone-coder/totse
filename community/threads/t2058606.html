Re: Best way to Increase 40 yard sprint time?
Try band sprints.

http://www.jumpstretch.com/

Also, don't make sprinting an endurance workout. Warm up, but not too much. Sprint. Rest. Sprint. Rest. Sprint. Rest. Sprint. Rest. Sprint. Go home.

Think of it as a high intensity low volume workout. Give yourself enough time to recover between sets, so your "force production" doesn't drop drastically. Vary the distances but keep them short.

Also, I read somewhere, oly lifters had the best 30 yard/meter??!?! sprint, highest vertical, and the second highest flexibility (second to gymnasts) out of all the athletes at the 'lympics. The page I read this what unsourced, and I don't have a source either so take it for what you may.

If you have the time, try each of these methods separately for 3 weeks each. Test yourself once a week and see what works and what doesn't.
