Re: p2p vs. psuedoephedrine meth....
Sorry, but it is not correct. It's all about optical isomers.

Methamphetamine that is from l-ephedrine or l-psuedoephedrine will always reduce to d-meth. Straight up d-methamphetamine is the strongest form of meth. It will have a buzz that is characteristic by an immediate intense rush, the kind with major teeth clench, with major concentration, motivation etc, but will not be as long lasting (and of course with other tell-tale meth shit like hair and body tingles). Now, whenever a non-active or slightly active meth-like substance (i.e. l-ephedrine, l-psuedoephedrine, l-methamphetamine) is introduced into the system, it is beleived that the optical l isomers take up the same enzymes as the d ones to metabolize, so that affects the buzz. d-meth with a lot of l-meth or l-ephedrine in it will not be a rush at first, but more of a mellow uppitty feeling, that'll still keep you going good, but not with intense rushes or major teeth clench, and the buzz lasts much longer.
So different reduction methods and how the chemists cleans his or her product determin the type of buzz.

Lets say that the chemist was cheap and just did a good a/b and re-crystalization and washing on their product. It would be clean, but it would have unreacted l-ephedrine in it. So meth from a sound high-yeilding reduction method like Pd + H2 or LAH or Birch will produce a lot of d-meth and little to no l-ephedrine, giving you the edgey teeth clench type. But something less yeilding like HI or H2/Ag/Ultrasound (yes I had to throw that in there ) would have more l-ephedrine, so a less edgy and longer lasting buzz, with not much rush, but still concentration.

Now if the chemist wanted nothing but d-meth and took the time to separate it, then the buzz will be balls to the wall rush teeth clench major tingles, and you'll need less.

Now P2P dope is different. Any product of reductive amination of an amine and ketone yeilds 50/50 racemate product. That means that after reductive amination of P2P and methylamine, one will have a product that is 50% d-methamphetamine, and 50% l-methamphetamine. A buzz rom straight up P2P dope is little teeth clench, uppity but not much rush, little tingly but long lasting type.
