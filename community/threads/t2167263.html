Re: Possible bong/pipe?
Thats gonna blow. You need a proper bowl, you can't be smoking off loose change. And don't smoke out of copper anything [aluminum is no good either]. I would suggest you buy a glass bowl and shaft, this way you can make bongs to your hearts content. If you're worried about your parents finding it then just get a stainless steel bowl. If you keep it clean you can toss it in a drawer with some screws and stuff and nobodies the wiser. And your last option would be some sort of brass pipe fitting. Just look at the brass fittings at home depot and you'll find like ten different kinds of bowls [BRASS not copper, brass is thick and dull and the fittings are threaded, copper is thin and shiny and the fittings are bare so they can be soldered together]. You could even check the house for a good sized stainless steel socket [you know, like for a socket wrench], its not ideal but it works.

Once you have a solid bowl making a bong is usually no more difficult that poking a hole in a bottle. But don't use some ghetto ass bowl you made from light bulbs and pennies. Brass fittings and sockets are ghetto enough.
