TBH, i wont be much help, but heres my 2 cents.

If you're looking for a fancy looking watch [imo] like those seikos, eco-citizens, bulova, rolex, etc then i cant really give you an opinion. Nothing against them, just that ive never owned one, but i do think they're kickass

If you're looking for a trusty brand then check out CASIO. Ive had 4 casios in my lifetime and is the only brand ive ever used. Only reason ive had 4 is 'cause ive personally switched to new ones, not because they fucked up or anything of the sort. Otherwise, im sure id still have that good ol' casio everyone had [model f-91]. Idk if you've heard about the wave ceptors by casio. You choose a city you live in, or the closest one and every night the watch syncs with the nearest atomic clock around the world. For example, i live in the northeast [usa] so i choose NYC as the city and every night the atomic clock in Colorado syncs my watch with the "correct time" in NYC. Pretty badass. You have the option to turn it off, too. They also have the G-Shocks which are beasts! Those watches look like they could get ran over by an 18 wheeler and survive - those, though, may surpass 200 bucks.

But yea. if you dont wanna check out casio.....
