Something that pisses me off here
OK, its most likely that none of you know me, but i've been lurking here for quite a while, so i feel i know some of you, or at least who you pretend to be here, pretty well.
Something that's been pissing me off for quite some time is that whenever anyone asks to be hooked up or mailed drugs for some reason, everyone jumps in and calls them pathetic, says they're a cop or narc or whatever.
what i don't understand is how asking to be hooked up through the internet is any different from asking a friend to buy for you, or ringing you dealer on the phone. why is it that when someone asks in the forum they are desperate? this is a forum dedicated to drugs, i don't see how its pathetic to ask for drugs, but totally acceptable to tell everyone about the time they were so smashed they couldn't get up to go to the bathroom. i don't have a problem with those 'i was so smashed' stories, they're amusing to read, keep them coming.
and as for the 'piss off, cop' attitude, as if a cop is going to bother trying to trick someone into selling them a few grams of weed, they most probably don't give a shit about anything except their pay. Anyway, if you did mail anything and it was a cop you were mailing it to, they aren't going to know where you mailed it from, or who you are. and even if you gave an address for payment or something, they couldn't prove it wasn't someone setting you up or something.
what a lot of people here don't seem to understand is that not everyone has an easy time getting decent drugs, and in some cases, such as mine, weed is like the only thing available. i mean, i'd love to try acid or speed, but because of my isolation due to where i live i can't.
what i am trying to say is that if you don't want to help people who can't get hooked up then don't, but don't flame them, and don't flame me either for writing this. i just think it had to be said. maybe it already has been said, i don't know, i'm not here all the time.
i think i'll go now...
