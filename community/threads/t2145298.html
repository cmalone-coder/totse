You made 50 pills last a month- and most prescriptions are 1-2 tablets PRN/4-6 hours, which would mean on a bad day, you could down anywhere from 8 to 12 of them. A typical junkie is going to "need" both available tablets every 4 hours regardless...and that rate of consumption will leave the pill bottle empty in less than a week.

Either way in your situation I think the worst that can happen if you gradually approach the possibility of an increased dosage would be for him to say no.
