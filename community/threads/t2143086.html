The word drug is a very broad term that people use to describe anything supplemental thing that "you take". We're talking about psychoactive drugs. Drugs are an external stimuli influencing certain neurotransmitter systems. An example of this would be that if one were to remove the rna segment that is encoded for serotonin production from a human cell, insert the rna into a bacterial RNA segment, and to insert the bacterial produced serotonin back into a human, the serotonin wouldn't pass through the BBB.
