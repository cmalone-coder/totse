Re: Making Techno Music?
I never liked FL. Right now I use Logic Pro and Reason. I have a vst wrapper so that I can use any vst in Logic. The only plugins I ever actually use are Vanguard and Melodyne. Really, any program will do it. Try out some demos of some that you are interested in and find which one you like most interface-wise. I think Reason is the best sequencer out there but it's lack of plugin support is a little annoying. Logic is more complicated, but it can do EVERYTHING that you would ever need it to. It's all up to personal preference though. I can't stand FL Studio or Ableton but they are both very popular. It's really up to what you like.

Re: Making Techno Music?
Kaossilator- fucking shit
Kaoss Pad/KP Mini- good for live, but all the effects they provide can be done with a computer with a joystick plugged in.
Ableton Live 7- complicated as fuck but incredible for live performance as the name suggests duh.
FL Studio- Easy to learn, and whoever said it will only make basic stuff is obviously too retarded to use it properly. I make breakcore with it and it is amazing.
Renoise- an oldschool tracker with modern effects etc built in, good for chiptune

Oh and what type of techno do you want to make. A lot of people group a lot of electronica under the term techno when the music they are describing is not actually techno.
