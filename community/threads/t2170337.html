A fun little survey.
So, I was watching some documentary thingie following the Essex police about in their Mitsubishi Lancer Evo 9, and I thought it'd be something fun to do to see what everybodys local constabulary uses for their patrol/pursuit cars.

My local Cleveland Constabulary use the successor to the Volvo 850 (I can't remember what it is called), but the T5 version, and obviously heavily tuned. I've heard one of them go past at full throttle. Twas orgasmic. Reminded me of my dads old Quattro. (EDIT: Looking it up, I think it is the V50 T5 AWD)

For a high speed pursuit they also have a bright silver Audi RS4, which I'm guessing has had the speed limiter removed. Never seen that moving, only sat controlling traffic at an accident on the motorway.

And for undercover cars they use Vauxhall Vectra's and Omega's, with the V6 engines, and some Lexus or other. Fortunately due to them having 4 extra aerials on the roof, you can usually tell one and cut speed accordingly......

So what does totse's local police use for their patrol cars? It'll be interesting to find out.
