Re: dry hydrogen chloride gas
The cheapest easiest way to dry HCl gas is by dumping salt into concentrated >98% sulphuric acid. The reagents are dirt cheap, and the gas is automatically dry cause there is no H2O in the reaction to begin with.

Muriatic acid when heated gives off HCl and H2O so you need to bubble it through H2SO4 to dry it.

If you can't get 98 percent H2SO4 you aren't looking hard enough.

I dump sulphuric acid and 35% hydrogen peroxide (peroxysulphuric acid) down my drain to burn out hairballs.

Re: dry hydrogen chloride gas
Nuglobe: Yes I know, but they will never need H2SO4 any purer so they won't need to vacuum distill.

As far as how to gas, just bubble through with your tube in the liquid. you can do this inside if you control the amount of gas produced. A little extra escaping into the room won't hurt anything. Alot escaping will rust anything metal and burn your lungs so if you go overboard with the gas generation or are worried do the gassing outside.

Alternatively you can rig up a gassing bottle that has another tube going off the (sealed) top going into a bottle of NaOH solution. This now can be done inside.
