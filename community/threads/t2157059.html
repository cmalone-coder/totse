And so my friend just committed suicide...
not sure if this quite belongs in here. But I figured as this is a relationship type thing then its relatively appropriate. If any mods think it should be moved then feel free to move this thread.

Might I just say before hand that I don't really expect any answer. I just sort of wanted to vent
Righto, so the story. I met this guy last december when my friend brought him as her date to our formal. Cool guy, we hit it off and talked heaps etc etc. Friendship blossomed I suppose. Unfortunately for me he lives in Canberra which is about a 3-4 hour drive so I could never go down and visit him. Also I'm 16 and my parents aren't all too fond of me travelling far away to see some guy they don't know... understandable but still. This essentially meant we just talked on the phone or the internet. We of course had our ups and downs as all friends do.
I knew from quite early on the he was depressed. He told me how much he wanted to kill himself and at first I just dismissed it, thinking that he wouldn't actually do it. This was certainly the case for quite some time as he first started telling me how much he wanted to in about January. I then started to realise that he was completely serious when he told me that he was getting cyanide or some other poison from another suicidal mate of his. Of course being the friend I am I tried to reason with him and all. But realised it was all futile, and although I didn't completely give up I didn't stress about it as much. In the end there is nothing that I can actually do.

Cut forward 4 months or so and things seemed to be going far better for him. We'd just resolved a fight between us, he was getting laid on a regular basis. Things were certainly look up. I suppose this was the calm before the storm in a sense.
Yesterday my friend (the one who brought him to our formal) told me that he had sewn his lips together during one of his depressed moods and run away from home. This was the day after his 18th birthday. So he was missing yesterday and understandably I was stressing out. Then today the same friend told me that they had found him. Dead.
I flipped out and had no idea how to react. I just stayed in my english classroom and cried for a bit. Then went on to my next classes in a bit of a daze. I'm still in a bit of shock and I thought a good idea would be to rant to you guys about it.

Anyway, I don't really have anything to ask from you guys so I suppose you can just tell any similar experiences you might have had in the past. And how you specifically dealt with them.

Then again you can always do the "dumb emo kid, good riddance" thing, although I tell you now that it won't be particularly appreciated.

Cheers,
