Morphine Trip Report
Well, I was bored last night, so I opened up a bottle of 60mg tabs of Morphine sulphate....

10:45PM-I took 1 60Mg tab orally. Immedaitly after I took the tab(Like within 10 minutes) I felt happy. I am assuming that this is a placebo affect because the drug requires at least 15 minutes to work.

11:30PM-1 Glass of Black Swan Chardonney(sp?).

12:15-12:50PM-Intense heart beat, so much that I could hear it. Lots of energy, But I was in bed trying to sleep, so it did almost nothing. A very scary feeling, like my heart was gonna jump out from my chest.

1:10AM-I fell into a restless sleep.

9:00AM-Woke up very dizzy, but can feel pain(When I tryed codiene, i took it about the same time, and the next day, I could not feel much pain.). No dry mouth, and no headache, but my head sorta feels "full". But its a very light feeling.

Well, there you go. Any comments? Was what I experienced normal?
