Dmt
So, some people I may or may not be very good friends with finally came in contact with a dealer who sells DMT. I was a tripsitter last night and just watching it got me excited to try it since everyone came out of it ecstatic, smiling, and overwhelmingly changed for the better.

Although my only problem is, the only experience I have with drugs is marijuana, some painkillers, and adderall. I have never hallucinated, with the exception of a few weird shapes when I would get really high. I am already an extremely....existential person. I feel as if I'm on a slightly higher intelligence plane than many of my friends.

I tend to overanalyze things when I'm both sober and intoxicated. Not to the point that I begin to panic, but I feel as if this type of extreme trip would put me into a panic attack I couldn't get out of. I am very curious to truly feel an 'alternate,weightless dimension', but I feel as if it will be so overwhelming that I won't be able to just 'accept it' as others have said they do. Does anyone have experience with trying this after only smoking marijuana? Were you able to handle it or did your anxiety make it a bad trip?

Re: Dmt
I'm kind of predisposed to making ridiculous connections and having dillusions of grandeur that seem like they should affect my life and all life for that matter, so I can sort of relate.

In my experience, when dealing with any powerful dimension altering drug, you have to go into it with the mindset that it is only really a drug, and that your mind is going to be in the end much stronger than a few milligrams of some obscure substance. It's all really just mind over matter. If you are paranoid and scared, the chances are you will have one hell of a scary ass paranoia riddled trip.

Of course you can't control what happens or how you will feel, but you can help it. Remembering that it is really just a drug, and that there is more to life should help you to some extent. Now on saying that, I haven't actually experimented with DMT, so I don't have any personal experience. But while using other drugs such as DXM, mushrooms, LSD, and Salvia, I've found this technique to be quite helpful, seeing as I myself had issues with ego-loss and seemingly life changing breakthroughs while just under the influence of Marijuana. Drug abuse becomes an acquired skill, and some intense drug abusers, do not even have that skill.

Good luck.
