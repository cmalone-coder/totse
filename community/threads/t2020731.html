Questions from new moderators
With so many new moderators added recently, with SEEMINGLY little discussion over them being added and all that, I thought that some of the newest mods might have some questions for the more experienced mods, and also maybe some of the more experienced mods might have some suggestions and things to keep in mind for the newer mods.

Just thought there should be a thread for this kind of discussion.

One thing I would like to say regarding moderating in general, is to be wary of over moderating. You don't need to go looking for problems to fix. Don't just close threads and edit posts to be using your moderator 'powers', only do it if its necessary. Doing this will lead to accidently doing things you shouldn't, like closing threads you don't need to, which is going to severely piss someone off.

So thats just my two cents.

Maybe some of the older moderators that made some mistakes in the past can relate some stories regarding those as well, as learning from mistakes is of course, one of the most effective ways of learning something.

Re: Questions from new moderators
Editing thread titles for fun is just like editing posts for fun. If you know the poster, or are doing it for a legitimate reason its okay.

For example, if someone posts personal information in a thread, you edit the post to get rid of the personal information. Regardless of punishment, warnings, etc etc, you have to get rid of the p ronal info.

I think editing posts shoul ONLY be done when its ABSOLUTELY necessary, though amongsts friends screwing around wi th things can be fun but theres a very fine line concerning editing posts.

I edit thread titles to remove "Official" when people start "The Official Music Piracy Thread" and other things like that. "Official" implies that its 'from totse'. If someone started a thread like that, then posted some really terrible incriminating shit like kiddy porn in it, it would be just like someone starting a kiddy porn club called "the TOTSE kiddy porn club".

Also I think editing thread titles can be useful for helping keep threads on topic. If threads have misleading titles, they can cause problems inside the threads, so editing thread titles can be useful in that sense.

But I think for new mods you should basically just NOT edit posts at all.

Its not your duty to micromanage threads, or even your forums to any real degree. I see some of the newer mods doing this, and it will just lead to prolems I promise!
