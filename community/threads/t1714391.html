Guide to Armed Robbery

By Jihad Joe.

People seem to be under the impression that armed robbery is a bad idea, but actually it’s very easy and quite profitable. I’m sure you’ll all say that this thread is shitty, but that’s just because you don’t have the balls to do it.

It’s really not as difficult or as risky as you think, even the lowliest of drug addicts and dim-witted people can get away with this crime, so if you have half a brain then you’ll easily be able to pull this off.

In this guide I’ll discuss all the things you’ll need to know to effectively rob a store such as 7-11 or somewhere similar. Bank robbery is not covered in the guide because it has a very low success rate and requires much more detailed information about the specific security systems that different banks use.



Research & Planning

Doing a bit of research and properly planning is extremely important and is vital to the success of your operation.

Firstly, you must make certain that you are familiar with the area so you know exactly how to get there and how to get out. You need to plan the place that you’re going to rob as well as the day and time of the robbery; they will have larger amounts of money on different days and at different times. If you’re unsure of what day they have more money then don’t worry, it isn’t that important, but if you go at night then they’ll have all of the days earnings. Don’t go too late though; stores that are open late are weary of robbers at night so they put the money in a safe a few hours before closing.

You also need to acquire a car which should be freshly stolen and ought to have false licence plates attached otherwise it may be identified as stolen before the robbery, and you also must decide where you’ll park the car and where you will drive to afterwards. (there’s plenty of info on totse about car theft)

Disguise

After you’ve committed the crime the police will talk to witnesses to get a good description of the offender (you). What the police will ask about are things such as height, build, gender, race, hair colour, clothing, accent, and any prominent features i.e. big nose, large scar, tattoos, lazy eye etc.

What you’ll need to do is convincingly alter or disguise your features. Obviously, it is very important to wear a balaclava or ski mask so that you can’t be recognized easily. That said, there will still be certain features listed above which you can’t hide with a ski mask.

Of course, you can’t change your height or build (in a short space of time) and men won’t be able to convincingly look like women, but it is possible for women to disguise themselves as men as long as you wear a ski mask – just tape down your boobs and wear bulky, masculine clothing and don’t talk.

As for race, get some brown make up (if you’re white) and apply it around you eye area, which should be the only part of your skin showing. If you have blue or green eyes then wear dark brown contact lenses. I’m not sure whether black people could wear white makeup and actually appear to be a white person and not just look like a clown, but it’s worth a try.

Hair colour can easily be changed and should be done shortly after the robbery. If you like you could dye it a new colour just before the robbery and then dye it back to your original colour straight afterwards before any of your friends or family see you with the temporary colour.

Always wear special clothing designated for the robbery, you mustn’t be seen in it before or afterwards, as someone may recognise you. Clothing ought to be plain black because it’s indistinct, I also think it’s best to wear a large jacket or coat because it hides your skin and build

These disguises are optional but I recommend using at least some form disguise, preferably gender or race, so that when the offender is described as a six foot tall black man with purple hair, and you are a six foot tall white guy with brown hair then you will be eliminated as a suspect completely.

Just a note though, your disguise must be convincing, otherwise you’ll have defeated the purpose of wearing a disguise.

Entry

There are a few different methods of entry -



#1.Walk into the store as a normal customer, collect a few items, and proceed to the checkout. When you get there, either tell them or pass them a note informing them that this is a robbery and they must hand over all the cash. The downside of this is that you will be showing your face.

#2.A group of people storm in through the entrance wearing ski masks making it instantly clear that this is a robbery.

#3. A variation of #2. If the register is nearer to the back of the store, you should enter though the back entrance that the staff use, so that the staff don’t have enough time to set off an alarm while you’re approaching them.

#4. Drop down from the roof. Not entirely practical for such a small operation, but there are situations where it may be appropriate, so keep this in mind. (I have heard of this method being used successfully at a Sydney shopping center where the thieves used the roof as an escape.)

#5. Have a friend inside the store pretending to be a customer and get them to call you when they think it’s a good time for the main group to enter i.e. when there are fewer customers or to ensure there aren’t any policemen already in the store. This is probably over-cautious.



The Robbery



Ideally, you should be armed. If you choose not to arm yourself then there’s a chance that you won’t be taken seriously and will just be told to piss off. You don’t want that to happen. On the other hand, armed robbery has far greater consequences if by chance you get caught. Either use a knife or a gun, or a fake gun.

It is imperative that you do not kill or cause harm to anyone. This will increase the severity of your crime. Instead of just being armed robbery, you will have also committed assault or even murder, thus making you a dangerous criminal and the police will allocate more money and resources towards your arrest. Your chances of success will have diminished.

The simple armed robbery of a store is something that can be done solo, but you might like to have a larger group until you have confidence. There is certainly no need to have more than four, and they’ll need to be close friends that won’t blab or boast.

If you do have a group, then have one person to give orders, another to round up the customers into one corner to keep them in view so they don’t go and call the cops, another to stand at the entrance to keep lookout, and if you have a fourth person in your group then they can either wait in the car, or they can give orders to a second attendant. You must all wear gloves.

Once you have entered the store and approached the counter where the staff are, then you must give orders either verbally or written. You can hand the attendant a note which simply reads “This is a robbery, give me all your money” or “Your money or your life!”. If you do choose to hand them a note, then it is essential that your writing is legible so as not to cause confusion or delays. Likewise, if you are giving orders verbally you will need to be clear and concise. Don’t yell too loudly because this will scare the attendant and make them piss themselves or be shaky and slow.

Now don’t go and get the money yourself, make the staff do it. This keeps them busy hence preventing them from activating any alarms or calling the pigs. Hand them a bag (you should have at least two) to put the money in and if there is more than one staff member, make the other one fill up a bag with cigarettes. Don’t use plastic bags; they’re too fiddly. I suggest using calico-type bags preferably with a zip although that’s not necessary and will possibly just waste time.

Remain calm and focused throughout. You need also to be sober and not on any drugs, so you have a clear head.



Escape



Once you have got the cash you will need to leave the scene very quickly. Don’t have the car parked directly outside the store but rather have it parked behind the store, or around a corner close-by. As soon as you leave they’ll be on the phone to the cops and you don’t want them to have a description of your car, so it must be out of view.

The driver of the car needs to be skilled and confident, and know his/her way around the area. Don’t speed; it will only attract unwanted attention. It’s a good idea to have a second vehicle at an appropriate location. If a cop car isn’t following you then you can park close by the second vehicle and swap, abandoning the first stolen car. Then drive to a safe house or leave the car a reasonable distance from your house and walk the rest of the way. Remember, the car is stolen so you won’t want to have it parked right outside your house.

If somehow you find yourself being followed by a police car (not likely) then abandon the car not too far away from the second car, and run through the streets or bushland or wherever and once you’ve outrun the police get into the second car and leave. Storm water drains are infinitely useful in these situations. I must emphasise the importance of the positioning of the second car. It ought to be tailored to suit your area, this is why you need to know the neighbourhood like back of your hand. You should aim to have the second car parked at the end of a long trail or area of parkland that you know well, the pigs will easily get lost in the dark if they’re not familiar with the landscape, plus they’ll have to chase you on foot, and we all know how fat piggies are.

Notes

Don’t worry about any cameras if you’re wearing a ski mask. Their primary purpose is as a deterrent for shoplifters and thieves, and so that if anything major occurs the police will use the footage to help determine the order of events. Don’t waste your time smashing them.

Fitness is also required if you need to outrun cops and jump fences etc.

I should also add that most my advice is over-cautious. You don’t need a weapon, or a ski mask, or two getaway vehicles. You could simply walk into McDonalds with a syringe and ask for money and a Big Mac, leave the store showing your face to the cameras and wait to catch a bus right outside the store and still not get caught. That’s what most robbers do. But I wanted to give advice explaining how to rob and stay out of jail.

Btw. I’m very tired, hence the poor spelling/grammar and repetitive use of the same words. And I apologise for the length, but I can’t possibly go into detail and still keep this short.
