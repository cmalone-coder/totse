Socks = ambitions?
I think I may be on to something here.

The past few days I haven't really done too much but fuck around my house, mostly because jetlag's still being such a damn bitch. But because of that, I noticed something even more that I thought of a while ago. I noticed that the days when I wear socks even around the house, I went about my business feeling completely normal, not wasteful or anything. Today I forgot to put socks on after my shower, and the entire day I've been feeling like I was being completely worthless, wasting the full fucking day.

About 2 hours ago I put some socks on, and now I'm feeling more normal, and not like I'm wasting my time anymore. Maybe it's because I feel more "prepared" to go out and do shit instead of wasting time inside my own house? Maybe it's because I always sleep with my socks off, so it feels like I shouldn't be doing anything if I'm not wearing any?

Could life's ambitions be hidden inside a foot-shaped rag of cloth?
