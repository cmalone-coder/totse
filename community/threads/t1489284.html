Please excuse my little outburst. I sometimes forget that this is all stuff I had to learn at some point too.

As far as reading signals goes, there is some reading that you can do, but it sometimes hard and more often, it is not that important.

What you need to do is be positive assumptive. That means you ASSUME that she is giving you the green light and you proceed with high levels of confidence till she makes it clear that she does not want that.

You have to understand that even though you may think that a woman has made up her mind 100% yes or no if she wants you at the time that you get the ambiguous signal, that is often not the case. Her mind can change quickly, you can sometimes change it, and there is a continuum of different level of interest. She probably likes you more than so and so, but not as much as so and so, and she really likes X about you, but not so much Y, and she wants to know more about Z etc. The point is that the whole thing is an evolving process and part of you that she is evaluating is how confident you are, and how much you are willing to do to have her (make a move). She may even be unsure of her own signals and your attitude can lead her into a decision. Its not like every girl knows exactly what they want 100% of the time. Usually they don't, same for guys. Often a woman who sleeps with a guy and then regrets it met some guy that she allowed to make her decision for her.

If you make a move, you give her a chance to say yes if she wants to, you make yourself more attractive (although that is often not enough), and you can sway a girl that is on the fence.

The down side? She could say no. If a bold move pushes her away, there is probably nothing you could have done.
