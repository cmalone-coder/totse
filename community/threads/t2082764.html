Just walk away from this relationship with the lessons learned (and the lessons you will eventually learn once you aren't so emotionally attached to the scenario) and be thankful for the fun time you had. Nobody is "wrong" in this scenario, the chemistry/timing wasn't there. Quit trying to "fix" something that either wasn't "broken" in the first place.....or can't be fixed any how. You are just delaying the inevitable.....you going on with your life and being better because of it.
