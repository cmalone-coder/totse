LONG: A friend and his girl
Once upon a time, one of my good friends, Jay, met this girl, Lisa. I met Lisa sometime near the beginning of the school year. She said that she knew Jay. I became pretty good friends with Lisa. One day, she told me that she liked Jay. She liked him a lot. This was about a month before the Homecoming dance at school, but Jay already had someone to go with, so I couldn't really tell him about it. Also, Lisa had a boyfriend, whom she ended up going with, but she really didn't like him much. She ditched him at the dance, which wasn't a really nice thing to do at all, but that's not part of the story.

Anyway, Lisa ends up hanging out with me at the dance for a long time, because we kind of got locked outside... but yeah. We talk about Jay, what she thinks of him, and what I thought she should do. I get to know her fairly well. A few days pass, and she finally decides to tell him online what she thinks of him. He tells me about it, and how happy he was to hear it, because he had his eye on her as well.

A little while later, and they are going out. Soon enough, they fall in love with each other and are practically inseperable. Jay talked about her all the time, and I must say, I don't blame him. She is a very smart person, funny, nice, caring, and very attractive. They are together for about four and a half months until their relationship comes to an abrupt stop.

She called him one day about two weeks ago to tell him that she was very confused. Jay, not knowing what to say, told her that she just needs to be with him again, just hang out, and everything would be fine.

But she insisted that it was different now. She said that she felt as if she was breaking away from him, and couldn't do anything about it. At that moment, Jay dropped the phone, completely speechless and emotionless. He had no idea what had happened.

The next time he saw her was at a party at a friend's house to which I was invited as well. It was definately very strange to see them both in the same field of view and not in each others' arms. Jay was in a state of denial for about two days until it finally hit him. Every Monday night, Jay and I go to jew school. He was in an extremely depressed mood the whole time; he looked like he was about to break. He was also like that in school that day. We skipped the second class and he took me where the two of them used to go hang out, behind a large hill in the back of the building. He basically poured his emotions out to me for an hour.

While the two were going out, I talked to both of them quite often, as they are both good friends of mine. Lisa told me many things that she didn't even tell Jay; she kept saying that I was the friend she trusted the most. If any of you remember, a while back I posted about a friend of mine cheating on his girl, but it was really accidental and he felt horrible about it afterward. He was debating on whether or not to tell her. In the end he didn't, and I respected his desision. That friend was Jay, and Lisa was his girl.

Something I want to add in is that since Jay broke up with her, he started smoking pot. He says he will only do it while he's getting his life back on track, but I fear that it will just cause more problems than it will solve. He's really getting into it now, and it kind of worries me. I mean, I have a positive opinion toward pot; if I got the chance to smoke, I'd smoke, although I never have. He began blowing all his cash on it recently, and it might get to be a big problem.

Well anyway, here it is now, about a week and a half later. This past Saturday, the three of us (Jay, Lisa, and I) were all invited to a party at a friend's house. Things are going a little better; Jay wasn't feeling as down as he was the past Monday. He told me he came to the party high though, which he regretted because he had didn't have any idea of what was happening half the time. That only lasted the first hour or so. About halfway into the party, a bunch of us are hanging out on the trampoline outside in the dark. Jay and this other girl, Karen, were "chillin", you know. They didn't kiss or anything, but they got pretty close. Now Lisa is just hanging out somewhere, talking to some people. She feels horrible about what she did to Jay, because she still loved him very much. She just didn't feel the same way anymore, and felt that it would be best to break the relationship off instead of dragging on a lie. I have to agree with her there; even though it hurt both of them, it hurt much less than it would have if she just continued in the relationship without the same feelings. In addition it is also very difficult to tell someone who loves you as much as Jay loved Lisa that she just didn't feel the same anymore.

A friend of Lisa, Carly, is an alright person, but she has a repitition of telling people things they really don't need to know (a.k.a. GOSSIP). After seeing Jay and Karen, she goes over to tell Lisa that she thinks they were making out on the trampoline, setting off a chain reaction. Lisa becomes very upset at Jay for something he didn't even do, and Karen becomes even more upset at Carly for spreading shit like that. I know I just confused a lot of you, but please try to bare with me.

Now, Jay and Lisa are both upset at each other; Jay because of the way Lisa broke his heart, and avoiding him at school (she had reason to, although not good... she didn't want to cry around him, which she undoubtedly would've done). Lisa was mad because she believed Carly. She wasn't ready to see Jay with someone else, and it made her feel even worse.

I know this is already excruciatingly long, but if you've gotten to here, it gets good now. And it's almost done, too. Everyone kind of forgets about the situation and goes and has a fun time dancing and stuff inside. Lisa and I were still outside though, on the hammock, and later on the trampoline. I talked to her about Jay, she told me how she felt and everything... I just needed both sides of the story, I guess. But after she was pretty much done explaining everything, we just kind of laid next to each other staring up. Then she said something to me. It went something like, "You know, you're one of the few people who I really trust. You are one of my greatest friends, and you've helped me through so much stuff." It made me feel good that I was really helping out between them; they both needed it.

After a little while of both of us just lying there, I just put my arm around her, kind of a half-assed hug thing, hah. Soon she kind of gets closer in with me. I had an idea of what was going through her head, but I'll never know for sure... Anyway, I start running my fingers over her side, and she makes this noise, kind of like a pleasurable sigh.. I hope you see where this is going. I didn't know why I was doing it at the time, it felt kind of wrong.. but so right at the same time. I could tell she felt the same. She was breathing heavily by the time someone came out and yelled out looking for her. We quickly stopped and he came to tell us her dad was here. She left. Shortly after I left as well, the feeling of her right next to me completely filling my mind. I couldn't sleep much that night, just thinking about it. I haven't talked to her about what happened since then, nor have I told anybody.

What I wanted to ask the wise members of this board is, what's going through this girl's head?! I mean, she's in love with Jay, decides that she just doesn't feel the same about him anymore over a period of about two weeks, they break up, his heart is broke, she feels like an asshole for doing it... then, she goes along and at the party and gets.. close with me. I don't know. I'm confused about this one. I've only really talked to one person about this, and she suggested two things: One being that she's getting feelings for me, which I highly doubt. Even if she did, I wouldn't be able to have a relationship with her, despite her good attributes, after what she did to my best friend. You know the saying, bros before hoes..

The other thing she suggested was that she was just trying to get back at Jay for what she originally thought he did with Karen. I thought about it, but then decided that she's really not the kind of person who would do such a thing; basically using me to get back at him.

Help?
