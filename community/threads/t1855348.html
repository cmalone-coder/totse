Re: AM, Gotta, Lucky, etc.. Please help Swim out?!?
Here is SWIL's recommendations for a 14g reaction:

Standard:

-14 grams Pseudo/Ephedrine

-11.2 grams of Red Phosphorus

-16.8 grams of Iodine crystals

-14 ml(cc) of dH2O

Adjustments:

-RP: It is first very important to have a relative idea of the cleanliness of one's own RP. An idea of what Lab-Grade looks like, and also knowing what really poor-grade RP looks like, will help one to define a personal range of RP. This range will help one to determine the amount of RP that is needed for any particular reaction.

When using Lab-Grade, the ratios can be scaled down as low as .3 or .5 to 1, RP:E. If one is using a big sticky ball of poorly cleaned RP, then the ratios can be scaled up as high as 1.2:1, RP:E.

With RP from match books, if it is cleaned well enough that it can be made into powder, usually the .8:1, RP:E, is the way to go.

-I2 crystals: If it is Lab or Regent grade, the ratios may be scaled down as low as 1:1, but SWIL believes this may be pushing the lower limit threshold.

If it is from tincture, which is most likely the case, then the ratios should be set from 1.2 or 1.5 to 1, I2:E. So when doing a 10 gram Pseudo/Ephedrine reaction, anywhere from 12 to 15 grams of Iodine crystals should be used depending on how clean the user feels it is.

-H2O: Only distilled (dH2O) should be used. A 1 ml to 1 gram ratio, of dH2O to E should be the standard. 14 grams E = 14 mls of dH2O.

When using I2 crystals from tincture, SWIL was always taught to scale down the amount of water in a reaction by 1 ml for every 4 grams of I2. The logic behind this, is that Iodine crystals can hold a lot of water, and it can become very easy to mess up ratios by the amount that it can sometimes contain. Another way around this is to dry the I2 crystals by storing them in an airtight container with some calcium chloride or silica gels(Kitty litter wrapped in a coffee filter should work in a pinch!)

SWIL always sets his reactions for 36 hours, using about 135-160 degrees farenheit. SWIL doesn't judge the time by weight of precursors. SWIL believes that 4 grams of phed will be reduced in the same time as 28 grams of phed, if all ratios and temperatures are kept consistent. There may be some debate on this, but SWIL would rather be sure that his goods are completely reduced, rather than settle for a mixture of pseudo/meth.

The container should be airtight(not allowing additional air IN). Therefore, a balloon, condensor, or tube into water will probably be used. SWIL would suggest the condensor, as it seems to get the most out of the ingredients. The reason the container need be airtight is NOT because air hinders the reaction, but rather because both H2O and precious HI will escape as they boil away. One will want to keep that all "inside", so as to more efficiently monitor the ratio of ingredients. The water level should stay very close to the same throughout the entire reaction. If it should lower, just add more dH2O back to the original water line.

Hope this helps.

Re: AM, Gotta, Lucky, etc.. Please help Swim out?!?
14 grams would probably be around a 500-1000 ml. If a 750 could be found, that would probably be optimal.

SWIL always stirred by grabbing the flask and swirling till the goop flings up the flask walls. Not too high though, because it should all be able to now start sliding down the glass. This helps the reflux effect, by allowing more surface area to come in contact with the precursors, and will also help to judge a complete reaction. Swirl as often as possible, although with a round-bottom flask it is not as necessary(as the shape encourages mixing of ingredients). SWIL sometimes swirled about twice an hour, and everything seemed to work out fine.

When it starts to slide easily off the glass, and the color of the fluid lightens from dark brown to yellow or clear, and the marroon RP will quickly settle at the bottom of the flask... then this reaction should be stated as done. NEVER base a "complete" reaction upon only color. A condensor will cause the fluid to clear quickly, and if too much I2 was added, it may never go yellow or clear, but rather just stay dark. This excess I2 can be removed during the A/B.

Lukky D
