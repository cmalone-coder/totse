International Havens for Weed
Weed smoker? Traveling? On the run?

I did research.

These listed havens have the most lax and kind laws (or absence of laws) regarding use, possession, production, and trafficking of psychoactive marijuana.

Most are known, but some I was surprised.

-Jamaica
Lax, thriving public trade

-Amsterdam, Netherlands
Very lax to no enforcement. In some areas, smoking is confined to 'coffee shops,' where marijuana is sold and used freely. Otherwise, public purchase and use is accepted.

-British Columbia, Canada
Lax enforcement, and premo marijuana grows everywhere. Almost everyone uses.

-Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Two coffeeshops in the downtown allow customers to bring their own weed and smoke within.

-Spain
Legal to grow several plants in your home for personal use. Personal use is allowed and small amounts can be carried without fear of any kind of indictment.

-Portugal
A daily dose is considered to be 2.5 gs of buds or .5 gs hash. You can have up to 10 daily doeses on your person or in your home legally, more than that and you may be indicted for trafficking.

-Emerald Triangle Counties, California, USA
Grown everywhere, lax enforcement.

-Belgium
Legal.

-Brazil
Can't do time or be fined for any weed-related charge. However, rehab time may be applied.

-Russia
Up to 6 gs, legal. More than that you can do up to 15 days in jail or be fined.
