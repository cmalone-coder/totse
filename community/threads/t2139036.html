my cutting tool.
ok i now that many people like using just razor blades but i like to be proud in what i use!

this is a very easy and quick way to make a nice cutting knife.

what you need.
a plain bic pen with cap like this
http://store.pensrus.com/Merchant2/g...001/pru_rs.gif

an x-acto blade kit like this
http://toolmonger.com/wp-content/upl...7/09/XACTO.jpg

tape

and some extra caps to the bic pens.

1.first strip the pen od the ink and the tip.
2.pick a nice blade that cuts well.
3.break off two of the things on the caps that hold the pen in you pocket.
4.wrap them in some tape and put the blade between them
5. wedge them into the pen body so they fit in snug and the blade does not move. ( you may need to add more tape.
6.make sure the original pen cap fits on and your done!


now you should have what looks like a basic pen but when the cap is removed, you have a very sharp cutting tool. i use one my self because its soo easy and works amazing

thanks for you time and comments are welcome!
