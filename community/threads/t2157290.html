Re: Is Making DMT Dangerous?
As well as all the research into acid base extractions and polar/non polar alkaloid washes, I'll try to clarify one starting point.

'making' in chemistry terms, means synthesising one product molecule from precursor molecules, ones that are not exactly the same chemical. You can go from tryptophan to tryptamine to dimethyltryptamine fairly easily, but is far easier to extract. This is what most people do.

Extraction is literally just that, your substrate matter is a ball pool, and you're picking out the yellow ones. Or you're taking a tomato and extracting the sucrose. (tomatoes will have sucrose in them won't they?) The point is, the molecule is, to most extents; it might be a salt it might be a freebase, the same molecule that you want to vaporise into your lungs. All you're doing is separating out the shit. No real synthesis step, but you will be slightly playing around with solubilities via prodding your DMT into being an ionic salt or whatever.

Also, you don't need to understand what you're doing to do a DMT extraction. You should though, smoking NaOH is not going to be fun, vaporised or not.
