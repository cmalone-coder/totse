IV'ed Oxycontin: Report
Earlier this evening I obtained a sterile needle and IV'ed ten milligrams of namebrand Oxycontin. I knew how to do an IV prep, but I had my friend shoot me up because I was pretty shaky. He tied off my arm, stuck the needle in, and drew up some blood to make sure he was in the vein.

As soon as he pushed down the plunger and pulled the needleout of my arm a wave of euphoria and warmness hit me, I felt so blissful and amazing that I cant even describe it using words. I kind of slumped back and leaned into the wall for a few seconds. Then I got up and my friends had cut me up 10 more milligrams to rail, so I did that.

I then proceeded to lay down on the bed and nod out for a good hour. Ive come down from peaking a little bit ago. Im glad to say that I finally got the balls to try IV'ing oxy. I wouldnt do it again, I just wanted to experience it once. The only time id ever shoot again is if I ever found some H.

How strong would you say 10 milligrams for a first time shot? I felt it pretty hard, but would it have been better if I had shot a little more for my first time?
