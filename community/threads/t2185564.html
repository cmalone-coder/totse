An update on my condition.
Original thread here: http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=2176784


Here's something I forgot: being sober is boring.

Television is boring. Reading is boring. Playing video games is boring. Hanging out with friends is boring.

All the normal things that normal people would normally enjoy are, in reality, quite boring. Atrociously boring.

Here's something else I forgot: I can't turn my brain off, even to go to sleep. All I do is think-- which some people might find appealing, but I assure you it isn't fun.

The other night I thought about how to create a peer-to-peer neural network that used wikipedia as a word association database so that a computer could adequately write a basic--but convincing--plot (for MMORPGS, not War and Peace). I sat and thought about it and stared at the wall from ten o'clock at night until four in the morning, then went to bed and woke up at seven. I've been doing this pretty much every day I go without drinking, though the obsession changes with the day. The point is, nothing I think about is actually practical or productive, and ends up making life in the normal world harder to bear.

Oh yeah, that's another thing: work is remarkably boring now that I've stopped the perpetual hangover. I'll be the first to admit, of course, that I don't have the most intellectually stimulating job, but I didn't expect to find things so unbearable. I am absolutely uninterested in anything that has to do with the chimp-work I've been dealing with my whole life. Not drinking has strangely made me a less dedicated worker rather than a better one. I want nothing to do with this bullshit [working] world, honestly.

=========

Anything good? Sure, a bit.

I get shit done. I actually meet deadlines (at least the few that I have). Laundry doesn't count.

While at first I felt horrible the day(s) after not drinking anything, I'm starting to feel better in the morning now.

Obviously, my stomach is *much* less irratable now. I'm actually kind of surprised on that one, at least in such a short time-frame.

I'm spending less money. I drink less, and therefore smoke less. I'm estimating, but I'm spending about $8.00 a day less than I was previously. Oh yeah, I'm better at chess. Not much, but it's noticeable.

==========

Difficulties? A multitude.

As mentioned, I can't turn my brain off. I always had this problem before, so I guess it isn't a surprise that it's happening again. I just wish I could go to sleep half of the time.

Being around people makes me want to drink... and a lot of the time, I do. To be honest, I get bored or annoyed with people overall when I'm sober... and sometimes both. I have a hard time dealing with any social experience, even in my own house, without drinking. I don't care if the other people drink, but to deal with them I am compelled to do so myself.

That's all for now folks. Time for some sleeping pills.
