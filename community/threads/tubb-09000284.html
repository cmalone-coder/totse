Re: Me and my friend's view on the universe
Read Shrodinger's Cat or The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy (or something like that). They are quite similar and deal with the multiverse theory. Shrodinger's Cat is the theory of the multiverse, and infinate number of universes. The difference though is that every possibility of doing something has happened. Therefore, if you believe in Shrodinger's Cat, you cannot look back on life and past events and ask what if you did it different because you DID do it different. You did every possible option. This is where the answer to Shrodinger's Cat comes from. In one universe the Cat lives. In one universe the Cat dies. In one universe the CAT DOES NOT EXIST. This also leads to Pavlov's Dog. Pavlov's Dog says that everything, including human activities, is mechanical. Everything we do is in a mechanical form, repeating itself in the Shrodinger's Cat paradox. What is the other answer to Shrodinger's Cat? Conscienceness. How one pertains it? Hell if I know. If you are interested in astral projection you definately should read this book. It does a wonderful job of talking about it.
