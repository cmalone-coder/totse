Are you left? Because if you are, get a left-handed guitar, playing a right-handed guitar won't help at all. But just have your 1st finger (index) over all of the strings on fret 3 and you can get the 5's in two ways: use fingers 2, 3, and 4 (middle, ring, pinkie) and press on the 5th fret for those or use your 3rd finger (ring) and press it down on the 5 frets. The second way may be harder at first, but after practice, it'll get better.

Re: Barr Chords
It helps to have big, strong hands (or just strong hands, depends on the neck of the guitar). Make sure you put your thumb directly under the neck, or the best place for you to get leverage. you might also be able to wrap your hand around the neck, depending on the neck size, hand strength and the chord. when your thumb is under the neck, you probably want the fleshy part pushing on the neck, it hurts my wrist sometimes to use the tip.

hope this helps

oh, if you want to learn a song with bar chords, try stairway to heaven. it isn't all that hard, and you can show up the losers who play greenday and blink 182 crap.
