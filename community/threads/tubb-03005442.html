Anyone with a goddamn year 9 level education should be able to work out how to add power to a circuit and also how to change a capacitor.
Easy as pie really. Some cameras its really simple- just pry off the little grey cylinder thing and replace it with a bigger capactor (try radio shite if you are an american or tandy if you are british). You have to solder it on but its easy to do. Next buy loads of battery clips (normally black, get the ones that you can hook up four 9 volt batteries to), (by loads i mean 3 or 4 of these clips). Also get enough batteries- now use some wire to solder these to the input points on the camara circuit.

Its really easy if you get a camera with a grey cylandrical capacitor. If you get one with a little black round capacitor (normally the more expensive cameras) you have to pry that off carefully without damaging the board or any of the contacts- now solder your big ass capacitor on.

These can be pretty bulky but they do work quite well. Don't be a dipshit and hook up 8 or 9 battery holders because you will end up seriously damaging someone (i am talking brain damage and death).
