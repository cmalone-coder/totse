The child must demonstrate the motivation and ability to go on to higher education while in high school by being responsible in regard to regular attendance, maintaining a high GPA, taking the appropriate college prep classes, and as soon as they're old enough by working and putting a percentage of that income into a college savings fund of some sort as well as by applying for any and all scholarships and grants that might be available.
