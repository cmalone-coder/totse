Social tips
If you're like me, and don't talk to much. Then this thread is for you.

Getting by life is hard when you get nervous around others, so heres some advice, When talking don't make more then one sentence, and if someone explains something to you just reply with one or two words. Also if you have to say something or want to agree with the other person use words they used in their sentences, I find this helps.

Also, watch out for groups of people. This is where socializing is at it's hardest. Especially when they are ones you've never met. When ever I hang out with my friend and he invites people over I just make an excuse to leave. Believe me you better do this or get stuck being the only person who doesn't talk.

Now I don't know about woman, but I don't find it neccassary to engage in coversation with them. It's not hard usually they don't talk to you if you don't start talking first. Very handy to know, one other thing I realized is if you have socializing problems AVOID smoking pot around others. This can damage your conversation skills dramatically. Usually it gets to the point where you don't talk at all cause you zone out, and then when you wanna say something you wouldn't, because you've been sitting there not saying anything in the group for over a hour or two. This is why I always avoid groups.

Also, dress normally. Make as least reasons for people to talk to you as possible, wearing headphones helps to. One time a girl talked to me on the bus I had a panic attack and had to get off the bus at the next stop.. waited 45 mins for the next one, this is why headphones are a must when using public transportation.

When shopping or in stores, just use please and thank yous and Byes. Thats all you really need. Post your tips here to.
