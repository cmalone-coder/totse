Totally agree with where you're coming from with that one. Anyone who has ever had a strong trip has revelations of some sort that don't really apply anymore when you go back to sober life. So far as I've noticed, the only way you can gain something beneficial from psychedelics is to learn about some other philosophical ideas and gain more knowledge about more conventional things. The nature of "true" entheogens is that they remove a lot of the conditioning you have gone through growing up with the cultural assumptions and paradigms ingrained into your experience. The only reason why you can't retain psychedelic knowledge into your ordinary life is because there isn't a context to put them in that makes sense with the world you see.

If you've ever had a +4 trip on shooms or something, You realize that everything is about love and interconnected and dissolution of the ego. I propose that the ego per se is actually the conscious/unconscious barrier that reinforces external reality and rejects the raw experience of psychedelics. If you were a baby again, you would see the world as a blooming, buzzing confusion that you are eventually conditioning to make sense of in a way that everyone sees.

The purpose of entheogens is not to lose touch with this normal reality, but rather to see it in context in a more beautiful and elegant way. Just like how people in the industrialized 1800's saw natural phenomenon such as speciation as different creatures that god designed, evolution put things into an elegant model that was not based on blind faith. Psychedelic thinking can provide those same models to more than biology to things such as the mind, history, and even natural processes such as evolution itself.
I would go into more detail about specific ideas that are applicable to real life/science/academia/social implications, but i gtg for now.
