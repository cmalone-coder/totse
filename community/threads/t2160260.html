Salvia. Never again...
So wednesday i went up to the lake to go camping with some people, drinking and smoking weed for the most part... Thursday i dropped a hit of acid. had a good mild trip, kayaked for a few miles and it felt like nothing. When it got dark the beer and booze arrived and we all started to drink, i had a couple of shots, a bowl of dank and a few rips off the bong. And then i asked about salvia, i said "so if we do any salvia can i do it in the tent? that fire worrys me..." and the guy said "no worries, if you get up we will not let you get near it".... so i put a bit of 19x salvia in the bong, couldnt find anything but a zippo and thought for a second "maybe it will get hot enough"... now i think they just thought i had put weed in it, not really announcing it was one mistake i regret making.

Took a large hit of it, held it in, sat the bong down, and gave the kid back his zippo. sensory contact with reality ends here. Soon after taking the hit i was thrown into another world, it is hard to describe but everything looked very strange, there were clouds and water was flowing on the ground, and i was being lead by someone around this circle, and eventually i started trying to fight these beings controlling me and i knocked some stuff over because it seemed like a tv set, eventually the scenery changed to some fucked up game show/intervention set. I thought for a second "have i died?" it seemed sort of like a final judgement, like there was no going back". Eventually i was in front of what looked like stairs. started walking up the stairs and after i took my first step something hit me and reality started to come back....

I didnt know what the fuck just happened, i though i had been set up on some fucked up show. When i started to come back i was surrounded by people just telling me to sit down, with a bunch of worried faces looking at me. Then i noticed a tent that i had fucked up beyond all repair. and someone said "you stepped into the fire".

From what other people were saying i took the hit, got up and quietly started to walk around the chairs, made it to the fire ring, basically slammed my foot down into the fire and then someone tackeled me out of it, and then landed on the tent...

I do beleive this will be my last try with salvia, the trip wasn't anything i couldnt handle but the sheer fact of being able to get up and move around with no clue what the fuck your doing worries me.

oh and had no one taken me out of that fire i might have stood in it for longer. After i came down enough to get my barings, i realized "these several 2nd degree burns dont hurt at all" and i had only had 2 shots so it probably wasn't just generally being numb. remarkably i got very lucky as far as burns go, my little toe and the one next to it have some skin burnt off and blistered the side of my leg touched the iron side of the pit and left another relativly small blister. soles of my feet, a little red but no blistering.

moral of this story, dont do salvia near a fire, any body of water, or anywhere you could possibly kill yourself in the course of 5 minutes...
