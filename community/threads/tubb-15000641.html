Re: Puzzled about the occult.
Based on your post I must assume that your term "atheist Satanists" refers to LaVey's church of Satan.

Allow me to answer some of your questions.

LaVey acknowledged and promoted a belief in the power of man in his own world. In addition he dissuades his readers from falling prey to the "empty promises" of religions like Christianity that claim to pay off after death for all the suffering endured in this life.

The Church of Satan believes in a higher power. That power is the self. Not the simple self of the ego, but the full self of the ego, id, reptilian brain, emotions, thoughts, dreams, goals, will, etc. They believe that the World is truth and devotion to the present existence rather than post-mortem salvation is the key to truth. Success in the world is success of the highest order. The rituals of the Satanic church are used to tap the more subtle and complex natures of the self, the power inherent to the mind but typically held back by the oppression of society's denial of the individual's power in favor of submission to the will of something stronger than yourself. It is the belief of the Church of Satan that to submit to a "higher power" than the self is to halt the growth of the self and to make yourself a slave to the will of others.

The tenets of the Church of Satan:

1) Satan represents indulgence, rather than abstinence!

In this statement, much is revealed. Experience is one of the best teachers, Satanism promotes learning through experience rather than doctrine. They also view self limitation such as abstinence as an aberration to the nature of humanity.

2) Satan represents vital existence, instead of spiritual pipe dreams!

Herein is laid forth the opposition to the "I'll gladly pay you Tuesday for a cheeseburger today" mentality of most religions of a Judeo-Christian nature. The rewards of Satanism are in this life, not the next (which may not even exist)

3) Satan represents undefiled wisdom, instead of hypocritical self-deceit!

Translation: seek the truth out, once you find truth, embrace it. Do not allow yourself to become a slave to misguided convictions and hypocrisies. Question all things, including yourself.

4) Satan represents kindness to those who deserve it, instead of love wasted on ingrates!

Love those who deserve your love...discard those who would use and abuse your compassion for their own ends. Love is too precious to waste on those who would use it against you to do harm.

5) Satan represents vengeance, instead of turning the other cheek!

Eye for an Eye, tooth for a tooth. Do unto others as their actions have earned. We all make choices in life, if they are not prepared for the consequences of their actions, they should not initiate them. Punishment will not come from on high, pursue justice for thyself and those whom you love. Turning the other cheek invites more pain and suffering upon yourself and yoru loved ones.

6) Satan represents responsibility to the responsible, instead of concern for psychic vampires!

Take care of and respect those who take care of and respect others, take responsibility for your actions and treat fairly those who also take responsibility for their actions. Psychic vampires in this statement are defined as people who consistently do not take responsibility for the problems they create for themselves and others, and who don't respect the efforts put in by others to try to correct problems on their behalf. Such people drain your energy and resources which are better spent on those who will appreciate and learn from the mistakes, not continue to draw you into a downward spiral of energy expense that will wear you into the ground.

7) Satan represents man as just another animal, sometimes better, more often worse than those that walk on all-fours, who, because of his "divine spiritual and intellectual development" has become the most vicious animal of all!

This one pretty much speaks for itself. The human species is the only animal who's hubris initiates wars, destroys it's own ecosystem, or thinks of itself as the pinnacle of existence. As a species, we are in truth one of the most savage animals on the planet.

8) Satan represents all of the so called sins, as they all lead to physical, mental, or emotional gratification!

This one ties back to #1 above, and is a statement about disowning the paralyzing guilt imposed by the Church over committing supposed "sins" which are in fact natural parts of existence. This does not mean they advocate breaking laws that coincide with supposed "sins"...they instead it is asserted that supposed sins are in place to soil the purity of experience and wisdom with guilt.

9) Satan is the best friend the church has ever had, as he has kept it in business all these years!

The Church's power is built on an adversarial position to the bogeyman figure of the "Devil" and the fear invoked by the existence of such a figure as they have painted it. The impoverished masses seek the church's protection from the bogeyman which is no more than a fictitional personification of all the problems in the world...all of which are caused by legitimate cause and effect rather than some supernatural being who is just trying to ruin humanity. By believing in such things as a force outside yourself, you remove your own power to confront and correct it, and are forced to turn to another force outside yourself as the answer...namely the Church.


I hope this helps you understand a little bit. This is all coming from my time as a practicioner of LaVey's Satanism. I am no longer a Satanist, but there is inherent truths in much of what the belief is based on still, however, there is a lot of misdirection there as well.
