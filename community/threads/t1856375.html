Re: Titrating
Assuming your rxn was "successful" and has been dilluted to about five times the rxn fluid's original volume with -"Dh2o"- You are obviously not sufficiently basifying the meth/Dh2o solution. Use "Red Devil Lye" its available at most Grocery stores. Basify, as I'm sure you already know- to between +12 & +14, If your testing the ph somehow. I don't use test strips anymore, they are not "critical". I use a 50% (20% adds too much water for me thanks) lye/water solution, and, as you know, add a little lye , and stir. Keep adding more a little at a time until: The bottom layer doesn't clear up for a couple of minutes, it'll stay "milky", you'll probably see tiny solids floating around and after a good stir you will see a foam-looking head of bubbles breaking up and shooting into the NP. I'm sure you've read about "the fish smell" I haven't found it to be consistant or reliable for determining sufficient base. If you're still not sure, or you still don't get any dope, Basify until you "lye-lock, this is when your solution turns solid. It is usually easily unlocked by stirring in boilng Dh2o. Then you know the ph is high enough.

Give it one more good stir and allow the two layers to settle. Collect NP, and repeat, and repeat again if you like. I add each Collection of NP to a 1 liter plastic bottle add 1/4 the volume of Dh2o, and add 10-20 drops. For an ounce (of E) I usually start off with a cap full of muriatic, you're are basing it hard so it'll be hard to drive down. Put on the beloved squirt/twist top lid, shake the fuck out of it, drain it.

There is also a factor that probably isn't your problem. You should evap on "low" heat, don't fuck around like some of these ultra-conservatives claim to do...setting it on a lamp-shade well above the light-bulb, or setting it on top of a "warm" heater, fuck that stupid shit. Get it as hot as you want as long as its not boiling or bubbling at all actually, it should take no longer than an hour or so.

Like I said, that's probably not the problem, but with the info you provided I could'nt rule out the possibillity.

Good luck!
