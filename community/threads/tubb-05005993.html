Drug Success Stories
we've all heard the drug horror stories ("my son was a fucking moron and took 40 triple C's and died! don't do drugs!"). what i'm wondering is if some of us out there have stories to the counterpoint, that drugs have changed one's life for the better in the end, or even saved it.

Drugs certainly saved my life. several years back, way back in high school, I found myself in the depths of depression. My life had no meaning, no purpose. i could not imagine a situation, past or future, in which i would be happy or at least content. I could not dream of the future as i could not think of it as a realistic situation; i had somehow always known that i would kill myself.

then, someone very close to me introduced me to marijuana, and the entire concept of it fascinated me. I was so intrigued by the whole process: one would vaporize plant material & inhale it, and soon, there would be wholesale changes in his or her thought patterns and perceptions. i needed to know more, to understand this phenomenon. I studied and read. Page after page, book after book. Pharmacology sparked an interest in chemistry; chemistry did the same for physics and math; theology, philosophy, and politics followed. A simple green herb had transformed my life from an utterly meaningless and empty experience to a relentless search for truth and the inner workings of all that takes place around me. I have future goals, interests, and plans. As such, I am happy.

Anyway, does anybody else have a 'drug success story' to share?
