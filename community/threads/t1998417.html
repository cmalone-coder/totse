Spiritual and Sexual, the mind/body split.
This may not sound as coherent as I'd want it to, but I've been observing the people around me for quite some time. I'm caught up in 3 places for 70 hours a week until the end of this week (its been a long 6 months) What I think I have realized is that the human mind itself is the greatest barrier to our freedom to be sexual, natural beings.

I notice some people always chasing something. Always chasing more booze, always chasing more cars, more money, more shit they don't really need. Craving, it is something in the mind. Now, consider a relationship. What is a relationship? Define relationship. What it is, is a product of the mind. Now let's keep clear here that the body is something real, the breathing, the fresh air, sexual desire for a woman and the natural foods that keep us healthy, all LIFE. Where the mind comes in is the poison that we have actually fallen complete victim to.

A relationship is, and I don't care what anybody says anymore, nothing but a fantasy created by the mind. A promise for something that can be broken, a grasping at something that can fall apart. Now, the mind is never truly in the present moment because in the present moment, there exists no mind. Read that again a few times. Sex, at least good sex, is something in the moment. We aren't thinking much during sex, just like the musician isnt thinking when hitting the right notes or the basketball player is making his shot. It's NATURE we follow then, not the mind.

Now it's funny how we see guys wanting sex and women saying "no sex until relationship" even funnier, is when they set up "rules" like one of my ex "no sex for the first 6 months" who ended up fucking me a week later, and then every single day after that. What this tells me is women are a little more afraid to let go, due to their feminine nature... let go of the mind that is. Mind is kind of like our security, our protection. It's helped us survive but it has also damned us in so many ways. So we agree with WORDS, yes, I will "go out" with you and you, me.... which are only vague, inconsistent symbols of reality that we've created with the mind. "Bad" relationships dont last because they were never real to begin with. "Good" ones last because the two people are with the moment, present, not identified with the mind so much.

I walked down the street and saw something that reminded me of the feelings of resentment in relationships. A little teenage girl shouting at a boy "I LOVE YOU" in a demanding tone, as he said in a sad voice "I love you too" so she would shut up, and they parted ways. He then scratched his head, looked around in shame and embarassment, and she walked her fat little ass up the street content that she now had the security of thinking an experience in the present moment will carry on tomorrow and the next day. But WAIT! That IS the mind's nature, to always be outside of the moment. Do you see what I am saying?

I look some women dead in the eye and see what stops them and me from communicating is the barriers due to the conditioning of the mind. When there is no mind, there is sexual desire, we want to fuck. When even a single thought comes up, she looks the other way, the guy gets confused, and boom, the temple falls down, there is no communication. The mind has taken over the body, so we go along our ways of buying shit/working/trying to get higher or drunker... chasing nothing but dreams, rich men puffing cigars, poor men puffing newports. There is no difference! The same mind in different bodies (Maybe this is why they say we are all one mind). all the mind seeking more and more and more.

Now I can never take relationships or marriage seriously. Especially marriage. It is the biggest sham of all time, if you dont see it this way, well maybe you should meet some of the couples I have met. It is a fantasy once again, a constricting one that we allow to control our lives, and this is where the misery comes from, OUR OWN MIND. Not reality, just our false thinking. We condition ourselves to these thoughts when in truth our bodies and our sexual desires for other people change every single moment, eternally, just like EVERYTHING ELSE IN THE UNIVERSE, which we are part of.

I think people have really lost touch with their simplicity. And when I chew mushrooms, I realize, the lesson has been taught and I cannot gain anything from them further. Life is perfect, reality is an amazing, beautiful experience, but our minds are something completely unreal, tools that we have allowed to become more than just tools, to the point of misery. I see people around me waking up though.

Not having time for a relationship these long months and observing those who do and dont has taught me VERY much about different mindsets. I now realize, I dont want a relationship at all, and if I dare get into one, I will be lying to her the whole time and pretending to promise that I will only want her. I may even end up "cheating" on her or breaking up without a hitch. The reason is Im not as attached to these things, while they are. To some that may sound VERY asshole-ish, but in reality, I guess thats just how I feel.... Women, no matter what, unless they are VERY sharp will never understand this. This is why guys "play" and "cheat" it's not that theyre assholes, its the communication barrier, its the mind and body split and it gets so bad that it causes murder. Murder! For fantasy!

I'm just writing this to make clear that the reason I have not given sex to any women lately is because they are speaking in mixed tongue to me. A married woman at my job is turned on by me but turns her head away, because of her mind. I look her dead in the eyes and see a divided person. "Whats up." "you workin tomorrow?" "same shit different day" "how old are you" "what school did you go to".... Absolutely stupid fucking pointless conversation we hear everywhere. Pay close attention to the TONE of voice, not the words. Other girls who want to have sex try to strike up conversation but cant even say 3 words before getting nervous and flaking out. We have been so conditioned that its pathetic, we cannot even communicate anymore.

It's this mind, the human mind ITSELF is the barrier, the disease, the cause of premature ejaculation, "cheating", "relationship", "marriage"... remember that WE created words, but we were here BEFORE words existed. To realize this is to look deeper at yourself and those around you. Someone invented this, and we have completely conformed to this fantasy of the mind, which is language. It makes you not hate Hitler or Jack the Ripper, it just makes you think... fantasies of the infinite mind expressing themselves on the chalkboard of life. But the whole time they've been nothing more than fantasies.


It is not until you break apart from the mind that you are able to observe the minds of others in their essence. It is the same as yours, but to not identify with it is to know it completely. The disease that plagues us all, the problem with this world, the cause of all of this suffering is in our own minds.
