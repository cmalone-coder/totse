Re: How many AMPs?
Ohms law is E=I*R
E being electromotive force(voltatge)
I being current(amps)
R being resitance(ohms)

do a llittle bit of algebra and you find out that if you increase voltage, you'll also increase the current if the resitance stays the same. The resistance in this case is the person getting zapped.

A fat drunk would probably be a little bit sweaty and his concentration would already be blurred, so I think he should get a bigger effect. The stun gun doesn't necessarily act on pain,(hurts like a bitch) but by confusing the nerves and muscles.

Wiring a second 9 volt into the gun in parallel would increase the current, while putting it in series would increase the voltage. Increasing one will increase the other, but it's hard to tell how you should do it without seeing how the circuit is set up. It might have a regulator to keep people from playing with the power supply, or simply to make it more reliable.
