Yes, the Divina Comedia is about all of it, and Dante wrote it because he saw a need for moral and religious renewal. Probably rightly so, as this was in the "dark" middle ages. Dante spoke of four levels of meaning to this work: the literal, the metaphorical, the moral and the analogical.

He also created an outrage from the Roman Christian Church, as he wrote the Divina Comedia in Italian, and not in Latin as was the rule for religious tracts; not meant to be understood be common man, even if read out loud. Dante obviously wanted to reach the people...
