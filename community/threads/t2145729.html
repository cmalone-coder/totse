Re: NA beer, anyone
I suppose if a brewery actually wanted to cater to the beer drinkers who critique and enjoy the flavors and complexities of beer, a NA would work okay, so long as they provided the variety.

As it stands now, it seems like NA beers are marketed at the designated drivers who don't want to look like designated drivers, or at people who, for whatever reason, can't handle being a social outcast who isn't drinking/doesn't drink. I can't honestly think of sitting down on a summer evening and going "Ya know what? I want the crisp refreshment of a Coors product, but not the 5% abv...what shall I do? AH! Coors Non-Alcoholic, of course!"

If you only want one beer, have one beer, and then have a snack, which will keep your BAC low enough to drive, have sex, throw stones at lepers, or whatever your heart desires. I've never seen a person drink more than one NA beer, and it really seems pretty pointless.
