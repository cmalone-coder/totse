Ahhh I hate long hair. If you must, be sure to wash it more thoroughly than you would normally, this sounds obvious, but I just want to make sure you are'nt cutting about with dandruff. Keep it healthy from the start. I mean really healthy... keep your hair hygiene immaculate, wash it often but you can go some days WITHOUT washing as the natural oils in your hair will help it stengthen. To wash, get shampoo all over, remember it will require more than you normally would, and rinse out (don't be cheap with hair products). Trim off all split ends. Get it trimmed every so often. It will need to grow into a shape if you don't get it trimmed you'll end up looking like a neanderthal. Be patient with this and make sure you want to do it now 100% because you will look pretty dumb for a short-period whilst your hair is still growing, don't go half way through then decide you don't want to do it.
