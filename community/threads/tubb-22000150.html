Not practical at all.

quote:
But the BATF would just have to crack down harder on the dealers


They already did, it's the form 4473. And the dealers requirement to hold that form practically indefintely. Also submitting a copy to the BATF.

In that sense all firearms purchased from a dealer are registered.

When someone commits a crime and the authorities recover the gun they will run the serial number to determine if it has been reported stolden. If so then they will contact the owner and take it from there. If not reported stolden they can determine what gunstore this pistol was sold from by purchase records held by the BATF and then obtain the orginal paperwork the buyer had to fill out prior to purchase which gives them all nessacary buyer information. If the gun has been since hold to another individual there should be a bill of sales held by the individual(s).
