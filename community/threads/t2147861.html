Re: shroom chocolate bars
I've bought many, many chocolate bars before. For a long time, that's all I could find. They're always a gamble. Most of the time, it's somebody putting 2 grams in it and telling you that it's 3.5. I have had quite a few, though, that were either an eighth of normal mushrooms or a little less of some dank mushrooms. I've made them with an eighth myself before.

Just FYI, though, it takes a good-sized chocolate to fit an eighth of powdered mushrooms in it. If they're really small, don't buy them. I've also noticed that the good ones still have a fairly decent mushroomy smell to them.

melt chocolate,
pour over shrooms, compress and dry.
i was just told the recipe.

That's kind of it.

You melt your chocolate in a double boiler. While that's melting, grind up your mushrooms in a coffee grinder (fuck people wanting to see chunks, that almost got me put in jail, plus you can't fit as much as if it's powdered) an eighth at a time.

Pour each eighth into a greased muffin tin lined with wax paper. Make sure the wax paper sticks up some. You want enough that you can wrap the entire chocolate.

Put one eighth in each hole, or fill as many holes as you're doing.

As soon as your chocolate is melted, pull it off the heat and let it sit there for a few minutes. You want it to the point that you can just barely still stir the mushrooms in to avoid heating them as much as possible.

Fill each hole with chocolate and stir the mushrooms in with a toothpick.. Since you cooled the chocolate, you're going to have to work QUICKLY. I do one at a time so that if need be I can throw the bowl back over the heat.

When they're all full, throw the tin in the freezer and let them harden. Give them 2-3 hours minimum, then pull them out and finish wrapping them in the wax paper.

Eat anything that sticks to the tin. This is your little extra bonus for doing it yourself.

Wrap them in foil and throw them in a Ziplock bag, then into the fridge or freezer.
