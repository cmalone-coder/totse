New Site Design
No matter how comfortable an old pair of shoes may be, sometimes we have to admit that they need to get tossed out because they're old, full of holes, scuffed up, and completely unstylish. Not to mention stinky.

I know that many of you like the look that this site has had for the past four years, and you've grown accustomed to it, like a pair of old comfortable shoes, but tonight I am installing a new look and feel for totse.com.

All of the pages are still here: Drugs, Bad Ideas, Ego and the rest still exist, but their look has changed and the site navigation has been modified. Instead of having 24 navigation buttons in the left-hand column, there are now only a few.

Trying to pick top-level category names was probably the hardest part of this change. I played around with various ideas for page taxonomy, and finally settled on About, Self, Community, Society, Technology, and Fringe. Under those categories you can find everything interesting about practically anything, as well as the usual assortment of lies, half-truths, and amazing factual data.

The main thing I wanted to accomplish with the new design was to speed up page loading time. The new page look has 1/5th of the graphic content that the old pages had. That means that the new pages should load about 5 times faster than the old pages. People with slow Internet connections can commence rejoycing now.

The other thing I wanted to accomplish was to increase the page color contrast for the articles. Cyan text on a black background may look cool, but it's hard to read after about an hour or so. The new design uses black text on a white background. Maybe not as hacker-chic as cyan on black, but you'll be able to read articles without going blind.

Finally, I just wanted a new look. When I first set up this site I promised myself that I'd update the design every 2 years. The current design has been up for four years, twice what I'd planned on, and it was due for an overhaul.

Welcome to the new totse. It's the same as the old totse, it just looks different.
