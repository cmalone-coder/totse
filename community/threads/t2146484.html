Anxiety/Panic Attacks
I know this is the wrong forum, but I feel like I'm trapped or going crazy and I'm looking for helpful responses ASAP... and the Headshrinkers forum isn't the most popular... please excuse this for at least a couple of hours, mods/jackketch.

A few days ago I had my second ever panic attack. The first time it happened a few months ago, I didn't know what the hell it was or how to classify it. I thought I was having a heart attack, and the panic attack PEAKED while I was driving myself to the hospital. Not very fun. An hour later, when I felt normal and I was hooked up to all the monitors and shit, I just felt stupid. I felt like I overreacted and (no pun intended) panicked, and that it was probably a one time thing.

Apparently no such luck. This past Friday I had my second one, and I thought that someone had drugged my food with some sort of hallucinogen or something. Seriously. I am normally a sane, rational person, but I couldn't help feeling that some horrible impending doom was on the verge of taking place, that the drugs were about to hit me and I would lose all sanity or my own life.

Since Friday, all I can think about is if/when it's going to happen again. It's always lingering in the back of my mind, and it's fucking crippling. I'm not the same person... sometimes I lose touch with who I am, where I am, who I'm talking to, what I'm saying. It's like I have to fight to feel normal, and it's fucking scary.

Obviously I've been going through a lot of stress in the past few years, and I guess it's all just finally catching up with me.

Right now I'm in Atlanta, in the process of moving from Tennessee to Florida, so I really won't have any way to see a psychiatrist for the next few days, but I plan on doing so ASAP; at times, I wonder if I should just check myself into a mental hospital.

Here is my question: Is there anything I can do to make the next few days bearable? I'm really at a loss... nothing seems to work. I try to focus on the 'here and now' and not worry about 'What if...' but I just can't fucking help it. I'm praying that one of you has experienced these things before and might be able to help me.

This is the scariest thing to ever happen to me... I mean, what would you think is worse, losing your life or losing your mind?

I deal with it though. I generally get most anxious about going out, or going to a crowded place etc. So if i'm feeling really shit, or it's ridiculously sunny, I just stay in. It helps if I can have someone with me.

If I have to go out, or i'm already out and I start getting anxious, I do a couple things. This is kind of lame, but I carry this orange polished stone, so I rub it and pretend it gives me like power and i'm not afraid. Also I have some lucky dice which I roll or just play with.
The other thing I do is like walk really fast and look sort of angry. I'm kind of a big guy, so people usually get out the way. That's again pretending i'm not scared I guess.

Also I go in a bookshop or something and read for a bit, that usually calms me down some.

As for longer term, a counsellor or friend you can talk to about the deepest stuff is pretty helpful. Also Benzos if your doc isn't stingy like mine. :P
