bamboo grows very well in the northeastern usa. though i would refrain from planting any until next spring. the key is the roots.

bamboo will grow as tall as you let it. i put some in my uncles yard last spring and it has easily grown 8 feet high since then.

your heard correct it does grow fast an spreads well. so fast an so well you may find your self fightig for your yard in a few years. if you have the time/effort/money/skills. decide how much space you want to set aside for the bamboo. then dig a 3foot trench around this area an fill it with cement. this will keep the bammboo, which is a grass, from taking over your yard in a few years.

but yeah you can use it as a wall. thats why we put it in my uncles yard. to act as a bit of a privacy screen/wall.

he loves it. it looks great. you should do it to.

While I was living in the southeast I had a large, dense plot of bamboo seperating my house from the adjacent one. It is very hardy, mostly low-maintenance and works great for privacy.

The biggest problem you might face in the beginning is people taking the chutes. We used to have at least one person a week sneaking around with garden scissors trying to take pieces of the plants to start their own.
