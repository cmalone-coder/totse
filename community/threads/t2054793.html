this girl problem has gotten so bad I'm going to cry myself to sleep
I drove this girl to school my junior year. She was a senior. I would just goof around with my friend and she was, if anything, just a fucking hassle to drive to and from school because she lived in this awkward cul-de-sac type thing.

This is going to be long.


Every once in a while she and I would drive home alone together when the other people I drove were doing things and there was always something really strangely intimate about being alone with her. The summer before she left, we didn't see each other much, but I introduced her to her first cigarette.


The day before she left for college a couple friends, myself, and the girl all went to the park and hung out and there were a couple times when she and i were just sitting around and a kiss seemed appropriate, but it never happened.

I started dating this other girl that I was really really deeply in love with and had been good friends with for about 6 years. The relationship immediately became pretty serious, we dated for about 4 1/2 months, passionate love was replaced by intense depression. Now, the girl, Emily, was in college dating my girlfriend's ex-boyfriend. They broke up about 3 days after we broke up. Both of us were devastated and we hadn't been in touch for months, but we talked via internets and we were both extremely upset and i started driving up to her college every once in a while and we would just hang out, cuddle, provide emotional support. I tried to kiss her each time I left. The first two times she didn't really respond. The third time she kissed me. And I don't remember from there.

As we both started to feel better about things we weren't in touch quite as much, but if she ever came to visit home we'd always end up having romantic and emotionally stimulating evenings together and we'd run around dams late at night and eat ice cream in hotels and that good stuff.

This past summer I started smoking a lot of weed. Five or six times daily. It became habitual and it slightly altered my personality, naturally. I was generally in a better mood, cared less about stupid shit, and wasn't quite as good at being there for Emily when she needed me.

There came a point where she became really anti-social, especially toward me, I think. She was home for 10 days and called me once to see if I wanted to go to a concert with her. We went together and I tried to call her on other days but she'd just ignore my calls and said she felt antisocial because she was having family problems.

Well...

I live five minutes away from her now. I feel like I've fucked up beyond repair. I don't know if the girl has ever felt about me the way I've always felt about her. She's one of the most genuine people I know and I know that she loves me an absurd amount, but I feel like she's just fed up with me. I quit smoking today. Had my last joint this morning next to the lake and I stared off and wondered what was going on in my life. I'm so fucking happy and successful at everything going on in my life, but my relationship with her is just awful and it's making me incredibly depressed.

I don't even think I can act naturally around her anymore. She loves the natural me, and all I can be self-observant to see what I'm doing right or wrong. I daydream about nights that we spent together and I long to relive them, but it doesn't seem to be in her agenda. When we do see each other, she's obviously really happy to see me, but conversations always go downhill into our problems and it's followed by silence. The goodbye hug is always intimate and long and involves staring at each other. I really just don't know how to get her back. I miss her. I want to sleep in her bed with her and hold her like I used to. Weed isn't even fun anymore because it just makes my memories more intense.

I don't know. I can't even properly socialize with other girls because I just think about how much they suck compared to her.

I'm also working on temptations right now. What should I do? Resist the temptation of calling her? Rid myself of her? Don't hang out with her until I've figured my own life out and recovered mentally from smoking too much pot? I see her as a temptation. The longer I go without seeing her the less I want her. Does there come a point where it's just simply healthier to avoid a person because they make you long for them and make you feel miserable?

Or should I try to wiggle my way back into her life? I miss her and I'd do anything to have that relationship back, but I feel like I'm just setting myself up for more hurt.
