Things to do:

-Creative. Drawing helped me immensely, even if most of my drawings turned out like this (http://img.photobucket.com/albums/v392/Twisted_Ferret/Portraits/help.png). It's just nice to get it out. Writing always seemed like too much effort, but once I started I always ended up feeling better.

-Exercise & other self-improvement. Learn a musical instrument, improve your creative skills, etc. Exercising not only will make you feel better in mood, but it will make you healthier as well, and the improvements it brings are easily discernable. Any achievement will help; just keep busy.

-DON'T sit in your room in the dark all day. Go take long walks, do push-ups, draw, something. It will help immensely, I promise.

-Long hot showers. Okay, it might just be me but I loved the isolation and the sensation and the peacefulness. People leave you alone, it feels good, and you can contemplate. I dunno, give it a try.

-Listen to happy music and lose yourself in happy novels. Stay away from sad, listless stuff.

Oh, and smile. Even if you don't really feel like doing it. I don't mean ALL the time, but the more you smile the better you'll actually feel. It works both ways.
