Re: Diablo II Necromacer Starter
don't be afraid of SAVING SKILL POINTS!!!! MY PALLADIN (mule) HAS 8 SAVED UP and hes lvl 12!!


put 1 point in these. no more than 1 is needed.

teeth
amplify damage
iron maiden
corpse explosion (not good in duels, but it will SAVE YOUR ASS when you are mobbed. i put 2 or 3 points into this.)
skeleton (dont waste points on this)
bone sheild
clay golem
blood golem
iron golem
fire golem
bone spear (im serious, it may be fast, but it wont do the damage a bone spirit will do)


for a strong dueling necromancer, i would suggest you use as many +'s to skills as possible.

look for these:

wormskull (unique bone helm. +1 to necro skills)
Stone of Jordan (unique ring. i use two. +1 to skills per ring)
Ume's Lament (unique Grim wand. +2 to necromancer skills, fastest cast)
Silks of the victor (unique ancient armor. +1 to skills)
summoner's amulet (magical amulet. +1 to skills)
--or--
Necromancer's amulet (magical amulet +2 to skills. high level requirement)

Sigon's Guard (set item tower shield. +1 to skills

if i were you, i would sacrifice the sigon's guard for a socketed tower shield with three perfect diamonds. they provide resistance to ALL elemental attacks. this is especially effective against a sorceress (i.e. spiffy's character).

skills you should concentrate on are:

bone spirit ( A MUST FOR A DUELING NECROMANCER!!!!)
fire golem is good to keep the enemy on their toes. (i garuntee that they wont rush you if your fire golem is hurting them with its 'holy fire' aura)
decrepify (severely slows and weakens the enemy. this gives you a 2 or 3 second window to squeeze off a few bone spirits.)
