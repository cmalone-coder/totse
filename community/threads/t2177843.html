A worthwhile 2C-E story
Well the 2c-e I received was in fact, not bunk! Today me and my friend picked up a jug of distilled water, and some infant ibuprofen. (for oral syringe) Dissolved our 1000mg of 2C-E in 500ml of dH2O, and dosed 10mg each.

Well 2C-E is awesome, and VERY visual. To me, it felt almost identical to two hits of LSD. I've only done LSD once, and it was very similar, but the 2C-E peaked much harder.

I was able to remain quite calm, even when tripping pretty hard. I functioned in a retail store, which was really weird. All the commercialized Christmas shit was sickening, I normally wouldn't give a shit.

I was even somewhat composed while me and my friend were eating dinner at Famous Daves (chain rib joint) with my family. To say the least, it was a challenge. Containing my laughter was a problem, when my rib that I was supposed to be eating began morphing into various shits, while sitting with my parents. There was fucking blues music playing, and the whole atmosphere was very cheesy, but not when sober (think the restaurant in Pulp Fiction, it had that "feel"). I managed to order my food while the menu did its best to make itself unreadable. The only thing my parents noticed was my very awkward position at the table, they haven't said anything about pupils yet. A challenge indeed, a fucking little kid dressed as a cowboy (for some reason) started square dancing with himself oh so ridiculously at the table next to us, I was only allowed to laugh for a moment. People plan this shit I swear, bringing out the most ridiculous shit for JUST the moment when someone is stoned or tripping near them and needs to be cool.

Also, the moon with those two planets near by tonight, oHHHHMMYYYGAWWWDD. Amazing! 100% clear sky, and surrounded by my visuals.


Although I describe this very light heartedly, and it was fun, make no mistake. 2C-E will kick your ass if you just let go, its like having a mechanical pulley that can lower you further down the rabbit hole when you'd like. When I just let go while about peaking, I tipped at the edge of ego death. I would've let myself go, but I needed to eat dinner with my family in 5 minutes, lol convenience.

Random facts future trippers may want to know:

OEVs: shifting, breathing, textures flowing, melting at times, kaleidoscopes on everything, tracers that will amaze you. Faces getting older then younger, shrinking and growing. Hair actually shrinking and growing. Very fucking intricate patterns on tiles/carpet/wallpaper. If it isn;t already patterned, it will be soon. These patterns shift, change, and twirl in at least 6 layers at once. Truely the most breathtaking visual display I've ever seen. Stopped me in my tracks. EVERYTHING IS ALWAYS MOVING IN SHAPE AND COLOR! ALWAYS!

CEVs: most immersive I've ever seen, DXM may have been more immersive but I was too fucked to remember or take anything from it. It is a lot like stepping into a world where visual input has no significance, so your brain just makes shit up that relates to what you're feeling to keep the eyes happy. And it does a good job!

Side affects: Pounding headache after coming down (loud environment played its role), my friend had nausea (expected), and I was often unable to recognize how cold I was (potential danger). My heartrate remained steadily normal through the trip. Slight appetite loss, that came back in waves somehow? No other negatives noted.

Hope you guys come across this substance at some point, it is very interesting. And there will be no telling everyone in your school how awesome it is after you do. Its only for us, I don't know what to call us however!
