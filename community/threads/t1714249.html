it's not really a matter of what kind of tape is used, it's more a matter of timing. but yeah that clear tape, not magic tape, scotch tape is stronger and lasts longer before it wears out. i know because this was probably my main source of income in high school. get the 3/4 inch scotch tape i think it's the 3M kind with the red label. put the tape on the dollar about 1 1/2 inches down the dollar so it will hold it then tape the other side so it doesn't stick to anything. can't put tape too far down the dollar or it won't work in any machines. the hardest part is finding a machine though. there's still some around if you look at an old people's home or some nicer apartments i'm sure there's some left just have to look. i'll describe what to look for in another post cuz i'm tired.
