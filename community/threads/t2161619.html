Now I'm curious...
After replying to some opiate/heroin related threads I've become curious about a past experience of mine with heroin during the time period I was addicted to OxyContin.

So one day my main dealer and secondary dealer couldn't get any oxy. My main dealer reassured me he'd have some later that night, so I went ahead and did the remaining piece of my last OC 80. Anyways my dealer ended up being unable to come through as his connect wasn't answering his phone (my secondary dealer couldn't find any either). So I made it through the rest of the day/night by taking 4 mg's of xanax.

About the skag -
The H didn't have any little rocks in it which I guess some people get in there bags? When I dumped it out to make a line, it had the texture/look of a line of oxy, not like the fluffy confectioners sugar look that coke has, and was brown. I can remember vaguely what the drip tasted like but can't equate it to anything, although it wasn't as delicious as oxy drip. It didn't burn my nose particularly bad, at least not more then an equivalent size line of oxy. And just to reiterate, my opiate tolerance was to the point where 240 mg's of oxycodone snorted would produce a slightly warm tingling feeling for less then 10 minutes, and it was very weak at that, it was probably the smallest weakest shortest high you can possibly feel from oxycodone and it vanished in less then 10 minutes, after which I was left with the satisfied feeling of having gotten my fix, and my tolerance had been this high for about a month, the two months I was using before that my tolerance was rising daily until I hit the 240 mg point, and that was around the same week I did the heroin. When I did do the heroin, it was approx. 15-18 hours after my next most recent dose of opiates (20 mg's oxycodone).

***Factors and Tolerance***
- Had been using 80 milligram Time Release OxyContin for 5 months.
- The first months I was using around 20-40 milligrams one or two times a day 8-10 times a month spread out (not like a 10 binge once a month.
- The second month I was using around 20-40 milligrams one or two times a day every other day on average, sometimes 2 days in a row on weekends.
- The next three months I started using 20-40 milligrams everyday, skipped only a couple days right near the beginning of the next three months when I tried to stop before it got to out of hand, but after two days went right back to using everyday and did not miss a day for around 3 months.
- Started using 1-2 times a day around 30-50 milligrams during one of the times (to get an actual high) and 10-20 milligrams (to hold off my impatience, it also gave me a light buzz but only did this for a week or so before 10-20 milligrams no longer did anything but stopped the craving for a bit.
- Eventually progressed to 3-5 times a day at doses anywhere from 10 milligrams (for a fix, during the middle of school, before I went to get more, right before homework, etc.) all the way to 60 milligrams (sometimes I would still try to get high because I realized my tolerance was rising so quickly I wouldn't be able to get the actual high for much longer.)
- And finally it got to the point where I was doing it 5+ times a day. My doses kind of started to follow a pattern at this point which I will explain below.

*I would use 5+ times a day at doses from 10-20 milligrams for quick fixes, and 50-60 milligrams when I would want to do enough to at least get a good mood lift (at this time it probably would have taken me 120 milligrams to feel like 20 milligrams to a person with no tolerance my same size and weight.
*I would still use 5+ times a day, but the doses ranged from 10 milligrams to 60 milligrams. Each time it was a fix, the only high I would get from here on out was a nice mood lift and occasionally a slight warmish feel if it was the first dose of the day and if I did 50+ milligrams.
*I would keep using 5+ times a day but the doses now ranged from 10 milligrams to 30 milligrams. Occasionally, sometimes once a week, I would do a dose in excess of 50 milligrams, but that's the most I would do. At this point it seemed worthless to me to blow 50 milligrams when I could blow 15-20 milligrams and still get my fix and a mood lift that was almost the same.
*Still using 5+ times a day, doses were still from 10 to 30 milligrams, also during this time (the 5th and last month) I did 240 milligrams, snorted, at night. It hit me that I should have done it in the morning as I might have gotten high and it would have been fun to go to school, even just because the knowledge that I just snorted 3 OC 80's made me feel good. Plus there would have been a 8-9 hour period for my tolerance to slightly drop. That same day I had already done around 50 milligrams, 20 in the morning, 5 during the school day, another 5 near the end of the school day, 15 milligrams about an hour after school, and then 5 milligrams right before dinner. 3 Hours later is when I did the 240 milligrams. During this same week I did the heroin.
*The heroin was done after being unable to find OxyContin. The most recent time I had done OxyContin was approximately 15-18 hours before I did the heroin. But my use had continued just as much and as frequently right up until that point. Immediately after the heroin my dealer found some OxyContin and I went right back to using it 5+ times a day
*When I did the H, I started with a small line the size of a 5 milligram line of OC and gave a line this size to the girl as well, that did nothing so I did another line that size about 3 minutes after, still nothing, did a line twice the size, still nothing, did the rest of the bag, still nothing. The only thing I noticed was that my withdrawal symptoms seemed to have went away, however it could have been placebo. The bag was a 50$ bag which, after reading prices, should have been around half a gram. The bag's actual volume of powder was equivalent to the volume of 1/4th a gram of coke if that helps you visualize it.

So yeah HUGEEEE story, its probably boring, I don't care I had nothing else better to do while I was waiting for the suboxone to kick in. I am really very curious about this, I have wondered about it since the day I did it if it was real or fake. So given my opiate tolerance, my most recent dose prior to doing the heroin, the actual way I did the heroin, and the facts provided about my dealer and his friend, and my dealer's girlfriend, could that much heroin really not affect me because of my tolerance? I assumed the feeling would be diminished, but that heroin was so strong I would feel the initial rush to some degree before it faded away. Anyways, possible answers to this scenario would be greatly appreciated as it would mean I didn't write this to no avail. Sorry for the enormous length, I expect plenty of flames, people saying "we didn't need all that info to answer your questions", maybe some too long/didn't reads and even a couple epic fails.

I just wanna see if I can find enough evidence to support either A. The heroin was fake, or B. (My preferred conclusion) My tolerance was so high that a 50$ bag of heroin, (the most I did in one sitting was 1/4th of the bag, maybe a little more) would not produce a high at all or even produce the rush of euphoria that heroin is famous for.
