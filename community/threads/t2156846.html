I don't know what you're trying to do.

Scan simple shit like meal cards at school or scan debit/credit cards and the like?

The reason debit card scams work is because all you need to do is read the card (and save the data) and have the persons pin. Then you simply write the same data to a blank card. You can't edit this data in any way, because the amount of money isn't stored in the card, it's stored in servers on the banks side. Same thing with credit cards, you can't edit them to raise the limit or anything, the only thing that editing them would do is give you someone else's credit card.

For low-tech things like gift cards, meal cards, etc, I'm pretty sure the amount of money stored is kept on the card itself, so you might be able to open it up with a hex editor (or if it's encrypted you'd need to decrypt it (nearly impossible unless you know what you're doing) and mess around with values.

There are editors out there that are better at detecting change. So if you scan an empty card, a 5 dollar card, and a 10 dollar card, and see what values have changed, you might stand a chance of breaking it.

Good luck.
