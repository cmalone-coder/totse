Re: Getting TOO close? (LONG)
I think your father's judgement is clouded by his own bad experiences. Normal first-love breakups usually unvolve a couple of months of feeling awful and then you're back to being okay (although it'll always have some kind of effect on you). The odds are against you (and every other teen relationship), of course, but all you can do is stick with what makes you happy. Try to explain to your parents that you need to do this for yourself; they would have wanted to do the exact same at your age in this situation.

And good luck. Enjoy first love, it's wonderful.

EDIT: Don't try to distance yourself from her. If anything's likely to damage the relationship (and these things are a little more fragile than they seem, unfortunately), it's behaviour that she does not understand and/or that you do not explain. And explaining that you want to spend less time together so that your possible breakup won't hurt as much will not go down well. It's very possible you'll end up hurt by this, but it's also possible you won't. This isn't the time of your life to live cautiously (I'm guessing you're 16/17?), go for it. Even if it doesn't work out (and it sometimes does, of course; my friend's grandparents have been together since they were 14/15, and they're almost 80 now), you'll remember it for the rest of your life, and you shouldn't do anything to make it less wonderful.
