Dreams-> problem
So, lately I've been having these sexual dreams. Not dreams that actually involve actual intercourse. More like just touching and kissing accompanied by strong emotion.

WTF. This is RIGHT after I decide to become asexual. I HATE dealing with other people, ESPECIALLY if I care what they think of me. I don't like mixing my life with others; I like the freedom that comes with a complete lack of responsibility. I like to be able to say "don't feel like it, would rather be alone" when asked to go out. Being away from my family, essentially living alone, has made me the happiest I have ever been. I love being alone; the freedom is incredible. And now these dreams have me filled with this obsession that I am unable to satisfy, and make a very good yet emotional argument against abandoning all hope of romantic contact.

What should I do? I REALLY don't think I want a girlfriend in the traditional sense. With most relationships I've seen, there seems to be either this huge rift in honesty and trust, with respective social armors clattering against each other and ritual methods of exchange being followed, or a kind of horrifying goopiness that makes it seem like a few organs are going to be pulled out if the two are separated. But nevertheless, I'm feeling this deep need to connect emotionally with a woman. I don't ever want to deeply love someone or have a completely shared life; power is the most important thing to me, and by trusting part of your life to someone else, whose feelings and actions you have absolutely no control over, you give that up. Still, I have this need, and the desire is painful.

I wouldn't even know where to begin; I'm very socially inexperienced, and usually very akward, though I no longer have a paralyzing lack of confidence. I don't even know how I'd find the time, let alone the right person. Which I assume wouldn't be exactly simple.

Desire and suffering go hand in hand, but that dream was beautiful, and I fear that I might be missing something. What should I do? Is this just a tragedy waiting to happen? I can see it ending badly both ways, with doing nothing and living with regret or trying and destroying myself in the process.
