Re: a little advice please?
I used to share your same boat. For five months this school year I dated a girl who was depressed and was also a cutter. I'm going to start out by saying that you have to be extremely weary of dating people who are depressed. It was extremely hard for me, my dad who went through a divorce based on her depression, and a baseball coach who dated a girl for three years. Someone can't be happy with someone else if they aren't happy by themselves.

I was able to really help my then-girlfriend by promising to go clean (of drugs). Worked well. For like three months she only cut once and that was during a pretty big fight.

You have to talk to her, IMO. Say that you're her boyfriend and in love with her. Nothing is as important in your life as she is. And, in order to have to have a good loving relationship, you two have to both be honest with each other. Not about the past, but about things that affect the other person. This is something important. No one should have to deal with that pain alone.

Of course, she should probably seek out professional help.
