First off, the PF tek is Brown rice flour and vermiculite, perlite is used to humidify your fruiting chamber.

was wondering if any experianced growers can give pro's and cons of the brown rice vs rye
Of course.
Whole Grains
Pros-
-Very cheap. about 20lbs for $10.
-Colonize faster. You can shake these and you aren't limited to tapered jars.
-Possibility of Grain to grain transfer: 1 jar can be turned into 100 relatively easily. You can also use 1 colonized grain to make an entire spore syringe.
-More nutritious than brown rice flour: larger yields.
-Easily spawned to bulk substrates.
Cons-
-A Pressure cooker is required to sterilize whole grains completely.
-It takes about a day to prepare whole grains.
PF-TEK
Pros-
-Still relatively cheap.
-Very easy and VERY forgiving.
-takes about a 1 and a half hours to prepare.
-Can be spawned to bulk.
Cons-
-Slower than whole grains.
-Vermiculite can sometimes be hard to find.

So yeah, PF-Tek would be best for your first grow.

I think you're thinking of dunking and rolling. Basically, dunking is a technique that fortifies the H2O content of you cakes(sort of like a casing). To dunk your cakes, all you have to do is place your cakes in distilled H2O, and let them soak for an hour or two. Then, you take them out, and roll them around in vermiculite, sort of like making battered fried chicken. It's an optional step, but it usually pays off to do it.
