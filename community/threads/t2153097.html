'Flying ointment', and natural deleriants and hallucinogens (warning - long).
I've been curious about this substance for a long time, and have decided to try making some myself. I'm not interested in this for recreational potential per se, as in I don't want to go out and party, but I'm not aiming for a spiritual experience either. It's curiosity, and a desire to experience something completely otherworldly.

Some background first. Flying ointment is the name given to the mix of herbs and plants that witches allegedly used to induce sensations of flying or other out of body, out of mind experiences. I'm not one for the witchcraft side of it, but the experience side interests me greatly. The most common referenced ingredients are plants of the Solanaceae family, which contain a wide range of psychoactive alkaloids, most importantly the tropane alkaloids. Datura contains some of them, which quite a few people here are familiar with. Other plants of the family include deadly nightshade/belladonna, angel's trumpets (brugmansia) and henbane, and other ingredients include monkshood/wolfsbane, mandrake root and fly agaric mushrooms.

That said, living in the modern world we have some benefits that weren't available to Medieval European witches, and so I'm planning on adding other ingredients which they'd be unable to procure. My list of potential candidates include cannabis, psilocybe mushrooms (the magic variety), DMT-containing herbs and plants used in brewing ayahuasca such as B. caapi, Psychotria viridis/carthagensis, mimosa bark and chaliponga (contains 5-MeO-DMT as well), opium poppy pods, possibly one or more plants of the Pilocarpus family and possibly dream herb (C. zacatechichi), which is used to induce vivid and memorable dreams. Yes, I had to copy-paste half the names. The other benefit is a wealth of information courtesy of the internet, which is going to be seriously beneficial because I'm not a lifelong herbalist and need to be as careful as possible.

A lot of these plants can be deadly in the wrong doses. The risk appears to be lessened when you're taking them transdermally (through the skin) rather than orally, but I don't want to take any risks. Any risks apart from actually trying this stuff, anyway. I'm posting the plans here because there are a lot of seasoned drug users, psychonauts and general fuck-ups on this forum, and with something like this I think the more advice I can get the better, even if most of the expertise here isn't really herbal.

For the base, I plan to use beeswax and almond or apricot oil, as well as some jasmine and lavender oil because they're used as relaxants. I like all the smells involved. The proportions recommended vary, so I'm gonna combine them a little at a time until I find a proportion that gives a good consistency. I'm aiming to make quite a lot of the base, for two reasons; one, I don't want the active ingredients to be too concentrated, and two, I want to save some and not use it all at once. I'm gonna make up a very small batch of the pure base first, just to make sure it smells nice (seems strange, but I want to be in the best possible frame of mind for this).

As for the ingredients, they've been selected for the psychoactive compounds they contain, which is kind of the point. Belladonna is one of the key ingredients, containing atropine, scopolamine and hyascyamine. I'm going to use the root, because the variation in the levels of tropane alkaloids is lower than in the leaves. Pilocarpus plants are a maybe because they contain pilocarpine, which is used as an antidote to belladonna poisoning. However, I don't know if they'll relieve all the effects or just the potentially dangerous ones, and I can't find specifics on whether or not that'll also counter the hallucinations and delirium. Brugmansia, datura and mandrake also contain various tropane alkaloids, atropine being common to all of them. B. caapi contains MAOIs (harmine/harmaline), which potentiate the action of the DMT which is found in the plants traditionally used in ayahuasca. Fly agaric mushrooms contain ibotenic acid, which converts to the psychoactive muscimol when dried. You know about cannabis and psilocybe mushrooms, and if you don't, you're probably not going to be able to help much. Opium/morphine from the poppies apparently helps to counter some of the negative side effects of the atropine and suchlike.

This combination should produce a completely delirious trip of 2-3 days. Experience reports on the various components describe various trips lasting 24-72 hours, and obviously there are other shorter-acting constituents too like the mushies and weed. I've discussed this with two close friends who have a lot of drug experience who would both be willing to take turns trip-sitting me and making sure I don't hurt myself or anything, and I'm lucky enough to have the kind of family that I could tell my exact intentions to and explain what's going to happen. I have a safe environment and before embarking on this journey I'd spend a lot of time making sure that everything dangerous would be out of the way, I'm planning on eating a large meal not long before I begin (not too large, but filling and nutritious, I figure I won't be eating when I'm tripping) and keeping large bottles of water everywhere around. Food, water, a safe, comfortable and trusted environment, and responsible people around who know what's going to happen; those seem like good prerequisites to me.

I'm aware that these trips are not enjoyable in the same sense that mushroom or good acid trips are. Trip reports of deadly nightshade and other atropine-containing plants in particular usually sound rather unsettling and confusing with people with missing eyes being a recurring theme for some reason, datura trips are all over the place (and usually very poorly remembered, so I'm not going to use a lot), ayahuasca trips are intense and deep, really sound like another state of consciousness... the combination should be interesting, hopefully. Does anyone have any experience with a combination of two or more of the drugs above? I'm not looking to enjoy myself so much as to have an experience I'll never forget, although obviously I'd like to appreciate it instead of freaking out or dying or something.

As for taking it, I'm first going to apply a small quantity to some sensitive skin like the forearm or inside of the elbow to see if there are any adverse reactions. After a day, if there aren't any (and as long as I'm not feeling off baseline from the little dab), I plan to apply the ointment to pulse points, other thin skin and the good ol' genitals. I'm not going to cover myself, but I'm going to apply a fair bit. I'll work out the amount I should apply after I know exactly what I've put in it, with some input from you guys.

I think that's everything. I'm aware that this is one huge post, but hopefully people with something intelligent to contribute will actually bother reading it. Save the TL;DRs, I already know. I want to get this right, so I'm hoping some of you can help. Suggestions, criticisms, advice on dosage, experiences, whatever. I want to hear from you.

And of course, a full and comprehensive trip report shall be compiled and posted for your perusal.

From what I've now gathered, muscimol and solanaceous alkaloids have very similar properties, but seeing as I can't really procure fly agaric easily (within the next day, anyway), I'm gonna leave it out. After reading up on interactions between MAOIs and pretty much everything (thanks, double dosed on sunshine) I've decided to leave out the B. caapi (harmine/harmaline), which means there's little point in including the plants containing DMT or 5-MeO-DMT, which I thought about again and realised was a ridiculously stupid idea anyway. The opium poppies are definitely staying in after further research; if the active dose of atropine or the other solanaceous alkaloids is greater than the corresponding active dose of opium/morphine then the deleriant and hallucinogenic properties of the alkaloids remain, but several of the negative side-effects (such as elevated heart and respiration rate and increased body temperature) are countered to some extent. Atropine and co. also counter some of the negative side-effects of the morphine, mostly shit like nausea.

I think the combination I'm going to go for is Atropa belladonna (deadly nightshade), mandrake root, opium poppy pods, psilocybin mushrooms, henbane (stinking nightshade), datura and cannabis. I've decided to omit poison hemlock because it can be fatal in much lower and less predictable doses than any of the other ingredients. The base will be a combination of pure natural beeswax and apricot oil. Far closer to true Medieval flying ointment than the prospective ingredients above, with the exception of the datura, mushies and weed. Those last two aren't really necessary since they're shorter-acting, but I figure it'll be interesting.

I need to acquire deadly nightshade (I'm going to use leaves for ease of collection), henbane and apricot oil. I'm going to get the two plant materials tomorrow morning, and the oil tomorrow afternoon. I've got everything else. The datura is in the form of Datura metel seeds which I'm going to loosely crush. The mandrake root I'm planning on grating. I'm planning on chopping the mushies fairly finely, crushing the poppy pods and giving the cannabis a slightly grind. I'm not crushing, chopping or grinding any of it too small because I want to be able to sieve out all of the miscellaneous plant material, which in turn is because I don't want to be using an ointment that contains little bits of mandrake or weed. I'm going to try to make the ointment thin enough to be able to pass it through a fine sieve, but thick enough that it's not runny or sloppy or anything.

Once I've got the oil tomorrow afternoon I'm going to start making the base and adding the ingredients. Advice is to keep the ointment over as low a heat as possible that'll keep it liquid, and let it simmer for a while. I'm thinking 24 hours will more than do the trick.

Alright, a small patch of 90-minute-heated ointment was applied to the inside of my elbows, the softest bit of my neck and my balls. It's ever so slightly tingly, and quite cooling. 90 minutes later and nothing. No redness, no allergic reactions, no itchiness, no visual distortion at all and no perceptual distortion as far as I can tell (although with these compounds, you often can't tell, so I might be tripping balls in a really boring manner, thinking I'm actually typing out this post while I'm lying curled up in a ball in my hallway or something). If anything, my skin feels really nice and smooth where I applied the ointment (I washed it off with warm water now), it's definitely softer than the surrounding skin. Bonus.

I've got the full ointment going now. 900g of beeswax and about 400ml of the apricot oil, an eighth of fairly good ground bud, the same weight of chopped, dried mushies, 26 mostly dried and shredded atropa belladonna leaves (12g or so), 260 crushed datura metel seeds (again about an eighth, 3.5g), mostly dried and shredded henbane leaves & a flower (~10g) and 15g dried and ground mandrake root. I've also got a solution on the go using more oil and enough poppy seeds to make a few cups of opium tea (a cup being enough to get one person nicely out of it once time), which I'm planning to add separately because there's too much to put in the ointment on their own 'cos I don't want to have to fish out a tonne of seeds. I think I've used too much base, but I added most of the ingredients before I realised I didn't actually want a kilo and a half of psychoactive ointment. I think I'm going to have to apply a thin layer over most of my body for this, because a thick layer won't absorb well and won't be concentrated enough.

EDIT: I came across a vial of 20x salvia that I'd forgotten about and considered chucking it in, but figured it would be counterproductive. /EDIT

This seems to be about five times too much for a single application that would lead to an intense, completely delirious trip, given what little I can find on transdermal absorption. I'm going to apply roughly a tenth of what I'm planning to apply as a test, probably on my chest or back, from which I expect to be a little off-baseline but in a functional mental state. I'll use this as a guideline for how much to apply.

Activepsycho, although I think a traditional shaman wouldn't be particularly impressed with my spiritual mindset (or lack of thereof), but I'm glad to have your support. I haven't read the Teachings, but I will take your advice and read some of them tonight to see what they have to say. I've read through a few extracts already and find some pieces interesting philosophically, although not on the spiritual level. Unfortunately my available datura is in seed form (well, it's now in crushed-seed-in-warm-ointment form), and as unwise as I know it is, I want as little prior experience as possible. What I'm aiming for is something as far removed from reality as possible, and to experience a little of it would link the experience with my current subjective reality. I hope that nakes sense.

Thank you all for your help and advice. I'll check on this thread again tomorrow and hopefully update when I'm about to begin. Wish me luck (if you're kind), and don't expect to hear from me again for a little while after that!
