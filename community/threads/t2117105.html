I did 14.5 grams (over half an ounce) once with a friend (he ate about 12 grams).

That was more like DMT that fucking mushrooms. It felt as though I was in some golden spherical object, not unlike a soccer ball, just meandering through space.

Stars were absolutely undescribable to look at...

Conversation was next to impossible... it largely consisted of:

Me: "I JUST FIGURED IT OUT!"
Him: "Was that a frog?"
Me: "I think I'm going swimming."
Him: "HOLY FUCK MY ARM HURTS!"
Me: "But... I thought your arm was... holy shit, I JUST FIGURED IT OUT!!"

And of course, we'd take turns asking eachother what time it was every three seconds... leading to a great deal of confusion over what time was.

Eventually, my friend literally collapsed to the ground -- psychedelic overload, I suppose. And I was just sitting there, completely unable to comprehend that perhaps my friend needed help with something.

I have a recording we made somewhere, I'll have to look for it.

Haha. Good times. Of course, my friend ended up freaking out and crying, he ended up spending the rest of his night with his mom, just pouring his heart out. That trip changed him a lot. For the better. For me... it was after that trip that I sort of "came into my own" -- i.e. developed a distinct personality / persona. Definitely changed me in many ways.

I guess what I'm trying to say with this is that once you go 5 grams +, it can be a bit life changing...

Quarter-ounce mushroom trips are pretty intense, but you're still somewhat connected with reality. Strips the ego away very nicely.

Fuck, writing this makes me nostalgic... I gotta have an intense trip sometime soon.

Re: quarter ounce mushroom trip
6-7g of Philipino Cubensis mushrooms...

Dropped in a graveyard and biked up and down hills for the first half an hour or so when they were coming on, and up towards the "downs" countryside-ish area out of my mate's town.

5-6g of mushrooms is SRS BSNS. Do not underestimate such a powerful mushroom trip, it can seriously affect your psyche and throw a lot of shit your way, no matter how good you may be feeling too. I ended up getting seperated from my friend, biking down a huge grass hill whilst the grass around me danced and swayed and shimmered with beauty, yelling at random people on bikes, reading William Blake in a field, trying to make rollie ciggies and finding it seriously hard and weird, talking about how a road looked like a dinosaur, bombing 100mgish of MDMA, getting cold, having the strongest CEVs i've ever had (think Indian Godess talking to me) and a hell of a lot of random shit.

I don't regret it, but I don't think it was solely good for my mind. This was the last time I did mushrooms and I feel I have pushed the boundaries with them, n now have few desires to ever do them again. Try and be with someone you trust, who is experienced and will be ok by themself if you do get seperated. Don't drop near people or cities or anything, countryside in SUNSHINE and warmth is what you want, and i'd suggest dropping 5 and then maybe another 1.5-2 rather than just a 1/4 at once, which would have a seriously mad come up. I guess it's worth it though, we rode over a "sea" of red, crunchy autumn leaves coming up, and the sounds.... omfg I almost cried, no joke, insanely beautiful...

Good luck.
