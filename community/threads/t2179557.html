Re: Liquor suggestions
If you don't care for Wild Turkey 101, but want to try a good bourbon that's easy on the wallet, I suggest Even Williams. Far smoother than Beam or Turkey, and a far cry easier on the wallet. Their green label (80 proof) is excellent for all applications (I had some over the rocks on Friday night, and was thoroughly enjoying it), and their black label (83 proof, if I recall) is excellent as well. Both are available for under $10/fifth, or the same you'd pay for a pint of Jim Beam's white label.

Whoever suggested Absolut vodka is right on. A very even, very serviceable vodka that doesn't cost an arm and a leg. Tanqueray is my go-to for gin (though I prefer Beefeater, because I like my gin extra dry), and learning to mix a proper martini with either gin or vodka is certainly something I'd encourage.
