My valuable life-lesson
I've done a lot of complaining here. I've brought all of my problems to you, poured out my heart. I haven't brought very many positives, probably because I only began posting frequently at a low-point in life.

I just felt like posting another part of my experiences, been doing a lot of reflecting and dont really have anyone else to share with. I know its a long read, but hey sometimes people get bored enough to read the long ones.

Someone like me shouldn't believe in love, but I still do. Foolish? Normally I would think so, but not in this case.

Did you know I used to be anti-marriage? I decided when I was 13 that marriage just wasn't for me. I didn't want a family. I never wanted to risk having something that I could lose or hurt. I wanted to be free, to keep my independence. I wanted to be accountable to me and me alone, and maybe a cat or dog. I didn't want to be responsible for/to a husband or a child.



I stuck to my anti-marriage guns all through high school and college. My boyfriends always knew that I would most likely remain an independent cuss, and that I could walk away from them if it suited me, no matter how much I loved them. They knew I wasn't interested in forever, but only as long as it suited us both.

Then I met him. He wasn't my type. He drank and had sex with his girlfriends, whereas I had never touched a drop and was saving my virginity for my husband. We shared the same views on church, but not God. We were also 6000 miles apart. No chance... but still there was something there, what the hell could it be? Something...

Neither of us were really looking around. I had broken up with my last boyfriend about 2 months earlier and was not keen on getting involved in anything with anyone, he had decided to take a break from women for awhile.

We had known each other for quite awhile, we were members of an online community (sort of like this one, only warez oriented) and we had had a few good IRC conversations during the preceding year.

We exchanged pictures... it seems horribly shallow but I think that's what sparked the interest. He was just about the best looking man I had ever seen, but it wasn't just that that drew me in. It wasn't that the picture was great, it was really goofy. He had this ridiculous grin smeared on his face, and he must have been pissed off his ass, but there it was again, that something. Something in his eyes, or his smile... just something.

He said he thought I was beautiful, I thought that was sweet but I didn't believe him. Based on looks alone I considered him way out of my league. Then there were his other qualities...

He was a sweet guy, a kind person something SO rare. He wasnt kind to the point were he would let himself get pushed around, but he was gentle and sincere, and funny-as, too. He had a beautiful sense of humour. He was the first real person Id met in quite awhile. He had real character.

He was just graduating (with honours) from Uni with a degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering... very smart guy, but he wasn't a geek. I learned about his hobbies, he loved to play Field Hockey, and was very good at it. He had worked as a kayak instructor, and at a bike shop. He shared my passion for mountain biking, score! He was creative, he enjoyed drawing and showed me his artwork. He actually could draw, now that's not a common ability in engineers. Our love of music gave us plenty to talk about, never before had I met someone with a passion for music that even compared to mine. He came from a good family, the stories he told reminded me so much of my own family at better times. He was a hard worker, a trait I very much admire and share. We never ran out of things to talk about and interests to share. We talked about everything, for both of us it felt as though we had met someone with an equal intellect. He became my best friend, with that underlying something.

Our relationship began like I really wish I could find someone like you around here.

Soon it was I wish there were some way for you to be where I am.

And finally it became I dont care how far away you are, youre the one I want.

We fell in love and were finally able to find ways to express that something that had been there all along.

We were both fairly cautious looking back its amusing because it really took us such a long time to become totally honest with each other about our feelings. We were both nervous about the distance, but the more we opened up the more we realized we were thinking and feeling the very same things.

We wrote letters to each other, we talked online every night, and on the phone most nights. It was never hard spending time together, never a chore, in fact it was always a relief to come home to each other.

I noticed a change in my perspective for the first time I felt like this was someone I could be with, forever, without losing myself, my life, without giving up on my dreams he was someone who would just as happily support me in accomplishing my goals as I would support him in accomplishing his. Not only that, but this was someone I could love enough to guarantee my lifetime commitment, for better for worse. Looking at us from every logical standpoint available we didnt have to worry about the possibility of this ever ending. A fairy-tale you may say, but that IS taking into consideration that we did have problems too. Like any healthy relationship we had angry moments, frustrating moments, I dont want to talk to you! moments. Aside from the 6000 miles between us everything about us was fairly ordinary, except our love. That was pretty extraordinary.

It was my mistake that ruined us. I allowed myself to lose respect for him. He lied to me about something, something BIG to me. I didnt handle it very well, but it involved my virginity so it took on a head of steam and I allowed it to destroy me. I didnt love him, thats how much of an impact I allowed it to have. I refused to feel anything for him but eventually I realized how little sense it made. While my feelings (or lack thereof I should say) were very real, and very powerful, I realized that I shouldnt allow them to take away something so special it didnt feel so special, but I refused to forget or ignore what had been, and could possibly be again.

I learned to trust him again, but I forgot the respect. I tried to control the relationship and him in order to avoid getting hurt again. I started to question his judgement, his abilities, everything. I gave him far less credit than he deserved. Thats pretty easy to see now, but at the time I thought I was only protecting myself.

I treated him like shit for months, not constantly, but whenever there were problems I turned into a monster. I was impatient and rude, demanding and bitchy. I complained about anything, everything. He was patient with me, I think maybe he felt he deserved it. How I wish he had told me where to get off, so many times I just needed a slap in the face to wake me up but he just took it.

He took it until I broke him.

Then I woke up, saw what I had done, saw what I had become, saw what I had just lost

All of the complaints Ive posted here on totse, all of the situations Ive described where he has been treating me like shit the thing is I actually deserve it. Taking the attitude that I deserve it will only get me about as far as he did when he thought I had the right to treat him like shit, so I dont just accept it. But I know deep down that I deserve it, that Ive fully earned it. I still refuse to just take it because I would much prefer to bear it, repair it, and still be given the second chance I know I really dont deserve. I could do wonders with a second chance, the lesson Ive learned makes me just about the wisest chick hell ever meet. I have never made the same mistake twice.

Im pretty sure itll never happen, but who knows. Maybe hell sit me down 2 years from now and ask me if Im keen on a date stranger things have happened. At the moment hes being smart, hes looking out for himself and refusing to let me have any positive effect on him. If he allows me to have a positive effect then he might consider a second chance, and if he considers a second chance then he opens himself up for another round of hell.

The strongest and most constant feeling I get from him is that I am the one thing that he perceives stands between him and the rest of his life... I don't know if he actually believes that, or if it's a thought he holds onto to help keep me at a distance. Fortunately it couldn't be further from the truth, and deep down I think he must know that because he is aware of the fact that I accept him, as he is now, and as he will be later. He knows that he still exceeds my expectations. Hell, he still makes me happy, after everything. And he knows that I am as interested in his future and happiness as he is. He knows I have no desire to control him or his life, and that I would rather die than see him lose a grip on his dreams.

The irony is that we share a lifestyle as friends/flatmates/whatever that 1 year ago he actually described as his ideal relationship, but in his mind we are miserable together, incompatible. All the love and sweetness I can muster up falls to the ground dead when it comes in contact with the barrier he has put up. The good things I have to offer can't get through anymore. But every drop of dischord and negativity finds it's way straight to his heart and pushes him even further away. We have disagreements no more often than we did before, the difference is that now the respect and cooperation factor is no longer in place so the negative impact of our small, most of the time petty, disagreements has about 100 times the effect that it used to.

His opinion is that the only way we would ever again stand a chance is if he somehow found a way to fall in love with me again. Its obvious to me that that will never happen unless we worked out these other issues. How do you fall in love with someone when you have so many unresolved problems? It cant happen. Im not going to try and convince him hes wrong, I did enough damage of that sort before. And how do you resolve these kinds of problems? That Im not really sure of, probably because weve never tried to sort them out. Were stuck in this place where we both want to cooperate for our own separate reasons, but we dont know how to go about it, how to even approach it because weve been hurting each other for so long. It would involve a lot of (almost foolhardy when you consider the risk) trust on both of our parts because we have broken nearly all of the promises we ever made to each other. He would be an idiot to ever trust me again, but am I worth it? I think so Im not known for having a huge ego, or being overconfident if anything I dont think very much of myself, but I know, I KNOW I do have what it would take to make Ben happy not just happy, but happier than anyone else ever could. As always I still want the best for him, not what I think is best, but what HE thinks is best for him. I'll always hope that he gets the best life has to offer, I want all of his dreams to come true and for him to be 100% content and after all of my mistakes I still believe that I have the potential to be the best partner there is I think I have more happiness to give him than any other woman... after all my fuck-ups I can still say that, fairly amazing. It's sad that it will most likely always be wasted potential.



So now you know, he used to love me, he really did. That's not just something I choose to believe because it makes me feel better, it would feel better to believe that it was all wrong and that he was never any good. It's just a fact that he was that good. WE were that good. He loved me so well. We loved each other equally, it was beautiful. It was true love from both sides. If I could convince myself that we really are wrong for each other then I could just move on. It simply isn't true. To this day I know that, regardless of what has happened, if we were to give it the tiniest second chance we could work it out and be happy, for the rest of our lives kind of happy. Neither of us will ever be able to find a more compatible partner, we'll never be able to make anyone else as happy as we made each other. I can say with confidence that he won't be able to replace me or the happiness I once gave him, the happiness he is now too stubborn to allow himself to feel. I won't be able to give that kind of happiness to someone else either. I also won't ever be able to find a love more true. That's what's so damn depressing, the fact that I know the best is behind me. I've fully considered the fact that I could've been fooling myself, that it was one-sided all along, but no-one could have ever pulled off such a fantastic charade.

I also think that he believes he has changed too much... and occasionally I think he has changed too. When I do something kind and he brushes me away, or when he says something particularly painful to hear I think to myself "where did the Ben I knew go?" but he's there, he's still the same Ben. Our situation has changed and the way we relate is askew... but when he smiles, when he laughs, the rare times he allows himself to be happy I can still see it's him after all.

"If it was so real, then why is it over?" I dunno, probably never will understand it. Human nature? I guess...

I've learned that true love really is as rare as 'they' say, and that it's NOT unconditional. I've learned to be much more careful when I open my mouth to complain.

He learned not to just take never-ending shit... and to stand up for himself. I'm glad he learned it, I hope he never lets anyone get away with the things I did... But I'll never be able to forgive myself for being the one that taught him this lesson.

The point of this post? Dunno really, just need to get this kind of thing out there, anywhere. Maybe it'll help someone else avoid the kind of mistakes I made?
