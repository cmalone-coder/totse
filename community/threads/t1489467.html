Re: shopping for guys
First of all, ignore everyone who suggests what you should buy... they don't know your guy or his likes/dislikes.

Second, shopping for guys is tricky business... but I'll bet that it's just as hard for them guys to shop for females.

You have to get inside his head... once you're tuned in to the male brain frequency then shopping for him becomes a cinche. Look at everything from his perspective, this can be really difficult if you don't know him well... concentrate on his hobbies, what does he spend lots of time doing? Computers? Video games? Music? Movies? Does he have a specific taste in clothing? Does he need clothing? Do you know his size or are you able to get that info from his mum?

If you buy clothing for a guy you HAVE to ignore your taste, and concentrate on HIS taste. Remember that even though YOU like it, he still might hate it. Even mum's screw up when buying clothing for their sons... so you can clearly see that clothing is the hardest thing, so don't jump into this unless you've got him figured out.

Budget is also a big thing, guys hobbies are rarely inexpensive. If he snowboards then even buying him a new pair of gloves will cost you $200... and it's not the coolest gift to give (it MIGHT be, depending on the guy)

Also, if you're going to get something that relates to a hobby and you're not prepared to spend enough to get the BEST of whatever you're getting, then don't attempt it... for instance, say he's into skateboarding... if it comes down to buying a low-quality board because it's the only one you can afford, or buying something else (t-shirt, high-end skateboard wheels, etc) that is the top if the line for the same amount of money, go with the less expensive, higher-quality gift.



My guy is a computer and electronic engineer, and he spends a lot of time on his computer. He kept mentioning that he was running out of space (he downloads heaps and also rips all of his cd's onto his hard-drive) so when his birthday rolled around one of the gifts I gave him was an 80GB Seagate HDD... now you raise your brow in disbelief and say "you gave a hard-drive as a birthday present?!" but I guarantee he loved it... I could've gotten a shoddy 120GB Hard Drive for a lot less money, but I did my homework and I got the best Hard Drive on the market for what I had to spend. I also got other things, girlfriendy meaningfull gifts like a spindle of 80min CD-r's, 2 T-shirts, a CD of nudey pictures of yours truly, a mushy lovey-dovey book that I hand-made (guys dig the romantic crap too ya know) and a dancing kung-foo hamster...

Just focus on him, pay attention to every little thing he says, don't be obvious or you'll give it away, but ask him questions that you think might show you the way.

Mum's are more than happy to help their son's girlfriends when it comes to size info, or snooping around to find out what cd's, games, or movies he already owns. They dated too way back when so they know how hard it can be.

Here is something I found on a website a couple years ago...Hopefully it will clear a few things up and help you!

Rule #1:

When in doubt - buy him a cordless drill. It does not matter if

he already has one. I have a friend who owns 17 and he has yet

to complain. As a man, you can never have too many cordless

drills. No one knows why.

Rule #2:

If you cannot afford a cordless drill, buy him anything with the

word ratchet or socket in it. Men love saying those two words.

"Hey George, can I borrow your ratchet?" "OK. By-the-way, are

you through with my 3/8-inch socket yet?" Again, no one knows

why.

Rule #3:

If you are really, really broke, buy him anything for his car. A

99-cent ice scraper, a small bottle of de-icer or something to

hang from his rear view mirror. Men love gifts for their cars.

No one knows why.

Rule #4:

Do not buy men socks. Do not buy men ties. And never buy men

bathrobes. I was told that if God had wanted men to wear

bathrobes, he wouldn't have invented Jockey shorts.

Rule #5:

You can buy men new remote controls to replace the ones they

have worn out. If you have a lot of money buy your man a

big-screen TV with the little picture in the corner. Watch him

go wild as he flips, and flips, and flips.

Rule #6:

Buy men label makers. Almost as good as cordless drills. Within

a couple of weeks there will be labels absolutely everywhere.

"Socks" "Shorts" "Cups" "Saucers" "Door" "Lock" "Sink"... You

get the idea. No one knows why.

Rule #7:

Never buy a man anything that says "some assembly required" on

the box. It will ruin his Special Day and he will always have

parts left over. No one knows why.

Rule #8:

Men enjoy danger. That's why they never cook - but they will

barbecue. Get him a monster barbecue with a 100-pound propane

tank. Tell him the gas line leaks. "Oh the thrill! The

challenge! Who wants a hamburger?"

Rule #9:

Tickets to a football game are a smart gift. However, he will

not appreciate tickets to "A Retrospective of 19th Century

Quilts." Everyone knows why.

Rule #10:

Men love chainsaws. Never, ever, buy a man you love a chainsaw.

If you don't know why - please refer to Rule #8 and what happens

when he gets a label maker.

Rule #11:

It's hard to beat a really good wheelbarrow or an aluminum

extension ladder. Never buy a real man a step ladder. It must be

an extension ladder. No one knows why.

Rule #12:

Rope. Men love rope. It takes them back to their cowboy origins,

or at least The Boy Scouts. Nothing says love like a hundred

feet of 3/8" manila rope. No one knows why.
