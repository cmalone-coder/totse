Re: Copper for cash
I was reading an article on Copper and it's likes. Seems like ATT and Verizon reported losses in the millions due to copper wire being stolen from their installations. They claimed copper is hot, because it is harder to trace. Also, 24,000 manhole covers were stolen in 2006 for their metal in China alone!. Pretty darn crazy I say.

Most recycling yards are increasingly asking for IDs, but if you can be a bit professional about your selling tactics... $3.25/pound of copper is very attainable. Also, I DO NOT CONDONE STEALING, BUT if you want to make serious money.... try to get your hands on catalytic converters and stuff. The Palladium, Rhodium, and Platinum in them is worth $150 scrap... while a manhole will get you $20 at most. So work smart, not hard!
