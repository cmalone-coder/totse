Starting a lawn mowing business
OK so I've been planning this for a while and am going to share it with you. Please let me know if I've overlooked something because I don't want this to be a flop.

I used my life savings to buy a lawn mower so that isn't a problem. I will be spending around $10 on gas a week and mowing 10 peoples lawns a week @ $10 each. I already have my clients lined up so ultimately I will be pocketing $90 a week. Times that by 52 and that's $4,680 a year. Let's deduct 4 weeks worth ($360) for holidays and I'm left with $4,320 a year. Now divide that into $573,700 and it should take me approximately 132 years to buy the Ferrari F430 I've always wanted.

Of course along the way I'll have bought some food etc. but that can be allowed for as the price of the F430 will most likely decrease over 132 years.

What do you guys think?
