For me:

1. The beginning battle of Saving Private Ryan, which I consider to be the most epic twenty minutes in film. Period. I also love the bit in the final battle where I think it was Upham who kills the German they had earlier set free.

2. The opening of Super Troopers.

"I'm freaking out, man."

"You are freaking out ... man."

3. The scene in Contact where the scientists first catch wind of the extraterrestrial transmission. Also, where Jodie Foster meets the extraterrestrial which took the form of her father.

Admittedly, the latter scene was much better in the book.

4. In Half Baked where the guys are arguing about spending the drug money.

(Jim Breuer) You said you gave Mary Jane a pearl necklace, how much did that cost?

(Dave Chappelle) Obviously you missed the point of that story, Brian.

5. The ending of The Lion, the Witch, and the Wardrobe.

(The Professor) "What were you all doing in the wardrobe?

(Peter) "You wouldn't believe us if we told you, sir."

(The Professor, smiling) "Try me."

6. Enemy at the Gates. The scene at the fountain.

Admittedly EatG is probably my favorite movie period.

7. The Clerks scene involving a discussion about 37 dicks.

8. Riot control test #2 in the second Jackass movie.

"We're here with Daniel Alvarez from ALS technologies, and, uh, why don't you explain what this is and what we're gonna be doing?"

"This is the 460 stingmore mine. It has approximately 700 .45 caliber rubber balls in it. They'll fly out of there at about five hundred feet per second. They use these charges on the outside of embassies to protect the gates from people trying to break in."

"It is, I promise."

9. President Thomas Whitmore's speech in Independence Day.
