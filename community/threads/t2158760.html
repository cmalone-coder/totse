But whereas I know that these pictures can be generated easily using long exposure or low light conditions or directional lighting or whatever, other people persist in attaching significance that isn't there.

The human brain is very adept at recognising patterns. It's evolved to pick out patterns and faces since well before we were human, it's had millions of years to refine it. The result of this pattern-recognition ability is that occasionally our mind returns false positives; ever look at the clouds and pick out figures or objects?
