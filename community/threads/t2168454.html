The whole theory that smoking out of soda cans is unhealthy came from the presence of elevated levels of aluminum in alzheimers patients.

This theory is complete bullshit because:

-There is no evidence that aluminum causes alzheimers. (It could be that alzheimers causes elevated levels of aluminum in humans. It is also possible that some third variable, like genetics, causes both)

-Even if ingesting aluminum did cause alzheimers, its boiling point is 4,473 degrees fahrenheit. That means that you would have to heat your soda can to that point before any of the aluminum turned into an inhalable vapor. Since your lighter (even a torch lighter) doesnt get anywhere near that hot, there is no concievable way that you could inhale aluminum from smoking out of a can.
