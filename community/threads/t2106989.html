Re: Keif
i always collect kif, and there's a few ways i smoke it.

you can use a vape. i use a light bulb. it helps to make a long "spoon" out of paper to drop it all in a nice little pile in the bulb rather than let it scatter.

you can buy some legal herbs (or use tobacco) and roll it into a joint. this is somewhat less effective.

you can press it into a nice, solid chunk in a number of methods. the quarter/vice method works. i made a press out of lego's (if you really want to know how, i can post instructions), but later made on a rapid prototype printer out of ABS plastic. once pressed you can just drop it into a bowl.

another thing to do after it's pressed and stick it to the end of a pin (carefully), and let it burn, like incense, into a bottle or cup, somewhere you can catch the smoke, and then when it burns out, inhale the smoke in the bottle/other.

or just make an aluminum screen and a bowl. a bic won't get aluminum hot enough to release harmful vapors.
