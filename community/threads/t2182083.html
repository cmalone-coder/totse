Re: Yes, it's another kidiot question RE: codeine.
1,500 mg of APAP is fine. I take about twice that amount for a headache at times. Not that I recommend that...

15.3 mg of Doxylamine is actually about 10 mg lower than the standard amount in your run-of-the-mill OTC sleep aid, which I happen to have a box of here I got at CVS that are 25 mg a pill. Yes, it could make you a bit drowsy, but a lot of people, myself included, find that they enjoy opiates moreso with a low-dose of antihistamine alongside it. To be honest with you I normally go with 50 mg diphenhydramine or 50mg promethazine with my opiates and have never tried it with doxylamine but considering that all three of those are antihistamines commonly found within roughly the same dosage formulation (25 mg), I'd be inclined to believe that it will work just fine.

90 mg of codeine certainly isn't much to be using recreationally, but hey, if you can get high on it, please accept my jealousy. I don't know how sensitive you are to the sedative effects of doxylamine, but if I were in your shoes with what I'm assuming is a likely non-existent opiate tolerance, I would pop four instead of three which would take it to 2,000 mg of APAP and 20.4 mg of doxylamine with 120 mg of codeine. Give three a whirl first, though, in case you find the doxylamine too sedating to you.
