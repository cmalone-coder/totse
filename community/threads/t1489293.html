Here is a rule that is true 9 times out of 10: If a woman is playing head games with you and you are a satellite boyfriend (no sex, she probably has a boyfriend, and she is stringing you along as a friend with flirting privileges) then you are probably being used. If you are the REAL BF and you are getting the head games, she is probably insecure.

Not all women are this way, but many will be so tough to hook up with at first, but then will stay with you no matter what till they find something better. They fear being alone when they are in a relationship. That is why so many stay with an abusive man. When they dont like him anymore, they give him the treatment youre getting, but they dont break it off. I have seen this several times, and a couple times up close and personal behind the scenes. It really is an interesting phenomenon.

More than likely, she has given you hints about what is wrong. There are two main ways this could have happened. First, in the beginning of your relationship, she described things she wanted in a BF or relationship that did not turn out like she hoped. Second, things could have changed in you, and she has complained about them. We tend to not give the same level of importance to these complaints as they do, since they are often infrequent and ignoring them is so easy to do.

You really cannot ask her right now what is wrong. She just is not going to tell you. Start with subtly letting her know you like her. Dont buy flowers. An indirect compliment works wonders. After you have done this for a while and you see her warming to it a bit, be a tad more overt. Direct compliments, quick kisses on the lips, ask if you can help with whatever.

Then ask her for a date like it will be your first. On the date, have a little speech prepared apologizing for being lazy in your relationship and letting her know you know she feels unsatisfied and that that feeling is justified. Make her accept your apology (much easier than saying she will stay with you) and then confess you are a bit of a dumbass and you need some assistance in figuring ways to please her or whatever.

Then send her the fucking flowers.
