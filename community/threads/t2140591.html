Re: Catalyic Converters
Seeing as I am on the buying end of converters at work, I will tell you some hints.

The weapon of choice is a battery powered sawz-all. With a good metal blade, you can be through the pipe in under 5 minutes.

The best vehicles to hit at the moment will be Toyota 4 Runners. The catalytic from them brings close to $200. Also good are BMW and Mercedes. Anything foreign will normally net you more $$ than most domestics. Some GM converters will also bring a fair amount of money. Stay away from Ford, they have the worst converters for the money. If the converter looks cheap, and has flimsy shielding on it, that converter may be an aftermarket unit, which will only net you around $10. Stay away from those.

Another good tip is to cut an extra section of pipe off of the converter before you sell it. Just an inch will do. It keeps the cops from being able to match it back up to the vehicle you stole it from, should questions arise later.

The best hint is, don't get caught in the act. That is pretty much the only way you can get prosecuted at the moment, since there is no efficient way to trace the converter back to the vehicle, other than physically matching up the cut marks.
