Re: Necessary Ext. for limited(?) base
If you are going to perform the STE, then it must be done exactly that way. It even states so in the text.

If you can't follow that exactly because of certain limiting factors(chems, money, location, smell, etc.) then simply choose another extraction and cleaning method.

Those pills are Dry Matrix Formulation(DMF) so don't get any H20 near them. Not a drop, or else it'll become a big gooey mess, as the DMF becomes engaged and the pill mass begins to quickly expand and gelatonize. If it gets to that point, might as well chuck it all in the circular file! Simply use dry solvents, dry them with pre-baked epsom salts.

With that list of inactives SWIL would personally suggest starting with the E-gull, if TCE is an easily obtainable chemical for SWIU. Then clean out the tripilodine with some dry acetone and non-polar soaks or boils. Use the dry alky of choice for extraction, then filter and let evaporate at LOW heat. Do not use high heat evaporation once the pseudo has come into contact with the tetrachloroethylene, otherwise SWIU risk the chance of SEVERELY lowering yield. Use IPA for at least one dual-solvent recrystalization.

With proper technique, the pseudo crystals will be pristine.

Dry the pill mass out thoroughly, powder it as much as possible, then let it dry some more in a dessicator of some type (a container within a larger container that has a few inches of dried epsom salt, plain salt and damp rid is fine. So if you want to use heat from a heater or other heat source (no open flame of course) use in moderation without fear of loss due to heat. It will not happen.

No matter which course you take, finishing with Worlock's dual solvent recrystallization using 100% isopropyl alcohol and 100% acetone will result in a product that is cleaner than it was by a factor of at least 3. Make sure its clean and finely filtered hot (under vacuum preferably) before you proceed with any dual solvent technique or the crystal formation will be inhibited and yield will be low. Even if your yield really sucks, its ok, its all still there. You just dry it out and clean it up some more with xylene or something.
