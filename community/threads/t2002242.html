Need help making a shocking alarm
Over the past two years of trying to get up at 4:30 in the morning (for most of this year, I've had to delay my wake-up to 5:00), I've gotten used to my alarms. It's gotten to the point where I now don't even wake up when my mom pushes me back and forth and tells me to wake up in the most annoying voice.

Here's my idea.

Disembowel an alarm clock. Find the part of the clock that leads to the speakers when it's time for you to wake up. Hook this up to an arm bracer (or something) that you will wear when you sleep. This bracer will give me a mild electric shock when it's time for me to wake up.

Now, I need your help.

I've had minimal electrical experience. I'm gonna need to know how to modify the output from the clock so it'll give me a mild shock, like a trick shock pen.

Help?

Re: Need help making a shocking alarm
Ha. This is pretty funny.

Well, the speaker to the alarm clock doesn't have much voltage or amperage (which is what I believe you want) output.

Connect the wires to some aluminum foil, and test it out.

Put the positive wire on your skin, and the negative end about 1 inch (2 cm) away from the positive one.

See what happens. The energy from the speaker wires shouldn't be more than 12V.

By the way, you don't want more energy than the stuff comin out from the speaker wires. 120V from the outlet isn't goin to feel so good goin into your arm.
