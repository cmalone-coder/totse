Uncomfortably Numb
Apart from the (VERY) occasional all-nighter, I've quit doing any type of drugs on a regular basis.

I guess in the past six month I've really slowed down, just because I really don't WANT to get fucked up. Anyway, I quit smoking pot about 2 months ago.

Anyway, what I'm concerned about is the complete and utter lack of emotion. It's like I'm too in control. I'm not moved, if you will, by love or betrayal. I don't get emotionally involved with people I meet and even my old friends I'm finding are more disposable in my eyes.

I just don't give a fuck about anyone. No one can really hurt me because it's almost like I don't care. And I HATE it. I want to love even if that means I might get hurt. But I just can't. This has been going on for about 4 years now. I figured it was the drugs and once I quit, my normal emotions would come back, but no.

I'm very apathetic towards people.

Has anyone else been through this or feel like this?
