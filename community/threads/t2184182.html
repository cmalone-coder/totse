Drinking pure everclear straight will damage the lining of your throat and mouth permanently as I recall. Different people will of course read differently into manly, however:
1. Scotch, Bourbon or Irish Whiskey.
2. Old Fashioned, Manhattan or Martini (GIN!) Cocktail. All are classics -- the old fashioned IS the first cocktail -- from a manlier era, they're refined, they consist mostly of alcohol, and they have large followings for being delicious. Use a good liquor to make them though in the case of the Manhattan or Old Fashioned it need not be top-shelf (it should be good, of course, but save your best stuff for sipping). Here's the clincher though: you sip a cocktail. You do not down it. You sip it. Savour it. Enjoy the flavour. Now that is manly. Anyone can snap back some firewater, it takes a man to enjoy the stuff. If you must snap something (understandable, it is a tradition of sorts in many places), have some vodka or ouzo or German schnapps(less sweet, stronger than US schnapps) or something meant to be taken in that form.
