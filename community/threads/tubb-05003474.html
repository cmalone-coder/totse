When I'm visiting my folks and, say, wish to partake in something that is usually accessorized with foil, before heading out to see what friends what were to chicken to leave LA [and move to the Bay Area as the rest of us did over time], I need to be careful due to the sharp chemical odor of the substance as it burns [Yes, even at my age I need to hide things from my parents; they're less than 20 years older than me; and as you get older you'll find that the age gap doesn't seem so far]

With a foil, if you are careful enough, you need not worry about any smoke coming from it as you would a pipe.

As for blowing out the smoke, I take 3 or 4 sheets of tp, fold it over itself in 2" folds.

What you want is something that you can blow through - but only with a medium amount of effort [trial and error before hand really helps, I might add].

Then you just blow the smoke through that forcefully. If you see some smoke pass through, make another fold.

It works. Believe me, my folks are practical bloodhounds when it comes to these things and I've never had a problem.

I've also used this method when smoking at work [in the restroom] and at public restrooms at various places [don't do that no more: almost got real busted real bad for other stupid sh*t]
