Best Soundtrack To A Game/Movie/Anime/Series?
Just been accquiring the Final Fantasy 8 soundtrack and it's bringing back some good memories.

It's also made me realise how much music actually contributes to a game/film or whatever. When it's playing in the background, I get the feeling that many people don't pay attention or realise it there, but it's presence can actually mean the difference between mediocrity and an epic success. It's a shame more people don't really appreciate the music behind the visuals.
