...which... won't smoke, at least not in the normal sense. Masking the amine on destructive distillation will result in an immediate loss of potency; I'd expect about 1/10th, from the dimethylamine analog.

Worse still is if they know this, and try to overcompensate... in which case they probably tried to encapsulate a shitload of HCl. In which case, they followed poor lab procedure and just evaporated an HCl solution to dryness. Do you trust your cook to use 99.999% analytical grade for food & drug applications?
