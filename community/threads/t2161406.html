Re: Hash from whole plants
Just because it's not growing (in size) anymore, doesn't mean the buds aren't growing (in potency). They're immature, and picking an immature plant will not only produce shit buds, but the plant itself won't have as much potential resins on it either.

Wait until the white hairs turn red, orange or brown, and the false seed pods (unless they turned out to be herms) swell with resins. When most of the pistils have turned color, they're ready to harvest.

And yes, when the plant is harvested, not only can you get good, mature buds, but you'll also be able to make any number of hashes or extractions from the left over plant material.

Hash oil, iso hash, kief extractions, cannabudder..lots of possibilities.
