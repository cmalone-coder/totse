Re: Why the hell do I miss these people?!
If you want my opinion..........then.........

It seems to me that you are going through a couple of different changes, or phases in your life.

1. As much as we want too, we cannot live in high school, or college forever. One of the most difficult changes in life is the separation of friends, those that have been together for most of their lives. All of a sudden it seems as if everyone sort of disappears. I live outside of a town of a little over 50,000 in population, and there are years that go by where I don't see the people that I thought that I would be close friends with forever. I haven't seen my best friend from high school in 5-6 years. We didn't have a falling out, it's just that life has taken us in different directions. I'm in the phone book, but I never get a call.
(Also... It sometimes seems that when we finally grow up, we suddenly realize what assholes our former friends really were.)

2. Nostalgia for the good ol' carefree days can lead to depression. The demands of today's hectic pace, and the stresses of just trying to survive makes us sometimes look back, and wish............ That is not good. It is good to remember the people, and the good times, but to actually try and revert back to such a fond period, or dwell on it obsessively for long periods, can have seriously bad effects on your mental state.
Like time, Life must move forward. New friends can be found. New, good times can be created. Don't forget that life is full of peaks and valleys. There will be good times as well as bad times. Sometimes we unwittingly create those valleys for ourselves, and can spend years dwelling there. Seek out new peaks (good times, friends etc....).

3. Counseling can help. Just having someone to talk openly with can be a valuable asset. A good listener can take the place of a best friend until you find another one.

4. Don't lose control of your life. A job, boyfriend, bills, children ect.. can all lead you into a feeling of being trapped. Make time for yourself. Find things that you wish to do that will create those new memories and friends.

5. Study, read, and educate yourself, and then don't be afraid to discuss what you've learned with other people. Sometimes it can lead to rewarding experiences and the making of new unexpected friends. (I talk to complete strangers all the time about all kinds of things. It freaks them out sometimes , but it also , in some cases leads to unexpected friendships.

Remember, above all.......... It is YOUR life. You only get to live it once. You can honor and give thanks for the friendships and experiences of the past, but you can't go back and live them over. Move forward with a smile and confidence that YOU can control your life and....... Dammit! If those other people liked you then some new friends will too.

Right? :D

Good luck.
