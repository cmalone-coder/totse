Re: Getting wisdom teeth removed, what meds?
First, OxyContin is not indicated for acute pain. It is only prescribed for someone that requires around-the-clock opioid analgesia for an extended period of time -- i.e. chronic pain. You most definitely will not get an OxyContin prescription for wisdom teeth removal.

Second, asking for opioid painkillers by name screams 'drug-seeking behavior' and will most likely result in getting a prescription for 600mg or 800mg ibuprofen tablets.

The best you can really hope for is Vicodin ES (7.5mg hydrocodone / 750mg APAP), or possibly Percocet (5mg oxycodone / 325mg APAP).

Your best strategy IMO would be, if the oral surgeon asks about your pain tolerance, to say that a few years ago, you broke your wrist or something and was given a prescription for codeine by the ER doctor, and that while it worked pretty well for the pain, it made you very itchy. You'll probably get a script for Vicodin by doing this.

BTW, I am a chronic pain patient and have learned how to 'jump through the hoops' when it comes to being prescribed opioid painkillers. I've worked my way through all of the obstacles and currently have a steady prescription for high dosages of MS-Contin (extended-release morphine sulfate -- OxyContin but with morphine instead of oxycodone) for around-the-clock pain control and Roxicodone (immediate-release oxycodone) for breakthrough pain. I know what I am talking about, and I firmly believe that what I stated above is your best bet to get what you want.
