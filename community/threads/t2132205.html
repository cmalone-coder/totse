I've had some minor hallucinations on weed. If you really want to experience the psychedelic power of weed, let your tolerance lower by not smoking for a couple months.

Then buy some disgustingly dank bud and blaze until you can't blaze anymore. Maybe make some brownies with it too and then smoke a bunch if you really wanna go over the top with it.

You will hallucinate, no doubt about it. Minor OEVs, definitely some tunnel vision. The real treat though will be the CEVs. They should be quite detailed and expansive, especially if you have a good imagination.

THC is definitely a hallucinogen, albeit a weak one. If you're really looking to see things, I suggest finding some acid or shrooms.
