Re: coming down off coke.
Wait, so you do a CWE on coke? How do you dry it? How long does it take? Do you really notice a big difference?

No fiending??

Thanks

It takes very little time, due to coke's amazing solubility... 2.5g/ml of water. That means that when you buy a 'gram' from your dealer, you will be able to dissolve all the actual coke in there with less than 0.5mls of water. I use a little more just to make sure I get it all, maybe about 2ml.

What I do is dump out my baggie into a shotglass, put 2ml of warm water into the baggie and swirl it around just to get all the residue, dump that water into the shotglass and stir for a minute. I stick a Swan rollie filter (smaller than a normal cig filter) onto a syringe needle, put it into the shotglass and draw up all the water. Empty out the syringe onto a glass plate/mirror and let it evaporate slowly. Don't try rushing it by heating, like I did the first time I tried this (and ended up with a black mass of crap). Once the water evaporates, you can scrape up the coke with a razor.

It really is much better this way - if you look at the stuff left behind when you draw up the coke solution, that's what fucks up your nose the next day, and you don't have any of that. I've never experienced any fiending since doing this. There's only one drawback (if you can call it that): since you're cleaning so much crap out of your coke, you end up with a lot less, but higher quality, powder (for me 1g normally ends up as 0.4-0.5g).
