ive come to learn a few valuable things about shrooms/psychedelics. and not even from my own use, but from people close to me. if you believe you may have issues of confidence and tend to be an anxious, jealous and/or mistrusting person, psychs are definitely not for you. see, sometimes my self i tend to over-think things and over analyze things. i tend to focus too much on peoples body language, what they might be thinking about me, worried to speak because i might sound foolish, whatever. and the eye contact thing comes in here too. awkward things might arise, you and a friend are tripping pretty hard, and you might look at each other at the same exact moment. ive had this happen and it can be sort of unsettling. maybe its just me, but this reply is starting to drag out. anyways, my friends that did these psychs before and have had problems make things awkward in everyday life. they find it hard to talk to people, look them in the eye, be trusting, etc. they have since quit and they have all told me that their lives have been "easier to handle." so maybe you should chill on the drugs and make sure your brain fully develops the ability to interact on a normal level with other human beings. and for my advice, mind over matter son. no one should make you feel displaced or uncomfortable in the first place. no one is superior to you in any way or inferior. they are just other people. you are the only person that has the power to change your way of thinking and having a positive outlook will make your life better. shrooms can sort of cloud your perception for a time but you arent facing anything permanent. alright. peace.
