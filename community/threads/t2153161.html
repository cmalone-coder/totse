Ok Here are the reasons why I prefer to smoke a spliff rather than pure joint

1) Coughing. Of course this is personal at best but I find tobacco smoke to be much more mellow when it comes to coughing. Yes it does taste harsher but it hardly ever makes me cough while a good hit of green makes me cough so hard I almost vomit. Once again, personal, maybe you guys are all fine with this but I find it a much more mellow hit.

2) Temperature. Tobacco burns at a lower temperature so less THC gets destroyed due to heat. In reality you are conserving your weed by burning spliffs. And of course it makes it burn more evenly and at a more constant rate resulting in smoother and more predictable hits.

3) Texture. No matter how good of a grinder or roller you are marijuana is not a very smooth substance to roll. because it is composed of buds rather than a homogenized leaf it is harder to roll an even joint or blunt with pure marijuana. If you prefer a nice and neat "cone" you are better off making it nicer by adding tobacco.

4) Weed quality. If you get some really nice shit and want to smoke a joint by yourself than you have to chose to either roll a skinny and barely passing joint or get completely blazed. If you want to do the latter then god bless you, but if you prefer to keep your level of "high" under control and one full joint of the stuff you have is too much you can measure out your dosage and roll a joint with exactly the right amount without worrying about the size.

Then again this is coming from a perspective of an ex-smoker. If you really hate tobacco and want nothing to do with it of course don't roll spliffs. But if you don't mind tobacco by itself you should see that there are some valid reasons people smoke spliffs. Oh and before I go, I know some people will bitch about #3 saying "you cant roll" etc. Well just do yourself a favor and try to roll a spliff once and you will see how much easier and smoother it is. No matter how good of a roller you are it would be childish not to admit it.
