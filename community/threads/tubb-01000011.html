File Submissions for Totse.com
Here at TOTSE.com we get a lot of submissions, and since we now have the system working for adding submissions to the website, I thought I would write a short summary of what we look at when we add new submission to the website.

First of all, we want to hear from you. We want your article, we want to put it out there for people to see. But we do have a few guidelines for submission.

1. Please learn how to spell. This is the most common problem with submissions to TOTSE.com. We can't read them, and neither can anyone else. Use the spell check of your favorite text editor(I recommend Crypt Edit). If you think that "ur" is a word, we will not be able to put up your article. With all the submissions that come in, we simply do not have the time or the patience to edit your article to be readable. It will go right into the void. Which is a shame, because you might have something really great to contribute.

2. Check and make sure that you are not submitting something already on the website. We already have enough duplicate stuff, and we dont need to spend more time sifting out the dups. Take the title, or author, or some phrase specific to the article, and do a search on TOTSE for it.

3. If you have an idea, try to elaborate on it, not just "Dude, found a great way to do X. L8er". Submissions of less than a page should be posted in the appropriate FORUMS of TOTSE.com. Just click on "community" and select the right forum. Plus, then everyone gets to see it right away, no waiting.

4. Complicated ASCII diagrams never come out right, and files that rely on them to convey their meaning will not be added to the website. SMALL ascii diagrams can be ok, but it's a very fine line. Instead, try to elaborate on the process, clearly explaining each step, or area, or whatever. This will be much more helpful.

5. Do NOT send personal messages to either of the Sysops in the submission section.
