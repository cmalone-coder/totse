Prices are rising everywhere because the price of gas is rising, so its becoming more and more expensive to transport the products to the stores to buy them. The price of transport is passed on to us.

The rise in gas/oil prices has very little to do with the war. (thats not to say it has nothing to do with the war) The more oil is produced, the lower the prices. And vice versa. Economies grow. And their need for more oil grows as well. Unfortunately the production of oil has remained more or less the same (give or take a few million barrels) since the early 2000's, while the demand from growing economies kept rising (US/Europe/india/china/brazil/ and other developed/developing economies)

This has produced a higher demand for oil, while the production has not increased.

Couple that with the on goings in the middle east(war, rhetoric from Iran saying they will cut oil exports), venuzuela (the same thing), and nigeria(open war/civil war in their oil producing delta)

Couple that with other oil powers who like the high price of oil (and why wouldnt they, more money for them!) Algeria said it will cut oil, this after bush talked saudi arabia into increasing oil production in exchange for sale of more advanced weaponry. (yes say what you want about him, but there is a few things he knows how to do without looking like an idiot)

Lets not forget how our refineries are mostly in the gulf coast, vulnerable to disruption of operations from hurricanes.

Lets not forget how we have billions of barrels of oil in alaska, but the democrats wont let us drill there (no this is not a dems bashing, republicans piss me off as well)

All that is not to say how our whole problems stem from oil.

The mortgage crisis, the losing confidence in the american dollar overseas (to the point where many oil exporting countries want to switch to the euro for their exporting needs)

The sheer amount of money we are spending on the war, and other defense/homeland security related things, just make people question whether we can afford all this. Speculations range everythin from we are just printing more and more money (leading to inflation) to borrowing MASSIVE amounts of money and dumping the responsibility of that on the future generations.

Plus congress is currently debating a $700 BILLION bailout (yes, 700 BILLION, just to be given to corporations to prevent them from going under) which is a double edged sword, If we dont bail them out, they go under, massive unemployment, our economy collapses (or very close to it) and depression. If we DO bail them out, we barrow 700 BIllion from foreign governments, when our debt is already over 11 trillion, meaning by the time we repay (IF we repay) it will amount to over 1 trillion on this particular loan.

Theres just so much more to it that it cant be explained by any one person. There is so much more that it cant be explained by a large group of the most smartest economists in the nation!

One thing is certain, if we had more oil, even just a little more oil produced here, things would just be that much easier.
