Although it is very very difficult to do,
try to see the big picture and have faith
in the human experience. If you feel that you are losing someone whether or not they are next door or in a nother country, the thing to do is voice your fears, on behalf of your heart and your sanity and your future
do the best you can to express what you are feeling to the other person, listen as you give them opportunity to explain why you are wrong or why you are right and hear what they say or don't and take it for what it is worth! Adding distance to a relation ship that is already unstable is in the eyes of anyone who has been through anything similar
very much an indication that this relationship is not going top fare so well in the immmediate future, however it soes not mean that you need to destroy your feeling for the person who you are losing, and is also far away. You can let them know how you feel, you can gracefully accept your
deletion from their life and you can treasure the love that you had while you had it. Maybe someday you'll find each other again, maybe not , point is that if your fears are unfounded, the communication will take care of itself. Your interest in the other person and theirs in you will do nicely to assure the ongoing exchange of correspondesnce and such. If this mutual interest is not present do youself the favor of accepting that fact, and carrying on better for having been in the relationship at all. When people get the chance or make the time to "love one another'
all good intentions contained therein are 14k
and ought to be revered as such , just because time reveals that it is impossible to subsist on each other alone or even exist together w/o great strife, doesn't mean that the "love and happy times that you fill your thoughts with when avoiding letting go of something that needs to be let go of have to be shredded or burned at the stake, just be glad for having had the experience of loving very much and carry on kind and candid until the opportunity presents itself again.
I know how much it hurts to think that the one you love doesn't feel the same way, but hurt as it does , if that is the case don't hurt yourself w/ deception or denial. Take car of yourself and be glad that gravity hasn't yet come a calling!
