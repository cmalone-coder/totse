Shit. That is terrible. It's probably a really good thing that you got her to tell you, so she at least got it of her chest to someone.

there really isn't much you can say to make it better. It's just one of those situations where she is going to need someone to lean on. Go and be with her. Give her hugs. You have two ways of going about it. You can pry, which could ensure that she tells the police and tells them all that she can remember and will hopefully get a rapist of the street. This may really upset her, however, seeing as how she was reluctant to tell you in the first place. She may feel more comfortable to talk to her mother about it. You could suggest that she do so. However, if she seems in hysterics, you may want to wait until she calms down to talk about it.

Or, you can just be with her. Hopefully if she is open, she will talk to you about it on her own. Again, she is really going to need someone to be with her right now, so go there. You dont need to say anything. If you are going to do so, dont just be with her and seem removed, you have to pay attention to her even if she is just crying. If you have ever been in a situation where you have lost a family member etc., you will feel infinatly better just knowing there is someone by your side.

Im really sorry you have to go through this, and i am sorry your girlfriend had to go through it also.
