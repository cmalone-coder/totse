I can understand where you're girlfriend is coming from in a respect. My last girlfriend dumped me for drinking a lot. I never got retarded drunk while I was dating her, and rarely even passed nothing more than a heavy buzz. Its a frustrating thing to be an alcoholic with a partner who doesn't understand it completely. This leads me to my serious point.

Is it possible she truely is an alcoholic, but is afraid to admit to it? I know myself, personally, before I admitted I was an alcoholic used to pretend I got drunk off of like 6 beers, when in reality I was sneaking around twice that amount of beers outside or in the bathroom where no one saw me.

Its a scary thing when the thought of being an alcoholic creeps into your mind for the first time. Unfortunately for a lot of us though, when you self realize this, its usually bordering being too late to turn back.

I just say try talking to her and telling her you understand. You understand but are concerned for her safety. Don't say you are embarassed by her because my last girlfriend said that to me and it sent me into a worse spiral downward. Tell her you care for her and want her to know you think about her and her safety, and thats it.

Belief me, letting an alcoholic know you care about them, makes a world of difference. So many of my friends thought I just needed to be left alone in my times after said ex, or needed to be yelled at to starighten up, ended up just driving me deeper into the bottle.

We alcoholics seem impartial, relaxed, and/or fine, but all we need in the end is someone to say, "Have a couple beers, and I'll drink a couple with you."

I don't know if this gave you any help what-so-ever, I just know I was in a similar situation and yelling and judging doesn't help things.
