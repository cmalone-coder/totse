Re: Broken Heart
I think there's no way you can really put a time limit on these things.

For me personally, it just hits. One morning you wake up and you're fine and ready to completely move on. I can't say there are really 'stages' to getting better, so to speak.

Focus on you, if that helps. Remember you deserve to be happy as well. You broke up and it sucks but it's done and most likely nothing's going to change that. Immerse yourself in what you love; your family, friends, hobbies, work, whatever. Spend as much time as you can making yourself feel good.

Drugs/alcohol can help pass the time, sure. But don't rely on those things only to try and get you out of this slump because in the end you'll probably feel worse for wasting your time.

There's probably nothing anyone can say that's going to make you feel better, really. Just remember that it'll pass soon enough and when it does, honestly, you'll probably look back and think 'what the hell was the big deal?'.

Just get on with life. Almost [almost] no one in this world is worth wasting your life over being sad and depressed.
