We should all have a basic understanding of matter before we begin:

At the lowest level, all matter is basically the same. Get past the atom, and everything is quirks and qwarks (or whatever they are called). Atoms, molecular structures, substances big enough for us to see, living creatures. It gives you an idea of how big the universe really is, from an atomic perspective.

All your cells are most like unconscience of you. Most likly, if they have any conscience, it is a 1st or 2nd dimensional one. They just do what they do to survive. God (you) created them, you bring them nutrition, you bring them disease. To your cells, you are God.

Prehaps the next step up, is somthing we dont understand because we cant. We cant see it because we were never ment to, we never needed to. But maybe our subconscience is all part of a larger collective conscience, and our own personal beliefs and experiances develop human ethics and morals.

I suppose if that is true, when using meditation and enthenogenic substances, you could potentially raise your conscience to this level, and experiance a connection with all humanity. If everyone could do that, i belive we, for the most part, would begin to stop recognizing this level of conscience. Or maybe we would just like the other one better.
