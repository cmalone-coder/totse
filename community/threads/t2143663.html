Re: handcannon
something hard like steel

and i suggest putting the barrel in another, larger tube, also open on one end. that way, in the event of it blowing up, more of the force would be forced out one end (which you will always have pointed in a safe direction)

do some calculations with the black powder reaction and find the maximum amount of gas produced. use the ideal gas formula to work out the estimate for the highest amount of pressure possible (that is, assume all the powder burns in an instant and the volume is the chamber size). of course not all the powder burns in an instant and the volume gets larger with time, so you will have some leeway.

from there, you can estimate whether your cannon can resist such pressure

edit: also try making smoothbores first before going to a rifled barrel.

Re: handcannon
It all depends on what you want the cannon to do. PVC pipe will suffice if you only use a small amount of powder. PVC is used in combustion cannons which are similar to the thing you are designing.

Using steel I suggest you buy a pipe. Casting is difficult, but achievable by most people.

Cast iron is too brittle to use to build a cannon.

Bronze is possible, but it is expensive.

Re: handcannon
Buy some threaded black gas pipe from a hardware store. They sell it for making pipe clamps. Buy a steel cap from the plumbing section to close the end. Drill the touch hole through the side of the cap where it overlaps with the pipe. This will keep it as strong as possible.

I haven't done any of this myself, so don't blame me if you die. You could easily buy a cheap muzzleloader instead and modify it to look the way you want.
