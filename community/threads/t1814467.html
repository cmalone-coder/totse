On Jan. 31, CNN held a panel to discuss whether atheists are unfairly discriminated against and how we ought to feel about that. You can watch the segment on YouTube.

Atheist-bashing with Paula Zahn on CNN - Part 1

Atheist-bashing with Paula Zahn on CNN - Part 2

You can read parts of the transcript here on About.com, which emphasizes some of the parts that have some atheists riled up: http: //atheism. about.com/ b/a/258728 .htm

2 of the panelists completely bash atheism. One of the two, a Jewish woman, argues that "we are a Christian nation."

Also, a collection of links and reactions here: http: //atheism. about.com/ b/a/258735 .htm

They cite a study that found, among other things, that "Atheists are the least trusted minority group in the United States."

A very interesting show. I would love to see more of this on CNN. Strange, though, how they held a panel on atheism without including an atheist on the panel.
