Re: Have you ever fallen for a best friend?
If you value your friendship, don't pursue her. I don't think I've ever seen a case where the relationship actually works out, my own included.

In the long run, you two being best friends doesn't really make a difference in the success or failure of the relationship. Success is dependent on how much time you two are willing to put into the relationship. The real issue at hand here is that, unlike other relationships, you have a lot to lose if you screw this up. Once you move past being just friends, it is extremely difficult, if not impossible, to ever go back. Oh sure, you might say things like, "Let's go back to the way we were before," or some such. But let's face it, no matter how much you trick yourself into believing that's the case, you're both going to feel that nothing's quite the same anymore.

As best friends, I'm sure you two have that special something where you lean on each other for support or confide in each other your personal feelings or whatever the hell it is best friends do. You have to ask yourself if you're willing to give that up.
