Small Successes- Post Yours
As anyone who is working on improving themselves will discover, motivation is an integral and challenging part of achieving your goals. Success doesn't happen over night, but nothing feels better or gives you an extra boost of motivation than noticing small changes in your self, and seeing the steps that lead to your hard work coming to fruition.

I've been working on eating healthy and limiting empty calories, making smarter nutritional choices, and working out to lose fat and gain muscle. I was ecstatic today when I measured my waist and noticed that I've lost an inch of fat (I'm at 26" now) in just under two weeks. It's a nice feeling to know that you're not struggling for nothing, and it makes me want to keep working at it to get in the shape I want to be in. It's obviously a very small step in the right direction, but those little steps show that there is always progress to be made with a little effort.

I'd like to hear all the improvements you RY folks have noticed in yourself and what you're doing to achieve them, and what your goals are.
