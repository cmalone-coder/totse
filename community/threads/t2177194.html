Trip Report: 2C-E
So last night I got my 2C-E, I weighed out 17mg on my milligram scale and put it in a gelcap, then swallowed it. Stomach contents: A turkey sandwich

Following times are rough estimates

T+0:45 Begining of onset, I was starting to feel the drug, but no visuals or anything that could be described as "tripping" things started to feel different though.

T+1:15 End of onset, definitely "coming up" at this point

T+2:00-7:00 Peak/Plateau intense open eye visuals and even more intense closed eye visuals, my mind was running wild outside my head. Some synthnesia. Auditory distortions, music is amazing and affects the visuals. Some body load. Words cannot really describe the experiance and I was tripping too hard to really make sense of everything I was experiancing. Amazingly intense experiance, for referance to more known psychs definitely much stronger than 3.4 grams of good mushrooms or two hits of weaker acid or one hit of amazing acid.

T+7:00-10:00 Coming down, everything slowly tappering off until t+10:00 where I am not "tripping" anymore but still experiancing after effects. Can't slee


My opinions, this drug is very intense(at least at this dosaged) and an amazing experiance. It can also be, as Shulgin himself said, "difficult" I almost freaked out a few times and I normally don't come anywhere close to freaking out, I was able to keep things going in a positive direction though. Definitely a drug to try if you can get it, just don't make any important plans for the next 12 or so hours then figure some time for sleep.

Oh yeah man, that shit's nuts. I know what you mean about freaking out, but it was during the peak and I also took 30mg. You puke at all? I was emptying my stomach and sinuses for 20 minutes straight and got random stomach contractions, sometimes resulting in more puking, for another 20 minutes after that. Once I had nothing left to puke, WOW!! The peak was too intense though, gradually tapering off into something I could actually just begin to enjoy. It's funny because at first, during the peak, I was just enduring the mayhem and told myself I would just go to sleep and not worry about all the wacked out insanity taking place inside my mind and all around me. I couldn't fall asleep so I just layed outside in complete submission to this drug until I felt less nauseous and more in control, which took about an hour since first feeling the effects.

2c-E is amazing after the peak. Once you settle down and accept your new world where EVERYTHING is alien and unfamiliar it becomes interesting and enjoyable. This is a drug that makes things I look for disappear as I approach them, and reappear when I do a double-take. It's just complete confusion and reality shattering nonsense with intense body euphoria and crazy visuals. I hear the visuals aren't as good as 2c-I though.
