Re: 1st Time Cid
If its been like 50 minutes should I feel it by now?

You should almost certainly have felt something, even if this trip's onset is slowed due to your body chemistry; you usually get the alert a fair while before the comeup, and you listed the long end of the time frame for "starting to take off."

OTOH, if it's really damned clean cid - like you can't get, nor anyone else here - there's always the risk of a first timer not knowing they're tripping balls until something melts into a puddle. It can happen. Hell, I've had times like that with experience under my belt and just good-street-quality, instead of clean.
