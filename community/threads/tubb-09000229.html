I have found that if you get in the habit of asking yourself quite regularly throughout your waking day "Am I Dreaming?" and then if the answer is "No" proving it with either of the means listed later, you will be in that habit when you are dreaming too, and will more easily determine you are dreaming in time to snap to lucidity.

Dream-checks: Read something twice to see if it reads the same both times (many times it won't in a dream for some odd reason, or you won't be able to read at all)
Look into a mirror for discrepencies in what should be reflected and what is actually reflected. (many times in dreams, reflections will be incomplete or inappropriate)

These are two means by which I verify mentally the awake state by habit, and thus can almost instantly identify the dream state by the same habit.

Once I determine I AM dreaming, I "find my hands", that is to say, purposely focus on my hands specifically, in order to gain control of the dream itself. From there it is simply a matter of maintaining control and staying in the dream. The "top" technique suggested earlier works well for keeping you in the dream when you fear you are about to lose it.

dreaming is just about the only thing in my life i can control.

here's a easy exercise for teaching yourself to lucid dream. forego sleep for one night, trying to stay awake for 24hours (or twice as long as your mind's accustomed to snoozing). if you regularly pull all-nighters, try to remain conscious for 48 hours.

next, turn on the boob tube to a channel with a lot of similar programming -- courtTV, national geographic, and A&E (when they're having lotsa American Justice and City Confidentials episodes) are all good choices. make sure you have the volume loud enough for it to make sleeping difficult.

now wait to pass out.

once you're zonked and past the first couple of REM cycles, you'll start to hone in on what the television's playing. if you can remember that you're trying the tv experiment in your sleep, you'll slowly start to pick up on dialogues and realize you're basically in TVLand.

i've spent many an afternoon on a shark boat or sitting in Oprah's audience. naturally, the dream process accounts for Ms. Winfrey's missing teeth or purple tentacles.
