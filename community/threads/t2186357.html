IF you could rename the numbers 1-10, what'd you change them to?
And why?

Let's just say us Totseans got ourselves an island and wanted to express our sovereignty with a number system ...

Personally I think the English numbers sound shit when you say one to ten - there's no pattern/rhyme or anything. Numbers are so fucking important in a language so I think they should at least sound beautiful when you say them, like a poem or something to show them respect for how much they've advanced our civilisation.

I'd personally go with:

1. Ace (like in cards. I think it sounds 100x more top-dog like than 'one', which sounds rather dull and zzzzzzzz tbh. A numerical system needs to start off with a bang to get language learners wanting to learn on, and I don't feel that 'One' is exciting enough.)
2. Deus (2 = Deuce in cardgames, but Deus means god in Latin. God has been a very influential guy in the last few thousand years so I think he deserves a place in the number system. It would be one, but it might get a little confusing for the French and Spanish and some other irrelevant countries.)
3. Trey (from cards again. Poker is VERY important, more than people think. It's been the number one driving force of the world economy for the last 1500 years so i think it should it's just right to have the first 3 numbers dedicated to it)
4. Qat (pronounced cat. cats are coolcats so they should be in the numerical system, and them crafty Arabs shouldn't be the only people to have a Q not proceeded by a U. The number 444,444,444 will officially be 'Longqat'.)
5. Song (everyone likes songs, and five sounds dull as shit.)
6. Fay (rhymes with trey, and fay is quite a nice name, reminds me of spring)
7. Slevin (lucky number slevin.)
8. Tots (paying homage to the temple)
9. Nene (fuck knows.))
10. Xen/Jhen/Gen/Jin/Gin (Xen's the final world in Half Life, so in order to commemerorate my 10th anniversary since I completed the game it gets the honour of having a number!

Now say it aloud!
Ace, Deus, Trey // Kat, Song, Fay // Slevin, Tots, Neen, Jhen! Doesn't that sound far better?
