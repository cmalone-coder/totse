There is some good advice here already, but I would like to add that if you do choose to bring it up directly, you need to pick the right time. Chances are there are times she would react positively and times she would reject the idea. The problem is that once she formally rejects the idea, it is not simple to reverse that. Make sure she is happy, in the mood, and wanting more when you bring it up. Also, I would be a bit vague and add some extra value to it when you do bring it up. Instead of saying, I want to have sex, say, I think we are ready to take things to the next level, or something like that. See how she reacts to that, and then you can determine what you meant by it.
