Right, drug-aided mind expansion is "bullshit". This is why people are going to have issues with your argument. Not all people use drugs just to fill "holes in their lives". Maybe near-everyone ends up doing that sometimes, hedonism is a pretty natural human inclination, but if you say that's the only point, people are going to argue with you, because you are wrong. You can't take what you or your friends find interesting about drugs, and relate it to all drug users, it's like generalizing the whole of humanity. If you attempt it as bunch of people are going to call you an idiot, because simplifications of this nature often tend to fail. Some people are really into the whole broadening of horizons that accompanies experimentation with powerful psychedelics. Sure, sometimes these things exhibit themselves as somewhat of a roller coaster ride, and are thus somewhat entertaining (in that you're not bored) but most people are relieved when a long trip has ended. Psychedelics generally aren't that much fun, at least once you dive into the deep end of the pool. The actual wasting of objective time has like no fucking significance on many drugs. Smoked DMT only lasts a few minutes, but it feels like it lasts way longer than that. Is a person annoyed when they look at the clock afterwards and realize they only "wasted" 15 minutes?! The point of drugs is and will always be the subjective experience. Some drugs make the passage of time more innately fun, but that is a quality of a drug, and something that is only aided by a long duration.

Also, there's the issue of how experiences are more meaningful if they're not experienced all the time. Drugs tend to get boring when you're high on them for long periods of time. By the end of an LSD trip I'm genuinely fucking sick of tripping for at least a few weeks. Not everyone uses drugs as an escape from reality. But if you are trying to escape, generally the more "permanent" escape is preferable, for those types of people. However, a fuck of a lot of people don't use drugs for that reason. People get angry when you simplify their motives to something that is really only true in your view. It shows intellectual laziness and an inability to grasp potential possibilities.
