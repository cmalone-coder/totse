gNewSense is one of the best distros in terms of freedom and usability. It isn't perfect for all hardware though; if you have hardware that requires non-free firmware or drivers, you should (buy better hardware and then not) consider using Ubuntu.

Others in this forum will say Fedora, which is also good (but less free than gNewSense). Don't listen to those who say Slackware or Gentoo; those are harder to use than the others, and if you want to go with those you should be prepared for what you're doing.

If you want a BSD-based OS, check out FreeBSD. NetBSD isn't used very much, and OpenBSD people have the maturity and social skills of grade schoolers. FreeBSD is a great OS. (If you must have insane amounts of security, go OpenBSD, but that isn't necessary for most.)
