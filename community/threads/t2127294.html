Re: Sneaking drugs into a Music Festival
MOST of those festivals just say that to cover their asses.

The search is generally nothing more than looking in your trunk for 10 seconds and asking you if you have anything that will get you in trouble.

Remember - at most of these events 95% of the staff are people who are going to the festival themselves -- they're just on staff to get to go for free. They want to check in everyone as soon as possible so they can go party too.

However, if you're going to one that IS going to do more of a search, simply hide it well in one of your bags among your belongings. They're not going to go through every item of every bag of every car, that would take them forever.

Smuggling drugs into a music festival is like illegal immigrants hopping the border back to Mexico.
