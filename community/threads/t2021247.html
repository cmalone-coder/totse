ICEMAN1968
Surfing With The Alien is a very easy rock song. If you can play any Van Halen tunes, you should be able to handle it (I teach it to all of my guitar students, its a good song for people to learn how to use rock guitar techniques for playing lead parts). Some advice would be to take it one phrase at a time. Its not cheating to look at someones transcription.

Also dont stress out on getting the tone right. Satch plays everything with a wah pedal (though he usually just uses it sparingly to very GENTLY change the tone and timbre of the notes he's playing).

If you are really having trouble with it though, post a thread about it with the parts you're having trouble with and I'll see if I can help you.
