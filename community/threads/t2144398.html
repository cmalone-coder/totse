No matter what studs or ribs or whatever shit they put on the outside of the condom, the guy will never feel a difference.

Do not use warming condoms. I've used these on several occasions, and girls almost always say they burn.

Do not use "extended pleasure" condoms. They're stupid. Learn how to last without chemical bullshit that numbs your dick. Try breathing deeply and evenly, you'll last longer.

Do not use condoms with nonoxynol 9 spermicidal lubricant. These can cause UTIs for the girl, and they have been shown to be no more effective at preventing pregnancy. Thus, these are stupid to use.

I've linked to www.condomdepot.com enough times that they should really fucking pay me something. I always buy in bulk from that site. One reason is that they list measurements of all the condoms. Another is that they have a ridiculous selection, and the last big reason is that shipping is free. Plus, I don't have to look retarded buying 50 condoms at walmart or wherever. So yeah, check out that site.

I've always had good luck with durex her sensation and durex extra sensitive or extra thin, i'm too tired to get up and look what it's actually called. They both feel alright, and I've never had one break. Put it on right, and it shouldn't ever break. Additionally, you need to make sure you're buying condoms that will fit you properly, hence looking at the measurements. It's really surprising how much variance there is in size even within brands. I've heard bullshit like "zomg durex are small" or "no wai, trojan are small." It depends on which kind you buy, not which brand you buy. Standard condoms are all the same size across brands, but every brand makes smaller and larger condoms. Every guy should spend a little money and try on different condoms to see which ones fit best.

ALWAYS make sure the condoms you buy are not going to expire anytime soon. Always throw expired condoms away. Never keep condoms in your car. Never keep condoms in your wallet for extended periods of time. The heat and pressure weakens them.

I know this is excessive and beyond the scope of the topic, but whatever. People need to be reminded of this shit every so often. I've been with a decent number of girls, and I've probably used 20 types of condoms. They all do basically the same thing. Just get a girlfriend and a sampler pack and see what you like best.
