My personal opinion is that religion in general is a terrible thing. It really seems to teach people to cling to their egos, which are the cause of pain and suffering. As far as Wicca in particular, I agree with SatansPyro that it is a watered-down form of witchcraft. However, my main problem with it is that it still clings to the old Judeo-Christian ideas of "good" and "evil" as concrete things, when you get right down to it. I agree with Nick Hall's statement that Wicca is really not much more than Jesus in a dress.
