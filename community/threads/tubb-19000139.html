Spelling is very important, especially in this line of things. In fact a mispelled work can actually mean an entirely different chemical.
methane and ethane for example, one letter difference, two different chemicals.
ethylene and ethylyne again one letter differnce.
2-octanol
4-octanol
one character difference, differnt chemicals.
I'm sure there are many other words that may be used in writing procedures that if mispelled could mean something else. Any of which could kill you.
Like 270C vs 270K 270 Celsius is much much hotter than 270 Kelvins.
There is large concern for mispelling and misreading when constructing such things. If you aren't scared of explosives you need not be around them, you will hurt/kill yourself or someone else. Attention to detail is critical, lack there of equals a very bad day, harmless or harmful. I've made mistakes, none that were fatal and many, luckily, caused no physical harm, but could have.
