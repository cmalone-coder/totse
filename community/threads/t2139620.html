Re: DIY Shed/Living area.
Cheaper foundation- build the main floor frame support beams out of some heavy duty lumber [4x4s], and rest the whole thing on cinder blocks. To set the cinder blocks, dig a small hole[1' deep], fill it 8" up with gravel, then the rest with sand. Level them out approximately, then use a level and wiggle the cinderblocks into the sand to get them firm in there. Begin frame construction. For Insulation, that solid foam stuff would probably be perfect, though I don't know too much about insulation so I'll wait for someone else to answer that. Propane would probably be the best heating solution though.

Poke around for some different designs, there are a few designers pushing a small home movement right now in the name of energy efficiency and stuff, and they've made some sick little cabins with tons of pockets and good functionality the size you're talking. I'd post a link but I forget the guys name in the article I read. A good place to start is to think of what you want in there- Bed, desk, couch, dresser, tv, stove, fridge, bathroom, etc. The best design I've seen is making the "attic", or just right under the peak of the roof, the bed. It allows lots of space to sleep and you can just have a ladder to climb up. stick a couch in a corner, throw the TV across from it, and start adding drawers and storage into the side. Folding things are prime, like desks, tables, etc.

I'll hunt around for some links and stuff if you respond- I don't want to waste my time otherwise cause lots of projects in here never take off or the OP never responds again, so I'll wait and see.
