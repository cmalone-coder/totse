Re: A few questions about this sack I just bought
Just make a ghetto scale with simple stuff like cups, branches, yardsticks, string, paperclips, etc. I'm guessing there are many variations on this, pick the plan you think will work best. I seem to remember the Improvised Munitions Handbook having a set of instructions like this.

Alternatively, go to the head shop and blow a whole five bucks on one of their super-cheap scales. Nothing digital, I'm talking about the ones that just look like a weight attached to a chain.

If you're too cheap and/or lazy to even put in that much effort, find a friend who has a scale. Pack your bowl as you would normally pack it, then have your friend weigh it for you. Weigh a joint or blunt too if you want. Then use that to calculate the weight of all your bags.

These methods are not the most accurate, but it's better to have a close estimation of the weight of your sack instead of just guessing.
Other than that you'll just have to trust your dealer. I'm sure he weighs the stuff out just right every time.

Ask if you can watch him put it on the scale next time. Most dealers at least extend you that courtesy.
