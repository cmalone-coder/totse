How to get over shyness / be more social
I swear to fucking God, if I hear one more person bitch about their shyness or their antisocialness, I'm gonna scream in their thread.

Look, I'm antisocial myself. Hell if I dont talk to people enough and go out enough, I go back into my hole and remain anti-social. So here's how I'm dealing with it:

1) If I see friends at the galley (cafeteria), I try to eat with them and talk. Even if I barely know them. Granted, there are days where I just want to eat alone, so I try to do it at least once a day.

2) I'm trying to do one social activity per day. Eating with friends count, but try to do more. For example, have a friend over at your room, or go see a movie with one. I'm thinking about getting my only 2 other classmates together so we can bond and kick butt for the next 8 weeks.

Hell if your really desperate, pick up smoking. Smokers are the gossipers here, because rain, snow or sunshine, they're only allowed to smoke outside in specific areas.

Other tips: Play pool (billiards), challenge others. When you realize you got your ass handed to you, ask for advice.

Find activities that force you to interact. Hell join the military or an organization that forces you to work together.

The fact of the matter is, your not going to wake up and be the most popular guy. No girl is going to come up to you when your sitting in a corner and go "Hey! Lets fuck!" --and if she does, thats probably a red flag. Granted, some girls will socialize with you because your quiet, but more than likely, you'll only be "just friends"

So take action, and force yourself to be social.
