Electricians have the strongest union and an amazing pension.

HVAC (heating ventilation and cooling) guys make amazing money and are always in high demand. Most people don't understand how these things work, even within the trades.

Concrete guys are in huge demand and it's decent money.

Demo is for trained-monkeys, but there's good money in it as you move up the ladder. Especially if you're willing to open your own business.

Plumbing makes wicked money and almost nobody is willing to do their own plumbing anymore.

General Contracting, or Project Management, is amazing money to babysit the other trades and do oddjobs, all you need to know is how to read blueprints. (This is what I'm involved in currently.)

Steel guys are in a disgustingly high demand. They really really need them in the city. There are jobs all over the place that are being shut down because they can't get steel.

Flooring work is amazing money, but it's bad for your back. There is lots and lots of work for it. The largest company for flooring in North America is headquartered here. I know the owners and their family; it would be a cool thing to do.

Framing is good work and good money, but seasonal. Same with putting on siding.

Bricklayers are needed, but I've never known a bricklayer that wasn't a dick.

Painting is needed all over the place and there's good money to it, but you're going to be braindead if you do it.

Door and hardware installation is wicked money and good exercise.

Industrial Cleaning companies are in high demand and anybody that can push a vacuum or broom can make it in the business. They make amazing money for the level of effort. New businesses of this type need to open in the city.

Drywall is good work and there's always money in it, but it's dusty and lots of people can't handle that.

Roofing is good work, but cold and dangerous in the winter.

Stucco guys get good money and do plenty of business.

Finishing Carpentry is booming and there's always good work, but it takes a keen eye.

Welders are in huge demand and are making lots of money. Everybody with their ticket is taking on a couple apprentices.

And there are still more trades and opportunities within the city.
