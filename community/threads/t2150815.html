Yeah, I've got a couple:

#1 - Depending upon the level of traffic the particular Starbucks you wish to procure employment with gets, it may prove a much more difficult job than you could have imagined. While I was waiting in line for a Sorbetto the other day, a kid came in and asked for an application. The harried assistant manager looked around her at all the customers, ran her arm across her brow and laughed as she asked incredulously, "Are you sure about that?" Just some food for thought.

#2 - Hollywood Video/Blockbuster = pretty easy work.

#3 - You should probably learn the fine art of tailoring your resume to the job you are applying for. While this requires you to change your resume for each new job, it will increase the odds of you actually landing one, since you will be telling your potential employers precisely what they want to hear and leaving out the shit they don't.

Good luck!
