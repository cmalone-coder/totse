Personally, I find that the benefits of using condoms outweigh the bad aspects.

With or without, the sex still feels warm and soft and silky. There's a diminished sensation of stimulating friction, but I find whatever keeps me from coming quickly is a bonus.

Plus, if you (or your current partner(s)) have multiple partners or extensive sexual histories, condoms give you the ability to crawl away from each situation without wondering about contracting STDs.

I've had sex with condoms on, condoms off, where the woman was on the pill, when she was on the pill and we used spermicide as an extra measure of precaution.

See, the tiny tiny tiny chance that something might go wrong and she might get pregnant gnaws at both of us. She's responsible enough to take the pill and take it regularly, and I feel that the condom fulfills my responsibility by keeping my sperm out of her. Spermicide alone, doesn't quite make me feel safe, since the effectiveness is dependent on so many factors.

If I could find a condom that was extra-strong AND had a spermicide in it, I'd buy stock in the company that makes it, and feel completely at ease.

If you don't happen to like condoms, that's fine. If you find that condoms are too tight, or too short, or break easily, consider switching brands or styles -- they're the most versatile of contraceptive devices and one of the few ways that men can unilaterally take command of their sexual choices and safety.
