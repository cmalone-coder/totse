Re: fractional distillation
The top of your column should be 35 degrees, but depending on the column you use you could be heating the reaction flask quite strongly. This is a very basic question, are you sure you want to be distilling ether? Are you sure you have an adequate setup?

Make sure the water in your condenser is 0 degrees C, with a serious ice bath in the reservoir. You also need your receiving flask cooled in an ice bath. And keep a few extra bags of ice in the freezer for replenishing while in progress.
