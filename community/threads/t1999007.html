Well, not really to be honest. I still don't know exactly what you want. BUT I have a general idea. If you don't know what MIDI is, then you dont know how synthesizers work, so you want something like a Korg Triton..

That would be my recommendation, a Korg Triton. Tons of great patches (hundreds literally) right out of the box and of course it has a synthesizer that is fairly flexible so once you learn more about it you can create your own patches. Onboard effects, and as far as how well it replicates sounds like.... an acoustic piano, classic electric keyboards (like Fender Rhodes, Wurlitzers, etc etc), organs (like a Hammond B3), its not as good as big heavy digital pianos, but they're good enough for you guys I assume since you obviously don't have a real keyboard player.

and a "digital piano" usually refers to something with built in speakers, taht strictly uses samples, and is meant to replace an acoustic piano. So it has heavy weighted keys like a real piano, more realistic feel, etc etc. They're meant to sound like REAL acoustic pianos as much as possible. They usually don't have many sounds other than a couple of acoustic pianos, and maybe a couple of classic electric pianos, a string sample, and an organ or two.

Btw, you will need to spend more than $200. A decent worstation like a Korg Triton will only set you back about $1700 though, so its not too bad. The more high end ones are generally at least $2000, with the most expensive being up to $10000!
