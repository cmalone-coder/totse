The grass is always greener on the other side
It seems when I'm in a relationship with a woman, there comes a point where it's lost all sense of viability. I get bored. I start wishing I'm single again. The thing is, I may really like her, be physically attracted to her and be generally happy but something in my convoluted way of thinking steers me towards getting out.

Flip side - When I'm single and I mean single for a period of time, I long for a steady GF, hate being single and generally "think" I want to settle down with someone I can reliably count on being there as my "better half" long-term.

I'm much happier while in a relationship until that feeling of being "trapped" comes about, and conversely I'm also happy being single again until I've been single long enough to forget the benefits and sense of romance that comes with having someone special in my life I can count on and know is there. It's a vicious cycle.

Anyway, I'm not really looking for advice (although it's welcome), but others views and/or experiences on keeping a relationship alive. I've seen couples that have been together for many years and still seem to be perfectly in tune and happy with each other. I'd like to know how they do it.
