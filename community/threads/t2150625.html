Re: Steaming the Essentials?
I would imagine in order to do an essential oil extraction and yield a decent amount, it would be necessary to have something on a much larger scale than a 24/40 set up. Making a crude, but comparatively huge set up for a simple steam distillation, running this on the substrate bark or plant matter, and then re distilling the essential oil into finer fractions or simply a purer essential oil may be your best bet.

Depends how big your flasks are
