Re: vaporizing v smoking
Which is best is highly subjective.

Vaporizing is healthier and save a LOT more weed then pipes/bongs/etc.

I've found the high to be great and sometimes extremely different from the high you get from smoking it but just like smoking, each time, let alone different strands, can be very different.

When you smoke it you're inhaling more then just THC which would add/alter your high as far as I'm aware.

After doing both for quite a few years I've noticed myself saying things like "This bud is probably best vape'd" or "This stuff is more of a smoking strain." I have no preference in general, I find myself doing both in almost equal amounts (smoking a bit more because it's easier to do on the go for me).

It all depends really depends on which type of high you like more and to see how it effects you, you would have to test it out a few times. If your low on bud and you want to conserve however definitely vaporize it, you'll be glad you did.

Lightbulb vapes are really easy to make but if you find yourself enjoying it I suggest buying a nice one.

If I understand your first question correctly then the answer is you will get much higher vaporizing .5g then you will smoking .5g but that's my experience and I'm not sure if everyone would agree though I would assume that would be the general opinion.

People have already mentioned this, but vaping gets you more for your money. They also mentioned that the high is different because you're getting fewer impurities. Though, surprisingly enough, there is a time to vape and I time not to.

Any mids/lower quality bud, vape it. The reason mids is mids isn't because it has some shittier version of THC, it's because there's nasty shit in it that when it burns, gives you a shittier buzz. But, when you vape mids, you get less nasty stuff.

Dro, though, you never want to vape. You buy dro for that other stuff that's not THC. You'd probably still get more THC out of a g of Dro by vaping than just smoking it, but you'd be missing out on that other stuff that is part of the dro experience.

If you want max efficiency (THC for your $) get the cheapest, shittiest bud you can find and make Butane Hash Oil. Even shitty, skunky, bud will produce oil that contains no nasty impurities, and you can smoke inside, and even if someone walks into the room 2 minutes later, they'd never know someone smoked in there. I know a guy who does this and gets fucked off his ass for days of a dub of fucking skunk. He also says that the high is not anything like low quality shit.

It's like the difference between taking a Vitamin C pill and drinking a juice with lots of vitamin c.

Smoking weed is like drinking juice. You get all the healthy stuff and can enjoy it too. But it comes with some extra stuff that you don't necessarily need, but makes it enjoyable anyway.

Vaping is like taking a pill. It bypasses all the stuff you don't need. Just gives you the goods. But it's not as enjoyable.

If you're with buddies and just wanna get high, smoke the weed. It's more ritualistic and fun that way. But if you're getting low on weed and don't want to waste, vape.
