Tips for the Onset

When the curtains begin to wiggle and the shadows start to dance, here are a few things to set your trip off in a great way.

In the event you can go outdoors:

Go outside, roll down the windows on your car and play some tunes or turn on the radio. Lay on the hood of your car and just look up at the stars. You should be able to see a great tale tale sign, the Purple Haze. Stare out into the cosmos and let your mind wander.

Also, take notice of the world around you. Feel the sense of freedom you get from being outdoors and not confined to a house or room. Breathe deep and enjoy the night air. Notice how 'organic' the trees seem. The natural world will jump out at you in a way you never noticed before you dropped. The life around you is electric and amazing.

In the event you are stuck indoors:

First, open up the windows if possible. Put a fan in and start some cross circulation is you have more than one. Turn on some music and turn off any monitors or televisions. If you want, put a screensaver of visualization (Preferably a darker themed one) on the PC. If you choose to do this take notice of the rhythm kept with the music. This should have you pretty interested for a bit.

Get yourself to a comfortable place and find a nice comfortable position. Lay back on a soft pillow and close your eyes. Let your mind wander to whatever it wishes.

Acid is a thinking drug, so whatever you watch on TV or movies, you're going to think a lot about. If you watch criminal investigation stuff, you're going to be thinking about that kind of thing wayyy more than you should. And depressing movies like Oldboy are going to give you even worse thoughts. It's kind of a cliche, but "trippy" movies such 2001 A Space Odyssey will get you thinking about more worthwhile subjects (the meaning of life, our place in the universe, etc). If you stick to very real topics, like murder, your trip probably won't be very enjoyable. Unless you really want to dwell on death, it's best to avoid the topic.

I avoid television altogether. The commercials can't be controlled, so it becomes a non-stop assault on your trip. Stick to DVDs or movies without commercials if you want to watch something. But being outside with nature is a much more worthwhile experience.
