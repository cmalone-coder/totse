Re: If you could talk to your future child....
I would tell him or her to trust in their own judgment, ask questions, learn how to make decisions, be adaptable, always be willing to listen and consider different perspectives, be tolerant, understand that there are always people that care and support them, have faith in people, learn from the mistakes of others, and never be afraid to stand up for their beliefs. There are a million lessons, but those are a handful of key ones.

The three major goals in life, outside of happiness, are to be healthy, wealthy, and wise; realize that wealth comes in many forms.

I never baby children, but I would be sure that the people around me knew that the child was to be watched over and treated with respect.

I'd let my child be friendly with anybody and everybody. I'd help and offer advice, but it helps to broaden a person's perspective.

Remember that your attitude dictates a lot in this life.

I would attempt to provide the child with some stability, but they would be subjected to my enjoyment of travel and adventure.

It's every generation's job to surpass the generation that came before. I'd try to pass on that interesting family value and see what comes of it.

These are the basics that spring to mind.
