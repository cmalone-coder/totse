Just as efficient as making cannabutter. Definitely one of the most efficient ways to ingest THC, because hardly any (if any at all) goes to waste unlike smoking.

I usually get the 1500ml bottles (at least I think it's 1500) of Everclear and pour enough of it out to fit an ounce of ground up bud in. Let it sit for about a month and shake it vigorously 2 or 3 times a day. It should be a dark color green when it's ready. To test the potency, just pour yourself a shot or two. It should give you a nice alcohol buzz and get you quite fried.

DO NOT USE VODKA. I read about so many people using vodka and it's absolutely retarded. You need high proof alcohol otherwise a lot of the THC remains unabsorbed and is basically just laying dormant in the plant material going unused. Even funnier, some of the people who use vodka just pour vodka over some weed and drink it right there without letting it sit for any period of time. It's called green dragon because the alcohol is supposed to turn green, not just because there's weed in it.
