Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Totse does not accept responsiblity for any incident involving the use of the information contained within this forum.

My Resignation.
Well this is it, folks. I'm done with this hobby. Long story short, a soda bottle salute ended up with my friends questioned and me in the back of a patrol car. They say it's a pipe bomb, and while it was a metal ("Rain" cactus drink from Snapple or something) bottle, I disagree. It should be obvious to any ATF agent that this is not a device designed to hurt anyone. I don't know if I'll get my video camera, though I got my cell phone and watch back. So I'm back home, and I'm quitting pyrotechnics. I wonder if this happened to Zero. The person I trust most here is Rat, so I'll email him in a few minutes about stuff. The bottom line is that I'll no longer be posting or moderating here. Any of you may contact me about anything except pyrotechnics. I don't really want sympathy, this is a risk we all take. I shall not return to the forums. As for my future, I'll be contacted or arrested by the ATF, there is no way I can get out of this one. In order to prevent anyone except me to use my account, I will immediately have it closed if that is at all possible. EP, Rat, and Smokey, it's up to you to keep this board running smoothly. Sorry if this is disorganized, I'm not in a thinking mood. I will send what I've written about the Ka-Fucking-Boom section to Jeff and JC. I'll also send my partial update to Rat, and I hope he will put it up. The tiny ass amount of media and the text I have written is now public and may be used or reproduced by anyone, so long as no one uses it in a destructive manner. That's all I have to say, goodbye.
