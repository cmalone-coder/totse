As I promised.

I currently own two iMacs. The first Apple computer I actually purchased was a 500mhz Graphite iMac. I bought it in 2000 so that I would have a computer to work on at home. I used it almost everyday up until last year and it has never let me down. I used os9 on it for a long time until it really started to hold me back. I still use it every once and a while, mostly for surfing and some word processing.

Last August, I purchased a 2.4ghz 24" Alum. iMac. I had used the white intel imacs before the alum. came out but as soon as I saw the new design, I knew I had to have one. It's my home computer now and i use it for surfing, word processing, photo editing, and graphical design.

I think that the iMac is one of the most important products in Apples line. At first I only wanted the iMac for surfing and word processing but now, as the line has advanced, the iMac has become a real powerhouse. At work I use a Mac Pro and at home I use the iMac and to be honest the Mac Pro is over kill for what I do. If you want a powerful consumer computer, you cant go wrong with the iMac.
