You want to know why drugs are wrong? I'll tell you.

First, we have to start by defining right and wrong. A philosopher will tell you that that's objectively impossible, which is why axiology is an age-old field of inquiry.

I would reply, that they were stupid and small-minded.

The closest we can get to defining right and wrong objectively is by looking at the roots of what makes someone say "that's wrong," or "because it's the right thing to do."

These things are generally said when it comes to decisions that either hurt or help others. Alternatively, it could be things that hurt or help a society. For instance, lining in a strictly regulated society, an individual's version of right and wrong might revolve not around the greatest benefit or damage to others but to the society as a whole.

So, I reach the conclusion that right is things that help people or society and wrong is things that hurt people or society.

Now, we get to drugs.

Drugs in medicinal use have been one of the greatest developments of human civilization. With medicine, we can stave off sickness, suffering, and death. Pure good.

Recreation, on the other hand, is much murkier. Addiction, violence, and death. These are the things that people think of when they think of drugs. Pure bad.

The question here is whether these ideas about recreational drug use are correct. Drug use is highly correlated with addiction, violence, and death. This is fact.

In my own experience, almost everyone I know who does drugs (~100 people) (most of them just smoke bud and drink on occasion) haven't gone anywhere. Most of them graduated high school, and with mediocre grades and test scores, went to mediocre colleges. Some of them dropped out. Some of them are currently doing time. None have died for more than a few minutes, but like I said before, most just smoke and drink. Only one of them has done well in HS (straight A's and a 34 on the ACT), but almost didn't get into college due to a series of drug arrests senior year.

What does this mean? Though drug use isn't exclusively wrong, it is generally so. Well this post is already going on many pages long so as for specifics, I'll go into it later

There really isn't any good argument for why it's wrong to do drugs. It's a personal choice based on a personal freedom. The drug in itself can be neither good nor bad, it is just a thing. In a more holistic sense, it could be said that using drugs is harmful to the mind and/or body; certainly there are instances in which drugs have caused grievous bodily harm, but this is entirely dependent on the substance in question and the biochemical environment it was introduced to. There is an incredible variety of psychoactive chemicals and substances available to the informed user today, some of which are used daily by millions of people around the world. Why should a social consensus on which drugs are good and bad be imposed on the individual human? A person's responses to these drugs is entirely idiosyncratic, it is as unique to them as their fingerprint. No person deserves to suffer because they are different, make different choices, or have different preferences.
