I havent made one but i asked this question before as well. You need to make a wood box big enough to hold you. Caulk it and make it water tight, then use enough caulk to smooth out all edges into smooth bends. Cover this in plastic that wont irritate the skin if youre rubbing against it for a while. I forget the constant temperature you have to maintain, but at a pet store you can get a fish tank water temperature regulator that go up to 25 c, (22 c is room temp) Gotta make sure its soundproof, possibly covering it with the foam egg containers at the grocery store will help. It also has to be tight enough on the door so light doesnt leak in, possibly goin to the dump and ripping the sealed lining off of a fridge door. I forget the water you have to fill it with, but its special in some way, maybe with salt or something im not sure.
