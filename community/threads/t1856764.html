no swim has not noticed lwr(long wet reflux) to be hotter, I think it may give that impression due to more water in lwr therefore more little bubbles boiling and toiling. swim keeps oil bath bout 120c for 36hrs for lwr. 1ml dh20/1g psuedo/1.25 I2/0.75 rp

mix I2 and psuedo together first, chop with razor finely, the finer the better.

Put this into reaction vessel cover top place in freezer for five to ten minutes.

add h20 then rp then put on condensor, there should be no violent reaction. Set temp to 120c and leave for 36hrs.the amount of water stated is for a 36hr reflux.
