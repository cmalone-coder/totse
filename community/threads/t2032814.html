Most people are uneven, but that's quite an asymmetry. If you do a lot of pushups to build your biceps, it's likely that you're favouring your right arm massively, even if you don't notice. Using an exercise ball is supposed to counteract this (I think you put your feet on it, and do pushups, and when you favour one arm you tend to roll over to the side, so it forces you to use your arms equally). Or you could just do lots of one-armed pushups. If you go and see a physiotherapist they'll design a proper set of exercises for you to follow to even you up.

I once had uneven arms too. My left one was much bigger than my right, and I was tired of that. It annoyed me so much. So what I did was take some good ol' 10 and 25 pound dumbells, and started to work out my arms. I worked each one out one at a time, doing a set with my left arm, then doing twice as much with my right. It was a killer for my right arm, though it really brought the bicep quite close to my left. No matter what, my left will be a bit bigger than my right (since its my dominant hand), though it will only be by so little you can hardly notice it. Good luck!

Oh and with the two dumbells: I worked out with 25 (doing the routine), then once I couldn't lift the 25's anymore, I moved to 10 until I couldnt lift them up anymore for the night. I also had light 5 pounders laying around, so I got those and used those till I couldn't. It was a killer, though it was totally worth it.

Hope this helped,

A.E.
