Alcohol Promotes Our Naturally Inherent Promiscuity
I have noticed in my past experiences with alcohol and with people that they go quite well together, especially when it comes to promoting our sexual desires.

Some people claim that alcohol makes people do things they would never do sober. I believe this to be true, but only because it reduces the inhibitions that prevent such actions from taking place, not because the alcohol actually distorts someone's views in such a way that they will do things completely out of their nature.

I hope that made sense.

Anyway, I am going to use some examples from personal experience, as well as from personal observation, that I'm sure most of you can relate to or at least understand.

The other day I was with a female friend with whom I know there is at least some latent chemistry. It would have been apparent, possibly only to a very keen observer, but it became more obvious as the night progressed (and as the alcohol consumption rose).

At first, while we were sober and just commencing our drinking ritual, we had a long talk about love and soul mates and sex and such. We both came to the agreement that we weren't too fond of sex, but still did it somewhat out of necessity, or at least (in her case) only with those with whom we have a high level of attraction.

By the end of the night, people thought us to be a couple. At one point, a bit of play fighting progressed to her on top of me kissing me. Another person in the room made the comment; "Someone's getting laid tonight". No one made any comment to counter that remark, not even my female friend.

Anyway, long story short, a lot of stuff happened that wouldn't have happened were we sober. I'm a fucking idiot and got too drunk and threw up on myself and passed out, so no sex occurred, and I can only speculate as to whether or not it would have.

But my point is that, we both went against the very topic we discussed and agreed on earlier that night in regards to sex.

Now here is something that we can all agree on: There are people that go out to bars and clubs with the sole purpose of getting laid. This is because the alcohol facilitates that purpose.

Also, I'm sure the majority of you have had a few drinks in you, and had a change in your stance on whether or not you would give into your romantic or sexual urges.

What I'm trying to get at here, is that deep down, we are all "sluts" looking for a fuck. Alcohol seems to be the ultimate lubricant for these situations to get things flowing more quickly and with less regret.

No matter how much you value the sanctity of the union of two souls as some spectacular magical event, it really just is in our human nature to get down and dirty and do it, even with complete strangers, no strings attached.

Promiscuous sex isn't as dirty as a lot of people make it out to be.
