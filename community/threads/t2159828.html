Not to hijack the thread here or anything, but how does adderall compare to taking benzos POC? (As in terms of anxiety, social, general, etc..).

Well, for general, as in, someone who worries all the time about anything/everything, it's probably not going to go over very well; same can be said for someone who is typically high-strung or of a nervous disposition.

My entire experience in struggling with anxiety has usually been based around feelings of being overwhelmed; too much to do, not enough time, not enough money...basically just a fear of inadequacy and failure for a lot of things where really the fear is/was entirely unrealistic.

As far as social anxiety, most of mine was centered around the worries about saying something stupid, having nothing to talk about, my clothes/appearance making me look like an ass when there was nothing wrong with it, etcetera.

A lot of that tied in with depression for me, as far as the self-confidence issues went, and although the benzos helped me to worry/care less about some of those things, they often added to the depression. Shot down my motivation and drive, too, and usually wound up clouding my head so bad during the times I would take them that I was basically useless for anything beyond normal function.

When my old psych tried me on Adderall, it completely got rid of my confidence issues; I didn't get the "I am God" effect someone might from being tweaked out on a high dose, but at therapeutic levels it was more of a sense of "I can do this; it's not that difficult," rather than the whole being-overwhelmed thing. I felt much more capable to take on bigger and/or more difficult tasks because with the Adderall my mind worked better to break things down into smaller, more manageable parts rather than make me feel like I had to do everything at once and save the world at the same time if I wanted to amount to anything less than failure.

When I'm on Adderall I'm a lot less nervous in conversation; for one thing, it increases my patience ten-fold, as I previously stated. A lot of my anxiety was based on a common point for its sufferers: racing thoughts. Barnum and Bailey's Circus in my head. A thousand thoughts shooting off in every which direction at once because my brain would try to plan out a strategy for the stupidest, most mundane tasks while at the same time worrying about whether I did everything right that day and whether or not I forgot anything and what I need to do tomorrow and what order I need to plan it out in for the most efficiency.

Now reading that, it probably sounds like some office executive mapping out details for some massive multi-level managerial project; it's not. It's me having a day consisting of doing my laundry, paying a few bills, and working a five or six hour shift later on in the day. The Adderall just shuts off the rambling in my head and allows me to ground myself in the present moment, which makes having a casual conversation a lot more manageable and enjoyable because I don't get the feeling that this other person's petty talking is making me lose focus/time on figuring out this complicated mathematical equation going on in my head at the same time that I'm talking to them.
