Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Post pics of your ignition system !
Thats right i would like you to post the pictures of your ignition system and how it was made if possible

the one i post here was designed by a fellow rocketeer that is an electrical genius the controler is way beeyound my knowledge the cost of the whole system seen below is about $1200 USD at the curent moment and thats the price without the labour put into programing it !

http://www.members.shaw.ca/pyrus/1.jpg (http://www.members.shaw.ca/pyrus/1.jpg)

this is a picture of the complete system as you can see there is the control system on the left and to "pod units" on the right



http://www.members.shaw.ca/pyrus/2.jpg (http://www.members.shaw.ca/pyrus/2.jpg) in this picture you can see a dial the dial is for setting the pod units as you can see there is pob unit AB CD EF GH IJ KL

above the dial you can see a row of 8 switches each sets the pads you want ! pretty much the for on the right would control POD A right now and would not fire any thing but if you look to the last 4 they ar currently set to POD B which is launching somthing on pad 4 as you can see there is power (yellow lights) and continuity (green) light on pad 4 there are 2 button were you can pick between a 10 second count down and a 5 second count down there is a saftey arming switch an abort button which there are a couple ways of doing (if your in the middle of a count down you can either turn the pad off (pad 4 POD B) press the abort button or switch the key off) there is a buzzer on the control which beeps each time a second goes by and the row of vertical lights indicates the count down when the blue light lights up (the one currently on) thats when it is firing

pretty much set your pod set your pad or multiple pads press count delay and switch key on press launch and it counts down

http://www.members.shaw.ca/pyrus/3.jpg (http://www.members.shaw.ca/pyrus/3.jpg) this is POD unit B as you can see there is a continuity testing button on the pod unit there is also a battery check button the row of Black and White adapters goes out to each pad so they can spread out there is now power that goes to the controler the power is feed through the pod unit (the white cord currently)



thats pretty much it (there were a few mods to tak this picture due to the fact that we don't launch indoors ...)
