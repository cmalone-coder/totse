Coffee-Shop Inamorata
It's a term I've coined recently that some have heard me use in conversation, yet never adequately explain.

It's really just a summarization of what, in my opinion, would be the perfect relationship. Wondering what an inamorata is? Well for those of you that are too lazy to look it up, it's a woman who is loved in an intimate relationship. Yea, that simple, it's just a more aesthetic and less verbose of a term.

Anyhow, a coffee shop inamorata would be a relationship in which the couple could be truly happy and comfortable just sitting in a coffee shop with one another. A bond where you could just gaze into the other's eyes and just understand each other without even having to say anything. More in depth, a relationship of equals, no one wears the pants, they both just flow together. A deeply romantic, yet respected friendship.

I want someone who would let me pamper them, but will surprise me as well; someone I could spend the entire night with, not really having to do anything, but just hold unto and talk about anything with. A loyal, passionate, honest, comfortable something.

Unfortunately, I have yet to be in a relationship with my own coffee-shop inamorata. What can I say, life's a bitch.
