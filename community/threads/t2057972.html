I do not smoke and i'm against that shit, not in general, but only when people smoke close to me and stink up my clothes or when people who i care about poison their health with it. I've only smoked about 3 or 4 cigarettes in my life and i didn't like them all too much. I wanted to see what the big deal was, and for me, it's pointless.

The girl i like is a passive smoker.

If anyone else smokes, i don't give a shit, but when i hear her talking about wanting a cigarette, it just kills my mood. I don't show it much, but inside i'm suddenly depressed and sad as hell. I just feel so damn worthless and defeated.

I told her to give smoking up... if she's not addicted and doesn't HAVE to smoke, why the fuck should she do it? I told her i don't like it and that i care about her and i want her to be as healthy as possible but she just keeps saying "i'm only a PASSIVE smoker... i only smoke a few times a week, bla bla bla"

i'm trying to accept it... but i still don't like it. Sometimes i just want to yell at her "if i ever see you smoke or even hear you talking about smoking again, i'm gonna cut off all contact with you and you can do whatever the fuck you want with your life, you can ruin your lungs, ruin your heart and every goddamn blood vessel you have for all i care". But that's bullshit... i'd be a goddamn fool to cut contact for stupid shit like this.

2 days ago, we were at a festival watching some band and she got a cigarette out and started smoking.

(my initial reaction thought was: "fuck... god damn it, i told her i don't want her smoking... i'm gonna take her fucking cig, put it out with my hand, get the pack from her purse and fucking throw it so damn hard that my shoulder dislocates!!") - Of course, it was one of those outrageous thoughts i get when i hate something so much.

I didn't want to let the shit mood take over and turn me into a sad fuck, so i asked her to let me have a puff (and thus, show her that i'm not gonna give her the usual "please stop smoking... you're fucking up your health for no reason" response)

She looked at me shortly, she said "she doesn't usually smoke, she's just a passive smoker" (as if i didn't hear that a million times) and handed me the cig. She probably thought i was going to put it out or something... I wasn't, but she was close though... i wanted to suck in most of what was left and leave about half a centimeter of it so she doesn't have to smoke that much, cause i knew she wouldn't smoke another one at that time. I didn't though... i just took a regular fake puff (getting air in my chest but holding the smoke in my mouth) and gave it back to her. She finished smoking it and that was the only cigarette she smoked while i was around her.

I still felt a bit down but not as much as usual though.

How do i deal with this? should i just accept it and be happy she's just "passive"? should i keep explaining again and again why her smoking is pointless? i don't want to make her smoke behind my back out of spite...
