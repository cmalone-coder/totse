From what I learned in psychology class and from TV documentaries, a DJ in the 1950s stayed up for over 100hours without sleep. He began to have visual hallucinations with regards to his food and became increasingly paranoid. This was during his telethon, trying to raise money for charity and seeing how long he could stay awake.



He became paranoid and wigged out and after sleeping it off, he was never the same man again.



Reports say that the brain can be permanently damaged from lack of sleep, even if you sleep 5 or 6 hours a night and feel normal...it's doing damage.



Sleep deprivation is also very bad for the heart and if you've noticed, your heart tends to pump a lot harder and faster when you are sleep deprived. Not a good thing if you have a heart condition... and couple that with caffeine or other stimulant drugs and you are asking for a heart arrhythmia which can be fatal.



But anyway, for most of us, staying awake for 2 days once in a blue moon is no big deal. Just don't drive.
