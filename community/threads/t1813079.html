Honestly, The Holy Bible and its Christian followers are quite narrow-minded and ill educated. There are many Apocryphal texts which are quite blatantly orthodox in their message and would well compliment the canonized texts.

Take, for example, The Gospel of Nicodemus (Acts of Pilate). Many of the sayings therein are identical to those which are said in the canonized Gospels. It simply has them in context of the history of it all. The basic events and understanding of Pilate and the situation is there as well, it is simply a more thorough investigation of the events surrounding the resurrection... Obviously, there are more texts which are like this. The Gospel of Thomas is probably the one you're most familiar with. Around 90% of what is in that Gospel is repeated in the canonized Gospels. It works reverse of the previous example though. The sayings in Thomas are put into context and given storyline in the canonized Gospels.
