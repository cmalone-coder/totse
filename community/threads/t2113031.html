Re: How come I don't see elves or dragons?
Somewhere in the word "hallucination" is the word "truth". Next time you drop some acid or munch on some shrooms, just keep repeating "hallucination" to yourself. You'll get it.

treating these substances as "hallucinogens", or chemicals producing fantasies of the mind really do a disservice to them. By calling something a hallucination you put up a barrier between yourself and the experience. This isn't going to lead to any sort of growth, understanding, or enlightenment. If you don't accept whatever is happening as completely and totally real as anything else you're fooling yourself.

So what if there are spiders crawling all over you? Investigate it. Be aware of it. Observe. Don't react. Don't judge it. Just let it be.

It is completely possible to have visions of otherworldly or just strange things, but this just means that your mind is cluttered with a bunch of crap. Stop focusing on mundane human concerns, and soon enough your "subconscious" will stop producing weird images.

My subconscious has dredged up visions of spongebob and the goombas from mario. Why? Who knows? But once I stopped thinking, they went away and I started perceiving pure energy.
