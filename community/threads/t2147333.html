Re: Mescaline extraction.. Failed?
I lol'd at the customary SoH response.

So you got crystals out of the AB?
Yellowy ones at that?
Have you even recrystallised once?
What do they dissolve in?
Are there enough to attempt a melting point test?
Could you scrounge together enough to run a ghetto TLC plate? (read: yes, search JPs threads)

I would say, first recrystallise, see what they look like, dunno if its the same as DMT, but an ice cold ammonia wash does wonders if they're still looking a little grimy. Then try to work out what they might be. I imagine a bit of test reagents may help you here, I seem to recall there was a response for mescaline.

Oh, and as a final tip that might be handy, read a certain thread on WD by Tramp, MEK should be easy enough to find. I think, I can't quite remember who did it, but it was a damn good thread, and gave excellent results.
