I know these have been answered already but I'm going to post my personal preferences regarding salvia.

1. Many headshops do require you to be 18 to view the "back room", which is usually where all the pipes and other goodies are kept.

2. For a Sally-D virgin I'd go with the rest and say 20x.

3. I much prefer a bong. MUCH prefer one. Salvia tastes funny and leaves an odd sensation in your mouth, so smoking as little as possible to break through is ideal for me. Plus a bong will allow you to take a significantly larger hit and hold it longer as well.

4. You don't need a big bowl of extract. As was stated before, keep smoking until you break through. If you can keep smoking -- you haven't broken through yet :)

My ideal setting is a dimly lit room, by myself or with a friend who you trust and won't try to do stupid shit to you while you're tripping.

I also like putting music on in the background. I like Shpongle and Younger Brother for most psychedelics, salvia included. Any kind of tribal music is cool too, and nature sounds. Gregorian monk chanting is pretty damn cool too :)

Anyway, trip safe and enjoy!

Edit: Not really relevant to your thread, but anyone who does acid should try breaking through on salvia during a peak. It's pretty awesome. :)
