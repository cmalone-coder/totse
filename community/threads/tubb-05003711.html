Re: Growing babies need HELP!!
Put on a 12/12 cycle immediately. Indoor plants do not need to be 3 feet tall in vegetative. You will stagnate the budding too much (the top buds will be done just as bottom buds begin to grow). They will still grow another 5-8 inches in the budding cycle. Next time, bud them when they're about 1-2 feet tall. AND GET REAL LIGHTS.
