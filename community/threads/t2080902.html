Do Christians actually read the New Testament or learn about the religion?
I always wondered this. Every day I see Christians doing just about every single thing that is in contradiction of the new testament and Jesus' beliefs. Most have never even heard of the counsel of 300, know when the Gospels were written, and by whome they were written. Some things that most Christians do goes completely against Christs teachings. Sometimes I wonder if Christians have actually read the New Testament.

Examples of stupid things Christians do that go against Jesus and logic.

Praying. Praying not only doesn't make sence, Jesus actually tells us that it's a waste of time as well as blasphemous. The reason is that God is omnipresent morons, and by praying your slapping God in the face thinking you have to tell him things he already knows. Jesus does say if you are basically retarded and must pray, it should only be the lords prayer in total privacy, and nothing else. However you'll be judged for being ignorant.

Jesus hates the Church. In the New Testament Jesus flips out in a temple after seeing the people who were running the temple had a market set up in the temple and were counting gold. He then declared that all those who attend church are heathens and only do it for their own personal image to glorify themselves, not God. So any Christians that attend church are not in Christs' good books and you will be judged for ignorance.


Jesus forgives. This is True, however he punishes too and you must be judged if you are not a truly righteous person. Most aren't so he might forgive you but you probably won't get into heaven if you're a sack of shit like most people.

I'm not a Christian, I don't follow any religion and feel religion is merely the futile answer to questions with no answers so stupid people's heads don't explode. It's impossible to know if there is a god or not, and only liars and crazies think they know.
