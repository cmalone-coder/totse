My favourites to play are Bert Jansch - Angie, John Renbourn - Another Monday and Debbie Anne. Various other Jansch like Smokey River and Veronica. Tommy Emmanuel is also awesome, but not very folksy, and you won't be able to learn it until you're a lot better. Your best bet is to find some Jansch Power Tabs.
