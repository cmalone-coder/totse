try to watch it again and reconstruct the way it "really" happened.
The acctual end is the 3rd part "the gold watch" where Butch shoots down Vicent and rescues Marcellus. I believe the fourth part contains the gist of the movie. Anyway, they say that it was Marcellus' soul in the briefcase, guys were just trying to get it back to the legal owner, that's why "God came down from heaven and stopped these motherfucking bullets" in the episode with the man in the bathroom. I've also heard that Tarantino never said that it was true. But that's not the point of the story:), the point of the story is that Jules got it, i mean he felt a touch of God and became enlightened ( may seem a bit exaggerated but one can draw parallels). He speaks of what alcoholics call "a moment of clarity" drugs can do the same thing, i guess.
The next point is the decision: Jules decides to quit the life and walk the earth till god puts him where he wants him to be. Message was delivered and accepted. Vincent, in the opposite, as every normal western gangster thinks that "it's weird, man, but shit happens".
Then comes the scene with bunny and pie that acctually has started in the first part and here Jules proves to be a very decent commentator of biblical texts (though T's version differs from the bible's one). His interpretaion of Ezekiel 25:17 sounds a bit kafkasque:) and then ... "but i'm trying, Ringo, i'm trying real hard to be a shepherd".
In the end, we have one dead white gangster in the bathroom and Jules who i'm sure still walks from town to town, meets people and gets adventures.
It's all about things we believe in, decisions we make and shizophrenie:)
P.S. i've never really known any africans or afro-americans but i guess the reason why Tarantino made Jules black is that they can freak out all of a sudden when it concerns religion or something like that. They seem to understand the whole thing somewhat different and take it more seriously. i hope it was politically correct, anyway, just an idea:)
