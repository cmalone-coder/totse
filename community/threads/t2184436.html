If you agree with someone, that doesn't necessarily mean they're intelligent.
It doesn't necessarily mean you're intelligent either. By "intelligent," I basically mean that your brain works well; you can process information, remember it, and use it all efficiently. All too often, however, people use the word "intelligent" to mean "that which agrees with my opinions, beliefs, and views." And they use it after just one brief post by one person. Example:

Poster A: "Blah blah blah blah blah, etc., blah."

Poster B: "OMG I agree, you are so intelligent."

That's a stupid (and, more importantly, unwise) thing to think, because your ego starts plugging up your analytical, objective thought once you think that. Dumb people can have smart beliefs and brilliant ideas. Brilliant people can have dumb beliefs and stupid ideas. I know dumb people I agree with on many things, and smart people I disagree with on many things.

Now, I would like everyone to respond to this thread by telling me how smart I am because you agree with me.:)
