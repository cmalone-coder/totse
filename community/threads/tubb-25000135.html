Re: Human Genetic Enhancement
Regarding genetic engineering, I personaly put different theoretical procedures in one of three classification.

Class 1 genetic engineering is where a person's biochemistry is tweaked slightly to produce a given result. A stronger immune system would require a tweak to the bone marrow, which produces white blood cells.

Class 2 genetic engineering requires a surgical procedure to attach geneticaly engineered tissue to a person's body. A prehensile tail would require that tissue samples be taken, geneticaly engineered, grown into a form, than grafted onto the body.

Class 3 genetic engineering is for procedures that are so complex or subtle that it is only feasable to alter the genome of and embryo and let it develop with the new set of instructions. Just about anything involving the brain or central nervous system would be class 3.

This is just how I keep it straight for the sci-fi I write, but I think it works as a valid system.

Fur would be cool, and may be possible with a class 1 procedure. Researchers have already developed a topical cream that can turn skin cells into hair folicles. Though that works by turning on and off genes that already exist in the cells, a viral carrier could be introduced to inject GE material into the skin.

Don't expect the afore mentioned cream to come on the market as a baldness cure anytime soon, there is currently still a problem in orienting the folicles, which results in lots of ingrown hairs (ouch!).
