Jack Nimbles Clandestine Lab Operation - This is a very good book which makes for a very interesting read:

Here is wiki link to download (that rapidshare waitng for 40mins etc sucks!)

Strike's Total Synthesis II, nothing else needs to be said...
http://rapidshare.com/files/39821759...nthesis_II.pdf

King's Chemistry - An excellent resource on producing those 'hard-to-get' chems...
http://rapidshare.com/files/40011280...ival_Guide.pdf

Cohen's Practical Oganic Chemistry
http://www.wikiupload.com/download_page.php?id=169207

Uncle Fester's 5th Edition Secrets of MA Manufacture
http://www.wikiupload.com/download_page.php?id=169212

The Chemistry of Recreational Drugs - A very, very interesting read detailing various pharmies and their routes of synthesis
http://www.wikiupload.com/download_page.php?id=169216

Vogels 5th Edition - Practical Organic Chemistry - The original and still one of the best
http://www.wikiupload.com/download_page.php?id=169221

These are good for reference but are no replacement for the real thing, I hope it spurs interest and results in a purchase or two.
