Mentoring younger people with relationships and sexuality.
Just like I am an apprentice in my career, Should younger people have mentors for relationships and sexuality? I've pondered this for some time now, as very few younger people have any knowledge of these two things, and as a result causes great chaos in their lives. When I say younger, I mean a person who is of legal age that consents to your instruction, not children.

Looking back, I wish I would have had such a mentor to teach me the things that my parents didn't. Obviously, I wouldn't really want to experience sexuality with my parents, however there was a gap. I went out of my way to learn things on my own, but man would it have been nice to have someone show me the ropes, instead of the trial and error method that I was forced to use. Now that I'm older, I see a lot of younger people, less gifted than myself, struggling with the same things. Often, I wonder if I should help a younger person 16-18, and teach them about relationships and sex. I can understand how this may seem weird, possibly perverted, but this is mainly to this not being the norm.

I'm just looking at this idea objectively, not planning to execute the idea or any such thing. Only theoretical.
