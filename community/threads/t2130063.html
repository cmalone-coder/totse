Re: Best Blunts
Dutches (preferably Strawberry or Grape)
Game blunts (Honey or Vanilla)
Wraps of all flavors
Backwoods (Honey Berry)

That's pretty much what I use, in order of decreasing frequency. Dutches have probably the best burn for a "fake" blunt (processed and all that). Games are ultra easy to roll if you unwrap and re-roll the leaf and I like the honey more than honey dutches. Backwoods take a lot more skill to roll but give you a more old-school blunt with a fair amount of nicotine in it, so it gives a different high than the others. And then of course wraps are nice for quick rolling.

Ever since I stopped unwrapping the leaves though, mostly dutches with the occasional game. I don't like white owls and swishers much, and phillies are for mid grade.
