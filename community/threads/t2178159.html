They're far more interesting than middle-agers. They have the uncanny ability to insult, slander, finger-point and mock others, which has a valuable, cathartic effect on both the accuser and the recipient. This cathartic effect has probably not been adequately defined, nor appreciated, by any real-world psychiatric journals to date. (Which is why psychiatry fails, in my humble opinion)

They don't ignore the mean streaks they have festering inside them, they exercise it. They don't cover up their anger in lieu of being congenial and appropriate. They more quickly say what they think, and don't tire themselves out trying to over-analyze the feelings and emotions that manifest erratic behavior; they embrace it, and run with it. The reaction they seek from their elders is of the most vile sort: shock, disgust, anger, and fear. They're not quite to the point of craving acceptance and praise from society, because they haven't yet worked out in their young minds what society truly expects of them.

Their egocentric bravado, false sense of certainty and the belief that they can effect change on a world that has remained changeless for quite some time now makes them true heroes in my book. They've yet to fall into a set routine, have yet to become hardwired into complete cynicism, and haven't yet felt the pangs of recollection that comes when you're old enough to admit that you knew more as a child than you ever will as a full-fledged adult.

In short, their brains and personalities are far more fluid and malleable than our own. They aren't yet shackled by the expectations society has on them. They haven't yet crossed the threshold into full-blown maturity and responsibility, and that alone affords them the freedom of acting on all the personalities they can draw upon at a whim without fear of ridicule or repercussion.

They're expected to wade through the disappointment life doles out with a blissful ignorance that most adults can no longer draw upon, because these adults have already graduated from being the wide-eyed explorers, the naive experimenters, the pliable personality-morphers. The adults are no longer free to act out, embrace absurdity and fight tooth and nail the very establishment that plans on sucking them into the black hole of redundancy, routine and the rigmarole of thinking with complete certainty that the sum of your life experiences is all you can ever hope to define yourself by.

Inserting myself into this pool of youngsters injects the vitality, apathy and general sense of humor that it takes to thumb my nose at maturity most of the time, while still being able to play the role of Responsible Adult in the areas of my life that require it, such as self-sufficiency and keeping one's head above water so as not to slip into poverty or co-dependence on others.

Besides that, it's kind of fun, in a cruel and unusual fashion, to watch you all stumble blindly through life while kidding yourselves into believing that all the hopes, dreams and aspirations you have today will follow you into the dull rigors of the inevitable stage of Full Maturity you'll find yourselves in after many more tomorrows.

I hope you all can regress backwards like I have once you've crossed over into adulthood. I can assure you that it's the only remedy that will keep you from turning into your parents someday.

LONG LIVE LIVING YOUNG!
