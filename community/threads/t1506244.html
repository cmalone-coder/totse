First Month Well Underway: Pregnancy in All Its Glory
Month 1

Today I had my first appointment for my new pregnancy. We got to the office about 15 minutes early to fill out paperwork and shit of the sort. Zek's in a bad mood today. He didn't want to go/be there. I was a little hurt by this and I'd have rather he have waited in the car then have gone in with me. I tried my best to ignore it.

I was given quite the hassel at the office. I have Tricare Health Insurance, which is what the military uses. It's very good insurance. It covers 100% of everything. The only draw backs are that my providers on base are very busy and understaffed. They hadn't put in my referral to the down town obstetrics clinic. I was forced to wait an hour after my appointment was meant to begin to actually be seen. I was feeling very irritable and wanted to leave.

My paperwork was very farmiliar. "When was your last menstral period? What contraceptives were you using? How old are you? Any birth defects in your family, or the baby's father's family? etc." I am once again to be considered a 'High Risk' pregnancy due to my age. Women under the age of 21, and over the age of 35 at the time of delivery are to be considered high risk.

Finally I am called in for my examination. I was seen by my nurse practitioner who helped me through my last pregancy and all my other gynocological needs.

Upon an initial appointment, you will have a pelvic exam, pap smear, STD test (Chlamydia), and breast exam.

My blood pressure was taken prior to my undressing. I was at 117/73. So I'm doing rather well. 120/80 is normal. I've always been a little low. So all is normal at current.

Having a pelvic exam is almost like getting a pap smear. Your naked from the waist down and have your feet in stirrups. My NP slid her fingers inside my vagina up to my cervix and used her other hand to press on my abdomen.

After the pelvic exam, she swabbed me for my STD test, and then for my pap smear. Then I was prompted to put my legs together again.

She lifted my shirt and bra and massaged my breasts checking for any anamolies. All clear there.

I then scoot up on the exam table and expose my tummy. She uses an instrument called a dopler to find the heartbeat of the baby. She squeezed a small amount of gel on the dopler to help receive a better signal. We couldn't find the heartbeat today. Because I have NO IDEA when my last period was, ( I recently had my IUD taken out. It had been messing with my period, so I don't know when my last one was) I will have an ultrasound done next week to figure out in measuremeants how far along I am.

At this point I couldn't be more than 5 weeks. But we'll know for sure next week.

I was given a prescription for Pre-Natal vitamins. I will have to take these everyday even after the baby is born until I am instructed to stop taking them. I was also told to drink 3-5 liters of water a day. (As if I didn't have to pee all the time already!)

With this first appointment, it is also the beginning of no more not approved drugs. I.E. When you are pregnant, your immune system goes down tremendously. Like anything foreign in your body your white blood cells will try to attack the baby. To accommodate the baby, your body turns your white blood cells on 'virtually non-existent. This means that you are much more likely to get sick and stay sick. Now I'll tie the drugs into this. The only medication you can really take while pregnant is acetaminophen (Tylenol). No cold pills, no OTC anythings. The only other medication I can take is my Welbutrin.

Right now, my feelings about this pregnancy are mixed. I am excited about it. But at the same time, I feel a little apprehensive. Already, I am experiencing a severe loss in concentration, my mood swings are taking over my sanity, and I'm having expensive cravings. (Sushi.... mmmmmmm.) I feel like I'm constantly being attacked by Zek and his constant bad moods. (He hasn't taken his meds in three days.) I feel very overwhelmed at the moment. I try to tell myself that I'm not going to get upset about these things and not to let my hormones get the best of me. But I'm in a vulnerable state of mind right now. Things will get better I'm sure. At present, they're not that great. Just have to wait it out.

I'll be sure to have another report next week after my ultrasound.



I apologize for my various grammatical errors etc. I'm far to tired to care too much about them right now. Although in the morning I'm sure there will be a nice edit. :P
