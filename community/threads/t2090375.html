Re: Does pure molly have a 'crash'?
Well, yes, it does have a come down; but it's not usually as bad as pills depending on the purity. Where I come from, pills are bulked out with speed, and we all know how bad speed can hit you when you crash... mdma is pretty easy on the depression.

Still, he's pretty dumb for doing it so early.. that is, unless he's got a shit load of it. I find that if I keep dosing on mdma I can retain that feeling of ecstasy; still, he'll be coming down by 9 at the latest anyway... bit of a waste if you ask me. You better have some pot for the come down, though, as it makes it easier.

Drinking alcohol is fine in my opinion. Just don't over do it. Some people say it can dehydrate you, but, generally, I get tired of drinking as the night goes on and often find myself switching over to glasses of water. Just do what feels comfortable and don't do anything too extreme... like shots of absinthe, for example.
