Re: DXM is a semisynthetic opioid?
I haven't watched the video yet because Dropkick is more important, but I did want to touch on the synthetic opioid thing.

Depending on who you talk to, yes, DXM can be considered an opioid.

That was a point of contention here before, that lasted for a few months. https://www.totse.com/community/showpost.php?p=8134505&postcount=23
https://www.totse.com/community/showpost.php?p=8349927&postcount=16

For those that don't want to read the threads, synopsis: someone said DXM was an opioid, I said that it wasn't, because it showed no affinity for opioid receptors. Then they found a citation that claimed it showed very slight μ-OR affinity, but no one had access to the article. Fast forward a few months, I got in touch with the author, he explained that despite earlier assertions, DXM DOES show very minute μ-OR affinity. To visualize how minute, morphine's affinity is 0.3nM; DXM's affinity for the same receptors is 1,300nM (lower number = higher affinity).

So yeah, DXM can be considered an opioid, if you don't have limits on how low the affinity can be and still count. In which case, salvinorin is an opioid, too...
