Re: old weed?
To avoid this problem either dry your weed out and keep it in an air-tight container, or shove it in the fridge.

Weed generally lasts a few months before it'll go bad. When it goes bad it usually develops mold, goes darker in colour, feels squidgy, and smells rotten. Smoking it is fine, but you'll find it pretty hard to burn and you'll hardly get high at all.

Re: old weed?
You can freeze it too. The strange thing is, it defrosts in about 5 minutes; just make sure you properly dry it, since a lot of the moisture will have frozen, then turned back to liquid when you thaw it, thus, making your weed pretty wet and 'orible. Drying it should solve this.

Freezing weed isn't really that desirable, though, since it will rot much quicker when defrosted (much like anything else you defrost).
