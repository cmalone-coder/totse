Re: Opening laundry mat washing machines?
Like many locks, you'll have to fashion a way to keep tension on it. This can be solved by bending a precision screwdriver (the smallest flathead) 90 degrees. Bend it slowly, and get a very high quality screwdriver, because the cheap ones break.

Now, from the sound of it, the lock your beating is a tubular lock. Keeping tension on it, this lock can be beat with an unbent jumbo paperclip. Keep light to moderate tension on the lock, and push the pins down. If the lock suddenly gives way, you've accomplished the beating of a tubular lock.

This type is used on other things like alarm box control panels, vending machines, gun cabinets, and high security lock boxes. Pretty much anywhere with a high security risk, which is why I figured out to pick it quite quickly...

Have a nice day...
