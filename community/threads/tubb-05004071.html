Re: Laced pot?
I had a panic attack a couple of times, and this was after having had smoked pot for years and years.

It was after my surgery. I had that halo bolted into my head. Four metal rods from that went down and were anchored to a full torso body cast I had.

When I smoked weed, though, I suddenly felt like the body cast was getting tighter and that I couldn't take in enough air to sustain life.

I felt like I had to get out of the body cast and looked for a way to bust it open. It wore off before I could find something to help get me out of it.

The same thing happened the two other times I tried smoking pot while I was in that body cast.

It's a good thing it I never got the body cast off: It would have killed me if I did.

I don't smoke much anymore.

Not because of that panic bit, but because I finally figured out that pot made my pain worse.

I thought that it was all because pot relaxed my muscles, thus not holding the bones of my spine together so much which caused the pain increase [this was backed up by the fact that speed reduces my pain, which I figured was due to it tightening the muscles which then held the bones together better].

I now realize that it doesn't actually make the pain worse so much as it makes me aware of it . Or, better put, it makes me focus on it.

I am normally able to sort of 'bury' a lot of my pain; I've learned to ignore it in a way. I mean it's always there, but somehow I've managed to learn to not notice it. I can't really explain it.

But pot has a way of making you focus intently on something, usually to the exclusion of most other things and for me that focus was my pain.
