There is one red flag that I see in your relationship that you described, and that is her calling you constantly. You never said how long you have been together, but I've had those kinds of girlfriends in the past (before cell phones, and it was STILL a major annoyance) and I can tell you that it gets old quick.

I also agree with the other posters when they discuss about having each other as the ONLY lover's and perhaps 5, 10, 15 years from now one or both of you will have a wandering eye always wondering how other people might be in the sack.

One of the other things I've seen from folks that marry their high school sweethearts is they tend to end up arguing about petty and nonsensical things. Like someone else said, you have to be in a couple of really BAD relationships to fully appreciate the good ones. I know for a fact my marriage wouldn't be so successful now if it wasn't for the psychos I was with previously. I'm not saying you WILL argue about stupid stuff, its just from my experience you tend to overlook the small petty things easier when you've had a rough road with people that had MAJOR issues.

The only thing I would advise you to be wary of is to understand that you ARE still somewhat young...... and your goals, dreams and priorities will evolve over time. I can guarantee in 5 years you won't be the same person you are now, and even moreseo 10 years from now. So while you two may be perfectly compatible NOW, things may change over the course of time once each of you has evolved into that different person. Hopefully you will still be on the same wave with each other, but there is the possibility you won't be....and if that does happen you can't fault either of yourselves because of that. Myself, I look at some of the relationship I was in during my teens and 20's and say...."what the fuck did I do that for?" However I do remember how my thinking and mindset was at THAT time, so I just chalk it up to the journey of life and realize not to be too hard on myself because it was a means to an ends to where I am now.
