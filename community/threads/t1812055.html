There is no one single best technique for meditation. Do whatever works for you. I like to simply relax and let my thoughts flow naturally. "Non-thinking" is not a state of thinking about nothing, it's a loss of ego - there's nothing "standing over your stream of thought with a club," as Bruce Lee put it.

I find that this relaxes me and provides valuable insight into things, but different things work for different people.
