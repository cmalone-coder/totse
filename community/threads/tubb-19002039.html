A visit today.
**sorry no pic's, Ill make it up by writing a detailed story**

Well, today was fun! I finally got to go to my suppliers house, and saw my beloved stash again. I only got to stay there about 2 hours, but it was enough time to do some fun stuff. We started by burning little chunks of white phosphorus. The phosphorus burns with a bright flame, with sputtering sparks. It put out quite a lot of white smoke, and the almond-sized chunk burned for almost 2 minutes! He then showed me how he made silver acetylide (double salts). First, he filled a small glass with about 20ml of white nitric acid (as soon as it was in the cup it started fuming) and then dropped in a silver dime (this guy has many silver coins) it started bubbling but then it stopped. He said the solution needed to be heated to make the reaction to proceed. We placed the glass into a container of hot water, and then the reaction took place. The coin started bubbling, and started turning the solution green, but it started putting out NO2 gas. The NO2 was a thick reddish orange color, but also very toxic! we stood back until the reaction was finished. Once finished the dime was no more, and there was a green liquid. Normally from this point on, you would just bubble acetylene gas through the solution, but we didn't have the glassware (he sold his a while back, and I forgot to bring mine) so to overcome this, he used a creative alternate method. He diluted the liquid with water, and the solution turned blue. He then took a some calcium carbide and wrapped it in toilet paper, and then in tinfoil, with a weight attached to it. He then dropped the package into the blue solution. The water reacted with the calcium carbide, and the package started bubbling acetylene gas, producing the white precipitate; silver acetylide. Unfortunately, we accidently ripped the package apart while removing it, and the spent calcium carbide, mixed into the solution turning it brown. He said �crap, I think we just blew the reaction�. We then still proceeded to filter what we could (doubtful it would work now), and spread the paste out to dry. Once we had taken it out of the filter dried some of it, we tested a small pile (about the size of a sunflower seed) I used my torch and **BAM!!!** it detonated unconfined! I was very surprised because, I am so used to peroxide explosives that just burn with a fireball unconfined. We then lit the used dried filter on fire and the whole filter blew up! I remember always burning my used filters after making AP or HMTD and they just sizzled and made a fireball. I must say, I am very impressed with silver acetylide (and it stores well too! [does not decompose after a week like peroxides do]). It is still a primary explosive, but only moderately sensitive, not very sensitive like AP. With the time I had left, we launched some cans, played with some .22 rounds, and burned some more white phosphorus.
Next time Im there, I promise to have some pic's, and vid's.
