I can't add anything to Streetjusticeforall's list. Just make sure you can steal things effectively. All I have to offer are some tips.

Make sure you read this: http://www.sfgate.com/cgi-bin/articl...BAPB1227KF.DTL

Hospitals are great for the homeless:
If you need a place to sleep in the winter, just make sure you don't smell bad, and go to a hospital. Even small ones have "quiet" waiting rooms designed to let people sleep in, or you can chat up a patient and see if you can sleep in there, grab some food they don't want, put your pack in a drawer, etc. In every room should be a cabinet full of supplies (alcohol pads, antibiotics, etc) you can easily walk in, grab a handful of, and walk out. You can do the same in the emergency room with gauze pads. They also have extremely cheap buffet food. Some waiting rooms also have a public computer and high-speed internet, free phones, a coffee machine, tea, etc. Great places to sleep & have breakfast. And free medical treatment/advice/questions at the clinic.

If you're really hungry, walk into a restaurant where you "sit yourself" and can't easily be seen by employees. Snatch up any tips or food left behind. McDonald's food is disgustingly made, but if you stomach it, they're $0.49 (or $0.89?) are fine if you don't feel like grabbing leftovers.

Wal-Marts are perfect spots to steal & sell shit, and sleep if they have furniture for display on the floor. Befriend some of the workers & they might let you buy shit under age (knives, etc), or vouch for you if you get caught lifting or sleeping.

You can sit inside Starbucks' for as long as you want, they never say anything.


EDIT: Get a good MagLite (the small ones are fine) and chain for your bike.

Re: ITT: What to pack if you were running away
I do a decent bit of hitchhiking and squatting as a cheap form of travel.

My list:

Backpack:

Map(s)
Compass*
Cell/wall-charger (leave it off and just stashed in your pack)*
mp3/wall-charger (works for those really long walks through boring areas)*
Digi-cam/extra memory card/extra battery/charger* or Film camera/rolls of film*
2 extra tshirts
Extra pair of pants
2 pairs of socks
2 pairs of boxers
Sweatshirt/Jacket
about 6ft of paracord (works for shoe laces if needed, and has plenty of other uses, doesn't take up much room)
2x 1L bottles of water
Alcohol/drugs*
Sleeping-bag
Foam bed-roll cut to torso length
Pens/notebooks
Broad-tip marker (making signs)
2x trash compactor garbage bags (cover your bag during rain)
4-6 books of matches and 2 bic lighters
3 Bugler R.Y.O. cigarette packs and extra papers (cheaper and less room taken up)*
Extra fixed blade knife
Fork/spoon or Sporks (raid taco bell for them, they are free, lightweight and usefull)
napkins or rolls of toilet paper from fast-food restaurants
Food (if canned food too, bring a can-opener. P.38 G.I. ones are like $2 for a 5 pack at surplus stores)

Person:
Tshirt
Long-sleeve work-shirt (sleeves rolled and shirt unbuttoned)
pants with good sized pockets
BELT (DON'T FORGET THIS)
Socks
COMFORTABLE BOOTS
Wallet/Cash/ID/Debit*/C.C.*
Knife
Baseball hat (they help)*

*=Depends on the person and the trip

Don't bring shit like laptops or guns, extra weight, pretty useless for the most part.
Public libraries = computers
Guns = more shit if you get stopped by cops
Small crowbar/lockpicks/etc. = no, unless you plan on squatting abandoned buildings, even then, remember cops
