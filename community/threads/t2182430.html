I think it is important to have a basic understanding of what causes 'spiritual' experience on a biological level, before one can make the claim that they have had said experience.

You can still be very spiritual as long as you accept the biological nature of spirituality and not some 'divine' explanation.

This theory is backed by evidence within the book entitled Why God Won't Go Away.

Technically, drugs are a better way of being more spiritual, and I always wondered why modern religions so fervently deny the importance of hallucinogens.
