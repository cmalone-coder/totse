Re: It itches so bad
Every time I take an opiate I get really moody, can someone explain to me why this is?

Do you mean opiod? Because a lot of opiods, codeine and hydrocodone in particular, release a large amount of histamines. So much that quick administration (IV) of them is actually very uncomfortable. Histamines irritate nerve endings (the triggers of itching and sneezing). You took a pretty hefty dose of hydro so it's not surprising, you should have probably taken more benadryl but still not too much because as you probably know it will fuck you up on it's own (in a shitty way). That's why a lot of people look down on hydro and codeine, most people can't really take large doses of it without the cons outweighing the high.

Most opiates don't cause that much itching.

I'm not a doctor though heh, this is just stuff I've gotten from a little research and my drug history.
