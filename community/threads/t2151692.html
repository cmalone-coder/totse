Re: The Mist
Quote:
Originally Posted by slasher_13
They drove around for miles and miles for an undetermined amount of time before finally running out of fuel. Don't you think that after all that you would've thought that maybe the whole world was covered with that fog full of those creatures?
Yeah, exactly. Which is why I thought the military magically appearing a second later (or at all) was just really sloppy writing. It also would have been nice to at least show them stopping at a gas station, or attempting to stop. As is, the movie just presents it as though they are driving in a straight line without any type of basic survival plan.

Even though Stephen King's version had a hopeful ending, I also think it did a great job of simultaneously capturing the hopelessness of the situation. When you read the novella, there's no doubt that the entire region (possibly the entire world) has been consumed by the unstoppable mist. That's was the point behind that insanely huge creature; it wasn't just intended to be a cool monster, but to show that the world had changed beyond repair. So, the book's ending was hopeful in the sense that the characters had a plan and weren't ready to give up, but it also implied that they ultimately were fucked. Overalll, I thought that was a much more logical (in the realm of the story) and chilling conclusion than the movie's ending.

To be clear, I liked most of the movie, but...the ending just ruined it for me. The fact that Darabont was apparently oblivious to the plot inconsistencies it raised also pissed me off.
