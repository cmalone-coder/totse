40g. phenylacetone 200ml alcohol 200 ml 25% ammonia 40g alluminum grit and 0.3g mercuric chloride warmed until reaction takes place stop warming emediately. cool if reaction becomes to violent. reflux with stirring 2 hrs. concentrated vacuum distillation to 200ml. pour into ice water alkinized with 120 g. potasium hydroxide. extract with ether. the extractions are treated with 20% hcl the resulting water layer extracted with 150 ml. ether organic layer dried over sodium sulfate the ether evaporated and the residue distilled in vacuum. yield 12.5 g. 99% pure amphetamine sulfate
