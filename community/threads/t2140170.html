"Dressing up" for nightlife.
Just an observation I've made lately (well, months ago to be honest) but it seems that everyone is wearing the same kinda shit when they go out for a night. You know, weekend wear. Now, everyone tries to dress a little nicer, pull out a nice top, or some decent jackets etc, so you look a bit swish. However, everyone (I'm in England) if you go out in the town seems to wear:

Shirt - More often than not white with a pattern, or ergh, a light pink/pastel color.

Dress pants or "desginer" jeans, the ones that you can tell cost a lot. Stupid prefade on them etc.

Diamond stud earings are popular although personaly I think they make you look likea fag.

Leather belt with large, designed buckle.

Shoes.


Now, don't get me wrong. A combination of some of the above items does look very smart. It's clean cut, etc. Thing is, with EVERYONE wearing the same kinda stuff it's horrifying. The type of people wearing this stuff cheapens it too. I don't want to be 1) wearing the same thing as everyone in the room and; 2) wearing the same thing as these loud-mouthed degenerates.

So, here's the dilema!

What can I/you wear out for an evening that still looks "good" but doesnn't leave you looking like everyone else? Oviously wearing something too out there just draws attention. Living in an area/country where most people are cavemen this is nmot a good idea, as you'll just get abuse. Leery comments etc. It's never really bothered me so much, just wondering what else there is to wear, and if you guys have encountered the same thing at all? What do you wear out?
