Okay, drop the shrooms first. This is a MUST!

Ahhh hippyflips, my first love :)

The most important thing to remember is to take the E WHILE (or shortly after) you are peaking! Then they both wear off around the same time, but most importantly, the shrooms are done by the time you get any 'comedown' effects from the E.

AND DONT FORGET TO DRINK! you will definately be moving around more than with a normal shroom trip, so its important to keep this in mind.

Try to be outside - I did mine inside because it was nighttime in the winter, but seriously, outside in the day(or even night) will make it better 10-fold. You wont need trip toys - moving around feels so good thats all you will want to do :)
