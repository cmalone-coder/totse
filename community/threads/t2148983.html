And if it's some DOx chemical instead (another psychedelic that can fit on blotter and is sometimes sold as LSD) then the trip may last a while longer than just 12 hours. All the shit I've gotten in the past has been LSD, though.

No, that's not really going to help. In fact. it might make it worse. I find when coming down off of LSD or mushrooms or something, I can smoke a bowl and I feel like I'm quasi-tripping again. It has a serious weed/stony flavor that dominates the experience, but part of my trip is sort of brought back by the weed. Maybe if you were taking xanax to fall asleep, but instead I'd recommend waiting. Last minute tripping always tends to be not as good.

Favorite things to do are hikes in a forest or something, bring a backpack with food/water/etc, and dosing and tripping in the middle of the forest. My best trips have been miles away from society. Solo or with friends, either way it tends to become something of a journey. Way more profound than just sitting around a buddies house and giggling at the TV because I'm on LSD and everything seems insane. Pristine places are really nice. I'd recommend having something fun to do on it. Like really make an evening of it, while leaving some things open enough that you can ditch whatever plans if you feel like it. It's not that LSD isn't interesting, it can just mean the difference between a mediocre trip and a fucking amazing trip. It's worth it putting in the extra effort to make sure it's really meaningful or whatever.
