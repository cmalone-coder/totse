Re: Rave, Extacy, Adderall
So if I start the SSRI the day after the concert, like while im in my E hangover, it'll reduce possible brain damage?

Ideally, right at comedown...

Neuron death forms when the serotonin reuptake transporter starts transporting dopamine instead, usually as a result of serotonin depletion. Serotonin reuptake inhibitors, such as the SSRIs (or MDMA itself, actually), prevent... well, anything... from being drawn back up into the cell, which... well, prevents dopamine from being drawn into serotonergic terminals.

So, yeah. It's helpful. As for SSRIs + 5HTP... it's [u]usually[/i] safe. Dosage is meaningful to the saftey margin, but there's a degree of tryptophan in food already (thankfully)... and if you're just starting, you're on the low end of the dosage spectrum. Citalopram is a bit more of a concern than most due to its rapid uptake, but... if your doctor didn't give you a list of restricted high-tryptophan foods, it should be alright.

Tryptophan and 5HTP prevent peroxidization by making serotonin avaliable to the reuptake transporter, btw...
