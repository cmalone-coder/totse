As one of the (I'd think only) badge wearing Totse mod on the forum, I'll give you all one big piece of advice:

"Does it break the law?"
Thats what you should be asking yourselves. Take a look at whatever is in question and ask yourselves that. Remember that no information, unless either held by a company as a trademarked "secret" or government held classified information, is illegal to release to the public. I think those are the only limitations, but I may be missing some.

Also remember that unless something illegal is being hosted on the servers that run this website, its not our liability. If 'dudebro6969' posts a link to his website that hosts child porn, warez, and snuff films exclusively, hosted within the US, then thats his problem, not ours. Law enforcement can threaten us all they want, but the most they'll ask of us is to take that link down, and if the problem persists, we can refer the law enforcement (to our discretion) the offending user's information so they can take action on them.

Remember, you wouldn't hold Home Depot liable if a murder is committed with a hammer.
