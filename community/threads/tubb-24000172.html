Cheezdip, I've just thought of something: try looking for a 1970's porsche 914/6. inexpensive, and quite snappy little things, they're mid-engined (which makes for good handling) and is not overpowered. it's only got about 95 HP, but it weighs only about 1800 ibs, so it can still haul ass if you know how to drive it. it's still quite easy to drive slowly, though, and it's the best way to learn how to drive a stick. also, this'll give your dad a chance to fix it up, cause you'll probably find one in rough condition.

a little background: porsche introduced the 914 as a sports car anyone could afford. sure enough, teenagers of the day were able to buy it, due to the small, lightweight engine and size, and the small amount of power. racing versions with over 130 HP were developed, but you had to be a dedicated racer to get one. however, they weren't too popular, because the body style didn't fit with the styles of the day (read: they thought it was ugly). porsche lovers didn't like it because it was so inexpensive and the complete opposite of what porsche was SUPPOSED to be, and thus it didn't sell well.

to this day, however, upon close inspection by someone who knows his shit, it's found to be highly underrated. it's not that ugly, in fact you could say it's "cute". it's easy to modify, with it's volkswagen engine, and has great balance and handling. it's also rear drive, a definete plus.
