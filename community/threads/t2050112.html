Regarding the 'Close threads after 10 pages' thing, and am I doing this okay?
Hey all. I remember when Jeff wrote this:

Originally posted by Jeff Hunter:
The Shoplifting Thread is also getting a large number of hits.
For now the best thing you can do is to split large threads up into multiple smaller threads (10 pages or fewer). If you see a thread getting large, close it, sticky it, and open a new one (Part 2, or 8 as the case may be).
I'm looking at installing more memory and using Squid to cache pages, which should take a large load off the database, especially in cases where a thread is hugely popular. In the meantime, try to keep threads to less than 10 pages.


So far, I've been sticking to this pretty much. The bioshock threads, the photo threads, the..having sex with dolphin threads..


But I've a few concerns, and I was hoping that you guys would be able to give some of your good advice which I appreciate so much.


1-Some people can not grasp that I'm doing this because Jeff has requested it. I'm getting alot more hatemail, despite the fact I explain why I close each thread and express how people are very welcome to make a new one.

2- Unless something has changed, there's not a method of archiving threads, is there?
This means that when I close the old thread and someone makes an new one, the old one gets bumped down and off the page and eventually dissapears.
This is a terrible thing, as alot of these threads are informative and awesome. It seems bad to obliterate such vital sources of information.

I know the suggestion of sticking the old threads has been made, but really, has anyone seen how many stickies there are in all the forums allready?

I dont feel that cluttering up the first page of a forum with more and more stickies would be good, especially considering how fast some of the big threads grow.

In S&A the photo thread is getting massive again, as this sort of thread tends to, and I can foresee, if I sticky each one, there's soon going to be "photo thread V.18 " and the likes.
I'm wary of doing things in forums that have more active mods, because I feel that it's stepping on toes somewhat to mess around with a thread that another mod has made and stickied.

I'm not sure what to do with "The cigar thread" in OI, it's brilliant and informative, and if I remove that thread then the chances are all that sage wisdom will be lost. Also, it was a thread made and stickied by a previous mod of that forum and I feel it would be..rude?.. aggressive..? To remove it.


Could anyone advise me on the correct procedure to get around the problems I've mentioned, please?
And advice?


Any word from Jeff on progress with using Squid to cache stuff at all?
