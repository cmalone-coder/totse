Re: Help with mushroom growing.
Hypothetically one would.....

Get some P. Cubensis, they're the easiest to grow. I'de go with something like B+ as its fairly common and forgiving.

All those 'substrates' you listed suck. They're not subrtates either. a substrate simply needs to lock in moisture and provide the proper nutrients, the thigns you listed can be used for casing though [aside from dog shit]. Using soil I believe is difficult though, dog shit isn't going to do anything, and cow shit is also difficult for beginners. For substrate and growing method just go with the PF-Tek [http://www.fungifun.com/pf/pf_en.htm] as its very easy. The substrate is just for the mycelium growth stage, after that you must move on to fruiting in which case I suggest a straight vermiculite casing. A lot of beginners think casings will be hard so they just fruit right off the cake [cake = mycelium covered substrate] which still works but effects the yield. You'll yield more with a vermiculite casing then you will right off the cake, and its really easy to do.

- www.shroomery.org (http://www.shroomery.org)

Read a LOT before even ordering the syringes. Basically, you should be able to resite your plan backwards and forwards, and have all the equitment you need before ever touching a syringe. Growing mushrooms is fairly easy, but thats doesn't mean you should do a half assed job of it.
