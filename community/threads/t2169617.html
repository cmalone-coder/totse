Your link quality looks OK, but at -60 dB your shit is not going to stay connected for long. Try something very simple, move closer to your wireless access point, and run iwconfig again, post your results or if you get this same reading of -60 dB Signal Level, take the interface down then bring it back up again, in Fedora systems it is: ifdown wlan0 ; ifup wlan0

Your system may or may not have ifup/ifdown.
Post your results.
