In some ways, yes. I have had closer relationships since then, but for the first time I did not have the experience to handle the pain or to see the end coming. I could not see the light at the end of the tunnel because getting thru something like that is something you have to experience for yourself. You can get guidance from someone, but it is not the same thing. Being able to handle a broken heart situation is a skill like any other. It is important to learn how to do it, because otherwise fear of it will prevent you from opening up again. There are some that never open up and never give themselves a chance to feel a broken heart, so they do not mature in this area of their lives and they are not capable of playing for keeps. I think that is sad, but if it works for someone, that is their call.
