Love Makes You Do Stupid Things (long story)
Just to start off I recently decided to tell my girlfriend that i love her, and so i decided to tell her this after school yesterday. I live in toronto, and in case you dont know, toronto had a bitching snow storm yesterday.

It all started when i was trying to get over there by bus stupidly. My first of two busses to take was full, so i said 'screw this' and started running to the other stop since its REALLY close and the bus was going slowly anyways. I get to the other stop and theres a big lineup of people, i asked a woman there how long she'd been waiting and she said 30 minutes, but luckily a bus came 10 minutes of icey snow pelting on my face later.

So... the bus starts to drive.. and then stops, as it is blocked by traffic, it took me a good 20 minutes to go 2 stops, i wouldve walked faster... great. Eventually the bus driver goes on his intercom thing and tells everyone dispatch told him that we have to turn this bus AROUND and that he'll give us transfers to get onto another bus.. WTF! So im off the bus and in the snowstorm again with nothing to look at but all of the traffic.

Now, heres where it gets stupid, I love this girl so much, that i decide to RUN (well... jog) for about 15 stops which took me a good 30 minutes (for torontonians, i ran from the laird and eglinton canadian tire to yonge and eglinton). I finnally get to the where we're supposed to meet (and the busses i passed on the way are nowhere to be found), and find out that she was waiting for me in a bank, which is also coincidentally where I tell her that i love her and got it back.

SUMMARY:

Im stupid. I pretty ran through a snowstorm for 30 minutes to tell my girlfriend that I love her.
