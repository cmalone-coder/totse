Re: Stereo System vs Computer
Yes, that will work IF you have an Aux setting on the stereo. Also you have to take into consideration that your stereo is meant for music not gaming and pc sounds, take the sub woofer for instance: On a pc set the subwoofers are made to be felt not always heard. On a stereo they are meant to be heard as the bass of the music. I would recomend using a pc system unless you are tight on cash or space. I use a stereo just cause i dont have much space. But a pc system is made for a pc and will suit you much better.

So think about what you are doing before you buy a new system (if thats what you planned on doing).

Re: Stereo System vs Computer
This is assuming you just want to play mp3s on you home stereo...

With at least a network adapter and crossover cable (preferably a home network) you can use this to play mp3s on your stereo system (assuming you have RCA inputs on your stereo):

http://www.slimdevices.com/

I'm considering buying one... the advantage is that your stereo can be located in a different part of your house than the PC hosting the mp3 collection.

If you are just attempting to play audio from your sound card through your stereo, I would recommend a Sound Blaster Live or Audigy Platinum edition, which gives you a lot more control over the ways in which you can manipulate sound output from your system. Figure out what you want to do and do some research! Hopefully this gives you some ideas.
