Re: What is a Valentine?
Get her something that means something to her. Spending £50 on roses won't mean as much as spending £1.50 on her favourite type of flowers. And that's applicable to anything. Make it something meaningful to her, not something generic. Make a mental note of things she says she likes or wants, and surprise her with them months later. Doesn't need to be for any special occasion; try giving gifts a few days before Valentine's day

EDIT: Never buy something just for the sake of buying a gift. And if you want to get her a CD from her favourite band, why not try to get a signed one or an old demo or something? As long as it's not a huge band, if you sent them a letter asking for autographs for your girlfriend, who's a huge fan, I'm sure they'd be more than happy to sign them. You've got a while, it's worth a shot and she'd love it.
