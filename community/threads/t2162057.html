Re: opiate/antihistimine mixing and dose?
Tussionex ER: Each 5ml dose contains 10mg Hydrocodone/8mg Chlorpheniramine

First off, what is your tolerance like?

For the syrup I'd suggest taking just a swig and giving it about 10 minutes if you've got a relatively low tolerance. The Chlorpheniramine shouldn't be a problem being mixed with benadryl but I'm unsure exactly why you'd want to do so. All that is noted about mixing with Diphenhydramine is that you should consult a doctor beforehand..meaning that it isn't generally dangerous but is most likely suggested to prevent the stacking of medications that cause drowsiness. If it's for the itch relief I'm pretty sure the Chlorpheniramine in the sizzurp should handle that for you alone.

I'm also curious as to why you'd want to add Dex into the mix. I'm pretty sure that wouldn't be a problem (namely in the small doses) but I wouldn't suggest trying to hit a plateau with the stuff.

As far as your buddies syrup it is slightly similar to yours. His is a weaker form of opiate and phenergan which is a medication that causes drowsiness and relieves upset stomach and nausea. Dosing with it would be similar to what I stated above but you may want to take into consideration that Phenergan generally makes people quite a bit more drowsy than an antihistamine will and considering codeine is quite a bit weaker you'll need more of the syrup to get the desired effects unless you've got a relatively low tolerance.

Hope this helps.

The Codeine solution has 10mg of Codeine and 6.25mg of Promethazine per 5ml.

Whats your tolerance like? No tolerance? Try 10-20mg of the Hydrocodone solution or 50-60mg of the Codeine solution.
If you have a tolerance, then you already know what you are doing and should dose accordingly.

I would just down the proper amount of solution and chase it with some soda or something, but if you must make this stuff, mix the syrup with some of your favorite soda (or use traditional Sprite) and add a Jolly Rancher candy. Mix up and drink.
