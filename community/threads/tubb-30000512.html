nihilism
This is a pretty vague thing to be angry about, so it's kind of hard to put into words, but here goes. It seems like real intelligence is being killed of by sarcasm and cheap cynism, which all boils down to nihilism. Intelligence to me (so far as communication goes anyway) is the ability to convey enlightenment, insight, complexity or just plain sense in your idea's. But sadly, all I see among so called
"intellectuals" around my age, and even on this site (not pointing any fingers, you should be able to spot it out yourself), is sarcasm and cynism. People think that they're being really smart when they make a short, witty attack on something, be it someones idea, or the state of politics somewhere, whatever. The worst part is that this actually works, and these people if they've mastered the arts of acting cynical and jaded (which aren't very tough to learn) well enough, actually gain listeners! This furthers their ego, and they keep on spouting cynical arrogant trash.

It's easy to look at things from the most negative angle possible, and critisize the way the things are. What impresses me is those who find it in themselves to have a little faith. These are the people who, having some goals to attain, and something to strive for, actually think of solutions to problems rather than dwelling on them.

Well forgive my incoherent babble. It was just a thought. I'll leave you with a quote, which I'm pretty sure is Dostoyevsky.
"nihilism is the servility of thought".
