It's All Over But For The Crying
I'm not the best boyfriend. I don't tend to make the grand elaborate gestures, I grope on her too much, and I know it. I make up crazy stories about my youth, and pass it off as truth (just to amuse her).

But really, I think I do pretty good. I call her often enough, every few days. I tell her that I love her. I'll listen when she has problems and not just nod my head and go "Uh huh. Uh huh." I never, never even try to pressure her into sex, no matter how horny I may be.

I have no doubt that even though we've been doing the long distance thing for a while, she's been completely faithful. But...

She never calls me. Ok, she called me on July 1st (the day before my dog died, that's how I remember), but since then she only calls to return mine. She almost never answers her phone, and won't return my calls for weeks at a time. I just feel that she doesn't care about me nearly as much as I care about her.

I know we'd do better if we saw each other more often, but how much, I don't know. We've broken up 3 times in the past 5 years, but always get back together.

I've talked to her about it, and let her know that unless something changes, we just can't be together. She was better for a little while, but we're back to where she says she's too busy with school stuff and stuff at her co-op to call me. How can you be that busy for weeks on end?

I'm inclined to give her one more chance, and make it clear that it's her last chance, to keep me in a relationship. I'll never stop being friends with her, but being together isn't working right now.

I don't relish being single again. I have a hell of a time finding women who will put up with my flaws long enough to see the good part (my flaws are very much on the surface, while the good part of me takes a while to come out). I can be charming for a few hours, but since I don't do the one night stand thing, that's worthless with getting chicks. Honesly, I hate being single.

Yet, I'd prefer it to what I have now. At least then there will be the remote hope of finding someone who wants to be with me for serious.

So what do you guys think? Should I break up with her now, give her the one last chance, or what?

As an aside, there's another woman who finds me facinating and thinks I'm a catch (go figure), but she lives several hundred miles away, so she's not really a viable prospect.
