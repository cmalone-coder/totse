Six months... six months in a week and two days. February 6th, 2003. Six months... and it's gone by so fast. I don't want it to go by this fast. I want it to be like it was when I was still in school, where every single waking moment was an enviable eternity of bliss. Last I knew, it was May, and now, here it is, damn near August, and I don't know where the fuck the time went.

I have a lot of thinking to do. A lot of thinking about how to deal with this hurting to which I've been subjected. I don't like it, and I don't want it anymore. I just want things back to the way they were. I want to feel the love that I know is there. The early events these past few days proves that it's there, especially what she said to me while we were trying to fall asleep. Even the first day of Chicago was a blessing. It was so much fun, even though both Katti and I were on a money and time budget and couldn't do all the things that we wanted to do. The second day fell apart right from the beginning. I bought tickets at the Field Museum so we could see "Eternal Egypt", which we missed because they had an exhibit downstairs that had damn near the same name. The tickets didn't cost nearly as much as the admission, so, monetarily speaking, I didn't think it was that much of a loss. I felt really bad, however, because we did miss it. The next thing we decided to do was go to the art institute. We had been carrying around my backpack and her duffel bag since we had checked out. The both the Field museum and the art institute had bag and coat checks. The difference was that a) the Field museum let you in without checking your stuff, and b) the Field museum let you check duffel bags. Neither of these were true for the art institute. Katti got mad, and rightfully so. But, the way she was acting, I felt like she was directing all her anger at me. Venting is one thing; this was not venting. All the suggestions I came up with (and there were only a few options) seemed to make her even more angry, so I simply gave up talking to her. On the train ride home, she seemed to distance herself from me, even though she did let me rub her back a few times. On the car ride home, we barely said anything to each other as I slowly crept into another bout of depression. When we got back to her house, she gave me a hug, I said "I love you", and she said something that wasn't "I love you", but I wasn't able to understand what.

I called Shawn and said I needed to talk to him as soon as I got back home because I was depressed. I hung up the phone and supressed the tears all the way to my house.

Things happen, yes. But this has been the start of a breaking point for me. It seems like this has happened almost every time we've seen each other recently. Everytime, it's left what feels like the weight of a semi on my mind, and me without a means to relieve it. It's made me have unpleasant dreams, and it's made me stay awake all night. It's made me hurt, emotionally and physically. When I went to see the doctor about my cyst this morning, he told me I was developing a stress-related ulcer and wrote a prescription for a sedative called Xanax (It's kind of funny: the Xanax bottle has a big warning label on it that says "This product may be additctive or fatal if dosage is exceeded"). He said that the ulcer was too small to be considered an "ulcer" and should take care of itself, and the Xanax would help get rid of a lot of stress. He then told me to stop taking the Vicodin because it would only make the ulcer full-blown. So now I have this $70 prescription that, thankfully, is covered by insurance, and a problem I still have to solve. I know that not all of the stress is caused by her, but she is a part of it.

I've changed so much of myself to keep her happy. For starters, I've completely changed the way I dress. I wear clothes that I normally wouldn't wear. I bought a pair of shorts at The Gap. THE GAP! I've all but stopped drinking. But... it seems to me that it's not enough. She still gets mad at me for things that I have no idea about, and in turn, it makes me upset... especially when I don't get to see her for weeks at a time. The silent treatment... I hate it more than anything... (To quote comedian Adam Ferrara: "If you don't know what you did, then I'm not going to tell you." "Fine. Then don't be surprised when this shit happens again.") The fact that I don't have the money to give her all the things she wants doesn't help matters, either. I'm starting to think that I'll never be good enough for this girl.

Apparently, I can have just about any girl I want. I've had girls try to flirt with me and hit on me in the mall, and... in the mall. The girl at the Gap in Rivertown Crossings is a perfect example. But not the only example. I've tried to explain to them in a friendly manner that I already have a girlfriend, but when they push, I revert to flipping the bird and saying "Step off, bitch! I'm taken!" If I wasn't already in love with Katti, oh where I'd be now.

I know what everyone is thinking. "So why don't you just break up with her?"

I don't want to break up with her. Like I said, I know the love is there. I can feel it. Because of her, I've walked in dreams... and I knew I wasn't dreaming. I want to keep her with me so bad, because I can make her feel like earth IS heaven. I can be the best thing in her life.

I don't know if she believes me, but I've told her I love her, many many times. I love that girl beyond the point of it being healthy. I want things to work out with her... not anyone else... I want Katti... I would rot and die without her.

Time for me to do some thinking (and drinking) to find the solution... because I know it's there.

Any advice you guys have would really help me out here...
