One of the biggest things I have learned on 2c-e that has actually helped my sober life is the concept of percptions and misinterpertations.

In a nut shell:

Anything that anyone does or says can and will be percieved and (mis)interpertated in any number of ways. This can lead to good or bad, obviously.

Depending on the person, they will tend to interpert things like they have learned to live their life through their experiences.

Personally, I am very pessimistic, untrusting and paranoid. If someone says something I could easily percieve it as them trying to call me out or get me introuble if they don't say it correctly. Obviously I have no control over other people and I'm not going to ask everyone everyware I go to make sure they are very very clear while talking to me.

I now know that I have a habit of misinterperting people and to not take anything personally, ever. If I think someone is starting bullshit, I'll laugh. If they are starting shit (they probally aren't, I'm just misinterperting it) they will obviously make it clear if I laugh.

The same works w/ other people, if I know someone has very high confidence and likes to laugh alot, I can joke w/ them easily and they will most likely not misinterpert that I am making fun of them. If they have low self confidence/been molested, I could be misinterperted as that so I have to make sure to say "just kiddding" or "I'm the same way" or something gay like that.

Also, the way we percieve things has GREAT control over our emotions. If I go thru life thinking that "the man" is trying to "keep me down" (I don't, I'm using this as an example) I will feel hatred and anger. If I go thru life thinking the man is trying to protect me and may be over protective, I will feel secure and intelligent in the fact that a) I'm being protected and b) I'm getting around the system.

Anyone can choose to percieve any situation any way. If I was jacked by someone, I can percieve it as "wow I'm a bitch I lost my money this sucks" or I can percieve it as a learning experience "never come to a drug deal w/ a random drug dealer drunk and high"

I cant fully explain what I've thought but this has helped me HUGE.
