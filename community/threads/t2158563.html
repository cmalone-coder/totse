My Thanks To Acid (long)
Okay, first of all, lemme just say that in the middle of august, I went to a massive, three day long music festival. Five stages, music almost twenty four hours a day, and tons of drugs; more of a rave, really. Anyways, I'd wanted to go to it for awhile cause my bro always talked about how sweet it is, and this was the first year i was able to go.

Before I went to it, I'd always been a very reserved, kinda loner sorta guy. I had a few friends and I'd sorta chit chat with everyone at school, but I'd never let anyone really get to know me. Why? Because i was scared, I guess. Scared of what? I dunno. Rejection, embarassment, humiliation, I suppose. I'd always sit at home on the weekends, by myself, lonely and miserable. Depression runs in my family, and that's what would always keep me from letting people know the real me. I'd put on an act for everyone.

So I go to this rave, and on the third night (we went a day early) I dropped a capsule of MDMA. About an hour later, my bro hooked me up with some acid. After awhile it kicked in, and it was really fun and funny. My gum turned to gooey liquid and melted into my gums. That was just the start of a soon to be life altering experience.

At first I thought acid was just something to make you feel funny, but as the night progressed, I explored the inner realms of my mind more and more. I was thinking about stuff I'd always locked away in the back of my brain. Stuff i'd never wanted to ever think about, ever; that's why it was locked up. Soon, i couldn't stop thinking about this stuff, it just wouldn't go away! The rest of the night is kind of a blur.

So fast forward a little while, I really learnt alot from that trip. People aren't gonna come and be my friend, i've gotta go make friends with them. Nobody's gonna wanna be friends with a stupid little twat that sits at home all day wondering why he's so miserable. That guy isn't going to be a very good friend.

So I decided to turn my life completely around, go meet people, be happy, look on the positive side, have fun, and most of all: do whatever the fuck i wanna do. If my few friends don't like it, they can fuck right off. I'm gonna be me, whether people like it or not and so far, it's been working pretty well. I'm not even sure if i ever want to take acid again, because, well, that was such a great experience and changed me so much, that I think that should be my experience with acid. I know I'll probably dose up again, but probably not for awhile.

I was drug out of depression, and turned my life around completely (so far atleast), all because of one little piece of paper, soaked with acid. Thank you.
