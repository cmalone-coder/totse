Re: Where is my bottleneck??
Quote:
Originally Posted by SephirothAngelus
Are the C2D's better than the AMD X2's? How do I determine a mobo has a good chipset for gaming? Am I looking for bus speed, l2 or l1 cache or what?
A multicore CPU (like the Core2 Duo) will only help if the game supports multi-threading, or if (as in your case) you want to run other programs at the same time.
As for the mobo, just get one that supports the parts you want to put on it.

Quote:
Does buying good RAM really matter vs. El Cheapo?
Not unless you plan to overclock a lot.

Quote:
What's better, two cheap graphics cards in SLI mode or one more expensive graphics card?
One more expensive card.

Quote:
What types of internal peripheral data transfer come with new PCs? SCSI? What's SATA? What is best?
SATA is Serial ATA. It's the current standard for most storage devices. While SCSI is somewhat faster, it's not as widely supported as SATA.

These are, of course, just my opinions. Other people will disagree with me.

Quote:

A multicore CPU (like the Core2 Duo) will only help if the game supports multi-threading, or if (as in your case) you want to run other programs at the same time.
Multi core would be better for everyone, the OS needs to process stuff in the background all the time, and then you have anti-virus, etc.

Quote:
As for the mobo, just get one that supports the parts you want to put on it.
Mainly the right CPU socket. Basically this is how the cpu connects to the motherboard. Also don't even bother with an AGP port.
