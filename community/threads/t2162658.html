*First time with LSD

The problem with on campus is that I just transferred so I am much more comfortable around my friend from home with the apartment, also I would be concerned about becoming bored as theres really not much to do. I planned on mostly trying to focus on self-evaluation, would it be possible that I could entertain myself with just that for a significant period of time? Any suggestions would be appreciated. Re: *First time with LSD
If you've never actually tripped I suggest you don't get too overconfident going into this. If those 8-10 tabs are even middle-of-the-road potency wise you'll most likely be smashed and will achieve ego loss. How intense or overwhelming it's going to be isn't really going to be up to you in a very significant way.

Make sure you're in a place you're very comfortable..so I'd suggest avoiding the campus if at all possible due to the fact you aren't quite at home there yet. An outdoor setting somewhere peaceful where you can see the stars would be ideal but if you take this route be sure to bring someone to watch over you. Also, the fact that your friend has nothing to do at his pad doesn't make a fuck. When you're tripping you won't be bored..trust me. I would however avoid that area due to the fact that you said it's not very safe.

Best bet:
Outdoors somewhere secluded and peaceful
GREAT mindset throughout the day or two before the trip
Having a few benzos on hand in case you do completely freak out wouldn't hurt..but isn't required.

Stay safe dude.
