One time I got fucked though- someone had tied a small string on the end of the rope so it would be easier to pull up to the tree, and when I was swinging it wrapped around my leg. I didn't notice in time and let go, and was promptly jerked back upside down, hanging from my leg. I got crazy bad rope burn. Since then the town has cut off all the good branches and the ladder to get up, so no more fun times at the lake. In texas visiting my friend I jumped off a 30' cliff, which my friends dad dove off. I've never really figured out how to track them down, but I got a car and lots of free time this summer so I'd love to find some places.

Re: Cliff jumping
A couple months back I went back home for a week to see old friends and see my family. Well, a couple of my buddies and I ended up kayaking in a pretty big creek, Soldier Creek. A couple miles down, we passed this bridge and that sparked an idea. We wanted to jump from a bridge into the creek.

A couple more miles down and we were in deep water and some yards ahead we saw a bridge. To be honest with you this would be my first time jumping that high down to a creek or anything in a long ass time, and I was nervous. We swam around to see if it was deep enough and it was. So we all headed up onto the bridge and I was nervous as fuck, so I let them go first. One went at a time doing front-flips, back-flips, other stuff and then it was my turn. First thing I did I looked down the bridge and it looked like it was going to be a long ass drop. I hesitated a couple times and then I said fuck it. Jumped and it was one of the best feelings ever. I've really missed that feeling. Pretty good feeling.

Not exactly cliff jumping but fun as cliff jumping.

Re: Cliff jumping
That malibu video sucks. Dudes have awful form that can/will end up getting them injured.

Up here in my corner of the NE we are all about our quarries, there's like 5 spots within 20 minutes with up to 100 foot drops. Biggest jump for me thus far has been 65 feet (measured). That height is already pretty scary, something I only did twice (plus I have vertigo and fear of heights haha) I usually wear light shoes to break the water better. If you fuck up your form above 50ish feet you WILL get hurt, mildly or otherwise. I've seen it from a healthy body-slap to a dude breaking his neck diving a 48 footer (idiot....no sympathy).
