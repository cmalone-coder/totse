Hot buttered rum

* 1 stick unsalted butter, softened
* 2 cups light brown sugar
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1/2 teaspoon grated nutmeg
* Pinch ground cloves
* Pinch salt
* Fifth of dark rum
* Boiling water

In a bowl, cream together the butter, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and salt. Refrigerate until almost firm. Spoon about 2 tablespoons of the butter mixture into 12 small mugs. Pour about 3 ounces of rum into each mug (filling about halfway). Top with boiling water (to fill the remaining half), stir well, and serve immediately.

Taken from a thread a made ages ago. It's not strictly a hot drink, but put more alcohol in it and get blasted. Or serve it warmed through, Eggnog:

Eggs + heavy cream + milk + bourbon + nutmeg. Keeps you warm (ok, so the booze doesn't ACTUALLY keep you warm) and helps you put on a protective layer of bulk in the long run for the upcoming winter months. It's got protein, healthy fats.... not-so-healthy fats and booze!

You'll need:

4 egg yolks.
1/3 cup sugar, plus 1 tablespoon.
1 pint whole milk.
1 cup heavy cream.
6 ounces bourbon (I used wild turkey, and while it sounds like very little, the flavor of wild turkey goes far).
1 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg.
4 egg whites.
1 Glass.

In the bowl of a stand mixer, beat the egg yolks until they lighten in color. Gradually add the 1/3 cup sugar and continue to beat until it is completely dissolved. Add the milk, cream, 3 ounces of bourbon, nutmeg and stir to combine.

After doing this combine the remaining bourbon with the glass. Drink.

Place the egg whites in the bowl of a stand mixer and beat to soft peaks. With the mixer still running gradually add the 1 tablespoon of sugar and beat until stiff peaks form. Whisk the egg whites into the mixture.

Chill, serve and enjoy.
