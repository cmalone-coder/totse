Drugs + Creative Writing
It being summer, and me wanting to be a creative writing major and being a avid drug user, I was suprised the other day when I figured the two might go together. I recently started reading Hunter S Thompson, who as most of you probably know, was a awsome writer who wrote about his experiences on acid, mescaline, etc.

I'm thinking of trying to do that kind of style, not imitate it, but branch from it, and was wondering if any of you have experience with writing while intoxicated. A idea I thought of would be to use a tape recorder to capture whatever thought pops up in my head, and type it later, and string it together, to prevent the buzzkill of sitting down and going on a computer during a trip.

Good idea? Experiences? What kind of drugs would be most interesting to record my thoughts on?
