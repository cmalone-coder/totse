Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. quote:Originally posted by LeggoEggo:

Yea, but let's say if I had made an electric timer, then hooked up to a solar fuse and stuck it into a salute, then set it, and went away. That'd be safe, no?

Yes, there are safe ways to set off pyrotechnics and detonate explosives. Pictures like those are just precautions of what COULD result from carelessness, or a freak accident.

Remember, MOST pyrotechnics or explosives related injuries are a result of doing something stupid. For instance, what the fuck was that kid doing putting an explosive in his mouth in the first place?

It's like this. A lot of people blow their heads and/or limbs off with firearms every year. Does that mean it's completely unsafe to own a firearm? No, because 99.9999% of the time those people were doing something incredibly stupid. Now with pyrotechnics and explosives, a freak accident could occur and something could be ignited prematurely, which might injure you. But then again something might accidentally trip the hammer on a shotgun when you're holding it.

In general, 1) Don't do anything stupid, and 2) Don't do anything dangerous unless you're SURE you know EXACTLY what you're doing and you are aware of the potential consequences.

Following those rules, I have never been injured or arrested due to experimenting with pyrotechnics and explosives.
