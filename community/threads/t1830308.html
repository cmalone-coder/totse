Even with my largest pressure bomb (over a litre of HCl acid) it still took about 20 seconds before anything started 'reacting'. I've never had to wait anymore than about 40 seconds, but make sure you fill the bottle up with at least 1/4 of acid IMO.

For the aluminium, just rip up some foil and crunch it into littles balls so they can fit thorugh the neck, you'll genreally want the acid just covering the top of them.

Pressure bombs are great and easy to make, you can always fiddle around with chems - anything that releases a great deal of gas will generally work (dry ice etc).

I've never used a vingar/baking soda one myself, but kmno4/glycerin and HCl/Aluminium would be the best, both are pretty different though.

Some people ask me if the HCl acid shoots everywhere in the 'explosion', but I've never seen it go beyond two metres so its a fairly safe device (don't inhale the fumes though).

I've also found if you have ungodly amounts of reactants, you can actually make a fairly good smoke bomb from the HCl one.

Never do a pressure bomb in a crowded city or sometimes even suburban area, as they are *extremely* loud.

And of course, even though there is usually a good safety margin get rid of any pressure bomb as soon as you can.
