Scraping money from the belly of society...
Over the last few years, I've realized that money is literally everywhere to be had. Those who have no money are not helpless and poor, they are simply not innovative or ambitious enough to find it. Don't get me wrong, I work my ass off at a job I love, and I earn a (semi)legitimate income. But there's nothing wrong with wanting a little more, and also getting satisfaction from having virtually free money. All you have to do is track it down.

Our economy is full of little cracks where money slips into nowhere, and while this may not be very significant to some, it all adds up in the long run. These "cracks" cut into every facet of our life, and listing all of them may well be impossible. One easy example would be to recycle items for the deposit. Often times I find myself getting up at 4:15 in the morning to drive around residential neighborhoods and steal their beer cans and water bottles. If I hustle, I can make about $10.00/hr cash all said and done, which is $2.00 over minimum in my area. That's nothing to sneeze at, especially to those who don't even have a job. If you don't mind getting a little dirty, then go out and dig for some money in those recycle bins.

Would anybody on Totse know of another way we can take advantage of floating money and slowly make ourselves filthy rich?

- Tom
