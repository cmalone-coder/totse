What to do if the fuzz comes a-knockin'
On prompting from Rat_Bastard, here's my old post on what the cops on a low-level raid of your house, especially if acting on an anonymous crimestoppers tip.

---

WHAT TO EXPECT FROM THE COPS WHEN SERVING A CRIMESTOPPERS TIP

In chronological order, what will happen:

1. A pair of plainclothes cops will show up in an unmarked patrol car. Both will come armed.

2. They will bring (I forget the name), but it is a slip of paper that acts as a warrant you can sign yourself. THE ADVANTAGE OF THIS IS that if you fill it out, they will probably only search your room (if you're a juvenile, they will most likely search your whole house if over 18 or living by yourself) and perform a simple check through the Find command on your computer for the words "bomb", "pyro", "Anarchists Cookbook", "Terrorists Handbook", and assorted other obvious words.

IF YOU REFUSE AND MAKE THEM GO GET A WARRANT they will come with a large group of cops (5 or more) and perform a thorough search of your house.

Places they will search:

Under your bed/mattress/sheets

In your closet

In your air ducts

All items of furniture (chairs, file cabinets, dressers, etc)

Questions they will ask:

"Do you know why we're here?"

"Do you know why we are searching for explosive devices in your home?"

"Do you take apart fireworks?"

"Do you like fireworks?"

"Do you burn things?"

..and other questions. use your common sense and say no, people.

Answer no to all questions that might mark you as a pyro of any sort, or that you might have ANY knowledge of advanced chemistry, or that you know how to make explosives. Do not be sarcastic in anything you say. Answer "Yes sir" or "No sir" to all their questions. You CAN, however, tell them that you like fireworks and/or model rocketry.

ABOVE ALL, DO NOT MAKE COMMENTS THAT THEY MIGHT THINK ARE THREATENING. My dumb-as-shit brother said "they think he's going to blow up the school!" and laughed. they took this very seriously and asked my parents all sorts of questions about me. While they were at my door, I was busy as fuck upstairs moving papers, diagrams, chemicals, etc. to my brother's room, where I took his drawers out and taped them to the back and stuck them back in. they made a quick check of his room(looked in the drawers, under the bed, etc-- i was nervous as hell when they looked in the drawers, my heart was going like crazy but they naver looked on the back so they never found them.)

while they were in my room one of the cops found a bag of brass filings from a key making stand, he asked "what are these" and i told him "brass filings". he said "these are very dangerous, if you mix them with water they'll catch on fire and explode." I thought "what a dumbass" and told him "no, brass doesn't do that, you're thinking of Sodium or Potassium." He became very suspicious at this and held a whispered conversation with his partner, and decided to confiscate the filings.

The worst they got on me was a diagram on making a Molotov cocktail with H2SO4, gasoline, and sucrose.

They gave me a good talking-to on THE DANGERS OF EXPLOSIVES in which I could tell they knew jack shit about pyrotechnics of any kind.

To avoid being searched:

Never talk any people who are not your CLOSEST FRIENDS about pyrotechnics. Do not talk about it at school. Do not talk about it with neighbors. Do not talk about it at all except in a private place.

Little computer lesson here (May be help to someone).

When you 'delete' something, it is not really gone. Even if you empty it from the recycle bin, or bypass it altogether. What that 'deleting' does, is marks that space as available for the system to use. If a file uses that space, it overwrites, and then that file is 'gone'.

Zeroing a drive is basically writing every sector over with 0s. Generally many times, to ensure that no one that isn't very advanced in computers will find anything.
