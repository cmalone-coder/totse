Re: Scared on Shrooms??
grungymagician-I dont think you quite understand the capabilities of magic mushrooms.
reasons people get scared, on top of what Junglist has already said, are things like:

lack of preperation, you need a sound mind before taking any psychedelic, if your emotional at all before dosing up then these emotions are likely to run haywire during a peak.
you need to be able to reassure yourself that your going to come up and that you will come down (whether you like it or not).

Also, the amount counts. because amount increases intensity. ive never had as little as 1/16, but i can tell you that ingesting a couple of grams is a whole different ball game than ingesting 1/8 or 1/4.
you need to realise that taking larger amounts can alter reality as you know it. e.g. what you see in front of you is probably not even remotely the same as what is really there, quite often you dont even realise that you havent even moved, youve just sat there with your eyes closed experiencing awsome visuals.
People can get really scared because they cant handle the intensity of the alternate reality.

And if youve got apprehensions about getting scared, then as cool as a secluded forest may sound to go tripping, i dont think your gonna like it. think for a second.

FOREST, DARK, SECLUDED, ALTERED REALITY - FUCKING SCARY.
i personally love trippin in forests and places like that because i love the intense feeling you get that your not alone, noises are everywhere and then theres complete silence, etc. and although it is scary, i still enjoy it. ( i was lucky enough to trip in the amazon jungle)

and in my opinion, take the chronic, but not
at your peak, start smoking a lttle when you start to feel the rising rush. usually around 45-60mins.

sorry this was long, but im just trying to help you out.
hope you have a good 1/4
