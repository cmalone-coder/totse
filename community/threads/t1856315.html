Acidmelts

Easy Extraction of Psuedo

This is the most basic simple way of extracting psuedoephedrine from OTC cold medicine. I know that in other Countries (Oz) they add a bunch of stupid shit to pills to make it hard to do extractions, but this is for places that don't do that. Here in America it is easy to accuire good pills that are easily extractable.



Here is how it works...

Purchase as many pills as you feel necessary for your reaction. In my dreams I usually get about 300 to 400 white pills containing 60mg of psuedoephedrine Hcl. Get the cheapest, most generic pills. (Hint... try dollar stores) Remember that if you get pills with red dye on them you have to get the dye off first. That is covered in another page on Chemhead.

Now put all the pills in a ziplock baggie and close it up good. Crush up the pills with a hammer. Now pour the powdered pills into a glass jar and cover with twice as much methanol. (the gas line antifreeze Heet containes methanol) If you have 2 inches of powder in your glass jar then cover with about 4 inches of methanol. Shake up really good.

Now put this jar into your fridge and let it sit for about 3 hours. Then take out of the fridge and siphon all the methanol through some plastic tubing. (aquarium tubing) Siphon this into a funnel with 2 coffee filters and a cotton ball in the end. The funnel should be sitting on top of another glass jar. Now set this aside for the moment.

Take your first jar with gunky pill shit in the bottom, and add a little more methanol to it. (about 2 inches worth) Now shake it up again real good, and set it back in the fridge for about 2 hours or until it the methanol starts to look sorta clear again. After the gunk all settles to the bottom again take out of the fridge and siphon the methanol back through the same funnel with the same filters and into the glass jar with the methanol in it from the first siphon.

Take all your methanol and put it a glass visionware bowl and put it on your ELECTRIC burner or stove. It has to be electric, if you use gas chances are you will blow yourself up. Now put it on medium heat and slowly boil down your methanol until it is almost all gone. Now take off the stove and finish drying with a hairdryer. Make sure it is totally dry.

Now add a small amount of d-H2O(distilled water) to the bowl with the powder in the bottom and stir with a plastic spoon until it looks all dissolved in the water. Now set this in the freezer for about 45 minutes. While this is chillin, set up another funnel with 3 coffee filters and a cotton ball or 2 in the tip. put this over a new glass jar. Be sure to pre-wet the filters with d-H2O. Now take the bowl out of the freezer and pour the contents of the visionware bowl through the filters. It will probaly take a while since the water/psuedo will be kinda frozen from being in the freezer. This freezing process will get all of the waxes and binders out of your solution. You will notice after filtering that there is a nice thick layer of binders covering your filter. After it all filters through, take out the top of the 3 filters and discard. Add a new one in its place. Now keep running your water/psuedo through you filter until the water looks perfectly clear. This usually take me about 4 or 5 times in my dreams.

Now you have to dissolve the d-H2O like you dissolved the methanol. Put it in your clean visionware bowl and set on the burner on medium heat (a soft boil). Don't go anywhere! Stand there and watch it. When it gets down real low again take off the burner and finish with a hairdryer. You do this cause it burns real easily. Now you have pure (or close to it) psuedoephedrine Hcl crystalized in the bottom of yor bowl.

All right your almost done. This final cleaning REALLY helps out your psuedo. what you are going to do is cover your psuedo with an anhydrous non-polar solvent (Toluene, Colemans Fuel, ect.).To make your NP anhydrous you will need to pour it over some cooked epsom salts (put on a cookie sheet in the oven at 300F for 2 hours). What this does is suck the water out of your NP making it anhydrous. Make sure you cover your psudeo well but dont put in too much. Now stir it up a little. The non-polar solvent wont dissolve you psuedo but will clean more crap off of it. Now put your visionware bowl ( with the psuedo and NP in it) onto your electric burner. Keep it on very low heat. Watch carefully.... when it starts to boil take off the heat and stir a bit till it calms down, and put it back on. you want to boil for 5 minutes. now just pour it all through a filter. This will catch all your psuedo. Discard the dirty NP. Now repeat the process 2 more times so that you have a total of 3 boils in your anhydrous NP solvent. Now just put your psuedo in a clean jar and pour in some anhydrous acetone (same as before... pour through cooked epsom salts). Stir very well and pour though a filter one more time. Discard the Acetone. Now in your filter you have some fuckin SUPER pure psuedo. Have fun with it!
