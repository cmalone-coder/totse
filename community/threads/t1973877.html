Another plan i had is as follows: Fred is boosted up the tower with a little help from his friends and once he reaches a high level without risk (opponents loosing on purpose), he starts using the Martingale System http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(betting_system) with an initial bet on a low level (such as level 5, winning 18 cents every win, regardless of the number of loses), Fred can then slowly move up the tower and make money.

Drawbacks to this plan are:

-Slow! getting boosted up the tower is no problem but winning much money after that takes a lot of work and time.

-Risk! Though small, there is a chance that the Martingale System can fail. If Fred looses too many times in a row, he will suffer a large hit to his account balance.

-Trust! It is too easy for Fred to claim he lost all the money when he in fact did not. Right now, Moola has no way for its users to know other players account balances and finding someone trustworthy to act as Fred is key.
