does anyone else get this with weed?
I'll try and keep this as simple as possible.
Whenever I'm high, I see things from a different perspective. Obviously that happens to everybody. But specifically I see myself from a different perspective. I become really self-conscious. Its like seeing myself from the third-person, not literally as if I'm floating outside of my body or something, but in terms of how I perceive myself. It's not something that I can really ignore because its very inherent to my consciousness when I'm high. I don't have to start pondering or even think in words, is just kind of there. It's more like intuition then actual thinking. I'm constantly aware of my appearance, exactly how I look, and what other people think of my appearance and what they think of me. It's as if I can see myself from the perspective of other people. I see myself for exactly how and what I am, like all of the 'filters' or 'buffers' I normally have are taken away, and reality is just lying bare. And it's really not good. It's not just some trippy feeling or something, it feels totally real. I feel like everybody veiws me as different to everybody else, as less then everyone else, no one takes me seriously or respects me at all, I'm must some inadequate, half crazy weirdo who has an inherent inability to have a realistic understanding of things. Kind of like if I was retarded, and everybody knew, but obviously when they talked to me, they didn't make a show of it or anything and just try and communicate with me. Only people don't really think I'm retarded, just really weird and 'not all there' and an inherently socially inept person who's just naturally a misfit in society. I hate looking in mirrors when I'm high because I'm so self conscious, but I stare at myself in the mirror when I'm high anyway and I can't really help it. I feel like alot of people talk about me behind my back, I even see and hear what people say about me and stuff in my head, and the way they talk about me. I feel like some other people, like close freinds, don't really talk about me behind my back as much, but just have their own private perceptions of me inside themselves, and it is always that I am inherently inadequate in some way.
