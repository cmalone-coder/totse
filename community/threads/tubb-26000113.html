Re: need advice from cranky smokers
I used to smoke crystal, but I never bought it from anyone. You can do,"The Burn Test" to check it out. Take a little tiny bit and put it on a piece of tinfoil and hold a lighter under it for a second. It should liquify right away. As soon as that happens pull away the lighter and watch the liquid. within a second or 2 it should recrystalize on the tinfoil. If the liquid has a yellow or greenish color to it, it probaly still has some I2 in it and isnt very clean.

I would suggest going by the burn test, how it looks (nice clear crystals), and of course how it smells. The smell should be the first thing that you should check. As far as what patterns to look for in the recrystalized meth.... well I guess I dont remember. Meth is a fairly easy drug to tell how clean it is.
