(Short version at bottom; background on top)

I've recently resumed a regimen of Adderall, which I have been off of for 9 months, in the amount of 10 mg IR twice a day for Inattentive Adult ADD. Within a month this will most likely be increased to 15 mg x2, and then 20 mg x2, which was my previous working dosage. I take this medicine legitimately and do not abuse it; ie., I take it orally and as directed, and for the reason it is prescribed.

This works well for the problems I experience with ADD, and both my doctor and myself feel it will benefit me to be on this medication for a long-term basis, with occasional breaks in between where the medication will be temporarily suspended in order to gauge symptoms and observe tolerance, etc.

From what I understand, regular and prolonged amphetamine usage in this way can affect brain chemistry relating to serotonin and dopamine and may cause depression. Besides the Adderall, I take Wellbutrin SR 200 mg twice a day and Celexa 20 mg once a day for Major Depressive Disorder.

(Short Version)

I've used 5-HTP in the past with decent results, and currently indulge in no other drugs or alcohol asides from the minor things like coffee, cigarettes, and Kava.
