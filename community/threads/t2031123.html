She liked to shop (which I do), she liked to play PS2 from time to time (which I do as well), and she was very close to her friends. I immediately concluded that this girl was to be my partner. I handed the application to my teacher who would be going on the exchange and she gave me Kristina's email. Naturally I was to start emailing her asap.

Her flight, along with all the other students, was supposed to get in the airport at about 9PM. We ended up leaving at about 10:45PM because of delays and waited at the terminal with the rest of the Americans until about 12:30AM. At this point we just got her luggage when she arrived and drove straight to my house. We showed her around the main floor and the basement, where her room was. After that we all went to bed because it was about 1:30AM by then. It was an awkward ride to my house since I didn't quite know what to say. However this was about the extent of it.

I won't say much about the 3 weeks that followed since they're *mostly* irrelevant. I will say a few things however that probably contributed to my feelings for her. I knocked on her door at about 6:30AM and slowly opened it as she turned her light on. I thanked her for being such a good guest and that I couldn't wait to come to Germany in June of '07. I felt my eyes tear up as I hugged her goodbye in her pajamas. I left the room as I said goodbye and shut the door. I walked upstairs solemnly and left at 6:45AM for the test. I couldn't concentrate the entire time.

So now comes the end of school. I leave for Germany on June 11th. School ends June 6th. I have 5 days to relax and get all packed. I'm excited as hell and so is everyone else. So I'm skipping the plane flight, the 2 days in Rothenburg and skipping right to the exchange. The moment we arrive at the school in a big-ass tour bus, I know I'm where I belong. We unload all of our luggage from the bottom and I stumble through all of the people as a friend says he found my cell phone on the floor. Damn I owe him. So I finally stumble through everyone, and there she is. She's looking right at me with a big smile wearing the AE shirt she bought here (which looks great on her btw). I get my luggage on the other side of the bus and greet her and her mom. We all exchange hugs and go to the car where we ride on over to her house, about 5 minutes away. They ask me how the plane flight was, etc etc. All of this is in English, even though I could've said all of it in German. So we get to the house and I drop my suitcase and backpack next to the stairs. I see my exchange dad and he looks just as funny as ever. I give him a big 'ol bear hug and grin, almost crying with happiness.

The entire 3 weeks were filled with bliss. I have never been happier in my entire life. I was always busy, always seeing new stuff, always hanging with friends. Kristina was always there smiling too. She took care of me like I took care of her in America. I helped around the house, I did what I could to change to their customs and stuff.
