Re: Acid by myself?
Time for a more detailed response.

Tripping with others will always mean outside stimuli during the trip. Distractions from your mind, and sometimes people imposing upon others.

With others around, it's hard to truly get lost in the trip (which is a good thing.)

Many of the times when you're starting to visualize -- get very good, meaningul (mostly the ones that are in your mind) hallucinations, someone around you can interrupt those. As a distraction, they make it difficult to continue with a hallucination and truly explore it deeply. Once you start falling down the rabbit hole, someone else will usually pull you out, and it sucks.

Tripping by yourself will ensure no distraction. In my experiences, no matter the dose, solo tripping is generally more intense simply because there is no one to interrupt your mind from wandering.
