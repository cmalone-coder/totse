Last wednesday (the 13th) I went out in my hometown after getting back from Poland (where I had been clubbing til about 5am). I was tired, and not aiming to pick up at all. Anyway, this girl "F", a friend of a friend, starts talking to me. She interests me greatly, being pretty much the female equivalent of Me; intelligent, narcissistic, sceptical and bold.

So we have a few drinks together, and she suggests we go outside for a smoke. I agree.

We smoke, then she kisses me (no effort on my part, she just kissed me), and puts her number in my phone. She says "I really enjoyed spending some time with you, we should go out again soon." (verbatim quote). We went to another bar for a while, good rapport continued and she was still very much into it. Anyway, the time came when we had to part (we are both home from uni and staying with our parents, so a same-night lay was out of the question).

I contact her a few days later through text/facebook, and it's like nothing ever happened. I was going out that night so I suggested she join me; she flaked ("I am too tired blah blah blah"). She didn't answer when I called, didn't reply to my text.

I know people do this sort of thing all the time and just lose interest, it's happened to me before and it's ok because it's all just part of the game. What I don't get about this is that she literally couldn't keep her hands off me that night (plus I could tell the rare connection I felt was a mutual thing); she initiated everything (including the kiss)... she wasn't even very drunk or whatever... But when I got in contact, I may as well have been the postman :S

I go out of my way to learn as much as I can about these strange creatures but the deeper meaning still eludes me. Worst thing is, I don't feel I've even learnt anything from the whole experience.
