Re: What should someone know before trying shrooms.
Well what are the risks
if they are poisonous, you could die... try to get them from someone reputable, preferably having people you know take them before you to tell you the strength, and if they are viable..

how much does some eat?
half of an eighth for a lighter trip, a full eighth for a more mind blowing experience.

What can go wrong? Is it safe to do it alone? Are there any permanet affects?
Some people get nausea, try to not puke for as long as you can if this happens, but after an hour or so about all of the chemicals that are going to be absorbed should have been absorbed, puke and you will feel better. an empty stomach helps.

If you have never done any psychedelics before i wouldn't go at it alone, have someone to trip with... its nice to be able to talk to someone who is experiencing the same thing... by yourself you are going to think more than your probably going to be comfortable with. There is always the possibility of a difficult experience or bad trip. before you take them right on a sheet of paper that you are on mushrooms and it will be over in a few hours. seeing that in your own handwriting can be comforting if you are feeling insane for no apparent reason. if your thoughts start going around things in your life that you would rather not think about its good to use this time to deal with them, you will walk out of it feeling better about whatever it is. Try to think of happy things and listen to some nice music, pink floyd kicks the mindfuck up for me... if it gets too bad for you to handle, drink some alcohol or take some benzos to calm you down or help you get some sleep...

as for permanent effects, your looking at mental things... ive always walked out of those really powerful experiences feeling stronger and happier, just happy to be alive... acid got rid of my depression once it helped me realize that life is better than ive been seeing it, happiness is always there you just have to have the right kind of eyes to see it... flashbacks are not a myth, but they are not as bad as antidrug makes them out to be, at least for me... i get random visuals when my mind starts to wander off sometimes, like the walls will breath and a person shirt will move for no real reason downward and upward.... and then i snap out of it and its over.. they may or may not happen, it sort of comes with the territory of getting into psychs...

shrooms are a great drug for the spring time, do them on a nice sunny day and go outside... even a back porch is fine... nature is simply beautiful, everything about it... have some cigarettes if you enjoy smoking at all because they are so much better while tripping... weed will intensify the effects...

keep an open mind going into it, dont expect anything because whatever you can come up with is going to be very wrong. even if you feel insane just keep in mind that you are on a drug, its sort of a leave your sanity at the door sort of thing if you take enough to trip hard. Just take them however you want, and let the lovin take hold.
