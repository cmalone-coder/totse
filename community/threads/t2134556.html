Tried Salvia for the first time
A couple of friends and I were hanging out in their basement and one had some Salvia on him. He had done it before and said he tripped harder then he ever had before. I didn't really believe him since I know that Salvia is legal. But then I smoked it.

I took a massive hit from a water pipe and held my breath for about 30 seconds. As soon as I exhaled, I started to see roads. But they weren't ordinary roads. They were roads through time. Kinda looked like roads from the movie Tron. Anyway, I was traveling these roads through time. And suddenly I landed. I was a little boy, with my mom, outside of a grocery store. We were walking towards the store when suddenly I was being pulled back up. I realized that I was sitting in my friends basement again. I was still a little loopy, but the trip was basically over.

Since I tripped so hard, I was surprised I didn't freak out. But I later realized I couldn't freak out while I was tripping, because I was tripping so hard, I didn't even realize I was tripping. My consciousness was completely altered. I was AT the grocery store. I WAS a little boy. No reason to freak out if you're just a little boy at a grocery store.

It was intense. And I tripped harder then I did on Shrooms or Mescaline. I've never done Acid so I can't compare. I just felt like I had to share my experience.
