Here's the real deal:

"We have shown that contrast defined arbitrary visual images can be reconstructed from fMRI signals of the human visual cortex on a single trial basis."*

All they did was recreate incredibly simple images - black and white, simplistic shapes - from data taken from someone's brain on an MRI scan. Furthermore, it was in an incredibly controlled setting, so one can't just do this in any environment. It's not nearly as 'futuristic' as one might initially think.

Here's a picture from the journal, showing the shapes that were recorded:


They will definitely not, however, be able to 'capture' people's dreams anytime soon.

Still, this is an amazing find with countless hours of research involved. This could develop into something truly incredible.
