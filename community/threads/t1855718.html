Re: Glassware?
Don't use regular glass jars for reactions like this, try and stick with borosilicate glass.

It isn't the heat resistance we are looking for when it comes to glassware. It is the resistance to thermal shock and the low thermal expandsion characteristics. You may get away with it a few times but there will always be that time the bottom cracks out of your vessel and your honey spills all over the floor.
