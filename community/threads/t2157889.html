The way I roll blunts? Did you just ask me the way I roll blunts?

Mk. this is the way I roll blunts. Iiiiiii...:

'm assuming you're rolling a Swisher Sweet cigarillo, as it's much easier for a beginning blunt roller. You can manhandle Swishers and they usually won't break. Lay the emptied out blunt on the table, swipe your weed onto the surface of a magazine or similar, cuff it, and pour the weed along the blunt from left, to right. If you pour most of your weed out of the mag by the time the blunt ends, you've got the right amount. Usually around 1.5 gs if you wanna get technical.

NOOOW: The foundationnnn:

If you get a good end, you can easily roll the rest of the blunt. I usually roll it from right to left. So take your right thumb, (assuming your right hand's palm is facing YO.. I mean UP, as if to cup said balls. Yes. Balls.) and pinch the bottom of the blunt around the weed, while your left middle finger slides the upper side of the blunt around the bottom one. Once you've got a good size pinch, lick the two sides together.

Rinse, repeat for the rest of the blunt.

oh if your gutting a cigarillo...

lick it, and i mean all of it...

the thin paper that covers it should be easily pealed off. save these.

moisten it again to avoid breakage...

take a razor and cut it lengthwise.

remove all the tobacco.

put a gram or maybe a little less where the tobacco used to be.

try to even it out on a surface or with your hands...
