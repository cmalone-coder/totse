Does it cost a shit load to fix?

Yes. If you get it down to the primer, it'll have to be sanded and re-painted. They'll probably have to do the whole car too because you can't really paint one part of your car and expect it to blend in. It can get to be a couple thousand, depending on how bad it is.

What if you're caught? Jail time?

It is criminal damage to property aka vandalism and carries a fine and a misdemeanor charge (meaning up to 1 year in prison).

Can it set off an alarm?

It can if it's a really sensitive alarm. Most alarms take a couple of good bumps though, so just do it really quick and don't bump into it.
