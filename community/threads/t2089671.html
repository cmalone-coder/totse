Ayahuasca
Ok this is the report of my first experience with this wonderful brew:

I fasted for 3 days before drinking the brew, all I ate was boiled potatoes and boiled broccoli, one the day I didn't touch any food at all to be on the safe side.

I took about 3.5 grams of rue crushed in grapefruit juice, as for the brew I used half oz. of yellow caapi, half oz. of Virola that was previously put in acidic water to help extraction and about 8 or 9 grams of mimosa.

I reduced the brew to about 80 ml or something like that and drank it very slowly over the course of a couple of hours while watching Apocalypse Now, by the time Marlon Brando was taking about the Horror I was seeing patterns on his face and the ending part of the film proved to be quite intellectually stimulating.

After the film I started to peak, I couldn't recognize my room, everything seemed to be bigger than usual and my hands lookes like ET's.

I seemed quite passionate about trashing my room as much as I could, I didn't do it in an angry way though it was more like the aya turned me into a 2 year old and I was just playing around in my room, throwing things around laughing by myself.
I was in that state for about two hurs then I came down, then I came up again but not to the same level and I tripped more for about another two hours, then I took some kratom and passiflora and just lied in bed relaxing until I fell asleep.

Overall it was an amazingly positive experience, from many of the reports that I had read, I expected something darker and scarier, but it was really funny and pleasant.

I didn't have any enlighting visions, I kept on getting childhood memories and other random things in my head, now I can't wait to do it again, next time I plan on using around 15 grams of mimosa and twenty grams of virola and see what happens.


After years of experimenting with other psychedelics, I think I finally found the perfect one, dosing is not really that hard as long as you reduce the brew to a drinkable amount, the nausea can be pretty bad but if you fast you'll manage to keep it down, it doesn't last a lot and anyway if you come down and you still want to trip more you can always have another sip of the brew.

As far as intensity, the only thing that goes close to it is liquid acid, but still the aya visuals had more depth, and I really had the feeling I was in another reality for a few hours, as in me being trasported to another place as opposed to being here but with distorted perceptions which is what I always got from acid, the feeling that I'm just perceiving reality in a different way, with the aya I was in another parallel reality.

If any of you is interested I can give you tips on how to brew it, just be careful though and respect the dietary prescriptions since it does contain MAOIs.

Later, stay safe!!

As far as the dieting goes, one thing to keep in mind is that all the warnings you find on the net concern pharmaceutical MAOIs, both Rue and Caapi provide reversible MAOI so the risk of fatal interactions is much lower.

Anyway the dietary restrictions only apply for the 24 hours preceding the trip, I kept myself light, I barely fasted for 3 days, just eating boiled potatoes, spinach and broccoli, and I didn't touch any solid food on the day I dosed.

As far as the material go, there's quite a number of websites you can get them from, I don't know about the legalities in each country, but Aya is quite unheard of, so most plants are not banned.

Thanks for the replies again, nice to see people here like tripping!


Ok as far as the brewing goes what I did is the following:

3.5 grams of Syrian Rue were crushed in a mortar into a fine powder, you can toast the seeds in a pan before and it'll be easier to crush them, then they were put in a glass with some grapefruit juice and left in the fridge.

For the brew, the half oz. of caapi, the Virola and the Mimosa were left to sit overnight in a pot covered in cold mineral water with the juice of one and a half limes.

The next day I boiled this 3 times, then discarded the plant material, all of the 3 washes were collected together and reduced to a drinkable amount of about 80 ml.

I then put the liquid to cool down in the fridge.

For the trip I drank the rue with grapefruit juice, waited about an hour and then I started sipping the brew gradually to avoid nausea.

Hope this helps those who asked me to drop them emails, a starting dose of mimosa would around 9 grams, like I did, some people would tell you to use less for your first time, but there's a chance you might throw up or do something wrong in the brewing process so I guess it's better to prepare a bit more.

Later, stay safe
