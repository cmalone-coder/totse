My breakthrough on Salvia 20x. Holy shit.
A couple of weeks ago I ordered some salvia 20x from a site on the net. It came in 3 days so I was excited to try it. Now I have tried 10x and it was kinda fun but I didn't experience any kind of mind blowing effects. I almost thought salvia didn't work on me.

Until Now.

After packing the bowl and sitting down with my bong, I light up my torch lighter and start to blaze it up. I clear the whole bowl in one hit. As I am holding it in, I stood up and went outside for a second and then came in and sat down.

Now, here is where the trip begins.

I don't even remember exhaling. All of the sudden I am not sure what is happening. I instantly lose my vision, hearing, and sense of being completely. I feel as if the world is stuck in a constant loop repeating over and over and over. My perception of time diminishes into nothingness. I lose my being as a whole. It seemed like there was no past, and no future, just the present, which was endless despair.

I wasn't sure if I existed anymore. I was trying to grasp on to something to confirm what I believed to be reality just for a second. I suppose I blacked out for a while because I was in the kitchen one second, then for a split second I find myself outside on the bottom of my driveway. Thank God my friend was here. He was the sole piece of this world that helped me grasp a hold of whats going on.

Next thing I know I'm laying on my bed, just trying to get out of the world I was in. I could describe it as being in eternity, nonexistent, not past, no future, no dreams, goals, hopes, or feelings other than endless despair. I believe I might have experienced what is known as ego death. It felt like I had died but only metaphysically. I went to hell and back and NEVER want to do that again.

About halfway through the trip I regained my sense of existence. You wouldn't believe how happy I was. I then went into the shower and washed off hoping it might help ease off my trip, which it did. Then I came out and just lied down until it wore off. My trip lasted about 40 minutes, which is longer than normal I suppose, but, I only weigh 100lbs so that might have something to do with it.

I would now say that I felt divine intervention coming out of a plant. I now know what it feels like to have nothing, and lose everything, including yourself. After the trip I would say I learned one thing, and that is to appreciate life to the fullest. I think that this was a life changing experience to me. I am so grateful to be alive, and now do not take anything for granted. Little inconveniences of life seem beyond trivial to me. I wish everyone could feel how I feel now, but the way to get there I wouldn't wish on my worst enemy.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TL;DR - Fuck you

If anyone wants to try salvia just to get "fucked up" heed my warning: This is not for the faint of heart. Expect something big.
