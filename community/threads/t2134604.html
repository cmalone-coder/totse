Re: Any UK ravers in?
Lots of forest raves in Canterbury, Kent. They largely focus on psytrance, K, and MDMA. You've just got to get hooked up - no one will know where they are until about 3 hours before, so find someone who can get that 'special number' to find directions. That said, because Canterbury is dominated by students (it has two unis) there's always an abundance of house parties/raves that can be just as good. At my 21st, for example, we had a lighting set-up, 3 DJs (with vinyl decks and a big sounds system of course), over 100 guests, and an ass load of illicit narcotics floating about; it was amazing :).

In Brighton there's illegal raves, but also lots of legal ones too: try the Concord 2, and their monthly Devotion night. Devotion is basically the big DnB night in B'ton; next Saturday we've got Mampi Swift, London Elektricity, DJ Fresh, and (America's no.1 female dnb DJ) DJ Shortee
Flier for details of that one: http://img517.imageshack.us/img517/1630/dvotionjunepostars1.jpg
Here's a youtube vid: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_V22XvzVWo&feature=related
Watch out for illegal raves in B'ton as they're often taken over by scag-heads and occasionally pickeys will turn up and rob the sound system...

If you're into DnB, go here for more details on events across the country: http://forum.breakbeat.co.uk/tt.aspx?forumid=29

If you're going out in London there's The Synergy Project (http://www.thesynergyproject.org/ - closest thing to a rave you'll find), Fabric (http://www.fabriclondon.com/ - London's DnB hub), and FWD (http://www.ilovefwd.com/ - not a rave, but if you like Dubstep...), to name a few.

Basically, there's always going to be raves in the UK whether you live in the country or whether you live in a town/city. I've found that illegal raves largely take part in the country-side, usually in industrial estates, farms, large open fields, house parties; whereas, your legal raves, that feature named artists, take place in cities.
