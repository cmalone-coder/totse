I never said you never had to use intelligence, far from it, but if you just make a trade off of a "hunch", you are an idiot. Granted, because it is almost a zero sum game (though still negative because with stocks you have fees AND a bid/ask spread) you can win half the time with luck since markets only go up or down. (Sideways too if you wanna be technical about it). But overall you need an edge to beat the market consistently over time.

Yes, it is a gamble in the sense that everything you do in life is a gamble. You have complete control over how much you choose to risk, where you will enter, where you will exit, and what your plan is when trades go against you. I'm telling you, trying to trade with fundamentals is going to be almost impossible for the average investor/speculator because they will never receive as much news, or as fast as the bigger investors. Trading with technicals is much more simple and efficient. The other main advantage it has, is that everyone sees the same picture from the big wig bank analyst to the lonely first account speculator. NO HUNCHES
