Re: Recommend me some depressing/thinking anime.
If its depressing your looking for, step right up and try 'Gunslinger Girl.'

Basically, its a Japanese anime about Italy and Italian government which takes young girls from troubled lives and gives them cybernetic enhancements so that they can fight terrorists. It seems childish and action oriented but actual shooting only consists of a few minutes each episode. The rest is just the girls and their handlers talking about very depressing things. Its definitely a mature anime (not ultra violent, or lots of sex, just adult-like), and seems like you might try it.
P.S. It has good voice actors both from the japanese and english versions. It was also released in Italian if your into that.

I've seen ergo proxy, and it is very good. But was lacking something for me. But if you lik ethat, you could look into 'Eureka Seven' personally my favorite anime, but it is a little more light hearted, especially at the beginning.
