Re: Avoiding Drug Dogs/Drug Police at Music Festivals
I've never been to a festival that had drug dogs, and I've been to over a dozen. More often than not the people at the entrance are event staff -- people who volunteer to work so they can go to the fest for free. They don't give a shit about drugs. The average search consists of them looking in your trunk for 5 seconds.

As far as cops go, they're not stupid. They know what goes on at these things. They're not so much worried about the small time consumer as much as they are the serious businessmen. As long as you don't toke up or buy anything right under their noses you'll be fine. They're not going to conduct 40,000 random searches.

Most festivals have camping. If you plan on buying or selling anything, just go in/near the persons tent. Like I said, they know whats going on, and obviously they're not going to be able to catch everyone.

I was tripping my nuts off once and for some reason decided to talk to a horseback cop about the nature of things that went on at the festivals. He says they know exactly what goes on, and more or less they're there for violence control. I asked him about drug busts and the like, and he said they actually bust a very small number of people.

When I asked him why, he asked me if I've ever been fishing. I said yes, and he said "Ever catch every fish in the lake?"

edit: don't be a retard. use your best judgment, if you have any.
