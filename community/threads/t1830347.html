I remember Zero doing a test about a year ago testing to see how much shrapnel was produced by cobs. He placed a black powder filled cob under a large cardboard box, and set it off. When it went off, he counted the holes in the box to determine how much shrapnel was produced by the cob. He counted five. When he started looking around for the fragments, he found a large fragment stuck in a piece of wood at least 1/2 inch deep, which is very deep becuase a pellet gun shooting pellets at 650 pfs cannot even penitrate that deep. I dont remember how far away the wood was from the cob when it exploded, but I remember it being at least 50 feet away.

Think about it. If the shrapnel can penitrate 1/2 inch deep into hard wood, think about how deep it can penitrate into flesh? You should all think twice about messing with cobs.
