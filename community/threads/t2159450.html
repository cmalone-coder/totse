One possibility is that you'll still be able to enlist, but your selection of jobs will be limited to those not requiring a security clearance. A similar thing happenned to one of my buddies in Basic Training. He had wanted to enlist as a 25B (IT specialist), but a felony marijuana conviction prevented him from getting any clearances, so he had to enlist as a 25L (Cabling specialist), which doesn't require any clearance. So, I don't know if you've shipped or not by now, but you may still be good to enlist; however, your MOS options may be limited to things like 25L, 88M, 11B, etc.
