Re: A gift of sympathy
Just be there for her to talk to, or be there if she doesn't feel like talking. Try not to "cheer her up" or "lift her spirits". People in mourning just need time. If you must say something, it's better to say things along the lines of "sure, it hurts to lose someone" or "I'm here for you, just let me know if you need anything".

I'd advise against getting her any presents at the moment; anything you give her will remind her of her grandmother's passing. Your time and presence is the best gift right now.

If you're inclined, help her and her family with any chores that need to be done, either around their house or packing up her grandmother's stuff or running errands.
