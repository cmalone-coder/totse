Re: How long to wait for refill?
Hydrocodone is Schedule III which allows one refill. Schedule IIs require new scripts every time but a doctor is allowed to write three scripts at a time, in order for the patient to not have to come back to see him for a while. For example I just saw my psychiatrist about a week after filling my Adderall and he gave me three scripts for January, February and March, which means I don't have to see him again until the April fill is near-due.

That's not true. I've yet to find a pharmacy that is Nazi about someone getting a 2 or 3 day early fill. People have erratic and irregular work/school/social schedules and if you don't seem like a fiending sketchy junkie then you're probably not going to arouse suspicion if you come for a fill a few days early.
