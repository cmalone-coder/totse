I do happen to believe that there are several monsterous (I use the word "monsterous" only in reference to the creatures size and/or the mystery that surrounds them, not the creatures disposition) creatures that still havent been found or proven to exist. These animals can be found on or near the bottoms of the worlds oceans. The bottom of the ocean is still practically unexplored. Every time there is an expedition to this region there is always some new discovery that will exite researchers (as well as the rest of us) and demand more study. It has been said that we know more about outerspace then we do about parts of our own planet. It used to be thought that the bottom of the ocean would be too hostile to support any type of life let alone anything the size of a giant squid. We all know that the ocean bottom is far from lifeless. We even found creatures that were thought to be extinct long before the dinosaurs made their first appearance (Celeocanth). The ocean bottom is a very dangerous and exspensive place to visit, its freezing cold in some places or boiling hot in others. There is no sunlight so it is in perpetuale darkness, any light that is present is made by the creatures that live there. The pressure at these depths will easily crush any terrestrial creature instantly. Its the area of these regions (sheer size) that prevents a thorough survey to be accurately taken so a creature can easily hide without direct observation. (actually seeing it alive and first-hand). You cant really take these creatures and compare them to any land based animal that may or may not exist for the simple fact that we have thus far never found any type of true physical evidence. All we are offered are eyewhitness acounts, several plaster casts of footprints (hence the name "Bigfoot"), photographs that are so blurry that they can be images of almost anything and one or two very shakey movie or video clips. The most famous movie clip, the one shot at Bluff Creek by Roger Patterson and Bob Gimmly ( I think thats his name). The clip supposedly portrays a female sasquatch (the Canadian name) high tailing it towards the dense forest after being surprised by the two men as it was having a nice refreshing drink by some creek. We then see a very shakey clip of it because it was being shot while running towards it. This film would turn out to be the most prized piece of evidence for believers. The sides were drawn and the battle of the experts commenced, Anthropologists, biologists, and the rest of North America argued for ages on the films authenticity and if the craeture actually existed after all. It turns out that the film was really shot to boost interest in an up and coming nature series that was being produced at the time, Patterson admited it before he died as did Bob Gimmly did after he died. They even told who the person who played the beast was. It was even listed as one of the greatest hoaxes of all time and even made it into the Guiness Book of World records as a hoax (I forgot the exact year but it was between the years 1998 and 2000). well so much for the keystone of the believers. The footage was never used as it was origionally intended, it created so much contraversy that it couldnt be used. I can just see these guys sitting down drinking a nice cold beer and laughing at the absurdity of it all. Another famous ( or infamous) photograph of a mysterious creature (quite possibally the most famous monster) The Loch Ness Monster also turned out to be a fabrication as well. The surgeons photograph said to show the head, neck and part of the body of a large dinosaur like creature taken while on a pleasure cruise of the Loch. It too was the focus of a heated debate on the merits of the image. I mean if you cant trust the word of a proper English doctor who can you trust? Well guess what, the famous head and neck turned out to be an unfamous toy submarine with a plastic head. The sasquatch has been sighted all over the west coast. It is a very populated region so the creature would have been found, a hunter or trapper would have come across some sign that would prove such a creature exists. We have never seen a live giant squid, but we know they exist because there has been several cases where they have either been washed ashore, or accidently brought up by human activities. The presence was also found in the stomaches of Sperm Whales killed by whalers. If you went up to some person who didnt know about the earths living history and told him/her that the Earth used to be ruled by Gigantic reptiles with very large teeth they might go into hyserics at the very idea. We know they existed because we have the bones to prove it (or fossils). We have direct evidence of our first ancestor, but we have yet to find one bone or single piece of tissue sample of anything resembeling a North American Ape, an eight foot tall ape at that. As our population gets larger the wild areas get smaller, there are no more lost cities of gold left in the jungles to find, Area-51 is a secret base for Earthbound technology such as the Nuclear bomb not to hold captive aliens that had an accident in order to steal their technology and the bottom of the worlds sea holds monsterous animals, but thats all they are; just animals.
