Re: Kicking power
To be fair, Tae Kwon Do is mostly about kicks. Punching exists, obviously, but the focus is clear.
Leg excersizes: Lunge: Take very long steps, and go down as far as possible without touching the ground. Your rear thigh should be at a 45 degree angle, and knee should almost touch the ground. Your front leg the thigh should be parallel and your shin should be perpendicular to the ground. Do as many as you can stand.
Do squat thrusts, squats with weights in 2 or 3 different arm positions, use your imagination here, I'm sure you can figure it out. And of course, practice those kicks. I use mostly sidekicks but I've been known to do spin kicks, fronts, and hooks too.

If you want to be a good\hard kicker, take an art that trains in kicking but for full contact matches. Like Kick boxing, Muay Thai, Kyokushin karate.
