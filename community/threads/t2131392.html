Am I in love? Is something wrong with me?
Sorry this is excruciatingly long but please read all of this. This isn't your normal love post

I'm seventeen. She's sixteen. We met two months ago but never really hung out until this weekend.

We and three of our mutual friends hung out Saturday, drinking and smoking like regular teenagers. The next day we all went to the beach. I took an extacy pill. She sent all the signs that she was interested in me (ignoring the other males in the group, being extra touchie, staring at me through the rear view mirror, etc...) She had to get home at six so I left my friends and drove her home. We were talking and I said I was into her and she later sent me a text message, on my way back to the beach, saying she was into me too (childish I know but I'm sure she had her reasons).

That night I only got two hours of sleep. I fell asleep and woke up at one. Then I started hallucinating (only way I can describe it). I would close my eyes and see a hologram of my room (like my eyes wear open) but all my friends at the beach would be hanging around having real conversations that I could actually follow. Then I would open my eyes and shit would be normal. I assumed it was the E because I can never sleep right the nights I take one.

The next day she called me to make plans to double date with her friends at the beach next Friday night. That same night she called, (last night) the same thing happened. I woke up two hours later but instead I would close my eyes and hallucinate about us on our date next Friday. I would close my eyes and we would be at a familiar beach with some other people. I kept roughing people up because they would try to cheat with her. I would remember and forget that it wasn't real. It might have just been a new type of lucid dreaming that I never experienced before. I'm curious to see what will happen tonight.

I never felt a connection with anyone before, like the one I feel for her. Especially not one in such a short time. Everything she says carries so much conviction that I would doubt my own beliefs; something that never happens.

Today I went to school feeling high as a kite. The only thing was that I wasn't on any drugs. It wasn't just like an ego boost. It was like a chemical reaction in my head. I was "inspired". Like I could write poems about this girl all summer. Like I could throw away my beliefs and the way I live my life. Her eyes are more addictive than heroine; the most beautiful and piercing eyes that I have EVER seen. It wasn't just the butterflies in the stomach type of thing. It was like all over and even my mind was different. I was even jittery. If I didn't know any better I could have sworn someone spiked my drink with something. I've never been happier.

These last two days have felt like two weeks. I can't sleep. I lost all my appetite. I've eaten almost nothing since Sunday. It doesn't feel long because of the depravation of food and sleep. It feels long because I feel like something's wrong if I'm not with her.

Three days ago I would have said that no teenagers are REALLY in love and no one can love someone else before getting intimate. But now I swear I'm in love.

I don't think this is an obsession. Is there something wrong with me? Or are loss of appetite and an endorphin megadose symptoms of love?

Either way I think it would be a bad idea telling her that I love her. I don't want to fuck shit up by scaring her off.
