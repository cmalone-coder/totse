Re: Best linux distro for beginners BUT...
If you want to be thrown at the command line and learn from it, go with Gentoo (and don't even think about using the gui installer).

The Gentoo Handbook will walk you through installing, using and maintaining your system, and explains everything you need to do instead of just presenting meaningless commands.

There is a lot of support through the documentation on gentoo.org, the gentoo wiki, mailing lists and the gentoo forums to help if you are confused or anything doesn't work.

I know most people won't recommend Gentoo to a new user, but just going through the installation process, compiling your first kernel and installing Xorg will force you to learn a whole lot about linux.
