Re: Constuction of a Smoke Bomb
You're in the US. Don't buy KNO3 at a pharmacy. Get it at a home-depot or garden center, it's sold as fertilizer (postassium nitrate fertilizer).

For the smoke bomb, use a 50/50 mix of KNO3/sugar, either powdered and mixed, or go to krimzonpyro for how to melt it safely. The advantage to melting is that it produces more smoke, is better all around, but it can be dangerous to melt KNO3 and sugar together.

Re: Constuction of a Smoke Bomb
quote:Originally posted by And1Drew:



He says to use a 60/40 mixture though, anyone have any comments on that ?

For a very basic smoke composition, the ratios aren't too critical. The more KNO3 you use, the more complete the combustion( think more flame, less smoke)...as KNO3 is an oxidyzer. Just keep it around the suggested ratios and you'll be just fine.

One thing I do is when making stovetop white smoke comp. is to heat the mixture in a vessel I'll never use again. I typically use coffee cans. Just be patient, and stir frequently. The main thing is to WATCH your chems. while the heat is applied. It's really simple, but like anything, it is not idiot proof.

Scuzzy, I've never done tests using the different ratios, but more KNO3 certainly does produce a much hotter, more complete compustion. I'm so lazy about smoke now, that I don't even bother measuring the chems.(and yes, white smoke is the ONLY comp. that I don't bother meauring) Anything close to published ratios seems to work just fine.
