Re: HELP DREADY! iodine crys??
Do some research.

1 pint 7% iodine

3 pints 3% h2o2

12 ml hcl, 31-37%

Into a empty 3 liter, combine the iodine, and h2o2. Then add hcl. Let get hot and react. Filter out crystals, and wash in distilled water. Wash until you are sure it is clean, and then do it twice more. You will have clean crystals, that way.

Then dry with dampgone in a dark place. Keep dark. Dry very well.

The reaction takes about 30 min. Or a tad longer.

Re: HELP DREADY! iodine crys??
-MAKING IODINE CRYSTALS-

2 CUPS - 7% TINCTURE IODINE / 2 CUPS - 3% PEROXIDE

25 MLS - HCI 37% MURAITIC ACID

___________________________________

USE PER LITER OF 7% TINC 25 ML HCI (37%) NOT CRITICAL, USE SOME,

USE ENOUGH, A LITTLE MORE WILL NOT HURT. ADD 500 ML WATER ADD WITH STIRRING,ONE LITER 3% H2O2 (PEROXIDE)

STIR WELL, LET IT SIT, GIVE IT ATLEASE 4 HOURS.

___________________________________

Decant the fluid into another jar and rinse out the crystals in the bottom of the first jar real well, 5-6 times with lots of swirling. I filter through a piece of bedsheet and get all the crystals in there and mash real well, dry good, transfer to fresh coffee filters, put in a jar that has epsoms salts (dried) in the bottom, put a folder paper towel in, the sit the crystals on top. Try to dry the crystals as best you can to start..but come back and change the coffee filters after about four hours, the next day, etc...after two changes, the I2 is generally dry. If you react them still wet, use 1.5 gm instead of 1.2 and decrease the water in the reaction by 20%.

Leave the solution in the second jar, add about another 125 ml of peroxide and let sit until the next day. You will get another batch of crystals, up to 25% as much as before, usally about 10%.

Works fine. On a good batch you can get up to 50 gm of I2 from a pint. Usually you will get 43-46 gms. A lot better than the one ounce I used to get with three bottles of peroxide.
