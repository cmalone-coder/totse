Re: Help me make alchohol,please...
yeast - doesn't really matter how much you use, but the more you use, the faster it will ferment. Any type of yeast will ferment alcohol, bakers or brewers. 2 tablespoons of bakers yeast in about a gallon of whiskey mash worked great for me. The package should say weather to activate it or not, but if it doesn't, the general method to activating yeast is to mix it with warm(not hot, it will kill the yeast) water and a little bit of sugar.

grape juice - fine to use but add more sugar b/c it's the sugar that is converted to alcohol by the yeast. There is a point however that the yeast will die b/c of the higher alcohol %, at this point, more sugar will only sweeten your brew.

2 liter bottles - fine to use, cap 'em without drilling if you want champagne. If you just want wine, forget the caps and put a cloth over the mouth of the bottle, secured w/ a rubber band. You may want to use more than one bottle though, more is better.
