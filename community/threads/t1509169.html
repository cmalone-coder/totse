J's not pregnant. It all adds up though she still denied it until I kicked her out. Haven't spoken to her since. She's just fat as hell, which can be explained through her constant eating.

I should be angry, pissed, with a feeling of loss or lonliness but I'm not. This experience has given me alot of knowledge and (as corny as it sounds) a better understanding of myself. As I said before, I hope at least I have helped someone going through the brutal hell of holding onto someone. It's not worth it.

You're better than that. Take care.
