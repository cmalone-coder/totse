BB zipgun 1 year old now...
For anyone who is unaware of the BB zipgun...
or has forgotten...
here is it's story...
not very nice one, but here is how it cam about...

in the beginning of 2001, Singapore was
introduced to airsofts, those made in china.
and cheaply sold to anyone who has the money
...
even a 3 year old kid could afford,
and buy it...

later in july of 2001, one kid went stealing
a airsoft from a toy shop...
which caught police attention...

they later banned and confiscated
airsofts from everyone...

further in august
i bought a box of party popper...

i was then keeping over 2000 to 3000 BBs
without a gun...

an idea came through....
'since party popper can shoot papers,
why not BBs...'

i started by inserting a straw into the
party popper...
and some paper into the straw...

the paper flew off with quite some speed,
but the straw cracked open...

at home, i thought of a 'cannon'
design...

the larger part of the popper was
cut off...
and the straw was straped with
the fuse of the popper...

Then the straw was reinforced with
tapes...

the first BB zipgun was born
the first, intended target, is
an empty CD box...
(a normal airsoft can barely crack it's
plastic casing)

the BB went straight through the case,
the power is more than what i wanted...

back then, the zipgun has no accuracy,
it was basically a party popper
which shoot BB...

after, the 2 planes hit the WTC, terrorism
rised, i was forced to take up a form of
defence...
i was carrying it in my pocket...

My friends, fascinated by the wierd looking
thingy. requested me to test fire....

i fired it against a locker's door,
the BB crushed into pieces upon impact,
the steel locker was marked with a small dent...

'holey shit, it has spark comming out'
the first word from my friend

later, in persue for accuracy...
i straped the BB zipgun (tube thingy then)
to a broken rubberband gun (another homemade)

the string was tied to a ring to form
the trigger...

BB zipgun, MK0 was born...
it did not have much accuracy, due to
the straw slipping off during fire...

a modified MK0 was made,
it has a top cover as a guide,
and fixed trigger guard...
only the tube is needed to be taped and
a ring to be tied...

MK1S1 was born...

Due to it's long barrel, it bacame bulky..
it's barrel and guide cover was cut down
in another building of MK1S1...
the cut-down MK1S1 was named MK1S2...

then, BB Started running out
as i also used it as pea shooter ammo...

due to the lack of BB, an alternative ammo
is needed

wax was shaped into slugs, and
wires was straped to the straw to rifle
the barrel...

6MM WAX rounds was created...

BUT until then, a whole Zipgun is discarded
after every shot...
it's a huge material waste...

meanwhile, i bought 5mm wide straws...
not very fitted for a BB...

i tried making 5MM WAX rounds for the
straw, but power is inferior...

the party popper's fuse was taken out
and a bead replaced the fuse case
(fuse case is part from the popper then)

the bead is straped to the straw,
then straw is straped to MK2...

MK2 was a modified failure design from past
(it failed due to lack of strong frame,
a stapler gun solve it)

MK2 is basically a popper gun with exposed
popper, and trigger design
(exposed for easy reloading)

the bead, unlike usual fuse case, was too
light...
the pressure built up blew the bead
onto my face, but due to straping,
the bead was much less harmless...

the 5mm design was later made into
cartriage...
a 6mm BB was sticked to the
mouth of the casing...

6 x 35 mm BB rounds was born...
it was fired in M1S1, a carbine
design to fire rounds with minimal material
waste... (not very effective)

MK3S0, a MK2 with a solid backing
(prevents bead from flying)
was created...

But the wide trigger made firing hard,
and the frame not sturdy...

MK3S1 was made...
the trigger was made like a T shape...
pressing th top of the T and the bottom
of T pull the fuse behind the grip...

MK3 was the first 'handgun' designed to
fire cartriage rounds...

at late october of 2002, MK3 failed,
due to the loose string...

at late December 2002, a design from
M1S1 was added it is a loop at the
bottom of MK3's T-trigger

the trigger pull length is reduced from
2 inch to 1.3 inch after testing...

MK4 is borned...

THE BEAD, which a failure in MK2, as
which the bead was blown off...
now made a new idea...
and blow-back design is now
in the way...

casualties of BB zipgun...

6 lobsters---all died
basically test fire samples...
1 hit by the eyes
2 hit in the head
1 hit in the lung
1 hit in the stomach
1 head was blown off (small lobster)

my friend--- injured his left arm
by an MK1 during a shootout...
i was surprised that at 5 metre he could
still bleed from that hit...
