Re: defamation of character
In order to sue he has to prove damages.

Did he lose his job over this picture? (financial damage)
Did his wife leave him? (emotional damage)

no?

Then the case will just get throw out. If he goes in front of a judge and says "baawww, the picture hurt my feelings", then the judge is just going to tell him to fuck off and stop wasting the court's time.

Also keep in mind what defamation of character really is, it is a "false statement of fact."
You were never trying to convince people that the event in the image actually happened. If you told his boss that he killed and raped a little girl and he gets fired for it, that's defamation of character. If told his boss that he's an annoying little bitch, that's not.

Read this:
http://injury-law.freeadvice.com/libel_and_slander/insults_epithets.htm
"What about insults and epithets? Are they defamatory?"
"Generally, no"

Your image is considered an insult, if anything. But like I said, it'll just get thrown out and make him look like even more of a douche bag.

Fear not, freedom of speech is your friend.
