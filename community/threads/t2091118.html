Thoughts on Shrooming
I do not enjoy mushrooms whatsoever.

I just read how some people are eating 15 grams of mushrooms and am in disbelief. I've tripped maybe 10-15 times. I don't know what type of mushrooms I get but they are always the same (Yes, I know this for fact). I buy them dried, I don't pick them or grow them.

I find that dosing is incredibly hard. I've had a few really bad trips and they happened on some of the smaller doses not the largest. The most I've ever eaten was 4 grams. I won't even attempt to describe my hallucinations. But my short term memory was reduced to about 3 seconds. Every 3 seconds I would forget what I had just been thinking about. Of course, I could do nothing but sit there for hours.

How does anyone find this fun?

The first 3 or 4 times I tripped, I loved it. I didn't even need to smoke weed (even though I did). I found the mushroom feeling euphoric almost. Now I can't stand the mushroom feeling, I just feel sick to my stomach and confused. I also get bored with the hallucinations.

But the memory problems on mushrooms just ruins it. On acid I don't get this at all and I get better hallucinations.

Anyway Totse, am I the only one who thinks this way? Also, how the fuck are people eating 15 grams of mushrooms and not ending up in the hospital screaming gibberish?
