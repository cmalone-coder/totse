You probably could do it, but it would take some serious skill with agar and a lot of time. You'd have to somehow rehdrate the tissue (maybe a H2O2 solution?) and use it to inoculate a petri dish.

You would then have to isolate the mycellium from the contaminants that would almost surely be there. You'd inocculate another petri dish with the clean agar, or maybe a few if you didn't get too bad of a contam problem on the first dish (not likely.)

Assuming you could only get enough clean agar to inoculate one more petri dish, you'd colonize that (and pray that you didn't transfer any contaminants) and do a transfer to another few agar plates.

Once those colonize and are clean, you could finally put a wedge in some grain and get something that will fruit started.
