Dude, it all about wind circulation. I've smoked in my room with my parents in the next many times. All you need is a fan in the window. Make sure on each side of the fan there is pillows or clothes or something of the like so that all of the air is going out the window due to the fan and hold your smoking device right against the fan. I would recommend a pipe for the easiest results but a bong (or even a J) would work with this method. A little smoke may escape but it's nothing a few sprays of fabreze or some spray on stuff like Tag or Axe won't cure.

Happy toking.

Edit: I just noticed you can't open a window at night..just open it when things are still going on in the house and close the curtains so your rents won't notice it. Your only really safe bet is out of the window bro. You are taking a much higher risk if you try without air circulation.
