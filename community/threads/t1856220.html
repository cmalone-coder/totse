Re: apartment style
It's not really the "cook" that is dangerous in an apartment. The actual RP/I synth is usually very well contained and SWIL would be suprised if even SWIFxxx's nose could smell it in his own apartment(let alone down the hall or next door!). The lavalamp method was created for exactly such reasons, it's fully contained, there is no high heat, and one can easily "set-it-and-forget-it" and return after the cook. Of course this method does take longer than many other approaches; however, the high level of safety and minimal babysitting, makes it a very viable option for the less-adventureous and the patient-minded.

The only problem that SWIL can see with an apartment cook is the cleaning of all the precursors. This tends to get rather "smelly" from solvents and such. The high flammability of these solvents also adds another element of danger. The e-gull was created for such reasons(besides to just clean pseudo!). No flammable solvents and the odor is minimal.

Do not heat solvents without adequate ventilation(SWIL refers to "adequate" as any place where there can be a breeze). Whatever SWIFxxx does, don't get the fire department called! What could be worse than burning all of your belongings(probably also your face) and then having a Fire Investigator put you on trial for intent to manufacture? Probably not a whole lot!

There are completely Fire-near-flammable free methods out there, if you search for them(or just think about it). There is no need to boil solvents, the only really necessary heated evaporations are done with dH2O(and even these could be done without heat and a little patience). Extremely safe, and very pure product.

Remember soaks instead of boils. It takes a little more solvent usually, but that's just how it goes. Acid/Base extractions and hot plates/light bulb heat sources are wonderful. Patience, patience, patience.
