Re: How do you know when to end it?
Depends on what kind of 'slump', the age of both of you and how long you've been together. If you mean that you've just 'lost the spark' then it can be regained or if you're heading in different directions then you can pull them together... But if you are growing tired of each other and don't put each other totally first in your lives, then it's time to end it.

The only reason to be with someone is because you can't stand to not be, in my opinion. If it's just because you're scared of being alone or 'wasting all the time we've already had!' then it won't work. Eventually, a firefly will come along, distract them and have them pursue. You get me?
