Blogging about the cannabis situation at large
I did a little bit of work doing adult blogs, but that wasn't something that was particularly fun beyond the initial shock value of writing porn. So, with what I know about wordpress, I one night registered the domain www.stonerculture.com and decided that I could blog about cannabis! Well, I've got a few (lame) posts up, but I'm not entirely sure what direction I should take with this. I have no interest in making it commercial at the moment. My ultimate goal is to have a well organized and accurate reference for all things cannabis, but first and foremost I want to create a site that will be of some interest to the casual potsmoking internet user. Things I've thought might merit potential inclusion in the future include basic info on cannabis, cannabis essays, budshots, strain smoke reports, paraphernalia reviews etc. I may decide to open a discussion forums in the future if I get the traffic to make one productive. Anyway, post any thoughts you may have about my site in this thread. If anybody here has something to say about anything cannabis-related and you don't mind putting forth a minimal grammar / spellcheck effort, I would be happy to post anything you write, as long as it's of quality.
