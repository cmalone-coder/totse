toolow
Regular
It's just a substitution command. The prefix % is a range representing the entire file (technically 1,$) for the substitution command, s. The first / says we're about to enter the pattern we want to match, which is just ^ meaning start-of-line. The second / says we're going to enter the string we want to match. \= says match as many as possible with 'att+'.
