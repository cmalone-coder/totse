Re: What places sell fireworks year-round?
Goat, if you're going to be making a large purchase, it would well worth your time to travel to Champny's Market in Bow, NH. They're still selling fireworks, at a discount and have new(as of this week) inventory. It's where I buy my stuff..and they are cheap. If you want to give them a call, the ph. no. is (603) 225-7353. It appears they they may be going to year round operation. I do not know that they are, but it seems likely.

I have a 10% off coupon(although I always do better than 10% off). If you call ahead and tell them you're buying a larger order, you too should be able to do better than 10% off.

I've been to Phantom..a long time ago, and comparatively their prices were very high.

There is also NH Fireworks in Hooksett, NH. A wholesaler. They happen to be only few miles from Champny's market.

Neither of this places has a web site, but if you do some comparison shopping, I think you'll be very pleased with the prices.

NH Fireworks may not be willing to sell to you because you live in VT. They've had legal troubles in the past.

If you travel to NH via I-89, Champny's market is about five miles from the I-89 terminus in Bow.
