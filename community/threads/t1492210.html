Kaposi's Sarcoma, as the other guy said, kicks in when your CD4 count (your helper T cells) are way below normal. They're like brownish-red patches, about a dime in diameter.

But by the time you have them, you probably also have oral thrush, plus a body weight that's way below normal (ie wasted).

DOTS on your skin aren't an indicator of AIDS, but with a prolific sex life (and unsafe!) you should do a blood test, and forget the damn skin lesions. You can get Kaposi's Sarcoma without having AIDS.
