The biting cold (long)
I'm not exactly sure where to put this thread, but I think this is the forum I read the most, and for some strange reason seems to apply.

Well, I had a very restful Saturday afternoon, Gears of War, my giant comforter, and the recliner kept me warm. It was near the stroke of midnight when I got a call from my friend, asking if he wanted to smoke some of the the finest resin (we had some shake too, it was a desperate affair). We went out to "the camper" which is across the street from his and my house. Living out in the country has its attractions I suppose, if you have close friends around.

As we sat by LED light and were chatting about life, he was getting calls from these girls that live near our houses, they are about a mile or so away. The cell reception wasn't too good, being out in the rural zones. Apparently they wanted to smoke with us, it wasn't ever really clear why they came.

So as we were about to part ways, both back to our houses, we hear these girls yelling quite a bit. The temperature at 12:00 on a cold February is pretty fucking intense, especially with a hard 22 mile wind blowing directly in your face. I think the temperature was 10 degrees, so with wind chill it felt like -5 degrees.

Being the savvy wintertime fortune-hunter I am, I was wearing my heavy duty winter shit. My olive drab insulated trench coat, made in the ukraine before it was "the ukraine," my cap, gloves, scarf, and boots. Of course under my coat i had a bunch of layers, and under my pants as well.

These girls, probably all about 15, had on very light jackets, jeans, and i noticed one even had a pair of these slip on type shoes. It was all very lightweight stuff, I could even see her socks. To compound matters, the whole lot of them were drunk. There were three in all, one of them, the one who was having the other two over, was in my eyes the most responsible. I've known her for a long time because of our close proximity.

They were all acting very strange, I'm still wondering if it was the cold, or the rum in their systems, perhaps both. In any case, they were falling all over the place (the other two girls) and were doing generally weird shit. The one girl kept falling really hard into the snow, and was yelling quite loudly. I touched one of their hands, it was incredibly cold. There was sort of a lot of confusion when they arrived, and I was just standing out in the wind slowly freezing. We conferred on a course of action, for we couldn't house these girls, nor could we stay outside with them. Being that is was about a mile walk back through the cold into the wind to get to their house, we thought it best to tell my friends mom, and have her drive them home. Its sort of "biting the bullet" but it had to be done.

These girls started walking back, unaware of our decision to have his mom wake up to take them home. So as they were walking down the road I sort of stopped in my driveway and watched them walk under the light of a full moon. They began to separate as one went off into a field and the other went up the road. I decided to head up the road and at least tell them to stick together on the road so they would be spotted and picked up easily. The "lead" girl wanted me to take her booze, i figured it was in both of our interests. The other two girls were delusional and crying, I decided to give up my trusty gloves to them, I was genuinely concerned for their welfare, something that I seldom experience. I walked for about 5 minutes with my hands exposed to the cold, and by the time i opened my door and stepped into the marvelous warmth of my house, they were beat red. I could barely feel anything or do any delicate motor movements for a good 10 of 15 additional minutes.

I'm just playing this situation over in my head. I'm wondering what they would do if they had to walk back home. In fact, I'm not absolutely one hundred percent sure they did make it. The only confirmation was a text message from my friend saying they did.

I'm writing this to gather my thoughts, and I guess to ask opinions on this. Its hard to truly grasp the intensity of the cold unless you were out there. I wonder if they would have gotten fucked up, if they had to walk back home. One could venture a guess that a passer by on the road would at least stop to investigate. People walking outside at that time, in this weather is a bit unorthodox.

I live in western NY, near buffalo. I'm 16 years old.
