RAM is easily upgradeable but it voids your warranty. I've got 2 gigs in mine.

Storage memory isn't too much of a problem - an operating system will easily fit on either the 2gb or 4gb model, and it has an integrated SD slot - meaning you can stick a 16gb SDHC card into it. I'm personally looking into attaching a 32gb CF card to the spare IDE slot the BIOS reports, so I'll let you know how that goes.

For more internal modding, have a look at "Add everything to your eeePC".
