Re: another combat post
Shoot in on him - kinda like a football tackle or in wrestling (real wrestling not WWF shit)...here's how it's done in shootfighting:
Step forward with your dominate leg and shoot in. Keep your head down a little (so he can't wrap you up in a choke) and hit his legs with your shoulders about halfway between the knee and the top of the leg. Try to wrap both of your arms around both his legs. Here's the important part: KEEP DRIVING THROUGH. A lot of people fuck up when they shoot in because as soon as they wrap the legs they stop driving through as hard....big mistake. Pretend like there is a window about 10 feet behind him and that when you shoot in your are going to drive him through that window - remember, that is VERY IMPORTANT. If, when you wrap his legs you can pick him up and continue driving then so much the better - I've seen guys get knocked out just from a shoot.
As soon as you go to the ground scramble as fast as you can to get on top of him - this is called "the mount". Make sure that you keep a wide base with your legs or else he could bridge out. Then the best thing to do is drop a couple elbows on him, like this: Imagine punching someone with a hook...well, same concept except it's with your elbow. Now don't just swing your arm like a madman - throw your elbow and drop your body, so he gets smashed by an elbow with the whole weight of your body behind it. And whoever posted about not catching him alone but rather doing it when his friends are there is right. Just get a few of your friends but have them stand back. Act completely insane when you are fighting him: be like a rabed dog. Fuck, growl if you want to. If you have a few friends there plus you are acting totally insane then they might not do anything. Remember, a little insane goes a long way...
