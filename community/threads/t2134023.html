So I finally (2 months) received my order of Modafinil (provigil) in the mail today, and I need to know somthing: What is tolerance like? Does it build quickly like amps? How long till im back at 0 again. I've taken 8-9 today already. Right now its 3:04 am and I've been informed that its a requirement that all posts concerning uppers need to be accompanied by a long, drawn out story that is only important to me and that no one else will even read (except for lurkers) (maybe) let alone care about.

Ok so I guess this could be of help to someone in the world that is like me: Has a script for adderall, and has never ever managed to make a month supply last longer than 2/ 2.5 weeks max. This stuff is ok. The only major bad affects I can see is that unlike adderrall, is that you actually can get very hungry but you don't feel like eating at all. Its a shitty feeling. It has the stimulation of maybe 30mg of amps and slight mood lift but it puts you in a place to get happy just interacting with people or doing somthing to like.

Another drawback I noticed is how much time I waste thinking about things that aren't that important. "Thought loops" I like to call them, because you tend to pick a situation and redo it over and over again in your head.

Overall I'd rate them in the perfect descriptive space of not bad but but not really great either.

I'm on amps right now myself so I'll bear with you on the rambling.

I tried Modafinil once; well, a "script's worth" or so, via free samples from my friendly quack-psychiatrist (when I bitched about the price). 200 mg tabs, only take one a day, I think...

Anyways, I do have some legitimate ADD/Inattentive symptoms, and Modafinil didn't really do jack shit for me by way of relieving any of those symptoms the way that amphetamines do (though I assume that should be a given).

What I did notice was a better sense of "wakefulness" with the Modafinil than without, but to be honest with you I wouldn't attribute it to much beyond the effects a few hundred milligrams of caffeine would give you, sans the jitters and acidy-stomach. I definitely felt less lethargic/drained on the Modafinil, but it was much more in the physical aspect rather than the mental.

I also noticed with it that if I wanted to sleep I could, but if I wanted to stay awake, I could do that, too. Kinda strange I guess but convenient nevertheless depending on the situation.
