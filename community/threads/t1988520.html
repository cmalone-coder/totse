Re: How can I improve my social skills with people/girls?
Just be confident. Do something that makes you more confident too, something which most people can't do. I picked up trampolining and parkour. As corny as it sounds, six months in, I could see how firmly rooted people are to the ground, how unconfident in motion they are, and I would just grin to myself.

Kick back, and remind yourself that you're not losing anything if a particular person doesn't like you. I was absolutely socially inept for a long time(I was "the weird kid", the "smart kid who lets us cheat off his tests", and, sadly, my high school's first "trench coat kid"), got fed up with it, and said, essentially, "Fuck it, I don't give a rat's ass anyways." I just acted natural, which to me is cocky, confident, and smart.

Next thing I know, my social circle became enormous. I haven't had a single problem with keeping anyone around either, except for the people I get rid of. It sounds arrogant, but it's the truth. I take control of what I can, let slide what doesn't.

I've never had trouble with girls when I approach them, I don't know what it is, probably confidence. I get bored and irritated with them far before they get rid of me. If I find one I like, I keep her, like I'm doing now.

If you honestly don't like people, don't worry about it, keep acquaintances for boredom/drugs/whatever you want, and make good friends of the ones you feel are worthy of it. Just get some confidence, and push it through in everything you do.
