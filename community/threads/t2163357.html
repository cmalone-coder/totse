Re: Tripping......Thoughts?
It's common when people go past the point of using psychedelics as tools for expanding their minds and understanding about what's really going on around them.

If you start to use just for the high and maintain a constant state of mind meltdown it's going to being to take it's toll.

If you're feeling this way I recommend you give yourself and your mind a good long break. At least give yourself a month. Begin a regiment of vitamins that promote healthy brain function such as Ginko Biloba, certain Nootropic drugs and the like. Give your head time to recover from all that it's been bombarded with and give your body a chance to rest.

Get yourself a good diet, drink plenty of healthy fluids such as things with anti-oxidants and try and implement a light exercise system that you do around 3 times a week. Your general mindset and health should improve if you keep to this and off the psyches for at LEAST a month. It'd be better if it was more.

Also, if you do get back into psyches, don't use them like that anymore. Utilize them as a way to gain a better understanding, tools to gain perspective. Don't use them solely for the high..that'll just get you right back in this situation.
