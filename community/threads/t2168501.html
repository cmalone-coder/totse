Re: Compacting Firework
Those fireworks are banned in some areas to prevent just what your trying to do. So know that if you get caught or something... you could fuck it up for EVERYONE.

Those have pressed whistle mix in them, by tapping them with a hammer, or more safely lightly pinching them with pliers in several placed the solid grain of the fuel develops cracks... Then when it is lit instead of burning from end to end the fire passes through the cracks and boom.

Whistle mix can be pretty bad shit. Its a lot like flash in sensitivity. AND since its compressed in those whistling fireworks its several times more composition than you would find in an equivalent sized bootleg m-80. So its much more dangerous than people realize. ALSO if it happens to be some funky illegally made and dressed up whistle from mexico or something... it could be chlorate based... then your really fucked as trying to crack the grain just got several times more dangerous.


Either way pinching or pounding on something that powerful and sensitive that near oneself is fucking retarded.
