Re: Shroom Cultivation
Lol, thanks Grunge, I'm just a research freak. I'm honored

The usual cultivation time is anywhere from 20-35 days normally. But it can take much more (not really less) depending on the growing conditions or given the fact that it was contaminated (and the spore actually won).

Also, you don't grow in soil. Most commonly used are: brown rice powder, birdseed, and rye berries/powder.

The image sequence you posted is after the Mycellium (what the spores grow into to spread around the growing area) had colonized the substrate (growing material). That usually takes about 3 weeks. After that (and after pinning), growing is very rapid.

I really recommend you read everything important in Fanaticus and let it soak in as it's the basics for growing.

Didn't answer the all important money question: I'm a poor kid so I've just bought the essentials (spore syringe, rice, birdseed, jars) and spent around $30. Considering a flush (amount grown on a cake at one harvest. (a cake can have multiple flushes)) usually has 5+ grams of dried shrooms and a gram around here costs $10..... Pretty good investment.


And no offense intended but I don't trust the Shroomery. Too many n00bs running their mouths.

Re: Shroom Cultivation
You might get lucky.

This might be over-elaborate but I'm also taking for granted that you don't have a syringe laying around.

Take a small clean glass and fill it with purified water (tap will work but more risky). Fill it with about 1/8 cup of water. Cover it with a piece of aluminum foil and secure it tightly with a rubber band.

Then place it in a pot (the stove variety, lol) and fill the pot up with water until there it almost touches the aluminum foil covering the glass (not too closely though). Heat the water to a medium-light boil for about 45min-1 hour. Let it cool till it's room temp.

After that, uncover the glass and quickly place a cap gills down over the glass (or holding it if it's too small, just wash your hands beforehand) and flick the top of the cap lightly for about 15 seconds. If you see many dark specks, you most likely have spores. Now cover the glass back up and now you have a makeshift spore "syringe".

Lol, or just order from fanaticus or thesporeworks. I'd suggest the Equador or B+ variety.
