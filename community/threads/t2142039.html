I'm sure any of the Canadians here know what I'm talking about, but for everyone else:

Omar was a 15 year old boy that came from a Canadian family with terrorist links. He was found at the age of 15 engaged in a firefight in Afghanistan (2003) and accused of killing an American soldier. He was arrested for that killing and brought to Guantanamo Bay, Cuba. The Canadian government has done nothing to help this child soldier (Under the Geneva convention as well as international law) and the Americans have interrogated and held him for the last 5 years. Omar was also an unwilling subscriber of the 'frequent flyer' program at Guantanamo - that is, every three hours a prisoner is moved around the base in order to cause sleep deprivation. They did this to a (then 16 year old) boy.

When CSIS agents showed up at Guantanamo Omar was happy that the Canadian government had finally arrived to take him home - in accordance with International law. Instead, CSIS under the direction of the Harper government continued to interrogate Omar on behalf of the Canadian government to the point where he suffered a complete mental breakdown..seen on the videotaped interrogation footage pulling his hair out and screaming 'help me, help me'.

Since I was a boy I have been taught that Canadians were seen in the International community as peacekeepers and a people who could be trusted as tolerant and respectful. The Harper government has done more to destroy this image than any government in the past 20 years (at least). How can we, how can Any of us support our governments when they are willing to torture a 15 y/o boy - break international law and the Geneva conventions...It makes me sick to my stomach that there is not more outrage about this and that it's looking like we (here in Canada) will be in for another 4 years of Conservative rule...
