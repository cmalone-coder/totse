Honestly? I'm pretty sure it happens to everyone, good and bad days. Its not really YOU that is inconsistent, it is the day. More specific:

You tossed and turned that night, dreams, whatever, so it takes you 20 minutes to get out of bed. You go to school (whether or not these two things are related) and just don't feel like talking, the subject bores you, you tire of the people. You come to the test that you studied for, and it really is just a HARD test, or you over studied, or you studied the wrong material. You go home and play your favorite video game, and you aren't in it all the way, you don't play as well. You go outside, toss around the football and drop it a couple times, there was dirt on your hands, your shoe slipped a little, whatever.

Whereas tomorrow, every little thing that went wrong, won't. The days vary more than you do. Happens to everyone, don't sweat it.
