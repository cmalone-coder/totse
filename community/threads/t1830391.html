Re: kno3/al flash
For a kno3 flash powder, I think 85% to 15% works best, but this is only if the end products are Al203 and 3(KNO2), which from a little research seem to be what they are. I know that Al203 is one of the products when potassium perchlorate, or any oxygen and Al react, so I'd assume it is with this as well. As for KNO2, potassium nitrite, it seems that would be the other end product because KNO3 decomposses to KNO2, meaning it losses one oxygen for the Al to take (KCLO4 gives up 4 oxygen which is why it is a better oxidizer). So you'd need 2Al and 3(KNO3)for a balanced equation and after taking into account molecular weight, 53.98 for 2Al and 303.21 for 3(KNO3), you'd get about 85% and 15%.
