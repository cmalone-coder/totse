Self-Sacrifice
What do you guys think of self-sacrifice, the act of heroically give your own life for some altruistic purpose.

Although I'd say I have a heroic nature, the idea of giving my own life kind of defeats the point of living, for me. I personally don't believe in any consciousness after death, so once I'm dead, nothing that's happening on Earth is my concern. When it comes down to it, I think that my experience is the only one that ultimately matters. Even if giving my life would mean saving the entire country or the entire world, I still wouldn't do it, as once I'm dead, the outcome makes no difference to me. Like, I acknowledge that the world will (probably) go on without me, but since it isn't going to affect me in any way...I don't care about it enough that I'd end my existence for the world's benefit.

I think I'm the type of person who would put himself in danger in order to protect others and myself, but when it comes to full, certain self-sacrifice, I think in any situation I'd rather take the consequences and have a chance of seeing another day. I'd be (hypothetically and presumably) okay risking my life to stop crazy Asians on Greyhounds, or crazy Muslims on airplanes, but knowingly and willingly giving my life? I can't see that happening under any circumstance.

How about you guys? What's your philosophy on martyrdom.
