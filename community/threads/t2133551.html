Level 5 trip. How to get there?
Level 5

Total loss of visual connection with reality. The senses cease to function in the normal way. Feelings of merging with space, other objects or the universe. There are powerful, and sometimes brutal, psycho-physical reactions interpreted by some users as reliving their own birth. Feelings of reaching to the beginning or the end of space and time. The loss of reality becomes so extreme that it becomes ineffable. Dream or movie like states, people have been reported seeing themselves in entirely different settings than their original setting. Many people experience religious phenomenon at this level. Often mentioned are an "all-powerful presence" or a "universal knowledge" which many equate to their idea of God or enlightenment. Earlier levels are relatively easy to describe in terms of measurable changes in perception and thought patterns. The only thing still reported to be working at a recognizable level, is the mind's voice of thought. Much is unknown about what a person actually experiences during this period, because most people actually come back explaining the experience as "inexplicable" or "uncommunicable". This effect can be produced in high doses of LSD, Ketamine, and extremely high doses of psilocybin or salvia divinorum. DMT is known to send people to level 5 with a common smoked dose, making it one of the most potent and psychoactive psychedelics known to man.

I recently took 10 grams of regular mushrooms (not the most potent strain) and got to low level 4 trip. I was just wondering how much I need to take to get to a level 5.

Also, what are the chances of "not coming back" from a trip like that, as those thoughts always run through my head when I have intense trips.

Summary: How many grams (minimum) would you suggest I take to reach this "level 5 trip?"

Re: Level 5 trip. How to get there?
http://www.mdma.net/alexander-shulgin/shulginscale.html

This is a state that you will undoubtedly know when you have it because it will blow away all of your expectations of it. First of all, I don't think there is any substance that can induce this state consistently. And by the way, DMT is NOT a +4 trip on the Shulgin scale. Neither is salvia. In my experience with them, they are extremely powerful and will take you out of your normal reality, but the key issue is that a +4 isn't about the raw power of a trip, but rather being connected to an ultimate state of unity and sublime love. People have intense trips all the time that dissolve the ego but it doesn't impart the clear knowledge or understanding of total harmony. Talking about these trips, most people see confusing images of something and think "oh wow that must have been a transcendental experience or something". When you truly have this kind of experience you can try to question it, break it down, analyze it, but it's so deep and unyielding that there is no uncertainty.

I had my first and only +4 with a shroom trip. I had taken shrooms before and new the power it had to play with emotions, send you into the depths of terror or ecstasy alternating every few seconds, etc. There were several things that made the trip that night so unique. I found myself in a state of total clarity and love, even despite my usually cynical observer that always doubts and questions everything. Shrooms are usually emotionally confusing and conflicting for me. I saw through all aspects of my life in the most accepting and loving way I have ever known and made peace with all of the good and the bad. Perhaps it was just a kind of fulfillment of my expectations of what nirvana should be, but I transcended life, death, time, space, self and other, pain, everything. No aspect of life and experience was dismissed, simply put into this knowing that everything is unified in the most glorious and eternally blissful way.

What was so strange about all this was that I was not confused at all. In fact I was questioning what I was experiencing and said to myself...yea you're tripping, this is probably just another ephemeral part of the trip and you will crash to desperation or something. But this did absolutely nothing to deter what I was experiencing and I had this feeling of certainty that "Nothing will ever be the same again. I know I'm tripping but it doesn't matter. There is absolutely no way reality will ever be the same for me ever again." I have always had this notion that one day something will happen in my consciousness and I will achieve a state of nirvana probably not in my lifetime. At that time I suddenly found myself faced with it, with the path to transcendence right in front of me and there was no going back.

During all this I experienced (not just thought about) shifting between all of the experiences I would ever have and moving through time just like moving through a hallway. I had access to the past, present, and future but it was actually all one. I saw all the times that would be considered painful and embraced it with this all-encompassing truth that was love and unity. At this point I still knew exactly where I was and didn't lose touch with my immediate environment but still my transcendence was as real as anything I've ever known. I was tripping with 3 other people and I could look at them and merge with their consciousness. Perhaps most people can't even imagine this but looking at others was the same as looking at myself, and this was the extent of the compassion and love I was feeling. Even more so, I realized I AM them. My consciousness and theirs were reflections of a single consciousness.

Phew...there's actually a lot more to that story and some events that happened afterwards, but I'll stop here for now. Without telling the rest of the story I can tell you guys now that I KNOW this state is there but we can only take rare glimpses of it. This experience was less than a year ago and I haven't had shrooms since then but I'm growing up a new batch that will be ready pretty soon. I doubt I can repeat this experience but even this single experience has been one of the most meaningful things in my life.
