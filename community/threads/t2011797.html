The number 7
So, most of us have probably seen the number 23? Seems that it's all coincidental that we see it in everyday life, and that we're not going to have a different fate after death than anyone else performing some bizarre 23 ritual. But that's besides the point. This thread is about why the number 7 is such a religious number, and why it should give clues as to tell apart fact from fiction.

Let's look at religion back in the day. The oldest religions all have something to do with astrology. Funny enough, this explains my point. These religions, (Romans, Greeks, Judaism, whatnot) and the religions spawned from them (Christianity, Islam, whatnot) will follow the same patterns. Patterns like symbols. The number 7 being common in almost every, if not all, religions. Why? Because as I said, many ancient religions are based on what was in the sky, or what gave the ancients their imagination. Planets. And how many planets can we see from earth with the human eye? Five, plus the sun and the moon. Seven. So is it not safe to say that the number seven when used in any religious text is purely a product of mythology and not of the real world?

Now saying this, please don't feel that because of this one aspect all religions must be wrong, that's not true. This thread covers one aspect, not every, and so should not be used to judge a religion as a whole.
