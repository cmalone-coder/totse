The follies and hypocrisies of the Roman Catholic faith???
Before I start the main theme of this Topic; I would just like to state that I admire people who properly follow any religion, and especially admire their faith. I would also like to say that, as yet, I am agnostic.

Also, the main objective of this topic is to clear up a few things that have become apparent during my study of Christianity, in particular Roman Catholics. And not, as it may appear to some readers, to criticise the religion.

Firstly, my understanding of the time after Jesus death is that he died, was laid to rest in a tomb given to him by a rich man, and then a stone was rolled across the entrance.

When he 'rose from the dead', the first witnesses to this were the 'Holy Women', Mary Magdelene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome. If Christianity can be defined as the belief that Jesus died, was buried, and rose again, then surely these women, the first witnesses, were also the first christians?

If this is true, then why does the Roman Catholic faith not permit women priests??

Also, Jesus preached that the only way to get into heaven was poverty, seving others, and humility (see the widows' offering, andthe rich young man, Mark's Gospel). Why, therefore, is the Roman Catholic Church one of, if not the richest organisations in the world??
