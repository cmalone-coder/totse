That's because it doesn't. Meditation will not change your eye colour.

Your eye colour may change when you are very young. When I was young 5-6 I had blue eyes and blonde hair by the time I was 12 I had brown eyes and brown hair.

(incidently that is why the blue eye blonde hair combination is considered attractive as it is a sign of youth in caucasians)

After that you eye colour will stay the same. In rare cases of disease your eye colour will change (such as cataracts) but this will cause severe loss to your vision or blindness.

If you want to change your eye colour contacts will work fine. Try them out.

In fact with modern cosmetics dyes and contacts you can change your natural appearance to what ever you want to be. Personally it's to much bother to do all the time but it can be fun now and again.
