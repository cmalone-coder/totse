Re: annie bircher's please help
lets say you have your a/b out the way and your ephedrine is in your hexane and is in a nice squeezee bottle with hose--your sodium metal is cut into match stick size peices and in a bowl of hexane---your quenching water is in its squeezee bottle(you quench to fast, your gonna wear ammonia)---your hose and rubber stopper are in place--the stirring bar coming down off rubber stopper is clean---bottle is marked to show wanted ammonia level--your bottle is hanging in a bowl shaped net hung from a rafter and has the desired level of ammonia in it(of course your put in 3 large marble to stop bumping and help stirring when you spin the bottle in its net)---you dont drop that sodium in to fast--and you dont squirt in that ephy/heaxane to quick(no matter how good you are ,one day your going to squirt in to much and you will have a volcano spitting ammonia and your goodies at you)--you dont spin that bottle to quick,watch the stirring bar inside ---always make sure during reduction you can see some blue --if no blue add match stick of sodium---if you must uncork to add sodium or quench then let sit for 30 secs untouched---after reduction is finished sit in bucket of room temp water,,then over ten mins increase temp to hot water---wash stopper hose with hexane to get the goodies spat up there--(hose running straight up and down lets it flow back in)--wash bottle after pouring goodies out with hexane and pour into gassing beaker with goodies--when your adding everything to the ammonia and spinning it,,be carefull but assertive, this isnt for the faint hearted, take command of it and conquer,,goodluck

Re: annie bircher's please help
friends use sulphuric acid98% dripping onto hydrochloric acid34%,,,they have two jars,,the sulphuric jar is higher than the hydrochloric jar(so when started it works on drip feed),,they have a hose(600mm length) from bottom of sulphuric jar to the top of the hydrochloric jar,,,they have another hose, top of sulphuric jar to blow start the drip feed(400mm length),,,they have 1 hose(300mm length) from top of hydrochloric jar to heavy walled 6mm dia glass tube(300mm length)--this hose/glass tubing is for immersion into solvent for gassing meth---to get it started; they blow on the blow pipe of the sulphuric acid,,this increases pressure in sulphuric jar forcing acid to flow down the hose into the hydrochloric jar,,when dripping is seen into hydrochloric jar blowing is stopped and drip feed is allowed,,when heavy smoke begins to appear out of glass pipe, pipe is immersed in meth solvent and gassing is commenced---all hose is 6mm od clear soft hose--jars are whatever you have but must be airtight--only place 100ml of sulphuric acid into sulphuric jar,,and 50ml of hydrochloric acid into hydrochloric jar---when smoking stops replace hydrochloric acid in jar--when using this system make sure its done in a windy or well ventilated area with fan as this gas is toxic
