Re: Best Flash Mixes?
The only problem with KNO3/Mg is that KNO3 is hygroscopic and Mg can ignight when it comes in to contact with moisture. Mixing the two could cause some problems. Just keep it dry and store it in an air tight container.

Al flash is much safer than Mg flash.

Re: Best Flash Mixes?
Flash powders I've made and a few comments:

1.KClO4/Al- This is the grand master of flashes. I used 2 micron flake Al so you know it was fast. And when I had chinese air- milled perc it was ridiculous. That perc flowed so easy it was orgasmic. The two components practically mixed themselves.

2.KNO3/S/Al- Heh, this comp was unusually difficult to light, but that was probably because my KNO3 was a little coarse compared to the other two components. It didn't light until a few seconds under a blow torch! When it did light though, it burnt faster than ,say, some fast BP. So it's a good alternative. Grind your KNO3 down to a fine powder and it should work quite nicely.

3.KNO3/Mg- Due to the large particle size of the KNO3 and Mg I used, I cannot say anything conclusive about his comp. It burned slow like crudely made BP. I'll have to try it again myself with finer powders.

Sensitivity- Keep moisture out of 2 and 3 at all costs. But you should keep moisture out all your comps. Who the hell lets their comps get wet/abosrb moisture freely? Don't store mixed comps either. There's no reason to, it's stupid.
