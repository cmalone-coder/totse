ITT: I help you find a job.
This is is a thread for young people who need a job, any job, most likely minimum wage. Alot of people dont know how to find one, or how to get past the unicru survey application, so here it goes.

Step 1:

Look for different workplaces around your home that you can see your self working in. Pharmacies, grocery stores, fast food joints, electronics stores, movie theaters etc. Don't worry about places that sell alcohol, most are allowed to hire people under 18. I work in a CVS, and we sell liquor, alcohol, prescriptions etc. Ask your friends around if they know anyone who works in a store where they can help you get a job. This makes things alot easier.

Step 2:

After you find a couple of spots you would like to work in, get online and find their websites. Look around for their phone number, you will need this for later. Now look for the career option usually located in small letters on the bottom of their home page. Now find the Hourly Store position employment application.

Step 3:

Start filling out all the information, name, social, etc. Now when you get to the survey part you need to remember this. No matter how you really feel, you always need to put your answer as either Strongly Agree or Strongly Disagree. You NEED to ALWAYS have a strong feeling towards whichever side you think is most appropriate. Never choose slightly agree, slightly disagree, or neutral, NEVER. For some reason this lowers your score, and you don't want that, you want to pass. Now finish the application

Step 4:

Ok, you finished the application, now go apply in a couple of more spots online just like this. 3 Business days after you apply you need to call the store and ask an associate to speak to a manager about employment. Make yourself sound professional. Once the manager picks up tell them you name and that you applied for employment at their store and that you would like to see where you stand. They will usually look around for the application and tell you that they will call you back on a certain day. Thank them, now call up the other places that you applied to and do the same.

Step 5

Now should be the day that they need to call you. Wait until maybe 2 or 3pm. If you don't get a call then call the store, and tell the associate you would like to speak to a manager about employment. When the manager picks up tell them your name and tell them that you applied for employment and that you would like to know where you stand. Now they will either tell you to come in for an interview, in which case move on to step 6, that they aren't hiring right now, in which case you just forget about it, or that they will call you back, in which case you need to repeat step 5 and call them back again.

Step 6

Ok, now this is important. Clean yourself up, put on a dress shirt, maybe a tie, some nice pants and some nice shoes. Dress formal, as if you're the manager of the whole place. Be on time for your interview! When you get there tell an associate that you have a job interview, they will call the manager. Put on a smile, sound professional, and take it from here. Know how to lie, they will ask you "Tell me a bit about yourself " make up shit about your interest in their company, your great communication skills, and your need for a job to help support your family etc etc. They will ask why you want to work for their company, why did you leave your previous job etc etc. You are on your own from here...




Just be confident, smile, try to sound professional, and fight for the job. If they say they will call you, and they don't call you, you call them. This is your job, you deserve it, fight for it.
