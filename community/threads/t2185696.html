The final chapter
Not more than half an hour ago, I closed the book on a part of my life that lasted for over four years. I can't even begin to describe the weight that's been lifted off of my shoulders. It feels like a magical new door just opened, that I will step through with a feeling of freedom and ironically, love, that I have never before felt.

I can attribute the inspiration to finally move on to another girl, who, through peculiar circumstances, somehow managed to show me what love actually is, and it was far from what I was feeling with the girl I was with. Fittingly enough, you all know the girl that gave me this insight. So in a way, I must give totse it's credit, for it let me cross paths with someone who showed me something that changed my life in a drastically positive way. I'm also starting to truly believe that all things happen for good reason. I urge you all to stop and think about your life in this respect.



I will also note the magnitude of this decision, as most people thought me and this girl would get married. Our families are now very, very good friends and she is more beautiful than all the girls on totse, except one of course. I only say this to put it in a perspective that you might all understand..:D So you can begin to see how absolutely enormous this decision was for me and those around me. And I will happily report, that I haven't felt this good for as long as I can remember. :)


May this perhaps be a call to action for those who are in the same situation that I was. To those people I must say: grow a pair (girls too), take responsibility for your life, trust in yourself, and fucking do it. I am a walking success story of the page turned over. For that I am thankful.
