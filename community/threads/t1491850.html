Back in my college undergraduate years, I roomed with biology majors (one in immunology). The "facts" at the time were that there were no documented cases of HIV being transmitted from woman to man via cunnilingus. This was 1988-1991, though. Since then a few cases have cropped up.

Vaginal fluid doesn't contain HIV in nearly as high concentrations as semen, blood, or even breast milk. It is unlikely, though possible, that HIV can survive oral ingestion. Between the two, it is safe to say that contracting HIV via cunnilingus is possible but unlikely from an HIV-positive woman.

As I said more than once, it's always better to err on the side of safety. I just don't like to see facts be misrepresented due to mass hysteria.
