LSD is my favorite drug.
The visuals are crazy as hell. The craziest thing is to look in front of a mirror and watch yourself turn old, young, decay, inflate, change colors. I just did it yesterday for my first time. Really awesome shit! I was in total awe of my surroundings I frequently found myself just saying "ahhh shit *sharp exhale*" in reaction to everything that was going on around me. I was able to see the emotions of paintings the artists were trying to convey by way of faces hidden in the pictures. I realized the reason tye-dye is such a big part of hippy/acid culture. It's because you see that shit! I felt as if I tuned in to the same frequency as all sentient beings and was one with all of the universe. Also, nothings cooler than watching your hand turn into a decrepet skeleton hand and back. No point to this thread. Just thought I'd share.
