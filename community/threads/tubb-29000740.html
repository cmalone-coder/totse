Re: fighting moves
The trick to punching is not to flail around like a ragdoll. It sounds stupid, but in some cases it takes ages to teach someone how to punch properly.

When you punch you want to cast your fist in a straight line with your body. It's kind of like swinging a baseball bat - You want to step forward and get all of your momentum behind your fist.

Don't repeatedly flail with one fist like you're knocking on someone's door. That's no way to do damage. Watch a boxing match sometime and keep a careful eye on how the fighters punch. They don't swat at each other, and when one of them does hook he pivots and puts his body behind it. If he knows what's good for him he'll follow up with a straight punch with the other hand, too.

If you can't hack that, I suggest the Power Dive strategy: Get a good running start, jump before you meet your target, and either elbow or kick him down with all your weight and momentum. Unless the guy's built like a sumo wrestler there's really not a whole lot he can do about it but dodge it. If he does, sweep him as soon as you hit the ground. As soon as he hits the pavement, proceed to beat him mercilessly.

Simple, really.

~Zero
