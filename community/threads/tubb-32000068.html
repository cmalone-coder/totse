quote:
Originally posted by SocialistX:
How do you loss say 50-60 pounds really fast,Within 30 days and not ending up with a eating disorder?


Is there a reason you need to do this? Safe weight loss is 2-3 pounds a week, you're talking about two pounds per day. If you're extremely hefty (which, if you can lose 60 pounds, I suppose you must be), doing the Atkins diet (as few carbs as possible) might get you a good chunk of the way there- the more fat you're carrying around, the more you can lose in a short time span.

Remember that if you do something drastic, something you can't possibly live with permanently (like a water diet), then when you start eating again, you'll balloon back up- maybe even further than before. If that's ok with you, fine, but for your general health in the future, you might want to try and find a method you can do comfortably.
