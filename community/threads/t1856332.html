Clear pale yellow liquid with the RP dropped like a bad habit!
Yellow does not mean done, CLEAR means done.

Whether you were going for a long reflux or dry p/p, I garuntee that rxn is no where near finished and the product is shit. There's no way in hell an HI reduction is gonna happen that fast. Period.

Yes it's very true that light sets of the reaction. It has been well documented at the hive. However, just becuase in four minutes all the iodine color is gone does not mean it is done (or the pseudo even began to convert) at all. All it means was that the I2 was reacted and turned into HI. Thats it. The reaction with HI and pfed to form iodo-pfed and water is fairly quick, but not that quick. And the dissociation of HI into nascent forms of both hydrogen and iodine, followed by the nascent I reacting with iodo-pfed producing I2, and the nascent H saturating the carbon forming meth takes way longer than simple conversion to the iodo-intermediate.

I can garuntee the product from a four minute rxn with 6g's of pfed is going to be extremely dissapointing and total crapola. If you think that the product is good, then you need to re-think your reductions cuase your producing nothing but half converted crap my friend (with four minutes more like 1/10th converted crap).

SWIM doesn't mean to insult you at all. SWIM just can't even count the amount of times SWIM thought he was producing very good high quality product, only to get better at the HI reduction and become surprised time and time again at how much better it was than what SWIM thought was already the best. SWIM has mastered the dry p/p to the point where the product is very fucking excellent and very clean and pure which is still a level of quality I would except even now (and I have very high standards). But when SWIM did his first long 12 hour reflux instead of the dry p/p, the product just surprised me all over again, and quality was something god-like.

You'll see with time as you experiment and learn.
