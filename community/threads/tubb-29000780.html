Re: kung-fu claw/talon weapon.
I don't have any historical information besides our bandito friends in India, but I do have a bit of practical knowledge to contribute.

I've seen plans and pictures of Vega style claws affixed to armlets and other devices that strap to the wrists; These are univerally useless. There will be entirely too much leeway and movement between your hand, wrist, and arm to make an effective blow with the claws.

The best way to handle claws is with the age old "punch claw", essentially three nails pounded through a dowel that you hold onto. Longer slashing style claws can be made by affixing a center handle (which you hold in your palm) to a plate that rests on the back of your hand. The claws are affixed to this. As long as you maintain a good grip on the handle the claws should remain stable enough relative to your hand to be a useful weapon.

~Zero

Re: kung-fu claw/talon weapon.
If you're going to go that route I would dull the bases of the blades with a file so you don't run the risk of slashing up your own palm.

If you constructed the blades right you could just drill some holes and bolt them to the back plate. Otherwise it would require welding, as that's the only other viable option for durability.

You could, I suppose, cut the plate and blades as once piece. It would take a little bit of work with tempering and such to make this work with regular plate steel, but I'll wager it could be done.

~Zero

Re: kung-fu claw/talon weapon.
����One of the main points of the claw is for it to look badass. Concealability is just not a factor in this case.
����I get your plan, but it could be improved. For attaching the claws I would use bent pieces of metal formed into a hollow T shape. You could bolt these directly to the deck, as it were, of the claw. Also, slashing with a device like this will put all of the stress of the blow on the straps holding the arrangement onto your arm. I think the best course of action would be to simply have a large piece of heavy fabric as the strap that is as long as the armlet portion of the claw and secures with velcro on the ends. This way the force of the blow would be spread along your entire forearm. This would make the weapon less prone to fall off and much less prone to cut of the circulation to and/or break your arm.

~Zero
