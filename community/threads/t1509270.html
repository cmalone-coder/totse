Age 8 I discovered my parent's drinks cabinet. I had a mouthful of gin and suddenly i felt 'normal'...or at least what I assume everyone else assumes to be normal.

Basically for whatever reason (and there is no end of psuedo science on this) I am physically and mentally always a double scotch under par.

My body needs alcohol. Or rather put another way, I am addicted to alcohol, not to being drunk. Or yet another way of expressing it would be that I am physically but not mentally addicted to alcohol.

It gets real complex, cos basically there are as many types of alcoholic as there are people who drink.

I can (and did last year) drink a litre and a half of spirits a day for 6 months, everyday and then stop cold.

I stopped last year in August and all i have had since then is 2 glasses of champus at New Years. I will have another couple of glasses of champagne on the 16 of May (wedding Ann.).

I can stay dry literally for years (longest was 8 years) but there will come a day and on that day I will be straight back on the same dosage.

OK I'm rambling, I know.

What I'm trying to say is, don't worry about it.
