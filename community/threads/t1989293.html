Re: DIY Taser Blowgun
Quote:
Originally Posted by 13579
Actually, I have done this.
Two recharge ports?

Could you take higher quality pics and show some instructions?
That his the best that I have. Its from my camera phone. If you look at the circuitry from a disposable camera it is actually quite small. I got rid of the flash bulb and the copper guides for the battery, and disconnected the capacitor from the board. I soldered wires into the boards spot for the capacitor. I made sure to remember the polarity, I didn't want a capacitor popping on me. Most of the parts came from a mechanical pencil. The ports are the black plastic tube w/ copper wire coiled inside. The battery connection is made with the spring from the pencil. The eraser is sitting on top of the copper "spring" that charges the capacitor. I pierced the eraser and ran a piece of wire through it to keep it from popping out of the front. Using the black tube from the pencil, I used them as spacers to raise the board off of the bottom, where the button would be functional. I wired it up and taped it up. Now i just put the capacitor leads in the two black ports and push the charge button until the red LED glows. I was just in the zone that day.
