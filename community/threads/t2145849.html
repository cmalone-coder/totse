City flourishes under anarchy
Ever heard of Kowloon City?
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kowloon_Walled_City

"Although the walled city was described as a hotbed of criminal activities, the daily lives of its dwellers were largely organized by the residents themselves, rather than by the Triad. Most residents were not involved in any crime and lived peacefully within its walls. Many charities and religious groups helped to improve the lives of residents"

"With the Triads weakened, a sort of synergy blossomed, and the Walled City began to grow almost organically"

I know that the Libertarian ideology, particularily with Ron Paul made a lot of noise on the Internet, so I thought this would make a good example on what a society becomes without any authority. I think that it really requires a deep thought to apprehend this ideology with a critical point of view.
