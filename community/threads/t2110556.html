I just got started the winter before last. The seeds vary in quality....some people grow them from a can of fucking McCormicks spice jars, which I find pretty damn funny.

I get my seeds from the natural/bulk/hippy foods section at Fred Meyer....Kroger I think it's called in some states. Hell, any bulk poppy seeds will work if you make sure the content is right....they'll say Papaver Somniferum, P. Somniferum, or P. Somniferum L.

I've gotten near 100% germination with these seeds :)

The legalities are hazy.....technically, you can't grow them. But my mom's neighbor (in the next city over) has tons of them growing in the front yard for anybody to see....fuck it, they're flowers!

I got all fired up, set up an indoor grow area, got lights, all this shit, then realized they grow much better outside. I live in Oregon, so lots of rain here during spring, hot as fuck in the summer...moderate weather.

Literally just sprinkle them where you want them to grow (now's the time, spring-time), don't even bother covering them up w/ dirt. Water daily until they get about 1-2 inches tall, thin them out because they're gonna get BIG and need a bit of room.

Try to put them in a place where they'll get sun all day (east-west).

Fertilize when they reach 6" with regular Miracle Gro mixed properly.

You'll know your getting close when the poppy pods are egg sized. Wait until they flower (my GF loves them), the leaves drop, and then 10 days pass after the last leaf drops. Score very shallow at night (like the width of a piece of construction paper), and in the morning, use a razor or spoon to scrape up your goodies! Continue until the pod won't give any more. Cut your plant down, hang it upside down stem, pods, and all to dry for a few weeks and you can make pod tea later if you get into a pinch.

Hope that helps....it's pretty basic instructions, I guess.....There are guides online, but don't try too hard, you'll just get frustrated trying to Over-fertilize and get all fcking technical....let God's poppies grow naturally and they'll take care of you.

You can PM me if you have any other questions, bro.

Re: Trying Opium..
I do know that McCormicks' (sp?) are legit, believe it or not.

I forgot to mention it above, but all poppy seeds for baking or that you buy at a store are P. Sominferum. All.

The problem is that some brands are lightly baked (!) at a low temperature before they are packaged for selling, rendering them useless for anything but baking, since they are destroyed by the heat and will never germinate.

There are 'low morphine' varieties, but ironically, they are the ones you have to special order or buy at a nursery......a couple names of low-o poppy strains are:

-Przemko
-Norman

Everything else is good to go as far as I know.

Happy growing.

Also, I know nothing about AUS, but aren't your hot and cold months opposite from the USA?.....you'll want to start your seeds when the days are 12 hours (or so) long, because poppies won't bloom outside until they get sustained 16 hours of light.

It takes about 3-4 months from seed to opium.
