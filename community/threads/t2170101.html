Sounds like there's a hygroscopic cut in there? It could be that the cut was a mixture of benzocaine & mannitol or some sort of sugar. I would guess the residue is due to the cuts, lactose doesn't tend to be absorbed very well through the nose.

Anyway, I recommend you search for 'Cocaine Handbook', specifically 'Chapter 7 - Purification' and read up on how to do an acetone wash. You could also try dissolving what you have in a very small amount of water, (~20ml) with a little alcohol (read:vodka) added, filter off any insoluble cuts, and use a miniature mister type bottle to spray it up your nose.
