When I dropped my first slide, only the handle part broke off. I proceeded to put a hole in a bottle cap and push the slide through to the rubber o-ring. I now own a nifty gravity bong cone piece. I just place it on top of any properly modified bottle and I'm good to go. I've never burned myself grabbing it by the bottle cap part.

Before I had the chance to get a new slide, we got a little desperate and tried to use it without the handle anyway. We had to slide it out with something like a lighter and then grab it by the o-ring but not touch any of the glass. That shit is difficult to do right sober, let alone stoned. After using my torch lighter on it, I couldn't even get my fingers near that thing it was so damn hot. I remember at one point when it was hot like this, I just pushed the lighter against it and flung the thing out in the air as I finished cashing it.

Needless to say, we were pretty happy when I finally got a new one.

Keep the slide wrapped in a towel when you're not using it. That's what I do now, and it's worked so far.
