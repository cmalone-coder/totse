Re: Should mods be able to check IP addresses?
It's also a lot easier for a supermod or admin to check an IP. A moderator would have to check it at every post. I can just go into a control panel and search by user for IP's and get a complete list of the IP's the user has used. I wrote a script that takes in two such lists and tells you how many IP addresses were the same between the two lists. If it's more than two, it's usually multiple accounts (there's always the possibility that two people have the same host and dynamic IP's and just happened to overlap on an address).

The old method of checking IP's was a bit invasive, I think. Since you can't get ALL of the IP addresses at once, you've got to put the IP's that you DO have through more tests to see whether or not the users are the same. Even then, it's not entirely accurate unless you happen to find an exact match (which is rare). You'd have to search for the user's posts, then pull IP's out of each of them, then run them all through arin.net or ripe.net to see if they resolved to the same host (to know whether or not you've got a lead). I think the step involving looking the IP up on a database to find it's origin is too invasive. Also, I always felt like I was invading someone's privacy since I had to go through such lengths just to figure out whether or not two people were the same.

With the script I wrote, it's a lot easier to just get a definitive yes or no answer as to whether the people are the same. You don't even have to look at the IP's much less run them through a database.

If anyone wants the script for use outside of totse, it's is at: http://zoklet.net/ip_comp.pl.txt but it runs in O(n^2) so don't give it big lists.
