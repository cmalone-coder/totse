Drug dogs are rare as hell in th US and mostly reserved for international flights (say coming from Columbia). If you're really worried then just put the bud in a plastic film cannister, spray the outside with Lysol and put it in inside of an xray-proof film bag.

I actually got caught once at SFO Airport with a quarter ounce of green bud.

I had put the bud in a big semi-clear (dumb) Centrum Vitamin bottle and kept it in my front pocket. I proceeded to go through the security metal detector thinking everything was cool. The goddam metal detector buzzed and the guard told me to go back through. I took off my belt, smiled and then walked through again.......buzzzzzzz - the fucking thing went off again. I couldn't believe it, I didn't have any metal on me! This time the security officer walks up on me, pulls me aside and uses the detector wand. He brushes it all over my body and wouldn't you know it... the fucking thing buzzes right over the pocket with the centrum bottle. He sees that my pocket is stuffed and asks me to take out whatever is in there. I REFUSE. Then the shit hits the fan. Before I know it....I'm surrounded by 4 officers. They pull me over to the side. The main security guy asks me what the hell is going on and I tell him I have a little weed in my pocket.
I give it to him. He examines it. He puts it back into the bottle, hands it back to me and tells me that he "knows that I'll dispose of it in the nearest trash recepticle" Can you fucking believe it? He must have been a smoker too. Well, of course I just went on my merry way and didn't happen to see any trash cans on the way to the plane.

Turns out there was a fucking quarter and a few dimes in the pocket with the Centrum bottle and that made the metal detectors go off. So....if you hide something don't be an idiot like me and keep change or any metal even close to it.

Just to note: The Bay Area and SFO are definately more liberal than most of the country, so I dunno what would have happened in say...Nebraska.

I was also caught with various narcotics and a big knife at Heathrow Airport, England. Suffice to say they just took away the knife and gave me back the narcotics. I LOVE LONDON!...but that's another long story.

Here is an old trick to get a small amount narcotics through any airport security or customs. (arriving)

You need to have a large leather document/multi-credit card wallet (about 8 inches long and 1/2-1 inch thick works best)

smash all your drugs flat, put in zip-lock baggies, wipe the baggies clean of fingerprints. Put the baggies deep inside the wallet. Wipe down the inside of wallet clean of fingerprints.

Keep no personal identifying documents or anything else in the wallet except the drugs. If you can get a hold of them...it would be smart to insert some stolen ID or some identifying papers of a stranger.

Upon exiting the plane, look for any cameras, if there are none then take out the wallet and just hold it and proceed to customs.

When you get close to customs you will survey the environment. If dogs are present or you look like you going to get searched then you need to just drop the wallet to the ground (pretend to tie your shoe or just drop with nobody seeing you). If you still have the wallet in your hand and you're pulled aside to get searched then simply hand the wallet to the customs offical first and tell them you just found this on the floor 20 feet back (there is nothing they can do to you...they can't prove you didn't just find it). Just keep the wallet ready to discard at all times. You'll lose the drugs but that's a lot better than potentially going to jail.

If they find narcotics hidden in any bag of yours....then you have no defense.
