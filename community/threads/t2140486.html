Re: power supplys
What the hell kind of keyboard is this?

You need to give it a 12v power supply. Also you need to know the amperage. If the keyboard draws more than amps than the powersupply puts out, it'll potentially kill the powersupply over time and the keyboard wont function properly.

It might be listed like this

12v 3A.

Meaning you need a 12 volt powersupply capable of 3 amps.
