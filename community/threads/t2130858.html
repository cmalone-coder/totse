You seem to be under the impression that the money SSI sends you is yours to do whatever the hell you want with. Save for college, blow on weed, eat out at McDonald's all month long, wtfever.

I don't claim to know all the ins and outs of SSI, but I'm quite certain that's not the case. That money they send each month is supposed to be for your care, feeding, clothing, everything else. A responsible parent isn't just going to hand it over to you. Disability means they can't afford to support you, so they're using that money to do it.

That said, it doesn't sound like your dad is very responsible, if he's using the money to support you for car payments. Fair complaint, and I think there are ways to report fraud to the disability people. You'd have to look up the number, though.

That money isn't for your college education or life after you move out of the house, unless your parents set it aside for that. Much as it may suck, society supports you until you turn 18. Not after.
