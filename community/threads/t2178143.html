Re: Is my dad in danger if...
While mixing sedatives and alcohol is never a good idea for anyone, I'd say that Valium is probably one of the most forgiving benzos you could pull it off with. True, the half-life is among the longest of all benzos, but the relative strength on a milligram/milligram basis in comparison with some of the other common commercial benzos is fairly low. Not that it's safe at all, because it isn't, and I don't condone and/or advise anyone to run out and start chugging Old Crow with a fistful of your grandmother's diazepam pills.

And by the way, OP, anyone who ingests Valium on top of a 12 pack is going to be "really fucking wasted"-- even with something as low as 5 or 10 mg. In fact, most people who aren't alcoholics, frat boys, or internet tough-guys are likely going to be wasted off the 12 pack alone.
