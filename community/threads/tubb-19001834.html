Re: i have no weight to weight the amount of KNO3 or S for BP
For the most part measuring any solid by volume will be innacurate, and may cause the end product to fail to work.

Anything powdered (which is every solid ingredient in pyrotechnics, generally) can have a varying density - Even among batches of the same ingredient. The volume of a material will vary with particle size, how densely it's packed, humidity, quality, and so on. Thus it is best to use weight measurements at all times rather than volume.

Weight measurements will rarely translate directly into volume measurements. Once you've worked out all the densities and particle sizes to convert weight to volume accurately you might as well just buy a scale and weigh it...

~Zero
