Re: Car hopping tonight. Tips please?
Here's how I do it. I go between 3am and 4am, and wear regular kind of clothing with something to cover my face (bandana, neckwarmer, etc). Don't bring your phone or anything at all with you, because it's just something that can be dropped accidently of you have to run fast. I then walk down a street (those townhouse areas of neighbourhoods with many little streets are good because they are so close together, the cars are much closer together, and they usually have many places/directions to run) and check all the cars I walk by quickly (speedwalk!) If there's no flashing light, check the handles quickly on the doors (Ive found cars where 3 doors are locked, but one isn't before). If it's all locked, just speedwalk down the street to the next one. Also if the car I'm checking is right up near the garage, I check to see if the garage is unlocked, it's very easy to open a garage door quietly by hand, and people don't expect it nearly as much. Once finding a car unlocked, get in, and CLOSE THE DOOR, neighbours glancing out there window as they take an early morning piss will notice a car door open, and the light on from it being open, then check the obvious, dash, between the seats, under seats, backseat, compartments, then pop the trunk as you get out and check the boot quickly then shut that and keep moving, use pathways, and try to stay away from sketchy streets (ones that are long with no turnoffs, dead ends, etc.) If I get something good, I move a few streets over, if I get something really nice (ipod, laptop, nice camera) I go home right away. I only started car shopping this winter, but for the hour I go out every few nights, I always find around 3-7 cars, and usually at least 1 garage. Winter might seem like a bad time because it's harder to run and stuff, but I ALWAYS go out if theres a snowstorm at night, thats the best oppurtunity, the wind will cover all of your sound, and the lack of visibility covers you too, I have NEVER EVER seen a single person in my area out between 3am and 4am in a snowstorm. Good luck.
