Walked out from my job on the 3rd day...
To all those that suggested I get a job: wait for it... wait for it... fuck you. That's right.

A bit of background: I got a job with this landscaping company. Mainly for experience in the field, but also to earn some cash. Monday and yesterday I was at some big house on a golf estate, then today I was at another, even huger house in our most expensive neighbourhood. The type of place you see on those lifestyle shows. $3m easy.

Shovelling and wheelbarrowing sand from A to B, carrying heavy blocks, loading 40kg sandbags into the back of a truck and an assortment of other pleasures. All in the hot sun from 8 till 5. And on top of that, most of the other workers don't even speak english... Total bullshit. So today after carting my 187th wheelbarrow of fertilizer from the driveway, up the hill to the pool area I just said 'fuck this'. I got my stuff and walked off. As I was a few houses down the guy I was working with told me to come back and told me I 'can't leave because I have to speak to the foreman first'. He wouldn't let me go either (wtf I'm a free man). I told him I was wasting my time there and refused to carry on digging. He called the project manager guy and explained the situation and got back to me, basically saying: stuff off. As I left again, he asked me for my overalls and boots back. Nice... I'm supposed to walk home 20km's in my undewear and no shoes? Fuck that, I told him I'd bring them in on Monday.

In all fairness though, I never *asked* to be paid for this. I joined to get experience, NOT to be a common labourer. I'll probabaly call the main guy later just to explain so it doesn't end all bitter.

I've learned at least one thing though - some people were born to work hard and others were born to live it up off the former's work. Whenever someone complains that criminals should stop stealing and get a *real* job, the jokes on them. Criminals win.
