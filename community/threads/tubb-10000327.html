Vanilla Sky
I saw this earlier this week, on DVD. I watched it 4 times including the time w/ the commentary.

This is Cameron Crowe's third (and best) consecutive project as writer/director, all quality work. What an amazing writing life he's had. It's no wonder the subject of pop media permeates his work. Although Vanilla Sky is what he calls a "cover" movie, he lends much richness to the concept, thereby making it his own.

This movie is a great mind-fuck; life, death, dreams and delusion... which is his master?

...and the scene where Cruise yells, "What the fuck is happening!!!" while he's hammerin away at Diaz, is classic.

I can't believe there was no topic already here, this is a great movie.
