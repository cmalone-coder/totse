Pagan (Derived from pagus; someone who lives in the country.) is a derragatory term used by Christians in the cities to group the peasants who didn't 'modernise' and become Christians.

Nowadays, to refer to yourself as Pagan, you're saying that your belief system is similar or totally like that of the Old, Pre-Christian and Earth-Centered Religions of Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. (Mostly Europe)

You don't have to worship all the Pagan gods to be a Pagan. In actuality, Buddhists, Hindus, and anything that isn't Christian, Jewish, or Muslim is Pagan.

I personally believe in the God 'energy' of the Universe which has a Male and Female side to it (God and Goddess). Most Wiccans believe that this is the case, and all the individual Dieties worshipped are just ways to contact the main Energy, usually the Diety matching the intent or comfort of the worshipper.

So, Paganism applies to all who share the non-JudeoChristian mindset. Hell, I've seen Athiests consider themselves Pagans because they believed in a spiritual existance, just not God itself.
