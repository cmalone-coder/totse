So I have stopped smoking. Been smoke free for a week now and no problems actually.

So today she calls me to see if I want to go to dinner. Fuck yeah I do. So I go and meet her, feeling like a new man the whole way not needing to light up.

So I get there, and she seems a bit distant but nothing too bad. We get to the place and are sort of standing around for a bit just talking some.

I ask if she is going to come do laundry at my place tonight because she usually does on Thursday night. She says no, she is going to her brother's place to do laundry. So I say, "So I guess I don't get to see you tonight." And sort of walk away.

At this point I am near on tears because 1) I am accustomed to her coming by tonight to do laundry and we spend the whole night talking and such and having fun. 2) I have made a concious effort to clean up a bit for her and shes not respecting that [I had told her that I hadn't smoked in a week].

Alright granted I know a week isn't that long but for someone like me who smoked everyday, almost a pack and a half a day, for a year and several months, this IS a big deal.

So I talk to her later at her place a bit. I tell her I am hurting and that I miss her. That I have been in physical and mental pain for a week now, but holding out for her because I love her so much.

A little back story, when she said she wanted to take a break she told me not to call her untill I had dropped smoking. This hurt a lot but I respeced her wish.

So today, I tell her I'm ready to have her back and I want her back. She then tells me she wants me to change first. I ask (very shocked and skeptically) "How?" She tells me she wants me to stop drinking so much.

Yes I am an alcoholic, have been since 8th grade (Drank at least a 12 pack of beer or a fifth of liquor almost everyday since then). But I am a functioning alcoholic. My grades in college are good and I get everything I need to done.

This causes a problem for me though. Because one, I could actually die from the withdrawl of alcoholism (I get delirium tremen after a couple days without drinking). And two this is starting to make me feel uncomfortable in the amount she wants to change me. Granted she is trying to change my BAD habits but still, its not like they are choices I made unknowing they were bad for me.

Anyway, sometimes I feel like she is trying to change me too much. Hell, she gets mad when I curse too much sometimes. And I've had friends and even she said she had friends who were telling her she was trying to change me too much.

So I turn to the people of TOTSE for final hope. What do you guys think?
