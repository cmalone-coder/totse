An angel is amessenger of God. We tend to be rather dense creaures, fearful and tradition bound. There are beings whose solepurpose is to act as God's messengers. I met one once--he was driving a Volvo. But that's another story.

At another level, there are humans who have been appointed by God to serve as His messenger. There have been many of us, and will continue to be. Our ranks include Shakespeare, Jesus, Robert Anton Wilson, Jung, and so on.

At still another level, everyone is a messenger of God, as we all are created in the image and likeness of God, and carry a spark of God in our hearts.

Verily is it written, "the kingdom of God is within you." To find it, you must go within--or else, you will surely go without.
