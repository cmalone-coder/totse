Re: Marijuana Impairs Brain Function?
Brain cells in adolescents develop quick, so dead brain cells would be replaced quick, anyways, it's like the way you workout to basically injure the muscle so it grows stronger...


Uhhh, the CNS doesn't undergo anabolism as muscle tissue does, as far as I know. Neurons also tend to be much more complex. All muscle tissue is, is a bundle of coiled proteins. If the right conditions are in place, muscle tissues basically assemble themselves.

Neurons are nothing like muscle tissue, the CNS has very limited regenerative abilities. The brain has the quality of plasticity, but it cannot regenerate itself to any significant degree. It can just compensate my making new connections between pre-existing neurons, to make up for the dead/missing neurons.

Also, brain damage that is incurred during times of heightened neurological growth tends to be more detrimental, than if it is incurred after one's brain is mostly done developing. This is why drinking alcohol in one's teen years is said to be so damaging. The fact that so much growth is occurring in the brain at this time, means that that growth can be more easily offset by other factors.

None of this necessarily has to do with the neurotoxicity of cannabanoids, but if it does retard brain growth to any degree, the effects would be more significant during periods of heightened growth, such as adolescence.
