Re: What kind of Mac should I get.
there are a lot of options out there but most importantly you need to decide between a desktop and a laptop. Each has their ups and downs so I will lay out some choices.

For a desktop, an iMac or Mac Mini is plenty sufficient. I have an iMac that I bought last August and I use it for some publication layout and design and it has plenty of power for what I need. A Mac Mini would also provide the power you are looking for at a slightly cheaper price (even more so if you have a monitor, mouse, and keyboard.) I wouldn't waste you money on a Mac Pro, as it is way more power than you require and cost more than double you budget. Both the iMac and Mac Mini can fit in your budget although the iMac is pushing it.

For a laptop, a Macbook is the way to go. A Macbook Pro is more power than you need and is way over you budget. You would have sufficient power with a Macbook and they are priced to fit well into you budget. You can get the 2.1ghz model for just over $1100.

You can take a look around and configure some models yourself
http://store.apple.com/us/browse/hom...ac?mco=MTE3MDg
keep in mind that you may qualify for an educational discount (I did and it took the price down a good bit)
