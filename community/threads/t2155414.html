Nice Dune ref.

Last fall, I extracted DMT and red spice.

DMT - I don't think it was pure enough, and I generally shared it, so no OEVs, but CEVs every time. I don't know how much depth you want, but generally I got alot of beings talking to me, interacting etc. No elves per se, but a lot of elvish (tolkien-esqe) persons who were dressed as jesters and represented different emotions, literally they were the emotion. A common theme would be that I'd pop UP into a city that was the city of emotion. Coming up in different parts of the city each time. One time I went into the ghetto and these very dark emotions (rape, torture, etc.) came after me until a old guru-like man came to save me. No one else who tried it reporting seeing things like McKenna.

~30 experiences

Red Spice - Much more visual success, but never 'happy' trips. I had one OEV on it. I 'sunk' down into another reality that had a graveyard feel to it, fog, dark, etc. No bad per se, just creepy. Then (sitting in my beanbag, transformed to a rock) these forms about 2' tall, black fuzzy balls, came over and were touching me, investigating. I got the feeling they were children, friendly. The best CEV I had, I was flying through a world of space encountering beings who were just sitting there, feelings of anguish and such. They came in really weird forms, like statue busts and bodies undescribable to humans. They knew I was there but ignored me to wallow in themselves mostly.

~10 experiences

All in all I think these realities lie within our brains. To put that in context, I don't believe 'I' am my entire brain, but only a small part. These drugs allow new synaptic connections, which along these connections conciousness 'rides.' You really are exploring your own inner realities, imo. So, the experience is also HIGHLY suggestible to your beliefs/expectations and your set/setting, of course. I think that we kinda invent these new realities and those who report McKenna's experience are probably believing it objectively rather than subjectively, so, of course, their belief about it is fufilled. Examining the psychology of my more personal experiences, the beings I 'met' had a correlation to people in my real life.

So, for me, I believe the experience is completely subjective and based on the psychology of the person. That said, my DMT trips are among the best experiences I've ever had, and the worst ones turned out to help me the most.

2012 - I think there will be a fundamental change soon as we start merging brains and technology, not necessarily as soon as 2012, though.

And, for novelty, I think the current hipster/emo crowd (i.e. regurgitation of childhood stuff, trying to be 'old school,' lolsers) is simply a mask for a lack of creativity. If anything we are only rehashing old symbols and ideas, not creating new, novel ones.

I bought a synthed gram of DMT, so I can let you know soon about how future experiments go.

Life is just a chance to see things from another perspective. Here the concepts of heaven and hell come into play. Morality can be distilled into a very simple concept: "Do no harm." Natural psychoactives feel different to me. While acid may open our perceptual filters and allow one to experience the larger picture, it always remains neutral, not speaking to me like DMT or even psilocybin does.

The smelves, then, would be noncorporeal entities. Ghosts, souls, whatever. But they are spirits which have taken an interest in us, that want to show us things or at least give us an enjoyable ride. I've never felt hostility from one of these beings. Indifference, maybe, but a billion-year soul may not be particularly interested in me, for good reason.
