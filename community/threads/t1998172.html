Depression when viewing fights?
This is hard to explain..

It all goes back to one day I was sitting in my form room at school looking out the window trying to get some last minute revision done, When I saw an ambulance going into the school next door... The Pikey School.

This is the school where people brought knifes.. the teachers where the underdogs, fights would develop from anything. In reflection - It was just a school, But it always appeared as some form of hell hole.

Now I can remember getting this weird feeling seeing the ambulance drive away... A curiousity, A anxiety or even a depression? Why did that happen? Who was in there? Did they really deserve whatever happened to them?

I can remember getting on the coach that day and hearing it was a first year breaking the ribs of a third year... (Owned..). Once again, This feeling came back...

Then yesterday, When I was hanging around with a group of chavs one of them was showing fights on her phone. It hit me again. This time it was 2 nigger girls, about 14/16 fighting.

It just looked mad... Seeing someone get thrown into a tree, hit repeatedly then bottled. Then to see her stumble away and collapse... What really made her deserve that? What happened? Why? Is she alright? I felt depressed and angry... But also I had an adrenaline rush. "Never, Ever, Shall anyone do that to me..." All of a sudden I wanted to train, just to make sure it never happens..

Whats wrong with me? I've never had a fight, I had some pussy put a key into my arm but thats it. Other then that, my approach has always been "Fighting is a last resort..." - i.e Run the fuck away. I've been threatened loads of times and attacked once.

That happened in school, I was a year 8 and he was a year 11.. and he went mental at me for throwing a snowball in his face. But.. I wasn't hurt, So he was pissed even more for looking a bigger dickhead then before.

Why would I get this feelings why others can view these videos and simply scream "owned" and burst out laughing?
