Re: Smoking weed alone
Honestly I prefer smoking alone. When I smoke I like to just sit back and read or fuck around online and listen to music. I smoke with other people all the time, but nothing beats sitting back at your desk with a fat bowl and some good music and smoking it slowly over the course of a couple hours. I'm kinda a solitary person by nature though, and weed affects me differently than a lot of people. Most kids I've smoked with are like "lets go get mcdonalds and watch south park" afterwards and I'm like "I'm gonna read about pharmacology".

People associate taking any sort of drug by yourself with being depressed or addicted because of the stereotype of the lonesome drunk. Alcohol is a very social drug for most people, and people constantly parrot bullshit about how drinking alone is a "sign of alcoholism". While hardcore alcoholics might drink alone because nobody wants to get shitfaced at 2:00 on a tuesday with them, just drinking alone has nothing to do with alcoholism if you ask me.

I don't see anything wrong with taking drugs alone, in fact some of my best and most revealing experiences with hallucinogens have just been me walking around in the woods with an MP3 player thinking about life. Hell, even sitting back with a glass of Wild Turkey 101 on the rocks after a long day and just relaxing is very enjoyable once in a while. Having other people around would just be disruptive and irritating when I just want to relax.

Smoke up and enjoy your own company.
