Facts that there is a god!
This is the biggest bullshit evidence written to prove a "god." A kid I know posted it on myspace and I wanted to see what The Temple had to say about it.

Some people believe that this world serves no purpose and has no end. They believe that it is all one big accident, one big assembly of atoms that just happened to come together in a chance fashion. A coincidence, if you will. Although some people may believe those Atheists and Evolutionists theories, it is hard for the human mind to disregard the incredible number of evidences that Someone has been providing for our well-being.

Here are some MAJOR evidences:

1. Let's focus on the mass and size of this planet on which we have been placed. It is just right. If planet Earth were any smaller or any larger, life would not be possible on this planet. Furthermore, it is just the right distance from the sun, giving us the right amount of light and heat to survive. If Earth were farther from the sun, we would freeze. If it were closer to the sun, we would not be able to survive.



2. Now let's focuz on the tilt of the axis of Earth. No other planet is tilted in such a way that our is--23 degrees. This angle provides that the Earth is slowly turned in all parts of its surface before the rays of the sun, much like a rotisserie chicken. If there were no tilt to the axis, the poles would accumulate enormous masses of ice, and the center part of the Earth would become intensely hot.



3. The moon. Many people do not realize that without the moon, it would be impossible to live on this planet. If anyone or anything were to ever succeed in deflecting the moon from its orbit, ALL life would cease on this planet. The moon acts like a maid and cleans up the oceans and the shores of all our continents. Without the tides created by the moons gravitational pull, all our harbors and shores would become one stench pool of garbage, and it would be completely impossible to live anywhere near them. Because of the tides, continuous waves break upon the shores of the ocean, cleansing the oceans of this planet and providing oxygen for the plankton, which is the very foundation of our food chain of our world. Without the plankton, there would not be oxygen, and without oxygen man would not be able to live upon this Earth. God has made the moon the right size and placed it the right distance away from the Earth to perform these functions.



4. The atmosphere. We live under a great, BIG blanket of air--78 percent nitrogen, 21 percent oxygen, and the other one percent is made up of almost a dozen different trace elements. Studies of other planets in the stellar universe show that no other atmosphere, no other part of the known universe is made up of these same ingredients or anything like this composition.



5. The ozone. 40 miles up into the atmosphere, there is a thin layer of ozone. If it were to be compressed, it would only be a quarter of an inch thick, and yet without it life could not exist. 8 killer rays fall upon this Earth continually from the sun; without ozone we would be burned, blinded, and broiled by them in a matter of a day or two. The Ultraviolet rays come in two forms: longer rays, which are deadly and are screened out, and shorter rays, which are neccessary for life on Earth and are admitted by the ozone layer. Furthermore, the most deadly of these rays are allowed through the ozone layer in a very limited amount, enough to kill the green algae, which otherwise would grow to fill the lakes, rivers, and oceans of this world.



6. Water. Nowhere else in the universe do we find water in any abundance except here on Earth. Water, the amazing solvent, dissolves almost everything upon this Earth except those things that are life-sustaining. The amazing liquid exists as ice which breaks up rocks to produce soil. As snow, it stores up water in the valleys. As rain, it waters and cleanses the Earth. As vapor, it provides moisture for much of the dry lands of this planet. Other than Bismuth, water is the only liquid that is heavier at 4 degrees Celsius than it is at freezing. If this were not so then life could not exist on this planet. Therefore, when it is frozen, it is lighter and it rises. If this were not so, lakes and rivers would freeze from the bottom up and all fish would die out. The algaae would be destroyed and our oxygen supply would cease; causing mankind to end.



7. The Human mind. The brain of a human only weighs on average about 3.3 pounds, and it can perform what 500 tons of electrical equipment cannot do. Containing 10 to 15 billion neurons, each a living unit in itself, the mind performs feats that boggle your own mind when thinking about it.

Therefore, men who reject or ignore God do so, not because science or reason tells them to, but purely and simply because they want to!

Romans 1:28 "And even as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind"

There is a God, and He is always watching out for us. If only we werent so oblivious to the beauty that surrounds us.
