Re: Overclocking
Don't use the software OCing utility. Go into the BIOS and up your FSB. You'll also have to up your vCore some along with your vDIMM, and you might have to loosen your RAM timings. What kind of RAM did you get?

Your chip can handle well over 80*C for a decent length of time, but to preserve the long-term life of the chip, most OCers try not to let their load temps get to far over 60*C. The only way you're going to frag your chip immediately, is if you raise the vCore to some insanely high voltage like 1.8v and not be using LNO2 or phase cooling (you shouldn't need to go anywhere near 1.8v though)

Your temps don't look too abnormal, but I'll need to know your current vCore to be sure (Use CPU-z for that). If you suspect that you jacked up mounting the heatsink, reseat it. Another thing might be the IHS on your CPU. This is the metal plate that covers the core and has the chips stepping info etched into it. C2D's have been shipped with bent and concave IHS's. Mine was extremely bent on one side. To correct this problem, I sanded the IHS down to where it was level. This is called lapping. However, judging by your temps, it does not appear that your IHS is bent, but if you rule out voltage, case air flow, and the mounting of your heatsink, lapping might bring your temps down a bit.
