Infractions: Things You May Not Know
Recently in B&M some issues were brought up (You can read the thread http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=2067183]here ) Some of them I didn't know and so I thought I'd bring it up here.

First of all, when you infract someone, they get an email - we know that. But what I didn't know is that that email does not say what Mod gave the infraction. And if that infraction triggers a ban, they can't access their UserCP either and so can't see who gave the infraction that way.

We have been telling people that if they have an issue about an infraction - or anything really - to first email the Mod in question. If they can't tell who the Mod was, they can't very well email them.

So I am going to start to put my name in all infractions in the user note section so that they know. And I would strongly suggest others do too. I would say something about locking threads too, so that people know who to ask about reopening it.

Granted in forums like SG it can be a little more work to post in every thread you close, but that should be part of the job of Modding SG [Yeah, I know, I didn't always do that myself. But since I've been spending even more time in B&M - and some in the forum previously known as SB - and see people's point in Mods who don't leave a closing note.

But that's really another issue for another time. My focus here is infractions.

The other issue brought up in that thread was that a user felt he had been double infracted by two Mods for the same violation. I looked into it and was unable to confirm or deny the charge. This is because both infractions were profile infractions. And as we know, profile infractions don't indicate what the infraction was for.

I know that we have kinda used profile infractions to infract someone in a forum we don't moderate. Again, this is an issue for another time and place.

But my feelings on profile infractions is that if you're going to give one, you should either link to the thread that had the violation or give the title or something. This way other Mods - and Admins - can see what it was for and so not to infract a person for the same thing twice.

If - and I say if - this person was double infracted, I am going to assume it was by mistake and that the second Mod to infract did not know the person had been infracted for it already. Of course it could be for two totally different things. So again, naming the thread or what ever will avoid such errors or such questions of error from occurring in the future.

Because if the person was infracted for the same thing twice - and the Mod[s] knew it, well I don't have to check with Zok or Acid [though I did] to know that this is wrong. That no person should ever be infracted for the same thing twice.
