Re: C4 grade plastic explosives
Yes it is the punch that matters, or rather the cocusion. No the case dose not.

First the differences between propelents and high explosives must be made clear.

A propelent is a substance that burns quickly, i.e. flash powder, most types of smokeless powder (the exception being bullseye from Aliant), sugar chlorate, ect..
One could place any of these on a table top, throw a match on them, and they would just produce a flame, hot gas, and smoke (no noise). To create a bang or use such propelents to destroy things with concusion or projectiles one must place them in some kind of container like a pipe with caps on both ends, a tightly rooled piece of paper, or the barrel of a fire arm with a bullet preventing the unhendered escape of gas. When this propelent burns it creates hot expanding gas that bursting the container or forces the projectile down the barrel. It is this rupturing of the container that causes the bang, and the small pieces of flying container that will do damage.

By contrast high explosives do not rely on a container bursting to make a bang. Further more many high explosives will not readily burn, and those that do and explosion does not always occure. I have heard stories from Nam Vets who used to use shavings of C4 to start fires, they said it burned like wood. Some will explode when exposed to fire like lead azidide and dynamite (not the modern stuff), however most like C4 and ANFO will not. You see, when they explode they are not technicaly burning, they are decaying rapidily.

To initiate an explosion one needs a detonator that will produce enough concusion to facilitate this rapid decay. Substances like Fulmulating Mercury are typicaly strong enough to do this but in some cases one needs to use a booster to achieve the desired outcome.
