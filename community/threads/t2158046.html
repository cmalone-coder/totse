Opened my Third Eye for the First Time, Saw Angels
I'm not really religious, raised in a religious family but other than being baptized and going to church few and far between times in my life, nothing.

I was sitting in class having telepathic conversations with people (go ahead call me insane) but the people around me were actually reacting to what i was saying. I told this one girl next to me that I found her extremely attractive and me not being bad looking either, she rubbed her leg against mine.

Anyway the class got extremely boring so I decided to open my mind's eye (third eye). It was the first time I did this voluntarily and no there was no tint of color, but looking at one guy in front of me and one girl next to me, well they took the form of angels.

They were both extremely tall and looked like the highest class of royalty. Their every cloth and feature was white and they each had a crown atop of their heads. Their faces ceased to look human and took a more "perfect" shape. They had eyes but they were all white, no color of iris or pupils. The features of their faces were pointy, most notably their noses, although they werent long.

The teacher at the front of the class then had a glowing halo around her head (not a round circle above her head but a glowing yellow-white light emanating from it.) It followed her everywhere she moved.

I turned back to look at these angels and their size, although not dwarfing me, was intimidating. The only reason I didn't freak out was due to the fact they were giving me the most amazing of vibes. They then asked me to join them. They sent me a mental picture of myself with infant angel wings that then morphed into adult angel wings. I told them that I would indeed join them, I'm just hoping they weren't some kind of demon or evil presence morphed to make me feel at ease. (It happened once before)

So have I found the endless spiral to insanity or could this actually be possible?
