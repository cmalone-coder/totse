"Well it kind of feels that way when you can't even give me a good example of what I'm doing wrong."

"I need to work and I don't need this stress right now. So once again I'm going to just stop arguing and say I'm wrong and you're right. But the next time you do or say what I'm talking about, I'll make sure that you remember"

Thanks.
