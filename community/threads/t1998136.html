Chairman Mao's Guide to a Successful First Date

Step 1) Look good, for your sake.

What most girls look for in a potential mate is confidence; the easiest way to gain confidence is to have something to be confident about. Brush your hair, brush your teeth, pick a nice outfit (don’t forget to have shoes that go well) and put on a LITTLE BIT of nice cologne. If you think that you look good it will do wonders for your confidence, which, in turn, will come across on your impending date.

Step 2) Pick a location that is conducive to chemistry.
A common mistake made by amateurs and dating gurus alike is that they pick a first date location that interferes with chemistry. Movies are a common but poor first date location as it is very difficult to get to know each other over explosions and whatnot. Many also choose a meal at an expensive restaurant in attempts to impress their date. Although a nice sentiment, this may make your date intimidated or uncomfortable, especially if said date is underdressed. I always recommend going to a nice coffee shop for a relaxing cup of something warm before perhaps taking a romantic walk. This allows for the parties involved to get to know each other while in a comfortable setting. After you have settled on an appropriate venue let your date know where you are going so she will know how to dress and feel comfortable in the setting.

Step 3) Be confident, funny and interesting.

I have found that these traits put together in the correct ratios will lead to a memorable date for all parties involved and increase your chances of obtaining that elusive second date. Try to be confident without being overbearing and coming off as an asshole. Be funny but don’t spend the entire time being a goofball; you will come off as immature. Be talkative, don’t allow any uncomfortable lulls (I mean lull as in quiet and not the misspelling of lol) in the conversation, but keep in mind that it takes two to perpetuate one (unless you are schizophrenic). Remember to stay relaxed and enjoy yourself, if you are uncomfortable and lack confidence it will come across in your body language.

If you follow these steps and remember to remain confident, you should find yourself having more dating success in the future.

As for your question, never tell a girl, especially this early that you like her. Like guys, girls also enjoy the thrill of the hunt. If you keep joking around and making her work for your affection the results can be surprising. If you shoot your wad right off the bat it takes away that "does he really like me" feeling that might give you an advantage.
