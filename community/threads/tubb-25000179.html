The liquid core theory came about through an extremly close examination gathered from siesmometers. When there is an earthquake there are different types of waves of movement. One type of wave(I forget which) travels through the earth, not around the curve. By analyzing the speeds at which this wave travels, it can be interpreted what sort of material it travels through. A wave traveling directly through the earth will have a certian speed, while it will have a different speed if it travels through the liquid part only. It has yet another speed if it doesn't travel through the core at all. By analyzing the zones at which these speeds take a drastic change it can be deduced that the a line from the epicenter of the earthquake to the boundry line will be tangent to the border between different substances.
