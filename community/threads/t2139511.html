Training:

Don't try to increase your running time and weight lifting simutanously, their goals are somewhat contradictory. What you should do is do them in blocks.

Ie. 4 weeks focusing on running, while doing a maintenance load for lifting. Then 4 weeks of specialise lifting while only doing the maintenance load for running.

The volume of the maintenace load should be about 60% of the max loading.

You might want to put deadlift instead of squat in your goal. I don't know anyone who are squatting (to parallel) 2xbw for reps but also run marathon.

Nutrition:

Can't say I know much about nutrition but you forgot about the calories that you burn just to keep your alive.

So eat more. And the usual blah about keeping carbs in the morning and afternoon. Less at night. 1-1.5lb of protein per pound of lean body mass.

Re: Reinventing Myself REDUX.
I'm the XC captain of my school team this year.

Here is the secret: Run longer distances.

Training for a 5k should involve practicing at 8 miles at least. By the end of the season, my team's long run is 10 miles.

2.5 miles won't help much for a 3 mile race. High mileage is all about building a strong aerobic base, so you use oxygen more efficiently. You can't hope to run an 18:00 5k if you run 2.5 miles for practice. Slowly up the mileage. Once you build a good base (5 miles in ~35:00), start on speed. Do sprint workouts (ONLY AFTER YOU HAVE BUILT AN AEROBIC BASE) in conjunction with long runs.

As for diet, just do 2000. Carbs before the run (6-12 hours before, so the carbs can enter bloodstream and be stored in your muscles), protein after. Fat is also essential in a balanced diet. Change calorie count based on how you feel.

Also, small meals. 5 a day.
