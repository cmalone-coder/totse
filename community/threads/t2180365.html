Especially with meth versus railing crushed Adderall or Dexedrine or shit like that, the higher potency by weight means you can get to the same place with less material, and less material means less damage. Snorting pills is actually kinda like snorting heavily cut shit considering the how much inactive material is in some pills. Sure with pharmaceuticals you have a guarantee that there won't be any toxic or otherwise highly undesirable inactives, but the binders and fillers in pills will tear up your nasal cavity as fast as the cuts in "street drugs", if not faster.
