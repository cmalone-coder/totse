A vague question about acid...I want to recreate an experience.
So I work in a hostel and we had some British guys come and stay for a few weeks. They're crazy fucks and on one of their birthdays I let them just run a muck and do what ever they wanted instead of kicking them out to pubs/bars. Well to repay me the guy said he'll let me do some acid. I asked how much it was, and he said it's free. "You'll be paying for it by showing me you're worth it, man - It's a one way ticket and it's no refundable so don't let me down". At this point the guy seemed like a total douche. He got me to put my hand out and put a single drop on the back of it. Sucked on the back of it for a while and licked it all up.

What followed can only be described as the single strongest trip I've had in my entire fucking life. Easily stronger than taking 14grams of dried, good mushrooms. I temporarily was incapable of walking and lost all vision. Instead of seeing the real world with hallucinations in it, ALL I could see was hallucinations. It was fucked up and off the scales of what I thought a trip could be like. At one point I fell asleep (I wasn't asleep I was just so fucked I thought what I was seeing was a dream) and when some people made a loud noise I awoke from what felt like the warmest most beautiful dream ever. It was incredible. I've taken 3 normal doses of acid and they were about as half strong as the come up/down of the trip I had. How many micrograms are on a standard blotter/sugar cube? I need to find out. Because here's where you all come in...

...I need to know how much acid you think I took, so I know how much I need to buy if I want to recreate that experience. It was amazing. Jesus christ, I've always been a huge mushrooms fan, but I think LSD has just won me over permanently.
