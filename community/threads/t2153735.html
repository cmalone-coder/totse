Quote:
Originally Posted by LuKaZz420
I've come to the conclusion that most public protests are actually counter-productive, I think you'd do more good by raising awareness about the issues online, on forums and blogs.
I disagree. With online blogs you only attract people who already agree with you. If you actually go out and make a physical demonstration you get the attention of all passers-by. Some will disagree with you (and not hesitate to tell you so ), some will pick up a leaflet and think "well, I never knew that" and possibly become a more active supporter. It's a little pointless "raising awareness" with people who are already perfectly aware. That's not to say web sites aren't invaluable; a lot of protests now carry banners with web adresses on as a means of spreading the word amongst an audience they wouldn't usually have.
