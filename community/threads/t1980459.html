Re: What is Heaven like?
Neither Heaven nor Hell is an actual location, but rather a state of consciousness, which we may all create for ourselves.

For instance, when one achieves cosmic or unity consciousness/exists in a state of grace/ achieves hozho (as the Dine put it)--moving serenely and effortlessly through life in such a way as to inspire the entire universe to conspire to bring us all tht we desire, we may well be said to be in Heaven.

When we lapse into fear or any of the masks that fear wears--such as anger or envy or guilt--then we consign ourselves to Hell.
