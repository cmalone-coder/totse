Problems With the Administration
There are some problems with the totse administration. I'll let this letter and chat log speak for itself.

First, the letter; I wrote this letter to Jeff a couple months ago. I emailed it and never received a response. I'd like to share it.

Quote:
Originally Posted by Letter to Jeff
Jeff Hunter:

You know me as Toraton on the &TOTSE BBS; I know that I do not have the best rapport with you due to my earlier actions, but I'd like for you to read and consider this anyhow.

Ever since the transition to VBulletin, the Mods & Admins forum has given me a greater sense of &TOTSE's core values overall. I believe the core values to be (in no particular order):

- Knowledge & Learning
- Anonymity
- Freedom of Speech

That said, I don't think that the current administration staff, with some notable exceptions, has &TOTSE's core values in mind. In my pursuit of becoming a helpful and supportive moderator, I have seen more than a few injustices. Let me raise a few examples.

Recently, a member has returned to the Lab Tips forum; he is called 'Jam'. You may have seen the copious amount of back and forth this topic has received. This poster is notorious for posting misinformation, however, I believe that misinformation is covered by &TOTSE's core values. Members should not believe everything they read and I think you would agree. I expect that a moderator would criticize the poster, but the moderator in question (stupid noob) has decided to infract and thus temporarily ban the user.

The injustice doesn't end here; in IRC, I was privy to a discussion between stupid noob and acidmelt, where acidmelt decided the correct course of action to be a permanent ban on the user. Unfortunately, his reasons presented on IRC had little to do with the user's actions on the BBS. acidmelt reasoned that outside of &TOTSE, the user had supplied the personal information (gained from &TOTSE) to the police; this resulted in their arrest.

While I don't support his supplying the police with other users' info, I don't think this is against BBS policy. What IS against policy is to post personal information; yours or otherwise.

To top it all off, this is water under the bridge; the user did this long ago and now, years later, he has been permanently banned for taking no further actions. I would call this unjust.

acidmelt has taken on an attitude of "I will do whatever I want because I have the power to"; unfortunately, this has resulted in many users being banned for absolutely no reason. As a lowly moderator, I would never have the gall to recommend that he be removed, especially after so many years of good service to the BBS. However, I think it would be prudent for you to talk to him and find out why he has adopted this attitude. The BBS does not benefit from his maligned dictatorship.

Zok, on the other hand, is cool headed and understanding; I think he does a phenomenal job with administration. I know that Zok relies on acidmelt to do the "dirty work", in a sense, because Zok avoids conflict at every chance. He suggested that I email you, actually, because he was frustrated with the situation.

I think that if you should contact Zok, he would agree that sometimes acidmelt takes things too far.

I ask that you please overlook my past mistakes, if only to take a good look at the current state of the BBS. If you find nothing wrong with acidmelt's actions, I would love to hear about it; perhaps I have misjudged &TOTSE and what it tries to be.

Sincerely,
Josiah Purtlebaugh
Toraton on &TOTSE BBS

And while we're airing grievances towards the admins, I feel like I should also send a short message Jeff's way.

Jeff this is my only beef with you: I think we need to go through the rules on the site again. Since they were written we've come across dozens of technicalities. This was never a problem in the past, because a lot of the elements that bring on these technicalities didn't exist back when the rules were written. For instance, we never had a problem with people from totse going to other websites and spamming their forums/communities with totse's name. Nowadays we have problems with people collecting personal info on totse members, posting it off site (on their own website) and linking to it on totse. There is no rule against posting pointless or meaningless threads (for instance: threads whose entire content is a link to another thread). A lot of forums have "forum specific rules" - better living through chemistry has a rule against posting Rx sources and online sellers. Nowhere in the rules does it say that some forums might have separate rules. Or rule #1 - Thou shalt not grief thy administrator. Who is that? I know Jeff is in that category. Does that extend to the rest of the mods? Only the forum admins? Only Jeff?

Now this was never a problem in the past, but now we seem to have a forum full of rules lawyers. If these things were part of the rules then it would be a lot easier to enforce without hearing "well, it never said in the rules that I can't post a wall of goatse pictures!" Because of these technicalities, moderators are enforcing the rules differently in each forum since nobody really knows what constitutes what.

There are a lot of decisions that only the site owner can make and I would really like to see some get made...

In short (with a clever analogy): The US constitution was written to govern a nation of less than one million. Since then, the population has exploded, many things have changed over the times, and new problems/solutions were introduced. Luckily for our nation, we've been adding amendments to the constitution to keep up with the times. I think totse might need to do the same.

I think this post and my last sum up my frustrations with totse.

Lots of interesting points here. Let me see...

I get thousands of SPAM messages every day in my totse.com mailbox. I also get 10-40 messages a day from actual people, most insisting that I spend some of my spare time doing something for them, for free, because they deserve it. Or asking a question which they could answer for themselves if they read the FAQ.

So right now I rarely read my e-mail.

I do need a better way for people to get in contact with me in case there's an actual problem. I should probably install a challenge-response system (to eliminate the SPAM) along with a whitelist of people I actually want to hear from, including anyone who's a mod or an admin.

I can (and sometimes do) review the bans/infractions handed out and the reasons for those bans/infractions. If I see a moderator handing out bans/infractions for dubious reasons I send them a PM.

I am considering creating a script to list the bans/infractions handed out, the names of the moderators handing them out, and the reasons for those bans/infractions. This information would be posted publicly. I think that would stop power-tripping abuse, if there is any. Feedback on this idea is welcome.

Toraton's list of core values is essentially correct.

Zok, your analogy of amendments to the Constitution regarding rules is dead-on, but somehow you reach the wrong conclusion. The US Constitution has only been amended 27 times in 227 years. It's rarely changed. It says you have a right to free speech, so why can't you libel or slander people without fear of consequences? It says citizens have a right to bear arms, but doesn't say that you can't own a grenade launcher, so why can't you? It says that judicially sanctioned search and arrest warrants must be supported by probable cause, yet today Congress voted that federal some agencies could wiretap at will without judicial oversight.

I'm not as smart as the framers of the Constitution, but I see their brilliance in not trying to cover every contingency. They list a few absolutes, legislators fiddle with details to fit the needs of the times (or not).

If we need to make the intent of the rules clearer we can do that, but I don't want to try to cover everything that someone might do with a rule.

As far as I am concerned, there need only be three rules:

You can say what you wish, provided:

1) You do so in an intelligible manner
This means attempting to communicate an idea, or make a statement, as opposed to stringing random words together, or advertising.

2) You give others a fair chance to have their say too
This means not communicating in such a way that it prevents others from doing so; breaking Totse, spamming, and flooding, basically.

3) You do so in a reasonably orderly way, and in the right time and place
This means posting in the right forum, giving the thread an appropriate title, etc.

That is all.
