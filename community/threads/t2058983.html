What about those who really ARE ugly?
It seems like whenever someone brings up a guy "being ugly" on the totse, people just seem to ignore his opinion on himself and assume that he just has low self-esteem, as there are very few men that are really too ugly to be considered attractive with the right personality traits.

So the people assume he just needs more confidence, and that if he comes to like himself the rest wil fall into place naturally. They never give the boy any credit to evaluate himself properly, and always seem to fail to regard the potential scientific evidence that the person is more likely to judge themselves accurately when they have a somewhat negative view of themselves.

But remember that this is totse, afterall. People from the fringes of society are attracted here for the reasons of anonymity, the presence of likeminded people, etc. We have political extremists, people with fruit fetishes, autodidacts, nymphomaniacs, druggies, amateur terrorists, peadophiles, and a good smattering of 13 year olds just looking for an outlet for their angst. In this multicultural hodgepodge of the best and the worst modern postindustrial society has to offer, it isn't unreasonable to assume that some of these people are going to be the truly ugly individuals, blessed not by their Creator with even redeemable looks.

I am not discussing women here, only men. So what becomes of these men? Are they doomed to a life of solitude?

Is there really any advice for truly ugly men? We all know that personality can make up for many physical deficiencies for most people looking for a relationship, but this can only go so far. What becomes of a truly ugly man? Does he have virtually no chance of finding someone who will find him attractive? Will he have to wait until he is much older? Or is even that out of the question, him probably being subjected to a life alone?

So keep this in mind if you respond. Many people that believe they are extremely unattractive are probably exaggerating a certain extent. However, at least even by the law of probability, some of these people aren't. Some really are as hideous as they claim, and this being the Internet, we can't truly verify it. So at least respond like you are giving a very ugly person the benefit of the doubt and that they are telling the absolute truth. What would you say?
