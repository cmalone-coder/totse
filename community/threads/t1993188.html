No, NO. H2O2 reacts with acetone 1:1. H2O2 has a density of 1.4, so 14 grams pure would be 10mL, not one. 1mL of reagents will yield just about nothing.

Personally I use about 11mL of acetone for a small batch, and this gives about 10g of AP. I like to let it sit in the fridge overnight.

No matter what anyone says about sensitivity, you really have to get a feel for it yourself. Take some about the size of a pinhead, and see what it takes to burn/detonate it. Repeat with another tiny amount. Don't work with anything larger until you get used to it, and you'll be fine.

So one day I had no HCl, only H2SO4, so I did my regular habit of dumping it all in, and it got fucking HOT as fuck, and upon cooling, BAM a solid fucking rock of AP. This product was ran through the regular tests after washing and drying, and there was still no difference between AP via fast hot sulfuric vs slow prechilled slow addition UBER FUCKING COLD HCl catalyst synth.
