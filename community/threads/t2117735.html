Re: What do you think about?
Well, I do empty my mind of contemporary life struggles and realize that the forest is my new environment and adjust. However, that's not to say I do not think at all. While I'm backpacking/camping I think about ANYTHING and everything that crosses my mind and catches my attention. Usually I ponder my own existence and the meaning of why each and every one of us are here on this rock orbiting an out of control thermonuclear explosion in the icy depths of a poorly understood universe. Usually going into "survival mode" triggers these thoughts because I start to see things for what they really are -- I'm just an animal with a highly evolved brain desperately trying to meet my energy demands, survival. The mind is an interesting thing to explore when you're really deep bush. Really helps me find myself and remind me I'm alive.

Re: What do you think about?
I do a lot of sailing. Some time is spent willing the wind to get stronger, or change direction. Reading books. Contemplating the universe. I think, but in a completly different manor than if i am going about my usual business.

I love catching the traffic reports on the radio. It is rather amusing when you can't see any civilization for miles, let alone a bloody car!

I have come to the conclusion that if i dont get atleast 2 weeks a year of being completly in the middle of nowhere, i get pretty damn low. It is the time to think of absolutly nothing for hours uninterupted, or have my head running at a hundred miles an hour coming up with ideas for things. It is the hours on end of uninterupted thought that keeps me sane.

Edit - Also, if it is fairly blowing a gale, the guard rail is under the water and you are holding on for dear life, that is when you think, "Fuck this is so fun"
