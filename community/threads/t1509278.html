No, shaving will neither make your hair grow back thicker nor darker, nor grow any faster Shaving will not increase the diameter of your current hair follicles or grow back hair that you have lost as these factors are determined by genetics.

With new hair growth, the short hair shaft will be more noticeable, since it now has a blunt tip due to shaving, as opposed to the normal tapered tip. In addition, by letting your hair grow out, the new hair will be healthier and undamaged, because it has not been exposed to the elements, chemical treatments, blow drying, or normal day-to-day wear and tear. By avoiding or limiting the risks of hair damage such as these, you will have an increased possibility of having a healthier head of hair.
