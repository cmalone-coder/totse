Re: long distance relationships - how to keep them going
Communication. And lots and lots and lots of trust.

My roommate is in a long distance relationship. He talks on the phone with her everyday for AT LEAST 3 hours (usually during nights and weekend to get free cell phone minutes).

They live a state apart, but they alternate visiting each other like every other weekend. They save up money to take an 8 hour long train ride to visit each other. On big occasions like anniversary, holidays, or whatever, they'll save up for plane tickets and spend a few weeks with each other.

Anyways, they plan on eventually moving in with each other. They had to go through a lot of work, planning, and arguments to get to that point. A lot of drama was endured (I hate dealing with other people's relationships, but I did my best to help him out since he's one of my best friends). It's definitely key to have an unbiased outside perspective on those situations. I was blunt and honest with him, and I think it helped him out a lot.

Sometimes it works, and sometimes it doesn't. From past experience, I know for a fact I personally can't handle LD relationships myself and vowed never to have one again. But that's just me. If you want to make it work, you can... but it will require effort.

Also, don't ditch your other friends. It's annoying when your best friend spends almost all of his time with his LD g/f and rarely every hangs out with his other friends. I mean, he'll come out with us, but not unless she is with him and they leave together early. It makes me sad that in a few years he's most likely going to sever all connections with his good friends because of he's so focused on his g/f and will regret it painfully. Shit, me and my other friend have already gone off with our own lives meeting new people, and he wonders why he's always being left out.

Good luck and I hoped that help.
