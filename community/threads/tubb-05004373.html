I think your problem might be related to the fact that you have cut the light to eight hours. I would increase the light to twelve on twelve off as an experiment to see if the growth resumes. There are many reasons why the growth might have stopped. Ph might be too high or low. You might have locked up your nutrients, causing the plant growth to halt. How long have they been flowering? What fertilizer regimin have you been using? How strong are the lights, and what size space are they in? You need at least 40 watts per sq ft to get optimal growth, and the lights need to be as close to the tops as possible without burning them.
Without more info though, I can only guess what the problem might be. Good luck, and maybe I can help if you can give more detailed info.

Re: Question about budding plants
Okay, Your problem is light deficiency. As I said you need at least 40 watts per sq. ft.
Also, The incandescent bulbs are most likely the wrong spectrum of light. For flowering
I would suggest a High Pressure sodium light and ballast. I would get a 400 watt system, but You could probably get by on a 250 watt system. HPS lights emit an orange spectrum light which is utilized by the plant in flowering. The incandescent bulbs are most likely too weak, and also in the wrong spectrum. The reason your plants were getting so tall is that they were stretching to try and recieve more light. I would suggest you cut your losses on this harvest, and move up to a HPS light. Your buds will be a lot thicker and much more healthy.
For reading I would suggest any books by
Ed Rosenthal, or Jorge Cervantes, and always take any grow files you read on the net with a grain of salt, as most are completely garbage. Good luck, and if you have any questions post them and I will try my best to help.
