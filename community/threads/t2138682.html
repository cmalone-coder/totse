Try taking a good bit Anirak. I'm pretty sure you won't regret it man.

Really strong acid trips and relatively low dose trips are completely different.

I've gotten many people to trip with me their first time. They usually trust my judgment on dose, so I ask them if they'd be against a pretty strong trip. Most of the time they say no.

I always end up tripping with them for the entirety of the night. Line up certain activities -- yanno, the truly enlightening experiences.

About 20 minutes after dosing I'll play the Yellow Submarine DVD to come up on. The movie is ridiculously psychedelic and generally will put someone in a good mind set. At the end, they're coming up pretty hard, and they'll have a pretty serious distortion of time, not having a clue how much time just passed. That movie can do it. Also have some cool posters to look at:

http://img76.imageshack.us/img76/7678/warriorwomanvk1.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

http://img376.imageshack.us/img376/8272/omnisciencebi3.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

http://img355.imageshack.us/img355/1314/magiceyecb5.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

http://img337.imageshack.us/img337/6127/0706082229pw3.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

Usually after the movie I'll take them outside to smoke a bowl. That'll usually kick things into high gear for them. I have an excellent place for tripping. Shitty quality but you get the idea.

http://img257.imageshack.us/img257/1979/0625080519ahs3.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

During the day, plenty of different colored trees and other plants around to look at, and a decent amount of room to explore. At night, usually have a very nice dark sky because of the little light pollution around where I live. Some nights you can see hundreds of stars.

Until sunset I usually play them some good acid music. Grateful Dead, Pink Floyd, Shpongle, Younger Brother, are some excellent examples. Also, if you like the Beatles, their acid phase has some excellent songs and albums. I highly recommend their CD "LOVE". Pretty much a perfect acid album. Look it up, you'll find it.

Once the sun starts setting I'll take them to the fire pit at the bottom. Here's where they get completely lost.

Staring at a fire on acid can take you places. I can't even describe it, really. It puts you in a complete trance state, and your mind wanders all over. Just...just do it. I have these color changing fire sticks that just make it all the more amazing:

http://cn1.kaboodle.com/hi/img/2/0/0/ce/b/AAAAAmeeHR4AAAAAAM67tQ.jpg

Once it's completely dark I'll put the fire out. By then they've done a lot of mind clearing. These are the beneficial parts on acid in my opinion. Acid is a good tool for cleaning your mind. 'Dusting' it off, if you will.

Fire's out, the sky is dark. I have a lot of cool LED toys to play with in the dark. I'll usually give them a bit of a light show to some music. Very good combination. Why do you think you see it all the time at festivals and concerts? This is usually when I break out the nitrous too.

http://img66.imageshack.us/img66/8198/0706082156rg5.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

http://img377.imageshack.us/img377/3778/n5426860376551824513jw3.jpg (http://imageshack.us)

Looking at the starry sky is just amazing on acid. Seeing the things stars do is pretty intense. Add a few blinking plane nights and hundreds of fire flies, and your jaw will drop :)
Spend a lot of the evening outside in the dark.

All the filler time in between can be spent doing whatever, but a lot of what I just said will occupy a good portion.

Eat fruit! Apples, oranges, bananas, strawberries. They all taste great and are good nutrients for the trip.

Use my examples as set and setting. Obviously they are a rough guideline, but doing those things has made EVERYONE who has tripped with me for their first time greatly enjoy the experience. They are by far the most important parts of the trip.

Trip safe.
