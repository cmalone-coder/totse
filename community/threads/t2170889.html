Serving God will send you to hell.
Exactly as the title says, Serving God will send you to hell.

Jesus said this to his Jews, " The law was made for servants-Love God as I love him, as his son! What have we son's of God have to do with morality!"

This statement is rather confusing for most Christians, but Jesus also told us to not follow the religions that would come in his name as well. If you really believe that Christ was the savior, you'll get rid of your bible right now, cease your routine of Church, no longer speak of religion, and only love God as your father. As his son, God will always spare you from your sins, cause he knows you are incapable of escaping them. All a person must do is live a life of love in their heart.

The reason I say serving God will send you to hell, is simply because the system of servitude is flawed, and you can never avoid sinning, and if you do not see yourself as one of God's children, you won't be forgiven, EVER! Servitude is not something Godly.

It takes us to one of the oldest stories. God is the ruler of Heaven and Earth, but only his angels where designed for servitude, as they can't sin, because they're not human. God never ever told us to serve. Only one angel challenged this system, and it was Satan. Satan wanted to Rule Heaven and Earth himself, and wanted the weak humans to bow before him. Satan demanded servitude, not God. God was silent on this. Satan was cast down to earth for trying to take God's throne.

Satan, in spite of God, corrupted God's humans, with knowledge. Knowledge lead to the idea of amorality, and amorality to morality, which born religion. Satan's greatest trick of deception was a system of worship of God, in the ways that God despised most. God created us without morality, and until we had knowledge, we didn't need it.

God realized the plight of mankind, and sent Christ to earth, to teach us a new way to live, through love. Love is above morality. When we love God, and act only out of love, we live in heaven, as we are always filled with love, and no deed that could be seen as negative to humanity can be performed through love. At this point morality has no purpose, except to those who are filled with love, but believe that they must follow a system of Good and Evil to find heaven. That is a lost cause, cause nobody can live up to the laws that religion has put upon it's servants. All religions are the same in this, and nobody can ever achieve full obedience. To follow one law, another must be broken, and it's impossible to not break a law. Therefore, heaven will always be out of the grasp of servants, and is reserved for those who love God as their father, and know the father will forgive his children.

Even in Buddhism, is it even possible to achieve enlightenment? Nope, you can't. You'll never find heaven through that path, because our human feebleness will never allow us to ever reach full enlightenment. I can go on and on how as long as we serve an ideal, we'll never find the Kingdom of heaven inside us, cause it's only found through love of God as his child.

When we love God as his child, we become an extension of God's love, as that's what children are of their fathers. Acting in love, there are no mistakes. A man who full of this love has no need for greed, no vanity, no pride, no sloth, envy, no gluttony, and no lust.

In Greed, if we are full of love, we may pursue to get things for ourselves, but out of love, we'll also share it with those around us, and greed is no longer possible.

Even in Vanity, if we are full of love, we do not reject love, and give love equally, rendering vanity to nothing, as it no longer fosters being above another, cause a person full of love, loves everyone despite their appearance, including themselves. Vanity simply no longer can plague us.

Pride, what pride, I'm God's son, but am I more chosen than any other son? No, God loves us all equally, and so do I.

Sloth simply can't exist either, as universal love is God's work, how is sloth possible?

Envy, what can a person envy when you love all as your brothers and sisters, with love being the most cherished thing, rendering everything else obsolete, what is there to envy if we can all possess this love.

It is also impossible to be gluttonous if one loves themselves. How can you abuse your body and food, if you love what God has given you, and strive to take care of that gift?

Even lust, If one loves all, their is no lust. Sex transcends animal instinct and becomes about the spiritual connection, and sharing of the feeling of love between two individuals. When love is the driving force, lust no longer exists.

Under the laws of religion however, you'll always be in default, at war with yourself, and never truly possess love. If you feel you must follow a strict code to be loved by the father, you do not love him as your father, as you shouldn't fear God ever. God is your father, he's there to nurture you, and servants are bowing before Satan. When you bow before Satan, you agree with Satan and his ideas, which have no place in heaven, which is why you won't.

As long as you follow religion, follow a set of morality that isn't a system of Love, you are at war with your human self, and you are in hell, and will go to hell.
