What the hell is in Perth?
Calling all Australian TOTSEers.. or anyone who knows jack about the place...

There's a study abroad program at my school that includes Australia. Now, when I think of that country, my mind sort of naturally curves towards the east and Sydney/Melbourne, not west. Having met a girl who did study abroad here in Sacramento from Ireland, I'm also damned disinclined towards trusting anything the school might have to say about the place- no one in their right mind would make their Great American Adventure a four-month stay in this city if they knew about it beforehand. It's just fortunate she was content with clubbing five nights a week.

But, of course, this is at Edith Cowan University in Perth. I'm not knocking the place; I can't rightfully do so, since I know zip about either the city or the college, even by random internet rumor. I'm just wondering if, were I to do this, I would end up being, like, goddamn, there's nothing interesting here and I should never have come (clubbing all the time is not my deal), or if there's stuff to see and do and explore that might keep someone entertained for a semester and not make them sigh about being so far away from the Opera House and quick jaunts to New Zealand.
