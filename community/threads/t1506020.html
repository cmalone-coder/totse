Let me cast some hope here...

I did it. I had a long distance relationship for two and a half years, and we made it work. I had a similar story you did: I met a girl near the end of my senior year in high school, and we fell head over heels for each other. She was going to one college and I was going to another, though, and they were several hundred miles apart. Granted, this is not quite as bad as your situation (Wisconsin to California), but it is the same principle.

We talked on the phone. A lot. I don't just mean daily, I mean several times per day. We made a commitment to see each other at least once per month, often twice. We would alternate visiting: she would come to see me once, then I would go to see her the next time, usually just for a weekend. We were from the same hometown, so we also got to see each other on breaks from school. After 2 and a half years, she transferred to a school close to me, and we moved in together. We're still living together, and still very much in love.

It can be done, but it isn't easy. You both MUST be in it. All the way. If one of you is not REALLY committed to the relationship, it will end painfully for both of you as one of you starts to drift away in response to new people and new situations. If you're not sure, you should probably just end it right now. In your heart, you already know if it could work or if it couldn't; just be honest with yourself, and go from there.
