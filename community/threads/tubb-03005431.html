I would never say this is the "best" country in which to live, but an individual stands a better shot at carving out their little piece of happiness here then they do in a lot of places.

We were amazed. ONLY in Canada could we find such a consistant quality of life.
My hat is off to you Canuks! The US of A would do well by following your example.
