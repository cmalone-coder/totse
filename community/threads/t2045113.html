This, sad to say, is remarkably uncommon; there are even those who might sex on the first date, or without any prompting for a date at all.

I know you think she's special, and indeed she is, but not terribly so to where obstacles don't matter; even one so banal as long distance. To site a slightly self-involved example I've had two long distance relationships: one a horrible failure errupting in infidelity and all things terrible, and the other doesn't even have official ties of boyfriend/girlfriend in which to tether but the temptations usually associated with the destruction of long distance relationships seemed to not exist. Of course temptations exist all day every day as the world is never at lack for trite, meaningless sex, especially for the beautiful, but we'd either be oblivious to them or think "aw, that's cute that they think they're getting some from me."

The point isn't about the aesthetics or snobbish behavior or me or anybody I've been involved with, the point is that everybody you become attached to in a relationship of such intimacy will seem extraordinarily special. Some are. Some aren't. The other point is fiendishly attractive indie girls with lustful libdos are in abundance; we live in the days of decadence and deviancy.

And I'm sure at least one of them might love you after getting her rocks off.

There isn't, essentially, anything novel about your situation, and what really drew me to the post was your name as I had just recently started re-reading the series out of a desire to pull myself out of a personal funk. I recommend you do something similar if you find yourself still too hurt or otherwise apprehensive about the pursuit of the booty. It's entertaining, can be insightful, and could give you conversation starters if you ever find yourself in the company of another compatible indie chick.

I refuse to let "read any good books lately" die as a cocktail conversation simply because nobody reads anymore.
