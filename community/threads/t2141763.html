Cops love mobile phones for the simple fact that nearly everyone uses one and they aren't really aware of the fact that police can get loads of information off them.

Re: Discussing Illegal Activities Via Text Message
All attempts at calling a phone are recorded. That includes ones where the caller calls, but the receiver doesn't pick up. The contents of the call are not recorded, unless the police believe it is necessary. All the information they have is what numbers were called from a phone at what times for how long. This is for every phone, not just ones under watch.

In some countries such as Singapore the first 10 seconds of every call is recorded. Some places use voice recognition on every phone call and listen for key words. I don't think this would be the case in a country such as the USA.

Text messages may be recorded depending on where you are. Pictures are certainly NOT recorded because that would take up far too much space.

A similar thing occurs with ISPs. For every IP address, the addresses it has accessed, at what times and for how long are recorded. There is nothing that can tell what information is being transferred.
