Re: German Music
I used to be a really big rammstein fan. They have very simple lyrics that are well enunciated. Unfortunately, rammstein carries a bad stigma nowadays - not as many fans today as their used to be.

BSK posted a link to Die Ärzte - another one of my favorite bands. They are also a great band for someone learning german. Though they typically sing faster, they enunciate well so it's easy to understand.

Wir Sind Helden is also one of my favorites. She uses an interesting vocabulary - lots of words that you probably wouldn't learn in a class. It's also a bit harder to understand what she's singing because she emphasizes her syllables according to the beat of the music - not how the word should be pronounced... if that makes sense. However, I absolutely love the music and lyrics (once I understood them).

Seeed is good but I don't like much of their later stuff. They need to stick to upbeat reggae - less hip-hop.

Sportfreunde Stiller was a band I heard an awful lot of while in germany. They've got a good sound and did an awesome cover of Friday I'm in Love (The Cure).
