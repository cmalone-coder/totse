Re: Tramadol
I find it to be a very useful drug. Great for socializing, school, physical activity, etc.

The come up feels like the come up of mdma or a like you're on sertraline. This is the seratoninergic effect, so tramadol acts as a fast acting anti-depressant as well as an opioid. After an hour or so the opiate agonism kicks in. It feels close to hydrocodone or oxycodone, but even more speedy. This is due to it's norandenergic actions. I feel like I have an endless amount of energy, and I actually enjoy jogging or running it. It's not as euphoric as the hydro or oxy though. It's very hard to urinate on this drug, which is one of the things i hate about it. After a couple more hours the gabanergic effects kick in and you feel tired and zombi-like.

If you want to take this drug recreationally then I reccomend taking it with a benzodiazepine like clonazepam (preferably) or alprazolam. It reduces the anxiety brought on from the speedy effects, and heightens the seizure threshold. Cannabis is also a very good drug to combine with tramadol. Tram isn't very euphoric by itself but marijuana makes it euphoric alright.
