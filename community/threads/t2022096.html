Re: eggs do not need refrigeration
They don't require refrigeration in the true sense. I remember my grandmother putting eggs in a clay pot at the bottom of her pantry shelf, covered in a wet cloth. They would stay good for quite sometime. But those eggs were ultra-fresh... coming from our own hens that we had in our farm.

In these modern times of fertilizers, crowded chicken coups, and hormone tainted chicken feed... the product (eggs) has become highly commercialized. Salmonella is a real threat, and considering the distances the eggs have to travel (farm -> distributor -> grocery store -> your house) it is a safe practice to refrigerate eggs. Again, it's all about quality and shelf life. Refrigeration helps to increase both.
