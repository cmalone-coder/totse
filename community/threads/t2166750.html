First time Salvia Experience
Technically, my second experience but the first time it worked.

About 3 years ago I had bought some salvia 20x extract from a internet headshop. I stuffed a fat bowl in my metal pipe that came with it, lit it up with my jet lighter and filled my lungs and held it in for a LONG time. I'm not sure why I never felt any effects.

So about a month ago I went to the local smokeshop and bought some 40x purple sticky salvia. Oh man...


I was a cocky shit about salvia since I had tried it before with no effects, and since I had tried mushrooms since the last salvia attempt. I felt more psychedelically experienced. Jokes on me. But anyways...

My girlfriend and I sat down in my room. I tried to make my mind as clear and open to new things as possible. I was staring at my window as I took a huge lungful with the jet lighter and I held it in for about 40 seconds. And the next thing that happened was the craziest, and one of the most frightening experiences of my life.

I was taken to another place. It looked like my room but it was somewhere else. I had this overwhelming feeling like I had fucked up, done something wrong by smoking salvia. Then the entire world started rotating. It was nuts, I felt gravity keep pulling me the way it was rotating. I kept repeating, "What the fuck?!" and I say the word fuck in the air as clear as day.

I forgot I had taken a drug and I believed all this was really happening to me. As soon as my girlfriend started laughing my trip took a turn for the worse... all of a sudden it was if I had seen my whole life up to this exact moment before like a movie and I had reached the end. It was time to die. I felt the universe starting to close on me from the left and the right. My girlfriend said I put my arms out and was screaming, "No!" like I was fighting it.

When I came to I just was kind of shaky and happy to be alive. I warned my girlfriend not to do it, and she tried it anyways, and started crying and saying she wanted it to go away. Which it finally did.

I'm never going to try salvia again and I dont recommend it to anybody, but the point of this thread is I want to hear other peoples experiences about salvia. How was your first time? I want some crazy stories!
