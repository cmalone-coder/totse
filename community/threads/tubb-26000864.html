Re: Looking for easy sources of chemicals
a) you need to either react HI with n-propanol which you will have to get from a chem supply or react an excess of propane with iodine in a glass vessel with light, Both of these are quite difficult.
b)DCM not suspicious if ordered from a chemical supply but it can be distilled from certain furniture strippers.
c)acetic anhydride can be made with sodium acetate and acetyl chloride, acetyl chloride can be made from phosphorus trichloride and acetic acid, so the easiest yet more deadly way to make it involves the pyrolysis of acetone to form ketene and then dissolving the ketene in glacial acetic acid thus forming acetic anhydride. Note that ketene bubbled through water produces acetic acid so you could just start with high concentration acetic acid or distilled water.
d)ethylamine reacted with two equivalents (preferably more) of isopropyl halide (not chloro)
e) You can distill it out of starting fluid, but you will need lots of ice or a dry ice/acetone condenser. If you have large cohojnes or the experience you can distill it out of a mixture of 95% sulphuric acid and ethanol. but it might just be best to get this from a chemical supplier.

None of these are really easy to get except maybe the DCM.

Starting fluid is a mixture of ethyl ether and hexane/petroleum ether. If you mix the contents of the can with water the hexane(which is very non-polar) will all be separate and some of the diethyl ether that you want gets sucked into the water as it is slightly soluble in it. And the rest of the ether stays with the hexane.

Essentially by using this water method you are throwing away good ether and not getting rid of the hexane.

Re: Looking for easy sources of chemicals
Acetic anhydride can also be made from vinegar. Household vinegar is mixed with NaOH until nuetral, then all water is distilled off. The remaining Sodium acetate is reacted with conc. sulfuric acid to make conc. acetic acid and sodium sulfate. The acetic acid is distilled. Then sulfuric acid is reacted with NaOH until nuetral, and all H2O is distilled off. The reamining sodium sulfate is placed into a glass gassing apparatus, and heated to about 250-270 degrees celcius, it breaks down and forms sulfur dioxide gas. This is bubbled through the conc. acetic acid to get acetic anhydride/acetic acid mixture.
