Keratosis somethingsomething. I have it too. The white stuff in the "pimples" is just accumulated skin oil. Basically the bumps are just hairs trying to grow, but your skin is so thick/dry that it attaches to them and hence raising it up. Skin oil can't escape, therefore producing the white heads, making you think they're zits. Pop them all you want, they don't spread.

Use Urea cream, you can purchase it a nearby pharmacy. It's a really effective moisturizer and loosens up the bumps. Doesn't eliminate them all, but it does cut down the number.

It goes away in your 20's I believe. Talk to a dermatolagist/doctor about it, they're very helpful.
