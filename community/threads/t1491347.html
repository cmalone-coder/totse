Re: A weird situation
Her confusing behavior is normal. Don't expect her to make a first move. It happens, but not often.

How do you know if she really likes you? Don't ever ask this question. There are a few reasons why:

First, you can't read minds. Sorry, you just can't. You may get good at guessing, but you could be wrong.

Second, focusing on this makes you uncertain and that will fuck up your game. Want to come off as insecure and unconfident...then try to read her every move and wonder if she really likes you.

Third, there is no real answer. If you were married for 30 years, she would know you well enough to know if she likes you. Right now she does not know you that well. She may think one way for a moment or longer, but things right now have no foundation and can change very quickly either way. How you act will influence her.

Then what should you do? Don't try to read her. Just assume she likes you and act as if that is true till she puts the breaks on. Don't care about the possibility of rejection AND don't consider it possible unless it happens. That is how you have the attitude that will make you act in a manor that will give you your best shot.
