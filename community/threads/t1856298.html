And you are absolutely right bone crusher. Phosphorus acid (H3PO4) is absolutely non-suspicous/watched, and is one of the most abundant acids on earth. It is in everything, even Coca-Cola. Hypophosphorus acid and salts (H3PO2), and phosphoric acid and salts (H3PO3) are highly watched, at least here in the US. However SWIM has heard that solutions of hypophosphoric acid are sold in some plating kits/solutions (as in metal plating) here in the US. Do some searching in that area.
