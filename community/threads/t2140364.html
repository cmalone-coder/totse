Reorganization on Totse has always been a fun thing to witness, in my opinion. It's been awesome to see the site develop slowly over the past 20 years. We started out with basically just a simple telnet session to ftp with bright, multi-colored splash-screens, which evolved into the Win 3.1 look with lots of bordered squares with text in them, right over to a more smooth appearance with automated functions and more robust links/menus, and now we have a place that's just busting with bells, whistles, and all kinds of easter eggs. Who knows what we could have tomorrow!

A little reminisce, if I may. These were the original login screens from The Temple of the Screaming Electron... very cool ANSI graphics with multi-colored presentations. I would guess that loading these for a look wouldn't be all that easy in this day and age, but if someone had the time... Enjoy
