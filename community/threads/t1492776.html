Alright, well, I might be able to give it to you straight forward since I'm a guy.

First of all, the guy that you are "madly in love with", tell him. I don't know about other guys, but I'll give almost any girl that's halfway goodlooking a chance if they just come out and be like "Listen, I've got something to tell you, and I think you need to hear it." Since you two ARE good friends, if he feels similar, or thinks there is a good chance at a relationship, then there will be one. Don't be afraid of getting hurt, because it won't be a GOOD relationship unless you put 110% into it. Most of all, just follow your heart.

As for this guy who you think you might wanna go out with, sit down and really think about it. Will you be happy with him? Why? Why not? Etc etc. I think the most important thing is to find someone who you will be happy with, and who you know. Take me for instance, I love my best friend (female) to death, and would do anything in the world for her. I truly am in love with her, and she knows this. Does it affect our relationship with each other? No, but her and I know each other REALLY well. I know things about her that she wouldn't even tell her boyfriends (special, I know ). You need to REALLY sit down, and sort out your feelings. If you know you're not gonna have a chance with the one you love, then sit down with him and talk to him about how you feel, preferably face to face, and trust me, it maybe hard, but once you get it over and done with, you'll feel much better. I keep getting the feeling that I must tell my friend how I feel about her (although she already knows), but I don't know how to describe it, so I know that it's love (well, not only that, a few more, but another story). Most of all, the person who knows you the most is YOU, and no one else.

Sorry about this long rant, but I love complicated relationships, especially when they are someone else's, because I like to put myself in their shoes, and do what my heart tells me to do. That's how I get through life, and it's done me pretty good so far. Good luck, and make sure to keep us updated since you've already gotten us, especially me, involved.

Hope this helps,
