A line between euphoric concentration and emotionless robot on adderall
Well first off, i have a bit of experience with adderall, done it around 8 times spaces out over a year, a recent one resulting from a misinformed decision to take 300mg of wellbutrin and 140mg of adderall that ended up in a night of dry puking, heart missing beats, not feeling any part of me, and thinking i was going to die. Well on with it..

Yesterday i woke up, ate some toast and eggs, took a 200mg caffeine pill, 150mg wellbutrin, and 40mg of adderall. (Yes i know I'm killing my precious little heart). Then i took a daily vitamin. Off to school. Throughout the day i felt happy, wanted to work, wrote a 3 page essay in 30 minutes, etc, etc. Lots of euphoria and well being. The crash at the end of the day was pretty bad so i just went to sleep.

Today, woke up, bacon and eggs, 200mg caffeine, 150mg wellbutrin, 50mg adderall. No daily vitamin. Went to school, during first hour i was constantly waiting for the euphoria and need to talk to people to kick in, but it never did. I just sat there and meticulously took down notes. The whole day i didn't feel one positive or negative emotions, just pure concentration.

I highly doubt it has to do with the vitamin... and i doubt my tolerance could go up that fast. So thoughts?
