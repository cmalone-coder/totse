Don't touch your face. I'd say from 14-22 everything my fingers touched turned to acne (on my own body that is).

Wash your hair regularly, wash your face with soap in the shower (but don't scrub TOO hard, or it'll just make your body make more oil). You can pop an occasional pimple if you can burst it such that ALL of the pus is drained, and you do so with clean hands, and you disinfect the area immediately. The medical community advises against it because most people will fail to do all of those and just drive the infection further into the skin, but if it's done right, abscesses should be drained.

Don't touch, pop safely to get rid of the troublesome developed ones, and don't let your face get greasy. Theoretically this should work better than any over the counter product.

You'll probably still get the occasional zits popping up here and there, but at least it won't be like braille.
