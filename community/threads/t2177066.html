Re: What is your cocktail of choice?
60ml Maker's Mark
30ml Amaretto (or less)
An orange
Martini glass
Zippo style lighter

Take a big piece of orange peel, cut a few slits in it and run it around the inside and rim of a full size martini glass. pour the booze in. Twist a piece of the orange peel so it spits the volatile oils onto the surface of the booze. After one or two sprays, do a couple through the flame of the lighter. It looks cool and adds a burnt orange flavour.

No idea what it's called. I picked it up from a bartender down South.
