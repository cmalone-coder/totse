Pineapple Pepper Martini

1 1/2oz pepper vodka
1/2 oz simple syrup
1/2 oz fresh lime juice
2oz pineapple juice

Shake ingredients cold and serve in a martini glass. You get a sweet, fruity pineapple flavor up front, but then the fire kicks in, and you get the slow burn in the finish.
