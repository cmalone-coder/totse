There's a reason Descartes's "I think therefore I am" is so respected and still used today. He's pointing out that that is all that you can really know with all certainty.

Someone else reading and understanding the same sentence does you no good. Your question is still contemplated and largely unanswered today.

If you're convinced that you will cease to exist in death, it's grounds for a nihilistic viewpoint. However, it's also grounds for renouncing all sense of conviction in any other "truths".

So you can't know, but the fact that you're posting here and asking the question shows something about your conviction that pondering the question is worthwhile, or at least that within your existence, pursuit of an answer, even by communicating with "robots", is significant.
