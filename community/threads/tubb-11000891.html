Re: Messenger Service?
In Win2k, a really good "fix" to eliminate this and the problem of the default drive shares is to simple STOP the "Server" service and change its properties so that it will not restart upon reboot.

You can do so by right clicking on my computer->Manage->Services and finding the "server" service.

This procedure does have the affect of causing you to no longer be able to share folders/files/printers but on the other hand it does eliminate one of the most abuse security issues in 2k.

Ok, so you want to know exactly what stopping the server service will do?

I thought that would be too much babble for this thread considering it has been deemed a poor solution. Anyways, here goes for SOME of those consequences, because I will admit I am not aware of ALL of them.

Here is what comes of the top of my head:

1) all sharing functionality will be lost. that means your shared folders/printers will be unaccessible and you will be unable to access other computers shares

2) your machine will not be able to browse resources via "Network Places"

3) other computers will not be able to see yours in "Network Places"

4) your ms networking protocols (netbeui) will not participate in any normal exchange of packets such as domain controller exchanges, computer will be unable to see published resources (2000 servers) and MS BROWSER elections (NT servers).

5) port 139 will be closed

6) your default shares (C$, D$, ADMIN$, etc) will no longer be available to the outside world.

7) NetBIOS will be disabled/rendered inactive (many of the things talked about above depend on NetBIOS operations).

8) your computer will be unable to access other machines by (windows domain) name.

Anything else you want to know? Anyone want to elaborate?
