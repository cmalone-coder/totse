Re: The BROWN mystery??
The "middle layer" is indeed an emulsion. The 2 main layers(Polar and Non-Polar) are having trouble seperating.

To fix this, just add a lot[don't get carried away though] of NaCl(table salt), trying to rest as much of it on the bubbles as possible. As the weight gets to heavy for them, they begin to sink and usually burst. Swirl the vessel.

Many things can cause this emulsion problem, the most common, shaking the 2 layers. Don't shake after adding a base like NaOH, rather, just swirl.

Mirror-like interfaces between the layers indicate a cleaner run reaction.

Lukky D
