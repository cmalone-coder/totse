Assuming a bike has a tire diamiter of 26 inches, and a gear ratio of 1:1, to get it spinning at 600RPM would be the same as going 46mph on a flat road. With a gear ratio of 2:1, it would be the same as going about 23mph, but twice as much force would be needed to do one full rotation.

It won't be easy to constantly go 600RPM. I'm not sure how much force would be needed to turn it over, but no matter what it won't be easy to maintain 600rpm.

Okay, to actually answer your question:

Take apart big machines that look like they spin stuff. Lathes, spindle winders, fans, and things like that. That's what I'd do. Just to clarify, are you sneaking into a working factory and stealing it straight up, or is it abandoned or something?
