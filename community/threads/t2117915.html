Research First, Talk 2nd...
Many threads I have seen in this forum ask a lot of questions about "why some things in the bible are a certain way" or "why do things in the bible contradict themselves". Some of the questions could have been solved before hand had you read more or all of the bible before attempting to come to conclutions about things. I'm not saying to believe this or that, all I'm saying is, before posting an idea or asking a question, make sure you are working with all of the information first and not just part of it. For instance, would you read half a page of instructions on how to put something together, then go on the internet and ask, "why the hell doesn't part A fit to part B"? Perhaps If you had read the instructions, from the top of the page to the bottom, in order...You would already know the answer........

This goes for anything, not just religion. Know what your doing before you just jump into something.
