Re: shrooms help
Although Coprinopsis atramentaria is a popular edible, several people every year (in North America) get sick enough from eating it to require medical attention (see Mechem, 2007). The mushrooms contain coprine, a toxin similar to disulfiram, the ingredient in the drug Antabuse, given to alcoholics. Like Antabuse, Coprinopsis atramentaria produces no ill effects--unless you consume alcohol, which will trigger nausea, vomiting, rash, dizziness, and so on. Theoretically, Coprinopsis atramentaria is a safe edible if no alcohol is consumed for about five days. However, there is evidence that the alcohol "consumption" can be as little as smelling rubbing alcohol or perfume! All in all, the mushroom is best avoided; I don't recommend it for the table.
