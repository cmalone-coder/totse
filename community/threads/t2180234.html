People with addictive personalities simply want to be 'high' all the time. I'm not high all the time, but I feel happier than I ever have. I consider suboxone my antidepressant, as it seems to act as one, and klonopin is a great anxiolic. I know it will be difficult to get off these particular drugs, but as long as they're working, why should I?

Panic disorders are a different thing but you may have conditioned yourself to counteract the anxiety with dope. And hence when your not on dope you will experience more anxiety from withdrawals and the previous mental conditioning.
