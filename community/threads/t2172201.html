The end of an era...
You may remember that I previously posted a topic in here about splitting up with the ex....Well, it was two months ago that she told me she had got with someone else.

That two months I have tried to re-kindle a friendship with her, she was willing but she was selfish and screwed up. All she seemed to want to do was hurt me. She did it without even realising she did it. Anyhow, she always avoided confrontation... She wouldn't want me to bring issues up. This was very contradictory to how I seen problems; i.e. they're only solved by confronting, rather than avoiding, the issue.

Anyhow, we've had ups and downs... fell out, made up, fell out again, made up. However, she went out with her new boyfriend and some friends for someone's birthday. So, trying to be a friend I just text and said "How's your night going?". She didn't reply till the next morning... "Yes I had an amazing time! Just in bed with Josh at the minute lol hungover".

Was it really necessary to tell me that? Anyways, I wasn't too fussed she's done worse. But I was made to be aware of pictures of her from that night out... The way she smiled, the way she was acting on the pictures, how happy she was... she was never like that when she was with me. It made me realise it was all a lie... This was a horrible realisation.

So, I just blocked her out from communicating me... ignored her texts, blocked her MSN/facebook/etc... And she found out, through a friend of mine.

It's all ended in a dramatic crash. A friend of 5 years, a true best friend, and I didn't even have the care in me to say goodbye...

Though, I wonder why I'm even typing this. I doubt you all care that much, I guess I just needed to say it. It's physically making me sob to type it, but it needs to come out...


I just don't know what to do. Is love always this difficult? I mean, this girl is my first love.. I adored her, and would have done anything for her... Having chose him over me, she denied anything I ever gave her... She's made that choice, and surely she has to deal with the consequences, right?

Five years isn't all that much in the long run... but it's a lot of time to waste...
