Re: horrible first time acid
Next time try sensory deprivation meditation (that rhymes!).

Basically, seclude yourself in a comfortable room, laying down is best for some. Make the room completely dark, and either use earplugs or calming music.

In that kind of setting it's much easier for your mind to wander. In your trip you had too much stimulus going on and made it harder for you to let you wander. When your senses are deprived of stimulation, you're going to be left alone with your mind, which will lead you into some very intense mental hallucinations -- which is where most of your self analysis, awareness, and betterment is going to take place, and much more efficiently.

The only thing I would change (sometimes), would be music instead of earplugs. I really like Shpongle for acid meditation. It may sound cliche, but the music was pretty much designed for it. The music is much more than ear candy. Those guys create sounds that guide you.

But you have definitely gained from this experience. It's a drug that needs to be treated with a good deal of respect, otherwise it's going to kick you in the ass in a way you could never have imagined.

Set and setting, bro. Never forget those words.

Take care of yourself, and trip safe. Feel free to hit me up on AIM any time, my screen name is in my contact info.
