Snuck into a DEA office
If you don't want to read:
We snuck into an abandoned Drug Enforcement building. Their security system is still active, motion detectors and door sensors.

If you like details:
Awhile back, Me and a friend went and checked out an Abandoned Government Office (Called “Drug Enforcement Administration”), and let me tell you. Its freaking creepy as hell. Its located on the third floor of a bank building along with several other sub-business offices. There are two doors, a front door which is off the main hallway, its glass and it has a government emblem on the front. We didn’t enter through there. Around back, down a side hallway, was a backdoor. It had no door handle or anything, So I decided to go in. This is when it starts getting weird.

Right as you walk in, there is a room to the left, Inside is a door propped up against the wall and a closet. But this closet is really weird. It has a peek hole looking into the closet and a lock on the outside, as if to lock something inside the closet. On the floor was three rust circles, maybe from some sort of drum. Across from this room was a kitchen, nothing interesting.

Further down the hall was another room on the left. This room was divided with a Chain link fence. The room also had an electronic keypad on the wall next to the doorway. This room also had a dome motion detector on the ceiling, when movement was detected anywhere in the room (360 degree coverage), 3 LEDS would glow red. We aren’t sure if its to turn off the lights when motion wasn’t detected for awhile, or if its hooked to a silent alarm, more about the alarm system later.

After these three rooms, the hallway connected into a big opening with windows over looking the back of the building and down into the parking lot. On the ceiling in here, there were two orange strobe lights. They weren’t blinking, they were off. But nothing else was in this room. Further down into the office was a closet, didn’t have much other than what looked like a switch board or something on the wall. Me and Akashe started looking at it and was about to jot down a model number. We stopped because this is when we first noticed a motion detector right above it. All I could manage to say while pointing at it, was “What the hell!? Holy $^#&!” and we got out of the room as fast as we could (Because this was our first time seeing one of the motion detectors). It was scary, I’ll tell you that.

The rest of the office wasn’t too interesting, just some coat closets and a conference room. Later, we made it near that glass door that we saw before entering the office. Across from that was an Alarm panel on the wall, (Honeywell 5505). We couldn’t see anything triggered or tripped, but there was a very dim red led lit. Before we left, We noticed a switch board for their phone system. This had to be the most interesting, there was a phone box to plug in a phone, and sharpied on it was, “Secert Service”. We then left after that.




Our second visit to the Government Office went really bad and unexpected.

At 4pm, me and Akashe met up at the building, He said he would wait outside as I went around and unlocked the door. So as I went to the second floor to loop around and to the locked door, I heard something. At the door somebody was talking to Akashe. I peeked around the stair well to see a man with a poloshirt. I figured it was security or something. But he was asking what he was doing. So I sorta paniced, I went back up stairs to take the elevator down. But somebody was coming up, So I went back to the stairwell and went down as fast as I could. On my way out the door, in the parking lot across from us was this truck, with an alarm going off on the inside. The side of the truck said “Recall Alarm Systems” or something of the sort. So I jumped on my bike and left as fast as I could.

The question:
We want to go back and take pictures. But we're afraid the security company will show up again and we really don't want to get caught.
