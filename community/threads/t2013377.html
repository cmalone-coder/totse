Food dehydrators ZOMG
Do you own one? What do you do with it?

My favorite thing to do is dehydrate pieces of fruit like sour apples with cinnamon and a bit of maple syrup dropped on top.

Works very well with wild gourmet mushrooms found in the field, and I would imagine this can also apply to drying psychoactive ones (on a low heat setting.)

These things are also good for drying fresh herbs like basil and parsely when you know you won't use them for awhile.

In my experience, bananas do not turn out to tasty, so I would recommend sticking to sweet or sour fruit.

Something tells me no one here has one of these or cares, but I thought I'd throw it out there.

Re: Food dehydrators ZOMG
Typical food dehydrators COOK at a low temp with moving air so therefore they do NOT dehydrate by rule.

Dehydrating food happens at a mild temp. Dry cool air is best for dehydrating. (Why you get so dehydrated during the winter).

Build your own! What you need:
1.) 20" box fan (get a cheap one)
2.) Cellulose (paper) air conditioner / furnace filters (20"x20")
3.) a couple of bungee cords to secure the filters to the fan.

That is all you need. Best beef jerky prep method ever!

Stack the filters together with whatever you are dehydrating. Bungee them to the front of the fan. Lay fan down on surface that allows unrestricted air flow. Let 'er rip!

Dehydrated food kicks butt!!!
