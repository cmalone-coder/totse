Re: acid situation
1.will we be able to ride our bikes home on acid (bout 40 mins away)

Bike riding is one of the coolest things to do on acid. As long as it's a relatively safe road, yes.

2.is it possible to get past his dad without him noticing (he is drunk almost every time i go over)

Yes.

3.how long will the high last cuz i need to be sober by the time i get home

The 'high' will last about 8 hours for the more intense parts. The come down isn't exactly 'sober', but you'll be 'sober' enough to function in front of your parents.

4. lastly this doesnt really matter but does acid show up in piss tests.

No.

-------------
Now, with that said, you're already making a few mistakes. Taking acid at a party is a bad idea, as is taking acid in a house with strict parents around. The setting in which you trip is one of the most important factors of having a good trip, and the options you've laid down are both bad.

Although, you're only taking one tab each. There's a chance they'll be strong enough to have you tripping nicely, but I kind of get the feeling that they're not going to be too strong, so the setting won't really matter too much if they're weaker.

It's peculiar that your one friend has tripped several times and still thinks tripping at a party is a good idea. With that many people around, they're going to be a huge distraction from the psychedelic, which can actually make the trip less intense because you can't focus on your mind. Among other things, though, it can also make the trip unpleasant, and worst case scenario can make you have a bad trip.

You're not playing with something like weed or opiates or anything like that. You're taking a very powerful psychedelic. Granted, you're only taking 1 tab each, but like I said, there's a chance it'll be decently potent.

Tripping in nature > everywhere else.
