Re: "best things to do on shrooms"
First time I tried shrooms, a friend and I just walked around town, got some Burger King, laid down in the grass and stared at clouds for a bit. Then we had to get a bus home, and amazingly, the bus ride I usually find mind-numbingly boring was awesome. I was lying down on the back seat, looking out the window, and just saw the craziest shit. Then we got off the bus about 40 minutes later (felt like hours); and we bumped into some friends and we just sat around having the weirdest conversation I'd ever had in my life.. contemplating how easy it would be to kill people, about fucked up views on life, as well as other things that I can't even begin to comprehend sober.

After the trip started to wear off I went home and played San Andreas on the Xbox and listened to some music, which was very enjoyable. Then it got pretty late so I just slept, and had some pretty fucked up dreams (not sure if they were due to me doing the shrooms; or whether it was coincidental).

I've done shrooms a few times since then (I haven't been doing drugs all that long), but my favourite thing to do on them remains just being around people I like, talking about random shit, listening to music, and eating food. I know I could be doing other stuff, but for me I just like doing shrooms with friends and seeing what our drug-controlled minds can come up with.

One thing I'd recommend doing whilst on shrooms is to write down all the thoughts and conversations you have. I did that one trip, and then when I read it sober it amused me no-end.
