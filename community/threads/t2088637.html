You dismiss this monster as a figment of your imagination or the sense that they shouldn't be friends. But soon, you find yourself talking to him more than you were when they were together. When he gets a new girlfriend, you're angry. You do anything you can to try and keep him off her. You flirt uncontrollably. You talk shit about her, even when you don't know her very well, only through a friend of a friend. You try to make him break up with her. But something happens. You lie to people you once loved, tell them you're going out with friends. And you go out with him. You go to a graveyard, do things you wish you could regret, but don't seem to be remorseful for. And things escalate from there until he asks you that one question you've been waiting for him to ask you, but almost dreading. You say yes, but she's not happy. She asks you to break up with him, and you do it, because that's what friends do, right? Days, weeks pass, and nothing. Until that one blessed moment, while you're toying with the idea of asking him out, despite what his old girlfriend says, and she tells you that you can. Like you needed her to say yes. Like you needed permission. Like she was your mother, finally letting you date when you turned 16. So you start dating him again, and you find that you don't feel the same way as you did when you were single. On one hand, there's the enormous pressure of sex. You want to, you really do, but you don't want to. You figure you really love this boy, but that's a hard thing to believe when you've forsaken love for most of your life. So what do you do? Eh, you wait it out, but then you see signs as if from some supreme being or beings unknown. They don't want that to happen, because every time, something happens that you don't expect, that gets in the way. So you let it slide, and move on to bigger situations. Does her really love you? Part of you, the romantic part, wants you to believe him when he says that he loves you. That when he kisses you, he's really kissing you and not someone else. Someone that people would deem more beautiful. But then there's that nagging voice in the back of your head that tells you that he's lying. That he's just a typical teenage boy. That he's telling you everything, tricking you, just trying to get into your pants. Because he's lost the girl he used to have sex with and he's trying to replace her. While the two sides of your personality fight, you realize that he might just be what you've been missing all this time. No matter his intentions, you have someone. Your friends are great, they love you, but they've been there all along. You have someone who will hold you in their arms and kiss your head just because you're there."
