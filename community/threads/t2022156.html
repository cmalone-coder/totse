Re: ubuntu problem switching resolution
you could try reconfiguring your xorg.conf file
go to a terminal and type
Code:
 sudo dpkg-reconfigure xserver-xorg
it may look a little intimidating, but its pretty straightforward, just select what your screen resolution is when it asks, and use the i810 driver when it asks you to select it
i had your same problem and that worked for me
