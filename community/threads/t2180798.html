Blackbird: the African American browser
It's good to see that developers have finally realized that the black community needs a web browser of its own.

http://blackbirdhome.com/

It features tabbed browsing, a pop-up blocker, and Black Bookmarks. It was developed by a team of African Americans. It also allows you to connect with what's going on in the African American community, and "empower your Internet to find what you want when you want it."

Check out their Black Search. It makes it easier for blacks to discover black related content:
http://www.blackbirdhome.com/search5.html

If you search the word "black" on Black Search, black metal, Black Sabbath, Jack Black, and the color black are all noticeably absent from the first page of search results. This makes sense, because these are all white people related topics. Instead you are greeted with BlackPlanet, Black Film, Darnell's Black Radio Guide, BET.com, and a community college website about Black History Month.

I have noticed some bugs with the spellcheck feature, as it attempts to replace the word "ask" with "axe", but otherwise it seems to be functioning perfectly. So far I am deeply enjoying this diversification of my web surfing experience.

What do you guys think?
