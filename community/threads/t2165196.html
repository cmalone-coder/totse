Re: getting adium to work on a school computer
OP: You might need to configure Adium to use your school's proxy, if it has one. You do this in the connection/network options in Adium. For example, in order to get the internet/other apps working on my school's network I have to connect to the statewide school proxy (proxy.decs.sa.edu.au); if your school doesn't have one, then not sure what it might be--have you checked to see whether your school's firewall is blocking it?

SLice_760: Only thing I could think of would be port forwarding; but I'm unsure how you'd go about doing that with IRC off the top of my head.
