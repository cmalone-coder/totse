Millisecond cuts/Shaky cam
I know this started appearing over 10 years ago, but as of recently with film, anime, and especially some T.V, this type of hyper-fast editing paired with a camera that looks like it has been attached to a vibrator and held by a drunk is really getting annoying.

One of the worst recent abusers is the new Battlestar Galactica. I swear... every... single space shot the camera is shaking around and doing sloppy zooms in and out really fast. A little tremble is fine, but I feel like I'm constantly watching something from the Blair Witch.

As much as I liked it, I could not stand the action shots in Hot Fuzz. I swear it was like having a seizure with a million fast cuts a second and the camera flailing around like mad.
It's funny how new CGI effects cannot be seen in all their glory because everything is flipping out all the time (Matrix Revolutions anyone?). A lot of scenes in Lord Of The Rings got pretty bad aswell

This is why I am still a big fan of HK movies from the 80's/early 90s. They had fast action but did it through the actions itself and not the camera... the most the camera movement made was the occasional crash zoom, but at least it stayed put. Now it seems that the same people who do music video's for the ADD generation are shooting movies.

I want to see the action, not be given a headache. Thoughts?
