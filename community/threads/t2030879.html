Re: AR7 in to pistol
You need a virgin receiver, first of all. Secondly, there is no legal federal limit on the barrel length of a pistol. As long as your receiver was never registered at factory as a rifle, or longarm on a 4473, you can make it a pistol. You can also attach a stock and go from pistol to rifle at will, however, you must maintain an overall length and barrel length that remain within legal rifle limits, otherwise you will need to get BATFE approval and register the weapon as either a Short Rifle, or Short Barrel Rifle appropriately.

Re: AR7 in to pistol
No. You may be prohibited from manufacturing a pistol if you are too young to own one in your state, though. If you are able to own a handgun at 18, or possess one, in your state, you should be legally able to manufacture a handgun regardless of whether you are allowed to purchase a handgun from a FFL. If you live in a state with an AWB, such a handgun will most likely be banned from manufacture and/or ownership.
