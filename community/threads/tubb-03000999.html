If one percent of the offspring develope mutations (an abnormality in DNA) that's 100mutations per day. This is starting with one fungus spore. The next day we have a thousand fungi that will each produce a thousand more, again 1% mutation.
So now we have a million fungi and 10000 mutants. The next day you will have a billion fungi and 100000 mutations, and so on...Soon you will reach millions and billions of mutations in one day. One of these mutations will be the right combinant DNA to allow the fungus to attack the new "resistant" hybrid seeds. And now you have the stage for a corn epidemic. Because the corn can't change it's DNA through reporoducing, every ear is susceptible to the new fungus. So the world loses much of what it made to fungus, because we thought we could do better than mother nature. But the epidemic will only last one year, for the next year we'll have new GMO's resistant to the new fungus. Farmers then buy the new more expensive seeds (if it's new it's expensive) and the cycle starts again. The net result is increased food costs, farmers more in debt, and more people hungry. It's better to let corn fungus have it's small share of non modified corn, let the corn on it's own develop it's own resistance. If it should need any help, then bust out the round up. Genetically modified organisms are thing of the future, we can't work as fast as mother nature so we can't compete.
