Get a ph meter and calibrate it yourself using distilled water as a ph=7 setpoint and 2 grams of lye in 100ml of water as a ph=13.7 setpoint. Then dissolve the DMT in just enough warm distilled water to completely dissolve it. Then add, a drop at a time, phosphoric acid until the ph drops down to 7. When it is exactly 7, you have pure DMT in phosphoric salt
