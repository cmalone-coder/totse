Re: Roughly speaking..
I googled it and couldn't find an answer for situps and calories burned that I believed was accurate for all body weights.
Like negz says, estimate the weight of your moving parts.
To make the math easy I'm going to say your moving body parts weigh 90 pounds (probably less) and your movement roughly equals 3 feet up and 3 feet down (it could be less).
I remember from an old time calculation that 150 pounds moving 6 feet up and 6 feet down = one calorie burned.
So if my math is right, for every 10 situps you will burn one calorie.

If you can do 40 situps a minute , you'll burn 4 Calories per minute and 40 Calories in 10 minutes.

Sit-ups are a terrible way to burn calories vs. leg exercises.
Use your legs (leg exercise) and easily burn 3X that much per minute.

It doesn't matter when you exercise.
