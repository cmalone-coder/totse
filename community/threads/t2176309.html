Re: are some people more susceptible to hallucinations than others?
More likely, people outside of your circle are more likely to use different terminology to describe different subjective effects. Most people I've read reports by had a small amount of drug experience, and this could also factor in.

Personally mescaline feels much more like "dreaming while awake" than diphenhydramine. I personally found it to produce a more sleepy version of being awake for several days on amphetamines or even just due to insomnia. The paranoia, the nature of the hallucinations, and the headspace feels that way, my reactions are totally different because I was sedated as hell. Why the fuck you'd want to take that shit more than once or twice is beyond me. It's pretty much how I felt throughout high school, but instead of becoming aggressive and alert, I feel helpless, retarded, and schizophrenic with an extra dose of "the world's ending? the satellites are in my head!" thrown in.

Plus I felt like shit the week after...
