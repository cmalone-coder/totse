Re: What would you carry as a back up?
Back before poverty forced me to part with my second 1911 I carried a comander size Norinco 1911 to backup my full size Kimber. I am in the process of purchacing a very basic and inexpensive fullsize 1911 that I can customize to backup my fullsize springfield.

I am going to checker the front strap, install and fit a threaded barrel, the polish the hammer and sear, and install a reduced power mainspring.

The idea of having an identical pistol as a backup may seem a little bit John Woo, Hollywood, but I belive it is not without it's merits.

First off, when I had a comander as a backup I grew to hate it. It didn't have the same sight radius, feel, and it recoiled harder than the fullsize. But it was nice to have a backup that took not only the same ammunition, but the same magazines. The controls and grips were also identical.

The fact of the matter is that if one is carying a pistol for self defence his life depends on it. And if his life depended on it he would be a fool to use anything but the best. A climber would not carry a kite screen as a backup rope.

I donot like small pistols. They have short sight radiuses (the plural form of the word radius?), small grips, and small controls. The Glock 30 is not terrible, but the glock 21 is a much more plesant pistol to shoot.

For a backup, I'll just throw the gun really hard. I carry a knife with me all of the time and that's about the only backup I plan on having. I carry my 1911 and a spare magazine, that's about all I care to carry. If all hell breaks out and my gun doesn't work, then fuck it, I can run really fast.

The KT P-11 has a lot going for it. 10 rds of 9mm in a very concealable and affordable (~$220) package. Most folks I know that have it recommend it highly (almost cult status), but even they will state it sometimes needs an F & B and good break-in before it will shoot reliably. The KT P11 has a much tougher DAO trigger than the Kahr, but has the advantage of restrike capability, and either 2 or 3 extra rds depending on the mag.

I went with the Kahr despite a considerably higher pricetag because it fits me even better size-wise (especially width) for CCW under light covering and I LOVED the way it shoots for me. Very natural shooter from the get go.

Hmmm...Ubermutant, I'd suggest you try one out and see what you think. Do some dry fire at least. BTW, my KT P32 has been 100% reliable out of the box and you simply cannot beat it for size and weight. Also their customer service is second to none. I suppose that's a recommendation for Kel-Tec.
