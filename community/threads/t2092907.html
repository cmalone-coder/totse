Re: Antidepressants
I agree with the person who said no Paxil. Try that as a last resort. Start with the standard stuff; what has been shown to work. That's pretty much Lexapro, Prozac, a couple others. If SSRIs don't work, they'll probably put you on Wellbutrin. The reason you don't try Wellbutrin first is that the side effects can be something creepy for a lot of people. They report feeling like robot-zombies or something. Weird shit. But if that happens, you just quit taking it. There is very little to no risk of withdrawal from Wellbutrin. The reason not to take Paxil is that the withdrawal is really fucking bad. The same can be said of most SSRIs, but Paxil is much worse.

Also, don't forget about the suicide risk. There is a statistically significant risk for people under the age of 18 that antidepressants will result in suicide. I don't think it's quite understood why this happens, but I'm pretty sure it has something to do with the drugs getting you motivated before they actually make you feel better. So you feel as shitty as always, but now you're motivated to do something about it. So resist the urge to kill yourself for the first few weeks.
