If your book on how to build the 9mm SMG is the P A Luty book. Research more. I know of someone who build the weapon, The breech failed. The barrel was sent flying and the bolt bent to the point that it was impossible to remove. I wish I could have had a video for you. I was too afriad to risk my new 300$ camera to watch something that I was sure would explode.

To anyone who cares. The device was tested in a scrap yard 2km from any residential land. the nearest house was 2.3km away. Myself and the builder of this weapon were behind two inches of steel and two compacted cars when the device went off. We used the 'Ragnar Benson' remote firing rig to test. Two rounds were in the magazine at the time.

I like the idea of a .22 subsonic machine gun. It would be a cool toy without being very dangerous. If I were you, I would go with either a short recoil or a Gas system to cycle the rounds. Gas might be out of your skill/equipment range though.
