It sounds like you guys would have the perfect relationship if you never got engaged. But unfortunately it is very tough for a relationship to go backwards. Im going to guess that you are a very young couple. He probably is not sure what he wants right now and it sounds like you are not either. Id bet that even if he said again that he wanted to marry you, you may have a few doubts.

Give him a little time. During this time, try to figure out if the life you have now, or something very similar, is what you want forever. Even if he decides to marry you, he will still be the same person. Remind him of how things are getting better by thanking him and showing appreciation for things he does for you that he maybe used to not want to do. Also reward that behavior by taking care of some of his needs that you may not want to. Like putting in the energy to stay out with him when you are out with his friends and not yours.

Ask for date or appointment or something and tell him that this is still on your mind. Dont force him to make decision, but say we eventually have to move forward and ask if he has an idea when he would be ready, or if he has any suggestions for the next step. You may want to give him 2-3 chances with this, but dont make it indefinite. Dont let him use you to avoid making a decision. There is a difference between figuring things out, and getting lazy and comfortable.

I hope things work out for you two, but keep in mind that it is possible for you to eventually be happy with someone else. Never forget that. This is about him, but it is also about you.
