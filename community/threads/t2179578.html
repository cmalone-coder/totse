I've driven one too. Horrible contraption. Typical awful French clutch, gearbox like wiggling a broomstick in a bag of marbles, driving postion like a gorilla, no proper feel to the steering - it gives no inclination of when it was reaching its limits. When it does reach its limits, it understeers like a motherfucker, due to the fact it props itself up onto three wheels, and because of the short wheelbase it was averse to some left-foot-braking drifting to correct the understeer. It may be a good car to some people, but them cars really don't suit my driving style. I like high-revving, long geared, long cars capable of four-wheel drifting, that actually give feedback through the steering and are actually nice to sit in, with progressive and constant power delivery.
