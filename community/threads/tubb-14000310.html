Does Anyone Know Anything About Real Anarchy?
Tell me if I'm wrong if you know better:

First off: Anarchy doesn't mean that you get to just go out and kill people and do whatever the fuck you want. Anarchy literally means your freedom ends where mine begins, just like Democracy; only Anarchy has a much different political structure.
There are no set laws, but there are trials. So, if you kill someone you will probably find yourself in jail and might even find yourself with a worse sentence than if you were tried under a Democratic government, because Democrats are fucking pussies and so are Republicans.

Instead of being run by a three pronged government and state, there would be several "unions" so to speak that would run everything. So, basically it would ruin Bill Gates, but it would do the little guy a great deal of good. But, business would continue. Just not in mass production. Everything would go from being a few major corporations to millions of tiny corporations.

Because of this, there would probably still be taxes. But, not in the same form we have now. (I'm talking about the U.S) People would probably have to pay taxes to whatever unions they belonged to, and it would probably be damn near impossible to avoid being in a union. Considering they would run everything from your water and electricity to your clothesing and agriculture.

Also, there would still remain a form of currency. There is always a form of currency. Though, if there was no set form of currency (which means that we would use all different currency from all different countries) then we would just have a trade system. Primative and ineffective. A major con in the whole Anarchy ideal.

Plus, if you go to trial you are tried in front of your fellow citizens who punish you anyway they see fit for the crime that you have commited. You don't get to just run around scott free. Sure, drugs would be legals, nudity legal, those sorts of things. But, you go around raping babies and you're going to end up with a bullet in your brain by jury orders in no time.

I don't know where everyone is getting these ideas that Anarchy is just a free for all. There's no such thing in a human society. We're creatures of habit. We crave structure and rules. If we didn't, we wouldn't create them. Now, sure, you can go ahead and tear down all we've evolved to in the last million years, but it wont do you nor I any good.

Cheers,
Lost
