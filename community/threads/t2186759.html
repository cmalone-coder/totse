There are a number of artists I wish to list, but I will only name a few, as this thread is quite long and informational as it is. From what I have read no one has as of yet mentioned any of these guys.

Typical Cats: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dSP5gwpU4iU
-Underground out of Chicago on the Galapagos 4 record label. One of the members, Denizen Kane, is a member of the spoken word group I Was Born With Two Tongues

Doomtree: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=toELT1uWDKU
-A crew of artists and DJ's located in Minneapolis who make music.

Dessa: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dgDpzOOCYGg
-A member of Doomtree, A professor, a writer, a spoken word poet, and philosophy major wound in to one to create my favorite woman currently involved with the hip-hop scene

Cecil Otter: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x7T6mnNX8rY
-Also a member of Doomtree, has more of a Johnny Cash feel to him, once a pro skate border, an accident brought him back to doin' the rap thing

Mike Mictlan: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-gZehXu9yhk
-Another member of the Doomtree crew, originally from LA brings the most traditional hip-hop feel to the crew. This track is off his collab album with Doomtree producer Lazerbeak.

Sims: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=563z3OqpHtE
-Probably the most political of the Doomtree crew, fucking great rapper.

P.O.S: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZWYZLg0R1M
- The final rapper in the Doomtree crew, POS is definitely the most punk influenced of all the members, shown here in this song "POS Is Ruining My Life"

Kanser: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tjSSa77ow5A
-Another group from Minneapolis, composed of New MC (aka Zachariah) and Unicus. Real grass roots hip-hop out of south Minneapolis

New MC/Zachariah: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mGeZGFsZEEk
-A member of Kanser, Traditional Methods, More Than Lights, and a local Hip-Hop legend to Minneapolis. Once the host of Headspin Hip-Hop night at Bon Appetit in Dinkytown, well known and respected.

More Than Lights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glsRBDCmHWU
-Basically Kanser with a live band and some different tunes

Heiruspecs: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmZ9umLuEp0
-A live band out of St. Paul featuring Felix and Mau'adib on the raps

Kristoff Krane: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6TijuW27lyg
-Yet another hip-hopper from the Twin Cities area, Kristoff Krane is a counselor, rapper, writer, musician, and member of Abzorbr, Face Candy, Sector 7G.
