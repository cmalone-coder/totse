Re: Do you ever feel so very, very, very alone?
Loneliness, fear, anxiety, anger, sorrow, pain...these all suck. You've got to stand up when you feel like shit and rely on yourself even then - when shit goes raw, you can't just give in and expect anything to get better.

Got problems with your girl? Talk about it. Don't let yourself yell at her...but talk about the things you don't think you should; you might find the relationship shouldn't be, or you might find it strengthened. But don't be afraid.

Afraid of being alone? Don't be. You're a human being with a funtional mind, a body, hopes, dreams, and aspirations. So long as you are dedicated to your goals, you really can accomplish many great things, even if you don't recognize the support you have. You can build anything you want...you just have to be dedicated to the work involved. Do the best you can with determination - you'll atleast be able to look back and tell yourself you've done what you could to make the best of things. Not all efforts will be met with the desired outcome...but the knowledge and experience gained will be just as much a success.

So long as you are alive and remotely sane, you can come back from the brink of death. Humans are a lot stronger than we often think of ourselves...we just have to rough it through some shit.

Another thing: It doesn't hurt to believe in some sort of purpose...whether it be honor, truth, god, love, peace, friendship, family...anything really. We're mortal...so don't fret over the destructibility of these things...just find something you care deeply about and work to attain and maintain it.
