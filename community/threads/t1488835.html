Re: Advice needed. About a girl.
It's good that you're stepping up now. It'll help you in the future.

Note the future part.

You can get around her being in the driver's seat right now, but you can't avoid the fact that you guys will be in college come fall. An hour may not be that far, but it's also more than a lot of people are willing to go for a relationship that's still in its formative stages, *especially* a girl starting college with all those big, dumb, beefcake frat boys around.

It honestly doesn't sound like she likes you that much. The appearance, from what you've said, is that of a girl who enjoys your company and friendship enough to agree that she would give it a shot, then realized her feelings weren't backing up the words. Just because girls say they only want to be friends, it doesn't mean they'll necessarily change their tune if you're persistent. They may, they may not. You have to learn when to fight on and when to let it go.

I won't say this is a lost cause, because you know the girl, not me. It may work out the way you want, it may not. Call it your first step on the road to being well-rounded.
