Sparkler bombs aren't tolerated here. Just too damn retarded.

Its going to be bright... and possibly a light "poof" sound then a cloud of mostly Al2O3 dust... which is really bad for your (or anyone else's) lungs... You just wasted all those fireworks.

Now the only thing i can think of to redeem this mistake... is to wet down the powder with around 25% alcohol solution and work it into a "dough" with gloved hands and make a very big, long sparkler "log." Giving it a long time to dry. Maybe like 3/4" OD by however long you can make it... Take pics and vid...

Or you could try to make dough with the powder like above and make cut or pumped stars with it... however you would need to prime it with BP... then you could make easter egg aerial shells for instance... using commercial 1fg black powder for lift... You could probably use the visco from those roman candles for the aerial shell fuse... however it would only be enough for a couple shells... now if you have alot of visco at your disposal you could possibly do it...

Should be feasible if you work enough and give everything enough time to dry to have home made aerial shells by the 4th...
