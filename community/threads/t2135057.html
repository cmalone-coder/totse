Break it in half. Chew it. That's 20mg, perfect for a novice w/ no tolerance. If you feel like you can do more, take the remaining half, break that in half the best you can, and eat that. Then eat the remaining half an hour or two later.

It takes 15 - 45 minutes to kick in, so give it some time before you redose.

Alternatively, you could just eat the entire 40mg - or eat half, wait 2 hrs than eat the other half. At any rate, 40mg all at once won't kill you. Worst that'll happen is you'll throw up. It's all about finding that right amount that gets you off.
