You can smoke salvia without a bong. I managed to get some decent effects off salvia (for the first time out of 5 or so previous uses) last night just by smoking it with a normal lighter. Keep the flame on it the whole time, take a looong toke, hold it in for 30 seconds, exhale, repeat, put everything down very quickly.

It's not particularly pleasant for me after the initial effects. I lost all sense of three-dimensional perspective and couldn't figure out which direction I was facing, or even if I was standing or sitting. Every time I moved my head or my body everything would fracture into different two-dimensional layers that started closing in around me. It was cool at first, but then I started feeling trapped and couldn't move. I'd also completely forgotten I'd just smoked the salvia. I came out of it in reverse, but felt intensely paranoid and suspicious of reality for another 15 minutes. Gonna do it again though, see what else it has to offer.

EDIT: Before that, salvia's done a range of things to me, and it's never been the same twice. Once I could only move in extreme slow motion (that was fun), once I just got giggly and acted stoned, once the walls started swimming slightly and I got a bit tripped out by a flashing light, once I got very heavy and fascinated by a pen and once nothing much happened. Each time before I did it with a mate and he got different effects each time too. It's a peculiar drug, I think the reaction you get is very dependent on your circumstances, and something has to set off the reaction. When I lost my sense of perspective, it started when I reached out for a glass of water and missed (I'd gotten stumbly and uncoordinated), then spiralled from there. It's an interesting thing to try.

I inhaled the moisture out of the mix & then heated it up a bit more & then switched the torch around & into the bowl.
