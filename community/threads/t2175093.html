I'm guessing you'll find Fedora > Ubuntu in all probability.
I'm tentatively recommending Fedora.
If you hadn't included the "just works", I'd have said Slackware, so if you're feeling ballsy, I still say Slackware.

EDIT: one more thing. You can repartition and try out various distros before deciding on one if you so choose. Click on "Start", then right-click on "My Computer" and click "Manage". There should be two little partitions and one big one, click on the big one and click Shrink, and shrink it by at least 30 gigabytes. Then you can install whatever distro you're considering into those thirty gigabytes before deleting everything (to see if you like it).
