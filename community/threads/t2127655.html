I work in a major chain Pharmacy that shall remain un-named....Of course all pharmacies will differ from chain to chain, store to store, and state to state....but for us:


As someone previously stated, when we drop a pill on the floor...it goes in the trash. And you'd be surprised how many we drop. They accumulate in certain areas...for instance; there are large turnstiles that have bins of pill bottles and caps ect. right near where we count. It's pretty common to drop a pill into one of those, and it's a pain to fish them out so we just leave them. Also, they accumulate in the bottom of our script-pro machine, which is a large cabinet looking device with a robotic arm that automatically dispenses the most common drugs, including controls.

Usually when we drop a pill in a hard to reach area (which is often), it just gets left there, and will be sweeped up sometime later and thrown in the trash...regardless of whether it's a control or not...If we drop it someplace where it's easy to pick up, we will pick it up and throw it in the trash, but usually we're more focused on getting the hundreds of scripts we fill each day done on time...

Also most pill bottles have a little plastic thing with a dessicant in it to keep the pills dry/fresh or what not...it's pretty common to leave like 1 pill in the bottle by accident after dumping it out onto a tray, thinking that it's the little plastic deal (a lot of times the plastic deal will block the mouth of the bottle making you think you dumped them all out but actually theres 1 or 2 left).


I should point out that "controls" are the "lower" level controlled substances, stuff like valium or vicodin, ect. The "higher" level controlled substances like oxycotin ect are considered "narcs" and are kept in a safe. Only the pharmacist can dispense these, and the pharmacists are much better about keeping them off the floor.


I should point out that as a pharmacy we have "special" trashcans everywhere to dispose of anything that has a patients personal information on it. These are kept locked up at all times and are carted away by a special trash company. 90% of the time we throw the empty pill bottles and dropped pills in there...but I'm sure some end up in the "regular" trash.

So if you "dumster dived" at my pharmacy (and I imagine most places)...you wouldn't end up with much....maybe one or two 1mg ativans and a stray vicodin, and assorted random pills. Maybe a few empty bottles of controls/narcs that you could scrape.


Overall I'd say the payoff is not worth the risk of being caught...but if you think it's worth it, go for it....

Private pharmacies are more likely to not be as cautious.

No, the only stuff that HAS to bee carted away specially (by law) is HIPPA stuff...that is anything with a patients personal information on it. Different chains might have different policies...but I have worked in multiple stores for 2 of the 3 chains you mentioned and I can say neither one was strict about empty pill bottles, ect.....just HIPPA info..

It just so happens that we toss most of our empty pill bottles and ect in the "special" trash cans because there's so many around. I toss empty bottles in the "normal" trash all the time...

I work at a "major" pharmacy BTW, one that has been mentioned in the thread....and AGAIN as I said it varies store to store and state to state.
