Re: Went frog hunting yesterday
well, typically you would use something called a "frog gig", around here in WV its just called going giggin or going frog giggin.
Its basically like a fish spear, a long spear like rod, with two or three tines on the end, with barbs. Then, when you see the frog, just spear it and pull your catch in, then have a creel or bag handy to keep it.

But seriously, whats the point of just going and killing frogs if you arent going to eat them? Frog legs are a delicacy as well as an easily obtainable food if you are in a urgent situation
