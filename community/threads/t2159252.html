Justified in feeling back-stabbed?
Posting about this hoping to get some insight, opinions, whatever really. I also do need to kind of vent.

So the other night me and my boyfriend are at this party that a close friend of mine was having. This girl isn't related to me, but she pretty much holds the status of a cousin as my parents and hers are good friends. It wasn't a huge gathering but it was a bunch of very good friends so the alcohol was cracked open and we were all getting fairly drunk. We're hanging out in her bedroom and its getting into the night and the three of us are lying around on her bed talking and the sum of it is, the lights were turned off and my friend starts stroking me, kissing my neck, and so on. I'd sort of seen this coming and was okay with it but at this point had a quick and whispered conversation with my boyfriend about whether he was cool with it as well. He was fine, we set down some quick ground rules about what we did and didn't want each other doing with her, and that was that.

One thing leads to another, all three of us are having fun, and eventually we realise its starting to get light outside so we decide its time to actually get some sleep. We all just kind of crash on the bed and manage to sleep for an hour or two before the sunlight got too annoying (she'd just had her house painted = no curtains). At this point, I'd figured that the sexual side of things had stopped when we'd decided to go to sleep. So I wasn't ridiculously keen about it when my friend is still occasionally reaching out to my boyfriend, hugging him and kissing him and such. This was not cool, because at the end of the day he IS my boyfriend, and we're not in an open relationship. And I'm not going to lie, I started getting a case of the green-eyed monster.

She got up for a bit to get a drink and this is when my boyfriend turns to me and asks if we can restart what was going on last night. He was the drunkest out of the three of us, so while he didn't seem bothered at the time, he couldn't get it up to enjoy himself more, and says to me he doesn't want to pass this opportunity up in case we don't get it again. I told him that, in short, I wasn't okay with that, and I promised him that if he was pent up then I'd do whatever he wanted when we got home that afternoon, but that I really wasn't comfortable with any more experimenting at that moment (this was my first time messing around with another girl, and before each other, neither of us had done more than kissed someone).

And here is where the problem is. After she came back, we tried to get to sleep again. I slept for a while but then my phone started beeping and woke me up, in time to hear my friend and boyfriend whispering and then start kissing and I looked over long enough to see him on top of her before I walked out of the room. Clearly they both thought I was asleep.

Now, I don't know. Am I justified in feeling like he's, technically, cheated on me? I'm not going to pretend that I'm not an insecure person - I've got some pretty deep issues when it comes to my femininity, stemming from being infertile and needing to have hormone treatment to even develop properly. The root of that insecurity is feeling like I'm not "woman" enough for any guy to truly be satisfied with me. I'd overcome 99% of that with my boyfriend, and I understand that, yes, we were messing around with my friend for most of the night so it doesn't seem that bad, but I point blank told him that I didn't want him to continue it in the morning. And then, less than a metre away from me, when they thought I was asleep, he went against what I asked. His reason was "I don't know why I did it, I was drunk and wasn't thinking" and he followed me as soon as I walked out of the room, and he does feel bad about it.

I don't really blame my friend - she didn't know I told him to stop, and neither of us had given her any serious indication that the sexual part of the night was over. Doesn't change the fact that I feel like slapping her across the face. Funny how emotions don't listen to logic. I know I'm definitely being a bitch about that.

But fuck. It just seems like one thing after another. He's not the most reliable, but I thought I could trust him about this kind of thing. It's a stab to my pride, my confidence and my heart. I don't know, opinions, thoughts, am I being uptight, am I being whiny, am I justified, whatever. Thanks.



SHORT VERSION:
Me and my boyfriend got drunk at a friends party and ended up having what I guess could be called a threesome with her, though no actual sex was involved, just everything but.
