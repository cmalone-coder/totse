It teaches the young humility by looking at their parents and seeing what the future holds for them.
Nature teaches the soul to perfect itself by weeding out the weak and imperfections. Only the healthy surviving; shown by natural selection; the most perfect specimens, as partners, to keep the breed healthy so that it will survive.
The natural harmony and balance of the two sides of nature: animal and vegetation that live together, helping each other to survive, becoming more dependent on the other. Animals depend on vegetation in order to survive, so animals must protect nature and keep it healthy. This shows us to treat everything the best possible way we can.

God makes direct contact with us and has given us a part of his infinite and eternal and incomprehensible self to live and dwell within us. Though there are many human-beings who are just animals, and are soulless. There are billions of us, fallen angels, 6 billion to be exact, but through human-animal circumstances could only have a few on Earth at a time throughout history, though now, we are all here, however continually swapping in and out of human bodies as we die and are reborn again.
