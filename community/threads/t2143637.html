A/S/L?

Do you know how nuclear power works?

Yes.

Explanation:

Assuming you are referring to nuclear power plants, it really depends on the methods and type of reactor. For instance, there are high pressure water reactors with neutron monitors and steam cycling and then there are those reactors that use deuterium oxide cooling methods. Independent of the nuclear technology, the concept of harnessing electricity from nuclear technology is the same. Through fission, Uranium-235 is often used with neutron bombardment in a controlled environment to produce Krypton-92, Barium-141, 3 neutrons (on a per atom basis) and a vast amount energy in the form of EMR to heat. The heat energy is then converted to electricity through the use of steam (or other fluid) which rotates turbines. Other less developed nuclear power producing technologies include the use of fission and other forms of radioactive decay.


Do you fear there could by another Chernobyl incident?

Yes and no.

If yes, how afraid are you on a scale of 1-10(highest)....

Yes, because there is always the possibility of another nuclear disaster; however, it is extremely unlikely. On your scale, I would give an estimation of approximately 2.


When if comes to the environment which do you think is better coal power or nuclear power?

Nuclear, because it is much more efficient, clean, and more advanced than the alternative.


The fuel for nuclear power is uranium. Do you think it is an abundant resource?

It is abundant in terms of percent composition of the earth compared to other elements, it is rare in large quantities because it is difficult to isolate, obtain, and produce large quantities on a world wide nuclear power plant network scale.


Do you believe that the world needs a radical change in order to solve our climate crisis?

Yes, although it is not really a crisis. The change would be about as radical as the development of our civilization since the early 19th century.


Would you be willing to pay more for a car knowing that it does not run on oil, gas, or another fossil fuel?

It really depends on a number of factors involved, including the price of those fuels. For the environment's sake, I would not. For performance, function, and efficiency, I most certainly would.


Briefly, whats your option on wind and solar energy as an alternative?

Solar energy production on a global scale is the best long term alternative source of energy available. Although for the sake of civilization development to the outer regions of space, improvements in EMR powered technologies is destined to be improved.
