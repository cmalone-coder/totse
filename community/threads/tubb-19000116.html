the ends of the baton are rounded. as to not puncture anything. it is a real "clean" weapon. things don't go down in a blaze of glory after being attacked with a coup baton. it can be jammed in a pressure point, it can strike pressure points, or it can serve as a decent bludgeoning weapon.

i have only been in a fight with it once, and it did VERY well. the first thing i usually go for is a swing at the thyroid. to stun them. that hurts like hell. so do the ears. after doing something like this, i would much rather run than fight. it is just better that way. im no coward, it is just a defensive weapon.

to dismantle someones face, one must know of the weak spots. two of my favs:

the part of the upper lip right beneath the nose (where the dip is on the upper lip. that little depression). that is a weak spot in the gums. it dosen't take much pressure to that area to knock the four front teeth out. very effective, and very painful.

the second, if they keep coming, part of the cheek bone. right below the eye. it is VERY difficult to get a coup baton right there without damaging the eye itself. feel your cheek bone. feel around the portion right below your eye. feel that ridge? that is a sturdy bone right there. but it is only designed to take pressure IN. not DOWN. so, if you can stick a coup baton right above the cheek bone, and pull down, that will probobly detach it where it connects to the rest of the skull (around the outside bottom portion of the eye socket). doing this may easily blind someone even if done correctly. the sign of a correctly done strike here is the cheek bone kind of drooping to the outside. i haven't had to use this one on any living thing. just the latter.
