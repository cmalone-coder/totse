Re: Breaking Somebody's Neck With Your Bare Hands
Quote:
Originally Posted by Generic Box Of Cookies
Is it based on a real life move? Is it even possible to break somebody's neck like that?
Umm... basically, yes.

It is my understanding that the US military spends a lot of work training in snapping necks... at least as a proportion of their not-to-specialized subsets of their unarmed training. Things might have changed since {unnamed family member or friend} got out of {ancient war}, and the rear assasination was slightly different... but there was a thirdhand rumor that some of their other final eliminations were essentially similar in angle.

In more firsthand knowledge, other arts execute somewhat like that as well - part of the reason I laugh my ass off when someone with three months of muay thai starts going on a "traditional martial arts are useless" kick... (well, that, and muay thai is a traditional martial art, 'n they're usually trying to portray theirs as "the one true way," but...)

Doing it properly is not *exactly* like that, but it would *look* exactly like that to anyone who wasn't busy critiquing minute form from their own training. I won't get into the details of difference when we're sitting here a few subforums away from BI, but... yes.

If you're interested in snapping necks, you might want to take up judo. They probably won't teach you to snap necks, but they'll teach you quite well everything else you'd use while snapping necks. 'n trying to just copy it off TV would be easier to actively defend against than someone who had been trained to do it properly (usually, by the time you're learning them, "paranoia" is a major component crafting technique - "then KEEP snapping their neck" is usually crafted into doctrine)... but you'd still have a risk of paralyzing or killing people, despite the slight differences.

If you have to ask if such a thing is possible, you wouldn't even notice those differences if they were occuring in front of you, and I'm not in a mood to teach people on the internet... but... yes, it's as close to that as you or most people would be able to tell, and close enough that you shouldn't do that to anyone because it just might work.

Now ya know...
