Why don't more people lie?
This isn't entire sex related, but it's a bit too much philosophical thinking for the SG crowd to handle.

Why are people so concerned with telling the truth all the time?

I lie on a regular basis, a couple of times a day type of deal. Sometimes it's a white lie to make people feel better, other times it's a con for my benefit, but it usually works towards a greater good.

So what's the big deal about lying if you're a relatively decent and honest person?

Maybe it's just my rationalization, but lying isn't nearly as morally reprehensible as being a bad person. To put it in a sexual example, I could pick up a girl one way, make her feel good, and have sex with her, or pick her up, make her feel bad, and have sex with her because her lowered self-esteem would get the better of her. But that's just one example.

Wouldn't the world be such a better place if more people lied? Imagine if we went around telling each other what we wanted to hear. Those of us with finely-tuned bullshit radars would pick up on people's shit, but most of the world would go along with it living happier lives.

There's times the truth is the best course of action, but I think it's so overrated in our society that people forget that lying is one of the most noble actions. Hell, since ancient times gods have been lying to people, kings to their subjects, and people amongst themselves. Isn't it time we stopped judging people so harshly for it?

(As an aside, no, I'm not high. I was wikipedia-ing Djinns and got directed to Genies. Then I read about Genie the little girl who's dad locked her up for 13 years. Then I read about feral children, psychogenic dwarfism, J.M. Barrie, and then I got an urge to read Peter Pan so I did just that. That was about 4 hours ago and I got to thinking about morality...)
