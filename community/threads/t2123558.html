Holy shit I'm so tired of people going "OH MAH GAWD don't take a vicodin and .5 milligrams of xanax U WIL DYE!" An overdose from mixing benzodiazepines and opiates or opioids is a result of respiratory failure. As in, you have to be pretty fucked up to die. Usually when you overdose on benzos+opiates you are passed out. The respiratory failure happens because both benzodiazepines and opiates are central nervous system depressants and muscle relaxers. Together they can both relax your diaphragm (thoracic, not vaginal) enough so that you are not breathing adequately and you can die. So, with that being said, do you really think that a small amount of opiates + benzos it going to kill you? No, it will just fuck you up more and will be awesome.
