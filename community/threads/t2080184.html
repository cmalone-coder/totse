I read somewhere that love has three stages:
First is the attraction and happy part.
Second is when the two people argue over everything.
Third after the two people have resolved most arguments the relationship becomes stable and remains this way.

This is really a simplified version of what I read, but it seems to make sense. After a while the euphoria wears off, and then you have to establish boundaries and what not. It's stressful arguments all the time. For me this is usually when I end up getting out of relationships. I've never had the time to deal with all of it, and it stresses me out too much. I guess I just don't care enough about keeping a girlfriend to deal with all the arguing and bullshit for months on end.
