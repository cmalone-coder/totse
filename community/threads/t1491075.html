Re: Quiet/shy people getting to know other quiet/shy people
Get her talking. Everyone has at least one or two topics they're willing to expound on, even if it takes some effort to get them to do so. To that end, just keep taking shots at various subjects that you know or think she has an interest in. If she gives a slight positive response, be forward (but not overbearing) about wanting to hear what she has to say.

Shy kids in general are quiet because they don't feel like anyone wants to listen to them. Many times they can be skeptical of initial attempts to get them to open up, too, so try to get her talking about something you might actually have an interest in hearing about. Your sincerity will count for a lot.
