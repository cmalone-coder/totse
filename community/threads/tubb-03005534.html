Re: Help with ROMS
You aren't going to be able to run PS2 games on your PC. As far as I know there are no working emulators yet, and even if there were you would need some insane processing power to emulate the PS2.

For the PSX, I suggest using ePSXe. It runs very well on my 1ghz machine with no 3d card. The only games it really gets hung up over are full 2D ones like Megaman X and Legend of Mana.

As for Playstation ROMs, you are unlikely to find too many of those on the web. They are, to use a technical term, hella large. Since a Playstation game can span an entire CD the ROMs are typically 500 to 680 megabytes, and not the sort of thing your average dialup user wants to download - And not the sort of thing your average ROM pirate wants to host.

Your best bet for (semi) free Playstation games is to take a stroll down to your local video store and copy a whole bunch.

~Zero

Re: Help with ROMS
Playstation disks are standard CDROMs. It won't hurt the disk or the drive to use one in a PC. You can copy PSX games with any vaugely modern CD writer. I suggest using CloneCD for this task. Registration is cheap, and it's a good program to have around. It does a good job of copying PC games with goofy copy protection schemes, too (Diablo 2, &c).

A standard copied game will play in ePSXe, but NOT in an unmodified (real) Playstation. PSX disks have a region specific boot sector that cannot easily be copied by a CD writer. That's what we have mod chips for, anyway.

Your machine should do just fine for PSX and N64 games. You have plenty of RAM and 3d acceleration power, so you should have no trouble.

I don't have much information on N64 emulation at the moment because I do not partake in it. There are only three games on the N64 worth playing (Bond, Smash Brothers, and Zelda 5, if you were wondering) and I have all of these readily available on cartridge. Everything else for the N64 is, to use another technical term, crap. Don't bother.

~Zero
