Re: How do you break someone else's relation ship?
Towards the end of March 2008, the angsty, teenage anti-socialite totse member Q, made a thread (or two, rather) in which he asked for advice on how to break up a relationship. You see, there was a girl he was after (one who had repeatedly shot him down) and his plan was to break up her relationship with her boyfriend so as to: "...isolate her from everyone in her life so she is forced to accept that i'm all she has."

What a lovely young man.

He eventually came up with this plan: "I will ensure that I am there the next time he goes to a party without her, I will then make sure he consumes a large amount of alcohol and is around a group of females. Then I will covertly take pictures of the scene after purchasing a digital camera, and will send the pictures to the girl, pretending I am one of many previous girlfriends that he has cheated on. I will tell her that he is an expert liar and will deny the whole thing and act confused, then she breaks up with him and I am there to comfort her. Everything will work perfectly."
(Original thread here:
http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=2110531
Re-post in Sex & Affection here:
https://www.totse.com/community/show....php?t=2110540
As you will read in these threads [if you choose to do so], Q was of the belief that isolating this unfortunate girl from everyone so that she was left with no other choice but him would be the best thing for her. I'm sure you can see the logical gap here.)

A little more than a month goes by and one of Q's real-life friends, limit2life, (who, presumably, Q forgot had an account) posted Q's address in the form of his longitude and latitude. After confirming that this person did in fact know Q, I directed him to the above threads. This person knew the girl who's life Q was trying to destroy and also attended University with her boyfriend. I recommended that he tell these two people and show them the threads. A few days later, Q posts this:
"Attn: Yial; I have to see a psychiatrist you fucking cocksucker.", though apparently not before "...a rather unpleasant experience with a group of chavs, a homicidal female, a policeman and my parents...

So that's that. Don't try to destroy other people's lives just so you can get laid, anti-social internet geek, because it sure as hell can backfire on you. Q doesn't post much any more, but when he does, he insists he's perfectly sane, even though he needs advice on tricking his phyciatrist into thinking so.
