The smell of black liquorice is not a good sign of purity - usually if it smells like that it means that it has not been purified correctly somewhere along the production line.

Don't hold me to it, but I think the presence of sassafras oil, usually from safrole, creates the smell fore mentioned.

The best purity MDMA i've personally had has been light pink in colour, but over the years I've had it ranging from deep purple, bright pink, red, dark brown, light brown, transparent and white.
