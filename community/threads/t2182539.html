Re: Growing shrooms in dorm
Lockable trunk sounds like a good idea, you could fruit a load in there although make sure the wood isnt going to get too damp and rot. To maintain the temperature and humidity rather than a heat mat I would be more inclined to use a container of water and aquarium heater. Heat mats get a bit hot and if your cakes are sat directly on them it will probably dry them out. An aquarium heater in a container of water on the other hand will heat the water which will in turn act like a radiator and also maintain a certain level of humidity. Read up loads on shroomery before you get into it but its really a great way to get a good supply cheap as fuck shrooms and you can see a return within 2 months.

You will also need a little bit of light to trigger fruiting if they are in a sealed container... The smallest fluorescent light bulb (strip or CFL) will do. When I did it I got one of those little fluorescent strip lights you get over bathroom mirrors (11w I think) and that did the job perfectly.

Look around on shroomery and look at the best technique for you, the great thing about growing shrooms is it can be done in such a small unobtrusive space and it is stealthy as fuck!
