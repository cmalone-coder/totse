I would argue that all drugs alter your personality. Even if it's simply opening your mind to that particular drug; whether that's a good thing or a bad thing is subjective. If you try something, there'll always be another occasion when you might do it again; if you never try somthing, then... err.. you'll never try it :S.

MDMA changed me: it made me more comfortable with people; I discussed things I otherwise wouldn't have while taking it for the first, second, and third times. The others (who were also on it) listened and gave me support. The next day I felt genuinly closer to them.

Coke alters your personality. It turns you into a lying, twitchy, and aggressive person. I definitly think coke changes you for the worse if you use it too much. I've used it, but not regularly. I know others who have (and do) and they've changed a lot.

Cannabis, that which I use daily, doesn't really change me. I still get up in the morning, work hard at uni, and maintain healthy relationships. Beyoned being more open minded to cannabis and its effects, I don't think it really alters my personality. That might not be the same for others, though. Some people take it the wrong way...

That's just three of the big ones. I guess that whether a drug effects you negatively or not is all down to how much you let it dominate your life.
