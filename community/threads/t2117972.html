Unfortunately, Pain Management does make a habit of drug testing it's patrons. When you start going, visits will be monthly usually and the tests are random but expect the first few visits to be tested everytime. After you have been on the same meds/dose for a while, you will more than likely get bumped to only having to visit the doctor once every two months. This won't happen for at least 6-8 months at the earliest, probably longer though. You should be getting two scripts per visit when it happens though.. One with a "DO NOT FILL UNTIL xx/xx/xx" 30 days after the date on the one that can be filled sooner. Sometimes you can have them mail it to you, or send it directly to the pharmacy of your choice (in the contract/paperwork you sign and they give you they highly recommend you frequent the same pharmacy).

Oh and some Pain Managements have instituted pills counts. So every time you visit the doctor, you have to bring your meds and they better check out, or rapid down dose. If you do fuck up some how and get kicked off, they still have to down dose you, they can't just cut you off. Can be dangerous if they did that and they won't risk a law suit.

My girlfriend could give more details. This is just a condensed version of what she went through. YMMV.

Morphine ER > Morphine IR > Methadone 5s > Methadone 10s > Higher daily dose of Methadone 10s where she is at now. sorry, don't remember the morphine dosages.
