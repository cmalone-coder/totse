Zok talked about this a while back in T&T, I'm bringing the issue back up.

We got two new forums, which is great. But we're still missing two: a programming forum and a troubleshooting forum. The troubleshooting forum could be a subforum of T&T, as could a programming forum.

However, I am also of the opinion that a programming forum is simply too broad to be made into a subforum. There's so many different, specialized techniques and features (COM, .NET/Mono, OS-specific development, embedded devices, etc.) and soooo many different languages that it really is in a world of its own.

Most of the users in T&T feel this is a good idea, too, as referenced by http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=2013861.

Personally, I propose making a help desk subforum under T&T and a broad, zero-subforums programming forum until it starts to define itself, at which point new subforums can be created.

Anyone have any other ideas?
