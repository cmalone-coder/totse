Master Keys
PURELY FOR EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY, DO NOT ACCTUALLY TRY TO DO THIS UNLESS YOU OWN THE ORIGINAL LOCK AND KEY YOU ARE COPYING.

I know much about locksmithing/picking. However I do not own a key cutter. I wanted to be able to copy keys, but of course I would have to use much more crude methods, however I found them to be successful, If your interested read on:

Items needed:

1 Rat-Tail File

----Not Cylindrical preferably a triangular shape. $10 at Ace hardware or similar store.

1 or more Blank keys

---$1-$2 each at Wal-Mart

Electrical Tape

The you wish to copy.

The process is devastatingly simple, yet remarkably effective. First off let me stress, make sure you have the same model blank key as the kind of key your copying. If not, the wards will simply get in your way and your key is worthless.

Procedure:

Tape blank key to master key, making sure tape is around the handle - lining them up exactly. Simply file down into the blank key, until the grooves and knotches match perfectly. How accurate do you have to be? Accurate enough to not be able to tell just by looking at the 2 keys lined up that there is a difference. (NOTE: this works only on house keys, and some commercial keys. Car keys and others of higher quality are lazor cut these days.) If you fuck up and cut too deep then start again, the blanks are cheap. My first key took me about an hour to perfect, and might I add IS perfect. I have gotten this to work many times. Much easier than picking if you have the time to copy a key.
