Final Fantasy 7, and how it ruined things.
I'll openly admit that FFVII was one of the reasons I bought a Playstation. I spent many nights cursing Square for abandoning Nintendo, then accepted my fate and got a PSX.

The first time I cranked that game up, I knew something big was happening. The graphics were absolutely incredible, and it simply oozed atmosphere. Midgar was such a perfect cyberpunk heaven. I thought the whole "resistance movement" may have been a bit of a FF6 rehash, but it was different enough to be fresh and entertaining. Cloud showed up and started acting like the SOLDIER badass he should have been, and Barret was quite entertaining. The first few hours were incredible.

Then it went wrong.

The group left Midgar, and everything went back to "green grass, chocobos, and villages of seventeen people" mode. This abrupt move from cyberpunk metropolis to pastures and huts just fucked everything up. It never made any sense. The previous games managed to keep a sense of continuity in the world. The entire game could have taken place in that dystopian maze of technology and I would have been happy. Blade Runner does not transition to Dungeons and Dragons.

Then Square introduced those damn unskippable summons, just to taunt the players with their spiffy new graphics abilities.

Then they fucked up the characters. Cloud starts the game as a solitary, kick-ass-take-names mercenary. He ends the game as a total pussy. "Let's mosey", he says. Then Cid even tells him he's being a pussy. The disgruntled engineer tells the professional SOLDIER how to fucking order people around! That more stupid than that goddamn androgynous cowboy fuck from FF8!

FF8 just took the popularity stick and ran with it. If Tifa was too fucking stupid to understand Cloud's advances, Rinoa is even more fucking stupid for seeing anything in that DiCaprio wannabe Squall. WHY SHOULD I PLAY AN RPG WHERE I WANT TO CONSTANTLY KICK THE MAIN CHARACTER IN THE GROIN?! I'd like to beat that Zell fuck with a large stick, while I'm at it.

Ugh.

Re: Final Fantasy 7, and how it ruined things.
There are a few quirks with the newer FF's that have never quite settled in my mind:
1) High tech - Swords and Serpents
They just don't pull it off very well. As compliant as I am with some games and having to seriously suspend logic, I just cannot take a game seriously that throws high powered guns, swords, and other mundane weaponry in the same mix doing the same damage (9999 this, 9999 that, etc)
2) High tech - Low population
The gripe about villages and small populations got me as well. I think the whole population of FFVII may have been something in the low-to-mid 100's (unless you justify this by believing monsters=transformed people).
3) Reiterating the last statement
Okay, so I'm doing okay suspending my belief, trucking along in FFVII, trying as hard as I can to not try and justify any of this stuff and simply get immersed in the picture show. Suddenly, the game tries to offer a justification of all the monsters roaming the plains. What, was that a music box that just attacked me? What kind of experiments are those Jenova people running? Don't corporations need people to hold them up? Or do the Shinra people hold near-to-reality VR machines and have access to some type of near-perpetual energy source so that they don't need people to do anything? Could be, but I really couldn't stand the attempt at trying to justify all of the monsters by saying that they were the result of the Jenova project.
But look at all the pretty pictures...
