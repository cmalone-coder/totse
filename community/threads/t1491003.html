If this guy isn't going to go out with you because of your brother, and you like him enough to pursue it, talk to your brother about it. A lot of times, the protective big brother thing is more a mindset of "don't fuck with my sister" than it is "don't touch my sister", ie. he doesn't want his friends hitting on you or seducing you. If you take the initiative and tell him you like the guy, but you don't want to see their friendship get fucked up, that alone may do the trick.

Of course, it may not. Then you'll have to figure something out. But unless you're brother is completely unreasonable, he'd probably prefer that you see someone he knows and (presumably) trusts- and if he doesn't trust the guy for some reason, he should tell you why. If that's not good enough for him, then you're going to have this problem no matter who you date. Get it figured out now.
