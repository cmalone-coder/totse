Re: Anybody ever have a bad Mushroom trip cuz U took too much ?
I ate a quarter of shrooms once and I had a kinda 'bad trip'. I think that the only reason it wasn't a good trip was because none of my friends were shrooming with me, they were all just drinking so I felt like nobody understood me. At first it seemed like I was speaking a totally different language that no one else knew (after eating a quarter of mushrooms that is not entirely impossible) and when people would talk to me it sounded like they were talking in gibberish. Then I started hallucinating and went off into my own little introspective world for a while. For some reason I was imagining that I was completely retarded and that everyone was just pretending that I was a normal person. After a couple hours I was still shrooming pretty hard and I was trying to drive everyone to my house, but I couldn't see the road so I had to stop and get out like every 10 feet to make sure I was still on it. It only took me about 20 minutes to realize that the reason I couldn't see the road was because I didn't have my headlights on. So after I figured that out I was driving down the road and the next thing I knew the white lines were floating in the air next to my window and the passenger side window, that was pretty scary. Then it started snowing and I thought that I had shrunk to about the size of a mouse and that somebody was putting salt on me because they were getting ready to eat me. I didn't really freak out, but I started driving faster to get away from them but it kept snowing so I decided that there was no way I was going to out run whoever it was that was trying to eat me. I don't really know how we made it to my house but some how we did and I spent the rest of the night curled up under the pool table while my friends got wasted and played pool. I'm not sure if they knew I was under there or not but I eventually made myself fall asleep. When I woke up the whole night seemed like a bad dream but it was all real.

So yes you can have a bad trip from eating too many mushrooms, but it all depends on the enviroment that you are in and what kind of mood that you, and the people around you are in. I'm not an expert or anything, but that is my opinion based on my experiences.
