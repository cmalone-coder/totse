For as long as totse has been around we have had a very lax censorship (one may post anything except personal info, credit card numbers, phone numbers, etc). We still feel very strongly about our anti-censorship policy.

However, recently we have applied a word ban to the goatse website and popular link-game websites. I am sure that you all know what the goatse website is. If you don't, I am sure you can find many similar pictures of men holding their anuses open all over google. The reason for this is not because we don't want to see it, anybody can just ignore it, but there lies a heavier problem than that. As stated in the Totse FAQ's:

Quote:
quote:

Posting pictures of disgusting things that have nothing to do with the topic at hand is annoying. We've all seen extruded anuses, wounds full of maggots, and German porn with people eating each other's excrement. Big whoop. Isn't that exciting. You can just as easily put in a link to the image and a description of what the link points to, so if someone really wants to see a woman licking a dog's ass they can click the link and see it, or skip the link if they don't care to see what you're pointing to.
Yes, it is annoying, but there is a strong difference between one person posting it to start a (mildly) interesting conversation and fourty people posting it because they think it is funny. The line must be drawn.

Because nobody can seem to control their urges to post such pictures and websites with no explanation as to what they are or why they are posting it, we have had to apply a ban to them. Perhaps in the future, when we have a more mature posting audience, we can lift the ban. But for now it stands.
