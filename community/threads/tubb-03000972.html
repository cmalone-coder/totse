About the screenwriter thing, I took a class on screenwriting in my hs for like three weeks for like 2hrs 30 mins a day and the teacher was an actual hollywood screenwriter. He was talking about how they were planning a strike.

The reason the screenwriters are striking is because they get absolutely no credit. Did you know that when they write a script and sell it to a studio, the studio becomes the "author" because they become the owner of the script. The studio then can do whatever they want with it; what they do, is bring in their specific team of writers and say "spice it up" or something of the like. So the actual author gets little to no credit for his work and the final script that is used in the movie is more often than not not his original work--it has been changed. The studio doesn't even need the screenwriters permission to do all this after they pay them the money! And btw, do you actually know ne names of ne screenwriters? of course everyone knows the name of the directors and actors like steven spielberg or whatever, but they are only setting up something that was already *created* for them. the screenwriter i feel is really the most important part because they actually make up the story--no other part of a movie can be made w/out a script, but a script can stand alone as a work. So these are the reasons the screenwriters are striking (that and the $$$).

Also, i thought that it was the producers who were also going to be striking w/ the screenwriters--they are the other guild who gets the least recognition out of the guilds that make up the academy.
