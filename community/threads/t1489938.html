Re: I dont know what the fuck to do anymore
This strikes me as one of those situations with no solution. I'm posting this not to be un-helpful, but to encourage you to focus your energies away from a futile situation.

You don't know what to do because there's nothing you can do. You don't have the upper hand and any agency that might be able to mediate will not look favorably upon you or Cristina in this case. She's an "attention-seeking cutter" and you're a "violent meth-head with priors" in the eyes of the law, medical professionals and social services agencies. There might be social services that will listen to you and sympathize, but chances are you'll still be clumped with the larger statistical group that fits the above description.

Like it or not, the law/medical/social services people make judgements on appearance and behavior. If you're raving like an angry lunatic (and you have every right to be) and Cristina's father is able to remain calm and rational, you're screwed.

Essentially, your only option is to move far, far away with Cristina, or deal with whatever happens as it happens if you choose to stay with her. I presume you're both minors.

As much as it seems justified, don't snap and commit homocide. You're better off running away with her than ending up on death row. Hopefully that's a no-brainer for you.

I know people who do all kinds of drugs and are able to function just fine. You may not be one of those kinds of people, so you might reconsider your use, if you ever want to be taken seriously and treated with respect. Referring to someone as a "spic" (doesn't that make Cristina a "spic" as well, unless she was adopted?) is indicative of a behavioral pattern that doesn't work in your favor.

So, in summary, if you want Cristina in your life, it will probably take the following: get yourself into a treatment program, help Cristina seek counseling, and continue with your own counseling. Eventually the problems with her father will come up when you're both more rational-thinking, and you'll likely be taken more seriously if she's truly in an abusive situation.

And to make it clear: I don't think all this is necessarily "right" that you have to suck it up. This is just the way life, and the law, works.
