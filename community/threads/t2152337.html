KOR Water/The KOR One
I think this is the first thread I've made in this forum, so forgive me if doesn't fit in, although it is about design.

http://www.korwater.com/

KOR Water is a company that set out to make their idea of the ultimate, 'green' water bottle (the KOR One), something to replace nalgenes and other containers contaminated with bpa. It comes with a pretty ridiculous $30 price tag, but I keep a water bottle with me all the time and would really like to step up to something better than just a recycleable. My two favorite features are the latch top, and the mouth that while narrow, is still wide enough to take ice cubes.

So, does totse think it's a good idea or a stupid one? Will design and "sustainability" win out over thriftiness, or will the whole "KOR Water mission" explode in the hangar?
