Because emotional pain and withdrawals will mean the worst time of your life.

But once you come out of the other side clean and clear-headed you can look back on it and know you can take anything.

It's challenges like this that define you. Cutting out something that's bad for you, yet helps you so much in bad situations and getting through it. Not necessarily on your own but getting through it and dropping a habit at the same time.

A recent one for me is I dropped the anger thing after I got dumped and fuck man, I feel great now.

In that situation, I would have gone fucking apeshit...Smashed things, smashed people, whatever...I would go mental. Then I'd get drunk and do some more stupid shit. My hands, elbows, knees etc would bleed and swell from the pure punishment I was giving them whilst giving it out to other things/people and the rush was fucking amazing at the time. I was on top of the food chain. On top of the world. Nothing could beat it. Nothing.
But like a drug, the come-down was unbearable...Depression, remorse, regrets, tremendous physical pain and having to look people in the eye and apologise for what I'd done. That was the hardest part.
The giving up part was hard aswell. Concsiously stopping myself from doing something that would make me feel great again. Constantly seeing it just out of reach, beckoning me to seize it and let loose.

But I kicked it.

I look back and think to myself "You did it son...You fucking did it and you don't need her or the anger anymore."

Greatest feeling I've had for a long time.
