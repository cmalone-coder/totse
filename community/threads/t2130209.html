Re: Convince me not to keep doing ecstacy.
Ecstacy is awesome for having a pretty guaranteed good time (it's an entactogen/empathogen, with a very intense "mood lift" quite markedly different from standard dopamine-related bliss.

It can be so awesome to talk to people on E, share things, profess your sense of a bond to another person, that you otherwise may not be as prone to saying. Empathy and a very deep and fuckong sublime mood lift where everything is just fucking awesome. Like, it;s actually awesome. there are much deeper effects than other standard club drugs like coke and shit... which are generally no deeper than just pure "pleasure" or gratification. Obviously this is a very seductive path, etc, etc.

MDMA isn't conventionally addictive, but some people will coe to rely on it. Addiction is far more remote with E than with sometrhing way more accessable/fucking everywhere, like benzos or booze, or something that serves as the ultimate escape (strong/euphoric opiods or coke,) Granted, a person could safely do coke or heroin much more frequently than they could safely use E too frequently. There is a chance of causing irreparable damage, but noticable brain damage only seems to happen in cases of ridiculous excess. I kbow one dude who did E and coke ridiculously frequently all summer, and he's still totally there... actually one of the smarter people I know.

So yeah, ecstasy is truly a wonderful and unique drugs. If you use it too frequently, the "magic" will die and become hollow. ideally every few months, but Shulgin uses 2 weeks as minimum rule of thumb, and he seems to be doing pretty okay still. So it's safe if you save it for special occasions. While you're a kid you can (or should I say probably will treat it a bit irresponsibiliy, and do it perhaps a bit more often than this, now and then. As long as that doesn't become the rule by which you set your limits.. Since it's a nice drug and release so much intense happiness, when it's new, a person's first tendency is to spam the good shit as much as they safely can, because it's good in so many ways. Not just good vibes. Whenever I'm on E, I end up making like a dozen more friends that night with comparatively little effort due to the openness/empathy...

SO just don't be one of the kind that throws safety to the wind and just says "fuck it" and use it irresponsibly, leading to a sort of damage, if not the objective/medical well-being of your brain, frequent use totally disrupts your life. You run out of the happy chemical and live in some of the worst depressive funks you can imagine, if it's good or you cross your limits. It's best to be saved for the right situations, and I think it's a drug people also outgrow a fair deal.
