Excellent weight training information...
I posted this in another thread a few weeks ago, but I'd like to make sure people have read it. It's a goldmine of information and will really get you going as far as weight training goes. It's helped me significantly. I'd consider it a no-bullshit resource, anyone who frequents the bodybuilding.com forums may have seen this anyway, I'm sure it's in a sticky somewhere, but I found it whilst searching for Rippetoe's Starting Strength, which is an excellent book, the guy who runs this site even put a link up to the book on the site. He isn't getting commission, he just likes the book.

Website - http://www.geocities.com/elitemadcow1/index.htm

There are a lot of pages on that site, lots of links, check the table of contents link, a few different programs and lots of information, spreadsheets available to download as well.

Training Primer (essential to read) - http://www.geocities.com/elitemadcow...ing_Primer.htm

"If you are on this site or considering any of the above programs. You need to read this. Bottom line. It provides a good overview on training for size vs. strength, efficiency in programming for adding muscle, overtraining, fiber recruitment, different types of hypertrophy."

Anyway, I hope this all helps you out, I'd like to see this stickied but we'll see what the moderators think.

Cheers,
