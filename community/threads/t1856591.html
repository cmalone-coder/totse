The vacuum pump actually doesn't suck the solvent out, it lowers the boiling point.

Whats needed somewhere 1/2 way thru this thread, is a jar with two hoses in the lid.

The hose from the boiling solvent setup goes into the jar, down almost to the bottom.

The other hose fitting starts at the lid level and goes thru to the vacuum pump.

Remember everythings air tight.

The above collection jars sorta like a bong,(we can all see that in our minds now.)

This stops the solvent getting into the vacuum pump, ( old freezer compressor pump)

There should probably be Two jar setups in a row, cooled in ice water too to condense the vapor out.

Also you maybe wanting to get the solvent/liquid out and using that, not the stuff in the boiling flask.

Most chemistry boils down to reaction and extraction.

Vacuum is normally about 20 ( mm/Hg or something) A freezer pump gets around about this, depends on seals, power etc and oil lube, Auto trans oil seems to give the best result, (from the reports here).

Two vacuum pumps in line can be used to lower the strain on each pump.
