Re: Grappling Hook
Seeing as how the general purpose of a grappling hook is to climb an obstacle it would be only logical to turn to the climbing world for equipment.

Before you get started :

How to use a grappling hook (http://www.ehow.com/how_2093033_use-grappling-hook.html)

Black Diamond grappling hook (http://www.bdel.com/gear/grappling_hook.php)

I'd still recommend using some quality climbing rope with it. It tends to throw a little bit better than normal rope and like I said before you can use Ascenders (http://www.bdel.com/gear/nforce.php) with it. When climbing anything of a height where a slip could be fatal you really want to be securely attached to that rope.

For anything else you could allways go the tactical route and get one of these (http://climbinggearinc.com/cmi-tactical-grappling-hook-p-1489.html).
