high potency E
To date I've been pretty interested in MDMA and have researched it alot. The main reason I never really got around to trying it was the fact that E pills tend to be adulterated or bunk more often than clean. And since I never really cared enough to get a test kit, I ended up refusing E pills every time they were offered.

I happened to run into a test kit stand on a festival however (6 euros for a dropper bottle w/ Marquiz reagent), so I got me 2 of those so I could finally get into trying E. After asking around alot and comparing the reputations of certain brands, I settled down on an old kind of pill (about 10 months since it first became available) that one guy I know (not a dealer) saved for 'special occasions' because he felt they were very potent and clean. "A guy I know tested these in a Dutch drugtesting centre", he said, "and they're around 135-145 mg". In my mind I immediately called bullshit, because that's WAY above the mean expected average. Unfortunately most experienced E users around here are straight E-tards whose motto is "don't think, eat the roll" so I'm not very inclined to take anything anyone says seriously, even when they mean it.

I scraped a pinch of powder off the pill (which is a "blue dolphin", btw) and dropped the reagent. In a fraction of a second, like *poof*, the reagent went really dark blue (even black). This means there's definitely MDMA in there (if it were MDA, the reagent would have turned blue more slowly).

Now here's the problem:
Another person who tried these for his second time rolling said it felt really good toward the end of the experience, although standing still made him very nauseous (almost puked), and he pannicked somewhat during the first hour because it was too much intense for him. This person has never tried hallucinogens in his life other than MDMA so he felt uncomfortable being "in a completely different world" in his words. Also says he was trying to play soccer but could barely see the ball in front of him.

Being a first time user of these (apparently) strong ass rolls, I would REALLY hate it if I pannicked the way that other guy did, and I want my first time rolling to be nothing short of perfect. I don't want my roll to be too weak either. Now, me having experience with medium to high mushroom and acid trips, how would you recommend I take this roll? Should I only use half for my first experience and save a full blown roll for a later time? Maybe take three quarters of it first and the rest during the peak? Take it all at once?

I'll consider the advice given, make a choice and report back later. Thanks.

If it's your first time and they're strong (which you suggest), then I'd advise you take a half to begin with. Be cautious in case you're allergic; it's always best to start at a low dose when trying a new drug. When you're up, drop the other half and you'll have a blast.

If you want to snort it, I'd suggest saving that for another time. It can be pretty intense for a noob, and you might end up panicking.

If you start feeling nauseous - which you very well might your first couple of times - just sit down with a friend, have some water, and chill for a bit. If you need to throw up, do so, but watch to see if you expel any of your roll... just in case.

I think the biggest thing to get over is the anxiety of trying something new. Try, if you can, to forget about taking it; go have a dance or talk to a mate and you'll come up like a motherbitch. The first couple times I did it I kept frantically looking at the clock and felt proper nervous - when I was up it was all good though :).

Above all, enjoy it. MDMA is a wonderful thing and always feels strongest the first couple of time you do it. You're in for a fun ride.

I'd take 3/4 and take the other quarter after 45 minutes to an hour if you're feeling great. 100mg is plenty for a first-time hit. It'd probably be fine to take the whole thing, but it can be a bit scary to come up too fast if you're not experienced with it.
