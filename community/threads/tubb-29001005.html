Affordable Homemade Weapons
Are you in need of a gun, but dont feel like getting a license, paying money for gun courses, etc.? Well then, you need a homemade black powder muzzleloader. just get a pipe thats threaded on one end, an endcap, and a steel drillbit.

Basically, all you have to do is secure the endcap onto the end of the pipe with some pipe cement, drill a small hole pretty close to the endcap, and tape the pipe onto some kind of stock.

To get some gun powder, you can either make the shitty homemade kind, or you can simply buy some fireworks and take the black crystal like powder out of them. toss some of this shit down the pipe, put a fuse in the small hole you drilled, drop in about 35 bb's, and then pack a little ball of paper down with a stick.

When you light that shit up its almost as good as the real thing.

Re: Affordable Homemade Weapons
����Building a homemade black powder firearm is indeed dangerous. The stuff ain't hairspray - Black powder can and will seriously injure and/or kill you if the gun is not built up to spec.
����Overall I do not recommend constructing a black powder firearm at all. Air pressure is the way to go. However, black powder weapons can be safely made. As Warweed stated, you want thick steel for your barrel. Instead of screw on endcaps I would suggest welding. You could also build a rudimentary hammer mechansim to strike primers (available at the hardware store as nailgun power loads) to ignite the powder instead of the uncertain timing of a fuse.
����As a matter of fact, if you wanted your gun to be small caliber (.177, .22, .25) you could use nothing but the power loads to propel the shot at bullet velocity. This would eliminate the need for measuring out the proper amount of powder (which is the hardest part) and ramming the ball, powder, and wadding down the barrel and so on. Smaller diameter pipe is also stronger and less likely to expode in your face than a great big musketball sized piece since it has less internal surface area for the pressure to act on.

~Zero
