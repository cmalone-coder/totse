Preludin has been discussed on some other boards and older members there have found it preferable to meth in that it is equally euphoric but calmer and smoother, qualitatively similar to meth/amp+low dose oxycodone.

The N-methyl derivative, phendimetrazine, is a popular diet pill, brand names Obex, tenuate, etc. It can be purchased from some online pharmacies. The same folks from above mentioned that it was similar in potency.

And there's also some conjecture that 2-phenylmorpholine is centrally active (phenmetrazine=3-methyl-2-phenylmorpholine), it is also available through some online vendors.
