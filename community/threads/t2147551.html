Ubuntus noob friendlyness is the most overrated thing in the *nix world.

1. You'll have to edit config files to display the correct resolution (puppy handled this fine)
2. It plays dick all media formats out of the box (puppy will play just about everything except WMAs)
3. Mounting and unmounting drives is extremely unintuitive, puppys "pmount" blows it out of the water.

IMO, puppy is a much better first linux choice. Give it a go.
