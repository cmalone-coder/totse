So I just bought an HerbalAire Vape
Quick Review:

I got it yesterday and played with it some last night after I got off work. So far it is awesome and I am pleased to say I'm quickly falling in love with vaporizing, since I was so nervous that I wouldn't like this "different, cleaner high" that I'd been hearing about. But it is awesome, the body high is intense. The bag system to me is just snazzy stuff too. Put a little dent in my wallet at $250, but I think that in the long run it'll pay for itself because it really does use a lot less weed than smoking does. It's really small too. At 7 inches tall I can put it in my sock drawer standing up and fuhgetaboutit. The pump is a little bit noisy when it's on when using the bag system, but it's not too bad and it's not like you have to use it anyway. This is the first and only vape I've used but from what I've read it seems like the way to go, unless you have the money for a Volcano. And even then the HerbalAire would still be a good choice as it has more jets and gets more out of the weed than even the pricey Volcano apparently. For those of you who've tried this and/or others, what are your thoughts?

QUESTION:

I've been using temps from about 370 to just under 400 and it does the job, but I really don't know what the difference is and what is best. For those of you who have this vape or other ones, what are the best temps to use? Why? What sort of different effects do they give, if any?
