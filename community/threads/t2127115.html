How I used to crush up the beads:

fold a corner of a piece of printer paper so the edges line up.

fold the other corner up so it makes two triangle shaped pockets.

I like to then cut off all the excess paper, so that only the two small paper triangle shaped pockets remain.

Pour the contents of the capsule into the pockets, and hold it closed with your finger.

Start chewing on the beads in the pocket, and it should only take about 10 seconds of this to powder them.

Pour contents into tissue, and down it.

Open the pockets, and lick the remaining powder.

Much easier than two spoons imo.
