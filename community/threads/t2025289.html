I had this opinion:

Ron Paul and Dennis Kucinich (moreso with Paul) are the only candidates in these days that actually stand up for what they believe in. Believe me, I'm no socialist, but Kucinich's views are nothing but admirable. His views on how to fix the economy (ridding ourselves of the drug "war", changing are trade policies) are something you can tell, he really wants to do. Ron Paul has the same honorable aura surrounding him. Dr. Paul is the most consistent voting record, a man that doesn't just preach "small government" he backs it up. Ron Paul voted against the Iraq War, WTO, voted against the drug war, NEVER voted to raise taxes, openly opposes the IRS, and opposes all law-making at the federal level... just as the Constitution suggests.

Ron Paul is exactly what the founding father's would have loved; it's really exciting to see such an honest candidate. It's sad to see how many people don't realize how much money we waste as nation on this so called "war"(referring to war on drugs).

It really surprises me how many people today vote on who they think they will win, rather than who's views most closely represent theirs.

That's what I said in the drug thread, and I honestly stand by it. What Ron Paul represents is just such a great message it's amazing to even see it today. Politics are just so corrupt today having their opinions swayed by what they think their "base" wants... while we go and see a great person like Ron Paul vote on how the founding father's would have voted.

He's got my vote in the primary. It's great to see his numbers rising so quickly... he may even have a chance at winning the primary.
