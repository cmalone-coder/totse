Was this LSD?
Hello BLTC, I was hoping that you would be able to answer a question that has been driving me insane. Last Saturday I received what was to be believed two hits of "LSD". Now, I have trouble believing this because I always think that LSD production died with the counter-culture and that no chemist would waste time making "low demand" drugs. The first thing I noticed was the appearance of the paper. It definitely did not look like the clothy ripped squares with stubs on the edges from where it was torn from the sheet, but rather it looked like plain paper with the Scooby-Doo logo printed on it. Now I've tried to do what I thought was LSD before, but only received a silly light placebo while burnt out on cannabis where nothing actually happened. Now, I dropped the two tabs I had at the same time and noticed no immediate effect until around 20 minutes. That's when I went to the bathroom and noticed the patterns on the granite mimicking linoleum swimming around. When I lay back down I saw my hands making a rainbow trail about a foot long. Everything else made trails too. For about 8 hours later, everything I looked at was distorting much like bubbling liquid and my vision seemed to "lag" behind fading into the next image in my brain. Still images of faces on the television morphed into much different looking people. Kaleidoscopes were REALLY visual on my ceiling and glowed every color accompanied by various fractal shapes. I then felt my persona change, I felt like a complete madman and couldn't trust my senses. Seconds seemed like minutes. In the morning, still tripping hard I went onto MSN on watched as the conversation box transformed into a bunch of different hues. I could honestly not tell what color the link text was on my browser because it kept flowing from a variety of brightly saturated hues. Long into the evening that next day, I was still noticing subtle morphing in objects. By now I feel completely normal, yet baffled at what a bizarre experience I had, and am now completely obsessing over it. Now I do not know if this could be a designer drug such as DOB, or LSD itself. I am only aware of what happened that long night. Any suggestions on what in the world I took?
