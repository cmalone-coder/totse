Quote:
Originally Posted by reidy-
I though one day, fuck it 4GB mp3 player at 17quid, meh what can go wrong.

well it arrived and here is the list:

1) it uses a 2.5mm jack as oposed to 3.5
2) the jack is located on the side? (its a look alike nano) so it sticks out in your jeans pocket
3)the headphones supplied broke whithin 4-5days
4)the GUI is dire
5)the actually memory is firmware hacked to display as 4GB the memory chip is only 8Gb (1GB) so anything added over 1GB becomes 'corrupt'
6)the Li-ion battery cooked itself so last about 1-2hours at best


all the mp3 play is now good for is data storage, moral of the story dont buy jap
The 4GB scam is all too common now - dodgy 4gb memory cards are actually hacked 1gb cards. If you read the feedback you'll se a LOT of people getting very annoyed about this. There are some genuine players though, and this is normally reflected in the feedback.

And yeah, a 2.5mm jack is a pain in the ass as you have to buy a huge long converter which will probably stress the jack inside and break it.

The Li-ion battery charging systems I've seen inside them are downright dangerous. No voltage/current regulation, just a 10 ohm resistor in series with the 5v 500mA USB power. It's no wonder it fried, it's lucky it didn't explode. Li--ions are delicate beasties to charge.
