Re: LSD Questions
1: do you need a sitter?

No. I much prefer tripping without sober people around.

2: can you control it?

To a certain degree, yes. As long as you don't take a lot a lot a lot, you can control many aspects of the trip. But you will not be tripping very hard on just one. Try buying 10 next time. Generally there is a bit of a price cut in 10. Obviously, don't eat them all for your first couple times, but you may walk away from the trip on one blotter wanting more.

There are many things you cannot control, though. A lot of things will trigger certain thought patterns, and will take control of you.

3: does it give you the same feeling in your head that shrooms do?

They are completely different chemicals, so no. Acid has always been an easier trip mentally for me than shrooms.

4: is it worse for your health than shrooms?

Use responsibly. Acid abuse can wreak havoc on your mind. Not physically, but it can make you a bit loony if you don't respect it's power.

Enjoy. Trip safe: Set and setting.
