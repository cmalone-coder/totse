Re: guns in the matrix
The military uses a thingy called a blank firing device. It's a red deal that clamps onto the end of the barrel and partially seals the barrel. That way, enough pressure remains to cycle the bolt. They make it red and easily visible so everyone knows that gun is firing blanks. For movies they might have something similar that fits into the barrel so it's not visible. Or, they might have a special gun that is designed to fire blanks only, so they don't have a bunch of ar-15s laying all over the place.
