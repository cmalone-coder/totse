Sometimes codeine doesn't hit people that hard, the first time they try it... other opiates too, it can take a while to "adjust" to the effects.

If you have zero opiate tollerance, and you're taking genuine DHC pills which aren't some crappy Indian ones which could potentially be low potency... I wouldn't try 200mg. Try maybe 100mg in a few days, and wait n see if it hits. You can always use grapefruit juice to potentiate it, but probably best to leave that out until you're sure what kinda dose feels good. I know for me, I get mild euphoria and I feel great chilling out off about 80mg of DHC, or 200ish-mg of codeine phosphate.

Necking loads of booze or smoking loads of weed probably isn't the best idea, until you're sure of how strong it is. I dunno, you could just have a naturally high opiate tollerance, but even still drinking lots and then taking high doses of DHC probably isn't the best idea. Codeine's subtle anyway; all warm n fuzzy, and alcohol can drown the effects out.
