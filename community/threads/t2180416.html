(Probably Stupid) Mortgage Question

The payments (including insurance and taxes) would be a little less than $400, but say they're exactly $400 for the sake of simplicity.

If I have extra money, I can pay $500 a month to pay off the mortgage faster, but wouldn't it make more sense to take that extra $100 a month and put it into some sort of interest-bearing account, then make one large payment to pay the mortgage off once I have enough saved up?

Re: (Probably Stupid) Mortgage Question
You're charged interest on a mortgage. It's a bank loan.

To get a fuller picture, you can look up amortization tables; or if they're confusing, then go with mortgage tables.

It's usually a better idea to pay the extra directly onto the mortgage; however, many mortgages regulate how much, or how often, you can pay down your principle. If it's carefully regulated, then saving and making balloon payments is a good idea.

Re: (Probably Stupid) Mortgage Question
I prepay my mortgage by $100 per month, but really...It's not making that much of a dent in my loan amount. In October of 2007 I owed 109,000 and now I owe 107,000./shrug The only way prepaying makes sense is if you plan to stay in the house for at least 5-10 years or more. A $45,000 house doesn't sound like a place you'd want to stay in for all that long. I mean it must either be really small, or really deep in the ghetto. If I were you, I'd save up a bigger downpayment and buy a nicer place. Then your property value will consistently increase and it will be a home you want to stay in for a long time.

My husband and I put $28,000 down on our house (We paid $136,000 for it total). It was more expensive than a lot of other homes, but the one we bought is a big 3 bedroom, 2 1/2 bathroom home with wood floors and a MASSIVE brick fireplace. It's in a really nice neighborhhod in the best school district in Augusta. I know you don't have any kids, but if you want to sell your house, the people who want to buy it might have them and then the quality of the schools becomes important.

Just something to think about.
