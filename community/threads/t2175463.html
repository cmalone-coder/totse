Re: DUI help
A DUI isn't the end of the world - especially if you weren't in an accident or something.

22 years ago I was arrested for DUI I wasn't even in my car. I was busted walking about 2 blocks from where I left my [wrecked] car. Granted back then the legal limit wasn't .08, but .1 blew a .28, so I was screwed no matter how you looked at it.

I know the laws all over have toughened up since when I got arrested, but not that much I don't think.

Judges still have a lot of discretion in how they sentance people. In my case, the DA wanted to make an example of me since I was so far above the legal limit and I had destroyed city property [I knocked over a defenseless pedestriant 'Walk' button pole]. The law required that I do three days in county. But the judge saw that I had a job, was enrolled in school [which I did to make it look good for court and itr sure did] and so he gave me close to 1,000 hours of community service instead.

That worked out well because I did my time working at the student center at the community college I was attending. So I did my time between classes. All in all it worked out rather well for me. That work I did turned into a job when my service was over.

Also, since I hung out in the stdent center, I ended up joining student government. There I met some people I know to this day. Most notably was my future wife and [after remeeting 20 years later] an ex GF who is still one of my closest friends.

But going back to school I got serious, graduated with straight A's and was accepted - and attended - UC Berkeley. Moving up to Northern Cali was one of the best things I've done in m life. And UC Berkeley obviously had no trouble with my DUI conviction. Nor did the issue ever come up in any job interview.
