Jeep Problem

Re: Jeep Problem
Has the Check Engine light come on?Being a 1986, it would not be OBDII, so you wouldn't get a Check Enging light to illuminate unless a sensor failed completely. You could still have a sensor issue (MAP, TP, EGR, etc.) without the light turning on.

Also, a plugged fuel filter can overwork the fuel pump, resulting in premature failure. Changing the filter may allow a weak pump to run the vehicle, but with insufficient pressure for it to run properly. I would recommend having someone verify your fuel pressure.

It would also help us if you could provide a more detailed description of the symptom. Does it simply lack power? Does it buck/jerk when accelerating? Does it hesitate when taking off from stops?

The more info we have , the better chance we have of diagnosing your problem correctly.

Re: Jeep Problem
If the symptom occured suddenly, it's not likely that it is the converter. I would be more inclined to agree with tauted, that your timing belt probably jumped a tooth or two. That would explain the lack of power and the hard startup.

Have your friend connect a vacuum gauge to intake manifold vacuum. If the cam timing has retarded, your vacuum will be lower than normal.

I would still recommend checking your fuel pressure with a gauge. Even though the pump may be working, it may not be able to supply the necessary pressure.
