I've been using Geranamine for about 3 1/2 months now. It is the main ingredient in Ergopharm's (bodybuilding supplement company) AMP and AMP2 pre-workout energy booster/fat loss product, though I mainly use for the former and as a coffee alternative sometimes.

Your first couple, maybe few times are the most you'll get out of it recreation-wise. AMP2 has other ingredients that synergize and potentiate with Geranamine to get that "original" effect you had from the first few times you took AMP1, because just like amps you gain tolerance rather quickly an after the first few times of using it straight the euphoria + other recreational effects go away (though the speediness is till there... but not as much good as some addy or dex,etc.)

So all in all it does make a nice amphetamine substitute the first few times you take it, but after those first times you still have all the speediness, and other positive amp effects, without the recreational effects. Its not as much fun as legit pharm amps, but its worth it if you cant get legit amps.

I would suggest buying a bottle of AMP1 or AMP2 instead of plain, bulk geranamine because of the ingredients Ergopharm put into the product significantly boosts the effect. The original AMP1 formula has something AMP2 doesn't have, and that is chocamine. After going through a bottle of AMP1 then getting used to AMP2 I realized the difference in formula, where as AMP1 had more of a mood-boosting effect I'd say because of the chocamine, unlike AMP2. They took the chocamine out though because people were complaining of headaches from the chocamine, which some people do... in large does of it.

Overall, the AMP2 formula is still better, but the chocamine was a nice addition to geranamine, so you could always buing bulk chocamine seperately from bulknurition.com and use a small dose of it when taking AMP2.
