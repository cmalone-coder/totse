I enjoy my 2 cups of mindfuck coffee every morning (double-brewed with about 12 cups of sugar and a few teaspoons of ginseng), as well as 4-5 caffeine pills (with 200 mg caffeine in each pill). I may indulge in a few energy chocolates as well (about 50 mg caffeine in each) when I get home. All in all, this puts my average daily consumption of caffeine at about 1600 mg of caffeine a day--there are about 130-180 mg of caffeine in a regular cup of coffee. This is the equivalent of drinking 11 1/2 cups. In one day. I have a little bit of trouble sleeping, I've been keeping this up for about two months now, and whenever I abuse caffeine to this extent, I get an incredible high that lasts many hours.
