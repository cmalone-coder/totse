Re: Frozen paintballs
����Frozen paintballs can work, provided they will load in your gun. Water expands when it freezes, so frozen paintballs tend to swell and are slightly larger than unfrozen ones. If you have a close ball and bore match between your gun and unfrozen paintballs chances are the frozen ones won't load right. Thus, you need to either swap out a larger barrel or invest in smaller paint for freezer duty.
In either event, using frozen paintballs for weapons against people is childish and cowardly and only serves to give the legitimate sport of paintball a bad name.

~Zero
