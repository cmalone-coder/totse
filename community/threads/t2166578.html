As long as you keep it away from heat, humidity, and sunlight, they'll be fine for a while. You don't really need to put them in a freezer unless you have a lot.

And also, would it be a bad idea to trip at alone at night in my room?

Tripping alone is much better than tripping around people who aren't tripping. But, does it have to be in your room? Is there any chance you'd be able to do some outside exploration or are you in a neighborhood/city? If you have a nice view of the stars, that, at least, is almost necessary. Either way, as long as you're not going to have to worry about anything, you'll be fine. I just don't like the feeling of being confined when I'm tripping.

Yes I am looking for introspection and epiphanies[, not to get fucked up

Good! :)

also how do you guys feel about combining 1 capsule with 1 tab?

Take both tabs at once -- even if they're potent. It's likely you would regret taking one at a time more than taking both. Also, I would test the strength of the MDMA before making that kind of decision. If you can, try doing some of the molly before you plan on tripping to get a good idea on how intense one capsule is going to be.

I imagine you're more than capable of judging the dosage from there. However much MDMA you decide on taking for the trip, take it about 2 hours into the trip, about when you're peaking.
