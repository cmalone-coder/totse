BLIZZARD:

1. (up and B+C)

use your index and middle fingers on B+C

jump at a standing opponent with a jumping low power. it should look like a downward punch.

after landing,

2. (down and B+C)

same index and middle fingers on B+C

JUST AFTER THE SWEEP, WHILE OPPONENT IS IN THE AIR!!

3. (hold start+A+C and tap down and up on your D-pad)

use your index, middle, and ring fingers on A, Start, and C

While opponent is in the air

4. (up and B+C)

index and middle on B,C

jump at him with another jumping low power

5. (whill still in the air right after the jumping low power, press A+B)

just shift your middle and index fingers over one button each.
