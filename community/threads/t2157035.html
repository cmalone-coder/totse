Much of the disinformation campaign surrounding nutmeg has exploited the fact that there is actually a condition called "nugmeg liver." First mentioned around the 1800s, it was noted in several instances of liver failure.

However, "nutmeg liver" is named after the color of the liver, which is a very pale reddish-brown, and in the late 1800s...

http://www.pubmedcentral.nih.gov/picrender.fcgi?artid=2150534&blobtype=pdf

...as noted in this historical article, was finally determined to be a bloodflow disorder of the liver.

It was also discovered at around this time that it was not caused solely by alcoholism, which is what they thought was the cause.

So, yes. There is a disorder called "nutmeg liver." However, contrary to the disinformation campaign, it is named after the coloration, and has not in the 200-odd years of its existance had anything to do with eating nutmeg.

In fact, this whole "nutmeg panic" is a lot like fearing that smoking pot will cause coral to erupt on your skin because they call it "reefer." Lulzy, in its historical etiology as a disinformation campaign, but completely baseless.
