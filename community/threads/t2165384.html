B&N: Laughingstock of the retail world.
Have you ever experienced any trouble lifting from Barnes & Noble? In my experience, they're just sad, security-wise.

I know for a fact that all the bottom floor cameras are fake, as the employees can cover the whole bottom floor themselves. As for the the cameras upstairs, the music section has one or two, almost always right in front of the checkout, pretty likely to prevent employee theft. Even if the cameras aren't all fake, there are still tons of blind spots throughout the high shelves upstairs.

Their RFID stickers can be peeled off or scratched to the point of being useless, and are not in all the books either.

The only real threat would be the security guard at the gate, but you're not obligated to consent to a search, and they can't chase you outside the doors. In any case, the guard can be avoided by going through the coffee shop door (if your B&N has one).

So for those who have encountered difficulty in liberating some books, have any details? Am I completely wrong about everything? Is my B&N a minority and a shitty store?
Are you the walrus?
