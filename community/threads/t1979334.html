With that, I continue: O the mines in the field of relationship. There seems to be one at every step. My trick is to always date men who are far too old for me because it garauntees the relationship will eventually have to come to an end. However, that didn't work with my latest relationship. So, a year later I find myself with the same man.

I started out the other woman and since we've been official we've broken up and gotten back together twice. Each breakup lasted about two weeks. I can't seem to shake this one nor the cheap feeling of our relationship and the bad taste it leaves in my mouth. We're dating on unholy ground, you know.

I even cheated on him, which I'd never done before. Not that I slept with anyone but before we officially were back together I made out with a very attractive guy at a speakeasy in front of all of our friends and my boyfriend was humiliated and cried. Now he's in uber boyfriend mode, which is nice because even when he's in uber boyfriend mode everything still feels very casual. May be that's why it's lasted so long. May be that's why - a year later - I still feel like the other woman.

Will this feeling ever pass? I don't know. I imagine our relationship will pass long before the feeling, though.

When I came home from classes today my sister told me Danny showed up randomly at the door. Danny? Danny was 21 when I was 11 and came up to me on my front steps. He had a cockney accent and told me one day he would marry me. I believed him, of course, considering when you're 11 and live in the projects and an attractive older man with a british accent tells you he's going to marry you, you fucking believe him. Three years later we became a couple.

For three years he was a devoted and gentlemanly boyfriend. Never tried to fuck me and never did. I left him a virgin. By the time I was 16 he'd bought me a ring and we had already sent out the wedding invitations. I was being fitted for my gown when he told me the girl he'd gotten pregnant during one of our seperations was keeping the baby. The wedding was off. I didn't see him again for seven years.

During those seven years I met another man and married him, and later got divorced. One day Danny called me up while I was in the supermarket. I fainted to hear his voice. It was like hearing a ghost. I hit my head on the linoleum and was taken to the emergency room in an ambulance. I have three stitches. Hair still wont grow there.

A few weeks later he came by my house and I was so nervous I got piss drunk. He'd gained weight and had had another baby. We later talked on the phone and he told me I was the one who got away, how he only ever loved me, and how it killed him how everything turned out. He told me he was having marital problems and wanted to know if he could take me out for lunch. I told him absolutely not.

I didn't hear from him for another two years. Until today when my sister said he'd dropped by.

Why had he dropped by when he could have called? My number hasn't changed. Why is he dropping by? What is this all about? O the mounting confusion. Now the question is, what will I do about it?
