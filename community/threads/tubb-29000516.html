Re: Flame-throwers
In response to the original question of how to make a flame-thrower that won't take your face off: Get one of those oil cans that has a trigger (they will throw a stream of oil at least 20 feet or more), a piece of cotton felt 4 inches long by 1&1/4 inches wide, and a small screw-type hose clamp. Wrap the felt around the nozzle (making sure there is still room for the stream of oil to shoot), clamp it down with the hose clamp, mix yourself some gas and oil in a 50/50 ratio, and fill up the container. "Slightly" squeeze the trigger until the 50/50 mix soaks the felt, then light the felt and fire away. Accuracy is not bad with a little practice, and you don't have to worry about it blowing up on you, unless you buy one of those oil cans from China that leak like a sieve. You will have to re-soak the felt from time to time, but a handy little thrower it is.
