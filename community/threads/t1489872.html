You may either tell her you'd like to become bf-gf, or you may remain friends. Those are your options. If you're looking for a way to pre-determine her answer, there is none. You can try to drop hints or ask "test" questions but often these are so transparent that things get weird anyways.

And if you're hoping for a way to suggest the bf-gf scene without making things weird, there is none. Too much depends on factors you can't know until the question is asked.
