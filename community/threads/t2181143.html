Re: Where to smoke weed on a college campus?
I know this runs contrary to your question, but you should try to find a a spot to smoke that's not on the campus first. No sense getting expelled and having your future ruined over a joint.

However, if you really can't find any spot off campus, or it's too far of a walk or something, look for secluded areas like people said. It's probably overkill to try to find a special secluded area though. On a big college campus, with hundreds of people walking around all the time smoking cigarettes, do you really think they're going to give a shit about one more person smoking one? You even have tobacco in the joint to cover up the smell a bit. Don't smoke a fat cone in front of people or anything, but if you make it look like a cigarette you'll be fine. Rolling machines are great for this. Or, if they're so nosy at your college that they investigate people with unfiltered cigarettes, get a one-hitter that looks like a cigarette.

I'm assuming you can't smoke in your room for some reason. If you can smoke in your room, just get a fabric softener roll and some air freshener spray. Open the window, turn on the fan, or both. Less walking, less po-pos to be scared of.
