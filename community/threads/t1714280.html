I am pretty sure you have some water stashed at the base, but in the event of an emergency such as nuclear fallout...not unlikely with todays world, you will need to be indepedent from all of the outside world. I would suggest you have some sort of water purification system down there that will be able to take care of almost all contaminents and spare filters availible.

As for food, once again I wouldnt depend on trapping. However it is a very good idea to keep traps handy. If I were you I would purchase some military rations from surplus stores. Even if they taste like shit, they will fill you up with proper nutrients and can be stored for a very long time. Also, if you have a base big enough, I would suggest keeping seeds and possibly clones of plants around...eh marijuana here is a must.

Weapons...only having one handgun and one rifle in .22 would not be good. What if it comes down to serious defense? Will you be able to depend on the .22 to penetrate armor? Well, you will probably not face armored enemies, but what about a car? If I were you I would get an AR15 or MINI14 with some .223 ammunition handy. You should definitely keep the .22's around however as they are the most useful of guns.

Then, there is the danger of air contamination. Like I said before, nuclear fallout is very likely if it comes down to total war between nations. You will need an air filtration system for your base and for yourself. Hazard suits that have battery powered fans and 5 redundant filters can be purchased online in the United States for under 500 each. A good air filtration system for a base can be gotten from many commerical air conditioning suppliers. ANd obviously, you will need many spare filters.

As for electricity, I would recommend you keep an ammount of different batteries besides your generators. I would go for lithium Ion ones, however they tend to be expensive. You can ofcourse just purchase normal batteries and use them as your replace them.

Now transportation. You can purchase an all terain go-kart for under 1000 dollars at most sporting good stores here in the states. These are small, easy to dis/assemble, and fuel efficient. In the event of an emergency, you would want a form of transportation which can be stashed at the base and is cheap enough to keep around.

As for fuel, I would keep that and other explosive/flammable chemicals away from the main base room. I would suggest you keep that in a seperate chamber if possible. However, most people with small bases do not have this luxury.

On a side note, you can buy waterproof, radiation resistant, bullet resistant, containers here in the States from many stores. These were real popular during the Sars/terrorism(I have no clue why people would want this for sars) scare. They can be obtained for around 10/20 dollars each. I found that these are truely sturdy, and dispite having doubts about their claims to be bullet resistant, I would keep most of my things in these.

Then there is the thing with building supplies. On one hand, you would want to keep some boards and nails handy, on the other, they might be some extra crap you might not need. This is cheap so I would keep some around.

Powertools...these are a must. Drills, saws, dremel, and others if they are availible. Battery powered if you can get them like that and still pack enough punch to do a good job. I beleive there is a kit here in the states made by Black n Decker that contains all these tools in a large case complete with battery. They are 18 volts, probably powerful enough for most purposes.

Cleaning...Well, you obviously have soap and things along those lines. However, did you think about brooms and vaccume cleaner? Great quality handheld vacs such as the EuroPro shark can be purchased for uner 50 dollars and are a great addition to your base. Then again, this falls under the extra, not neccessary but nice to have catagory.

Clothing. Once again, it is obvious that you have plenty around. However, what kind? If I were you, I would only keep tough things made from denim, leather, and a few flannel shirts around. You should skip the tshirts and other things which dont last a long time. Also, for underwear, you might want to consider the new neoprane(sp) ones. They are suppose to be very durable, water proof, washable, easily dried, and very warm, not to mention comfortable. I beleive you can purchase socks made from the same material. Socks as anyone would tell you are a rather important part of survival.

Medication: Painkillers are a must. I would try to purchase some morphine if possible. They can be obtained from most large drug dealers that sell heroine and other opiates. Other drugs such as OTC medication should also be kept. Also, pills for radiation(forgot the name) will be very helpful to have, as well as those anthrax(and other bio weapon) shots that the US government has kept from the public.

Finally, I would once again stress the fact that I am only 15 years old and do not have any experience with this. All the above are only my ideas and should NOT be taken as serious advice.
