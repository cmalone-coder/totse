Last night's trip
Last night I decided I was going to trip again on DXM, the setting was just so perfect for it. It was probably the best trip o've ever had. I had spent most of the day in Boston, with an old friend that I hadn't seen in forever, and i'd been on the subway for a large part of the day. Anyway, around midnight I took the DXM (enough for high second/low 3rd plateau), and the trip started about half an hour later. I was sitting in my battered old armchair with my g/f who was on E, looking out the window at the stars, listning to techno. The whole universe seemed perfect right at that moment. It was by far more intense than the feeling of everything being all right that I get on E. I got up to go to the bathroom, and the cat was laying there. I remember saying, "you don't know what you're missing, cat". I went back into my room and lay there on the bed with my g/f, in the dark, and I closed my eyes, and it was like the stars I had seen earlier were there, and they were somehow alive, and moving with the music. Each one of them seemed to be some sort of creative energy. My g/f says that I muttered "from nothing comes creation, and from creation comes beauty, and all beauty is one". The trip was still getting more intense, and I kept thinking back to the riding on the subway, and I remember the lights going by in the dark, from earlier on in the day, and I started to see the same type of lights going by me, and I felt as if I were flying through the darkness. I was tired, and the trip slowly faded into sleep, and I got up at about noon today. I have taken larger doses of DXM before, and never got the same intensity of effects. It was incredibly intense, and I would have to say that after this experience, DXM is now by far my favorite drug. Definitly not a last-resort drug, or a kiddy drug. It was one of the best experiences I have ever had.
