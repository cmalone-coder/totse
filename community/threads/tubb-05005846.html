Re: Is this Oxycontin?
You definitely have an opiate, that's been established. The itchy face is a dead giveaway. Only a select few types of narcotics do that, Opiates and Disassociatives mostly, but none of your other results fit the generic Disassociative trip.

The wax-like texture of the crushed pill is the result of Chemical Binders. Oxycontin is loaded with chemical binders that slowly slip the chemical into your bloodstream, whereas codone will hit you like "a gold brick wrapped in lemon peels" and keep you under a little longer.

Those of you who are uncomfortable with the itchiness may want to try a few readily available solutions before they write off these kinds of trips. I had the same problem until I began receiving Welbutrin SR for a compulsive disorder, which fixed compulsive scratching, and also some go-fast side effects like picking at my skin and teeth.
