Re: Best Nitrous Options
I <3 my dispenser, for several reasons:

1. It can hold more than two chargers worth
2. Don't have to worry about letting go of the balloon when you're frying
3. Easier to huff than a balloon, IMO.
4. Don't have to wait for the nitrous to cool. You gotta be careful when huffing a balloon that was just filled.
5. If you DO prefer balloons, you can still use it to fill them.
6. Balloons wear out eventually and pop -- which can be really fuckin' scary when you're tripping (personal experience here)
7. A little quieter than a cracker. As many of you know, cracking a whip into a balloon has a very distinct, loud noise. It's a little easier to hide with this should you need to.

I dunno, I just really like them. Of course, it's always nice to have both.
