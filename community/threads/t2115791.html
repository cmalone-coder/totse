whats the diffrence between standardized and not standardized?

standardized is taking a measured amount of salvinorin and infusing it onto foliage.

non-standardized is evaporating the solvent used in the extraction directly onto the foliage without measuring. for example, extracting the salvinorin from 4g of foliage and infusing it onto 1g of foliage would make ~1g of 5x extract (because it's the salvinorin from 5 grams of salvia crammed into one gram of the stuff)

non-standardized is fine, if you're getting it from a reputable source.
