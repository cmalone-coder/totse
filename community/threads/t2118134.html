You may need to configure the card for your network (essid, WEP/WPA key, etc).

You may also need to obtain drivers for the card. The best way to do this is to search on the distributions documentation for the card chipset (not model or vendor!) and see where to obtain the drivers.

Generally if you can see the card, you have drivers for it. However, some drivers may recognize cards and not allow them to connect.

If you cannot find any documentation on your card, post the chipset here, as well as in a question thread on the Fedora forums. You also might want to try the Fedora IRC channel.

You can find the chipset of the card by using the lspci utility (if the card is PCI or MiniPCI) or lsusb utility (if the card is USB).
