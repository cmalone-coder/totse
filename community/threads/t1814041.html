Two yank missionaries turned up at my door this evening, I answer the door and they want to talk God. We had a real good chat for about 15 minutes, they didn't even try to convert me - they just wanted to know why I'm an Atheist and how I can be happy with no prospect of an afterlife, whilst I wanted to know how they can dispute the Archaeological record. After that they left me with some literature and moved on to the next house with us both having gained an insight as to the views of "the other side". They were quite pleased as they don't usually get such a nice reception from students!

How do you guys react to Mormons/Jehovah's witnesses/Random God people? Do you invite them in and have a chat or tell them to piss off? Do you think that these people have a right to canvas your neighbourhood?

If all of them are as nice as those Mormons, I'd like some more! I don't get enough philosophical discussion in my house - I'm fine with this kind of thing as long as they don't try to convert me! Discussion I enjoy though.
