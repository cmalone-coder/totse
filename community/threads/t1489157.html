Re: I love this girl but she doesn't love me...
Ok, stop right there - no desperate gifts or flower bouquets or "take me back please" cards. That's just a bad idea - you want her to know you have self-esteem and confidence, that you're not desperate and sad. Just continue hanging around her, and you know what? The reason she might not seem to take interest in you and push you away is because she DOES like you, as strange as that may seem. She may be in her own mental prison of denial, guilt, uncertainty, whatever. For all you know, someday, she's going to cave. All you need to do is play it cool - be a good friend, try not to be a moody fuckup (I'm not saying you are), be nice, be you. Even if she doesn't have the feelings I just mentioned, she may realize why she liked you again, and may start to grow close. You never know. Final advice, though: Don't get your hopes unrealistically high, ever.
