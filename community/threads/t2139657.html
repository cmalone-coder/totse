Recreationaly (and I use that term loosely) they fucking suck. Allow me to attempt to explain.

There is no dose that will give the delirious (not I did not say psychedelic) effects that wont also give the nasty side effects. The side effects I speak of are both physical and mental.

Physical ~ You feel weighed down. You literally feel like another 70 pounds has just entered your body. Well, you don't really feel heavier but your movements and coordination are as if you are and it is a strange and highly unpleasant physical sensation. You also get a general feeling of discomfort that is difficult to explain but can be summed up as shitty as hell. Your eyes become glassy like never before, reading becomes next to impossible. Many also complain about painful leg conditions similar to restless leg syndrome that persists for the whole duration (although I didn't get this when I did it).

Mental ~ No euphoria and no easily to describe change in mood. Emotion basically ceases to exist with generally dark insights about miserable things. You see shit that is 100 % not there. You even can hallucinate people who are not there and they will talk to you and they will talk back. It is not scary nor 100 % presistant, you are not visualy tripping at all times. Expect to hear your name being screamed out.

Not enough time to fully sum this shit up. Do it once maybe but never again. It can cause HPPD and similar long lasting dysfunctions. You will regret it but that has not stopped &t members in the past.
