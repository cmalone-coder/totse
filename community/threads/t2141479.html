So After a recent break up, I took it upon myself to completely re-work myself from the ground up. Physically, mentaly, style wise- the entire works, yet somehow I predict that my choice and goal won't do much if I make it that far. After all, once you are on top, there isn't any turning back; there isn't some magical "back button" like in our browsers whether it is Firefox, Safari, I.E, Opera, or one of the many others.
