How to bitch and moan
This thread is to help explain to people why I'm closing their threads. Trousersnake I'm sure will add to this list too.

First off "discuss" should NEVER be the entire content of your thread. If you want us to discuss something, you'd better be willing to do it yourself. So if you're pissed about something, don't make a thread with the title "FUCK (whatever)" and content "Discuss" because I will close it and probably give you an infraction. Instead try: "FUCK (whatever)" with content: "This is why I dislike (whatever):"

This is NOT a spam forum. Long ago before Half Baked did not exist, this was considered "totse's garbage can" It is not anymore and never will be. This is a legitimate forum for discussions, debates, flames, and heated arguments.
