Describe your un-drugged (no, not the website -.-) "hallucinogenic" or "HPPD" type experiences.

Mine:

I personally, have peculiar hallucination like waves in darkness that wash/pulsate over my field of view, repetitively(zooming in and out of the same pattern).

If i concentrate i can make the patterns progressively more intricate.... also i have noticed, among the dim, thin, multi colored, static overlay, intricate patterns that only exist for a tenth of a second as one precise "thing" progressively evolving from intricate flowers, and arbitrary parts of objects to butterfly designs.

It is difficult to even define what you are looking at, because it progresses so fast that you cant define what exactly you see, if you see anything at all, because all of this is "over-layed" on a "different dimension" of vision.

It sort of exists while simultaneously not existing.

Sometimes this is accompanied by weird sensations, such as confuzzling sensations of body part; size/weight/dimension/location distortion....

At other times, particularly during meditation, slow, expanding, breakthrough, begins to pulsate through my consciousness.

'splain

drugs merely (for the most part) mimic the effects of chemicals that naturally are produced in yr brain. for instance, LSD mimics serotonin, cannabis releases loads of melatonin, the "harder" drugs release dopamine, etc etc. You see where I'm going with this?

well, spirituality is for the most part a way to maximize yr body's production of its own natural chemicals. meditation, concentration, mantras, etc etc - they're all tools just like drugs are.

http://www.serendipity.li/mcclay/pineal.html

So all this nonsense about drugs producing hallucinations and HPPD being a disease is a complete misnomer. HPPD is basically another word for having opened up yr third eye and not knowing what to do about it...

What truly produces hallucinations is the separative thought structure of the mind known as the ego(s).

in the terms of Castaneda/Don Juan, through the use of drugs yr assemblage point has become more fluid and doesn't just stick around on its habitual position, but it moves around erratically because you don't know how to act in the proper manner according to the new found fluidity, thus you think yr crazy or diseased. yr neither, you've just opened up worlds of possibility and don't know how to deal with it yet.
