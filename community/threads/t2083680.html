Trouble with girlfriends..
<venting>
OK here goes. I had a girlfriend of nearly 4 years, she broke up with me about a year ago, I've pretty much dealt with that and moved on.

But, I remember when she broke up with me I was hurt so bad- beyond words. The good thing was, with all the hurting it gave me mass amounts of energy to change my life around. Within a year I changed from someone who didn't have any friends (except my girlfriend at the time), was lacking so much confidence that I even found it hard to buy stuff from the local supermarket, because there were so many people looking at me. I always was afraid to talk to strangers and even people I knew quite well, because I thought my opinion didn't matter- I was ugly in all ways from my soul, to my appearance.

But now, im really confident, have a best friend, quite a few close friends and masses of casual friends. reasonable job, awesome new car and the main thing is im pretty much always happy- and people see that in me now.

Now the thing is, I've realized that being single gave me time to find myself, chase my ambitions and develop myself personally.

I'm with my 3rd girlfriend after breaking up with my ex, the stages are exactly the same...

Meet girl and hit it off.
I get with girl.
Think she is great.
Start getting serious.
Then I realize that most of my energy is going into the relationship and can't find the time or energy to chase my personal ambitions. Then I end up becoming depressed so I break up with them. All this happens in around 2 months.

But now, I'm with a girl who I utterly adore and I want her to have my babies. She is amazing, and I've never felt quite like this about anyone before. First we had heaps of fun, but now that we are starting to get serious, the usual stage of events are happening again. But I don't want to break up with this one.

I feel as if I have the choice of love and depression, or the absence of love but happiness. grr its so annoying, maybe I'm not looking at it right.

</venting>
