Re: Help with EMP devices
Alright.

The guide with explosives, if I remember correctly, won't work. An explosive EMP is amazingly hard to build.

The guide without, if I remember correctly, is just a shoddy magnetic field oscillation generator or something of the sort (well, technically a real EMP is too, but on a much larger scale - this generates the kind of fields used to power speakers) and won't do a whole lot of anything. Even a degaussing coil would be much stronger.

As for the distance problem, that's like saying "If I make a sound, at what distance will it be audible?". Yes, it'll depend on coil size, but also the amount of power going through it, and similarly, not only will it not just stop working at a given point (attenuation is inverse-square of distance) but it's going to depend on the surrounding environment's magnetic fields and ferromagnetism.

Short version:
There's no 100% reliable way of knowing for sure whether an EMP will do what you need it to at a given distance without trying it. Or maybe spending hours doing experiments and calculations.

And neither of the designs work.
