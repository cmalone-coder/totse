Re: Becoming Healthy
a generalized healthy diet isn't as difficult to make as some people would have you believe.

Basic rules to follow:

-A healthy diet for a normal person is balanced: Atkins-like diets are not healthy. A good diet balances fat, protein and carbs and doesn't reject one or the other.

-Whole Grain over purified or simple sugars (whole wheat is a better choice than white)

-Healthy fat over Unhealthy fats; Healthy fats being things like uncooked oliveoil, nuts, soft cheeses/ Unhealthy being fried foods, heavy creams etc.

-Vegetables and Fruits are obviously part of a healthy diet; for vegetables a general rule of thumb is that the darker the color, the healthier the vegetable.

-And you want to be minimizing your simple sugar intake: Soft drinks, Candy, Sweets etc

-And lastly, white meat and fish should take priority over red meat. Many diet guides reccomend limiting red meat intake to once or twice a month.
