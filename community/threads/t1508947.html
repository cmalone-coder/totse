We woke up the next morning, and everyone went their seperate ways, but me and Tanya ended up going back to our hometown.

I spent the day with her, and at some point, she was on the phone to her parents, and it turned out she had nowhere to stay for the night. She lived with her mum and sister, who were extremely abusive to her. I said she could sleep in my squat, and i'd keep her company.

We stayed in the squat for a while, then i went back to my mums house with Tanya. I asked if tanya could stay the night, my mum said 'yeah that's fine'. One night turned into a week. I loved the whole time, it was fantastic coming back home and having someone there who loves you.

Then the police found out she had left home to come live with us, they tried to take her back to her parents... If anyone but a copper had tried to take her from me, i would have ripped their fucking arms off. Anyway, i got rid of the coppers, and they left us alone for about a month. That was the best time of my life, ever. I would trade anything for another night of it. No money is worth that time i spent. Anyway, the coppers took her away, and we lost touch, she wasn't allowed to call me, and i didn't know her house number. That was about 6 months ago.

Now, she's had another boyfriend for 3 months, she told me that about a week ago. She told me then that she still loved me,as much as before, but she stayed with her boyfriend cos he cared about her... I just told her to fuck off and stopped speaking to her, because i couldn't handle that. I thought it over for a few days (the most depressing days of my life) and realised today:

what a stupid argument, i'm saying i care but she doesn't, she's saying she cares but i don't.

So i got talking to her, but i was still upset that she said she cared but stayed with her boyfriend. She said she was breaking up with him. i was dancing with joy, i never felt so much relief. I talked to her a little, expecting her to have left him for me. but she didn't. She left him because he cheated on her.

I love her, but i don't think i can handle just going out with her because her boyfriend cheated on her.

She sounds like she's just taking me for a ride according to my best friend, but she is the sweetest person i've met, and i can't imagine my life without her.

Any wisdom would VERY appreciated. I'm sure you can imagine how much this means to me.
