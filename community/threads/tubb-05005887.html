I got four pulled at once...

I received a looooot of vicodin (hydrocodone/oxycodone) and for the record....

I'm almost positive that most facilities use sodium penathol (or sleeping agent) which used anasthetically knocks you out completely. You wake up entirely disoriented and confused like you're in bumble-fuck, but rare cases will they use N2O...

That's sweeeeet stuff, but nearly not as effective for the current pain during wisdom teeth removal...

Also, the pain of removal does not compare AT ALL to that of the pain a few hours afterwards, the pain right after doesn't exactly initiate due to the disorientation and lack of total conciousness...

Don't let anybody talk about throwing up, I was feeling great and my dad kept telling me how the medication made him throw up, and the effect made me vomit right in my soup...

The analgesics (painkillers) won't seem as effective as you may presume, in the given dosages. They generally just provide sleepiness and semi-detectable narcotic effects, unless in larger dosages...

I didn't bleed much until 7-30 hours after my surgery (I had an afternoon surgery)...

My stitches took a while to dissolve, but every body's case is different...

I recovered rapidly, some slowly, don't think so much of it and remember to stash some of that vicodin for a rainy afternoon!!
