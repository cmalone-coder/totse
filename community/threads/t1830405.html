Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Re: How many of you have ever had cops show up at your house in relation to explosives?
Hiding HEs or illegal compositions might not be a bad idea, but it seems to me like it would stupid to hide just the materials used. If a cop ever found material that you had hidden somewhere, the very fact that you hid them in the first place will most likely alert him to the fact that you planned on doing something illegal with them. Think about. People who use acetone as paint thinner don't hide their acetone under the couch, and rocketry enthusiasts don't hide their KClO4 inside the wall.

Obviously the goal is never to have anyone show up at your door in the first place, but, it seems to me that if you ever were searched or visited by the police, you would be much smarter to claim a legitimate use for the chemicals you possess rather than risk serious questioning after they find them hidden under a couch or inside a wall.

I've also noticed that cops are often a lot nicer to those who appear to be religious, so it might not be a bad idea to leave a conspicuously placed Bible and a couple religious magazines lying around if anyone ever showed up at your door.
