I know alot of this basic information has already been posted, but I am a true lover of psychedelics, so here are my 2 cents:

Whenever I take shrooms, the visuals are secondary at best, and are more like the icing on the cake when it comes to the overall experience. Tripping for me is not about making shit melt and seeing castles materialize in the sand, but instead about the deep introspective moments you have while under the influence. I have never had a bad trip on mushrooms, and I truly believe that if you are at peace with yourself and a mentally stable person in a good environment, then a bad trip is near impossible. I would recommend going out into a large woods (I say large because you really won't want to see many other people as that can add to paranoia) such as a state park or national forest. Even if you aren't and out of doors person I guarantee that you will gain one of the deepest appreciations for nature through a good solid trip on mushrooms while out in the wild.

For me, acid is a lot more of a social drug, and you will in general be focusing more on the visuals. While I have made a fair share of epiphanies while on LSD, it seems to be a bit less introspective and a bit more 'fun'.

Both drugs will change your life forever, and should not be taken lightly, but at the same time not necessarily avoided. If you are going to do either, do it right and prepare beforehand. Proper hydration and reasonable nutrition a couple days in advance can make a HUGE difference and make tripping one the greatest experiences of your life.


Oh and btw, I would recommend mushrooms, as they are definitely my favorite.
