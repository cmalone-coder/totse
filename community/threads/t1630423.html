Sobriety Test
Sobriety: The Harshest of all Possible Realities.

Not to say that life without drugs isn't all good and fun and fulfilling.

It is.

Rather instead realizing you wished you were high sucks, and I'm not talking about "I wish I was high cause being high in an amusement park (or whatever else fun place) would rule!" I'm talking about: "I wish I was high because I'm so fucking bored" or "I wish I was high because I'm so nervous" or "I wish I was high cause school (work, whatever) sucks."

Toking up to avoid reality sucks, and I've been doing that of late. I need to stop. I'm not thinking of quitting completely. I'll be a drug user my whole life, it's part of my personality (alteration of consiousness, I mean). I'll still toke up with friends and when I paint or compose or am about to do something worth toking up for. I need to stop using everyday in order to regain a grip on reality (not to mention motivation).

So this is my self-induced (paranoia driven) rambling/declaration of independence (from marijuana).

If you are feeling the same way, try this with me. Try not toking up for any given length of time.

It's not about quitting drugs absolutely. Drugs are grrrreat. I have no objection to drugs. I have an objection to using a substance to avoid reality. I need to think clear.

My length of time: 6 days I haven't toked since Thursday, so that would put me at staying away from ganja till Wed.
