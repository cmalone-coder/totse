reconstituted sheet tobacco

This paper-like filler is made with inferior-quality tobacco that is a mix of scrap parts of the tobacco plant. By scraps we mean stems, tobacco dust, rejected leaves, and shreds from rejected cigarettes which have been chopped up and rolled out into sheets.

processed stems

These fillers are steamed and pressed tobacco leaf stems that are cut and added to tobacco blends in order to fill out the cigarettes and cut costs.

expanded tobacco

This tobacco is expanded using chemicals, such as, liquid carbon dioxide. This process increases the volume of the tobacco, so less tobacco is necessary to fill the cigarettes.
