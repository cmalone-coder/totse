Re: urgent question about the storage of NC (nitrocellulose)
Well depending on the time the NC has been nitrating, and the proportion of hno3 and h2so4; the NC can be nitrated to diferent degrees. There are 8 spots that can be nitrated, so NC can be as high as octanitrate or as low as mononitrate. Low nitrated NC has been used in clothing, mid range as smokeless powder and highly nitrated NC is an HE. When you get over 3 or 4 nitro groups on the NC it become less stable.

And well if something does happen, glass makes for a bad thing...
