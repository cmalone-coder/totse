Re: Purifying Automotive N2O
Yes, Baking soda NaHCO3 can act both as an Acid or as a Base. In the presence of strongly dissociated acids (eg. high [h3o+]) it will accept the proton and become H2CO3, whilst in basic solutions (low [h3o+]) it will act as the proton donor and become NaCO3 or more generically MCO3 or N2CO3 where m is any metal ion with a +2 charge and N is any metal ion with a +1 charge

A nice easy test to prove that is simply to mix baking soda or powder with vinegar or another household acid. in a sealed container you will see an increase in pressure of the system as CO2(g) is released

or in the typical science project form, the volcano "flows" out in the bubbly mixture
