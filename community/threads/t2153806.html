Re: I want to move up in Job AS FAST AS POSSIBLe
Ok.

Get your forklift certification, then get your First Aid certification, then sign up to take a course on workplace safety and get your credentials in it. Get involved in the union, if there is one. Getting your trucking license isn't a bad idea. Also, become familiar with the systems that the organization have in place.

Build your resume, apply for internal job postings. Make sure your bosses know that you want to move up within the company; demonstrate competence.

Don't hesitate to bring a dozen donuts to work every so often. If you're going to grab a coffee, ask if anybody wants one.

Good luck.
