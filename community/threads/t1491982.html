To figure out where you stand, is the point. This soul-searching takes TIME, though, man, and time single. I know you might be lonely and might be wanting someone again, but try to refrain: Just continue your life, and all the knowledge and wisdom out there, in whatever form (a song that makes you reflect on what went wrong and figure something out, one of those "just because" hindsight glimpses that makes you say "damn, what was I doing?", etc) will come to you.
