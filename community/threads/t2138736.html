Places To Stay / Accomodation

Hospitality Club (http://www.hospitalityclub.org/)
an excellent resource for free accommodation all over the planet with everyone from students to grandmothers. takes several weeks to register, but this place is invaluable. [Captain Asshat]

Couch Surfing (http://www.couchsurfing.com/)
a very similar website to the one above, though it's had some issues with website administration and is having to rebuild it's user list. [Captain Asshat]

Helpx (http://www.helpx.net/)
probably the best resource i know of, and one that is highly important to my style of travel. here, you pay a small fee for access to farms all over the world that will give you room/board in return for some manual labor. [Captain Asshat]

Wwoof (http://www.wwoof.org/)
the original inspiration for the above website. more people use this, but it's more expensive and you have to pay for a listing of farms for each country. [Captain Asshat]


Hostels:
Hostel Bookers (http://www.hostelbookers.com/)
Hostel World (http://www.hostelworld.com/)
Hi Hostels (http://www.hihostels.com/)

How To Get Around

Car Rental
Avis (http://www.avis.co.uk/)
Hertz (http://www.hertz.co.uk/)


Rail
National Rail (http://www.nationalrail.co.uk/) - For UK train maps, times and fares.
Amtrak (http://www.amtrak.com/servlet/ContentServer?pagename=Amtrak/HomePage) For US train maps, times and fares. [nshanin]
Oyster Cards (https://oyster.tfl.gov.uk/oyster/entry.do) - To travel around the London Underground more easily and cheaper.
Eurostar (http://www.eurostar.com/) - For inter-european rail travel
Trans-Siberian Railway Info (http://www.seat61.com/Trans-Siberian.htm)

Buses/Coaches
Greyhound (http://www.greyhound.com/home/) (US)
National Express (http://www.nationalexpress.com/) (UK)

Cheap North American Airlines
Travelocity (http://www.travelocity.com/)
Orbitz (http://www.orbitz.com/)
Priceline (http://www.priceline.com/)
Airtreks (http://www.airtreks.com/)
Sta Travel (http://www.statravel.com/) Cheap domestic/international deals for US students

Hotwire (http://www.hotwire.com/)
Great if you can be real flexible about your flight times, and have no real hotel preferences. If you can fly on a red-eye, you'll save beaucoup $$$ [AngryFemme]

http://www.flysunwing.com/
This is a Canadian travel agency that offers flights across Canada and to sun destinations. They have the option of payment plans, so it can be a useful agency. [Euda]


Cheap Inter-European Airlines
Ryanair (http://www.ryanair.com/) - The cheapest European airline company.
Easyjet (http://www.easyjet.com/)
Aer Lingus (http://www.aerlingus.com/)
Wizz Air (http://wizzair.com/default.asp?slid=clear&language=EN) - Good for UK - Poland flights.


International Airlines
Farecast (http://www.farecast.com) A 'smart' flight comparer which tries to predict future flight prices.
Travel Supermarket (http://www.travelsupermarket.com) (For UK)
Fly Zoom (http://www.flyzoom.com) (For USA, Canada)
Flight Centre (http://www.flightcentre.ca/) (For Canada)

a fairly interesting website dealing with people who travel by any means necessary, legal or otherwise. [Captain Asshat]
