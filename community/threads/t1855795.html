Terribly sorry to hear about your accident... Hope that you are feeling better soon. Here's SWIM's setup if interested.

Elevated 5 Gallon Gas can connecting to about 8 foot of 1 inch hose in U to elevated drying tank (hose in the bottom of drying mixture of choice with screens to avoid clogging) Hose then running up and over then down precooler and thru rubber stopper into Liquid Nitrogen Condenser.

---[Pre-Cooler]Dry Ice packed PVC pipe with coiled tubing---

---[Condenser]Liquid Nitrogen Condenser consists of good quality 3 layer SS 1 quart thermos. (Readily available at your local wally world) By drilling hole into outer layer of SS at the top of thermos, Liquid N is poured into surrounding second (inner) layer of thermos but is still insulated by outer (foam) layer to minimize evaporation.---

Annie condenses on central container of super cooled SS within 1 quart (1.1 litre) Thermos. Handy for toating around and because of the nitrogen, Annie tends to stay where she belongs.

The nitrogen cuts condensing time dramatically.

As always, I do not recommend this type of storage for any kind of long haul or transport between locations. Also all parts except for the thermos need replaced on a regular basis. Tight seals and paranoia are our friends.

One Side note.... DO NOT!!! try to seal the hole where the Nitrogen is stored within the make shift condensor. You will need to refill spuratically while watching your reaction vessel (gas can). A bit of Electrical or Duc-Tape with a pin whole will work wonderfully. The great thing about this setup is that the nitrogen is so much colder and that with the proximity of the (core) of the thermas and the second layer minimize the Use of your liquid N.

The reaction produces water, heat (about 180+) and annie. a drier is not necessary. i have tremendous quality without the drying tube. but use 3/8 x 1/4 inch vinyl tubing. the smaller dia. gives the gas longer to cool. be generous with the lenght between the generator and condenser. i use 6 ft (U shape)between the 2liter generator and the condenser. empty the water from the bottom of the U each time you reload.
