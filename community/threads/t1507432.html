quote:Originally posted by Demoiselle:

I thought that the period you got while on birth control was like... fake, or something. I didn't know it could be missed unless you were pregnant.

A period happens because every month your uterus prepares itself to care for a child, should you get pregnant, thickening the uteran walls. If the egg for that month is unfertilized, the extra lining will leave your body, along with the unfertilized egg (which is why you bleed).

It still prepares the same way when you're on the pill, but (and this is where I get a little confused on the details, so sorry if I mess them up) the pill provides your body with extra estrogen which doesn't allow the egg to be fertilized. Feel free to correct me if I'm wrong with this exactly.

My point is, the period happens the same way, and for the same reason (the egg wasn't fertilized). When you skip a period on the pill, it could be from stress, just like skipping a period from stress when you aren't on the pill. It's still a real period, though.

If you really are stressed, I wouldn't worry about it too much. Take a test, just to be sure, but don't stress yourself out or it probably will take longer.
