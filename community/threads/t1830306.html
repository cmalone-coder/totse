Re: Flash Powder help?
United nulcear dosen't have info on that subject nomore so I'll go from my own memory.

Weight out the amount of aluminium powder and potassium perchlorate, the ratio should be 7 grams of potassium perchlorate per 3 grams of aluminium, A scale, tiple beam balance is a must for every pyro or chemist.

Dump the aluminium powder into a plastic zip lock bag.

Spary static guard in the bag.

Dump in 7 grams of potassium perchlorate. Zip the bag.

Now mix well.

When finished the flash is ready to be used.

Note for best effects you need a high mesh aluminium powder like around 600mesh german dark.
