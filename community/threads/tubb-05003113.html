There are two different stages to a salvia experience: one pre-breakthrough and the breakthrough.

The pre-breakthrough is a very trippy heavy stoned feeling where one is still in contact with reality. The world may appear in 2D, you may get the salvia "pull" in certain direction, you may be very confused but you are still able to move around, use your senses and hold somewhat of a conversation with someone.

The breakthrough experience is something enitirely different. It happens in just a few seconds after the critical mass of salvinorin enters the brain. This is like a hammer to the back of the head.... POW!blackout from ego and reality as you know it. You may not have any idea who you are, where you are, or if in fact you are even a human being. You have little or no contact with the physical world (you should be restrained by a sitter at this point as you could do damage to your body or your environment). You may see the "zipper"... a multidimensional winding tear in the fabric of space-time. You may see or feel that life as you once thought of it has ended or that life as you knew it was just a "sick joke". This all may be very frightening like a very real nightmare but upon coming back to reality you are comforted by the fact that it was all just a drug you took.

200mg of standarized 10x salvia was on the order of 3 times more powerful than 12mg's of 5-MeO-DMT for me....by far the strongest hallucinogen I've every consumed.
