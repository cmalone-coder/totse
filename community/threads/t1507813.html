quote:Originally posted by lauren7249:

he sounds like he's playing games with you. wtf. stop focusing on him so much or you'll go crazy.

Not so fast, Lauren...I am not disagreeing entirely with what you are saying but there are a couple other options we haven't weighed up.

First, he could still be nervous.

I remember when I was a lad, I would get all jittery, sweaty and nervous when I found out a girl I like had something for me too.

I had been told many times that she liked me by her friends and that I should ask.

I would then be told by the girl herself that she liked me and I still wouldn't ask for a while.

It can be kinda daunting.

Second, he could have just lost interest...I don't say that to be a cruel bastard but now knowing that you like him means there's not much "thrill of the chase".

My advice would be to ask him if he's still interested, that way you can eliminate or verify Lauren's suggestion or my second suggestion.

If both are eliminated, it would point to the first suggestion I made in which case you say something like "Look...If you like me then just ask...It's annoying liking you and getting no lovin'!"

Or something along those lines!

All the best.
