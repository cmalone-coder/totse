Re: Rocket engine flash powder?
According to PFP, Estes class C model rocket is

Potassium Nitrate : 71.79
Sulphur : 13.45
Charcoal, 150 mesh : 13.81
Dextrin : 0.95

So yes, its a modified black powder composition. If you knew much about flash, you would know that it wouldn't be much good as a rocket propellant, and really couldn't be safely pressed into rockets anyway.
