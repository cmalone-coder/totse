Re: improviseable impact sensitive explosives
Alan, Ammonium nitrate is insensitive to detonation by impact, Better lay in some factory made primers or percussion caps if your'e concerned about long term survival in case of anarchy or whatever. Mercury fulminate with potassium chlorate added can be used to reload primers, But the case can only be used once if ths is done, The byproducts of combustion weaken the brass.
I've seen strike anywhere match tips used to reload primers,But I don't know how reliable the primers would be.
Calcium nitrate can be treated with ammonium sulfate, Giving ammonium nitrate and calcium sulfate as a byproduct.
As calcium nitrate is found in nature, It is a universally available material, And reaction with sodium or potasium carbonates will give sodium or potassium nitrate, Respectively.
Hope this helps, CrAzEdByTcH

Urea nitrate won't make a primer, Alan, But I have some formulas for primers,you can get an idea from them what makes a good primer mixture. (For reference only, I've never made primers, And have no other information or instrutons.) #1 Mercury fulminate 40%
Antimony sulfide 20%
Barium nitrate 10%
Pb TNR 30%
Pb TNR is lead trinitroresorcinolate.

#2 Pb azide 5 parts
Cu Silicide 10 parts
BaNo3 15 parts
Tetrazine 15 parts
This one contains Lead Azide, And must be kept from contact with copper or brass. REACTIVITY HAZARD

#3 Mercury fulminate 90#
Potassium chlorate 10#

All of the above are too sensitive for any amateur to attempt self production, And are provided as study materials only.

You can see what you're up against, Alan, I've never even heard of some of the required chemicals, and the actual prouction methods are trade secrets.
