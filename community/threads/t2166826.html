Re: Interview tips
Interview tips:

Ask questions.
Talk as much as the interviewer, if not more.
Sell yourself to the interviewer.
If there's more than one interviewer, talk to all of them. Don't simply pick one. They'll be discussing you later.
Research the organization ahead of time.
Bring a notepad with you.
Be aware of the types of questions that you could be asked and come up with answers ahead of time.
Dress business formal/business casual.
Eye contact.
Handshake.
Stand when necessary.
Be polite.

Don't be afraid to ask for feedback.

http://www.cnn.com/2005/US/Careers/0...ask/index.html
