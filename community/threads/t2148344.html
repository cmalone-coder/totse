Okay, I'll give a brief trip report and then rephrase my question.

The entire day I was eating xanax, a few here, a few there (the .25mgs), I probably worked up to a total of around 3.5mg over the course of a good 6 hours.

Later at a friends house I was convinced to smoke weed for the second time, thus meaning no tolerance. The weed was pretty high quality, some of the better stuff that is around here.

I took 3-5 hits off a bong, as did everyone else, and then we were sitting there just talking for a while when it hit. I felt completely disassociated from my body, and was more of an observer in the entire situation. It was difficult to move and talk, not because I couldn't do it physically, I just couldn't make the mental connection. Talking was especially difficult. I was trying to decide why I was having such a different reaction than everyone else. There was another first time smoker who was having the normal experience, euphoric, social, all those nice theres. And here I was way out in left field.

When I would close my eyes there were amazing amounts of closed eye visuals. Little rows of pyramids, as if viewed from the top, forming another pyramid which eventually converged into a tunnel, as the little ones were doing. Basically I was traveling down a tunnel made of other little tunnels.

I layed down on a couch with a blanket over my head and this is the hardest part to explain.

At the end of my tunnel was existence, what the universe is, maybe the afterlife, or a second dimension , who knows.. not me. Its very hard to explain because the sense of physical space was gone completely, everything was composed of knowledge. There were thousands of lines arranged in geometric patterns in glowing blue light, but I didn't see them with my eyes, I simply knew they were there. I could call them forth to look at them, examine them and experience what they had to offer visually, but mostly it was just a knowledge.

This place was so farmiliar. A profound sense of dejavu hit me. I had been there, I had done this, I was completely comfortable in that dreaming world, I had been here forever, and I would return once again, and everyone is okay, was okay, and will always be okay. Everything will simply be.

I was alerted to the fact that I was alive by my heart beat and my breathing. I wasn't sure what the thumping sound was or the wooshing of my breath, only that it didnt belong. I had a choice to make, or so it felt. I felt like I was being born born, agian with that sense of dejavu. I KNOW that I was being born. The faint beating of my heart alerting me that It was time to come forth, the sense of tumbling into my senses, everything.

Durring my tumble (a difficult word to use, yet the only one that fits what I was doing. The sense of space was as impossible to describe to a human, as 3d would be to a stickman) I saw everything. Every persons life, every outcome, yet I didnt SEE it, I knew it. Its impossible to describe it any other way, I just had the vague knowledge of everything that has ever happened. I felt as if I wanted, I could choose to call up and being, and explore that, or I could examine any memory anyone had, or live the current ones with them. By everything, I mean everything. I saw how the dimension of time works, and its as impossible to explain as to give you a location of where I was.

I then woke up and was actually able to work most of my body again (talking and walking and such)



Now, from that long winded experience, I have a few "religious" comments, and a few scientific questions.

1) I feel I was in the place you go after you die. I know that I was there, or at least something very similar, or very close. It was not a negative thing to die, it was just a thing, and being a thing, being in existence, essentially made it a positive thing.

2) I am almost certain I experienced what it is like to be born. The sense of dejavu of the entire thing was so complete, that I KNEW I had been there, it was too familiar. It was as if I knew things I never had the opportunity to learn.

3) I have had a glimpse of how time functions, how distance means nothing, it is all just.. there.

Now on the scientific stuff.

Why the FUCK was it that intense? I smoked the same stuff as everyone else, they had not nearly as strong as a.. trip, it was a trip. I've never heard of someone having that intense of a time with pot. I've done dmt/acid multiple times in pretty high doses and the hallucinations were defiantly as strong as those two drugs.

The only thing I can think of for this is a) the benzos contributed b) I'm more "in tune" with the other planes of life than those around me were


and the last question

I know there is little death from either weed, or benzos by themselves. But, It is pretty common knowledge that when downers are combined the chance of death is increased. I was REALLY gone, completly disconnected from my body, and i wouldnt have even known if I died. I'm wondering is there any actual physical danager from combining small to moderate doses of a benzo with large amounts of weed.
