rlpinca

Re: bullet proof vest?
A flak jacket is used to reduce the impact of fragmentation off of grenades,mines,etc. It might slow down a bullet, but not enough to get optimistic about. Flak jackets are cheap heavy and retain alot of heat so they pretty much suck. All they are really good for is to provide marginal protection against relatively slow big chunks of steel. Bullet proof vests are designed to actually stop the bullet from penetrating.
