Drying your weed and rehydrating it with various scented herbs works great. We did a 1/4 lb once to see if we could create an upscale market for fragrant weed, we used orange peels, rose petals, and a vanilla bean. The resulting herb smelled marvelous, and the smell carried over somewhat to the smoke. Nobody wanted to pay any more for it though so we kept it around as house smoke and a treat for guests. After 2 months in the mason jar it had cured a bit more and developed a smell that was out of this world, I would have liked to have made it into a "pothead perfume"
