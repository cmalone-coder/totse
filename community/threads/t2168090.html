$20's good for 50mgs, provided it's not cut...

2C-E has a very heavy body load for me and many of the people I know who have done it. Maybe it was an impurity in the batch we got, but I doubt it. Be ready to puke your first time out. 2C-E can be described as not having a euphoric push the way LSD does, it's more neutral or even dysphoric. This can lead to a bad trip in combination with how strange your body feels and the fact that you're puking your guts up. But in my opinion 2C-E is a raw psychedelic, no sugar coating like 2C-B or 2C-I, just straight mind fucking psychedlia. At common doses I find 2C-E more introspective and ego stripping than LSD or any of the halide 2C-Xs, and has visuals comparable to those of LSD.

If you're shit isn't cut you'd be stupid to do 25mgs your first time out even if you were a pretty solid acid tripper, 2C-E is mind fucking and the dosage scale is not linear, meaning that response curve rises quickly once you pass the 10mg mark.

Do yourself a favor, don't blow it...
