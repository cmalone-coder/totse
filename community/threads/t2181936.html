Re: So realistically
Zoloft is your biggest problem, actually, not benzo's. At ~2mg/day of benzo, you might get anxty, maybe some minor withdrawl effects, but nothing too bad that won't go away in a day or two. Zoloft, OTOH, discontinuation, especially on such a high dose, will cause SSRI DISCONTINUATION SYNDROME. Look that one up. Long story short, you're likely to get crazy/moodswingy/reckless/suicidal. Not stuff you want to happen if you're trippin' on 'cid or withdrawing from benzos. My condolences. There's a reason I religiously opposed to SSRIs.

Re: So realistically
Even if you stop taking the SSRI now your LSD experience is still going to be dulled. Trust me, I speak from experience; I've taken month-long breaks from SSRIs in the past in order to have a proper LSD experience and every single time the trip was very lackluster, even when the acid in question was of high quality and the other people with me who ate it too had an incredible time.

Benzos won't really dull that much of a trip, in my opinion. If anything, I think a low, therapeutic dose actually provides for a more enjoyable trip if you're someone of a high-strung disposition. Other times when I wasn't on SSRIs but I was on benzos and ate acid then, I would eat a smallish benzo dose a bit beforehand and still have a great time.

Also, how long have you been taking the Klonopin, and when you say ".75 to 2 mg", is that in a dose, or in a day? Me being unsure whether you're taking .75 mg of Klonopin a day with one dose or 4 mg over two doses makes it somewhat difficult for me to form a helpful opinion as far as withdrawal symptoms go.
