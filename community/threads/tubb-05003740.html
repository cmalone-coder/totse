Re: obtaining whippets
If you buy them at a head shop [or porno, I'd imagine] you'll be paying for a recreational substance.

At a culinary shop or restaurant supply [or grocery, if you're lucky enough], you are buying a baking supply.

The difference?

About 3 to times the price.

When I used to buy them [I won't say when or how long ago: I'm tired of reminding myself of my age ;-) ] I could go to a head shop and pay about $12.00 for a box of 10. Or I could go to COnsumers [a dept. store] and pay about $4.25 for the same box.


Before there were breakers we used to use an old seltzer [sp?] bottle.

They used CO2 cartidges, which were the same size as whippets, and fill balloons that way.

I also had this one great pipe I made. It was a 2L sidearm filter flask - a flask with a place to attach a tube on the side - to which I had about 1 to 1 1/2 feet of surgical tubing with a mouth piece taken from a tobacco pipe.

I used a 2-hole stopper; the first hole had the metal tube going down to the bottom of the flask [ala all water pipes of the type].

The other hole was used as a carb.

But; I also go one of those pocket welders from Radio Smack. They use gas cartridges that are also the same size at whippets.

I ran a tube from the pocket welder to the carb hole.

Thus, while smoking, we could add a charge of NO2.**

That pipe was too cool: We called it 'the Gas Chamber'. We even made a commercial on video for it [as sold by Dr. Bong]. Ah, if only I had a way to share it here, I'm sure most would get a kick out of it].


**: In all fairness, I must confess: the idea for the NO2 came from a pipe that used to be sold through High Times - before the California anti-paraphernalia laws of the mid 70's - called 'the Buzz Bomb'.
