quote:Is matchbox RP contain only 1/3 the P5 of lab grade RP? (I make this conjecture from Jam's statement that you should triple the amount of RP if using matchbox RP in a cook)Correct. If using labgrade the uniform ratio is when compared to E-1/3rd:1[rp/E] Thus the ratio required for matchbook RP is three-times more as it is one-part-RP-to-one-part-E Now in the event that you mange to be refine with a total sense of eficacy then you can drop back down to a 1/3rd-to-1-ratio, but this is only so if you are doing a reduction of which the pseduo net-weight is one ounce(28-grams) or more.

If it's a say little 14-gram'er, then you can drop down to a 1/2-to-1 ratio.

Now before you get misunderstood here!! This pertains to an utmost clean and refined RP!! But it can be done man and it really is the extra efforts in the long run.

Those glues gotta be gone andthat clay ihas got to as well as it interferes with the surface area of the RP in that it prevents the total surface area of the RPto make and recyle the HI with the iodine crystals........But clay will run away with water man and RP won't so boil you some water and in a very fine strainer place your cleaned RP(cleaned as in glues are gone---&gt; ) and like your frying french fries at McDonalds, dip the basket of the wore-mesh strainer into the boiling water........let it sit for thirty seconds and then pull her up........the idea here is to sufficiently expose the RP/clay to the hot boiling water and to pull her up and let the clay that got absorbed into the water to leave into the water.

After a ten to twenty minute run depending on your size, turn down the temperature to a slight boil............then no boil.......the clay will be settling to the bottom and not resurfacing onto the RP.

The RP will float if by chance some worked their way through but do yourself a favor and buy a super-fine mesh screen.

And don't start refining until you've got ten grams or more in gross weight!!
