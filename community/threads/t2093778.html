Heres the official site (http://www.globalmarijuanamarch.org/) and the wiki (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Marijuana_March).

This year the Global Marijuana March will take place on May 3rd. You have until then to figure out where you want to go, make a sign, and think up kick-ass slogans and mantras to chant while you march through the streets of your city.

http://calgary420.ca/rallies/gmm/2008/poster/wwmm_calgary.jpg

I'll be at the Calgary march. The more people, the bigger our voice is, so take the time to take part in the cause we all believe in. Have fun, be loud and proud, and remember that joints are easier to conceal and ditch then a gas mask.
