Re: Easiest to synthesize
GBL to GHB,
DMT/mescaline A/B (not a synthesis),
p-aminobenzoic acid to benzocaine,
or perhaps chlorobutanol or chloral hydrate.

I, of course, do not recommend or condone the synthesis or extraction of illegal drugs.

And questions like these would be better suited to the bullshit thread, I imagine threads like these will be locked. We don't spoonfeed here, and the information you want could be easily found with about 10 minutes and google. Read the FAQ and the archive thread, and if I could give you some advice, it would be that trying to synthesise a drug as a starting practice is foolish, especially if a product is going to be going in someone's mouth. Start with simple reactions, procedures; distillation, nitrations, refluxing, extractions...
