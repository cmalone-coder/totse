This is where you can sign up to be a moderator for Spurious Generalities. We like to rotate moderators in and out of this forum because it is so high traffic - moderators tend to "burn out" or moderate poorly after a while.

In order to be a moderator of Spurious you must post in this thread. It follows that you must be a moderator before signing up to moderate spurious. If you do not post in this thread, there will be no record of when you submitted your name, when you are to be added, and when you are to be removed.

We will try to keep at LEAST one "experienced SG mod" in the forum at all times (someone who has been through more than 1 rotation or more than 3 months in the forum). This is because Spurious is not like any other forum on totse. Moderating this forum takes a lot of tolerance, patience, and skill in order to keep the forum clean while everyone is still happy. Having an experienced moderator to show you the ropes is an invaluable asset.

To start out (we may end up changing this) terms will last for 2 months. A rotation of two moderators will be done every month (if the situation permits: one experienced and one new). For the most part, things will work on a first come first serve basis. It is, however, possible that you do not get to moderate the forum until the next month because we were unable to complete a quota of at least one experienced mod in the forum at all times. After we have enough people who are experienced at moderating SG, we won't have that problem.

If you must leave in the middle of your term, let me know and I'll see to it that we get someone to fill in the rest of your term. We can work out the details about that when it occurs.

In order to keep this from being a mad dash to sign up first, I'm going to take all the names that were submitted in the first few days of this thread and randomize them. This way, if you happened to read this thread late, you still have a chance at being in the first batch of SG mods.

Sound good?
