A few questions about smoking weed...
#1. What do you usually smoke out of?

#2. If you or a friend made whatever you smoke out of, how is it made?

#3. What's your favorite way to smoke weed?

#4. What's the most inventive way to smoke weed you've ever tried?

I'll answer my own questions, I usually smoke out of a bong made out of a 1.75 liter vodka bottle, with a stainless steel bowl I turned on a lathe that goes down through the cap of the bottle, and is attached to a stiff plastic tube that goes down to under the water level, and a plastic tube coming out the side near the top. My favorite way to smoke weed would probably be frosty mugs, and the most inventive way to smoke weed i've ever tried would probably be a setup where we had 2 glass jars, one of which had a plastic lid with one of those holes with a little pop-up cover, and a hole with a piece of plastic tubing in another hole drilled through it. This glass jar was chilled in the freezer, and the tube went to the second jar, which had a little water in the bottom. The second jar had a metal lid, with a soldering iron and two plastic tubes sealed into it. One came from the first jar, and went below the water, and the second was near the top and you took your hit from. The soldering iron heated up a little aluminum foil thing with weed on it, acting like a vaporiser. How it worked was like this: You blow a frosty-mug hit into the first jar through the hole in the lid, then you suck that through the second one, which passes the smoke through water and then past the makeshift vaporiser.
