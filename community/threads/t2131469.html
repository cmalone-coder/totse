Well most recently, when taking crystal MDMA, I was getting some real wobbles and mild visuals - open and closed eyed. I saw this "dotted line" bike man riding through the air towards me and my friends. I blinked and rubbed my eyes and of course I knew it wasn't "real", but hey, didn't change the fact my visual senses were very affected.

I could also barely read my digital watch, it just became a blur, like it was "too close" to my face or something, with the numbering just spilling out of the watch. Pretty crazy. That happens to me quite often, with MDMA, though.... plus closed eye visuals (which i can't remember so well) when falling asleep. I guess that's the MDA it gets converted into when you're nice n "saturated". I'm a skinny guy, and I guess I must have done about 180-250mg, and sure as shit were a lot of my senses affected..... touch, of course, duh... ;) :D

Basically though, psychedelics can affect any of your senses. It depends on the chemical, the situation, the dose and the person. I hadn't done MDMA for months, and i'd been getting loads of sun, a good diet and lots of exercise.... lots of serotonin does some crazy shenanigans.
