I don't know why I haven't posted this before it's been on my mind for a while now. I'm 18 and I have a little brother who is 13. I started getting into drugs around that age and that's exactly what he's doing. The problem is I don't want him to go down the same path as me. But I have a feeling that's exactly what's going to happen. It's like watching myself grow up it's so weird I just want to tell him shit like "don't do that or you'll end up like me" but it's just not that simple.

I was arrested when I was 14 for weed and put on 10 months of probation. By 15 I was in rehab. Then earlier this year I went to jail for drug charges, and now I'm on house arrest with a year of probation ahead of me. Both sides of my family have horrible horrible genes when it comes to addiction and drug/alcohol use.

This kid looks up to me and he's always in complete awe of the things I've done whether they're bad or good. But at this point I feel like it's too late to get him off this destructive life path. I try to keep a level of trust between me and him so that he'll at least tell me about things he gets into and his problems and shit like that. So last week he came home with a bag of weed and I made him smoke a couple bowls with me. But now I feel like shit because I feel like I just confirmed for him that this path is the right way to go.

In no way am I trying to say that weed is a gateway drug for everyone and that it isn't fun and all that stupid bullshit because I love marijuana it's been there by my side for a long time now and I will always smoke. But I feel like since I kind of "cheered him on" in a way by smoking with him this early in his life he's gonna do the same stupid shit I did. I care about the little fucker because we've been through a lot together it's not just your normal older brother/little brother relationship.

What can I do to steer him in the right direction? I know some of you will just say "tell him to quit smoking weed" and "don't do it with him anymore" but I already know this so if you have nothing better to say than that then don't bother posting. I need to find a way to stay on his side AND steer him in the right direction at the same time. A big part of the problem is that all his friends smoke weed and are the typical middle schooler little shitheads we all hate so that's why I think it's gonna be harder than just telling him to stop.
