If he did fuck the guy up right there, even if the boss understood and didn't want to fire him, he may not have had a choice. And if the guy's wife doesn't work there, since there was no sexual harassment/assault in the workplace, the asshole would keep his job.

Ok.. look.. if you need to ask what the best thing to do in a street fight is, it doesn't sound like you know much about fighting at all. If you did, you'd know. I totally understand sticking up for your wife, but this is no longer a passionate fit of anger that's leading you to beat his ass; it's a planned assault, and it would be wise to remember that.

Does this guy have any fighting ability? If not, is he physically more capable than you? If either one is yes, you may want to re-think taking the fucker head-on. When dealing with a planned attack, especially in vengeance, you absolutely can NOT lose, or else he will have power over you until such time as you do get your revenge.

Ask yourself what's more important to you- temporarily seeing him at your mercy, or getting the last laugh? If it's the former, there are a lot of things you could do- jump him, knife him, physically harm him in some other way- that would serve your purpose well. All of them, however, have drawbacks; the one that they all share is that, short of him dying, he'll heal. Assholes have a tendency to feel that your revenge is an uncalled for attack, and seek their own revenge- you then wind up with a Hatfield/McCoy situation. Not so good.

As for a long-term "victory".. all I can recommend is patience. Take a martial art. Become his superior. Outwardly, it will appear as though you're just taking it; inwardly, you'll know the truth. Don't study anything with the purpose of revenge in mind, though, or else you'll miss the true purpose of the art. Take it to be able to protect yourself and her from any future confrontations. If the opportunity arises for you to even the score with your co-worker, so much the better.

Most importantly, talk to her, speak honestly, and get her to speak honestly. If you go to her and say you think it should be forgotten for now, but you're going to learn to fight, she may get the feeling that you're wimping out. Mentally, she very well may know you're not, but deep down she could doubt. There's no easy way around that.. you simply need to be careful with your words.

Ask her what she wants, and demand the full truth. If she wants that guy's head on your dinner table, she has to tell you that. When you both know where you stand, then you can make the choice that's right for both of you with regards to what to do about this.
