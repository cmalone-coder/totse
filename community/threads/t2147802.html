Re: grinded up weed
A grinder is worth it if you want to conceal the smell. I have a grinder that's stainless steel with a nice screen which I can collect trichomes with and separate them from the green plant matter. I think it's called a pollenator or something.

If you use a grinder like this the trichomes don't really have any smoke or smell when you smoke them, so it's a good way to get high stealthily. But be sure to keep a layer of white ash or something on the bottom so the trichomes don't just get sucked through the screen.. You can press them to make hash too, either with your fingers or with something else, and then they can't get sucked through the screen.

I don't know about a homemade grinder, but I wouldn't use my grinder if I'm just smoking out of my pipe. Fingers work good. You should buy a 10$ microscope, from a radioshack or some place like that. So you can see the trichomes.

Re: grinded up weed
Yes, grinders are worth it even to the occasional smoker. They're small, easy to conceal, and often overlooked by people looking through your room.

The real reason you need one is that it increases the surface area of the weed if you're using a bong. It makes individual hits more potent, and shortens smoking time.

They're also essential to rolling good joints, otherwise your shit will be all lumpy.
