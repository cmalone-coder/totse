Re: need rp help
Nah, its just an old toothbrush, melt the neck with a lighter first and bend it to a 45 deg angle, slip some gardenhose onto the handle.

The wire brush will dig out bits of cardboard from the box. so don't use.

Don't soak the match books.

You want to be able to put the match book ( cut the backs off it first, so you have two brown strips, one on each side of the frount panel.) into a small 5inch dish with 100ml acetone, and scrub from the frount panel forward and down to get the MBRP off into the acetone.

After a while of scrubbing Mbooks the acetone will 'fill up' with MBRP, you will see grains of MBRP still on the strips.

Pour loaded acetone into a glass jar, and start up again with some more clean acetone.

Towards the finish you can use the acetone that the MBRP has settled out of.

Chuck the cardboard and matches into a garbage bag, dispose of it properly, if you set fire to it, start from the top, or all the heads will burn ,making lots of stinky smoke, then the wood will go charcoal and burn badly. Take into account (if you put it in the rubbish,) that someone is garanteed to throw a lit cig butt in on it.

To filter, get a funnel, a grommit (i love that word) that fits onto the funnel neck airtight,a pasta sauce jar and lid.

Cut a hole in the lid to fit the grommit ( be bloody (sic) carefull its sharp.

Seal round it with silicon sealant.

Put the funnel into the grommit for a airtight seal.

Heat up the jar by putting it into near boiling water, slowly, so it don't break.

Oh put 10ml acetone in jar.

Stuff a chunk of cotton wool into the funnel neck, real tight, push it in with a spoon handle, it'll still work and best tight.

Take the jar out and put it on a wooden cutting board or such, so it don't break.

Fit cap and funnel.

Pour acetone/MBRP mix into funnel.

The cooling jar will suck the acetone thru the filter, leaving MBRP in funnel.

If the drips get down to 1 per 8 seconds, chuck acetone into other container and carry on as again, evap acetone to see the paint if you want, its 50/50 MBRP/paint.

Put MBRP onto a sheet of ali foil and let it dry (it not very wet as the vac pulls out moisture too).

Do the whole thing again but with IPA.

You will find a baby blue colored glue?

Ali foil tray again, dry.

Now put MBRP into a glass jar and cover it well with HCL acid.

Let it react with the cardboard in the MBRP. When its finished, add 5X water, let settle, pour off water.

Funnel again, with say,5X water.

Remove and dry on foil again, crush with a plastic something. Not metal it can ignite!

Can't remember the box/MBRP ratio now.

Its still got glass in it, so its called MBRP not RP.

MBRP= MatchBox Red Phosphorous

IPA = IsoPropal Alcohol

HCL = HydroChLoric acid = Muriatic acid

Re: need rp help
A couple of years ago I used flares which came from the railroad. Easiest way I found was to go through and remove all of the caps (where the R is) from the flares. Take all of your caps and drop them in a bucket (I guess size depends on how many caps you have) and pour some "tone" on them and work the bucket in a fast circular motion. The caps will spin around in the bucket and in 5 minutes all the "r" is removed. I then filter with a piece of aluminum window screen pouring into a large dish. After you have filtered and the caps are in your filter put them back in the bucket and pour on another splash of "tone" and shake to get any remaining "r" that might have collected in the caps, then filter like before. After you "r" has settled in the pan pour off the tone. You might also save the "tone" poured off as it will continue settling if you pour off too quickly.
