I disagree that there aren't many definitions of love.

I think there are many definitions of love. And some pertain to some relationships in a person's life and others pertain to other relationships.

Like, I don't even think you have to be that serious with a person in order to 'love' them. I think you can see someone on the subway and know that you love them and that you've always loved them and that you will always love them even if you're never going to see them again. I guess, for some people it's a connection that's grown up after many times of contact and I think that, too, can be a form of love. But for me, it's just a strong connection. Almost like if we all had little life strings that had branches that connect at different times with other people's life strings. The connections that are strong are the ones where there's love. I think you can love friends; I think you can love family (although for some cases, I think that's a different form of love, where you love someone because they give you the physical commodities that you need and keep your body alive and sheltered); I think you can love inanimate objects; I think you can love ideas.

The phrase that I hate the most is 'love lasts forever'. Love lasts just as long as everything else does. You die; some people think everything that your life held is over (besides whatever connections you've made and actions you've done that have changed the world); some people think that everything, past, present, and future are all still alive. As one. In that case, love is forever, just as that burp you just made is forever.
