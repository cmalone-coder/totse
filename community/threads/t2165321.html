Re: making conversation comfortable and natural?
Yeah making sustained smalltalk is pretty difficult to do. When you don't know the person/have nothing in common, you either have to change topics every 2 minutes or keep making general inane comments to keep the convo going. My problem isn't shyness, it's just hard to keep up conversation when there's nothing to talk about.

My only advice is to just hang out in groups. You can make comments every now and then, but you don't feel compelled to keep up an entire conversation. Listen in and learn more about the other people, and maybe when you do have to talk to someone 1-on-1 you'll have something to talk about.

Of course, it helps if the other person likes talking and leading the conversation...
