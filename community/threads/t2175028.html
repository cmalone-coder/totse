Re: trying LSD (acid)
i dont know if it will help you have lucid dreams, but it is a wonderful substance....

a good intense acid trip can be one of the most amazing experiences of your life....

Expect for it to alter how you see the world, yourself, and those around you, for many this altered perception of things is considered positive.... Acid changed my entire outlook on life and has made me a happier and more optimistic person.

It is not addictive in any typical kind of way, and you can not die from it... well 1 person did die but he mistook some pure crystal for speed and injected a massive quantity.... probably shooting up more at one time than most of us will see in a lifetime.... I say its not addictive in any typical kind of way because after you trip most people get their fill for a while, there are no withdrawals or even an unpleasant comedown, and tolerance builds so fast you really cant psychically make it an every day habit. its more of some people getting the desire to see just how deep the rabbit hole goes.... their is an insane difference between 1 or 2 hits and a dose in the milligrams...

It is worth a try, i would suggest 2 hits for your first time, with 1 you will probably be left with a feeling of "well that was ok, but i wish i had done more".... The only reason i ever do just 1 is to test the strength for someone, I plan to trip with someone who does not like it to be very intense and i wanted to know if she would be alright with 1, and i think she can handle it... but i will be doing more.

Have some good music, cigarettes if you smoke them, oranges, and be able to wander around outside.... Day or night trips are fun and each have their advantages, during the day there is an insane amount of color to take in and everything looks incredibly vivid, my last day trip i spent a while out on a kayak in a lake, sky had hues of purple, the paddle was leaving trails, the ripples in the water were forming electric looking patterns, and the trees in the distance were a beautiful green...

Night trips are nice because everything outside is peaceful, an insane amount of stars fill the sky, the light and shadows shift and move, and it just provides for a view that looks like something that should be in a painting....

Try to not be around people who would care until your comfortable enough around them... On a 1 or 2 hit acid trip i can be around my family and pass off as sober, as long as they dont see my pupils.... But its not recommended...
