Re: Diet Pills...which one works best? (OTC)
Almost all the diet pills that will actually make you lose weight do so because they contain ephedrine - which does funky things to your heart so you burn more calories.. everything else in the pills (such as caffeine) is usually intended as appetite suppressants etc..

Hydroxycut contains both ephedrine and hydroxy-citric acid, which according to someone I know is one of the more effective drugs, which doesn't hurt in the same way as ephedra..

If you're going to take any of these things, please be aware of the risks you're taking. I would recommend that you do NOT take them for a prolonged period of time, and NEVER take more than the recommended maximum dose. At the most, you should use them as an adjunct to a healthy weightloss program that involves a decent calorie controlled diet (that's what makes you lose weight, restricting the calories you consume.. the actual content of your diet is less important in that regard) and most importantly exercise.. you are never going to have the body you want without exercise..

Use the pills to give you motivation, to help you along, but be careful. Know what you're taking and what the risks are, and if you feel any ill effects (pounding heart, nausea, lack of sleep) CUT BACK.
