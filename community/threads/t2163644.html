The return of the cartridge.
Okay peeps. Maybe this is just wishful thinking on my part, but bear with me here.

Due to the fast evolution in solid state drives in recent years, I can totally see video games being released on cartridges again in the future. Of course, now it would be commercially impossible due to the high price of SSDs. But if their popularity was to rise, their prices would drop substantially. Just look at stuff like CompactFlash cards. A couple of years ago, they were expensive as hell. Now you can get a 2 gig card for less than €15 in retail.

Think of all the good things this could bring. Much faster access rates, smaller media, no installation, instant loading times, no disc scratches, etc. It would also make consoles themselves cheaper and more reliable because they wouldn't need hard drives anymore. It would make copy protection much easier, as it can be integrated into the hardware of the cartridge itself.

More info on SSDs:

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid-state_drive
