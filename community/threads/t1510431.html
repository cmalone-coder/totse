So here's the LD...

I've been with my current gf for about the past 4.5 years. We get along really well, and whatnot, so everything is just peachy....or rather was just peachy. The thing is, it's a long distance relationship, and we've never met. We plan to meet sometime soon ( like in the next 2-3months).

About this time last year, we decided to take a break from each other, to kinda stretch our limbs and whatnot. We've recently gotten back together....but we've decided to not be an 'official couple' untill I actually move to her state next 2-3months). So we basically have agreed that it's okay if we see other people and stuff....and yet we both refuse to.....well for me, that has changed. I think I would like to date other people, if not just for a little while, just to see what it's like. I've never really been on a date before, and I just wanna know.

All this time, I've been so absorbed in school and work, that relationships didn't fit into my schedule. The funny thing is that long distance relationships aren't nearly as time consuming. I'm wondering if the way I'm feeling is wrong....

Recently ( and this was a new experience for me) I've come to know a girl that I think is totally awsome....the thing is that I've never really given the whole relationship/love thing a realy chance ( the long distance thing seems to be more or less of an inferior type to me). So the whole thing of girls noticing me, and going out of their way to flirt, talk to, and even just hang with me, really knocked me off me feet. But there is one....or two girls that I've grown to be fond of.

Herein lies the problem....I like them...really really like them, but I don't know if it's love that has come as a result of my neglecting "the dating scene", or simply love. I pretty much can't successfully analyze or assess the situation, and it's really becoming bothersome. Like today we ( a group from school) went on a trip, and the whole time we were there, this group of two girls have been pretty much just been in my face....I know that they like me quite a bit....like whenever we see each other they start blushing, and can't stop smiling...then they start laughing and talking to me. One of the girls even went as far as to grab my notebook, and write down her number, and the give me one of the longest stares that I've ever experienced....then she walked off...looked back and smiled...then kept walking.

So after all of this I start to wonder....would it be morally/ethically wrong of me to tell my gf that I want to see other people? Even though I don't know if I actually want to date these girls or possibly be with one of them. Also, the fact that there are 2-3 girls that have my interest, leads me to believe that I could just be over-reacting, or just taking it too far.

Sorry for typing up such a long post, but I really need to figure out which path to take. Thanks in advance for any help, and at least reading my post.

----------

And wouldn't it be weird if I tell my gf "hey...I'm going to date, possibly fall in love with, or have sex with someone I've only known a while....but when I'm in your area, I'll be with you"..?
