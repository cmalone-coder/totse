It also creates a need. I thought I felt loneliness before, but now I realized that if I have a relationship, then lose it, the loneliness won't be what I experience now. It will be a hundred times worse. If I have sex, it'll only be a matter of time until the relationship ends. Then I will have to find someone new. It will be a need, a need that I don't currently feel. I know that if I have sex with this girl, I will not only want to do it again, but need to do it again. I'll experience a desire for sex like I've never experienced before, and quite possibly succumb to the irrational pursuit of pussy most males follow.

If I choose to remain celibate, that need will never exist for me. As it stands now, I'm completely independent. I don't need human interaction for any kind of pleasure. It's been almost two months since I've even spent any time with a friend. And I'm fine. I entertain myself well. But if I get a real hunger for sex, for once in my life I will have a need that I cannot satisfy on my own, and that frightens me a great deal.

I don't really know what discussion can come from this. I imagine those of you that are sexual won't understand, but I thought I'd see if anyone here has any idea what I'm talking about.
