Yea that's the same problem i had, and i know a few others who have had the same issue. I don't know why it does that. I was stuck on 800x600 and any other options were just not visible.

For some reason it just doesn't detect the display. However, what you have to do is to add additional options to xorg.conf..

Here a tutorial that i found that solves the problem. It says to copy their entire xorg.conf and replace it with your original.It will work, But don't do that because you'll only put other options out, that you might need.

Just back up your xorg.conf file and within this tutoral just copy the "section monitor" and "section screen" and apply them to your xorg.conf. It works as normal then.

http://tinyurl.com/5uzs54

Someone should probably file a report on it, so when the next release comes around, we wouldn't have to keep doing this.
