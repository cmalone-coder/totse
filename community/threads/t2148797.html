Re: Request me some music
Judging by your love of Pink Floyd, I would suggest Riverside, Eloy and Hawkwind to you. They are all similarly space rock-ish, with Riverside also having touches of Tool and other metal in them. I would highly, highly suggest you get the rest of Rust in Peace by Megadeth as that is the best thrash album ever. You would likely enjoy the first three Meshuggah albums: Contradictions Collapse, Destroy Erase Improve and Chaosphere. Best Buy carries tons of Meshuggah. Get the first four Metallica albums: Kill 'Em All, Ride the Lightning, Master of Puppets and ...And Justice For All. Also, just get all the Porcupine Tree you can.
