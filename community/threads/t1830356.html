quote:Originally posted by Shadow88:

How can you make the foil into powder? I tried putting mine thorugh a mortar+pestle but nothing worked

Cut the aluminum foil into 1 cm squares and ball mill it for two weeks for a powder that can be used in flash (be sure to open the ball mill every 24 hours or so to let it air out). I've also heard that putting the Al foil squares in a blender for a few minutes before ball milling saves a lot of ball milling time.
