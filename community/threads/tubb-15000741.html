Why mankind needs a god.

1) Because man is not the sole arbiter of his destiny. There have always been, and always will be, outside and unyielding forces that help make us what we are. Our gods are our interpretations of these forces.

2) Because societies with gods, or pseudo-gods, have more strength, prosperity, and humanity than societies that dont. (Though to be honest, I can think of not a single society that existed without some kind of deity or superior force/being)

3) The weaker among men check their weaker and baser impulses and cravings out of fear or reverence for a god/pseudo-god. These societies work better than other ones, and starve out or conquer other societies, either with their ideas or by martial conquest. In either case, the older, less successful ideas die.

4) We experience things within ourselves that no amount of rationalising and logic can do justice to. Gods are how we interpret these gifts to humanity.

5) Mankind likes a good story, and there is typicaly nothing as entertaining and fascinating than the supernatural.

Thats a good start. Id like to see what you have to say about those.

Re: im lost... will someone explain why we need a god at all
quote:
Originally posted by coldblueice47:
so why dosent someone tell me about religions and why we need them at all.


Religions were created to fill a strong need humans have. Our very biology instills a desperate need to live and survive. Dying or ceasing to exist makes most of us very uneasy, and goes against all of our instincts. We, unlike the other life on this planet, have the ability to question our existance. I don't believe the other life can even comprehend death and what that means. They don't give it a second thought. We, on the other hand, can and do question death.

Once humans evolved their super brains, they suddenly begin to ponder things that go against their nature and biological desire to survive. In the absense of believable answers that we could accept, we simply created elaborate fictions and trained ourselves not to question them (faith). We humans created an acceptable solution to fill our biological need to survive. It's not important whether or not the solution is true or not. What's important is that it fills that inherent need.

Now, you can begin questioning things again. However, we are no better equipped to answer these death/existance questions than our caveman ancestors were. All you will achieve by questioning your religion is removing that convenient solution to your need to survive.

Someday I believe we will either be able to answer these questions or discover a way to live forever. When that day comes, then we can finally get rid of the whole religion thing. Until then, I'm afraid we're stuck with them.

Of course this is all my personal opinion. Good luck, and let me know when you figure it all out. I'll buy two.
