B&E
By: GoAnis
you will need:
- getaway car
- knife or gun or bbgun ( anything for protection, gun is best)
- rope AND duct tape (incase somebody is inside and you can tie them up)
- backpack ( to put your shit in
- mask ( wear it only when your within 10feet of the enterance as not to arouse passerby suspicion)
- dark clothing
you should case the joint first for 2weeks. start by sitting in a car across the street watching. make sure the windows of the car are tinted and that you sit in the back. take notes of employee leaving/entering times,etc. see if anyone lives on top of the store. its better if nobody does.
early on in the stakeout trip the alarms 2 nights in a row ( make only 2 because the third time they will have someone hide and watch to see who is doing it). this is mainly useless because it all depends on whether a cop is in the area or not. take the lowest of the 2 times and cut that by atleast 45seconds ( if the reaction time is under a minute find a new building to hit up).
go in 1week before doing the B&E, enter the store and ask to speak to the manager ( wear a suit and carry a briefcase with a tape recorder hidden inside it all miced up and recording. talk to him about installing your companies alarms ( make up a speech earlier on and company name,etc). if he says he already has alarms say "which kind?" and whatever type he says just say those types are inferior to your companies product. leave soon but try and take a quick tour around the building. check out the entrance your planning on. check ofr any abnormalities in the ceiling and/or lights ( lights not working,etc). if the door or windows you are entering from has a magnetic alarm cut it and try not to make it noticable.

now go home and listen to the tape you recorded many times. if your lucky he said he didnt have an alarm system or else he hopefully said which type. now that you have checked the building and have some idea where the alarms are you just need to figure out how to defeat them.

Go to totse or the store's alarm manufacturer ( if you were lucky to find out the company and/or model ). find out what these questions:

- what type of alarm is it ( motion, silent,etc)
- in case of power outage, does it have backup power?
- when tripped does it dial the police, the alarm company OR both.
- how does it respond to somebody cutting the power lines?

now these will aid you in the beating of this alarm. if it calls the police or alarm company you must cut the power AS WELL as the phone lines.
If it responds to the cutting of power/phone lines or if it has backup you must either kill the backup somehow or else forget about the place.

Now you basically have what you need.

now on the night of the breakin double check you have everything you need, you have reviewed your notes, have ways to defeat the alarm memorized and still have the balls.
Now when you go to the place take off your backpack, put your mask on mometarily, put your gun in your pocket or whatever and grab your knife. stick your pack behind a nearby dumpster. now go as quickly as possible to the phone/power box and cut that SPECIFIC buisnesses power/phone lines. if you get the whole neighbourhood or something your going to have bell or whatever around pretty soon.

now close up the box as so it appears normal.
go grab your pack and go to the planned entrance. get inside asap and have your (hopefully indiglo) watch set for the in/out time set beforehand. now run like fuck!

grab what you want and try to empty to registers as quickly as possible. try not to make the place look like its been broken in to and try not to make anything look missing (ie; if you grab a brand new notebook computer, replace it with a slightly less noticable,older one). this will give you more time until discovery ( either right when the owner enters or hopefully a hour or maybe a day!).

When you leave make sure to walk on grass ( as not to leave footprints) and dont run.
if you hear a siren and it keeps getting closer drop all stolen goods and run.
now here is the most important part which in time, usually seems to lapse from the more experience burglars mind: DO NOT LEAVE A THING. NEVER EVER FORGET OR SIT ANYTHING YOU BROUGHT DOWN. this is like the camping and beach rule " make sure the place is tidyer than it was when you came". when you get home take everything you took with you ( excluding your bb/pellet gun unless you shot it cause cops could find the bb and trace it to you OR someone saw it with you) and destory it. clothing, backpack, knife ( especially the knife as it was a integral part in disabling the alarms), everything.
Watch the news and read local papers for the next few days to see if your robbery is mentioned and see if there is a suspect mention that fits your description. if so destory every single thing you took and get an alibi.

And it is good to live by " sell as you steal".
