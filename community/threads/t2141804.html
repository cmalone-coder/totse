What do you consider to be excessive drinking?
Not necessarily talking about alcoholism (i.e. compulsive, self-destructive usage and/or physical dependence), but just excessive drinking as far as what's considered acceptable, health effects, and so on. The general consensus among doctors in the US is like 2 drinks a day being the max, but you know they gotta be really conservative about that shit, I mean you know how they are with OTC dosages. Plus they have to give an estimate across the board, taking elderly people and other more vulnerable types into account, not just healthy guys in their 20s like me.

Am I out of line for having, say, 4-6 drinks a night on average, taking breaks occasionally? I rarely drink to get shitfaced or drown my sorrows, just to unwind after work and whatnot. This is also usually beer and I'm a pretty tall guy so it's not like I'm even getting much of a buzz from this, just some relaxation. Once in awhile I do drink a lot more and get stone drunk, but not nearly as often as I did in college.

I'm posting this because my girlfriend thinks I'm a drunk and I feel that she's just a lightweight. On nights I don't drink, I don't have any real withdrawal symptoms, just takes longer to get to sleep and I feel a little bored -- about the same "withdrawal" as quitting weed.

Is it such a crime to drink on a nightly basis on this level? And how long until my liver hates me in a significant way?

Re: What do you consider to be excessive drinking?
Whilst there's evidence that drinking some alcohol makes you less susceptible to risk of stroke/heart attack and possibly some neurological n mental problems. It's really hard to say though, cos people's ability to tolerate alcohol varies. Definitely not drinking doesn't seem to have any significant benefits to health... that's been kinda showed in some studies.

I guess excessive drinking is when it really affects the way you're living your life, your relationships, your finances and day-to-day-decisions, for me. I guess I think you can drink at a level which is "excessive" in that it may harm your health, but you can still be happy, functioning and capable of taking days off. Drinking every single day, day in, day out, isn't something that appeals to me personally, although I guess I do drink a fair bit (majority of days in a week, but with breaks and stuff). It's the way a drink becomes such a "necessary" part of a day which means things are maybe a bit worth thinking over...

It's definitely been shown that getting really really drunk on occasion can be more damaging to your health than moderate regular drinking, plus if when you go out, you really really get drunk, socialising becomes quite linked with alcohol. If someone feels serious social issues if they are sober around others, then that could be "excessive" drinking in some ways, regardless of amount. I don't know really... in some countries in Europe, alcohol as a drink rather than just a way to get drunk is more in the social mindset, considering traditions of drinking alcohol when water wasn't always clean etc. England's a bit of a mix on that one.... as we do get pretty drunk, but some people still view it as only a social thing, with the aim of getting as pissed as possible top priority. Personally I don't like being really drunk, but if I go a week or two without any cider/wine/beer/whatever, I do really miss a drink.. :)
