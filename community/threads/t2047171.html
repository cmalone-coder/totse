Re: Selling your soul to satan
Firstly, you can't sell your soul to satan, at least not permanently.

As long as you are alive it is not too late to repent of your sins and accept Jesus Christ, no matter how many sins you have committed.

Jesus Christ died on the cross so you can have eternal life and be forgiven! God doesn't want to condemn you to hell!

That being said, what an absurd idea. Why would you accept an eternity in hell for a finite amount of pleasure?

---

A common misconception is that satan has a side that can be joined, that you can be his ally. This is not true. Satan has no allies, no friends, his only mission is to destroy you and whoever else he can. Satan has come to kill, steal, and destroy! (John 10:10)

Satan has read the Bible and he knows that in the end he will be destroyed and cast into the lake of fire. Satan is like a bank robber who is surrounded by the police and knows he will not escape with his life, he's not interested in making alliances, he's only trying to take down as many people as he can before he himself is taken down.

Furthermore, Satan is not like some sort of unholy merchant. He's not going to offer you a million dollars for your soul when you've already rejected Jesus Christ and he can have your soul with a $2 porn magazine.

---

Lastly, you might think you can make some sort of arrangement with satan and then repent on your death bed. This might sound like a profitable, albeit evil scheme; however, you never know when you will die.

Perhaps satan will manage to kill you before you have repented? He's not going to give you all sorts of worldly possessions only to be cheated out of your soul on a technicality. Repent & accept Jesus Christ now while you still have a chance!

---

In short, trading your eternal soul for a finite amount of pleasure is the worst bargain in the universe. Satan is a liar, his only goal is to destroy you; however, Jesus Christ came so that if you trust in him you may live!
