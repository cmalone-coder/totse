Viper:

I joined to learn electronics repair. Many of the jobs under that category require clearances of differnt levels. My main job was microwave radio tech(2831 multichannel equipment repairman) which needed secret. I eventually got to work on sat com stuff(they were short handed) which needs a top secret. In the Marines people don't do just the job that they signed up for, there are alot of jobs that don't necessarily have a category(MOS) but still need to get done. For example while I was in I was a radio tech, an operator, a rifle and pistol coach, military operations in urban terrain instructor, the office jobs that I mentioned above, a janitor(all lower ranks are), a baby sitter (E4), platoon sergeant, and alot of other shit. My clearance was primarily just so that I could handle the active crypto gear and in case something was transmitted over my gear that was classified.

You're most likely talking about the Royal Marines, so they might be completely different.

Nox:

The crypto gear for radios is considered a controlled item. They have a little ticker tape looking thing that actually contains the code, that is what requires a clearance to handle. Once the ticker tape looking thingy is loaded into the gear, the gear takes on the classification level of the code.
