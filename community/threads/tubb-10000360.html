The News
The news can be good for us all. You know to know whats coming up in weather, and some information around the nation and in your local area. Then there is the bad side of it, were you hear false information and you dont know whats true or not. Ive also seen stuff that is true but they over exagerate it. The news will try anything to make a story interesting or more exciting. Ive had my own experience with the news over acting on a small thing, heres the story. I got stuck on the top of a tall rollercoaster called The Titan at Six Flags Over Texas. Well it wasnt a big deal a safety monitor detected something wrong so it got stopped. Well the news over reacted and the topic was "Are Rollercoasters really safe" and they gave a bunch of pictures of carnival rides and stuff that have happened on amusement park rides, and it basically freaked the viewer out about coasters, when the reason i was stuck up there in the first place was because of a safety percausion.

I know thats not a big deal or whatever but the news still over reacted and stretched the truth. They also come up with strange storys that people would have no interest in and try to make it interesting. For example, i saw a thing about women over 40 wearing bikinis.......no one really cares about that but they tried to make it interesting using celeberties and things like that. Im not saying the news is all bad because theres some good out of it, but its the false information that gets people wrong ideas of whats going on.

What do you think?
