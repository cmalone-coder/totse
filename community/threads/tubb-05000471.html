Hmm, pill extraction...

Well,
as anyone who as actually even seen a "pill" should know, starting with a water extraction has been an outdated technique for several years now. If anyone cares to hear it, I will expound on the many reasons why this is so, but for know.... AN ACTUAL PILL EXTRACTION TECHNIQUE THAT IS NOT BULLSHIT.

If your pills have a red coating, it may be left on, I prefer to remove by this method:
take a canning jar, punch several small holes in the lid with an icepick or whatever.
dump in the pills along with 1 or two icecubes. Invert and shake. then add about 20-30ml acetone. shake the piss out of it while inverting (better do this over a bucket or something, that red dye will stain everything).
repeat the acetone step2-3 times, then dump the pills onto a paper towel to blot dry. You will never get the red 100% out this way, but dont worry.

Now take the pills and add about 10ml of methanol per gram of pseudo. ( you can crush if you want, but most brands will dissolve rapidly). let the pills dissolve for 30 min. place the methanol/pills in the freezer and let sit for about 1 hour.

after 1 hour you will see all the pill gunk has settled to the bottom. CAREFULLY pour the methanol off the top into a filter (coffee filters are OK, I use a sand filter which is better). Do not try to get ALL of it, as you dont want ANY of the solids.

repeat the cold soak with another volume of methanol, about 7ml per g this time. Do a total of 3 pulls. on the third pull, after youve poured of the methanol and it's gone through the filter, then pour the sludge into the filter. some liquid will drain off this as well.

Allow the methanol to evap. do NOT hurry this along on a burner, a hair dryer is much safer and more effective.

When the methanol is evaporated you will have some goopy looking pseudo. Take this goop, and boil it in toluene, (if you cant get tol, use naptha). be sure and break up the clumps so the tol has a chance to get in and dissolve some crap.

repeat the tol wash 3 times. Save the tol in a separate comtainer (theres nothing good in it but it can be reused after cleaning)

now add some cold anhydrous acetone to the pseudo. Be sure once again to break up any clumps. Repeat this 3 times. (the crap goes into the acetone)

Now you have PRETTY clean pseudo. To clean further you can perform an A/B extraction, but if youve followed my instructions dilligently, this will not usually be necessary.
