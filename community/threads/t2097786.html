Why do we vote on candidates?
If you mention a new candidate to a sane person, they will immediately ask "Whats his/her's stance of <issue>?". People dont really vote for candidates, they vote for their stances on issues that concern them.

So why do we have reps intertwined with issues like we do? Why not vote on the decisions themselves and then elect someone to carry them out? Seems like it would have far less leeway for corruption and far less lesser-of-two-evils going on to me.
