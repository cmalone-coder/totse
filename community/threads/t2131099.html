Obsessive by nature
Hi Totse. Need to talk to you about something.

I am extremely obsessive by nature, especially when it comes to boys and matters of the heart. One example, I talked to a totsean on msn like twice, and immediately fell in like with him. He disappeared for a good two months, and during this period I became obsessed with him, searching all his posts, asking forums where he frequented what he was like, (semi-)harassed his friend on msn to get more information about him (Sylph can attest to this).

Now I am doing this again, this time with some other totsean. He hasn't been on for about 3-4 days and I just can NOT stop thinking about him. Like, when I am driving, buying food, going to interviews, every time when a thought springs to my mind I imagine telling him and how he'd react. Now this person in my opinion is really cool and everything, however a part of me knows that whatever this "thing" we have does not warrant this kind of obsession. But I just can't stop. Every second spent not talking to him is pure agony. I say without exaggeration that I am addicted to him.

I am worried about myself. I know this isn't normal. And it's not even a real relationship, it's just an online thing. I greatly fear that my obsessive nature will one day be my ruin. I have a feeling when I finally do have a guy, I will scare him away with the abnormal intensity of my feelings for him and its accompanying obsessing.

I thought about seeking help however I doubt it would really help my case. I don't think there's a condition called obsessiveness or that there's a med for it. But furthermore, I am not sure if mine is a real mental condition, or a mere character flaw. The former might be treatable whereas the latter is hopeless, in my opinion.

I don't want to obsess over a guy. I just want to be able to take it easy, sit back, enjoy life, and take things in a stride. But it seems like an almost impossible task to me.
