basically, you can't make anything because there is always the chance of you getting hurt


some good stuff that you can try out is triacetonetriperoxide (AP) or HMTD

there is a difference between triacetonetriperoxide and the dicyclo version, the dicyclo version is less stable and less powerful while the tricyclo is more powerful and more stable. You can make either one by making AP at a colder temperature or making it at a higher temperature. When it is at a colder temperature, it will form the tricyclo version but when made at high temperatures (higher then 5*C) it will turn out to be dicyclo. Thus, giving you a less stable substance.
