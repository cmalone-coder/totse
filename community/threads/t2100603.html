If somebody could put into words the exact way I felt the whole way through college, you have put it down to a tee.

All those waves of panic and nausea, anxiety and fear that control you and literally keep you in a dark bedroom is the only way your brain is able to tell you that it needs a rest. Smoking buds habitually and to forget problems does put you into a warm and comfortable 'bubble' almost, kind of putting your mind on standby. In reality, this just pushes all of these problems deep into your subconscious, so none of them ever seem to get resolved. This creates the long dream-like feeling that you might experience when it gets bad, making you feel as if you have no control over your life and you're a voice in a head trapped watching somebody live their life.

You need time to mull shit over in your head, you won't even realise how much you did it until you quit smoking buds. Being stoned all day every day gives you no time to subconsciously sort shit out.

Just like causing physical laziness and apathy, weed can do it to your mind man. It's going to be fucking hard, and it will fucking suck not blazing, but after a few weeks you should start to feel on the mend.

All i can suggest is healthy eating, good sleep, exercise, no drugs and taking the sober time to sit down, maybe put some music on, and investigate your own head.

Remember man, you never used to feel this way. You can be happy and feel real again.

If you need to throw some of your thoughts at somebody rather than just against the inside of your brain, send me an email (theatomiser at gmail dot com)

I should have realized something was up when I felt like this, but its taken me another two years of drug abuse and losing everything I ever loved in the process. Take me as a lesson, and a view as to how much worse things could get if you continue with this self-destructive mindset.

Make the mind proactive rather than reactive.
