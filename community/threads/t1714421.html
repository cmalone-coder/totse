If you use the backside of an exacto knife you can unfold the shrinkwrap on the bottom without cutting it.

Slide the game about 75% out of the shrinkwrap.

open the case as much as you can only the top has the seal on it. You should be able to open it enough to pull the game out, replace it with a free AOL cd or a blank cd, grab the instruction manual and replace it with a few pages out of the phonebook.

close the case and slide it back into the shrinkwrap

use a clear glue stick to reseal it

after the glue is dry a little heat (a bic lighter is too much) will undo any stretching of the shrinkwrapped. a hairdryer works great for that professional look and feel.

take the game back and say you bought it for your brother and he already had it.

playstation games have a shrinkwrap like a pack of cigarettes, so practice on those.

if they open it and find out you are scamming them, you blow up on them for selling you this and then trying to make you look like a thief. demand the case to the game and your receipt back, and have your buddy try it at another one. I've been caught 3 times and I even got directions to other locations from the manager. I have about 350 ps1 and ps2 games.

not sure if this works at GameStop but i've yet to find a place I couldn't return at, and I've gone to 9 different stores. (wal-mart, k-mart, eb games, best buy, circut city, fred meyer, compusa, fry's, target)
