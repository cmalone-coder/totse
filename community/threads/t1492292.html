Re: hey cindy!
I don't know...Australia to England? That's a long way to go. If you're a grown adult with a lot of time and money on your hands, then I'd say why not, take a trip to England just for kicks. Just go as a tourist, without her being the main focus of the trip.

If you're on the younger side, and money isn't yet easy to come by, and you'd only be going to see her, then it doesn't strike me as a great idea. A lot of things are idealized when you're just on an internet basis. While this sort of communication allows you to get to know someone, you can't really know them on a moment-to-moment, day-to-day basis. A year is a good span of time, but it's not enough if your sole contact is through IMs.

And she has a man? That's not good news. You might just be a pleasant diversion for her; you might be the guy that makes it easy for her not to feel needy and dependent in her relationship, which serves her relationship with her man well, but does nothing for you.

It's true, you never know the possibilities until you actually meet someone. But if you're not yet 20 then time is on your side, and it seems more likely you stand to lose more by taking the trip at this time.

I appreciate that you think my advice is good, but this could have been directed at the general audience here because a lot of people give good advice. Anyone with an opinion should respond to this post.
