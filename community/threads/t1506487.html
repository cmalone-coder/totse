since you're framing it like it's a problem, it's obviously not okay with you to go without social interaction. you say you don't like it, but i suspect you're really just afraid of it or don't know how to approach it, because it seems unfamiliar. first of all, you're not better or worse than anyone else. people know that they're just talking about shallow stuff a lot of the time -- but that doesn't make them shallow. if you got to know many of them you would find a lot more depth than you realize. they just try to find a common ground when they're socializing, and that common ground is often pretty superficial. it doesn't mean they've sold their souls to the devil.

you don't have to try that hard to make friends. there are usually people who aren't uptight about newcomers joining their group. ask if you can sit with them at mealtimes, and relate to them in whatever way you find you have in common. even if it's just for a couple minutes a day.

you say you don't find social interaction interesting. well guess what? this board is a social interaction. and anyway, social interaction's primary purpose is not to be interesting -- it's to provide a sense of comfort and belonging that you seem to be lacking. so not to be harsh, but get over yourself. other people are just like you in so many ways!
