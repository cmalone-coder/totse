Yah', my habit started when I had a headache, I was living in shitsville, I used to count the used rigs in the hallway to use as a divination of how my day was going to go. I had used lots of different drugs off and on, pot regularly, shrooms and dose occasionally, and the occasional blow binge. I had just read Junkie, and as such had both a healthy respect for and great curiosity about smack. One day I had a pisscutter of a tension headache, felt like my brain was being twisted, so I went out to find some t3s and a couple of valiums, the street in front of my hotel was pretty much an open air pharmacy, anything anytime. But the only thing around was down, so I bought a flap and railed it. I won't go on about the effects but lets just say I fell in love, heroin penetrates the blood/brain barrier quicker than all other opiates and the rush is the best thing you will ever feel, aside from seeing your child being born. So soon I was buying by the point, about 5 doses of columbian beige for $60, chasing the dragon and playing Sega became an after work ritual. Using weed and other drugs soon became irrelevant, heroin takes over quick. Then the supply of beige dried up, white #4 smack doesnt smoke as well as beige, and there was a little bit of a drought on so snorting it was getting expensive. I am not a criminal by nature, so stealing to feed a habit was out of the question, the area already had 4-5 dealers on every corner so that was not an option either. I bought a rig, a clean one, a nice little unit with replaceable points. I tried mainlining first, into the vein on the back of my hand, but I did not alchohol prep first and caught tetnus(sp?), my hand swelled up like a balloon and I had to buy some penecillin.(strangely enough it didn't take that long to find, people probibly used it to treat abcesses) From then on I just skin popped, the rush wasn't as fast, but I was mixing in a little up too, and I loved the big numb patch on my arm. Then one day I woke up and realized I was a fucking junkie like all the other unemployed fucks out hustling and turning tricks, the people I used to walk by thinking how great it was that I could live here and not become a fuckup like them. I freaked out, I knew I had a habit and that it would suck to come off it. So I quit my job, bought 20 Dilaudids and moved back home to live with my mom for a while. She wasn't particularly surprised, she's a biker momma, and she figured for her son to have a habit would be a good learning experience for him. I tapered off using the morphine, after 2 or three days my point got a barb and started doing a lot of damage when I shot up so I went to smoking it off foil, I drank a lot and smoked a lot of weed to get through the ever increasing in-between times, once I was down to 5 caps I decided I was done and flushed them down the toilet while I was drunk. The next morning I stole $100 from mom and went out to try and score, I got burned by a hooker and shot up some crap that made my liver and kidneys shut down for a while. I went to the hospital with my eyes and skin turning piss yellow, acute hepatoxia or something like that. I spent 3 days on dialisis and got a 21 day rehab ticket. I hated rehab, I know why Cobain jumped the fence and shot himself, the nurses treat you like meat and even the counsellors are so jaded by the volume of patients that their attempts at relating to you seem pretty fucking weak. I have been clean of heroin for 7 years now, the 2 year weed growing and freebasing story is for another day. I have used opiates since, but never junk, junk spoils you for all other drugs, nothing is as good, nothing will make you feel like a pool of liquid gold like heroin. Still want to try it?, I dont blame you, some will, some won't. I will say one thing though, the only time I have ever felt as good as I did on junk is when my daughter was born, and that is the ultimate high.
