you gotta be smart about everything--

1. safe meet up spots (not always the same spot either). if possible deliver or have them pick up directly (if theyre trustworthy enough to see your stash house)

3. knw your weights at all time so that you arent guessing how much more money you can make.

4. be very smart when rolling arond with baggies. hide in clothes well or within car extremely well.

5. SELL WHAT YOU KNOW.. dont go out buyin yayo if you havent got a clientel base for it. ive seen many people get themselves in the whole buying out of their league.

6. make a good impression on your upper levels so theyre more likely to save you the lasts of their shit, more likely to sit it straight or fat on the clock, more likely tobump their prices down over time.

7. dont allow yourself to be put in a position of being robbed, dont leave anything near people unattended (even GOOD friends), when out on the streets making a transaction keep the higher ground to avoid potential risk. dont allow anyone to know where your stash is within the house.

8. the risk you speak of is relative to your desire to get high. if you are constantly gettin stoned all day and breaking even at the end of the day and you're okay ith that then continue to do so. if you are breaking even at the end of the day and want more loot, you either gotta
a- cut back on your smoking
b- increase clientel
c- decrease the price you purchase the bulk in

9. with clientel, be very charasmatic so they dont even conceive you as a dope dealer, just a nice guy.

10. keep clientel to a minimum, the less players the less risk. if your regulars have a friend wantin the hookup, the regular buys it and delivers it to their buddy. let them middle man its better than risking potentially sketchy people to gank you.

11. keep your regulars around because their cash is the core of your operation. treat them like royalty, give them fat bags, let them kick if they feel the urge to etc.

the main thing im tryin to say is you just gotta keep your eyes open and be thinking in a business market sense as well as a paranoid cop fearing person. given both of those traits you will keep out of govt suspicion as well as profit more and more each day.
