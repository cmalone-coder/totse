Re: Shrooms Schizophrenia Trigger?
No one can tell you if it is safe or not.

No one can say you have X risk of developing X mental illness because, quite frankly, no one knows what the fuck is going on.

All we know is that some people who used some drugs developed some mental illnesses. No one knows if the drugs caused the disorders, or if they merely exacerbated a pre-existing condition, or if it's all just an unfortunate coincidence.

I mean, if it makes you feel better, people have been using shrooms for thousands of years, and the number of people to develop schizophrenia is pretty low. Any risk of mental breakdown is probably quite small.

But only you can make that decision for yourself. You know yourself better than anyone here does.

If you're hesitant, though, that's generally a good idea to not go rushing into anything, you know? Take your time and weigh things out.

From my experiences, all forms of diagnosed mental illness and heavy drug use in general do not correspond very well together. It can be the result of any drug's direct effect on the brain's chemistry(both short term and long term), or the potential lifestyle and situations drugs can often put people in that could possibly have a negative effect on somebody "vulnerable", for lack of a better word.

Obviously it varies heavily from person to person, but moderating your usage, knowing your limits, and keeping your head screwed on is all you can really do.
