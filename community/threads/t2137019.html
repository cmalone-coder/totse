Re: Is acid dangerous?
I know several people who say acid "killed their happiness". They felt emotionally numb and distant after taking acid. Usually that's just a first reaction to having a bad trip, and it passes. But acid can bring out all of your repressed memories and realities that you don't want to face. When you're having a bad trip, all the things you don't want to think about will be put right up in your face, and you can't ignore it. That's why it's very important to take steps in ensuring that you'll have a good trip, otherwise you'll be in complete mental madness.

After the trip is over, and the "hangover" has passed... you'll more or less be back to normal. You'll take along plenty of insights and thoughts with you, but you won't turn into a crazy person.

Moderation is key. The crazy acid freaks are the ones who do it daily.

No, acid is not dangerous. Acid is a compound which sits around being perfectly unaware of it's own existence, and quite content with it's lack of influence over anything.
