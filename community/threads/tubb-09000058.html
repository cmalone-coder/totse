Anyways, we were talking last night (always an interesting experience) and somewhere along the line he mentioned that people only dream and remember in black and white except for rare occassions and those who do dream and remember in colour are psychopaths.

When I told him that I only dream in colour and remember in colour, he didn't believe me at first. Then he said he wouldn't be surprised because: "You are my daughter." He has three nicknames: Married Man, The Lord, and Flipo... This should give you an idea of what that means.

I have a hard time doubting him when he's always so right about things like this.

Psychologists have performed an interesting experiment, to demonstrate that many people dream in color and that dreams are some sort of 'processing' of waking experiences.

Human test subjects had to wear red-colored glasses. Immediately after a nights sleep, they were interrogated. Many of them reported that their dream looked as if they were still wearing the glasses, i.e. they dreams had a red haze over them.
