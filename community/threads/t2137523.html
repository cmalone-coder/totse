Re: Jobs for a business grad
I work at a bank and I have a year left for a bachelor's in business administration. I work with people who have only a HS diploma, some college, and then various degrees. My advice is to finish up with your bachelor's because it can help open a lot of doors. Some companies don't really require a degree, but a degree will help you get your foot in the door, along with some good experience. This is where I recommend something where you can get some business experience and at least start thinking more on the right track of things (maybe a bank?). Or, you could get an engineering degree or something like that if you're wanting something more specific. Industrial engineering is a nice blend of things. It's sort of hard to answer your question because I don't really know what you strive to become. Hope this helps.
