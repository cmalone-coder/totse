The logs that were leaked: A while back one of Jeb's friends repeated something to MLoR (which I'll let him share if he wants) which was said in #mods. It is still up for debate whether or not it was him, but almost everyone who was in the channel at the time believed it was him.

Someone else probably knows more about the screenshots better than I do - I believe it occurred while I was on vacation. I'll let someone else tell that story.

Aside from that, he's gone out of his way to question almost every ban and thread closing by meta, acidmelt, and mlor - many of which were entirely legitimate. Usually there isn't a problem when someone questions a ban, but I will outline how Jeb usually did it: 1) Harass the person who set the ban until it gets reversed, 2) when denied, go to a higher power and repeat step 1 on the higher power. Now I don't mind people taking other moderators' bans to a an admin if they don't agree - in fact I encourage it. But when an admin makes a decision and you don't agree with it, tough cookies. You don't bother them with it every time you see them, you don't undo their bans, and you don't go and make comments in B&M about how the administration is censoring everyone. Moderators are supposed to use this forum to discuss serious issues that they have with other moderators or admins.

There may be other feelings that Meta, acidmelt, or MLoR would like to voice, but those are mine.

Outside of the reasons above, I think he's a good person who has good intentions. He's just going about things all wrong. It is my opinion that we have given him enough chances. Him being demodded recently had little to do with what occurred in B&M and SG. It had been talked about many times before. I in the past I talked acidmelt out of it, but I don't think I can fend for Jeb any more. However, that doesn't mean that what Jeb was fighting against (unjust bans and censorship) is void. In fact, I think that there have been a lot of unjust bans and an unusual amount of threads being deleted or edited. Jeb may have the right idea, but I think he is going about it all wrong.

1: An exemption is commonly used to allow a particular person into a chatroom. It is an anti-ban. If I ban everybody in the IP range of 134.204.20.* (the * means "anything") and then set an exemption on 134.204.20.201, then that address will be allowed into the channel without taking the ban into consideration.
