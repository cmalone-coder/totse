Monogamy has two kinds of cheaters.
Something many people don't realize is that there are two kinds of cheaters in monogamy

The first, is the well known kind that has sex with someone else.

The second, is less thought of or identified as a cheater, which is the person who doesn't have sex with their partner enough.

The reason why I consider it cheating, is because part of the agreement of monogamy to me, is that you must satisfy your partners needs within reason, with the trade-off being them only sleeping exclusively with you. It is unfair to keep someone to yourself, but not give them what they need.

In all relationships I start, I tell my partner right up front that if they want monogamy, they have to keep me satisfied within reason. If they don't keep me satisfied, I will then bring it to their attention that I'm not getting enough. If they fail to keep up, I then ask to seek an outside partner to fill in what they can't give me. It is then up to them whether they can handle me having another partner, or if they want to leave. I simply refuse to let someone guilt trip me with love to deny myself happiness, and I'm not happy unless I get enough sex. It's not cheating in my opinion if you are open about the lack of sex, and seek a second partner to alleviate the problem, with their consent.
