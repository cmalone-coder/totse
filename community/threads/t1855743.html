Re: cooling tube for NH3 generater: tryp?
A downwards spiral out of stainless steel plastic or some other inert material inside a vessel that can withstand the extreme cold. To make the most of a small amount of dry ice the coil should be only a few inches in diameter with some space between each loop for maximum cooling.
