I'd go out on a limb and say just about anything by Studio Ghibli, Miyazaki's work or not. Branching out a bit you'll find Tales from Earthsea, Whisper of the Heart, and to a lesser extent The Cat Returns (it had its charm). There's also Grave of the Firefiles.

On the Ghibli front, one of my favorite examples for people is to have them watch Totoro and then compare it to what Disney, et. all were up to around 1988. Oliver and Company? The Great Mouse Detective? No fucking comparison.

Metropolis is also very good and has very unique and stylized artwork. Calling it "beautiful" would be pretty subjective, but I thought it was great.
