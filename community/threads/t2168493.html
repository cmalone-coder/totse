Depends on the pill and how much...and how well you can drive. I personally find that when on any decent amount of E my cognitive ability tends to shrink with my dick (could have sworn it actually managed to get into my body a couple nights ago ) to the point where I can't make those slit second decisions often needed to drive & survive.

Shrooms on the other hand, are DEFINITELY NOT a drug you should be on while at the wheel.
