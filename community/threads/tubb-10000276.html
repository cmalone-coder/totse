Re: sonic foundry videofactory 2.0
Blue Screening is a term used when the movie industries take a live actor and put them in front of a large green, blue or red screen. They film the actor doing whatever he does then they take that film and digitalize it and then apply that footage over an existing footage. The Blue Screen software then takes the footage of him and tell the computer to keep all colors but the color of the background such as green or blue. All things that are blue or green will disapear allowing the background footage to show through giving the actor an effect like he is in the other footage.

Or another term used is Compositioning(sp).

I hope that makes sense. Its was they do in space movies where the actor looks like he is on a spaceship or floating above a planet.
