Re: killing the point of suboxone
You got some shit you need to balance out on your own here. No one else knows your current mentality or emotional state at this point of your abstinence but you.

If you feel that the Suboxone is absolutely mandatory to ensure you steer clear of the opes, and you truly value doing so, then, finances willing, you'd probably want to consider sucking it up and giving in to the drive/cost/gas factor to get to the old doctor.

If you feel like you might be ready to maintain without the Suboxone, and have some left at this point, maybe you should think about tapering off. You likely won't (and shouldn't IMO) be on Suboxone forever, and assuming you've been clean from opes for a little while now, the bupe is, at this point, moreso for the psychological craving aspect than the literal physical withdrawal. If you feel secure enough, psychologically and emotionally, to cope with your cravings cold turkey, then maybe a gradual taper is in order for you (assuming you have the necessary pills to do so). I'd definitely keep a few left over in case you feel yourself buckling and on the verge of a relapse, though.

But basically, this guy won't help you out if you fail your test for weed? Or is there something else in your system? If it's just the weed...well, have you thought about the possibility of not smoking anymore? Yeah, it'll suck for a good while at first, but if that's the only obstacle between you and convenient access to Suboxone then I'd think you would consider the Suboxone more beneficial to your ongoing recovery.

I don't know, really, so don't take anything I'm saying literally. I've never been through severe opiate withdrawal/recovery myself and can only offer advice based on simple research and being witness to more than a couple personal friends going through it themselves. I've been through benzo withdrawal, though, about four different times, and I can certainly attest for the psychological aspect of long-term recovery.

Good luck, though. Hope something works out...
