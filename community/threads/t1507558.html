The best way to get to know someone better is to "go through something" with them, if you know what I mean. For instance, if either you or her were coming out of a bad relationship and needed someone to talk to about it (this has happened to me) you might choose each other for emotional support. Of course, those sorts of things only work if they're real and actually happened.

Otherwise, just spend more time with her one-on-one like Docta said. Tell her things about yourself, and ask her about herself. Sometimes, as regular friends you skip over these sorts of things. Also the disclosure of personal information (not TOO personal) is a sign of trust. A girl likes to know that she can confide in a man.
