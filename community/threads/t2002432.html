Fighting A Riot Policeman
Let me start off by saying obviously, I don't plan on actually fighting a riot cop. I'm not a idiot. It's just an idea I had.

But watching a thing on the news about riot police at some kids massive party got me thinking...

Riot police don't tend to meet that much resistance, and when the do, the cop next to them just beats on the attacker. But if you happened to get a riot cop one on one, how would you go about it?

Personally I would throw something at the cop to make him block, then charge him as fast as I can. I would keep my head down and my hands up with my elbows in to protect my head and ribs, because I only plan on letting him get one hit in if that. If he went for my knee I would raise my leg and check it, a shot to the shin is still better than the knee. I would push him off balance then continue to rush foward and grab him in the clinch and yank his head around visciously and knee him. Because at this range there isn't much you can do with a baton. After almost ripping his head off I would hopefully throw him to the ground and just stomp. If he tried to tackle me when I was clinching him I know how to sprawl so I should be alright.

Obviously I this most likely wouldn't go to plan and my ass would get handed to me, but still.

I didn't post this in BI, because I don't plan on doing this, nor do I suggest anyone else attempt this. Ever.
