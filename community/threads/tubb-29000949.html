Your tax stamp (or fee for registration of a nfa firearm) is a one time deal.

The only other time you need to contact the NFA branch is if you change your address, espically if you move out of state, or god forbid misplace your nfa firearm =/

Only licensing (if you plan to become a dealer or manufacter) is due annually.

A suppressed pistol is considered a title ii class iii nfa firearm and does require a tax stamp aka fee of two hundred dollars =/
