Re: adderall into meth?
It can be done with cheap reagents. Make your own sodium dichromate, and use that and some sulfuric acid from the hard ware store to dehydrogenate methanol to the aldehyde, then the aldehyde to the acid (formic acid). React this by refluxing in some methanol with amphetamine base to get n-formyl-amphetamine. Rehydrogenate the formyl group back to a methyl group by reducing the intermediate with an aluminum amalgum (or better yet, Pd/C and H2). Main product would be methamphetamine. Byproducts include di- and tri- methylated amphetamines, and unreduced formylated amphetamine, all present in minute quantities.
Another way would be to react methanol with HI and zinc iodide to make methyl iodide. This is reacted with amphetamine base in a sealed vessel at about 150 degrees celcius. This forms methamphetimine and HI. The rxn mixture is then diluted with alcohol (preferrably methanol) and reduced with an amalgum. This reduces the imine to the amine, or methamphetimine to methamphetamine.
Of course this is only practical if you had an ass of amphetamine laying around. But usually, not worth your while. If you had say, save up enough rp and iodine to make an ounze, but for some weird reason could only get PPA instead of ephedrine. The amphetamine produced could be converted. Or maybe if you had an ass of P2P, and was too lazy to make methylamine, you could react the P2P with ammonia acetate, and formic acid, reduce with an amalgum, which would give you methamphetamine, instead of cleaving the formyl group with HCl acid to get amphetamine.
Here are the equations to that:
First method:
NH3 (imine base) + HCOOH (formic acid)----> HOOCNH3(n-formyl derivative)
HOOCNH3 ---(reduction)--->H2CNH3 (CH2NH3, n-methylated amine)

Second method:
P2P + NH4COOCH (ammonia acetate)-->NH3COOCH (amphetmine acetate imine)
NH3COOCH (imine with p2p, not ammionia) + HCOOH--->HOOCNH4COOCH
HOOCNH4COOCH + NaOH--->base HOOCNH3
HOOCNH3 --(reduction)-->CH2NH3 (methylated amphetmine).

If ethanol is dehydrogenated, acetaldehyde is formed. If this is further dehydrogenated, it forms acetic acid. Acetic acid can also be purifed from vinegar. Acetic acid used in place of formic will attatch an acetyl group, which will reduce to an ethyl group, forming all your n-ethylated derivitives. Other alcohols can be used to open up all your other n-alkyl derivitives.
