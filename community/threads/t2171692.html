Best Experiences On Erowid
In this thread, we post the most entertaining reads found on Erowid's experiences vault.
The categories for a good read include:

The quality of the actual events that occur.

Reporter's overall ability to describe the events that occurred and their emotions felt at the time- all the while utilizing necessary language, structure and form.

Reporter's ability to create vivid imagery.

The overall level of entertainment and fulfillment received during and after the read.


Here is a quick example of which i believe satisfies the above criteria:

A deliciously terrifying Datura read- it frankly disturbed the shit out of me:
