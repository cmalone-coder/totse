Oh make sure you clean out the tube before heating it )

Re: glass pipe help
Its almost a given that the pyrex has been tempered in a kiln. You won't be able to blow a hole through it, you'll just break it. More than likely if you try to drill it you'll also break it.

Just get a cigar tube and some MAPP gas. Thats how my old roomy taught me how to blow glass before I used the real thing.

FYI, professional glass blowers use an oxypropane setup. Its almost exactly like an oxyacetylene setup for plasma cutting, welding and heating. Just a different torch )and obviously propane instead of acetylene.) After the glass is blown it is tempered in a kiln just as metal is. Think of tempering as "setting" the object to hold a certain composure. Your pyrex flask is "set," its not moving. ;-)

Re: glass pipe help
Best Way to make the perfect size intake hole!

you need: 2-3 mm pyrex rod, Plyers (for cutting thin glass-Arc Joints), Torch

Directions: Take a 6 in. long peice of 2-3 mil pyrex rod and place in torch flame till molten. Let the molten tip gather into a little ball and smash it into a surface (Carbon plate) that wont melt. Smash so it forms a perfect, flat small, circle at tip of rod. Now place a small section of pipe where u want your hole in flame till molten. While still molten, place the rod end in the flame briefly and attach to molten spot on pipe (Do this in the flame quickly). Take it the joint out of flame and pull up so the glass streches into a small thin cone. Cut the cone near top so the new hole is some what small. Fire polish hole while blowing into the pipe. Done.
