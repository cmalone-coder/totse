For any who remember, my grandfather passed away a few months ago. It hurt like fuck because, even though he wasn't a blood relative, he was more family than most blood relatives I have. But most of all he was my best friend. And he still is.

That's what's making me wonder.

His relatives came over earlier this morning to get my dad so they could go bury the ashes. They're burying them in the West Ham football ground (UP THE HAMMERS! :D ) and I know he'd be proud to know that his remains are with his lifelong team and passion.

But he's not dead to me. Physically it's obvious he is not here but to me mentally, he's still right here.
It's strange to think about it at times...A weird way of living.
I won't leave things leaning in certain places in the house because he might kick it over with his big old grandad shoes or I won't leave anything in certain places so he doesn't break his neck tripping over them! Little things like that. They're still here in my mind.
I know it's residual second instinct of a life I was used to but in a strange way it's like keeping him alive.

He's still very much in my memory. I haven't gone a day without thinking about him and how much I miss him. All the silly little things he'd say about things he didn't agree with..."You silly old sod!!!" or "BALLS BRUVVA!"
I still have a problem going near his room. I went there once to get his West Ham scarf (one of the only things his relatives didn't take. Fucking tight bastards they are) so I have something of him to keep with me. It's strange being in the room without him and his stuff there.

I refuse to let him go. He's not dead unless he's forgotten. And I refuse to forget him.

I don't know why I decided to post this. I really don't. But I must say, typing these things out and letting my mind and fingers do their work, it's helped me get some things of my chest.
