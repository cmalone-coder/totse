I think I can pin each disorder down to a specific events.

SPD: I smoked weed to the point of abuse when I was still very young (11-14), and stopped. Soon after I was diagnosed with the symptoms.

What do you think, headshrinkers? Can specific events trigger mental disorders to come out?

Re: Triggers that cause disorders to surface
I think yes since most disorders come out or surface during times of stress. However, the triggers can be continuous or the triggers are more than one event.

In my case, my OCD worsened or surfaced due to me being perfectionist and being anxious (thinking of problems) most of the time. At first I think I only did the compulsions just to please myself that those bad things will never happen and after some time they already became a habit.
My manic-depression might have come from my OCD or vice versa. Though I think it triggered due to my inability to have a long term relationship with a girl and most of the girl I like or loved didn't liked me at all (rejection). Or maybe due to the fact that I am moody and temperamental. I became suicidal during my depressive mood especially when something bad happens or sometimes I just feel really sad and hopeless.
