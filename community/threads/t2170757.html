The topic of addiction pisses me off in general. Not the concept of habituation of course, but the term addiction, and the stigma it carries with it. Anything can be "addictive" or "habit forming". Fuck, some people develop severe "habits" that don't make ANY sense, such as hand washing, eating, watching TV, etc etc. But they actually DO make sense. It's simple. We find something we like, and we like to do it. Simple as that. The thing is, some folks can get too engrossed in whatever their habit is, and that's when things get out of control.


The key to beating "addiction" or habit formation is to simply STEP BACK from your life a minute and examine things. Psychedelics are fucking win for this, especially mushrooms. The more the better :D After actually laying things out and being logical about it, you can learn to use drugs as the tools they are, and not simply for gratification, which is really the root of any addiction, or habit.

Humans are weak. It is our nature. The difference between a junky and a responsible user is that the junky gives in to his weaknesses without fail, and to extreme, while the responsible user pretty much looks at things from a totally logical point of view.
