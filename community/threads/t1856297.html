Re: cocaine
You can't say that the cut burns at 100C because you don't know what it is cut with.

Do an a/b to start with. Dissolve the shit into water, filter any insoluables, use ammonia to base it, extract freebase into non-polar solvent, wash, then titrate using HCl and extract back into fresh water. Evaporate to get the cocaine HCl.

However, realize that most coke thats powdery (not rock), white (not yellowish), and gotten from the street (not straight off the columbian freighter) will be very very very very cut (more than you think). In reality, a lot of street coke can contain less than 20% cocaine. So the weight of the final product after you purify will probably be way less, and you will be pissed.

Unless you got the hook-up staright from Columbia, or your making synthetic cocaine yourself, leave that shit alone. Street shit is so bad for you and definitely NOT worth any time or money.

You need to switch to amphetamines my friend.
