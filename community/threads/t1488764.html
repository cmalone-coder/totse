Re: I just figured out that I'm really lonely
You must be a really strong person if you were able to go through all of that. At least you're on your own now and away from your stepmother.

quote:I looked up schizophrenia a while ago and was shocked that I had had most of the symptoms multiple times over the past few months (especially auditory hallucinations and paranoia).

If you have the symptoms, you might have schizophrenia or you might just be having a major depressive episode - I don't know, only a psychologist would be able to determine that for sure. From your posts, you sound too normal to me to be a schizophrenic, but as I said before, I'm not a professional. Some people with severe depression get misdiagnosed as being schizoprenic because major depression can share some of the same symptoms including:

*hallucinations (most commonly hearing voices telling you that you're worthless, hearing someone crying, hearing someone calling your name, seeing a distorted image of yourself in mirrors, etc.)

*feelings of persecution or of being punished by outside forces (example: my life sucks because God hates me)

*flattened affect (when you talk, you're monotone and don't convey much emotion or facial expression)

*inability to take pleasure in activities you once enjoyed

*anxiety

*social isolation

*thoughts of suicide or urges to harm yourself

For schizophrenia, your symptoms need to be present every day for the last month and for major depression, your symptoms need to have occured every day for the last two weeks.
