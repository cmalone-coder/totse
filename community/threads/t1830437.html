Re: M-80s, M-160s, Am I doing them right?
With good flash powder, plugs is harly ever a problem.

Last 4th of July, I set off an M-80 (1/2 ID, 1/16 wall, 1 1/2 inch long) and the paper end plugs were only 2 layers (craft paper)thick, and when it went off it was rattling the windows on houses over 100 feet away.

I really think its your measurements that are wrong. I use a homemade scale more accurate than just about any digital scale you can buy off ebay or UN, but scales accurate to 1 or .5 gram are plenty accurate.

You might also want to experiment with other plugs. I rarely use paper plugs unless they are with my homemade tubes. With bought tubes, I just push the plug in concave first a 1/8 inch deep into the tube, and fill the empty space with hot glue. They have never blown out, and hot glue hardens much faster than white glue dries, so you can use your salutes quicker.
