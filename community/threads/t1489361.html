Re: What to say?
Are you actually friends with her, or just acquantices (as in, you both know each other's name, and occasionally talk).

IF its the former, it shouldn't be a big problem. Start exchanging hugs with her whenever you see her, talk about class, sports, some event/topic that she might be involved in (ie. are you going to homecoming; isn't Ms. Teacher crazy?).

If you are acquanitences (I have to learn how to spell that word!), then try to build to a friend level. Become for physical (don't feel on her, just play with her, give her hugs, put your arm around her a little when you talk to her, etc.)

The key to conversation is to find common ground. Talking about class/school gets a bit boring once you've been talking about it awhile. As someone else said, think of topics before you talk to her. I literally outline (so to speak) my conversations in my head. Oh shit, now I have to keep talking so I can explain something.

Okay,the way I think about life is that you can change it simply by opening your mouth. I get a thought during class or something, and it occurs to me that I can change the course of the class/ day just by saying my thought or keeping it to myself. When I think of it that way, it makes the situation a lot simpler. It becomes just a matter of opening your mouth.

So, what I do is think of what I want to say, make sure it sounds ok, and then just spit it out (not literally). Maybe I'm not explaining this well enough, but basically, I like to simplify everything down a simple matter of opening my mouth. I don't like to be stuck thinking of what to say.
