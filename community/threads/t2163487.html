Bisexuality
I've heard so many different people have so many different views on the idea of bisexuality- that they're gays in denial, or over-sexed sluts, or that they're attention whores, they're going through a phase, or that it's a totally legitimate sexual orientation. I have a good friend who says she is bisexual and has dates both guys and girls, and is so sure about her sexual orientation that she's planning to get 'love knows no gender' tattooed on her hip (which I think is a terrible decision). My boyfriend today shocked me by saying that he doesn't find bisexuality attractive in females at all because he claims that bisexuals are sluttier than straight people and more likely to spread diseases. Personally, I don't really have a concrete opinion about the whole idea of being bisexual, but I do know that I am sexually attracted to girls just as much, if not more, than I am guys. But this thread isn't about my sexual orientation, because I honestly don't care enough to label my preferences.

I guess what this thread is really about is your views on bisexuality. Do you think that it's possible for someone to be genuinely attracted to both genders, or do you feel that it's a phase some people experience, a way of getting attention, or a sign that someone is so slutty and horny that they'll hump anything that moves? I've heard some people say that all humans are inherently attracted to both sexes; does that sound like it could hold some truth? I'm just interested in getting an idea of how people feel about bisexuality, whether or not it's generally seen as legitimate, or if it's doubted and looked down upon.
