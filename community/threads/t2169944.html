I had some people over tonight, and she came over. We talked a little, but it was awkward, I feel strange even just hugging her. I can tell that she has no interest in pursuing a relationship right now, and she really won't let her self be attracted to me. I called her and told her that I can't do this. I feel like we could work through it, but i feel like she doesn't really want to. My reaction to the barrier she puts up turns me in to a person that she can't really be attracted to. So yeah, I told her, that I can't just hang out and not think about her. I wish it was just casual and fun, but we can't do that when she wont let herself have feelings towards me at all. If she was ready to let go and not worry about the relationship escalating, then I would be the person she was attracted to. But hanging out with everything the same just renforces the anxiety she causes me, and her not being able to be attracted to me. I miss talking to her so much. I don't know how it just died. And I can't stop blaming myself. If I didn't care so much about her, she could love me.

Does totse think this relationship is dead? is there any way to repair it?

How can I deal with this? I am in love with her, but she won't give me a chance.
