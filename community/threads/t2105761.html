fun with legal hallucinogens!
So yesterday I went to a park with my girlfriend and her friend and we had three purple capsules (purple ohms?) between us. We've been considering acid for a while, and wanted something as a bit of preperation and fun to end the weekend.

It was a wonderful experience. Given I've never really done anything more than drink alcohol I believe I felt the effects the most. We all seen black and white dots when we looked up, and strange shapes floating past and towards us. It fascinated me how three dimensional it looked, and mild enough to handle easily in a public place.

The feelings played a bigger part than the hallucinations, I felt very, very happy and thoughtful, talkative and happy to just watch anything. And I had music on my headphones, which sounded pretty awesome.

We went into a busier part of the city centre, and I got a little uneasy. Something happened whilst I was waiting for my girlfriend and I thought a bag was going to be stolen, I immediatly became irrationally paranoid and nervous, but was spoken out of it and totally relaxed within half an hour.

My favourite part of the whole experience was probably coming down though. Everything seemed to be turning back to normal, I had the most brilliant conversation and felt as though I had made it out of the experience in one piece and for the better. I'd probably never do it again, I'm just pleased to say I did it once.

I'll update on the acid thing. Anybody had any similiar experience, on that specific brand or anything else?
