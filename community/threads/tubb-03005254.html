Re: Reality?
Hmm. First off, limpet, how do you know UFO's, ghosts aren't real?

A lack of personal experience of something is not a proof that it doesn't exist, it just means you have yet to experience it.

That being said, I'd define 'reality' as the common consentual agreed-upon MODEL of consciousness and existence.

I say model for the exact reason that syphon pointed out - our perceptions, senses, are the raw information we take in, then we file it, arrange it, place a little sprig of parsley next to it, and we have conclusions about our sensory data.

Our conclusions, then, are a creation of ours, NOT the raw stuff of experience itself, but what we've concluded about it.

Alan Watts said, "The map is not the territory; the menu is not the meal." Ponder that.

A part of something cannot percieve the whole something, so whatever it is we are part of - whatever it is your mind/consciousness/sentience is part of, you'll never really know. Not genuinely. Not in my opinion, anyways.

I prefer to see it like this: Reality is a game we have all agreed to play, for our own individual reasons. There are some game-players who play so well (or so poorly) that they've forgotten they're playing, and there are other game-players who are playing jsut to remind themselves that it's all a game.

Regardless of whether or not this is true or not (and here's the crucial part) - seeing existence as a game makes itfun.

But this isn't like Scrabble, or poker. It's not a zero-sum game. Not a win/lose dichotomy. It's more like a roleplaying game - you play to develop your persona, to further your experience, to learn, grow, and enjoy the game for the sake of playing.

It works for me, and provides an overall optimistic attitude towards most aspects of this game.
