Depends on which route would, theoretically speaking, to be followed. If Ammonia is to be used then one can get away with an unbelievable amount of gak (it has been known for persons to just chuck straight pills in as is - Gup Chucking); however, if the RP/I route is to be followed it's a different story - RP n I is very sensitive to gaks or even fairly innocuous additives.

Best to test on foil to see how it burns. RP/I can handle a bit of crap but not much. On the foil, ideally, it should melt to a clear liquid and start smoking, keep heating until all is vaporised and check for any marking to the foil or carbon residues.

Anything other than a black mark with no carbon residue or the tiniest amount, is not good enough. Sometimes the melting pse liquid may go yellow, this is also a sign of resident crap but the main indication is at the end when checking for residues.


*Micro membrane filters are good for aqueous extractions but think aqueous extracting was stopped in the USA many moons ago but may be worth a try on 5 pills to see.
Also, when doing alcohol extractions, after it has evapped use minimal alcohol (enough to redissolve the pse only, use less than what is believed to be available, one can always add more) to disolve the pse once again and it will force out any other crap as pse loves alcohol and should dissolve first. One can then filter crap out, works with all solvents but check solubility (it may be that the gak has a better solubility than pse in some solvents, in which case you can use ultra minimal solvent to isolate crap and leave pse undissolved) - one will see whether the pse dissolves first.
