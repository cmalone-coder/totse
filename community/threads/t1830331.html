Zinc sulfide shouldn't be too hard to make. Stoichiometrical amounts of zinc powder and sulfur powder(like the rocket fuel), pressed into a wide but shallow cylinder. Have this suspended a foot or so over a bucket of water with the open end down. Use electric ignition and it should all burn very rapidly firing the resulting zinc sulfide into the water where it can tyhen be filtered out. It probably is tougher than it sounds. Maybe just throwing zinc powder into molten sulfur would work too.
