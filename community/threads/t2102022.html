Re: Broken ROOR?
glass is real easy to fix if you have access to a furnace (kiln) and a MAAP or propane torch

the process goes like this. you heat the piece first to 1-1,200F degrees to reduce thermal shock, and quickly while its still hot you would use a MAAP or propane torch to spot fuse the broken piece together. then you'd place the whole thing back in the oven once more at 1500 degrees for an hour to anneal it.
