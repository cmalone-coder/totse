Re: Best place to buy a hookah?
Judging from your post, you dont know shit about good hookahs.

Strictly speaking of the hardware(not including the base).

You should look for things like: Metal composition of : the valves and down-stem, tower(brass, stainless steal , ect.)

Look for wide hose ports like those found on Kali M. Hookhas.
KM used to be the rave, but frankly, i don't find that the quality is as good as it used to be.
Stainless steal parts still corrode, particularly things like the "down stem".
Proper care and cleaning of your hookah is vital to good service.

Look for good, weld work.

Double hose hookhas are quite tricky, and leave more room for imperfect work.
Not to mention the decreased quality of smoke(tends to sour faster, if 2 people smoke).

Chinese manufactured hookhas like you show in your links have increased in quality within the last 10 years, but in my opinion still are not up-to-par with say a genuine KM hookah.

You should aim to spend a minimum of 100$ for a quality hookha.

Or else you are just throwing money way, its a crappy 50$ waste, which you will end up using ~4-5 times, and then either something will break, leak, or corrode.
