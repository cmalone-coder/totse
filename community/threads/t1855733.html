Re: Why is sucrose soluble in water?
sucrose is soluble becasue it is made of two polyalcohols. Sucrose is made of two more essential sugars fructose and glucose.

Both of these are 6-carbon chains with 5 hydroxyl groups hanging off them. The large amount of polar functional groups renders them soluble.

Remember that it isn't all about being ionic, there is an infinitely variable sliding scale of polarity.
