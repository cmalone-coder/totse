They do have recipes from pre-ban distilleries. The only difference is that alot of them had "filler" alcohols. Absinthe from the 1800's didn't give you any hallucinations just as todays absinthe doesn't as well.

Since the lift of the ban I have treated myself to a few different brands and it became my favorite spirit from the first drop that hit my tung.

I love the relaxation that comes as a result of the careful preparation of this refined drink. Tenderly balancing the sugar on the spoon, Lovingly pouring the ice cool water over the spoon and marveling at the near mystical transformation known as the loushe. Then upon consumption, the flavorful hints of anise and fennel hit your palate and your are suddenly swimming in the most refreshing alcoholic experience ever conjured...

Yeah, you could say that I like absinthe.

As for hallucinations, do what I do and just go out and buy some mushrooms. You won't find any visions of green faeries in the bottom of an absinthe glass. I do, however, feel much more alert and creative than with other alcoholic beverages. Could be placebo; or perhaps the result of millions of synaptic connections firing away into a euphoric manage et trois between the drink, my tung, and my mind. What ever it may be, I simply love it.
