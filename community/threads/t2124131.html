Drinking fast vs. Drinking slow
Alot of people drink slow and they say it gets you less drunk but you'll be drunk for longer. From my personal experience drinking slow gets me a shitty, drowsy, sloppy kinda drunk. When I first started drinking when I was about 10 me and my friends used to have to wait outside the liquor store for ages waiting for someone whod go in and get our drink for us and when we had it we always had to watch out for the pigs so we'd drink in alleys and drink it as fast as we could so thats how I started drinking and I before I tried cannabis I thought alcohol was the greatest thing known to man.

Anyway I kept the drinking fast habit well after I turned 18 but after a while I started drinking slowly and I noticed I was drinking way less and I lost my joy in drinking. Alcohol became complete shit. I didn't enjoy it anymore. At first I put it down to me getting older and dealing with the substance differently but then I decided to start drinking fast again and I got the real buzz back again. I can compare the difference now. I get drowsy and sloppy but at the same time I get euphoric and I get a buzz in my stomach a bit like ecstasy.

This has got nothing to do with the alcohol concentration because when I drink vodka or whiskey slowly I still don't get the euphoric effect but I do when I drink it fast so I'm not sure what it is. Maybe its all about overloading your body with alcohol as quick as you can and its the transition from having no alcohol in your belly to having loads that causes the euphoria. I'm know scientist though. Not yet anyway so I don't know.

I suppose its the old drug rush effect. Like the way your first joint of the day is always the best but even if you smoke 15 joints after that none of them compare to the first one. Same thing with heroin. I never fixed the shit but the first huff of smack of the day is always the best. Even if I do way bigger tokes afterwards they never compare to the first one. Crack is a different story though. Every hit of crack I do in a day seems to be equally good. I think its a matter of depressants and stimulants. Alcohol, smack and weed seem to be more on the downer side than upper side. I'm no pharmacologist though. Not yet anyway.
