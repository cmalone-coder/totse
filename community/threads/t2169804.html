Rolling or not?
Location : LA, Socal

Last night at a rave I took a small, blue pill, with the nike swoosh imprinted upon the back. It had a few white specks at the top of the pill, but besides that, that was it.

I took it with a bottle of water, assuming that it was ecstasy (going to be my first time.) After a while, the lights got more interesting and I felt a small burst of energy. I started chewing on a mint gum, but it tasted a little more intense, not the amazing taste I was told to expect. The mint flavor hitting my eyes through my mask, made my eyes tingle slightly. I swallowed my gum on accident and my stomach began to cramp. I felt full of energy and all I wanted to do was dance. I noticed things more than I normally would, like glasses on the floor, lightsticks on the ground, and people looking sick. I danced harder and longer than my sober friends, but got worn out after about 3 hours after my pill. However after about 3 or 4 hours I felt fine, not the come down I expected. Just a little tired. I danced for a few more hours and then went home. I ate a burger with a little difficulty through slight nausea, and then went to sleep without a problem... I will also mention, a very annoying tight pain where my sternum is located, much like a cramp, except more painful, and it lasted all night.

In addition to these effects throughout the night I felt rather hot, but I enjoyed the heat, I wanted to dance harder to sweat more. I felt very overheated, but not to an excessive point. I also consumed about 4-5 bottles of water from the hours of..9pm - 2am.

This morning, around 1 pm I feel tired, but not overly tired, I possess no after glow, and I feel tired, but fine.

My question to you totseans, is the following, was the pill I took ecstasy, or was it just caffeine or ephedrine?

where would i purchase this test kit?

Dancesafe.org has Marquis, Mecke, and Simon's reagents(http://www.dancesafe.org/testingkits/). Stupid Noob sells Marquis and Ehrlich reagents(http://beesafe.goshopper.net/).

For testing E if you were only going to get one kit Mecke is probably the best. Marquis has the ability to test a variety of compounds, simon's is for distinguishing MDMA and meth from MDA and amphetamine(not useful unless you already have a marquis or mecke). Ehrlich tests for ergot alkaloids(acid), unrelated to this thread but thought I'd mention it anyway.
