My Anti-School System Poem.
You call me a failure, I just laugh.
Making light of all the books on your back.
I've made shackles out of my past.
But you've made them out of your future.

A vine that flourishes free
Won't grow for you in criss-cross lines
A bird doesn't go straight from point A to point B.
Why formulate human minds?

Minds in classes fit for no other name.
With complex castes all their own.
What else are we, but products of shame
Fit for no other purpose, but to be grown.

One out of many, Out of many, one.
No war to fight, but battles to be won.
So tell me, as you say I failed.
Which of us is doomed to be jailed?
7:34 PM 5/13/2007
