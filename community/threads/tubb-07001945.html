Re: Burglarizing houses????????
Although its very risky, I absolutely loooove breaking into peoples houses (90% of the time theres no "breaking" involved). I realise I probably sound like a moron when I say that, but its the truth. Although I have gotten some nice items out of it, I find it more interesting just examining the way people live.


Anyway, as for tips, I always make a run to the bathrooms and check the medicine cabinets. I'd say 1/4 households has something that even if you don't find interesting, you're friends will enjoy.

Another tip, make sure you have a good escape route. The best escape routes are either 1) The way you came in, or 2) the closest door to the outside. So, stay as close to these as possible. If you say, know for a fact (not sure how you gained the info, but thats not the point) that person X's DVDs are on the opposite end of the house from any exit, don't bother. Unless you know the people you're stealing from are gonna be gone for a while. In short, stay on the same floor as your exit.

A couple odds and ends...
1) Look around briefly for a phone and answering machine, if there are several messages on the machine, its likely you can relax, as the people living there aren't gonna be home for some time (considering they haven't been home in a while).

2) Don't tell fucking anybody. If someone asks you where you got your shit, saying "I stole it" is quite alright, but don't fucking think about telling them when, or from who.

3) Don't wear your fucking football jersey, t-shirt with your name on it, anything like that. It may seem obvious, but I know a guy that wore his football jersey while in someones house, he got caught, he got away. Next day, he got arrested.

3) Be quiet. Not just so you aren't heard by other people, but so you can hear them. You aren't always gonna get a good visual of the house, but if you listen, you're gonna be able to tell if anyones home.

4) Don't be seen going in. Its ok to be seen leaving (not a good thing, but the situation is still a win). Some basic things to think about, even if you're entering the house from the front, approach it through the back, backyards are frequently used as shortcuts when walking around neighborhoods, so this is nothing suspicious. However, you walking through someones front yard, then around to the window on the side, looks very suspicious.

5) If you're gonna steal a vcr or something ("Nigga stole my tv!"), bring a backpack or something. No, not one of those huge utility things people use at schools, but something big enough to hold things, so that you can have your hands free.

6) Don't be stupid. Theres no way in hell you're gonna get away with stealing the 54 inch tv. I don't care how big your truck is, how many guys you have with you, its not happening. Don't even think about it.
