Activist dichotomy
I was talking to a friend the other day about environmental activism. This person belongs to a group that has been branded by the mainstream media as "radical". Our discussion focused on whether or not it was advantageous for these groups to destroy the so called "technical assets" of corporations in order to slow or halt the "capitalistic destruction" of our environment. My argument was that vandalism and/or destruction of machinery and developments of corporate entities only delayed the inevitable. That when groups such as the ELF wreck a bulldozer or torch a building. The victim of the act then replaces the damaged item with money from their insurance company.
This results in the corporation or whoever ultimately getting better equipment and/or more money than what the destroyed property was originally worth. While I did agree with my friend that just protesting the actions of corporations benefiting from the degradation or destruction of the environment was not enough. I still think there has to be a better solution than vandalism. I decided to post this in hopes that maybe the &Totse community as a whole. Could help come up with a way, in which we as a people could find a different means in which to address the subject of environmental destruction for profit. Any suggestions?
