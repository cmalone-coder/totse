Re: Hash Oil
The basic point of doing and extraction is to turn leaf or shitty bud into a smokeable/sellable product. Doing an extract on bud will yield a very high test product with slightly different psychoactive properties, but all in all will not be worth it because some resins will remain in the buds. If you extract your 1/8 you will end up with about 1/4 to 1/2 gram wicked oil, but collecting it from your evaporation pan will be a bitch. Find out where your dealer gets his buds, find the grower, offer to cook his leaf down in return for a share of the final product, don't blow up your house in the process, a lot of the extractions do not emphasise the dangers of eveporating a lot of flammable solvent.

Re: Hash Oil
DO NOT USE ACETONE! Yes, it is a great solvent. Yes, you can evaporate MOST of it. (Same for CCl4) But it is very toxic to the liver. I find it most wise to use ethanol. Isopropyl and Methyl alcohol are more toxic and taste bad. With ethanol, you will have adequate yields, and a toxicity your body can deal with if any traces are left after evaporation. Ethanol does not produce an off taste, either.
Acetone may very well be the culprit in the purported liver toxicity of Kava kava extracts as well.
Don't put a solvent in or on your body that you would not drink!
