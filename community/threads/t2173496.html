Re: Dumping over the phone...
If he can talk you out of it, it means you're unsure about doing this.

If you can breakup with him in person and stick with your decision, no matter what he says or does then you've made your decision.

The phone way works well but it leaves too many loose threads for him to pick up. He'll call, he'll write, he'll email, IM and telegraph you until he can see you and try to work things out.
Doing it in person tends to be a finality. You tell him it's over, say you're sorry, tell him it's not him but you (that's an old one), say sorry again, tell him your reasons, say sorry yet again and get the hell out of there.

Only if you're sure though.
