No matter how many trip reports you read, no matter how much you think you understand what it'd be like, you'll still be blown away.)

That. There's no way anyone could possibly properly describe what acid will be like. It's different for anyone so you're going to have your own journey.

When: Some time when you won't have any responsibility that day, or the day after.

Where: Preferably nature. Somewhere secluded where you can be by yourself. Avoid being around people who aren't tripping. It's a lot more comfortable that way. Bring plenty of water/fluids and food, preferably fruit.
