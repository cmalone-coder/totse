THIS is how I remember totse.
So we all stumbled across this site, most searching for illicit advice. But what actually caused you to join the forum?

Just searching through the archives, I found the first thread that I ever read. I came back for days just to read the updates in the thread:

http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=1546783

That very thread is responsible for NuclearWinter. If it wasn't for Pooktaro making that thread, I would not be here.

Posts #3, #8 and #9 in that thread was my first impression of the community. Whenever I read them, they always gauged a laugh... That was humor. None of these fucking "meme" bullshit a lot of these fags carry on with now. Memes came with the flood of '06.

Back then, it was a big deal to make a thread. If a newbie was to start a thread, they would usually put a lot of thought into it and took a lot of time. Back then all the newbies wanted to just fit in. But I'll tell you this much, a lot of the "noobs" back then were much better posters than 70% of the regulars today.

Notice there are no threads like that in totse any more.... That was totse for me, until opening registration of '06.

This is ultimately what I want to see totse achieve again..... Take the history lesson and read check that thread out afterwards.


So, how do you remember totse? Why are you here and how was it for you when you first joined?
