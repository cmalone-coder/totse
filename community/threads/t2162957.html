Re: hooking electricals up to a car battery.
car battery wouldn't work. as for the hydroponics $50 a year is probably about right depending on what type of system you're running because mainly all thats running is an air pump and or water pump and those don't take a lot of juice. the light is where you're power consumption comes from. i'm not sure about what a 400w would come out to cost wise but growing indoors in dirt with a 1000w hps and some fans set on thermostats averaged about $50 a month or so for a friend of mine.

I should add in too that you're not running for 18 hours a day all the time. We grew AK-47 and it was something like this

24 hours for clones for about 2 weeks under 150w hps
18 hours for veg. for about 30 days under 1000w hps
12 hours for flowering (budding) for 35-40 days under 1000w hps

but all strains are different and some flower quicker than others.

This. You can't save money by using a rechargeable battery. Charging and discharging use energy in the form of heat.


Quote:
Originally Posted by scovegner
.. And that running a 500w load off of a car battery will run it totally flat in about 40 minutes? ..
And this.

Running a normal car battery flat a few times in a row will destroy it as well. Standard lead-acid batteries don't like to be taken down past 70% of their capacity regularly.


Slacker is also correct in that running 1000w worth of lights for 20 hours a day is probably about $50 a month. He was saying that running the pumps costs $50 a year, which in my opinion is probably a low figure.

If you're using 20kwh a day, at 10 cents per kwh that's $2 per day. 12kwh (the 40 day cycle) per day would cost $48 for those 40 days.


Just think yourself lucky you're paying half what I do over in the UK for electricity...
