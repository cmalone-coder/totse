Inspiration coming in from the outside, as influences often do (unless they're ingested), are invariably poignant, but not so stringent in sources.

What I mean is that one does not require a sexual, if only in thoughts, relation to the muse of the moment, and that inspiration can take many forms. A great deal of my inspiration, for example, comes from nature, but more frequently from nature in its relation to humans. How terrains producing wheat, and big dumb animals flourished inspiring humans to attempt to conquer the world only to find this same area become desert inspiring humans to, still, conquer the world. The balance that un-historic (shouldn't be read as pre-historic) civilizations such as !Kung and yanamamo have obtained with the world creating no desire to kill those with different gods, or to expand their territory in search for forms of wealth to satisfy their scarcity. How human life in its entirety seems analagous to a vast ocean of currents, full of diversity and cycles, beautiful and endless, while society in its contemporary seems more comporable to a tornado, short-tempered and shorter-lived.

That being said my girlfriend inspires me like none other, and it is one of the reasons that I love her so. Not just creatively either, but in life. If it wasn't for her I may not be here as my all day, every day, habit of drinking was begging to finally slit my throat (not a drop in three months, and my drinking had reduced to a few times a month since I had met her leading up to completely stopping.) She has inspired me to leadership roles on my campus; inspired me to pick up old, better habits of tae kwon do; inspired me to quit smoking.

Yet, it still seems that I haven't answered your question, doesn't it? In terms of creativity, yes, she inspires me, though I haven't written much directly about her except for one unfinished song in lyrical form and finger picks on my guitar, but a plethora of philisophical prose has been produced as progeny of her propensity at making me perceive my potential possibilities.

She inspires me in many ways: as a midwife to brilliant thought as she is devastatingly intelligent, as a means of expression as she is editor of an expressive, artistic, mostly literary magazine, as a goal to win her fancy as her beautiful smile is something well worth coaxing out, and, most importantly, she makes me believe in myself.

She's truly amazing, and I'm really sorry that none of you can have her.
