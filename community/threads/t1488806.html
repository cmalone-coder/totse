Re: am i wrong by doing this???
Jealousy is, among other things, suspicion of the faithfulness of one's partner, or over-protectiveness of one's possessions. While a girlfriend is hardly a possession, the same theory applies (she is, after all, "my" girlfriend).

He said that he's bothered by the fact his friends will look at her in a sexual way, to the point where they very well may start trying to get her to break up with him and date them. That touches on the first definition. If he were totally comfortable with his girl being loyal regardless of what those guys try, this wouldn't be an issue.

I'm not totally positive the second definition is relevant, but it seems very possible, so I mentioned it. What else would you call it if a guy didn't want anyone else looking at his girl when she was showing off of her own accord? Overprotectiveness seems like a good fit, doesn't it? That's another form of jealousy.

Also note that I said it was weak. It certainly sounds like he's handling everything well, not letting it get the best of him, which is how to make things work out in the end. If jealousy could ever be considered a positive, it's to the extent he's showing it in this situation.
