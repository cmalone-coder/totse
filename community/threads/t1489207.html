There's ups and there's downs. People, for some reason, tend to dwell on the bad and ignore the good. Here, this is more general than specifically to relationships. But if you're outlook is bleak, try some of these, hopefully at least one will help.

1.)Get rid of negative people in your life. If this is not possible, always feign happiness around them and perhaps itll rub off.

2.)Throw out and stop listening to depressing music. Ive experienced the power of music and its ability to influence you in a good way or make you want to go smash someones face. Try to listen to the early years of The Beatles, hell, even Black Flag can be somewhat uplifting.

3.)Get rid of the Feng Shui! Yeah, you know that glow of newness when you move into a new house or room or when you redecorate your room? The glow makes you feel happy, makes you enjoy the space. If youre no longer enjoying your space, change the furniture around. It might sound petty but it really does get out the bad vibes.

4.)Dont depend on people or things to make you happy. Namely the opposite sex and drugs. Sadly, you cant depend on anyone to be there forever and you have to turn within to bring true happiness. It might be a good filler at the time but sex and drugs do not have good effects on you if youre using them to make you feel better. Yes, sex does make you healthy and I believe that certain drugs can help you on the road of enlightenment but using them as a comfort is dumb.

5.)Strengthen your chi. Meditate, pick up a hobby, explore the world around you. The more time you have alone the more time youll be able to discover who you are. When you figure yourself out, youll be able to pick apart emotions and feeling and have a more objective view on why you act the way you do. Ignoring your feelings and desires can only hurt you.

6.)Accept the bad things you cant change. If theres something specific that is getting you down, that you cant help, acceptance is the road to serenity. It wasnt meant to be the way you wanted, get over it, and concentrate on something else. Something better.

7.)Take care of yourself. This may sound like a given but people dont have as good hygiene as they should. If you time out a schedule of things to do everyday, youll have more self esteem and confidence. Confidence=happy. Take a shower, exercise, eat healthy, wash your face. Give yourself a pedicure! And guys before you skip this one, try it. Your feet soaking in warm water feels very good.

8.)Find something with meaning in your life. Anything. Mine is my little brother. I love him so much and there isnt one thing on the planet that means more to me them him. Just being around him has made me happy when I was upset. Has given me a reason to keep fighting when I wanted to give up. Find something youre passionate about and think about it when youre being a killjoy.

9.)Take a step outside the box. I am proof that a couple days vacation can have a huge impact on your life. Get out of environment for a little while. Let all your problems escape your mind and go somewhere relaxing and fun.

10.)Dont dwell on how it used to be. Yes, we have a lot of great memories but sitting around and wallowing is only restraining us from going out and making new happy memories. Its true, things can always be worse and you should be happy about your current status. Its also true that thing can always be better and you can strive for that
