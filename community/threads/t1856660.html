take a strip of foil 6 inch long and few inches wide. fold long ways a few times. dull side up, crease long ways. put shard on one end, pass lighter underneath quickly to melt and stick onto foil. tilt foil with shard on up side. hold the other end, stick tube in mouth an inch over shard. pass lighter underneath it so it starts to smoke. you have to be kind of quick. if you taste it too strong, remove the lighter for a few seconds, if you barely taste it at all, you will get a small hit, or you can leave lighter under it a bit more. oh, and inhale through the tube.
