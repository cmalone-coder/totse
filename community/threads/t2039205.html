I hate to rain on this parade but it sounds all too familiar to me. Granted this girl has shown her unwaivering love for you, but lets think on the shitty side of things for a minute, its where my mind tends to linger.

Now this girl who has a boyfriend who treats her like shit, as you say, seems willing to break it off/cheat with you. Is this something you could handle over seas in your mind? I have lots of friends who were in similar situations and came back home to find either their girlfriend is engaged to someone else, or seeing someone else and just couldn't find the right words to say in a letter that Steve down the road was "there for her."

I would being willing to bet the best thing you could say to her before you ship off is that her happiness is all that matters to you and you want to be that happiness. However, you don't know what is going to happen, but you will use that thought of making her happy and making up for every lost moment you were apart to just see her smile when you get back.

Granted this is a bit of a guilt trip, but you are leaving her to her own thoughts, instead of, "When I get back baby were going to get married and fuck like rabbits."

Big plans made in advance that won't come to be in the next 6 months or so put strain on people and they start to question what they are doing with their' life.

It sucks but leave it semi-vaque (vague?). When you're over there, write her whenever you get a chance and tell her how much you think of her, but don't make it a giant love letter. Be sure to leave some mystery in what you're talking about, like, "Oh, today I saw this beautiful sunset/I saw the most amazing mosque... I'll tell you in more detail next time we talk."

This is my advice, take it or leave it.
