Why Stores are stupid
This may be more about the stores and cheating them than the games, but its important for gamers to know if they get a crappy game they don't want or something like that.
---------------------------------------------
You know how stores have that policy that you cannot return the games if they are opened?
Well I have found a way around this.
You may not have noticed but if you attempt to return a game (which didn't run, or really sucked), they wont let you return it, but they offer you the option to exchange it for an unopend copy. Take the exchange and walk out. Return at a later time or a diffrent shift. Then return the exchanged game. It's surprising how companies will bother attempting to prevent returns but allow a loophole.
