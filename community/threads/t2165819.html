Re: Bout to try salvia for the first time, few questions...
1) use a bong
2) regular lighters work fine if you keep the flame going
3) take massive rip, hold in for AT LEAST 20 seconds and repeat. ignore the chills and the "rushing" sweaty feeling and keep hitting.


4) I've only ever broken through twice. The first time I was ashing my bowl thinking nothing was happening but I heard someone say, "give him a short, sharp shock" from Pink Floyd's Us and Them. I looked to see where the voice was coming from and I was laying on a hospital bed with doctors standing around me, one holding a defibrillator. I knew he was going to shock me so I ran out of the room and some lady took me down the hallway and took me into a storage closet. She told me she could show me all the answers I'd been seeking and proceeded in turning on a strange machine that appeared. The machine was very stereotypical like a cartoon machine with pistons, gears and levers and once the machine started, I was back in my buddy's bedroom I started at. I thought it was over but all of a sudden I heard the humming of the machine and the walls started to compress and crush me into them like a trash compactor. This sounds scary but I was laughing uncontrollably. While being "crushed" against the wall it turned into a painting of the English countryside with grassy hills, yellow flowers and castles in the distance. The compression pushed me through the wall into the painting [it was soooooo beautiful] and I once again returned to the room where I spent the next 10 minutes or so in a daze and then came good.

The second attempt was an outdoor trip I can't remember much of, unfortunately, but it came down to rolling around in the dirt drooling all over myself while the fallen dead leaves were trying to tell me something I can't remember.

The cool thing about salvia is that it is Earth shattering and unbelievably intense but you have no consciousness to say that the world you find yourself in isn't right, so you think everything is normal until you snap out of it 20 min later and go, "Whaaaaa!?!?!?!"

30 secs....not 3...you'll only get mild effects if you just hold it for three and exhale even if you continue to take more puffs...

And holy fuck, this this such a commonly asked question...5 steps to a successful trip:

1) Smoke it in a bong (or pipe, preferably bong)

2) Hold the lighter (torch or bic will both work) over the salvia for the entire time you're hitting the bong.

3) Hold the toke for thirty seconds (count in your head, or get someone to count for you) you only need one (take a second if you can, I doubt you will)

4) Get someone to take the bong from you, in a few seconds it'll probably cease to exist.

5) Salvia is NOT a party drug
