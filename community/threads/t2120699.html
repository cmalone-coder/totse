That is telekinetics.

In response to the OP, I would like to hear your thoughts in their full detail if you can expound any further.

This is a very strange topic. I strive to be as rational as possible in evaluating concepts, but there is absolutely nothing rational or empirical about my fun little theory on telepathy. I do think that it is possible that there is some kind of currently unknown flow of information that interplays with experienced reality that we can access in certain states of mind. In the past few months, I have been very thoughtful of the coincidences and synchronicities that have occurred for me, and it seems that for some reason they are coming closer and closer together. Random notions or hints of concepts seem to pop into my head, chancing a few minutes later to be the subject of the talk radio show that comes on when I get into my car or the topic of conversation that I am approached with by a co-worker as I walk in the door. The other week I was waking up for school when I thought to myself: "You know what would be nice? If I brought in donuts for the whole class, for no occasion other than for fun." When I got to class donuts in hand, it just so happened to be my professor's birthday (he had never mentioned it before, and verified it with his ID at my amazement). Stuff like this just seems to happen to me too often for there to be no mechanism at work but coincidence; I'm not saying or thinking that it is "god" or faeries or anything like that, but it is just fun (although not very intellectually principled) to toy with explanations of these phenomena that lie in the gaps.
