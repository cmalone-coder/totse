I feel for ya bro, I've been battling opiates for years now. As a matter of fact, I just ran out of suboxone (as planned) so here goes my 3rd 'real' kick.

Don't expect miracles. Acute methadone withdrawal can last up to 5-6 weeks depending on how much you were using and how long. Generally, it will get 'the worst' between weeks 1-2.... by week 3 it should be easing up. On average, most people feel much better and start to sleep within a month. After the acute w/d, you're gonna be dealing with PAWS.... so you'll have to stay strong. For now, just hang in there. Smoke, drink, hang out with non-users.... just do whatever to pass the time. Remember that if you slip up and abuse now, you will warp yourself back almost to the beginning of W/D.
