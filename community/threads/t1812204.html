Re: pentagram
a quick history of the pentagram.

The pentagram has been used for a long time, but it really gained popularity with Pythagoras who saw it as mathematically perfect. It was used by other groups as well, but not as the main symbol.

Christians started using the pentagram, it represented the five wounds of christ. Although rare compared to other christian symbols it was worn in the crusades as a symbol of protection.

In the mid 1800's a picture of Baphomet (a creature, most likely purely symbolic with a goat head) was drawn with a point up pentagram on it's forehead. This was the first time goathead and pentragram come together.

Bephomet has mixed origins, it's possibly a symbol of wisdom or a pagan idol (this is unlikely, although it has become a pagan idol), but was never considered Satan.

Bephomet became connected with Satan when in the 20th century LaVey created the goathead inside an inverted pentagram. In LaVey satanism, "satan" is symbolic and not considered an actual creature.

Around the same time as LaVey, Wicca was created and took the symbol as it's own. A bunch could be writen about the wiccan use but it generally represents the 5 elements and is used to invoke spells. Inverted it can be used as invoking earthly spells (point up used for spiritual spells) or as a symbol of rank. Different Wiccan groups have different views, but none of them link it with Satan like LaVey.
