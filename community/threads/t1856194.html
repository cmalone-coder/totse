Re: Acetone standard -> Acetone dry
make sure you have purchased 100%acetone,,,,go and buy a kilo of epsom salts in the soil supplement section of,,hmm you must have hardware shops in india but now that i think of it i never saw one,,,,,anyway,,,get a baking dish and spread the epsom over it evenly..then place in oven and bake at 300c for 3 hours,,,allow to cool,,,,then tip out of baking dish and crumble into knuckle size pieces,,,store pieces in dry airtight container,,,,,,,,,,

get the can of acetone and place a small handfull (of the dried epsom) per litre into the acetone,,put lid on and swirl around for 3 mins,,,then allow to sit for an hour,,,filter acetone just before use,,,,,thats how you get dry acetone mr atome
