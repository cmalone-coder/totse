Yay...

Well, here's the story. During summer, I did acid and had a trip that kind of woke me up. I realized how retarded I was drinking/smoking everyday, while my parents were working hard to keep my stomach filled. My brother is just coming to an age of learning (4), and I just don't want him to be any bit like me.

Anyway, in the trip I was basically presented with my worthless life, and how I got by classes with the lowest of the grades, basically a huge reality slap. I realized that the reason I couldn't see myself ten-twenty years from now, was because I'd be dead before that even happened. As soon as that thought came in, I ended my trip with a downer.

After that incident, I basically avoided most of my friends. I never went out, I worked, I babysat, and I played computer games. My friends are starting to realize that I'm putting distance in between, but I really don't want be akward between us later on. Sometimes I just get sent on guilt trips and feel shitty later in my house.

Just now, a friend who I hung out with everyday talked with me, and said "i want to smoke, but i guess you don't want to."

Half these friends are good kids, I don't want to let go of them. Nor do I really want to touch the thing again.. I thought maybe this could be a phase, but maybe it'd be better for my life if it weren't.
