Yup so every summer there is this music festival held in this place called vasa park, anyways its a privately owned park so no cops are allowed inside and this place is filled with any and every drug anyone could want. Personally i like to go to just chill and listen to all the music but this year was quite different from the past three summers. First off friday night i ran into some crazy raver kids from chicago who had the BEST molly i have personally ever had.... night continues on for a while see some other local people i knew from the area... start drinking and of course im not mixing drinks and taking any that pass my way. this is when the night gets interesting turns out one or many drinks i drank had been dosed so around midinight i was tripping balls, rolling my face off and this is when an outrageous lightning storm started to happen (anyone from chicago knows what im talking about from friday night) and this was quite possibly the most inspiring / awestruck moment i have ever had in my life. it was like everything was perfect and i was in the center of the universe, i was feeling all the energy all around me and i felt like i held the key to all the secrets... needless to say Blottopia is a bad ass music festival and if anyone in the midwest loves festivals i highly recomend, its held in vasa park in elgin illinois every july, just that i would share that, oh and i think i took anywhere from 3-6 hits of acid but i dunno cuz i didnt see who dosed me haha
