This thread seems to be a lot about finding tobacco alternatives because of fear of addiction. Well here is my philosophy on addiction: Your mindset changes as you become more comfortable with drugs, but so does your understanding.

I remember being 8 and saying to myself that I would never smoke a cig (we all probably said that). Then that changes to just one a week, then just one a day, then two a day, and so on.

I use to say weed is the only drug I'll do (most non-druggies say the same thing). That changed to anything but speed, meth, heroin, and cocaine. That changed to anything but heroin. Now? Well, who knows what I'll do.

To the non-druggie who doesn't understand drugs, doing something like coke seems really scary and stupid. Eventually you learn that just doing it once or twice in your life probably isn't going to kill you. Because of that, you don't mind doing it just once or twice. That progresses until you reach a point where you realize that if you can keep yourself under control and not become a crazy crack head, then you could probably do coke whenever you want and still live a normal non-sucking-dick-for-coke lifestyle. In the end, you are still rationalizing your drug habits. Its difficult to not let it progress too far.

Moral of the story: Before you've done the drug you can't make a fair judgment because you don't understand it. After you've done the drug you realize that your understanding of drugs changes and your "self-control rules" change with it. Its very difficult to make a personal law that is set stone because your old personal laws have fallen down and been replace with more relaxed laws (remember when you were 8?).
