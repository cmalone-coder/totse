Well, I have students that vary in age, and I use different methods with children and adults. With children I use a self-defense approach, where if they get attacked, chances are they will lose because they can not properly use their techniques yet, due to their young age. With adults I teach with a more "seek and destroy" attitude. I teach for them to always be the attacker if they feel threatened. I dont believe in all this self-defense bull usually, only with kids, cause I dont need them to go kick someone in the neck for cutting in line at school. With adults on the other hand, they know where they are threatened and wether or not it is a probably cause to attack. I teach a lot of power kicks. I have classes devoted to strength in their strikes. Main kicks are Jump Round/Side/Straight Back. If you can master these kicks and get them to take down a Bag (weighs 250 pounds) then you will be invincible if you are the attacker and have little chance of being injured.
