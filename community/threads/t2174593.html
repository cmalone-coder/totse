Make sure you wait at least 24 hours after your last dose of meds to take it. If you are dependent, it will cause precipitated withdrawals (look it up). Even if you're not, it might suck.

Overall the likelihood of you getting off on it is low, but when I had a light oxy addiction (30-40mg or so per day) the FIRST times I got suboxone it got me pretty high. It IS a strong drug but it works differently on opiate users and also the more you use it the less it will produce a "high". Plug it for better and faster effects than sniffing.

It WILL decrease the effect of your meds for about 24-48 hours after taking it. Your meds should still relieve you of some pain but they will just feel weaker. Don't even bother taking other opiates sooner than 12-18 hours and even then anything less than 24 is pretty much a complete waste.
