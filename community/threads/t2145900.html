Kitchen Woes
I am a restaurant bitch.

Washing dishes, legions of them. 10 hours of it! Woe betwixt all ye who enter here. I hate my dishwashing "pit" because its in the fucking kitchen. No separate room, no high powered blasting, just three sinks. I have figured out the perfect routine, and now its just auto pilot.

Going through hundreds of songs that I know in my brain. A man can do a lot of thinking during his time spent scrubbing.

I've noticed that the restaurant breeds scum and villainy. The people I work with have been there for years, with no hope or advancement, beyond the realm of food preparation.

I work with a opiate addict. He gets real anxious on Fridays because he has extra money around. He started tripping out real hard, craving those good hydros. Yelling and screaming about how we all needed to go faster, faster!!!

These are just the simple musings of a petty underling.


So who else hates the job they have to work? Simple mindless garbage?

Particularly looking for responses from fellow food servicemen.
