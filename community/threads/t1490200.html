Young people get sores/blisters in their mouths from exam stress, bad diet and herpes. Syphillis causes a canker (chancre) to form with an ulcerated base that takes a while to heal, either in the mouth or on the genitals, although it's less common these days. STD's are common, the biggest ones at the moment being herpes, chlamydia and gonorrhoea.

After a new boyfriend, i usually go on a course of antibiotics at the first sign of itch/soreness/unusual discharge. It's life, and it's usually a small deal if you treat it early.

AIDS is a different story and kind of wrecks everything.
