Re: Shameless Plugs.
Thanks yet again. The skulls were quite a weird thing to try and do, and in real life they're only postcard size or smaller. Been wanting to give that technique a try again but haven't had the tools to do it, and anyone can make some seriously interesting prints by doing the same thing.

The two brown skulls are the prints I'm talking about, being reversed, the one in sort-of-colour's a drawing just with an artliner, masking tape, a few cotton buds with black, white and orange ink.

Anyway, the printing. "Intaglio" they call it, not sure about the spelling. anyone with a small press or ghetto homemade heavy roller thing should give it a shot. You get a drawing or start from scratch, doesn't matter, but scratch it onto a sheet of acetate. If you're in school or otherwise it's the clear plastic they make photocopies for overheads with. Anyway, scratch it on, slather it up with thick water-based ink and gently rub it off with different materials.

You could just use a rag, but those two were started with gauze and finished with cotton. Mabe a bit of newspaper. It's like the opposite to relief printing, all the texture and shading comes from the material, you can get some really crazy results. Forgot to add, you print it onto WET paper that's sat around for a good hour or so.

Only works for about 4 or five prints though. Once you flatten out the scratches, it's stuffed. No idea why I wrote all that. Give it a shot though.
