An odd question
What do you guys think is better? To have a general, multipurpose weapon that works well for a variety of purposes (hunting, self defense, staving off alien invasions or bands of marauding politicians), or a collection of specialized weapons that are superb in a certain setting but don't shine very brightly outside of it (a rifle or rifles for hunting, a revolver or automatic for self-defense, a shotgun for belligerant trespassers and the like)?

Myself, I don't think that there's any one Super-Firearm outside of James Bond movies, but having enough weaponry to take up three rooms in your house is taking the term specialization to its illogical extreme... not to mention the Boy Scout Motto "Be Prepared".

Thoughts?

Re: An odd question
Now I'm definitely not obsessed with the P90 but it does perform certain mission specific functions very well. While its true that you won't make any 800 meter kills with it, at CQB range, it goes through body armour like a hot knife through butter. Not much stopping power, but double and triple taps will do the trick. Overpenetration is not much of an issue when it comes to the 5.7 bullet. With the 50 round magazine, that translates to between 15 and 25 targets before reloading (or going to a sidearm).

Problems include: (rumor) If dropped on its butt, the magazine jams up and becomes worthless.
The lack of choice in optics. The factory relfex sight is adequate but some may have other preferances.

Desert Eagle is worthless in combat. Put more than 50 rounds through it and it'll refuse to cycle. Time to go home and dunk it in the tank. And then detail strip it. .50AE puts out a lot of gunk.

Zero and rlpinca are definitely on the right track. The best gun is the one that YOU shoot best, not the one with the biggest bullet or the fastest full auto rate of fire (usually inflated anyway). If a .22mag purse pistol is whats best for you, then use it.

Remember, people: Shot placement is crucial!
