Re: School Pride...
School doesn't exist to screw things up for you. The people that have "school spirit" don't see school as a negative thing. They see it as a building block for their future; it's a building full of friends and offers interesting opportunities.

There is a lot of busy work in school, but it's there for a purpose. Different people learn in different ways, some people need that repetition to understand the concepts involved.

Some teachers have attitude, but they are there to help you. Teachers aren't administrators, they're there to help you.

Security guards are a terrible necessity, but that isn't the fault of the school. If security guards aren't needed, then they aren't employed.

The only reason not to wear school clothes are if they're ugly and cheap. If it's a nice shirt, then there's no reason not to wear it. I have a hoodie from my high school; I've never worn it, but it's sitting in a closet. Clothes are just a question of personal taste. Some people take unthinkable levels of pride in their school because they feel that they've accomplished something. University students are big on this pride; I think it's because it dominates so much of their life.

A pep rally can be a lot of fun, but it depends on the school and the people involved. It all depends on your attitude and the attitudes of the people involved. Fire and loud music often helps. Your involvement, and the involvement of your friends, will dictate your level of interest.

It's simply a choice, you can get involved in your school or you can keep your head down and slip through it. You don't have to get into a pep rally or wear school clothing to get involved, it can be as simple as joining a club or going to a party.
