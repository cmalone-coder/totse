Have you talked to your doctor?

Bupropion is metabolized to hydroxybupropion, which is a CYP2D6 inhibitor. CYP2D6 breaks down mirtazapine (remeron), so you maybe want to start with a very low dose of mirtazapine.

I'd say, if you're going to take it, and I don't recommend it without a psychiatrist's (or at least a gp's) approval, you should take the bupropion early morning-midday and the mirtazapine right before bed. And start with a low dose of each, particularly the remeron.
