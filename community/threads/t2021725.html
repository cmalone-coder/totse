Re: extracting deet
Don't listen to them, half of the previous posts will get you killed or seriously injured.

Deet is n,n Diethyl meta toulamide.

I know what you mean though, I've seen an old can of insect repellent that was 45% deet.

Look up MSDS "deep woods off" It is the highest deet repellent concetration commonly sold that I know off.

It's 19% Deet, 50-60 percent alchohol, 18-25 % various small chain alkanes, and some other junk.

Out of the bottle, the alkanes should vaporize immidiatly. Let it stand and the alchohol will start to evaporate off.

Several options are availivle. One is to let the liquid dry until crstals start to form, bottle it and then reuse that. The other is it let it dry all the way and disolve in ether or dichloromethane and then perform an acid/base extraction.

Another option if you just want the spray to stay on longer is to mix the dry deet with a liquid glove product. I'm not sure if this will work, but if it does you'll get 4-6 hours garanteed protection.

Your non-deet options would be to wear fabric head to toe that has been treated with DDT or Pyretherin.
