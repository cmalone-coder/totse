Re: Hairstyles for a guy with a babyface?
I want to believe you're joking but it seems like you're serious? So rubbing your hair with wool adds volume? And then add wax? Do I do all this when it's dry or when it's wet? Can you recommend some specific products?

Your hair has to be as it can be. Some good products? I use "Rockstar: Classic Rock". Any thick wax that's good for molding, shaping and holding will give your hair volume. Get one that's matte, you don't want greasy Greek looking hair.

Wool definitely adds volume, rub your hair gently in a circular motion making sure to get all of it. First thing you'll notice is you look like you've been electrocuted because your hair will be all over the place, even standing up vertically. Then you need to start putting a bit of product in it to weigh it down. The next step is learning to style it, you can figure that out I guess.

Basically you get a 'dirty rockstar' look. It will lead to natural dreadlocks if you keep it going, you want it a step before that where it's just crazy messy.

Finally, if you're going out and got your hair how you like it and want it to stay that way, give it a really quick spray with hairspray.

We used to call this "filthing", and my hair was ridiculously thick at the time and I naturally have really thin blond hair.
