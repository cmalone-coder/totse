Fucked up shit happens when I smoke weed.
Ever since this one time about a year and a half ago, crazy things happen when I smoke pot. The first time it happened, my buddy and I were ripping a bowl in my car while cruising around the suburbs. I stopped at a stop sign, toked the bowl, and coughed my ass off. It was some lemon-skunk type shit. After a couple good coughs, my vision went blurry and I heard a buzzing sound like a cicada (you know, that gayass fly).

Now, however, the effects are even more intense. Even with poopy weed, it happens. When I smoke some chronic, it's like my head is a gong and someone just fucking hit me with a mallet. Then it's sounds like "OM" with some distortion/reverb. This is fucking LOUD, guys. Vibrations go from my dome to my extremeties and back like a Slinky. Vision is like a tunnel and blurry - but sometimes kaleidoscopic. I can't speak at all or think, just emotion.

Anyone experience this before? Is this dissociation or ego death? This happens like 6/10 times that I smoke, as long as it's potent or I take the first, green hit. All kinds of bud, different dealers and methods have done this.
