There's something you're not telling us. I wish I could accuse you of hiding something, because then we might get the whole truth.. but, sadly, chances are as good as not that you don't even know what it is.

You're 32? Your initial post gave me the impression that you were 18 or 19. Even if you do look 26 and never tell a guy your real age, that's still a point where most guys will expect certain behavioral traits out of women, ones that you don't appear to show (such as a more grounded mindset). When he doesn't see that out of you, he may think, "Damn, she's never going to change, fuck this." Then he bolts.

Is this your fault? Not necessarily. Most people have their prejudices, and when those fuck things up, there's not much you can- or should- do. Let them float off to someone else who will make their bullshit-laden minds happy.

However, if you're into drugs and such (if not, skip the next couple of paragraphs, I'm only going by John's post and assuming for the moment it refers to something I didn't see).. I'm not about to start lecturing, but the simple fact is that a 22-year-old who does drugs and a 32-year-old who does drugs are going to be seen in very different lights. Kids (and to say even a 22-year-old is a kid in this case is being generous) who do drugs are expected to clean up eventually and straighten out. That's why partners or potential partners often dismiss that part of their lives, unless it's out of control.

You, on the other hand, are a grown-up. Even if they think you're in your mid-20's, getting high is much less accepted for you than it was when you were younger. Weed alone would be a turn-off; not many guys will want a 32-year-old stoner girlfriend unless they themselves are into that. (Note that it doesn't matter how much or little weed you smoke- you'll often find that you're assumed to be a stoner if you're still getting high at your age.)

I'm not saying you should change how you act, but if you're trying to figure out why other people react to you in a certain way, you have to take into account the different ways in which they might view you, your behavior, your activities, et al. You may find that, to get what you want, you'll either have to change some of the things you do or search very hard for someone that will like you as you are. But that's a level of introspection you have to take upon yourself to reach.
