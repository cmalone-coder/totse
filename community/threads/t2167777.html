Advice for first 'cid trip
My buddy just recently got an acid hook, something I've wanted to do for years. I've done plenty of stuff in the meantime, DXM, e, weed, salvia, even LSA. While it's not the greatest idea, it is kind of important to me that I do it tomorrow night. Problem is I have bullshit for school the next day, but not until around 11. So what I want to do, is take it around 9 or 10, enjoy the trip for a few hours, then go to sleep.

I've heard it's impossible to sleep on acid though, so would popping some Benadryls or a couple Lunesta be enough to take care of that? I also have been told that tripping on LSD can be really physically tiring, so do you think I will be too pooped out the next day? I really only need to be able to stay awake, and give a couple basically impromptu speeches, it's nothing that big. The event goes till 8, so as long as I stay away and participate minimally, it should be fine.

So basically the plan would be dose around 9, trip till sometime between 12 and 2 when I take the sleep aid, get around 8 hours of sleep. That seems like enough sleep to me, but I don't know how the factors of having come down from a trip will factor into my fatigue. I also do need to be with it enough to at least sort of interact with the people at the event.

Is this a safe, and plan that will work?
