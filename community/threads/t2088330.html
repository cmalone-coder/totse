Re: Found some 10mg OC.
Chewing it works, but dissolving it in a little water works better. When you chew it, some gets stuck to your teeth. Not only does it help the taste to stay in your mouth, but some of the oxycodone stays there to. Also, if you just drink it, theres hardly any taste.

OP, I suggest you dissolve it in the water. You will start feeling subtle effects within a few minutes, and a peak in about 30-45 minutes. There will be some particles that don't dissolve. Don't worry about them, they're filler and time-release. The stuff that you want dissolves very easily in water. Crush it up on a piece of paper and put it in the water. Swirl it around for ~5 seconds and down it.

Don't redose until you feel the effects kind of level out. Since it's your first time with any opiates (right?), no one can predict how high you will get. With 20mg you will get nice and high. It is possible that you get nausea when you start to come down. If that happens, try nibble on some crackers or something. Weed also helps a lot.
