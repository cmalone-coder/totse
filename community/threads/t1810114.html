Evil is just as viable a route for omnipotent and omniscient behavior as it's just the animalistic characteristics, whereas good is defined by altruistic characteristics. Altruism might be the ultimate value in your mind, but I'm sure there are plenty of other people who define the ultimate value as being in touch with your base desires. Neither one is really good or evil, we just give them misleading labels for our own peace of mind.

God A: Promotes benevolence, forgiveness, loving, caring, generosity, humbleness, abstinence.

God B: Promotes selfishness, grudges, distrust, lust, greed, hate, indulgence, pleasure, pride.

While I'm sure most people would be eager to label God A as good, I find it quite easy to label them both as good, and both as evil.

God A is evil for worshipping it and adhering to its rules would be denying yourself of your primal urges and some of the more satisfying areas of life, you are being denied life experience in the name of a god. They're good though for promoting qualities which bring humans together.

God B is evil for worshipping it and adhering to its rules would possibly force you into a small and barely trustworthy group of friends, and likely chisle away at certain social bonds, denying you life experience in their name. They're good for the reasons that it makes each individual adherent of the faith feel complete within themselves rather than of their relationships; their urges satisfied with no restrictions met, so no part of them needs to reside in the subconscious; their mind is whole and it is all their own. There wouldn't be much personal physical suffering as people would be driven to satisfy the wants for pure self pleasure.
