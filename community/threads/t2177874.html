The Take Down Thread
Post a take down fighting move that will put an end to the beginning of any encounter. Literally just ending it on the spot.

First, always get the person to hit you first, get in their face but be ready to expect anything. Wait until you have hands on you first. I'm going to explain this assuming you attack from his rightside (your left)

Scenario: A punch or a shove coming your way.

Step 1: Step to side, using outside hand to "clap" the person's hand out of the way.

Step 2: Now use your inside hand to elbow in the temple, then the nose.

Step 3: Use your outside hand, and place the point of your elbow directly down between where the bicep and forearm meet.

Step 4: Go under the person's arm, and place your hand behind his back.

Step 5: Get behind him, with your right foot back.

Step 6: Push to ground.

With that technique you can do a number of things to the person before sending them to the ground, with the way you have their arm wrapped, you can break the arm in several ways.
