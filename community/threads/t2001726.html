I really dislike the anonymity of the rep system. If someone's going to give you rep, be it positive or negative, they should own up to it.

There is no ability to give anonymous rep. If it wasn't displayed before, it was a display template problem -- the identity of the person giving the reputation has always been recorded.

Currently reputation is totally meaning-free. I'm still gathering data. Once I have enough then I need to analyze the data that's been gathered to see what, if anything, should be done with it.

Also, what makes you think that a negative rep that says "Cunt!" in the comment will count negatively towards someone's rep? Or if someone tries to do some rep-boosting (i.e. give me some rep and I'll give you some), what makes you think that either person will have an increased rep afterwards?

There are lots of ways to score the data...

Basically, once I get enough data, I'll write an algorithm that comes up with a total rep score based on multiple factors, not just a sum of the points given. Then you folks tell me if you see someone with a rep that seems completely out of line with their character, then I adjust the algorithms. Repeat until everyone agrees that the reps shown more or less represent how helpful or unhelpful someone is.

IF we get to that point -- where we all agree that the reps shown are an accurate reflection of reality -- THEN I might do something with the rep data. Until then, it's meaning-free.
