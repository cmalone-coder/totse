I usually don't have a very steady income, and either way I don't like to spend a lot on alcohol. I usually get the least expensive stuff I can find.

I love beer. I used to drink a LOT of 40's of Colt 45 and Olde English. If I'm getting cans I get Pabst Blue Ribbon. If I'm not on a budget though or it's a special occasion I'll get a nie pale ale like Moosehead or Keiths. I love Moosehead.

Whiskey is my second favorite. I usually get Royal Reserve, which is the cheapest stuff. Jim Beam bourbon whiskey hits the spot like mad.

I've been drinking rum a lot lately as well. Appletons Jamaican rum is good, not too pricey. Screech is good too.

When I'm really low on cash I get a 40oz bottle of cooking sherry. It's like seven bucks and 20% alcohol. Tastes a little rancid but you come to love it.
