Re: got someones left overs
Was the gas dried throughly? If it is not dry you can get the water content of the HCl gas absorbing the MA and I think that can look apparently, like 'snot' - just go for a titration of the NP - take the Ph straight down to 1 - don't worry about it for now - going for Ph 6 and that is not really necessary - take it to 1 - evapporate the water after you have removed it from the solvent and then if thereis any MA in there it will show up - NB- when swiy is evaporating there will be a nasty HCl gas fumes coming off (they aren't too bad - just don't have a good sniff -e specially if swiy is trying a speedy evaporation on heat). After evaporation take the MA and give it a good acetone wash - this will turn the acetone yellow as it removes the excess HCl acid and returns the MA to a neutral ph. If, there is anything in there it will come out.
