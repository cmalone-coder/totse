Acne and it's effect on a young persons sex life...(how to cure acne)
Acne is terrible for a young persons confidence. It causes low self esteem, as it is often teased. A lot of people try simple solutions to beat it, but it takes more than "Clean and clear face wash". Most of the shit you buy on shelves is for people who get a 'break out' of one pimple a month. Some people, every day, will wake up with new pimples.

A lot of the shit on the shelves barely work, and are there to just sell false hope to people desperate enough to buy it.

It's shattering to one's self esteem. I had that, and learnt how to beat it. I'm a lazy person and don't have the persistance to keep my skin looking great, but I know I can.

Things I've tried:

Proactive, Minocycline, Clindatech, Clean and clear, clearacil, blah blah blah.

Lots of shit. None of it worked.

Not everything that works for me will work for you, that's why I'll list a heap of things that over time have worked for me:

Extreemly hot showers: It dries out the skin and makes my skin clear up in 1-2 weeks. Oils cause pimples. Dry skin is more easy to cure than pimples, all you need is a..

Oil Free Moisturiser: Easy to get. They work well. You can get them with sunscreen as well (important for Australians, you know, with the whole 'hole in the ozone layer' thing)

A Healthy Diet: It gets said constantly, but it's so fucking true! It doesn't mean "dont eat *insert anything*" it just meants "Dont eat too much of *blah*". A balanced diet is important. This includes...

Cutting Out Sugars: Sugars are a quick source of energy, unless you're using that energy, say good bye to sugars.

Don't Squeeze Pimples For As Long As Possible: Do you know what's under your finger nails? Do you know what's on the surface of your fingers? Germs, a lot of them. Germs are also a cause of pimples. Not to mention that squeezing pimples aggrivates skin and often makes it worse. What's worse, having a zit for a day longer, or having a scar for several weeks?

Try Not To Touch Your Face: Same reasons mentioned above.

Heal Your Scars With Aloe Vera: Where did I find most of my stuff out? From the best plastic surgeon in my area. He works with burn and severe acne patients. He told me that Vitamin E healing scars is true, but hugely overrated. It's actually aloe vera with amazing healing properties. Apply an Aloe Vera gel to acne scars nightly. Watch them disappear.

(If shiva reads this post, tell people about my skin. Sure it's not great, but it was that bad I had to see a plastic surgeon about it.)

Don't Use Scented Washes: Plain and simple, things that are scented are generally bad for sensitive skin.

Gently Exfoliate: Dead skin cells can cause black heads and pimples, this is widely known. What isn't is that scrubbing too hard can also cause acne.

Use A Cleanser: It helps, a lot. A gentle cleanser is best.

Use A Spot Treatment: Anything that you use vary sparingly, that will dry out any soon to be pimples. I own a "Salicylic Acid 2% with a 15.5% AHA complex buffered to pH 3.7" ...what ever that means. It works wonders. It's from ASAP, I got it from a plastic surgeon.

Drink Lots Of Water; I wont lie to you, I have no idea why. All I know is when I do, my skin is clearer.

Stress Less: Ever notice when shit starts going your way, and things start facing up, your skin gets better? Stress has been proven to directly relate to acne. People who overanalyze and worry are prone to break outs. I know saying "stress less" is unrealistically easy, but there is such a thing as a positive outlook. Train your self to have one.

Excersise: We've all heard it a million times. When I work out, skin clears up. Again, I have no idea why. Might have to do with the body's reward chemicals giving some one a 'stress less' attitude, but what ever. I just know it works. Fuck, even if it doesn't work, atleast you'll have a better body.

Get Some Sun: Not too much. But like 20 minutes a day is good for you. It helps dry out the skin a bit (for you oily skinned people). If you want to go out longer, remember to use sunscreen, and if you do, use a OIL FREE sunscreen. After surfing, my skin gets worse if I used an oily sunscreen, and better if I used an oil free.

Ever see surfers? Notice how they have fantastic skin? Sun + salt water. Dries out the skin. Like I said, I'd rather moisturise than have acne.

Change your pillow sheets often: Dead skin and oil builds up on your pillow cases. No matter how much you wash your face before going to bed, it wont do any good if you're sleeping on a dirty surface.

Things to remember:

MOST CHANGES TAKE 1-2 WEEKS so be fucking consitant people! A lot of mistakes we make are like follows:

Some one starts eating healthier for 1 1/2 weeks, sees no difference, so they start eating lots of junkfood again. The change will start taking place two weeks after you started eating healthy. But in the 1/2 week between starting eating junkfood again and your skin clearing up, you try something new. Like a new facewash for example. You think this new improvement in complexion has to do with the facewash. In a week or so when the pimples start coming back, you'll be confused.

That was just an example, I made up all the times in it. What I'm getting at is STICK with things. A lot of what I said is common knowledge. The thing that worked THE best for me is having LONG HOT showers with the nozzle pointed directly at my face. Then everything after that makes it better as well.

I hope this helps some one (except byss, I hope his acne give him cancer).

Minocycline and Chemical Peels all make your skin more suseptible to UV light. So a sunscreen has to to be worn outside. In Australia, that makes Minocycline a bad idea. Especially if you love the outdoors. Chemical peels only need sunscreen in summer or prolonged exposure in winter.

Chemical Peels: Are fucking painful, but amazing. The truly do work (for me). I started on 20% and worked my way up to 50%. 50% peels hurt a lot. It was the most pain I've been in my whole entire life. It felt like acid (haha, probably because it is acid...).

"Salicylic Acid 2% with a 15.5% AHA complex buffered to pH 3.7"

Salicylic acid is a BHA (Beta Hydroxy Acid) which chemically exfoliates your skin. AHA is a Alpha Hydroxy Acid, which also exfoliates your skin, but is composed of larger particles and doesn't exfoliate as deeply, depending on which AHA it is (glycolic, lactic, etc.). buffered to a pH of 3.7 is just the same acidity as your skin. Just so you know, however, washes generally won't treat acne as you rinse them off before they have a noticeable effect, and 15.5% AHA is much too strong anyway, especially when used with BHA, and they're likely doing more damage than good.
