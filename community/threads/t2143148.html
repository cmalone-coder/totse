By this point we realized we were probably gonna get pulled over by the cops so my friend ditched the knife off this bridge.

Out of fucking nowhere three cop cars fishtail around another street coming in my direction, and 2 other cops did the same from the other direction. They all got out with their guns drawn so I threw the car in park and put up my hands. Then they searched my car and found no knife, but they did find a bunsen burner I stole from my school and made into a bong and an empty weed sack.
