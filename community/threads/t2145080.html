Re: Fort benning Georiga
I just graduated from a BCT cycle (i.e. 9 wk pogue cycle) at Ft Benning today. I didn't enlist to be an 11B hardass. I just want to work with computers. Nonetheless I got fucking crazy 11B drill sergeants. Worst 10 weeks of my life.

No 60/120's in full battle rattle, but plenty of low crawling, "too slow, go back", and "FRONT LEANING REST POSITION... MOVE!" in that shit. I hope to never wear an IBA again. Days with ranges were real fun too. I remember 50 cal range. We were hurried through chow to make the 0730 trans, waited 2 hours for range cadre, used 1 hr for our whole company to shoot, then back to our bays by ~1300 where it was 'barracks maintenance; clean your rifles' until 2100. That was pretty typical of white phase.

Oh well, driving over to Ft Gordon for AIT tomorrow.

Try not to ship in the early summer. You'll be stuck with a bunch of 17 year old split-ops. Some of the most annoying people on earth.

Go to the Jewish services on Sundays. The service itself is pretty cool, you get frontloaded for breakfast chow, you get bagels and sweets afterwards, and the whole thing lasts 2 hrs (all other services are 1 hr.)

It's 100% correct about 30th AG. Fucking horrible. BCT sucked really hard, but 30th was worse, which says a lot.
