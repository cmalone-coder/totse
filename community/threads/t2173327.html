How Marijuana made my life better
I've always lived life in the fast lane. At some point you have to stop going 140 on the highway before you run out of gas or cause a wreck. Thanks to Marijuana I think I'm ready to get back on the road. For years I've had problems sleeping. I can't put an exact date on when it all began. I just know that shit got pretty fast for me a few years back and I had to start working extra hard to get where I wanted to go in life and do the things I wanted to do. Skipping a night of sleep is nothing to the average person. Going to bed late a couple of hours every night for a week won't have that much of an affect on the average person as long as they can go back to their normal routine. Years of getting less than 20 hours, sometimes much less, sleep a week will have a tendency to fuck you up. Always needing to be in different places at the same time and having to much shit to do was very frustrating and stressful. I felt like I was living three lives at the same time. When my body finally said it was time to slow down, my mind didn't agree. This is where the problem actually begins. It begins with me wanting to go to sleep at 10 pm on Monday night and not going to sleep until 4 AM Wednesday, waking up at 9 am Wednesday, and going to sleep sometime Friday. I could never tell how long I would be up or how long I would be down. It is like a catch 22. The harder you try to sleep, the harder it is to sleep. You just can't will yourself to sleep. It is like a curse. You lie in bed for hours with your eyes shut just thinking. You're too tired to move or think but your brain just won't shut down. You'll figure out in a few months that it is completely useless so you spend the night hours doing god knows what while others sleep. Eventually you turn into something of a zombie. Just a hollow, soulless humanoid slowly being drained of its life and energy. When the time comes that you do crash, you crash hard. It can hit you anywhere; on the road, in a restaurant, at work, during a conversation, during a shower, etc. I became withdrawn and antisocial. Sleep to me was like a powerful, addictive drug that was very hard to get. You are always wanting it and if anyone ever stands between you and some sweet unconsciousness there would be hell to pay. I would always become violent if awakened from my treasured moments of unconsciousness. Kind of like when you try to take a piece of meat from starving dog. You don't know how long you'll stay under. Maybe 2 hours, maybe 20. Sometimes you hope you never wake up.

I decided it was time to take measures into my own hands. I thought, "This is my body and I'm the boss." Sleep schedules, diet, and exercise failed me. I began drinking. It didn't take more than 6 months before I was buying everclear every week and spiking everything I drank. Drinking myself to sleep worked well and I never got any hangovers. I knew it was becoming a problem and that it was only a temporary solution. I also knew that I couldn't go to sleep without something. That is when I started seeing the doctors. They gave me the works. If the pill is an anti-depressant, some kind of anxiety pill, sleeping pill, or anything to do with slowing you down and putting you out then I took it. One doctor who I was seeing once a week was giving me different prescriptions every single visit. This and a few scams I pulled off led to a very large stockpile prescription drugs. Ambiens were like my M&Ms. My favorite though would have to be temazepam. You see, the thing about most sleeping pills is that you can quickly build up a tolerance to it. I fell into the category of people that would end up taking more than the prescribed dosage and trip balls. Another thing about sleeping pills is that it makes it very hard for you to focus and remember things. Sometimes you forget how many pills you've taken or even what you're supposed to take. Waking up to empty pill and liquor bottles scattered everywhere is scary. Knowing you took far more than enough to kill is a sign that things aren't going well. I started having hallucinations while conscious. Most of the time I couldn't tell if I was really awake. I did a lot of really, really stupid things because of this. After several months of my prescription drug abuse I learned that it wasn't the answer I was looking for and stopped. Drugs and alcohol were not the answer to this nightmare. For months I stayed sober and carried this curse with me.

Then one day I got high. It was some hydro my friends talked me into smoking. The card they played was that they spent all that money on me, and it was so special, so I had to at least try it. We drove out to the country and walked in the woods and smoked it. It was a cool summer morning. I got so high I just laid in the grass for hours staring at the clouds and listening to them talk. I had never felt more relaxed and at peace. Afterward we went to my friends house and I crashed on his couch. I slept like a baby. For months I smoked every day, worked out vigorously, and ate well. My smoke time was a time to relax and not stress about anything. It was a time for me to get to know myself and think about things instead of worrying about things. It wasn't more than a few weeks of this before i started to develop a healthy sleep cycle. The great thing is that I don't need it to fall asleep like I would need a bottle of vodka or a handful of ambien. I always keep a joint around just in case though :). After several personal experiments I've found that I don't need to get high anymore to go to sleep. I still work out, eat well, and try to minimize stress. I still smoke pretty regularly because getting high is awesome and safe.

I wrote this for all the people out there wondering if this plant has any real use other than killing boredom. I also wrote this for people who are too stressed out, depressed, angry, or suffering from sleeping problems. It might just be the chill pill you've been looking for.
