We believe that the world we percieve with our 6 senses (touch, hearing, taste, scent, sight, electromagnetism), is the distorted view of a more real existence. It's not that we don't all exist together, it's that what we define existence as-and believe we are living in-is not true existence.

The aim of this group is to realize - and discard these filters so that we see the world through only self-evident truths, or axioms.
