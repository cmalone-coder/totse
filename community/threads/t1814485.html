Alright, the seven deadly sins. Pride, Wrath, Greed, Envy, Lust, Vanity, and Sloth.

When written, they had different values to them. People worked, physical labor, for hours upon hours. Today a person may work behind a computer screen for 5 hours and book it back home. Does this constitute sloth? When culture has changed and that's become the norm, and to them, that IS a hard day's work. Is it really?

This is but one example.

My question is.. Do these sins change with the times, or will they always and forever mean the exact same thing, with the exact values as the time they were written?

Should cultural differences and advancements through time be considered?
