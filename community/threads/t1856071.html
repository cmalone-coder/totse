quote:

6. added all three liquid extractions of np and gassed it with hcl.

7. filtered the powder and used the leftover np and gassed again and filtered again. did this a total of 3 times.

While I am not implying this is indeed the cause of your problem, it could be one reason why you have salt in your goods.

Since:

NaOH + HCl ---&gt; NaCl + H2O

Typically, after combining all of your NP solutions you will want to do a few washes with dH2O to rid your extract of any residual NaOH.

A good way to tell when you have done this, is by testing the pH of the dH2O after seperation. When the pH is no longer abover 7-8 you are ready to gas.

While technically, Non-Polar solvents are not supposed to have a pH, you will find in application this is not always true, they can retain residual pH from solution.

Here's a few things I noticed:

"1. turn off lavalamp...let it stand and cool till room temp."

Although its ok, you don't need to cool it down to room temp. Cooling it for 10-15 minutes is plenty.

"2. filter out leftover rp.... the honey was a little redish/maroonish."

It is a good idea to add some hot (boiling) DH2O to the flask before you pour it into the filter. You can also use this to rinse your flask and subsequently, your filtered rp. This greatly reduces loss from the filter paper sucking up product. It also helps to get everything out of the flask and rinses the rp more completely.

NOTE: This is the 5X dilution water you used in step three. You can also use a small cotton ball in the neck of the funnel to get that color out. This will make it necessary to filter the diluted post rxn fluid only once.

"4. she raised the ph with 20% sodium hydroxide and 80% dh2o(she did drop a little pure sodium in though without dilution due to so much water seeming to be present, maybe 1/8 to 1/4 of a teaspoon)till ph12.

I don't think the water makes a difference unless its extreme. If there is a solubility gradient I am missing here, someone please correct me. pH 12 is on the edge. It won't hurt anything to take it all the way to 14 (unless that orange gaak thing is in your area. Its not around here.) Since you only went to 12 that blows the shit out of my whole excessive NaOH forming salt thing. Good.

Two things about the pH. One, you may still have some goodies in there. Two, a technique along the lines of kerplunking will more than likely get you another 10% or more out of the leftovers. It may not be much now, but to a 50-60g. batch, you can get another 8 ball out. Try this on the left over post rxn fluid.

Make sure you have extra NP in your post rxn fluid jar or whatever you are heating in.

Before you add more base, put it on medium heat with a lid on it, not tight, but not loose. Just enough that it won't build up pressure. When you see the crap in the bottom churning around, turn the heat up a little more (a liter or less of liquid usually takes about 20-30 minutes to get here.) When it begins to boil, watch it closely and let it boil gently for maybe 15 minutes, then add a few drops at a time of your leftover sodium hydroxide solution. When the bottom layer changes dramatically and stays that way, thats it. Add a ml. or so more NaOH just to make sure you have reached 14 and take it off the heat. Let it sit undisturbed overnight. In the morning, pull off the np and do whatever you did the first time (gas, wash whichever.) I believe you will be suprised what you'll find.

"5. seperated from acidic water and used toulene as my np, shook the hell out of it and let it sit and seperate. seperated the np and repeated the pull with her np 2 more times."

This has me a little confused. If you are saying that you pulled the freebase meth out of the water using toluene, then ok. Consider this:

When basing, the freebase needs somewhere to go as it forms and suddenly doesn't find water attractive any more. Its a good idea to have your np in the post rxn fluid before basing. During this stage, it is not necessary, and actually can be a bad thing, to shake the jar. How you got away without producing an emulsion layer as big as the whole jar is beyond me. The meth gods must be proud of your efforts. Stirring while you base, basing slowly over a period of 30 min, and stirring a little more when basing is complete, will be sufficient.

The salt comes in right here. Since our molecule of choice forms hydrogen bonds with the water and apparently can get "stuck" in the water layer, adding plain salt at this point will release the meth from the water and allow it to move on up into the NP. Do that to the leftovers jar described above also.

Make sure you learn Worlocks dual solvent recrystallization technique with acetone and alcohol (denatured or isopropyl) before you look into other techniques.
