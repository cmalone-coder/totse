Re: Making and continuing conversation etc
look at people in the eyes and smile once. Then stop smiling (you dont want to be the creepy peron who keeps smiling for no reason). Then as the conversation progresses, smile as frequently as reasonably can be done, such as when the other person says something remotely funny.

Also, to keep conversations going, all you have to do is keep asking (relevant) question. Example, the other person said, "I am so tired" to this you can ask maybe "how many hours of sleep did you get that night", or "really? what time did you wake up this morning?" Things like that. It takes practice to know exactly what kind of things are appropriate/not weird to ask. Basically nothing too too personal and rather neutral is the rule of thumb.

Good luck lil buddy.
