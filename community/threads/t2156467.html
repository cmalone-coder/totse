Pothead Cigar Reviews
Green Garcia y Vega-
Real popular in Chicago, 2 for a dollar and burns slow. If you have a razor or a blunt splitter you can cut the thing and roll it as is. Some people take the outer leaf off carefully, then crack the inner leaf which breaks and rolls like a swisher. After you do that you put the outer leaf back on making sure to make a good mouthpiece.

Game Green-
Like a green vega but these are sold in an airtight ziploc package of 2. I think these are made by the same company as Garcia Vegas. Some people say these smell like cookies. These are consistently moist unlike cigars that come in cellophane. Rolls just like a vega, tastes the same too. If you can get these for the same price as 2 vegas it's well worth it.

Blunt Wraps-
These are great for inexperienced rollers but if you can roll a cigar don't get them. It's way too much paper and they can taste nasty after a while. Not having to dump out tobacco is convenient and you can even rip apart the pouch and use it as a surface for breaking up your smoking herbs.

Swisher Sweets-
Cheap, easy to split, and easy to roll. Defenitely one of my favorites. I usually get peach or regular.

White Owls (in the plastic tube)-
These are good for rolling blunts with more than a gram of herbs. Easy to roll like a blunt wrap, and the tubes are great for keeping blunts from getting broken or crushed.
