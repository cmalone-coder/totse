In detroit:

Schwag = brick weed, lots of seeds, poor quality.

Regs = Normal weed, contains some seeds, could be somewhat compressed (that definitely doesn't indicate poor quality around here though), but has a wholesome pot smell to it, as all weed should. This type of weed is mostly what daily smokers who aren't rich buy. It's actually very good here, compared to "regs" from other places that is actually schwag.

Mids = Idk. I have never seen them around here myself, but I have heard of people getting them, probably from some white dealer kid. No spot owned by black folk would know what you're talking about if you were to ask for mids.

Dank/Ganz/Chronic/Kush = Duh, the best kind of weed. Most of the names people call a particular variety are made up. Sometimes this kind of weed is sold in .5 - 1 gram tubes for $10 - $20. Or you could buy it for $300 - $800 per ounce, depending on strain, quality and whether or not you have the hood tip.

Around chi-town slang varies from school to school, town to town, block to block so far I have heard:

1. Shwag, dirt, chi-town-brown, garbage, brick, ditch, mexican, stress, brick, commersh:
http://tbn0.google.com/images?q=tbn:EnYm3-zMXYCE5M:http://www.septicisle.info/uploaded_images/shwag1-791642.jpg

Which is basically shitty, seedy, stemy, trichromeless, brown crap, that is only smoked by nigs in blunts, or by 8th graders. This shit is almost free (oz for $60)

2. Mids, regs, nugs, beasters
http://i20.photobucket.com/albums/b203/herbsINtheBurbs/nug.jpg
http://www.thehomegrown.com/gallery/3/previews-med/mids.jpg
http://forum.grasscity.com/photopost/data/732/hairyandcrystalized.JPG

Better than shwag because: its seedless or very little seeds, barely or not compressed at all, visible trichromes, usually bunch of red pistils, doesn't smell like dirt, but does not smell like fire either. It is not quite dank because it usually wasn't grown in optimum conditions, high temp and nutrient problems are the most common reason. Average 250-270 per oz

3. There is transition between mids and dank, and people usually reffer to it as beasters, 50s, flame mids, fire regs, etc:
http://img480.imageshack.us/img480/9314/chitownnugs9rb.jpg
http://www.420magazine.com/gallery/data/502/Robs_no_name_com.jpg
http://www.thehomegrown.com/gallery/3/previews-med/high.jpg

As you can see, the problem here is either premature harvest of underdeveloped buds and/or incomplete drying, curing, trimming, and storage/handling. Usually sold for 45 for 1/8th or 270-315 for oz.

4. Dank, flame, fire, headies, kronik, chron, 60s, etc.
http://i96.photobucket.com/albums/l197/CripGirl420/hemp___dank_weed_f_jpg.jpg
Around here most of the commercial dank has skunky scent, is well trimmed, usually not cured properly for some reason, and goes around for 60 an 1/8th and 320-385 an oz. Typically skunks, white widow, blueberry/bigbud offspring.

5. Exotics, superflame, strains (yea some people really ask you "got some strains bro?" lulz), kush, diesel

Mostly homegrown, by people with quite some experience, mostly soil and organic nutes, always comes with a strain name, and it is usually in the purple/diesel/kush family. It is never avaliable in bulk, unless oz for >$400 but 1/8ths go for 60-80 (80 only for rich kids, 60 for close friends) It is always sold in jars if >1/4oz (shitty pom jars).
http://i34.tinypic.com/25q7lt5.jpg
http://i188.photobucket.com/albums/z209/str510/GrandDaddyPurplexHinduKushMacro.jpg
http://www.pinkeyedjim.com/wp-content/uploads/2006/10/sd_hh_800x600.jpg

I fucking hate all these terms, especially piff (wtf is piff ? who came up with that) and 30s 60s, and even heard if 90s (some dude selling 1/8ths for $90... and also said to me "it so fire it could be 120s" and I promptly laughed at him)

But yea thats all the terms I have heard aroun here.
