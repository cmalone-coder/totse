ok,,,acid base extraction--psuedo is soluble in an alkaline but is insoluble in an acid--(so you add your psuedo to the water,ph6.7 and then make the water an acidic solution ph12.8 to ph13.1,,no more or lye lock occurs,,this kicks the psuedo out of the water but leaves all the crap still in the water)so disolve your psuedo into DISTILLED water(about 300ml per oz of psuedo),,,add your solvent of choice(10mls per gram is fine),,now i use to spoon in 2 teaspoons full of sodium hydroxide(red devil lye,,caustic soda)(id do about 2 ozs each time id A/B)and stirr it in--now keep adding sodium hydroxide in small increments and stirring it in and then letting it sit--when yourve added enough sodium youll see a white substance appear that will move to the top of the water but the bottom of the solvent(youll also see the water go clearer)now drain off EVERY DROP of water and your ready to put solvent and white layer into the birch----if its redphos/iodine---forget the adding the solvent to the A/B ----filter out the white layer,,,throw away the water,,,dry white layer in filter and its read for the redphos/iodine reduction
