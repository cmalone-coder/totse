Re: Socom Navy Seals
Ain,
First you disarm the nukes. This is accomplished by disarming just one. To disarm, you must hold the button down until the little timer on screen is completed. (this is similar to when you had to paint the bunkers with the laser for the airstrikes) Then you rearm the nukes the same way and they will reset, giving you a time limit to get the hell out. If you killed most of the guys when going in, you shouldn't run into too much difficulty leaving. When you exit the set of caves where the nukes are and find yourself in the rocky open place that leads to the first series of caves, head left and you can take a shortcut which will lead to the cave where the first inside generator was located. Watch out for guys everywhere but the front caves should be clear. Run out into the open area from the beginning of the mission and soon the chopper will come for you.

A nice trick in this level is to shoot out the light bulbs in each cave. This makes it a little harder for the enemy to spot you and gives you more time to kill them. Also on the way out, I found it helpful to pick up one of the enemy's assault rifles. You have no more need for stealth, and the fast heavy shots of the ak-74 and the likes will kill pretty efficiently. Plus there's plenty of free ammo.

Hope this helps. Peace.
