Re: LSD for the first time
My first question is what should I expect from the acid?

Keep in mind acid, just like all psychedelics, is different for everyone. There's really nothing anyone can say to prepare you for it.

If you take a low dose, expect mild visual distortion. Objects warping slightly, light trails, and things like that.

High dose? Get ready for a mind fuck. There's no way to describe what will happen here because it's entirely dependent on the setting and your mindset. The kind of person you are and the life you have lived also play key roles. Just know that it's going to be a profoundly amazing experience. Visual hallucinations, auditory hallucinations, physical hallucinations, and mental hallucinations. With high doses you're going to be in for a helluva ride.

Second, is the myth that acids potency has gone down over the years true?

Yes and no. When LSD first hit the recreation scene, it was still legal to manufacture. This obviously meant that it was being largely produced, so the price, even with inflation, was much lower, if there was a price at all (it was often given out for free.)

The common blotter dose has declined a little, but this really only applies to 'street' acid. If you go to a concert or the festival scene, you can bet 3/4s of the time the acid you get will be high quality. Even better, if you can find liquid, prepare to be floored. It's always obtainable at a festival given you look hard

Re: LSD for the first time
If it's not highly diluted, you're in for a serious mind fuck. You should definitely take some pointers from that thread to assure you don't have a bad trip.

If you're in an uncomfortable setting or around an awkward crowd, or any crowd at all for that matter, there's a very good chance you're going to have a downward spiral. Do it in solitude, maybe with a good friend or two who are dosed up, and just enjoy the ride.

Do not try to control the trip. For the most part, it will control you, however sometimes you'll find yourself in the drivers seat. Once you learn how, you can manipulate your trip for the most part, but for a first timer don't expect to be able to do this.

Don't go into the trip nervous, scared, or anything like that. Meditate beforehand to clear your mind a little.

Going into a trip with anxiety is just asking for trouble.

Since it's liquid, there's a chance it's going to be very potent. I would suggest having some benzos on hand if you can get a hold of any, or at least a couple beers. Either will help calm you down a little should your trip start going in a negative direction. The former is preferred though, as it's a bit more effective, and tripping while slightly drunk can sometimes be unpleasant.

Have fun, and as always, trip safe.
