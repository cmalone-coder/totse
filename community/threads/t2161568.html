Re: How has the economic recession affected Drug Dealers?
The effect on the drug dealer would be relative to the amount of the recession, but let's talk worst-case scenario.

There would be a shortage of legitimate cash flow, so in order to support their families some individuals might take up some illicit form of making money. This would then increase the supply of the drugs. What must be taken into account, though, is that these larger orders take more time to fill. The drug supply lines are not well developed enough to support the newly recession-imposed demand, so initially prices would increase. As supply increased, though, prices would lower. Eventually things would even out and prices would be forced to decrease. The dealer's game would be then to move high volumes at lower cost, like Wal-Mart. This would be harder though, because there would be an increasing number of dealers, with the increasing supplies of drugs. In other words: there's an initial spike in the price and demand and there's more product being sold, but it's at lower prices, and you have more competitors.
