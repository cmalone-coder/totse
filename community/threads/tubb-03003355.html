Your rising sign is dependent on when and where you were born...a birth chart. You need to know your exact time of birth and the longitude and latitude of where you were born. The internet provides fast and accessible ways to find your rising sign w/o seeking the longitude and latitude of where you were born. All you have to do is type the time you were born and the hospital/city/state you were born in and voila....

A cusp is when you are born on a certain date that is close to two different signs. For example...I'm born on the 19th of December. I'm still in the Saggitarius category but the "cut-off" date for Sagg's is Dec. 21st. Therefore, I'm on a cusp of Saggitarrius and Capricorn.
