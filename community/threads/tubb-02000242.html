Nuclear California
We can't help but note that rising electricity costs and rolling blackouts now have a majority of Californians favoring the construction of more nuclear power plants. A Field poll last month found a surprising 59 percent of Golden State residents support building more nuclear plants, which emit no greenhouse gases and are newly competitive against power plants that use high-priced natural gas. In 1984, the last time Field polled Californians about nuclear power plants, 61 percent were opposed. California, of course, is one of the nation's most "green" states, with more than its share of environmentalists, including environmental extremists.
