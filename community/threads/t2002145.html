Re: Confidence
Well there are better ways to answer this. But a short answer, that really helped me is:

Realize that nothing you do is significant. Realize that you are just one out of 6 billion people on the earth. Realize that each one of these people care a million times more about what they are doing then what you are doing. Realize that if you fail at something, countless others have failed at the same thing. Realize that anything you can do can and will be forgotten when the next big thing happens. Realize that whatever you fear, there are fears out there that make yours look like a child's toy. Realize that whatever feelings that come over you when you face this fear is nothing but a simple physiological reponse brought on by emotional triggers in your brain.

So who the fuck cares but you? Do whatever the fuck you are going to do.
