Too Long/Didn't read
When I was eight, my little brother fell 25 feet onto concrete, leaving him paralyzed and unable to speak at only five years old. I always kind of blamed myself for it. My cousins wanted to play hide and seek, but I didn't want to. I didn't say anything and played regardless. After 2 minutes I decided to give up and walked back upstairs again. I saw my brother hiding behind the curtain and didn't think anything of it. I went into the room beside and told my cousin I didn't want to play anymore. We heard a scream. My brother had leaned back against the window and it flew open, causing him to fall 4 stories onto concrete. I always thought that had I just spoke up or told my brother to move when I seen him, he'd still be alright.

After this, I began to eat a lot as a way of coping, and got very fat. I was also the smartest person in my school. This combination left me getting bullied a lot in school. I became rather violent and would get into a lot of fights.

High school came along, and we were forced to play rugby in the first year. I played it, and turned out to be rather good. Within a few years of playing, I had lost most of the fat I gained in my youth.

When I was 14 my brother took a turn for the worse. I was woken up by my dad one night, telling me to come say goodbye to my brother. I had not been told that he was getting sicker, but there and then I was told that he wasn't going to make it through the night. My big brother, my parents and I all gave a final goodbye to my little brother, one by one. My dad then took us downstairs, and said "we wanted to tell you since his accident that he wouldn't live a long life, but we couldn't do it." It was the first time I'd ever seen him cry. Seeing my dad cry, and knowing my brother was dying tore me apart emotionally. The whole night is implanted into my head and makes me cry every time I think about it. The worst thing was, my brother thankfully somehow survived the night, but we had to go through saying goodbye again the next day. My dad said that night, crying once again, Dont put yourself through all this pain just for us, please. If it hurts too much to hold on then dont do it just for us. Both nights I cried myself to sleep. Somehow, my brother survived and still lives to this day.

All of this happening in my youth screwed me up somewhat. In dealing with the accident, I missed out on all of the social interaction with members of the opposite sex. I had a handful of friends, but I would never tell them my thoughts or secrets. In fact, Ive never trusted anyone with my thoughts or feelings. By my third year in high school, I had become popular through rugby. I wasnt like the other players  I was kind, polite and had a child-like nature about me. My friends said I was like a little kid.

Just as I felt I was finally happy, my mum and aunt developed cancer within 2 weeks of each other. My mum was the one that looked after my disabled little brother, so we all had to step up and help where we could. One of my best friends had an abortion a month later. Another one of my best friends then took a terrible bout of sickness and had to stay at home for months and was always crying, so I visited her nearly every day.

All of this happening so close together really shook me up. My confidence took a complete hit. I havent told anyone this entire story before. Ive told different parts to different people, and they all just feel sorry for me or tip toe over everything they say to me. I dont want pity, I want friendship.

The girl that had the abortion began to get very hostile with me lately, but I kept forgiving her until she said to me two weeks ago, out of the blue, Youre shit now. All of your friends have talked about it, and they dont even like you. She hasnt spoken to me since. I thought she was just angry and making stuff up, but recently it seems like people have been talking to me less, being a little ruder towards me and generally acting different. My big brother is moving out of the house and my friends seem to be getting further away from me. To be honest, I dont recall ever feeling so alone and confused. I dont even know if Im imagining it or if everyone really is leaving me.

This probably doesnt even make much sense to anyone. I needed to let out what was on my mind. Its all been piling up in my head and I feel ready to explode. Does anyone have any advice?
