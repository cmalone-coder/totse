I never thought this was possible before. I've been in love, I know the utter devotion of feeling towards somebody. Recently, I fell in love again (it's a strange case of love; platonic, but real, true love), with someone who has a boyfriend, who is both utterly in love with her, and who she's completely and totally in love with. I've honestly never seen a pair of people so in love as them; you can see it in everything they do together. But she's in love with me too. The same love as I have for her; platonic, but so incredibly in love. None of us (all three of us know exactly the feelings of everyone involved for the other, and there's no jealousy or anything, thankfully) know of anything like this happening with anybody else, but it does happen.

I know some people will say we don't know what we're talking about. Go ahead, but we do. I know what it is to be in love. She does too, and it's her that's in love with ywo people at once. I think it helps that one is platonic; whether it would be or not if she didn't have her boyfriend, I have no idea. I suspect it might not, but as it is, there's no sexual desire. Not to say she isn't very sexually attractive; she is, extremely, but I've spent nights sleeping with her and not had the desire to do anything sexual at all. Never been happier than just lying there, us both holding each other close. I'm not sure this would work if they were both non-platonic loves (and really, that's the only difference between this and conventional love), but at least you know it can happen, albeit seemingly very rarely indeed.
