Basically, deciding which women to go out with can be done in two ways:

2.) What I should have been doing was mentally listing them from best to worst. French Waitress would have been at the top, of course, but others would have been listed below her. You're still aiming for the top of the list, but if that's an impossibility you still have others that are around the top too.

Example: You're in a relationship. All of the sudden a new girl comes along that you would rather be dating. According to theory 1 this is an impossibility because according to this theory, all acceptable prospects are of equal value, so there are no more 'desirable' prospects. They are all the same, either worth it or not worth it. Theory 2 accommodates this situation because the new girl is higher on your list, which is why you want her and not your current partner.

Bottomline: French Waitress raised my standards so high that I ignored possible prospects that would have been acceptable. The whole Theory 1/ Theory 2 thing I now realize is only proof of how people list their priorities, and is only slightly related to this thread.
