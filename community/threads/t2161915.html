Re: Just turned 18!
A 10-22 seems in order, or some bolt action .22. I love the bolt action .22's; they teach you a lot about shooting before moving on to bigger things.

As for bigger things, I'd get a Mosin, a Mauser, and the Lee-Enfield. If your resources stretch that far maybe an M1 Garand or a P17 Enfield.
No collection is complete without at least one of these grand old shooters. .

For when you're ready to move on to even bigger things, I'd recommend both an AR and AK of some kind, an FAL if .308 is your thing. You might want to get a Ferret .50 someday, but that's a long term goal.

As for handguns, when you're ready to move to those, a BHP, an XD and a SIG are the best choices in my book. Replace BHP with 1911 if .45's your thing. Stay away from the .40's and 10mm's; ammo is more expensive and they don't give you all that much of an advantage. You only need 5 calibers for handguns actually: .22LR (Walther P22 seems to be a good choice), 9mm, .45, .38spl, and .357 Mag.
A good revolver in .357 Mag is also in order, Ruger Redhawk being a good choice, and you can shoot .38 with a .357 handgun.

Get a Luger if you can. It will give you major prestige. If you can find a .45 Luger for a reasonable price, by all means sell your car and buy it. One day you'll understand .

This is a good list of guns you can work towards.
