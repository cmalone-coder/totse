Re: how do you guys talk yourself out of a bad trip?
Change whatever it is that you're doing. If you're having a bad trip inside, go outside (if you can have privacy). If you're watching TV, listen to music instead. Or try tripping in silence. Or if music is giving you a bad trip, try a different genre of music, maybe even something you don't normally enjoy. If the lights are on, try turning them off. If you're laying down, try standing up. If you're with a lot of people, try to make your group smaller.

Your mood before the trip is a big factor, but bad trips can easily be converted. Just catch them before they become the "omg i'm about to die" type of bad trips.

And as others have mentioned, your outlook on the drug itself plays a big role. Mushrooms deserve respect, and I look at them as a gateway to new thoughts and feelings. For me, this leads to each trip showing me something I've never seen or thought of before.

Re: how do you guys talk yourself out of a bad trip?
It is very very difficult to do by yourself, which is why you shouldn't do it alone until you know how you're going to act on the drug.

But the essentials of getting out of a bad trip is to get your mind focused on something else. Acid tends to let you really only focus on one thing at a time and once you get it off what ever it is that is making the bad trip, you'll be on the road to being ok again.

One thing that many noobs to tripping have in common is that they tend to get to peaking and then think that they are never going to come down and that they are the only one feeling like that.

So I have noob write on a piece of paper before the trip starts: It is only a drug. No matter how I may feel when I read this, it is still only a drug and it will wear off. Then, if they start having trouble, I show it to them and when they see it and see it is in their own writing, it usually goes a long way in helping them.
