Re: Price of computer? (who would buy it)
you should include the specs on the video card, if it has one, and if not say "onboard graphics." also, how many watts is the power supply? i think $900-1000 is high, since one with those specs or better, with no monitor or anything, can be built cheaper. i would guess a good asking price would be $600-700-ish. that makes it still worth your while to sell it, and puts it in a price range that isn't too high that someone will just pass up at a glance.
