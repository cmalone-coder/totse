Re: How long does it take you to hook up a semi-reliable connect when you're on the road
What exactly are you looking for? There are certain places around here (Yes, I'm southeast PA) that are well known for have a supply of different chemicals.

Philadelphia has a load of pretty high quality smack, if that's what you're into. Also, Camden (Philadelphia's retarded cousin) is pretty much the dope capital of the east coast.

You can also find E pretty easily in Philly. Just look around the clubs (there are a lot of them.)

If it's psychedelics you're looking for, the easiest way to track them down would be at a concert.

If you're going to be there on the 15th of this month, Bodega and Marco Benevento are playing in Philly. I can guarantee you'll be able to find a number of psychedelics at either of those shows.

Less likely, but if you're around on the 23rd, you're in luck. You'll be able to find all sorts of goodies then. The Allman Brothers, Bob Weir (grateful dead) and Ratdog, and Slightly Stoopid will be playing.

You have my solemn promise that you'll be able to find all sorts of goodies at the Allmans and Ratdog shows. Great acid, mushrooms, AWESOME weed, dmt, and probably a lot of the less popular chems like any number of RCs. You can probably find mescaline there, too, if that's your thing.

And hey, it's not THAT bad here. I live about 90 minutes west of Philadelphia, and although I live in the middle of nowhere, there are a lot of hippies here, so, there are also a lot of good drugs. :)

I like my middle of nowhere, thanks.
