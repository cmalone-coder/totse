Re: been in a fight?
It's been a long time, but no, it doesn't hurt. In fact, it's really weird once it's over and people look at your bloody face all shocked and you're like "what!?". Really, being punched/kicked in the face/head hardly hurts at all and it seems to be what really gets the adrenaline pumping. I have found though, that getting kicked in the gut, ribs, kidneys, or nuts can still hurt a bit when you're high on adrenaline. But chest, face, head, arms, legs, and upper back, can take a lot with no pain at all.

And Criso is absolutely right. You need to keep calm and focused and use your brain. Sometimes, when I used to get in fights I'd spaz out and make all kinds of mistakes that I could've avoided if only I'd concentrated on what was happening. It might sound hard, but I don't really think it is. Your brain really "speeds up" when you're fighing making it possible for you to actually calculate what you're doing. But it's up to you to control your temper.

And realize that serious fights don't end until one person can't continue. When you've got that going on, you have to keep fucking the dude up until he stops showing interest in harming you. And remember, it doesn't hurt him either when you punch him in the face. Probably the best strategy for the untrained is to figure out how to dominate when you're rolling around on the ground like gay chipmunks. Also, elbows and knees are often more effective than punches and kicks. And remember what I said about the gut, kidneys, ribs, and nuts.
