I have this little creation that I made that works great for when you want to smoke a water cooled device, but don't want to carry the fucker around.

You need a few things, a water bottle lid, about 2 feet of rubber tubing, a bowl and stem preferably metal, and something to drill with, I just heated a screwdriver and pressed it through the plastic until the holes were the proper size for the tubing.

Ok, drill two holes into the top of your lid opposite of each other. Make sure that the tubing fits snugly, you want the rubber tubing to create it's own seal so make sure it's a bit tight.

Now, take your tube and stick it into one of the holes until it can reach to the bottom of your standard water bottle and cut it off about 2 inches above your bottle cap. Now you want to do the exact opposite for the second hole, make sure there is a nice length of hose coming from the top of the bottle so that you can hit it without lighting your face or hair on fire.

The rest is pretty self explanatory, you can wrap this little thing up to fit into almost any pocket, it'll cost you around 2 dollars at the most and is a great little thing to have with you and you don't even have to worry about ditching it, just the fact that it's so cheap is an incentive to chuck it and make a new one if you get busted.
