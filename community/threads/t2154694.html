Re: Probation?
How does probation work?
After the initial hearing, you're given a list of terms & conditions to complete within a certain amount of time, either before or after the next hearing.

If you don't follow these terms and conditions, you will be given a probation violation, and will have another hearing where you discuss what you did wrong and your terms&conditions of probation will be modified.

You don't want to quit smoking?

You got charged with a drug possession charge?

At this point, it isn't up to you if you 'want' to keep smoking or not.

You fucked it up. You will be drug tested, and it doesn't matter when.

Now, you can't smoke... unless you want a probation violation for every dirty test you get and put up with the consequences.

Usually, a probation violation involves jail time, and you will be assigned to some rehab class after your jail time.

If you continue to fuck up and keep pissing dirty, you will not be 'assigned' to a rehab class, but you will be shipped off to a residential program + more jail time.

If you continue smoking, this is a lose/lose situation.

If you quit smoking, you're in a win/win situation.

Many people can smoke, but now that you are in the system, you can't smoke. That is something that people have a hard time realizing, and it fucks them up in the long run.

I'm sure you got a little taste of jail when you got locked up in the holding cell.
Trust me, it gets much worse.

If you continue smoking, you're in a downward spiral for failure of life.
