the fact that you even have to ask what god refers to should give you a clue as to what kind of answer you'll get... the label "god" is vague because what it refers to is vaguely understood... it seems to point to many different things, some more fantastical, some philosophical, some comical... in some minds, god may mean something directly opposite to others.
