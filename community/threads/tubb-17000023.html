sorry to bring back such an old topic, but i believe the old 1988 hit, Fallout 2 is an instant classic.

you start out as "the chosen one" from a nuclear vault tribe. see, there was this HUGE war, and atom bombs were dropped all over the united states. there was a man called the vault dweller (the player's role in fallout 1) who had a quest to find a new water chip for a vault numbered 13. he went through this whole quest to find a water chip and destroyed an entire mutant army (spawned from a nuclear waste material called F.E.V.)

in Fallout 2, you are a descendant from the origional vault dweller (the vault dweller was banished from vault 13 because he became too much like the outside world) and your tribe requires something called the Garden of Eden Creation Kit (GECK for short) which is said to make even a barren wasteland firtile. the people in your tribe are dying. so you have no choice but to save them. there are strong messages about bigotry and sexism involved in the plot. near the end of the game, you travel back to your village, because you have FINALLY (this took me three game years) found the Garden of Eden Creation Kit, and you realize that your entire village was either killed or abducted by a group of people called the Enclave. They live in an oil derrek off of the coast of California (the entire game is in California). They believe that they are what is left of the United States governemt and that it is their job to exterminate the mutants (still around from fallout 1) living on the mainland. they believe that everyone on the mainland is a mutant. they do tests to check for being "guilty of mutation" from the nuclear fallout particles that dropped before. the enclave plan on using a mutant strain of the F.E.V. material (now made into a virus) to wipe out every single living thing on the mainland. your final quest is to destroy the enclave before it can preform further tests upon your tribesmen and unleash the deadly virus to the outside world. in the end, you have to fight the head of the CIA (the enclave is a mockery of our current government) who is beefed up on muscle pluses and walks around in an alien powered armor. after that, the oil derrek goes to hell with you and your tribe safely ashore.

i was and still am addicted to this game. mostly because it is based upon the old pen and paper RPG's. it took me 4 months of constant playing and pondering to finally beat it. there is an endless possibility of sub-quests because they are dependent upon your characters abilities (strength, dexterity, charisma, endurance, luck, and perception)

well, not that i have told you basically the entire game, try it for yourself. its like 9 bucks at Best buy.
