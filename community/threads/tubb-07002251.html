Re: fake car registration
It's nothing to worry about. My solution costs around a dollar... You need an example to look at, but just scan and print out a temporary tag (it's the paper tag as you would see on a recently purchased car) on a poster board. I use a cut sheet of posterborad to fit in my HP Deskjet 712c printer. It looks very real, yet isn't... I've done it for my friend every month for four months so far. Since a temp. tag is only valid for a month be sure to replace it when it's due. As long as you don't do anything illegal driving like running traffic lights or crashing, you most likely wont get caught. I think a counterfeit temp. tag is your second best option.. opposed to actually buying insurance... but c'mon... that's legal! And it costs money.
