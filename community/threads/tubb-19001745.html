ALCOHOL FUSE
Materials:
Isopropyl Rubbing Alcohol (must be at least 70%pure)
Yarn
Plastic Cup

Procedure:
1. Pour your alcohol into the cup.
2. Cut a two and a half foot long section of yarn out and put it in the cup. Let it set for 5 minutes.
3. Pull it out. Let it dry.

NOTE: Test the burn rate of said fuse. Just light it with out putting it in a bomb.

Storage Guidelines:
1. A water proof container is optimal for storage.
2. Shelf life varies, although it is usually two weeks, best results occur when used in 72 hours (3 days).
3. Do not allow it near heat, or open flame.
4. Do not leave it in direct sunlight.

Usage Guidelines:
1. Attach it to a bomb
2. Light it in accordnance with the device it is on

Tactical Guidelines:
1. Vary the setting time and drying time of the fuse. This can change the burn rate.
2. Vary the length of the fuse for what operation you are using it with. For longer delays, bigger devices, and allowance for you to be on the other side of town, use longer fuses. For short delays, cut the fuse short
3. Longer fuses cause a slight loss in reliability, use discretion in constructing longer fuses...
