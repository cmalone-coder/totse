For those of you who are unaware, the Caliph is Arabic for successor. Under the Islamic Sunnah, he would be the chosen leader of the Sunni Muslim (around 90% of Muslims) faith. I believe it should be reinstated: look at the chaos in the Muslim world. It is largely because the only authority that exists is a bunch of militant leaders. They need authority now.
