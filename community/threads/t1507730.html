I feel like no one tries anything anymore. No one gives a fuck.
This is gonna be a bit of a rant, so read it if you want, or skip it.

I feel like everyone thinks they can have everything now. I generally blame TV and the media as a whole, but when it really comes down to it, its simply a lack of determination and integrity. People think they can have every beautiful person they see. They think cheating is okay, simply because they can get away with it. They think going out "clubbing" and sleeping with some random stranger every week is a-okay because its not hurting anyone (which IS true, except that its diluting life).

No one wants to work for anything. If things get difficult in a relationship, the other person is crazy or something, and they just break up. No one is willing to sacrifice anything. You cant have everything...sometimes to get something great, you have to give up something else equally good. You will never have the freedom of being single, while having the perks of a relationship. Sometimes you cant have a beautiful AND smart girl/boyfriend.

There are simply some sacrifices you must make for someone else.

Often I console myself by saying that the people who dont work for the good things in life get that they deserve, by not having a full, good life...but rarely that helps because I end up living around those people.

No one trusts anyone, because they know they themselves are untrustworthy. I dont trust people because I know THEY are untrustworthy. This is not a "holier than thou" thing. I'm not putting myself higher than everyone else, or thinking that my way is the right way...I just feel like I give a shit, and put in effort while everyone else looks for another fast-food insta-gratification fuck.

I dont know if this was ever better in the past, or if its always been a big whorish pile of shit, but I am sick of it. Both in the frustrated sense, and the disgusted sense. Sometimes I go out places like "clubs" with my girlfriend because she enjoys the music and whatnot, and I literally feel ill looking at all the people trying to sell themselves. They arent people anymore...just fucking meat.

There are vital core attributes that I think universally matter. Of course there are tons of things that are variable and matter depending on the context...murder is generally something that is inherently "wrong", but in certain situations it can be justified (self defense, or protecting someone unable to protect themselves, etc). I feel that there are some things though, that just ARE. Truth, integrity, justice, sincerity, fidelity, determination...all these things matter no matter who you are, or what the scenario. There are some rules that I think always apply.

When talking about this with friends or family, I just call it "rightousness". Doing what you yourself KNOW is the Right thing to do. I know there are times when people do things that, somewhere deep inside, they are ashamed of. Things they know are just not supposed to be done. And thats where the determination and integrity apply...they do it anyways because it is easy, or available now, or whatever the case may be.

I hope those people feel that pain later in life. I delight in knowing that some days they just go home and cry alone because they feel so empty.

I dont believe in spiritual karma, but I DO think that everything has a consiquence, and I think that everything eventually comes back around. The punishment for leading a shallow life is that there is never going to be any depth. The pain for never applying yourself to a single cause is that you will never be able to when you need to.

I want to see more people outraged at the whorish, justifying behaviour of pop stars and TV shows. "Promiscuous" by Nelly Furtado?? Are you FUCKING SERIOUS? I'm not talking about censorship in the traditional sense. I'm talking about good taste and morals...sincerity and...well...god damn righteousness.

Sometimes I feel like I should see someone professional to figure out why I hate everyone, but other times I'm glad that I have the clarity to feel that way.

Anyways, I think I'm pretty much done.

/rant
