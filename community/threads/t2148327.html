The game of politics... why buy into it?
I've just been reading the posts in PLRC and not posting as much as I (postdiluvium/mvpena) used to. What I see commonly discussed from regulars on this board is either people talking about the game of politics or people playing it. Either people are discussing what one candidate or another can do to gain a demographic or some political mojo or people are just spitting out the same exact lines a candidate and his/her campaign fed into the media. Is it entertainment or have you somehow fallen into the game as the media has?

I'm just curious. When I read these posts, it seems like people forget that those candidates out there have made everything that will affect the rest of your life into a game. They will be the ones in control of the laws that tell you what you can and can't do. They will be the ones that will make laws that indirectly regulate how our market is ran, which in turn decides the wealth and power of our nation. They are the ones that will send us aggressively into foreign lands as they see fit. All of that is some pretty serious shit. I know that people understand the kind of power that is being handed over to these candidates, but when I read these posts it seems people are perfectly contempt with something as serious as what was mentioned is being made into a game.

When Hillary was running, the main argument her supporters spat out over and over again was that she is a Clinton and Clintons know how to play politics. But is that what everyone really wants? They want to trade actual substance with image and pander? Do people just want a good salesman and not a good leader? Are we so entrenched into our buy and trade market economy that we see everything as such?

To me, all of the candidates that ever had a chance in my lifetime, so far, are used car salesmen. Except the used car wasn't sold to them or traded in for a new car. The used car was their car. They really fucked it up, but made it pretty on the outside so they could actually sale it to poor saps like the average American voter. They will pander to the voter in a really dumb condescending way trying to convince the voter that the used car is like new. It is different. It is everything they need. Then the poor saps buy it and find out its a lemon. Its exactly what they were expecting to get when they initially were thinking about if they should get a used or a new car.

Its always, at first, a new car. Something new, refreshing. Something that will be reliable for the next 120 k miles. But then again, they can't risk spend all that money on a new car. There are bills to be paid and mouths to feed. To be on the safe side, financially, they'll go for a used car. Its cheaper and it can get from point A to point B. Maybe some other time they can get something new. Just not now. So just trust the used car salesman on this one. Be happy with mediocrity. Don't reach for greatness and excellence. Mediocrity is safe, while excellence is too much of a risk.

I guess what I am trying to get to with this analogy is that, when I see people talking about the game or being part of it; I just see people discussing strategies for a used car salesman to sale a heap of junk with a new paint job. I see people just using the same lame ass sales pitches the used car salesman threw at them and they in turn are throwing these pitches at others. As if there was some kind of genius to these pitches that made the pitch worthy of being repeated over and over again. Why? Is it interesting? Do people get lost in all of it and accept this mediocre act as reality and how its going to be?

For TL;DR - Why do you guys buy into politicians making the government into a game? The founding fathers structured this government so we would have control over it. Yet we hand over the control to a bunch of people that are nothing like us that think its all a game.
