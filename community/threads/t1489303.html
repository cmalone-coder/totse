That is one of those really touchy subjects. I agree with Easy you can't choose who you fall in love with but what are some more of the circumstances in the relationship. Have you known her for a long time? How did you meet in the first place? I mean were you out at the mall and went up and hit on a 16 year old? Or have you known her for a while and over time developed feelings for her.

The one other thing that you have to think about with that big of an age difference is are you sure it is boyfriend/girlfriend type love or could it possibly be the type of little sister or possible daughter type love?

Do you have kids? if so what do they say about the situation. If not can you honestly see marrying this girl and having kids with her?

In short I personally do not think that it is wrong to fall in love with someone of an age difference but when it comes to that much of a difference you have to be careful what type of love you are dealing with. A 16 year old's love most likely differs from a 33 year olds love. Yes love is love but at her age it means something alot different than it probably means to you.
