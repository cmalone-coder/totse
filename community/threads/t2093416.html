Oh word? English IB oral? I got that jawn coming up too. ;)

Fun Fact: Colleges do not see whether or not you received the IB diploma.

2 kids in the grade above me failed to receive theirs last year. They were already into college, so it didn't matter. All that extra work. And for what? Nothing. Much lulz for IB's uselessness.

You receive your IB diploma the summer after you graduate high school. So what does it matter if you get it or not? You've already been accepted to college.

So why even do it? Well, IB is considered "the most rigorous curriculum your school offers", and colleges want to see you pursuing that. They want to see you challenging yourself. Ultimately you become a bitch to IB for colleges. You toil for 2 years just to have that extra edge on your app, which ironically may not even help you get into your college of choice. I don't have that many extra-curricular activities, so it better fucking help me.

Wrapping up my senior year I advise you to not throw everything IB related away, but don't kill yourself over orals and the like. Make an attempt but don't stress out over it to the point where you're contemplating using drugs as a way to gain an edge. If you get >5 on the HL exams I believe you can use that as credit to skip Bio 101 or w/e at college. So maybe you want to try on those, but again, don't break your back studying unless you're an academic nazi.

Moreover, since you've never ever done any benzo I don't suggest your first time be during something like an IB oral. First time experiences like that and semi-important things like school don't seem to mix in my experience.

Relax, take your time, and answer your teacher's questions to the best of your ability. He/she is there to help you, not fuck you over. The questions aren't impossible either.

Btw, we did a "pratice" oral commentary w/ recorders. Man, talking into a tape recorder is...different. Talking to a person about literature is one thing, but when you're talking to an inanimate object, like a tape recorder, quite frankly, you just feel like a fucking idiot.
