2c-e and The Concept of Perceptions and Misconceptions
My hallucenogenic use has involved LSD, mushrooms, 2ce and ketamine/dxm if you can count that.

I have never thought about the concepts of perceptions and misconceptions on any of these other hallucenogens aside from 2c-e. This drug has opened my eyes completley to this. I've known about it forever but never thought about how I can a) not let it work to my disadvantage and b) make it work to my advantage.)

Basically, Perception is King.

1)Anything that anyone says or does can be misinterperted for something else.
2)Anything that is in reality negative can be looked at as positive using the right words.
3)Anything that is in reality positive can be looked at as negative using the right words

Misinterpertations can happen because of a 1000 different reasons. These reasons can be based off of a person's past personal experience w/ similar situations, ones tone/facial features, ones emotions carying into tone/facial features, etc. 100s of different reasons.

How to combat perception/misconceptions:
Do not overanalyze others words and actions too deeply. You could quite possibly be misinterperting their actions and they could also be doing a shitty job at making their point clear. You are not a fucking mind reader. If it appears that people are dicks, awkward or depressed social misfits, there is no reason to judge them immidiently and block them out of your life. There is also no reason to blame yourself for their actions and feel insecure.

Make yourself clear, people aren't fucking mind readers. If you have figured out something in your head and only verbalize one or two details, people won't know what your talking about. You need to make your point clear but also concise.

There are about 1000 different things to analyze this concept on but I can't write down a good 50% of what I now know..hard to explain.

The point of the post:

One time I was talking to a friend who I gave the drug to and we both told each other that this drug really opened awareness to perceptions. I then started a theory based off the loose evidence that when one thing happens while another thing happens that it must be proof :)

All the people that I've given to I've asked them the following question "2c-e makes you think how of perceptions"

They all get wide-eyed and are like shit I've thought about that too on 2c-e!

Never on the other shit.


Anyone else see this? Or is this just coincidence.

I only want 2c-e users to respond, or hallucenogenic users who have thot about perceptions.

edit- ya, suicide mouse forsure this is all common sence, and so is literally 99% of everything I "learn" about on hallucenogens. It is amazing to see how I choose to ignore these things and then drugs open it up.
