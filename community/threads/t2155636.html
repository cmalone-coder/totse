Re: Paranoia
Well... we all do have things to worry about. Many things that we usually deal with by ignoring. Even if it's only homework we've been slacking on or some other mundane tasks that we need to do.

Then there are larger things that people tend to freak about. People often pick something big & large that they can freak out about as a group, such as Y2K/Global Warming. These kind of things are a stand in for all the more complex personal/social/global issues that are too hard to describe & relate to.

What I'm getting at is that the worries that a person has generally can tend to fixate on some random point. They then blow it all out of proportion. Go beyond all reason. I know that personally I stupidly prefer to obsess about freaky non-personal problems because my self & my "own backyard" are a lot more work than I care to take on.

When they might well worry about smaller real things, such as they're irrationally freaking about generic stuff instead of realizing they're hungry/thirsty or left the tub running or pot boiling on the stove. That perhaps while they're getting so weird that they've been sitting too long & their body is cramped & their bladder full.

That if they got up & moved a bit a took care of these real things, they just might be cooler about all the Big Shit that they're obsessing on.

I can even imagine you freaking over your mother nagging you about things you know you should do: chores or in relation to that stuff (I'm not going to mention all those ads that start in July talking about The Thing that always starts in September! I won't name it if you won't... :( ) :D

Besides authority figures like a mother can be freaky, even if they're cool at the moment & not even paying you any attention.

I know I have a lot...A LOT of stuff that I slack on. When I get up high the blinders come off & I can see them. It burns me that I could get a lot of little things off my mind by doing them & I often don't. It's way easier to freak out about stupid random stuff than take care of business in the RW.

Lastly...I'm pretty sure I (I don't think that I'm the only one) that I pick up the fears of people in my area. Right now on Saturday morning in the broad daylight it seems as quiet as any 4 am. Actually being Saturday, most people are not working... Whereas on a weekday people are more uptight in the morning & I think I can tell.

I like to think that if I was right here in a soundproof box & without a clock: I could tell what day it was or if it was a work day/sleepy quiet time or weekend just by the vibes. In other words: don't suck up the negative vibes from the ether & call them your own. It can be hard to do, sure.

Worrying or slacking is way easier than getting your own stuff done.
