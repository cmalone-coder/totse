Re: DOC/LSD question
I'm sure it's already been answered elsewhere, but, what's the difference between DOx and lsd? Also is there anyway that a trip could last about two weeks? Just a curious question.

It works differently. The DOx family are psychedelic amphetamines. They require a small dose [around 1 to 3mg] to have effects, last for 12-48 hours [lasts for more than 2 days with high doses] and gives an extremely light "acid-like" trip. Because of the duration, the low dose required and the fact that it will give mild visual distortions and wavy visuals, DOx can be sold in the form of blotters trying to be passed off as LSD. It's interesting because increasing the dose just prolongs the drug's mild trip and does nothing to increase the intensity.

Anyone who has done real LSD will know something is up. It tastes very bitter, the onset can and usually takes up to 3 hours and the trip is distinguishably different from an LSD trip. It's a mild, speedy and dirty sort of trip. I didn't like DOB very much, and I have a friend who claims DOI was no fun either.
