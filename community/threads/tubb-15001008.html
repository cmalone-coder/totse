This entire paragraph is just one giant example of bad logic, obviously written by someone who believes in creationism yet doesn't understand probability.

It's not illogical for something with a one in six million chance to occur. For example, if you play the state lottery, everyone has a chance of about one in six million of winning. Yet in spite of this, someone wins almost every single time. Likewise, the universe is very large, but the only example of life we know about occured right here. Does that make us special? Of course! But that's because we're very self-absorbed.

If one is going to argue that the chance of evolution creating humans is so low, one has to provide good evidence that the chance of an omnipotent being created the universe outweights those odds. Until I see this evidence, I'm betting on evolution.
