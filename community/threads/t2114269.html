Quitting
This is for those of you who are incredibly sick and tired of being a junkie/crackhead/drunk/etc.

If you are coming off of alcohol or benzos, do so in a detox center or a hospital - the withdrawals are potentially lethal.

Days 1-7 - Drink lots of water, eat lots of ice cream, smoke lots of cigarettes, call non-using friends/family, stay occupied with anything you can.

Days 8-Rest of life:
1. Quit lying to your self. Getting fucked up won't fix a single problem. There is no problem in life that drugs can not make worse.
2. You are the cause of your problems.
3. Wake the fuck up, try telling the truth.
4. Do something that doesn't fuck people over.
5. Continue to talk to your family and clean friends.
6. Learn to enjoy the sunlight (Chances are you'll need to see light every now and then as you start to grow up and take on responsibilities)
7. Pray if you pray (or do it just to help focus your thoughts)
8. Meditate
9. If this doesn't work, get your ass into a support/recovery group like one of the _________ Anonymous groups. There're groups for coke/crack, various narcotics, alcoholics, gamblers, etc.
10. Once you've recovered, help any and all addicts that wish to recover do so. (Do not give them money or a place to stay, most of them will lie about wanting to sober up so they can rob your medicine cabinet, etc.)

Just for the record, I'm an alcoholic and drug addict. I drank daily for five years, did coke every opportunity I had the money for it, choked down benzos like they were pez, had my way with opiates, and rolled my head to the moon. I've done my share of hallucinogens, from shrooms, to acid, to DMT, to 5-meo-DMT, 2ce, and more.

I was a junkie to the max. I was homeless off and on for 3 years. The last time I was homeless, I decided I needed to end this shit once and for all. Life with or without drugs was worthless - I needed something different. And Krees, you describe the underlying mindset to addiction pretty damn well. I do know what it's like; I know that my mind lied to me constantly...it would find any justification possible to excuse another high.

You want to really know how I quit using drugs? I started praying because people sure as fuck couldn't help me. I was forcing myself to hang out with straight-edgers (even though I hated them), started meditating to the best of my junked out ability, and reading tons of books.

When I found that this wasn't enough, I started going to 'meetings'...I'm guessing many of you have gone to these meetings on court orders, so you know what I'm talking about. In there, I went ahead and started working their 'program', and I haven't had a drink or a drug since.

And stupid noob, I know that there are many out there who are heavy drug/drink users that can return to drinking/using in moderation. I'm not one of them, though. I tried it after the first time I sobered up because I wasn't sure if I was a real alcoholic/addict. I have no doubt in my mind today, though. (The distinguishing factor between an alcoholic/addict and someone that is simply physically and psychologically dependent on a drug is that the craving is permanent in an alcoholic/addict. Any reintroduction of the chemical into the body will bring on the craving, followed then by the pattern of unstoppable usage.)
