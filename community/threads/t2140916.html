A Possibly Free High, Need Help
I was reading up for my upcoming scuba certification and I was reading about increased nitrogen absorption. This occurs when one descends and the air pressure increases the same volume of nitrogen is taken in to the blood stream but at a higher density. This causes euphoria and altered perception, similar to a strong pot high. Now you might ask: Why in the hell would I use some shitty, possibly lethal gas that effects divers to get a pot high when I can use pot? Well there are a few reasons, Firstly nitrogen is legal, Secondly nitrogen is one of the most common gases on earth, air is 70% nitrogen, Thirdly an OD can be treated easily by a medical professional. The only problem is condensing air/pure nitrogen enough to get you high.
