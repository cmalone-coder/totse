Re: how big exactly is a "line"?
You're not going to "stay up all night" off of one gram. How many lines you cut and how long it lasts you will depend on quality. Honestly, unless you've got some quality blow, which is unlikely as a new user who has nothing to compare it to, you'll probably wind up cutting out about six lines and doing two of them every fifteen minutes or so-- more or less as you yourself see fit. If you're lucky, you'll be high for about two hours; such is the nature of (most) cocaine.

Once you're using it for a while, that gram will be little more than two or three lines that you rail over the course of ten minutes for an hour high, tops.
