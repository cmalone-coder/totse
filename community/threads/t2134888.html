Re: A question about McCain's Iraq stance...
McCain's stance essentially consists of the position that whether you think Iraq was a good idea or not, the consequences of pulling out before the country is stable (and thus allowing the terrorists to sweep in and take over) would leave the country in the same state as Afghanistan pre-9/11, which is to say a haven for terrorists and a staging ground for attacks on the west. This, he predicts, would just necessitate our going back there later when it's the training ground for the next major attack on American soil. As far as improvements go, yes, his surge idea has pretty much worked. It cut violence in the country dramatically and the infrastructure is starting to develop rather rapidly there. McCain's philosophy is that we should stay until the Iraqis can take care of themselves, which, considering that most of the military operations are being handled by the Iraqi forces nowadays, with coalition forces there merely as backup, doesn't seem to be that far away.
