Are you talking about reincarnation of the body you now live in? I doubt it. However, St. John in the Book of the Revelation of St. John (the world's first written record of an LSD trip) refered to the actual reincarnation of human bodies on Judgement Day.

The Law of Conservation of Energy tells us that energy is neither created nor destroyed. Our entire conscious being is electrical energy. Upon death of the body, this energy departs the body and goes forth. Where to? Another body...and not necessarily right away. There's more, but enough for now.
