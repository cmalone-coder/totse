Re: powdered dxm dosage anounts?
well lemme start by saying you are gonna have more than just FAIR visuals at a dosage that could potentiate an 'outer body experience'.

Moderate visuals are normally start around 300 mgs for average size males.

Outer body experiences are more in the neighborhood of 1000-2000 mg.

2500 is listed as the LDlo(lowest recorded lethal dose) at erowid. On another site, a government one, it says 150mg/kg is the LD50 for human males(median lethal dose). Be careful if you ever aim for that outer body thing. Good weed could help possibly, i'm not sure you might wanna look into other chemical potentiators of dextromethorphan hydrobromide.
