Dirty People with cardboard signs..
They are everywhere, it seems.. Carrying signs scribbled on a piece of cardboard, they stand at the exit ramps of interstates, the exits/entrances of department stores, gas stations, etc.

It pains me horribly when I am forced to pass one and not stop. I'm struggling myself. It is very rare that I'm in a position to help these people.. Every time I cannot stop, I honestly do cry. I know what it is like to be in a position where you are forced to rely on others for help. It is humiliating. I think of how degrading it must feel to have to hold a sign a beg complete strangers for money, food, shelter, clothing..

This month we happen to be a little ahead and had a whole $300 extra! (A huge rarity)

We were leaving Target yesterday, where I had totally splurged. Hahaha.. They were having a sale and I stocked up on clothes and other things for the kids. Anyway, as we were leaving the store parking lot and about to join the main traffic flow, I noticed a woman with a sign. "Need food and shelter, anything will help." That pit opened up in my stomach and I made HandOfZek roll down his window.. "Are you hungry?" I asked, "I'd like to buy you something to eat if I could." The woman was grateful and explained that she and another woman were stranded here in town. I bought her and her traveling companion some McDonald's. Then I went to the grocery store next door and bought them a loaf of bread, bologna, cheese, mustard, crackers, water, bananas, and two candy bars.

I brought them back the McDonald's and groceries and even gave them a $20.

The woman was so grateful, she looked near tears thanking us.

I wanted to cry myself, it just didn't seem like I did enough..
