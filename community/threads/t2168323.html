Weigh your dose out if you have a scale. I fed these two chicks shooms last tuesday; it was their first mushroom trip. Re: first time shroom tonight
Ok, so last friday I tried the same shrooms again. This time eating an eighth and for the first time experiencing a profound shroom trip.

I went over to my same friend's house and bought an eighth. I chewed the caps up one by one and swallowed. I spent about an hour waiting for the first effects. I drove out to this guy's hotel he was staying at to buy oxycodone for the comedown. This guy is the epitome of an opiate binger and is never coherent when he speaks, so naturally I was a bit confused on where to turn. He said something like, "Oh you'll see a bank sign so turn right after it." I asked which bank it was but he didn't know and then mentioned that it was unlite...right. Anyway so not being able to find the turn I said fuck it and decided the spend the roxy money on gas. I began to trip at the gas station.

The pumps looked unfamiliar, and I watched the sides of every straight edge have multiple edges. I got lost staring into the branches of an oak tree next to the fuel station. When I was about done pumping an old guy who didn't know where he was dove up next to my car and asked for directions. All of a sudden I knew the town we were in but nothing else. Everything went blank like I was trying to grasp wherever I was. Suddenly after studying my surroundings while the old guy muttered on about having to get to this other town, I realized where I was, gave him his directions and was on my way. Just weird how I brain lapsed like that.

I got back to my friend's house, who had been asleep all day and was still passed out on his bed. The other people who had gotten me the shrooms had piled into a car and left just as I had left the house, so arriving in a slightly different state of mind with nobody there was sort of creepy. I felt like Depp in Fear and Loathing when he's in the hotel lobby on acid after they first arrive in Vegas, and he's sitting at the bar and turns to talk to his attorney and he's not there. I used the bathroom and sat down to watch TV, waiting for these 2 guys and these 2 chicks to come back from the store - they were getting a bottle. They eventually came back and we all chilled. They asked me how I was doing and I said I was definitely tripping but it was really gentle. The house walls would change color from green to blue, object seemed like their textures were "crawling", walls were "breathing" slightly, and I had mad giggles and was just having a great night - best state of mind I'd ever been in on a psychedelic. I tried to put my finger on what this feeling was and then I got it.... it felt like the psychedelic aspect of MDMA greatly intensified. It sort of felt like I was blowing up.

They offered me some shots but knowing I'd be driving home later that night I turned them down. I don't need a DUI if I was to be fucked by a pig. The house was extremely lively at about 2 hours from dosing. Objects stood out more, I felt like everything moved around me. All of a sudden I got this sharp stomach cramp/pain and so I asked my friendwhat was up. He said I might not even be peaking yet and I would find he was certainly correct. Trying to cope with this strange stromach pain, a bunch of people started showing up and this one dude was trying to score heroin, so I dipped out of there.

I have this 60's and early 70's CD in my car with bands like Strawberry Alarm Clock, Jefferson Airplane, Three Dog Night, Mamas and Papas, etc so I listened to it on the drive home. It was the second most insightful car drive of my life, the first being on cid. I started blowing up really hard listening to all that music and I truely realized what the hippies had tried to do. I started thinking of the "wave speech" by Hunter S. Thompson and what it really meant. By the way, White Rabbit and Combination of the Two REALLY tripped me out. By this time I was arriving home at around 2am. My trip had changed from a outward sensory trip to a profound head-high. I got out of my car and stared at the half moon and the stars. The stars danced around in the clear night sky. Trees and plants boggled my mind. It was like a strange, alien being sapping chaos and raw materials from the Earth to create order and symmetry. I started thinking about animals and how they are the same thing but free-standing and mobile, like a bubble of life that had sprung out from the ashes of existence. It was a cold night with fog hovering a few feet above everything. The night just didn't seem to be the right environment to trip on these blessed mushrooms. I started thinking about how much more amazing it would be outdoors during the daytime. I decided to go inside where it was warm.


-----------


Basically for the rest of the night I had an intense trip. It was like a true experience of life - what life is supposed to be like. I was so connected with everything. I found loopholes in practicly everything which later didn't make sense. My eyes watered with joy and happiness in this state of mind and I couldn't wipe the smirk off my face the entire night.

Downsides were the fact that I couldn't put together very well structured sentences or convey what I truely felt to others. I would loose my train of thought a lot during conversation. That sharp pain in the pit of my stomach didn't go away til about the last hour of my trip. Laying down made me feel better. It felt like the muscles around my stomach had been stunned and went lax allowing my stomach to move around in its cavity. Not a pleasant feeling but bearable.

Overall an amazing experience. I'm doing them again very soon. I guess a half-eighth of shrooms just doesn't cut it for me. An eighth put me where I wanted to be. :)
