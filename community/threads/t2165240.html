Holy Shit, I cant be sure of what it is I just saw, but it was def something up in the sky. I didn't have a camera on me, so I don't have a picture. I will however describe in full detail what I saw.

First and foremost, I do not really believe in UFOs or anything like that I think that a lot of what people "claim" to see is just their imaginations getting the best of them. I kind of wondered if that is what happened to me, but it couldn't have been. What I saw was very vivid and the image is still incredibly clear in my mind.

So, I had heard about this, reading some info on Totse about it. Again, being extremely skeptical I figured this was just a bunch of internet rumors. So I went about my regular day as normal, not doing anything out of the norm. Hell, I wasn't even looking to the sky for some special spaceship.

After a long day at work and then running a couple errands afterwards, I finally got home and knew I would be able to relax. I made some dinner and then started to get the kids ready for bed. I usually try to have them in bed by around 8:30 pm or so, that way they can be a sleep by 9 pm.

Well just like I often do after I lay the kids down, I took the dog out for a walk around the neighborhood. The dog gets to do it's business and I get to unwind. So there we were just like any night. The time was right around 9pm and it was nearly completely dark outside. This kept me from getting a great look at the huge vehicles shape.

And now on to the actual description of the object...
I guess I first notice the object in the sky due to the fact that it had blocked out a couple of stars in the night sky. As I was walking around outside I often find myself gazing at the stars. This time though one star vanished and then another. There was nothing sinister happening, just this flying craft passing between me and various stars. Lets just say that the item was massive enough to block out patches of the night sky. As I noticed that some stars were being blocked out, I then heard a light rumble. The sound must have been coming from the engines needed to power this craft. I couldn't see a standard rocket on this craft and it only traveled in one direction. The speed of the craft also seemed very strange. I assume it was traveling faster than it actually appeared, but the object looked like it was moving very slow. The only shape I could make out in regards of the craft was the front of it. The front was more pointed and it had blades shooting from the side of it. Oh and also I noticed once or twice that a light on it flashed. This whole experience was truly amazing and has changed how I view this crazy world. Shit, I just figured out what I saw. A plane. Damn, now I feel bad you just wasted your time reading all this. Oh, well maybe someone else will see this.
