Some Stuff I Think About.
Maybe the real beings that we are, as Sons of God, 'overshadow' our physical incarnate person. Helping those who hold all their attention on physical desires and earthly activities, and wait for us to remember that the real us is there, our 'Higher Self'. But because of the gift of free will, we can either choose earthly things or heavenly things. For if all comes from God, the most high, how shall we find anything at all witin influences of the earthly nature?

Maybe the Higher Self, that part of us that still remembers who and what we are, waits for us to call upon it, to seek, to ask of it that we might turn to it, rather than earthly desire, rather than earthly Self.

For if we have a part of us that is part of God, the collective, then there is little room for outer Self, because God is the all and the One, the unchanging creator, from which we came and to which me must return.

The our life on earth must be 'ended' and we die to the earth and become born again of the Spirit, give ourselves to the Truth and the Will of that which creates all things and works all wonders.

That if we know ourselves to be not of this earth, but born of God then do we not have the power and counsel of God, and to use that power in ways that bring the Word or message of God to the earthly peoples so that they may be brought back up from whence they have fallen?

That the experience of knowing ourselves to be part of God yet on this earth, can we not do all things that God would have us do?

Would we not perform miracles in the sight of men, and prophecy and do great wonders?

For those of earth look for this, and are starved from this, and are dead to their self realization.

But in everything is seen the laws and ways of the most high, but they have blinded themselves with the desire of Self and desire for earthly things.

Would you rather experience Self or God? That is what is set out before us, and we choose.

But those who know not themselves as Sons of God were alive but are dead and will live again if they raise themselves from the ground and take from the Tree of Life.

We left the Tree of Life, and it is guarded from us by ourselves. So if we defeat ourSelves we should be able to eat freely and live as Gods as we wish.

Because we will do all those things which Jesus did and more.

Then how shall we believe those who say they are of God or are of power and wisdom if they do not these miracles as Jesus did (and much more) before men?

For how shall they know a good Tree unless they see good fruits?

Comments?
