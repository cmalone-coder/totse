From the classic American novel, "Sitting on a Hookers Face."

"Yeah, it's actually pretty popular with my friends. Here are my prep steps:
1. 1.5 g of plant material per intended dose.
2. Grind up into powder. Place in container.
3. Cover in EtOH. Let sit 3 - 5 days.
4. Strain into pyrex dish and dry in oven at 200F. (Note: Leave oven door slightly open.)
5. Once dried, weigh out and divide by number of doses. I typically get .14-.20g of extract per dose, with more as a function of time.
6. Load into capsules."

Now, obviously, analysing this essential text, we can see that it's easily changeable to whatever amounts we desire. However, there is more:

"One to two capsules are consumed and in about 15 minutes heavy sedation is found that is contrastly accompanied with crystal clear thinking and a severe reduction in anxiety, which continues for a few hours. Visual systems are affected but not psychedelically, instead the visual experience is more profound, as if more details from the environment can emerge. The physical sedation is often met with a feeling of being underwater. Sleep and dreams are affected for days after, particularly with larger doses, so the conscious affects of C. zacatechichi may be enjoyed without worry that one misses the affected dream state.

Killing hookers for blow is Satan's urge to all humankind."

Truly a strange text, indeed.

3 questions.

1) Does it have to be dry first? I'd assume it would.

2) does the alcohol content matter? Do I just want some cheap 100 proof vodka, or do I need some crazy stuff like Everclear.

3) you said to strain the liquid. So, when dried, the alcohol evaporates, leaving only a purified form of whatever causes the psychoactive effects?

From the appendix:

"1. Yes, I let it completely dry out first. It doesn't form a sticky resin like most EtOH extracts. Usually once the dish dries out, I scape it out.

2. For the extraction it matters. I use Everclear because it dries out fast and I need alot less to extract. While both EtOH and water are polar solvents and either will extract the alkaloids, Everclear does a much better job (and at $15 a bottle, insanely cheap) and dries out much faster than water. You can use vodka, but it will take much longer to evap if you want to put in capsules. I prefer Everclear because it's cheaper, quicker, and better than other alcohols sold. However, of course, since the EtOH is evaped off, it makes no psychoactive difference, just a difference in extraction time and ability.

3. To strain, I have a wire mesh strainer and coffee filter that I filter the solution through, and the filter catches the left over plant material. The solution and eventually evaporated extract contain ~all the alkaloids of the plant, which I load into capsules so I don't have to taste the material. It's by no means 'purified,' but, hell, we don't even know what we're looking for. No one (I believe), knows yet what is the active ingredient, though a few ideas have been passed around.
