There's only two things that I have found that do the job - and I'm afraid they're not going to be too much help to you I'm afraid.

1.] Time. I've found that a broken heart is a lot like quitting heroin in a couple of ways - I've had both, so I speak from experience here.

They both are painful - though quitting heroin is more painful, physically, if you quit cold turkey. Both keeps you from going about your daily routine without a lot of effort.

They both take a long time to get over. Notice I said get over and not get rid of. That's because both are with you - more or less - the rest of your life. But after a certain amount of time they both fade into the background of your mind allowing you to almost forget you have it.

The amount of time it takes to get where you can move on and get one with your life obviously is different for each person. But I can tell you that it will be longer than you hope or want, but shorter than you expect.

The other way to get over your ex is:

2.] Get another GF. If you do, it is still going to take time to get over the ex, just not as much time as it would without a new GF.
