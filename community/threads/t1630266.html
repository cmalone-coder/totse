Re: Drugs that change you most?
When I was thirteen, my world was divided into two groups of people--those who had done acid and those who hadn't. I couldn't wait until the day I would wake up and could say to myself, "I'm no longer an acid virgin." A year after that I got ahold of a ten-strip and really had fun.

But for me, the problem with acid, as well as mushrooms and maybe even DXM, is that along with all of the standard "I am god, I am alive, I am beautiful, I see all" feelings that surface, (which for the most part I do think are true) there is just so much bullshit. I really enjoy it when I'm tripping, and it seems great, funny, amazing, but when I look back on it I feel a little cynical about the whole thing. The revelations, no matter how monumental they seem or may actually be, end up appearing a little trite when you realize that everyone who's done significant amounts of potent hallucinogenics ends up with similar conclusions.

I'm not unwilling to preserve the sanctity of mushroom-land or the walking on water that I've done while on LSD, but although I've learned a lot through use of both of the above, they really didn't change my life like the big bright yellow slogan on the packaging said it would. Of course, I do recognize that what I get out of it depends on me, and I suspect that my character or personality just doesn't take to the one-try life-altering ideas.

Talk, talk, talk. Listen to me endlessly orate, really not even about drugs at all but about myself. I'm sorry. I'm such an asshole for posting this much about it, but everyone always seems to have so much to say for God Acid, and how it gave them a completely new outlook, and blew their mind, and so on.

Dont get me wrong, I love it. I've never really done it solely "to get fucked up"--though I may give that a shot when it starts rolling back into my area. So far I've got a mix of good-natured silly fun and introspection/personal growth that comes from it. But I don't know. It never pushed me over any line or edge.

The one that did that was heroin. BAM it smacked me in the face and that was it. It's a cliche, but it's like there's something inside me now that wasn't there before.
