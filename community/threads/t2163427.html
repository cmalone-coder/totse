Beyond the 360, PS3, and Wii.
Between the console wars thread and Snoopy's curiosity regarding the post-Wii Nintendo, I thought that a thread would be in order concerning predictions of the upcoming eighth video gaming generation. If the mods wish for this discussion to be integrated into the console wars thread, I understand.

Here are some thoughts I'd like to offer:

PS4: Personally I would love to see this console be backwards compatible more than a decade all the way to those games released on the PSone. Let me use my PS2 controller, and no, I don't need it to be wireless. Take out the Blu-Ray shit, or at least make it an optional add-on, I don't need it. Have a reasonably sized built-in hard drive, but I'd like to be able to use memory cards to take my shit to a friend's house. Oh, and please convince Konami to re-release Suikoden and the first sequel to that series.

X-Box 720: Work on decreasing the failure rate to one more in line with the consumer electronics industry. Backwards compatibility with all original X-Box and 360 games, or at least a Virtual Console style system of downloading those original titles. The ability to use my non-wireless Controller S, because the original X-Box controller was a fucking hulking monstrosity meant for beating someone with in a street fight as a blunt object.

Nintendo #6: I'm calling it the no. six because that'll be the Nintendo home console generation we're considering. A controller as ergonomic as that of the GameCube would be nice, and give me an extra-long cord, not wireless capabilities. Continue the Virtual Console shop but try to include such third-party games as GoldenEye, and network 'em so that I can kick my friend's ass in the Complex at 3 a.m. while I'm in Ohio and he's in Georgia. Give me enough Hard-Drive space to store all those games, or an add-on so that I can download them to a detachable unit and take 'em over to a friend's house and play.

So, I'd love to hear what you all have to say/predict/want/etc.!
