Re: Shroom Storage
Oh, I just thought of another factor that might influence storage conditions. High pressures encourage gases to react to form solids or liquids. Low pressures encourage gases to remain gases, or encourage liquids or solids to react to form gases. For instance, when you take the cap off a 2-liter bottle of pepsi, carbonic acid reacts to form carbon dioxide and water, and pushes the reaction in that direction, hence the bubbles.

A pressurized mushroom storage environment would encourage oxygen to react with mushrooms. This is precisely what you don't want. Vacuum storage works very well because it discourages the small amount of gas inside it from reacting with the solids. Canning is another way of using this to your advantage, but you have to heat stuff to can it and this isn't so good for mushrooms.

I've noticed a slight vacuum when I've opened my mushroom jar in the past. This is really good for the shrooms, since it means the pressure favors fewer reactions.
