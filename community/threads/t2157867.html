Reading ability is not something to stress over, if anything pressurizing her is exactly what you should not be doing. That just leads to anxiety which then becomes directly associated with the act of reading, which is obviously counterproductive*. Just read with her in a relaxed manner as much as possible, make it fun, laugh, joke, clown around - bring the words to life.

Teaching a child phonetics (http://www.cambridgeenglishonline.com/Phonetics_Focus/) (I don't mean the phonetic symbols themselves) is very helpful, as it allows you to pronounce words you've never seen before purely on the basis of the phonemes (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoneme) the word is made from. If you're already doing this then that's great and just stick with it.

And as for testing the ability to read backwards... who in God's name cares whether a person can read words in the wrong direction. Maybe I'm missing the point, but this sounds like one of these stupid modern-learning activities that seem to actively encourage retardation.

Stop it.
