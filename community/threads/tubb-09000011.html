General Theory of Aliens, UFOs, etc.
Here is my thought on what the flying UFOs in the sky actually are. Let's first figure out what they aren't.

Extra-terrestials:
The classic gray looking alien with the humanoid appearance could not be from another planet. We have evolved this way over millions of years. To think intelligence evolved on another planet is logical enough, but to think that this intelligence evolved with coincidentally the SAME physical features is a bit of a stretch. Especially since there are animals on this planet that might have intelligence that look nothing like us (dolphins).
Also mathematics tends to put the probability of two totally separate planets separated by MILLIONS of light years, finding each other in the infinity of the universe as the same probability that Pat Buchanan and Dr. Laura will win the 2000 election! And also, imagine yourself an alien who has just travelled a billion light years to find a bunch of apes that seem to have mastered a few tools, talk about a let down!

Trans-dimensionalists:
Ok, there is the theory that aliens could be from another dimension, and that there is an infinite amount of dimensions where every possibility is played out. However, if it were possible to cross over, then one of the possibilities would be that right now another group of aliens would cross over and appear in your living room. Now, I may be making a generalization here, but I'm betting there are NO aliens in your living room right now!

Now lets look at what characteristics we place on aliens:
Humanoid
Doesn't want to be discovered
Documented throughout history
Advanced technology
Curious about humans

So my theory is that aliens are not fun-loving explorers from beyond the stars, but simply human time-travelers from the future. They look that way because that is the way we will look after a few millenia of genetic engineering and evolution. All they want to do is observe humanity, and remain unnoticed so they don't do too much damage to the timeline.

So, that is my crazy theory, what does everyone think? PLEASE REPLY!

So, you, relatively speaking, skipped some time to catch up with your destination point. All in theory. So in essence the speed of time is equal to the speed of light.
