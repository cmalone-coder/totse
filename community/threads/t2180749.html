Re: my pictures of the moon and planets 2008
lol. I never said what I took these with (I was about to)

Some (the large ones) were taken with my regular digital camera. A Panasonic DMC-TZ3 or something along those lines (the number might be a 4) which has something like 8 megapixels and since I don't resize, etc etc. If you hadn't noticed, btw, tinypic also changes the file format to .bmp which might alter the exif data. The smaller ones were taken with a modified webcam that has a max resolution of 640x480. Both mosaics were taken with that.

Here are more:
Solar eclipse on Jupiter (look for the black dot on the bottom right) -
http://img397.imageshack.us/img397/6...dow3bigrs6.png

Moon-
Apeninne Mtns: http://img397.imageshack.us/img397/5...mpositeou5.png
Caucasus Mtns: http://img131.imageshack.us/img131/9...untainsnr2.png
Clavius Crater (the big, dark one at the top): http://img179.imageshack.us/img179/4107/claviussy0.png

I do not "expose" these for a certain time per se...the way it is done is by recording a video, then finding the best frames through a program and stacking them to increase contrast, signal to noise ratio, and remove atmospheric blurring.

Some of these, like Saturn and some Jupiter, were recorded by holding up my digital camera to the eyepiece. Others were recorded by using the modified webcam that cost about ~$99.

Here's the modified webcam I used: http://www.telescopes.com/telescope-...rwsoftware.cfm

The newest generation of my digital camera: http://www2.panasonic.com/consumer-e...00000000005702
