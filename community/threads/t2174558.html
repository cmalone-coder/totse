OP: Go to pretty much any hardware store and buy a box of alligator clips, or get an antistatic wrist strap and take one off of it. Aligator clips are small, portable, indefinitely reusable, and easy to use. You don't even have to remember to squeeze them like you do tweezers. Best of all, they don't actually touch your weed, they just touch the paper. No offense, but I wouldn't want to take even the smallest risk and stick a paperclip in my joint when I have such a better alternative.

I used to always roll filtered joints. Nowadays, I've started rolling them without filters. It's easier to just throw an unfiltered roach in my bong to finish it than it is to painstakingly pull the roach out with tweezers, while trying not to tear the paper open. Filters are great for keeping those tiny bits of weed out of your mouth, but when I know I'm going to want to finish that roach in the bong, I just save myself a little hassle. Plus, I figure filters could possibly filter out the THC, so I'm getting the strongest smoke possible.
