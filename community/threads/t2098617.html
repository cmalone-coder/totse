Re: Distilling Sugar Wine into Moonshine
Quote:
Originally Posted by TheBlackPope
Ok, well if I make wine and distill it, it is not like any moar methanol can just randomly appear, so what do I have to worry about?

Because whilst in your 2L of sugar wine the methanol wasn't enough to cause problems, once you've removed most of the excess water, fusel oils, and other yukk stuff to leave you with a potable sherry, the methanol will have increased in the comparitive concentration. So (and I'm just making up numbers here, don't know the actual concs but the principle is still sound) if you have 1mL of methanol in 2000mL of sugar wine, in a 100mL glass of the wine you're only drinking about 0.05mL of methanol. However, with the other stuff removed, so, say, 800mL of sherry, if you then drink a 100mL glass of that, you're looking at 0.125mL of methanol per glass.

Even though there's no more methanol created, with less dilution it becomes toxic enough to cause damage.
