Will you get addicted? It's a very real possibility. Maybe not right away, maybe you can manage a weekend habit or monthly habit. Or maybe you'll fall for it right away. It doesn't matter how much you think you can handle it, somewhere along the line you could find yourself stuck in the vicious cycle that is addiction. When you know the joy that heroin can give you and you're feeling down or depressed or your girl broke up with you or whatever reason, dope can find a hold on you. I know someone that did heroin once, didn't touch it for years then tried it again and over a not so long period of time found himself hooked.

It is possible to use heroin without getting addicted. But know that it's a tough game and once you get into it the rules tend to change.

Also, 1.5g is quite a lot of diesel for having no tolerance. Where I'm at (northeast, real good stuff) 1.5g would probably be around 3 bundles. I know a lot of people would say that's bullshit, but I also wouldn't trust a junky to eyeball small amounts of powder.
