I'm not sure why hash is different, really, but it certainly is. Good hash is infinitely more pleasant for me, than good weed. I find it more relaxing, there's no burn out, it smells and tastes better.... s'like comparing strong weed (cheap vodka) with a good red wine. The THC argument isn't really that simple, because the number of canabinoids which affect a high are numerous. Strong indoor weed though, especially, has been bred for higher THC levels, and lower CBD levels. There's something about the ageing process with hash which breaks down the canabinoids a bit; even with a sativa hash you never get that "buzz" of weed, it's much more of an uppy mellow feeling.

I was smoking some of the best Nepalese a few weeks ago, and no weed I have smoked has ever really compared as a "day to say" smoke. Sure, i've been more high before, more crazy, more tripped out, more whatever.... but high on hash i could read and concentrate fine, go climbing, talk, go into town, explore..... all was good. There has to be some truth to the "anti psychotic" (almost...) properties of CBN, cos when i smoke hash, the high feels so much more balanced out.

Personally, as I smoke cigarettes, and like spliffs anyway, I like just picking bits in, or rolling really think bits of hash and making a nice joint with tobacco. Failing that, pipes/bongs are pretty nice, as are vapes. In the words of a crazy hippy I met though.... "the ganja and the toba-ccccooooo..... mixed together man, is tantra, you understand man? Is perfect! It's TANTRIC!" lol! :D ;) :p
