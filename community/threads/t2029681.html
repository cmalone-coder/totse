I like to have things separated. I have different groups of friends with which I share different interests. I have just generally had some bad experiences with certain people from one aspect of my life crossing to another. Especially since some of the people I hang out with are major gossipers, some of whom even have ties to my family. So once that barrier is broken, there is no distinction. Everything I say under any circumstances is at risk of being dinner time conversation at the next family gathering.

Frankly, I can't take that kind of pressure. Especially when I'm out to have a good time, and let loose.
