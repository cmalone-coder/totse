Re: first time with crystal
pharmaceutical meth, aka desoxyn, is speed with all the standard pill fillers. other than that, they are one and the same.

crystal meth is freebase methamphetamine. it can be smoked.

explanation, if you care:
you can't smoke desoxyns or powder speed because it is in a salt form. the free base has been mixed with an acid and now it consists of positive meth ions and some other negative ions. the attraction between positive and negative ions makes something very hard to vaporize and thus smoke, and usually before it vaporizes, your meth will have broken down and oxidated and become useless to your CNS.

hopefully that helped, or at least didn't confuse you. flame me if i'm wrong.
