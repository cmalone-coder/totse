Re: Simple question from a simple man.
"So what are you reading?"

"Anything good?" (if she's looking at the computer?)

"What are you listening to?" (if she's got an ipod with her, etc)

Ask her about what she's doing at the moment and if it's something you are genuinely interested in there shouldn't be a problem. If it's not, move along, there's nothing there for you anyways.

TO explain further: Don't ask a question you know what the answer will be. Ask a question that she will have a unique answer for that you can expand on with further conversation. "Whatsup" will always (or usually) come with a reply of "not much" or something similar. Ask a question that will give you a conversation starter as an answer.
