Re: experiments
grind the buds up as fine as you can,

spoon into a rotating flask,

add 250 ml of isopropyl or methanol per oz,

attach flask to your roto evaporator,

spin flask and heat to 60c whilst running icy water through the condensor.

after 3 hours,

filter out buds and sell,

evaporate extraction solvent,

the brown oil is your hash oil..



or just add it all to an erlenmayer flask,,add the condensor

heat flask and cool vapour,

etc etc
