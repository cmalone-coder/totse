Re: smoking pot
If you want a picture with billowing clouds of thick smoke, take a big hit into your mouth but don't inhale it into your lungs. Open your mouth and don't blow it out, but kind of suck in your cheeks a little and waft your tongue. Voila; thick, slow-moving clouds of smoke that looks great in a picture. It's a huge waste of weed though.

I like to 'waterfall' the smoke as a little party trick. Basically, you do the above with mostly empty lungs, then inhale through your nose as you slowly push the smoke out of your mouth with your tongue. If you do it right you can get a constant stream of thick white smoke running out of your mouth and into your nose, although it's damn hard to do it without letting smoke escape and it hurts your nose a bit.

Ghosting does get more THC into your system, but generally I can't be bothered to do it unless we don't have a lot of weed left or we're playing smoking games.
