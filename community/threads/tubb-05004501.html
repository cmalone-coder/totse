Idea for smoking at home
I have moved away from home now, but back in the day getting stoned at home was pretty hard. But the following is a little method I came up with that is almost foolproof for smoking at home without getting caught.

Basically, the idea is that you go into your bathroom with your bong and mix already prepared, then strip down and turn the shower on. Have just the hot water on to create a lot of steam (covers the smoke). Blow into a ceiling fan. Then once you are finished smoking jump in the shower and wash yourself completely to get rid of the smell. Finally, give your teeth a brush and if you get red eyes put some clear eye drops in.

Unless you are one of those weird ass families that share bathrooms at the same time ect, this plan should work perfect. The only ways you will get caught is either by acting too stoned in front of your family or by a family member actually finding the bong/weed in your room. Apart from that their should be no trace of weed smoking.

I hope this helps a few of you out...happy smoking.
