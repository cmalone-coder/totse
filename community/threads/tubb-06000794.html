Re: Samples and Programs
quote:
hey everyone, where can i find free good samples for hiphop or reggae that i can use in fruityloops


1) Easiest way. Go on a search engine, and search for them

2) Better way: Record your own.

3) More tradtitional (but slightly controversial way): Take samples from old records. (And make sure you give everyone proper credit!)

quote:
also does anyone know any voice changing programs that can make ur voice sound like if it was on the phone or sometihng?


Yes. I've seen little programs that ONLY do this to a .wav file, but thats kinda useless. Before you ask this question, you should have asked, "Why does a voice on the phone sound the way it is?" The simple answer to that question is that phones only trasmit certain frequencies, and can only send a certain quality signal to begin with (which further limits the frequency range). So, what you need is a program that limits what you hear in a sound. I use SoundForge by Sonic Foundry. Its probably the best audio editing program you can get. Downside? Its a bit pricey (Good thing for you theres a demo).

quote:
also, does anyone know any other good music creating program?


If you want something full featured (as in, not JUST a sampler or a sequencer), I'd check out Reason by Propellerheard. (http://www.propellerheads.se/) It probably does the best software job of emulating the 909 and 808 than any other piece of software, I'm lucky enough to own a 909, I don't really have much use for it. Its MUCH superior to Fruity Loops, simply because Fruity Loops takes existing samples and manipulates them (although there are plenty of fun soft synths for it), Reason instead emulates the synths used to create them.

Also, what kind of hardware are you using? That may open up your choices some (and eliminate a few).
