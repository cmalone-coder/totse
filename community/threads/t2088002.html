Just someone my mom knew through a friend when she was younger. I still don't know the whole story there. Not sure I ever want to dig into it.

10: Mother arrested again. The day before my birthday. At my school. In front of my friends.

11 (3 months after the above): Grandmother decides to send me to live with my aunt in Florida, who I hadn't seen in 5 years, while my mother was in jail.

12: Aunt and uncle decide I'm too much trouble after acting out at school and getting expelled. Therapy was unsuccessful, so my aunt bought a plane ticket to Baltimore, and put me on it. My uncle gave me a $100, which I promptly spent on a Gameboy color when my plane landed. I learned a few years later that no one was supposed to be there to meet me, and my aunt had just sent me off. When my mom called that night to talk to me, my aunt basically said, "yeah, I put him on a plane to Baltimore. It should be landing in the next hour. Might want to find someone to pick him up." After sitting alone in the terminal at BWI for 3 hours, a friend of my moms arrived to pick me up. I had never met her before, but I would be spending the next week living with her, until she sent me to my grandmothers. I stayed at my grandmothers for 3 weeks before being put into a foster home run by insane conservative Christian fundamentalists. What was left of my faith and self-esteem was summarily destroyed.

14: Mother released from prison, I move back with her. Severe emotional and trust issues intact.

14-17: Did exceptionally poorly in school, because I refused to do homework. Evicted from 2 houses during this period, spent a few months living with the woman who picked me up from the airport. Got mono, missed a lot of school. Had to leave that ladies house, and we spent a looooong time bouncing back and forth between cheap motels, sleeping in her office, and living out of our car. Of course, we lost every possession we had during this time, as well, so that was fun.

17-18: Moved in with one of her co-workers. Who was certifiably insane and beat her children, but I couldn't do anything about it, because I didn't want to lose a place to stay. She was especially hard on the youngest daughter. She was 9, and was, I think, this woman's niece or something, and was the sweetest, most loving little girl around. She was blamed for everything, and was routinely beaten with a breadboard. I still hate myself for not stopping it. Even typing this now, I'm crying, because I realize that I am a horrible, disgusting person for not saving her. This is when I developed my 2 year amphetamine addiction. I also got hepatits A from the well water and missed more school. A few months of living in that hell and we moved in with a friends friend in her 2 bedroom condo. Which means I was sharing a room with my mom. I stopped going to school, and fell into an insanely deep depression which I have yet to escape. The woman we were staying with was a fucked up white trash whore with just as much drama as myself, if not more. An alcoholic in an abusive relationship who took our share of the rent money and bought cases of Coors and cartons of Marlboro Lights. Needless to say, we were evicted from there, as well, though this time, not really our fault. I dropped out of school, because they said I missed so much that I couldn't graduate anyway.

18-19: We moved in with a different friend, where we stayed for a few months. Then, we got our own place in April. I turned 19 in May. IN August, I woke up nearly paralyzed from the waist down because of an apparently worsening disc problem. I spent months in agony because I couldn't afford health insurance. I went through with a bunch of chiro/pt kind of crap because I could afford it, but it never worked. Cue my switch from amphetamines to opioids. By mid-December, I am able to get health insurance, and see a doctor to find out what the condition is.

19-20 : After seeing a bunch of different specialists, turns out is a routine slipped disc and massively inflamed nerve root. Fortunately, I get great pain meds from my doctor, eventually getting up to 50mcg fent patches, which I supplemented with 120mg of OC. Until she sends me to a pain management doctor who immediately drops me to neurontin, 600mg of tramadol and 60mg of morphine. I say fuck him, and keep using my own meds, because his plan would have sent me into withdrawals, and he was an idiot. I go through 4 incredibly painful epidurals in 3 months, but nothing stops the pain. A week before my birthday, I have surgery, and spend the next 4 weeks in insane pain, the last week of which in withdrawal because I've run out of drugs and can't score. When I'm finally able to move, I get more OC and use throughout the summer, when I finally get a real job and quit. Even though I can move now, I will forever have peripheral neuropathy, and a 20% chance of the disc slipping again, and I just won't know until I wake up crying in pain again.

20 : Apparently, in May, my mother's work, a non-profit, lost major funding and stopped paying. So rent backed up, and she didn't get another job because she was dedicated to what she was doing. September rolls around, and we get evicted again. Move to a new place, which is where we are now. She's still not getting regular paychecks, which means I'm supporting our entire family on $35K. Which can't really be done where I live. So bills are piling up once again. I'm >$10K in debt, but at least, we're not getting evicted! And just recently, I found out that the one thing I was counting on to save me, going to college, isn't going to happen, because the one school I was planning on going to doesn't accept my GED. I'm a 20 year old recovering opioid addict, and probable alcoholic.

This is a lot longer than I thought it was going to be, and there's still more I could go into, but don't really want to. I'm not entirely sure why I wrote this all, but what the hell, I guess...
