Re: Being a "nice guy" or a bitch?
Definition time:

Being nice: Doing something that benefits other people without putting yourself at a disadvantage (ie. holding a door for someone behind you, helping someone find their way, giving away a spare cigarette or a few shots, etc.)

Being too nice: Doing stuff that benefits other people at your own expense. (ie. if you give away your last cigarettes or beers just to accomodate others, and you are left wanting)

Being a bitch: Doing things that others order you to do. Whether the task actually is difficult or not is irrelevant, because you lose respect and self-esteem by taking orders.

You strike me as being "too nice", but you could easily fall into the "bitch" category if you're being bossed around. While their technically isn't anything wrong with going out of your way to help others (ie. that could bring you joy), I suspect it's probably because you want people to like you. It's counterproductive, as people that are "too nice" are basically just viewed as "useful" and "convenient", as opposed to good company or interesting individuals.
