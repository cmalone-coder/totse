Re: Ocho Rios, Jamaica (Beaches resort) and marijuana
Nice, sounds like you're going to one of those higher-end all inclusive resort. I went to Rui Ocho Rios about a year and a half ago and drugs were so easy to get it wasnt even funny. If you have the patience to get the best bud, go on a tour of the Fern Gully(this jungle road that takes you through the jamaican rainforest) or another sort of jungle/rainforest tour and chances are pretty good that somewhere you stop on the tour someone will have some awesome buds. Like 1.5'' thick bud thats like a foot long, absolutely covered w/crystals, and you can negotiate to buy however much you want worth of it, 5, 10, 20 bucks, whatever. I think I got like a 4-6'' piece for like 10 bucks, and I was blazed out of my brains for like 3 days. THey have great hash too, and they'll hook you up cheap w/that too, like 4 grams for 20 bucks. Mix it in w/your bud and get ridiculously blazed. Almost all the bud there is sativa, the stimulating bud rather than the indica couch-lock bud, so you'll really be able to enjoy you vacation, your food the girls the beach all of it.

If you are over 16 or look over 16 then you can get as much to drink as you like if it is included in your all-inclusive bracelet. Just look around and see what other people who have the same color/type bracelet as you and see what they're doing at the bar and do the same. In terms of other drugs, you can get coke pretty easily there too, I thought it had heroin or something in it because it was brownish cocaine but its just not completely refined coke.

You can get almost any sort of drug you want there except prescription pills towards the higher-end opiates. If you do want to get some prescription stuff there some is over the counter, Soma for example is sold under the brand name Scutamil/Schutamil, this eastern european brand that is sold freely in Jamaica. Benzos are negotiable, meaning you sort of do need a prescription but the pharmacist is usually "open to negotiate" if you phrase your stuff right. Nitrazepam, for example, is a nice euphoric benzo usually used for sleep, and if you say something like "I'm having trouble sleeping, I have ambien/lunesta w/me and its not helping me here, but temazepam has helped before, is there something similar you can give me?" then luck can go your way. If you cant get presciption stuff in this manner you'll have to go to a local doc, but he/she is usually pretty convincable to be empathic towards your problems, just as long as you dont ask for Oxycontin dosages excessively (like more than 160 mg/day with no records). Prescription stimulants/ADD are very hard to obtain as in most countries outside the US and Canada, as most countries dont care for such "addictive drugs" to be given for such minor conditions. Some docs/pharmacies are exceptions to this rule though.

There's also a good amount of Exctasy floating around the island usually consumed by tourists. Thats a tricky drug to work with there b/c quite a bit is bunk and can be sort of expensive, the lowest I had negotiated was down to 3 pills for 40 down from 30 bucks a pop at the start of negotiation. Its a fun place to do some mushrooms and acid though, I would go for mushrooms b/c its cheaper and less likely for you to get ripped off. If you like X and never tried mushrooms Jamaica is as happy and good a place as any for your first mushroom trip, and that way you'll spend less than you would on (probably bunk) Exctasy and have an interesting/very pleasant experience, esp. w/weed.
