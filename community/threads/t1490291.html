Can't we beat them to death with maces? What happened to the good ol' days?

Take a positive out of this- intellectually, you realize this is fucked up. That's more than a lot of people in similar situations can say, and that's often where you need to start.

Now you have to work from there. Dump him. Sob holes in your pillows if you have to. Visualize this: You own a piece of land. Think about the worst thing you can imagine anyone doing to it- carving out pieces with a bulldozer and hauling them off to Never Never Land, sticking wooden spikes in every foot at fucked-up angles that make it look like a mass grave for vampires, knocking down your house with a wrecking ball.. hell, maybe they smash your carefully tended azaleas. That would drive some people to homicide.

Now imagine your heart as your most cherished piece of real estate, and understand that AT LEAST from the instant you got that text message, he has been repeatedly shredding, stabbing, wrecking, and/or smashing it in that worst possible way. You live on that land. If it were going on in the physical world, where land and ownership are as so much dust in the wind, how long would you tolerate that shit, no matter who was doing it? Not as long as you've tolerated him, I'm betting, yet you still linger in confusion over what to do with him.

I won't be the one to say you're not a weak person. I don't know you well enough to discern that one way or the other. But even if you are, realize that weakness is not a permanent state of being. You can become strong if you want, but you have to do it. No one's going to dump him for you. No one's going to make time pass any more swiftly so that your emotional wounds heal faster. It's all so cliche, and it's all so true.

You do not have an open-ended question in front of you; you have a decision to make and two options to choose from. For all the words and tears that can be spilled over this, everything's going to come down to that simple fact. Go one way or go the other, just don't stand there.
