When your boot loader comes up (the first thing after the BIOS POST), look for an option like "recovery shell" or something along those lines. I know Ubuntu makes them, and IIRC so does Fedora.

However, you can also use interactive init to stop cups from starting. Look in some of the logs in /var/log (probably it's in syslog) for CUPS related error messages, so you can troubleshoot the problem.

You might also want to check on the Fedora forums and the Fedora wiki for any known bug fixes related to this.
