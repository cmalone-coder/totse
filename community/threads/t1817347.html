Re: THE BEST ever game stroy line???
Deus Ex-- If you've played the game you'll know what I mean by "best storyline in any FPS"

System Shock 2-- The twists and turns the game goes through make it one of the best games I've ever played... Not to mention all the other stuff.

Max Payne & Payne 2-- Pure Gaming Bliss for more than just gameplay.

Chrono Trigger-- Time Traveling and all that shizzit. Floating Kingdom of Zeal? Epoch? End of the World? Fuckin' LAVOS?! How can you not love it!?

Final Fantasy III-- Usually you're out to stop a bad guy from destroying the world. Well, you just happen to lose in this game. The second half of the game is just about picking up the pieces...

A Link to the Past-- Remember this one, kids?

Final Fantasy X-- Some may disagree, but I really enjoyed this story.

Xenogears-- What may well be the granddaddy of all RPG stories... Let me put it this way: This game was almost banned in the U.S. because the storyline dealt too heavily with religious themes. Sure sometimes the actual storytelling was a bit strange sometimes, but the story itself... OH, man.

Pac-Man-- Dude, okay, so there's this yellow greater-than-or-less-than sign and it goes around eating these white dots and getting chased around by ghosts... And... Uum... Yeah.

Ogre Battle: The March of the Black Queen--Yeah, it took two Queen song titles and stuck 'em together, but the story of this game was SWEET. The only catch was that you as the player had to actually take the time and search out the story for yourself.

---In other words, you could just play this game through and you ended up with a fairly decent early console tactical RPG. But if you REALLY played the game and kept up with what was going on in the game world as you made your way across the land, you ended up with a very rich story that makes some fantasy games look somewhat trivial in comparison. To top it off, towards the end there's this kicker that makes you wonder exactly what you're fighting for... As strange as it is, Ogre Battle is a very personal game, because what you get out of the game depends heavily on what your personal views on many things are. Come on, a game that starts off with your very own TAROT CARD reading has got to be somewhat personal, right?

Super Metroid-- How this game was able to take a funny little adventure game on the 8-bit Nintendo Entertainment System with a one-page introduction to what's going on and turn it into one of the best sci-fi stories is beyond me. But it worked. And it was incredible.

Speaking of Metroid...

Metroid Fusion-- The story of Prime "There's bad guys on this planet, so go kill them" was so-so for me, but Metroid Fusion was a kick in the ass in terms of storyline. It even managed to expand upon the ideas set forth by Super Metroid. The idea of missions and such in a Metroid game was a turn for the better if you ask me. It gave a sense of direction to a game that would otherwise be... Well, just about exploration and getting lost. It added a focus to the game and gave background to a lot of things. Plus, seeing metroids again after they'd supposedly been wiped out brought a smile to my face... And seeing an Omega... Whoo!

I'm sure there are others... Give me time to think.

-Dust
