Re: Weight Lifting question
Don't add 7kg. That's quite a lot even for a deadlift or a squat.

Add 5kg if you feel like you can do it, but generally stick to around 2.5kg per session. Or whatever is the least you can add.

Better to add less and be able to still do it, than add more and fail.

IMO your routine should look something like this:

Monday: bench and curl
Tuesday: bench and curl
Wednesday: bench and curl
Thursday: bench and curl
Friday: bench and curl
Saturday: bench and curl
Sunday: off

But seriously, you need 48hrs to rest. If you're getting that, then you probably aren't going to experience overtraining.

15 sets though? That's what most people would do in an entire workout, spanning multiple excercises. Bring it down to something more like 5 (with warm-up sets too). 10 reps isn't that bad. Just make sure that you get to failure.
