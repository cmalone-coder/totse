Re: Feudal Era Anime?
Shigurui

FOR THE WIN, really!

I strongly advice this one. It's a very close description of Japan in the Edo period. The samurai caste system etc. Let me spoil the first scene for you just to get you excited:

It starts off with a woman commiting seppuku (ritual suicide by disembowelment). You have to cut open your own stomach and then a second cuts off your head. Thats the common myth. However they made this so accurate. Being she is a woman, she doesn't have to actually pick up a knive and cut herself, she can just lean over and touch a fan. At the moment she does that, her head is chopped. And no, the head was never chopped off cleanly, they tried to leave a piece of flesh there so the head wouldn't go flying through the room. Don't mind the detailed insides of a person's body though!
