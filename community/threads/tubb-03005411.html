If I may, I would like to address the dissention in the ranks regarding the value of one's natal astrological chart.

As far as prediction is concerned, an astrological chart may provide a glimpse, but certainly not the whole picture. One's chart shows the pure energies and natural tendencies that are imparted to someone at birth, but a chart cannot rightly predict what the individual may choose to do with them.

I could give you the unlabelled astrological charts for my child, my cat, the United States, and my new business venture, and you wouldn't be able to tell which is which. So how could one discern the foreseeable future from that?

"Good" astrologers use a healthy dose of intuition when giving their readings, relying on both the spoken and unspoken information garnered from the client.

However, the most valuable application of one's astrological chart is for self-analysis and self-realization. The awareness of the energies available on any given day, coupled with knowledge of one's natural inclinations, can help avoid catastrophe or gain favor.

As well, natal astrology has a keen insight into early childhood experiences, particularly with one's parents. Upon delving into this a little deeper, one can grow to understand that all events occurring in his/her life were present for a higher purpose: to give clues to uncovering who s/he is, where s/he came from, and most importantly, where s/he may choose to go.
