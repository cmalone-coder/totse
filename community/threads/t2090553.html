To this day, Coca-Cola uses as an ingredient a non-narcotic coca leaf extract prepared at a Stepan Company plant in Maywood, New Jersey. (straight from wiki)

Yeah, if it's from Wikipedia it MUST be true.

That is actually not "straight from Wiki"; here is the original quote:
"To this day, Coca-Cola uses as an ingredient a coca leaf extract prepared by a Stepan Company plant in Maywood, New Jersey, using a process monitored by the Drug Enforcement Administration."

The sources (The New York Times and the Washington Times) seem to be pretty reliable.

But even the common man can import a kilo of leaves off the internet for I think usually around $50 - $80.

Actually, $50 will get you ~300 grams of coca leaf according to my Google results. If you buy it in tea bags it equates to 4g for $1; either way you will end up paying around $200 for a kilo of coca leaf. Fresh coca leaves contain anywhere between 0.1% and 0.9% cocaine (http://www.cocaine.org/cokleaf.html). With a liberal estimate of 0.9% cocaine content and 100% yield from a theoretically perfect extraction, that is 9 grams per kilo. Do the math - not a very big profit margin for such an effort. Realistically, yields would probably be much smaller (inefficient extraction and low coca leaf content comes to mind; Wikipedia cites a 0.2% figure but it's not cited), meaning a rapidly narrowing profit margin.

According to crack-cocaine.org, one kilo of cocaine was about 75% pure in 2000 and the statistics have remained fairly steady. Do the math real quick; even at $100/gram you are still only making about $200 net (assuming a halfway figure of .4-.5% cocaine content in the leaves), which is less than 50% profit. Note that I have discounted the weight of the HCl ion as its molar mass is 1/10th that of cocaine base.

Furthermore, cocaine production is not that simple. There is an intermediate form of cocaine between the leaf and the final product which is cocaine sulfate, called basa in the South American slums where it is usually found. Basically, you mix coca leaves with water and H2SO4, mash it up into a paste, and dry it.

From there, the basa is washed with kerosene, chilled, and evaporated. The resulting crystals are dissolved in methanol, evaporated, washed with sulfuric acid, and then undergo further purification procedures with oxidizing agents, benzene, and sodium carbonate. That is an AMAZINGLY simplified version of one of the more low-grade ways of producing cocaine hydrochloride illicitly. I suggest you read this (http://www.erowid.org/archive/rhodium/chemistry/cocaine.illicit.production.html) as it will clear up many of the misconceptions you seem to have about cocaine production.

By contrast, methamphetamine is produced by the reduction of pseudoephedrine or ephedrine (a reaction which is astoundingly less complicated than the cocaine production process) - both of which are available commercially for a very low price relative to coca leaves.

Unless you live in South America and are willing to undertake large-scale production (we are talking many, many kilos at once in order to be profitable), methamphetamine production will be many times more profitable to you.

And guess how Meth is made most commonly? Through reactions starting with Ephedrine, coming from the Ephedra plant which contains about 1  3 %
