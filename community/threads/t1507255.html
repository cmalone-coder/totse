Infidelity & The 3rd Parties' Moral Responsibility
By 3rd party I am referring to not the cheater or the person being cheated on, but the person who the cheater is cheating with.

I've been thinking about this, and I really think that the degree of responsibility this person should have really varies quite alot depending on the circumstances.

I mean, in some cases, they don't even know that the person with whom they are having a sexual or romantic relationship is already in another monogamous relationship.

It's always pissed me off when people (especially guys) will say "If I ever caught my girlfriend cheating I would kill the guy."



This has always baffled me so much. How can people be so retarded? It makes me hope that one day, they will unwittingly have sex with a girl who is in a relationship, and then their psycho boyfriend catches them and delivers the same punishment.

Anyway...

There is also the flipside, like when the other person is a long-time friend of the person being cheated on. In such a case, I would be MUCH more inclined to take action against the 3rd party.

In any case, the cheater is of equal responsibility. They are the one who is in the wrong no matter what. The other person, however, might be completely unaware of any wrong-doings.

So anyway, while on this topic, I have recently begun thinking also about the difference in cases when the cheater is seduced, and when the cheater is the one who is looking for some side action...

If some girl with a boyfriend pursues me, then why should I feel anywhere near as guilty as she should feel? Of course I would still be in the wrong to go after her, but I really think that it's in a whole other league than if I were to pursue said girl while she is with another person.

So, what are your thoughts on the 3rd parties' moral responsibility?

Do you agree with me? Disagree with me?
