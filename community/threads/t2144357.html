I burned down my Grandma's house [accidentally]
Well, for the past week I've been working at a A&W (fast food place) in a town 40 minutes from my house and occasionally at lunch I go to my Grandma's for a free lunch that is homemade/actually good and today I went there and knocked. There was no answer, not really unusual as my Grandma is nearly completely deaf. I went in, she wasn't there so I decided to just make myself an omelet instead of just walking all the way back and buying some shitty burger. I made it, ate it and headed back to work. About three hours later my Dad called me at work telling me that Grandma had burnt her house down but that she was ok. My Dad came to pick me up early from work and we went there just in time to hear my Grandma crying about all her lost stuff (loads of old china and valuables brought with her when she immigrated) and my mom telling her it was her own fault for being forgetful and leaving the stove on.

Should I confess?
