The refuelling ban and standardising cars - people say that this will make F1 more interesting because the races are about the drivers rather than the cars.

Well, I've been to see an A1GP race at Brands Hatch where all the cars are built by Lola, and no, they're just as boring. The race was still a fucking procession but I didn't get to see and hear all the (albeit marginally) different cars.

I can't see it being a good thing for the automotive industry in general. Formula One is supposed to be the absolute top end of motorsport. If automotive engineers can't push the envelope here, then they can't hope to develop their road cars. Things like seatbelts, rear-view mirrors, rollover bars, carbon brakes, traction control, practically all major mechanical developments in the motor car were all developed for racing cars. True, in fifty years we might all be driving ultra-clean hybrids that do 150 miles to the gallon and have heated massaging blowjob seats, but I'd hope that they perform better than today's cars too.
