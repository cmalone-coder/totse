Your Homemade Bong!
I would like to hear what everyones home made bong is like. [for those of us who have them]

For me, it is a 2 in 1 device. It can be a small pipe or a small bong. You only need 3 things to make it:

*1 TV cable end piece. [the metal piece]
*1 length of hose with an inner diameter just smaller then the TV cble end piece.
*1 half pint mason jar

All you do is get your TV cable end piece, [which can be a bitch to get off and get the wire out of but isnt too hard] and stick it into a length of hose that is about .25 inches shorter then the space between the jar bottom and the lid. Stick it in upto the turny part. Now, get another piece of hose about a foot long.

Now, take a nail and hammer and hammer two holes into your lid. Then using a star head screw driver jam it through both holes to make them bigger. You want one hole a bit smaller then the hose, and one hole just the same size. Take your foot long piece of hose and stick it into the holes that is smaller then the hose. To help with this try cutting the end of the hose on an angle. Once you get it through, make sure it only sticks in about a half inch, if that even.

Now, add some water to your jar. Fill it just a bit above half way. Screw on the lid and youre done. You now have a 2 in 1 piece.

To use it as a pipe, well, the pipe is already constructed. The hose is obviously the hose part [erf!] and the metal cable end is the bowl. To use it as a bong stick the 'pipe' into the hole thats the same size as the hose. Stick it all the way down. Near the bowl piece the hose widens to wrap around the bowl part, so just push it in a bit to create a seal.

Pack and light! This bong pipe combo OBVIOUSLY isnt desinged for hugely packed bowls. I use it mostly for roaches or to actually smoke from it Ill just take the wed I wanna smoke, and break it into a few small pieces.

How about your homemade ghetto ass bong? How'd you make it?

This wasn't so much of a bong as it was a water pipe. Even so, it was still way cool.

I started out with a 2L Erlenmeyer filter flask [the kind with a side arm on it for tubing].

The side arm had about 2ft of surgical tubing with a mouth piece taken from a tobacco pipe at the end.

I used a 2 hole stopper. In one hole ran the metal tubing that went into the water and had the bowl at the other end. In between the ends I had a resonating chamber.

The other hole was used as a carb.

But; I also go one of those pocket welders from Radio Smack. They use gas cartridges that are also the same size at whippets.

I ran a tube from the pocket welder to the carb hole.

Thus, while smoking, we could add a charge of NO2.**

That pipe was too cool: We called it 'the Gas Chamber'. We even made a commercial on video for it [as sold by Dr. Bong]. Ah, if only I had a way to share it here, I'm sure most would get a kick out of it].


**: In all fairness, I must confess: the idea for the NO2 came from a pipe that used to be sold through High Times - before the California anti-paraphernalia laws of the mid 70's - called 'the Buzz Bomb'.
