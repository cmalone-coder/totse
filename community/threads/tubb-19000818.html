M-80's the real thing
I've seen many crappy home made fire works on various sites and most of them start with "how to make M-80's". It's obvious however that they don't really know what an M-80 is.

The M-80 was developed for the military, not as weapon, but as a training and disinformation device. It's purpose was to simulate gun fire. Military specs determined the dimension and that the fuse be placed on a side, rather than an end.

They never contained dynamite, nor can they be compared to it. M-80's use the same thing about every other firecracker does to make it go boom, flash powder. They just hold much more of it, 3g worth as compared to less than one gram to be legal, and not considered a "destructive device" by the ATF.

M-80's were banned from commercial sales to individuals due to there extreme danger. Flash powder is not the stablest of things, the more there is confined in one tube, the more of a chance there is for it to go off. They also do possess the power to remove small appendages and large chunks of skin. With this info along with the knowledge that our young'ns were playing with these, Uncle Sam decided we should no longer have them.

The M-80's you see in stores are not the real thing, nor are M-90's nor M-1000's any more powerful. I personally know people that have had this misconception. It's a good marketing strategy, but all legal fireworks have a limit to the amount of powder they can contain.

I hope this sets the record straight, I did read some of this a while back, but I forgot where. If I happen across it again, I'll post it. This article also went on to show that even cops don't know what the hell they are talking about in this area. Supposedly they held a person thinking he had illegal fireworks or something for having firecrackers stamped as M-100's. Being a higher number than, 80, they thought what he had was more powerful than a real M-80. M-80's of course being illegal, so then were this guys M-100's.
