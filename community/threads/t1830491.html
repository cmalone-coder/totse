Re: I just successfully detonated an explosive by remote controll.
If you're looking for the best possible signal quality and the least inteferance, you would want to go with a PCM setup. Only problem is that the reciever/transmitter combo (collectively, "Radio", in R/C terms) would be prohibitively expensive.

If you were using an R/C device for ignition, and you were not in a situation where the equipment needed to be disposable, you could likely build a miniature blast-resistant box, containing the radio equipment, batteries, etc.

If you're on AM, which most toy R/C cars run on, then the 75mHz band would probably be the best to use. It offers a wider range of available frequencies than the 27mHz band, as well as slightly clearer signal transmission as well as slightly less inteferance (compared to the 27mHz band).
