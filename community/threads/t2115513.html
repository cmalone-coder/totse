Your body learns through experience. If you get high, and talk a lot, then your body will associate being high with talking. If you get high, raid the kitchen, and fall asleep, then that's what will become normal everytime you smoke. The more you do these things, the more it becomes muscle memory. You could even try smoking placebo weed and the same thing theoretically will happen.

If you smoke weed almost every waking hour of each day, then your body will get confused when it's not high. It adapts. So yes, you can learn much better while high if that's what you always do.

At least, that's my pseudo-science understanding of how we adapt to psychological changes.
