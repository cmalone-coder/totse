2c-B + Ketamine?
I heard this is an amazing combo. More or less "the two are made for each other" sort of combo. The synergy is supposedly amazing, and the 2C-B actually stimulates memory so you remember much more of your ketamine experience. As far as "things to combine with ketamine" goes, I'm lead to believe by at least one person that 2C-B is perhaps the best. Though I can't speak from experience, I can definitely see the slight mood-lift/empathic elements adding to the more emotionally cold and removed/logical nature of ketamine. I'm sure there's more to it than that, though... as the cold/logical nature of ketamine doesn't necessarily bother me.

But yeah, I'm excited to try this combo, but I haven't read that much about it. Both 2C-B and Ketamine are very high up on my list of favorite chemicals I've experimented with. For starters (I'm planning a series of experiments, if I enjoy it,) I'm thinking of just doing the 2C-B, and after the peak I'll keta-bump myself to near-hole doses, get a feel for that, and then decide what I want to do next.

Has anyone had experience with this combo? Is it awesome or over-hyped? Any pointers or experience offered would be nice. I've experimented three times with LSD + Ketamine, but that's all I've mixed K with. I imagine happier phenethylamines would be a bit different from LSD. Though if I can handle LSD + K I imagine I'd probably be okay, since I've always found 2C-B to be milder than LSD. And the K will be making me sufficiently docile, as K always does. With k I really like going all out and sort of losing myself for a while, so in any case I expect it to be pretty intense.

Re: 2c-B + Ketamine?
I'm a raver, and despite the "stupid drugged up kid" image, I have done just enough to know what I'm talking about and observed and researched a lot.

From my understanding, 2cb is a research chemical, created simply as an experiment. I've done it in combination with ecstasy and it had a mild hallucinogenic effect, making trails of light (I never get those when I'm on E, even in much larger doses). This is a widely accepted effect. It is also fairly dangerous in combination with many other drugs, and a bit of a downer. It's volatile in the sense that it makes most people nauseous and some people vomit, though not copiously. Most people experience short term memory loss, as is common with most drugs

Ketamine is a disassociative animal tranquilizer, and in higher doses has hallucinogenic properties. It is, of course, a downer. Most people experience more than mild confusion simply by taking a bump or a small line

You CAN do them in combination but in my opinion it would make you forget more, not less. It would make you confused and sluggish. You would act like you had taken a hit of weak acid and gotten drunk, possibly. I wouldn't suggest it.
