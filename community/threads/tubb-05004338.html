Take the 2L bottle and slice the whole bottom out and get a bucket of fairly hot water... Put the 2L bottle in the bucket, leaving only the mouth sticking out... fill your soda-cap bowl and screw it onto the bottle. Now hold a lighter over the bud and slowly lift the bottle up so that the bottom of it is only barely in the water... the vaccuum created by the receeding water should suck the whole thing full of smoke.
