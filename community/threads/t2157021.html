We went back to her house and I met her friends, and we talked for a little bit, we kissed goodbye and I went home feeling great. At this point she had given me all the signals she was totally interested.

Basically, I'm wondering WTF was she thinking? She's an awesome person but I just can't understand what's going through her mind. I'm not desperate about her but I'd at least like to be friends.
