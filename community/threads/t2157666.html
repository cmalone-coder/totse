No, I'm not talking about weed. I would gladly hook my friends up with dank tree all day, every day. I'm talking about the other good stuff.

Ecstasy, Cocaine, oxycontin, etc. What do you do when you sell to friends? I'm talking about friends, not drug buddies. And what I mean by, "what do you do" is once they start continually buying more and start to get addicted do you say something? Do you refuse to sell to them? Do you say nothing and make mad profit?

Am I an asshole?
