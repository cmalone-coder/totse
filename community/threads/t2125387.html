I was under the impression that UV lights didn't do shit when growing anything really. I believe the UV percentage present in sunlight is used by the plant to tell it which way is up, when to start flowering, when to begin producing THC etc, but you need straight up visible light for photosynthesis and therefore growth to take place.

I believe that although LED lighting is very power efficient, it is not only expensive, but not actually that effective at producing enough lumens to grow pot. Most people stick with HPS, High Pressure Sodium lamps to produce large quantities of light without getting things too hot. Enough halogen or filament bulbs would probably burn your plants, especially the seed leaves.

If I were you, obviously I'm not condoning growing an illegal drug, but I would reccomend growing outdoors if at all possible - although its harder to control, your plants will grow bigger due to the increased area over which they can grow, you can't be busted unless you are caught there multiple times in a row, even then you can claim you merely found them, and its much much cheaper than buying huge quantities of lights, extractor fans, etc...
