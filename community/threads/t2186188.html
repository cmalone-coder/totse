I've only used melatonin, but I think it's more of a sleep regulator. When exposed to sunlight, our bodies produce melatonin, but at night when there is no more sunlight our bodies start to release the melatonin which makes us drowsy. You can buy 5mg pills at CVS, like $6 for 60.

If you want something that will straight up knock you out instead, I'd imagine any OTC sleeping pill would work pretty well. Just take the recommended dosage.

There are also prescription sleeping pills that claim to be non-habit forming, but that's a lot of trouble to go through and a good deal more expensive as well.
