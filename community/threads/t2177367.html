Fractal vision
I was recently experimenting with e, and I had heard that it can give you closed eye visuals. My mind was absolutely blown as when I closed my eyes I saw beautiful vivid and rapidly changing fractals. Naturally in my state it inspired a revelation that we exist in a fractal universe.. but that's beside the point. I discussed this with some friends and they too said they had seen fractals. This piqued my curiosity as to whether most people see fractals normally, and if so, HOW AMAZING IS THAT? How could I have not heard of this before if such is the case?

Also, opening my eyes after having them closed for an extended period of time made the fractals extend into my regular vision and it made me see objects morph into fractals... again, pretty intense.

Any similar experiences with your own fractalized vision?
