driving people away
there is a problem that i experience in my romantic relationships. it has happened once before, with someone who i cared about deeply. it is not because i didn't want to be with her. in fact, when it was broken off i was pretty devastated. now, i am in a relationship of the same sort. the girl i am with is great for me. we understand each other and have had similar life experiences.
i have, since entering adolescence (now 21) experienced some kind of depression. there are a lot of things that feed into this depression. my family went through some drastic changes over the past ten years, and being in the midst of these changes has left me with some pessimistic ideas regarding the possibility of enduring love and true fidelity.

i also have a low opinion of myself. i can only guess at the different things that have created this perspective on myself. for the past 7 years, i have abused drugs and alcohol. mostly, i have used this stuff to forget and to shut off the part of my mind that constantly envisions the worst possible scenarios, ones in which the most negative aspects of life are manifest.

this outlook on life helped to contribute to the breakup with the first girl. i was a pretty good boyfriend, i think. i never cheated, i was sensitive to my gf's feelings and tried to support her in what she did. but the lack of dynamism and charisma in my personality eventually drove her away. she became tired of my bleak outlook on the world.

i dont feel like its right to sacrifice my outlook for another, but i want to. i'm sick of worrying about when the (seemingly) inevitable driving away of my current girlfriend will take place. im also not happy with my outlook. i want to change it for myself, but i dont know how. i can change my perspective for a little while, but i always fall back into the hole.
this probably belongs in reinvent yourself, but it involves a significant other.
