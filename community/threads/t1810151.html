I'm sick of people telling me to respect their beliefs, or to respect the beliefs of others.

Now, I am not prejudicial against your beliefs or those of others. Quite the opposite; I am fully open-minded, as I am of all theories.

However, I do not give my respect blindly, to beliefs or anything else. In my mind, respect must be earned or it is not truly respect.

The beliefs that I am told to respect have no merit. While I give them equal chance, they do not earn my respect. I will treat them as they merit: as silly stories with far too great an impact on society.

This is not an act of disrespect; it is merely a legitemate lack of respect. After all, you don't usually have a lot of respect for the crackhead spouting gibberish in the back-alley...
