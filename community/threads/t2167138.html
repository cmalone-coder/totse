You could always remove the cat and try to break up the catalyst inside. then you have a hollow, free flowing can and can still use the stock O2 sensors. if the sensors are in the pipes on either side and not the cat itself then you could bolt/weld in a straight piece of pipe and still have the cat to swap back in if you ever need to pass an emissions test.
