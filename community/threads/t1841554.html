Here is how the categories are being defined:

Classic

Anime that have stood the test of time in terms of popularity, merit, and impact on the medium as a whole.

Action

Focus is on combat, gunfights, or fisticuffs in general. If high technology is involved, it goes in Sci-Fi/Giant Robots.

Comedy

Main focus on amusement, jokes, or parodies.

Drama

Main focus is on the emotions and/or interactions of specific characters with one another, not necessarily in solely romantic situations. Frequently set in war-torn or otherwise hostile environments, but action is not sole focus of the show.

Family

Main focus is on the simpler things of life: exploration, imagination, and so on. This does not mean kiddie shows, but rather shows that could be seen and enjoyed by almost everyone in the family.

Hentai

Main focus is on sexual, perverted, or otherwise erotic subject matter. This category encompasses all Hentai, from soft/hardcore porn to tentacle-rape to furries and beyond.

Occult/Horror

Main focus is on something strange, scary and/or mysterious, usually with ghosts, spirits, or demons. Also includes shows that are just plain weird.

Romance

Main focus is on the romantic relationship of one or more pair of characters. Typically, the theme boils down to "love conquers all," but not always.

Sci-Fi/Giant Robots

Main focus has a lot to do with the high technology present in the show. Spaceships, star colonies, and mecha combat define this category.

Unclassified

A temporary category for anime that have yet to be classified into the others.



Putting anime into strict categories is really kind of difficult, so try and choose the predominate aspect of the show and go with that.
