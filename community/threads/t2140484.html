Re: Epidemic among psychiatrists?
Its a different situation in England. If you go to your GP, for instance, and tell them about your depressive feelings, they'll prescribe you prozac. If its out of their sphere of influence, they'll refer you to psych nurses, psychiatrists and the like, but this can take a very long time.

The average psych appointment waiting list was eight months here in the UK in 2007. Doctors have so many patients to see in their day that it has become the normal route to try meds first. Usually when one strikes up a rapport with their psychiatrist, the more time they have to listen. Some people see the same doc for years and years. Meds first > Talk therapy second.

(It's about trying to reduce the immediate problems before you're in a space to talk or explain yourself clearly)
