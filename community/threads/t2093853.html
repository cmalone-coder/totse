Re: Stories you remember from trippin balls?
The first time I did LSD, it was me, the drummer in my band, and the guitarist. The guitarist was driving us around while we enjoyed the effects of coming up and such (we listened to Radiohead's "In Rainbows"...Soooo good). When we started to peak, we returned back to the guitarist's house to play some music.

We started off with some Villa Nova Junction (Hendrix), and it just felt soooo good. Faces in the walls, floors, as well as constantly changing patterns of all sorts superimposed across my entire vision, and constant melting/molding distortions (you know the ones).

This is where I really started to trip balls. I looked at my bass, and started reassessing everything. "Why do we know how to play all this music? What IS music? Why does it matter? How do I know all of these key signatures?" turned into "What am I even playing? A bass guitar? What is that?"

I concluded that a bass was made up of wood, large metal parts, and small metal parts. Then I asked myself "Where does metal come from? Rocks? Where do rocks come from? Nothing...Wait...WHAT THE FUCK? I'M NOT PLAYING ANYTHING? I'm holding this material object that is making sound, but is essentially nothing?"

After coming to the conclusion that I wasn't actually playing anything, I struck an E note, and started laughing (I must've resembled that of a crazy person). At this point, the drummer must have been thinking the same thing, because he stopped and went "What the fuck? I'm not hitting anything!"

At that point, we decided it was time to stop playing psychedelic blues music, and time to watch a movie. We put on Citizen Kane (which was impossible to understand...even though I'm sure it's easy to understand when you AREN'T on LSD) and the whole black and white movie seemed to be in full colour.

Tripping balls? I think that counts.

After an hour or two of alternately packing bowls and lying on my back, getting constantly blasted into different dimensions by the music I managed to figure out who I was, roughly. I stood up and decided to go out the back door for a cigarette with another tripping friend(not the one who was claiming it as bunk). The sky was a shade of blue that doesn't actually exist (or possibly just one that I can't recreate with any imaging software I've found, blue doesn't get that bright, it turns white) and there was a nice warm breeze winding through town.

I think that was the tail end of the peak, but nothing makes any sense temporally of the trip in my memory. I think I peaked listening to the Hawkwind album which started throwing me into crazy synesthesia and into odd places in my head.

To this day my other friend swears he felt absolutely nothing, and I just shrug and laugh when he asks me if there's really mescaline in the cactus.
