Re: Can't eat because of boyfriend
Break it down simply.

You're not eating because you're stressed. Therefore, you need to de-stress. Easy enough concept, right?

You're stressed due to relationship problems of a general nature, ie. it doesn't sound as if there's one sticking point between the two of you that can be resolved to make things better. Obviously, that's a complication, but the sky isn't falling.

Yet.

Your physical health and your emotions are intertwined. As one improves or declines, often the other does as well. In this case, emotional stress is affecting your physical health, and you need a solution before you waste away. Could it not be as simple as talking to him?

He's part of this. He may not understand exactly why you're not eating- given the ludicrous percentage of respondents to this thread who are in the same boat, I suppose it shouldn't be a huge surprise- but you have to make him understand, lest he continually tell you that you look fine, you don't need to lose weight, etc. If it doesn't sink in no matter what you try, it could be that dumping him is the only answer- I'm not sure someone that brain-dead would make you happy over the long haul anyway.

Of course, hopefully he does catch on, in which case it will be easier to talk things over and figure out what the hell is going on, and what the hell is going wrong. I've noticed that one's state of mind can improve dramatically, even without an answer, if you just find a starting point to work from.

There aren't a whole lot of potential outcomes, if that makes it any easier. You can dump him and get it together again, perhaps slowly, perhaps not; you can stay with him and continue to suffer; or you can figure it out and go back to being happy with him. For all the myriad possibilities, everything points towards one of those broad paths. I'm sure number two isn't a nice option to consider; you have to figure out whether the first or the last is the right way to go.
