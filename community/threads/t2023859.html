Re: Transformers *no spoilers*
My complaint with the movie is NOT the bad acting, NOT the comedy (its Michael Bay what do you EXPECT??), none of that stuff.

I'm actually complaining about what was the coolest part of the movie:

THE GIANT ROBOTS FIGHTING EACH OTHER

Was it cool? Yeah... but it could have been WAY better.

First off, the sound design was terrible. When giant robots are punching each other and crashing into each other, shouldnt there be loud bangs and crashes? The Transformers were all so damn quiet! I understand as part of the plot they were made to WALK quietly, thats cool. But when Megatron first laid a little smack down didnt you expect the subwoofer to make you shit yourself? Well I sure did, but my shorts are PRETTY DAMN CLEAN. What gives?

Also a lot of the Giant Robot Battle Action (GRBA) just wasnt that good I thought. Michael Bay wanted it to be understated, I understand. He wanted the focus of the story during the fighting to be the human elements of the story. But come on. Optimus Prime and Megatron get to FIGHT, at the end of a SUMMER BLOCKBUSTER. And we barely see any of it!

WHAT THE FUCK?

I want more GRBA!

I want louder GRBA!

You have to choose when making a movie like this. You either make it a human drama, or a GRBA drama. You cant have both (I'm biting my lip not to go in on the comedy, but whatever...). Not at the same time.

Am I the only one that during the first GRBA sequence (with Bumblebee and uh, Barricade I think), felt like standing up on my tip toes so I could look over the boring humans shoulders and watch the GRBA?!
