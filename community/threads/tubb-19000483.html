Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Re: Co2 BOMBS.
My experience of CO2 bombs is that they lob a single bit of shrapnel very rapidly directly away from the sealed end of the cylender. Im not saying thats always what will happen but 4/4 of the ones Ive set off did. The risk of this bit hitting you is obviously minimal, but its not inconceivable. The chance diminishes in proportion to the square of the range. Explosion you'll see will be a white blast of smoke maybe with an orange flame in the centre.
Has anyone had any luck with sugar/ sodium chlorate cylender bombs cos in my experience the mixture doesnt burn fast enough to cause an explosion and you get a worryingly unstable rocket.
Anyone considered the lazy mans CO2 bomb? Just put an unpunctured CO2 cylender on a few firelighter blocks and allow the pressure to blow it open. You got about 5 minutes getaway. Trouble is about 20% of the time the thin puncturable metal film blows open. Even then you get a very fast projectile. One of mine that did that made 2 perfectly circular holes in 2 thick wooden fences. As the shrapnel/cylender generally flys away from the sealed end, you could direct it at a target.
