Snakes!
I don't really remember the first time I saw a snake. But I've been long respectfully fascinated with them. In my opinion they are the most evolved, efficient land predators that exist today. I however haven't always been so kind to them, but I always did respect them for what they are. Having been bitten several times by both poisonous and nonpoisonous species, I think most people would just leave them alone.

Well I did something that may have been so smart, but I am happy. Most snakes I have found in the wild I have killed and yes stretched their skins. Most of them have been Southern Copperheads and a few timber rattlers. Why I sought them out was for the fascination of just seeing them, the people I was with made some money off them, and I really didn't see anything wrong with it, so of course I helped.

I saw a show on tv not to long ago about a rattle snake round up. During this event thousands upon thousands of snakes were captured and killed. I was saddened almost why people would kill so many and some died in not so humane ways. But it brought my fascination with the creature back, like it was in the days of my childhood.

So off to pet stores I went today. Many of them had big advertisements for reptiles, yet their displays only consisted of a few sickly gecko's. No one was really helpful and I was rude with a rep at about the 5th store I went to. On the way back, disgruntled, I saw a pet store I that wasn't even in the phone book. I stopped and met a very helpful sales person, and they had snakes! So he set me up with a starter kit, a few books (free of charge), 2 mice again free, some scenery at half price, and the coolest ball python they had.

I got home with my prized pet, a snake that I could handle, one I could see behave (though not natural), one that would not bite me, and one that I would never kill for any reason at all.

I handled him for a bout 20min then placed him the cage with an awaiting meal. I knew it wouldn't eat right away, but I wanted to be sure that it had something. It's been 2hrs now, and it's just laying there, like a snake does, and I'm happy to watch it.

I know this hasn't gotten anywhere and there really is not major point. But it's been a neat day. I have always been fascinated with them and now there is one living with me, so I'm pretty excited about it. It also cost me damn near $200 so it better freakin kill something ;^)
