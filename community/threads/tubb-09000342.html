reincarnation
Here's the theory I know of reincarnation. I believe it's the Pagan theory.

Every person is a soul & a body, connected. The soul exists on the higher vibrational levels of reality. As a fetus is being constructed in the womb, a soul takes notice of the mother and starts to stick around. The soul gradually filters itself down until it exists at a low enough vibration to connect with the physical body. This is a traumatic experience for a soul, and is why infants come out crying. The spirit body is, at it's lowest level, a full outline of the physical body. When a limb is cut off, the spirit body holds true to it's form: thus, a phantom limb is experienced. When a person dies, the soul leaves the body and returns to it's own, comfortable realm of existence. The soul wanders the (astral?) plane for some time. Before the next incarnation, the soul makes decisions about all the major experiences it will encounter in it's life. It makes these decisions based on what will be best for it's own advancement. The purpose of the life cycle is spiritual advancement. The Nirvana that Buddha achieved, in example, is the culmination of spiritual mastery.

Does anyone have any thoughts? Agree? Disagree? Can you expand on something? What is the Christian view on reincarnation?
