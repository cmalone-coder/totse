A porche suddenly braked with squeaking tires. The man inside looked in his mirror. "There!" He put the car in his reverse and turns the wheel. A police blockade prevented him from going any further. He turned the car around and wanted to race off in the opposite direction but just as he put the gear into drive again more police cars came from that direction too. Soon he realized: "It’s over!"
Police officers stormed the car. All of them wore balaclavas and helmets. The man, desperate about his future saw no other way out. He pulled a revolver out of his pocket and put it to his head. Just as he wanted to pull the trigger something tore it from his hand. It was followed by a loud bang coming from above. A single sniper's bullet ended it all and the man was apprehended.
When fully restrained a policeman asked him: "How did you do it? Kill without trace?" The man sighed: "Very well then! It’s a note! If I write down a name in it, this person dies from heart attack" The officers look at each other for a moment. “You’re not serious?” Gambler_Justice replied. The man shook his head and said: “I’m dead serious. Why would I lie, I’m done for anyway!” 16bit searched his pockets and soon recovered a black note. He looked up and his face turned white. He fell back and screamed in terror. “What is it!?” “What’s happening!?” the others yelled. “Its..its a monster!” 16bit stuttered. Muffin King stepped up and picked up the black note. “Don’t be redicu…” before he could finish his sentence he too was struck with the image of the monster. He dropped the note and ran away and hid behind the car. “What is that!?” He yelled. The other investigators were curious about what their colleagues saw. DarkSkye then came with the solution. “Both touched that note and then panicked! Our man already said that the note can kill people! I think we need to touch it to see it!” The rest agreed and everyone touched it. Nightmare Syndrome gazed up and stared for a long while. Rabbhimself saw the hideous monster and took a step back but said nothing. Supperfreek saw the monster and then looked away. “Its hideous, how can it be alive?” Insanecrazy joined him and looked at the monster for a while. “I think it can help us” he replied finally. Muffin King regained his composure. “How do you think that?” Rabbhimself joined in the conversation: “If this monster comes with this note, he must know more about it!” 16bit added to that: “Then we should take the note, read it and store it somewhere where no one can reach it!” Everyone agreed upon this.
Just as they wanted to clear the area the apprehended man fell to his knees. Water came from his mouth and he moaned of pain. His head fell back and he was dead. “HEART ATTACK!” Darkskye yelled! “Kira is still active!” Everyone trembled in fear as they might be next. Supperfreek calmed them down. “If Kira is still active then he can kill us at any moment now! We should act like professionals!” DarkSkye looked at him for a moment and then asked: “How so?” Supperfreek continued: “Only this special team knew of this man being Kira. That means…” Rabbhimself interrupted him: “That the real Kira is among this same outfit!” “Exactly!” Supperfreek added.
