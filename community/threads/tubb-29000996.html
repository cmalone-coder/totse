First Firearm
As of today, I will be eighteen in one month and twenty-one days. This momentous passing will (finally) allow me to legally possess firearms. I've got the cash - so what do I get?

My first impulse was to buy a handgun of some sort. They're superior for personal protection purposes, and more importantly, they're easily concealable. This is important, because I live with liberals. Even after I move out, I'll be going to a school (probably the University of Iowa) that frowns on weapons in the dorms.

It was at this point that I became familiar with federal laws concerning handguns. Apparently, I'm not allowed to effectively defend myself until the age of 21. Well, fuck you, too, Uncle Sam. My problem was simple - how could I procure a firearm that was both concealable and legal?

I've found an apparent compromise - the Henry Repeating Arms AR-7. It's technically a rifle, but it collapses into several components and folds up into a neat little package. Even better,it's semiautomatic, a definite bonus in my opinion. And it fires cheap and simple .22LR's. AND it's dirt cheap. So I might just go with that.

Thoughts? Comments? I'm new at this, so please feel free to critique my logic here. Just keep it constructive.

I once considered the AR-7 as my first gun. Then, I asked and read around. I changed my mind. The AR-7 has a poor reputation of reliability. The sears tend to wear, making them go full auto when you least expect it. The thin barrel also affects accuracy.

The Ruger 10/22 is one of the cheapest and most popular .22 LR rifles on the market. There are accessories galore.

Later on, get a Ruger MKII pistol, or similar, like a Ruger .22 revolver (what can I say, Ruger makes good guns). Learning to Use a pistol well takes practice, and with a .22 you can do lots of that.

After that, the sky's the limit.
