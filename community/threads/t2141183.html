Power Trio
As many of you know I'm a psychedelic researcher, enthusiast, and taste tester :)

There are a lot of combinations I've indulged in that have intrigued me to the levels at which one can bend and twist reality, or just destroy the concept altogether.

I have smoked n,n-dmt during an acid peak, and conversely, I have also k-holed during an acid peak. Both experiences are very difficult to describe but have brought me to unprecedented extradimensional levels.

Now, another combination that has had me curious for quite a time now has been combining n,n-dmt and ketamine. The only proper methods of doing this that I can think of would be to either shoot both at once in the same rig, or IV the ketamine and right before the moment of falling into the k-hole smoke some demetri.

I'm leaning more towards the synced intravenous injection.

So, I ask, have any of you done such a combo? What was it like? If you can, are you able to put it into words? Recommend or not?

That's the first part of this thread. Moving on.

I've explained my interest in the previous combination, but after some thinking (about the two mixtures done during an acid peak) on the subject, I've been wanting to throw a dose of liquid lsd into the rig too.

All at once, in a matter of seconds, the wonderful threesome will work their magic on my mind. I will be shot into the cosmos by the dmt, I will fall into a spiraling hole from the ketamine, and the acid will be great stimulation to my mind, soul, and spiritual entity. I cannot even fathom the mental boundaries the combination will allow me to transcend. I suppose this is part of the reason I'm so curious. I've always been psychonautical in the sense of exploring as much of my mind and soul as I can.

And, so, does anyone have any input on the subject? Hopefully I can find at least one of you who have k-holed during a demetrigasm.

I've k-holed before (snorted K alone), and it was perhaps the most intense experience of my life (although smoking salvia on mushrooms and nitrous was insanely strong for a short period). There is no way to adequately explain being "consciously" totally removed from your body and everyday senses, when it makes no difference to your eyes being open or not barely (well, not in the dark) and you are zooming around "exploring" this new... space.

I found ketamine so weird; this removal from everything in my everyday life, and yet also these confirmations and questions of everything i've ever experiences and have felt being just.... thrown at me. Or rather, me being shot into them on a cosmic rocket, through this world of colour, and dimension, and beyond dimension, and everything, and nothing. Seriously fucking nuts. I actually got the same feelings as being on a roller-coaster half the time, there was this g-force being exerted upon me by every movement, as I explored these "caverns". I can't really remember so much of the trip to be honest, but my imagination has never been visual in the way you get in a k-hole.

I don't know how it's really affected my views on dimension, and the "normal" world though to be honest. Perhaps not a huge amount.... but I find some things intensely interesting, like how someone out of body due to ketamine can explore and witness things they are not in physical proximity to. Either way I think it shook my mind up a fair bit, and not just in good ways, but I have no regrets. K-holing on a regular basis though, seems to me quite strongly escapist, and it's just so overwhelming and literally in "your face" (but beyond that, in your very senses...) that I just can't see the appeal of doing so regularly.

It's worth exploring I guess, but you have to be careful, because such weirdness is not just necessarily a play world to get lost in, I think it can strongly affect how you view yourself and interact normally. IVd psychedelics like ketamine and DMT must be insanely mind blowing, especially mixed with acid and the like... I guess I don't feel i'm at the right stage in my life to truly push those boundaries toooo much, though.
