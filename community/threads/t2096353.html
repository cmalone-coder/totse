How many dealers at your school?
Of course, don't be retarded and tell us the school or dealer's name or anything stupid like that.

Mine is a pretty wealthy, private, k-12 new-age learning system-based school with a student population of around 1500.

For a bunch of rich kids, stuff comes moderatly cheap. Generally, $15 AUD a gram for bud, $20 for a bikkie.

The dealers are mainly in the senior years, but there's probably at least one in the junior-high agegroup - there was when I was there.

We have 3 or 4 weed dealers throughout the highschool (years 10-12), excluding personal friendships with supply. If you included them, the list would be too huge to count.

We only have one guy who's selling harder shit, eg. shrooms and X, that I know of - but there's probably a few others; those sorta people don't tend to advertise as much.

There's only ever been one bust, in 2005. And that was from an idiot dealer not keeping his mouth shut.
