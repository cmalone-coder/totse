There is no magic configuration for engine balance the best way to do it is experiment and find out what works for you. This isn't supposed to be a lesson on how to make a race car it is supposed to be a guide for you that gets you started on tuning an engine.

The biggest mistake that I commonly see (yes it may be biased but most often in "ricers" or import wannabe racers) is that they focus more on "bolt on" over the counter modifications than they do on actual tuning. It seems as though people will buy every external accessory that they sell at the local auto parts store from mufflers, to intakes, and think that there car is a race car. Yes these things do help but just by adding some bolt on accessories and stickers to your car does not make it a race car

It is all about internal engine work, gear ratios, cam timing, shift points, bore, stroke, compression, etc, etc. That is where the term "sleeper" comes from. I have seen many of cars that when you pop the hood they look like stock engines blow away those bolt on sticker infested pep boy’s cars. Yes you do need some bolt ones like high flow intakes and less restrictive exhaust. But if your internal engine is not set up to make the most from these enhancements it will only get you so far.

Example of internal engine work. Take an older small block Chevy 350 drop a 400 small block crank shaft in there and bore it .30 over and you have your self a nice little 383 stroker engine. No one will know the difference.

Ignition: I will swear and live by MSD, accell makes a very competitive part but from trial and error I will stick with them. Take an ignition box such as the 6AL or for you ricers they make an import box. And get a MSD blaster coil and the internal components for you distributor and you will have enough spark to burn any thing you can toss into that cylinder.

Fuel Injection. On most imports and a lot of domestic engines the fuel injection works off of "ports" or "runners" of some sort to guide the air to the cylinder, and most of the time they will have some sort of "plenum" that the air amasses is after it leaves your throttle body. Well Port those out. Or polish the inside of them to allow smoother airflow. Also the throttle body. A real easy mod is to replace the throttle body with a slightly larger one. No one will tell the difference by looking at it.

Gear ratios. This is more for the rear wheel drive vehicles but I cannot stress enough how important the proper gear ration for you car is. This is often over looked but by a slight change you can change your car from a high low end torque filled drag race machine to a high top end max speed machine.

Auto Transmission gear ratios and Shift points. If your engine is producing more torque and more horsepower or has been modded to either have high revolutions or a more low rev torque chances are your stock shift points and gears in your tranny are not going to be adequate for your needs

Other little things I am not going to go into detail in are. Reduce the load on your engine, if you are looking for power than there is a lot of shit on cars that are luxuries that you do not need. Remember the basic things an engine needs to make power. Also don’t forget about good valve seals, bearings, tire size, there is tons more information just do NOT overlook the small things.

Once again this was not a “how to guide” it was just for showing how many things are often overlooked in the compression engine. All it takes is a little basic knowledge on how the engine operates, what it needs to make power, and what it does with the power it makes. How you build your engine is up to you.
