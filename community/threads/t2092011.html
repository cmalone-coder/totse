Re: Schedule II & IV drugs RX.
Keep them in a bottle with your name and prescribing info printed on them at all times. This will help prove the story that they are yours if you get caught.

Secondly, if they are on you and out of sight, a cop can not legally pat you down, feel that it's a bottle of pills and reveal it; it is simply illegal for a cop to reveal any item other than a weapon during a pat down... period.

I got caught with about 500 benzos (All schedule IV) and the court filed them as a class D misdemeanor (CII may be a little more strict and could go up to a class C felony or higher depending on what the charges are, aggravated trafficking, carrying, or dispensing). I also had a can of mase on me, so I was also charged with carrying a concealed weapon (On top of the "carrying a scheduled drug"). Both class D charges from what I can remember. though I had a great lawyer, and because of the illegal search after the pat down and them not finding any weapons, except for the mase, which I already handed to them, the both charges were dropped.

So just be careful, trust who you deal with and don't be stupid. And you will get caught eventually, everybody does, just a matter of time.
