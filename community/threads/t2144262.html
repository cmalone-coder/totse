The big difference between being an adult and being a child is that when your a child your parents are responsible for your welfare. When youre an adult (as your son practically is/almost is) you are singularly and wholly responsible for your life and your problems. No cop out. No blaming daddy, the girlfriend, the mom, the school, the this or that. You are what you make yourself and your life.

Realize that he has this responsibility and most importantly, give it to him. Let him make his own decisions and his own mistakes. Now you are not the decider you are the helper and the one who will help him up when he falls. You are his rock and stone. Its u to him. Warn him of all you can, be a parent, nag, but respect him.
