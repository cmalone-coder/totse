There is a difference between the caliber of a bullet and the caliber of a firearm...

For example the 40 S&W and the 10mm may share the same bullet diameter of .40. But the over all case length is different, Thus making it a different caliber. Or lets us the 45 cal bullet. Multiple weapons share the same bullet diameter. But they may be chambered in 45 Colt, 45 acp or even or even the 45-70 rifle. The cartridge length, width and shape all has a bearing on what the caliber of the firearm actually is...
