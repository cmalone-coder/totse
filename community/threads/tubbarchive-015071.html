The ebook thread :)
Now this technically comes under knowledge and should be posted in the knowledge thread, but seeing as i prefer ebooks myself i figured it would be good to have a seperate topic on ebooks.

These ebooks account for about a third of my collection, simply because they are the only ones under 750kb, freewebs' file size limit on a new account

If anyone can find me a permanent place to upload the rest of my ebooks, that would be great.

NOTE: if the links dont work, i'm in the middle of fixing them.

Anyway:

Sleep Science

Vivid Dreams + Chart - sharpen up your dream recall.

Night shift/Jetlag - How to get sleep with those problems.

Sleep Evaluation - You can kind of get a feel for what you have to do to fix your sleep from this. I have a 3.5mb ebook that goes into greater detail, as soon as i find somewhere to upload it.

Nutrition

Alternative Health - Pretty damn good, but a bit long and boring to read. Worth it, though.

Psionics (Psychic stuff, telepathy, psychokinesis etc)

The Psion Handbook - Introduces you to basic techniques on how to develop psychic abilities.

Psychokinesis - This concentrates on spoon bending

Astral Projections - you have to be extremely open minded not to laugh at the person who wrote this. Not for the faint hearted

Cold Reading

Wonder Readings

Red hot Cold Readings

The gentle art of Cold Reading

The art of Cold Reading by Nelson

Sequal to The art of Cold Reading by Nelson

Ebooks that dont come under any specfic category

Running a car on tap water - Supposedly teaches you to convert your car to run on tap water. If anyone good at electronics could look over the circuits to tell me if it should work, that would be pretty cool .

Truth - teaches you how to detect if someones lying. Very detailed and very accurate .

Voice health - not very usefull unless you sing or have an illness affecting your voice. I used to be into singing And looked up a few books on voice health which is the reason i have this.
