Quote:

Yes, unless told to do so Fedora does not automount filesystems from removable devices. I don't want shell users to have access to my USB stick or any other users of my systems if I just happen to have a file backup or two on a removable drive and plug it in, I don't want it globally readable without me telling the system to do so.

While Ubuntu may seem "better", the definition of better is debateable, in your case "better" is "less secure".

Anybody can mount a device and edit /etc/fstab to mount the device with options they want as far as permissions at any time.
