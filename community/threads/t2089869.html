Don't escalate your dosage so quickly. Try a few more times with the same amount. Sometimes, it just plain doesn't work. You have to hold it in as long as you can. A common mistake among people new to salvia is assuming that you need more because a certain amount didn't work the first time you tried it. Think about it: you're taking a drug that gives you the potential to truly believe you are in a different universe or dimension. It's probably wise to ease yourself into it. Rushing into it and doing an unwise amount is a recipe for a bad time. Just because you've tried mushrooms and weed before is not going to prepare you for the mind fuck that salvia brings.

You're experimenting with an unproven drug on your brain, a very carefully tuned and expensive piece of equipment. Take baby steps.
