Wicked,

I included my "tubing technique" in that post about 'coon huntin'...I slipped it in there...easy to miss.

I use clear (pvc) "rubber" tubing (about 1') that fits snugly "inside" (tubing-male, lamp-female) the lamp, and secure it with electrical tape. Its a lot more secure and practical this way.

However, this type of tubing is flexible...I'm thinking of something rigid, str8 up and down, for maximum reflux and condensation recirculation.
