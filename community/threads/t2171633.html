Re: carrying a relationship into college
It's working for me so far. I'd been dating my boyfriend for 2 and half years when he left to go to university. I see him every three or four weeks for a day or two. To be honest, it's difficult. It requires a lot of extra effort, communication and trust. However I find that we fight a lot less and in general appreciate each other more because our contact is so limited that we try to make the best of it when we can. It's stressful and challenging, but if you really care about someone it is entirely possible, and very worth it.

However if you're already doubting your future together, it might not be best to keep dragging it on, you know? Like, I don't really think about putting a time limit or any expectations on my relationship; it goes where it goes, but if you're already sure that your relationship is going to time out at some point, it might be best to break it off while it's easy to immerse yourselves in your new lives.
