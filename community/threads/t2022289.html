cut with speed?
I'll be the first to admit I'm new to anything harder than weed/salvia so bear with me.

On the weekend I was offered a pill I was told was E and decided to go for it. It was a grainy looking pill that was completely red, with no other markings. The individual who gave it to me mentioned it might be cut with speed.

Either way, I took it and waited for the effects that I had read to expect from E. About a half hour later from the pill I started to get ansy/energetic which was odd because I had been drinking and smoking all day and should have felt tired. I felt very active and surprisingly clear headed. One particular thing I noticed is that any weed I smoked after this point made me feel very good, almost more energetic (any of this may have been a placebo effect, I'm just writing everything that I thought I felt). I experienced next to none of the feelings that I was told to expect with E. All in all, I ended up staying up until 5 in the morning without feeling very tired, odd since I had been drinking and smoking since noon and I took the pill at 11pm.

Can anyone speculate as to what I actually took? I don't believe it was E, and if it was then it was dissapointing compared to what I expected.
Any ideas?
