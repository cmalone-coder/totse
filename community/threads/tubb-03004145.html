have you ever heard of the placebo effect?

in case you haven't:
when scientists are testing new medicines, they have test groups. all patients are treated for the same thing, but are medicated differently. one group is given a current market medicine, the next is given the medicine being tested, and the third is given a placebo.

the people who are taking the placebo think they are taking medicine, but really it's just a sugar pill or something. they use the data from the placebo group to compare and contrast to the group actually taking the medicine to judge it's effectiveness.

the funny thing is that some of the people in the placebo group recovered on their own, without taking any drugs. this is because they believed that they WERE taking drugs, so they believed they would get better. and they did.

the god stuff is the same damn thing....
