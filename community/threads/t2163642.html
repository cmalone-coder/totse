Atheist dating a christian
So I'm an atheist, and the girl I'm dating (currently three months) is christian. I should preface this by saying that she's not a fundamentalist, born-again type; she has no hang-ups about sex and is very open minded and intelligent. Her brand of religion is very moderate, Church of England and personal, but she does pray and go to church. We've talked about religion a bit, but I've tended to divert the conversation towards less controversial areas, often with some inane philosophical platitude. I also tend to downplay my atheism, emphasising the uncertainty of all belief and how I have more of a problem with religion as an institution than with personal faith.

I love her, we get on incredibly well and the sex is fantastic, but does anyone think this will present a major problem in the future? More generally, does anyone know about long-term relationships between atheists and religious people? How do they raise their children?

If you can both be truly accepting and respectful of each other's religious beliefs, it won't be a problem. Problems happen when people are too pigheaded to see the value of someone else's values; when an atheist thinks someone who follows a religion is stupid, when a Christian believes an atheist is doomed to hell.

My boyfriend is quite religious, and I'm quite atheistic. But we both realize that we are both people of faith; he has faith that there is a god, I have faith that there isn't. Neither of us can prove our beliefs; but everyone takes a leap of faith at some point, and we are all in the same boat really, trying to explain the things we can't understand. As long as you aren't going to force your opinions on her and she won't try to convert you, it really shouldn't be a problem.
