- Gnosticism is basically a form of Christian Manicheanism, for lack of a better term. It holds that the True God never created a physical universe, but instead simply presided over a spiritual cosmos. However, a being called the Demiurge (the Lesser Power) created a material universe to worship him. Salvation is achieved by transcending the physical universe and returning to God's perfect spiritual Creation. That's Gnosticism.

- There's a great body of evidence saying that Christianity began as a Jewish resistance movement, or the exact opposite of how you describe it. You make it sound like a bunch of guys just got together and made Christianity up to fool people. It was a bit more complicated than that, m'kay?

- To the gentleman who started this thread, yes, Christianity does seem a bit muddled on Good and Evil. Remember, it's largely a jumble of Judaism (where God kind of seemed to have wanted Adam and Eve to eat the apple, and Satan is just an angel who does his dirty work) and such faiths as Zoroastrianism and the Greek Mysteries (which embrace a more dualistic view of the universe). It doesn't all fit together perfectly all the time.
