The new trendy way to scare the shit out of yourself?
I saw on the news where a couple of guys, in England I believe, built themselves a catapult that can throw a person about a hundred feet and land them in a big net. They were saying it will be the new "bungee jump" for people who feel the urge to piss their pants.

I want one, and will have one, probably at the compound.

The trouble is they only showed the entire thing for a few seconds, mostly the camera focused on the guy getting thrown, numerous times.

What I need to know is: Is there anyone on totse who has the brain power to do the math that needs to be done to figure out how long the catapult arm needs to be and how much counterweight will be necessary to throw a 200 pound man 100 to 150 feet?

Maybe catapult is the wrong word, it was more like a French trebuchet, it consists of a long arm with an axle/fulcrum about a third of the length from the bottom. There is a bar/hook at the top end to drop the looped end of a rope over and the other end of the rope is a harness that the tossee wears. On the very bottom is the counterweight, then there are two more posts sunk into the ground and the axle/fulcrum rests on those. Easiest way to think of it is to hold a spoonful of something in one hand and hook your finger over the end and let fly.

So who can do the equation?

He just obviously doesn't get the physics of the thing. If you throw a 150 pound item from the trebuchet, barring a heavy wind (above 10 mph) it should land within a 3 foot circle just about every time. If your catch net is 30 feet wide by 50 feet long the chances of missing the net are astronomical. Only if you alter the equation will the impact area change. If you make the arm longer, add or subtract weight from the counterbalance, try to throw a heavier or lighter person, etc.

Naturally if there was a high wind you wouldn't throw. Same if it was raining, storming or there was a tornado coming, just common sense.

Now if your tossee is lighter or heavier the only thing that changes is throwing distance so you will need a longer net and it will have to begin closer to the trebuchet. So you go with a 100 foot long by 30 foot wide net. No matter what, you cannot argue with physics, they do not change.
