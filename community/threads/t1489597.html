Love and The Art of Relationships
It's been three years, yes, and here I am, still paralyzed with cynicism and - ultimately - fear. I've been convincing myself that's there's no point in relationships because they can only end one of two ways:

No. 1: You eventually break up and then die.

No. 2: You eventually die and break up.

I have a plane ticket to Florida to see a man I love: Darren, who saved my life, and who loves me (or at least thinks he does) and I can't even make myself happy about it because I'm also in love with someone else.

The worse part is the relationship I have with this other person is completely dysfunctional. He tells me he loves, then acts like an asshole, he wants a monogamous relationship, and then we're wrestling on the bathroom floor biting and bitch slapping each other... Together we burn each other with cigarettes and rant about our paranoid delusions.

I fear the dramatically doomed, codependent, drug and booze addled, relationship that is looming - inevitably - before me. And when we break up - and we will - it will be a horrible crash and burn that will be unbareably painfull and will leave me with even more emotional baggage than before. And worst of all, I know we wont be friends anymore. I know how he is with his exes.

Even worse I don't even think he knows how I really feel, though I've told him. But I think he's just pissed at me because I wont agree to stop seeing other people; and It's not that I want to see other people. It's just this thing with Darren: I don't know what more frightening, his inescapable love or the thought of losing it.

It's a very uncomfortable situation, you see. I love them both but, the thought of having another relationship is paralyzing to me. Or is this just cynicsm? Is this just irrational fear? Residual feelings of a previous unresolved and painfull break up.

Right now, I really don't know what to do and I want to figure it out before I go out to Florida (in less than two weeks). At least, after then, I'll know what I want when I come back, hopefully.

I can't believe I'm even actually posting this, but, I guess this goes to show how desperate I am for advice.

Or may be just some recognition.

Cheers,

Lost
