Re: I am gonna try LSD for the first time
don't mix alcohol or benzos unless things get to crazy for the setting... it brings it down...

stop trying to mix shit with it, at most smoke a bowl at the peak if its not too overwhelming yet... or smoke one after you take the hits to kill about some time during the come up...

im not even going to try to tell you what to expect, don't expect anything, no amount of preparation is going to prepare you for what you will think and feel... in the words of the girl who introduced me to acid, "just let the lovin take hold"... write on a sheet of paper "im on acid, things will be normal soon" in case you forget your on a drug altogether, when your feeling insane for no apparent cause and you see the cause written on a sheet of paper in your own handwriting its a bit better than having your friend try to convince you... if you start getting into a place you don't want to be in look at your thumb and index finger and try to conceptualize the distance between them, that can cause your train of thought to jump to something else, it occupies the thoughts and soon the bad parts are replaced with something else... i think this technique was created by shulgin if memory serves... the writing on a sheet of paper thing came from meta...

get an mp3 player, put on some music that you like, try to stay away from the darker stuff... Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band is one of my favorites, its hard not to be happy listening to that album in any state of mind... pink floyd provides a good mind fuck... to each there own i guess...

go outside, day or night... its the most beautiful thing you will have ever fucking seen... going for a walk on acid for me generally seems like walking into some beautiful painting but more alive than a painting...

movies and video games aren't that great on acid, ive attempted to play gears of war while tripping pretty hard, and i sucked and quickly lost interest in favor of music from my mp3 player.... movies are hard to understand unless you have watched them to death, but if you must fear and loathing is always interesting.... across the universe might be good if only just for the music. even if you don't understand what the fuck is going on with the plot the music is still there...

on my first trip i couldn't even remember what sanity was, i felt insane but it was a blissfully insanity where it felt good to laugh and be happy, so i laughed till it hurt and then i kept on laughing, to feel that happy you kind of have to go over the edge of sanity and reason, acid did that for me... but i was able to keep that thought of "whatever sanity is, it will be back by the time the sun comes up".

have a nice trip...
