Everyone is looking at the higher up lawyers but just take a step back and look at the whole picture.

Theres a ton of different types of lawyers out there (Assaults Lawyer, Bankruptcy Lawyer, Compensation Lawyer, Defense Lawyer, Divorce Lawyer, Fraud Lawyer, Insurance Lawyer, Medical Malpractice Lawyer just to name a few)

An average salary for a computer programer can range from 40k-120k

An average salary for a lawyer an range from a paralegal (42k) to a trial attorney (86k) and even upto 120k

You also have to look at the schooling aspect. How long, how much you would pay. And the hours. A lot of lawyers work tons of overtime just to try and win the case they are on.
