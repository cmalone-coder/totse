We're back!
As you probably saw earlier on the front page, the motherboard on totse.com's main server fried and died a few weeks ago. I pulled the old server apart, ordered the parts I needed, waited for the parts to arrive and waited, and waited... It turns out that finding a replacement dual-CPU motherboard with a slot for an Adaptec daughterboard that would fit in the chassis and work with the rest of the 5-year-old technology in the server was more difficult than I expected.

Anyhow, the parts came in and I fixed everything. I upgraded the OS at the same time and that broke a few things -- like e-mail -- so I'm still reconfiguring a few odds and ends to get everything back to where it was.

I'm turning off the new user registration until I get e-mail working again. (You can't get your new user password without e-mail, so there's no reason to allow new users to sign up just yet...)

We're back!
