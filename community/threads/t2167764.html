Re: Alcohol & Fruit
Does nobody else make PJ?

I always use everclear, but I'm sure any clear liquor would work.

Take a cooler. Fill it up 3/16 to a quarter of the way up with chopped fruit...Grapes, strawberries, watermelon, anything you like. Then, put a gallon of choice liquor into the fruit. Let this sit for an hour or more...i usually go about half a day. Then, pour four to five gallons of Hawaiian Punch into the cooler. Any juice/drink will work.

Its called Pj here in the southeast. I know in wisconsin its called "wop". I'm sure there are a million names for it. Its a cheap way to booze up a party, or stretch your shit out.
