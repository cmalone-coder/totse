Getting universal healthcare will be a difficult and long process in America. I support universal healthcare but too often I see people refer to how well it is working in Europe. The reason it works so well is that most of Europe was rebuilding after being completely devastated by WWII. However America hasn't had any major rebuilding since Reconstruction which will make our transition to a national healthcare service much more difficult because we're going to have to do it very slowly to allow people to become accustomed to it.
