Never thought about it before one of my mates just asked me about my first relationship last night. It's not something I talk about much just because it's so far in the past, now. But, he says it was sick and twisted.:

When I was seven years old, he was seventeen and had just moved into the neighborhood from White Chapel, Britian. We lived in a latin neighborhood and I remember him very clearly because he stuck out with his accent and that he'd never known many latinos.

When I was eleven and he was twenty one and that's when he started to notice me, I suppose. He would look out for us kids when we were playing in the street, but he took a distinct interest in me around that age. He started taking me to eat at the taqueria, the ice cream parlor, buying me little candies and toys all the time. And he told me that one day he was going to marry me.

And when I was thirteen and he was twenty three, he kissed me for the first time. But, he waited until I was fourteen to make me his steady girlfriend.

He talked to my mum about it, and swore he had good intentions, and my mum was okay with it. So, I was his girlfriend and the girls at school thought I was cool because he would pick me up in his car, take me to lunch, we'd go and get stoned in the park, get drunk on the hill.

We made out a lot, but nothing more. And as time went by we kissed less and less and he became more of a father figure. He buttoned my jacket up, brought me soup when I was sick, made sure I did my homework, etc...

After a year of this, I broke up with him to date someone else. He was upset and ended up getting some girl knocked up. Two months later, I took me him back, even though the girl didn't want an abortion. That's when he asked me to marry him.

We were going to move to White Chapel, as far away from my parents as possible. Besides, our marriage would be legal there. His aunts flew in from England to help me pick out the dress and then, a week before the wedding, he told me that he was still sleeping with the other girl.

Granted, in the three years we dated, we never slept together. He only saw me with my shirt off once. So, I was very upset, and despite that he promised it would end, I called off the wedding and went home to my mother.

I was living with him, in seperate rooms.

Now that I look back on it, it was strange.

But, would you consider this paedophilia? I mean, he was in love with me and he never slept with me or even touched me very much. But, I do wonder 'why me?'.
