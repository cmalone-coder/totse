Re: You can eliminate evil
So long as peoples lives are tied to one another, there will be hard feelings. When you're forced to attend school for 12 years, you get a little pissed off. When you interpret the rules of a competition differently than someone else, you feel angry when you lose whatever you otherwise would have had.

So long as people want anything, be that wealth and pleasure, social change, love, competitive superiority, or anything, there will be negative emotions.

This isn't to say that negative emotions can't be minimized. I believe universally accepted standards and agreements of mutual respect would do a lot to mediate the negative feelings people have for one another. But you can't do away with them altogether, because not everyone agrees on how the world should be, and they never will.
