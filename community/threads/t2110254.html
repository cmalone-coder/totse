Re: To shroom or not to shroom? [by myself]
I ate 10.5 grams by myself once (3/8ths)

I just remember looking around, laughing maniacally then stopping suddenly, and then laughing maniacally then stopping. I eventually fell to my knees, everything hovering and morphing. And the colours! Oh my sweet baby Jesus the colours were something to behold!

I also remember sticking my hand in my pants and fondling myself and it just felt UNBELIEVABLY awesome. I wasn't even masturbating, just feeling it gave me such a thrill.

I blacked out and woke up in random places. I once woke up underneath my keyboard craving to read some Chuck Norris jokes. Oh and to watch Hostel.

The bad part to this trip, however, was that after the peak was over, I woke up thinking that I could actually do ANYTHING I wanted to do. It is true in a sense, but I didn't understand the concept of consequences. I lit up a smoke right in my room, went upstairs and blew it in my parents' face (it was only 7 at night).

All hell broke loose, they searched my room and found 9 empty bottles of robogels. I hadn't taken it that night, but I was in no state to explain this. They called an ambulance and I was sent to the hospital.

Perhaps the neatest part about this whole thing was that the entire time I was tripping out on the floor, Infected Mushroom's Converting Vegetarians album was playing on repeat through my guitar amp. The song "Elation Station" was playing through my head the entire ambulance ride, it it was something intense.

Whenever I hear any of the songs on that album now, I get the most obtuse mushroom flashback until it stops playing.

Mushrooms are a very unique drug. I'm thinking that because I had eaten so much that it made me feel like I was an animal living in the wild like we used to again, so I didn't have to worry about consequences. Its the only way I can figure it.

To OP, if you eat an eighth on your own, just make sure you've got some trippy music and some delicious juice to drink. Sunflower seeds are a nice touch, too. I don't recommend watching tv or anything like that, it is a huge waste of a trip. If you are experienced with psychs already, and eighth won't be any need to worry.
