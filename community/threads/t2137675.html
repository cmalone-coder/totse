So it looks like we need a lot of new mods...
In the past few weeks we've lost a lot of moderators. Instead of making 5+ threads for discussion on each one of these forums, why don't we do it all here.

First things first, these are all of the people who have told me that have quit or would like to be removed from one or more forums:

Kelsokid18 - announced resignation on IRC (#mods)
kAtE- - mentioned that she would like to be removed from all forums but Art and Design (Moving Pictures, SG, and Noah's Ark - speaking of which, how'd you get 4 forums in the first place?).
lifejunkie - quit just the other day
robinhoody - quit the other week
yoyobeck - quit just before robinhoody
panthrax - nobody has been able to get ahold of him to figure out whether or not he can stick around - I'm starting to think not.
Jackketch - wants to be removed from Cunning Linguists

This leaves a lot of vacancies. These include:
Radio Free Amerika - Has 0 mods - needs 1 more
Moving Pictures - Has 2 mods - needs 3 or is it ok?
Noah's Ark - Has 1 mod - needs 2 or is it ok? iSoap seems to be doing ok
T&T and it's subforums Hello World and Cellular Mods - Has 1 mod - needs at least 1 more
BLTC - has 3 mods - might not need another
S&A - has 4 - probably won't need another
Oral Indulgences - has 1 - jack, does it need another?
Cunning Linguists - has 0 - needs at least 1

I think that's all.

If any of the mods who wanted to be removed from a forum have had second thoughts, I'll take you and the forum off the list.

I would like to say one thing up front: I would really like to get some new faces on the moderation staff. We have 55 (or 54) moderators and 56 forums (discounting the above people) - when we removed mlor we had 92 moderators and fewer than 50 forums. As you can see, we're stretched thinner than normal. We're at a ratio of one moderator per forum. We seriously need outside help - we can't just keep modding people who are already moderators. With that said, when I say "admin approval" in the following paragraph, that may involve your candidate being denied because he/she is already a mod.

Normally when someone leaves I let the moderator who's leaving and the remaining mods (if there are any) choose who will be replacing them. If the admins have no problem with it then the person will be added. In the event that all of the suggested users are denied by the admins OR the remaining moderators can not come up with a good replacement OR there are no remaining mods and the leaving mod suggested nobody, we'll talk and/or vote on it here. If still nobody can be chosen we'll just make a poll in the forum.

If the remaining moderators and leaving moderator are ok with doing a forum poll right off the bat, that's also fine - but it will still have to go through admin approval.
