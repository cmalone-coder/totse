I've hustled the price of hops oil, chemically similar to valerian, up to $50/g before, with repeat customers... 'n not sold as another drug.

...then again, there is a large difference between "concentrated extractions from raw herb" and "crap from GNC," usually.

As for melatonin... I've... never tried it with melatonin. I can tell you that it works better in combinations - 0.05g or so on top of a bowl of herb will kick the bowl's ass, whereas alone it tends to be a mild sleep aid at best in those dosages...

im an ex heroin addict. and would like something somewhat to just chill me out. not so much euphoric like smack at all. but something more along the lines of a benzodiazepine.

Well... the GABA agonists of valerian/hops are about as along the lines of a benzodiazepine as you're likely to get off the herb market. 'n I'm guessing that as an ex heroin junkie, you've probably got a bit of anxiety as well, which they can help with...

...as far as quality relaxation, well... whole kava root was mentioned earlier...'n, ironically, capsella bursa-pastoris ('shepherd's purse") has an oxytocin analog, which is fairly anti-anxiety...

...if you really want to mellow, high doses of the GABA antagonist thujone, actually, do remarkably well. However, the comedown is a long-lasting rage, and is rather a bitch at the dosages where you start actually noticing and enjoying the pleasureable relaxation (and mildly irritating at subpsychoactive doses).

...there's probably a lot more if I could be arsed to sit 'n think of it... but more important to a great degree than what you're taking - kratom, lactuca tincture, dagga - is that you get some quality whole plant that has been well-treated, rather than cut-rate herbs which were stuffed in a gelcap five years ago and are no longer potent. 'n cleanup is seldom a bad thing, as long as you can keep yourself from ODing...

Best of luck...
