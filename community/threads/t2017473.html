Being A Bum In Flordia
This may seem odd but bear with me. Some time in about a years time I plan on going down to Florida (via bus) and living for a week with nothing as a bum. So that I don't have to explain too much here let's just say this is to win a bet. Anyway I have 3 main concerns, which hopefully some here would be able to address.

1. Getting arrested. What are the panhandling laws like? How strictly are they enforced? What kind of penalties are there? I won't have any form of ID what so ever, and I'll be hundreds of miles from home, what sort of problems might this cause? My friend has suggested making a fake press pass for a fake newspaper, and to claim I'm a freelance reporter. I like this, but it causes a web of lies, which I'll need to address all possible answers. What are the odds of police just throwing me in a drunk tank or something and letting me go in the morning, keeping in mind I'll have no proof of who I am, or address.

2. No making enough money to survive. I plan to bring some emergency money, but if I spend it I fail. I will make all my money by begging, unless some crazy other method presents itself. How much money do you think I could make begging. I figure a quarter every 15 mins might be reasonable. That would be a dollar an hour, and would be enough to live off of.

3. Getting raped, mugged, killed. I guess a bum isn't very likely to be mugged, and despite the high profile stuff you here about I doubt very many bums are kill for the hell of it.

Other than those 3 main issues any other advice on being a bum or Florida (which I've never been to) in general?

To anyone who thinks this idea is just dumb or silly. Well I have to agree, but I'll still be doing it, and expect it to provide a wealth of hilarious stories.

As a resident of Tampa, I'm not really sure what he means by a "hollow shell" of a city. Tampa has plenty of bums. They mostly congregate at the north end of downtown where the Salvation Army soup kitchen is or along Kennedy Boulevard. Tampa has plenty of back alleys and abandoned buidlings to hang out in. And no, you won't get stabbed. Violent crime isn't much of a problem here. You may get heckled in some of the neighborhoods, like near the projects, but that's it.

St. Petersburg is pretty bum-unfriendly. Recently the police came and slashed all the tents at a bum camp. In both Tampa and St. Pete, volunteers are not allowed to give away food on public property.

Sarasota is the worst though. From reading a news article awhile ago, it was ranked one of the most bum-unfriendly cities in the country. I'm sure it's mostly based on crimes towards bums and the actions of the police.

Maybe that helped a little.
