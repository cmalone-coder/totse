Re: amphetamines for weight loss
Well, Adderall is an exact copy of the formula for Obetrol, which was formerly marketed as a weight-loss aid back when amps were popular for that. Whilst XR would most definitely help to curb appetite all day, a possibly better approach, assuming you had access to IRs, would be to take a therapeutic dose around your regular meal times. I'm not saying don't eat-- because you still will, and still will have to-- but the amphetamine dose should help to keep your consumption at a reasonable and likely lower than usual rate.

Seriously though, if you take this route, you gotta incorporate diet and exercise. It is essential. You can't just swallow the amps and continue your sedentary lifestyle as pounds melt off your body-- sorry, it doesn't work like that. Aside from curbing appetite, amphetamines speed up the metabolism, which makes it easier to burn calories once you put yourself up to doing so. Careful on over exertion, though...especially if you find yourself consuming doses of amphetamine above the therapeutic range. Heart attacks aren't fun.
