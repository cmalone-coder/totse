Re: If you killed someone how would you dispose of the body?
IMO, it's always better for the corpse to become a missing person than a resident of the morgue. So what you do is kill him away from his home, in a way that there is no blood (i.e. break his neck). Make sure the location is secluded-witnesses are bad. Stick the body in the trunk and take it back to your place (assuming you have property, if not, you're on your own). Lay down a bunch of plastic bags and plastic wrap on the floor of your garage. Set the corpse on said surface. Now, shave the head and all other parts of the body with excessive hair. The reason for doing this is that when you burn the body, most people will recognize the odor of burning hair however, the odor of a burning corpse is more than likely unknown to them. Set this aside in a plastic bag. Pull out the teeth and set these aside with the hair. Chop the body up into six pieces and dump what's left of the corpse into a garbage bag-triple layer it. Now find someplace private that you can have a nice bonfire at-if you have property and a fire pit this will do fine. Start a large fire with the corpse buried underneath all the kindling and wood. Stay and watch it all night until NOTHING is left but ash. Now, dig through the ash and pick up all the leftover bones. BE THOROUGH. Take the bones back to the house in a garbage bag. Now take the teeth and stick them in the blender with a cup and a half of water. Puree until all that is left are tiny little bits of enamel. Flush this, take the blender apart to make sure that there are no little bits of enamel stuck in the blades, walls, etc. Now clean up the garage. BE THOROUGH. There must be no blood whatsoever in the entire garage. You should be able to eat off of it. Now, Burn all the extra bloody bags and rags that you've got left over. Take the bones and stick them in something like a horse trough or a big bucket. Take a hammer to them and smash them into tiny little pieces. Load everything into the back of your car and drive to the nearest large river. Dump all the little bits of bone and the hair into the river, dump the ashes that you've collected from your fires as well. Do not leave them in the bags. Make sure your house is spotless. As long as you dont do anything stupid you should be fine.
