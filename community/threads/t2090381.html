Re: Does Chantix block THC effects????
I was prescribed Chantix when I was trying to quit cigarettes. First of all, let me say that it does wonders for reducing the physical desire to smoke. I went from smoking 1.5 packs a day to smoking 1.5 cigarettes a day within two weeks. I'm sure I could have stopped entirely, but fuck it, I enjoy smoking.

Chantix does not reduce the effects of THC. If anything, it potentiates them, although that could just be placebo or attributing the potentiation to something that had nothing to do with it, so take that with a grain of salt.

Yeah, it can affect dreaming, but that wasnt too severe for me. The real bitch is the nausea. Terrible, constant, severe nausea. When I was taking it, I would wake up literally every morning and just vomit for awhile.

This was while I was smoking weed. While taking a break from the marijuana, I took the chantix again for a few days, and experienced no nausea. Once again, I can't be sure of this correlation, but it may indeed exist, so, hell, let me know if you experience anything like this.

Re: Does Chantix block THC effects????
I'll keep posting with updates, if nothing else I could be a good case study for anyone curious about this drug.

I'm on day 4 of taking the pills with my eventual quit date being Sunday the 27th. I've been taking one dosage in the morning, and start my nightly/morning cylce this evening......which will be the rest of my cycle for at least the next 30 days.

I've experienced very slight bits of nausea here and there, and don't seem to be eating as much or as regularly. Although I do think about food alot, moreso than I used to. No crazy dreams as of yet, although I feel more tired and seem to fall asleep quicker and deeper.

I feel "altered" somehow, but not quite sure how to describe it. I'm lucid, but more subdued. I'm not able to concentrate or read as easily. My eyes feel heavy. I'm quite forgetful with short term memory, but not longer term. Just yesterday I couldn't remember an item at the grocery store my wife told me 20 minutes earlier, yet remembered a casual reference of a different item days before. Not just an "oops I forgot", just couldn't remember WHAT that item was. With my lack of concentration, I'm having a harder time communicating....verbally and in text. I can't think of certain words that are normally a part of my vocabulary, and am making a shitload of text/typing errors. My concept of time is thrown off, back and forth. Sometimes I can't believe an hour went by that felt like 20 minutes, others it seems like 3 hours past when looking at the clock and seeing it was only 1.

As far as smoking goes, I think its already slowed me down. On Wellbutrin after a week of dosing I would feel a "yick this taste yucky" feeling about 1/2 through a smoke. With this it isn't so much about the "yuck" factor, as it is...."why am I doing this?". I can smoke it sure, it just ain't really doing anything for me.....other than the mundane task of walking outside and the psychological escapism behind the whole process. For me I think that will be the toughest demon to beat right there.

As for the weed, I agree with the poster that said if anything it has "heightened" it somewhat. It just adds to the already detached weirdness I tried to describe above. I'm not as intensely involved with things going on in my life, whether emotionally or mentally.....so smoking the weed just furthers me into a more "spectator" rather than doer. Not that I'm lazy doing nothing, just not multi-tasking in my mind for the next 5 things. The weed seems to add to all of that.

Overall I just don't feel on my "A" game right now. More like a B- or C+. I don't believe anything I'm feeling is a placebo or psychosomatic sympton, as I'm a pretty intelligent person. I do admit to feeling a bit unnerved by the fact this is a drug purposefly targetting the BRAIN......rather than most drugs just changing your chemical makeup. But I'm still going into this with a hopeful yet objective stance, and won't get too spooked by all the shit I'll be enduring.

And yeah for all that are reading this, I could somehow see a recreational value based on what I just described. Would be a tough nut to crack for the underground though, because at $130 for a months script it ain't like they are handing it out like candy.

I've had exactly one cigarette....the morning of quit day 3, which did absolutely nothing to satiate my nicotine cravings. I could smell the smoke, feel the smoke, go through the mundane task of the whole process.....but no true satisfaction. This drug truly is the real deal. I even pretty much talked myself into having that smoke, having a little doubt if all this was legit....or just my mind playing tricks on me. I purposely succumbed WANTING the relief of a cigarette, and chose to do it just 2 days in rather than weeks or months in and being curious in all that time wondering. Nope, smoking is now pointless while on this drug.

Anyone that's thinking of trying this....keep in mind it pretty much does nothing to help those nicotine urges. That is all on you, it's just preventing you from cheating and breaking down. In essence, for the next 12 weeks I have that time to re-condition and re-train my mind to live a happy life without smoking.

The side effects aren't so bad any more, although I still have what I call "waves" of feeling icky. Kinda like the nasuea/tired feeling I decribed earlier in this thread. I'm still slower mentally and it takes more to concentrate and read......but it isn't as profound as before. I did have some problem driving on the highway on day 6, and haven't tried since. My spatial depth, speed, and sense of time was all off. Almost like I was in the Matrix or something. Mind you, I'm a very prideful driver......and have driven stoned, drunk, coked, trippin.....but I just didn't feel like I was in control driving that day. Driving around town, no problem.....but highway driving was weird.

And yes I'm still toking, and still enjoying a good buzz. The weed is definitely more potent too. Whereas it used to take me packing my Dugout One-Hitter around 4 or 5 times to get a nice buzz on.....I'm pretty much baked now after just 3. The weed has obviously been a huge help/crutch dealing with the nicotine cravings, and right after I get high I'm good for about 2 hours before I start jonesing again. Actually my daily weed intake seems to remain the same, while now I'm smoking less to get high....I'm smoking weed more often to help the nicotine urges. Before starting this I was toking twice a day @ 4 or 5 dugouts each time, whereas now its four times a day at 2 or 3 dugouts.

This will probably be my last post for a while on this thread, unless someone has something else to add or question. I'm in this for the long haul and expect things to just keep on keepin on for me. Hopefully the nicotine cravings will get better sooner than later, and I already don't feel them as strongly the last day or two as I did the first couple of days. Sure I'm irritable, sometimes moody, and shorter tempered than usual....but that is just the natural process of trying to quit smoking. This ain't just a chemical change, but an entire lifestyle/psychological change as I battle fighting all of those triggers where I always would have a smoke.
