She had already became, far and away, my best friend. I was closer to her than I was to anyone else, and vice versa. But we both thought thats all the other person wanted, and were afraid of what would happen when she left, and never said anything to the other person. Eventually she left, after that school year ended, and it sucked. I spent the next two years depressed as fuck. I tried my best to forget her, and there was at least one very lon period where I didn't really talk to her at all. I IMMEDIATELY felt like a huge burden had been lifted from me, literally, and I shit you not, things really did look visibly brighter. During that conversation, knowing that afterwards I might never talk to her again, I had cried, and actually passed beyond any form of sadness to a state of just being numb, and not feeling anything; by far the worst feeling I've ever felt. I suppose thats when things started to seem more dim, but things really did brighten.

For quite awhile afterwards, we talked for hours everyday again, until it just became unfeasible. Before she left, she said that every time I missed her, I could write (ala a diary) things down in a notebook as if I was talking to her. She actually did this, and she sent me her notebook,even though it was very hard for her to do so. Although biased, it was the most beautiful thing I've ever read. While reading it, I laughed to tears, but more often was brought to tears through other means. Its in my car, but I'll post a couple excerpts in another post. I realized she had went through so many things I had. Apparently, we both had seen life as hopeless, but the thought of not being able to see the other person again kept us alive.

But to wrap this whole thing up, I still don't know how this whole thing could end. Its been 901 days since I've seen her, and I thought by now I'd be over her, but I'm not. I absolutely see her as my soul-mate, we're just sooo similar in ways that would take me hours to describe adequately. I want to marry her, I want to spend the rest of my life with her, I want to start a family with her. But the obvious hurdles are so huge. I live in the U.S., she lives in Ukraine. I don't speak more than a few dozen words of Ukrainian, and she loves Ukraine, although she'd be willing to move here. I don't want to ask her to do that, I feel like its my turn to make a sacrifice this time.

Basically, I just wanted more opinions about this. I'm I a fool for this? Is it completely out of the question to feel like this, still or even at all? I've met no one else in this time period who interests me for very long, and every female falls far short of the benchmark shes set in my mind. I feel like if this doesn't work, the moment when I see her for the first time in so long next summer will be the happiest I'll ever be.
