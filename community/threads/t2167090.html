Re: So I can't seem to find help...
I feel for you duder, I just got out of rehab myself but I'm not doing so well....

It sucks that you don't have any resources. My town is a veritable goldmine for its' size, but I suppose that is because of our ridiculously prevalent drug problem. Are there any places within a (realistic) drive of you that you could check out?? It could prove possible to go drive a ways for a suboxone script, after all it's not like daily methadone dosing. Failing anything at all, (And I know this is SO SO SO much easier said than done), just try to cut down. Try to buy some sub on the street instead of your DOC. Cut out the speed. Find what holds you longest for cheapest and DON'T take a lot. An oxy 80 for me can mean a brief happiness then full-on sickness within 12 hours, or taken in pieces, orally, can hold me for more than 24 hours. A half gram of good dope is like the equivalent of 3 80's (and costs 10$ more than ONE here...), but again can either mean a high 24 hours or 2+ days of 'maintenance' dosing.

I can't say I truly ever managed to taper with these suggestions or anything but I HAVE frequently stretched my resources and gotten a lot of precious, precious non-sick time.

Don't tell your doctor. If you literally see no way out, then maybe tell him later on.... but telling him 'just cuz' will jeapordize your medical chart, script, and relationship with him/her. I know some doctors are cool, but really the odds are against you on this one.

Last thing, that I tell everyone (and is kind of my issue right now). Suboxone is a good thing for a failing addict, and can/will help you. However, especially after taking it for a while, it has a strangely nasty withdrawal. Now, it is not the kind of withdrawal where you're shitting puking and dying on the floor for days, but unfortunately it lasts forever. General malaise, depression, chills and INSOMNIA for weeks after. Definitely taper as much as possible.... I've always jumped above 2mg/24hr so I suppose if you tried hard enough and got your taper to like 1/2mg a day, it may be easier.
