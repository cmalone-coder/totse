Hope Chests
When I was a young girl some girls would start a "Hope Chest" around the age of 16. It would become filled with things for their future life and usually with the intent of a future husband.

So popular this was back then, that a local furniture store would get a list of female students of that age and go door to door selling "Hope Chests". He would also leave a free miniature sample of the style he was selling. My parents couldn't afford to buy one, but I still have that miniature with me today and as I was putting some jewelry in it I began to wonder about how things are today.

I am curious if that tradition has died out, or do some girls still do that. If so, then is it still with the intent of marriage or is it with the intent of moving out on your own...or simply both?
