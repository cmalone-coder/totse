Re: Music for pregnancy
Pavanne is a beautiful classical piece. I could listen to that song so many times over and over again. Your baby will definitely be quite relaxed. Try the version by Chris Botti if you enjoy trumpet or if you like the gorgeous sound of oboe, there's a version by the New Zealand Symphony that's really pretty.

There're two more classical pieces that I would really recommend:

Concierto de Aranjuez, Movement II (Adagio) by Rodrigo

and

Finlandia by Jean Sibelius

But both have drastic dynamic changes. They're not just all quiet, although both have a large section with quiet, beautiful melodies.

Hope that helps.
