Re: 92f versus 1911
I'd go with the 92f, for a number of reasons. Most importantly, it is an easy gun to shoot. The 1911, even though it is a very effective weapon in competent hands, is prone to accidents and mechanical failure if the user dosen't have the necessary of training. The 92f also needs less care to work reliably. Cleaning your weapon is a major embuggerance when you are in the field, and soldiers tend to skip it, given the chance.

IMO, the 9mm is also a better suited caliber compared to the .45. The rounds weigh less, enabling the soldier to carry more rounds, and they take up less room in the magazine. The 9mm has better penetration performance than the .45, and while this is not very important in a home defense gun, it may very well be a decisive factor on the modern battlefield. After passing through a full magazine of 5.56 (on the webbing) and a flak jacket, the .45 has very little energy left, if any at all.

M9 vs 1911

Capacity is a poor substatute for stoping power. Neither 9mm nor 45 will penitrate a flack jacket, but 45 will deliver more energy. That is the name of the game; start the bleeding, stop the breathing, and bring on shock. The .45 is more well suited for that purpose especaily in a military context (as ball ammo must be used).

The 1911 has a consistant 5 lb. trigger pull(military) where the Beretta will have a 10 lb. trigger pull for the first round and 5 lb. pulls after that. This inconsistantcy will lead to follow up shots having different points of impact from the first.
