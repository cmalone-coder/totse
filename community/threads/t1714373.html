You'd actually be surprised at the number of people who idiotically leave their car doors unlocked at night. I've been out a few times with friends to steal car audio equipment and it never seems too difficult, even without using a slim jim, etc.

Keep in mind that for a car alarm to work, the car has got to be locked. A flashing red light located in the lower-middle of a car's windshield means there is a car alarm installed.

If you can't obtain a lock picking device, dont worry. You'll more than likely be able to find an unlocked car with something worth stealing inside.
