Cheap Reliable Cars

What are some cheap reliable cars or cars that can be profitable?

I'm thinking Civics, Integras, late 80s early 90's (2.3 liter)Mustangs and (v6)Camaros, Probes, and Supras.

How much do you think I could ask for it? (Assuming it has a great paint job, good motor, really clean interior with no scratches or anything, new or spotless carpets and seats, and other little things like that.)

I'm only 15 and can't even get a job at McDonald's; any money is good money and my time isn't valuable at all.

I got the idea because my Mustang was only $650 and it had a newer engine with 10,000 miles on it's rebuild. It needed one hose replaced, new tires, the A/C clutch needs replaced, and it looked like some dumb ass tried to paint it with a paint brush and off-white interior paint. Where I live atleast, some people sell their 2.3 for $1,500-$2,000 in worse condition than mine.

I think if I gave a car a tune up, cleaned it well, and painted it, there shouldn't be any reason I couldn't make decent money off it if I found the right person to sell it to. Someone who is looking for a first car or something cheaper on gas.

I already have a HVLP paint sprayer and a D/A sander. $20 for sand paper, $15 for primer, $60 for paint, $20 for clear coat. The price is not too high really.

I don't know, it seemed to be a good idea to me.

Re: Cheap Reliable Cars
Profitable if you can find a reasonable quality of car for less than your dollar or your in the know with repo yards or auctions.
Free advertising like back packer hostel message boards or local super market bulletin boards (I'm not sure if they're free where you are) we have a T&E which is free to advertise in. Cool for buying as the only ones that seem to sell products in it are those without internet, so they don't compare prices to online.
And I sell online on trademe (kinda like e-bay)
You really need to spend money to make money in this game.
If your doing the work yourself that would save you $$$, but paint will knock you back a shit load, so the less panels in need of paint, the better.
Make sure you take into consideration the price of parts, paint etc.
And as stupid as this sounds your time spent on the vehicle, (give yourself an hourly rate), just so you get a ball park figure of what the vehicle owes you all up.
Theres a market for cheaper cars here and a few low income earners & back packers are after cheap cars.
But thats here and I'd assume the situation would change where you are.
This may help as a basis or scare you off.
Good Luck you'll need it.
