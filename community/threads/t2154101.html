Think think think think think.....

What does Seroquel (and in essence all the atypical antipsychotics) do?

It restricts the amount of available dopamine and serotonin in the brain. As in, it makes your brain use less.

Now, what do serotonin and dopamine do?

They make you happy, they give you emotion, give you motivation (especially that dopamine).

What do low dopamine and serotonin do? They make you depressed. Notice why people who are depressed get given an SSRI (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor)? To make more serotonin available in the brain, or make it available for longer. Or something.

Anyway, unless you are experiencing acute psychosis, there is no need to be taking antipsychotics.

They are also addictive and hard to come off.

Not to mention the fact that they aren't sure of how they work, just that they do.

And they steal your soul and ability to have enjoyment and feel emotional, either happy or sad.
