Re: De-clawing
I personally think declawing is mean and not necessary. Like having all your fingernails ripped off! You can try and trim them yourself, and can probably get little specialist clippers from a pet store, but lots of animals are fussy about it, and make it as difficult for you as possible. My advice in this case would be to get a vet to do it; they'll be careful not to cut into the quick too. A nice scratching post might help a little too. If your cat is allowed outside, it should naturally wear down its claws so they're not too long... but, claws are sort of designed to be sharp really!
