Nihilism takes away any moral dogma. It is believed killing is wrong, nihilists will ask "why?!" and refuse to accept it without some sort of proof. It's extreme doubt in everything.

But the main flaw in nihilism is that humans do not function without some sort of emotion, and cannot see outside that emotion. This has led to the nihilist belief that all decisions are arbitrary. But funnily, doubting your own emotion and mind means doubting your decision to doubt your own emotion and mind (this is what made me agnostic). It lacks direction, which is fine. But for the emotional, faith driven machine that humans are...it doesn't really work.
