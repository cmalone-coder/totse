Re: still have a few questions
hey miss...this is how swinc does the pills. so far so good... crush as fine as possible. add 3x denatured alcohol to mass, shake for 1 min, let settle for 1/2 hr, use turkey baster (glass), carefully extract liquid only. if any pm in liquid, let sit for 1/2 hr, re-extract. using same baster, stuff 10 cotton balls in narrow neck and pack tight. wet with clean denatured alcohol before filtering. after cotton balls are wet down with denatured, filter extracted solution through the cotton balls. take filtered fluid, carefully pour onto large mirror or glass, let air dry, don't rush. you will see contaminents, also a lot of pinwheels. process not finished yet! after completely drying, scrape mass off of glass. put pm in glass container. use generous amount of mek. swirl for about 15 secs let sit for 5 min. psudeo will not dissolve in mek, but contaminents will. simply pour off mek, let psuedo air dry until you no longer smell mek. now you are ready to re-disolve in denatured alcohol just enough to dissolve mass and then a pinch. pour out on mirror and let air dry. watch some of the prettiest pinwheels form!!
