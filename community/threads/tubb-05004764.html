You get resin, not hash. Hash [well, good hash anyways] is simply resing glands [crystals] that have been pressed together. Resin on the other hand is not only a build up of THC but other shit too. Just a little FYI. Its still a nice bonus though.

I'm pretty diverse. Ill hnever pass up a nice rip off the bong, but I'm not adverse to blunts, joints or pipes either. I'm pretty stingy with ym weed wimply for lack of cash.

However, unless you out the bowl after every hit, it really wastes no less weed than a joint. And even if you do, then each time you apply a direct flame to it, you kill a bit of THC.

All in all, it really doesnt matter. All the methodes out there work well, and though they all waste a little, the outcome is always fantastic. As well blunts have one great benifit, and thats flavoured wraps. Gotta love smoking a fat strawberry blunt out on the balcony with a few friends on a breezy summer day, listening to the wind chimes all around peoples houses. [those truly are memories I wont forget. Damn this winter and screwing up my making memories! AAA!]

In the end I never dwell on how much of my smoke floated into the air. So long as I can lay back and chill out, and just let my mind wander to wherever it pleases, Im good.

No matter how you smoke, just make sure you have a good time doing it.
