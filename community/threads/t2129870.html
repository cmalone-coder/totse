-How long have you been taking them (the SSRIs)?
-How old are you?
-What's your condition? Severity? How long have you been experiencing whatever you have (assuming depression)?

SSRI's take awhile to become beneficial because your body takes time to adjust to them, usually two weeks to two monthes. And by all accounts, this period of adjustment is a living hell and there are little or no positive effects from the drug.

That being said, I believe that anti-depressents should be a last resort, only to be used after therapy alone has been proved ineffective. How long were you in therapy before starting a drug treatment? I believe that many people, including therapists, see anti-depressents as a cure instead of a tool. Maybe give therapy a chance. Be aware that there's a difference between clinical and natural depression. Everyone gets depressed. Some of these people can get back themselves, some need time, and some might even need professional help. Maybe your condition isn't permanent and life's just got you down.

However, I'm no psychological professional and neither are most people on totse; talk to your psychiatrist first.

Also, be aware that there are alot of bad shrinks out there. And there's alot of good ones that aren't going to be compatible with you.
