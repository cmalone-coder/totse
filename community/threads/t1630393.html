Re: Are you addicted to anything?
Just to make any future posts better, I'll make a bit of a guide for all 5 levels:

1. You would like to have it at least once a week, simply because its fun. You're kind of disappointed if you don't get it, but really you don't care that much [not really an addiction].

2. You do it upto 3 or 4 times a week and you generally get disappointed/mad when you're dry and your deals fall through [this is more of a recreational habbit, still not quite an addiction].

3. You do it everyday and will get despressed/pissed off if you can't get your hands on any.

4. You NEED to do it every day or you're really depressed/nervous/pissed off. You're also willing to do things to get what you need, such as steal from family or friends. You risk withdrawls when you're dry.

5. You would rather be dead than not have it, or you can die without it [opiates]. You'll also do pretty much anything to get it as its more important than anything else in the world. Withdrawls are inevitable when dry, however seeing as how the drug is now your life you do everything you can to avoid being dry.

Based on this, I could say I have a level 2 with pot. And I do feel a level 2 is not an addiction [based on what I've said].
