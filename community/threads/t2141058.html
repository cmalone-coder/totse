My experiences with pot, pretty bad so far
I think I got too high for my second time or the weed I had was laced. Here is my story:

A few weeks ago:

So, the parents were gone and I had some weed. I went out and smoked it. I was very proud because I believe I was taking good hits, but I didn't feel much right off the bat, so I started walking back to my house, thinking my attempt was a failed one. When I began walking, my heart rate started racing and my vision became really weird. I had trouble opening my door and putting my pipe away. I felt like I was going to fall over so I laid down in bed to wait for the high to pass.

This was a very unpleasant experience, I was basically paralyzed on my bed for a total of two hours, my thoughts racing the whole time, and my muscles twitching in a circular pattern. My left ankle would twitch, then my knee, then my wrist, then my shoulder, repeat for the other half of my body.

Maybe I have low THC tolerance. I just can't figure out why I keep having these bad trips.

The first time I smoked pot ever, I thought it didn't work. I was in a hotel room with some other guys and we smoked in the bathroom, using the shower to suck the smoke out. We had a close call with some hotel employees after a pizzaman delivered our pizza. This freaked us out quite a bit so we all decided to hit the sack

The next day I felt like the world would end. I was walking around Mall of America with a buddy and I would suddenly feel like breaking down and crying. It was fucked up.



I have some leftover weed from the ordeal a few weeks ago. I think I may try it again tonight. The smoke always seems to bother me a lot. I know the smoke is harsh but everytime I take a hit, I feel like I might puke, which seems a bit odd. I'll try making a bong or something to make the hits more smooth and I'll post my next experience later tonight.

I find joints to be smoother than pipes or bongs, but that may be personal preference I'm not sure, so try rolling a joint with a little tobacco in it and see how that goes.

As far as freaking out goes, try and remember it's only weed mate. You aren't going to die, and the world is still in one piece. You could try taking a benzo before you smoke to relax you a bit. Also your tolerance is low, you are getting pretty high.

Twitching and heart racing is common for me, the twitching at least, it just lets me know I'm pretty high. Don't stress too much about it.

Worst case, weed isn't for you. Not everyone likes every drug. Give it another chance and just chill, get some munchies and download some simpsons episodes.
