I've been in a lot of similiar situations.

I have a friend who never really has the best luck with relationships and girls; they always turn out to be crazy.

The thing is that they really only dated for maybe a couple days in total (this lasted at least over a year, maybe around 2). This girl was a total bitch to him all the fucking time: hypocritical, liar, went off on him for no reason, made him feel like total shit all the time, and he would still tell her he wished it wasn't like that and he really cared about her. It's not that he didn't realize how she was treating him, he's just...odd...and cared about her a lot.

You can't really tell this guy what to do, in ANY situation (even if it's something he would typically do anyways). He gets nervous and feels pressured at almost any sign of expectancy and just freezes up.

His best friend is more of an outgoing kind of person and would always say shit to him about not doing things. He's that person that gets in people's business and inputs his ideas like they're part of the bible, is an asshole and likes it, and always has something to say.

So (ironically) the two together don't work out a lot of times because when the one guy gets nervous or tries to work shit out on his own, the other guy is always on his ass which only makes it worse.

And the first guy has stated many times that people can't tell him what to do and he just wants to handle his shit on his own.

I always try to fight for him cause I understand where he's coming from. I try to explain to everyone to just leave him be because he's not a retard, he just needs to think.

But I'm digressing. Those situations suck. My advice is to not tell him that someone is being a bitch to him; like I said, he's probably not retarded and can figure it out for himself. It's just better to talk to them about what's going on and help them realize for themselves the extent of the situation (and the effects it has on other people) and how to go about handling it; don't preach. Remember that it's really NOT your business, even though it does have an effect on you. And don't assume you know the entire situation, from either sides or both sides, because that's when you'll wind up looking like a total assclown.
