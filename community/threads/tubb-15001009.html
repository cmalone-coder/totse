Here's a real doozy
Blah blah, "God" doesn't exist. Blah blah, you can't prove he does, you can't explain him, blah blah blah.

Alright, given that, lemme turn the situation around for a moment. I'm not a Christian, but I am a universal religion advocate. I believe that everyone should practice the religion they want, and I *DON'T* then proceed to say that anyone who follows religion X is stupid.

For all you "atheistic knowitalls" who demand proof of God or other divinity, answer:

Gravity is the force that allows the Earth to orbit the Sun, the Moon to orbit the Earth, etc. All objects exert gravity, but when you get lots and lots of an object in "one place" (like say, a planet...), then the object has enough gravity to affect other objects on a large scale.

But... what causes gravity? A particle pulls on other particles with an extermely short-ranged force that causes particles to stick together, we know that. But if these particles exhibited a gravitational pull as well, atomic structures would collapse in nanoseconds.

Think of a heat source. A heat source, like gravity, radiates out this heat/pull based on how big it is. Except, the heat being radiated is the production of a series of chemical reactions. There is no such effect producing gravity.

Gravity, additionally, works on an inverse square formula for strength. That is: objects twice as far away are subject to only a quarter the gravity. Yet, gravity is a constant based on the mass of the object. One would think that being on one side of the planet, the gravity from 99% of the planet would be negligible. Not the case though!

And that's just a common, everyday concept, not even stepping into esoteric fields like Quantum Mechanics or Chaos Theory. So explain that one to me before you talk about being unable to prove he existence of God.
