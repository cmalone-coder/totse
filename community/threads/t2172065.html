Dreaming and intoxicants
One of the fundamentals of drugs i think is the ability to tap into the sub/unconscious, and the idea that ultimately it is all derived from you, and they are just unlocking inhibited aspects of you( particularly, deleriants, dissociatives, psychedelics) is marvelous. And i wonder how similar the process is to dreaming.

Clearly the divide between a good trip and bad trip seems similar to a nightmare or dream. The altered state of consciousness is also present, and although it has that hazey, out of control, 3rd person perspective to it REM sleep is in many ways a highly conscious state, what i want to focus on is lucid dreams.

Everyone has dreams, but not everyone has lucid dreams.

Do you think the ability to be lucid within dreams, and learn from them will mean better living through dreaming, and this can be a preparation for psychoactive's or they can potentiate and amalgamate with each other in some way?

For example sleep paralysis is a scary and fearful event, much like a "bad" visual/hallucination; but often there are things to be learnt from like the ability to distinguish reality from artificial reality (perhaps spritual nature from material nature).

Edit: It seems deleriants have the most in common with dreams
