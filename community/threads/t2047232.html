The scientific (not Christian LOL) thing to do would be to say, "there is no proof of the existence a divine being but there is also no proof against it. Therefore, we can not be sure of the non-existence of a God but because there's no proof for it, there probably is none." It is not scientific to say that something is necessarily false because there is no proof of it.

You probably consider the people who challenged Galileo asinine for sticking to the Aristotelean principles and doubting that things fall at the same speed regardless of mass. They were following your principle. There's no evidence that two things of different masses fall at the same speed so it obviously is not true. Once Newton provided evidence, it was clear that wasn't the case. Someone tomorrow could make some sort of geometric proof which proves that there has to be a God. That's not going to happen but you should be able to see why it's not scientific to say something is certainly not true just because of a lack of evidence.
