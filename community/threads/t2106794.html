Actually, the tagamet doesn't have much to do with stomach acids.

Tagamet clogs cytochrome P450's isoenzymes CYP3A4 and CYP2D6, which are what oxycodone is metabolized through. By slowing their action, you eliminate or reduce first pass metabolism, resulting in significantly higher blood plasma levels. The stomach acid is just an extra bonus, but it takes a while for the reduced stomach acid to come into effect, so I would still recommend tums\baking SODA if you're eating your opes.

And rebound acidity doesn't matter, because by the time your body produces extra acids, the opiates are already absorbed. And the Tagamet will stop rebound acidity from taking place, anyway. ^_^
