Anybody know what "sass" is?
I just got back from All Good (which was AMAZING) and I encountered a new drug while I was there. I'm fairly versed in substances, especially of the psychedelic type, so this shocked me. Here's the description:

It's a light brownish, kind of tan, powder.

Got sold to us as MDMA and ID'd by a guy we bought acid from as "sass." He said it was supposed to be extracted from sassafras root the same way you'd do a cocaine extraction.

It wasn't too chunky, but it needed crushed.

I'd say each piece was maybe 3/4 mm.

It didn't seem to be too hygroscopic or heat sensitive. It was around 90 degrees and it didn't melt or degrade at all in my friend's pocket for 3 days.

It took about 100 mg IN to get you high as shit. My friend did about 300 mg over the course of a few hours and felt it was too much for him, but not a dangerous level. I can do a little less, but I tend to be sensitive to stimulants.

It felt almost exactly like MDMA, except it was smoother and the euphoria wasn't as much.

The side-effects weren't nearly as pronounced as MDMA, except for nystagmus to the point of being intolerable on my part. No one else had that problem, though.

The jaw tension wasn't nearly as bad, but it was there.

My heart rate seemed to stay very low for being on a stimulant as well.

It was very visual for a roll. Lots of edges waving and such.

Audio was much enhanced, just like with MDMA.

That's all I can think of right now. If you need any more info to ID it, let me know. The thing that really made me interested was the fact that it felt almost exactly like MDMA, except my heart rate didn't go through the roof. I like this, as my family does have a history of heart problems and I smoke a lot of rollies.

Thanks in advance, and sorry if some of this is badly typed. Like I said, I just got back from All Good. :)
