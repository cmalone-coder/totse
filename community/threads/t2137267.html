Re: Run or dont run?
This is really bad advice that I'm giving, but if sticking around would result in serious charges, then why not run? Running on foot is a misdemeanor (check your state's laws), and if you're going to jail anyway if you stick around, you really have nothing to lose by running. Just be prepared for the cops kicking your ass if they catch you.

Never run in a vehicle though. That's a felony, you can ruin innocent people's days (and get homicide charges if you happen to kill any of them). If a pursuing officer complains of so much as wrist pain from anything resulting from the chase, that's another felony.

So in summary, never run in a car, and don't run on foot unless you're pretty sure you can get away (it's hard, but I've seen a few people do it ;) ) and sticking around would get you in a ton of trouble for sure. Use your best judgment, but being in a situation where you need to evaluate whether to run from the police is probably an indication that good judgment isn't your strong suit.

the cops didnt see me but later (a few days) they see me walking down the street and pull me over...

Lesson 1 : they didnt' see you.

This is something that most people don't know, yet is one of the most useful things in existance. Even if they're staring at a chalkboard of photographs of you when you walk by, are able to match up every scar and tatoo to the micrometer, etc, etc, etc... if there's not fingerprints or DNA or the like, and you're not dumb enough to confirm your identity, they didn't see you... they saw some citizen, with all the rights of a citizen, whom the want to ascertain whether or not it is you.

Now, with a warrant against fingerprints on file, this may be probable cause to detain you to see if they can get a warrant to compel a fingerprint check to confirm your identity.

However, "some guy of x-foot-y with z-colored skin and q-colored hair was doing this" is not even close to being something that could get an arrest or search warrant. The best they could really do is talk to you (possibly detain you, but probably not - it would be slightly legally dangerous for them)... and hope that you incriminate yourself.

Running, otoh, is reason enough to arrest you for attempting to elude, allowing them time (and your prints & docs) to find an angle on figuring the rest out later.
