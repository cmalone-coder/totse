Become genuinely interested in people and what they do and you'll find you'll get same thing back. Ask people what they've been up to and listen and ask questions/share stories around it. Try and be confident in yourself and just talk to people. If you talk to a lot of people and don't act retarded, having friends really isn't that difficult.

I wasn't much of a looker in high school, I was pretty much the complete opposite and I was still considered one of the 'popular' kids. Be GENUINELY interested in people and if you're a funny person, don't be afraid to make the jokes.

Probably my best advice is to get busy. Get busy - get pussy. Remember, the more you do the more you have to talk about. Try and experience everything.

There's a quote I heard once when I was younger that I've tried to adhere to. It goes something like, "If you want your life to become more interesting, say yes more often." Do it. Say yes to every offer and invitation and see where you end up. Makes life a lot more exciting. Fuck the self-limiting beliefs.

Be social, be confident and say yes more often. Friends and girlfriends fall into place.

EDIT: As a side-note; I worked at Target through senior high school and it was the best job I had. You work with about 30 people around your own age and it's a great place to make friends, have a laugh and find people to get blazed and drunk with. Look into it. Once again; get busy. Play sport. Join communities. Exercise. Go to parties with new and random friends.
