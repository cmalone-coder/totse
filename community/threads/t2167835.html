Re: Cleaning Soapbar
The method iv used:

Grind into fine powder with nutmeg grater, put in a pan of boiling water (not too much) and boil for about 30 mins. This basically takes out any soluable crap but leaves the solid shit and THC, Filter this and discard the water. Then bosh it in a little Iso Alcohol, butane or if your hard up acetone. Let this soak a while and agitate it loads before filtering it again. This time chuck away the solid crap or run it through the solvent again just in case you missed any. Evaporate the solvent off and with any luck you should have some pretty clean strong oil. Personally though I think its only worth doing if you have more than a 1/4 preferably an oz+ anything less and you might as well just put up with it.

Edit: In the time it took me to write it the water method was posted (pretty good step by step btw) If you want to go a step further and get extra blazed off even cleaner gear then take my second step too.
