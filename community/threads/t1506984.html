hey arquin.

do you like the way you are when you're on your meds, or do you prefer the person you are when you're off them? it's not a trick question. if you prefer the person you are when you're on them, that's you, and there's nothing fake about it. but if you're yearning for your bipolar highs and lows, or bored with your mellow stability, it's not you. a person's essence is so much more than their behavior or their natural brain chemicals. it's their consciousness, and where their consciousness prefers and chooses to dwell.

i've tried a shitload of meds to help me. prozac and cymbalta calmed me down. the seritonin made me not care and not get angry or upset. i didn't like it, though! i felt flattened. i had no artistic inspiration, and though i was happy, i felt listless. i much prefer the effects of wellbutrin, which allows me to have bad moods, creativity, and highs and lows. it does help me, though, by giving me more motivation and energy. since i like how i feel, i believe the way i am on this medication is a true reflection of who i am. it's that simple.

as for your first question, i can't generalize. some guys who are bipolar would be worth it, others wouldn't be.
