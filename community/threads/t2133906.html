Two different philosophies.
For the past year, which is the complete run I've had with my ex, I've lived by the philosophy "He don't mean to be mean or hurt you on purpose, Take a tip and do yourself a little service. Take a mountain turn it into a mole just by playing a different role". I became fond of this saying when we broke up, about 8 months ago.

Basically, if he is cold to me, or if he hurts me, turn it around and fuck him over. But this doesn't work anymore. I can't suppress my feelings for him and act like I don't love him, or need him because then I am hurting myself. I can't truthfully play his role, of hurting me and taking me for something simple, that isn't how I feel.

But I feel that I can't show him how much I love him because he hurts me very badly, I am vulnerable with him and he takes me for granted. I have a bad feeling that by showing my vulnerable side to him, he will only take advantage of it and hurt me worse. Instead of trying to win him over, I push him away and reply with sarcasm and false strength when he brings up a problem. But I only fool myself when I know that the feelings I have for him are stronger than any strength I build up to fend him off.

"For well you know that it's a fool who plays it cool by making his world a little colder. "

I am the fool who makes my world colder when things get heavy. I try to numb myself to the hurt by thinking about how insignificant he is, but he is the most significant thing in my life anymore. I can't keep numbing myself to the feelings I have for him. I can't predict the future and I can't find out if completely opening up and loving him with everything I have will turn out for the better, or if I will just end up more hurt than I would've been if I'd just made my world a little colder.

I don't know if I am asking anything, just helps to get that out to people who could potentially understand.
