the first "The Punisher"
A version of this came out in 1989 starring Dolph Lundgren as the titular hero, Lou Gossett, Jr., as his ex-partner, and I think it is in many ways superior to the new version.

For one thing, Lundgren's wooden acting style is appropriate for a hero who has been rendered inartculate by grief and rage, and the film has a moere comic book "feel"--such as the character of the drunk actor.

I welcome opinions from others who have seen both, and who generally love the subgenre of "comic book movies."
