Wildfires.... Good or Bad?
Summer is on its way, and that means lots of new wildfires, both natural and manmade. Do you guys think that these are good for nature, or just harmful to the environment? On the one hand they promote growth of new plants and clear out dead brush, but then again there is massive damage to fauna and lots of smoke. I think in the general sceme of things they arent as harmful as some people make them out to be. Even if fauna dies, animals live at a much faster rate than plants, so they will come back quicker than a dead forest would.
What do you think?
