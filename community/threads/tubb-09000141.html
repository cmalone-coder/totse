Re: Dreams coming true?
This is kinda related, but I have several memories of events that happened about 15 to 20 years ago; except that I can't be sure if they are real or not. They are either things that I did while heavily under the influence, or it was a dream. No matter how hard I concentrate I can't tell which. I've even asked some friends [who appear in the memories] and they are no help.

Like I said, that is only kinda related to the topic.

Closer to it, yet is this other thing that happened a few years back.

Due to various things relating to the bone disease in my spine, I don't get very good sleep; and haven't for years, which means I either don't get to the dream stage often. Or, if I do get there, I am so deeply in it that I don't often even know that I had dreamed upon waking up, let alone remember what I had dreamed.
Sometimes, though, the gods of sleep & pain look kindly on me and I do dream [well, I remember them I mean]. When I do recall a dream it sticks out and stays with me for quite some time. I've been able to use a couple as the basis for songs. I also have a few that, while I wouldn't really consider them recurring, they do have certain aspects which form a sort of basis of continuity - for example: there is one which has this particular cemetery [and to a lesser extent, the accompanying mortuary] as the basic setting. Now it's not a scary dream or gory or anything [I was a mortician at one point in my life, so it's not all that surprising. Many people regularly have their dreams set in work or other well known environments]. Anyway, it's not that the cemetery looks exactly the same each dream, but there are certain features that I can count on being there. It's kinda weird; I've had many dreams set there.
I've kinda got off the point I was going for, whoops. However, while I am here on the subject of dream mortuaries and stuff, there is one other dream I want to share here: I may not get another chance.
This one was set in the same mortuary that I worked in [this dream occurred while I was still a mortician there]. In it I had walked into the prep room [the embalming room]. In the prep room my Don [my Boss] was embalming an old lady. For a bit I just stood and watched: as I had done many times in real life. Soon, though, I realize that the lady being embalmed was having a conversation with my boss. Seeing the puzzled look on my face, the lady stopped talking to Don, looked over to me and said "Oh, I'll bet you've never seen this happen, have you?" I told her that I hadn't, but I knew that it could happen [That, I'm certain, had been related to the fact that in real life I had recently had my first experience in seeing a body sit straight up during a cremation: something I had been told could happen yet hadn't seen for myself...Sorry for the further side tracking - Can you tell I've been off my lithium and not slept in 3 1/2 days?].
Ok, so this dead lady is looking at me and talking, and I'm just taking it as if it was the most natural and expected thing in the world. I don't recall much of what was said, except for the last bit - At some point I had asked her where she was from . She said: Italy. I then said "Really? What part?" Her reply: "All of me".... Well I thought it was pretty funny; not to mention a unique joke, as far as I know.
Ok, so maybe that wasn't worth the space that I took to write, I'll get to the thing I wanted to say to begin with [Now you can see why I don't contribute much here when my bi-polar is in full swing].

Ok, here it is: About 4 or 5 years ago I had this dream that I had only partially remembered. And that part that I did remember was this pool. It was kinda like an indoors pool, but more like in a cave; still a man made pool, but in a cavern type setting. The water in this pool was a deep, deep royal blue. and I remember the layout of the cavern it was in; all the exits, hallways, and what not, along with a bunch of other tiny details that I need not list here. Outside of the fact that I had remembered so much detail about it wasn't really surprising to me; like I said, on those few occasions that I do [recall] dreaming, it usually is something vivid. Other than that, though, there really wasn't anything special about that dream to make me write all of this. Until sometime back in 2000, that is. At that time I was staying with a couple of friends, one of which was [and still is] very much into video games. Now he knew that I didn't have much interest in them so he usually never mentioned them to me. One day, however, he called out from his room and said that I had to check out this new game he had brought home [I don't remember which game it was]. So I went in to check it out. When I got there there, on the TV screen, was the same damn pool that I had dreamed. I mean, I know that the one I saw was animation, but it didn't matter; it was real enough. It was exactly as it was in my dream, right down to the color of the water - all of the hallways and exits - everything. Needless to say I just stood there and stared for a few before I could even tell my friend what had happened. He thought it was really no big thing, but to me it was - and still is - one of those Twilight Zone moments of life.

So, that's it. Too much build up for such little substance, isn't it?

I'll go now. My bad.
