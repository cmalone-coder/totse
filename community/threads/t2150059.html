Use your mouth to inhale, when it seems like you cant inhale anymore reduce the volume in your mouth by pushing your tongue forward/ closing your jaw and open your lips. The smoke comes out thickly, then you suck it back in. You can do this a few times if you want before you exhale.

If it's windy allowing the smoke to sit in your mouth for a few seconds before inhaling helps, similar to smoking a cigar.
