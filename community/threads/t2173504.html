You can't judge a console on how many preowned games you find at your local store. What you need to do is look at the quality and volume of decent games (say, everything that scores over an 8 on metacritic, or whatever) and then make your decision.

That said, the Wii has - in my opinion - had the veil of 'innovation' lifted to reveal a predictable gimmicky reality.

The PS3, while better than when it was launched, is still lacking in killer apps. Most half-decent games that are released on the PS3 get released on the 360 as well (with little to no differences in favour of the PS3 - a shock, since the PS3 was labeled as a vastly more powerful system than the 360 prior to its release). I used to be a Sony fan - I owned and supported both of the consoles prior to the PS3 (PSX; PS2) - however, I feel fully let down this generation, hence why I own a 360. Sony were too late in the game this time round and it has cost them dearly.

Face it, you're more likely to see a 360 attached to a TV than a PS3.
