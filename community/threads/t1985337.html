Re: Your Ghibli collection
Ive never heard of Ocean Waves or the Flying Ghost Ship,any place I could find those?
Ocean Waves (sometimes known as "I Can Hear the Sea") is a TV movie made by Ghibli in 1993. It was basically a chance for the young staff members do make a film without the help of Miyazaki or Takahata (the two most senior/well-known directors at Ghibli)... nice but not essential, your typical love triangle plot with some nice animation.

The Flying Ghost Ship is a movie made at Toei Douga in 1969 for which Miyazaki was key animator and made some contributions to the script. It's very dated, but it's still a nice sci-fi story.

Neither of these movies have been officially released in English, and I just have VHS copies of the fansubs. I may be able to scrounge up some links to the fansubs if you don't mind downloading.
