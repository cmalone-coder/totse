I'm thinking of locking myself in a room, and putting all breakable and dangerous objects away. Though there's still a risk, though I'm not sure how much of one.

Anything to say totse? I know at least 50% of BLTC knows what they're talking about.

Re: Salvia, do I need a sitter?
a sitter really depends on the setting...in a room with nothing breakable or with anything you could hurt yourself with, worse case scenario you fall and get a concussion...

but then again i did salvia with some people when i was camping... there was a camp fire... i didnt really announce that i was about to smoke some salvia, so when i got up no one really stopped me and then i took a step into the camp fire... someone tackled me out of the fire and i landed on a tent, and destroyed the tent... i couldn't even feel the pain of being burnt while on salvia... but when i came down i saw my foot covered in ash with 4 small 2nd degree burns on a couple of my toes and my ankle... could have been much worse, i lucked out...

so hey, if you can get a place where your not going to break anything or hurt yourself... then go for it... but most people don't have padded rooms in their house... an open grassy field would be an option... but a sitter for salvia is something i recommend, take turns with it... its one of those drugs where when you come down your asking "what the fuck just happened?" and sure your pretty sure what just happened inside your head but i like knowing what i was physically doing... from what people have told me im not the giggly sort of person on salvia, i either just sit quietly or walk around with an intent look on my face...
