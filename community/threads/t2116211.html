Re: Having a good trip on shrooms
Alcohol dulls your senses, while psylocybin heightens them. If you enjoy tripping and seeing cool things, but don't enjoy the thoughts you get, then alcohol can numb the "heady" part of mushrooms. It can also ease your body if you get physically uncomfortable during the trip.

Marijuana can make you more docile and playful on mushrooms, but can also take away from the full experience of a clean mushroom trip. Mushrooms should be enjoyed on their own.

Like others have said, all you need to do for a good trip is be excited about it, relax, and let it take its own course. Don't be around people who will try to fuck with you, and have some good music playing.
