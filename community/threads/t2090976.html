You fuckers kill me talking about ODing on weed, and 10000 joints or pounds or what the fuck have you.

Ok, look, if you little fucks are going to be doing drugs, and running around TALKING about drugs, at least try not to look retarded.

Drug overdosages are measured in what is called LD50. This means lethal dosage 50%, as in, 50% of subjects died at that dosage level.

Now, every single fucking compound on earth has an LD50, including your super skunk ak47 primo budder #1 g13 medical ganja.


Now this is measured in milligrams per kilogram. How many milligrams of compound per kilogram of the total weight of the subject.

LD50 of 1 mg/kg means that a 70 kg man has a fifty percent chance of dying on a dose of 70 mg of the mystery compound.



According to the Merck Index (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merck_Index), 12th edition, THC has a LD50 (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LD50) value of 1270 mg (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milligram)/kg (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilogram) (male rats) and 730 mg/kg (female rats) administered orally dissolved in sesame oil (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame_oil).[4] (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahydrocannabinol#_note-Erowid)
If this were scaled up to an adult human, the LD50 would be between approximately 86 g and 50 g for a 68-kg (150-lb) male or female person, respectively. This would be equivalent to 1-1.8 kg (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kg) of cannabis (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_%28drug%29) with a 5% THC content (roughly average) taken orally. The LD50 value for rats by inhalation of THC is 42 mg/kg of body weight.
