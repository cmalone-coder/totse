Running GNU/Windows Vista x64
I've been talking with Prometheum, and he's convinced me that GNU/Linux is the GNU OS (consisting of executables and libraries) on top of the Linux Kernel. Since Cygwin allows running almost all of those same executables and libraries on top of Windows, it is therefore Cygwin/Microsoft GNU/Windows (The GNU OS running on top of Windows libraries and executables, as well as the Windows kernel. In fact, through Cygwin.dll, the entire DLL layer of Windows acts as a *nix kernel, without the hardware compatibility problems or having to give up games or other paid software.)

So, GNU/Windows is pretty easy to install. It can run X11, with GNOME or KDE (last I checked), the full GNU toolsuite, and lots of additional software.

So, has anyone else had experience with GNU/Windows? I know that the GCC is the heart of this distribution, and I've used it a lot by command-line, but rarely by makefiles. Is automake worth trying? Should I try setting up a CVS repository? Should I try running KDE as my main desktop system?
