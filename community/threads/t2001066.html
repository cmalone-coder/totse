It sucks don't it. But never tel yourself that it is inevitable. And if you want to stay with her, do it, don't listen to what anyone has to say.

Just suprise her with things. If you know one day is special to her for some reason, be it to do with you or not, do something with her for it. But don't give up on it, because it's not over yet. You've got that still. If you get worried about it then ask her. Not flat out do you not want to see me, just ask if she has a good time with you, or if there's anything in particular she wants to do, or even in an intimate case is she still enjoying it? Just find out what she wants, and try and do it.

It could end in a hard crash, but if you work at it then it'll stop and things'll be back to normal. I know what you mean about it being so hard and wanting it to go back to normal, because my last girlfriend and I were together every day for a year, and then the same thing happened. But at least you've still got her, so you've got something. I've lost that even.
