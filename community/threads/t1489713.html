Re: Am I intimidated by women?
Whatever age you're at it sounds like an intimacy issue. I used to watch Paula White (the televangelist) and she described pretty much what you're describing, about being perfectly comfortable in a crowd, speaking to a million people.. but, she said "Put me in a room with someone, one-on-one and I freeze up."

Different people react differently with others and it's just part of who you are. It can be worked on and developed like any character trait but it takes determination, will and practice. And even then it may take a long time or never happen exactly how you expect.

I have to speak at board meetings occasionally, to groups of 5-15 or more people and I get nervous every single time, all the way to the point of feeling like I'm having an anxiety attack.. but then a minute into it I'm fine. I also play the guitar and have played in a band on stage in the past.. same thing, nerves on edge until we get started on that first song, then cool. Alone with a girl is the most natural thing to me, no tension, perfectly at ease.

So you're normal, some people just deal with things better (or worse). Whoever ^^^ said practice on girls (just friends) where there's no pressure on trying to pick them up had a good idea... that will help give you the confidence you need and strengthen your self esteem. Best of luck!
