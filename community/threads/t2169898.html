After This Life
Your Ultimate Tragedy or
Your Ultimate Triumph,
Which Will It Be for You?


If you are not a true, born-again believer and follower of Jesus Christ, this message is especially for you. I hope that you will take time to read all of this and give sincere thought to it. People perish continually due to a lack of knowledge. Don’t allow yourself to be one of them!

I’ll now get right to the main points. Your most extreme experiences will occur after you die. When that time comes for you, your ultimate tragedy would be to descend into the everlasting fire burning hell, a place that God prepared for the devil and his fallen angels (demons). Besides all the unspeakable horrors of an eternal existence in hell in a body that is not consumed by fire, you will be forever mindful that you are there because you foolishly rejected, or simply neglected, or were indifferent toward the personal salvation that God offered you. You would realize that you spurned and rejected God’s great and costly act of love for you. God made a way for you to be saved and become one of his children when He allowed His son Jesus to die on the cross and pay the penalty for your sins. All you had to do was to simply ask Him to come into your life and help change you into the person He wanted you to be. I'll say more about personal salvation below.

On the other hand, your ultimate triumph can be to have God’s angels to escort you into heaven when you die. There you will forever be with the Lord and other saints, redeemed from this sinful world. You will have joy unspeakable and come to understand many things that you didn’t know and wondered about while on earth. You will see things from God’s perspective. You’ll have a new, incorruptible body and be reunited with friends and loved ones who have preceded you to heaven. You might later help to greet those who follow you to heaven. You will walk the streets of gold and dwell in the mansion God has prepared for you. You will experience God’s love like never before and find great joy in giving Him your sincere worship and adoration. Eventually, you will take part in Christ’s 1000-year reign over the earth which will then be like the Garden of Eden, no longer under the curse. You will rule and reign with the Lord. Later you will dwell in the holy city New Jerusalem which will come down onto the new earth. You’ll drink of the river of life that flows from the throne of God. This is just the beginning of eternity with the heavenly Father, Jesus, all His children, and the holy angels. What will eternity be like? We don’t fully know but can only imagine. However, we are told in the Bible that “Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, the things which God hath prepared for them that love him.” (I Cor 2:9). God’s universe with all its planets, stars and galaxies appears to be larger than we can fathom. There are seemingly an infinite number of places to which we could be dispatched for God’s purposes!

So, which will it be for you – your ultimate tragedy or your ultimate triumph? It is only during this life that you can pray for and receive salvation. The Apostle Paul tells us, “... behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation” (II Cor 6:2b). The Prophet Isaiah tells us, “seek ye the Lord while He may be found, call ye upon him while he is near” (Isa 55:6). God is, “not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance” (II Pet 3:9b). He will not force you to come to Him. You must come of your own free will. As a sinner you are not eligible to enter heaven (Rev 21:27). You must be healed of the sin nature that you inherited from Adam and Eve. That is why Jesus said that you must be “born again” (John 3:3). Church membership, baptism, and good deeds have their places but none of them will make you born again.

You might not believe in God and that the Bible is His word. You might not believe that God created all things, or that Jesus Christ is the Son of God. If that is the case with you, I urge you to start reading the Bible. Begin with the book of John and continue through the New Testament, and then include Matthew, Mark, and Luke. The Bible is the living word of God and if you are really open and seeking the truth, God will speak to you through His word. Faith to believe comes from reading and meditating on God’s word. Speaking through the prophet Jeremiah, God says to us, “And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart.” (Jer 29:13).

You need to be aware that you have spiritual enemies influencing you now. They are Satan (the Devil) and his demonic beings. They hate you because you are created in the image of God (you are a triune, three-part being having spirit, soul, and body). They know that they will eventually be cast into hell; but, in their rebellion against God they want to drag down to hell as many human beings as they can. Satan has many tools of deception, temptation to sin, and discouragement that he uses against people to cause them to not accept the good news of salvation and follow Jesus Christ. Contrasting himself with Satan, Jesus said, “The thief cometh not, but for to steal, kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.” (John 10:10). All harm and evil in this world are caused by Satan.

Satan and his demons have worked through man to devise a host of different religions - all false – as traps in which to lead people away from God’s truth. However, Jesus said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man comes to the Father, but by me” (John 14:6). Jesus said, also, “... broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be that go in thereat; … and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it” (Matt 7:13b;14b).

You might not agree with what I’ve said about there being a hell or even a heaven. You could say that is just my opinion and you and many others have different opinions. Yes, each of us has the right to his/her own opinion but not his/her own truth. Why? Because truth is universal! There is only one body of truth. The body of knowledge we call truth transcends all opinions, all viewpoints, all claims, all cultures, and all other bodies of knowledge. God is the source of all truth and it transcends the claims of all religions and the opinions of each of us.

Jesus Christ, the Son of God, is described in John 1:14 as being “full of grace and truth” and in verse 17 as the source of “grace and truth”. Remember, Jesus said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me” (John 14:6). Through the Word of God – the Bible - you can, as Jesus said, “know the truth, and the truth shall make you free” (John 8:32). This means freedom from the bondage of a sinful life, and most of all, freedom from the eventual consequence of sin which is eternal separation from God in hell, a person’s ultimate tragedy. The Apostle Paul tells us, “For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.” (Rom 6:23).

Your future here on earth and throughout eternity depends on what you do with the truth, i.e. what you do with Jesus. Only through Jesus Christ can you receive the gift of eternal life. In John 3:16 –17 Jesus said, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.” Jesus is ready and waiting to receive you now, to give you a new and abundant life in him. He will not reject anyone who comes to him (John 6:37). Come to him now, just as you are. Other than being willing to give your life to Christ and let him become Lord of your life, there is no preparation you have to make to receive Christ and become one of his children (John 1:12). A simple prayer that you can pray now to receive Christ is as follows.

Lord Jesus, I need You. Thank you for dying on the cross to save me from my sins. I repent and turn away from sin and receive you as my Savior and Lord. I surrender my life to you. Thank you for forgiving my sins and giving me eternal life. Guide me throughout the rest of my life and help me become the person you want me to be.

Having sincerely prayed this prayer, you can be assured that you are born again (saved) and have eternal life. In John 6:47 Jesus said, “Verily, verily, I say unto you, he that believeth on me hath everlasting life.” Start your new life in Christ by reading and studying the Bible (start with the Gospel of John). Also, start fellowshipping with other believers and attend a good Bible believing church. Set aside a period of time each day to pray and read God’s Word. Ask the Lord to fill you with His Holy Spirit. As the Spirit leads you, share your faith with others. Trust the Lord to guide you throughout your life and help you to grow in Him. When you sin (still being mortal, we don’t become perfect after receiving Christ) confess it to the Lord and ask for His forgiveness (I John 1:9). He will not allow you to be tempted above what you can bear (I Cor 10:13).

You will never be more successful in life, with eternity in view, than what you can do and become by following Christ daily as the Lord of your life. In Matthew 16:26 Jesus said, “For what is a man profited if he gains the whole world, and loses his own soul? Or what will a man give in exchange for his soul?” Also, He said in John 10:10b, “I have come that they [us] may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly.”

Now you can make the rest of your life and the time of your death your greatest and ultimate triumph, not your ultimate tragedy! Open the door of your life to Christ now by sincerely praying the prayer shown above. In Revelation 3:20 NKJV, Jesus said, “Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come into him and dine with him, and he with me.” This means that through the Holy Spirit He will come into you and you will have ongoing fellowship with Him.
