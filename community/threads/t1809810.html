Most people do do good things at least some of the time. Yes, I'm guilty of a lot of what you listed. And those are all bad things. But not every moment of my life is a selfish endeavor.

As far as why people do good things, here are a few reasons I once read:

Divine reward and punishment.

I don't personally subscribe to this theory, but it's a driving factor in most people's lives. What will get me in good with my god? And that's what you do.

Because good people are happy.

This has more credibility for me. It's a selfish reason, but so is divine reward. I do enjoy knowing that I've done something good. There's a comfort that comes in knowing that I've taken a step toward not being the worst person in the world.

Because it is natural to be good.

For most people, it's not. That's just part of the list I read, so I included it.

Because it is rational to be good.

That's a big part of it. No one has a logical reason to do something wrong. Doing right makes sense. There are forces within society and within ourselves that lead us away from doing rational good acts, though.

And society is a huge factor. We want to be accepted. (Of course, this leads to people doing bad things as well.) We want to be viewed as good people and be invited to cocktail parties.

And there's fear. Sometimes we do good because we're scared to do something bad. It's not the best motivation. Anything that keeps people performing good acts, though, short of being forced, is beneficial.
