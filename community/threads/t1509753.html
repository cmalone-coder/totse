Anyone deal with close death lately?
My best friend is dead.

Life is not real.

Sunday morning he was in a car that crashed into a tree. No alcohol, just wet roads and speed. He wasn't the driver. He was half a mile away from home. He was 20 and will always be 20.

I really don't know what to do from here. I can barely smile for 3 seconds before I think of him and I'm sad again. I miss him. I can't believe he's gone.

I think of all the songs he'll never hear. I don't know if I'm just being indulgently selfish or what, but it's hard not to be sad when such an amazing person just.. ceases to be.

It was such a freak accident. So not expected. I guess it's better he's dead than paralyzed or retarded or something horrible like that, but what an alternative.

I spent a lot of time with him. I've known him for almost 5 years and we became as close as friends could be. He liked me. His parents keep reminding me that I'm the only girl he was ever serious about. His 3 closest guy friends tell me "He really liked you. He loved you."

It's hard to let go of someone I loved so much. Someone I developed with. Someone who helped make me, me. It's unbelievable. It's like a part of me has died with him. The memories I've forgotten, they're gone with him.

I know death is a part of life. I even like to believe that death isn't the end, like some do. But the notion that I will never get to sit on his back porch and smoke a blunt talking about high school and whatever's going on, is hard.

I don't like many people. People just don't regularly impress me or intrigue me enough for me to want to be friends with them. Danny was one of the few.

I've never been so sad in my life. I have no idea to cope with this. I spend a lot of time at the tree, there are tons of balloons and flowers, beer bottles, liquor bottles, pictures, posters. I just look at his face n this one picture. I sat there till 1 in the morning last night.

New memories come up here and there. I often think that inevitable, "what if" question. His little cousins are as funny as he was. He said better than I could, "Who decided to plant a tree here?!" Hahahahaa...

His viewing was yesterday. That was horrible. His body looked nothing like him. I thought I'd have some solace, but it was so bad I had to leave. I came back and knelt by his coffin with his parents. I held his hand and the first thing I noticed was how I recognized his hands. I know his hands, we were sexually involved for a few months in 2005, so we share intimacy on a whole other level. His hands we cold, his skin was hard. He was hard. His hand was heavy, not the warm embracing hand I remember.

He has short hair, religiously cut it once a week. I was running my hand across his hair and I realized half his head was wax. The right side of his hair was spray painted on. His lips we fucked up. His beautiful long eyelashes we hidden behind swollen lids.

And his neck...

I can't believe it. Sorry if this is a little graphic, but this has been very traumatizing.

Today was his funeral, we all met up at various places around town. The church happened to be down the same street where he lived and where me met up. The cars all going the same speed, I know what the occupants are thinking. It was overwhelming as this Red Hot Chili Peppers song played and the steeple of the church came over the houses and trees.

The world isn't the same. Sunny days aren't so sunny. The trees aren't so pretty.

I suppose time will heal, but this is a bad part of my life. It's hard to wake up every morning and think about him. To keep his myspace on my top8, to keep his number in my phone. It might sound petty to you, it's fake to me.
