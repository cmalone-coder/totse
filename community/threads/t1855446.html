Swirh does not know if this is novel (prob not since most everything has been thought of or tried-and Swirh does no claim to new technique) but I have not seen it anywhere necessarily verbatim. Anyhow, Swirh uses about half gallon of denat per 1000 strips. Using drill and paint stirrer (that is from another tech) agitate until strips are clean. Remove strips (enjoy the woozy head if one does not use proper hand protection). Swirh has found that a good squeeze of strips (as opposed to rinsing small amounts of them in a strainer) does about an effective job of removing excess RP.

So now Swirh has bucket with half gallon of bloody denat. He then takes some distilled ice (ummm, you know storebought ice is distilled) about 3-4 of the glass two-cup measuring cups (so about 8 cups or so) and dumps into the mix. Then take very cold DH2O and pour about 1/2 gallon in there. Stir slightly with a big spoon and wait. Red foam should pop to the top and just take afore mentioned spoon and rake foam to the side (letting all the liquid run out of spoon) and transfer the foam to a vessel of DH2O. The level should not be so great as when a full boil is acheived that it comes over the top. Swirh uses a Vision to see all the action. Boil for couple or five minutes (good rolling boil). Remove from heat and pour everything through a coffee filter. Squeeze excess water out and repeat.

Most of the paper pulp can be decanted once one gets down to the bottom of the pot (it looks like a big white wad of snot). Repeat boiling until the DH2O looks clear after a minute of so of settling. If cloudy keep on cleaning. Usually takes about two boils but that is dependant upon the amount of water being used.

The remaining denat/DH2O/RP blend can be filtered (decanted the grey liquid after it settles) and one can remove some more super-gluey imbedded RP that will take considerable more time and attention to clean. Like a highschool crackwhore with a bad yeast infection. But still--waste not...

After final boiling and letting the ball (should be crumbly) of RP dry somewhat; then screen thru a strainer (1.00 variety). If any big pieces of paper are in the ball; they will ball up in strainer and prob not go through. So, that is one simple minded person's view of things. Hope it helps (or at least gives a different spin on things).
