Re: Favorite Pipe/Bowl Cleaner?
Paper clip with a lighter (it helps if you have a BiC), you can stick the end of the paper clip in one of the two vents on the back like 1/8th of an inch, and bend it to a 90 degree angle.

Then you heat the pipe and the paper clip up with the lighter, don't heat the pipe too much, if it starts to smoke you heated it too much. You're just trying to make the resin melt down a little, and the heated paper clip helps loosen any stubborn pieces.

You just scrape it all onto a small folded piece of tin foil shaped like a V (make sure you don't wrinkle it up), and the funnel it all into a waterfall bong cap. This is ideal because you can poke the holes just above where the resin is, and create a little bowl for the resin to sit and bubble/boil in. It won't boil through and you'll trap all the smoke in the bottle. This is better than a waterfall because given that you have the right size of hole in the bottom of the bottle you can burn all of the resin down to a brown ashy substance (which means its totally gone).

The only thing about it is... it tastes nasty. Not as nasty as cigarettes, but still pretty bad. Its pretty much like what you'd think inhaling burning tar would feel like.
