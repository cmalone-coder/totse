It all actually has to do with calculus and infinite series of numbers.

.9999999999... = .9 +.09+.009+.0009+.00009+....

.9 = .9*10^-0
.09 = .9*10^-1
.009 = .9*10^-2
...etc...
.9*10^-inf

so when we write it using a sigma (I'll try):
.9* [sigma from n=0 to infinity] 10^-n

10^-n is the same as (1/10^n) which is (1/10)^n
so now it's a geometric series.... this is where calculus is used
where a=.9 and r=1/10

.9/(1-1/10)=1

It makes about as much sense as two missiles turning into a whale and a pot of flowers, but it's a mathematical reality...

It's the same kind of thing which proves that e^(i[pi])=-1 freaky stuff, isn't it?

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_series_of_nines
