Re: Looking for a small pet...
Chinchillas can be cool. They need a dust bath once a week, they eat prepackaged chinchilla food, and they can't really handle temperatures above eighty degrees fahrenheit, so you need to keep them in an air conditioned room in the summer. They're not much for sitting still, so if you're looking for a pet to cuddle or whatever, go with something else. They are fun as hell to watch though. Also, they'll typically run you $80-$150, plus another $50-$100 for a good tall cage.

Ferrets are a lot more work. Think of them like getting a dog. They cost aorund $100, need about $200 worth of equipment to get started, require distemper shots once a year, and typically require a cage mate because they're very social. They're absurd amounts of fun though. They can stink if they haven't had their musk glands removed, but otherwise they can be litterbox trained, so their shit isn't that big a deal.

Personally, I'd recommend a pair of rats. They're about three bucks each(from the feeder bins at a pet store), require minimal care, and make great, great pets. You'd want to get a pair, because they're very social like the ferrets. Obviously get two males or two females, unless you want a shitload of babies. They're pretty easy to sex once they're weaned, males have big ass balls hangin under their asses, females don't. They liked a good sized cage, with vertical room, so don't stick them in some aquarium. In fact, they'd be very happy with the cage you'd have gotten for the chinchilla. They're very, very smart and as long as you spend some time with them they'll grow to recognize you by voice, smell, and appearance. Make sure you get them as young as possible(right after weaning). Since you're most likely going to get them from the feeder bin, you should check to make sure they're healthy. Their noses and eyes should be clear, their breathing should be strong and steady, without any bronchidal-type noise in their chest, and they should be active and curious.

As far as other small animals go, hamsters are fucking evil, gerbils are cool, but as hyper as the chinchilla and dumb as rocks, guinea pigs and rabbits are cute, sedentary, and boring as all hell, degus are like giant, slightly more intelligent gerbils, and hedgehogs are like prickly, high-maintenance guinea pigs.

This is all coming from years of experience in the pet store business, so I may come off as slightly cynical.
