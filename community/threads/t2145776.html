how to mess with myspace groups(such as delete stuff when you're not a member)
you still need to be a member of myspace to do this, but you don't need to be a member of the groups you are targetting.

some of these may need the firebox browser to work.


view messages from a private group
at the end of the equal sign, put the target groups ID number
http://forum.myspace.com/index.cfm?f...egory&groupID=

if you aren't sure of where to find a category that has a private group, try these two. both of them have

many private groups.
Gay/Lesbian groups
http://searchresults.myspace.com/ind...&categoryID=15
pets and animals
http://searchresults.myspace.com/ind...&categoryID=24



view videos from a private profile
at the end of the equal sign, put the target person's myspace ID number.
http://vids.myspace.com/index.cfm?fu...vids&friendID=
http://vids.myspace.com/index.cfm?fu...nel&channelid=

delete bulletins from a group that you don't control
1. create a group, call it whatever.
2. copy and paste your group id somewhere, to a file, whatever.
3. find a group that has bulletins. click on a bulletin that you want to delete.
4. now on the URL bar replace the group ID with the ID of your group. refresh the page.
5. now you should see a button on the lower right, 'delete'.
6. now go back to the original group and notice the bulletin is gone. lol.


instructions for stickying and unstickying topics(pin and unpin). you can do this even if you
are not a member of the group
1. login to your myspace account. create a group, call it whatever you want.
2. create two topics, call them whatever you want.
3. PIN(sticky) one of the topics. now, point the mouse on top of the PIN button, then right click the mouse and choose 'add to favorites'. now point the mouse on top of the UNPIN button, then right click the mouse and choose 'add to favorites'
4. now open another window, search for a group with a pinned/sticky topic that you want to unsticky. click on that topic. now get the thread ID(the number after viewthread&entry) from the URL bar(copy, it , save it somewhere, whatever). now go to your favorites, and RIGHT CLICK the 'pin(sticky this topic(or unsticky, if you want to unsticky something))' thing you just saved in your favorites a minute ago, then choose properties. replace the number in there with the thread ID of the group i asked you to save, then resave it to your favorites.
5. open another window, and go to YOUR group that i asked you to create in the beginning. go to your list of all topics. go to your favorites menu and choose either the pin or unpin link depending on whether or not you're trying to pin or unpin the topic. the page will reload. now go to the list of topics for the target group, and notice that your choose topic will either be stickied or unstickied depending on what you did..



how to post in a group that you are not a member of or a group that you are banned from.
1. first, create your own myspace group if you haven't already.
2.copy your group ID # and save it somewhere.
3.go to a thread in the group you want to post in.
4. in the URL bar change the group id to the ID of your group, then push enter. then you will be able to post in that thread.



view videos from a private profile
(put the user id of the person you are targetting after the equals sign)
http://vids.myspace.com/index.cfm?fu...vids&friendID=
http://vids.myspace.com/index.cfm?fu...nel&channelid=
