Left 4 Dead Review. It sucks.
So I downloaded a pirated version of L4D that allows me to play online (with other steam players, and yes this is possible), after playing the demo. The demo was very upsetting but I thought to myself, hey, it's just a demo..

I went ahead and installed/played the full version. What the fuck? This game could have been made by a small third party developer team within 6 months, and they're asking for 50/60 dollars for this piece of shit? Wow. The game really has nothing unique or amazing about it, just shoot zombies and move on, shoot more, move on, oh shit a fat one, ok dead, move on. The zombie AI is horrible, and the trailers were incredibly misleading. Playing online with close friends can be fun for 30 mins, then you get the vibe that everyone is as bored as you are, because they are. IMO the zombie mod for counter-strike source is 10x+ better than this game. Not to mention there's no real story line to this game (I know it's supposed to be more multiplayer oriented but they could have tried a little harder.)

One thing I will admit is that I can see this game having extremely good potential, while tripping with other friends/lan, and I have yet to try that.

Re: Left 4 Dead Review. It sucks.
L4D is a pretty decent game. Even though the trailers were deceiving along with some other things.

Weapons -

It says there are 10 weapons in the game , but the dualies and pistols are counted separate. They also consider the propane and gas tank weapons ...

I was really expecting more guns and much more diversity within the game as it was portrayed.

Content -

For the wait , it sure is lacking a lot in this area. The campaigns are well designed but they are incredibly short and get boring after awhile. Playing with friends will no doubt make this experience better but still there should be many more campaigns

Specials -

I like the idea of this just for the reason it makes the game a lot harder and enforces team work. Although there should be actual "bosses" instead of the same shit running around.

Bugs -

There aren't too many bugs that I have found , mainly for me the game has problems connecting to servers. Also many servers that my group and I get connected to have very bad ping and make it nearly impossible to play on.

Future Development -

Valve will obviously be planning on creating more extreme gameplay and maybe an actual story to go along with the game. I've been hearing of new weapons (such as a flamethrower) and different game modes.

L4D was definitely rushed , but has a lot of potential to become a very popular game. Since it did just come out , I am very impressed by the AI and general gameplay so far.

My 2 cents :)
