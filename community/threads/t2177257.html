Manhunt - i was going to give it a thread, but i'll just mention it here. david simon (homicide, the wire/corner, generation kill) reunites with homicide buddy tom fontana for another mini with HBO. Named after the book it's based on, it's about the chase for john wilkes booth after lincolns assassination. it's a lock for mini series of the year.
http://tinyurl.com/4qcxpe

Last of the Ninth - "tv's maverick genius" david milch (hill street blues, NYPD blue, deadwood) is working on a new cop show that takes place during the early 70's amid the knapp commission hearings. i'm personally looking forward to this more than anything else, as milch is one of the reasons i watch tv for a living. http://tinyurl.com/5tcdf8

The Pacific - here's the big one. while not technically related, this is the pacific theater version of band o' brothers. it's produced by hanks and spielberg, headed by all the b.o.b folks and has a $200 million dollar budget. plus ordelani from oz is gonna star. this will be, to say the least, fucking epic. http://tinyurl.com/6om766

Boardwalk Empire - Scorsese is teaming with marky mark's production company (in treatment, entourage) for a period piece about the rise of atlantic city in the roaring twenties. they tapped terence winter, who you (doubtfully) know as the no. 2 guy behind the sopranos, to write it. details are sparse, but it should fill the void we've all been feeling since the tony and co. faded to black. http://tinyurl.com/6742ew

The Goode Family - you just knew Mike Judge had something cooking, and here it is, a new animated series for ABC. the goodes are basically the anti-hills: super liberal, obsessively politically correct, etc. http://tinyurl.com/58rfc4

Class Dismissed/Sit Down, Shut Up - based on australia's sit down shut up, this is a new fox cartoonfeaturing many arrested development alumni, such as creator mitch hurwitz, jason bateman, and will arnett. it'll have animation over live action backdrop. should be interesting. http://tinyurl.com/6fwh5m

The Cartoonstitute - a lot like Cartoon Cartoon, this will basically just be an environment for cartoonists to experiment and create new characters and stories. bravo, i say. http://tinyurl.com/63du7c

Them - if it's sci fi, i'll watch, especially if it's on tv. such is the case with Them, an upcoming fox sci fi drama about a sleeper cell of aliens living in L.A.
