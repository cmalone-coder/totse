Re: A present from me to you: Rocket Simulation software
This program is amazing. There are simply too many features to cover in one post on totse, so I'll only mention some of my favorites.

Firstly, the ability to design schematics and 3D models of your rocket is greatly useful. All schematics can be scaled and printed as templates for machining. The 3D construction allows drag calculations to be simulated before a physical model is ever made.

Secondly, the ability to chose construction materials and engines allows you to calculate the total weight of the rocket, as well as that of individual segments.

Lastly, I'd like to bring attention to the simulation feature. It's simply mind blowing, try playing around with the weight of your rocket and see how it affects flight dynamics.
