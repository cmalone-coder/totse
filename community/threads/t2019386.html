Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Re: Proper amount for a good detonation
I hate that feeling. Technically drying AP shouldn't be so bad because it's in no way hygroscopic, but I almost always let it dry at least several hours. On newspaper.

The alternative is of course taking it dry. If you choose this way, be sure to divide it up into smaller amounts and store it somewhere heavily padded.

Advice for the future: Make some secondaries. That's where the real fun of HE lies, and even if you can't find a better primary you only need to make small (<5g) batches. A 1g cap works well in many situations, if not there's always making a train.
