Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Yeah, I ball mill it, but I never seen any magnesium foil, so if anyone sees any please tell me!

It just depends on what my magnesium source is. Sometimes I will cut short strips of magnesium ribbon up, and ball mill that for a few weeks.

If it is a firestarter bar, I either cut it with my knife, and ball mill the shavings, or I will hammer the bar down until it is about 1/8 inch thick, and use those really big metal cutters to cut it into small sections, and ball mill that. Remember when ball milling metals to open up the ball mill every 6-24 hours to prevent them from becoming pyrophoic and possible igniting when it is opened after a while.
