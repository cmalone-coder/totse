Re: Worst news ever.
I don't want to sound capricious, but is there any chance that she wanted to break up and is using this concussion thing as an excuse ("right, who are you again?").

If not, and by the sounds of your relationship then probably not, man it sounds like she had a serious concussion. Even the depression fits in with post traumatic syndrome.

In general, the severity of the injury is guaged by the amount of memory loss of the events that occured after the concussion. I'd say that not knowing who you were is a pretty severe indicator.

Other symptoms of the post-tramatic stress syndrome are irritability, inability to concentrate or cope with simple things and a persistent headache. People usually gradually improve after, even up to 2 years later.

Just make sure that if there's a big personality change, fuzzy thinking or bad balance that you encourage her parents to get her head scanned for the possibility of a small bleed inside the skull. Not a big deal, just important to know about.
