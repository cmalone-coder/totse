Re: First time psychs
Luckily you are planning on taking them for all the right reasons, however i would not start off with oral DMT, from what i've read, ayahuasca can be extremely overwhelming even for the most experienced tripper.

I would personally start off with mushrooms, don't be afraid to take 3.5 grams your first time, this will give you a good idea of what the world of psychedelics is like. I've found that mushrooms are much easier to handle than LSD, they feel much more natural to me at least. As well as if the trip is going badly you will start coming down sooner.

I would say for your reasons of taking them you should do them alone to be the most fulfilled, though I would make sure you have someone to call that you trust in case things start going to shit when your on them.

Also, no matter what you've read about any of these drugs, you have no idea what they will be like until you are peaking on them. I might also recommend trying marijuana before the pyschs just because its kind of an introduction for the highs that LSD and mushrooms produce.

Anyway good luck, i'll try to respond to this thread often if you have questions etc.

My first mushroom trip that wasn't a complete wreck (don't ever do shit like that at school) involved my friend and I sitting on my other friends basement floor and it was the best trip I've ever had on mushrooms. I find mushrooms to be much more intense and unmanageable than LSD or mescaline. I don't mean it in a negative way, I just found them to be much more intoxicating and intense than acid. Then again, I ate the equivalent of somewhere between 4g and 6g my first and only experience with them.

Mescaline = peace, at least so far as my 30+ trips have informed me.

I'd seriously suggest mescaline for an introduction to psychoactives, including before marijuana, provided that you're ready to set aside the sixteen hours for the trip. But marijuana is another good starter, I'd recommend using it before mushrooms or LSD at the very least.
