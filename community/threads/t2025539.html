An 8086 that my father built. I used it to type papers (in elementary school) with Word Perfect and play "F-15 Strike Eagle". In the late 80's/early 90's, my father did some work for Tandy (now defunct branch of Radio Shack), and we got a few 386 & 486 server machines. I got a 486 DX2/50 w/ SCSI hard drive fitted out w/ 16 1MB SIMM's, and I got to keep it in my own room! It played "Wing Commander 2" like a champ!

Edit:
By the way-- I still have and use two of the keyboards from those Tandy machines-- best keyboards I ever used, really well made with great key feel and no fucking Windows or "multimedia" keys.
