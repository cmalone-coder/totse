Tilda brand tikka masala sauce. it was the only decent one from a jar and is no longer available in australia :( I even emailed the distributor asking if it would be back any time soon.

Pop tarts. That's right. You can only get them at specialty 'us foods' stores in Australia now. I liked the cinnamon ones.


So sad.
