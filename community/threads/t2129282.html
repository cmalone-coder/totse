Re: How has Windows Vista ruined your dreams?
There are plenty of XP cd's you can torrent.

The good thing about Vista is that it has a tool where you can actually lower the Partitioned space you allocated in the beginning of your installation.

That means that if you decide you want to dual boot with XP, you can just lower Vista, then install it on the free space on your HDD.

XP doesn't have that tool. You would have to actually reformat everything and create 2 partitions to dual boot another OS.

My brother is in the process of doing this to his PC so he can run FFXI on the old Graphics Card Driver for XP, then run higher graphics games like TF2, Crysis, COD4 on the newer NVIDIA/Vista Graphics Drivers.
