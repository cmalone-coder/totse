As far as reputation goes, I think that having non-anonymous reputation would make people less likely to give legitimate bad rep, for fear of just getting down-repped in return. It's only really an issue in SG, I don't think people elsewhere are using it just as a way to insult people much. And the SG posters complaining about getting a lot of negative rep with anonymous comments like "you're a cunt" are the ones who flame and bitch all the time. I personally think it's good as it is, despite the few people just using it on posters they don't like for no reason.

With infractions, I think the points for spamming are fine as they are, but I also think you're giving them out too readily. A single thread that doesn't belong in the forum doesn't seem like spam; if you feel like you're giving out too many infraction points, maybe try posting a warning and explanation in the first thread and just closing it, and infracting for re-posts and "OMG MY THRED IS LOKD NAZI META" threads?

Hack or deface seems like it should be worth more points (although it's useful as a fairly generic 'warning' infraction, being 1 point), but the multiple accounts one seems fine as it is, since you can infract every post made by that account. Two posts on a multiple account isn't much of a problem, but ten would be more punishable.
