Most of those sound more like things that are those in which the outcome is up to you, whereas Murphy's law are those things that are totally beyond the influence of mere mortals.

Example:

Every time I get an unexpected windfall [hell, even just get caught up with my bills], something comes around to take it [or put me back into debt]. Like when I got a bonus at work for around $200: within the same week I got a notice out-of-the-blue of some unpaid fine that had to be paid right then - or a warrant would be issued.

That is what Murphy was on about [and he was an optimist.
