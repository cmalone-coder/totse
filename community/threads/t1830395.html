Re: Making Flash Powder
If you're persistent on making smaller salutes, there are plenty of tubes lying around the house for use.

Find old dry cleaning coathangers with the cardboard tube, or empty saturn missle battery cakes. Be creative.

If you don't have any suitable tubes at your house, you can head down to the hardware store and buy a wooden dowel of your desired ID to roll your own tubes.

Simply cut up paper bags to the proper width and length, apply kraft glue, and roll to the desired thickness.

Allow the tubes to dry slowly in the house to avoid warping.

If you're too lazy for that, then you can simply add less 70/30 flash into your tubes.

Don't worry about the small amounts not igniting in the tube, the inherent 'side split' burn of american visco will ignite anything in the tube.

A firestarter that weighs two pounds!? Where did you get yours, the ones I got from wal mart only weight about 35 grams.

Magnesium firestarters are pure magnesium, except for that peice of flint on them. I just take a hammer and beat down on it until it is thin enough to cut into small peice with heavy duty wire cutters. After thy are cut into small peices, just ball mill it for 2 or 3 weeks, making sure to open the ball mill every 12 hours to keep the magnesium from becoming pyrophoric and igniting when you first open the ball mill. After that you should have a high mesh magnesium powder.
