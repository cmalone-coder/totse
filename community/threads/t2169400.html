Yeah you have typical social anxiety. Should get help one way or another.

The doctors in America seem to hand out benzos all the time, but here in the UK they are only given short-term in most cases. Probably says something about your private health service and pharmaceutical corporations.

To be honest there isn't a problem-free chemical solution for your problem -- it's a choice of either getting rid of the anxiety and dealing with addiction, memory-loss, lack of inhibitions, etc., or taking an SSRI that is arguably addictive too and isn't likely to be the sort of solution you look for in a drug (i.e. you need to put a lot of effort in yourself in therapy type stuff).

If you have any plans of completing education, benzos aren't the answer. You won't really learn while you're on 'em. That said you might want to keep a few around for recreational/"as-needed" purposes.

If you don't like the idea of SSRIs, there are some drugs you can find on the internet that could help. Kava, 5-HTP, selegiline, GHB, and more. Obviously, some of those are addictive too.
