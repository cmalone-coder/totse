The ultimate tramadol potency thread!!
Tramadol is an interesting drug, its main metabolite, O-Desmethyl Tramadol, is responsible for the majority of the effects, being it is many times stronger than tramadol itself. One way to increase the potency of tramadol is to INDUCE the CYP2D6 enzyme, unlike most opiates, in which you want to inhibit the CYP2D6 enzyme. Unfortunately, inducing the CYP2D6 enzyme, which we want to do is in the case of tramadol, requires only a few specific drugs, which are very hard to come by. Also, by inducing the CYP2D6 enzyme, the overall duration of the tramadol will be shortened, which is a step backwards. Additionally, most medical professionals will tell you that it is very hard to induce the CYP2D6 enzyme. A full list of CYP2D6 inducers is available here:

http://www.uspharmacist.com/oldformat.asp?url=newlook/files/Feat/druginteractions.cfm&pub_id=8&article_id=704

See Table 2

So what am I saying, its very hard if not impossible to increase the potency of tramadol? No, of course not. We have another option. What we can do, looking at the same link as above, referring to Table 1, is INHIBIT the CYP3A4 enzyme. But why would we want to do this? Simple. O-Desmethyl Tramadol is broken down by the CYP3A4 enzyme, so by taking CYP3A4 INHIBITORS, we can extend the period that O-Desmethyl Tramadol is active in our body. CYP3A4 inhibitors are much easier to come by, specifically two of them. The first is grapefruit juice which contains (6, 7 -dihydroxybergamottin) as well as other flavonoids, especially bergapten, which inhibited CYP3A4 activity by 67%, quercetin, which inhibited CYP3A4 activity by 55%, and finally naringenin which inhibited CYP3A4 activity by 39%. So a few large glasses of 100% white grapefruit juice is the best way to inhibit the CYP3A4 enzyme, and is the best way to increase the potency of tramadol.

http://www.ualberta.ca/~csps/JPPS4(3)/S.Wanwimolruk/grapefruit.htm

Moreover, quinine is an inhibitor of the CYP3A4 enzyme, and a one liter bottle is available for under one US dollar. Unfortunately, I do not have the percentages for the amount that quinine inhibits the CYP3A4 enzyme, but it certainly does. That information can be found by following the first link and referencing Table 1. It may be more effective to take straight quinine, but for those with limited access to it, the tonic water is an easy solution. Unfortunately, the amount of quinine in each bottle is not listed, so the amount you must take is unknown to me.

In summary, Grapefruit juice and quinine are CYP3A4 INHIBITORS, so they can be taken to extend the effects of tramadol. Grapefruit juice is favored, but it MUST be 100% white grapefruit juice to work. A bottle can be purchase for under four US dollars. Also, quinine INHIBITS the CYP3A4 enzyme, and is a cheaper, but less effective way to increase the potency of tramadol.

On a personal note, I will only smoke cigarettes while on tramadol, as I find it increases the effects for a few moments, but of course nothing like I mentioned above. Also, during the last two or three hours of the tramadol experience, I feel two to three drinks MAXIMUM really does a huge part in adding to the experience. It is important to remember that you want to add to the experience, not over-power it. This may be because ethanol has been suggested to induce the CYP2D6 enzyme, but this a fairly uncommon belief. I feel a little alcohol just mixes well with tramadol, and does not increase the potency.

I hope this helps anyone interested with tramadol and any information on the exact amounts of grapefruit juice or quinine needed to produce the best effects would be a great addition to this thread. I'll research more myself, but 3 or 4 big glasses, one before dosing, and a few more during the experience will help quite a bit. I also would suggest dosing as follows: 150mg tramadol, wait 4 hours, dose an additional 50mg, wait 3 more hours, dose final 50mg, totaling 250mg, along with the grapefruit juice before and throughout the experience.

Hope this will help someone, and keep inhibiting that CYP3A4 enzyme!!
