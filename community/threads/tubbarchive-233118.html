The Jedi Knights are central to everything. They are an ancient order of royalist sorcerors that are vigilent about keeping their secrets unknown and a corrupt Galactic Republic in power.

The Republic claimed it was about freedom and democracy and self-determination, yet it resorted to force to keep systems from peacefully leaving the organization. So, a group of systems had to band together and fight the Republic, and it's evil ways.

This group was known as the Confederacy of Independant Systems, or CIS.

The CIS fought a heroic war of independance against the tyrannical Galactic Republic for several years before eventually yielding to the Republic's relentless military forces.

The Jedi Knights were instrumental to all of this, helping along the forces of evil and oppression while they filled their own coffers through shameless war profiteering.

However, the Chancellor of the Republic, who happened to be the Senator from Naboo mentioned earlier, saw through their lies and deceit. He used the Grand Army of the Republic to finally arrest and annihilate the Jedi Order, bringing down their thousands of years of royalist tyranny.

The heroic Chancellor himself was badly mauled in fighting with the Jedi, and this led to his disfigurement.

To insure that the people of the galaxy would no longer have to live in fear of the Jedi anytime in the future, Chancellor Palpatine created the Galactic Empire, a government free of the petty squables of the Old Republic, and secure from power-hungry Jedi.

Despite Emperor Palpatine's heroic efforts, the Jedi were not completely destroyed. In fact, several of them lived on. These Jedi manipulated the weak minded into forming a group known as the Rebel Alliance, a terrorist group dedicated to the sole cause of creating chaos and death in the galaxy.

In fact, the Empire has been waging a war against this group ever since. However, things have been difficult.

The Rebels are not above thievery, extortion, threats and even murder to achieve their ends. Reports even indicate that their leader, one Mon Mothma, even engages in cannibalism and infanticide on those that refuse to back her cause.

*deep breath*

Episode 1-

A planet named Naboo was blockaded by Trade Federation ships. The people of Naboo asked the Republic for help, so they send two Jedi (Obi-Wan Kenobi and Qui-Gon Jinn). But a dark Jedi (also known as a Sith) is controlling the Leader of the Trade Federation (the Viceroy). He, who we all later are told is Darth Sidious, tells the Viceroy to kill the two Jedi. They kill a lot of droids and make it to the surface of the planet, where they decide to warn the queen of the planet of the invasion. (they go to the Gungans for help but the leader, Boss Nass, says no). They get to the queen (Padme Amidala) and get her off the planet (this is where we meet R2-D2, an astromec droid). Unfortunately their ship was hit and they have to land for repairs.

They go to Tatoonie because it is controlled by the Hutts (like Jabba if you have seen episode 6). They don't have money for the spare part they need, so a kid they met (Anakin Skywalker) suggests they enter the Mos Espa pod race to win some money (he has already begun to flirt with Padme; we meet C3-PO, a protocol droid Anakin built to help his mom out; his mom and him are slaves to the guy (Wato) who owns the parts the Jedi need). Anakin just so happens to have been building a pod racer in his backyard. Gui-Gon Jinn 'convinces' Wato to free the boy if he wins. Anakin wins the race, goes off with the Jedi to become a Jedi (Gui-Gon believes this is a child of a Jedi prophecy, will bring balance to the force, was conceived by the force (no dad was involved in producing this kid).

They go to Corescant (entire planet covered in one city, home to the Jedi temple, Republic Senate, the so called heart of the galaxy) where the queen makes her plea to the Senate. They don't care all that much. A senator from Naboo (Palpatine, aka Darth Sidious) becomes leader of the senate (Chancellor). The queen and the Jedi go back to Naboo and take back control. During the fighting Qui-Gon Jinn is killed by Sidious' apprentice, Darth Maul. He is killed by Qui-Gon's apprentice (aka Padawan) Obi-Wan Kenobi.

He begins to teach Anakin the ways of the force, because Qui-Gon wanted it, even though the Jedi council (Yoda is there) are uneasy about it.

*didn't realize so much happened in one movie*

Episode 2-

10 years after Episode 1. Amidala is now a Senator. Someone is trying to kill her, because she is the head of some council that is bad for the CIS. Obi-Wan and Anakin go and protect her (more flirting on Anakin's part). Qui-Gon goes after a bounty hunter named Jango Fett after another attempt on Amidala's life. Found him on Kamino, where clones of Fett have been growing for 10 years. The clones are an army for the Republic, but the Jedi council nor the Senate asked for them (some Jedi did). The Jedi council wants to speak to Fett. He runs (because he was hired by a Jedi turned Sith, Dooku, who is Sidious' new apprentice (even though he is ancient)) to Geonosis (with his clone son Boba) where Dooku and the leaders of the CIS are.

Obi-Wan is captured. Anakin and Padme stop flirting on Naboo and go to Tatoonie to rescue Anakin's mother (who was sold, freed, then married). She dies in Anakin's arms; he goes mad and destroys the entire Tusken Raider camp (children + females). He is upset about not being strong enough to save his mother (this is where we see where Luke gets his attitude about hardships from). They gets Obi-Wan's distress call, sent it to Corescant and go and save him.

They get captured in the new droid army factories. Before they are killed, Padme confesses her love for Anakin. Anakin, Obi-Wan and Padme free themselves and some Jedi Knights come to rescue them. Here Jango Fett dies because of a Jedi named Mace Windo. They are all about to die when the Clone Army and Master Yoda come and rescue them. Anakin and Obi-Wan go after Dooku, but lose (Anakin gets his arm chopped off by Dooku's lightsaber). Yoda comes in and kicks his old apprentice's ass, but stops to save Anakin and Obi-Wan, leaving time for Dooku to escape.

The Clone War has begun; Anakin and Padme go to Naboo and get married. This is bad because the Jedi are not allowed to form relationships, it causes them to lose control of their reasoning and become Sith (maybe/eventually).

*is sorry if left out anything major, is trying to make a summary*

Episode 3-

The war continues. General Grievous has kidnapped Palpatine, Anakin and Obi-Wan go and save him. Anakin listens to Palpatine when he tells Anakin to kill defenseless Dooku (btw, it is against the Jedi way to kill, especially an unarmed opponent). All three escape down to Corescant.

Padme is pregnant, Anakin is happy. Anakin fears losing Padme during childbirth (has been having visions through the force, like those he had when his mom was being beaten). She says don't worry.

Obi-Wan goes after Grievous, while the council make Anakin a member (because the Chancellor wants it) but don't give him the rank of Jedi Master (if he was he could take on an apprentice, wouldn't be an apprentice to Obi-Wan anymore) Anakin is pissed. He learns Palpatine is the dark lord they are looking for, but since they are friends he doesn't turn him in (plus Palpatine says he can teach Anakin how to save Padme from dieing).

Anakin tells Windo about the Chancellor. Windo doesn't trust him, tells him to stay put. Windo and three other Jedi go to make sure Palpatine gives up his emergency powers (Obi-Wan killed Grievous, war is over). He says no, Jedi attack, Anakin shows up, sides with Palpatine in order to save Padme (kills Windo). Becomes Darth Vader, leads Clone troops to carry out Executive Order 66 (kill all Jedi). Only Obi-Wan and Yoda survive (Yoda tried to kill Palpatine but failed). They learn it was Anakin who helped destroy the Jedi Temple (killing the kid Jedi also).

Obi-Wan goes to Padme to find out where Anakin is, he learns Anakin is the father of Padme's child. He tells her Anakin is in trouble. She goes to him (to Mustafar, where Anakin was killing the leaders of the CIS/Sidious' puppets), Obi-Wan hid in her ship. She tells Anakin what she heard, Anakin sees Obi-Wan and force chokes (out of anger) Padme. Obi-Wan and Vader fight, Obi-Wan wins and leaves Anakin to die (no arms, no legs and is falling into lava). He takes Padme off to Polis Masa, where He, Yoda and Senator Organa learn that she is carrying twins and is dieing.

Palpatine shows up and rescues Vader. Vader gets suited up in the black armor that keep him alive. Padme give birth to Luke and Leia. Padme dies, Palpatine told Vader that Vader killed her, he goes into an emotional rage. Organa takes Leia to Alderann as his adopted daughter while Obi-Wan takes Luke to Tatoonie where is step-uncle and his wife are (Uncle Owen and Aunt Beru).

Episode 3 ends with the Emperor, Darth Vader and a young Grand Moff Tarkin over seeing the building of the (first) Death Star.
