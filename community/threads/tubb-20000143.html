Re: Home made booze
The easier type of alcohol in my opinion is cider and it's fast. I'm the impatient typr and prefer not to wait three months to enjoy my booze. Anyway the recipe I use is 1 gallon of apple juice, 1 cup of sugar, 1 packet of champagne yeast, and some cinnamon and nutmeg mixed in. First mx the apple juice and sugar in a pot. Then oil it for about ten minutes. Then, funnel it into a sanitized container (SANITZING is very importance, more homebrews are ruined through not doing this than anything else)- I used a teaspoon of bleach for this. Then add the yeast, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Cover and leave in a warm, dark place for 7-10 days. Open and drink.
