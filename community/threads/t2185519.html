There are several forms of encryption, each one has several different methods to crack.

For example, cracking WEP is different to cracking WPA. There will also be different approaches depending on if there are any clients connected.

That being said, assuming you want to crack a WEP encrypted network, good range, with a client connected. It will take less than 5 minutes.

Firstly get familiar with 'Backtrack'

http://backtrack.offensive-security.....php/Main_Page
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BackTrack

For BT help:
http:../../forums.remote-exploit.org/forumdisplay.php@f=35

Download a program called 'uNetBootin'
http:../../sourceforge.net/projects/unetbootin/default.htm

uNetBootin is designed to simplify the process of creating a bootable USB.

Once you run uNetBootin, under 'Distro', select "Backtrack 3".

Then make sure the device selected is your USB, (at least a 2gb stick). And let the program work away.

Once it's done, restart your laptop, making sure to set the USB as the primary boot device. Then BT will load.

Have a play around, get used to the terminal. Have a read about basic commands.

Once you've got your bearings, try going through guides like this:
[WEP]
http:../../www.i-hacked.com/content/view/231/42/default.htm
