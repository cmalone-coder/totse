When You crack your back you are flooding your brain with sensations, and that in laymans terms is what is causing the sensations you get.

It is produced in the midbrain area called the substantia nigra.
There are also twenty different types of endorphins in the brain. Endorphin is the abbreviation of endogenous morphine. The strongest endorphine is Beta Endorphin, the most euphoric of endorphins. They are found in the the thalimus and in areas of the spinal chord that are major pain receptor sites. The alpha sigma and gamma endorphins, and dynorphin play lesser roles in pain managment and control,stress relief and immune system health.
