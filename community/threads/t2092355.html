UFC 81 - Thoughts on Lesnar's UFC Debut?
I've been reading around on this fight lately, and was just curious to see what you guys thought about this.
For those few who don't know, Brock Lesnar - former WWE wrestler and NCAA wrestling champ will be fighting former UFC HW champion Frank Mir.

As far as who's going to win goes, I have no clue. K-1 didn't really show off Lesnar's ability very well so I don't know how well he's going to do. Supposedly Mir is in really good shape cardio-wise, but he likely still can't go an entire 3 round match without tiring. Lesnar is highly unlikely to be on par with Mir as far as technique goes, but physically he's faster, stronger, and unless he's really been slacking off, his endurance should be just as good as ever.

The most obvious scenario I think people see happening is that Lesnar will throw Mir to the mat, attempt to ground and pound, and Mir will catch him in an armbar. Others think Lesnar will succeed at the ground and pound and TKO Mir.
Either way, most people agree that Lesnar will throw Mir down and try to ground and pound him.

Again, I have no clue what might happen. Lesnar might try to avoid any submission attempt by staying on his feet, Mir might catch his jaw with a kick. Lesnar could have a glass jaw.
Looking at the K-1 fight, Lesnar seemed pretty awkward to me on his feet. A lot of people have said his stand up technique is poor, and considering Lesnar has been training for what? A year and a half? I definitely believe it, many of the guys in the UFC have been at least doing some striking since they were around 14.
Another strange thing about the K-1 fight was that Lesnar's opponent actually TAPPED from strikes, not a submission. It could be that Min Soo Kim was just a complete wimp, or maybe Lesnar has some serious power behind those punches. If that's the case, and he can hit that hard while on the ground like that, it'll be interesting to see one of them connect while he's standing up and can throw his body into it.

Well anyhow, what are your thoughts on this?
