Re: LSD and Social Anxiety
I would recomend against it. You could potentially make your social anxiety problem way worse. It's best you quit all drugs altogether until you get it figured out.

For instance I developed social anxiety but continued to use drugs, once I eventually quit I was a wreck. Basically no friends, hardly left the house, didn't answer the phone, if I left the house it was with extreme discomfort and awkwardness in social situations. I've been off drugs a little over a year and just now recently am I starting to get myself together. The last year was a mess.

Do whatever you like, I'm not saying this is what's going to happen to you. Perhaps the LSD may help you. I'm just sharing my experience, personally I think it's unwise to use drugs (especially psychedelics) when you have mental health issues.
