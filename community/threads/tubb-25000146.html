Annunaki & Nibiru Part 2
I thought nobody was interested in it so I never made Part 2, but I guess I will for the 1 or 2 that are.

And to Reply to Spectrum and InnitRa, see bottom.

Anyway.

The Annunaki were supposed to be beautiful giants that influenced the evolution of the human race early in our history.

They came to Earth from Nibiru (the 10th Planet)for some unknown reason, possibly for the Gold that exists on earth.

The Annunaki set up colonies here, the largest being Abzu (Apsu)in Mesopotamia where the leader of the team, Ea, oversaw things.

Judging from myths, Ea changed his name to Enki after this (which means Lord of the Earth, and that makes sense) and he asked the leader of the Annunaki (Maybe still on Nibiru), Anu, if it was alright for him to create a hybrid species, which was probably to be used for slave labor.

Anu eventually agreed, and Enki took genetic material from some of the more advanced Apes (Neanderthals?) and genetics from the Annunaki themselves and merged them to create this hybrid, which was the Homo Sapien, humans.

Enki's half-brother Enlil (who was also heir to the rulership of Anu, again by Myth) saw that some of the Annunaki and Humans had bred to create ANOTHER hybrid, called Nephilim, and he was pissed.

He didn't think that the Annunaki should be interbreeding with the lowely servents, and convinced the higher Annunaki that the humans and Nephilim should be wiped out.

Interjection here. We need to go over the effects Nibiru has on the Earth each time it passes...should have done this before.

Nibiru has a very strong magnetic charge, and when it passes by Earth, our core locks onto it, stopping it's rotation for the duration of Nibiru's presence in our system (about three days).

Anybody could tell you what this might do to the Earths surface, as the crust is wretched off and slides over the mantel, sort of like how you go flying out of your seat in a car accident.

What happens is both a magnetic and physical Pole Shift, I'm sure you all have heard about this before. Earth's magnetic poles twist as the core is locked in place, rearranging about 90 degrees, and as it does, the continents do too.

Fortunatly, it doesn't turn the surface into a pool of molten magma, but the effects are catastrophic for us.

Of course, we get earthquakes of unimaginable size as the Earth turns 90 degrees in a matter of hours, and these quakes cause huge Tidal Waves (of course...we couldn't have an ELE without Tidal Waves!) that could engluf the East Coast of the United States up to the Appalaction Mnts. Every volcano on earth could erupt, and the weather...you get the idea.

This doesnt mean we all die, but life sucks for awhile after the Earth is covered by a blanket of ash and dust for a few decades (I'm not sure to the point ALL sunlight is blocked at all times, I've read that plants do grow a bit, although not very well.)

So now we're passed the Street Corner end of the world scenario that turns so many people off about this theory.

Back to the Annunaki.

Enlil has now convinced the other Annunaki that the humans and Nephilim should all be destroyed. The way he suggests this is to be done is simply NOT giving them warning nor shelter of the coming Pole Shift, which is to be a pretty bad one.

Enki is upset that his creations are to be destroyed, and though he is forbidden to directly warn Humans of their inevitable demise, he indirectly warns a man named Gilgamesh (or Noah, or any other possible names from flood myths) of the upcoming deluge and that 'anyone who hopes to live will build a boat of such and such specifications and...yeah.' He was a little over specific in his musings out behind Noah's house...Noah got the idea.

I'm not entirely sure if he actually rounded up a bunch of living animals, if he recieved Genetic Material from animals, or if he even saved any at all, but Noah built the said Ark and was saved from the said flood caused by the Ice melting in the Pole Shift.

Enlil was severly pissed that one (or maybe more) of the pesky humans survived, but Enki calmed him, showing him that not all of the humans were disobedient whores that wanted to weaken Annunaki bloodline.
