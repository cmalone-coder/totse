Re: Coloured Contact Lenses
Yes, there perfectly safe providing you use them properly. If you already use contacts, you'll know how to take them in and out, but I suggest going to an optician to show you the safe way for putting them in and taking them out. Make sure to get cleaning solution, too, without this, the lenses will become dirty and dry, that's when it damages your eyes. You'll need an up to date eye test from the optician, even if you don't need lenses already. The lenses are made of a harder material than 'normal' lenses so may cause a bit of irritation if your eyes are'nt used to it. Handling and cleaning are VERY important to keep your eyes healthy, I strongly suggest seeing an optician to show you how to use them safely, other than that there totally safe.

Re: Coloured Contact Lenses
A couple things:

1. It doesn't matter how dark or light your eyes are, colored contacts will make your eyes the color you're going for.

2. You technically have to have a prescription to get purely cosmetic contacts, but this can be avoided by simply buying them online (I've bought a couple pairs from fashion-contactlenses.com and was entirely satisfied each time; also, the shipping's cheap, even when international. And they're very well priced, actually).

3. Most colored contacts are one-size-fits-all and should work for you; if not, you will have to get a pair custom-made by an optometrist as previously suggested.

4. These contacts do NOT make you go blind! My friends and I have been wearing them for years and have never had any problems.

5. Keep in mind that most pairs have a "life expectancy" of a month or so. So choose when to wear an expensive pair wisely

6. And also, it really depends on the person rather than the ethnicity. Some Asians look great in blue contacts :D
