Re: easy temp exhaust fix
Ditto to what red_eyed_wonda & AP2k said.
Duct tape is just going to melt.

If it's just a section of pipe that's <1ft you can maybe go up to Autozone or other auto parts place and get a flexible pipe (this (http://www.autozone.com/selectedZip,90210/initialAction,accessoryProductDetail/initialR,438058/shopping/selectZip.htm)) and some clamps and cut out and replace the bad section. However, in my experience, it can be damn near impossible to get those clamps to make a perfect seal with that flexible pipe.

Or you could maybe try one of these exhaust rapair kits (http://www.autozone.com/A,true/N,100436/shopping/accessoriesResults.htm)
