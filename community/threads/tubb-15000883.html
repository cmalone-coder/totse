I'm an agnostic and I think you people have an incorrect view of agnosticism. First of all agnosticism is not a belief and it has no dogma or code of conduct or any specific views on the universe, agnosticism is an intellectual principle. By saying that agnostics are dedicated to any one thing is false because each agnostic develops their own personal view of the universe. To my understanding the only thing that ties agnostics together is a single intellectual principle: Explore new ideas. Any one who calls agnosticism a passive, sit-on-the-fence philosophy does not truly understand the active, intellectual nature of agnosticism. Agnosticism is not a set of defined beliefs, it is an intellectual principle employed to explore the world around us without the bias of a belief system.

Well said Quantized.

Ytrewq, your analogy is flawed. 1+1=2 has been proven to everyone's satisfaction except professional smartasses and people who have done so many psychedelics they're of no use to anyone. Relating something like that to proof of god's existence is, to say the least, stretching it.

The point of being agnostic is not to be politically correct (that's like saying christians have their beliefs because they are sadistic cannibals). It is not a set of beliefs or dogma either. It is ONLY the intellectual freedom to explore reality and truth without the urge to validate anything else at the same time.

In another thread, I was defending Islam from someone who was making inflammatory, unsubstantiated claims about it. Once he calmed down and started citing real sources, I was satisfied. If I had some religious creed, I would have felt the urge to passionately (and not very reasonably) defend or attack his position purely for the sake of doing so. Instead, the use of reason and fact in the discussion were most important to me.

All I ever wanted was to find those universal truths (if that's even possible). Agnosticism is the lack of theological inhibition that has best allowed me to fully explore all sides. Otherwise discussion ALWAYS degrades to:
"God exists!"
"No, god does not!"
"Yes he does!"
"No, he doesn't!"
"Yeah-huh!"
"Nuh-uh!"
