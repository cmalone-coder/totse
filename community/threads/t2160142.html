I was born in the USA, and I still am in the USA.

This is a major misconception that a lot of people have. You might be able to get your record EXPUNGED, but even then your record is only "hidden" from the public. I used quotes because it is still possible for a thorough record check from an employer to find out your entire record.

The police, probation officers, parole officers, judges, public defenders, attorneys, judges, constables, etc. will still see your entire record, expunged or not. No matter what happens, EVEN IF YOUR CRIME WAS WHEN YOU WERE A MINOR, your actual charge will remain on your record until you die, NO MATTER WHAT.

What, do you think someone can sell pot, get caught, complete their probation, get their record expunged then do it all over again and expect the same treatment as before, like a first time offender? HAHA no. Doesnt work like that.

Like I said before, even a THOROUGH BACKGROUND CHECK from XXXXX employer can bring up your EXPUNGED record. This usually does not happen, but it can happen, and does happen.
