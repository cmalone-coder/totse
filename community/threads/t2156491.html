That is true, but MDMA will raise the body temperature, along with pretty much most stimulants out there. And one of the best ways to regulate body temperature is either to keep yourself hydrated enough or to changed your enviroment. But unless you feel like sitting in a meat freezer for the duration of your trip, you really cant change the enviroment to that level of degree. Hydrating yourself is also a safe bet against hyperthermia and hyponatremia(rare, but anything is possible, considering the general shitty quality of xtc pills these days).

I like to think of it as sitting on a beach on a hot day: Sun is shining, your hot, you become thirsty. Now is it a necesity to drink water? No. Will it help cool you off and make you more confortable? Yes.

The whole you must drink water is overrated, but I still say its a safe bet. As he said, if your planning on raving or clubbing, its a good idea to get a good amount of water in you to keep your body in check. But if your sitting around with a couple of your buddies and your girls, commuting with nature or whatever the fuck your doing, the 'water rule' shouldnt really apply. Still it wont hurt to drink a couple bottles of H2O. Better safe then sorry...
