����You could probably get away with putting the smokeless powder in a heavy metal container and adding a fuse. Smokeless is generally not as good for making salutes (read: small nondestructive bombs) as black powder.
����The tobacco can won't work for two reasons. First, it is made of thin plastic and is not a strong container. Second, it has no way of holding itself shut. Low explosives produce their explosions by bursting whatever casing they are in. If the casing is not strong enough to provide confinement for the explosive there will be no exlposion - Just a lot of fire and wasted powder.

Re: how big of explosion will this be?
You could always go to a hardware store and get some half inch PVC pipe, some endcaps, and PVC cement. Cut the pipe just short enough so the two endcaps meet when you push them together. Glue one endcap on, drill a hole in the pipe just big enough for the fuse, fill the pipe half full with smokeless powder, and glue the other endcap on. These things are expensive and produce a lot of plastic shrapnel, but it's about the only thing you can do with smokeless powder.
