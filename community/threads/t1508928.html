I'm an oblivious fool
I've never been popular with the ladies. I've only ever had one girlfriend, and I've only ever had sex a few times. I've been friends with a few, but never very good friends. In high school I was real quiet, and I rarely spoke to girls. I've always assumed that this lack of luck was becuase no girl would ever want to have anything to do with me.

Then, a few days ago, I learned that one girl who I barely knew in my senior year had a "crush" on me. (I'm loath to use this childish term, but I can't think of any other way to represent the idea) The thing is, it never occured to me that I could ever have a chance with this girl. She was so attractive that it's almost painful. I hardly ever thought aout her, since I looked at her as being waaaaaaaaay out of my league. When I did think about her, it was always, "Some lucky guy gets to be with her", and never "I wish I could be with her". The whole thing raises questions like, "How many times has this happened before?" and "Will I continue on being retarded in the ways of love?"

Basically, I want to know what the fuck if wrong with me. Is this some sort of mental/emotional disorder, or just good old-fashioned lack of self confidence? What can I do to change?
