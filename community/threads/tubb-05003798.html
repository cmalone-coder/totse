You lucky bastard, welcome to the world!! Between mushrooms and acid I have probibly tripped about a two hundred times, and the first few times were the best. Psychedelics are great recreational drugs but can also be a bit of a learning experience too. I would love to ramble for a while but I'll just give a few tips instead.
Check out the Erowid psilocybe mushroom vault, if not for the trip reports then for the pictures. This is shroom season in a lot of places, so if the shrooms are small and brown the are probibly psilocybe semilanceta or liberty caps, a good dose of these dry is about an 1/8th or so. Potentcy varies greatly between types of shrooms, if the ones you get are dry and have blue/white stems they are more potent than field shrooms and 1/8 dry might be a little much, take a gram on an empty stomach and if you are not getting pretty baked within an hour and a half take another gram, and so on.Don't eat too much before you take them, I reccomend washing them back with a couple of brews. I will assume you trust this dude not to sell you bunk. As for the buzz, expect tracers, patterns (look for mayan runes in your jeans!), giggles like the first few times you toked, some movement depending on the strain of shrooms(picture frames and doorways are a good place to look). Try to keep things light, your moods may be a bit sensitive to your environment, I usually play the Ramones or some Damned. Try to avoid being the only person tripping, be alone if nessasary, straight people will seem fucked up and wierd, you will feel like you know things they don't. Oh yeah, I said I wouldn't ramble, what a fuckin' liar I am. Have fun! (I wouldnt bother smoking the shake, but if there is a lot you should save some and look into some shroom cultivation sites)

That Gomez bit is some pretty trippy shit but might be a little misleading, you are quite aware of reality(unless you take a lot, ask me about my 15 orange barrel mini mike trip sometime), there are just shapes, colors and patterns overlaid, you feel different emotionally but are not delusional. One neat thing that can happen is called sensory kinesthesia, you see sounds or hear a person touching you, loud noises especially can show up as brilliant flashes across your vision. Try closing your eyes and letting the visions and ideas bubbling up from your subconcious form images in your mind, one of the best trips I ever had was when I drank a shitload of booze and ate a bunch of shrooms after, I just laid there semicomatose while this science-fiction/amusement park trip pieced itself together behind my eyelids. Smoking weed will flavor the trip a bit differently, I like to not smoke anything till near the end, then the weed seems to re-activate the shrooms a bit a reduce the minor burnt out feeling, a couple of valiums will help you sleep.
