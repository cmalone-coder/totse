There are a lot of big questions out there and every one of them has an even larger number of answers .

But I think these four questions are sometimes thought about more. I've taken them from another site. Lets share our answers in this thread, and see what the regulars of My God generally think about them.

1. What is the Ultimate Power or Basic Energy which moves the universe, creates life? What is the Cosmic Plan?

2. What is life, where did it start, where is it going?

3. What is man, whence did he come, and where is he going?

4. What am I? What is my place in this plan?

My answers:

1. The illogical, chaotic infinite. No plan.

2. Life is like an on-going chemical reaction. It started randomly, and is going towards the infinite.

3. Man is life with the ability to introspect upon itself and reality. Man came from Earth, and appears to be on a path of destruction.

4. I appear to be no more then another minuscule cog in the mechanical order reality appears to have. But there is no order, and I am the God, the illogical chaotic infinite through which all is possible.

My answers may change with time.

1. What is the Ultimate Power or Basic Energy which moves the universe, creates life? What is the Cosmic Plan?

I don't know. I have absolutely not idea, and I'm not afraid to admit it. It takes more balls to say "I don't know" than it does to BS your way out of a situation by talking out of your ass and inventing a God to explain everything. The Greeks created gods to explain the unknown, such as lightning. Now that we have a greater understanding at such things, we dismiss their beliefs. In time, we will learn more, and in the distant future, we will be laughing our asses of at present-day religions.

2. What is life, where did it start, where is it going?

Again, I have no idea, and I could care less.

3. What is man, whence did he come, and where is he going?

Man is an animal. Man is an animal that became cognitively advanced and convinced himself that he was the most important animal, and that he should have a purpose. We're so smart we're stupid. Other animals simply exist, they don't need a reason to.

4. What am I? What is my place in this plan?

I'm an animal that lives because it is encoded into me to survive, fornicate, and fit in with others.
