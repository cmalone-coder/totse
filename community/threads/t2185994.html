after you rescue the snake. there should be a bridge nearby with a guy holding a fighting stick. go across and there are a bunch of enemies there including one of those white gorillas and a few archers on top of a rock. frag all those niggers, then get on top of the rock were the archers where.the bouncy cloud should be right in front of you. the next cloud is hard to see, but it was right on top of the rock. then go save the monkey


and to all you who havent got the game, heres my review of it.

its overall kind of crappy, and was obviously make to get easy profits. the movement is kind of poorly done, and the graphics are crappy for a 2008 game, similar to that of the xbox360 launch games. the combat is very button mashy at the beginning. you will find it pretty easy in the beginning but it is a challenge later on, though i find that you can just beat 99% of the combat areas with just XXX, XXY and the charge attack. replayability is decent, with the constant improvement system, really, its just playing the campaign all over again.

however, the story was told quite well, even if it was taken from a film. the voice acting was pretty good, though Jack Blacks voice is retarded if your over the age of 8. music is overall generic, like the rest of the game. although the a few battles are kind of frustrating at times, there are plenty of autosave points. and the environments are nicely done too for a kids game

overall, the game is generic and monotonous, but its good for the 4 hours i spent on it. and coming free with lego indiana jones doesnt hurt it either
