Re: What Kind of Vaporizer?
I've tried 2 kinds: the hotplate one and one with a digital temperature setting. With the hotplate one, it was very easy to go too hot and burn it, which is basically smoking rather than vaping. The digital one on the other hand was awesome. I found out the vaporisation point of THC (around 390F, IIRC) and set it to that. It took like 2 minutes to warm up, and a green light goes off when its reached the set temperature. They're a lot more economical too; a pinch of weed about the size of my thumbnail lasted about 5 minutes before needing to be reloaded.
