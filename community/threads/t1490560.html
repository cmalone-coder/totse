Lose the glasses. Seriously. Thick glasses suck. If you don't want to deal with contacts, shoot for something small and with polycarbonate lenses. They're designed for athletes because the material is nigh unbreakable, but an additional upside is that they're thin and light.

If you have insurance, you'll have to pay extra for the material (for me, it's $27 above what insurance will cover). Hopefully you have insurance that pays for frames as well; if so, they should dish out a set amount, which will decrease the cost of more expensive frames. Save a little and get yourself a set of slick designer glasses. Bring someone whose fashion sense you trust to help you find the pair that suits you best if you don't think you can do it yourself.

For extra smoothness, get them tinted. They can be like sunglasses, but it's not necessary; in fact, if you prefer a color like blue or red, it's easier to pass them off as normal glasses in places where sunglasses wouldn't be kosher. That's optional, but it's a nice touch.

If you've got the honest-to-god nerd glasses I'm envisioning (quite clearly, since I used to have them myself), this will be a gargantuan improvement. Your face is the first thing people look at; this alteration will overshadow anything you lack in choosing clothes.
