Now i have never been an all out religious nut. I have spent a good deal of my life basking in my atheism. But after the events in my life i have taken up my own beliefs.

You may be wondering what this thread is about :

Atheism : is the new crusades

Ok you have your beliefs atheists and we have ours please respect humanity enough by not deliberately stamping your boot all over something that you don't believe in i have spent a long time resenting all religion all workings of the soul , shit even the existence of good will overcoming instinct.

I personally disagree with the Christian faith due to many many factors but that does not mean i personally try to seek out Christians and break their beliefs and make them believe my opinion. Strip them of their most intimate values just so i can feel bigger and smarter.

The only real humanity lies within respect and care for each other.
