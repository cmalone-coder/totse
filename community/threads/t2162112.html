Something some of us strive for in the linux scene is to revive old computers, crappy laptops and make our friend's jaws drop as they see you running your old Pentium 0.5 with 15 windows open in 8 different desktops and wobbly windows... and you're only using about 200MB of RAM.

How does one accomplish that? LIGHTWEIGHT APPS! What are your favourites?

File Manager: Thunar/PCManFM
Window Manager: Openbox
Browser: Opera
Media Player: MPD (Music Player Daemon) + Sonata
Torrent: Deluge
Terminal: aterm
Word Processor: Abiword
Spreadsheet: gnumeric

Any others you'd like to mention? Any kind of program you wanna include?
