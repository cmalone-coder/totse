Re: Something dark and psychological....
This may be a far reach, but... Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion has its dark moments and complete plot twists. The thing is, you have to give it time as the series grows REALLY interesting towards the end of season one.

It's most definitely psychological. I know you'll be able to appreciate that part of the show.

Season two is 8 episodes in, join the fun.

My Summary of it:
"Lelouch De Britannia is a fallen prince of Britannia living out his days as a Britannian in Area 11 once known as Japan until conquered by the Britannian Empire. Lelouch despises the empire and is one day given the ability to make his word absolute to anyone he says it to. If they are told to die they die, if they are told to live they live. He soon uses his power to amass followers behind the anonymous persona 'Zero'. It is then the Black Knights are born and lead to take back over Japan for the rightful natives."

The art is beautiful and the production value is top notch. The story keeps you on edge, except for that episode they made the giant pizza... that was kind of gay.

The premise that Lelouch is able to command people is similar into how Light was able to control people in Death Note.

Very worth the watch.

If you like reading manga, I'd suggest Berserk. That's completely dark and twisted to the highest degree. Seriously, that's as fucked up as they come. It's a complete horror-fantasy. I've never seen the anime, but I know it only covers a small portion of the over-all story.

Gantz is pretty good too. A Sci-Fi horror on par with Berserk in twistedness. Again, I read the manga only for this, wasn't a fan of the anime.
