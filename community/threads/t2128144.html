Skip the three ounces. Get a one-hitter and sling off the half-ounce, leaving nothing preweighed. Your nickels and dimes should still make smoke-plus, and weigh more than the kidiots you're making your target demographic could get otherwise... but when they break down your door, they find the lowest level of personal stash and no evidence of dealing - there's lots of reasons to have a scale that break the "reasonable doubt" threshold to smithereens, though less so for large quantities and/or many weighed bags.

Don't take anything to school. Don't take anything within 500-2,500 feet of your own school, depending on jurisdiction. Either you hold and weigh off your half, and your friend does the business, or you do the business, and your friend holds and weighs.

You'll have to find some way to obfusicate the runs between you two - stenography and flooding with random datapoints ("paper avalanche," here outside the domain of document subpeona) are possibilities, but you'll have to figure that out on your own.

Learn countersurveillance. Keep your ear to the ground about the heat... 'n keep your ear open about beefs on the street, 'cause you never know when some bitch think they'll be a snitch-rat to do what they can't in a fair fight.

Not that I've ever done this before, thankfully. Just a hypothetical consideration. Feel free to upgrade your security protocol beyond these basics.
