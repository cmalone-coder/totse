Ive done acid probably about 10 times...

i dont have time to trip very often, and usually end up tripping every month or two...

as far as pure usage goes weed is my drug of choice, but LSD is probably my favorite....

it hasnt messed me up mentally but with enough some people develop HPPD... its not always as much fun as an interesting learning experience.... every trip ive ever had has had a different character based on my mindset and setting... my first trip i thought i had an idea of what to expect, oh how ignorant i was.... no matter how much we tell you your trip might be like, its likely nothing like you expect... erowid says "mild mood lift", i can only describe the euphoria i experienced in my first trip as the collective joy of the universe being forced into my brain, i was so happy that you would have to go beyond all sanity and reason to be that happy...

my trips then haven't been as euphoric... but its a drug that has great potential to make you see and think of things that you would rather not see or think about... my last trip was 2 hits of some damn good blotter... i watched the skin in my hands sink through as if all the muscle had vanished leaving only skin, bone, and veins... all the while watching what looked like things crawling through my veins... try to stay away from mirrors or looking at your self if that sort of thing might bother you... on a couple of hits you can still rationalize that your on a drug and its causing you too feel the way your feeling, and as long as you can rationalize that everything will eventually go back to normal seeing these sorts of things isn't that bad...

you will learn alot about yourself, those around you, and the world you live in... if you have an intense experience expect to possibly see the world in a whole new perspective...

do it with a friend your first time, psychs tend to be easier around good friends you trust.... alone your just left to think, with a friend going through the same thing you have someone to relate and talk to...
