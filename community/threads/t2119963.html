alcohol and doing things you regret
ok, I'm making this thread to discuss those times that we've been very drunk and done and said things we regret.

When you're so drunk, that you can't even figure out why you have any inhibitions about making an ass out of yourself when you're sober (or you don't even realise you're doing it and afterwards, you can remember or you're told about things you were doing and saying, and you realise how embarrassing that situation would be sober). Or just saying things that you normally wouldn't say because they reveal personal things or you just wouldn't want whoever listening to hear that under normal circumstances.

You're so drunk that you can't even understand your sober state, and you think and act as if you're going to be drunk forever, or you don't care or can't bring yourself to care how you'll feel and what the consequences will be when you're sober.

I think sometimes, it can be sort of helpful, because you realise sometimes how little people care about many things that you're worried or embarassed about when your sober. You have alot of confidence, and you can look people in the eye and your completely numb to the awkwardness you might sometimes feel from looking someone in the eyes when you're sober. You realise that alot of the awkwardness and stuff is actually all in you and not like some transference from the other person that you're getting, or at least you stop worrying about what the other person is thinking because all that really matters is whether or not you care.

I don't like the way I act around people I don't really know when I'm drunk, normally, I'm pretty reserved, and I come out of my shell as I warm up to people, which is not many. And my personality to those people I warm up to seems a bit different with each person. I'm sort of a bit particular in what I say and how I act, or at least I would like to be. I like to put on airs of sorts, so people will think of me in the way I want them to, and I'm comfortable like that. My real personality is kind of inside my head, and when I speak, it doesn't feel like my real personality. But when I'm drunk I couldn't give a shit and I just blah blah blah without thinking. Then I've met some people and acted like that in front of them, possibly making them think I'm a douchebag, and maybe a reputation spreading, that would never have happened if I was sober, and then if I do see those people again and I'm sober, I'll be quite different. When I'm drunk I usually have an inkling of what I'm doing and saying, I will regret. But I can't really bring myself under control and to start acting in the way which I think I'd be comfortable with sober, I just can't give a shit.

What are the things that you've done and said while drunk which you really regretted later?
I think a guy once made a post in spurious I think where he was at a park and really drunk, and he raped a girl, maybe not the whole nine yards, but groping her etc. So if anything as bad as that has ever happened when you were drunk, please share.
