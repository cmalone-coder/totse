I'd say the shock was in a good way though, not in an overbearing or frightening way, just utterly beautiful.

any time is good, i have tripped in the middle of the day, at night, and woken up at 5am to watch the sun rise while peaking.... my favorites are morning trips or night trips, i like tripping late where im peaking after midnight because i can wander around the streets staring up at the sky listening to music and smoking ciggarettes without any worry of people, your alone in the world....

I agree with the day after having nothing to do, dont have work or anything after your first trip... your going to want time for rest and time to sort things out, and there is a lot to sort out...

As for what to expect, that is about like explaining the color red to a blind person, words just don't do the experience justice..... But i will try to explain what tripping is like for me, just know that however much your believe you know what to expect, your going to be wrong...

The first hour is coming up, i put blotters on or under my tongue and just leave them there for about 10 minutes then chew and swallow. With acid dropped on candies like sour patch i put them in between my cheek and gum to avoid the amount of salvia that builds up and just leave it there for a while. Coming up on acid tends to feel a bit anxious. Eventually thoughts will start to speed up, time will start to slow down, fractals and patterns will form on just about everything if you look at it. Stationary objects will shift, morph, and breath. Colors are vivid but sometimes they make things different colors, i get this more during day trips, kayaking across a lake under a blue and purple sky was amazing... Erowid says one of the positives is "mood elevation" the amount of happy you feel during an acid trip can vary from almost neutral to happier than you have ever been times 1000.... My first trip was the single happiest night of my life, ecstasy is the closest word i can think of but it doesn't do justice.... Expect to be completly overwhelmed by the chaos in your mind, and the best course of action is not to fight it, your not going to win, just let go and stop worrying...

Re: ive never done lsd, but i want to
I did acid once at about 6 pm with 3 other guys... it was the best experience of my life. The first 4 or so hours we just spent laughing uncontrollably at everything, making the most ridiculous jokes which made absolutely no sense. We went driving at night (don't do this) and it was incredible, the night sky was clear and the stars just looked amazing; they left light streaks and seeing shooting stars was mind blowing. We went on a hike, and into some caves. This wasn't as fun, I wouldn't recommend going into caves. We went back home, and spent a few hours of climbing things, looking at things, etc.

Finally, we were all coming down, and the three of them went to bed. I didn't feel like sleeping though, so I stayed up listening to music and drawing on our whiteboard. It was now early morning (about 7-8 am) and I ventured off on my own. This was equally as incredible. Everything was so interesting, posters, sprinklers, grass, wildlife, etc, I strongly suggest you spend at least a couple hours coming down, alone.

There were some scary moments for me while we were hiking in the dark woods. So scary that even when I remember them (for example, right now) I get a strong sense of fear. The day after I would become so scared I would instantly tear up when I remembered these events. There were moving shadows, scary noises, the some trees looked like ghosts to me, etc. However, I just looked away and kept moving, and the scary things I saw also went away.

Oh, and the day after is weird, as others have said do not have anything to do. It's like your mind is slowly piecing together exactly how the world actually works, and how to comprehend it. Very hard to explain.
