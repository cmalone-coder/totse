Re: Nitrous Questions
I tend to inhale-exhale 2 or 3 times, then leave the balloon full and breathe a couple times, then repeat till I pass out. it prolongs the high a bit. Then again, I seldom do it when not stoned at least... It's very possible to just hold it in until you black out (at least for me...)

I have found hyperventilating before doing it makes you conscious for the experience longer, and lets you hold breath for far longer. I start with fully empty lungs, because a balloon almost entirely fills my lungs. I usually hold my nose to avoid accidently breathing in through it when i inhale.

In terms of re using the air, I've been told that although you don't absorb a large proportion of the nitrous when you inhale it, it doesn't seem to work. I hypothesized that it's just being mixed with the air that's still in your lungs when you start inhaling. (even when you've fully exhaled, some decent proportion of lung capacity has air in it, afaik)

It's.. Somewhat similar to duster. In the way that it does the whole auditory hallucinations and makes you generally out of it. It's shorter in duration than inhalants i've tried (duster, Butane) Far, far, far better though. It's hard to compare it to anything I've tried. It's extremely intense.

Alcohol makes it more intense in my experience, but weed does a far better job at that. MDMA or acid are both utterly amazing mixed with it. MDMA particularly. the effects will hit you again repeatedly for several minutes after the high has died off. Probably my favourite drug combination, heh.
