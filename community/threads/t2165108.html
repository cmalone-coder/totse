Shadow Bugs
I have been thinking lately about common hallucinations people have on various drugs, and the reason behind these shared sights.
It seems many people would attribute these to a "universal consciousness", but until that's scientifically recognized it's bullshit in my book.

So I have been pondering reasons why this is, and decided to start with insects. It seems that seeing insects is one of the most common hallucinations there is. I have read countless trip reports of people who have seen these shadow bugs, I have seen them myself a few times, and recently my friend told me that his grandfather who is sick with cancer sees them when he takes his morphine. Shadow bugs seem to be most common with deliriant's, but also crop up with all sorts of other drugs.

My guess is that it comes down to hard-wired instinct that when you see something that is very small and moving to automatically label it as a bug before you even consciously realize. Small twitch's in your vision caused by some drugs are then interpreted as a bug and your brain superimposes an insect memory on that twitching spot.


So since I'm sure BLTC has had much experience with these little guys I am wondering what you have to say about it.
