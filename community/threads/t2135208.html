Re: Candyflipping
Personally, I REALLY REALLY enjoy this combination of drugs and is probably my favorite.

Since the LSD lasts the longest and will overpower almost anything else, I almost ALWAYS drop the LSD first.

After I've gone thru the comeup and awareness of energy and then actually start tripping, I'll wait til the peak or a little before the LSD peak to take the MDMA.

Once the MDMA hits I'm usually still peaking or just slightly coming down on the acid, and the MDMA brings back the LSD and adds an awesome effect to the visuals and experience.

I'll enjoy this amazing synergy for a while, and wait til near the end before I smoke some herb.

Depending how I'm feeling, the herbs will almost always bring back some of the acid visuals, but with different tones and hues, it seems the herbs add more pastel colors to the trip.

I've candyflipped a bunch of ways, and this is the way I Really enjoy it and get the MOST out of the experience.

It also depends on your acid, if you've got some good clean acid, the whole experience can last a long time if you plan your dosages right.
