Ninjutsu was founded by groups of Ronin. Samurais with no masters. They lived in the mountains and made their own weapons (they were exiled, working as assasins or mercenaries). They kept itching powder (just dust and sand and other powders) and would throw it in their attackers eyes. Most ninjas carried bokkens or bo staffs (wooden swords) As they couldnt cut themselves and there was no blood to clean up or hide from other guards. They could just club an enemy to death or stun them.

They wore hand and foot spikes for climbing trees etc. They were very well equiped. Grappling hooks were also their thing. All i can think fo right now. Hope this helps.

Now, ninjutsu doesnt teach these skills, only the hand to hand and weapon based fighting system they used. The stealth skills were seperate from their art of combat.
