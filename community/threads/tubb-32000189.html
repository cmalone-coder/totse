exactly where the hell should I look?
I have this problem. I'll be walking down a hall at school, and I don't know where to look. My eyes flinch away from people, for some reason.

So I feel uncomfortable when I end up staring at someone in front of me. It's very strange. People look at me funny when I glance at them, and then quickly look away, after I identify who they are.

Where the hell should I be looking when I walk? Forward, of course, but it just seems strange to me to be looking at some person who's walking down the hall towards me.

I don't know. It might be a result of my highly antisocial tendencies resulting from a move to a new school. Or it might just be my paranoia. In any case, I feel highly uncomfortable about it.

Another thing is, when I talk to someone, I look them in the eyes. Some people seem to be uncomfortable with this. Where the hell should I be looking, then?

In addition to this, when I look at someone of the opposite gender, I don't know, *how* to look at them. I can talk, etc. just fine, but, it's just this.

And consequently, I'm always paranoid about how people will read my eye movement, especially since I'm in Japan, where people seem to try to read into things too much.

Don't concentrate on where you're looking, concentrate on how you walk..

That's honestly what I do.. before I walk into any place where there are a lot of people, especially if you feel kind of uncomfortable about it, take a 10 second break to throw your shoulders back, lift your chin, and think to yourself "I own this hallway/room"

You'll hopefully find that your eyes will find their own natural space. If you DO make eye contact with someone, you have two choices.. instead of looking away, which will make you look shifty/nervous, either flash them a quick smile, or give them an attitude look similar to "I wouldn't wipe shit off my boots with you".. all depends on who it is you see, and what impression you want to give..

People react to how you appear to them.. if you look nervous, they automatically rank you in the social scale below themselves.. if you give them a confident reaction, believe me, most people will rank you above themselves.. Makes life easier if you can master those things..
