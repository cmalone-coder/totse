TCE= available at your local auto parts store. ingrediants are: Tetrachloroethylene 127-18-4 & carbon dioxide 124-38-9

Japan Dryer is used when varnishing or shellacing in humid weather. it is added to the varnish or shellac to speed up the drying process. available at hardware stores in the paint section. NO SUBSTITUTIONS



the mix desired is 10 to 1

example: 100 ml TCE 10 ml JD
