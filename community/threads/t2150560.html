Re: datura.... wanna help me out here?
Well there are multiple ways to feel the effects. You could order some seeds and plant them or find plants...but for the sake of theory lets say you have leaves, seeds, or flowers of the plant already.

The first most common method would be to take a leaf, yes just one, and make tea out of it...then slowly sip the tea until you feel the effects. This could possibly result in overdose though if you have a low tolerance(everyone's tolerance is completely different and it doesn't just matter on weight)

The other method is to smoke it. Believe it or not this is actually safer because you can judge how strong the effects are since it hits you faster. This would actually be the better route to go. I recommend smoking the leaves.

Another method would be to just eat the seeds. Yes you can do this but it can be dangerous since you don't know exactly how much you are getting.

The last method is a bit unpractical but it is the best method. You could make a salve using the leaves/flowers and spread it to the soles of your feet, armpits, and other places on the skin. Yes it absorbs through the skin which omits other harmful alkaloids that you could get through other method. This was the way the shamans did it back then.

Good luck, and be very cautious with this plant.
