Re: bar scene
Bartenders are trained to spot fake IDs, but will probably not take it away from you if they do--that makes more paperwork for them, as they are legally obligated to report it to the police. Don't be surprised if you get turned away a few times, though. A few pointers:

Don't have other cards in your wallet with your real name on them. Bartenders will sometimes ask for a second form of ID to confirm, and are trained to watch when you first pull your wallet out. If they see debit cards, student ID, etc. and you say "uh, I don't have anything else" when asked, they'll know you're faking.

Don't be a jackass or seem nervous. If you're acting like a kid and the bartender has any suspicion, he'll kick you.

Go with older people who will vouch for you. If you're with a party of 21+ and seem a little off, you might still slide.

If the bartender asks "are you 21?" say "Yes, I am." Don't evade the question, as they're trained to spot this. The government sting operations that come in to catch bartenders serving to underage drinkers are not allowed to lie, and so will try to deflect the "Are you over 21" question with humor, sarcasm, or other tricks. Don't try it, or you'll look suspicious.
