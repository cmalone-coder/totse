Here is my works cited page

Erowid, "LSD Dosage." The Vaults of Erowid. May 28, 2007. 19 Mar 2008 <http://www.erowid.org/chemicals/lsd/lsd_dose.shtml>.

Hoffman, Albert. LSD-My Problem Child. Saline, MI: McGraw-Hill, 1980.

Horgan, John. "THE ELECTRIC KOOL-AID CLINICAL TRIAL." New Scientist 18526/02/2005 9-36. < http://www.newscientist.com/article.ns?id=mg18524881.400&print=true>.

Kurtzweil, Paula. "Medical Possibilities for Psychedelic Drugs." FDA Consumer 1995: 25-8.<http://www.fda.gov/FDAC/features/795_psyche.html>

Leiby, Richard. "The Magical Mystery Cure." Esquire 128.n3 (Sept 1997): 98(6). General OneFile. Gale. CCLA, Hillsborough Comm College. 19 Mar. 2008 <http://find.galegroup.com/itx/infomark.do?contentSet=IAC-Documents&docType=IAC&type=retrieve&tabID=T003&prodId=AONE&docId=A19711030&userGroupName=lincclin_hcc&version=1.0&searchType=AdvancedSearchForm&source=gale>.

"LSD." Encyclopædia Britannica. 2008. Encyclopædia Britannica Online. 17 Mar 2008 <http://www.britannica.com/eb/article-9049184>.

"LSD reveals schizophrenia treatment." News.com.au February 25, 2008. 19 Mar 2008 <http://www.news.com.au/story/0,23599,23270356-2,00.html?from=public_rss>.

Ossebaard, Hans, Nicole Maalste, and "The Bastiaans Method of Drug-Assisted Therapy." Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies 9(1999): 3-9. <http://www.maps.org/news-letters/v09n2/09203maa.html>.

"Schizophrenia." National Institute of Mental Health. National Institutes of Health. 19 Mar 2008 <http://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/schizophrenia/complete-publication.shtml#pub1>.

Simmons, James, Stanley Leiken, Ivar Lovaas, Benson Schaeffer, and Bernard Perloff. "Modification of Autistic Behavior with LSD-25." American Journal of Psychiatry. (1966): 1201-1211.

"What is Autism? An Overview" Autism Speaks. Autism Speaks (tm). 19 Mar 2008 <http://www.autismspeaks.org/whatisit/index.php>.
