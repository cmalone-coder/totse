This isn't the first time I've heard of someone with ADD not experiencing obtrusive effects from cocaine, though with the quality running around these days it's hard to deduce a firm opinion on whether or not it's truth. That said, like others have mentioned, cocaine is very subtle, it tends to act like an "on" switch for people and I think the euphoria experienced is largely dependent on the individual taking it.

For instance, I'm somewhat introverted, so the extroversion qualities given by cocaine provide a lot of the euphoria I'm looking for. I think a lot of people are looking for euphoria akin to the rush provided by opiates through cocaine, but the fact of the matter is, it's just not that kind of high. The euphoria, in my experience, comes from its ability to heighten whatever I'm already experiencing. Say, for example, I'm jamming some music with some friends and already have a decent amount of euphoria and happiness, if cocaine is added it just takes it to the next level in a subtle way.

If I'm fooling around with a female and, again, there is already some "reward" chemicals firing, it heightens it immensely. If I just sat here and did cocaine, I would get all the effects such as numbing of the face, feeling content and at peace, etc, but the rush would be much smaller than if I were doing something more fun.

It, for me (and I a/b extract fairly high quality product) is like an on switch. It puts me in that zone where everything I do is excellent, I'm just flowing perfectly with whatever is happening and THAT gives me incredible euphoria. Inhibitions are lost, so THAT gives me euphoria. The drug alone, does not provide me with an instant euphoric rush and I think looking at it in that way is a mistake. It makes me want to do "things" that already give me pleasure and it heightens those activities.
