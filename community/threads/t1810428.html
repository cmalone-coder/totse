I think you should step back and really consider the possibility that only you and those around you control your life, and that there's no supreme being watching over you. Ideally, that would be the case, it's always nice to think that you're being watched and protected by an all-knowing, all-loving being... but it doesn't look like that's what's goin on. If you're waiting for a hand of God to pull you through your current situation and help you out, don't hold your breath. Instead, take it upon yourself to pull through and come out on top. I suggest you find the strength and courage from within yourself, because only then will you feel true accomplishment, and in essence much better than you feel now. It may seem like you cannot do this all by yourself.. and if that is the case, look to those around you for guidance. Doing this will get you an immediate response, and you cannot get that from God while praying every night. People will answer your prayers, God won't.

However, I know you do have the power to help yourself, and to find this power you have to have faith in yourself. Believe in yourself. Grasp this situation by the throat, and look it dead in the eyes; make it work out the way you want to. You do have the power. Only you can find it amongst yourself and use it to it's fullest potential.

While "finding Jesus" (and like experiences) do seem to make many people very happy and "solve" their problems, I think most of it is a mask. Honestly, I don't think "finding god" rids you of problems, whether you've truly found god or whether you just want to. It may give you something to look forward to and/or something to blame your misfortune on, but other than that, I don't really think it does anything but cause a sort of "god high".

I don't discourage your quest for faith and from what you've said it sounds like you naturally sort of believe in god only you've been disappointed in his treatment of neglect. I think this is where your problem lies. If god exists he doesn't owe you anything, god might even look at us as if we were cockroaches - there's no sense in trying to communicate with a cockroach, now is there?

I think you should try to stop personafying god and stop expecting things from him, even help.

If you're really down about it, there's one thing I always say:

"The only obstacle god throws you that you can't jump is death."

Cheers,
