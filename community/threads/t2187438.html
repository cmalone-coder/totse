For me my golden rules are as followed..


1. Never snitch.

2. Loose Lips Sink Ships. It's probably human nature to want to brag and shit after you hit a sweet lick. People runnin their mouth sometimes get caught this way.

3. Don't talk to police. Don't disrespect police. If they have you pulled over or questionin you don't be an asshole, if your atleast respectul you may catch a break. If they ask to interogate you for some licks you may or may not have hit, don't do it without the presence of a lawyer. IF you dont have to do it, don't. They record that shit and will talk to you for hours waiting for you to slip up or make slight changes to your story. It's best to remain silent. If they have enough shit on you they'd arrest you, your just making their job easy.

4. Weigh Risk vs Reward. And as someone else says no the law. Ask yourself is it worth it to make a few g's doin this then get locked up for 10 years.. hell in 10 years you could make far more than a few g's doin low risk shit.

5. Do it yourself. Then theres no one to tell on you if shit gets deep. Sometimes you need someoen to help you move some shit but if you don't have to dont do it. Its more money for you anyway.

6. Don't get greedy. Greed gets people caught.

7. Have patience.

8. Have a good game plan, don't go into shit half assed. Think. Think of all possible things that can go wrong, think of all possible ways to get away if the need arises.

9. Make sure no ones home during burglaries, it turns it into a home invasion.. you get ALOT more time. Once inside the first thing you want to do is unlock every door open every window so you can get out of there fast.

10. Don't hold onto stolen shit for too long, if police suspect you and they get a warrant your ass is grass. No matter how bad that tv or computer might be, aint worth the charge.

11. Don't deal with in the state where you did the shit pawnshops on hot items. I've broke this rule before once or twice but thankfully was lucky. Police have a database all they have to do is search for the item that was stolen and boom your busted.

12. Don't carry drugs on you if you dont have to. 11 months in jail aint fun for just havin 1 pill.

13. IF your doing dirty with someone. And it gets a bit to hot, say you get popped or they try to interview..
