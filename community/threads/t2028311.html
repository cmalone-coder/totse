Re: $ by google adsense
Waste of time, don't even bother thinking about it.

Alarms will be going off around your account because of high clickthrough ratio, multiple clicks per IP and mostly public proxy users.

Also, if you somehow managed to fool them, the value of each click would drop sharply as none of them convert to goals at the advertisers website (purchases, sign ups or whatever they're trying to publicize).

I'm an honest user and have made very little with it. Take the last three days for example. I've had over 7,000 hits to an article on one of my websites which has resulted in only 8 clicks on Google Adsense (I prefer to make them unobtrusive, could probably triple that if I lost all self respect). Those clicks converted to about $12.

In short: Google adsense is a waste of time for frauds, and it's not a get rich quick scheme for legit publishers either.
