Rules of engagement, rules for a lasting relationship.
I here a lot shit on this site coming from both the men and women here about what they expect in a relationship regarding sex. Most of it I find to be rediculous, and if you really love the person, you're setting yourselves up for failure, which is what the other threads are usually about. So lets create a code of rules for relationships as a guide for people to keep themselves and their partners happy. This is only dealing with sex right now, but maybe a large set of rules could be created and unified over time.

One big thing that can't exist and both partners should come to terms with is Jealousy. This ruins relationships, as it often pushes your partner to the people you're Jealous about. Once you read more about the rules and conduct I suggest, it'll be clear why jealousy cannot exist in healthy relationship. I do recognize their is reasonable jealousy, but when it starts controlling your behavior, there will be problems.

Here is a short list of rules that my wife and I have adopted.

The main theme is compromise, and be reasonable about it. Your purpose in a relationship is to make your partner happy, and to be happy. No particular order here.

1. Fidelity. Most people don't see any option but monogamy. This can be good, but it's also a relationship ender if certain criteria can't be maintained. One thing that has to go hand and hand with monogamy, is that you have to satisfy your partner within reason. Some things you can set in stone, and others should be written in the sand as you travel through life. (a) Establish a minimum for sex. I find 2-3 is reasonable, as most people can handle 1-2 days without sex, but make your own number as some people need more or less. (b) What you're willing to do in the bedroom. This may change as years pass and monotony drives new experiences, but be clear about what you're comfortable with, and don't freak out if asked to do something you aren't sure about, discuss. It could cause some problems if you find out your husband/wife has an anal fetish, and it repulses you. (c) If fidelity isn't for you, decide on rules of engagement with other partners with regards to respecting your partner and their health. Be clear about this if you don't believe in fidelity, although sometimes this evolves over time.

Rules of broken contract. Life isn't what you think it should be all the time, accept this. (a) You don't want sex anymore, or less than once a week or bi-weekly. Your partner might not like this, and you are going to have to compromise to make them happy, even if it means a third party. However, if you didn't have reasonable cause to want less sex, you can't get your own second partner unless its mutually decided. Your partner should let you though if no other options are available. Living in contempt for lack of sex equals divorce, or they'll just cheat on you and destroy your trust, which also equals divorce. (b) Possibly you or your partner have developed new tastes. This could be a range of fetishes. If you don't wish to take part or your partner isn't willing, you or your partner should weigh your willingness against letting the other person engage with a third party. Make the decision while keeping in mind that you should keep each other happy in life. (c) If you've decided that you want to be in an open relationship and then retract later, you're breaking the contract. You may find a compromise in this situation through discussion and it might not be a big deal to your partner, or you may have to live with them continuing to have other partners.

2. Third party partners. When choosing a third party partner, you must give respect to your current relationship. They must recognize that you are in a relationship, and not play any malicious role in trying to break that union down. I suggest against prostitutes, as well as random women or men from the bar. Your partners should be comfortable with each other, and a contract of non-competition should be agreed on. Essentially that means that is the third party breaks the rules, they have to leave, respecting the original union.

There will be some added complication with the second wife as I'll probably have a couple kids with her as well as my current wife in a few years time.
