My parents are jewish and I was raised jewish, however, before my father settled down he and my mother studied "the occult" in great depth. My father was even in a coven and when I was a little kid (I can't remember I was so young, about three or four) he started teaching me tarot, basic rituals, how to read palms. They would set up a stand for me at fairs, parades, or just at the park or something and I would read peoples fortune.

What I'm getting at is that I do think there are people who are more psychic than others but most of that stuff can be taught. If you have a trained eye/know what to look for you can tell a lot about a person by their appearances. The lines in your hands and face form a particular way due to several different factors. This can tell a lot about not only a persons upbringing, past, birth (even birthday), their jobs, education, even day to day life. You can tell things by evidence of bones that have been broken, calluses, scars, clothesing, condition of teeth, accent, age, etc...

When you fully assess somebody like this you can often generate what trained psychics call a "net". You start off with this physical blueprint and you form a net of questions that can pull more information out of them so you can create a more accurate map of them.

It can often be eerily accurate. So, I'm not saying this isn't a real study or science, but I wouldn't put trust my spirituality with a phone psychic.
