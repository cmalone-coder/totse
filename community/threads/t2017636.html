You need three things to post.

Ram CPU and Video Card. I would check those first.

If not that then pull the battery and check that it is good and keep it out long enough to reset the bios.

I would check the PSU and see that it is delivering power to the MB and drives.

The Hard drive is good, right?

And make sure the monitor is good. It could be booting and the monitor does not work.

You might as well post the MB brand and your bios too. Someone will say that we need that info.

No beeps is a code for some machines that the MB is faulty.

Also a weak battery will not hold the cmos data.

Re: trouble with newly built computer
Here is a basic troubleshoot that I found. If you want to try it.


and now a basic troubleshooting for a Dead PC (No video or beep codes):

1. first thing, disconnect (or remove) all external devices such as Drives, video card, or PCI add on cards. make sure you disconnect all power to these devices and unplug from the Mobo side.

2. now power up system and see if you a beep code error.

a. if you get a beep code error, then reinstall your video card. if it does not POST, then your most likely suspect is the video card. if it does POST, then one of your Add on cards or the external drives are the problem and it is just a process of reconnecting each one at a time till you find the one that is causing the problem.

b. If you do not get a beep code, Then remove your Ram. If the system gives a beep code, then your most likely suspect is the Ram. If it does not give a beep code, then you have a bad Mobo, cpu or Power supply.

This is just a basic and simple troubleshooting procedure and 80% of the time will find what is causing the problem.
