Re: best liquid for flamethrower?
Super soaker flamethrower --> Bad Idea. Do know that, especially with alcohol-based fuels and other things of high volatility, there is a great danger of blowback. This means fire going backwards towards the nozzle from the ignition source. Normally one would say that's ok due to the constant stream of fuel coming out, but think about this: you have a plastic nozzle. In that intense heat, it will melt quickly and allow fire to pass through and suddenly your fuel tank will be on fire. Several litres of burning methanol is NOT the thing you want on your arm and chest.
