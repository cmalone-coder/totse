I miss the life what killed me.
The first drug I ever did was heroin and Ive always had a personal little joke
"i draw the line at heroin, the starting line! One line at a time!"
But that was 4 years ago. since then I've tried pretty much everything and had gotten myself a pretty nasty coke/dope habit IV.
Drugs had made me do so many things I regret yet i still chased it like it was the last light around in.a world of darkness.
I've Od'ed a few times, gone to detox, rehab and back and done just about every fucked up thing you can imagine but I still loved the shit. I loved the life that was killing me. I loved the oblivion. I loved the taste and the bloom of blood the rush the not caring about anything in the world but feeling so good you wouldnt give a shit if you died.

The last time i used hard drugs was 7 months ago. I got to the end of my road, broke and homeless in the dead of winter shivering with dope sickbness just comming down from coke that caused me psychosis. I jumped out a 3 story window, not really to end my life but just becaues there was nothing else I would do at the moment. Fortunately, I only broke a few vertebras and ever since then I've been on methadone. I thought I was done with the life and all it's shit and that I was reforme and lookingb forward, ready to change and live a new life. I repaired my broken relationship with my parents and since then have been trying my best at being a good daughter, a nice well mannerd person and just about everything i was not when i was using.

But the urge is back. I dream about using just about every night and I'm fiending. I already have a plan on how i wld be able to get some coke..it's too easy.
and this has been killing me. It would mean going back on everything I've been trying for for the last half year, and I would again betray the trust my mother has finally granted me again. I feel so guilty but yet the urge whispers in my ear and tortures me everyday. I dont see away out.

I'm at wits end, split by who i want to be, who i used to be and who I am.

It's the hardest thing in the world, quiting, I'm not sure if I can do it.
