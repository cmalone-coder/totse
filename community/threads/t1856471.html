SWIMs SS is pretty good, and awsome price, but SWIM still likes to tone wash and recrystalize for purity and taste.

The recrystalize (recry for short) always seems to produce a substance that is oily, salty, powdery, anything to piss SWIM off. SWIM has tried: Dual chem recry, H20 boil and freeze recry, Iso alchohol recry, and the DH20 slow evap (7 days and still no crystals, even threw in some from the stash to try and start a reaction:Nada)SWIM even tried the "cleaning street meth" forum. here is what swim has done so far to several SS batches to try recry:

A - took crystals and added dh20 to them stirred till disolved. put on heat till boil, then lowered to warm till almost evaped, then dried with hair dryer.

result: powdery substance that is oily when wet, dries in clumps, when pinched clumps break to slaty powder. The high is the same as before recry just not the crystals.

B- took crystals added dry Iso alchohol, stirred and had to heat to disolve everything, warm heat till almost gone, then dried with hair dryer.

result: same as above.

C- Took crystals put in coffee filter and washed with ice cold acetone, took crystals and dried with hair dryer, put in dh20, boiled, warm till evap, dried with hair dryer.

result: same as above.

D- Took crystals added Iso alchohol and a few drops of acetone, boiled, turned down to warm for an hour. Put in freezer over night.

result: same as above but with a few crystals on side of evap dish. They were perfectly clear, about 1/4 " wide, and flat.

NOTES: all chems are dried, SS (street stuff) is in 1/2" size crystals when start. Very hard crystals, nice stuff. The crystals are yellowish, to milky white, but never crystal clear (no pun intended).
