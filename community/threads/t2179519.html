Organising an Outdoor Rave
Some of you might remember the thread I made about throwing a small sized rave somewhere indoors for this winter. And I still am, it will just be a more privatized one. (Like 50 people). But as for the larger event I was thinking... "hey, why not go all out?" So I was like hey... why not throw a real rave.

When winter is over and it's no longer muddy out I plan to organize a rave to be thrown somewhere in Western/West Central Massachusetts. It'll be an outdoor event and I'm almost 100% positive that I can nail a good open field for free, it is Western Mass after all...

Music: I'll be borrowing sound equipment from my friends and renting anything else speaker wise we need. For DJs I'm going to post up the event on forums like ravelinks and thenewrave order. I've I get any responses like other rave organizers I've talked to got, I'll pretty much have DJs flocking to me wanting to play for little to no money. Especially if it's out here in Northeast where the rave scene is almost dead.

Lighting: Renting professional equipment and also several/one beast generator.

Location: If you know anything about Western or West Central Massachusetts, you'd know the area is very unpopulated. I know a lot of people who own copious amounts of land in the middle of nowhere, I'm pretty sure finding a location won't be hard. Plan to get liability insurance as well so nothing legal... well of that nature... goes wrong.

Advertisement: Posting thousands of fliers around my half of the state, on craigslist, getting in contact with friends at college (I'm 17) to put up a print out of said flyer places, and on web forums. The information on the fliers/notices will provide prices/an e-mail address to send to and become apart of a mailing list and a number of a hotline placed on a prepaid cellphone. Come the midnight before the rave, we'll e-mail everyone where it is and put up a voice mail message on the phone explaining the same.

We plan to get the party started around 11-Midnight and have it go until sun rise.

With some effort and a few million phonecalls this should work right? For signing off on stuff my friend will do it. Is there anything I have not considered or things I should do differently? Comments? Would any of you come to this event? (If you're in the Northeast why not, come on, it's pretty much a totse rave)

EDIT: Oh yeah- to pay for this all we plan on charging $10 at the "door". We'll obviously fence off a huge area. For that I got mad fence connections.

Re: Organising an Outdoor Rave
Start off small & move up.

50 people & you can use car sub woofer setups with 12V car amps (4 speakers).
Just keep adding to it.
Like, 3 double subs & 4 speaker surround sound (times 3)

& chuck in a 240 V AC 700watt Generator & throw in a home stereo or large outdoor speakers & amps.

Lights, Strobes & a Laser show made from laser pointers & mirrors (there kits for this & you can buy the whole setup for cheap somewhere.)
Bubble machines & smoke machines too.

You can get 12 V spotlights in many colours & the cheap 'police lights' as well.

At about this point you need to sort out shit houses, dig a big hole & throw in some lime & cover it in before leaving.
Get a small drum & cut out the bottom end & a smaller hole in the other end & screw a dunny seat to it. Use metal pegs to hold it in place.
Use a shitty old tent as a room for this.

For the guys a urinal is better as its quick & leaves the dunnys to the Girls.
I saw a setup where they got a long tarp & wired it to a fence in a 'U' shape but 30 feet long, one end lower so the piss runs into a hole or something.

Its something I want to do when we get around to it, people are overseas now. Theres a tunnel a way from here that could be used, we have been there to check it out. Theres other facilitys around one end of it, car parking, water, shit house etc.

Yeah, go for it.
