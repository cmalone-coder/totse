Synthetic THC substitute
So, I was talking to a friend a few days back, and he mentions that a guy he knows is doing pharmacology in university, and is looking to sell some relatively unknown research chemical that he's just ordered in from China.

Talking to the guy who was selling it, I was assured that it was similar in effects (and to some degree, structure) to this. The dosage, when vaporised, ranges between 1 and 2 milligrams, basically meaning, the smallest quantity that it's easy to place into a pipe to vape.

The chemical name is: napthalen-1-yl (1-pentyl-1H-indol-3-yl) methanone according to the guy I attained it off. This substance, being structurally distinct to THC, is of questionable legality (meaning most likely not going to cause legal trouble unless it becomes more mainstream) in NZ, with it's present level of unknowness. It's a yellow crystal, which smells slightly plasticy (not very nice tasting smoke either). An image of the substance that I recieved is here:http://i135.photobucket.com/albums/q148/dumbrella/DSC02434.jpg

Since getting the substance, I've tried it twice, both with very similar effects to smoking bud. I vapourized it out of a lightbulb vape, but since have purchased a meth pipe (oh the looks from shop owner when I bought that, being 18 and all) From what it's been described to me like, the high off this stuff is more similar to vapeing pure thc crystal (budder), because the other cannabitoids in marijuana change how the high acts (leading to a more strong body high).

The high is very lacking in the body load (stonedness) of smoking weed, and more focussed on the mental side. When I smoke it, I feel extrememly calm and relaxed mentally, but still physically able, and far more coordinated than when normally stoned. The high lasts longer too, I was still feeling strong effects at T+2 hours.

Has anyone here had any experience with similar or the same research chemical? I've been looking over Google, but there really is very little information on chemicals in this family. The guy who I bought it off has developed his own substance, which he intends to get produced by a chemical manufacturer, and market as soon as he can afford to fund it (through selling this stuff).

That's all for now, any questions, ask, I'll do my best to answer. Just thought the community would like some information on this in case anyone else has run across it. I'd definitely recommend giving it a try, as it seems to be very similar in effects to weed, with no noticeable unpleasent side effects
