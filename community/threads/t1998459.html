^Except that goes against known specs for the 426.

It isn't a false reading, it is just a old and shitty copy.

If you were to buy a crate engine, made in the identical image of the mighty 426 Hemi, it would make 465 Horsepower, and 486 ft. lbs of torque. ( P5249667AB*) If you cross reference these numbers with other published specs (real ones, not the factory rating crap) you will see that the Hemi makes all of its horsepower (in stock form) at or shortly after 4,000RPM.

Just like on that graph.
