Wellbutrin XL with Adderall IR
Has anyone else here had experience with this combination, either recreationally or medically? I'm prescribed 300 mg Wellbutrin daily and 30 mg worth of Adderall IR (2x 15s). I always take my Wellbutrin immediately upon waking, and usually wait about half an hour to an hour before having an Adderall.

Now I'm aware that the Wellbutrin potentiates the Adderall; the thing is, as I take a once daily dosing of the bupropion via XL formulation, it releases throughout the day in spaced intervals. Pharmacologically speaking, considering the differences in XR/SR/XL etc., do you think this 300 mg is delivered in a smooth, once hourly amount (ala Fentanyl patches) or in two 50% shots spaced a set time apart (ala Adderall XR)?

The reason I ask is because throughout the day, I notice what seem like "boosts" to the Adderall's effects; I can take 30 mg IR in the morning, do some things, get past the initial stimulation and into the more mellow, focused part of the dose, and then at times I suddenly feel boosted back up, like I just bumped another 5 or 10 mg. Not "tweaking" style, of course, but just closer to the feeling of the stimulation I get at the beginning of a dose or when using intranasally.

At times this even happens towards the ass-end of the IR duration, like at 4-6 hours or so when it's usually wearing off.

Does anyone think this could be caused by the Wellbutrin XL releasing small amounts of bupropion at different intervals which consequently "pull up" the amphetamine a bit? In a way it makes sense to me, from a medical standpoint, but naturally I still have my doubts.

From wikipedia:


"Bupropion (INN; previously known as amfebutamone,[1] Wellbutrin, Zyban) is an atypical antidepressant that acts as a norepinephrine and dopamine reuptake inhibitor...Bupropion belongs to the chemical class of aminoketones and is similar in structure to the stimulant cathinone, to the anorectic diethylpropion, and to phenethylamines in general."

So it prevents the reuptake of norepinephrine which is basically adrenaline, and dopamine which is the "feel good" neurotransmitter many "street drugs" operate on. Thus "trapping" them for longer than they would normally persist.

I take Wellbutrin XL(Well the teva generic). It comes in tablet form, which means it has no mechanism for a "controlled release" of bupropion. Therefore it probably just delivers a steady dose over the course of the day. When I say steady, thats the average....there's gonna be peaks and valleys in the release of the buproprion from various factors like metabolism ect.
