Questions about Needle Exchanges and NA meetings
First of all, does anyone know how to do searches for Needle Exchange programs? Normally I can go pick up a 10 pack down the street at my Walgeens for 2.65 a piece but they claimed I was the only one buying 10 packs and they couldn't split up their boxes anymore for me. AKA they decided to stop selling small amounts to a junky because they are assholes.

At the moment I am jobless so don't ask me why I can't fork up $15 for a 100 pack and that's too much damn money for too damn needles. A 10 pack will last me a month or so at least.

I've heard that Walmart does 10 packs, and even does 1 packs for like 10 cents. Is this true and if so how sure
are you that it's a nation wide deal? As in not exclusive to your area/walmart.


Second part of this message is NA meetings (narcotics anon). What do you think about them? Have you been to one home group or a bunch? I got fired from my job like 4-5 months ago because someone saw me throw a bag away, retrieved it and found my rigs and an old spoon and stuff, called my boss who in turn called the police. Judge didn't believe me that I was throwing them away, gave me 60 hours comm service, 10 hours of ADEP and weekyl NA meetings for a year.

At first I was sort of bumming the meetings but I have found things in the meetings I apprecate. For one, hugging all the good looking chicks is pretty cool and hugging the older ladies gives me that warm tingly feeling like giving your ma a hug.

There is some corny shit of course too, like how everything repeats things at the same time when reading the general message or when thanking someone for sharing. A bit too cult like for me at times.

I am the type who in my mind I don't need rehab or methadone or anything to quit opiates. If I am going to quit it has to be on my own. The NA meetings have given me a better grasp on what it truly takes it quit and the mentality you adopt to aid your efforts (one day at a time, one minute, one second, one hour, one day at a time you can't look at it any other way).

Overall I'd say the meetings are pretty corny and fun to get nodded the fuck out prior to going. I've met a couple of cool dudes and a few real cool chicks and these are the closest thing I've got to a sober support system. My only friends are either worse into addiction than I am or on the other side of the field serving the shit.. which makes it fuckin impossible to avoid unless I want to stay at home all day every day.

The only things I can say I truly took away from NA is a less stigma oriented perspective of my problem. Yeah, it is my fault for getting to this point but at the same time I have a disease that doesn't make it any easier for me and my willpower. I feel like I am no longer demonized when connotated with the Addict now. Secondly, it has prompted me to begin cutting doses down. I try to only IV twice a day and my limit is three times. Lastly it taught me I surround myself with some pretty shitty people, I was in shock with how much these people cared about me, how much they wanted to see me succeed when they don't even know me. The compassion in their hugs, despite how fruity I find it hugging guy after guy after guy (i try and sit near the young honeys). Without NA i think I wouldn't be nearly as mentally progressed as I am.
