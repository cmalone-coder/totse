Life sucks...
An overview of my pitiful situation:

Ok, I am 15 years old, and never have been in a relationship, and I am usually to afraid to engage in social interactions. I have 1 friend (more of a neighbor actually), and I rarely see him now that he is usually at football practice.

There's this girl that I like in my math class, but I am usually too afraid to say anything to her, and this lack of interaction usually ends up in explicit sexual fantasies (that I act out when I am at home). This is not the first time that I like a girl but can't speak to her, but this "crush" has brought with it the nasty side-effect of "debilitating mental crashes".

That brings me to another point: the kid who sits next to me ("Larry") asked me if I wanted to go with him to the party store to buy a mask for Halloween (naturally, I was elated, because no one asks me to do anything with them). So after school, I met him and went to said store.

The school that I go to is located far away from the party store, so I used public transportation (subway system) to get there. Meanwhile, Larry asked me if I was going to watch High School Musical 3 when it was released in theaters (Oct. 24), and I told him that I wasn't planning to such such an idiotic film. I also told him that he should not go to watch it because he will be the only 15 year old boy in a crowd of preteen girls. He told me that he was going to watch the movie with Lauren (the girl I like).

Immediately, I felt sad and discomforted. How could I have been so blind? Now there are no chances of me talking to Lauren, not even getting into the "friend zone"!

I tried hard to avoid crying, so I just pretended that it did not matter to me.

After leaving the party store, Larry ran to the nearby bus stop (to go home). I walked in the opposite direction, and immediately I felt horrible. I started crying, and I felt all of the horrors in my life (so to speak). It suddenly became apparent to me; all of the years of loneliness and social rejection. I realized how much of a loser I really am, and how I have no reason to live anymore.

The train ride back home was a particularly melancholy one. I felt that I would be much happier if I was put out of existence. I am a failure, and how everyone is better off then I am (later on, I realized that there are people who are in much worse situations than I am).

What should I do, Totse? I have to end my negative thoughts and stop thinking of myself as an unwanted animal.

If you did not feel like reading what I have said:

TL/DR: The girl I like has now caused me to become a very sad and emotional child. I feel like shit now...
