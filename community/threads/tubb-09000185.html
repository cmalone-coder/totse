When I 1st had that super-scary, can't move, seems like you'll die if you don't move, sleep thing, I really was terrified and wanted to know what it was and if anyone else had ever had it and what they did.
In fact I was looking up websites about sleep disorders the night I discovered totse.
There seemed to be little or no hard facts or scientific data about it then, at least that I could search after search my way to, but there were a few sights where people were very excited to share their experiences, very similar to the ones described in this and the paralysis thread(those about paralysis)
Anyway I asked one of my closest friends, well he was almost my ex, but I respected his opinions in many areas..I asked him what was this thing I described and he replied matter-of-factly "Oh that was the darkside trying to take you down" Well I didn't like that answer one bit and didn't readily believe that he was correct.But he, with no formal education in the sleep disorder field had a very definite and candid perception of the sort of episode I was referring to.
All I can add is that over time the episodes themselves came to represent something most definitely undesirable to me, to say it was evil, I don't know if I'd go that far, but it was something that I was able to learn to consciously counter, not eliminate, but deal with.
To try and sum it up I did what I could, with my sluggish(as opposed to the sub-conscious) waking mind and reasoned that if it was about good and evil, then good would be what I wanted to will to prevail.

My free advice ,lest I be reprimanded for charging 2cents, is to consider that the most desirable state of being, I would think, would be one in which you were aware, as fully aware, as possible, of life, in yourself and your surroundings.

Having said that, there will always be plenty of good and plenty of evil to go around and no one is going to affect that reality in this existence.

The only ,and ideally sufficient, thing anyone can do on a personal level is to acknowledge both good and evil, but make a descision as to which they hold most affection for, in other words ingoring mr. evil walking by you will not destroy him but choosing not to stop and embrace him or go out of your way to make chit chat will not encourage him. But to live in fear of him gives energy to that side of things simply by dignifying it with that fear.
If you see good-quality evil, acknowledge it, you don't have to cheer it or get it's autograph, simply to acknowledge it will suffice. Not acknowledging it will not make it go away.
Likewise if you do pause and greet goodness when it is overtly apparent then it is nourished with your energy, expended in a positive way-your choice......
One thing I found helpful was to use my waking imagination to generate worst case scenarios for day to day involvments and situations, knowing that I embodied the ability to create a suitably formidable darkness in my own life, and choosing not to, made it more possible to finally tell a dream demon of sorts that I was pretty sure he was a dream, one of mine in fact, and bid him go ahead and exact whatever horror was imminent on me, where upon I would wake up.

It has been a long time since any of that stuff has been going on in my life, but when it did, it was a very serious source of discontent, but also, in a way, it provided new experiences that in retrospect were interesting, exciting and novel as opposed to boring,mundane and tedious.
When I figured out how to initiate sleep paralysis, I never did it again... I don't like that shit.

~nuff said, more than that I suppose. So take away my birthday, there was a time I wanted just to have a chance to talk to others who'd had this happen to them, take care and don't dance with the darkness. but don't fear it either.

I've got it, shine the darkness! ok I'm done~
