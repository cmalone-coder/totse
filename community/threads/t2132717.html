Drugs don't get me high anymore (rant)
I'm sorry totse, i don't normally rant about anything. I don't normally post either. But i'm curious as to everyone else's experiences.

I've done little psychedelic drugs like weed and DXM and LSD for about 4 years now, and RX stuff for like 2 years, although not a lot. I've taken special precautions not to get too into anything and i've always managed to keep myself in good standing. I never wanted to move on to harder street drugs and I don't think I will.

But my problem is, I don't get high like i used to on RX stuff or DXM. LSD and weed still work like charms of course. But everything else seems so boring, like taking oxy. I could rail 2 oxy (which is good for my tolerance lvl (80mg)) and I would feel the same, but I don't find it as good. I couldn't just sit there high on oxy or DXM for hours (esp. DXM, it's so boring even at like 1.2 grams).

Does the magic ever come back? Will I ever be content with these drugs again and just sitting around "tripping" or "nodding"? Or have I just grown out of them?

I'm really blazed right now and it always makes me think of things a lot differently from when sober, or any other drug for that matter.

The reason i want to ask BLTC is because i know a lot of you guys do some crazier drugs than me. So does it get you high anymore? Do you still have times that feel "epic"? Even after X years doing the same drugs?

Weed is the only drug that makes any sense to do anymore..
