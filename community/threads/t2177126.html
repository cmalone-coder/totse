Chillin really does get you surprisingly high. It is alot like weed, but not nearly as enjoyable. It has all the general 'fucked up' sensations of weed but lacks the complexity of the high that marijuana provides. It certainly feels like there is something missing to the high (as do all the legit legal smokeables) but chillin does have a few unique effects of its own. Chillin for some reason really messes up my vision, especially when I' trying to read. It also has a completely different body high, it effects your coordination and balance more so than any of the spices. It tastes like shit (worst tasting out of the spices and spice-a-likes), doesn't last too long, burns really fast, and is a tad harsh, but it definitely produces a powerful high. Weed and chillin are two completely different substances with distinct differences in effect. While they do have similarities, it is pointless to compare the two. Chillin or any of the other legit legal smokeables will never hold a candle to weed, but they get you high and are legal, be happy with that.
