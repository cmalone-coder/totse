Re: Need some bulk food ideas..
Drop the potatoes and add something like lentils, chick peas or fava beans (or a one pound bag of each), for that extra bit of (reasonably cheap) protein. Also, switch white rice with golden rice. For breakfasts, I'm personally very partial to a bowl of steel-cut oats and chopped dried fruits (dates, figs, apricots, bananas etc.) with a topping of honey or maple syrup. A tin of oats, a (large-ish) bag of dried fruit mix and a jar of honey/bottle of MS (darker is better) should do.

Re: Need some bulk food ideas..
Everything listed buy in bulk 1lb or more at bulk stores.
  • Flour (20lbs min)
  • Baking Soda
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Rice
  • Oats (20lbs min)
  • Vegtable Oil
  • Ramen
  • Beans (20lbs min)
  • Flak steak
  • Cheap, tough cuts of Beef
  • Bacon
  • Chicken
  • Potatoes
Now all of these things are the food basics, you can make a lot of meals out of these, for the cheap beef, buy an old fashion hand meat grinder, and grind that shit into ground beef, or use a meat hammer and beat the living shit out of it, and make it into stew. Keep beef and chicken bones (don't buy deboned things) and make broth. Everything should be stored in the freezer or in a cold, dark, dry place, the rice, oats, beans, flour, should be placed in airtight garbage can (new plastic ones, of coarse). Also pick up a cook book or two. Good luck!
