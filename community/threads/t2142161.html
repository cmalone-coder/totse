Man smashed police station windows to get locked up
15th July 2008, 8:30 WST

A 32-year-old man who pleaded guilty to smashing the front windows of Rockingham police station with a tomahawk on Sunday, causing $6000 damage, was sentenced yesterday to six months jail.

Jason Paul Elam, of Safety Bay, appeared in Rockingham Magistrate’s Court charged with one count of criminal damage after police arrested him about 5.50pm on Sunday.

Police prosecutor Sgt Kevin Woldseth said Elam had smashed 12 windows of the police station before the tomahawk became stuck in one of the panes and he was arrested.

He told the court Elam had a similar conviction for damaging the Geraldton police station last year.

When Magistrate Robert Lawrence asked Elam why he had done it, he said: “It was something to do.”

Elam said he had been drinking since 10am on Sunday, had got the tomahawk from his mother’s cupboard and caught the bus to the police station.

A Community Corrections Office representative said he committed the offence because he needed somewhere to stay and knew he would be held in custody.

His alcohol and amphetamine use was of some concern and he was epileptic, she said. She suggested the magistrate remand the matter for a psychological report.

“He expresses no remorse but only regrets he has not been able to have any cigarettes since his arrest,” she said.

Mr Lawrence said Elam had some underlying issue with authority but that it was not necessary to obtain presentence and psychological reports.

“You have absolutely no remorse whatsoever for your acions,” he said. “Your actions on this occasion demonstrate a course of conduct that was premeditated.”
