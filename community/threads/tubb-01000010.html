Banners for totse.com
I'd like to get some new banners up for totse.com. We've been using the same three banners for the past three years, and I'd really like to see something new. The banners I use were all contributed by members of the site, and I'd like to continue that tradition.

My Photoshop expertise has been limited to creating the "Read", "Know", and "Do" logos, and the version of Kai's Power Tools I used to work those up is long gone. I've tried to create a few new banners, but haven't liked anything I've made.

Do you feel artistic? Would you like to see your artwork displayed to hundreds of thousands if not millions of people? Make a banner for totse.com! Anything that passes the scrutiny of our Right and Honorable Review Board of Bad Taste (me) will be given a place along with the other totse.com banners.

All banners submitted MUST be 468 pixels wide by 60 pixels tall and include the domain name "totse.com" somewhere on the banner. You can also use the phrases "Raw Data for Raw Nerves", "A Safe Haven for Visionaries and Paranoids", make up a slogan of your own, or use none at all.

Submitting an entry does not guarantee placement. No cash or prizes will be awarded, just recognition. Entries may be submitted via email to webmaster@totse.com and must be date-stamped no later than July 4, 2087.
