Jackass: Number Three
D'ya think it'll happen?

As a long-time fan of the Jackass series and its spin-offs (Viva la Bam, Wildboyz) there are few things that could excite me more than the prospect of a third feature-length film in the series.

So here's a few points of contention.

Number Two wasn't even supposed to happen.
Probably my biggest concern as to why there will not be a third film. Two only came about because Johnny Knoxville was shooting on the set of Wildboyz with director Jeff Tremaine in Russia, and Tremaine saw Knoxville's dedication, then suggesting, "If you're going to go all-out, why don't we shoot a sequel?"

Both Jackass films garnered huge amounts of cash.
I'd say this leans towards the better possibility of a third film. Jackass: The Movie was made on a budget of $5 million, and took in nearly $80,000,000 at the box office. Few movies can claim that kind of performance, earning $16 for every $1 spent on production. Number Two also did quite well, on an $11.5 million budget and earning around $84,000,000. Paramount would be stupid to ignore these numbers.

The Howard Stern Show
On the September 25, 2007 episode of the Howard Stern show, series regular Steve-O claimed, "I was told to rest up for Jackass three which will start shooting in February." However, Steve-O is a well-known abuser of alcohol and other drugs, so I'm not sure I'll keep any faith in this statement.

So, do you think there'll be a third Jackass film?
