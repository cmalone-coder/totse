So we're driving around in my Camaro, her in the front and my best friend in the back passenger seat. All of a sudden I come around a curve and theres a truck in my lane. I slam on the brakes and they lock up. As fast as im going I hit the ditch. The car flips. If you saw my thread in Gearheads back then, you know the rest.

Anyways, if you've ever been in a crash like mine you know that everything seems to slow down. All I really remember is thinking to myself "I hope she doesn't get hurt" and putting my arm across her.

Then its back to the normal speed and we're upside down on the side of the road. First words out of my mouth are "(her name) are you alright?" Shes sobbing and my friend is moaning (broke his left orbital and sinus) Still all i'm thinking is "god i hope shes alright" and im working to get us all out of the car and some guy helps.

Forward 10 minutes- I'm sitting on the side of the road trying desperately to comfort her and figure out if shes hurt. The EMT finally gets there and looks after her.

At that moment i'm more pissed off than I've ever been.
Not because I lost my car, I could care less about it.
Not because I hurt my best friend. (I was sorry and he knew it but we have this kind of unspoken agreement that neither of us is going to beat ourselves up over the grief we cause each other)
It's because she's hurt and im to blame.

An hour later I'm at the hospital waiting to see them. I feel like shit. Its this deep horrible feeling in my gut.

I finally get to see her and it doesn't make me feel any better to see her laid out on a stretcher in a neck brace. I leave quickly because frankly i'm scared of her dad. I follow them both through x-rays and all and after everything I get to talk to her.

She tells me she's not mad and i believe her, it's sincere and even if it wasn't I wouldn't blame her. She tells me not to beat myself up over it and that it wasn't my fault and i say i'll try but i know that i'm lying for her benefit and maybe she does too.

(two days later)
I go to pick stuff out of my car and go to her house to bring her her things. She seems happier and we stand in the yard talking for two hours, but it's slow and awkward and we basically repeat the same things over and over.

I feel a bit better seeing her like that but I'm still nowhere near myself.

(two days later)
I go to my friend's house that's near hers. Shes been over a few times and i'm talking to him asking about her to make sure she's doing okay. Turns out her dad isn't letting her take her pain pills or muscle relaxers and shes having a hard time sleeping. She's real emotional and has been talking to him alot about it.

Secretly i'm pissed at him for not calling me right away and telling me but I can't stay mad because i know that the reason i wasn't told was because she didn't want to worry me.

After this we all kind of drift apart for the summer and don't see each other much. My friend called me a few days later and told me that she was doing alot better and i could tell he wasn't lying. I felt alot better and eventually got back to my normal self.

(2 weeks ago)
I wake up one morning and the first thing I think of is her. The whole day I can't get her off my mind. I realize that its these feelings I have for her resurfacing.

I know what it is, I love her (or something very close to it) , there's no getting around that, and after a few days I decide to call her.

Her cell has been disconnected. I figure she just switched services and got a new number, so i shrug it off and say ill just wait til school starts.

(today)
I show up and wait for her. She never comes and im disappointed but not many seniors come the first two days. I wouldn't have myself if i didn't have auto shop.

Later I talk to my friend (same one she lives near) And he tells me quite a tale:

Apparently her dad threatened her and she ran away. The took her car, turned off her cell (why i couldn't call) and gave away all her stuff. She's living in an apartment with a college guy and going out with him (just recently started)

I feel like shit again but even worse this time because under it all theres still this love that i have for her. I feel even worse because the wreck might have caused some of it, as i heard her and her dad were fighting alot after it. I feel that my chance might have slipped away and it feels horrible.

So heres my questions

1. Am I wrong for still feeling about her the way I do, even though she's with somebody else?
2. Why do I still feel so much remorse over what happened even though she was barely hurt?
3. Should I tell her how I feel?

I don't know how she feels about me anymore and I don't know how she feels about him.
If she really loves him than I wouldn't want to try and intervene. I'd rather be without her and have her be happy than to have her and her not be as happy.
I don't really know how she would react if i told her how i felt either. I guess it really depends on how involved she is with this guy, but i'd rather keep her as a friend and never say anything than lose her forever by making it weird.

I'm going to go offer her a ride to school because she has no car and this guy won't take her, but the only place I can is her work so im going there Saturday to talk to her and I've got to figure out what to say by then.

Please help me, I'm so fucking confused right now and I can't take feeling like this for much longer.

UPDATE: So now she's moved back in with her parents. I don't know if this means it's over with the other guy or what, but i'll be finding out soon. And shes back at school, which made me really happy because it sounded like she might have been planning to just give it up and that really bothered me.
