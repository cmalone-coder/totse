This rather upset me...
Just one of those silly little incidents of daily life but its really got me thinking about how I deal with people and the impression I make on others.

Mrs Ketch and I were just in the town for a chinese (one of those very cheap take away places where you can also 'eat in'). After we'd eaten we went over the road cos I needed to use the ATM.

Standing at the machine this very drunk short guy comes up to us and says "Ah noo ya, ye teh beek yin , whoo nay'er smiles".

For those here who don't speak Sozzled Celtic that translates as "Well hailed my good man, I have made your acquaintance before and know you are the large man who never smiles."

I'm always on edge when using an ATM anyway and if I didn't know from painful experience (I lived there for a while) that small scotch blokes tend to be as hard as nails ...

And anyway I doubt Mrs Ketch's rather fragile hold on sanity at the moment would take watching me knife someone again.

So I engaged in conversation and it turned out all he wanted was someone to roll him a ciggy, which I was happy to do.

But on the way home it really started to bug me, you know? DoI really never smile when I'm out?

Am I really such a miserable git?
