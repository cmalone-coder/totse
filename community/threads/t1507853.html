Re: She doesn't seem to care anymore
i'm really sorry to hear that. from the sounds of it you have a pretty good grasp of the situation -- good job being perceptive.

how should you feel? well, she's very far from you now and has somewhat detached, probably to protect herself. it is likely that she had feelings for you, but did not want the troubles of a long-distance relationship or the heartache of a breakup. try not to dwell on the fact that she is meeting other guys. i think she is trying to be realistic, even if it is heartless. the fact that she is treating you as less of a friend now points to the fact that she previously had romantic feelings for you. she may be starting to get involved with this other guy, and therefore does not want to get into the murky territory of "emotional cheating" or something. it's really her loss, because it sounds like you guys were great friends.

try to talk to her. what you should communicate is that you have no romantic ambitions with her whatsoever, and want to maintain a close platonic relationship like you had before. you both will be involved with other people, but your relationship as friends will not suffer. you do not need to state this directly, but communicate it through a combination of your demeanor, words, and tone.

keep in mind that it is hard to maintain closeness over distance for some people. maybe the best you can hope for is to be part of her "group of friends" you described. in order to do this, though, you have to show that you are ok with her seeing other guys, otherwise she won't confide in you about it and you will remain out of the loop. of course, if this is too difficult you don't have to keep up the friendship.
