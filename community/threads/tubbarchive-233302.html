The most noticeable character of the time, was in fact a group of characters dubbed �The Gang.� That's right, I am referring to the infamous group led by none other than Scooby-Doo himself. Who other than Scooby the great could lead a group so vigilant, so young and full of life? While at a fast glance this group of teens appears to be the picturesque representation of friendship and youth, upon further inspection this intrepid group's many flaws become extremely evident to all but those who are irreversibly retarded.

Firstly, Fred was fucking Daphnie and eventually left her to become an alcoholic. Daphnie comes to realize that she is carrying Fred's child, and Daphnie is urged to get an abortion by Fred. When Daphnie reveals the father of her baby, her parents became enraged and kicked her out of the house at the young age of sixteen. Daphnie, soon after the birth of her son Daniel, would begin a job working at a strip club, turning tricks on the side. After having been hung out to dry by her family for many years, eventually they welcomed her back under their wing after they realized the direction her life had gone after they had abandoned her. She is currently happily married and leading a good life in Seattle, Washington.

Snap back to Scooby and Shaggy, whom have been attending a liberal arts college for eight consecutive years. They made their money during this time by selling small time to freshmen at their college, dubs and dimes mostly. They made the decision to move on up, eventually, and rounded up five-hundred dollars, and picked up a QP of Matanuska Thunderfuck using their undoubtedly amazing connections to growers and big time distributors alike. Upon receiving this QP, Shaggy and Scooby couldn't help but indulge a little. After smoking an entire ounce of this amazing strain to themselves, they panicked and realized they needed to sell off the last 3 ounces at extremely high prices. After pulling some strings and selling off the rest of their QP, Shaggy and Scooby used the money they made to get a pound of high-mids. After selling off this pound gradually over several months, Shaggy installed spinners, a footprint gas pedal, a chainlink steering wheel, and a massive ornate custom hookah in the Mystery Machine. Scooby and Shaggy, being enterprising young gentlemen, grew their business rapidly. All of a sudden they're slanging ounces to to half of the college. Everyone wanted a piece of Scooby and Shaggy, until one day, it all came down. Scooby sold a quarter to a guy in a mask, who turned out to be DEA. Shaggy was all like �ZOINKS!� and ran for the window, hopping out of their third story apartment and onto the lawn. After sustaining minor injuries, Shaggy sprinted off, leaving Scooby to be incarcerated. Scooby was taken in, and bail was set at one-hundred-thousand dollars. Shaggy, meanwhile, had picked up a fake ID, and was loitering outside the courthouse, debating whether or not to bail Scooby out. Eventually, Shaggy decided to go inside, and Scooby's bail was paid for in cash. After hooking Scooby up with a fake ID, they both ran to Peru, where they were last known to be pilots for a small commercial airline.

Now, we go to Velma, the quiet one. Velma graduated first in her high school class, as can be expected of someone of her intelligence. She receives a full-ride four year scholarship to Oxford University, where she hopes to study law. Midway through her sophomore year, she begins to enter a downward spiral of depression, mostly caused due to the stress of her move and the lack of friends in her new environment. She soon began a life of masochism, and became the first Emo. However, Velma proves herself not one-hundred percent Emo in that she finally follows up and commits suicide. In the mid seventies, velma plummeted eight stories to her death from the top of her dormitory. A suicide note was left in her room, blaming all of her turmoil upon her parents forcing her into getting a good education and always placing her social life second ever since she was a child...

...Her parents were Asian...It was a very backwards adoption. The conception of Velma occurred within the uterus of the fourteen year old daughter of a lower middle class family. Due to the family's very conservative values, they refused to pay for their daughter's abortion, and put the baby up for adoption immediately after delivery. The baby was soon adopted by Tong and Zang Ling, and was initially brought up under a traditional Korean name; However, the Lings decided to change their adopted daugter's name after she was made fun of in every school in her mid-western school district. In the early sixties, Velma met Shaggy and his dog Scooby. This duo led Velma to a brief interest in psychedelics and other soft drugs, which landed her in a mutual friendship with four persons. Scooby, Freddy, Shaggy, and Daphnie.

Thus, the famous group known as �The Gang,� mascotted for by Scooby himself, was begun and ended.
