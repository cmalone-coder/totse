Are you saying you took both ecstasy and Zoloft together? You are a complete and total retard.

First, if you are a continuous SSRI (Selective Serotonin Re-uptake Inhibitor) anti-depressant taker, you won't be able to feel yourself rolling, or your 'roll' will be greatly diminished.

Why is this? I'll explain. An ecstasy experience is a 'serotonin experience' and dopamine for the most part. Ecstasy gains access to your serotonin axon terminals (storage bin) via the re-uptake valves. The re-uptake separates the axon terminals from the synapse area. When ecstasy reaches your 'unguarded' re-uptake valves, it causes the huge release of serotonin that causes you to 'roll'

People take SSRIs (Paxil, Zoloft, Prozac) because they are re-uptake inhibitors. They block or clog, if you will, the re-uptake valves, not causing serotonin (or anything else) become re-absorbed. Theory and studies imply that when serotonin is released and does not reach it's receptor, it does not get used and causes people to be depressed, have OCD symptoms, etc.

So if you are taking zoloft and ecstasy together you are wasting your pill{s} because the ecstasy cannot reach the serotonin axon terminals to cause the massive release, that causes the rolling experience.

My point is proven because you said your brain was 'fucking killed'. That feeling was probably caused by the PMA or other desinger speeds that was mixed in with your pill. You weren't rolling. But what happened?

The Zolofot wore off, and you were 'off to the races' What you explained in your second paragraph can best be described as the average rolling experience. And the reason I can say that the Zoloft wore off is that SSRI's have to be taken for weeks to have a heavy enough concentration build up before your brain can become 're-trained'.

SSRI's and ecstasy are not necessarily a bad mix, but not at the beginning, and definitely not at the middle of a roll.

Avoid mixing MAOIs and SSRIs.
This can lead to serotonin syndrome and can be dangerous.


Do not mix MAOIs with Stimulants (including MDMA).
This can lead to hypertensive crisis and can be deadly.


SSRIs in Combination with MDMA
Generally reduces the effects of the MDMA significantly.


SSRIs in Combination with Psychedelics
Generally reduce the effects of the psychedelic a bit.


MAOIs in Combination with Psychedelics
Generally increase the effects of the psychedelic significantly. Be extremely careful.


SSRIs
Strong SSRIs are significantly more common than MAOIs. Many commonly prescribed pharmaceutical anti-depressants are SSRIs, including Prozac (Fluoxetine), Paxil (Paroxetine), Zoloft (Sertraline), Celexa (Citalopram), and Desyrel (Trazodone).

MAOIs
Strong MAOIs are less common, but include prescription anti-depressants like Nardil (phenelzine), Parnate (tranylcypromine), Marplan (isocarboxazid), Eldepryl (l-deprenyl), and Aurorex or Manerix (moclobemide). Ayahuasca also contains MAOIs, generally in the form of Banisteriopsis caapi or Syrian rue (harmine and harmaline).
