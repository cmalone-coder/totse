Best Buy

Guide by: scuzzy-elo 2003

Introduction

In this text, I will share information noted about the electronics store Best Buy.

Surveillance

I have noted most cameras around the DVD, CD, games, and software sections. They keep better surveillance on these areas because a smaller item is easier to conceal than a larger item. This leads me to my next note, which is that there are few to no cameras watching the television section. This would make a good area for doing work as I will discuss later.

As for actual workers, there are a few that walk around looking for customers to help. A simple No. Im fine, will leave any worker free to go to the break room as their job of asking has been done.

Security

Most items are secured with anti theft devices. These will set off the alarms at the main doors.

There are a few personnel selected to watch the doors on either side, which looks something like this:

WDDWWDDW

W-Worker D-Door

These workers, in the stores I have visited, are usually teens that dont seem to care too much about their job. If for some reason the alarms go off, after you go through measures to prevent this, I dont think they would be too much of a problem.

Theft

Lets say that you wanted to take one of these so heavily guarded DVDs. First, you will need to make an opening device.

Materials

Razor blade (the short long type from a disposable razor)

White plastic fork (with reinforced neck)

Creation

Take a pair of scissors and cut the head of the fork off just above the first reinforcement, which should look like a horizontal line going across the neck. Now, remove one blade from the disposable razor. Take the blade and use it to cut two small slits in the centers of the two walls reinforcing the neck of the fork handle. Now wedge the blade into the slits with the sharp side pointing out. You should have a tool that crudely resembles a scalpel.

Procedure

Take your opener into your selected Best Buy, concealed in your pocket. Also have your other pocket lined with tissue.

Browse around the store a bit and make your way to the DVD section. Find one to your liking and look it over. Hold it down to your side in one hand and continue to browse the DVDs. Pick a few up and read the backs of them. This makes it look like you want to buy more.

Now, take your DVD to a corner of the television section and look around to make sure there are no workers in the area. Pull out your tool and press it firmly into the crack of the DVD case through the shrink-wrap and the label. Slide it around the crack of the DVD until there is a opening all the way around the case. Open the case, remove the disc and booklet, and stick them into your tissue-lined pocket. Now, close the case back and mash the edges of the plastic down so it looks closed from far off. Put your tool back in your pocket and go back to the DVD section. Browse through them a bit and make your way back to where yours came from. Put the DVD in the back of the row it was in and browse some more. Walk out the door.

Notes

I suggest you have a clean appearance about yourself. You will be monitored the entire time if you come in wearing your ripped jeans and Misfits t-shirt.

Alternately, you could use an X-acto knife but I found that the following could cause problems:

The X-acto has a cap and it would take more time for you to take off to use in the store.

If you kept the cap off, the point of the X-acto could stab you in the leg or stab your hand when you reach in to get it.

Could be mistaken for a weapon if searched for some reason.

With the plastic tool, you can remove the blade from the slits after use and have a cut up fork handle in your pocket, rather than a makeshift weapon. It is also hard to feel a short thin razor blade lying curved to your leg inside you pocket.
