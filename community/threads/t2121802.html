Re: Using Hallucenogens for Fun rather then Introspect.
Nothing wrong with taking hallucinogens for fun. For example, marijuana, which has weak hallucigenic properties, is often smoked by newcommers to the world of drugs because they think it gives them introspection. Then they get all they can out of that introspection, then continue using marijuana for fun, and perhaps try LSD a few times to gain more introspection while having fun with marijuana at the same time. Nothing wrong with taking LSD for fun each weekend, aslong as you understand the possible mental health risks to that. Most people say that LSD is risk free unless your family has a history of mental illness, however this is not always true when taking it each week, but because of the very few users who end up doing that, LSD is generally regarded as safe and not habit forming. But LSD is a very positive - negative drug, it can go either way. If drugs can have a negative impact on ones life, by the laws of physics, they can also have a positive impact on your life. Perhaps taking LSD each weekend will make you want to live life to the fullest, or it could make you apathetic towards social status and money aslong as you have love, etc.

I say go for it.
