quote:

how do u get the spores from ur shrooms when u do it?


The best time for picking a cubensis is right after the veil breaks on it's own. After this the cap begins to spread out and become broad, and the mushroom begins to sporulate. If you let a mushroom or two grow until the cap becomes very flat, you'll start to notice a purple substance collecting underneath. These are spores. You get a knife, and a couple of pins, and serilize them with flame, and then with alcohol. Take a half pint jar, and put tape over the innocculation holes. Place the lide on the jar, but leave it loose. Wrap the top in tinfoil and boil the jar for 15-30 minutes in water. Then let cool and remove. Place a sterilized pin in each sporulating mushroom cap. Spray the hell out of the air around you with lysol. The remove the aluminum foil from the jar. Quickly open it, hold a mushroom cap by the pin, cut it with the knife, then quickly place the cap gills down into the jar. Close it. Spray more lysol, and keep doin it for each mushroom. ALso take a shower and put on clean clothes before you do this. Let them sit for about half a day, then spray more lysol, and remove them of course handling them by the pins only. Then quickly close the jar. Put the lid on tight. You should now have beautiful spore prints at the bottom of your jar. Inject sterile water in with a sterile syring, and shake it all around. Instant jar of spore solution.

quote:

what exactly is a pressure cooker


A pressure cooker is normally just a thick metal pot you put on the stove. The lid is usually hinged on, and there is a rubber or cork seal. The lid is thick metal and sometimes has a pressure guage and spring loaded pressure valve. Other use rubber coated weights. Basically you just put a little water int it, your jars, then shut the lid and latch it. Heat it up till it boils. If it's the type with a hole and a weight, the weight must be heavy enough to support 15psi before it "jiggles" and starts letting steam escape. Get it hoter and hoter until it does jiggle, then keep it at this level for 45 minutes. Then let cool. You will have jars with a damn near 100% sterilization rate. The reason why is because the extra 15 pounds of pressure mixed with the high temperatures kills pretty much everything deep into the jars. Plus as the pressure rises, the b.p. of the water rises, getting higher temps.
