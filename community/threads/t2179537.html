Had. Z32 '89 TT t-top. Great car, best I have ever driven.

I was getting maybe 250km to a tank (compared to the 700ish I get in my current lexus). Just could not afford to keep it. Suddenly insurance also jumped from 600 in 2005 to 1800 in 2006. It's a bloody joke. Not to mention everything breaking off day by day.

If I had 15k today, cash, without doubt I would have one. Buy one in which-ever condition you can for 5-8, spend the rest doing a total overhaul of the engine, transmission and interior and maybe one could get somewhere. But if you can't invest that much, it'll just be a pain and keep dragging you down.
Also, if you are in the market, watch out alot of the "manual" TTs out there are auto-conversions. Thats a bad idea. I have yet to see a decent conversion done, most will not start anywhere but neutral. The engine is detuned and the turbos are different.

Still a great car though. Easily outclasses the GTO/3000GT and Skylines in terms of handling. Probably the cheapest car out there to get HP out of per $. Massive potential.
Or, if you are that way inclined, LS1 conversion. The engines weigh nearly the same (the LS1 is slightly lighter i believe). So it will not hurt the balance of the car. But where's the joy in that? :)
