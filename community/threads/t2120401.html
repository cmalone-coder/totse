Re: san pedro
swish out your mouth with a lindo/novo-caine solution.

I think you would still smell it though, wouldn't you? I've never tried it but it does sound promising but olfactory senses make up a great deal of what we perceive as taste (hence food loosing its flavor when you have a cold).

Alternatively, just take the ground-up plant matter and soak it in methanol, let it sit for three days, subjecting it to occasional mixing.. Filter out the liquid and save the plant matter for another extraction. Leave for two days. During these two days, have your filtrate in a casserole dish under a light, receiving an air current provided by a cheap desk fan. This should be mostly dry after one day and by day two you're good to drop your next batch of filtrate in the casserole pan (on top of the existing gunk). Once again, fill the vessel containing the cacti powder (I've used an empty wine bottle) with fresh methanol and let sit for another two days. Keep your methanol under the lamp and allow it to dry, then dump the final batch of filtrate in and allow the mess to dry for at least two days within close proximity to the light bulb and fan.

This will leave you, presumably, with the alkaloids of the cactus, with mescaline being one. Also, pending the type of cactus, I think I remember reading that in a particular species of cactus, one (some?) of the isoquinolines contained therein was an MAOI, thus increasing the potency of the mescaline. I'm not sure how reliable that source was, though.

Regardless, a (relatively) small amount of gunk should be a shit load easier to swallow than 100 capsules of powder or death flavored snot.
