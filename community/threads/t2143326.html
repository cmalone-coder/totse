You can make a great one use pipe out of a 2"x2" piece of heavy duty foil, and a soda can. Just stab a hole in the center or a little lower of the side of the can . Use the pen to enlarge the opening, about 1/4" bigger than your finger. Put the foil over your fingertip, poke you finger in the hole, fold down the excess foil over the can, then pok a few holes in the bottom of the foil bowl with the pen tip. Oh yeah, foil is metal, so if you go through metal detectors don't put the foil in your crotch , or near anything you don't want inspected at least.
