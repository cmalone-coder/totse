Re: Could someone describe a "crator maker" to me?
I've only made 2 cratermakers ever, but I think they're usually 12 gram cartriges, but almost any size will work fine. The important thing is the casing has to be thick. Anyway, flash would work fine in a casing like this, but it's kinda a waste, and it's missing the point. The metal casing was chosen as the casing because it works well with slower burning LEs. A paper casing would be better if you're going to use flash, with almost no dangerous shrapnel.

Oh yeah, to let the CO2 out, just tap a nail into it while it's held with some channel locks or pliers or something.

Re: Could someone describe a "crator maker" to me?
You can buy CO2 cartridges for carbonating seltzer water at the supermarket for a relatively cheap price.

I personally don't make COB's and never will, but I picked up a 10-pack for ~$6 USD.

Any type of CO2 cartridge can be used, because they're all made of metal, no matter what size you choose you're not sacrificing casing strength.

Krimzonpyro.com shows varying sizes of CO2 cartridges (ranging from small 'whippits' to lifevest inflaters), all appropriate for COB's.

You can use a BB gun to release the pressure, either by shooting the casing itself, or by using it up in a bb gun. (several bb guns are CO2 powered)

Personally, you shouldn't waste flash powder on a COB, just pick up some thick parallel wound tubing and some end plugs and use flash in that instead.

If you're stuck on making COB's, you might as well use black powder,

but personally I think because of the shrapnel risk posed, if you treat flash with respect, you'd be safer making flash salutes with paper tubes.

Information regarding flash salute construction:

Just cut a parallel wound tube to a length no greater than 4 times the OD of the casing.

Drill a fusehole, insert a fuse, and glue in one end cap.

I use plain elmer's glue or even carpenter's glue to glue in end plugs, no need to use epoxy or hot glue guns.

Although I've personally put gobs of hot glue directly on small piles of flash (&lt;5 grams) with no ignition,

with any aspect of pyrotechnics, you really don't want to take any chances.

Add the appropriate amount of flash, and contrary to what you might think - more isn't always better.

(In Advanced Homemade Fireworks, the literature insists that the powder should "fill no more than 1/3 of the interior area of the cracker")

Personally, I fill around 1/2 the casing with flash, and you can get equal or better reports with that much flash compared to filling the tube up. *NEVER EVER PACK FLASH*

Add flash until its about 2/3 full.

Lightly tap the tube on the table to settle the contents. (it settles a lot!)

If you drilled the fusehole in the center of the tube, you can gauge the '1/2 filled' mark pretty well.

To make inserting the last end plug considerably less messy, take any dauber (bottle rocket sticks can be used in a pinch) and line the inside with glue, then insert the plug.

Preinserting the end plugs into an empty casing can make initial insertion a lot easier.
