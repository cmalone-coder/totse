If you're looking for a legal mj alternative I suggest spice gold and/or spice diamond. I've tried both, they both feel very very similar to mj, there is something missing from both blends that I cannot quite put my finger on however. Nobody truly knows what's in the blends, obviously, they are not FDA approved or regulated in any way, so they aren't required to state every single ingredient. Word on the street is they are plant materials listed on the package sprayed somehow with THC chemical analogues.

A word on both spices and especially spice diamond however, much like vaporized THC analogues, you will feel only preliminary effects immediately after smoking, the full effects take 5-10 minutes to kick in. Don't overdo it, spice diamond especially is some strong shit, even for every day multiple times a day smokers.

I underestimated spice diamond the first time I tried it, smoked two bowls out of a bong right away. It was almost too much, had a very speedy feeling, my heart was pounding for the first hour and a half or so, it was a very intense heady stone but alongside it was an odd almost mdma-like body buzz that came in waves, more muted than mdma though, kind of like a heavy indica buzz. After the hour and a half only the heady high remained and stayed so for at least an hour or two to follow.

There are many vendors in the uk and europe for these blends, I know of only one vendor in the USA. You might also try "chillin mix" which has recently changed its name to "eclipse". I recently gave this blend a shot, strong heady high that comes on fast and hard but doesn't last more than an hour or so. The guy always has limited stock though and seems to sell out within a few days of getting a shipment.

I have tried various rc's and various "party pills" with some success. Avoid anything by the london underground, their original line of products were good supposedly but these days they will either have no effect or put you in the hospital. They seem to have turned to using chemical analogues that have just about zero history of human use and can cause all kinds of fucked up things. Mind candy and Neuroblast have both hospitalized people in nz.

That said, there are some that work but are either expensive or a bitch to get. I have personally tried some of the new neorganics range, Neodoves2 and S.C.D are both fucking EXCELLENT.... but as always there is a catch. They are rather weak, so you have to take at least two at a time which means spending a lot of money and waiting for them to ship from NZ. The other drawback is they, at least the one time I tried them, are very fiendish. I ordered 8 pills from an nz site, and after 4 weeks of waiting and thinking I got ripped they finally arrived half melted. Anyway, I ended up taking all 8 pills myself throughout the night. I went to bed fine, but had I had more I might not have stopped. They are very good in their own right, they do not mimic ecstasy but they caused some great euphoria and waves of a very pleasurable body buzz.

So anyway, there are good alternatives out there, but you have to do some research and some sifting through all the scams out there. You can find what you're looking for but no one's gonna hand it to you.
