quote:Originally posted by aloneandbroken:

condoms dont protect against HPV and Herpes

It's worth pointing out that in both cases (HPV-related genital warts and Herpes sores) the skin-to-skin (or oral) contact must be with either the active warts/sores (which are highly visible) or with warts/sores which are invisible to the naked eye (and usually pose a lesser risk of infection).

So, while condoms won't protect against these STDs, a quick visual check of the area and having clean, unbroken skin on both bodies should help reduce the risk associated with these circumstances.
