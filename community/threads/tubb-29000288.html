Learn how to get him on the ground, submission techniques, and joint locks that can result in bone breaking. A personal favorite of mine is when someone has their hand on my shoulder or by the side of my neck, I grab the hand with the same hand they used on me (ex. right-to-right) and hold it on my shoulder, then swing my arm of the shoulder they grabbed over their arm while holding their hand in place and effectively getting them in an elbow/shoulder/wrist lock.

If you can maintain it you can break the wrist, elbow, and radius, maybe even the shoulder if your strong enough. there is also another joint lock that involves you wrapping your arm under their arm (left to right/right to left)and securing it under your armpit and pushing upward, then you push on their other shoulder with your free arm and they can't punch you in the face because your arm on their shoulder is blocking them.

Although I think they could get a good shot on your ribs which could be a problem, but with enough leverage on that locked joint and you pushing it the opposite direction you can fuck their elbow up good. If they are alot taller than you then this will not work though.

Keep in mind that it is hard to get someone in these situations, in a real fight you can't put someone in the situation and then use it, you have to wait for an oppurtunity to present itself and then take advantage. I have seen people try to force others into their moves, didn't work.

If all else fails, theres always the good ol' ankle lock, either they submit or get their ankle snapped like a chicken bone.
