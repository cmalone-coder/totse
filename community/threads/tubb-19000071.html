Re: electronic timers
I made an electronic timer some time ago, I used a 12V relay, which was what I had (I made a small circuit for the relay to activate with the clock alarm). The circuit is shitty, but it works. With a 1V relay, it's much easier, just connect it to the speaker and throw the spk. away. Then connect a 9V battery to the relay, which burns a 1N4148 diode when the relay closes. A small diode such as the 1N4148 will easily burn with a 9v bat.(other diodes don't) and ignites any kind of gunpowder, matches, etc, which could set off the blasting cap for a high explosive. It usually takes 3 seconds for the diode to make the bomb explode.
