The first time i smoked salvia it wasn't that crazy or anything- but i can describe it for you.

I was standing still after taking a couple of bong hits with a bic and i was looking at the floor just totally zoned as it started to hit me. I visualized everything being made of visible, tiny pieces, held together in a manner not far distant from lego blocks. When i was spacing i suppose i had the feeling of being pulled back and it had the influence on my brain to hallucinate that the world split into two right where i was standing- my legs up to my knees were in front of me and the rest of my body was falling into the white void beneath me. Instinctively, i took a step and everything was back to normal. Time elapsed: ~1-2 minutes.

Another time, I was on the highway in my cousin's car after taking two or three pipe hits with a bic i imagined- more like entered a dream, not hallucinating- my seat sliding back into a world of nonexistant beings. When they saw me, they all wanted to be the one sitting in my seat so they could exist. I protected my seat and said, "no!" and i slid back forward and into reality, thankful that I still existed. I don't remember what the nonexistant beings looked like. Time elapsed: ~1-2 minutes.

Aside from those two distinct "trips", I experienced a lot of minor effects, such as (several times) spacing out looking at things and imagining the small parts of the thing communicating and flowing... kinda hard to describe. Impossible to describe, really. Watching them made me realize that the flowing and communication led to something incredibly important, but I never found out.

It bothers me to this day. Well, not really.

In the end, my trips weren't too severe or crazy. Before that, the hardest thing i have ever done was weed (lol noob, not hard at all! lolz!!!111) and i was not overwhelmed. My advice is: use your best judgment. And aside from that, do it with a friend or with a babysitter that makes you feel really relaxed. someone you can trust, who makes you feel good about yourself, etc. in a place where you don't have to worry about anything. These subtleties will almost ensure a good trip.

There's currently a bill in the wisconsin congress in effort to make the extracts illegal. we'll see how that goes.

I wanted to point out that salvia is a non-descriptive misnomer- the classification of "salvia" is Salvia Divinorum, or Diviner's sage. So literally every time you say salvia, you're talking about smoking a non-specified species of the Salvia genus. ha, ha.
