Re: How can i make the most of my coke?
You could try taking Magnesium supplements. JoePedo was talking about it in the phenylalanine thread a while back. IIRC, The main way cocaine tolerance accumulates is by dopamine causing Calcium ions to flood your brain, burning out dopamine receptors. Magnesium blockades the Calcium channels (NMDA gates), stopping the flooding, allowing the receptors to stay operational. Another possible way to do the same thing (stop the calcium flood), I think, is DXM (it's also a NMDA antagonist). A few (15-45) mg (1-3robogels) of it about 90min before cokin' up should provide the same effect. You could also try an OTC MAO inhibitor, like St. John's Wort. This should prolong the life of the dopamine cocaine keeps in the synapses, theoretically keeping you high longer.
