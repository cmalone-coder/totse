Re: Whiskey and Aspirin
Mixing aspirin and alcohol isn't something I'd recommend. They can contribute to a number of factors, including stomach bleeding. This would be especially true if you're drinking on an empty stomach and took the aspirin.

Other things that are side effects of OTC painkillers such as aspirin and ibuprofen are inability to form blood clots, increased stomach irritation (contributing to your bouts of vomiting), and in the case of acetaminophen (Tylenol), a greater risk of liver damage.

It also, according to this NY Times article (http://query.nytimes.com/gst/fullpage.html?res=9C0CE2D81231F937A25752C1A9669582 60), inhibits your body's ability to break down alcohol, so you wind up drunker than you would be if you hadn't taken them. That may be a pleasant side effect, but in tandem with the complications I listed above, it sounds like a small gain for a lot of potential pain. Seems like the 30% increase could easily be achieved by having another shot or two.
