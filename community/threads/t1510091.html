Re: reasonable expectations?
Back in the day when it was assumed that people did not have sex until they were married, there was this (can't think of the word for it... not really an "old wives tale) thing that went like:

On your wedding day, take a big jar and put a bean in it every time you have sex for the first year of your marriage. At the end of the first year, reverse the process and take a bean out of the jar every time you have sex.

Legend has it that you will NEVER empty that jar.

In most relationships, the original thrill does wane over time. In your case, it does seem that the decline is on the excessive side. (this coming from a fellow who in the later years of an 18 year marriage felt lucky to get it once a year)

Bottom line is to look seriously and honestly at the balance of your relationship. If it is good in all other respects, I would think it best to hang in there and see if it is just a transitional thing. By all means, try to have a discussion about it with her (preferrably outside of the bedroom, but not in public). You may be surprised to learn what her perspective is.

If the situation persists, or gets worse, consider counseling. It took me a long time to realize that in our case, it had less to do with true libido as opposed to just an overwhelming control issue on her part.

God luck, my man!
