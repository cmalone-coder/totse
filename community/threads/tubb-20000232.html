Re: Absinthe
This is the entire recipe for absinthe.

Ingredients:
1 tsp Wormwood (click here to purchase)
1 cup vodka
2 tbsp peppermint leaves
1/2 cup sugar
lemon juice

Procedure:
Find a container and soak the wormwood in the vodka for 2-3 days.

Strain the vodka from the wormwood. Throw away the wormwood and pour the vodka back into the container.

Add the peppermint and either several drops of lemon juice or a squeeze from a fresh lemon. This will give it some flavor. If you skip out on this step, your drink will be bitter.

Let this concoction steep for 7-10 days. This will be more palatable with time. Does anyone want to try a 30-day steeping period?

Strain the peppermint, pour into a glass on the rocks, dilute with water, and sweeten with a sugar cube. Generally, an absinthe spoon should be used to hold the sugar cube. Then, allow the absinthe to run over the sugar until the cube is entirely gone. Your end result should be a milky white beverage.
