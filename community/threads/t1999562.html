A question about evangelical church beliefs
A friend of mine recently suggested i watch the documentary 'Jesus Camp'. I quite liked the movie; i found it fascinating and somewhat troubling.

One scene thats struck me as quite strange, was one in which a young girl was sharing her thoughts on various churches and brought up what was referred to as "dead churches". She had been indoctrinated to believe that "god doesn't visit those churches" due to the fact there was no overt singing/dancing going on inside them, and that the prayers were made in solemn tones.

I've been raised catholic and i got the impression this was the main church she was referring to..and for some reason this really pissed me off. Now what I'd like to know is basically how long has the evangelical movement been around for?

I have a feeling not very long, so why would they have this self righteous attitude towards other sects of Christianity? Especially ones that have been around for over a thousand years..To me it would be like having a child (laying the foundation for their church) and then having that kid turn around and shit on you even though you brought them life...

Re: A question about evangelical church beliefs
"Evangelical" Christianity is a rather broad term. In many cases, it's considered synonymous with Protestantism, and would therefore have originated on or around 1517 (a la Luther).

The church in Jesus Camp appears to be Pentecostal, or some related denomination. That movement is more recent, dating to (I think) the mid-1800's.
