Re: Lent!
While you are 'fasting', I would suggest some inner reflection, and to understand why you are doing what you do. Make your intention clean, and pure, then affirm it yourself, and stay true to it. God willing, when you have completed it, affirm to yourself, preferably vocally, "I have completed my intention for the sake of God". It will help you in other times.

Don't just give something up for the sake of giving it up, or because someone told you.. try to understand why.. and the purpose behind it. To give you a slight glimpse, realize that the tree where the fruit grows, is in your heart.. not in your outer actions. Dwell inside your heart... and renounce this material world. Let go of it. Don't cling to it like you cannot live without it. In your heart's poverty, you will begin to see the world and all its pleasures and conveniences as a blessing and a gift which you should be grateful for in your heart. Remember God often, for hearts find rest in God's remembrance.

Whatever the truth may be of that reality, I don't think it is right to belittle the good intentions of others. Afterall, it is not the outer accomplishments which are of any value, but the condition of one's heart, and the effort we put in, in accordance with our capacity.

A wealthy man could spend in charity, just so he could hear the people say he is a generous man. Would you see any beauty in his intention?

If we are going to do something for the sake of God, we have to remember that God is very rich. God is the one Who gave us everything to begin with. A famous saint of the Islamic tradition once said, that giving something up as a gift to God, is like taking gold to the Gold Mine, or water to the Ocean. Everything we could ever come up with is like, bringing spices to the Orient.

One of the things which we could give back to God that is of real value, is a clean heart, and sincerity. The world and it's riches will fade like dust, in the presence of the heart which reflects God's beauty and divine qualities.

Just some thoughts.
