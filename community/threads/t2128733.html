It will work, barely, but that's fucking expensive. For a beginner I'd suggest buy whipped cream chargers and a cracker. These are pretty expensive compared to a tank. I have a 10lb tank that a friend and I empty in about 2 days. It only costs $30 to fill, which is nothing compared to the amount of chargers that you'd have to buy to equal that much nitrous.

If you do get a tank you can't inhale the gas directly because it's got a bitterant added to it, usually sulphur dioxide or hydrogen chloride gas. These are easily removed by bubbling the nitrous, very slowly, through water with a little sodium hydroxide added, then filtering it through an activated charcoal filter for good measure.
