Love turned to hate question, has troubled me for 20 years.(long & ketchian)
Back in the mid to late 80's I spent some timing living rough in Hamburg. Its where I first started to dip a tentative toe into and then paddle like a puppy in the 'warm waters of the Sins of The Flesh' (+100 for me for slipping in a RHPS quote)!

I used to drink most days in a inner city bar which was a 'in' place for martial artists (I was training under the now legendary Ralf Schmoll at the time).

Back then the only German I really knew was 'ficken' and 'bier'-the two most important words in any language I guess. So I tended to drink with the people who spoke English.

One of my drinking mates was an old guy, must have been 70.

He was a tall sinewy man, and had an air about him that kept the trouble makers away from him.

Anyway I got to know him a bit and it turned out 'Zvi' (not his real name, neither Israeli or German)was a Jew who had survived the death camps as a young man and had gone to Eretz Israel on being liberated. There he had risen to be a major in the paratroops and was now retired.Which basically means he fought in some of the bloodiest nastiest wars of the last century and had no doubt killed more people than he could care to remember.

Before anyone cries 'bs'. Over the years i have met countless 'my life in the SAS' type type pseudo hardmen in bars and also the real deal (I used to work with a lot of Ex special forces guys).

Infact if I remember rightly he and i first got talking because he'd seen my hand drift down to my boot during an argument with someone and he had torn me a new one for carrying a weapon in a 'friendly' bar.

This was one old fart you didn't want to try mugging.

Then one night , buoyed up with dutch courage, I asked him why the fuck he had come back to the country, nay the town, that had treated him so inhumanely as a kid.

He gave me that look that old soldiers probably reserve for such teenage drunken mouthy 'wannabe tough guys' gits like I was. Then he said simply 'I have come to find my wife and kill her'.

I know he married in Israel and had a son, who was an adult.

But he had never spoken of his wife before and he refused to elaborate on why he should want to kill her.

Men kill their wives for several reasons but none of them seem to fit in my mind.

And from the way he said it 3 things were clear to me; 1. it was personal(ie not just because he wanted to cash in her life insurance) and 2. She was dead, no ifs or buts. If he found her then she was dead and 3.He hated her when he had once loved her.

I can't imagine him being the kind of guy who would have hunted her down if she had simply emptied his bank account, shot his dog and run off with a younger lover. And obviously it wasn't because of some custody issue with the kid.

I also don't think it was just because he wanted to take a younger more nubile wife. Infact I know it wasn't (I'll recount my other Zvi story maybe later).

Anyway a few days after I had to leave Hamburg to go do something in southern Germany and I didn't return to that bar for about a year.

When I enquired about Zvi I was met with the expected wall of silence and all I could find out was that he left never to be seen again a few days I had.

I didn't enquire any further, and my German wasn't good enough to wade through the back papers of the local rag in the 'morgue'.

But over the years the incident has always troubled me.

What could his wife have done, what 'crime' had she committed, to turn his love into such murderous hate?



There is a slight postscript to this anecdote.



Normally I'm about as psychic as a housebrick wrapped in lead foil. But years later I had a sudden 'premonition', an overwhelming feeling, that Zvi was dead and so I went to the synagogue and said kaddisch for his soul. Very 'Odessa Files' I know but it somehow felt the right thing to do.

So S&A, what could a wife , with whom he had sired a son, have done that would cause her to flee the country and 2. get herself 'deaded'?
