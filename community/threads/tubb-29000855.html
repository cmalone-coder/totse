Re: crossbow with magazine
����I've heard that the things exist, but they are small, expensive, and not very powerful. Supposedly for competition target shooting or something.
����In any event, making a repeating crossbow is much more diffuclt than making a repeating firearm because there are many more variables. Not only does the clip have to move the next bolt into place, but it also has to line it up with the string and be able to release it when you fire.
����The third hurdle comes when you have to recock the bow. Crossbows are 1:1 input/output devices - The bow fires with X amount of force, which means you have to put X amount of force back into it to pull the string back. At that rate, making anything that could pull the string back for you would be better put to firing the arrow itself. Then you'd be done with it.

~Zero
