Re: Just got contact lenses...
If you're having trouble getting them in it's probably because you haven't got your eyelids propped open enough and they're getting in the way. Use the thumb and forefinger of one hand to really pry them open while you stick in the lens with the other hand.

Make sure the lenses are very moist on the inside, and that the finger you're using to stick it in is nice and dry. The lenses adhere to wet surfaces, so you don't want it to stick to your finger, but you want it to latch on to your eye nice and quick like.

Also, look straight ahead with the eye you're putting the lens on. You should apply the lens directly onto the iris/pupil, not on the side like it sounds like you're doing. You should be looking straight ahead, and putting the lens in from the front.

Didn't they teach you this when you bought the lenses?
