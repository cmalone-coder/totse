Re: Are we moral?
And for both of your responses, this is exactly the point I think Mencius was trying to make. The way I read it, Mencius felt that the "heart" dictated correct morals and that the "Gods" determined/favored correct action. Here comes the dilemma as I see it. That Brahman could have been absolutely correct. And as long as the Gods didn't interfere with the Brahman in any way, they (the Gods) were giving tacit approval to the Brahman's action/lack thereof.

This brings up the major point: it appears that in Chinese religious philosophy the individual is always at the mercy of the Gods. They (the Gods) either give or withhold their favor/s to the individual without specifically giving mandates for specific behavioral conduct by the individual. Curious.
