Re: i need help on having cable modem for free
Service is the key word there.

When you subscribe to roadrunner for instance they come and install a cable modem, but that isnt all they do. Back at yourtown.rr.com they are now sending your house new signals through your cable service, not just TV signals but internet signals. Without the internet signals going to your house you cant get on the web. Since with cable you are always on the net it would be pointless for them to have a per minute charge for the service; instead its just a flat rate. So it does not matter whether or not they think you are on the net, you still get charged for them sending those signals to your house (through your cable line).
Now we have established that it does not cost money to be on the net but rather it costs money to subscribe to a cable service. So the only way for them to not charge you is to cancle the subscription, thus they stop sending the internet signals through your cable lines and in turn: no internet. So if you were to make a device which would make it appear as if you were not on the net you would still get charged for the service.
You cant cheat the cable company.

However: there is an exception. Some newer cable services charge you for the ammount of data which has been sent to your house. So the more you do the more you pay, time does not matter. I suppose you could make a device which would trick the cable company into thinking that you are only doing small ammounts of browsing and downloading. But this would not work if the device which counted the ammount of data were on the ISP's side of the connection (which i would assume it is.)

I beleive my explanation is fairly accurate, please correct any of my mistakes.
