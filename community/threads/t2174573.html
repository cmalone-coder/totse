Re: Well, I got an Omega..
Oldschool, cool. I don't think you should wear it everday, sounds obvious but it will increase the chances of you breaking/scratching/loosing it, scratching the glass is a pain in the ass and happends more often than not, you can never tell how that little nick got onto the glass. Just wear it when you're going out or socialising, it will make you look classy. I think you should stick with the 'simulated (http://i2.iofferphoto.com/img/item/496/402/76/o_IMG_2715.jpg) snakeskin' band look. It's a nice colour of Brown and would be magic if you could get a pair of pointy shoes to match. Other than that, I would personally use a plain Black band, I think that would look good with the gold.

I don't know how much you're willing to spend but these 'genuine alligator' straps are quite cool: Straps (http://www.watchworx.co.uk/pages/lwb/elitePop/Genuine%20Alligator.htm) the first one if practically the same colour as the one in your picture.

Apart from that site there are: Watch hut (http://www.thewatchhut.co.uk), Watch retail (http://www.watchretail.co.uk/), Strap factory (http://www.strapfactory.com/)
