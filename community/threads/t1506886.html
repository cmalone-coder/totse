lunch with Nick
today i had lunch with Nick. we flirted last year, so i emailed him and asked him to meet up.

i was incredibly nervous and intimidated. he's very tall, happy, confident, and excited about life. i tried to keep my cool, but my nerves made my words get all scrambled and left my mnid blank for conversation topics. i even STUTTERED. :0 the high point was talking about our wilderness experiences, something we had in common and i could kind of rave and get excited about.

we'd met at 12:20 and then he had to go at 1, to "prepare blah blah for the lab blah blah." if that was due to my boringness, i don't know. i really wanted to make some sort of plan to do something some other time, but i lost my nerve after getting up and saying "so...." so i think i seemed a little weird at that moment. then he was like, "thanks for coming by," even though i'd asked him. i thanked him back. and then he said, "have fun at mirlo [my dorm]. you're with friends, that's what's important." then we parted ways.

i felt defeated for several reasons. 1) i was nervous and did not perform at my best, 2) i hadn't made/suggested plans to do something else, nor had he, and 3) he'd said "have fun at your dorm," which to me (correct me if i'm wrong) appeared to assume we wouldn't see each other again.

i bought some chocolate and felt like a dumb loser. then i had an appointment with my therapist, who made me feel WORSE by telling me to forget him because she didn't think he was interested. and then she basically told me a bunch of shit that synthesized into the message, "you repel people and don't know how to pick up on rejection cues, because you're in denial." i got mad and was like "wtf?" i left on a bad note, convinced i had no friends in this world except those i'd invented in my head or clung to out of sheer desperation.

i'm now feeling pretty depressed and diseased.
