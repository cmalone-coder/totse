Re: Growing weed as a teen
First time I grew I was 14, I had no experience and got all my equipment from work (aquatics store) and used a shower extractor fan with DIY in-line carbon filter. I learned everything I knew from overgrow.com and managed to grow under my parents nose in a confined crawlspace. This was no mean feat considering this was back before auto strains were produced and required some pretty techy pruning and training. Needless to say it all worked out fine in the end and although I had some pretty shitty yields I was 14 and it was free weed! Not long after though I looked back and realised how much of a bad idea it was, it was my parents house and with me being underage not only was I risking my parents trust but also their liveleyhood. If your a teen and live at home DONT grow in your parents house unless they know you are doing it and you have their permission. To do that is just downright disrespectful towards the people you should respect the most and also is risking a hell of a lot for both of you if you get caught either by them or even worse by any kind of law enforcment.

Since then Iv grown either outdoors, in my own various college accomodations (also a pretty bad idea) or at friends house's.

If your that desperate to grow and assuming your in the northern hemisphere just wait till next years season to grow or find somewhere away from home. Either that or wait till your older and have your own place.
