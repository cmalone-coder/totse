The best thing to do is of course, experiment over the course of several months to find what you can handle. Drink vodka every time you go out. Start out with a shot an hour. See how you feel. I used to drink 14 shots of Bacardi or Canadian Mist in 2 hours. I pregamed hard then didn't drink the rest of the night. I always drank water before/after and took a vitamin before bed. Most of the time I didn't get a hangover. I only got sick when I drank cheap vodka. That was heinous.

So step 1: Always make sure you're hydrated before you drink. This is extremely important. If you are not hydrated before you drink, you will have a shitty hangover because you probably aren't going to sit around for a few hours after you drink to properly hydrate yourself. So yeah, drink water before you drink.

Step 2: Do not eat a lot. Make sure you're eating fairly healthy. Eat protein and carbs, and wait at least 2 hours before you start getting trashed. Take a vitamin with your meal too. For fuck's sake, make sure you eat a decent amount of food, just don't go binge at a buffet.

Step 3: Once you're hydrated and you've eaten properly, wait a few hours.

Step 4: Choose a quality drink. Do not drink cheap vodka and expect to make it out ok. If you pound 14 shots of Barton's/McCormick/Viaka or some other post industrial waste shit you're probably going to get heinously sick. Spend a little more and get something decent: Bacardi, Appleton's or Cruzan for rum. For vodka get Svedka, Kutskova, Shaker's (if it's cheap where you live), or at the very least get Smirnoff. For whiskey, Evan Williams black label is a good cheap choice.

Step 5: Now that your body is ready and you have your drink you can proceed. Drink slowly. It's not a fucking race to see how fast you can drink. If you race and you don't know your limits, you will probably get sick.

Step 6: Go have fun.

Step 7: Recovery begins. Sit around for a little while and drink some water. You need to drink a liter of water or so. Space it out over a half hour or something. Don't go straight to bed. You need to hydrate yourself to avoid nasty hangovers.

There you have it. Simple steps of knowing your body, hydrating yourself, eating beforehand, and not drinking 10 shots in 30 mins will keep you from getting sick.

Another suggestion is to work out in your spare time. Men process alcohol way better than women because on average men have more muscle mass than women.
