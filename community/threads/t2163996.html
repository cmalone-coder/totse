Re: People cheapening 'I love you'
Eh, I'll say it casually, now and then, outside of a relationship situation to express platonic affection or whatever. I'll often just casually say "I love you" to someone I think is awesome as a form of high-praise... There are many forms of 'love', though, and I make no indication of it being the romantic kind. Also, the surrounding context generally makes it clear what I mean. I'm not sure if this is 'devaluing' it in the way you're talking about, though.

In any other sense, I can't imagine saying it at all. I mean, there are people I've been quite fond of, romantically, but no feelings so heavy that they could be called love, so in those situations, I don't say it, because it's too easy to be misinterpreted, which tends to lead to a lot of resulting shit. I'd rather just say something safe and non committal like "I like spending time with you/sleeping with you," because that is more likely to be true. It doesn't have that same ring to it, though...
