Re: liquor: most bang for your buck?
cheap vodka will kill. if ur gonna get some, get a decent brand like smirinoff or grey goose.

Smirnoff and Grey goose are opposite ends of the spectrum. Smirnoff is a pretty average price, around $13. Grey goose is ridiculously overpriced, usually over $30 a bottle. I think it costs around $40 here. Absolut is also another highly advertised, shitty vodka. Buy svedka. It's actually a really good vodka, and it's fairly cheap. Here's it's $20 for 1.75L. It mixes really well, and you can take shots of it with no problems.

Don't go drinking Everclear or anything 95% ABV. That stuff will ruin you. Save up and buy at the very least one of the name brands like Bacardi or Smirnoff. They're the bottom line you shouldn't cross when it comes to quality.

If you want good, cheap rum, I suggest Appletons and Cruzan. Sailor Jerry's is more expensive, but it's good. Gosling's is nice every so often. Flor de cana is excellent if you can find it. Usually I just drink Cruzan Light Rum aged 2 years. They now age it for only 14 months, and the taste difference is huge since they age it in oak, unlike Bacardi which is pointlessly left in steel for a year (which does not age it at all). Try Bacardi anyway. It's good for mixing or taking shots.

For cheap whiskey, you've already found my drink of choice. I like to get Evan Williams every so often. The 1L size is great. You can also try Canadian Mist, Weller's, or something in that price range. Jack Daniels is over priced. Evan Williams tastes similar enough that I don't buy jack anymore. Jim Beam is pretty harsh, in my opinion. The black label (aged 8 years) is good, but a little overpriced considering it's still harsh. Evan Williams is pretty damn smooth for the price.

For vodka, get Svedka, Kutskova if they have it, or Sobieski. Kutskova costs $9 for 750ml around here, and I can't taste any difference between it and Grey goose. It's far better than Stolichnaya or Absolut, both of which are way overpriced. Vodka is the cheapest spirit to produce. They don't age it, and they only need to use one type of grain to produce it (or potatoes, etc). There's no mixing and no special process it goes through. Vodka should be cheap.

Save up and buy decent shit. Don't buy the giant, cheap 1.75L jugs that cost $7. That stuff is disgusting and it's far better to just save another $5 and buy something that actually tastes good. You can buy good rum, vodka, and whiskey for $10-$13. Most of the ones I listed fall within this price range. Since you're just starting out, you should try a variety of things and see what you like. Since you're drinking whiskey, I'd suggest trying out a few rums too. Just remember to drink in moderation, especially since you're new to it.
