Not really anythng to do with psychosis or paranoia but this was pretty fucked up.

I bet this will make me sound like a kidiot but I tripped pretty hard on DXM yesterday. I took two bottles of Robotussin (600mg) for the first time as I usually only take up to one bottle. Basically, the trip started out pretty normally except a lot more intense than usual. I walked around and even harassed some /b/tards I met outside the Church of Scientology they probably grew pretty tired of my company after I puked. Everything was extremely surreal then and I hardly remember anything. Eventually though they convinced me to go because the cops were there and they were apparently staring at us. I kind of feel bad about that whole incident as I embarrassed myself. So after I left them, things got extremely weird. I basically don't remember anything except walking around for what seemed like hours. Everything moved extremely slowly except for me. I couldn't hear anything. As I walked by people, it seemed as though I passed through them. I actually thought I was dreaming and later, even possibly dying. Then I woke up on a curb in some random neighborhood. I became convinced that I passed out and it was the next day but I was still tripping balls so I got scared that I might have gotten brain damage somehow. I have summer school for a subject I failed and today was the first day. However, since I was convinced that it was the next day, I began looking for the school. Immediately after waking up, I was pretty lost and had no sense of direction. I passed out momentarily several more times, "waking up" at different places not really knowing how I got there. In my search for the school, I asked people for directions including some people playing music at the park who I spoke with for a while. I imagine I sounded like a complete retard to most pedestrians I asked for directions but I couldn't actually hear myself speak. I was already coming down when I finally found the school. It was locked and I felt disappointed that I had missed the first day and I was quite worried because I didn't want to take the course I failed for another year. I later asked what time it was and it turned out to be around eight. I headed home, not knowing how to explain the condition I was in or where I had been the whole day and the entire night before. When I came home, it turned out that it was still the same day, July 1st. I had taken the DXM only around 4:00 and as I can remember, I was with the 4chan fags until about 6:00 but the trip seemed like an eternity. It actually seemed like a whole night and then an entire day had passed. My vision didn't clear until around 10:00 and I was lightly buzzed until I went to sleep at around 12:00.
