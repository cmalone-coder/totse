Actually, thats not how most motion detectors avoid being set off by animals. They are passive IR sensors which detect rapid change in heat energy generated by a person moving within its detection zone. Small animals and pets do not put off enough heat to active the sensor, while large animals, mainly humans, do. Other motion detection systems exist, but this is the most likely.

Blue BDU's actually match the color of night more than black, believe it or not. Remember, we're talking dark navy blue here, not sky-blue or anything.
