Be careful of ordering off unknown suppliers.

Undrugged.org used to be useful for verifying apparently, but I'm not sure if it's still decent. I was given my supplier by a friend IRL, so I never had to go through the process of finding a legit one online...

As for RC's worth trying, give 2C-B or 2C-T-7 a try. Both are great fun, 2C-B being pretty much my favourite drug I've tried yet.

2C-I and 2C-E (if you can handle barfing) are also worth a try according to people I've talked to, though I personally have no experience with them.
