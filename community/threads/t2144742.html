This is not music.
Okay, well a few days ago whilst browsing the internet a few days ago and came across what seems to be the new type of metal (Deathcore, grindcore, new death metal, call it whatever you want.) And honest to god it is utter shit. Don't get me wrong, I like few bands from this genre, and even those bands I can't listen to for extended periods of time. Let me break it down why these bands suck, I have very good reasons!

First of all, here are some examples of what I'm talking about:
The bad category:
"Suicide" Silence.
Brain drill. I'll admit these guys are a bit better, but not much.
Annotations of an autopsy. Sounds like someone fucked a pig.
Hymen holocaust. What the fuck.
Cock and ball torture. Creative name!

The good category:
Job for a cowboy. Yep, I gotta admit these guys are better then most of those idiots. But...NOT MUCH!
All shall perish. These guys are just awesome.
The Acacia Strain Very interesting guitar work in some songs.

That's about where the good list ends, without putting on cannibal corpse. Ugh, where do I start?
1) The "bad" bands just sound like they wanted to hit the drums very hard. Job for a cowboy does this too...I can't really explain why they're on the good list, but they ARE better then those other ones.
2) There are so many of these bands out I can't take it seriously, it's like a new one comes out every week. Seriously!
3) The lyrics would get a F in a grade nine english class. Here are samples from different bands:
Quote:
Felt my pussy clench as I watched the guy's
Juice-slicked dick sliding in and out of her ass.
He used his cock to smear anal juices
...all over her bum!
Guess who wrote that beautiful piece of literature? (Hint: They sound like frogs.)

Quote:
WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT
Drinking and fighting, thats how we roll
Getting fucked and breaking some skulls
Never living on a "X"
Annotations of an autopsy. Yep, the song I linked to. These guys are really hardcore- you betta watch out!

Okay, but enough with that...want to know my biggest complaint? The one thing that really is fucking horrible? Here it is:
These guys think that they're hardcore. All of there lyrics are the same, and all they do is play..."noise" we really need to take a step backwards to the time where playing extreme metal meant not only did you just say things, you actually did them too. (Like stabbing gay guys in the forest at night). Or maybe norwegians are just weird. Anyway, people like burzum are just so into it that it makes it good, and they believed in what they were doing. I honestly think these new guys just want to make a few bucks for the shock value- even in jail Varg still wrote music- which is a perfect example of how you don't need to be hardcore to be absolutely badass. Listen to "Rundgang um die transzendentale Säule der Singularität" by him. Afterwards, if you're not asleep, you certainly won't feel like killing someone. I think people in the metal scene are trying to hard...way to hard.

Eh, I'm done ranting. I don't want to cram to much bitching into one post so I'll let people respond and we'll see where it goes...
