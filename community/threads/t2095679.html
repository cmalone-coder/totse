Thermal

Re: Identifying good weed
1. Seedy shit is shitty shit

2. Dense nugs = proper lightning/temperature/humidity and probably other conditions. Airy nugs = shitty conditions = shitty grower

3. Fully developed trichromes (mushroom shaped) = harvested at right time. underdeveloped trichs = premature harvest (like most beasters) = shitty quality
Also, overly swolen ovaries and mostly dead/dry pistils also indicate maturity.

4. Trichromes all clear = harvested bit too early. cloudy white/amber trichs = harvested at right time. All amber = harvested too late (thc broke down to cbd and cbn making for a lazy high)

5. Stems COMPLETLEY snap when slightly bent = properly dried.
stems are flexibe/break but still connected by fibers = too much moisture (so your probably paying for water weight as well)
Bud crumbles into dust = too dry (some ppl like it like that, not me)

6. Cashed buds have greysh/whiteish ash = quality plant that was flushed properly. the darker the resulting ash the more unflushed fertilizer.

7. Harsh smoke = unflushed fertilizer / moist buds / overdried / uncured
(Taste of chlorophyl (burning hay) = barely cured, or uncured buds )

8. Properly trimmed/manicured = more thc per weight ( buds more potent than leafs)

9. Trichrome density / resinousness = relative to potency

9. smell, taste, looks, strain, grower's skill&methods = obvious indicators of potency

Nugs overall color rarely has anything to do with quality, ive had brownish/blackish weed that was 100x better than those pure-white beasters (commercial WW? its everywhere)
