Ahh, yes. The reclusive teen. Hear me out: The world totally changes when you move out.

Once you move out from underneath their roof (even if you ARE paying for it) they'll start looking at you as more of an equal. A citizen who feeds himself and contributes to society. Right now they look at you as a parasite. They are entitled to bitch at you and give you shit.

All through my teens I hate my parents (specially my mother). I vowed the minute I got all my hands my bank account (that she hid from me) I would never speak to her again. This was a very passionate vow that I repeated for at least 3 years. Eventually I moved out and I didn't really talk to her. I didn't pick up the phone when she called. All I could think about was everytime she fucked me over. Everytime she was a fucking bitch. And now that I'm on my own, now that I have my OWN house, and live by my own rules she doesn't have shit over me. It was freeing at first. Now I could have sex whenever/wherever, I could smoke pot on the comfort of my own couch, I could have a party.

The novelty wore off within a year and after going through a serious transitional phase from dependence to complete independence I started examining myself. I realized I had a lot of hatred and resentment for my parents. For fucking up. For letting me do whatever the hell I felt like and for not TALKING to me. For not MAKING me talk to them. For not having enough balls to try to have a relationship with me. Cause I did the same shit you're doing. All through high school I came home to sleep and take a shower and I was out the door. The few words my mother and I did speak throughout the week were curses and yelling.

But I always stayed close to my brothers. I'm the oldest, so I pretty much directed how the relationship went. We talked a lot and now that I think about it, he's probably the reason I realized how important my family is.

Only now, that I have forgiven my parents and let go of shit that's been poisoning my life for so long, I feel better. Family's very important for your MENTAL HEALTH. If shit is fucked up with them, shit's going to be fucked up with anyone who gets close to you.

Bottom line - You're young, you have plenty of time to hate your parents and ignore your family. But the day is going to come where you're going to man up and embrace them - be aware.

And by the way, age difference means SHIT. I get along better with my professors that I do my classmates. The moment I started asking questions, my parents opened up I learned a lot about my dad's past that I had NO CLUE about before. We were so estranged at one point, he barely knew my boyfriend's (of 2 years!) name. I doubt he could name one of my best friends from high school.But now that I've taken the time and effort to get to know him, we're practically the same person.

Parents don't know what the hell they're doing. They take a mix of their own upbringing and their judgment and do the best they can. Sometimes their best sucks but you have to look past that. Children teach their parents. You might not have any interest in building a relationship with your parents and that's alright. Just try to keep your mind open.
