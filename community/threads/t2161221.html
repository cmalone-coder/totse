The Smilies/Rainbow Smiley tabs are real nice, they certainly feel like a clean shot of acid but a relatively low loading. Chromosones and Third Eye tabs are both DOET and rather unpleasant on the comedown. Purple Ohms are DOC (unsubstantiated but suspected based on subjective results) but real fun. Pharoah tabs are a decent loading of straight up LSD. Where's Wally/Rollercoaster Tycoon (same tab but named different things) are stronger then the Smilies but also real smooth. There's usually cubes, liccy, and random 'brown spot' tabs at doofs, break into that scene and youll have all the psychs you could ever want, for a while at least until your demand outstrips the supply of the entire subculture (Don't laugh, this happened to a mate and me in perth, we bought more and more mescaline every weekend for about two months until the guy couldn't grow cacti fast enough.)

There have been reports of a tab being sold as Purple Ohms that was instead just dipped in a liquid hypochlorite solution - This resulted in chemical burns to the mouth of about a dozen users at the W.A Earthdance festival last week, but this is believed to be a one-off occurence.
