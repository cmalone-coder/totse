It's Not Me That's Crazy... It's Everyone Else
It seems like everyone has some kind of mental disorder now. I'm sure people have always had mental disorders, they just were never diagnosed, but it seems like, in the last twenty years it's become more socially acceptable to wig out. All sorts of medications and everyone is manic depressive, everyone is bi-polar.

And, really, may be it's just where I live, or where I go, or who I meet but it seems like the majority have mental problems of some sort or another. So, if the majority has mental problems, doesn't that technically mean that it's the people who don't have mental problems who are the odd ones? Doesn't it sort of lead you to believe that the "normal" people are hiding something? Because how can the "norm" be a smaller percentage?

And I'm getting really tired of people calling me crazy. I'm some skitzo... I was diagnosed so long ago I don't even remember the actual name of it, manic depressive, and some kind of anxiety disorder PTSD something or other. And I don't take medication for it and I keep it under control.

I hold up just fine in job, school, and social settings. Sometimes I feel like I'm the most grounded person around because I have absolutely no room for added drama in my life. Things are turbulent and with whatever I have it makes it hard to deal. But, I do and I do it without medication and I want to continue living without medication for as long as I can. Screw the fucking doctors. They don't know what they're talking about.

How can they? How can anyone know unless they have what you have? And how come I'm the "crazy" one? I'm holding it together just fine.

Cheers,
