Re: 2,5 dimethoxyallylbenzene- What the hell is it?
2,5,dimethoxyallylbenzene is nothing more than what tryp said....

an inactive precursor, that is similar in structure to safrole.

A wacker oxidation would form 2,5-DM-P2P. Reductive amination with ammonia would yeild 2,5-DMA (2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine). 2,5-DMA is inactive itself. However, dissolving the freebase in glacial acetic acid, and adding Br2, will precipitate DOB (4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine), a very wonderful drug, and 2-CB's amphetamine cousin (see PiHKAL).

On the flip side, oxidation of the side chain with something like chromic acid will yeild the aldehyde, which is condensed with nitromethane to yeild the nitrostyrene. The nitrostyrene is reduced via CTH, to yeild the phenylethylamine counterpart, 2-CH (2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine). Bromination of it's freebase like the DMA will yeild the drug 2-CB (4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine). Look DOB and 2-CB up on Erowid to find out more info on these drugs.
