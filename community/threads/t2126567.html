I've taken a quarter of shrooms, still no visuals.
I've done shrooms about 4 times, adding 1/16th to the dose each time, but I've never really TRIPPED OUT. I've never gotten any visuals or hallucinations, and I've always wanted to, so it's a real bummer.

Trip 1: 1/16th. I didn't get anything out of this trip. I'm 6'4" 200 lbs, so it's really no surprise that such a small amount didn't do shit I guess. That said, my friends did trip, and saw some minor hallucinations (Things looking like they were breathing for example).

Trip 2: 1/8th. This trip was interesting. I got feelings of euphoria, and I was reeeeally talkative. I was coming up with some funny shit and had some great conversations. That said, I was all there mentally, and acted as the voice of reason in the group for the most part.

Trip 3: 3/16ths. This trip was real similar to the second trip, just more intense. I was still real talkative, but I didn't see anything or lose grip on reality.

Trip 4: 1/4. This trip was pretty crazy, but only borderline overwhelming. It was like the third trip, but I started to reach the edge of reality. I had times where I was completely unsure of what was going on, and I had to look to my friends to for reassurance that nothing was wrong. That said, I didn't see a single visual, it was purely a mental trip. After I got over the initial spike of "WTF IS GOING ON" it felt similar to the third trip.

They've all been worthwhile experiences, but I've always really wanted to have a visual trip, and it just hasn't happened. I don't know if I could handle any more than I took on the 4th trip (People already look at me like I'm psycho for doing a 1/4, some can't imagine it). My dealers don't really know anything about different strains, and I don't either, so I really just have to take what I can get.

Do you think if I come across a different strands I might be able to get some visuals, or do you think I'm just not a person who can get them? My friends have gotten some decent visuals during each of these trips, and on the last trip the friend I was tripping with only did an 1/8th and got some visuals (He's about my size too). I plan to try acid soon, do you think if I gave that a shot it would be more likely I'd get visuals than on shrooms?

Also, do you think smoking weed would make a difference? I've smoked a bit on shrooms, but for whatever reason when I did it was kind of half-assed because I was so distracted, so I never smoke enough to get absolutely blazed when I'm tripping. Should I try facing a blunt or something? :D

Thanks in advance for any advice guys.

Re: I've taken a quarter of shrooms, still no visuals.
It DOES have something to do with the 'strains'.

You get either ones that will make you feel how you felt. Almost like you've done MDMA. Sense of pure euphoria etc. And others that will create 'visuals'. But said 'visuals' are just mere distortions of colour, light, objects, whatever.

There are certain shrooms that have the mix of the two, while others which are just more dominant in either feeling or tripping.

But what I've found is that the ones with the mix of the two, the euphoric side completely diminish any chance of heavy visual tripping. It's hard to really concentrate on objects ect when you're feeling fucking amazing and just want to talk about how great stuff is. Although, sometimes it does come in layers and you get the best of both. But this is contradictory while 'peeking'.
