Actually I'm writing an essay about this right now. Maybe you guys can help me clarify my points.

What I am proposing is that ALL drugs should available legally, but only with a doctor's permission.
(For those of you versed in criminal policy formation I'm opting for the "positive license" model)

Basically, my proposed system is this:
You can buy any drug you like after you visit a doctor for a consultation on that drug. The appointment will have a minimum duration time, and the doctor will be required to go over a certain curriculum about the drug you wish to be licensed for during that time.

The curriculum will include pharmacology, harm reduction, and general info to make the drug use as safe as possible

After the consult the doctor signs a form saying that you have completed the consultation for "x" drug. You then take this form to the DMV and take a test on the info. If you pass the test you get a license thats good for a specified amount of time (probably between 1 and 5 years) and allows you to posess a specified amount of the drug (not enough that you could feasibly do anything more than personal use).

Note that who has these licenses and for what chemicals will not be public record. It will be treated with the same privacy as current laws give to what medicine you are prescribed.

For as long as your license is valid you may purchase and posess up to the specified limit.
You may not distribute the substance, even to other people who are licensed to have it.
With the exception of marijuana and other plants that require no refining to become psychoactive, you may not synthesize or otherwise produce the drugs without a DEA license ( the same as is required now).
One person can have multiple licenses, but he must undergo the same process for each one.
After your license has expired you must pass the test again to have it renewed.

This model is based off of the California medicinal marijuana system, where you get a doctor'r permission and use that to get a license from the city that you keep on your person whenever you have your drugs.

Why the restrictions i have included are included:

1) I am using a positive license model because research on successful drug policy has shown that this model has the lowest harm to society of all other models. This includes absolute prohibition, absolute legalization (where drugs are sold as freely as milk), and everything inbetween.

2) The doctor's appointment has a minimum length and curriculum because of what we have seen in CA. Here there are tons of "pot docs". Basically these are licensed doctors who put out ads in newspapers for $100 consults. Patients come in, pay cash, and say they have some disorder that can be treated by weed. The doctor then writes a prescription without doing any tests or anything.

So, requiring a minumum appointment time and curriculum ensures that doctors dont just go around signing forms to allow people to buy heroin after a 10 minute consult.

3) The test is there because requiring a doctor's appointment won't do any good if the patient isnt responsible for learning the information.

4) The records of who has what license is secret in order to suppress the re-emergence of the black market. If anyone can see what drugs their therapist or attorney or teacher is taking that would encourage those "reputable" people to buy from people who do have licenses instead of getting one themself.

TL/DR:
You can get a license to buy and posess any drug you like after you talk to a doctor about it and take a test.

Seriously, who needs amphetamines to get by in high school?
