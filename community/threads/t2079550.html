It's the little things in life.. you know?
Yesterday, due to a slight mistiming, Liverpuddlian traffic and a shit public transport system I ended up missing check in for a flight back to Belfast and I've stopped in with a few of my stoner friends.

However, when I was at the airport, I met this girl who was in a similar position as me. She was from Southern Ireland and had had the worst day of her life. We chatted for a while, flirted even and it put my troubles in light. We had both reacted to similar experiences in the same way - she had ended up in tears, I had been prepared to kill somebody just to get home, but there we were having a bit of a chuckle with one another.

The thing is, I'll probably never meet her again. We only exchanged first names, no surnames, no numbers. Her town is hours away from mine and when we're at uni we're still miles away. Just for what must have been an hour and a half at most, it felt like we connected.

We were both saying that if either one of us got stuck we could crash at a friend of our's house. (Her friend in Dublin, mine in Liverpool). It was sweet.

Now I might live in the slight regret I never took that extra step to keep contact with her but I think I'd prefer to just reflect over that small chat with a happy smile on my face. I'd love to meet her again, probably even do naughty things with her (she was hot) but knowing that there are beautiful girls out there who I can get on with and have a giggle with is good enough for me.

Yeah, I'm kind of having a dry spell again..
