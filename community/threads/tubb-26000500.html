Re: wax
Seeing that no solutions are forthcoming for my original query I'll submit MY method to decolorize wax, just for the record:
Roll out the beeswax as thinly as possible. Fill a transparent container (the bigger the better) with water and immerse the colored (red in my case) wax in it. Keep the wax under water (s.g. 0.9). Put the whole setup in strong sunlight covered witha sheet of glass. U.V. does the rest for after about a month the color starts to fade and the original color returns. This method really works but it is laborious, time-consuming and messy.
