Ok here are ideas that i have had for a while but have only just noted down. They are written here exactly how i noted them down so don't mind the short stubby sentences.

At the begining, nothing existed but God, with a mind so pure, and uninfluenced by anything (since only God existed).
All of God's 'thoughts' happened at the very same moment. Since, at that very moment' nothing existed but God, his thought were based completely on his own mind. In other words: his thoughts are his own creative imaginations about hisself.
Since there is no physical container for his mind (a brain for example), his mental ability is not contained by anything, and so his thoughts about hisself are complete and absolute.
'Time' was created the moment God 'thought' (since the idea of time was included in his thoughts). God 'however' exists outside of time, and his imagination of 'time' stretches for ever in both directions (ie: the past goes on forever, and the future goes on forever).
'Time' is Gods imagination in process, although it has already been 'imagined' by God outside of time.
Life as we know it, is the process of God 'imagining', which has already happened, but has been stretched over time. 'Life' is God expressing his thoughts through the act of 'imagining'. God knows everything, because 'everything' is what he has already imagined to have happened.
Since everything exists within Gods imagination, it is impossible to prove that God exists to anyone exept yourself.
You can prove it to yourself, by realising that you are part of the God. As part of God you are also imagining. You are not the 'imagination', you are only experiencing the 'imagination' as part of God. In effect, you are experiencing yourself, or your 'Godself'.
You 'think' therefore you are. Only God exists. He is a thinker, and everything else exists only as an imagination. We can 'think', therefore we are not a part of the imagination, but are an imaginer.

Make sense of this. If you give it thought, you can see how many spiritual beliefs, religeous concepts, and spoken wisdom, arises from this theory.


I know i'm right with what i say, and i challenge anyone to ask me a question about it that i cannot answer.
