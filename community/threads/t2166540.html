Re: Which bong should I buy? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ?
A percolator (perc) seperates the smoke into multiple channels into another chamber of water. This does make the smoke smoother and less harsh on the throat, its also for filtering the smoke a bit more.

I suggest when you get your bong; you also purchase a diffuser and an ash catcher if your chosen one doesn't have them already, the resulting smoke is so smooth, you'll clear a nice big bowl without realising.
