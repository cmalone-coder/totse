Habitual marijuana use
Recently I have been given many speeches about how marijuana is fine and dandy until it becomes habitual. I am going to be honest in saying that i have only stopped smoking for two days this summer, but I don't believe it really affects me. I still pay my bills on time, I missed one day of work due to lack of sleep, and I have only spent $30 of my money on weed. Now I understand I have only been an everyday smoker for a few months, but I haven't noticed any lasting effects. I was just fine today spending time with my parents, my brother, and his family without smoking. I have fun and I believe it is appropriate to enjoy myself. The question is, Where do I draw the line? Is getting high to go to work inappropriate? Is getting high to watch a movie out of line? Is it wrong that sometimes I get high and do nothing but talk to my friends? Where do you draw the line?

My personal rule of thumb is:
Its ok if it messes with your life a bit but,
If your life messes with smoking a bit, you have a problem.
