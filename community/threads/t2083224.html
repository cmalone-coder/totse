i have dinner plans with this girl... i think she's going to kill me.
There's this girl. She's unlike any girl I've ever met. She's special to me and we've gone through a lot together, including experiencing our former lovers cheating on us with each other, which was really the bonding experience of a lifetime.

I'm more than in love with her and usually just embarrass myself in front of her because I become EXTREMELY self conscious of everything I do and say and think around her. I can't even think properly around her, really.

Well... I've since found other relationships that I can maintain control over. I feel like myself, I can be charming and funny and think and solve and I don't get nervous or self-conscious. I find that I enjoy these relationships a lot more, even though I never feel like I'm fully in love.

Anyway, she wants to take me out to dinner. We haven't seen each other in several months and before then we've only had brief one or two hour encounters, one of which I passed her on the street while on E. She gave me a kiss and we parted. Ruined my night. Another night I slept at her place and she had to get up early and leave. We didn't talk too much.

Overall, she's been a little bit disappointed in me, in a sense. It's an interesting relationship. We used to be really close and then I started smoking a lot and became distant and since then I haven't enjoyed being around her because I just feel miserable.

Right... so dinner. I'm terrified. I can't look at her without my brain going dead. My heart speeds up and I say stupid shit because I try to impress her. I can't help it. I can't be normal. I have to lie down to talk to her on the phone briefly. I have to think for absurdly long periods of time before responding to text messages. I just don't function properly around her.

I feel safe to assume that she was completely in love with me for a while, but we had both just come out of devastating relationships and neither of us were ready to do anything. She still sees me as that person, but we usually just get frustrated with each other nowadays. I'm not the same person because I cant' stop fucking thinking about what to say to her and shit. It's terrible.



Anyway, if you read everything, I apologize that you had to do that. I'm afraid that if I have dinner with her I'll do what I've been doing for the past six months, feel stupid at the end of the night, frustrate us both, and then come home and go through the same fucking withdrawal (yes, i get withdrawal from her. it's pathetic) i always go through and then I'll detach myself again and continue life happily.

She is a source of misery and I should probably cancel the dinner date, right? Self preservation.
