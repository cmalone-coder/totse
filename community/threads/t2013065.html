you need to eat more. if you lift and eat 1 meal a day you will lose muscle, because your body is just burning extra calories and isnt supplied with any, so it will eat your muscles. -eat more, 6 meals per day, every 3 hours
-Dont work the same muscles every day, give at least two days for them to recover. You gain muscle when you are resting, not lifting
-Keep workouts under an hour

Honestly, 40 rep exercises for 2 hours is nothing more than an endurance workout. The only way to gain strength and "get buff" is to lift heavy weights, I'm talking about weights you can only do 8 reps with for the specific exercise.

If you don't want to go to a gym, there are several bodyweight exercises you want to focus on: pushups, pullups, dips, pushups, and lunges. Throw your weights into a backpack and wear that for extra resistance. Bring your arms closer together or elevate your legs on a chair to make your pushups harder.

As for your little 15 pound weights, the only things I would use those for would be wrist curls, military press, and possibly some bicep curls. Be creative and look for other heavy things to lift, like furniture in your house or big sandbags. Sometimes I deadlift my dog for fun (he weighs 100 pounds).

Also, you need to set an exact time to eat each meal. Set your alarm clock, and when it goes off at the set time, force yourself to eat a meal.
