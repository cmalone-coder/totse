Don't forget to read the edit in my post.

To answer your question about what could be disastrous about it? Any number of things. He could very well be psychotic, abusive, AIDS infected, clingy, emotionally unstable, or maybe he just likes eating babies. The point is you don't know him, and you can't expect to have a real relationship with a person if you don't know them. Yeah you can be attracted and go from there, but why the hell would you say you want to have an exclusive dating relationship with one person in such a short amount of time? The disastrous part is that it will lead to high hopes, high expectations, and when it fails after 2 weeks, you'll be heartbroken and feel like shit. Breakups suck.

You show yourself to be less clingy or co-depdendent if you wait a while before you decide to be with someone. It reminds me of that commercial where a guy asks a random woman to marry him online, and then it says "you shouldn't invest with a broker you don't know either." I'm sure you get the point: you don't invest your time or your self in someone you don't know, because most likely you are not compatible with them, and you'll just break up in a few weeks or a few months and the whole experience will be agonizing. Bide your time, and it'll turn out better.
