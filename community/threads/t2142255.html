Working with nylon tights
Okay, I know it's a longshot asking for actual sewing advice on totse but it's worth a shot.

Alright, so I've got a pair of really rad cheetah print tights. The only problem is that the legs are a little short and the crotch is too low. I've worn them a few times but spent the entire evening reaching down my skirt to hike my tights up, not so sexy.

So here is what I want to do with them. I just want to cut the legs off and hem the top somehow so I can just wear them with a garter that I already have. Basically, I just need to chop the legs off and hem the top. Which would be an easy task if it wasn't this shit.

I really don't know much about working with this type of material other then if I were to cut it it would just fray like mad. I have no idea how I would even go about sewing it.

So any ideas? Someone suggested I go around the top where I was going to cut with fabric glue, then cut it to stop it from fraying. She wasn't sure if this would even work though.

And I'm going to try any suggestions on another pair first before I mangle my slightly expensive cool ones.
