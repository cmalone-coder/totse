Is love real?
I was thinking about love earlier today and I decided that belief in love is roughly equivalent to belief in god. If attraction is just an instinct to get humans to reproduce then love is just a biological impulse. If you believe that love is anything other than biological or that love can't be explained scientifically then what is it? Spiritual? I know spirituality doesn't equal god but spirituality isn't science. So if you don't believe in science then what do you believe in?

On the other hand if you do believe that love is biological then the word "love" would represent intense attraction or desire to mate. I think this is a pretty cynical outlook because it's admitting that all human interaction like attraction and friendship is instinctual but I'm starting to lean towards love being biological. I mean, wolves mate for life, unlike most humans. Do wolves fall in love? Or do they just choose the strongest mate?

I've never been in love. Sure I've been attracted to women, allot, but I've always recognized it as physical attraction. The women I am friends with I'm not really attracted to at all. I used to think about stuff like this allot but I'd get depressed allot so I stopped. I just read a topic in HB and it got me thinking again so I figured I see what &totse thought.

Relevant replies only please.
