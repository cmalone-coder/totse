Re: Wrapping a joint
Joints.... I think it is the best way to smoke weed. Learning to roll is a bastard though. I just bought some shitty cheap packet of ciggies, (�1.50p ish), and a packet of skins, and just rolled until I could do it well. Best thing is, you can use the same baccy over and over again, and skins cost nothing.

Use green rizlas, or you can get special skins from tobaconnists and dope shops, e.t.c. Don't bother with rainbow skins or giant skins or shit until you can roll with green rizlas (or swan).

Take a ciggie, and roll your skin around it, so the skin stays in a sort of curved shape when you put it down. Then, lick a strip of the ciggie so it goes damp (not too much, cuz you don't want to wet the baccy...).

Tear the strip off, and empty the baccy into the skin (I know you won't, but I'll say it anyway - don't put weed in until you can roll properly!!!). Then burn the weed into the J, all the way along.

Now get the J between your thumbs and fingers, and roll the baccy in the paper, and then roll the skin around it until you've got the last little bit. Lick it all the way along, smooth it down.

Get a 'pokey' (a matchstick or summin), and poke the baccy in at each end, so's you get a bit at each end with just paper in. Twist one end like a cracker, and you can either do the same with the other end, or put a roach in. I suggest you use a roach. Get your skin packet, and tear off an oblong-shaped strip. Roll it up so it fits into the end of the spliff. It should take up about the last centimetre of the J.

Now carefully tear off the twisted end, and light up, man...
