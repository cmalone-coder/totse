Heh, I was a nihilist for quite some time... until I realized it wasn't nihilism but chronic depression. Reading Descartes removed my doubt about there being absolute truths, and the discovery of R.M. Hare's ethical theories changed me from being simply misanthropic to becoming a humanitarian-misanthrope with a realization of the incredible importance of morals. And then I discovered pantheism and process philosophy through the works of Heraclitus, Spinoza and Whitehead, and Richard Dawkins and Einstein really fueled my love for knowledge and the search for truth. I'd also say that reading about Hinduism and Buddhism has had a great impact on me. The one good thing I got from my "nihilism" phase was a keen form of scepticism... and that's my ranting over.
