Salvia 20x. Apple. BIC Lighter. Amazing.
Just posting a little info to you all, nothing long.

Earlier today I made a pipe out of an apple (obviously not explaining, common sense).

The lighter I used was a normal BIC, made the blue part of the flame touch the substance.


The experience was quite amazing, although it was on the scary side, but that mostly had to do with the setting.

About 5 seconds after I took the hit (only needed one), I could tell I was fading fast. The first feeling one receives after the intake is the ability to feel one's soul, or second body, moving out of sync with the physical one. For instance, I could move my physical arm up in the air, but my astral one (spirtual, second body, soul etc) stayed in the same spot [EDIT: Maybe this is vica-versa, and I was on a verge of an OBE]. When I would move around, I had a split consciousness, in the sense that I was in two dimensions. It was the weirdest thing I have ever experienced. I was constantly being asked if I have found 'it', and I remember looking as hard as I could to find 'it' (later translated to the secret of life).

What I was able to retain from this experience was that there are many worlds within this Earth. We live everyday among beings unlike we have ever seen. This ordeal also re-affirmed in my mind the sense of purpose in this life, that it is vital to our innermost being to find 'it' in our lifetime.

Anyways, kinda went into a trip report, oh well.

Hope you all were able to learn from my experience, and teach others something different when you reach this level :)
