That's my general religious outlook. The universe clicks together very well. I am very different from christians though. I don't claim outside creators of stuff; I think everything that is, is the creator. In a sense, I create myself every day.

This is a healthier outlook because it means that instead of a God dictating things to us, we are all accumulatively a part of him, and we are him, and so is everything. This means our job is to go out and continually discover what God is just by discovering the universe itself. As we explore every level, from quantum mechanics, to evolution, to the cosmos, we learn more and more about God, and, ultimately, about ourselves.

If God actually did hand down a perfect infallible book that told us everything about everything (like 42), I would be quite upset with him.
