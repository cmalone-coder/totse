I don't know if this is "the right thing to do"- but I would (with condenser/p/p/balloon etc.) in place, do what you would do under normal circumstances, but much longer, until the tell-tale signs of completion are there. And after sometime, if I didn't like the way it was progressing, I would increase the heat- gradually- and if progress continued to be unsatisfactory- I'd push the threshhold of what you consider too hot. I really don't know though, that's just what I would do in that situation.
