My Boyfriend's Father
Mike is a great man. How can he not be? He raised Ty and I love Ty completely. But sometimes Mike is a terrible man.

Mike has high stress and high blood pressure. He'll freak out at every little thing. Once Ty offered to make supper for the family and Mike spent 30 mins critisizing everything he did, saying it wasn't cooked properly, or wrong. Ty is going to school to be a CHEF. Making fun of his cooking is the ultimate insult. He can make a fuckin stirfry thank you. But Mike just made him feel so horrible. I can't believe Ty finished making supper. The next day Mike told Ty to start getting supper ready and I outright refused that Ty do anything. He was hurt so badly by his father.

Mike and I have run ins too. I try very hard to make him laugh and lift his spirits. Yesterday he asked someone to put the kettle on for him. I did right away and he grabbed it when it whistled. I said coyly, "What makes you think that's for you?". I understand this could have be misunderstood but I said it in such an obvious joking tone. When he started to stutter and put it back I laughed and said it was indeed for him. Then he started yelling saying he felt he was being attacked. Just a little joke will set him off.

A couple months ago Mike's wife was feeling overworked and down so I went and bought the family groceries and some nice perfum and flowers for her. When Mike got home he was outraged that no thought of him. Maybe he felt neglected but I did something nice for his WIFE. He could have just not made a scene and been happy we made her smile.

Now yesterday I had my nephew over. He is very important to me. Last year I quit school and spent 8 months raising him. He's like my son. I hardly ever get to see him and was excited. He's nearly 3. At supper, which I made, Mike started yelling at his daughter like hardcore, and cursing. Then they brought out the pot infront of my nephew. I'm okay with pot, Ty does it, but not around a child. I was so upset I started to cry and grabbed the baby and took him away. Their fightng made ME uncomfortable, it must have been so scary
for such a little guy. That wasn't even the worst part. I put the baby to bed and heaven forbid the dishwasher wouldn't close. Mike started slamming it and yelling. Ty told him to shut up, there was a baby sleeping downstairs, but Mike only yelled louder. How does he get off acting like that? My nephew means the world to me, and I'm sad I can't see him all the time. But I decided not to bring him over again. It's a bad environment.

Now I know I've written many examples of what makes Mike terrible. These things have just been circulating my mind for awhile. But he is a good man too. When he's happy he's really happy. He laughs and plays and loves his children more than his own life. He's been extremely stressed for money and work lately and I think that is what causes the outbursts. I dunno. I just needed to get this off my chest so I don't bring it up with him.

TL/DR - My boyfriend's father is prone to explosive and angry outbursts. It disrupts my, my boyfriend's, and sometime those I love's happiness, Mostly just ranting. Not really expecting any response.
