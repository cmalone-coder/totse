Originally Posted by Captain Cannabis
Get some bubble bags and make hash.


Yes, agitate it in ice water for a half hour and then use bubble bags/5 gallon buckets to strain it out. It makes excellent hash, and the quantity will probably surprise you. The ice water makes the THC crystals fall right off the leaf and the bags strain them out, rendering a very pure product. The final product looks like a greenish grey to black silly putty. You can then mold it as you desire, wrap it in saran, and slow dry it on a very low heat.

You may be able to DIY the bags out of different micron meshes but I'm not the gal to ask about that. Just Google "bubble hash".

You can also make honey hash out of it with butane and PVC but I'm not a big fan- it's a pain in the ass to scrape it all off into usable product, and I don't enjoy working with volatiles personally.
