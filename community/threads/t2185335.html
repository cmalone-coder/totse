Re: Future of Drugs
I'm certainly no expert, and I base this off-of nothing other than personal opinion; but I think that on a worldwide basis that the use/abuse of methamphetamine/other amphetamines and cocaine/crack will become more prevalent. The popularity for alcohol will increase significantly, whereas the popularity for tobacco will decline at an exponential rate. Marijuana will increase in popularity, and there will either be very non-severe punishments, or no punishments at all for simple possession.

Both the legitimate and non-legitimate use of benzos will increase on a massive scale. There will be many people, who as a result of the current economic decline will be going to their doctors complaining about stress and anxiety. As the amount of prescriptions issued increases, so will the non-legitimate use of benzos. Since there will be more prescription benzos lying around the houses of families - more teenagers will decide to experiment with drugs, and will take their parents meds.

The use of hallucinogens, in my opinion, will still only be used by a certain group of people, and won't really become any more wide-spread. The drugs that will become the most common will be drugs that are A) cheap and B) stress-relieving/euphoric - so meth, MDMA, alcohol and pot.
