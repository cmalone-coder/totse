The first method I recommend is certainly the most efficient, but you have to be familiar with how to use IRC.

If so, log on to the network named Undernet.

Once logged on, join the room #bookz

Once inside, search for whatever you want by typing the following command into the main window:

@seek (the name of what you're looking for)

After a minute or so, an automated bot will send you a list of any results from your search.

He'll send you a zip file, containing a text file. Open the text file, and look at the results.

If you recognise what you are searching for in the list, copy that line (the ENTIRE line) and paste it into the main window of the chatroom, then hit enter.

When the relevant server has an open slot, it will transfer you the book you asked for.

Simple as that!

One word of advice: make sure that your chat program doesn't automatically ignore zip files, or this process will not work.
