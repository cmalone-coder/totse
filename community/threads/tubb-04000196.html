For the most part. An erotic story is generally written with more intelligence and, often, more background and detail than a simple written-out sex scene. If you picture in your mind what's actually happening, though, both of those are likely to bring forth similar images. There's a gray area there, where something like K9's post would fall- it's detailed, it's more poetic than "I sucked him off", but it's very to-the-point.

That's my opinion- to some people, if it's just about sex, it's porn, but if there's a romantic background to it, then it's erotica, no matter how much skill goes into the writing. Either way, it's all good. Enjoy reading what you like.
