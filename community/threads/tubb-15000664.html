Originally posted by grasshopa:
Are you the same religion you were born into? Just curious, I dumped my religion


I have a good memory of early childhood and don't recall any religion before i was seven. At seven i was taken to a Presbyterian sunday school, i rememember sitting on a long wooden stool with a bunch of other kids while a young lady told us the story of the Good Shepherd.

The 23rd Psalm mixed with descriptions of David(the physical precursor of the spiritual) as a young shepherd, a powerful young man capable of fighting off all predators, a friend when in need. Needed one of those at the time, and from then on envisioned the Good Shepherd as the ideal.

Mum got mixed up with a couple of cults and before you know it our lives turned to crap, took years to become free of that, in young adulthood, i went atheist, but it just did not make sense. Searching for answers went evangelical, gave away bibles and advised people to read it for themselves, don't just take someone else's word for what it all means!

One night the missus and i were trying to sort out the origin of the universe, we were following up the discoveries of science to do with matter and anti-matter. We spent hours brainstorming using the assumption that there is nothing inherently evil in the 'theory of evolution, just kids trying to work out how dad did it!

Afterward, in the midnight darkness, headed for bed, in a millisecond life changed, i was confronted by a being of light. It seemed as though a flash went off, for the shortest of moments i was eye to eye with .... eyes of light, the brightest eyes one could possibly imagine, light shining from within. These eyes lit up the hall and silhouetted the shape of a person, no words were spoken, but a thought seemed projected into my mind - "I love you I died for you".

Then .. gone, i was stunned, could not believe what had happened, thought perhaps i had taken leave of my senses, but straight way had that dispelled by the missus. I thought little of this incident, in fact it made no sense, a being of light just appearing? "I love you I died for you"? What did this mean? Made no sense to me, try as i could the meaning of that phrase escaped me, the identity of the being was a mystery, How can someone die for another?

Could not make sense of it so left it alone did not mention it to anyone, not even the missus, then she was killed, crushed in the impact from being flung off an out of control motorcycle/sidecar outfit. Well i was pretty p'd off at everything in general after that and God in particular, had good reason to question God on the meaning of that event.

Anyway buried the missus, and a bit later partnered up with a motorcycle riding, guitar playing, singer lass i met while out punting the BM. Six kids later, i'm out bushwalking the local mountains, and bite off more than i can chew. Wound up rockclimbing a vertical clifface in hiking gear, no ropes!

Almost at the top of a 300 metre drop, my foot slips and faster than the most powerful accelerating bike, i'm plunging headlong down the mountain. I was a dead man! Desperately i grabbed at bushes and rock, no chance, bounced off one ledge on my right thigh, then the next off my left, then headfirst over a sheer cliff. I was looking at a 300metre/900 foot near vertical drop. I knew i was dead, the immediate thought in my mind was sadness for my baby son who would grow up without a dad. Then the thought Jesus came into my mind and i yelled Jesus as loud as i could. At that instant i slammed headfirst into the rockface, blacked out, and later came to cradled by a couple of small bushes growing out the side of the cliff - blood pouring out of a split head.

The very first thoughts that ran thru my mind was a scripture "Whoever calls on the Name of The Lord will be saved".

True story, the Emergency Service Volunteers came in the middle of the night on a rope and dragged me up the cliff. Here in this area of OZ the newspapers and tv made a thing out of it and after a while i took to wearing a Praise The Lord beltbuckle - saved telling the story over and over!

Since then i have also "dumped religion", the spiritual life is so much better, no fear, no negatives, no bigotry, no dogma. An open and enquiring mind is the result of my life experience to date, i'm keen, eager to learn everything there is to know about the spiritual life of every person possible.

Nothing to sell, no indoctrination, no bias, no discrimination; intense curiosity - a burning desire to know all spiritual paths and the travellers thereon.
