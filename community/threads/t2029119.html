Alright, I think Michael Moore is a douchebag, but even if he blew it out of proportion it is still a problem. The United States is suppose to be the greatest country on earth, but we have a generally average health care system compared to the rest of the world. I'm not a big fan of taxes because some of our money is used for stupid programs that never work out, but this is something I expect to be taxed for. I'm from a middle class family, only because my dad works his ass off. My mom can't work due to a medical procedure that accidently fucked with her diaphragm. She went in to a simple surgery and came out disabled, she's now on Social Security, which isn't much. We have medical bills after medical bills, most of them prescriptions that she has to take. We're just lucky enough that my dad has a good job and it comes with insurance. If we didn't have that we'd be fucked, so I feel grateful. Although, I do feel that this could easily happen to some family without insurance and how tough it must be for them.

I know some people argue that they don't want the same people who run the postal service, social security..etc running our nations health care system. I just don't buy that arguement. If we funded enough money into a socialized medicine program it would be just as well run as Australia's, it's possible it could be better. How is better when a bunch of rich people run a private healthcare system than the people who run other government agencies? These people don't care about general health, they care about the money that's coming their way.

People who argue that they don't want to give their money to the program because they take care of themselves is also a weak arguement. One of these days you or a friend/family member might be put into a tough finacial situation and won't be able to afford insurance.

I'd be willing to take a chunk out of my income for a socialized medical program...

And this is coming from someone who is a moderate right down the line not from someone very liberal.
