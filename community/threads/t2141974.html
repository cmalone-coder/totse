Re: Fallout 3 gameplay footage!
I'm becoming more and more fearful of the final shape of things. Not to sound negative here, but...

The environment looks bland and boring. Needs more color, more garbage, more retro-futuristic decay. I'm hoping the washed-out look is mostly attributable to the projector/camera combo.

The battle system looks awful. Leave the quasi-action stuff to Mass Effect, please. The turn-based tactics are one of my favorite elements of the series. I like rolling dice to score headshots on giant scorpions, thanks.

The bad guys look as boring as the rest of it. Also, how about some pre-scripted Bloody Mess action? The bouncing body parts don't cut it, and Havok doesn't fix everything.

The radio seems like a nice idea.

Also, all this bitching is rather pointless, as I'm gonna end up buying the damn game any way. Just seems very unpolished for something three months away...
