Re: slacker synth
The "vaccuum" is not necessary. Don't worry, it doesn't do anything for the actual reaction. It only helps to tell at which stage the reaction is at.

A reaction could be done in a jar without any condensor, push/pull, balloon, etc., as long as the water level remained the same, and the precursors were ALL lab-grade. Yield wouldn't be too great, but it could be done.

Tip: Look into other synths. This one is a little out-dated, and requires previous "cooking" experience(IMHO). Try the Long And Wet. Good success rate.
