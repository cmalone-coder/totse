The biggest problem that you have with spanish is the number/gender in adjetives; remember you must use them for example: en la país sudamericano de Venezuela
That phrase looks horrible in spanish, but it is correct. I would have used simply "en Venezuela, Sudamérica".

"Esta cascada es novecientos setenta y nueve metros alto" should be "esta cascada tiene novecientos setente y nueve metros de altura"

"esta a muchas millas de la civilizacion"

"porque está mucho lloviendo y los rios son hondo" should be "poreue en esa estacion llueve mucho y los rios son mas caudalosos."

And something advanced:

"Yo admiro la área" should be "Yo admitro el area"

area is female so the article should "la". But as area starts with 'a', the article is changed to the male one el, to make it sound better:

Other examples are

el ancla
el agua
el aula
