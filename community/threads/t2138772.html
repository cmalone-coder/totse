Weird I stumbled opun this thread; I was in a situation where I needed a trip sitter not to long ago.

I've taken mushrooms before several times before, and I have never had a problem keeping my head. Turns out, these mushrooms were waaaay more potent then the ones I have [reviously tried. It was just me and my friend. I remmember for the first couple of hours I was having such a great time, I was crying because I was laughing so hard. Then I saw something that completely fucked my night up.

As I turned around to look at my freind, I remmember him looking like he was swelling up. At first I was like, your just trippin. But after a few minutes I really started to freak out. One of my freinds recently died of a methadone overdose, and when I went to his funeral his face looked all swollen. I tried as hard as I could to stop thinking crazy, but I couldn't help it. My best friend looked like he was dying.

Right when the thought of my best friend dying hit me, he started coughing. His face looked like it kept swelling up and at this point it looked like he was bleeding out of his eyes. He tried to tell me he was fine, but I couldn't stop seeing what I was seeing. I knew I was just tripping but it didnt change anything. I started yelling at him, telling him to help me find a phone so I could talk to someone sober. He went to stand up and he couldn't walk. I see him fall and at this point all I can see is him swelling up and shaking and dying.

I knew previously that it was pretty much impossible for us to overdose, but it didn't matter because it didn't change what I was seeing and feeling. Everything spun out of control, and I thought I was dying to.

Then I started to really act crazy. I ran around inside the house as fast as I could jumping on things, and just basically losing my mind. I was trying to do whatever I could to convince myself that I wasnt dying. If I can run I cant be dying right? If I can jump I cant be dying right?

No matter what I did I couldn't calm myself down.

For some reason I ran into the bathroom, and my friend kept trying to open the door. I bugged out because I thought someone was after me, and I punched the window and shattered it. This is when all hell broke loose.

I started bleeding everywhere, and at this point thats all I could see. Even though it was just my hand bleeding, it felt like it was my entire body bleeding. I thought that I was dying, and I was going to hell. My friends voice and my voice started coming out real low pitched like we were demons. I ran outside and just started running all over the yard trying to escape hell.

My friend called 911, and the cops and a ambulance arrives. I dont remmember a whole lot about the cops, but appearantly I charged at them and they had to wrestle me to the ground. When they had me on my feet I looked into their faces and I could see them turning red, and their voices were really low pitched. I thought they were taking me to hell.

They strapped me to a hospital bed and took me off in the ambulance, and for the next few hours everyone I saw looked liked demons except for one nurse, which I thought was their to save me. It took me till 6 or 7 hours into the trip just to remmember my own name.

When it was all over, and I realized where I was and what happened I felt really stupid. I know if I just had a sober person their to tell me I was just trippen I would have been fine. I think it was a combination of my freind dying recently, my trip partner not being able to communicate with me, and being on some really potent mushrooms just made eveything spin out of control.

Ive had some really good times on mushrooms, but this experience totally changed the way I look at psychedelics. I never in a million years would have thought that I could have a bad trip like that.

Honestly, it's better to have a sober person around and not need one, then to need one and not have one.
