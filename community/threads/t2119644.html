I ate an eighth of mushrooms last night.

Well... me and a friend went out on a day long nature walk. We realized how amazing of a day it was, not windy, not cold, not hot... just great!

We started getting home and my friend says "lets go to the fuckin' bar"

I didn't agree, I told him I would rather order booze, which didn't happen.

So, I went on a mad hunt for mushrooms (which only took 20 minutes)

And on we went to pick up a quarter ounce to split between us.

We bought the 1/4 for 40 and each ate an eighth... we then began walking and smoking cigarrettes.

At the T+40min. mark I mentioned to my friend that I felt nothing, he said he could tell he had eaten mushrooms, but nothing really yet.

Around the time he said this, we arrived at a large school playground, and as we walked along the jagged brick wall, I noticed something was happening, something was REALLY happening, those bricks were shifting and moving in a way that mushrooms never do!

I watched the wall for a moment then complained of severe stomach cramps and a numbness in my right thumb.

We went to sit down and I busted out my pipe. I ground around .5g of decent pot, and I began toking it. I cough real hard and felt myself half vomit then gag. I said aloud, "that's it, let it out!" and I took another toke of the pipe, coughed, and out came everything.

I felt like a million bucks, and I checked my ipod to see the time, it had been 1 hour and 10 minutes since we ate them.... strange.

I then got strange static fields surrounding me, they would crackl and float through the sky. I also watched a tree fall over to a 45 degree angle... which was strange.

My question is though... that after feeling these really really intense feelings and seeing these intense visuals... after I puked, it was pretty much done.

I puked, and MY FRIEND no longer felt high.... we went to my house a little later to smoke bongs and I ate a mg of lorazepam on the way home, because I didn't want to stay up all night alone with the megalomania head... so we smoked bongs, both became very and he went home.

Did I get shit mushrooms or did I ruin it by puking?
