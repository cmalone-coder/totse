A relationship for the long run (tips from people who have made it needed)
So. The other month, I read a pseudo-fairy-tale. It was set about five years after the dashing prince rescued the beautiful princess. But something was wrong; after they married and lived together, they found themselves fighting. Sour. Tired of each other.

It was only a bitter "fairy tale", true, but I have noticed that this does seem prevalent. Look at the divorce rates! Look at how many unhappy marriages there are, that started out with whirlwind courtships! It scares the shit out of me every time I read something like "my fiance' dumped me after five years." How does that happen?!

What I'm looking for is advice on keeping everything going smoothly. I love my girlfriend and she loves me; we're moving in together as soon as we can, and I'd like to marry her one day. But I don't ever want the magic to go out of our relationship... I don't want us to end up some bitter couple who don't really enjoy each others' company any more. May that never happen!

So I guess what I'd like to know is... What can I do to ward this off? What are things to watch out for? What have you learned that has helped you, or would've if you had learned it in time? This forum has lots of good advice on catching someone's eye, but I haven't seen much on keeping them, on getting along with someone for the rest of your life.

Thanks in advance for any helpful replies

Re: A relationship for the long run (tips from people who have made it needed)
Don't move in as soon as possible. Really, having separate areas will REALLY help. My boyfriend and I moved in together on accident pretty much. We moved from a 2 bedroom to a one bedroom and we're feeling it.

It's very important to not meld things too quickly. Or you'll end up feeling like you don't have a second to think in your own head. Hang out with your friends, but let your SO know what's going on.

Don't use their stuff like it's yours, unless it's specifically talked about. Little things can get annoying really quick. For instance, my boyfriend didn't buy body wash for 2 weeks so he used my expensive shit and used more than half of it. Everytime I got in the shower I was like "WTF!?"

Talk about EVERYTHING that comes to your mind. Even negative shit, otherwise it can build up and break you. (This recently happened to me leading to a huge fall out with my b/f, who, thankyoujesus, has the understanding to deal with m craziness.)

Another HUGE thing: always be willing to "work on it". The second you get defensive with "Don't try to change who I am" is the second you doom your relationship. Relationship don't just happen, they are achieved. Just like jack said, there's arguing, but that's the only way you resolve things. Your partner is by no means a mirror image of yourself, you might get along ridiculously well, you might even say the same things at the same moments, and call each other at the same times, but that doesn't mean they know what you're thinking or 100% agree. Don't assume they have ESP, but that's more of a women's problem.

Try not to play the blame game. Take responsibility for your actions.
