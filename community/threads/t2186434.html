Copy pasta from my Rok-it bong thread.

I look for everything that OP looks for. It's the next addition into my collection, whenever I can afford it.

----

So, I've been on the lookout for a new bong, and I've seen these Rok-it bongs for a long time on the everyonedoes it headshop, and I've always been intrigued.

A few things that really turn me onto the idea of owning one:

1. Durable. I've gone through way too much money on broken glass.

2. Very smooth hits. Glass joint bongs are all the rage these days, but this bong looks just as effective - if not more so. In fact I think they're even the originators in smoke diffusion in bongs.

3. Small. I dunno why, I just like compact bongs. ESPECIALLY if they're as smooth as this one claims. Easy to pack, easy to hit, easy to pass, easy to transport. They even come with their own nifty case for easy packing.

4. Novelty. Glass joint bongs are everywhere now. I'm sure most of you know someone who has one. But what about Rok-its? You don't see very many of those. I've smoked with TONS of people, and have never run across someone who owns one.

5. Video below demonstrates how quickly the bong clears. I hate bongs that take most of your lung capacity just clearing a bong. Not only does this bong hit smooth, but when you inhale you're going to get much more smoke than the bigger bongs.

6. Easy to clean. These pieces can be disassembled completely and each piece can be cleaned to work brand spankin' new. Some of the big bongs have so much stuff in them, ice catchers, diffusers, percolators, ash catchers, large chambers...many of you know how much of a pain in the ass it can be to clean them. And lets face it, after they've been broken in, they just don't have that oh-so-nice fresh bong taste to them.

Anyway, here's some stuff for those of you who don't know what I'm talking about:

In action: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRZX3pTR6rQ

So, anyone use one? I've read good things but I'd like some of your guys' input. I've almost definitely got my mind set on this thing. It just meets all of my preferences in smoking technology.
