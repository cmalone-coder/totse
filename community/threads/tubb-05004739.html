Re: How Much Is Too Much?
Your dxm losing it's "magic" is maybe the least of your worries. A little friendly warning for all you dissociative users out there from your friendly BLTC pharmacist. Go to Erowid .Org, or do a google search and look up Olneys lesions. Or maybe Olmeys lesions, one or the other, can't recall which spelling is correct. Dissociatives include DXM, PCP, ketamine. A little preview, because I am to lazy to explain fully, and also wan't to startle or intrest you just enough so you will go look it up. Olneys Lesions are simply a negative effect on your cerebral tissue(brain, Grey matter)pruportedly caused by use or abuse of dissociative substances(DXM,PCP and Ketamine). Lesions are in laymans terms "scrapes, or small holes" in your brain tissue, and Olneys lesions have similar effects to alzhiemers according to my memory...which may or may not be working properly at any given moment. ONCE AGAIN, GO TO EROWID.ORG, SEARCH FOR OLNEYS LESIONS, OR DISSOCIATIVES, OR KETAMINE, OR PCP, OR DXM...LEARN WHAT MIGHT BE HAPPENING.
I am not trying to bullshit you, or scare you. I am just letting you know what some research is showing. Learn about all effects of your drugs, not just the high and dosage.
