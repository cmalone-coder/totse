Intravenous Ether Administration.
journalled by Intra Venous Esq.

"From a study of intravenous ether anesthesia, it was concluded that ether diluted to a 5 per cent solution in 5 per cent dextrose and water may be used to induce and maintain a smooth and easily controlled anesthetic state similar to that obtained with inhalation ether but without the dependence of the latter technique on ventilation. Cough and laryngospasm were absent. Adequate spontaneous respiration can be maintained with this technique. The technique is particularly useful in endoscopy during which the airway is often not available for anesthetic administration."
http://www.pubmedcentral.nih.gov/articlerender.fcgi?artid=1515100

Now that proof has been supplied regarding the possibility of this action to be undertaken, for I have been accused of mistruths by children on this forum regarding my drug use more than once, I can start with what is an abbreviated form of what is quite a lengthy report. I'll email it to you SN, ahah.

We've all seen Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas. We all know the quote. "There is nothing in the world more helpless and irresponsible and depraved than a man in the depths of an ether binge." But well, just how depraved can a man get before he's spending time with a cellmate named Sunhil who's in for serial rape? How far can this ether induced depravity be pushed? Surely if the dosage can be upped to that wonderfully semi-lucid place just before total anaesthesia we can have a lot of fun with this sociological experiment into ether use and the depths of a particular ex-convicts very own depravity. Something pointy and filled with a controlled solvent would have to enter my veins.

One thing lead to another and I was to find myself headed to the land of ether and Thierry van den Bosch, Belgium.

Belgium, I must say, the ladies who work in your chemists are far more pleasant on the eyes than those I'm accustomed to, and they're made ever more delightfull by their willingness to thrust a bottle of ether into the clammy hands of one whom is clearly, as they say, junked the fuck out. With several bottles of this delightful substance in my possession it was time to head home.

The next stage in this experiment was the acquisition of a 16 gauge needle, some saline and a drip bag. This was fairly easy given the fact that I frequently stick my dick in a charming young nursing student named Pia.

Now it was time for the fun to begin. Or self-mutilation depending on your point of view.

After doing my research, I'd discovered that 300-600ml of a solution (either dextrose or saline) with 5% ether was administered to a person to give an anaesthetic effect. In my case, I was using saline. I decided that seeing as I didn't really want to go under completely, the base level would be where I began, so 15ml of diethyl ether was added to the saline solution, then shaken so as to mix the solution rather then have a layer of ether and layer of saline. This was then elevated and connected via a drip, more of a flow to be honest, into the already discussed 16 gauge, and from there into my arm. After a period of time, it either speaks wonders for the ether that I don't remember, or speaks volumes about what a decade of drug abuse does to the human brain, I felt warm, floaty and altogether sloppy. This was the peak of the effects at this dosage. Almost an alcohol intoxication but with a more psychadelic feel to it, everything seemed far more surreal. However, this is not particularly different to the effects garnered from huffing ether. So once completely sober, though now pantsless, I decided to up the dosage to 30ml. I do not know what happened from here on in.

After waking, naked and cold with only my pheasant screaming jesus and the epileptic church for company, I decided that perhaps 30ml was a tad too much. My only real recollection of that experience are of some extremely odd dreams. I decided to try 25ml of ether in solution. It was here that paydirt was hit. This became one of the most out of control, fucking hilarious, drug-related experiences of a lifetime full of out of control drug-related experiences. I was hit with a sense of supreme superiority to the rest of the world, my coordination was shot to hell (I'd say it was out the door but I doubt it was even able to get that far), and I felt an intense euphoria. It was rather on par with a high dose of ketamine to be honest, not a k-hole, but a fairly good dosage of the shit. Everything was spinning, I had no real grasp of the concepts of location and time, and for some reason colours appeared slightly different to me, almost as if on a very low dose of mushrooms. I happily knocked shit over and generally fucked up my apartment like this for hours, re-upping with the help of a willing assistant whenever my speech became coherent enough to ask for it. Duration of peak effects is around 35 minutes, with the entire experience lasting for around an hour, with an intense burning in the vein it was administered to for around 2-4 minutes after the entire solution was delivered. It's really difficult for me to say when peak affects were reached due to the length of time at which it took for the entire solution to run into my arm, but if pushed I'd say around 45 seconds after the entire solution had magically disappeared into my body.

I was having so much fun using ether, that me and said assistant managed to go through 2L in a 30 hour period, with some hopefully being spilt.

The next day however, was not so much fun. The hangover from this shit was unlike anything I've ever experienced. Think the day after st patricks day + a week awake on ice + the feeling of disgust and self-loathing after becoming rapemeat for some big nigger in prison. You aren't even close sunshine.

Unfortunately, this massive bout of the Tuesday blues wasn't enough to stop me from using several more litres of the shit over the next 2 or so months, and it's now come to the point where I'm no longer interested in it, bar occassionally mixing it with jager and doing shots of it.

Really, there isn't much point doing this to yourself unless you're having endoscopy done. Ether is far more recreational when huffed than IV'd. I only really did it because I was curious and well, curiosity kills the cat, or at least a lot of his braincells in this case.

Have fun and be safe.
