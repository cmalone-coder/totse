Childhood hallucinations
This thought came to me suddenly a few days ago. I remember when I little, uptil about 12 years, I used to have hallucinations every night. They always occurred in the dark and were red outlines of different objects. They would gently float around me and move slowly.

A friend of mine also remembered having hallucinations as a child that went away around entering teens. Her hallucinations were always of tiny atomic structured balls. And she never saw them in the dark. Only in sunlight coming in from windows etc.

Anyone remember having extremely vivid hallucinations as a child? I used to consider them a normal part of life and thought everyone saw things. When the hallucinations went away I slowly forgot about them and till a few days ago had never remembered them once in a long time. Now it seems strange, how easily I forgot them.
