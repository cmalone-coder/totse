The box was already open when I bought it, which sparked an idea in my head. After I smoked all 200 of the cigarettes(a week later), I wondered if I could somehow reseal the carton box and return the package.

I kept all of the packs of cigs after I smoked it and put them to the side. I then put some paper towels from a community bathroom, and folded them up inside the packs to weigh them down. I then sealed up the carton box with superglue, and now I'm about to return them.

I just hope cigarettes are returnable, so I can get my $28 back. Sure, most people wouldn't go through all this effort for that little amount of money, but I am a poor college student who needs to buy more cigarettes(I hope to quit soon... hah). If for some reason I get shit for returning it, I'll say that you sold these to my brother who is under 18, and get all pissy and loud. It usually works in other situations.

I'll let everyone know how it goes, even though I don't think I'll have any troubles. I realize this isn't the most original idea, but if it works, you can probably apply it to other products and make some good money off of it. Let me know what you think.
