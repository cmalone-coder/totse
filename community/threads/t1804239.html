First thing I would like to say is why hasn't anyone else posted something like this before?

Now let's get a couple things straight. American jobs are moving overseas, mostly IT (if you don't know what this is, jump out the nearest window), to India because labor is plentiful, cheaper, and Indians are more hard working. This is contrasting to times before in which Indians were being brought over on the H-1 visa to work for American companies. Now Indians control many major IT companies (Sun, Lucent).

How does this impact Americans?

Less higher paying jobs for American educated people. More layoffs for American companies. But productivity and price of labor drops.

Is it worth it?

No.

Why do I have a problem with it?

Because I want a job when I get out of College.

Whats my place in American Society?

I m an Indian, born in India, parents came here in after waiting 6 years for a green card. My mom and dad both worked at a newsprinting company lifting bags for a couple of years while my dad went to school during the day. He went to a local community college and worked to become a computer programmer(He did his master's in A.I. while he was in India). Now he makes 100,00+ and my mom now runs her own set of printing presses in the same company.

Living the American Dream?

Yeah, but if we keep getting screwed over for jobs I won't be.

I'm not for immigration laws or quotos, but I am for keeping the American Industry at home so we can have a future.
