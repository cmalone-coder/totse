Re: How do i get some balls? (Confidence of course, guys)
In order to gain something, you have to know what it is that you want. Sure, you want confidence. But in what sense? The ability to go up to a random girl and ask for their number? Or to belong to part of the main popular group in high school? Confidence can be applied to any situation, and usually it makes you look a better and stronger person, unless you use it too far. But the attainment? Well essentially I would suggest that it fundamentally is doing or saying something, and not caring if you are wrong, or if someone else disagrees, i.e. having the balls to do what you want.

Now, as I see it, confidence can be used in two ways; either in accordance with other human interaction, or own your own. Probably the easiest to start off with is on your own. How I hear you say? Well, take pride in whatever makes you feel good inside. Say you play guitar. If you make a kick-ass riff, take pride and say "wow, I made this". Or etc. with any subject, e.g. art, writing, school work.

In social situations it can be hard to first apply it. Especially with people you see a lot. So perhaps (and this is where I gained mine), you have a job. Where people come and go, and are for the most, different each day. If you begin to interact with them, who cares if you fuck up? Chances are you wont see them again. So if you do, just move on to the next person. Practise social skills, talking and interacting. But remember, confidence essentially is saying what you want. If someone disagrees, well that's their opinion. But don't back down, you will appear weak. In fact, a side note here, even if you are wrong, those with the highest amount of intellectual confidence say, will change what they said (if actually wrong), and make it appear that they in fact are agreeing with the other person.

Now, once you have gained confidence in yourself, and with interacting with random strangers, time to apply it to those who you see regularly. This is the hardest stage, because, especially at young ages, people tend to judge. Yet perseverance is the key. Without trying, we are all doomed to fail.
