Re: DJz Pleaz !!!
if you have a problem beatmixing you can get mixers with led indicators pulling the beat from the turntable. in my opinion vynle is far superior to cd 1: its a better media i don't feel the satisfaction of mixes working with cds it feels to automated. 2: you can't lay down any good fx with cd like spinbacks scratching etc. 3: vynls worth more and ages more gracefully than cd. if you wanna hear some superb mixing and scratching get dj assault's "straight up detroit shit vol 4".
Good artists Photec, autechre, underground resistance, subhead(look out for a tune on napster "subhead remixes (dave tarrida)!!s" i only managed to get half of it but its superb), scan 7, aux 88, dopplereffekt, drexciya, the hacker, surgeon, takkyu ishino(great japanese techno), green velvet (look out for a tune called flash), I-f, jeff mills, japanese telecom or 808 state. all good stuff but most of its really dark techno or euro electro it may not be your scene.
