SWIM has used the TCE/JD extensivly. SWIM has test run 5 different brands of 60`s. SWIM has done em with chlor. mal. and with trip. SWIM has ALWAYS had most excellent results. SWIM has experienced two things when it comes to bees not being able to get the blue out.

1. they are not powdering the psuedo before each step by putting it thru a fine sieve strainer.

2. they are using to much JD in the mix and/or they are letting the pill mass set in the TCE/JD to long thereby causing heavy metal (cobalt) to stay with the psuedo causing the blue color that wont leave.

this is the proper way to do it:

when you have all the pills broken out of there bubble nests mortar & pestle them to as fine as you can get them. then run them thru a fine sieve strainer. the pills will be like coarse flour or fine silica sand. run the TCE/JD wash making sure that you mix the TCE/JD at a ratio of 10 to 1. thats 10 parts TCE to one part JD. EXAMPLE: 100 ml TCE=10 ml of JD, 500 ml TCE=50 ml JD. etc. DO NOT use more JD than what SWIM has outlined. let the pill mass set in the TCE/JD for 3 minutes. after its has filtered thru a SINGLE coffee filter spread it out on a glass plate and let it dry till bone dry. DO NOT set the plate on any heat scource to speed the drying, strictly room temp. after the pill mass is dry and before adding the denat alky run them thru the fine sieve strainer again. DO NOT skip this. it is important to powder them down at each step SWIM has outlined here as doing this exposes the entire surface area of the mass to whatever chems, solvents etc. that are involved in each step leaving no place for gakks to hide. after the 3 denat alky pulls are evapped and scraped up, again make sure its bone dry and run the psuedo thru the fine sieve AGAIN before the tone wash. after the tone wash dry the psuedo out bone dry again. the final step is passing the psuedo thru the fine seive yet again before it go`s in the flask. SWIM has traced every failure of this method that has been brought to his attention to operator error NOT ONCE has it been because of the pills provided they were white 60`s.
