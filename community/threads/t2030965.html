Re: Question about purchasing guns
First thing is you need to know this as gospel.

Jeff Cooper's Rules of Gun Safety

RULE I: ALL GUNS ARE ALWAYS LOADED

RULE II: NEVER LET THE MUZZLE COVER ANYTHING YOU ARE NOT WILLING TO DESTROY

RULE III: KEEP YOUR FINGER OFF THE TRIGGER UNTIL YOUR SIGHTS ARE ON THE TARGET

RULE IV: BE SURE OF YOUR TARGET

Since you live in a free state, you don't need to give 'good reason' i.e make something up. So I wouldn't.

How much do you want to spend? I've listed shotguns because they're generally cheaper than rifles. Of course if you want you could always get one of the cheaper AKs.

The big pump shotguns are:
*Mossberg 500 (Maverick 88 is their budget version); Mossberg 500 is around $300, Maverick 88 is being sold for $200.
*Remington 870; prices are all over the place on this one.
*Saiga 12; if you want a shotgun in an AK format. Price seems to be about $300-500.

Budget break action shotguns are around $200-300.

The most important thing when buying a gun is how it fits. Its a lot like clothes. If it doesn't fit well, you're not going to practice, and if you don't practice, you're going to be in trouble should the shit hit the fan. So go to the rifle range and gun shops and feel them and shoot them before putting money down.
