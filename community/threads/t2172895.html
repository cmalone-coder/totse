How old are you? While some people do go through a bi-curious 'phase', I'd say it becomes less likely the older you get, since it tends to be associated with all the hormones and such in earlier adolescense.

Also, you have to consider one thing: It's comparatively easier for a male to be attracted to females, because it's socially accepted. So you recently discovering your attraction for the same sex doesn't necessarily mean a whole lot, because it's generally not natural for a person to expect for homosexual attraction to be there, while it is natural to expect one's self to be straight, most times, due to all the social messages we've received growing up and such.

So, one is directly in line with the social norm (heterosexuality) and one is obviously a bit more opposed (homosexuality), so I think it's a bit natural you'd discover you're interested in males after realizing your interest in females, whatever your orientation happens to be, because the one is just easier to come to terms with, socially. It's easier to fulfill social expectations than to discard them.

If you think it might be a 'phase', just don't bother trying to classify yourself. The whole 'gay', 'straight', and 'bi' thing is a recent cultural invention. It's just a word that's an easy way of saying what genders you tend to be interested in. It's more for convenience than anything (communicating to others what people you're interested in sexually, demystifying the very complex world of human sexuality so it can simply be navigated a little bit eaiser.) But none of those things are of utmost importance to the individual. A person can easily live in a state of uncertainty about one's orientation.

Also, in the case of sexuality, it's rarely black and white. A gay person can feel attraction for a woman, just not as a rule. Likewise, bisexual people can have preferences for one gender or the other, etc. Needless to say it's kind of complicated, and there's no nned to rush in 'figuring it out.'

I'd either just see myself as tentatively bi, since as you said you're attracted to both. In most things, it's better to just go with your first impressions. You say you're attracted to both, therefore it's unlikely you'll be classified as strictly 'gay' or 'straight', at this point. Just enjoy the middle ground you happen to find yourself in.

Or better yet, not even worry about it at all. Feel attracted to people you find attractive, but don't bother trying to come to an 'identity' about it.
