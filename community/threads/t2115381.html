Trip Report - HBWR/LSA surprised the hell out of me (very good trip!)
After reading over erowid and what people have said on Totse I deemed HBWR/MG/LSA a shitty drug. Friends of friends said they have used it and that they just threw up and had a shitty body buzz w/ barely any visuals. I assume those people just read on the internet and claimed they have tried the drug to be cool (you know those people who claim to have tried every drug (..once or twice).

Well, I've decided that anyone who has aten 6-10 HWBR seeds and didn't trip are a) liars, they didn't even take them or b) small minded, and they react to the drug like society tells them too (in this case, erowid/totse saying you throw up)

I tried to blend some seeds up because they are hard as hell to crush, but after that didn't work I said "Fuck it" and chewed 9 seeds. I was going to do the whole H2O extraction but decided to just do it. My girl ate 6 or 7 seeds.

I went to the beach and lied down for a while. I don't know how long, I would guess an hour or two. I got up and noticed that I had a body buzz. Nothing big, but it was chill. My friend accidently ran into me and he had a joint. I haven't smoked weed for about 2 months or so because I'm on probation and I only took about 2 hits total. This was enough to get me REALLY high, not that bullshit stoned feeling you get from indica weed but that nice ass sativa buzz where you are fully alert and ready to go. The thing was, this definitley wasn't 100% sativa, and it wasn't even that dank of nug.

Right before I smoked weed I noticed that all the colours were getting pretty and I noticed shadows (This is key for me when I know I'm about to trip, when I'm sober I NEVER notice shadows but tripping I do..) but now it was getting intense.

I walked home and kicked it for a bit talking w/ my girl. I was thinking pretty intensely like one would do on LSD or 2cE but not about uncomfortable things..all happy thoughts of how amazing life and the world is. It was also really good to connect to my chick (lots of love).

I walked outside to smoke a cigarette and then I knew I was tripping hard. Can't really explain it.. it is definitley not like LSD but shares similar qualities.. I noticed qualities of LSD, 2c-e and a tad bit of mushrooms (pretty colours popping out) all mixed into one but no real bad qualities.

Very hard to explain the hallucnations.. basically I could notice patterns in everything, nature just felt so connected.. like all the plants/bushes were connected to each other trees sky etc. If that makes sence. They were zig zagging moving at me ..not like breathing though. Not at all like LSD breathing, again, hard to explain but it was BEAUTIFUL.

Lots of trails, though.

I walked inside and my walls started glowing and everything was amzing. Not like LSD, again.

Regardless of the hallucenogen I'm on, I have never been able to look at a TV and see any hallucenogens of what the people are doing on TV. I was watching TV and I could actually see the trails of a persons hand moving accross the TV, the colours and everything.

Really interesting.

My girl did throw up, but it could have been because she ate a bit and smoked a cigarette on top of the HBWR, I did the same thing but I didn't throw up or feel the need to at all.

At the very begining, I believe I might have experienced the slightest HINT of nausea, but I have now discarded it as placebo and small-mindedness.

This is a great drug, regardless of what any faggots who haven't done it but say so to look cool tell you.

Oh, the best part, I literally ate a full meal minutes before taking the 9 seeds. MINUTES! I DID NOT THROW UP! And I tripped hard. Normally if I eat food and then take a drug, it is almost cut in half.

LSA all day.
