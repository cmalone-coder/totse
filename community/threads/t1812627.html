Nothing will ever be right with the world as long as we are in the grips of memetic programming and mind viruses. A meme is a unit of information (knowledge) that can be copied by others. It's success and longevity and stronghold it can have on whatever mind "it's" presiding in all depends on whether or not it can survive by replicating. Since the mind is the space in which memes proliferate (much like the cell is the space in which genes proliferate), it can only be considered "fit" if it has the means to spread to other minds. A completely blind mechanism, yet it has us by the throat, so to speak, in that it completely shapes our cultures and our routines and our most prized beliefs.

Everything LostCause mentioned in her post about the wrongs of the world and the inconsistency of why we feel one way and yet act another, all the while being unable to stand up and admit that something's gone horribly wrong with society - is a good starting point to really getting to the meat of it all and realizing that there is a force at hand that is driving us to Think The Way We Do, and it's a force that is blind to the welfare of us, has no agenda towards the goodwill of us, and has the same kind of equilibrium as a selfish gene insofar as it has only ONE motivating factor: Replication. We think the way we do and act the way we do (even though it sometimes seems in conflict with what we really "believe" because we are infected with mind viruses. Not all mind viruses are bad, but unfortunately the most powerful ones penetrate your way of thinking and alters your decision-making. It makes us think one way and act another, all at our expense, all using our minds and voices as vehicles and totally dependent on us (or, more rightly, our minds) to spread, spread, spread. An unsuccessful meme is a meme that doesn't get itself copied. The successful ones that have us pulled around on leashes despite the fact that we are intelligent, self-preserving beings are the ones who survive, and they are the ones who are "running things" right now.

Examples: Capitalism, Fundamantalism, Failed Educational System, Government Practices. All are driven by memeplexes, units of knowledge or information that is copied, repeated, spread and recycled. It's what we call "our way of life". It's why we think the way we do.

The human race long ago evolved and harnessed intellectual capabilities to the extent of which we question the Why's and How's of the universe around us. We need to start paying closer attention to our minds and the mechanics of why we think the way we do. There is more to it than just reasoning by logic. We, or rather - our minds, are so easily influenced by outside sources, they could stand to be guarded more carefully. We already came so far as to have learned the intricate, inner workings of our physical selves, breaking it all the way down to cellular activity, genetic activity, and DNA. We know our existence relies on it. We do this as a stoic act of self-preservation, congratulating ourselves on being intelligent and inquisitive enough to find out How We Work. We do the same for the universe around us. We feel compelled to know why everything is the way it is.

Now that we've figured out how genes work, it's time to start paying closer attention to their counterparts, the memes. It is, after all, a great tool at shaping our minds into believing what we do, and acting the way we act, and supporting beliefs that we hold so important to our survival. Understanding memetics, to me, is imperative to getting down to the meat of my actions and belief systems. It's not "The Man" who keeps us busy producing and consuming and worrying and hating ourselves. It's us being so tethered to our belief systems and unable to trace back exactly HOW we came to those belief systems that keeps us in this same tired cycle of following the rest of society without being bold enough to ask "why". Once we recognize that the memes and mind viruses that lead us into believing what we believe have their own agenda - sometimes hidden agendas of their own - we can strip down to bare bones and ask ourselves "why" we allow this memetic conditioning to lead us around like dogs on a leash.

People in important positions are paying attention to memetic programming. Want to practice more free will? Identify which memes you are infected with and replace them with those can be used to benefit humanity's well-being, thereby preserving your own. Want to make an impact on government, education, and society in general? Spread your own memes. Like LostCause said:

To speak up and claim you humanity, your sexuality, your philosophy, your capabilities.

It's the only way to get on the OTHER end of the leash. If you could choose which viruses or afflictions your body could be infected with over time, and choose only the ones you felt your immune system had the ability to conquer, wouldn't you do so? We have more control over our memes than we do our genes. If we educate ourselves and others on recognizing harmful memes and memeplexes, we are closer to taking full responsibility of our thoughts and actions.
