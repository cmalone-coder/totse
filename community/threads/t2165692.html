Re: Bong Resin
Is it true that smoking weed stems can get u high? cuz Ive saved em up, and I am out of weed till Sat. Do u think its worth smoking tonight? What about seeds?

First piece of advice I can give you, get all the kief off those stems before you proceed any further. I had so many stems in one bag, it just accumulated at the bottom as a very fine green dust. This stuff is way finer of a powder than your average broken up bud. If you want to press your luck even further than bottom-of-a-bag shake, I'd suggest sifting it across the appropriate size screen. Probably easier to find a screen at the store than ice hash bags. If you don't buy/build a machine to do the sifting for you, keep in mind you'll be doing it by hand.
You'll need "zero-zero" grade muslin (or other screen-like fabric?) for this, according to wikipedia.

Alternatively, you could make ice hash after ordering the special bags for the process. Or hell, for all I know you can make it with just a glass of ice water.

I recommend smoking this fine powder in a bowl. I stupidly put mine in a joint, and I had to draw way too hard to get the hits. I probably would have gotten higher if I had put it in a bowl.

After you've got those stems thoroughly de-kiefed, grind them into a powder using razor blade, coffee grinder, whatever. Then do an isopropyl or butane extraction, your pick. See how much you yield after that. I've never done said extractions, so I can't offer too much advice in that area.

And most importantly, don't just put plain ol' stems in a bowl. You might get a slight high, but it won't be worth it for the nasty-ass taste/smoke you have to endure.

P.S. Smoking seeds? Come on, I'm sure you can find other uses for them :) As far as I know, you're really not supposed to smoke those either.
