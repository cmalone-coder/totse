Shoplift Plan, please evaluate
This plan basically allows you to buy two things for the price of one, and doesn't really rely on a blind spot in the store. Please let me know what you think. I'm not actually going to go forth with this plan, but I'm curious if it might work.

-= Requirements =-
- You previously bought an item from the store
- Store allows exchanges
- Store places stickers on items you bring in that you want
to exchange (Besst Buy does it)
- You can hide items well in the store
- You have a razorblade
- Sticker can be removed without damage
- Store carries items with opened boxes (that previous
customers have returned)
- An open box is available for the gadget you want to steal
-= The Plan =-
1) Identify a popular item 'GADGET' you want that:
- Other ppl touch alot in the store
- Other ppl exchange alot
- Is light in weight
- Is fairly expensive
- Is small in size
- Has other similar brands
- Can be grabbed off the shelf (salesperson doesn't have
to fetch) eg. video card
2) Visit store in the AM with a legitimate item "to exchange"
(eg. a 'CAMERA' you bought there)
3) Collect the sticker for the day ('OLDSTICKER') on CAMERA
from the security guard at the front door
4) Find a GADGET in stock where an open box model exists
('OPENBOXGADGET')
5) Purchase a _new_ GADGET:
- Hide OPENBOXGADGET
- Do not exchange CAMERA (you need the sticker for later).
- Be sure to get a big bag when purchasing.
6) Leave store, go home
7) Remove sticker from old item with razorblade at home. Do
not damage sticker!
8) Open box for GADGET and remove GADGET
9) Insert el cheapo duplicate ('FAKEGADGET') in box
10) Return to store:
- With FAKEGADGET in your big Besst Buy bag
- With undamaged sticker
- Around nood
- Preferably when store is busy
- Preferably at a time when there are alot of
returns/exchanges (after holidays)
11) Collect the sticker for the day ('NEWSTICKER') on
FAKEGADGET
- Must be identical to the sticker you brought with you
12) Go to where OPENBOXGADGET item is stocked (your hiding
place)
- Act like you're checking out different models
13) Place OLDSTICKER on OPENBOXGADGET ("i brought this one
in") discretely
14) Put OPENBOXGADGET in your big bag along with identical
FAKEGADGET
15) Walk around a bit
16) Go hide OPENBOXGADGET again
17) Leave store with FAKEGADGET
18) See if security stops you
- You haven't stolen anything yet!!
19) Go home, remove NEWSTICKER from FAKEGADGET
20) Come back a few hours later that day and bring along
FAKEGADGET
- Come back with a lot of money in your pocket if you get
caught
21) Go to find OPENBOXGADGET item with OLDSTICKER (your hiding
place)
22) Put OPENBOXGADGET in big bag along with FAKEGADGET
23) Walk toward exchanges, pause, call someone, turn back
around and return to where you found OPENBOXGADGET ("you
changed your mind")
24) Put FAKEGADGET on shelf (OPENBOXGADGET is still in your
bag)
25) Leave store with OPENBOXGADGET as soon as possible
- Let security guard at exit see OLDSTICKER on
OPENBOXGADGET
- Security guard will assume OPENBOXGADGET is FAKEGADGET
- Security alarm will sound as you exit
- Act coy and a little annoyed, it was a false alarm,
accomidate security guard if necessary

Disguise tactics for your 3rd visit:
- "You feel welcome here"
- Inquire a salesperson about the return/exchange policy on
your 3rd visit there
- Check out similar items
- Write down prices
