Scholastic anti-drug presentations
Epic fail...

I was just forced to watch an anti-drug presentation by this guy who knows jack shit about drugs. Fucking high school...

He started out like this: He took the stage, and told the assembly about his political background, which included but was not limited to, busting pedos. Afterwards he explained that he had no experience dealing with drugs as far as the law went, and had only a vague idea about the effects or differences between different kinds of drugs. Then he just flatly said, "drugs are bad," In which I added under my breath a nice "mm'kay.."

He was the kind of guy that lumps in weed and mostly harmless psychedelics with hardcore drugs like meth and heroin. He told us a story of a dude that started out smokin weed and drinking, and progressed to doing "5 bags of heroin ad day."

The dude in question is now in 3 years of prison for trying to rob a bank to get his fix.

Even worse, was that he told a story of "personal experience" in which some guy sold his sister's $1800 wedding ring for "some dope." He led us to believe that he sold it for marijuana, but realistically it was probably heroin.

At the end, he showed us actually a rather moving presentation about his 14 year old son who died of a drug overdoes. Here's the bad part: the kid died of a mixture of 2 valium pills, weed, and beer. He emphasized the weed part, saying just weed, some beer and valium, can kill you. Later he he emphasized the weed and beer part, leaving out the valium entirely.

...because everyone knows it's perfectly safe to mix alcohol and valium, but GOD FORBID you decide to smoke weed too!

My favorite part was the "dramatization" of the kid that got sent to jail. In the dramatization, he was smokin some weed and drinking at a pretty chill lookin party. Later they show some dudes snortin coke and doin some other drugs. The only thing they were missing was the hookers... and HEROIN.

Later the dude OD's and passes out due to an overdose which we are led to believe is just weed and beer. Epic fail. After that a fucking SWAT TEAM raids the party... LOL.

The whole assembly was flooded with anti drug propaganda, understandable.

Of course, they failed remind me of my favorite drug story. Two years ago, this senior at my school, we called him "Kuba" (I forget why) smoked weed every single day, and was widely known as the biggest pothead of the school. Did he drop out and fuck up his life? Nope, in fact, he graduated as valedictorian. Smoked weed every day and was top of the class.

I wish I could recall the whole outrageous assembly, but that's pretty much the gist of it.

So... anyone else want to rant? Maybe tell some real life drug stories, good or bad.
