As mentioned last time this was bought up, you would be better off storing large quantities and having it for personal use. you won't make money, but you would save more than what you would of made.

Gasoline does lose its integrity after about 6 months of sitting. It breaks down to some degree and can lead to varnish in fuel lines. Fuel stabilizers such as STA-BIL help prevent this occurrence, but you would need about one 10 oz. bottle for 25 gallons. The price to keep gasoline stable would cut into your profit if you intend to store a large amount of gasoline for any extended period of time.
