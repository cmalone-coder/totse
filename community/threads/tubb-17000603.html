GTA:Vice City
I'm surprised nobody has posted a topic yet on this. I see alot of people like gta3, so I decided to post info on the newest installment in the gta series.


  • This new game is set in a city that is much like Miami. So we can all expect it to be much cleaner than the previous game.
  • Re-made engine. Expect much brighter surfaces on cars, as well as an improved fram-rate.
  • 40 Weapons compared to gta3's 15. Some new ones this time are the katana, and chainsaw. Also many of the 40 are makeshift you create at the local AmmuNation.
  • Nine hours of music, compared to gta3's Three hours. Many by real artists:Kool and the Gang - Summer Madness, Flock of Seagulls - I Ran, Judas Priest - You Got Another Thing Coming, Grand Master Flash & The Furious Five - The Message, Hall & Oates - Out of Touch and Cutting Crew - I Just Died in your arms tonight.
  • The main character actually speaks this time! Voice talent done by Ray Liotta, star of the hugely popular crime flick, Goodfellas, will be voicing the part of Tommy Vercetti(Your Char)
  • Entire City will be opened from the start. Unlike gta3 which you had to unlock all three islands.
  • This new city is 2.5times bigger than Liberty City
  • Many indoor areas, including hotels, casinos, live clubs etc.
  • Improved A.I. Pedestrians have new routing path. Cops actually arrest pedestrians, and gangs this time. And a big plus is the gangs this time steal cars, get in shootouts, and high speed chases with the cops, and just mainly wreck havoc.
  • Some of the many new pedestrian activities include roller-skating on the beach board-walk. Sun-bathing, and jogging.
  • New targeting system based on danger level, rather than distance.
  • 120+Vehicles compared to gta3's 50. Those included are motorcycles, helicopters (yes you can fly them this time) jet skis, and houseboats.
  • One last thing is you have the chance to change your clothes many times during the game.


Personally I can't wait for this game to come out October 29th. Rockstar recently reported 4 million pre-orders for this game. So you better reserve.

Re: GTA:Vice City
Over Hyped, YES
Like The First, YES
But, I'm digging on it anyway. For the first two hours I just ran around killing people and getting a feel for the environment. I played a total of five hours last night and I'm hooked all over again.

First, the cops are tougher, they are all over you and once you start a good chase, in come the choppers, once they come after you there's virtually no way to escape.

Second the other drivers in the game will not hesitate to swerve into your lane to fuck your car up, it happened to me several times where I would jack a nice car, cruise along and some dick would swerve into my lane just to hit me. I had to pull one guy out of his car and chop him up with my meat cleaver.

I like the cars, though many are very fast and a little more touchy than the cars in the last game.

There are lots of back alleys in the game where you can kill people to generate cash since you have none.

Rollerblading babes abound as well as very funny male prostitutes who wear only shorts. Which leads me to another thing I was walking down the street and I bumped into an old lady who says "I think I just shit myself"... too funny. There are lots more funny things people said on the street, the Cuban women are some of the funnier ones. "You're nothing but a wannabe".....

In one mission, I had to take out a dude with the chainsaw. Though it was cool, the chainsaw is efficient and it only took a little swipe of it to kill.

Motorcycles are badass too, I've ridden the Moped, a Ninja, and the Harley types they have in the game. These are my favorite new editions so far. Once you find an Uzi, you can spray bullets from the side of your rice rocket it's fun.

Overall, though there's not much new in the game, there's a general improvement in most categories from the last one. Personally, I enjoyed it very much and I'm not complaining about anything in it so far. Of course I'll be playing more tonight so we'll see.....
