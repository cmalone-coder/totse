imma rant about games
Right now i am playing Saints Row, and besides the larger more complicated city, the better graphics, and better cars controls its got a 'if crackdown was GTA and had a story' feel to it. Most the people who defend that its not almost exactly like GTA have only played up to Vice City, and havent played San Andreas.
Now this is not about how bad or how much a rip off Saints Row is. Its actually a fairly decent game. With some Original side missions, but the aiming system and online play are lacking. In fact I think its a good idea that game developers take ideas that work from other people and expand upon it. The original developers will see the changes and make the original game better in the long run.

Part 2.
Now, I like Download able content and all, one of the few things that made PC games so much better on on counsels, Expansion packs. But when you get a game like (Its name just became lost to me but its a PS3 game that you race off road. came out a few months ago. really pretty) that is only half a game with only a hand full of tracks and cars, but they promise more for download, at a price. You just paid 60 bucks for (Motor storm?) and they want you to pay even more money for things that should have been in the game to begin with.
And then you hae half assed downloads like, The Horse Armor pack for oblivion which was a waste of money, in my opinion. Download able content is a great idea, companies just need to watch what they do or they might ruin it.

Part 3: Killzone 2.
Why are people excited for this game? I am taking a very objective look here, no opinion, just looking at the facts, and I see nothing to get 'Halo' excited about. Yea, its pretty, but so are almost all new games. If you look how poor Killzone did, you will see that the majority of games, and game magazines, thought it to be an average game. With that said, why would number 2 be so much better? I hope its a good game, I hope all games are good. But honestly all it has going for it right now is a pretty dress. Its not even on a popular system.

And now your responses. which will most likely be mean, rude, and angry.
