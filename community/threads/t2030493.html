I'm pretty sure the only reason I didn't kill myself is a girl...
I've had a whole bunch of shit go wrong lately. Yesterday it just kind of struck me that life is a bunch of misery with just a few good moments, and that if I was dead I couldn't feel any of it anymore. Nothing would matter, it almost seemed like the safe thing to do - like a game I could just quit playing. I felt like I had just woke up, and wondered if this wasn't a simple truth, and the only reason that people didn't understand was because of some genetic blind spot - some adaption that kept us breeding and living in misery, when simply uncreating ourselves would be the easy solution.

I got my dads gun, and loaded it, and wondered for a moment if I'd really be gone, or if I'd be doing something else in a few minutes. I thought about being trapped forever, or going to hell, or coming back, or getting shot and not dieing -- about going out with a bang, getting revenge or cocaine, about killing and raping with the only consequence being a death I didn't fear.

And then I thought about my girl. About the life we could have together, and I put the gun away. Thats why I'm here. I know that I wouldn't miss her, that her memory would be gone the second my neurons quit firing - but I didn't really want to forget her. It still seems like the dumb choice to me, but I just can bring myself to leave her.
