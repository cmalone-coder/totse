Re: In need of some guidance on a friend/potential relationship
give upHer priorities have clearly changed, and you are no longer one of them.

Forget all of that "absence makes the heart grow fonder" bullshit. All time and distance do is destroy friendships. It doesn't matter how close you were, or how much you loved each other in the past. Spend enough time apart, and it all falls apart.

That sounds like what happened with you.

She came back, realized she didn't feel the same way, and instead of confronting the issue directly, she thinks it's easier/less hurtful to just push you away.

C'est la vie.

Have a drink and move the fuck on.

By that I don't mean "go out of your way to be an asshole to her", I mean stop making an effort. Don't call her. Don't track her down. Don't try and make plans. Just push her to the back of your mind and get on with your life.

If she still has feelings for you, she'll try to get in touch with you. If she starts calling, or she tries to make plans, or whatever, then congratulations. It means she still values your friendship, at least.

And if she doesn't call or write or anything, well, then it means those feelings she had for you are gone, and at least you'll have detached yourself early enough to minimize the pain of this inevitable heartbreaking realization.
