If your having trouble getting it to run to completion, try a higher temp, and smaller wood. Toothpicks would turn into charcoal much faster than a block equal in volume to 100 toothpicks.

I like old coffee cans and Al foil lids.

Or a 55gal drum and a serious bonfire.

You dont even need willow, there are loads of other good charcoals. If you are curious, get some balsa, and make charcoal. That stuff is amazingly fast. Pretty much any willow, alder, plum, whatever makes good BP. Conifers for sparks, and hardwoods for slower stuff.
