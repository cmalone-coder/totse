Banjoa Kazooie: Nuts and Bolts is fucking awesome
Wow, I can't believe no one is talking about this game. I really don't even know where to begin, it's just pure fun.

Sure, it sucks for people who wanted a true Banjo sequel, but honestly I think this is better. When I watched the trailers for this game, I wasn't very impressed. Even after the demo I was unsure. But I got a copy for $10, and I decided it was worth opening up to give it a shot, and after 2 hours, boy am I glad I did.

It's basically like a badass Lego/K'Nex/Erector Set playground, and you can build pretty much anything you can dream up. They give you missions, and then you build the vehicle you think could best tackle that mission. In my last mission, a race against a cheating pussy, I built a dragster with guns on the front. You can build helicopters, tanks, planes, boats, submarines, motorcycles, and anything else you can think up. The satisfaction you get after completing a mission with a machine you dreamt up is unparalleled. Also, the graphics are fucking gorgeous.

This game is not getting the attention it deserves. It's MSRP is $39.99, and at that price it's a no brainer. Blows most $60 games out of the water in terms of innovation and fun.

EDIT: Banjoa is Banjo's Mexican cousin...or something.

I like the sound of constructing all your own vehicles for missions, sounds like a laugh riot.

So far I've had races, battle races, attack missions, collect missions, an escort mission, a find and rescue mission, a taxi mission, a launch mission (build a vehicle to launch a character past a certain point), repair mission (fly a helicopter inside a "Logbox 720" and repair certain areas), and a quite a few more. The missions seem to be pretty varied so far.

The game's also got a cool, self-referential, self-deprecating sense of humor. When you start Banjo and Kazooie are both overweight and sporting triple chins thanks to an 8 year hiatus, and your first "mission" is to collect like a thousand items. Seriously, you start it up and there's all these glittering gold items all over the place. You walk around, quite slowly thanks to being out of shape (if you jump, the ground shakes when you land), and after you collect a couple the main guy, "The Lord of Games", says "Nah, that's so last decade, let's try something new". That's when the real game, and the real fun, begins.

Re: Banjoa Kazooie: Nuts and Bolts is fucking awesome
I'd say this game was better than LBP.

Yeah, from where I'm sitting, creating a level in LBP is a huge amount of effort for what I feel is very little payoff. In Banjo, it takes a very small amount of effort to create something fun as hell to play with. The editor is so simple and user friendly, and you can literally create a car in under 5 minutes. That's not to say that LBP's editor is confusing or hard to use, it's just creating an entire level is a massive undertaking, and unless you've got some kind of talent it might not even end up being very fun after all that effort. In Banjo, you can whip up cool shit really fast and if it doesn't turn out how you expected, you don't feel like you wasted all that much time on it, so you aren't too burnt out to start up on something new right away.

What's sad is that from what I've heard neither game is doing very well at retail. It's kind of hard for quirky games with cute aesthetics to compete against the neverending barrage of "gritty" shooter sequels.
