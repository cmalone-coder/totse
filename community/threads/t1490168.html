Re: All they can say is "no"
good for you for not leaning on drinking as a social tool...it doesn't work.

Truthfully, learning to be more confident is lifes best lesson. I'm sure you are shy and all, but most of that stems from personal insecurity. I know it sounds dumb and cheesy, but sit in front of a mirror. Think of all the good things about you. List your talents. List your good features. List the goods in your personality. You need to come to realize that you are a good person and people are lucky to know you. Forget your flaws. No matter how perfect someone may seem, they aren't. Everyone has flaws, who cares! If you feel that you are worthy of asking out the girls, then you will be able to approach girls with more confidence.

Even if they say "no", as you will come to find girls will say no, who cares. Try to remember the good about you. Not every girl is going to be interested in you, the same as you are not interested in every girl. Some girls are not ready for relationships. Some have been hurt and will say no because they are scared. Some just want a different kind of guy. It isn't your fault, and you shouldn't be smashed over a rejection. I know that is hard, and after the first couple rejections, you may still feel the "I am not good enough" deal. But you have to get your heart broken a couple times in life. Dwelling on that will not help. Growing up is a learning process, and you will learn to deal with rejection without being utterly destroyed. It just takes time.

Dont ever forget the good in you...sorry if all that was dumb, but it works. Good luck!
