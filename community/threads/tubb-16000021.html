quote:
im new at black and white wicca and doing spells,in fact i have never done a spell YET,can any one give me a few pointers and tips?,thanks


The first thing to keep in mind is that, in Wicca, there is no "black" or "white". Power is a neutral force and it is your intent that defines it.

The second thing to keep in mind is that Wicca is a religions that includes magic rather than a strictly magical system. The religious symbols and metaphors associated with Wicca encourage a life of individual responsibility and that applies to how one directs power.

That said, by all means check out Crowley, Spare and the others although I recommend Spare over Crowley for his information regarding sigil magic and gnosis. Read Robert Anton Wilson. Read Dion Fortune and Gerald Gardner. Read folklore and mythology. Read Starhawk and Margot Adler. Read Castanada and Eliade. Hell, read Gimbutas for the Women's Spirituality perspective.

Read it all with a healthy filter in place - choose what works for you and discard the rest. Remember that everyone writing has their own biases, including those long ago writers of myth and saga.

If you're already settled on Wicca as your system, read the others anyway It will give you a good perspective and can only round out your magical technique.
