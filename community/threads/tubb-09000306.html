Re: was roswell a fake?
In my opinion, the Roswell debris was a high-altitude military surveillance balloon that crashed. (This was around the end of WWII and the beginning of the Cold War, remember). Of course the military would then rush in to retrieve it in case any details of the technology got out. (It would have been fairly advanced at the time, I'm guessing). At the first press release, they showed the balloon debris, but were later told they shouldn't have, so they recanted and said 'we don't know what it was' and showed different bits of metal. When the UFO idea appeared, they would have let it take over: if everyone thought it was a crashed flying saucer, no-one would worry about a crashed military test balloon. The whole UFO story is in fact riddled with holes and contradictions; it falls apart under any close kind of anaylsis.
The more I think about it, the more it's just another example of modern myth-making.
