My father had one when I was a kid, I shot the piss out of that gun. The only time it ever jammed was when it just got too dirty, otherwise it was a hoot. They cheaped it up some from the originals, the butt plate is plastic now, the wood looks like walnut...but isn't, and the workmanship on the stock could be better, but as far as the action and shooting ability goes, you won't be sorry, it is a damn fun gun.
