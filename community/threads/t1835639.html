Re: Help making a bong
take a 20 ounce bottle and make a hole in the cap, squeeze a straw of some kind (metal is better than plastic) and attach a foil bowl on top of the straw. fill bottle with water so there is about a 2 inch empty chamber. have the straw in the water as much as possible. at the top of the side of the bottle, cut a hole and put duct tape over it. then poke a small hole in the duct tape and squeeze a hose or a stem in it. everything is fairly air tight and a small chamber with the smoke going through a lot of water makes it a easy hitting bong. go to lowes and get some of the small plastic tubing. it is vary flexible and is the perfect hose.
