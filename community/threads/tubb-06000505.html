Re: Pink Floyd: The Wall
The wall is a complex album with many themes.
The one that you are talking about is the most obvious and the most pronounced throughout the album.
The man in the movie, who is assumed to represent roger waters has become sick with society, its conformism, and basically 'the system' at large.
He builds an intangible wall around himself and his feelings and blocks himself off from the rest of the world searching to make sense of himself, both macro and micro.
"another brick in the wall" refers to all the people and things that make up this delerium around him. The movie isnt the only interpretation the wall is open to, far from it. It is simply one view someone could have of the album and it is certainly not difinitive of the lyrics, meaning it is not all to be taken at face value that the image MEANS the words...
If you're still following ?......
He ignores his wife and the groupie because they are insignificant to him...
This is just one of my interpretations of it no doubt some other member will be able to offer you a lot more insight into this profound album.

Re: Pink Floyd: The Wall
I thought, for sure, that someone would have by now mentioned the person that the Wall was written about [and Wish You Were Here, and, to a lesser extent, Dark Side of the Moon]: Syd Barrett.

I find it hard to believe that none of you know about him. And if you know about him you would have to know that he's the person that the character 'Pink' was modeled after.
In that wedding day scene from the movie [in the 60's], it looks just like Syd did then.

If you don't know anything about Syd, you should check it out: There would be no Pink Floyd today without him.
