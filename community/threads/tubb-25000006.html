Re: what is needed to contain a black hole?
Freeman Dyson got this wierd idea into his head that we could harness all the energy of the sun (or any other star), rather than the tiny percentage that earth normally absorbs. He figured that enough solar collectors could be arranged in a spherical fashion around the star to soak up all of the radiation, thus providing an extraordinary amount of usable energy. Sci-fi writers expanded the idea to create a solid sphere, having living space on the inside between the collectors. A sphere with the same radius as earth's orbit would have a surface area about 600 million times that of earth. No more overcrowding for awhile, huh? The third iteration called for an extremely thin membrane to be used for the sphere, supporting its shape by the solar radiation pressure or "wind".

Basically, the idea is to construct a matrix of solar collectors around a star to collect nearly all of its radiated energy.
