Re: that black gunk in your pipe
my standard procedure when dealing with pipe resin is as follows.

soak pipe in 90% iso alcohol, should disolve a considerable amount of the shit in the pipe, next pour a little bit of alcohol into the pipe, cover holes and shake the hell out of it to knock out whats left, i repeat until im happy with how clean it is... collect all of the alcohol you use to wash it with in a container. filter the solution through something, nothing extreme a tee shirt or a cotton cloth will work well enough, make sure you don't want to ever use it again though... once you filter that black looking liquid should be something around that of the color of bourbon. looks much less disgusting... it helps if whatever you are going to do the evap on has a flat bottom like a pyrex dish, bring some water almost to a boil, pour this water into a larger pyrex dish, place pyrex dish with the alcohol into this hot water, the alcohol will evaporate off leaving the THC... replace the water with hot water periodically, good to have decent ventilation and do the hot water bath away from any flame of extreme heat source... do not evaporate in the microwave...

once all of the alcohol has evaporated off its easiest to smoke if you mix this stuff with keif, or finely ground weed to give it a puddy consistency... or take a bit of it and disolve in a shot...

oh and another note, if you were to do this with some ever clear, it would be fine to drink... clean pipe, filter out the little bits of plant matter and ash, drink... no guarantees on taste but it would work...

Re: that black gunk in your pipe
there's no shame in smoking resin.

tips:

-use a unfolded, straightened paperclip with about a 1/8" piece bent off at a 90* angle at one end, to scrape with.

-run hot water through your piece before scraping it, it makes the resin come up in globs and not just stick to the scraping tool

-resin sticks to your fingers and whatever else it comes into contact with. rub your fingers together to rub the resin off them into little balls.

-mix resin with dried, powdered weed until you can't mix any more in and still have it stick together in a ball. then break it up into little pieces and smoke it on top of a bit of weed as a screen.
