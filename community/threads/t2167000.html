Re: What have phsychadelics helped you appreciate more?
Man, the list goes on....

Nature - nothing is more amazing on psych's than being outside on a warm sunny day. You feel almost animalistic, like that's where you were meant to be. We are all made of the same stuff, and mushrooms really helped me see the cycle that nature goes in.

The universe - have you ever just closed your eyes on a psychedelic and tried to visualize zooming out, away from where you are, high above the trees, over the clouds and tallest mountains, until you can see the whole sphere of the earth, and continuing to go out, and out further, until there are just speckles of light, and then everything keeps getting more and more expansive, until you realize that whole universe is contained inside a small speck of and atom, where you zoom out and see yourself again? Well i have, and it fucking blew my mind.

Society - I dont appreciate society the way i used to - it always seemed so dynamic and meaningful, the way we (humans) have organized ourselves and our lives. Then on mushrooms, i got to thinking 'why?'. With the universe being as it is (see above) why do we do the things we do? It really is mind boggling how redundant EVERYTHING we do is. We are literally a speck of dust, sitting on a speck of dust, orbiting a speck of dust, in a giant spiral of dust that is one of a near-infinite collection of those. It really makes me wonder why the fuck i should care if i got a 79 instead of an 85 in school. Or why people put so much care into the little details that really dont matter.

Mathematics - I always thought math was neat, but you really can't understand the intricate beauty more than when you think about math on psychedelics. It is one of the few things that is true - numbers never lie. Mathematics can be used to describe nearly anything, and no matter how long time goes on, people will be dependent on mathematics because it is the only way people know of to quantify the universe around them. I love math!

Animals - im sure ANYONE who has done shrooms can tell you how animalistic you feel - it's just so natural. I always get the feeling that this is because instead of planning ahead, on mushrooms i just live every moment as it comes to me - no worries, no inhibitions, no common sense sometimes. If you've ever petted/played with a family pet, you kind of know what things are like from their perspective, where the only thing that matters is having fun, being comfortable, and eating when you're hungry.

Emotion - Psychedelics make emotions REAL. It might just be me, but when something is scary, it freaks you out, when something is funny, you'll be laughing hysterically for hours (literally, at least 45 minutes for me before). The best example I can give is when i tripped with my girlfriend. Twice, when i tripped with her, she had bad trips. I stayed with to take care of here while everyone else kept tripping for fun, and I felt like a motherly figure (which was wierd, because I'm a guy) - all i could focus on was staying with her and making sure she was ok. I know exactly how a mother feels with a child, because nothing made me happier than when she was safe and happy. The second time, I had a bad trip too - (scariest fucking thing ever), and after a while, we both started talking to each other, and saying how we were worried for each other. Then everything just brightened up because "I knew she loved me and cared for me" and all that mushy stuff. Its really hard to explain, but after tripping (even still), i feel alot more in tune with my emotions because i can positively identify what i'm feeling.

Sorry if i lost you with that last one - I can't really explain feelings and stuff, but i know alot more about mine now. (and for those who care - love IS real...its fucked - its pretty fucked up how it works, but when you say that to someone (and mean it), you make a bond with that person, and mushrooms really showed me that it is undeniably true)....(of course i cant stand the fucking bitch now)
