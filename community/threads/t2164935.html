Re: Get rid of dark under eye circles
Usually it's hereditary and happens as a result of thin skin under your eyes that lets the underlying blood vessels peek through. There is little you can do short of getting lots of sleep, drinking enough water, and avoiding rubbing your eyes. There is a surgical method of removing them (fat injections under the eye to prevent blood vessels showing through), or you could try a topical under-eye circle product, but usually there's next to nothing you can do about it.
