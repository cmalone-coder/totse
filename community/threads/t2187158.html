Can someone explain this to me? (OC)
Hey BLTC, something weird is happening to me. Maybe you guys can help me understand it.

First, a little background. For just over a year now, I've been using oxy 5-6 days a week, anywhere from 75-250mg per day. Recently up to two weeks ago, my usual dose was 75mg (I take the roxi 15's), but I could go as high as 120mg without trouble. Two weeks ago my supplier ran out, so I didn't dose for a week, then dosed once last Monday (75mg; felt alright), and today (Tuesday) I bought 25 pills. It's interesting but maybe irrelevant to note that I've never had any kind of physical withdrawal from oxy, despite using a fair bit more than many of you who seem to have big problems with w/ds.

Anyway, I've always found that my tolerance drops very quickly after stopping for a week or two, so today I sniffed 30mg. I got a good buzz at first, but then I felt shitty and puked. This was actually going on for a little while before I stopped, too, but it was much more pronounced after the break. Oxy used to be great, but now it just makes me feel like shit. It totally sucks.

So what the hell happened? Why don't I enjoy it anymore? I'm not using more than I used to--I'm using less, and even little doses suck now. It's not like I'm chasing my first high (pardon the cliche) and not being satisfied--It's like my body rejects the drug. Any ideas what could have caused this? Anyone had a similar experience? Is it gonna go away, or are my oxy days over? I suppose either would be welcome.

Thanks
madfast

EDIT: I just puked again, five hours after my small 30mg dose today. Now I feel pretty much normal.

Re: Can someone explain this to me? (OC)
I can't speak from experience because I don't take opiates, and have never tryed OC; however, i'll try to help you out. This is my suggestion:

Take a good vacation from the Oxy. I mean a full break, try to totally get off them for a while. I'm talking a minimum of a month, however two months would be better.

It seems like you've been doing them too much that you're rarely even getting 'high'. You're just getting a minor buzz and it just fills the mental addiction part. You'll stop for a week to try and get tolerance down, and just start cutting into your withdrawal- then bam, you do a little bit and your body goes "wtf noo". A full detox so they are out of your system physically, then wait a while for the mental part to go away. Once you try again after (hopefully) two months, you will get a good strong high that you haven't felt in a while, plus your body can take more of the abuse.

Whats happening to you happens to me often with Speed. A long detox works wonders; really makes the high worth waiting. Just sucks trying to wait it out- stupid addictions :P

Like I said, I can't speak from experience with opiates; however, this is my .02 :)
