*Raises hand and franticly waves from the back of the room*

I happen to be one of those of whom he speaks.

I can assure you that nothing that you've yet experienced even comes close to the problems that I have in my life.

And like rlpinca says: I keep on going on. Granted, though, there have been times - and I'm sure there will be times again - when I wasn't sure I wanted to; When it felt like the universe was a living, sentient entity that had it out for me. Like I would never get ahead [though never totally sink], and would be denied happiness, should I try to do something that would bring it about.

And while the second part is true [so far, in this life], it ain't due to the universe. One thing I realized [one acid trip, I do believe] is that - even should the universe be a living thing - it certainly don't give a f**k about you or me.

And, granted some - probably many - of the pot holes I've hit on the road of life [Note to self: watch it, you're in danger of going way over board. Take the waxing poetic down several notches].

Many of my problems are self induced or self inflicted. But many are not. I have no control over them. Yet it at times still seems uncanny how, just when I see light at the end of the tunnel, there's a cave-in. But never so bad that I can't, eventually, dig myself out. Or if I find myself in a situation that threatens to be something that could make me happy or enjoy my life -WHAM! [and I don't mean the band].

But I go on because I have no choice, really. And, what the hell, if I keep going long enough maybe, well, just maybe.

Besides, the way I see it, no body really
knows what comes after death? If anything? [I, personally, don't think there is any life after death; that it's just something created by human kinds collective psyche as a buffer against dwelling on the thought of eventual nothingness. I could be wrong, though. I'll be the first to admit it. Thus am I an agnostic].

Either way, though, I want to live as long as I can. If there is an after life, it will be for the rest of forever. So you might as well put as many years in this life as you can on the chance that there isn't any. 30, 60, 120 years is nothing next to eternity.

And if there is no life after death? Well, obviously you'd want as much of life as you can, while you can.

Y'know... damn you Meta... What if one of us had something insightful or interesting to say? Now we can't because you had to post a long, meaningful rant that is better than whatever we could. I hate going second. First is even worse.

When contemplating the level of suckyness that you have achieved so far in your life, do not look to others. Do not compare your life to others, because while you may find that you could have it worse, you will also find you could have it better.

To me it does not matter if someone, somewhere has it ten times worse or if they have it 100 times better. What matters to me is that right then, right there, my life is a depressing shit. What I usually do is think about different ways I can improve the situation...

Damn, more later.

Woo, more now.
Luckily no one posted.

I have never found thinking of others suffering as a way to alleve my own. So what if others have it so much worse than me? Maybe that will remind me that I shouldn't be getting so distressed over the situation, but even that lasts only moments. I am empathetic of others who have hard lives and I am not a self centered bastard, but I have just never found looking to others helpful in dealing with my own life. I think about my own life and ways I improve it, if only for a moment more. I ponder all the things I could say or do, but I don't usually do them (because I'm an introvert, shy kind of guy). But this still helps. Because now I know that my life is bad, not because of others but because of myself. And I suck it up and just move on. I don't dwell on things because most things last only days or less. Why harp on a moment? Move on.

That's my main philosophy: Move on. If something happens you deal with it. You don't just stand around complaining about it. Deal with it. Deal with it and move on.

I understand that by looking at other's lives you can compare yours and theirs, realize that theirs is worse and then come to the realization that if they can be tough enough to deal with all of their shit why can't you. But if I could deal with my shit the same way someone else could, then I wouldn't be who I am. Everyone of us lives our lives differently and must deal with things accordingly. Find your own way to push past the depression and anxiety.

I don't know... I always wonder why it seems so important to people to dwell on mistakes. You make a mistake and you learn from it. Life deals you shit and you deal with it. Why kill yourself? Why give up all the things that could be just because of the one or two things that are? And if you can't deal with it, just forget about it. Bury it inside you and deal with it later when you're strong enough. Always stay cool, never let anger or depression overwhelm you.
