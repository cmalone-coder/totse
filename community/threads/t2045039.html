The plot in the book is hard to follow. It jumps around quite a bit. Some advice......DO NOT get attached to the characters in the first part of the book. God seems to lighten up A LOT by the second half. It seems like it was written by more than one person b/c there are tons of plot holes, contradictions and inconsistencies.

It is good for some laughs though. Like the cure for leprosy.....hilarious. God does have a sense of humor on that one. And the whole dont look back thing while I destroy this city.....classic reverse psychology.

All in all it isnt a must read. I would recommend the harry potter trilogy or a good john grisham or stephen king book though. Catcher in the Rye is quite good. Anyway take your pick of fiction.
