Re: Chrono Cross and Valkyrie Profile
no offense Kanibus, but you're reading things a little wrong. The DVD drive has absolutely ZERO to do with low polygon or high polygon rendering, and the compression won't affect that either.

The reason games use low-polygon characters and backgrounds etc, is only slightly to do with how much memory space they take up, and it has a lot more to do with the rendering engine.

What happens when you play a game is this. Say you're playing a game like Tomb raider. Lara's walking down a tunnel, with rocklike walls. You can turn and look at the walls from a number of different angles right? Well rather than having 360 degrees worth of pictures on a disc (this is where the memory part DOES come in) what will be on the disc is maybe 8 or nine different pictures of the wall. What the rendering engine does, is takes what it has calculated the wall position to be, finds the pic it has that's closest to that orientation, and stretches and moves it around to fit. Now, bear in mind, that this all has to be done in real time, as you're walking or running around the screen. The more polygons (or flat surfaces) the rendering engine has to work out how to display, the slower the game plays. So, conversly, the better the rendering engine, the faster it is, the more polygons it can handle before your game slows down. Thats why the best CG scenes are non interactive, because they come prerendered on the disc, you only look at them in one orientation, so they can be prerendered and triggered to play just like a movie. I guess the amount of memory or compression affects this part, because you'll be able to have longer CG scenes taking up less disc space, but thats about it.

I'm not saying the cube is going to be better or worse than the PS2, but whats the point of having the best system around if there are no games to play on it?
