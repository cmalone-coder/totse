Re: Scared As Fuck to Eat Red Meat
The prion which causes BSE and vCJD exists only within the brain and spinal cord matter of the cow, so you`d be pretty much safe to eat anything that is recognisable as part of a cow, i.e. a steak or joint of beef.
Even if there WERE any trace of infected matter present it would only be on the outside of the meat and during cooking would be subjected to temperatures sufficient to destroy it. The risk really came from processed beef products where the possibility of the presence of brain/spinal cord material is more likely, and also because any such matter could be distributed right through the product meaning it might not reach sufficient temperatures during cooking to render it harmless.

We all have to make choices about what we eat, the best you can do is make them informed choices and weigh up what you think the risks are. If I took heed of every food safety scare that comes up I`d be existing on ricecakes by now (although I`m sure someone will probably now inform me of why ricecakes are bad for you!)
