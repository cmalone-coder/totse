I would like to see the lights and maybe go down in flames like Icarus, not because of burned feathers but because of simple helplessness. Sky is not the limit, we are the ones who are limited. For we are human. We are imposing countless rules on others, and most important, on ourselves. We limit ourselves till we cannot move. No one wants to pay the price of freedom, no one wants to sacrifice what was once sacrificed for him. God knows what else we do not want to do, yet given a chance we would all fly...
As strange as it may sound, hope is always the last one to die.
