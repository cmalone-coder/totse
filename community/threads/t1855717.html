Re: Looking for something need help.
Natural opals are an amorphous silica mineral. Basically opal is just a quartz with no consistent internal crystalline structure with water molecules to give it light diffracting qualities. When you look at an opal, the flashes of color you see are light diffracting off a broken crystal plane. The more planes get broken the more "fire" an opal has. In my theory the zirconium serves to break up the natural structure of the quartz crystals on a microscopic level thus yeilding lots of "fire".
