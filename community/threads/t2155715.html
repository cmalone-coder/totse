Crazy night...what could have happened?
Ok so before I start I should introduce everyone. My name's Matt. I came into town f or the weekend from school to hang out with some buddies. I show up at my friend Dave's house around 7. So it's me (I'm 18 btw), Dave (17), another buddy Dan (17) and this older friend of ours Scott (20).

Let me begin by saying that we all smoke pot and drink. I've done shrooms a few times and so have my buddies. Now Dan and another friend who left earlier to go home had done shrooms the same day around 3. Dan ate a bit but I'm not too sure if he was drinking anything (just like water or whatever). Anyway we decided we want to smoke and go see Tropic Thunder. Dave and Dan hadn't seen it yet but of the two ironically Dan wanted to see it while Dave didn't feel like it (the irony is coming up). So 8:30 rolls around and there is a show every hour at 5 til. By the time we decide to go we've missed the 8:55 so we're getting ready for the 9:55. However by the time we get the blunt and bud and get it rolled it's 10 so we're screwed. We phone 411 and find out there's another show a few towns over at 10:30 since we don't wanna wait for the 10:55. We hop on the the highway, spark this almost 2g blunt and get rolling.

Dan's being a pussy and doesn't wanna hit the blunt when it's down to about and eighth of it left so we're like whatever and finish it ourselves and get to the movies. Now we're all in line for tickets and Dan's last. He's babbling about he thinks he's not going to get in because he doesn't have his license because he got a ticket a bit ago (which is fine because that'll still get him in). I was the one that drove everyone btw, Scott walked to Dave's and Dan left his car at Dave's. So as he's about to get his ticket Dan fucking faints. He just fucking goes down on his ass like he's drunk as fuck not knowing what's going on (he wasn't drinking though for sure). We all freak out and he wakes up like 5 seconds later in a daze not knowing what's going on. He says he's fine then does the exact same thing again. Falls over and wakes up saying he's dizzy and can't really stand up. We help him up but by this time the manager is walking over.

Everyone's freaking out and the manager says he's gonna call an ambulance. He says Dan can refuse to go with but he'd feel safe if they just checked him out. We go outside so Dan can sit down and get some fresh air. We're all baked out of our minds freaking out but thank god Scott's an amazing talker even when high and is just perfectly talking to this manager who's meanwhile interrogating us asking if we've been drinking or smoking reefer haha. We all deny and tell him we just picked up Dan literally a half hour ago and we're trying to catch the last show.

Ambulance, fire truck, and 2 squad cars. Now luckily no cops are interrogating us or asking to search my car which is what I thought was gonna happen. Instead the medics are just asking him what he's been doing throughout the day. His story checks out saying he ate around 3 and is probably just dehydrated, they asked him if he'd been working but he said no he had school today. Stupid him though babbles about some muscle growth pills he's using that he got off ebay so they're freaking out. We got him water and he started getting better so most likely it was a combo of shrooming earlier and a fucking awesome blunt. The medic (gay bitch btw) tells him they have to call his parents if he's going with cuz he's 17. We don't think about it til later that the guy told us he could refuse but I don't know if there was any truth to that.

The cop tells us the directions to the hospital too quickly but even if we did know the way we weren't about to go there high as shit having to explain to his parents we all smoked together. So we went back into town knowing we can only wait now so we catch the 10:55 haha. We all go home after knowing Dave's going to have to talk to Dan's parents since his car is at his house. I talked to Dave today and he hasn't been able to reach Dan but his parents called his parents telling him they drew blood and they found thc supposedly (not sure what else since i don't know what they test for or whatever). Now I'm waiting for everything else to happen before I get more info.

Sorry for the long post but it was just a really crazy night. What do you guys think could have happened? I'm sure he's fine now so I'm thinking he was just out of it and the blunt put him over the top. Don't know why he couldn't handle it though he's bigger than all of us. (maybe a bit more foolish than us though).
