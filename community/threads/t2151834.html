Re: Mushrooms this Saturday.
Well there have been like 5000 of these threads before, use the search button.

I will however provide with my advice.

I know you are excited and want to plan out every little bit because "oh I'll be tripping and I need to do this and that and this and that". But my advice would be DO NOT PLAN SHIT. What you need to do is just take the mushrooms, and then let the trip guide you. The best thing you can do would be to go outside in nature and go smoke pot. You will get more fucked up and feel better. Plus nature and all the noises of the world just sound so cool while on mushrooms. Trust me you will not freak out if you go outside. You may however, freak out if you DON'T go outside. Well you won't freak out but you may be uncomfortable.

You're only doing 2 - 2.5 so don't expect much in the way of hallucinations, it takes doses of an 8th and upward to have vivid hallucinations. So spend most time outside with friends who are also tripping and just talk and enjoy their company and smoke pot. Then when its time to go inside don't watch movies, either listen to music and chill, or play some GTA because thats fun as fuck on shrooms.

Anyways don't worry about 2+ grams, you would probably be disappointed with only 1 - 1.5 grams. Just chill and let the trip guide you, and go outside. You have nothing to worry about. Just relax and realize its just a different state of mind. And also what you think mushrooms feel like now, is NOTHING what it feels like. It will be a surprise unless you've tripped before on something else. Well have fun happy tripping.
