Hi all,

I'm an avid Art Bell fan and I still listen to George Noory(the new host) on occasion.

Art left the show permanantly on 12/31/02(that was his last show). However, he still guest hosts for George on occasion.

Art left the show a couple of years back because of an allegation of molestation of his son by his teacher.

He then came back to the show and hosted again for nearly two years at which point his back pain was too great to bear and he retired. George Noory took over and has been doing a good job so far. But he's no Art Bell.

Unless you get lucky and find a bootleg copy of that show, you won't be able to find it as Premeire Radio Networks hold the copyright and Art has personally asked that they not offer them for broadcast nor for sale or distribution. There have been ongoing negotiations over this and maybe someday we will be able to hear these classic shows again. If that happens, you'll probably have to become a member of their Streamlink service to hear them. You can get more info at www.coasttocoastam.com. (http://www.coasttocoastam.com.)

Hope this helps clear up some confusion.
