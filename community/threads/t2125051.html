Re: Just bought DMT
I've got quite a bit of n,n-DMT sitting in front of me, anyone have some advice for a first time smoker? I want to smoke it efficiently and break through, but my lungs are not the strongest and I have heard it is surprisingly harsh.


Pipe is the worst way to smoke it, really.

A lightbulb vape will work, but it will still be pretty harsh and taste like plastic.

If you have a waterpipe (bubbler, bong, whatever. anything but hookah) try putting it on top of a bowl of ash and hitting up.

You don't have to hold the smoke in too long to get the effects, so it really shouldn't be that difficult for even weak lunged smokers. I've seen people who don't smoke anything at all do it successfully.

Anyway, do it somewhere you can lay down, and have some chill music playing in the background. As soon as you take the hit, put the bong down.
