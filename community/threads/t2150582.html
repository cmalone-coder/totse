After this, you can take the remaining mush from the used coffee filter, and put it in a new one and repeat the process if you wish; or you can do it in batches depending on the amount of powder.

Be very careful (Especially if you don't you are opiate naive, meaning do not to opiates on a regular basis or have a very low tolerance), as there have been reported deaths from "poppy tea" and we don't need another one. Start small and work your way up... technically/theoretically speaking... you can always sip a cup, sit back for an hour or two, see how you feel, then drink more till you are where you wanna be... but once you've drank too much... game over... :-\

If they are dry and dead, then so is the "latex".

The way the latex is harvested is when the plant is alive and planted, after the flower petals have fallen off and only the pod remains, you feel the pod to see if you think there is a good amount of give/latex to give up.

If there is, you score it with a razor, scalpel or some other very sharp tool that will not pierce through the actual membrane of the pod, but just the out skin. If you cut too far deep, you will break or puncture the wall within and the latex will seep back into the pod instead of seeping outside of it for harvest.

Now after the pods are scored, 24 hours later, it is checked usually right in the morning as the dew is just dissipating. Making sure there is some there. If so, you let it sit until it is completely or very close to dry and harvest it, allowing to dry into pure opium.

This is done for each pod and each pod will yield very, very little.

Also what most do is use the seeds after the plants die for the year and re-plant them for next years harvest.

If you are planning to get A LOT of use-able opium/heroin, grow some good Tazz's or Hens and Chicks or other good P. Somniferum strain; get the seeds, plant the seeds, research it, know what the fuck you are doing, and you'll need about 100-200 plants to get a good yield each harvest.

Remember... they MUST be alive for any latex harvesting, after this is done, they are dried and sold online as decoration or whatever they sell them for. The reason why so many are available/sold online is because of how many it takes to get the amount of opium needed for others to make a good profit from (Usually), basically why they are usually sold in the hundreds/thousands?

Anyway, read up on it, do your research instead of having other people educate you. I'm feeling particularly lenient tonite so that's why I'm not flaming you.

I have given you two options in my previous two posts, so pick one or both and enjoy; but as said BE SAFE! And remember to check all legal laws as to legality of growing and possessing dried Papaver Somniferum flowers/pods/plants.
