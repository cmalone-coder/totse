Pride
Pride has been my biggest downfall in the dating world and in overall life.

It has held me back and made me unlogically stubborn. It took me many years to figure this out, but at last, I did. And with came a burst of thoughts, ideas, and emotions.

When I get these burst (I do often) I tend to write them out as if so I won't lose them and won't forget them. I cast my pride aside and turned to the girl I love. I gave her what I wrote and made her question her own life. She told me that I meant the world to her.

I casted my pride away finally, and it worked out well.

I was never scared to tell people how I felt, I was just too prideful.

I write this thread here to show my fellows what amazing things can be achieved with a change of thought. I hope y'all take this in and don't make the mistake of grasping onto your pride for too long.

But rather as a mistake, I don't admit mistakes as everything is simply just a learning experience. So actually learn from it.
