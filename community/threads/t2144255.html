Reinventing Myself - Lost 15 pounds in 2 weeks...
Hey guys, it is time for me to crawl out of my shell and show the world what I am choosing to do.

I have always told myself that I will be going on a diet and get my body to a normal physique, and every time I have tried, I fail. I made a website to FORCE myself to not give up and shed the excess pounds. Under the eyes of the world, I can not give up, for if I do, I will be laughed at and humiliated for not achieving my goal. The only choice I have is to stick with my diet and exercise plan and win.

http://www.davidloses.com

I weigh myself every Friday afternoon and post a picture of what the scale says and every 3rd week I post progress pictures of myself. Since my last weigh in, I have lost about 15 pounds in a total of 2 weeks. Not bad considering the fact that I was only doing about 20-30 minutes of exercise a day. Now, on the 3rd week, I am going to the gym and going for about an hour and as each day passes, I feel I can stay much longer than the day before.

I have gotten tons of support from my friends and family and that is what keeps me going and stops me from eating things I should not. Well, I DO take a day off on Sunday but make sure I don't go overboard and go right back to my routine the next day.

I am posting this for myself. The more people know, the more I am able to keep what I am doing.

I do want to ask you guys for a favor that will only take you 2 minutes of your time. Tell 2 of your friends, and tell them to tell 2 of their friends, which will eventually grow exponentially. The world must know that losing weight is simple and anyone can do it without some crazy diet or gimmick sold on TV.

Thanks for taking the time and reading my thread =D

Thoughts, suggestions, comments welcome!
