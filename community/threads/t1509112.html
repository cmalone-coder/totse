The song Hey Jude came on, and our faces drew closer. Then, out of nowhere, she kissed me. She actually kissed me, which was beyond what I expected. We kissed for a very long time. By the time we stopped kissing, everyone was gone, and we were the only ones left in the place. It was the most wonderful night of my life, and I'll never forget it.

I spent many times over at her house, where we'd kiss and have fun for hours upon hours, and then have dinner with her parents. We had so many wonderful and incredible experiences with each other. We both knew we were very in love with each other.

Leaving her was the worst moment I ever had to endure. Our hearts were broken and ripped apart by having to say goodbye to each other. For months after I moved back to America, I was more depressed than ever. I moved back to the same town which I had lived in previously, but it didn't feel like my home anyway. It just felt like a fake town in the middle of nowhere, and I came back to the same school with the same assholes that always disliked me before.

The whole time, I was very sad over the loss of my Lady. People kept encouraging me to leave her, and move on to other girls. Now to make things more precise, I presently live in Southern California. The whole time I've lived in this place, I've come across nothing but uninteresting girls. Here, they are all clones of each other. They all wear the same stupid shit, their bodies all look the same and are skinny as hell, they are all whores, and are all equally stupid. I hate them all, and as much as I tried, I couldn't even begin to associate with them. We kept in close touch.

The year dragged on, and finally she decided to come and visit me here in America. I was very excited indeed. It gave me something to look forward to.

Though talking over MSN and the phone made things different. Over time, we felt as if we weren't talking to the same people anymore, and because we couldn't see each other physically, we weren't sure if we'd get on with each other the same as before or love each other as much.

As soon as she arrived at the airport, we immediately realised that we still loved each other just as much, and over time, we discovered that we loved each other even more than ever. I took her out to eat, we went to the beach, and I took her to various attractions in California, such as Hollywood. We had a very wonderful time, but my parents would only let her stay for one week, and no more than one week. To this day, I still don't understand why they wouldn't let her stay longer. We felt very much in love after this.

After the week ended, I had to say goodbye at the airport, and we cried. Afterwards we both discovered that we didn't feel as bad as the first time that we had to seperate, and we agreed that now we know that we still love each other, we feel we have more to look forward to, so we don't feel as depressed. I keep assuring everyone that both she and I understand the consequences, and are willing to stick it out until the day when we can both live with each other.
