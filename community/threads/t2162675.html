Re: Best way to get resin out of a caked bowl
There are quite a few different methods. What kind of bowl is it? For future reference, metal pipes seem to hold resin far better than glass and tend to be a bit easier to get the resin out of.

Method 1: Take a paper clip and bend it out. Heat up the tip of it and begin. Use something like a card (ID, Library card or W/E) to scrape the resin on to. After you've got enough make a little ball out of it, mix with tobacco if you like and enjoy.

Method 2: Toss the pipe in a ziploc with some iso-alcohol and shake. Once the pipe is clean pour the liquid into some tupperware and let it evaporate overnight. This method is generally used to just get the pipe clean but I have smoked the resin after it was good and dry. Some people use a bit of salt with the alcohol to get the resin to come off easier..I don't recommend that though.

I tend to use the alcohol idea...

Wash pipe out with iso.

Evaporate if off over time but if time is an issue i use a hot water bath, or break out the lava lamp...

Alcohol solution goes onto a plate, plate is sat on top of the metal base of a lava lamp. the heat from the light evaporates the alcohol off without an open flame. the light is turned off periodically to keep it from getting to ~400 degrees... once almost all of it is evaped, a scrape it with a card until the plate is pretty clean, put the resin ball in the middle to evaporate any remaining alcohol off...

I hear light breaks down THC and everything but i have had good results... if that worries you, dont use a clear plate... find some glass that the light wont pass through, or put a square of aluminum between the light and the plate to block the light...

oh and ventilation is good, try to have a fan of some sort going to keep it from building up... no open flame or sparks around where your doing this... bad things happen around solvent vapors and flames...

Re: Best way to get resin out of a caked bowl
Method 2: Toss the pipe in a ziploc with some iso-alcohol and shake. Once the pipe is clean pour the liquid into some tupperware and let it evaporate overnight. This method is generally used to just get the pipe clean but I have smoked the resin after it was good and dry.

Do this. Leave the ziplock bag pinned to a wall for a few days and shake it every few hours. After the alcohol is jet black and your bowl looks more or less clear, snip the lowest corner of the bag and let it drain into a glass pan or dish. Wait a few days for it to dry and then scrape the resin off the glass with a razor blade. If it's still moist, let it dry for a few more days because that's the water in the alcohol that didn't evap yet. Get the highest precent isopropyl alcohol possible.

It basicly moves the resin from the inside walls of your piece to a flat accessible surface.
