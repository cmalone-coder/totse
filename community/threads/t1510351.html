Oh man, same fucking problem here. Knew her for two years, fell in love a year ago, and we're still goin' strong. But... I am so insecure. I'm scared shitless of something happening to the relationship. I worry she'll find someone better, or stop loving me, or I'll be boring, or... It's torture. Like you said, I've never had anything like this before.

I used to be just like you. Paranoia and jealousy ate away at me. She talked to another guy? She didn't respond immediately to me? Anything small like that, and I'd feel hurt and angry. It was horrible.

The good news is that I got better. Bad news is that I'm not sure exactly how. I'll list some things I think might help:

1.) Growing older. I don't know how old you are, but from age 16 to age 17 my emotions stabilized a good deal.

2.) Relationship growing older. The longer I know her, the more I trust her. The more I realize she's not going to just suddenly dump me.

3.) Every time I got mad or hurt or jealous, I'd pause and think to myself. I'd think of how much I love her, and how much she loves me. "I love this girl, and she loves me. Why am I being so irrational and destructive? Is this thought reasonable? Is this worth losing our relationship over?"

4.) Waiting. When you first feel the feelings, don't show them. Just push them away and wait a while. Chances are, they will die down and you will see more clearly. If not, you can then discuss it with her (but calmly).
