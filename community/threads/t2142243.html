Making kids hate reading?
I don't know if this is better left for SG (feel free to move, mods) but, I was thinking the other day.

Now, I'm not saying ALL these books suck - but for some reason, the VAST majority of books on a school required reading list seem like they're designed to make kids fucking hate reading! They're incredibly boring, unimaginative, or too complex for most kids. This makes them frustrated - and they're frustration of course, turns into dislike.....a dislike for reading in general.

This upsets me, for some reason. My ma was a librarian - so of course I was bred for a love of books. It just baffles me that our schools are trying to force such uninteresting garbage down kids throats, then scratch their heads in bewilderment as they try to figure out why all of them hate reading/just read the cliff notes.

WHAT THE FUCK IS GOING ON? Is this a trend other people notice, or am I out of order here?
