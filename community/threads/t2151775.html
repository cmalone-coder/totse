Life
It's yet another fucking detective show, but it stars Damian Lewis (the awesome motherfucker who played Major Rivers in the equally awesome miniseries Band of Brothers). The premise is not too bad, Lewis has been exonerated after serving 12 years for a triple homicide he didn't commit, and aside from different cases, the show follows his quest to find the real killer.

Where the show shines is the humour, and while it isn't too much of a comedy, the show is hilarious at times, and the one-liners help you forget it's another cop show. That and Lewis is a pretty charismatic guy.

I'm enjoying it so far, give it a shot if you're looking for something to watch.
