Do you prefer ecstasy or cocaine?
Appart from booze, the two big "party drugs" for the average joe. In the U.K. at the moment, they're both incredibly popular - coke cos it mixes with booze, has fallen in price, and seems to be everywhere, and E because pure MDMA powder is freely available, even though the pills are crap.

Obviously it's gona depend on whether you've had good stuff before, and personally, i've never had AMAZING coke - very good stuff, yeh. I definitely prefer being gurned up on E when i'm with friends though... the comedown is worth it, and people feel so much more at ease. I do coke occasionally, and almost always people have this adhd-child urge to ramble away for ages, and not be interrupted. Most people realise what they're doing, and seem to chill out after a joint and a beer, but you get some right nauseatingly arrogant wankers who are attracted to coke (especially selling it...) in my experience. It's still fucking expensive in the U.K, think $120 for a gram of really good stuff, which is a fucking rip off, considering it's really just a bit of energy, euphoria and confidence boosted for about half an hour.

I never feel like i've "learnt" much from coke, it's fun to party, and if someone else's paying, it's kinda hard to say no, but there's somethin' about it that's pretty unsatisfying, quite apart from the cost. Ecstasy, when taken irregularly always makes me feel i can learn a bit more, that i've connected, that I can improve friendships... that I can change my life however I want, really....

So BLTC, which do you prefer? I know a hell of a lot of people prefer coke, just because it's easier to do it regularly, for a variety of reasons. Probably "both!" for some of you drug-monsters, right? I'm intrigued...

crimsonsmoke
Regular
  
Re: Do you prefer ecstasy or cocaine?
MDMA every fucking time hands down. I've had amazing coke, but it still doesn't compare to MDMA in its crystalline form.

For me, this is how it works:
Coke:

Pros:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*goes well with alcohol in that it makes you more alert and focused.
*talking shit to your friends is fun.
*aesthetically it's fun to do considering the rituals involved.

Cons:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*it's incredibly over priced and equally impure.
*it inflates your ego, often resulting in huge arguments for absolutely no reason with the people you're closest to.
*it's very addictive, although I find that it appeals more to certain types rather than everyone.
*it doesn't last very long, considering what you pay and in relation to other drugs.
*I've seen it completely change people for the worse with continuous and frequent use.
*my body aches for days after a heavy binge.
*if you get good shit, you WILL NOT sleep.
*putting shit up your nose is WRONG!
*etc, etc, etc, etc...

MDMA: (crystals, not pills that are often mixed with speed or meth)

Pros:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*coming up is orgasmic.
*dancing to music you like is orgasmic.
*getting close to your friends (or even total strangers) is orgasmic.
*sex (if you can do it) is... well... ORGASMIC!
*it works to complement both raves/parties, but also small gatherings; often resulting in completely different experiences.
*if you take enough (upwards of 250mg) you will trip the fuck out.
*it's widely available where I live and costs £30 per gram, making it a cheaper evening than coke. You also need much less to get you off.
*when you peak you often become totally content with your life, even if you've had a shit day/week.
*it lasts an evening (if you have enough).
*it takes away all inhibitions.

Cons:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*come downs can sometimes be irritable; however, if I get pure[ish] MDMA, often I just feel really stoned for a day or too. It's nothing compared to how bad coke can make me feel.
*sleeping isn't really sleeping - it's more of a doze, where you drift in and out of consciousness. This isn't really a con, but it's not a pro.
*you have to plan ahead since you're unlikely to get up before 1pm the next day (if at all).

There you have it. I can totally understand why people do coke, but for what it is it doesn't even come close to what MDMA can make you feel. Of course, there's a time and a place for everything, but the good times I've had on MDMA far outweigh the good times I've had on coke.
