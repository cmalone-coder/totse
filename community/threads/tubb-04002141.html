Live in the real world, treat people well, treat them with respect, trust, honesty and love, and you might just be surprised at what happens..

If you stop playing games now, then that'll help the people around you stop playing games too, and hey, maybe we'll all live in an ideal world where noone has to suffer the stupid bullshit games anymore, and it'll all have started here..

Just think about it..
