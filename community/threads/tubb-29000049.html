well, apart from your vaugness, i'll try to answer as best i can. Kung Fu is a very ancient style. Literally translated it means something like 'greatness in hard work' so it could actually apply to a lot of stuff, but kung fu as a martial style is very widespread and popular. Many other styles are based on it, drunken monkey being one. Other styles are kenpo, northern or southern style, etc. Street and cat fight are just slang and are not really a set style. If you're looking for something to study, I'd recommend straight up boxing or wrestling. That'd help you deal with ppl who fight with no style. Kung fu will work too, but it's set up to be used mostly against other kung fu. It's probably best to learn several styles to maximize your versatility.

You'de do best to start out learning something like Asian Grappling. Since most fisticuffs end up on the ground, it would be wise to have your primary advantage there. From there, an aggressive, offensive style might be appropriate, as aggression tends to unnnerve opponents more than good defense. I would recomened Tae Kwon Do or Tang Soo Do. this is one of my favorite moves.

the person would have to be on their hands and knees for this one to work

with you on their right side, (their head is on the same side as your right arm)

hook your right leg around their right arm from the outside. lock your right ankle in the pit of your lift knee.

from here you can do either one of two things.


1. fall foreward onto the person's back and slide onto the floor while maintaing the arm-lock with your legs. make sure to put PLENTY of pressure with your pelvis on the elbow. be prepared for a little pain around the groinal area, but this is damn painful. it can break the elbow with enough force. and breakage through hyperextension is hella painful.

2. while having your enemy's arm still in the lock, put your hands on the ground on the other side of your enemy. spring off with your left foor and go into a roll. with the other persons right arm being controlled by your legs, he will be forced to turn over.

you two should now be perpendicular on the floor. you on the bottom, on your back, and him on his back across your body. IMMEDIATELY, hold his right arm (the one still by your feet with your left leg (just cross your left leg over his right arm. it should be good enough) and while you are doing this, hold his left arm against your chest with your left and hug it so he can't get it free. put pressure on the elbow with your pelvis (push up).

now for the kicker. this can and most often makes the person pass out and can very well suffocate him. while still in this position, (notice that your right arm and right leg should be free) with your right arm, grab the right shirt collar (this should be the farthest away) and pull it towards you fiercely and with your right leg, bring it up around the outside so that it is not hindering what your right arm is doing, and push your enemy's head into the choke (pulling the shirt collar can be a nasty choke hold if the enemy's throat is pushed into it) by using the bottom of your right thigh and pushing away from you.


follow these steps: if he is the other way around, just reverse the left and right directions

1. hook right leg around his right arm.

2. lock right ankle (or foot) on left kneepit

3 (a) Push down with your pelvis to put pressure on the elbow and see if you can touch the ground with your waist.

-----------OR-----------

4. hug his left arm to your chest with your left arm. keep this very strong so it can't be freed. (apply pressure to the elbow with your pelvis if nessesary)

5. from armlock position (on your back now) switch legs holding his right arm by crossing your left leg over his right elbow.

6. with right arm, grab the shirt collar (called the lapel) on the far side of his neck and pull it towards you.

7. bring your right leg up and using the bottom of your thigh, push his head away from you and consequently into the choke.

8. HOLD IT as long as possible.

To answer this question:

There are only so many ways to hit, kick, punch, and wrestle with another human being. Until we grow a third arm, this isn't likely to change any time soon!

In other words, "styles" of fighting do not exist!!! When a karate guy throws a kick, it often looks like a Tae Kwon Do guy's kick! Because it's just a fucking kick! It doesn't matter what "style" it comes from, or what area of the world it comes from--a kick is just a kick, a punch is just a punch. Don't get all bent out of shape regarding STYLE!

If you really want to learn to fight, put the gloves on and go at it. Allow takedowns as well. Then you'll begin to see what REALLY works and what doesn't! You'll be VERY surprised!

The ONLY way to learn to fight for real, is to FIGHT! Put equipment on (headgear, gloves, groin cup) and go at it. You don't need to immerse yourself in years of martial arts. That's just bullshit someone sells you to make a buck!

Good training to all!
