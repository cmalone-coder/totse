A word, in this case 'drug' is as ound or symbol that represents a concept, which is known as its definition.

This concept must be agreed upon by at least a majority for it to have any use.

If nobody agreed on a definition of a word it would become useless.

So, we have this definition that the majority agrees upon, but you can't expect everybody to remember every definition so we invest an organization with the responability of keeping, as it were, the definition.

These insttutions keep the definitions in a large tome, which we call a dictionary.

So these dictionaries are the holders of the sacred definitions which bestows meaning onto each word and thus by agreement we can have a conversation in which all parties involved have a statistically good chance of understanding one another.

So, should there ever be a dispute over the exact definition of a word, we bust out with the Dic-tionary ;-).
