Re: breaking up before college...
Stay together if you really want to. Or break up if you really want to. Simple.

This is an important time of life transition for you. What's the point of staying together? Plan on getting married? If you two were really that serious then you should have planned to go to the same college together. Or if he's not planning on college, then he should move with you anyways. Or if he has a good job, then you could have settled for a college closer to home and get married right away.

Sound ridiculous? In a way, it is. Who, at (I'm assuming) ages 18-20 is ready for that? There's a whole world to explore out there. Or, at the very least, college life.

So I ask again: What's the point of staying together? It could be co-dependence, insecurity, or fear of being alone. None of which are really good reasons to stay together.
