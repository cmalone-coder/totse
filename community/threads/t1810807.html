One big idea that one of my favorite philosophers, Aleister Crowley, was fond of was that if you believed something that there should be a reason. Now, I fancy astrology (note that I said that I didn't believe, it simply fits my aesthetic). I haven't really done any tests on it, though, so here's my idea.

I don't really know any of you, so what I'd like to do is for 10 of you, the first 10 to reply to this writeup to send to my e-mail address (theyhowl@hotmail.com) the date and time of day of your birth. Please post that you are sending your birthdate and time here so that everyone can know when 10 have been sent.

(dis)qualifications:

Between 18 and 40 years old

Born in the USA

Without any knowledge of your natal chart beyond your sun sign (i.e. Gemini starts May 21 and ends June 21)

The way I will run this test is that I will send each participant 2 natal charts both with the same sun sign. They will reply to me with which one they feel fits them better. If I can, I'd like to run the test more than once on more than one test group.
