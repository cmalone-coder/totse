Re: My bleach amv :)
he he, I can recommend you Bleach, its cool. You've got some catching up to do though, ep 196 is since yesterday. And AMV stands for Anime Music Video. Basically you pick a theme (a battle, certain character or event) and then you pick a good song that fits with it. Then you cut up footage from your theme and make it a choreographed whole.

Tips:

a. Use raws, not subbed.
b. When the character in question is ment to sing the song make sure its lip synchronized well.
c. Make sure the footage fits the melody.
