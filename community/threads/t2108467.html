I'm curious to know how many peculiarities people may have while they smoke cigarettes.

I myself, NEVER inhale the first puff that has the lighter fumes in it. I don't know why, but from the time I started smoking, the dude who got me into it said not to do that cause it was unhealthy(er). That may or may not be the case, but its become second nature now. I'm actually a little OCD about it.

I normally smoke whatever I can get my hands on because I'm still underage, but when I do have a choice, I get Benson & Hedges. There aren't any Marlboro's here in Canadia.

I also never smoke past about 1/2 inch till the filter. Same reason as not inhaling the first puff. Unless I'm really niccin out.

My favorite drug to smoke cigarettes on is most likely blatantly obvious to those who know me in BLTC, but its DXM. DXM reduces your tolerance to every single psychoactive out there, so when I smoke cigarettes while dexing, I get that same headrush that you get when you smoke for the first time in your life.

I usually smoke cigarettes slowly unless I am in a rush; I like to take my time and enjoy it. I also prefer smoking indoors because its much easier to blow smoke rings than outside. Sometimes I'll smoke it like a joint without realizing it, but I generally hold it between my index and middle finger, like 90% of smokers out there. Makes me think of another question, do you have the tobacco yellow on your two fingers? I don't. I've only been smoking for 4 years though.

And my record for flicking it is about 5 meters. It was a little windy though...
