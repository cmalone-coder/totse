Spice- Legal High.
So yesterday me and a friend were in brighton so we popped into the local headshop - Smokers Heaven.

I saw these pre-rolled Spice Joints
[link http://www.everyonedoesit.co.uk/online_headshop/Spice_Prerolled_Joints.cfm?iProductID=3730 ]

So we bought 2, for £3.00 each.

I didnt expect much; being legal (and freely sold to me, a 16 year old).

We went to the beach and smoked one each, about halfway though each one, we were talking and so they went out.

About 10 minutes later, we decided to re-light them as we werent feeling much.

First off, they suddenly felt very hot, unbearably so.

I then lit a ciggarette, just to check, and yes the ciggarette was just as unnaturaly hot. It must have been the effect causing that. It felt like we were breathing in the air when you open an oven, very strange.

After finishing, we peaked about 15 minutes later, went into giggling fits and spouts of dizziness, all in all it was very strong, and very good.

I would describe it as a very good alternative to weed, puts me in almost the exact same mind-set, except when i',m baked i think of all these cool things to do, but i'm too lazy to get up and actually do them, with these you have the energy. You can keep up a decent conversation, but when trying to explain something you do tend to trail off, similar to weed.

It lasted a lot longer though, after about 4-5 hours we were still a bit giggly, and when i woke up this morning i was quite groggy, and through-out the day i have still been mixing up words.

That, in someway i hope, will explain the poor syntax of this post.

But yes, who else has had similar experiences?

And can you recommend a similar, yet more mild substance for casual smoking, as long as its cheap it doesnt matter if its weak.

:)
