Even though meta touts dilution as the single most effective way to pass a drug test there is little concrete info out there how to pass the adulteration/dilution test so I'd like to help you guys with this.

There are 5 things you need to worry about
Creatinine, Color, Temperature, pH and Density/specific gravity.

Creatinine:
Creatinine is the natural metabolite of creatine. The problem is that only a tiny amount (iirc ~1%) of creatine is naturally converted into creatinine which means that there is no point in trying to take creatine before the test. To beat this you can do one of two things. You can start taking creatine regularly A LONG TIME before the test. Taking creatine the day before the test will not do anything but if you take creatine regularly over a long period of time you will ultimately raise your creatinine levels. This is more of a prevention strategy rather than an action one. If you are short on time then you can try to order pure creatinine from a chemical supplier and add it directly to the sample. The cutoff rate is 2mg/dL so for 8 fl oz you would need about 4-5 mg. If you don't have a mg scale you can dissolve 1g in 0.2 L, homogenize and then draw 1 mL (1 cc) using a syringe or an eyedropper. You now have a 5mg solution. Eating red meat or such will not significantly raise your creatine or creatinine levels compared to taking performance creatine.

Oh and the cutoff might be 5mg/dL in some places so change the math accordingly.

Color
If you dilute your sample it will look clear so you need to make sure it looks normal. Yellow 5 and other food colorings should be able to do the job. Usually colorings are added in miniscule amounts for desired effect so there is no reason why it would throw off a pH test or specific gravity. The B complex gives it a neon-green-yellow color which is suspicious.

Temperature
If you dilute by drinking then don't worry, if you directly dilute the sample then keep the water or fake/clean urine in a ziplock bag taped to your stomach. Make sure it is warm enough BEFORE you go to your testing site as it might take some time to heat up to near-body temperatures.

pH
This exists because dipshits added draino and bleach to their samples. Don't do stupid shit and you are fine.

Specific gravity
Your sample needs to have a specific gravity of 1.003 to 1.030 or 1.003 to 1.030 grams per mL. This is determined by solutes in the urine such as salts and protein so consider adding salt to a diluted sample. As far as I know they don't test for "saltiness" so it doesn't matter how much you go overboard, just don't exceed 1.030 g/mL
