To become root, type...

su -

Then type..

gedit /etc/X11/xorg.conf

It'll open in gedit, and you should see a "monitor" and "screen" section. Add it as a value within there. Don't worry, when you see what it looks like within side the file, you'll understand. There is just basic settings with values. Change the values and save/exit the file.
