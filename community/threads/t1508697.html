Re: i have no life. lonely. help?
Having a social life is overrated. Yes, you need some interaction occasionally or you will begin to feel a unique mental anguish. This is not loneliness; it is made worse when you are surrounded by people who are near yet do not talk to you.

The first step is to stop caring. Stop judging yourself. It will only make you desperate, and desperate attempts to force friendships with people you don't have much in common with end badly. Desperation also tends to make you clam up in fear of saying something stupid. The best way to make friends is to figure out how to speak fluidly and appropriately, and do that. This is done with practice.

I practiced on totse. This helped, considering that throughout highschool I got next to no practice. The internet is good practice; post, chat on irc, im people. Now, although people make jokes about me always staying in my dorm room, I have conversations with people on a daily basis. A bunch of people. I say stuff in group conversations when I have an idea of what to say. Sometimes it doesn't work, but most of the time it does; otherwise I just shrug it off and go back to whatever I was doing.

Essentially, you need to live in the general vicinity of others, and have a need to do things with them to have friends in rl, although in my experience it's just as enjoyable to just talk to people over the internet.

The most important thing is to be confident. If you are not genuinely confident, you will not be as effective as you would otherwise be. The path to confidence is apathy and being stubborn; learn from your mistakes, but resist the pain that comes with them, because you will mentally flinch every time after if you care.
