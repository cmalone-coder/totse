kinda long, but worth a read...

so, been dating my gf for about 1.5ish years, i'm 20, she's 19. been having sex since month 6, neither of us were virgins.

well, me being naive to what sex is like without a condom, i talked my girlfriend into trying it for a little bit, just to try it (only for a minute or two at a time). it blew my mind.

later on, we eventually started using condoms less and less (however i would not let myself get anywhere near ejaculation as to be safe). this was pretty good, but it was always disappointing having to put a condom on before finishing. it was also quite risky despite the amount of precautions we took (no recent ejaculations, shortly before/after her period, withdrawing way way way before anywhere even close, etc.).

so i've become spoiled to the point that i'd really rather just not have sex than to have to wear a condom. i've also decided we can't really continue running this gamble every time we have sex without one, only to end with a disappointment on my side, as well as on hers occasionally (sometimes she wouldn't be able to get off because i'd have to start/stop so often to control ejaculation, or sometimes when i put a condom on it would take forever to get off).

unfortunately, my gf is not too keen on just about any of the other birth control methods. she doesn't like the drugs, she's afraid of side effects plus permanent infertility. she won't get an IUD because she thinks having the minor operation to have on inserted is a bit too overboard. 'rhythm' methods are unreliable at best and take too much work, having to track temperatures, days, mucus secretions, etc. she won't get a diaphragm because they require a fitting, aren't entirely reliable, and can be a pain to put in/take out.

needless to say, its kinda disappointing on both sides being in a young, strong, and relatively 'fresh' relationship without sex. its like being in a sexless marriage.

although i agree with my gf, if she doesn't want to do any of the forms of birth control available to her, she shouldn't have to. but that works both ways, in that i shouldn't have to wear a condom if i don't want to either.

if there were birth control methods for men that weren't as permanent as a vasectomy, i'd glady have them done. the RISUG would work well, if it were available but unfortunately its still in development.

so totse... what 'ta do?

other things i've considered:

*oral sex. it would work, if she were good at it. unfortunately she isn't, and i don't think she'll ever be.

*anal sex. she said no. thats the end of that.

*the 'heat' method of temporary sterilization of men. might work, but there doesn't appear to be enough research on it to make it reliable.

*mutual masterbation. just seems stupid to me.
