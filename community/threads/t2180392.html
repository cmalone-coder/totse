Re: roasted duck
I've been shooting at ducks for years, and I've learned that nobody really knows how to prepare it well, but that well prepared duck is fucking delicious. It is a very gamey bird, and everybody has their own way of preparing it. I met an old man whose wife HATED duck season, because he swore the only way to prepare good duck was to hang them, upside down, somewhere cool and dry (his basement) for at least a week. And I've met people who swear all you have to do is marinade it for a bit, fry it up, and bam delicious.

I've tasted both, and everything in between, and I've come to the conclusion that is really is just random. Try duck somewhere, and try it again and again at more and more places, and you'll see. Sometimes its fantastic, best meal you've had. Sometimes you'd prefer squirrel (which I find is rather similar to duck). Sometimes its worth eating, sometimes not. Always worth shooting at though. Because even if you miss, you're still drinking beer with someone worth drinking beer with. And in a canoe, even better. Ducks not withstanding, you're drinking with friends while canoing. Bam.
