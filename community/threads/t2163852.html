Hitting the Boof in Be'er Shevah Israel.
I'm From California. The northern part, where there is no shortage of Strong Kind bud. Recently I've come to Israel to try and find work and as of late I've been in Be'er Shevah. You might have heard of it, before its one of the larger cities, it pretty old and it dates back to the times when fools like Abraham where digging wells and trenches, and its where Jacob supposedly had his dream about Angels climbing a ladder down and up into and from heaven. We're in the desert, lots of longs low hills and craggy dry brush.

When I first got here, I was staying in Tel Aviv which in some ways is a very international City, and when I asked around about the Marijuana situation I was told that there is almost no weed here in Israel. After poking around a bit more I found out that ther eis actually weed in Israel and it is divided into two categories. The first, being largely bush weed that comes out of the Sinai Peninsula and is smuggled across the border whether through Gaza or not. Buds (Nitszanim) of this variety are rare as few people even bother to smuggle the bulky contraband through a heavily patrolled set of borders like the one at Gaza, Israel and Egypt. The other type of Weed that is found here is reffered to as Hydro although I'm fairly sure that most Israelis have no idea what hydro even means or what it is a shortened word for. While this genre of Man's favorite flower does exhist here throughout the State not limited to Tel Aviv. It is commonly thought of as being too expensive to be worth (80 NIS per gram, around 20 dollars US.) it when compared to the Israelis preferred method of getting stoned... Hash

Its strange to be explain I suppose, coming from a place (Sacramento) where Hash is somewhat of a delicacy (out side of the clubs) but here Hash is by far cheaper than even shitty weed. Typically sold in what translates to "a Finger of " Hash, bars or blck of ten two thriteen grams are sold for around two hundered shekels. which goes a quite a way even for a an experienced stoner; who has no problem helping a legit eighth dissapear in a matter of hours.

I bought mine from a man named Amit in a nice and friendly apartment in the student area of Be'er Shebah, he offered me anything to drink from his bar which was extensivly stocked with flashy and expensive Liquors from around the world as it seems common for most young Israelis to have. And proceed to Refuse my offer to smoke a boof (a piece of hash one breaks off of their stash) saying that when Iwas there it was his hash that we would smoke.

When Israelis smoke hash they rarely if ever smoke it straight, they usually make what is called a Ksessah. Which in aout a dime (coin) sized piece of hash mixed in with the tobacco from two to three cigarettes that have been toasted. Many differerent groups that I've smoked with here all have a variet of custom and traditions to go along with their Ksassah making, but some commonalities include always using a rolled up piece of cardboard as a filter for what are typically joints. Always passing to the right, (sometimes) beaking the tabbaco into an even finer powder than in the ciggarettes, and never letting anyone pass the join quickly, as if one must spend their time with it smoking slwly and concisely not a puff puff pass but a relaxed moment or three spent kicking it with the j.

The hash it self is said to come mainly from the Beduins (around here) and in other places it is said to have come from Hizzbolah and Lebanon (more likely) and everone tells urban legends about what terrible chemical the Arabs are puitting on their Hashish. Which is what I know as Soap Hash. And now I right and my Finger of Hash has dwindled to a mere boof and I contemplate frying to find a way to find a direction (the tanslated word for dealer) and decide what to do as the Sun sets here in the Desert ten hours in the future.
