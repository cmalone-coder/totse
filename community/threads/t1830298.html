Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Can someone try to guess how powerful my salutes are? (Sounds kewlish, lemme explain)
For a while I've been making salutes by filling up a plastic cigar tube with whistling pete powder, sticking a fuse in so it lights it in the middle, and wrapping the shit out if it with duct tape. So far I've never put it in a confined space - that is to say, I've never blown anything up with them. I've always lit them out in the open, laying on concrete or wood, so I don't even get to see the crator (if there would be a crator). In fact, I've never even stuck around to look at the "blast site" (I do it in a safe area, but sometimes cops show up, so I have to leave right away). These things are loud enough to make my ears ring from at least 50' away, but I don't have much experience with explosives so I'm not sure how powerful something like this is.

So, my question: From what I have described of these little salutes, how much damage would you estimate one of these things doing? For instance, if I held one in my hand, would it just bruise/shread my skin, blow off a finger, blow off my hand, etc...

I'm mainly wondering how much trouble I'll be in if I somehow fuck up one day. Oh, and is potassium perchlorate:aluminum powder 70:30 more powerful than this powder? If so, how much more, on my scale of body-part removal?
