Ego
I just got shot down. Maybe I shouldn't say shot down, because she didn't say no to anything.

Basically I made a subtle move, and her reaction, although not overtly negative, did show me there was nothing further to be accomplished. A shame women in my country are so open to the point of carelessness about the signs they give off.

But to the point. In seventeen years I have been shaped to resist such things. It all started when I was ten, and to this day, little has changed.

To put it simply, in every year I would start to like a girl, and I would then realise I would have no chances(either because she didn't feel attracted, or lack of timing on my part), thus nothing would happen. Such events were always followed by some chick ending up crying because I was excessively picky. Looking back now, they would have ended up in tears after breaking up, because I wouldn't turn them down, had my current self taken charge of my past decisions.

The consequences of this were no more than two - I had developed a nice, big ego. The sort that's usually present in very attractive people - aware of his/her potential but nevertheless, generally insecure. Besides that, I recently started sort of building a wall around me - to protect myself from shot downs and the occasional social embarassments. It worked. I find myself with many friends, and knowing a lot more people that I could have hoped for.

But walls of concrete still fall, do they? Indeed. So I just felt like all of that work towards becoming a better person fell to the ground, crushing me in it's path. It is not dissapointment, nor sadness, nor depression. The feeling was sheer agony and despair. With a bit of helplessness thrown in the mix. It didn't feel like "The End", but rather something beyond it.

I'd just like a damn bottle of Martini right now. While I am feeling better as I type, the shock hasn't stopped bothering me completely.

Any comments? I'd like to hear them.
