Re: Recommend me writers (/books)
Irvine Welsh is pretty cool for the "o shit i cant believe i just read this" addictive, filthy and mindbogglingly brilliant style. Go for Trainspotting; seriously, once you get past the Scottish dialect (which might be hard at first, seeing as most native English speakers have problems) then it really just flows. I found reading it out loud like a play helped... lots of his other books are equally sick and gripping, too.

Haruki Murakami is pretty awesome. He's written loads of stuff, and those who've read most of 'em say they're all pretty consistent. I've really enjoyed "Kafka on the Shore" though, and "Norwegian Wood" is well regarded. He may well have been translated into Dutch, too.

Kurt Vonnegut. Judging by who you have on your list, I reckon you'd really like his shizzle. Slaughterhouse Five and Breakfast of Champions would be well worth grabbing, if you see 'em.

Bill Bryson is good for fairly light, amusing, travel-based stories. Great for bedtime reading, in my opinion.

If you see any of the short story books by Roald Dahl, then grab! He's the master of the short story. Another good 'un for bed time, or when you just want a book that's easy to pick up n put down.

Khaled Hoseini has hit it big with like...... everyone, at the moment in the U.K, I bet you could find a copy of The Kite Runner or A Thousand Splendid Suns, neither of which i've read, but both are meant to be superb.

Douglas Coupland is pretty cool for "edgy" stuff. Generation X is pretty fuckin' readable, it oozes this kinda.... abrupt, classy, slightly pretentious nineties post-teenage-angst. Very interesting read, although not much happens, it's just just weird how Coupland develops the characters.

Definitely just check out the big recommendations list, much better suggestions than I could give there...
