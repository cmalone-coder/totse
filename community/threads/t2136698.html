IMO, Buprenorphine (Suboxone/Subutex) is far superior than Methadone. It is much stronger mg for mg and also, Buprenorphine is a partial agonist/antagonist, so the addicting "euphoric effects" the opiate(s) produce are not as intense as a full agonist like Methadone.

On top of that, withdrawing from Suboxone is like a cake-walk compared to the effects from Methadone.

And, I find that Suboxone's affinity for the brain's receptors is much higher, therefore it is much harder to try and get high/break through the Suboxone [when on maintenance] than it is for Methadone, no matter what the dose is.

Methadone is also known to cause lethargy and take away motivation/add depression over time, where Suboxone seems to have an almost stimulating effect and has actually been used for off-label prescribing as an anti-depressant.

I'm not going to get much more into it than that unless I am given a reason too, but I think most everyone will get the point. And as it is with everything else, effects and opinions WILL vary from person to person, but the majority as of late definitely seems to be leaning toward the favor of Buprenorphine.
