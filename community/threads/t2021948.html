I have a lot of free time during the day, and I'll admit that maintaining physical fitness is not very high on my priority list. Therefore, my routine is spread out across the day, and very elementary. After waking up in the early morning, I usually stretch for five or ten minutes, do 20 push ups and sit ups, and go for a five mile circuit around my immediate neighborhood (depending on my mood, I'll either run, walk, or bike this.) In the afternoon, I usually take a half-hour power nap (because my sleep schedule is fucked beyond imagination.) Before the nap, I'll spend about a half hour skipping rope, and immediately afterward, I'll stretch and do twenty more push ups and sit ups. Before bed, I'll go for another five-mile circuit, and do twenty more push ups and sit ups for good measure before sleeping.
