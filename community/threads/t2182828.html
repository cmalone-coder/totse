On a National Level:

First, I'd dismantle parliament, and the senate. All MPs would be arrested or reassigned to areas where they could be used, and party leaders executed.

What constitutes a "contribution", however, would not be the same as it is now. The machinist, the scientist, and the artist all serve to enrich our lives and we must support each in turn.

An end to the prohibition of drugs with few negative health effects and little potential for addiction - this includes marijuana, LSD, psilocybin/psilocin, and the like. Heroin, meth, ketamine, and numerous other street drugs would remain illegal to produce, but possession would only result in confiscation and enrollment in an addicition program. Alcohol would be increasingly regulated and have special taxes associated with it - while under my drug philosophy it should be illegal, I doubt Canadians could deal without their booze. Tobacco would face a similar fate.

Freedom of religion would be preserved. However, the freedom to impose that religion would be completely eliminated. Belief would be as it should be; private. Groups who spew hatred ("God hates fags!"), bigotry ("God hates niggers!"), and ignorance (creationists) would be arrested under anti-defamation laws, and sent to prisons where they would be forced to work and educate themselves about the stupidity of their beliefs.

Removal of "special rights" for natives. Open the reserves up, and treat any resistance as hostile. No more claims that they "own the wind". Bullshit. We ripped their great-great-great-great-great Grandfathers off. Move the fuck on.

Education would undergo a massive reform - with children being taught that their duty extends to everyone in society, not just themselves. They would be encouraged to seek professions that they would both excel at and enjoy. By age sixteen they would be given a test similar to the G.O.A.T. in Fallout 3, or numerous career aptitude tests the world over. From there, their education would become specific to a "field" and would then continue onto more specific training as the situation warrants (clearly a scientist requires more time in school than a coffee shop attendant). This would hopefully produce a young workforce who are dedicated not only to their jobs but those around them. In the end, it won't really matter what you do for a living - the state provides all the essentials and then some.

The police and military would be merged into a more cohesive, better armed, better trained unit. Non-lethal solutions to civilian situations would be those primarily used. Deadly force should only be met with deadly force when all other options have been considered. Possibly streamline research towards creating a "phaser"-style weapon.

Go Green! Move away from petroleum fuel, and turn the prairies into a giant energy-generating station. Solar panels on every building.

Initially, dissent would be forbidden. However, as the years moved forward, those raised in my society would be permitted to take a test to become "citizens" - in essence, to get some of their democratic powers back and be allowed to vote on certain issues. The eventual goal would be a complete return to democracy, but a socialist democracy with a different philosophy than our current one.

Internationally:

Rapid troop recall from Afghanistan, and a note to the U.N./N.A.T.O. that we will never be their bitches again. If we go anywhere, and it will be a long time before we do, we want the support of other countries going in with us. None of this Rwanda bullshit again. Ever.

Begin work on a nuclear program with the intent of making Canada a nuclear superpower. No more relying on the U.S.A.

Militarization of the arctic for national defense.

Limited trade of primary resources would continue with the U.S., until other markets could be found. The border would be defended and no civilian traffic permitted.

Negotiate free trade with Cuba.
