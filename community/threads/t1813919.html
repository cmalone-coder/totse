I tend to agree with OP. As Heinlein wrote "I am God, thou art God, all that groks is God." Jesus said much the same thing when he said "All that I have done, ye shall do also--all that and more." In other words, we are All One.

God has no conscience, having no need for one. In a similar manner, God does not fogrgive us, for we can do nothing he/she/it/them/whatever has a reason to forgive. God accepts everything we do, and does not love us any less, regardless.

For convenience' sake, I do tend to anthropomorphize (sp?) god, which may be misleading.
