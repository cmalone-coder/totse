never use soaps, always prewet filters, always have good ventialtion

chemicals

7% iodine tincture, no povidone (horse supply stores)

E

1. crush pills and run through a fine sieve.

2. spray the TCE into a jar to collect liquid.

3. mix 10 parts TCE with 1 part JD in a jar.

4. mix 10 parts TCE/JD with 1 part pill powder.

5. stir well and let sit for a total of 3 minutes, including filter time.

6. pour through funnel with 1 filter. spray sides of filter with TCE can using tube.

7. pick up filter and wring out. dry filter and chafe material onto plate. can save liquid to reuse 2 more times.

8. chop up the dry chunks and pass through the sieve.

9. pour 250mL alcohol onto pill powder in a jar, stir, let sit 1 hour.

10. pour off the top liquid layer and evaporate it.

11. repeat steps 9 and 10 twice. chuck leftover pill material.

12. pour 100mL acetone over crystals from evaporated liquid, and stir until the crystals crash out.

13. let settle, and pour off the liquid. spread the crystals onto a plate and dry.

I

1. put 1 pint tincture in a jar.

2. stir in 2 cups of water.

3. stir in 25mL-30mL acid.

4. let sit 15 minutes.

5. stir in 1 pint H2O2.

6. let sit 12 hours.

7. filter and rinse filters with water, then dry it.

RP

1. place strikers in a jar.

2. add enough boiling water to cover strikers by a few inches.

3. swirl for 30 seconds and pour off the water and chuck.

4. pour in enough acetone to cover the strips by an inch, screw on cap.

5. shake like hell, releasing pressure as needed.

6. when there is no more red on the strikers, pour through a strainer to remove strikers. chuck strikers.

7. allow liquid to settle as much as it will, then filter slowly, chuck liquid.

8. empty filters into a jar, add enough acetone to cover to RP.

9. stir and let sit until it settles, then filter slowly. chuck liquid.

10. repeat steps 8 and 9 again.

11. empty filters into a pot, add enough acid to cover it.

12. boil lightly while stirring outside for 5 minutes, then filter. chuck liquid.

13. repeat steps 11 and 12 four more times, but use water instead of acid.

14. rinse filters with water, and dry it.

???

reaction

1. mix 8g E with 12g I with 10g RP and 1 coke can top in a glass bottle (300mL+). stick tube into opening and tape up, vent outside. swirl contents.

2. when the reaction starts to slow down, place bottle in pot of water heated to 150 F.

3. slowly heat up to 200 F as reaction slows.

4. when reaction is complete (golden oil, doesn't stick to sides) cool bottle and take off tube.

5. add 100mL water, shake, then place back in pot to heat up.

6. filter through a funnel with filters and cotton plug until it is clear. collect liquid in a jar.

7. save the filters to clean and reuse the RP on it.

8. add 100mL naptha.

9. make a solution of 1 part lye to 4 parts water.

10. add a little solution slowly, stir, let layer, check pH of bottom layer. repeat until pH&gt;13.

11. add a tablespoon of salt, stir, let layer.

12. siphon top layer into a jar. keep bottom layer in the jar.

13. add half as much water as liquid.

14. add a little acid, stir, let layer, check pH of bottom layer. repeat until pH=7.

15. siphon top layer and add it in the jar with the saved liquid from step 12.

16. evaporate the bottom layer in the dish, finish with a hairdryer, scrape out crystals.

17. go back to saved liquid in the jar, and do steps 13-16 with it.
