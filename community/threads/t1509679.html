He said something about why he doesn't call people back but I wasn't listening. I asked his friend if it was worth talking to him and he said "yes, definitely. Do it."

I did, and he said that it's fine and asked me to call tomorrow.

I know that the first thing I have to do is figure out what I want, which is proving difficult. I've been extremely stressed out recently, due to things completely unrelated, but I want to pursue something.

But I guess the real point of this thread is that I feel really guilty about kissing that other guy. No matter how much I thought about how much I wasn't enjoying myself, and how much I tried to pretend it was B, or how drunk I was, or how I stopped it, I still feel like I did something completely stupid.
