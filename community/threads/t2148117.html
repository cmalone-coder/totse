Re: Questions about working for the USPS
It depends on what type of job you want.

The mailman job obviously good money, but you gatta work in all weather, and drive those gay little trucks...

I personally HAVE applied there, and been accepted, but turned down the job cause I got a better offer (in the industry I wanted, I just applied at usps cause its been like 3 months since I applied everywhere else.) I applied as the mail sorter (I think thats what its called.) The pay was good, but it was an overnight job. Which was fine with me cause I been battling insomnia since I was a teen. Besides it was good exercise.

But yes, there ARE drug tests, background checks, the whole shibang. Its a government job...

There are also pre-employments tests you need to take. The highest scores get chosen to go further in the application process, and those are offered the job provided they pass the drug screen.

if I remember correctly you need to go on their site and find the job you want, and see if the exam is being offered in the near future in your area (there is an exam for EVERY job they have.) You cant just send them your application and have them call you when they DO need someone. You keep checking back until the exam you need to take becomes available.

hope that helped a bit.
