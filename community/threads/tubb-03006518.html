don't let it get to you, in a few years youll have moved out and be on your own way in life, and this will all seem like a bad dream.

find some kind of escape, i spent alot of time out with friends, or fail that, by myself (something i still carry on to this day) to keep me away from here. it works, except when you get home again.


it does get to you, but don't make it any worse than it has to be.
