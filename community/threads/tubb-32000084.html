Re: Skinny and fat at the same time?
A few things:

There is no such thing as spot reduction. To lose weight somewhere, you will have to lose weight everywhere. This means diet and cardio.

creatine and whey are fine together.

for visible abs, diet and cardio are the keys, with ab work in third. A common misconception is that you get abs by doing lots of situps. Lots of situps will simply be overtraining. Go with a couple of sets, with a moderate amount of reps (6-12, whatever you feel comfortable with) 2-3 times a week.

also, make sure that the extra calories you take in are CLEAN. low sugar, low saturated fat. regular fat is not as bad as everyone has been told.

last, whiskey has calories. Quite a few, i believe. It's no better than beer. drop it.
