Twice I've had a gun pointed to my head and a knife pulled on me once. I've also attempted suicide eight times. However, since they were only attempts they were obviously subconciously mere attempts to piss people off instead of actually killing myself. If I'd really meant to kill myself, I would have succeeded.

Still, the fact that I've been faced with death (and other near encounters) twice I can honestly say that I am not afraid of death, as a person might think.

The knife was the first time anyone tried to kill me and I was only a kid. I was about eleven or something. May be twelve or thirteen and these two older African American kids jumped me because I was white and they beat me and cut me up. But, when they had the knife to my throat I told them, word for word:

"Go ahead. Kill me. See where that gets you." I can actually remember the exact words, it was so traumatizing. After that they beat on me some more and left me by some bushes near our flat.

The second time, and the first time I'd had a gun to my head was my mums ex-boyfriend. I was about thirteen and he was beating on my kid sister, who was only four, so I started hitting him. Then he went in his bedroom and got his gun. He had me up against the bathroom wall with the gun under my chin. He told me he was going to kill me and he shot a hole in the wall. And I told him, there

"I'm not scared. I want to die." Again, I was beaten and he left me in the shower with the water on.

The third time, and the second encounter with a gun, I was fourteen and I was walking through the South Side of my neighbourhood when I was pulling into an alley by these five Latino guys, from the opposing gang. One had a gun to my head while the others were riping off my clothes. I thought they were going to rape me. But, one of them kept telling the other ones to stop in Spanish. I asked the guy if he was going to kill me and he just told me to shut up. I told him to do it fast and then the gun went off, but the bullet didn't hit me. It hit the wall behind me and the guys ran for it.

So, accounting for these experiences, I think I can honestly say that I am no longer afraid of death, really. Not that any of them were very pleasureable. But, they were realistic previews to the real thing.
