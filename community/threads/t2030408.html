"I am the end of all"
Or at least my mind is on salvia.

I got some "Purple Haze Salvia" from a local smoke shop, it's only 5x.

So I decide to test it out. It's green, unlike the 20x salvia I smoked for my first time. I packed a bowl in my bong [I didn't have water in it] and used my regular Bic. I took as big of a hit as I could, held it in for as long as possible, and then repeated it. I cleared the bowl in two hits, and set the bong down and laid back on my bed.

I was alone in my room when I smoked it, and I was pretty baked. Parents and siblings were asleep, so I was a little hesitant, I didn't want to be loud or uncontrollable. My first time, all that happened was intense deja vu, intense heat, and intense laughter. However this time I promised myself I'd be in control at all times before I smoked it, and I even hesitated, which isn't like me.

So I'm sitting back listening to music, waiting for a full effect, and I start to notice a weird angle made by a laundry basket and my bong layering over eachother in my vision. Then I notice my bed is laying at the same angle in my vision, and then I realize all of the objects are making this INTENSE geometrical spiral, and everything else in my room, and the world is making one giant pattern, which I'm the start, and the end of. I noticed in my head I kept repeating "I am the end of all", and I think I even started saying it to myself aloud. I couldn't move, I felt I was stuck there for 5 or 10 minutes, and then I just had an afterglow type of thing.

My first time I used 20x extract, and smoked a bigger bowl with a torch, and had minor effects, although still powerful. It was almost undesirable, just because of how hot I felt. I also couldn't stop laughing like a dumbass my first time, to the point I was drooling a little.

This time I smoked a smaller bowl of 5x, didn't really laugh or get hot, just had an intense visual/mind trip.

All in all, a definite keeper of an experience, and now I want to do salvia again
