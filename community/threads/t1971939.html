You should know though, in highly populated areas, if high-priced items are stolen from a warehouse in that area, police search for users in that area selling an abundance of electronics that match the description of the stolen goods..

Lets say you live in Cleveland, OH and you steal 40 Sony Plasma TV's from a warehouse on the east side...

All one must do is go to ebay.com, typeSony Plasma TV into the search box, search through the result pages to find someone selling from Cleveland or the neighboring areas. If they feel that they have reasonable cause to suspect that you are the theif, then guess who can get a warrant to search your house? Cleveland Police Department. And trust me, Cleveland prison sucks.

It'd be safer to sell the stuff out of the trunk of your car, which I dont reccomend either.
