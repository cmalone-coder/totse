I think the actual reason for that is that he makes sure he gets the first swing in, and that makes a big difference. In all the fights i've seen or been in, the guy who hits first, typically wins.

My first hit are usually for the nose and as hard as I can. This is painful and usually tears up the guys eyes, now not only is he suddenly on the defensive, but he can't see. Their move after that is to usually charge or back off. If they charge an uppercut to the nose again is always great, if I can't do that I'll turn and push him, they always either fall or hit something. If they fall I try to get on their back before they get up, if they hit something, it's open.

Well he had me hurtin for while and he taught me the pain of hard rib shots. I'm surprised I didn't get anything broken. But that guy actually bruised me, which doesn't happen often.

Here's a tip straight from the marines...Make sure you get the first shot in, and if it's a good shot then don't stop - throw fast punches non-stop...it's called the "Rapid Fire Technique". If you want to see it in action go rent the Ultimate Fighting Championship where Vitor Belfort fights Vanderla Silva....I'm not sure which tape it's on so just rent the one called "Night Of Champions"...I know it's on there. Here are a few more tips...have you ever seen this guy fight? Do you know someone who has? Try to find out his style - if he throw a lot of punches or is known for knocking people out then learn a little about groundfighting, ground control, and submission and as soon as he punches then shoot in and take him down. If it's the other way around - if he's a wrestler or groundfighter then stay on the feet and try to stay away from him - hit when he comes in but don't let him grab you or shoot in on you...Also, don't let it be known that you are going to fight him - the element of surprise is a powerful weapon. Once you do start to fight him then go completely insane, like you're some kind of madman...this may intimidate the hell out of him, no matter how bad he tries to act. Remember, a little insane goes a long way...

Re: Fighting????
But also if you know anything about boxing then you know that the techniques taught and the methods used to fight aren't used to win street fights, they're used to score points with judges. Sure, you don't want to punch wildly - you want to throw accurate, calculated punches. You also want to move around. Throw a few punches, then duck and sway and throw a body punch, throw a few more to the face, then duck and sidestep and throw more punches. Once you land one good punch then keep it going. Remember this though: 90% of fights go to the ground, so don't rely on your ability to punch to end the fight. Learn some shit about groundfighting. You can start here: http://www.submissionfighting.com
