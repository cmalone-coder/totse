Quote:
quote:Originally posted by erbus:

So using sodium bicarbonate is a possibility? Granted, I'd have to use a lot in comparison to everything else, but it doesn't have to ionize entirely in water, right?
Yeah, it's worked pretty well for me in the past. The buffer doesn't really matter all that much, sodium will *still* outcompete as a base. If you were using a slightly stronger acid that was still weaker than HCl, the sodium would get the chlorine and the alkalai would get the weaker acid - used alot to liberate acids, in fact, though the other way 'round - and as far as I know, carbonate salts aren't particularly soluable unless they're with a reactive metal. Hell, by all the bubbling from an allegedly-neutral alkaloidal solution, I don't think the base even bothers to form the carbonate.

...type "make crack" into google to read about other people's experiences.

Quote:
quote:Originally posted by erbus:

I found some draino laying around the house that has NaOH, NaClO, and Na2SiO3. I assume that this will release Cl2, though, which isn't something that I especially want. I'm correct in that, right?
I'd be far more worried about the oxygen.

The underlined compound, NaOCl, is known as sodium hypochlorite. It's the active ingredient in most bleaches. It degrades readily to give NaCl - table salt - and O-.

Reactive oxygen species tend to rape the shit out of organic compounds, which is why it works so well as bleach. And, apparently, at annihlating organic masses which clog one's drains.

I'd reccomend keeping brutally reactive oxygen species out of the mix unless/until you have a specific use for 'em, m'self.
