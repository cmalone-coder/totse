Re: Re-rocking coke?
This method works if you have between a teener and a quarter. It should only take 15 minutes or so. What you will need is your blow (if it is cut to shit, then dont even bother), acetone (anhydrous acetone preferably since coke is water soluble), sandwich bags, a thumbtack, an old t shirt, a microwave, and a freezer.
First, take two bags and put one inside the other so it looks like you have one bag. Put the coke in the corner in the inner most bag so it looks like a nigger knot, but dont tie it. Add acetone to the coke by spoon until the mixture has the texture of a gritty toothpaste. DO NOT ADD TOO MUCH ACETONE OR ELSE!! Now, twist the corner of coke about ten times so it is sealed off and poke holes into the bag surrounding the coke.
Now twist the corner of coke in an old shirt so it has a lot of pressure on all sides as hard as you can. You should notice the acetone seeping into the shirt. You should now have a solid but wet rock; gently take it out of the bag and put it on a plate.
Now put the plate in the microwave for 10 seconds and immediately move to the freezer for 30 seconds, or when cool to the touch. Repeat this process of heating and cooling at least three times. Now drop the rock on a plate.
It should make a distinctive "tink" sound. If not, microwave and freeze it until you get that sound.
Now score the round edges so that it does not look like rerock.
Simple as that. :D
