It is easy to think that just because you feel better after you eat, that everything is "A-Ok". The cells of one's body/brain take time to repair & grow. Just as an injury that you can see or feel on your skin or in your muscles does.

It's way better to eat 2.3 crackers or a 1/3 of an orange, 3 ounces of OJ or 2 or 3 bites of beef jerky than nothing. A little now...a bit later. A short sleep is better than none. Though I know that when I'm really tired & hungry a bit of sleep might tempt me to stay up way longer than I should.

Science ( apparently...) shows that some healing/repair/growth only happens during deep REM sleep. Thus: you can only recover totally with sleep. A short, deep sleep can really help. I know that when really burned out, I sleep so quickly and deeply for an hour or two that I become wide awake. The trick seems to be to eat a little more (not too much!!!), not stay up too long!! & go back to sleep.
