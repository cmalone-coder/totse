The Material Bartering Thread
TRADE SOME SHIT! New shit is always fun!

So I figured most of us DIYers are the kind of people who collect random things to use for future projects, but as I'm sure you're all aware of, things often sit around unused or you can't use them for some reason, but they're still good pieces of whatever that could be useful. In order to make all of these things useful, I figured we could set up a bartering system.

Mailing stuff doesn't cost too much, as long as it's reasonably small, so if we all post what things we'd be willing to part with that might be useful to other people, we could get rid of some junk and get some useful stuff for relatively little money. This system is also nice because we don't have to deal with money and stuff like paypal, which only people over 18 can access. As far as I know, anyone can buy stamps or mail a package.

For trading I figured we could base fair trades on the value of the item vs. the shipping costs, and as long as it's roughly equal to what you're trading for [well as long as both parties are cool with it] we'll all be happy.

In your post please list your location, a description of the item, a pic if possible, and the approximate weight and dimensions. Here's a shipping cost calculator from UPS.
https://wwwapps.ups.com/ctc/request?...&WT.svl=SubNav

I'm sure there will be retards that try to send empty boxes and such, so avoid trading with people you don't see as regular posters here or anyone who doesn't type in english.
