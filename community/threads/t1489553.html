Re: Need a little advice.
"Have you ever thought of me as more than a friend?" is the quickest way to get a deer-in-the-headlights stare unless she's completely on your wavelength. From the sounds of it, that's not the case.

If you're already friends, asking her out on a date can be easier or harder than if she was someone you just met. Believe it or not, just asking her out might be harder.. or it might not. Depends on the girl and your approach. That's a judgment call.

If you've got a good feeling, but you're not sure, I would recommend patience. Chill out and build things up slowly. Touch her a little more, hugs, arm around the shoulder, things like that. Look at her. If you catch her gaze, linger a bit. This will not only begin to show her that you might have some interest (since she very well may not be aware, and could even be in the same unsure position as you), but it will help you see if you're right about how she feels. If it becomes clear she's not interested, you can back off without quite as much of a blow to your ego or the friendship.

More important than that, however, is the heightening of tension. It can become difficult, I'll warn you now. You stand a better chance of making something with her stick and be worthwhile this way, though. If she's not interested, she won't be regardless of what you do. If she's very interested, again, that won't change unless you completely fuck up. If she's on that middle ground, though, creating a buildup to the point where you finally start going out will hold her attention and make you both feel like you've spent time and, in a way, worked to make it happen. Were you to simply say, "Hey, want to go on a date?", and she said yes, her interest could quickly fade because the challenge of winning you over never existed.

It's a bit complex if you try to absorb it all at once. Keep in mind that I've been seeing this shit for over a decade now, while you're new to it, so it's easy for me to explain, maybe much moreso than it is to understand. For now, it's enough to take it slow and easy, but always try to progress things a bit if she appears open to it.
