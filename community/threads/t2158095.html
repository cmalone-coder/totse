I have made multiple attempts at overcoming this fear through conversation and "throwing out" things about Jews etc... Upon completing these things I do feel some relief, temporarily. It appears that I cannot be 100% free until I make the real plunge.

I hate that I have to do this. Somewhere my mind must have split, the feeling and the thinking separated.

Well I need to do something before I lose another year to this bullshit.

It is effectively ego suicide, my entire ego is fear.

I almost wish something real bad happened to me so I would have a legitimate reason to feel so bad.

The fact is nothing that bad has ever happened to me, except what I do to myself.

I was bullied in 3rd Grade. I was the "smelly kid". Everyone laughed at me and one kid especially took an enjoyment in making me squirm.

My reaction to that harassment was an intense fear characterized by a hard tensing of the stomach and a few of the other symptoms attributed to anxiety.

At some point a little bit later, I started pretending I was Jewish. For some time only in my mind and in front of the mirror (angle face to have nose appear larger, raising eyebrow, etc.)

Later on I would tell others that I was indeed Jewish and sort of developed a loud outgoing persona, partly based on faking being Jewish.

What happens next I find hard to explain. I myself have a hard time processing, how and why I am in this screwed up state. There are a number of things; my high-school years were not a very fun time for me. I was plagued with anxiety and I couldn't make any friends. People teased me abit and I acted in my usual pussy like manner.

In public school during my last 2 years I had established myself as a kind of funny-man entertainer and proudly said I was "half-Jewish" (even though it was not true). Well when I reached high school; the status of me being funny and popular did not remain and I found myself lost and in a kind of shock.

For some reason I started as I got older to look Jewish. Something I cannot understand as when I was younger I had blonde hair and typical Aryan facial features. Over the course of when I started to fake being Jewish, my face seemed to have change aswell.

People would repeatedly think I'm Jewish at school. They'd ask me if I was, some would merely assume I was and I got exposed to some prejudice (probably mere childishness, but it still fucked with my head).

I made some new friends at one point, although I don't think I actually like them. They enjoyed flirting with the ideas of Nazism and other crap and sometimes I'd play along. Eventually they started asking if I was Jewish. They would tease and call me a Jew. I noticed my reaction to any of this was my typical "pussy" reaction that I first did in 3rd grade. I would also think I was acting like a Jew would towards an Anti-Semite. I began identifying with the people make funny of me and really hating myself hard. I went on a downward spiral of depression until where I am now.

I am fucked up and I hate living my life. I have talked to a counseller about my problems which did help somewhat.

At this moment my gut is seizing up and tensing, as it usually is to some degree in all my waking life. I hate how much of a fucking pussy I am. That I wont just say "fuck this! and tell people the whole story". That I wont do what I know will help. That I have no compassion for myself and I prefer to live in this perpetual torture. I have no fucking balls. I fucking hate myself.

So anyways, I need to fucking do this. I am giving myself 15 days. I have a suspicion it wont be successful but I am going to make as much as an attempt as I can. I am a pussy. All through this article I was hoping that the writing of this article would be enough to solve this problem, but I suppose there is really no easy way out or shortcuts. I have to do this. I have to face this god-damn fear. FUCK!
