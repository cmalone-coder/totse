my thoughts on your god
okay, lets all assume that there is a god, and that he created everything. he is this really excellent person who knows everything and is everywhere.

so, why are there stupid rules? he is one super smart being that knows everything, so why does he care if someone has a wank, is gay or works on sunday? these are the type of rules that a spiteful person in power does to humiliate and belittle others. so, this leads me to two conclusions, either god is a complete prick, deserving of no praise, or these rules are complete bullshit made up by people with serious issues.

i assume that god is a pascifist. there is no reason that anyone who has created life wants them to kill each other. for any reason. he did, after all say 'thou shalt not kill' (one of ten definite things in the bible, and the most overlooked). any intelligent person knows that killing is wrong, and avoids it. god would be no diffrent, esspecially since he would not take sides and made everyone with the same amount of attention and love. a really understanding friendly person in the sky would not be petty, he would have the greatest perspective of all.

also, why should god care if anyone belived in him. as i said, he has perspective, so what does he care if someone refuses to belive he exists, or thinks of him under a diffrent name or form. he knows the situation down here, and he would be the most caring and compasionate fellow in the world, so its not like he would hold it against you. he exists whether you belive in him or not, so he is hardly going to take offence, and if was taking offence you think he might correct the situation and pay us a little visit. it wouldn't be hard for him, and would settle a fair bit of fighting down here.

he would only want us, his creation, to have happy lives before joining him in heaven. that means that he does not want us raping and killing each other for any reason. it seems only right. he loves everyone of us, playing no favorites, so therefore he would make no distinction between the murder of a catholic priest and a buhddist. he would not care if it is christians killing islamics or vice versa, all he would be concerned with is the fact that he has invented a phsycotic race. there was no asterix next to 'thou shalt not kill'.

you get the whole 'no one knows gods will' thing that seems to be of great satisfaction to grieving parents who have lost all their children in some bus crash or landslide, but i think that it is pretty safe to assume that god is a friendly dude who would defend us to the death in some bar argument.

i guess my point is that god is not how he is portrayed in the little books that are taken too seriously. it defies logic. the books were written by people with thier own agenda, not dictated by god. why on earth would god care what i do with my time that is of no consequence of anything else. he is not some pedantic overbearing paraniod egocentric who only cares about what people think about him. do you think a person like that could make the world in seven days?


this is all supposed, and some of it is why i shall continue to be a nihilist. no one who loves his creation could let it go to this much shit.
