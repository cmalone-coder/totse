Failure, Failure, failure. Its a HALF life, that means the molecule is HALFWAY deteriorated in 4.3-12.6 days.

That's the biological elimination half-life, half-wit, meaning that it takes 4.3-12.6 days for blood plasma levels of THC to reduce by half.

The terminal elimination half-life of delta 1-tetrahydrocannabinol (delta 1-THC) was investigated in eight men who were heavy users of marijuana. A stable isotope assay, following smoking deuterium-labeled delta 1-THC, was used to determine plasma concentrations. In two additional users plasma levels were followed after administration of unlabeled delta 1-THC. The subjects were asked to smoke a "loading dose" of 56 mg delta 1-THC during two days and then abstain from all marijuana use for 4 weeks. The pharmacokinetic behavior was consistent with a multicompartment model with a mean plasma elimination half-life of delta 1-THC of 4.3 days when concentrations were followed for 10-15 days after smoking. In the two subjects with detectable plasma levels during 4 weeks, half-lives of 9.6 and 12.6 days was obtained.
Emphasis added.

This all means that heavy male users that 6 of 8 men had an average of 4.3 days for elimination half-life while two men had 9.6 and 12.6 respectively. Honestly, with such few subjects and such obvious exceptions (not to mention this covers only heavy using men), both the scope and size of this study should not be used as the final word on THC's elimination half life.

Biological half-life has nothing to do with decomposition rates. And the rate was listed under "Pharmacokinetic data."

Pharmacokinetics (in Greek: "pharmacon" meaning drug and "kinetikos" meaning putting in motion, the study of time dependency) is a branch of pharmacology dedicated to the determination of the fate of substances administered externally to a living organism. In practice, this discipline is applied mainly to drug substances, though in principle it concerns itself with all manner of compounds ingested or otherwise delivered externally to an organism, such as nutrients, metabolites, hormones, toxins, etc. Pharmacokinetics is often divided into several areas including, but not limited to, the extent and rate of Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism and Excretion. This sometimes is referred to as the ADME scheme.

So, yeah, reading pays off, especially when analysing scientific data and then attempting to educate others.

THC is mainly metabolized to 11-OH-THC (11-hydroxy-THC) by the human body. This metabolite is still psychoactive and is further oxidized to 11-nor-9-carboxy-THC (THC-COOH). In humans and animals more than 100 metabolites could be identified but 11-OH-THC and THC-COOH are the dominating metabolites. Metabolism mainly occurs in the liver by cytochrome P450 enzymes CYP2C9, CYP2C19, and CYP3A4. More than 55% of THC are excreted in the feces and ~20% in the urine. The main metabolite in urine is the ester of glucuronic acid and THC-COOH and free THC-COOH. In the feces mainly 11-OH-THC was detected.

To the OP and those interested:

How to make THC blotters:

Items Needed:
Blotter Paper - Ebay/websites - $20-30
Bud - Any amount is fine. - So cost depends on your preference.
Coffee filters - $2
Everclear - 375 ml - $7-8
Funnel - $3
Glass baking dish - $3-10, depending on size
Glass jar (2) - Snapple bottles work well because of their large mouth. - $1-2
Grinder (electric is preferable) - $20-25
Strainer - $3
Steps:
1. Manually break up bud and discard any seeds.
2. Grind weed until more or less fine (powdery preferable, about 4 or 5 goes with a coffee grinder).
3. Use funnel to place marijuana in jar and pour in just enough Everclear to cover bud and then some.
4. Shake for about 5 minutes and allow bud to soak for at least half an hour. (Obviously, allowing it to soak longer allows more cannabinoids to be extracted. You could extend it to days, weeks, etc, but that's overkill.)
5. Place filter in strainer and allow your now 'Evergreen' to pass through into your collection jar.
6. Scoop gunk in the filter back into the jar. Repeat steps 3 through 6 at least once.
7. Line pan with blotter, then pour as much of your Evergreen as you'd like and allow it to evaporate in a dark, cool place.
8. Repeat step 7 until Evergreen is gone or save some for Green Dragon drops.

Initial cost will be between $70 and $120, depending on what is on hand/what can be borrowed/what deals you find/the amount and quality of weed.

Note about blotter:
A blotter square holds up to about 2 mg. Depending on the quality of your bud, your cannabinoid concentration will vary of course, plus any other non-cannabinoid chemicals. I'd personally use the rule of thumb of .5-1ml/tab. Any excess should have no problem staying on the outside of the blotter, but the green color will obscure any tab design. For best storage, wrap in foil inside a freezer bag and keep in the freezer.
