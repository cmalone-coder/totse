First time cid trip report
I usually don't have much time to visit here, but since there are so many posts about first time cid trips, I figured now would be a good time to write about my first time experience. It was on September 6th at an all white rave and the following day at the local amusement park...

I start by taking a white rolex on the drive. I take in with me another 5 rolex's. I party around for the first hour or so and find my dealer I was supposed to re up from in the morning. I had the money he had the ills and I quickly bought a 30 bag and popped one. I walked back to the dance floor, danced and slanged some ills for about an hour...

Suddenly my friend i see at every show shows up. We talk for a while and he says I found a guy with cid if you need any. I was like hell yeah wheres he at? I follow him upstairs to the VIP room. I didn't have a pass, but was with him, I know most of the security and am well known for my dancing. We find the hidden spot and talk business. He says i got 14 left and im selling them for ten a piece...He seems a bit nervous. I said I know its normally cheaper...He says hes selling them for a friend and needs to bring at least $120 back and wants $20 profit to take two hits, but he mentions he hatesdealing...I say how about you sell them all to me for 8 each, I'll throw you a free hit, you get to trip, and then you don't have to focus on dealing, don't have to worry and can have extra fun. He agrees, we take a hit and talk for a little while. i stay in the vip room for a little until I could take another hit.

I contact and find my friend who also wanted to try it. We each took a blue cupid and a hit of cid. Another two hours pass. We re dose one of each. The first hit is starting to kick in. People are looking more defined lights are way brighter, my dance style had changed, my voice had changed, everything had seemed to change somehow. Sometime later,

I took another hit and a rolex. I became a lot more social and people were noticing my ability to use any type of light and perform any style of raving very well. During shows while looking into their eyes I felt like I could literally travel into their mind or control their every action or even their emotion with a simple change in my facial gesture. Sometime later,

I took another hit. I had a planned photo shot for the event and had finally found my photographer. At this point I was on three ills and 4 hits of cid. For this photo I used 38 lights no straps. 10 different color glowsticks, 5 per hand, and 9 LED finger lights per hand. The flash from the camera was so intense. I could feel its energy. the photos looked amazing and appeared to be slightly moving. After cooling down, Sometime later,

I took another hit. Everything was so intense. Pictures i had seen earlier were no longer the same picture. The colors would move, vibrate, or change colors. Outside the plants were swaying, and the buildings off in the distance seemed two dimensional. The rave was over...We got to the car and waited to get the info to go to the after party. Sometime later after getting to the party, probably at around 3 or 4 I took another hit. We were kicked out of the party house at around 8 am. A few friends were still awake rolling with nowhere to go, so we all went to Starbucks. Sometime later,

We took another hit, in the parking lot. We discussed the rave and let the first timers talk about what a good time they had. We explained the meaning of plur and other raver knowledge. The artwork never stopped spinning, and the hair on my arms and everyone else constantly shrank and grew. We had to be back in my main town by 11:30 to pick up people to go to the amusement park. Sometime later,

We take the final hit and a rolex. Ive been to this park many times after raves while rolling or just rolling, but it was a way different experience after a rave on 4 ills and 8 hits. If you have never been to an amusement park on drugs, I highly recommend it. There were just so many things happening it would take a while to explain and I simply don't have the time.Sometime later,

The park had closed and the trip had ended. I went home and took my normal after weekend comedown sleep aid trip consisting of ten benadryl and a lunesta. When the sleep aids kick in, I feel as though it immediately brings back the acid trip, and will keep it alive for a while if you are able to fight the sleep aids. I slept and had one of the most memorable days of my life. Moral of the story an intense and memorable first cid trip is worth saving up for is priceless and worth every penny...

PS, I don't get ripped off for normal cid. I have a vile for my area and personal use I got for the average $350 and will sometimes get ripped but be able to get the best cid I have ever had. Goes by the name sunshine cid...Anyone else hear about it?
