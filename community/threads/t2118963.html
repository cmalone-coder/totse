um.... well.... you see, these feelings that MDMA gives you are 100% unnatural, fake. MDMA is enjoyable on a "have a great night", or on a "gain insight" basis. However, you have to realize that everything is relative; the weight of emotions, desires, actions, even most sciences (chemistry, physics), etc are all relative to a perceived "norm". If MDMA is making you feel good, it's because it creates a serotonin paradigm that produces enjoyable sensations relative to your "normal" (WTF is normal?!?!?!) state. If you take MDMA every day (or relatively frequently) you will not have the "magic" that MDMA is known for. This, i believe, is because you adapt to the induced sensations and your mind makes them the "norm"

I feel the same is for any 'feel good' chemical, for example paxil. It may make you feel better temporarily, but essentially your mind will adapt to the synthetic effect the chemicals provide. You will feel 'normal' when on these drugs, and depressed, anxious, etc when not on these drugs. This is not natural in any sense. Your mind doesn't operate on a quantitative level (ex: amt of substance in your system), it operates on a relative level. That means that your mind creates a reaction (ie, sensations) to whatever chemicals you introduce that create a difference from the current paradigm your mind operates upon.

My advise is that you should stop looking for your philosopher's stone (in greek times, the philospher's stone is the unknown substance that will induce and make possible all human desires; for greeks it was turning matter into gold, in current times it's creating the perfect existence and mind state). It will be useless, because you will feel "good" temporarily until relativity kicks in (it's time dependent), and you're back to your normal state. Then you'll need the chemicals to remain "normal".

My advice is if you feel you have to take something, to take 50mg 5-HTP, a supplement of vitamin B6, and a small dose of St. John's Wort every other day for a good month or so. I repeated the same regimen and all i can say is that I am much more level headed than i was before. The effects are subtle and take time to actually notice; but they do help. Overall, I feel much happier after this regimen. I have tried antidepressants for prolonged periods of time, and after a while they force you to become dependent upon them (because the "normal" paradigm would change upon stopping the meds) and they no longer help you.

Conclusion: Using chemicals to be happy, and have the "perfect existence" is ignorant. Don't attempt it; this kind of mindset will cause you to fuck yourself over in the future. Meditation, diet, etc will provide a much better effect if your looking to always be "happy".

You have to realize that there is this stigma in the western world that if you differ from the "norm", you require chemical (drug) assistance. This is a stupid and ignorant thought. For example, 4X as many people are prescribed drugs for ADD than could statistically exist in the US; people believe that they differ from the norm and desire "help". Don't be one of them, it's a false path to integrate into your values and life

Humans have existed for thousands of years without chemical assistance, why should you need it now? Mind over matter sir. That's all I can say. You probably don't feel happy because of something other than a chemical imbalance; deal with that first and then analyze your position in this crazy and hectic world after.
