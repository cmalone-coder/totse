Some slightly complicated opiate tollerence questions
I know this post is very long so do not read if you do not want to. If in any event you feel the desire to post TLDR, Flames, helpful comments, links or funny pics after reading please feel free to do so bellow. I know this is long but some of you may find it interesting:D



At $10 dollars / 8mg the brand name Dilaudids were the best buy on the block followed closely by OxyContin 80mg pills at 20 - 30 bucks a pop. Ones bank account would indeed shrink and so may their activities beyond taking drugs... all this while ones tolerance may shoot through the roof. I am LittleFootOnLSD and I have a tolerance (not addiction) to opiate based drugs.

Needless to say using once a week in large doses would result in a large tolerance increase. I started off with needing one dilly 8 all day and one OC80 was more than enough to go around. I would use Oxycodone or Hydromorphone almost exclusively primarily snorting the drugs but also eating them. After a couple months I would need to ingest around 3 - 5 Dilly 8's for a good snorted buzz or eat around 30 - 60 mg's of Oxycodone but would eat upwards of 70 - 80 mg's at one time with essentially no ill effect.

Back up, I have to explain a bit more. The drugs did not by any means become boring to take and life was grand even when not high but I saw no problem with what I was doing and as such continued to take these drugs in varying (all be it increasing) quantities once every week or so. I eventually got board though (especially with Dilaudid) and wanted something new and exciting.

Vancouver is home to Canada's one and only safe injection site meaning you can bring any drug and the nurses there would assist in prepping them for injection. This oppened up all new possibilities. Case in point, IV drug use would become the new prefered method of administration.

Switching to using needles as means of administering a drug did not bother me and changed nothing except for me needing to spend less money and making morphine a drug that I would now be able to effectively utilize in my goal to attain a pleasurable custom state. This whole concept struck me as almost a fine art and is what got me interested in drugs to begin with. Anyways, my use stayed at once a week only now I needed as little as 10 dollars or less to have a great time.

For those of you wondering, the fact that IV Dilaudid in partiqular feels similar to yet better than orgasm did not increase my desire to get high but it did result in me taking dangerously high doses in my quest to obtain "The Ultimate hit" which I figured is what I wanted to do when I put drugs in my system. The fact that opiates are to my knowledge relatively harmless as far as damaging the body and mind go also gave me no incentive to cease use.

At times I would take a combination of drugs. One time I ate 10 5mg percocets in one single mouthful along with several T3's, injected a 20 mg dose of dilaudid and chased that with a large hit of morphine as Dilly has almost no legs. Around this time I noticed that although I did not use more than once a week I was spending just as much if not more than when I was snorting and eating the drugs and as such came to the obvious conclusion that my tolerance had again gone (way) up. Ill sumarize dosses of what I would do bellow.

All IV :

Morphine ~ Anywhere from 90 - 150 or so mgs per shot but up to around 200mg in a couple hours. I say around because the beads gell and require multiple washes but I am sure you never get everything. Still though, I would need at least 2 100mh ethix "greys" to ensure a whole days worth of being high. This only cost 20 bucks but still, that is allot of morphine. Also, I loved taking shots of morphine after a shot of dilly as it enhances and prolongs both drugs.

Hydromorphone ~ This one pisses me off. Although it gives one of the greatest rushes known to man it builds tollerence like none of the other drugs I took. When I snorted them prior to using IV I would snort at times over 70 mgs in a day if supplies lasted (I once went through 20 Dilly 8's in just under 3 days). When I started shooting them I would only need around 4mg but quickly I ended up requiring between 16 - 24 mgs for a good shot. That would nod me off and have me creaming my pants for sure if only opes didn't constrict the urinary tract and make you senile.

Oxycodone ~ Never shot it but often combined it with shots.

And there you have it. My chemical love affair where the bitch demands more money to keep you happy. Strangely throughout all this time Codeine did not seem to be that effected by my tolerance and at any time with or without other drugs in me popping 5 - 7 T3's would give me a rush that not even oxy could give but not as good an overall high.

Now for the important questions.

On a trivial side note. I find it strange that I have never suffered withdrawl either mental (cravings) or physical during the week when not using. I also wondered, when I would say eat some oxy or codeine before a shot but then did the shot it almost completely canceled out the mental effects of the codeine or oxy. Do strong opaites overide other drugs or sumthing?

Obviously I am wondering. How long realistically for tolerance to diminish? I am taking a multi month break as school is starting. If some time mid October I wanted to eat some Codeine or oxy would I need far less ( would like 20 mgs oxy give a great high again) and if I did that would it raise it right back to where it was after one dose? Also, if after only dossing once every couple months after 10 months would my tolerance essentially be back to where it was before ever using them (remember my large doses).

MOST IMPORTANT QUESTION ~ I read that taking opes actualy increases the number of opaite receptors and as such can pernamentaly change how opes can effect you. Do you think I created many new ones after only 10 months or so of using once a week but really only 2 months of IV use and if so after many months will it still go down notably. I mean, it wont really effect my high will it?

Be safe and have fun, LittleFootOnLSD
