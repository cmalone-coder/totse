This could be placed in another forum, but I think it's fine here.

I've worked as a psychic medium before and can say for the record: 25% is truth, 25% is bullshit, and 50% is "net throwing".

There are a lot of things you can tell about somebody just from evaluating their sex, age, state of their skin (lines, wrinkles, blemishes, etc...), dress, etc... Combining that with a persons birthday (place in the universe) and what positions the universe is in, along with many other factors, you can get pretty specific with stuff about the past, present, and future.

However, is it really being psychic or is it just being a really good judge of character?

I do know how to communicate with the passed on. But, even while I can do it (under the right circumstances) and I believe it when I do it, I don't even know how much of that is real and how much is just coincidence.

I think it has a lot to do with how much the person being read believes.
