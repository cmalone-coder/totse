What is the most important thing you've learned
...with respect to drug use. That is, what is one thing that you think all responsible responsible drug users should know.

It can be a fact, a preparation or ingestion method/technique, an important piece of information about drugs in general, a certain drug or type of drug, wisdom you've developed about the drug community/scene, or life advice from someone who has 'been there'.

If someone else touched on something you were going to discuss, expand on it or choose something else.

While not everyone here is an expert on everything, I'm sure we all have something we think is important to contribute.

I'll start. Some people like tomatoes, some don't. It depends on the person. Drugs are the same way. Their effects vary by person. Your experience with a substance is never the full measure of that substance's effects. Different people react differently to different drugs. If you've never taken a xanax before, just because you saw someone with a similar build eat ten the night before, drink a twelve pack, and be just fine, by no means does this mean you can do the same safely. It seems like common sense but if everyone here had common sense....well, read the first page.

There is a lot more I'd like to add, but I think I'll turn it over to you guys.
