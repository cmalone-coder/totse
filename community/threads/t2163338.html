Parachuting is an alternative oral method used by people who can't stand the taste of the pills or just prefer to do it this way because the pill is nice and powdered when it hits the stomach.

My method:
1) Get yourself a shotglass and toss the pills inside.
2) Use the bottom of a screwdriver, a lighter or whatever to crush the pills into a fine powder.
3) Dumb said powder onto a half square of TP or other small piece of thin-ish paper. I wouldnt recommend writing paper :P
4) Roll it up into a little ball, place on the tongue and wash down with something to drink.

As far as Oral vs Nasal it is in fact the onset vs duration. Orally the onset is generally quite a bit longer but lasts considerably longer as well. Nasally it'll hit you far quicker but peter off much sooner.

My preferred method is just to chew the pills up and swallow.
