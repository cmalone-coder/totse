My DXM Experiance
Last night, I consumed about 1.5 grams of DXM (2 bottles of Delsym). I weigh 145lbs, and I've never taken over a gram before, and this time it was definaly a mistake. I like DXM highs, but the upper plataeus can get a bit too intense if you dont work your way up to them. But anyway, on with my story.

I mixed 10oz of Delsym with 10oz of Juice (in a 20oz bottle) and downed the whole thing really quickly. I walked downstairs into the basement, and watched Leslie Neilsen's "Wrongfully Accused" for about an hour, waiting for it to kick in.

When I shut the movie off, my legs felt kinda shakey, and I was a bit light-headed. I walked up to my room, shut the lights, and just listened to music. Usually, when I'm on DXM, I just want to lie on the floor, and listen to the music peacefully. But this time, I wanted to dance. It must've looked like I was having a seizure. I was on the floor, rolling around, shaking my arms and legs, doing somersaults. I guess I'm sorta weird when I'm high. Usually, I'm very sedated on DXM, but last night I had alot of energy.

But that wasn't the worst part. I felt really mentally slow. I tried talking to a friend of mine on the phone, but I just went insane trying to hold a conversation with him.

I went on IRC, and started talking to xenosss and Ironz. I'm looking for the IRC logs now, but cant find them. I just remember telling them that I was on a really intense trip.

After I shut off the computer, I went back into my room, and tried to sleep, but I had too much energy! I was just tossing and turning for hours until I finally fell asleep, and I was only asleep for about 5 hours.

All in all, it was somewhat enjoyable, but I definatly wouldn't take that much DXM again. I do like dex, but the upper plateaus were scaring me a bit last night.

Also, does anyone know why I was really energetic last night?
Usually, I take DXM HBr, but last night, I took DXM polytirex, if that's any help...
