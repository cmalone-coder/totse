Re: acetone again
Ok here is the final post regarding this subject, just to clear everything up with everyone.

Acetone is a wonderful solvent for many uses, it is amazingly non-toxic, doen't absorb through your skin and is NON-carcinogenic. It is largely used in my lab to dry cleaned glassware. It completly evaporates out of extracts because of its very low boiling point. Unfortunately it makes for shitty hash oil cause it takes out waxes, sterols, pigments, chlorophyll and pretty much everything out of your material. So you get dirty extract.

Alcohols are shitty for making extracts, you get dirty oil again because so much extra stuff dissolves in it. Black tar oil results.

Remember THC is absolutely non-polar.

So that means that your best bet is petroleum ether. I mean really pure redistilled stuff. Not coleman's straight out of the bottle cause you will get gross bitter rust inhibitors in your shit.

The tops for those of you technically competant is supercritical butane extraction. Very easy to do in the winter outside when it gets below zero. You've heard of the pressure pipe extractor? Enough butane can be had cheap at wallmart etc. not as cheap as acetone or alcohol but for the extra ten bucks it's worth it.
