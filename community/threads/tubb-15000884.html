The beautiful thing about someone preaching the Determinist doctrine is they're contradicting themself.

If Determinism is true, then everything you do is predetermined by certain chemical and physical rules which are applied to the world and continue to act in a predictable fashion. Except...

1 - Determinism leads to Fatalism. If everything is predetermined, there is no point in trying to do anything, because if it has been predetermined, then you'll do it, otherwise you won't. You should just sit on your ass because that's what you've been predetermined to do.

2 - No matter how much a person may claim that determinism is true, they will go on thinking they have free will. You don't walk around, believing your every action is governed by set rules and circumstances which make it impossible to act in any other way.

Oh, and regarding the bit about Quantum Mechanics, yes according to our current understanding of it, certain things are entirely unpredictable (like the point in time at which a radioactive substance decays). However, the idea is that you have free will, not random-chance-actions (which are [u]determined[/u] by the decay of random particles in your brain). Therefore, the Quantum Mechanics argument is invalid in the Free Will vs Determinism debate.
