Mephedrone also goes by MethylMethcathinone (MMCat as well). AFAIK, it's most common methods of administration are oral. It's assumed to be the chemical contained within a popular european "legal" ecstasy tablet called Neo Dove but that is just speculation.

It might have a bit of a distracting euphoria however, so I suggest you make the decision on what to get based more on what purposes you need the drug to fulfill. (that is, I'm unsure what type of job you have)

If it was me, I would use the mephedrone if I wanted to stay euphoric and stimulated (that is, I would definitely use it at school or work) whereas I would use modafinil at home to stay focused on schoolwork or any task that needs to be done without being around people (as I found it sometimes made me psychologically tired while around people, but on my own I felt fine and stimulated).

also, you can always re-dose, IMO instead of taking a high dose that is going to burn you out quickly for the night, if you're looking simply for the stimulation, try a low dose for that purpose and just re-dose later on as it wears off.
