Ripping Off Bar with Tab
I usually do pay as you go when I go to the bars, but they always ask if I want to start a tab. I noticed that when you start a tab, you typically just leave them your debit/credit card and they'll charge everything at once whenever you're ready to leave.

My question is this: would it work if one were to find an old/stolen/lost credit or debit card, use it to hold a tab, and then bolt when you're finished without paying?

It's not the most complicated of scams, but bars get really expensive really fast. Is it even worth it?

Re: Ripping Off Bar with Tab
I've been a bartender for going on 9 years now, so I'll give ya some insight.

In most cases, yes this can and has worked. I've personally seen it several times and I really could care less as long as the card goes thru. Keep in mind that as long as it goes thru and you run out I'm still adding myself atleast a 30% tip on it.

If it's stolen, so be it, you get a free tab, and I still get my tip.

On the other hand, I just started working at a brand new club on my off days from my normal place, and it has one of the new scanning systems, that lets us know if you have the funds on the card.

What I mean is, when you come in and tell me you wanna run a tab, ill take your card and swipe it thru the reader. It wont charge anything at that point, but once I start putting drinks on the tab, it will already be saved in the computer and let us know if you max out.

Just a warning, not all bars are doing this yet.

Oh, and dont ever shit the bartender. The bartender has the keys to the palace.
