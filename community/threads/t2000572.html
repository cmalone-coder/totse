Yeah avoid the south.

Texas is just too big and full of texans to get to the few cool places. Alabama needs to be purged, mississipi is poor and depressing. Florida probably not worth the time (especially in the winter). Re: U.S.A Road Trip
I would check out N. Cali, Oregon, Washington, Colorado. They offer some amazing forests and mountains...but they're heavily populated enough to have fun in.

Wyoming, Montana and the likes are pretty cool as well, but be prepared to drive 4 hours without seeing someone.

If you go to chicago...the aquarium downtown is my favorite thing to do (Shedd aquarium). There's also a really cool park a long lake michigan with some great sculptures...lots of places to shop but Chicago is really boring otherwise.

Wisconsin and Minnesota may be worth checking out. Minneapolis/St. Paul and Madison are both pretty fun areas. They get some pretty good shows...there's a lot of smaller festivals between the states. In northern minnesota you have Voyagers national park too, great place.

But yeah, you're from Canada right...so I don't know how much some of this stuff will appeal to you (Minnesota pretty much is Canada once you get passed the twin cities).
