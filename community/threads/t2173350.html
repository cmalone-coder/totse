If you are not doing this you are wasting your weed [pics]
If you have a bill piece and a grinder you can make fucking decent hash from your kief and the resin in your bill.

http://i35.tinypic.com/1uko.jpghttp://i33.tinypic.com/29l05eh.jpg

Those are both the same piece, it is 0.6 grams. I took the pictures with a scanner so I could not show it on a scale.

That hash is from 100% recycled marijuana. It has a really nice mellow high and its just as potent as real hash. Its hard as a rock at room temperature and it becomes like putty in your hand. I mainly use it for brewing (see:http://tinyurl.com/3wt6xz) and it lasts a very long time. Its not very good in bowls though. Basically when I buy weed its as if Im getting a little bit of hash too for free. None of my friends suspect that its made from custy resin until I tell them.

All you do it take a clogged bill piece and wait until its more or less dry of bong water. You can dry it faster by holding the bottom of the stem over a lighter. Then you scrape everything out of hit into some sort of receptacle. You can use any thing to scrape it, like a paperclick or a bobby pin or even a clothes hanger. You will probably need a pocket knife or something like that to scrape out the bowl, which is the best part because its the most dense and its always completely dry. I use a plastic container from cough losenges to hold the resin scrapings. Then you either wait until this is dry on its own which can be from 1 to 2 days. Or you can dry it faster by folding piece of paper and puting all of it in the paper, then holding a lighter or a candle along the other side of the paper. Letting it dry on its own usually works better and is alot safer. I made huge mess of burned paper ash on my carpet yesterday by accident drying resin like this.

Now that you have dried resin scrap most people who scrape resin usually smoke it like this which is fucking disgusting, but if you keep do this you make bomb hash like I have in the picture. Take a pocket knife and start mashing the resin together into one big piece. This can be the hardest part because its very sticky and alot of this will get on your fingers and on surrounding objects. So much that you can actually lose most of the resin. This is why it is important that it is properly dry but not too dry that it wont stick together. Easy ways of pressing it are by breaking an old bus card or credit card or anything like that in half and then squishing it inbetween the two halves. Doing this will squeeze out most of the remaining water if there is any. You will know you have pressed it enough when you can squeeze it in your fingers without it coming apart and sticking in your skin. You should be able to shape it. Now you can put it in the freezer for like 5 minutes.

Squish your ball of resin into a little flat disk. This can be difficult for a minute if you just took if from the freezer. Then take a grinder if you have on and get all the kief out if it that you can. If have weed you can even grind it and shake it around just to get kief. If you pile your weed on a table or scale it then loads of kief with just fall and remain on the surface of the scale or table. If you keep it in a container you can just sweep it off the side of the container. Pick up all the kief you collect directly with your flat piece of hash and then fold it over so that the kief is totally surrounded inside. Then squish it around so that it is uniformly mixed. Congratulations you are done.

You may need to repeat these steps many times before you have a significant amount of hash so save up your resin and keif. Also I find it is nicer when you smoke alot of batch in your weed, because the batch affects the resin also. If you do this properly then what you end up with is a very nice piece of hash and its not at all stinky or gross like it seems in your bill.
