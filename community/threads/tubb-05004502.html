Re: Acid rumours
Okay..time to dispell a couple of myths.\
1. LSD does not build up in any areas of the body. The half life of LSD when injected is 5.1 hours. 80% is excreted by the liver, bile system, and small intestine within three hours. All LSD is gone in 24 hours. Look on erowid to verify my facts.
2. there is no strychnine in LSD in any active dosage range. A 5mm x 5mm square of blotter weighs approximately 2 mgs. That is below the active threshold for strychnine. The cramps, nausea, and other unpleasant effects are most likely caused by poor LSD synthesis that produce ergot derivatives.

When I have more time, I will write a file to contribute to &totse covering these myths, and giving good facts to the people that are too lazy to research the topic on their own. Have fun dosing, but remember to respect the drug, as it can warp your mind for short periods of time. If you can get ahold of thorazine keep a couple of tabs on you, as it will terminate most effects of the LSD. You never know when you might need a parachute.
