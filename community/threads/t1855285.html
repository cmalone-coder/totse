Or if you subscribe to the way of thinking, "Hell, I'll just dump in twice as much. That oughta do it."

monkey--

Use the matchbooks from the wal and it ain't no mart. Its a mixture of blue and yellow. At this drugstore, you can buy their store brand, 50 books per box for $0.99. This stuff is smooth, and comes right off with a little 5-10 min. acetone soak followed by shaking the hell out of them for about 5 min. After the tone, hcl, H2SO4,DH2O soaks, the remaining powder will be very close to lab grade RP. Smooth and easy to powder.

You've got two ideas here, try them both. SWIG guarantees easy to obtain, easy to clean, and very few (if any) sand and glass fillers, not to mention very reactive RP for those fireworks you're working on.
