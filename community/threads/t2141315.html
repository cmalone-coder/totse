Re: Resistor wire? (for use in mj vaporizer)
Looking at how to make the control circuit. I see a KTY84 thermistor that has a resistance around 1640 ohms at 374 *F. I was thinking a voltage divider to turn on a power BJT. This BJT would be between a high voltage DC power supply (~24-48VDC) to operate the heater coils.

You'll want to have some ceramic standoffs over the circuit board as well to isolate the heater coils from the board. Not sure how you would isolate the heat coming to the board form the conecting wires, though.

One the heater end it's just the power supply inline with the heater coils, a 500 *F thermal fuse, and the BJT. On the BJT input side its just a voltage divider arrangement with the base tapped off the thermistor to ground. The input voltage to the base side can be a 5VDC or it could be powered off the heater power supply if the voltage remains fairly constant once the heater turns on.

The HVDC supply would be something simple, robust, and, of course, fused.

Fairly simple stuff.
