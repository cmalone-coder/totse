You will need Nitrous in a balloon or plastic bag for this.
& only the nitrous, there should be no air as well as the gas.

To get the most out of your nitrous you need to hyperventilate first.
That means breathe real fast untill you start to go dizzy.

Your body gets overfilled with oxygen, so that you can hold your breath longer.

Then empty All of the air out of your lungs, All of it, every little bit of air.
Practice all that if you have too.

Then inhale the gas, try to get it all into your lungs.
Hold your breath hard.
Squash your lungs using chest pressure so the gas gos into your blood faster.

About now everything will go buzzy, like your lips, face, head, body.

Hold your breath untill you absolutley have to inhale air again.
Now exhale the Nitrous back into the balloon or plastic bag.

Now hyperventilate again real fast untill the nitrous buzz starts to go away.

Now reinhale the Nitrous gas again.

Hold your breath & push again.

Wow, lots more buzzy.........


Homemade cracker.

You need,
Clear plastic tubing, larger internal dia than the gas bottle, about 6 inches should do,
Sharp nail,
Hammer,
Plastic bread bag, or balloon (make sure theres no holes in them, check again)
Plug to fit one end of the tubing.

Plug one end of the tubing,

Hammer the nail right thru the plastic tube about a inch away from the plugs inside end,
Now pull the nail back into the plastic tubing so the sharp tips now inside the tubing,

Where the empty nail hole is, insert the neck of the gas bottle (you might have to heat the tubing or the gas bottle or enlarge the hole so the neck will fit inside the hole.
It should (has too) make a good airtight fit.

Now put the bread bag or balloon over the open end of the plastic tubing, squeeze the air out of it.

Move the nail around & shift it so the sharp tip is nestled into the lead seal on the gas bottle.

Now balance the hole thing so the gas bottle is vertical, the plastic tube is horizontal & the gas bottles base is sitting on the floor or solid base.

Bash the nails head with the hammer, the gas should come out & fill the plastic bag or balloon.

Things that go wrong,
Theres a hole in the plastic bag, duh.
Theres a hole in the balloon, double duh.
The gas bottle spat itself out & shot around the room, ah shit!
The whole thing fell over.
The bag/balloon wasn't sealed over the plastic tube, oh uh duh.
There was air in the bag/balloon, ohhh, ah ha!

Things to look out for,
The gas bottle shooting out & smashing the light bulb (true)
The gas bottle freezes up, heat the bottle up a bit in warm/hot water first.
It helps to use two people so everythings steady.

An extra,
Its called "Laughing gas" NOT because it makes you laugh but because the sound you hear goes all 'echoey cohy ohy y'
That 'laughing gas' bit is I guess is a common misuse of the words.
Also it affects/effects the higher frequancys too, so do this to heighten its effects.

Blow up a balloon & spit a bit on it & rub the wet spot so it squeaks. Keep doing it.
Or use polystyrene on a window to get the squeak.
