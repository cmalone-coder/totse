How does the success of your family affect you?
I was sitting around last night talking to my family (my parents and brother, as we're the only ones in my extended family out here where we're living) and I realised that although we're a really happy family unit the vast majority of the time, there seems to be a lot of angst about how we're all doing achievement-wise.

My brother dropped out of school at Grade 10, for no reason other than he didn't want to go anymore, and he doesn't really have any ambition to go back to any kind of education. It's disappointing to say the least, and I don't really want to see him floundering about from job to job in later life not making enough to make ends meet - I have an uncle like that and whilst I really like him he's a case study in regretted youth, he did exactly the same thing.

My father retired when he was fifty, as the branch of the company that he was working for (the reason we came out to South Africa) closed down, and he couldn't get another job here because his work permit explicitly states he works for abovementioned company here, or none at all. He reflects on this to me occasionally and says he'd really enjoy working for a few years more, and that he feels that he's wasting away. I can't help but feel sorry for him, I know I'd go nuts if I was in his situation.

My mother came out with my father on a house-wife permit (which has got to be the most restrictive thing I can imagine) - she cannot apply for any kind of work despite being thoroughly qualified as an engineer, and has not been able to for the last ten years - however she's taken it in her stride for the sake of raising myself and my brother, something I'm eternally grateful for. To me it just seems like the most titanic waste of potential, and you can see it in her eyes some days when she's mooching around the house.

Being surrounded by these three makes me feel kind of guilty that I'm the only one that still has the opportunity to make something of myself over an extended period of time - I really do feel quite bad, like I'm stepping over the mark.

So my question to you, S&A, is - how well are your family members doing in terms of reaching their ambitions (if you know what they even are) and how does it make you feel? Proud? Disappointed? Apathetic?
