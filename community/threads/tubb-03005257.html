Re: Good Hiding Places
alright.. .here's how you make a book into a hiding spot, this works best with encyclopedias, dictionaries, textbooks, etc. Make a solution of 1/3 glue, 2/3 water in a bucket, elmers works fine for this. hold book by the covers and dip all the pages in the glue, shake it around a bit to let the glue soak into the pages. take out of the bucket, and wipe off some of the excess glue.
Get some wax papers and place the book on the paper, remember, the covers should still be fully open at this point. Put wax paper on the topside and throw some phonebooks on them for a few days. When the glue is dried, drill a hole about an inch or so from each corner of the page, then saw a big empty rectangle out of the middle. Glue the back cover to the glued pages, and you have a nice little hiding spot.

Re: Good Hiding Places
I always loved these improv threads. Storing very small items (or easily malliable ones, such as drugs) there are a multitude of options.

Anything with a reasonably thick solid wood element such asdrawer faceplates, desk surfaces, doors (very good), and so on can be drilled along the edge to create a narrow little hollow. You can stash whatever you want in here and plug it up with a cork or whatever strikes your fancy.

If you have a dresser with big gaudy wooden knobs you can do the same sort of thing. Metal doorknobs are almost always hollow and can be filled with whatever you like right off the bat.

Desks with wide beveled edges present lots of places for modification. A good trick is to build a small teacher's mailbox sort of arrangement underneath your desk, built such that it is flush with the bevel. You can have a small friction fit piece of wood on the end to keep your things from falling out.

Hollowed out pens and markers work very well, as do other small and normally sealed items - Batteries, lightbulbs, empty aerosol cans, and whatnot. I know someone who keeps a considerable stash inside a slightly modified aluminum baseball bat.

When I was a wee lad and had all manner of illogical restrictions imposed on me by my mother I kept a small weapons stash inside my computer. I fitted the side panel with a quick release mechanism and built a set of pouches onto the inside of it. I also had a small coathanger wire rig to hold my pellet gun horizontally. On a whim and under the influence of complete and total boredom I drilled a .22 inch hole in the metal back of the computer that lined up with the muzzle of the gun perfectly. The gun was held sideways between the expansion slots and processor assembly, and could be fired while it was still in the computer by reaching in through an empty drive bay (after removing the cover, of course).

Now that was pointless.

~Zero
