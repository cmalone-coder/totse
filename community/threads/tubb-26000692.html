ether, whether it be petroleum or ethyll, is extremely dangerous. the vapour released in the noxious fumes is far heavier than air, and will leech on to floors and walls. this vapour is explosively flammable, and any source of heat, even an electric heater, or any form of spark will result in the fumes and ether source combusting explosively. when working with ether, work on a day under 25 celcius, in a reasonably moist environment, far away from any heat source. if a heat source must be used in an experiment invloving ether, wait a few hours before lighting things or using a hotplate. this should help erduce accident risks.

The best way to do it is to make an Ice Bath first place your flask in it and then mix in RP/E then add I2... then place a reflux condenser. Let it set in the Ice bath for 25-30 minutes. Then remove it and let it set at room temp untill you notice that the reaction had stopped (usually another 20-25 minutes for swim). Then you add it to heat. Starting with a temp of around 125... then slowly raise it to 170 over a time of 1 hour. Then you get a nice liquid, after filtered you can still reuse the RP, but you end up with a nice clean clear liquid. What yields do you get with your method?
