The 'Official' Recipe thread.
I figured it'd be a good idea to make a thread completely dedicated to recipes (perhaps make it a sticky?). This is where we can share our favorite recipes that contribute to good eating and proper fueling for whatever Athletics we embark upon. I'll share a couple for now and post some more when I can be bothered!


Guilt Free Hamburgers:

1/4 lb of 99% lean ground beef or ground turkey patties (or if you prefer hand-rolling your burgers you'll need a couple of eggs or whatever you use to hold the meat together, I use eggs because there's nothing wrong with them).
whole wheat bun
1/2 cup or so of mixed veggies (I prefer bell peppers, zucchini, perhaps squash, onions)
1 teaspoon either steak sauce or BBQ sauce
Dash of pepper
Dash of salt

You can cook the burgers any way BUT frying. Foreman grill, open-flame grill, or even broiler if you don't have grill access. Broilers are underestimated a lot. If you use one of those broiler pans where the fat drips into a lower pan you can eliminate some of the fat from the meat. Though ground turkey won't have a lot to begin with. You can either marinade the meat in the sauce or add while cooking, doesn't really matter but I prefer marinading overnight. Cook to your preference of tenderness.

Sautee the vegetables to preferred tenderness.

Toast the buns if you like.

When you top the burgers with the vegetables, done just right, the consistency of the veggies will resemble cheese, thus, you won't miss cheese at all.


Flapjacks for MASS:

12 egg-whites and two whole eggs.
1 cup of oatmeal.
Couple drips of vanilla extract.
Couple dashes of cinnamon (you can use a good amount, cinnamon is actually pretty healthy)

Throw it all in a bowl, whip into batter.

Pour into skillet (use PAM)

Makes two 7-inch pancakes.

Top with Splenda.

Sentinel's Quick-n-Dirty Carb Bowl

Make a two cups of rice (usually this means starting with one cup of dry rice, as it expands), either the "real way" or just using instant rice. Chop up whatever vegetables you have in your fridge (bell peppers, pea pods, carrots, et cetera) and mix it with the rice. Put on a bit of velveta cheese and pop it in the microwave for 40-60 seconds. Done! You can also add hamburger or turkey too. If you can't be arsed to cook any meat, some chopped onion will add some flavor. It's more or less a whole meal; just add some milk or whatever. I've also eaten it in a tortilla.

jacked-up noodles

Fill a pot about 3 inches up with water, and make it a little bit salty.

Add a few cloves of rough-cut garlic, and a big ol' pinch of loose green tea leaves (recommended gunpowder green) and a little bit of red pepper flake.

Bring that to a boil and ribbon some ginger into little bits. When boiling add noodles (soba are good, they make it clumpy and sticky at the end which I like) and the ginger bits simultaneously.

About a minute in to the boiling, add some pizza-style sliced mushrooms (cremini, anyone?) [optional add a big squirt of nuoc mam/fish sauce].

When the noodles are almost done, add some more garlic in the form of the dried granulated bits in a big pinch. Shit should be smellin' like a felon.

When noodles are cooked drain by using a pan lid and tilting carefully, then turn over the noodles a bit by flopping them in the air- this will dry them and cool them a bit making them slightly congeal together.

Dump in a bowl and add a dash of either A. olive oil or B. tamari depending on how you feel.

Finish with cracked black pepper over the top and some parsely if you are an appearance freak. A little sprinkle of salt over the top never hurts too.


These noodles clear you nose (depending on red pepper added), make you horny, and wake you the hell up due to the green tea absorbed in the noodles. Now tell me all of those things aren't good.
