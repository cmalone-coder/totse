This is for all you atheists, agnostics and multi-religious people.

Let's say you had to pick a religion to stick to for the rest of your life. You'd have to follow any tenets, holidays, traditions and all the crap that comes with it. You'd have to be an active member of the religion, for example, should you pick catholicism, you'd have to go to church every Sunday, follow the commandments strictly and all that. You'd also have to at least pretend that you believe in the religion you pick.

What would you pick?

Atheism and agnosticism are beliefs and not religions, and there will be no discussion on that matter in this thread.



Personally, I'd probably follow the Necronomicon, just because it seems fun. That may be a stupid choice with a stupid reason, but that suits me well, as I'm a stupid person.
