Re: Legal Highs
There are many "legal highs" and they come in all forms, ranging from smokeable herbs, to "organic ecstasy" pills.

From my experience, most of the legal highs don't do shit, or if they do, they're only marginally cheaper than real drugs, and aren't worth it.

Damiana is a common one down in my area, and I've gotten some very mild, chilled-out effects from it, but you're better off buying weed. Kava Kava is enjoyable, to a degree, but it tastes like ass. All the organic ecstasy I've bought has just been 5-htp mixed-in with a few other herbs and vitamins, and it does effect dopamine and serotonin, etc., but it's certainly no substitute for MDMA.

Some of the legal highs are worth a shot, but really, if you want a legal high, drink booze.
