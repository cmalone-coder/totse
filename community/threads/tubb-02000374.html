Before you lash out at a particular group of people, you should educate yourself on why they take the actions that they do. When it comes to logging, most activists understand the necessity of cutting down trees. Although there are also reasons to oppose it. Some of the reasons to oppose logging in a certain area:

-The cutting of trees in a area may do damage to the local ecosystem.

-Unnecessary urban sprawl.

-Opposition to bio-engineered trees.

There are other reasons in which activists may oppose the logging of trees. The point is, the stereotypical view of people chaining themselves to trees just to save the tree from being harvested is foolish.

Self-preservation is what motivates them to take increasingly antagonistic positions that are carefully manipulated to destroy any possibility of consensus, cooperation, or a lasting solution. In the case of forestry their campaigns are largely waged in Europe, as far from the actual scene as possible to give them control of the facts and nullify any opposition, and because they know local populations are too close to the truth to accept their bullshit tales of horror in the woods. I could wish that they hadn't chosen the effort to clean up humanity's act to corrupt, but that wouldn't change anything.
The activities I witnessed took place in an area where the truth was right before their eyes, which means that honesty is not something they take very seriously. My advice is to just see them for what they are and try to avoid thinking that those whose efforts are sincere deserve the same contempt. One day those same people might realize that a good way to clean up the environment would be to dispose of environmentalists.
