Maybe I'm Just Being 1.OLD and 2. Anal?
But youngest son has been at catering college now fulltime since September and hasn't been taught how to hold a knife or cut julienne/brunois!

I know this because he went for a partime job interview at the local Restaurant this weekend and while he got the part time commis, one of the things the Chef Patron did say to him was that he was obviously going to have to teach him how to cut and chop. Youngest dutifully reported this to me afterwards.

Teach him how to handle a knife??! After 6 weeks of catering school?????!!!

So this morning I asked youngest to show me how he holds a knife.....

It was appalling. My mother in law has better technique than that!

I showed him how to do it and explained to him that 1. there is a reason why chefs hold their knives the way the do and 2. that the first thing any prospective boss will see is that he has NOT been trained properly if he holds knife with a noddy or housewife grip.

Back in the day just about the first thing students were taught was basic knife handling skills. And unless kitchens have changed a lot since my day then the a major part of a commis prep time is still mirepoix?

Is it just my son's college or do all catering schools now not teach such fundamental skills??!

Now, son is going to be working in an Italian kitchen and they probably won't call it brunois and they will no doubt call 'mirepoix ' making da sufrito' or whatever the fuck the italian for it is but the skills and technique are the same!
