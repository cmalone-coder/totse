Re: making crack and bagging up
Take 7 grams of coke and into that mix 1 gram of baking soda. Put it into a baby food jar or such and heat it slowly! Very slowly!!! Oh yeah-DUH- add enough water to break it all down. Open flame will work, but I personally believe that if you set a pan of water to boil then make something that will allow you to dip the little jar into the boiling water it works much better. That way you cant possibly burn your shit up--know what I mean? When you see the layers start to seperate give it a few more seconds, then dribble some ice cold water down in there and the crack will begin to form. now stick your metal stirrer down in there and it will collect onto it. At this point, you can even drop an ice cube down in the jar itself to sped up the cooling process. Voila!!!! CRACK. Or you can just go get some cheap HOUSEHOLD AMMONIA that has NO detergent in it. It'll say right on the jug if it does. If you line near a FAMILY DOLLAR. Their store brand works fine. Anyway--- Get a mason jar or something of that approx. size and fill it probably half-way with ammonia, then just dump your coke in there and start stirring[no baking soda this way] Have a glass of ice cold water right there next to ya. When stuff stops forming to stirrer, then pull it out, if it still mact kinda gooey then put back in ammonia for a few more stirs, then pull out and stir around int he water to get the ammonia off of it. You can actually sniff the rock and if you still smell ammonia then back in the water a bit. Hope this helps ya
