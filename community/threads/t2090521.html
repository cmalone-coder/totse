I don't think so, as long as you don't do anything stupid while you're actually on them... I've been taking various benzos for about 3 months now and haven't noticed any change in my memory or learning ability.

That's what happens to me anyway, so now I stick to Xanax and Dormicum (shorter half-lives, Dormicum in particular).

I'm pretty sure that any changes in brain chemistry resulting from the use of benzodiazepines would be rescinded within a few months of cessation. The brain has an amazing ability to recuperate from drug induced changes, and I don't think benzos are that hard on it.

I know that benzos cause some amnesia, and can cause clinical depression, but as for actually killing brain cells, thats terribly unlikely. Few drugs do that, and no prescription that I'm aware of is capable of that at the indicated dosage (of course, the fact that you're on this forum indicates that you are likely to be abusing/misusing them).
