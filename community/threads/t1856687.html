I wonder if a 5 or 6 hour reflux was stopped and worked up, and the result ran through a 2 hour dry push/pull would give the same results as a long reflux. Premake HI using another method other than with rp, and use it to reflux your pseudo in with only a small amount (like maybe 1/10th) of rp. Then work-up, and do a dry p/p with the rest of the rp. It's worth some looking into, cause you may produce the results pf 12 or 24 hour refluxes in shorter time.
