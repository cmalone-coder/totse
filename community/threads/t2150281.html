Frozen pirogies. You can buy a big bulk bag of them for cheap and prepare them two ways, one good enough to serve to company and the other takes like ten minutes of sitting on your ass.

First way (this is how they tell you to make them on the back of most bags):

boil those suckers up for like tenish minutes, then cook them with some nice sauce and veggies.

Second way (lazy way I discovered, they turn out fully cooked, just not crispy and flavorful like the first way)

Put some hot tap water in a bowl and soak the little guys for like five minutes, until they're mushy and unfrozen. Take them out of the water and then microwave them for about three minutes. Then eat them with sour cream/mayo or whatever else you want. I personally like to coat them with super garlicy pesto.
