Re: "Mesh" and other measurements
molarity in acid refers to how many moles of solute are in a litre. A mole is a set amount for each element. A certain amount of grams will equal one mole. e.g. 12 grams is one mole of carbon. 14 grams is one mole of nitrogen. 1 molarity = 1 mol of solute in a litre of water.
