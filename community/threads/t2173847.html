Re: Halp?
Don't just use clothes - confident people alway's stand out no matter how shit their clothes are, use your body language, laugh, voice modulation, and eye gazing. A clean business shirt and good tie will always bring attention to people, but I know you're not just going to start suiting it up just yet. Infact theres many things you can do; always wear clean shoes, it will make you stick out like nothing else, especially white ones. Buy designer clothes; T-shirts, Polos, Jeans, Shoes, Jackets. You get what you pay for; people know money when they see it and a good brand will bring everyones attention. Sunglasses, not many people can pull off sunglasses, but if you can, good on you. A good watch - this will always make you stand out - I tend to wear big silver shiney watches with diamond studs, when the sun catches it... Fuck yeah, homes. Bracelets and necklaces, try everything to badazzle an outfit. Scarves, I don't know about you but when someone wearing a scarf walks past they grab my attention, I prefer silk scarves. Belt and earrings. Good colours that stick out, are of course; Red, Orange, Yellow, Blue, Green, Purple. Try not to over do it (too much, too bright) Try and find two peices of clothing with the same colour... example: Green tshirt and green sneaks, blue jeans. The hairs cool, man. Just try different styles and see what you like. You should first try shaving it off - That's what i've done and i'm keeping it. It's niceee.

- Diesel

Re: Halp?
Try wearing more button-up shirts and dress shirts. You can also experiment with polos. Get the ones that fit you nicely and are not overly fitted or too loose.

For the jeans, buy some Levi's 511s, the slim-fit ones.

Get some nice casual shoes like converse Jack Purcell, Lacoste Carnaby etc. The ones that look too athletic or running shoes should only be worn when you are training, going to a gym, sports etc.

Definitely get a nice watch. Try to avoid all those shiny big-looking gold and silver bracelets and necklaces.

Get a nice pea coat for the winter.
