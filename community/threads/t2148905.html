An upper versus a downer
In my experience I've found people that take uppers are much more edgy in general, and with that comes extreme emotions. Peaks of bliss, fits of anger, feelings of feelings that no one else can describe after being up on a several day coke binge. Meth, MDMA, even you adderall loving highschoolers and caffine/nicotine strung adults.

The people that indulge in downers seem much more down to earth, passive, and normally only show irritation when they feel they are upset. Downers can almost give you a outsiders perspective on yourself, not to mention many times a "better" high for the risks than uppers. As long as your not shooting smack, I feel its easier to carry on a opiate/benzo habit then a coke or meth one.

So BLTC, what the hell are you, and why? Or do you cross the line sometimes with both, or stray into the vast vast unknown of Psychedelics?
