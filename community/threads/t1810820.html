I'm an agnostic. I have an overwhelming feeling reached through all I've learned and experienced that there is indeed Something Going On. A Grand Design, if you will.

But exactly what, none of us can say. I don't believe that God writes books, or sends sons down to save us all, or that any religion thought up by humans has more than a few grains of truth to them. Religions I think are to be learned from, not taken as Gospel (no joke intended). Religion is an extremely useful tool, nothing more, nothing less.

Anyway... There is definitely Something Going On. What, exactly, is the mystery. Why is an even greater mystery.

There probably is a god, or a higher power, or a grand design, whatever you want to call it. But it also probably isn't like anything you'll learn about in religion in the year 2004 on earth.

My "higher power" is my consciousness. But it seems kind of silly and absurd to even call it that because it's not more powerful than me, and it's certainly not exalted to any kind of supremacy.

It only exists as long as my brain is functioning. It wasn't there before me and it will no longer be there when I cease to exist.

There is no separation point. It doesn't directly observe "me" and I do not directly observe "it". We are one in the same.

It's a beautiful thing, really.

It serves me as a "tool" only as long as I condition and exercise it. And it's there for my taking whenever I choose to utilize it. Without it, I would cease being. Without me, it would not matter. We're co-dependents. I refuse to acknowledge it as a god, and it refuses to take me seriously.

I don't blame "it" for all the injustices, suffering and strife in this world and it doesn't hold me accountable for having the reactions I do to all this excessive stimuli.

Did I mention that it was a beautiful thing?
