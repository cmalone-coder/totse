Re: Define PIRATE RADIO
How did the government come to run the airwaves? Well, back in 1909 when "San Jose Calling" first started broadcasting from San Jose, there were no rules. There was no government agency to oversee anything.

What happened is that lots of experimenters started operating radio stations. The known radio spectrum (in those days, about 800 to 1050 kHz on the AM band) filled up fast and the it became a sea of noise.

The federal government took over licensing of stations because it was clear that the people who ran radio stations weren't going to police themselves. So, by 1920, the Federal Radio Commission was in place.

The official philosophy stated in the Communications Act of 1934 is that the airwaves belong to the people and the goverment gives a license for a set period of time to allow a station to operate in the public's (1) interest, (2) convenience, or (3) necessity. When a license is renewed, a station must show that it has done this.
