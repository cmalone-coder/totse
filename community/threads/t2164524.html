Re: Looking like a drug addict who hasn't slept in days
Do drugs and don't sleep for days.

This would be the obvious way of doing things, but we're assuming you don't want to go down the method acting route, and are looking for a look which is swifter to acquire, and has fewer drawbacks.

To draw together what has already been said, what you neglect to do is more important than what you make a conscious effort to do or wear. For example, the longer you neglect all aspects of personal hygiene, the better, particularly not shaving, not washing or grooming your hair, and not washing your hands or under fingernails.

Unfortunately by far the best way to look sleep deprived is to be sleep deprived, it only need be one night of wakefulness before you need to affect the character to work wonders when combined with the above. Beware of using make up, though it can create a very convincing look on stage or on camera when applied by a skilled person, in closer range and in real life the means by which you acquired your ostensibly dishevelled state will be plain for all to see.

In terms of Clothing, it is the state and fit of your clothes that is important rather than the age or price. Remember that down and out types can come from all walks of life, and that a very good look can be achieved with more expensive clothes that have obviously been neglected and not cared for. Wearing something that is old and ill fitting can help to add the suggestion that you are wearing donated, stolen or the very cheapest of second hand apparel.
