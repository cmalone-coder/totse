Re: Music like this.. (i dunno what to call it)
That's stoner doom metal.

A few albums by Black Sabbath, in particular the Master of Reality, Vol 4 and Sabbath Bloody Sabbath albums.
Sleep, their Sleep's Holy Mountain and Dopesmoker albums are great, but Dopesmoker might be a bit hard to listen to though (1 hour long song that is incredibly fucking slow and heavy).
Bongzilla, I'd particularly reccomend the Amerijuanican album, then maybe the Stash album.
Weedeater, I'd proabably say their album God Luck And Good Speed, if you like that then check out their other 2, ...And Justice For Y'all and Sixteen Tons.
Down aren't too bad, kinda have a bluesy feel, but their newest album III: Over The Under is pretty fucking good, haven't really thought much about their others though.
Earthride have a kinda bluesy feel in their music as well, both their albums Taming of The Demons and Vampire Circus aren't bad, I haven't listened to them in ages though so I can't reccomend a particular album.
Om aren't too bad either, it's just a bass and drums, but they have some great stuff. I'd particularly reccomend the Conference of The Birds and Pilgrimage albums.
Spirit Caravan are another band, I've heard their first album Jug Fulla Sun and one of their EPs, Dreamwheel, I'd reccomend checking them out too.
