At festivals people sell balloons for like £2-3, and THAT is profiteering, but tbh i'd still pay it if I was fucked on E or K; so worth it!!

Get a proper whipped cream cracker anyway OP...

There's a bit of a "knack" to it...

1) Inflate the balloon before hand, to stretch it out and warm it up a bit. You can reuse balloons quite easily I find, but if you fill them too fast they "can" pop if the nitrous is mega cold, so warming 'em up a bit helps.

2) Put the 8g (or 16g) cartridge into the charger bit at the top, which should unscrew. Then just screw that on, firmly, you should hear the nitrous fill the charger, with a lovely sound. Wait a few seconds... then unscrew it again quickly. You should now have a "primed" charger full of nos.

3) Insert the balloon over the nozzle of the whipper carefully... if it's sharp, make sure you don't puncture the balloon and pop it/waste it. Best to hold it on with one hand, whilst inflating it with the other. Put the whole lot in one balloon, and take it off carefully, keeping it "pinched" so none escapes.

4) Balloon time! I find the best way is to take big deeeeep breaths, get your blood full of oxygen and your heart racin'. Breathe out most (but not all) of your air, and then inhale from the balloon. Don't just inhale and hold it, keep breathing it back into the balloon, inflating and deflating it, and keep doing this for a good 10 seconds at least. You will feel reality start to "pop" if ur doin it rite.... then stop, and get a nice breathe of fresh air.

5) Enjoy!!
