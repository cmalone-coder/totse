The often aggressive lyrical matter in that kind of music, usually calls for an equally aggressive vocal style.

Amon Amarth for example. I listen to them for the viking/battle imagery it offers. I interpret Johan Hegg (the vocalist) as some viking warchief telling his stories (the lyrics) over the crackle of the fire in the bitter winter wind (the wicked intrumentals).
If some soft-spoken singer were to do the same thing as Hegg, it wouldn't have the same effect. Even if he was replaced with Hetfield or Mustaine, it would be a shitty thing all together.

So, I wouldn't say that music you posted is ruined by the vocalist; it's made complete with the vocalist. You need to broaden your horizons or just stay the fuck away from true metal.
