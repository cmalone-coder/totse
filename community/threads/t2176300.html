Re: finding shrooms in Australia
In Victoria, mushrooms do not grow in cow dung.

Most common areas are landscaped garden beads. Tan bark once it's gone gray, lawns, etc.

The place where they grow most is in the forest, on natural mulch you'll find them, but THE BEST PLACE is Pine Forests. They're abundant, impossible to identify incorrectly, and potent as hell. Look for places where there'd be a lot of rain, but they're slightly covered. Like behind a tree, around fallen trees, etc.

In pineneedles they should have a thing, long stalk that's solid (not hollow), with a flat top as an adult and a dome when young. They bruise blue, and are usually already bruised. The top should be light tan/brown with a little 'nipple'.

Cut them off at ground level, don't rip them out. Leave the wee babies, so there will be more when you get back. People think that if you give a mushroom a good flick before hitting it some of it's spores fall out (thus more shrooms next rain). If it's true or not, it can't hurt to give it a little flick.
