To the right a little?

Or perhaps to the left.

Where do you stand in space and time, the answer is right here.

Life is entirely spent moving in and out of memories of a dream previously dreamt.

So what is your religious belief?

From what i have observed, there are several types of religion, that most people seem to join together like some kind of hybrid plant to make their own merge of beliefs, ideaology and individualism.
Of course these ideals change throughout an individuals life, but many of the fundamental ideas remain the same.
Old beliefs evovle to new ones for example when someone is young they may think they do what is right simply because it is right, or they may do what is wrong, simply to do what is wrong. As they grow and the mind ripens often this behaviour changes to a more formed attitude. A poor child may think that life has dealt them a rough hand, that society is against them and they feel rejected and therefore reject society aiming at a rebel non-conformist view of life. Conversely, this child, had it been the child who did the right thing, they may see life as a challenge and strive to succeed.
This is what an individual may think as a type one person.
A type 2 individual may see oppertunities at either face value and not read into the situation or they may see the opposite, reading into every situation and experience.

What has this got to do with religion?

Well religion is very closely associated withthe inner belief system, any critical decision made by an individual can gauge their religious beliefs, as religious beliefs are: "a system of belief in a spiritual, or supernatrual power."

Arguable, that this supernaturual power is within oneself, that the higher being we all search for is not just one's higher self, but a collaboration of all human-kinds higher self.
We look to dreams and premonitions for guidance, and the answer is within ourself.

Simple to come to this conclusion really, when you look at how many, catholics, for example, will not have read the bible, and their beliefs, while largely shaped around catholic framework and the ten commandments, will include their own idealology.
Their own moral value system, their own exceptions, and they will like any other religious member of any religion or denomonation, break these lawas that they hold so close. Why? its human nature.

From this assumption that religious people have their own religious, moral, and ethical belief system, it is viable to hypothosize that each and every individual sees their own higher knowledge, as such, (by this i mean a sense of something greater than the consious mind, intageble and difficult to reach through linear thought) and therefore, we are our own god, remember im speaking fundamentally here.

Because this belief in our higher self is fundamental, a catalyst is needed to express it, this catalyst is religion.
A set religion helps us act a certain way, while relying on others, humans need to rely on others, it brings a sense of belonging and also in turn a sense of feeling needed.

Different religions are but different elements of catalysts for this type of thoughts, but i am not saying that all people should be drawn to all religions, there are obviously significant cultural ties associated with different religoins, and the all important eastern vs western philosophy. By this easternVwestern i mean that few middle aged men who live the american dream would be all too quick to embrace buddhist spiritual laws.

A personal experience is probably suitable here, however im not going to recount my more personal thoughts on this topic as it would be rather contradicotry to what i have written above.
I welcome everybody who reads this however to post a personal experience or any kind of input on this topic

have a nice day
