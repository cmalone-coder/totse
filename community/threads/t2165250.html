Why I think people don't like immigrants/are racist
Typically, the immigrants are non-white. Non-whites have broader noses on average than whites. Broad noses are considered less cute/physically appealing than smaller noses. They also tend to be less able to elicit feelings of cuteness/protection in the observers (conversely, babies, who all have tiny noses, tend to elicit feelings of cuteness in observers who then wish to care for them). Therefore, it's understandable that compared to whites the non-whites tend to engender feelings of animosity more easily because they do not present emotionally appealing facial features (which tend to mitigate feelings of animosity).

Same thing can be said about their hair colour/skin tone. Darker hair and skin colour are associated with maturity in age (babies tend to have fairer hair and skin than their adult counterparts, and this is uniform across the races). Therefore, by virtue of possessing less than fair skin and pure black hair, the immigrants/non-whites fail to exhibit baby-like physical features and as a result of this, tend to encounter more animosity than they otherwise would have.
