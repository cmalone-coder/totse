Dead loved ones 'haunting' us...
Okies, before anyone asks why I didn't post this in paranormal forum I should point out that this isn't in any way about the supernatural. I stopped believing in ghosts about the same time i quit believing in other mythical things like Santa Claus,the Easter Bunny, honest politicians and girls on the interweb.

Nor am I am claiming any psychic ability..I have enough trouble dealing with the 'Quick' let alone the dead.

As some of you know i am doing some DIY at the moment. Its one of those long laborious jobs, which will take weeks to finish properly.

The longer I spend on it the more i can 'feel' my granddad's presence. Childhood memories (some I'd rather forget again) keep on surfacing and i have even found myself having mental 'conversations' with his shade.

You see, my granddad was an A1 arsehole. My sister, who is surprisingly literate for a librarian (users of public libraries will know what i mean), always describes him being surrounded by a distinct 'whiff of sulphur'. He was, among other things, a sadist and a deviant...who ruined not only my mother's childhood but mine too.

However he was a truly gifted craftsman and taught me (usually using annoyingly smug homilies) a lot about woodwork and tools in general.

Soooo now i find myself wiping down tools before i return them to the hire shop and can feel him nodding on approvingly and I hear him say 'keep your tools clean and sharp and you'll never lack for custom'.

Or i think about cutting a corner , not doing a bit that no one will ever see or care about and I suddenly have a conversation with him:

Me: "Gan-Gan I'm gonna leave this bit, no one will ever know"

Him:"Boo(my childhood nickname!)you'll know...take pride in your work boy"



Anyone else get guidance and 'help' from departed family members, dead partners?
