Re: Marriage.
A lot of people here have the misconceived idea that a prenup will save their ass in court. They are wrong for several reasons:
1) a prenup only covers assets that the person has before the marriage.
2) a prenup cannot stipulate child support or wage garnishment or alimony payments - those are all products of the marriage and as such if you are not married you cannot stipulate them.
3) any bank account you ever withdraw from - even once - to buy something for the marriage, immediately becomes joint marital property, and as such the wife is entitled to half.
4) the house is joint marital property, so she gets half.

So basically, anything you have going in is safe, unless you do something stupid like put all your money into one account, or withdraw from your account to buy shit "for the marriage" which can be anything - a candy bar, toilet paper, or paint for the house.

So there you go. Prenups will not save your ass. If you're rich or have significant assets then it's the way to go. Otherwise it's pointless. Just don't get married and you can't get divorced. "Don't get divorced" is stupid advice. You never know if your spouse will cheat on you or just become completely intolerable. The best option is to simply not marry.
