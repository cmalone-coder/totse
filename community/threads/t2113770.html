Beryl is officially deprecate; they merged back with Compiz to form Compiz Fusion. You should be installing that.

It's pretty stable, provides very nice eye candy (very good for conversions, aero looks like a dung heap compared to it), and can run on fairly low-end hardware. Your ram is more than high enough to not feel anything (compiz uses about 50M or so of RAM, emerald uses usually <10). You'll need non-free binary drivers to support it, but if you already have that, installation should be painless.
