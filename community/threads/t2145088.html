Computer batteries generally won't charge from about 95% up. That's standard, with all batteries, to prevent the battery from constantly having to top itself up, as they run out slowly through self-discharge. If you need to charge it let it run down to 93-94% and then plug it in.

My battery seems to always be on 99% full.
