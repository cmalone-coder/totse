Re: Problems with PH and E.C. for the T.H.C.
First off I've found that General Hyro is shit. Do they still use a series of 3 different solutions? You can't always go with the the cheapest stuff.

You don't need to use distilled water. You need to regulate it's pH using either pH up (base) or pH down (acid) for the plants TO BE ABLE to take in nutrients. Optimal pH in the nutrient solution should be about 6.5.

Rockwool substrate will slowly turn acidic because of nutrient build-up, so I's advise you to tear off the paper around the Rockwool and then bury it in Geolite substrate. Those roots need to be set free to obtain more oxygen when the plant gets a little bigger. You will litterally drown your plant if the roots can't get enough oxygen.

So yes, answering your question.. proper pH of the nutrient solution is critical. Buy a pH test kit and either acid or base depending on the current pH of your local water supply.
