Re: A couple of questions about addiction...
Any psychoactive drug (and many non-psych meds) can be 'addictive'. There are many different definitions for addiction, but the one I find most useful is to examine the pattern of drug use. If using is compulsive in nature (you may not feel like doing it but can't stop), if your thoughts are constantly either modified or focused on the drug and how much is left and when you can use again and where to get more, well, then you're addicted in all but the most technical terms. A drug needn't cause tolerance nor produce a pronounced physical withdrawl effect (these are older criteria for addictiveness) for it to be addictive. Hell, I'm addicted to the internet. So ANYTHING can be 'addicting' when defined according to the behaviour and attitudes surrounding the use of the drug (or technology, or gambling etc.) in question. Take it or leave it? Not addicted. Can't say no even when saying yes causes problems? Addicted. No addiction is desirable, but some are safer than others. An obsession for the Simpsons will probably affect your life and health a lot less than an addiction to heroin for example. This is known as 'harm reduction'. The only REAL safety lies in abstinance.
