Re: Is weed really that bad for your lungs?
As far as lung effects go, if you exclusively smoke weed, you probably won't get cancer. This doesn't mean that you won't be coughing/wheezing though.

It can be a big help when you're sick, but I think it depends on the strain. Smoking a strain with depressant effects is great for laying around and recuperating. It provides some mild analgesia as well. However, a strain with predominating stimulant effects is the last thing you want when you're sick. I smoked one of these "speedy" strains when I had a cold once, and I was immediately motivated to do a shitload of physical activity. Needless to say, this wasn't too beneficial for my cold.

Quote:
I think the guys over at MAPS actually proved that bongs are the worst ways to get the waccytobaccy from the weed into your brain.
The 2000 MAPS-NORML study didn't evaluate traditional unfiltered smoking methods. So even if bongs do filter out more more THC than tars, I'd be willing to bet joints would filter out less tars, even if they did deliver more THC. So while a joint might theoretically get you higher, it would be worse for your lungs. Also, I honestly think bongs get you higher, and I'm basing that on a lot of experience, not just because they look so cool the first time you use them. However, even if they do get you less high, they conserve more weed so you're getting less high with less weed instead of wasting more of it in a joint. Seems like it balances out to me.

Also, don't forget that water-filtered tobacco smoke puts you at a lower risk for carcinoma, and here's another source stating that water actually does help filter out toxins. http://www.ukcia.org/research/Effect...uanaSmoke.html

I absolutely agree that vaping or eating would be safer for you overall. However, if you're going to actually smoke weed, a bong is the healthiest option (although that doesn't necessarily mean they're healthy.)
