Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Re: tennis ball launcher
Ah, another Anarchy Cookbook reader.

You can't make soda can tennis ball launchers anymore. They don't make the rolled steel cans anymore, so finding the parts will be a bit tricky.

What you CAN do, though, is build one from PVC. Just make a basic potato cannon with some 2.5 inch schedule 40 PVC for the barrel and tennis balls should fit perfectly.

If 2.5 inch schedule 40 PVC pipe isn't available in your area you can use 2.5 inch sch 40 conduit. With the pressures generated a hairspray combustion cannon it should be quite safe... Provided you aren't the one getting shot with it.

Re: tennis ball launcher
The amount of fuel you use depends on your chamber size. I have a 3x11" (or thereabouts) chamber on my golfball cannon, and I use exactly three seconds' worth of hairspray in it. In general, if your cannon won't fire you probably used too much fuel. If it doesn't fire very hard you may have used too little. Experiment until you get it right, then write it down...

Static isn't a concern at all. These things are hard enough to get to fire when you want them to - I've never heard of let alone experienced an accidental ignition.

~Zero the Inestimable
