Better Living Through Everything. Not only the internet and drugs.

This is my last post, so here are the only words of wisdom that I can come up with right now:

Don't over indulge, but at the same don't under indulge.

Don't base yourself around drugs, and don't base yourself around the Societies and Cultures that you are forced to live with.

Base yourself.
