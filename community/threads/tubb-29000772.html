Re: Real Gernade?
A real grenade has a green handle, not a blue one. This is not to say that somebody has not rigged a trainer to go off like a real one. I would procede with caution if I were you. Carefuly unscrew the fuze body assembly and pull it out of the grenade body. If there is 1 1/2" of metal cylinder sticking out of the fuze body it is not live, however if there is a shiny cylinder extending further you have an armed fuze body at least.
