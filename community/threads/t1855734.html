hi angel--just some thoughts on your question: what is the temp of canned air....---the temp of canned air is ambient(the same as the enviroment it is in)--when you finsh a dive and go into a decompression chamber, they pump in gas to increase the pressure to first decompression stop (wether that be 110feet through to 10feet)this gas pumped in is quite warm(lovely after a long cold dive)and it is warm because pressure= temperature----increasing pressure will increase temperature--stop increasing and the temperature will fall back slowly to ambient----the reverse is true--let the gas, under pressure, escape rapidly and there is a sudden decrease in temperature---pressure going in creates heat--pressure going out creates cold--it isnt stored at that temp--its created by movement---hope that helps angel---good luck
