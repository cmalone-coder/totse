Re: slavia questions
Yeah, you can chew the leaves. Put them under your tongue or between your gums and your cheek and let that shit sit there for like a half hour.

But you first have to activate the chemicals in the salvia that get you high. To do so, you have to get your hands on both Cypermethrin and Imiprothrin, which can be found in pretty much any general/grocery/home improvement store. It comes as Raid. This is what you do:

1. Lay your salvia leaves out on a baking sheet.
2. Spray with Raid.
3. Heat oven to 125 degrees and put the salvia in. Let sit for 8 - 10 minutes, but no more. You don't want them to burn.
4. Take them out of the oven and let them cool off.
5. Now you're ready to trip balls!

Re: slavia questions
Salvia is not really a recreational drug so to speak. The high that it produces when smoking is extremely intense and most of the time isn't very pleasant.

DO NOT underestimate the potency of salvia, its not like weed were you can toke when your driving or about to talk to your boss or Parents shortly after. It will totally immobilize you if you do it properly.

Each trip varies greatly from dosage, to potency, to person, etc, so its hard to make a general description. Some people say they felt like they were going to die, some users have noted a feeling of being wrapped up by some external force. Some black out completely.

My trip was crazy cause after I did it I swore that I was awake and had my eyes open perceiving everything that was going around me. But from everyone in the room I had apparently blacked out. So my mind was constructing these visuals that were so detailed I thought I was still conscious and functioning.

Another time I did it I had a visual of a 3D cube that was made up of millions of smaller cubes all packed together. And I put my hand up to touch it and the surface had like a membrane coating and as I pushed my hand further into the visual I "popped' the outer layer and the smaller cubes burst out completely dismantling itself from its original form (One large cube). It was beautiful on such an indescribable level, so detailed and real.

To smoke it properly I suggest a bong or steamroller, take about 2-3 huge hits, and hold them in for like 30 seconds. That is if you have an extract. The foliage itself might take a little more.
