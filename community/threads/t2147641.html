Re: Cleaning up..
If you want to give the Suboxone thing a try its not hard to do at all, you just cant really just go to the doctor you normally see b/c docs have to have a special certification (they have to take a class to get it) in order to be allowed to prescribe Suboxone. You can find a doctor that has this certification easily through the Suboxone website: suboxone.com

You just type in your zip code and how far around you want to look for doctors with the certification to prescribe the Subs. Remember though, it'll be expensive as hell for a prescription if you dont have insurance. For me, even with insurance 30 Subs had a copay of 30 bucks, and it would've been 300 without insurance (plus the cost for the doctor). Plus the withdrawal from Suboxone is longer (and thus worse for most people) than withdrawal for short-acting opiates like heroin and oxy. Methadone has the worst withdrawal of all. But you gotta do what you gotta do.

You dont have to go just cold turkey though you know. There's lots of meds (nonopiate meds) that make withdrawal easier, sedatives like Valium/Xanax/Klonopin, weed, and alcohol (helped me a ton, getting hammered all day every day can be nice). Stimulants can also help with the emotional side of things. "Replacement therapy" works well when the drugs doing the replacing arent physically addictive (like stimulants and weed) or at least not as physically addictive (like the sedatives and alcohol) as the drugs being replaced. And in the end if you end up just switching from one addiction to another, at least being addicted to other stuff isnt as bad, you're not gonna get muscleaches and hot-cold sweats from a day of missing your doses.

The best detox regimen I think is decreasing your opiate dose by 1/3 every 3 days. You could go slower of course. And while you're decreasing your doses, drink alcohol and/or smoke weed and/or take other things to help you fill that void that the opiate high was filling for you. So you wanted to go slower than 1/3 every 3 days you could do something like go down from 60 mg oxy/day to 50 mg/day for 3 days (while taking other stuff), then after 3 days take the dose down to 40..etc. I think its sometimes better to do it faster that that slowly b/c you just get ansy and upset, and while other shit helps out it still doesnt replace that nice blissful high.

If you did like 60 mg/day ---> 40 mg/day (3 days) ---> 25-30 mg/day ----> 15-20 mg/day ---> 10-15 mg/day ....etc then that would work within 2-3 weeks and might have a good chance of working.
