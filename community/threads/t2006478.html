The No4 Enfields have great sights, but I'm not so fond of those on the SMLE in comparison.

too many guns, too little money.

Since you said this is your 1st and only rifle for 2-3 years (and it has to be one of these 2) get the Enfield.

Surplus ammo is almost always reliable and safe for a bolt gun. SOMETIMES cheap and/or corrosive, requiring careful cleaning. The biggest problem with surp ammo is availibility. I once bought 10K (308 South African surplus) from that place (aimsuplus) at ~ <14 cents a round. After I shot it up, I looked for more, but there is NO .308 surplus anymore (unless you cound that Indian garbage or stuff hoarders sell at gunshows for ~50 cents+ per round.)
It's not the end of the world, commercial 308 ammo is everywhere---even walmart. But 8mm Mauser, once it's surplus is gone..... Never buy a gun and think surplus will always be available and dirt cheap. Surplus is just that, surplus; extra ammo being sold off for a low price. Buy your rifle with the knowledge of the cost of the commercial ammo you'll be using when the surplus stuff runs out.


As far as which rifle will still be available.....Well, both will be available somewhere, but there are many more 8mm Mausers, than Enfields converted to 308 win (probably a 1000:1 ratio), so Mausers SHOULD be cheaper assuming similar condition.
