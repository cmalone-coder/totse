Re: Shure SE310 Earphones?
Earbuds are so fucking bad for your ears. They damage them alot. Get over-ear headphones. Don't be a bitch

They're in-ear canalphones, which tend to do a lot less damage to your ears than over-ear phones because they (good quality units, ie. Shure, Etymotic, UE etc) tend to seal and cancel external sound very well, requiring much lower operating volumes for high-quality output. The only over-ear units that can compare are the closed and sealed type, but the seals on those either have to match your facial contours or need to be very thick and foamy. Sure, in the end, high-end cans beat everything else out for sound quality, but they'll set you back a lot more and unless you're using them in a quiet environment, are more likely to lead to hearing damage.

That and you don't look like a moron walking down the street with a massive pair of cans. Bitch.
