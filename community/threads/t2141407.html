Re: a question on dope addiction..
The worst of the withdrawals should only last 3-5 days, maybe a week but I would think not, more like 3 days. After that 72 hour hump you should still be feeling kind of off, like post withdrawal for a few more days. I would if I were you taper down how much you're using, like from 6-8 times/day (if you do that much) down to 4 times a day at first, when you wake up, after you eat breakfast/lunch, then around dinnertime if you need it to help you eat, then again at bedtime so you can get some sleep. Do that for 3 days, then cut it down to 3 times a day, or 4 times with 2 large doses an 2 small doses around mealtimes if you need it to eat (dont get high around when you need to eat). Again do that for 3 days, then try to cut it down to twice a day, after you wake up and at bedtime, and when you've cut down to this point you will def. feel some discomfort during the day (if you havent begun to in the previous tapers) and it'll be hard to not get high during the day. Use a little just to make the withdrawals milder during the day but DO NOT get high during the day b/c you'll throw away all the progress you've made. Do this 2x dosage/day for 3 days, then you should be more or less stable an not withdrawing on day 3. You might not want to quit at this point b/c you'll feel you've made enough progress. Thats your choice of course but that progress is an illusion - you wont be able to keep on using only twice a day an getting high both times. When you are down to twice a day start to reduce how much you use in the morning, with the goal being to only use enough to not withdraw, not to get high. Remember you can still get high at night, but not twice a day. Once you start to decrease your morning dose you'll see yourself needing less at night to get high. Using just a little to take the edge off in the morning and more at night is a good jumping off point for cold turkey that wont leave you feeling like shit. You can easily amaze yourself when using 2x/day b/c of how little dope you'll need an how much you'll feel it.

Re: a question on dope addiction..
Benzos will help a lot with the psychological aspect of it, ie. irritability, insomnia, anxiety etc. etc.

If you can get Klonopin or Valium that'd be more ideal than Xanax IMO. Xanax will work, too, but it just doesn't last long enough, and you're going to be stuck multi-dosing and dealing with rebound anxiety when you misjudge your re-dose. A solid K-pin or Valium dose will tide you over most of the day, and it's a lot smoother of a "come-down".

Alcohol will help the same as benzos, of course. Again, long-acting benzos hold the benefit of duration.

Benzos will help you out a lot at getting rid of the sweating and hot/cold flashes.
