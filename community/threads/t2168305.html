Re: lsd has given me Depersonalization disorder
Psychedelics made me feel very alienated and cold around other people for 1-2 years... the only times I felt truly at ease was when I was alone and doing the things I enjoyed. I feel that it had a lot to do with tripping alone. When I tripped alone, I felt like I couldn't relate to anybody for the next week. Everything in my head was unexplainable and I felt it hard to talk to people because my head was in a totally different place. But when I tripped with other people more often and talked to them for most of the trip, I would feel much warmer to the entire world the next day. All that peace, love and happiness stuff. When I trip alone, that's how I feel. Alone in a strange, unexplainable world. And that feeling sticks with me for a long time. But when I trip with others, the feeling is that I'm not alone. The great feelings I'm having can be shared with other people having great feelings. Or we can work out bad trips together and turn them into good ones.

The nature of your trip will influence the nature of your life, in my experience. Each trip should be a ritual. You need to share the ritual with other people, do something special, and take something out of it.
