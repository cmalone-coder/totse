Back to practicality: You will notice that ink tags have a large side and a small side. The small side is usually round, usually on the inside of the garment, and usually does not contain ink. Bring a small (sharp) knife with you and cut a slit starting at the post on the ink tag and extending the length of the smaller side of the tag itself. You can then slip it out like a button from a buttonhole on your shirt. Go home and sew up your "modifictaion" to the garment.

Also be sure to drop the inktag in someone's shopping bag so it sets of the alarm when they leave the store...
