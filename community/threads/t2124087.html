Not everyone has time to learn the ins and outs of linux. some people just want an OS that works, even if it does some things for you or dumbs it down a bit. Who cares if (some) of it's users ask "stupid" questions? The more people using linux the better driver support, and the better chance of getting games ported to linux.

Honestly, this just smacks of elitist bollocks. Like linux is some sort of exclusive club and you must have x amount of computer knowledge to use it. And a lot of people resent that less computer illiterate people are jumping on board.

And finally, distro bashing is pointless. It's all linux, same kernel, you can (in most cases) get the same apps, and you can configure/hack it however the hell you want. Who cares?
