Re: Drumming
Rudiments are usually very important. Basic exercises like playing sixteenth notes to a metronome are vastly important to becoming a good drummer. Rudiments will improve your capacity to keep a steady beat; I hear drummers that can�t keep a steady beat way too often.

There are many very common rudiments; I would recommend buying a metronome if you don�t already have one. Practice keeping very steady sixteenth notes to the metronome beat. Put a slight accent on the first not of every set. (For the rest of my suggestion L=left hand; R=right hand.) The paradiddle, when even at a high speed is impressive in itself: LRLL, RLRR, LRLL, ect. (The strokes are done in 16th notes.)

Remember: keeping the notes stead is more important than speed; speed comes with practice.

There are many rudiments for the set as well. (Search for them at Google.)
