Re: Emo
It's both a type of music and an aesthetic.
The music started in the early 80s and was an offshoot of hardcore music, and has slowly evolved in to some of the most obnoxious whiney drivel you will ever hear.
The look typically involves guys in really tight jeans, black rimmed glasses, hair hanging over their eyes, a messenger bag and a t-shirt of either one of their favorite bands or something "ironically retro."

Edit: I should add that the main facet of emo everyone knows it for is how they seem to glorify how depressed and unloved they are, but just come across as whiney little bitches to anyone who isn't also emo.
