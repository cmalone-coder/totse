Re: What is the best weed you have purchased or smoked?
The best weed I ever smoked was called "weed". It was the first and only weed that was just called "weed" and not "dank" or "dro" or "killer shit" or "white rino" or "white widow" or "purple haze" or "amsterdam" or "skunk" or <insert overflattering name here>.
It was green. It didn't have a shitload of white or yellow "hairs" like the usual good shit. In fact, it was a really light green. It didn't even have red hairs.
My dealer just said "ok... I know you're going to think I ripped you off but I assure you that this is DEFINATELY a whole gram." and to be honest, I seriously thought he ripped be off so I asked him if I could put it in his scale... it was smaller than any half-gram that I've ever seen in my life. In fact, the whole gram was exactly enough to fit an average metal pipe bowl.
Oddly enough, his scale read "1.00g" so I tried to ignore the possibility that his scale might be fucked up and took it home, knowing that if I complained then he'd give me a little bit more to compensate. I put it on my scale and it also read "1.00g"... I thought I fell into the twilight zone or something... there's no way this pathetically small piece could possibly weigh a gram.
So, I broke it into three pieces, 0.33g each, put one of those pieces into my pipe... I got like 2 or 3 tokes off of it... and suddenly I find myself not only higher than a kite, but I guess I was in orbit around the earth... at the time I had a pretty good tolerance to weed too.
I almost shit myself.
It was not only the strongest buzz I've ever had, it was also the most pleasant and forgiving buzz.
That kind of weed never made it to my city again.
