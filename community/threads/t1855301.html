How to Make Anhydrous -- Very Easy!!!
How to Make Anhydrous Ammonia from Ammonium Nitrate Fertilizer by Lord Mighty

-------------------------------------------



Materials:

----------

1 2.5 Gallon Gas Can

4 ft. of 1/4 in. rubber/plastic hose

1 32 oz. Gatorade Bottle

1 Cooler

Ductape or Electrical Tape

2 cups Ammonium Nitrate Fertilizer

1 Bottle Red Devil Lye [sodium hydroxide]

1/2 bottle water [red devil bottle]

dry ice



Procedure

---------

1) Take the top off the gas can and tape one end of the hose to it. Get the cap off the gatorade bottle and poke a hole the top big enough to put the other end of the hose in it. Tape around it to make it air-tight.

2) Wrap the hose around the gatorade bottle 1 time counter-clockwise ad secure it to the side with the tape.

3) Fill your cooler with dry ice and sit the gatorade bottle (with the hose attached to it) in it.

4) Pour the 2 cups of Ammonium Nitrate fertilizer into the gas can. Pour the bottle ofd Lye into the gas can. Fill the Lye bottle half-way with water and pour that into the gas can.

5) Quickly screw the top onto the gas can, because it will start shooting out gaseous ammonia.

6) Shake the gas can for about 2 minutes and sit it down.

7) As the gas cools in the gatorade bottle, it will turn into a liquid. This is anhydrous ammonia.

8) Release the pressure for about 10 seconds every few minutes using the valve on the gas can so it doesn't explode.

9) Now you can cook your dope with this just like the juice that comes from cotton farms.
