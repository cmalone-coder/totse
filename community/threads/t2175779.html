Re: How do I begin ?
About 8 months ago I was in roughly the same position as you.

Do you have any attraction to any specific path, or any specific part of the occult? Are you just interested in theology and mythology or are you interested in becoming a practicing magician. Do you want to use magic more enlightenment or physical gain? Are you in any religion?

Anyways, get ready to study your ass off.

This (http://www.occultforums.com/) forum is a wealth of information. Please don't go there and ask the exact same question, take the time to look around and use the search function.


I found from my studies that Hermetics and Kabbalah resonate the best with me, you might prefer something like Yoga, Witchcraft, Chaos, or Satanism.

I also have some book suggestions for you, these are just off the top of my head and are in no particular order- Chicken Qaballah by Lon Milo Duquette, Garden of Pomegranates by Israel Regardie, Modern Magick by Donald Michael Kraig, Initiation into Hermetics by Franz Bardon, Summoning Spirits by Konstantinos, Astral Dynamics by by Robert Bruce, The Magus by Francis Barret, The Book of Law by Aleister Crowley.

Modern Magick and Initiation into Hermetics are both great places to start, but it is very very important that you read just about everything you can get your hands on.

For Shamanism Armsmerchant suggests The Way of the Shaman by Michael Harner.

You should learn a divination method or two, such as Tarot or I-Ching.

You would be amazed how many books you can find online, such as websites like sacred-texts.com (http://www.sacred-texts.com). I also found, amazingly, wikipedia to give really good general overviews of topics.

It is imperative that you learn to meditate.

Get a good understanding of all kinds of religions and spiritualities and their histories.

Understand Chakras, the tree of life, and as much occult symbolism are very important.

From one of the topics on the forum I mentioned earlier, someone suggests studying these 35 topics.

1. History of religion
2. Pagan theology
3. Theories of magick
4. Magickal ethics and etiquette
5. Breathing
6. Centering and Grounding

16. Astral projection
17. Correspondences
18. Guardians
19. Seasonal celebrations  Esbats and Sabbats
20. Trance induction and altered states
21. Ritual dance
22. Color and music magik
23. Magikal tools, weapons and temple arrangements
24. Sacred space and circle casting
25. Massage
26. Healing
27. Magikal names and power words
28. Amulets and talismans
29. Spellcraft
30. Weather magik
31. Tree and plant identification
32. Herbalism
33. Portable beasties, elementals and thought forms
34. Evocation
35. Invocation


I haven't even began to scratch the surface (in this post and in my own studies). If I think of much else I will post it later and if any of the more experience members see anything wrong with what I said please point it out.
Studying the occult is both exciting and fulfilling, good luck :D
