i dont understand how anyone could believe such utter nonsence. why does this happen? what could possibly make them believe "thats the way it is"?

several people i know think that if you do anything considered "wrong" by a book compiled near the dawn of humanity as we know it,you will suffer for eternity in unspeakable ways.

these people also think some *white male* *rules* over a *finite* universe,with earth being the center of this finite universe,and the only planet to harbor life.

for those reasons alone,im beggining to think theres something wrong with them. some sort of mental disorder if you will. they feel a need to "follow a leader" and lack the ability to think for themselves.

on top of all of that,i have a cousin who thinks he is jesus christ. im not bahsing him or anything. hes a very cool person and all. but let me give you some of his logic.. "me and brad went to the mall today. he bought two "jesus is my buddy" T-shirts... im brads buddy.. dont you think that means something?" i shit you not,thats what he said,and thats how he thinks.

we're all still just monkeys when you think about it. if you look at human history,we've,as a people,always had to follow some sort of leader as a pack of wolves follows its alpha male. it seems logical that most people would like to think theres always going to be someone "in charge" of things. for me atleast,i dont like the idea. i think its just a primitive concept and we're much better off on our own.

i just dont understand it. its all so bizzare an nonesencical. when will humanity finally,as a race,evolve spirtualy?

the way i see the world just makes sence to me. i wont go into detail because there isnt any. i just "believe what i see". i know live is unending and there isnt some *guy* damming people to suffer forever for not doing "what he tells us".

im not saying im looking forward to it,but it will be nice when im chillin in "the afterlife" and i finally get to laugh in the face of a pushy religious fanactic.
