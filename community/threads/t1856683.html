quote:

i've been told that silver nitrate can be used as a substitute for rp



No, it cannot. There is no reaction that produces HI using AgNO3 (lunar cuastic) and I2 or any of it's salts, nor can it be used to substitute rp. Unless your a photogropher, AgNO3 is useless for that purpose. However, you should find a small privately owned photographic supply house in your area. Not like a small camera shop in the mall or some shit, but an actually store dedicated to photogrophy as a whole. Don't even bother trying to order from Kodak or any big namer's like that, they sell pretty much all premade solutions. A small home town shop is more for the hobbyist/home tinkerer.

Walk in there and tell them you are doing a project for your photogrophy class, or that your just trying to start a new hobby and your gonna start by making your own pinhole camera, using home made photosensitive index cards. Explain how you already have compositions for developers and stop baths worked out, and you tried using silver bromide and chloride salts and wanted to try out some IODIDE salts. Also slip in a complaint how you accidentally knocked your bottle of silver nitrate into the sink with your elbow or something.

VIOLA! Now you have them all buttered up and ready to sell you a plethora of usefull salts and organic intermediates with no hassles or suspicion at all.

Goddamnit what I'm trying to say is for the hydrogen iodide people out there, say all of the above. Then ask for some AgNO3 (just to make it seem less suspicious, and make you appear more sincere). Since you rambled on about wanting to test iodide photonegatives, also mention that you want a few hundred grams of whatever iodide salt they have on hand, which'll more than likely be KI (NaI works just the same). Some places pratically give it out by the pound. See in photography, halogen salts mainly the bromides and iodides of silver are suspended in a gelatin, and put on a plastic sheets. This is your basic photonegative. Index cards and be impregnated with AgI, making a basic photonegative for your home built pin hole cameras. Solutions of KI and AgNO3 are mixed, in which AgI precipitates, and the KNO3 remains in solution. Gelatin is made and AgI mixed in to for a suspension that can be used to coat an index card.

Plus you can do all kinds of cool stuff with the AgNO3. Ever mix a solution of AgNO3 and drop a penny in it? Instant silver metal. Pretty cool just when your bored. Plus photogrophy is actually pretty neat, it's amazing to think you can make a photograph using a couple of basic salts, a box with a hole in it, and an index card.

Also, for the real chemists out there, you can get all kinds of organic intermediates that are generally used in developing solutions. Like p-aminophenol (p-hydroxyaniline) or hydroquinone. As well as all kinds of salts including cyanides and chromates. A whole lot of shit used in chemistry is very useful for all kinds of other shit to. It's funny to, cause on a few of SWIMS bottles including the hydroquinone have "For laboratory use only, not intended for drug use" written on them.

quote:

and there's also a few different ways to product HI



Thats right. HI isn't the problem. It's

a no brainer to synthesize, and as stated above iodide salts are easy as hell to acquire. Either phosphoric oxide (P2O5) or phosphoric acid (H3PO4) are non-watched non-suspicious and very abundant and cheap chemicals. Phosphoric acid is said to be one of the most abundant acid on earth besides sulfuric acid. H3PO4 is a great acid to use to generate HI. You can do it straight from the KI to, no conversion to I2.

1H3PO4 + 3KI-----&gt;3HI + 1K3PO4

P2O5 is just the anhydride phosphoric acid. Add water to make it H3PO4.

1P2O5 + 3H2O----&gt;2H3PO4

Aqueous solution of KI and H3PO4 is refluxed, then the 57% HI is distilled out of the mix.

Another method to generate HI is from Na2SO3. The only problem with that is your gonna need some type of phosphorus compound present during distillation, or very little HI acid will come over, due to HI reducing the formed sulfate ions. It would also probably be a good idea to set it up so you distill out any formed 57% HI as it's formed. Otherwise you end up with a boiling iodine suspension and some hydrogen gas.



I think a lot of people misunderstand the reason why phosphorus is used in HI reductions. HI reductions (besides your dry rp/I2 alchemy) in chemistry are generally done using 57% HI thats either purchased or synthesized, either way it wasn't made using phosphorus.

Yes in a dry rp/I2 synthesis, or a reflux without pre-made HI utilizes phosphorus to produce the needed HI, the original and main purpose for phosphorus is to keep the HI stable during storage and usage (namely reductions). Phosphorus is considered a recylant. HI is unstable, and just boiling HI can turn it into hydrogen and iodine in no time. A bottle of HI stored without any phosphorus to stabilize it eventually breaks down.

In the case of meth synthesis, the reaction between iodoephedrine and HI is slowed considerably if an abundance of its byproducts are present. This is a cheap ass way of giving an idea of what's called The Law Of Mass Action. I2 is a byproduct of the said reaction. Plus you have to deal with any breakdown of HI. Phosphorus compounds can take remove both I2 and H2O from a said reaction. As you know the formed PI3 reacts with water giving HI and phosphorous acid. Plus the phosphorous acid reacts with I2 and water also producing HI and phosphoric acid. Thus, the byproduct I2 is removed, as well as any from degraded HI. Then you have the removal of water at the same time, which effectively keeps the concentration of HI high. All major byproducts are converted back into more reducing agent. This is why the reaction will go to completion (iodoephedrine to meth).

Originally white/yellow phosphorus was used (P4) in reductions as the recyclant. But when red form (P) was discovered it took over. Hypophosphorous and phosphorus acids can both be used as recyclants as well. Generally your commercially available bottles of HI acid use a little hypo to stabilize it for storage.

There are a ton of ways to reduce a benzylic alcohol. Especially one like pseudo. Besides the ones already mentioned in this thread (birch, HI, electrolytic), you also have a myriad of other reductions to choose from, pretty much all utilizing an ester of the alcohol.

Lets ponder this for a minute or two.

Lets see....if you can acquire palladium, either by buying it (very expensive) or by scavanging it tediously from things like catalytic converters and such, there are a few different hydrogenations to choose from.

Sulfate or perchlorate esters are best used for direct hydrogenation, and are typically done in alcohol. When using pressurized hydrogen, pseudo can be dissolved in the alcohol, with some sulfuric or perchloric acid added. This is your most basic meth synthesis. Dissolving the precursor in ethanol, adding some H2SO4, the catalyst and pressurize with hydrogen. Rig up something to shake the mutherfucker and in a couple hours you can have high ass yeilds of very clean methamphetamine (it's a clean reaction).

To avoid using pressure and hydrogen gas, you can reflux an ester (sulfate or acetyl esters easiest and cheapest to make) in glacial acetic acid with the catalyst and ammonium formate.

An alternative to using rp in HI reductions is by utilizing LAH. One could spend an afternoon whipping up a few liters of HI which has a lot of potential no matter which route you take. But when you've obatined a bottle of LAH you can say fuck scraping the matches. Literature at rhodiums for the older school HI reductions say to treat the freebase in ether with LAH to reduce any iodoephedrine left over. You can just reflux pseudo in excess of 57%HI for 5 or 6 hours, which should be enough to make sure it's all converted to iodoephedrine. Basify and steam distill, and extract the distillate with xylene. Dry the xylene as if your life depended on it, then distill it all off. Then add dry ether and use LAH to reduce all the iodoephedrine to meth. And that would ensure full completion, not geuss work on whether or not you have enough HI, or whether you need to reflux another 12 hours or so. Plus it would be quicker. I'm not saying that any of these suggested methods are cheaper or easier, it all depends on what you have access to.
