Atomic
All and everywhere in anything and everything
the lover's voice i've longed to hear can be heard to sing
in the drifting melodies of light wafting gaily on the breeze
and in the empty hearts of men trudged up to their knees
as they give their all and everything fighting through the wind
the lover's voice I've longed to hear, the boom and then the ring.

the saddle's dry and cracked, the horse is skin and bone
and here I hear my lover sing, to me and I alone
the drifting melodies have passed, alas there is no sound
no heart of man to sacrifice, no pathways to be found
no clarion is trumpeted upon this sordid earth
since we spent all energies on filling up the herse
only my dear love's voice is there, forever singing clear,
in wistful booming voice to me; the ringing in my ears.
a.p.e.x
