Re: Taking the pill.
Let me inject a hint of common sense and some paternal wisdom into this thread.

For every action there is an equal or greater opposite reaction

ANY drug/herb/medication you take for an effect will also have side effects. Thats basic human physiology 101.

The pill is very very effective at what it does, and anything that changes or influences the way your body works to that extent is going to have both positive and negative effects.

Stupid example, I'm on antibiotics at the moment . They'll probably stop the lung infection from killing me this time round. Unfortunately they also kill the 'good bacteria' which means I have the shits.

The younger you start taking something regularly and the longer you do it, the more the risks pile up.

Also remember there is a lot of financial and fem-political interest in the pill being portrayed as harmless. Also some meds only show side effects in the next generation.

Am I catholic or against the Pill? Well I agree that it has done its bit to destroy the fabric of society. But no I'm not anti as such. I just think common sense needs to kick in and for people to realise there is no such thing as a free lunch. Capish? Gonna be pay back somewhere down the line.

I often wonder if the pill is the hidden cause for a lot of female depressions/mental illnesses. No scientific evidence as far as I know but I do know that anything that effects a woman's hormones/fertility can have nasty mental repercussions.
