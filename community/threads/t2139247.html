Opinions on Muscularity
In college, I read probably a dozen studies this last year in various Psych and English classes about how women arent really attracted to extreme muscular male bodies, but yet males almost unanimously think they could use more muscle on their frame.

Now, Im a guy and gym rat. I work out, eat right, take care of my body quite well, and it would seem that girls dig this. And, being relatively educated on the subject, I consequently like girls who take care of their bodies... the right way. IMO, too many girls resort to cardio without even considering resistance training, when it's pretty well know that a good combo of the two produces the best results. I mean, I know and accept and totally enjoy that women are supposed to be slightly curvaceous and less muscular than guys, but its kind of a downer for me when girls are totally weak and out of shape no matter how cute they are. And probably most important, people who workout generally have much better posture than those who dont, and to me posture is KEY!

So totseans of S&A, what do you all think? Girls especially, Im interested if guys can be too muscular for you. By this, Im not really asking if you think Pro-Bodybuilders are handsome, because honestly, who doesnt think they look like freaks? When I say muscular guy, Im thinking like Matthew McConaughey's current level of fitness. And guys, do you also like a some muscle on your girls? Or are you chubby chasers and hoggers?

That's the important part right there in bold. Sure, women don't want extreme muscularity on men, but that in no way means most men shouldn't want more muscle on their bodies. Remember, most men aren't one step away from being Ronnie Coleman, so they shouldn't worry about being "too muscular" at all. The kind of physique acquired by guys as cut as the first picture Silverfuck posted requires something almost every man isn't up to, and by that I am NOT talking about hitting the weights really hard in the gym. Ask any bodybuilder(I'm not talking about anyone who happens to lift weights) what the hardest part about being a bodybuilder is and they will say it is the nutrition. Bodybuilding and getting an extremely ripped physique is 20% gym, and 80% when and what you eat when not in the gym. Muscle isn't built in the weight room, muscle is built while resting after the weight room.

But yeah, I do agree with your points on people and fitness. Keep in the mind the following statement I will not respond to, as I am not going to get into it right now:

I have nothing but contempt for anorexic people, as they refuse to take responsibility for their own health and physical well being enough to use real and useful knowledge to look better. Eating right, lifting weights, and jogging is what fat fucks need to be doing, not starving themselves or in the case of bulimics puking unhealthy foods after binge eating. Call it a mental disorder(which they call everything, even drug addiction), but that's just a bullshit excuse for people wanting to take the easy way out and not the relatively hard way of sound diet and exercise.
