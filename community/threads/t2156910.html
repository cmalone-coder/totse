Don't know too much about opiates, but I can definatley help you out with the benzos. Tolerance and withdrawal is exclusivley dependent on what benzo you are taking and for how long. Shorter acting benzos such as xanax/Ativan can show signs of dependence within a little over 2 weeks, while longer acting benzos such as valium and klonopin can take a bit longer and the withdrawal symptoms don't seem to be as severe(take that with a grain of sand), still probaly one of the worst in the drug world(Benzo withdrawal can kill you, unlike opiate/cocaine wd's).

Take happyhitters advice and just take them when you are feeling the withdrawals, not just in general if you know what I mean. You'll keep your tolerance lower and there is much less of a chance of dependence (just in case). Hope that helps...
