Today. . . I Shave My Head
It has been approximately 2 years and 5 months since my head has last been shaved, in that time it has been slightly cut on two or three occasions and has grown to roughly 25 inches.
Why am I going to shave this part of me that has grown and experienced with me through a very important transitioning point in my life?
Two reasons:

I am going to be hair tested sometime in the next couple of months. Quite a bit of evidence for marijuana use is up there.
I am becoming a monk. I am going to dedicate me life to expanding my consciousness and deepening my connection to God (Consciousness). The shaving of my head symbolizes the shedding of my skin and reBirth, the transition into my new life.I am going to spend much of my day in meditation, two thirty minute sessions, once when I awaken and once before I go to sleep. Before and after I do anything a five minute meditation will be done, deepening my connection to the study. In time, these lengths will increase.

Any suggestions or comments?
