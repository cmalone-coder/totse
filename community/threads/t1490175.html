Without even knowing it.

No. 1: I was (am) in a very manic phase and didn't feel like leaving the house. It had been a very long day. I'd had an anxiety attack at the grocery market, caused a scene, the night stalker came back, etc... So, I told him it had been a long day, I wasn't going to dress up, I didn't shave my legs, I looked like crap, etc... But, he insisted he still wanted to hang out because:

"I've been at work all day. I just want to talk to someone cool. I don't mind if you're a little stinky."

I told him I wasn't at all stinky and accepted the date. I think proceeded to change into my hot first date clothes.

No. 2: Then - when he picked me up - instead of bringing me flowers or candy or something he brought me a little painting. It's on a red background and it's like some sad, legless, bunny-man-creature looking all somber at a prostrate bunny doll on the sidewalk. It's super duper.

No. 3: I told him there was this uber seedy arcade/pool hall on Broadway (which is the designated strip club street) where we could play Pac Man and get in a fight. So, he wrestled for parking

No. 4: then bought an umbrella because it was raining,

No. 5: and then when we found out it had been shut down, he asked me what I felt like drinking. I said whiskey, so he ran across the street (almost getting run over, which was oddly adorable) and returned with a pint of whiskey. We drank on the wet way back to the car.

No. 6: he then took me to this super cool Mediterranian place on Haight street where people danced on tables, smoked hookahs, and broke plates on the floor. Because he had been such a doll all night, I even got up and flossed my belly dancing skills, since I recent took lessons. The look on his face was priceless.

No. 7: When he took me home, he kissed me, and then he got this goofy look on his face and said

"You look super cool."

It was such a dweeby thing to say, I laughed. Then I shadow punched him in the shoulder and returned the compliment. He tried to take it back, but I thought it was so cute I just said I'd see him around and got out.

After that I notice he really sped away fast.

No. 8: I got inside, fed the fish, made myself some tea, washed my face, turned on the late night infomercials, got undressed, put on my bed clothes, etc... and just as I was hanging the painting up my phone rang and it was him. He called to tell me he hadn't meant to make me uncomfortable, if he had. He knew it was a dweeby remark and said he was sorry but, well, you know. Anyways, that was unbelieveably adorable.

I'm not a particularily friendly person, especially when I'm in an introvert manic phase. But, he managed to make it a really awesome date, despite the circumstances.

So those are some pointers for any guys who are interested in knowing what can work on a date.

Cheers,
