Re: Two points, a compass, and a question...
Most compasses don't have scales, particularly the expensive ones. Bisecting an angle is extremely similar to bisecting the line segment, though. Simply draw an arc across the rays of the angle, then use the intersections of the arcs and the angle as the center of two more arcs (of equal radii). The intersection of these arcs inside the angle mark the midpoint, and a line drawn connecting the vertex and this intersection will bisect the angle.

This is junior high geometry, ya know...
