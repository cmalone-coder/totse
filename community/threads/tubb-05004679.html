A chemical in the grapefruit juice is metabolized by the same enzyme that turns DXM into its cousin DXO (DXM gives more of a mental effect, while DXO is more body and "stoning" effect.) By drinking the grapefruit juice you slowed down the rate at which the DXM is metabolized. Typically people drink the grapefruit juice before ingesting the DXM. I'm not sure how well it works when they're taken at the same time. If you find that you have trouble keeping shit down, try DXM without the juice.

As far as strategies for avoiding nausea, there are a few things that help me. I like to eat a little snack either while drinking syrup or a little while later. I tend to go for carbohydrates and foods that aren't too heavy like bread (I like french bread), chips, french fries, and not too sweet cereal. I haven't tried this with DXM, but the herb ginger is pretty good at suppressing nausea. I think you can get it in capsule form or you can just buy some gingersnap cookies. If you have access to it, pot is very effective at controlling nausea and goes fairly well with DXM.

The honey syrup that you bought is good for DXM. Being so thick and sweet it doesn't really sit that well especially on an empty stomach, but it tastes a whole hell of a lot better than the regular syrup. It also seems to me that the honey syrup starts working a little bit faster, but that might just be me.

I can get other drugs. I prefer DXM to a lot of them. I can order powder, but it's really convenient to be able to get one of my drugs of choice at the local Kroger.

Use the dosing method described in the DXM faq at Erowid.

You need:

  • A measuring cup with mL graduations
  • A drinking glass
  • A sink
  • A toothbrush (w/ toothpaste)

Hold the bottle at arms length and open it. Measure out your dose. If at any time you get near it, hold your nose. The important part is never smell the shit! Fill the glass with water. While holding your nose, down the entire dose as quickly as possible. Hold your breath and drink at least one glass of water, preferably two or three. Immediately after, brush your teeth as vigorously as you can stand. Make sure you get your entire mouth, your tongue, and even your lips. Rinse.

With any luck, you'll hardly taste the stuff. That makes all the difference in the world. If you start to feel queasy, eat some bread or crackers. Don't burp.

I've managed to keep down an entire 8oz bottle that way.
