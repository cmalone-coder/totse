Re: Chivalry and Respect
I think that the reason "chivalry" is dying (note: has not yet died) is:

1: What is intended to be chivalric is often misinterpreted as the guy trying to ingratiate himself.

2: "Chivalry" is seen as being part of the old system of social sexism - like the glass ceiling, etc. - which should be done away with.

3: People who recieve help (e.g. door opening) often show no gratitude. This is probably because of reason 1 or 2, or because the receiver /expects/ to have things done for them.

The concept of "chivalry" is a social contract. Men display chivalry, women display gratitude in some way. In the words of Scott Adams: "The deal is OFF!"
