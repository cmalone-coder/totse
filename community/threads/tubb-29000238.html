It was a fun little project. All told it took me about two hours to make with a Dremel and some hand tools.

Re: My knife is weirder than yours.
I have some nice Pakistani fixed-blades, but only one folder. That one folder has turned me off of Pakistani folding knives for good, more than likely. At least it only cost me a dollar.

I've found that the brass knives' blades do rust a little, but a good refinishing and a little oil solves that problem. I've yet to break or bend one, and I use two of them as general purpose knives when I get bored of using my spring folder. They also hold a very good edge, even with those brass sheaths scraping against it when you draw the knife carelessly. Of course, the first thing to go an all of those knives was that irritating locking mechanism...

I've never actually bought a knife from QVC, but I've been toying with the idea of picking up one of their 200 knife sets or somesuch and peddling the extras off for a profit. The only reason I haven't is that I don't trust QVC in the least, especially watching the way they try to trick people into buying their computers.
