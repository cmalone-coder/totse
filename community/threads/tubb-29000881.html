Re: anti gun bull shit?
quote:
silencers, folding stocks, flash suppressors, barrel shrouds and bayonets which are ludicrously unsuited for civilian use.


A "silencer" is suited for civilian use. Guns are really loud. They can be dangerous to hearing, and an annoyance to those who live nearby. Because it's difficult to get a suppressor in America, we have to wear hearing protection when firing guns. The hearing protection can be bulky and uncomfortable, but more importantly it decreases one's awareness of his environment. Wouldn't you rather the guy holding the gun be as aware of what's going on around him as possible? It makes much more sense to make the gun quiet. It decreases risk of hearing loss to the gun operator, allows him to hear what's going on around him, and decreases sound pollution. The anti's have managed to decrease gun safety by making suppressors hard to obtain.

Folding stocks are great. A folding stock means that the gun takes up less space when it's being stored and can be fit into a smaller carrying case for easier transport. A folding stock is perfectly well suited to civilian use.

A lot of flash suppressors also act as compensators making the gun more pleasant to fire. A flash suppressor prevents a person from losing their night vision when firing weapons at night. I guess the antis want the guy with the gun to not be able to see.

A barrel shroud is a safety device! It's there so that you don't burn yourself on a hot barrel!

In any case, the author of that statement has a thoroughly skewed definition of civilian use. All of the features mentioned (with the exception of the bayonet) would be nice on a hunting or target rifle. They would also be nice on a self defense weapon, but I doubt the author recognizes self defense as a legitimate civilian use of small arms. He is wrong. Is a woman about to be raped wrong to try to defend herself? Is a man about to be murdered wrong to defend himself? Self defense is a legitimate civilian use of firearms. Further, if I'm trying to defend myself, I need to do it as effectively as possible. I want the most hard, ass-kicking, lead spewing, death raining gun this world has ever seen.
