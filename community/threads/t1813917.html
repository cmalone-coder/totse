At the mundane level, I live to support those who depend on me--my wife, her son, and a bunch of dogs and cats, all of whom I love dearly.

At a higher level, I live to express and declare Who and What I am, to recreate myself on a daily--even a minute to minute--basis to become more loving, more accepting, and more compassionate.

In general, I just enjoy life, appreciate the really neat planet I have to live on, get a huge charge out of seeing the play of light over the mountains around dusk.

On a more material level, I also enjoy gloating over my coin collection, wheeling and dealing for firearms when I work gun shows, and consuming mass quantities of buttered popcorn whilst watching cheesy horror movies.

Life is good. Or rather, life is just exactly as good as I choose to make it.
