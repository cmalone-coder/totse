Long Ranting Version (Scroll Down For Nutshell Version)

---------------------------------------------

Two and a half years ago, when my band first got together, my guitarist developed a crush on me. I ignored it on account of he made it sound like I was coming on to him/leading him on whenever I made a comment or joke about it. I figured it was best to just wait until he made a move on me so I could finally say

"A ha! I knew it! Now, I'm very sorry, but you're not my type and this is a very bad idea."

But, he's kind of strange guy and tends to have emotional outbursts, and it took a long time for "it all" to come to a head. By that time I knew him much better and for some reason, when he finally kissed me I was paralyzed with fear of him freaking out if I turned him down.

What I did instead was I jumped up and left.

After that I think we had two more incidences when he kissed me before I finally got up the courage to say 'no'. (Mind you, he's wierd, emotional, and I'm not attracted to him.)

But, this had kind of all faded into the distance until just recently.

-

See, I've had a crush on his best friend since we first met, 2.5 years ago. But, he had a girlfriend. Then, when they broke up I didn't want to be the rebound girl. But, he moved to NY for a few months, and then came back, and all kinds of stuff got in the way.

Until about a month and a half ago.

Which is when the problem with my guitarist began to resurface.

He (my guitarist) is incredibly protective of me. They all are, but particularily him. If we're hanging out at a bar and someone - anyone tries to approach me, he'll blatantly stand right in between us and block them. He's always hugging me, putting his arm around me, trying to make me kiss his cheek, etc...

It's so bad, when we're out together people ask me all the time if he's either my boyfriend or bodyguard.

And it's gotten annoying enough that I've brought it up, fleetingly twice, out of sheer frustration.

Anyways, as soon as he found out I was into his best friend he put the flirt on overdrive.

And see, I thought we were past all that so I didn't take a lot of it seriously at first.

I made a pass on his best friend after one of the shows. Things took off.

And it was nice going out with a guy who I really really liked for a change. I'm always going out with blokes who are either (not to be concieted) not in my league or have nothing in common with me.

It gotten tiresome.

So, we were dating loosely for about a month and a half and things we're actually starting to look a little... serious. Meaning, we were building a repor. We knew each others schedules relatively well, saw each other about twice a week, held hands in public. (I have done that since my divorce 4 years ago!) And I was really happy about this and apparently he was, too because one day at practice my guitarist said that his friend told him we'd been going out and that things were feeling serious.

I - being - a girl turned bright red and started grinning. I mean, I had noticed Jamie was being more flirtatious again and I had even noticed him talking shit about his best friend to me, obviously to stop me from liking him. But, I really didn't think it was anything to worry about. I figured, he's my band mate, we've been playing together a while now, we went camping together for chrissakes, he's my friend, and your friend would be happy to see you happy, even if it's not the reason to be happy they've had in mind.

I guess I was wrong, though. Because the next day his best friend and some of his friends met me at work to bring me some extra nail files. And everything was cool until my guitarist suddenly showed up.

He didn't say 'hi' to me and he didn't say 'good-bye'. But, after he left his friend started acting all wierd. And after he got kind of blotto he asked me to go outside with him.

But, then he asked if he could kiss me and I knew something was up, because he's not the kind of guy who asks shit like that, and he kept rubbing his sideburns, which I know he does when he's nervous. So, we were making out some and then he told me that my guitarist gave him an ultimatum that either he stops seeing me or they stop being friends.

This is where I'm really heartbroken.

No. 1: He's supposed to be my friend and he intentionally hurt me! Shallow!

No. 2: They'd been best friends for over a decade and my guitarist was willing to throw it all away over a girl?! Shallow!

No. 3: We're not talking about teenage boys here, people. These are men in their 30's! Acting like babies.

Of course, I told him

"I guess that's that, then."

Then really quickly he said he didn't care if I didn't care. But, that was so godawful I pretended not to hear it.

'If I can't have her no one can!' What're we 15 here? I couldn't believe it! I mean, couldn't he have just sucked it up for six months and let us have our fun? And what kind of a friend would do something like that?!

Anyways, to make matters worse, today, at practice, he still kept trying to flirt with me (though I was visibly infuriated) and even tried to get me to go out to a bar with him after practice! What a dillhole!!!
