Re: Counterfeit Clothes
Yup there are some Class A counterfeits out there with good quality but they cost more than regular fakes. Some can even match the quality of the original though they are hard to come by.
One thing to note when you own counterfeit clothes is to also have some originals. For example, if you have fake Lacoste shoes at the same time get some originals. It doesn't need to be of the same style and color. Ordinary people doesn't really care much what you wear and even more if they are fake or not. Be wary though since some fakes do look like fakes and make you think that you should have bought an original instead.
I don't recommend buying from websites especially fake ones. You cannot try them until they are already mailed to you.
Buying fakes are like gambling. There's a risk that you might get something totally shitty and something that can totally match an original.
