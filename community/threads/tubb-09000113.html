Ok, my (unique as far as i am aware) theory of creation. Ok, so I've looked into this and as far as I can tell this is not forbidden by any known (and commonly accepted) laws.


Weak Anthropic Principle (W.A.P)
"The observed values of all physical and cosmological quantities are not equally probable but they take on values restricted by the requirement that there exist sites where carbon-based life can evolve and by the requirement that the Universe be old enough for it to have already done so."

Creation and the Big Bang
"Fifteen billion years ago, give or take five billion years, the entirety of our universe was compressed into the confines of an atomic nucleus. Known as a singularity, this is the moment before creation when space and time did not exist. According to the prevailing cosmological models that explain our universe, an ineffable explosion, trillions of degrees in temperature on any measurement scale, that was infinitely dense, created not only fundamental subatomic particles and thus matter and energy but space and time itself. Cosmology theorists combined with the observations of their astronomy colleagues have been able to reconstruct the primordial chronology of events known as the big bang."
Taken from Here

Ok, now the Weak Anthropic Principle basicaly says that we observe the perfect world, because if it wasn't perfect we wouldn't exist. However, the chances of this world evolving perfectly are so remote that most people that believe in the W.A.P. also believe in the infinite universe theory. This theory basicaly states that for every possibility in an event happens in a different universe, and so there are potentially an infinite amount of universes.
But therein lies the problem? An infinite amount of universes? Infinite amounts of mass? This for most people is hard to comprehend. Therefore, I have formulated a new theory.

Simply put, Before the big bang, Time did not exist, the entire universe was contained in an infinitely small singularity. I believe that this is a 'closed' universe and therefore gravity will eventually slow down the movement of the stars, planets, and everything, and eventually cause it to collide back together, slowly forming super-massive back holes, and ultimately that infinitely small singularity same as the one with which we started. Now, here is where relative time comes into effect. Time is not a straight arrow as many people believe, instead it is relative. Therefore it is plausible to suspect that the first and second singularities (as they exist outside time) are one and the same singularity.

This opens up the possibility that this cycle can and has continued for an infinite amount of 'time'. Therefore an infinite amount of possibilities have occurred, and therefore negating the need for the infinite universe theory. This also accounts for the W.A.P. that would have, as required, an infinite amount of possibilities, all realised.

Any one who actually knows what they are talking about see how this might not be a possibility (please try not to argue with the time is like an arrow argument). But have I made any serious errors? Any fundamental facts I've missed out? Can you improve it?

Please reply (Constructive stuff would be good)
