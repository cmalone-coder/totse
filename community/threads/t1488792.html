Exuding sexuality
Edit: This is a long-ass post. You've been warned.

A recent comment this past weekend (which I've made reference to in other threads) got my brain working. This is rarely a good thing, but I'm utterly stumped. I wouldn't classify this as a problem as much as just the way I am, and it's almost sure to be classified as an unanswerable question, but how does one exude sex (sex appeal, sexuality)?

I am not a sexual person. This is not due to a lack of desire or sex drive on my part. I like all the good things about sex. I definitely would like more. I enjoy watching attractive women pass by, although my interest in that has waned (more on that later). I could talk about the topic for long periods of time, were I ever drawn into a conversation about it, much like I enjoy being an active part of this forum. I wouldn't mind having a worthwhile girlfriend, either.

As much as I despise blaming things on one's youth, part of this is due to the way things were when I was younger. I was chubby, fairly out of shape despite enjoying all kinds of sports (and being piss-poor at the whole spectrum), and shy as hell. I didn't know jack shit about confidence, to the extent where, knowing what I do now, I'm positive that if someone had said confidence gets chicks, I'd have told them, "Yeah, but the rest of me will send them running."

I used to believe hitting on a girl was insulting, and it took me a long time to realize that it wasn't the attention which was offensive, but rather the disrespectful style I constantly saw. Didn't have a girlfriend until 21, sex until 23. In short, when it comes to the one slice of life that requires firsthand experience, I have nearly none.

I'm not hopeless. I get attention. What few girls I've chatted up (due to needing a good vibe to feel comfortable with it) have all been smart, good to talk to, and we've gotten on well. Save the one occasion where I absolutely bludgeoned my chances (inexperience problem), I haven't gotten any dates because they all have boyfriends (bad luck, I suppose). Short and sweet, when the situation presents itself, I know what to do.

And that's where I start to get confused.

I have no lack of confidence. I'm in shape. I look good. I look at girls, but I don't leer. I'll talk about sex, but I'm not inclined towards initiating those conversations (or any conversations at all, for that matter). I'd like it as much as anyone else, probably more than most, but yet, for the second time in my life, I've been called damn near asexual.

I understand why. I don't give off a vibe of wanting to fuck the living hell out of that hottie that just walked in, because I'm not consumed with the idea of fucking the hottie that just walked in. Part of it is that, since she's a random person, odds are she's dumb as a brick, and there are so many other females out there who are just as hot and have equal odds of being dumb as a brick that it's like, what makes that one so special? Plus, relating to what I mentioned before, I'm tired of just looking. It bores me to know that it's all I'll get to do, so the interest isn't there anymore.

But why would a girl be sexually attracted to a guy who doesn't appear interested in sex?

This is where the dilemma occurs. Yes, most random people are dumb as rocks. Not all. And why would those smart people be any more attracted to me than the dumb ones? Just because they have functioning brains doesn't mean their hormones work differently. But while a girl might look at me and think, "He looks good," she'll look at my friend Greg and say, "I want to fuck him senseless."

Maybe this isn't something I want to change. It may simply be a side effect of the philosophies I hold, to initially view all people as equals until they prove otherwise, rather than view women as nothing more than potential sex partners until they show they can be more. It may also be due in part to the fact that, on the occasions I've tried to be more friendly, I've been substantially disappointed by the inanity of my fellow humans, and thus naturally hold out little hope for the ones that just happen to come in attractive shells. In those cases, it's a side effect I can live with.

But it's been over a year since I got laid. I've never had a girlfriend that lived in the same state as me. I want to be able to hug someone besides my fucking mom. These are not new problems, and they're ones I deal with well enough. It's simply a bit of a personal earthquake to discover that you may, in fact, have all the qualities needed to get a girl, and be failing miserably because they don't get the feeling you want anything to do with them.

I have no mojo. (She's got a boyfriend. Ba dum bum.) Were I to decide to change this, how the fuck would I go about doing it? Or am I stuck in a vicious cycle, where one's sexuality tends to come forth naturally the more they use it?
