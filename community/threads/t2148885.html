Re: I fucking suck at rolling
Here's a tip: use a credit card to roll the paper over.

* make a roach using card equivalent to that of a rizzla packet.
* grind weed up
* mix with tobacco
* set up the paper with the sticky side facing you and facing inwards
* put roach at either end depending on your preference
* sprinkle mix into paper with slightly more at the end the roach is not - PRO TIP: spend time doing this so that, when you come to roll, it rolls evenly and doesn't hold air bubbles or creases
* roll your spliff, rubbing you index finger and thumbs together. It is sometimes easier to put a firmer grip on the end with the roach since this gives you some leverage. Roll across the joint until it's nicely packed in (not too tight tho!) and evenly spread
* now the trick: use a credit card (or card of equal size/strength) and press it into the part of the paper that is closest to you; fold it over your weed and tuck it into the other part of the paper that is furthest away from you (with the sticky still facing you). Use the card to ensure your roll is without creases, moving the card along until all of the paper is tucked in.
* now just simply lick, roll, and toke.

In case someone else didn't mention how to use the roller, I will (I didn't read all the posts).

Place weed into the roller broken up while the roller is open. Once enough weed is in the roller, close it and roll the closer wheel towards you. Once it's rolled over a few times it'll be in alright shape. Get a paper, usually a 1.25 will be great for a roller, and place the end WITHOUT the gum into the small seam on top of the roller. Roll it over a few times and then pop that bitch open and you have a cigarette size joint.

Some tips I learned when I first started rolling joints:

- Use thick papers. They are easier to move around and are less prone to ripping, and it's harder to get them 'soaked' when you try to lick the gum. Once you get the hang of rolling, I suggest going to thinner papers, they don't have a burning paper taste, and can roll better joints.

- Know your steps before you start your roll. You can waste countless minutes trying to 'correct' something. If you start the roll, finish it, before the weed becomes messy and starts falling everywhere (believe me you will see this a lot with newbie rollers).

- Practice, practice, practice; with anything that has a texture like busted weed. I've practiced rolling tobacco, this odd spice I found in my kitchen, anything that feels like weed. That way, when it comes to rolling a REAL joint, your set.

- Don't bother watching rollers to see how to roll, many people have different style. I've seen people roll joints in a REALLY odd way. Just get yourself a pack of papers and start.
