Re: &TOTSE diablo 2 clan?
My rule is to imbue what you can use.. if you've got 170 str, go for the ornate.. I tend towards big things (armor, weapon), but if you're completely set with those, you could try the belt and see what you get. You're in a different position than me- I have lower strength, so by needing to use normal items, I can gamble gloves, belts, boots, etc. easily.. I dunno what to suggest.

Basically, my rule of thumb is, if you can do better with the armor, do that. If not, go for a weapon. Otherwise, do whatever you want. Just remember that the longer you wait to use your imbue, the better your chances of getting major-league abilities on whatever item you use.
