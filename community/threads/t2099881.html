Re: Eating .5g weed?
small cannabutter cook...

figure out a way to make a small alcohol burner... something like removing the top cap out of a small CO2 canister, filling canister with rubbing alcohol, inserting a small piece of cotton cloth that fits in it snugly... find a way to make a stand, clay or something... or find a way to mcgyver something else... i have alcohol stoves made out of fucking coke cans that i have cooked off of but the heat would be a bit much for this sort of thing...

grind up your weed into a powder, add to pyrex measuring cup.. using a typical glass and your risking it cracking and sending hot oil all over the place... add enough oil or butter to cover up the weed. light the burner and gently heat the oil or butter for a while... don't allow it to get hotter than a few hundred degrees... simmer it for as long as you can stand, but i would do it for at least a couple of hours... if you dont care about the taste you dont have to strain it, but its a simple step that makes it a little bit better..

cool and put it on whatever you want...
