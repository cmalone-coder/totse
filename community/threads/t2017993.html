Re: Make Freedom of Expression UNIVERSAL Law.
Only State actors should be prevented from interfering with freedom of expression. Private actors should have no such restrictions. The owners of this website, for instance, have every right to control its content, particularly as they are the ones held legally responsible for it in other areas. Every person has the right to control what messages they associate themselves with. If the Jeff Hunters of the world cannot control what is said on their private websites, then they are forced to implicitly play host to and associate themselves with whatever message people want to spout here. Just like you shouldn't be able to shut people up entirely, you shouldn't be able to force words into other people's mouths, or interfere with the messages and beliefs with which they associate themselves.
