What did I see?
About eight or nine years ago my brother came running in the house and told me to come outside. He said that there was something in the sky and he wanted to make sure he wasn't going crazy. So I went out with him and I saw what he was talking about.

What I saw was a light that looked about the same size as most stars. I couldn't make out any type of shape connected to the light, I could only see a dot up in the sky. The light appeared to be extremely high up, but again I couldn't see an actual vehicle.

Anyways, I sat outside with him for about 30 minutes watching this light move around the sky. The light stayed within a parameter of space that was equal to my viewing of the Big Dipper. I would flash, stop, and then flash in another part of the sky. The light never moved while lit.

Has anyone see anything like this before or have any ideas on what I saw? I still get a sick feeling when thinking about this, because I have no logical answer. It was not a plane or satellite. I believe there is life out there, but do not know why they would give a rats ass about us.
