Yeah, pack a knife just in case -- a 3" folder will be fine, and bring the strongest friend you have. As soon as it starts go for the face or groin -- pick one area, go for it two or three times, and then just shift to the other. If you can get them in a good position, punch them as hard as you can in the throat. And if they get your leg, make sure you can recover from it. Actually, do that right now. Rear up for a kick, extend your leg, then drop onto your hands and flip over. If you do get grounded, take the opportunity and kick them in the shin or groin. If you get behind them, punch them as hard as you can in the neck, or grab them back into a choke hold and just bash their face in in that position.

Remember to use the landscape and items around you. If you can generate enough punching force, and there's a rock next to you, grip it and resume hitting. Same with your knife -- you don't need the blade out, the extra mass will add more power to your punches.
