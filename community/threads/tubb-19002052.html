Re: Flash Powder
Atomized is tiny round balls and flake is tiny flakes (obviously...). Flake has much greater surface area, making it much easier to ignite. So basically, you blew it. Next time do more reading before you go spending your money...

Here's what you need to do: Buy dark flake aluminum. Measure by weight. It'll work. Now that you have this atomized, you can mix it a bit with your flake (maybe 25% or so) to use it up, but not too much or it won't light easily and won't work as well.
