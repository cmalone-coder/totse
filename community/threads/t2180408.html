Help: I've Hit a Weight Loss Plateau
Heya,

I lost about 60 pounds by the end of 18 months ago and I've been struggling ever since to lose the rest of the weight. I'm at 217 pounds right now.

I eat pretty healthy. I regulate myself to 2000-2300 calories a day. For my activity level, age and weight my base metabolic need is about 3,000-3,100, according to the online calculator I use. I did starve myself for about a year though so I think I may have fucked up my metabolism.

Most of my carbs are high in fiber like hemp granola, whole wheat bread and tortillas, peanut butter, etc. Most of the time for breakfast I'll have hemp granola with 1/4 cup whole milk yogurt mixed with berries and a bit of honey. Lunch is often a peanut butter and jelly sandwich, or these healthy burritos that are loaded in fiber and protein. Dinner is usually salmon, steak or chicken or really healthy salads. I eat alot of organic stuff and drink water almost exclusively.

I excercise 6-7 days a week.

Two days in a row I do cardio, burning 300 calories on the treadmill with interval training where I walk for 3 minutes at 3mph then run at 7 mph for a minute, repeating this for like 26 minutes with a cool down period.

Every three days I do weight lifting where I bench press as much weight as I can in 15 reps, then do bicep curls for as much weight as I can tolerate in 12 reps, then I do 50 sit ups. I repeat this two more times, and sometimes I mix in a little boxing or some other random lifting.

I'm still not fucking losing weight. I have noticed a better tone to my muscles and my abs, but nothing dramatic. Any advice as to how I could improve my diet and excercise plan?
