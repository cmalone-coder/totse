Clutch Slip
I have yet another problem (good ol' Rovers), on which I would like an opinion or three.

The clutch currently on my car was only fitted at 90,000 miles, and the car is now at 125,000 miles, and it's beginning to slip. Now, first off, even with harsh driving (which I do infrequently) should a clutch be starting to slip now?
Secondly, as I mention in almost every post, I'm saving for a new car (620 Turbo, so excited!!), and the slip problem is only in extreme cases (i.e it can be covered up for the purposes of selling the car), it's fine if I change gear around 3000rpm, all smooth like and everything, and it accelerates with no slip once the clutch is properly engaged, but earlier there was an absolute knob head with his Peugeot 306 jammed full of his mates in the back and his girlfriend in the front, who needed showing up by an old Rover, and I noticed when I changed from second to third really quickly as you do, the slippage was so extreme, the car carried on accelerating from 60mph right up to 90mph all at the 6000rpm where I changed from 2nd, even though I'd completely engaged the clutch as soon as it was in 3rd.

So, second question: as I'm selling the car within the next month or so, once I find a good 620ti, and it drives fine under easy driving conditions, would any of you bother forking out the £100+ to get a new clutch fitted, and then having to wait another two weeks to get paid and recover that £100 so you can buy a 620?

And third question as I asked for an opinion or three, isn't it great when a boy racer gets shown up in front of his girlfriend and mates by a fat bloke in an old mans car?

EDIT: Actually, is there something I can do, apart from modify my driving, which I will do, to cut down on the extremity of the slippage?
