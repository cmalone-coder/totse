death
most often i think about death. is it like being stuck in an eternity of sleep or is there really a life after death. we go on living our lives knowing theres that wall and not even comprehending what it really is.

what if we have the wrong ideas. we condemn murder but what if we werent even supposed to live in the first place. we wouldnt know because the only ones with the answers are the deceased. perhaps when you die your eternally stuck in your corpse. then we dig your grave and you go insane in a coffin for eternity. we make conclusions about life and death when we might have a totally wrong impression. We condemn death because we fear it. We fear it because we dont know what IT is. There are endless possibilities of what death might be.

Well, there certainly could be some kind of afterlife, but I and no one else has any idea what it is. Its impossible for us to know.

Personally, however, I really do feel, deep down, that in the end its the end, there is no afterlife. Its an eternity of being an object, not a being, an object, that slowly rots and then spends the rest of eternity as dust and bones.

Hard to comprehend being nothing but dust for eternity, isnt it? Personally, I think that is why people invented the concept of thee afterlife. Im not ruling out the afterlife, but I doubt it, and if there is one than I shall be pleasantly surprised.
