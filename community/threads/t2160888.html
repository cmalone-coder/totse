Acer laptop overheating constantly
Yeah, like the title says. Got a laptop from my dad a while back when my older one gave out. Acer Aspire 5315 laptop overheats constantly. Not just getting really hot to the touch, but getting really hot to the touch then just shutting off. I tried cool-mats but they don't help. Basically my setup right now is propping the back corners up on 4 hockey pucks (2 on each side) and the front on two pucks (one on each side) and sliding ice-trays wrapped in a rag, then stuffed in a Ziploc freezer bag underneath until it cools down. That works, but this thing overheats CONSTANTLY. I don't overload it either. This thing will overheat in under 15 minutes then will get so hot it shuts off at about 20 minutes of start up if I don't have my ghetto chill-mat under it. I usually am running Firefox 2.0, MSN messenger, and AIM. Along with the factory system files, Vista, and Windows Live One Care.

This is my second Acer that has done this. The first one was my last computer and it didn't do that until about a year and a half. The other problem is I can't run the Zune software anymore, at about half-way loaded the computer temp spikes and decides it is time to die on me. This laptop is about 9 months old now. My last one finally burned up the CPU, and I mean I took it to a shop and they showed me on the board where the CPU connections had caught fire, which I guess is a perfect reason to explain the dying.

I was considering picking up another Acer here in the next 6 months, because they are inexpensive compared to other laptops, but if it over heats with that low of a load, I am scared to even risk running Creative Suite 3 (which is why I am looking at new computers anyways).

The operating stats on this one are:
2GB RAM
1.73GHz Intel Celeron 530

Any suggestions to get this computer to last for about 6 more months until I can pick up a decent laptop that won't have over-heating problems and will run the programs I want to run. I was looking at building a desktop, but I travel too much from house to house to have that be useful.
