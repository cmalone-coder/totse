Moments of Clarity
Do you find yourself having moments of clarity, where feelings or memories slip into the forefront of your mind?

The other night, I woke up in the night thinking about a moment from years ago. A vivid memory of moving out of my Mother's house sprang to mind.

These are moments where you stop to think about the emotions involved in your life.

For the past few years, I've heard the term 'emotional intelligence' bandied about. It's a term that leaves much to be desired. Are there any issues in your life where your emotions are unclear? "I don't know how I feel" seems to be something you hear fairly often from people.

What do you think having a high level of emotional intelligence means? Does it matter?

That's the whole point, though. What if you don't understand what you're feeling other than the emotion is intense. Sort of like how anxiety can be good, but it can also be bad. You can be anxious to meet a girl for a first date or dreading a test result that you know isn't going to be good. If you can't tell which is which then you don't know how to deal with it.

I agree that you have to let your emotions be what they are, but figuring out how you feel is very important, at least in my opinion. Your emotions are one of the few things in this world that you have to deal with and can't change yourself, at least not in a direct conscious fashion. You can't think, "Oh, I'm going to be happy now" or magically pull yourself out of a bout of depression. But you can do things that you know will make you happy, and thus satisfy your subconscious, causing you to feel happy.

So I do think that it's important to have a pretty solid amount of emotional intelligence, because our emotions are very important, and largely indicators of the actions we should take in life.

Someone who is living in ignorance or misunderstanding of how they feel about different aspects of their life is ignoring or misunderstanding those parts of life. I don't know about you all, but I want to understand my life.
