there is no such thing as 2 dimensions or three dimensions or even a fourth dimension.........

all this is a figment of your imagination...

the ultimate truth is ............



there is only the dimensionless......

from this which is the mind.

all the senses and other dimensions appear to spring.

to understand reality and who you are and the answers to all the big questions.....

one must understand the one sense that you truly have........

that sense is the mind.

in reality there are 6 or more senses

seeing hearing touching tasting smelling and thought(mind)

if one analysed the thought or mind sense thoroughly, one would realise that this is the epicentre from whence the other 5 senses spring........ to some people this is the epicentre from whence the other 6 , 7 , 8, or 9 senses spring.

stop studying that which you see(read)

stop studyiing that which you hear.

stop studying that which you taste

stop studying that which you smell

stop studying that which you touch.........

and start studying ones own mind.......that is the only purpose of life.

when studied thoroughly ....one will find that the mind does not really exist ......

that which exists is like movement and rest.

it is something that moves and then stops......that is all

reality is a movement and a rest.

nothing more ........nothing less

i think you will find this hard to grasp....

stop looking on the periphery.....ie the 5 senses and start looking at the epicentre.....look at being.......

im sorry if this sounds rather confusing.....

but to the very very wise ......this will make a lot of sense.

ego will be lost in the process..for that too is the periphery

peace to all living beings
