The reason I have slacked off posting here is to play more DII and all my chars are built for pvp so i know what i'm talking about. but i'll give my 2 cents on pvp

I have a lvl 66 bowzon (*JavaSlayer on USeast)built like this

stats
135 str (the hydro bow in xpac needs 135 str)
400+ dex
50 vit (use +hp stuff, i have 400 hp)
20 energy (just use mana leech)

eqip
twitchthroe uniqe studded leather
sliglty incressed attack speed +17 dex gaunts
fastest hit recovery/fastest run walk boots
27-150 dmg goth bow
goldwrap uniqe heavy belt/deaths sash
47% mf tarn helm
eye of eltich uniqe amulet
leech rings with +dex

skills:
max guided arrows
15 multi shot
20 critical strike
1 slow missle
1 valkyre
1 dodge
1 avoid
1 evade
1 decoy
20 penetrate

Ok when you get to the right lvl and have a good bow, jump into a dueling game (hit auto map and make sure its not a nightmare or hell game as you'll be losing exp if you die (which you shouldn't)

now put guided arrow on your left click and make sure sure you have your hotkeys setup so you can switch between valk, slowmissle, and multi shot.

vs lance barbs
this should be the easy match as the barb build is max a mastery max whirlwind and leap attack a few times no real surprises just keep ahead of them and pumping ga's (guided arrows) into them.

keep in mind no matter what they have to stop to iniate any attack (like ww) so keep on your toes they have to stop moving to start a whirlwind so when you hear that sound make a 45 degree turn move out of range of the lance and pump them full of arrows (the cold dmg from eye of eltich will slow them down which helps too)

vs sword shiled barbs

these guys are just as easy just take a little longer. adjust your stratige and use multi shot instead of guided arrow (you'll hit them more and block stun lock them)
if you have a ias bow sias belt sias gaunts and sias armor you can just block/stun lock them. whenever sombody blocks something with a shiled they go into a blcok animation and they can't do anything while like that and if you do 1/12 of somethings hit points it will go into a stun animation so if ou can shoot arrows fast enough you can make it to where they just lock up eating arrows and they can't do a damn thing. if you don't shoot that fast just keep in frount of them and when ever you hear that ww sound make a 90 degree turn (not 45 a exec sword doesn't have the range of a lance so turn harder and shoot even more)

vs necros
these guys are a cake walk too just summon a valk to keep his golem busy, and slow missle him. he will probably decrep you but you will still be faster than his bone spirts so just stay ahead of them and turn him into a corpse.

vs pallys
you'll probably want to switch to death sash if they are using holy freeze so you wont get chilled and slow down and hit them with multi as opposed to guided arrow as its harder to block with a sheild

vs other zons
you will probably end up slow missling each otehr if you can get close if that happens use multi shot as it acts like a barrer the more the better try and box them in with it.
if they don't bother slow missleing you keep shooting guided arrows at them and try and keep your valk between her and you she will act as a meat shield. resummon her at need.

vs sorcs
if they are good your screwed. sorcs are very unbalanced for pvp. the good sorc stratige (teleport frost orb teleport lather rinse repeat) will beat you every time. guided arrow will not ever catch a teleporting sorc they are 3x faster if you must try put on your deaths sash and just shoot guided and multi in there general direction you might get lucky

if anybody would like to duel or trade or have questions try and whisper me on east
