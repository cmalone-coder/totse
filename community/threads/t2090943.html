Re: is the erowid reccomended dose for oxy a bit low?
Well, let me first say that, with no tolerance (I mean never used it before and have no natural tolerance), 10mg would fuck you up pretty good. But the thing is, that everybody's body is different. I usually see on here that people want BLTC to calculate how much they should start out with by saying their weight, height, etc. It doesn't really work that way most of the time.

For example, I'm a pretty big guy, I'd say 6' and 170-180lbs. Being the size I am, everyone would expect me to need a lot more of a drug to get the same effects as everyone else. But the thing is with my body, is that I start out with an ultra low tolerance to everything I take. When I first started using oxy, I would basically be able to get high off a single 5mg percocet. So if I had started by taking 20mg like people say is a good dose, that'd be pretty overwhelming.

My point is that we can't really tell you how much your body will need. You're going to need to find it out for yourself. I completely agree with the people that said to just take 10mg, wait and see how you feel, then re dose if need be.
