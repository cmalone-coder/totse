Re: Can anyone tell me how to make a silencer?
Contrary to popular belief, they are not hard to make. I won't go into those expedient "pvc pipe" types or anything else like that.

Your standard metal silencer consists of approximately 8 inch of barrel extension, an outer tube a little shorter than the extension, two end caps(one threaded to attach to the barrel), a center spacer, and some window screen and steel wool or fiberglas packing.

The pamphlet by Mr. Bill Holmes on silencer making is by far the best, pictures, directions, and tips that are readable and understandable by anyone with a junior high education. It explains how to make a silencer for a .22, .25, .32, and a .38 caliber.

But this is all hypothetical, because it is illegal to do this, and none of us would ever want to break the law.

I'm not sure if this is up, but for low-caliber handguns and even some rifles, you can make a very basic silencer by taping a plastic coke bottle over the muzzle. If you fill it with steel wool 1/2 way, it reduces the sound even more, but is REALLY innaccurate. As in you've got about three feet to bank on. This removes a lot of the bullet's rifle marks as well - not enough to prevent positive ID but enough to make it weak evidence.
