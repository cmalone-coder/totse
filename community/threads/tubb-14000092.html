"Evolution and Social Darwinism

War and oppression have always been components of human history, however with the advent of Darwin's theory of evolution man had a new justification for his cruelty. Recall that the prime component of Darwin's ideas revolves around the notion that life progresses by natural selection - the survival of the fittest. Couple this with the racist teachings in the scientific world of his day and you have the rational to pursue any exploitive agenda. "Might makes right", so why not declare yourself the master race and conquer others?
Both sides of the Franco-Prussian War of 1870 used Darwinism as a rational. When Cecil Rhodes established the famous Rhodes Scholarships it was for uniting English-speaking peoples for greater racial development. Theodore Roosevelt maintained that a racial war to the finish with the Indians was inevitable and there should be a spread of English-speaking people all over the world (Morris 1989, 68-70).

The notions of racial superiority were more pronounced in Europe. Friedrich Nietzsche of the "God is dead" fame adapted Darwin's ideas and promoted a "superman" or "super-race" philosophy. He took the idea of natural selection further, suggesting that warfare, eugenics, and the merciless extinction of inferior races was appropriate. Nietzsche would later go insane.

Another German of influence was the great evolutionary biologist Ernst Haeckel (1834-1919). Haeckel is known for his "Ontogeny recapitulates Phylogeny" (the embryo re-traces evolution) contribution to the field of evolution, a theory which is now discredited but still lingers on. (Incidentally, the illustrations that Haeckel used to show embryonic development were fabricated.) Haeckel established the Monist League (monism is the philosophy that bases all reality on matter alone) and "became one of Germany's major ideologists for racism, nationalism, and imperialism" (Morris 1989, 73). One could argue that the ideas of Darwin, Nietzsche, and Haeckel were the seeds of World War I - British Anglo-Saxonism vs German Teutonism.

The ideas of racial supremacy and the survival of the fittest race reached it's zenith with the National Socialist party of Nazi Germany. The legacy of Haeckel and the Monist league turned many German scientists to monism and atheism. Adolf Hitler himself was a evolutionist. His book "Mein Kemp" (My Struggle), which sold 11 million copies in 1944, has a strong theme of the necessity of struggle, a struggle between the races. "The Jews formed a sub-human counter race, predestined by their biological heritage to evil, just as the Nordic race was destined for nobility" ... "History would culminate in a new millennial empire of unparalleled splendor, based on a new racial hierarchy ordained by nature herself". (Morris 1989, 78 - quoting Gann). One has to ask - would Hitler act the way he did if he were a creationist?

Others in Nazi Germany were evolutionists as well. The notorious head of the Gestapo, Heinrich Himmler stated that "the law of nature must take its course in the survival of the fittest" (Morris 1989, 78 - quoting Schaeffer). Joseph Mengele, the "angel of death", infamous for his gruesome experiments on humans at Auschwitz was indoctrinated in evolutionary racism in school. One biographer notes that Mengele was influenced by the notion that "some human beings afflicted by disorders were unfit to reproduce, even to live" (Morris 1989, 79 - quoting Posner and Ware).

And finally, Benito Mussolini was an evolutionist who mocked perpetual peace and asserted that "the reluctance of England to engage in war only proved the evolutionary decadence of the British Empire" (Morris 1989, 81 - quoting Clark).

Re: the kid who eats the most marbles.......
Let's ignore genes and DNA and all the little stuff. Boil it down to the nitty gritty. Evolution is variance within a species, taken to its logical conclusion.

You have a species. Over a thousand years, the species changes, adapts to its environments, and after the thousand years has passed, you can see obvious differences. That's variance within a species.

Take that same species, and extend the time frame to three million years. That's a lot more time for the species to change, and for the environment the species must adapt to, to change. Slowly, it evolves, and the variance from one end of the three-million-year time span to the next becomes so great that, when placed side by side, it becomes clear that the creatures can no longer be logically considered of the same species. In most cases, were the two to try and mate, they would fail.

Yet, one came from the other.

Evolution is a VERY slow process. You never get the proof you seek because the proof you want is based on a belief that isn't true. There is no father = ape, son = human. Scientists can make no clear deleanation (sp?) between the ancestor species and the offshoot species because there is none to make.

You can hold onto your arguments of "where did those genes come from", and I can't give you an answer for that, because I don't know. All I can tell you is how the, yes, theory of evolution functions on the surface.
