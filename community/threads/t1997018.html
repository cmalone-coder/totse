Re: Ring of Fire Accidental Discharge?
I remember what you are talking about, but IIRC, it was some sort of .22 pocket pistol, and he wasnt disassembling it. I think he thought it was unloaded and pulled the trigger.

I cant remember what gun it was, but either a Beretta or a Pheonix Arms HP-22 are the guns that I seem to recall it being, but im not 100 percent sure.

FWIW, I have a MP-25 sitting on my desk as I type this, and the disassembly doesnt seem dangerous IMO. Like all firearms, you must release the magazine and unload the chamber. The disassembly is done by releasing the firing pin spring by pulling the trigger, and simply pushing in the retainer and lifting the slide. Extremely simple, and suprisingly, the gun has been 100 percent reliable, and pretty accurate at 7 yards.
