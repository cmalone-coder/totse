Re: Anhydrous Ammonia
Its not that difficult to produce. Hell of alot easier than stealing. Mr. Clean's method on rhodium works well but can be simplified. The precooler and drier are not all that neccessary but may improve quality.

Never had much luck using a gas can for a gen. I prefer a 2liter coke bottle generator. Use an empty lye bottle for measurements. perfect ratio-add 3 lye bottles of ammonia nitrate then 1 bottle of lye and a few ml of water. i dont add any water, just rinse out bottle and sling out water leave it alittle damp.Produces around 4-6 oz of ammonia per 2L bottle generator with good system. When generator has stopped (about 45 min.)just unscrew and replace with new 2L. fittings seem to leak so just push the hose through a small hose in the cap. make sure its a tight fit, a leak is your worst nightmare. believe me.

Use 3/8' x 1/4' clear vinyl hose. longer the better. i use 60" in whole setup. This gives the gas longer to cool

moisture condenses in the hose before the dry ice condenser. Length of hose b/t gen. and condensor is setup similar to mr.cleans but without the precooler and drier. be sure the hose goes up then below the gen. then goes up above the condensor. hose makes a p-trap kinda thing. water collects at bottom and can be drained back into gen or when gen is removed.

i would use a very small diameter hose for a relief line instead of the duct tape over hole shit. no need to regulate the pressure. never had a suckback.

i found the perfect catch bottle at super wallys. go to the tupperware type section there is a 1$ 500ml sorta clear plastic square juice container witha white screw on lid and pop top. it has measurements on the sideof container. ok you have that now look for the grey sippie cup type lid that would screw on your bottle. its there findit. the grey lid (comes on more expensive container) has a rubber seal and the thing you drink from folds down to seal. this lid has 2 holes. one small one perfect for a relief line and the one under flip up straw that is perfect tight fit for 1/4' hose. ok ok this is the exciting part.. Swap the lids! get a rush every time. something about cheating wally out of the expensive lid. umm...

if you dont have fancy glass condensor or whatever mr clean uses, make one. use a 2liter, start at the bottom, take hose (leave a foot excess to go to catch) and wrap around 2or3Liter.from bottom, up. coils must be tight with absolutly no gaps between coils. this is most important part of system. you need atleast 8 coils (12 is good) that gradually spiral down no gaps no twists. smooth flowing. tape with duct tape. cut off excess top of bottle.put in ice chest with that foot of hose coming out a hole in the bottom and the long ass piece coming out hole in lid. fill chest with crushed dry ice. ice chest is set up with small ice chest below to hold catch. have about 4' of hose b/t catch and bottom of big ice chest to watch the juice drip down. looks like a chick pissing. 30lb of dry ice is perfect for my setup. not much ice is lost.

what else... uh, i usually do 1 gen bottle per 10g of pseudo. better to have more than you need. process is really easy and produces clean strong ammonia that i prefer to any tank juice.

Please dont be a fucking retard and start throwing lithium and ether into the 2 liter. i'd hate to see you on the news.

hope this helps
