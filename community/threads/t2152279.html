Here's an idea that is useful for winter lifting (which is thankfuly near =D) because a coat is helpful however cargo pants could work fine too.

With a small but decent can opener and pocket knife opening those pain the ass plastic packages can be done very quietly. Just use the can opener to cut open the long side of the packaging and then slide the knife around the other 4 sides. This plan kind of requires being in a bathroom but I do most of my lifting in there anyways, it makes a lot less noise then just prying apart the plastic. Just remember to conceal your goods before you enter the bathroom, conceal them well as if you were just going to leave the store with them in a regular shoplift because if they see you enter the bathroom with something and come out with nothing its game over =(
The inside pocket of a jacket is a good place to conceal the opener and pocket knife, I also like to bring a plastic bag in my jeans pocket and place all of the packaging in the bag when I'm done, tie it off and bury it in the garbage can under some paper towels.
Hopefully this is helpful to someone
Cheers!

If the alarm is set off though use it to your advantage, they'll think it's being screwy; just lift what you want and walk out.
