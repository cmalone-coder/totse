For there to be any decent debate or an answer from the "God-haters," you're going to have to define God. What is it exactly that they hate?

People use the term God to mean all sorts of things, and I doubt there are any two people alive who view "God" exactly the same way. Then again, no two atheists have the same worldview either. If you mean God as a generic creator (that somehow trumps the requirement of its own creation) that could or could not exist and hasn't revealed itself to humanity, well, that really gets you nowhere. You'll have to define your God in explicit terms; what its physical characteristics are (anthropomorphic/vaporous/invisible), whether it is omnibenevolent (so that the God-haters can point out the fallacies of that), whether it is omnipotent (so that the logical contradictions of that can be demonstrated), whether it is omniscient (so that those deiphobic motherfuckers who hate God can elucidate their concerns over the logical contradictions of omniscience), none of the above, etc.

I agree with you that ad hominem bullshit is stupid in debates, but frankly, I don't see any of that on My God ...

Most of the stuff here is decent discussion, and the amusing topics are good for entertainment.

I guess most atheists view the religious as idiots. To them, beliefs in God, praying, worship and "sin" seem as stupid as taking mythological creatures, Santa Claus and other blatantly human constructs seriously. It's the very nature of atheism to think religion is stupid. It's obviously in other people's natures (the religious) to buy into fantasies and easy, a priori explanations for the unexplainable.
