What happens is a pressure vessel is used to do the Birch in, with sodium.

.

First the ammonia is put in the pressure vessel.

Then the sodium, mixed.

Then the pseudo, mixed.

The reaction happens.

The other fridge gas is now put in, with the above mixture.

Shaken.

Let to settle out.

What should happen is that the meth has gone from the ammonia/sodium/pseudo mixture in to the other fridge gas.

The tank is now tapped to let the other fridge gas/meth mixture out.

This is poured into a s/steel pot and the fridge gas left to boil off, leaving only the meth.

The stinky ammonia gas/sodium/shit is still in the pressure vessel, meaning that the horribles can be disposed of somewhere else (into water?)
