7mls to 4.2 is about a 1:1.7 (I only used the pseudo for reference because you used a standard 1:1.5 with the iodine) The long time will probably make this alright too. It has been learned that about 1:1 pseudo and water is plenty.

I know for sure that it is possible to do exactly (except for the cleanliness of the precursors. That I cannot say.) the reaction you have done and come out with beautiful, very pure crystals. They look almost identical to the picture that jacked posted at the hive recently. Thats coming out of a post reaction nasty nightmare.

Post reaction fluid must be diluted with five times (minimum) its volume of hot DH2O, then run through a filter (or two coffees with a cotton in the neck, but thats under vacuum.) Dilute it even more when you get funky results that cloud and/or discolor the fluid. Don't worry. You can clean that shit up.

What most people don't understand is all that A/B shit is just the preliminary cleaning stage. The real cleanup is in the recrystallizations. That's why they are called REcrystallizations. If you didn't need them, then crystallization would be the only word you would ever see.

Gotta go.
