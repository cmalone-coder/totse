Re: Best Video Game of 2008
Left 4 Dead is by far the most innovative of the blockbuster releases this year. There's no other game that does co-op half as well, and playing as the Infected is unlike anything in any other game on the market. GTA4, MGS4, Fallout 3, GoW2, SSB:B, MK:Wii, FC2, R2 and pretty much anything else you can think of released this year are just derivatives of previous entries in the series or other games on the market. Some of them may still be great, but I wouldn't call any of them innovative.

There are definitely legitimate complaints to be made against L4D sure, there's a few bugs, some cheap strategies, and a dearth of content (but I trust Valve to keep supporting it like they have TF2), but saying it's just like all the other Source games is completely and totally retarded, and the game is just so fresh and fun that it rises above it's flaws to be one of the best gaming experiences to come along in some time.
