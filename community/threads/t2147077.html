I called up my local scrapyard and they said in order to buy the tanks off of me i would half to cut them in half. Now my question is How in the hell do i cut the damn thing in half? Im thinking of just purging the tank out and using a plasma torch. Any advice or better methods would be highly appreciated.

Re: Cutting Oxy Acetylene Tanks.
That's about all you can do. Or use an OA torch to cut the OA bottles:P. I would drill a hole in the bottom or side of them, and leave the valve open so the expanding air inside the tank can escape, if you don't the first few inches will just have the expanded air coming right back through the hole you just cut, throwing sparks and flames towards you.

I also find it weird that you need to cut it in HALF for them to be able to accept it. Most places allow just a 4-5 inch diameter hole to be cut in the side. That's the way it is around here anyway.
