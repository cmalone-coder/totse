Non-mainstream bands you should hear:

Sleater Kinney: Riot girl post punk.

Pulp: Self loathing Brit rock. Sort of RadioHeady but with more melody.

Tricky: Dark hip hop with a little slow rapping, but trust me, it's not like P-Diddy crap.

The Eels: Quality indie rock.

Persephones Bees: Quality indie rock with a Russian twinge.

The Moldy Peaches: Anti-quality indie rock.

Los Fabulosos Cadilliacs: A lot of their songs are in Spanish, but even if you don't know Spanish it's worth listening to. Just find their cover of Strawberry Fields. It's great.

Medalia: Indie goth rock.

The Velvet Teen: Indie rock.

Sonic Youth: Creepy industrial cry rock.

For Stars: Mellow indie rock.

Pavement: Mellow indie rock.

Casket Creepers: Mellow goth rock.

Ithica: Indie rock.

Higher Ground: Grunge inspired. Lost of heavy guitar.

and a lot of others. But, those for starters.
