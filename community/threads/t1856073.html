Re: kirby and australian co.
ok,,this is how friends clean the brand pills you wrote of---the solvent measurements are for 30pks--all solvents are dried----grind pills down to finest dust---spoon into 2 litre beaker and pour in 1.5litres of mineral turpentine100%---stir for 30secs then let sit for 24hours--filter off turps and throw turps away--bring 1 litre of toluene to simmering where 1000s of little bubbles appear-- then spoon in pill mass and bring back to simmering, stirring and scraping beaker bottom continuosly (this avoids any pill mass sticking to bottom and burning)---when simmering again,keep at simmering for 8 mins whilst stirring and scraping continuosly---then take off hotplate(NEVER FLAME)and allow to cool and settle for three mins---then pour through filter(for minturps/toluene/acetone washes i use three paper towels folded into one,only use handee ultra crisp white paper towels manufactured by carter holt harvey tissue australia bought in any chemist) and throw away toluene---repeat toluene wash twice more--after each wash spoon back into beaker pill mass from filter---after filtering 3rd wash allow pill mass to cool to room temp--then pour in 1 litre of freezing acetone---stir for 3 mins--filter and throw away acetone---repeat acetone wash once more---now immerse pill mass in 600ml of methylated spirits 95% and stir for 3 mins---filter off metho(for this i use coffee filters,mellitta or harris coffee brand, 10 to 12 cup size, brown unbleached papers,DONT use any other brand)(place 3 filters inside each other)and save metho(always spoon pill mass from filter back into beaker) in 4litre container---rewash pill mass with 800ml of metho stirring for 3 mins--filter and save metho---rewash pill mass with 1 litre of metho--filter and save metho--pill mass can now be thrown away---place metho into freezer for 5 hours---take out of freezer and refilter(place cotton wool balls down the funnel tube and 3 coffee filters in funnel)always return unfiltered metho to freezer whilst filtering--metho should now be clear--if not refilter using new filters--when metho is clean, then evaporate off the metho--as you get to the end of the metho, take off hotplate and allow to finish evaporating at room temp---now do an A/B extraction on the mass left after metho evaporated---filter out (coffee filters)clean white psuedo from the A/B and thats your freebase ready to go into reduction
