As has been stated, there would have to be a major event to cause a draft. Something so radical would happen most people wouldn't avoid the draft, as they would get whipped up into another patriotic frenzy. You know, "nuclear weapon being detonated in a major city" big.

But a draft definitely does produce low-quality soldiers. Almost every nation that has mandatory military service(with the notable exceptions of Israel, Switzerland, and S. Korea) is known for poor training quality, immense amounts of brutal hazing, and lack of discipline. That's not what we need to fight the kind of wars that will be waged this century.

Hopefully, any new draft wouldn't involve blatant discrimination against the poor. College waivers and whatnot were fucking disgusting. I remember that's what Bobby Kenedy said, something along the lines of "doing away with college deferments would end the Vietnam War in 6 months".

Remember though, what many people seem to be forgetting, is that the military has a "long tail and a small mouth". That is to say, actual combat units consist of a small fraction of our total strength. The vast majority of soldiers are in support roles, and will not see combat. If you could, a 4 year stint in the Air Force or Navy sounds a hell of a lot better than giving up citizenship, but that's for individuals to decide for themselves.
