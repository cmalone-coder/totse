Oh and by the way if you're looking for "enlightenment" through stream of though practice, acid is a good choice drug. While tripping one time I stopped and sat on my skateboard for a few minutes and while sitting there tripping I suddenly started to "hear" myself think. Like every moment's thought would be heard as my voice speaking it in my head and as soon as I became conscious of it, my brain kinda felt like it just slapped me and said, "Hey! I'm right here! I'm YOU!" Really weird.

Another time I tripped I became disassosiated from previous states of mind and past selves. Granted on this occasion I dropped 900ug of cid. I would think of myself 5 minutes before the present state I currently found myself in and would have trouble associating myself with my past self. I would literally ask myself, "Who was that guy, and what did he want?" when that guy would just be me previously in another room or wherever gradually making my way through time, to where I am now consiously asking who my past self was and what I was trying to accomplsih or do. Really interesting insight on time.

I can't describe all the crazy insights acid gives you. I've had entire songs written in the English language cease to have verbal meaning. Instead of hearing my language in a way I could understand, for a period of 15 seconds all language ceased to register meaning and I was hearing a song in the English language like a foreign guy would listen to an alien language he doesn't understand. Instead of words, I was hearing sounds created by an English speaking tounge that temporarily sounded like a foreign language to me. And let me tell you, English is NOT a pretty language. It sounded like a mix between German and Hebrew.
