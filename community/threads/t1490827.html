Re: Scrotal Piercing
Did you do it because you thought it would look neato? If so, be aware, scrotal piercings are a big deal. Infections are not rare, and they can be devistating. It probably doesn't look great to begin with, seeing as you aren't a piercer. Think how it will look like when your scrotum falls off. If you want to keep it and have it look good, get it done professionally. It's probably going to cost a max of 100$ to get it at a really quality studio. Putting a ring in the yucky hole you have made will not make it look good at all.

How did you pierce it...Like does it go back to front, go all the way through or go side to side. Either way...a ring isn't what you need. If you want a ring for show, get one, but dont plan on wearing a scrotal ring all the time.

Scrotal piercings should be AT LEAST 12 ga, if not bigger depending on how it is pierced. A safety pin is around 18 ga. The chances of your piercing rejecting due to the sheer weight on a TINY hole is rather large, and the chances of it getting ripped out is even larger given the tiny hole.

If you are just doing it as a pain/ cbt deal (i am assuming that you want to keep it, but in case it's the pain you enjoy...) check out the bme shop, buy play piercing needles...there you go.

In short...if you want to keep it and make it look good, take out your safety pin and pay the piercer to do it .

BTW...if it's back to front and all the way through, its going to cost you a lot more...that would be considered a trans-scrotal, wouldn't heal, and is a minor surgery, not a piercing.
