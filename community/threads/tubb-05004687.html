Want an EASY AS FUCK way to smoke your weed?

Make a toilet paper/paper towel roll shotgun. Either one will work just as good, and construction is very simple. All it reuires is the tube, a piece of tin foil, and a pen or somehting to poke a hole.

So, take you EMPTY roll, and make sure theres no paper left on it. Now, about 1.5 inches form on end, make a hole with your pen. Now, use your thumb, and slowly p[ress it into the hole, widdening it to the size of your thumb. Now take a piece of tin foil about 3 inches high/wide and place your thumb in the center of it, then push the rest of the tin foil around your thumb. This will be your bowl. Now, while its still on your thumb, gently poke about 5-10 SMALL holes with your pen. A pin would work a bit better but it doesnt really matter. Finally, place the 'bowl' into the hole you made. Stick it in so as it goes in about .5 inches, then just spred the excess foil along the sides of the roll. You now have a homemade shotgun.

To use it, place your bud into the bowl, and put the furthest end from the bowl against your mouth [as if youre yelling through the tube] and put one hand over the other end of the tube, covering it well. Then, light it and VERY VERY VERY SLOWLY INHALE!

You must inhale slowly, trust me. It doesnt matter how experienced you are. Smoke comes out of that bowl at a very hot temperature and it does have much time to cool before it reaches your mouth. [there is a slight advantage to using a paper towel roll as its bigger, giving the smoke mroe cooling time, but dont stress yoruself out searching for a paper towel roll if you cant find one] And even te most experienced smokers will tell you that yes, it does burn your throaght! As you gian experience the burn doesnt really bother you that much though.

Another reason to inhale slowly, is because weed tingles your thraoght/lungs. I cant explain the feeling, but I know everyone who smokes out there knows what Im talking about. This is worse then the burning, as this is probably the main reason for coughing. If you inhale fast youll get too much smoke in there andyoull cough it right back up. Dont worry, as you do get used to the tingling sensation as you grow in experience. The only tingling that really effects me to the point of coughing now, is the tingling I get when I take a nice fat bong rip [the water in the bogns cools the water, so your throaght wont burn, however this makes the hits a lot larger, and the smoke expands in your lungs. I dont suggest bongs for beginning smokers too much. Its best to get used to the regular burn] and its not even that bad.

Happy smoking.....
