Terpenes.

"while THC is the "fuel" behind a marijuana high and the other cannibanoids present in marijuana do cause certain effects, the main determinates of a marijuana buzz are terpenes. What are terpenes? They're aromatic hydrocarbons that produce odors and flavors when they come into contact with our nose and tongue. (if you smell or taste something, it's because of a terpene). The cannabis plant is capable of producing (in various levels) all the terpenes known to man--which is why different strains can have such wildly different flavors and odors. Blueberry, lavender, even chocolate (Chocolate Chunk Strain). Aside from triggering odors and flavors terpenes can also induce other effects in humans and animals (this is the basis for aromatherapy). When the terpene that produces the lavender scent was purified and applied to rats they became extremely lazy and didn't move unless they had to (sound familiar). Of course not all terpenes are the same and neither are all strains of Marijuana (some makes you up and motivated). "

http://www.nevergetbusted.com/ngbbb/about791.html

http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&q=cannabis+terpenes

Certain strains will make you feel like you've become super man almost instantly. I have no idea which terpenes that would be though.

And it's more noticable sometimes, depending on the particular terpenes and how fresh the bud is and when I say that, I don't really mean when it's harvested, although that plays a role too. When the calyxs are not damaged much and the bud isn't that dry, opening up those calyxs and smelling them can be very interesting.

Most bud sold loses much of those original terpenes because it gets smashed and molested and many are very volatile..

The environment, growing conditions, etc, can play a large role in which terpenes and in what amount.
