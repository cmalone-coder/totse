These are quite common here, especially with the bodyguards of high ranking criminals and drug lords. They will usually sport either a G17 converted to full auto or a G18, either with a 33 rd. mag, plus an extra pouch with 2 or 4 mags. I bought a 32 rd. mag for my HP once, out of impulse. When I went to the range I found it consistently jammed on rounds 25, 17, and 5. The only advantage was it afforded a vertical two handed grip on the pistol which neutered recoil, dramatically improving shot placement. But never with more than 5 shots.
