Re: Possible to convert dry poppy pods to smoke-able form?
do a water extraction on the powdered pods. (very hot, not boiling, water for a good amount of time, filter, reduce). do not evap all the way though, just reduce until you have half of what you started with. now put it in a container(water bottle or something) and place in freezer. freeze about 75% and throw that out. evap the remaining liquid, scrape, smoke, super potent, match head size piece is a recommended starting point.

the larger latex molecules go into the ice while the smaller opiates fall out of the mix right as it's freezing. best tek i've ever heard of. enjoy.
