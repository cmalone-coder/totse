I'm with you 100% and I believe I have asked the same question, back when Pinay was a newbie. She did come into the forum with an attitude, but thats nothing new most people have a gripe or two. I think I'm on the same wave length as you. The question is what can make a person give up on love. The only reason Pinay's name comes up is that her posts make it seems she might have an answer.

To try answer the question myself, I had a long "non-relationship" that I have spoken about many times on here over the years. U sed to write to... I wrote over 100 pages in the form of letters (I have a soft copy archive) and 90% of the content is about this "non relationship" and what happened, what I thought it meant etc etc. Love does run your life when you let it. Some here have said love is a "danger" to logic. This is true. I have masses amounts of work due sortly (honours year and thesis is almost due) yes I wasted an entire day I didn't have on poletics. Why? Because the poletics involve a student club I set up and although I am no longer on the committee or anythign like that, I felt very strongly about the need for it and when new people try things that could endager it... I want to protect it and what it stands for. My comment to a member of staff the other day was that my head says I shoudl just ignore it and move on, but my heart says I shouldn't let it destroy itself. This is commitment to an ideal rather than to a person, but its fairly similar when we talk about battles between head and heart.

To dip into religion for a second, there is a section of the old testiment that read "you shall love the lord your god will all your heart, with all your mind and with all your resources". The heart is what rules us as people, the emotions are what make us human and love is the strongest of them all. To lose ones heart is to lose ones humanity. The mind is next most important, to believe something and not give thought to it... is a wasted effort. Blind love is dangerous, in some cases it is madness, to kill someone because your love told you so... is the plot of too many bad movies. Resources can be explained simply as put your money where your mouth is.

So to summarise, you need both the mind and the emotion to be a full human being. Any less and you are either mad or without humanity.
