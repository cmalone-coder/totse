Deleting Inactive Users
Since UBB stores all user data in text files, rather than in a real database, eventually we're going to hit a point where there are so many users on this system that the system will start to break in strange and unusual ways.

To avoid this problem, as of today anyone who registers as a new user and then posts no messages for 90 days will have their account deleted.

Lots of folks sign up and then never post messages. Since the only reason you need an account is so that you can post messages, there's no reason to waste disk space on these inactive accounts. If you ever post just one message, your account is safe.
