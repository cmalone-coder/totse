Your maturity and the preferances and intrests you've expressed throughout the forums at totse, are what led me to believe you to be alot older than you are and just the kind of person I like to be around, if I'm around anyone that is, generally the company of cats is my preferance.

Compassion, that's the word.

My son is growing up very quickly now, twice as quickly as the first 7 years went the second 7. I don't know when it was, but I wish I'd known that the last time I read him a story would be the last time I read him a story.

Life is good! Whether you are happy or sad at any particular time. Bittersweet irony can be a very intoxicating thing.

I wish I could say that seeing well-thought out plans of action coming to fruition was equally as intoxicating, alas my good advice is based mostly on the irony thing, in conjunction with an eternally curious intellect and an aversion to finding myself thinking "Dammit!,now how the hell did this happen and, how am I going to/can, I fix it?

I imagine that the fruition thing wouldbe just as powerful an emotion, imagine being the operative word.

I envy you in a way, if your openess persists and your curiosity isn't squelched by anything or anyone, you should be very wise and very comfortable with your life alot sooner than many other people will.
If they ever are at all.

Knowing your age makes me a little sad, but I am not a pessimist, so I'll cast no negative aspersions or make dismal forcast's regarding those special qualities,(desire and ability to care)

If the good advice of others was something that was widely utilized, by those to whom it would genuinely be helpful, I can't help but think the world would be a much different place. I could/would never listen to anyone,.. hardheaded to a fault.

No one had ever been able to prove God to me, or anything else to explain existence and the cosmos, so I figured my brain was as good as anyones and made a habit of using it rather than being able to comprehend and embrace the notion that someone else could know what was good for me, or what wasn't. Oh well

My part in this thread has made me happy, which is a really nice feeling, and somewhat of a novelty in my life.

But it's about something you've been troubled by and so, more than anything, I hope that what's been said will really be of help to you. If it is, then that is waaaaay cool.

I used to pray for my little sister, To(unproven)God because I was told to, as well as, in the same breath,(abundant proof available) Mother Nature, I'd pray that she was always happy and that no one ever hurt her feelings or made her as sad as I'd been at times, that nothing bad would ever happen to her and that she would always have the things she wanted and loved.

My friend Greg told me that since I hadn't been praying to Jesus, that God had not heard any of my prayers at all, Nada! zip!..He had been raised going to church and was unwavering in his assertion that only through Jesus Christ could one communicate with God, he couldn't prove anything, of course. I had said my prayers because I wanted to believe and because I'd been raised by people I loved to do so, really I always hoped they were heard, but always had my doubts as to the existence of the intended receiver.

I told Greg I thought he was an idiot and that if he really really believed in a God for whom that was the rule, then I didn't know what to say other than what the fuck ever, that isn't the God I want to believe in, after that we didn't really see each other too much and to this day he is still sort of, well, what to say???? Still Greg.

What's funny is that recently I was thinking and something occured to me. What if Greg was right? What if not including praying to Jesus ,to forward my prayers to God, had really renederend them to the "dead letter file?" I have always doubted that vehemently.

My sister recently told me that she has never had a cavity, filling ,or any other anything wrong with her teeth, she is 34yr.s old!.......nada! She did marry a guy who turned out to be a real jerk, he hurt her feelings, made her sad, took away the things that she liked and loved and I am certain that she has been just as, if not more heartbroken than I ever was.

So here's the funny part, could it be that as Greg said, God didn't hear a word and that somehow all of my prayers had ended up at the Tooth Fairy's??????hmmmmmm

That you retain your compassion through good times and bad, is my prayer for you.
I haven't prayed specifically for anyone in quite a while. Do your part and don't forget to brush your teeth!
