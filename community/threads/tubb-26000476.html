Re: Meth Lab - Equipment Needed?
Alright, for educational purposes only...

First, you need your recipe. Your also going to have to have a reactions flask. Filters, coffee filters doubled and tripled will work. Funnel, and a sepratory funnel. You will also need several other glass containers, jars will work. You will need a heating source, and electric hotplate will work best. You will need your precursors, and distilled H2O. You will need hoses of various lenghts. You will need a glass dish that can go from a hot oven to an ice cold sink of water, without cracking. I think this should be about all that is absolutely needed, I'm sure if i've missed anything, someone will let us know. Hope you find this helpful....
