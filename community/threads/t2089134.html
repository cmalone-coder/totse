I'm the last person on Earth.
Well, that's how I feel a lot of times. It's not really a physical loneliness per se, but more that I don't connect with anyone. Women are all just meat, men are just tools for personal gain, and I can't seem to shake this mentality. A lot of times, I feel like I'm the only real person and that everyone else is just kind of...scenery. The result is that I'm very, very bored a lot of times. I have interests and hobbies, but when you feel like you're the only real person, things starts to feel futile.

Now, I don't literally think that I'm real and everyone's just in my head or anything like that, but I feel disconnected from everyone. It's like watching a movie. I see all these interactions, but I can't be a part of them. When I go hang out at my friends' house, I see them doing things and bonding and such, and I'm there, but I'm just not responding to anything.

I'd also say I'm a fairly sociable guy. I go out on weekends, I can initiate conversations, and I can hold conversations. But at the same time, I'm usually consciously aware that I don't give a shit about what these various people are saying or doing. Even when I try...I just feel completely unmoved by anything. Honestly, it's weird to use the word "feel", since a lot of times I don't feel anything. Things inside me are just static.

So...1) Does anyone else have this feeling of being alone even when they're surrounded by others?
2) Can it be fixed? Should it be fixed? On one hand, I feel more free than others, but on the other hand...I still feel alone since I don't connect with anyone.
