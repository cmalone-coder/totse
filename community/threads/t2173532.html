This^. I've never personally had a full on bad trip, I've had periods in trips though where I get really angry, or down about something, but I've read SO many times that with hallucinogens, if you're having a bad trip, you need to try and make it through it. If you do, it can have extremely positive effects on your life. Think about the bad trip as kind of like suppressed feelings or thoughts that came up, and if you don't deal with 'em, shit's just gonna get worse.

But if you're really freaking, change the setting you're in. It's been proven that setting can affect your trip. Change the music, change where you are, etc. And finally, if you still don't feel better, just take a small dose of benzos or sedatives, just enough to calm down a little bit. If you end up ending your trip though, I think you'll regret it later (so don't take enough to pass out). Hope this helps.

Let me tell you how I fixed my first bad trip off shrooms.

My friend got a hold of some and we decided to skip school and try them out. I had done them before so I was somewhat experienced in what the effects would be. I consumed about 1.5-2 grams at 11:00am. I had not eaten any breakfast yet.. anyway were sitting there (3 of us. 1 didn't eat any he just smoked some hash with my other friend who had eaten some.) Were at the dudes tiny ass house and it was hella messy and cluttered. i started feeling nauseous so went to the backyard to get some air. I could tell they were starting to kick in as i was looking around at trees and rocks in the yard. I still felt sick but i went inside to try talking to my friends. We sat at the kitchen table, which was cluttered with junk, roaches, guts from swishers, things like that. I tied drinking some ice water to maybe settle my stomach but i just couldn't anymore. i went to the bathroom and started yakin, conveniently just as the peak of the high started kicking in. And let me tell you, the only weakness i have when it comes to doing drugs is nausea and vomiting. Not fun times at all. So after i threw up i didn't feel any better. i went back to my friends and still wanted to throw up. I ended up calling my other good friend to pick me up. We all got in his car and just cruised around, listening to music and talking with the windows rolled down for a nice breeze. After that I felt sooo much better and forgot completely about feeling sick.

Just try to be in a relaxed place with comfortable surroundings and close friends. Maybe smoke some weed before to relax you and then again after you consume them. Another thing to try is take a small, comfortable dosage to begin with, then take more once you're ok with how you feel. Also, don't over think it. just relax and let it kick in don't ask yourself if you think you're feeling it yet. Pretend its just a regular day.
