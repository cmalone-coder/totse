Re: how long for OC withdrawl help please
People say that addiction and withdrawal to opiates are all the same.


Not true!

They're all basically the same but some are worse and take longer than others. Oxy is one that is a little worse than heroin. But methadone is the worst!

W/D's should last about a week or two for the physical, but will last most of the rest of your life mentally.

Quitting cold turkey is bullshit. There's no reason to put yourself through that hell if you don't need to. Some people say that they need to go through the pain so that they won't go back. Well, that just doesn't work. I had one friend who quit cold turkey about 5 times over as many years and he always slipped back.

I smoked heroin for over 15 years. I quit by myself and I did it by detoxing [slowly backing off the dosage and lengthening the times between doses]. It took about 2 week, but there was almost no pain at all. I've been off for three years now almost.

Now for those ready to jump up and say: Well yeah it was easy for you, you have morphine and hydrocodone. There was a 6 to 8 month gap between when I quit heroin and when I found the Dr I have who prescribes me the meds.

Good luck.
