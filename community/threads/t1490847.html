Re: Why does high school hurt so much?
If you want to take a martial arts class without all the martial arts crap, you're kind of missing the point. That's not to say they're necessarily using the learning of Korean as a tool in your martial arts training; it'd be nice to think so, but not all teachers are like Mr. Miyagi. If you don't like it, find another class. Having a good teacher and a program you enjoy is more important than the art you're learning.

Anyways, back to the point. How to ask a girl out depends on the situation. Just walking up to someone and doing it is hard as hell (or rather, it's easy, but extremely difficult to get a yes). Don't think along those lines. It's a bit of an "advanced move", if you will. Keep it simple. If you're hanging out with people, open your mouth and start talking. Easy, relaxed conversation is the way to go.

You don't have to do it with the intention of asking her out; in fact, it's generally better if you don't, since that puts less pressure on you. You won't click with the majority of girls you come across, probably the vast majority, so don't go in thinking you want a date. Just see where her head is at, and if you two get along, ask her if she wants to do something sometime (the more specific, the better). If she has a boyfriend, oh well; if she simply says no, oh well; if she says yes, great.

In short, the best position to be in is the one where you have nothing to lose. If you go up to a girl all nervous and shaky, or even confidently, ask her out, and get rejected, it's a letdown. You had one purpose in mind, and it didn't work out as you had hoped. A friendly conversation is something positive whether or not she goes out with you, so you either gain less or gain more in that instance.

The only downside is that if you don't get out much, your chances of coming across this situation regularly are slim. But if you don't get out much, then deep down, meeting and getting to know new people isn't that important to you. Sure, it can get lonely; I'll gladly attest to that. But the stress of loneliness is many times less than that of not knowing where you stand with some girl you have the hots for.
