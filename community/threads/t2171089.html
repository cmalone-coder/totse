Vyvanse is dextroamp with a lysine group attached. To turn it into pure dextroamp, it is NOT a simple a/b extraction, so please know what you are talking about before trying to make others look stupid. The way the time release works isnt the capsule, but the stomach and intestinal enzymes, specifically trypsin, which cleaves that lysine group. A very crude extraction without proper reagents and labware will get you pretty low yields so yea, it's pretty "prodrug" and most ppl looking to snort or make it release faster are SOL. It does go extremely well with over-the-counter cimetidine (Tagamet). 400mg cimetidine and 50mg vyvanse taken at the same time will get you speedin for a good 10 hours (almost a constant peak). After that, your comedown won't be very bad at all but you won't sleep for another 5 hours.
