A Voice for Moderation
I love a good pun! Others might also find it ironic for me to be one calling for moderation in the moderation department.

Anyway, this is just a suggestion from me to the new Mods: Even though you have the ability to edit someone's post, try to avoid it unless it's really called for. I didn't always hold this view, but experience has shown me that it causes more trouble than it's worth and can even boarder on the abuse side of things if taken too far.

I still will take exception to when people say something very out of line about me or mine - or other Mods. But even then, most times I will just delete the offending text....Not always though.


One other little thingy I'd like to pass on. This is sorta an unwritten rule or guideline or a sorts....If there is a thread you feel should be locked, but another Mod from the forum has posted in it, that is kinda an indicator that the other Mod approves of the thread and so please don't close it.

Of course, if the thread has turned to shit since said Mod posted, it'd then be fair game to close.

If you see a thread that is closed and you feel rather strongly that it should be open, go ahead and reopen it, but state why you're doing so, so the other Mod doesn't feel any toes were stepped on.

When a Mod from another forum posts in one of my forums, and it is something I would normally close had it been any other user, I try to give a little extra leeway to them. But I have closed threads - not often, mind you - made by other Mods because they had no place at all in the forum.
