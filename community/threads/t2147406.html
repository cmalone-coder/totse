nah. It has to do with conformity. Through clinical studies on conformity, Soloman Asch found that our psyches and logic will just go to complete shit if others around you(confederates in the study) are doing it a certain way, even if your logic is correct. In the study, volunteers werelined up in a straight, horizontal row. Participants were required to take an extremely simple test: researcher would hold up a card with a 3/4" line on it, and participants would have to match the line on the card with four lines of different lengths( a la Multiple choice". There was only one real volunteer(that didn't know what was up), and the rest of the "volunteers" were actually actors that were told by Asch to purposefully give wrong answers, even going as far as matching the 3/4" line with a 2" line. As long as the confederates unanimously chose the same answer, the real volunteer would always go with the group. This was found to be completely unintentional.

So basically, Take, for example, a kick back with a well-sized group of straight-edge friends, and you are the only one doing acid(a "hard drug" for most still tuned in), and that we assume that the majority of your friends believe that acid will burn holes in your spine. When all of your friends start to "worry" about you( fuck drama whores), then you actually have a chance of agreeing with them against your welfare. When you have a large group of people that all have strong opinions, another effect can be observed. Groupthink. Groupthink is basically a newspeak(ha APA uses Orwells words for legitamate science) term for the psychological attributes observed in a dynamic group. Anyways, what happens is that the group's(including yourself) thoughts about drugs will steapily escalate relative to the belief density in the group. This leads to bad trips and self doubt/ ego death, which are not a good combination.
