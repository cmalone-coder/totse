Re: simple weed questions
1)how do u tell weed apart, wat should i look far wen looking at weed

Tell it appart from what? Weed looks like clumps [in all different sizes] of green stuff. Sometimes it'll have some white powder on it, or maybe some little red hairs in it.

2)how does good week smell like, like is there a certain smell to look for

It smelles like weed. I can't explain the smell, but it has a very distinct odor. Before you buy your weed or whatever, take a whiff of some oregano just so you dont end up buying that. It also has a distinct odor.

3)does the qaulity of weed affect ur high or anything, or is the quality just so u do less

If the strains are the same then the quality will not effect the type of high, but yes, just how high you get. Better quality, means you smoke less and waste less money. HOWEVER sometimes bad quality doesn't simply mean low grade weed. Sometimes bad quality refers to weed thats been laced [sprayed] with chemicals and other toxic agents. Ask specifically, no, demand to know whether or not the weed was sprayed with ANYTHING. And if it was, don't listen to the person if they say 'its okay to smoke'. And the better the quality, the less chance of it having been laced with anything.

Edit: The red hairs and white powder are signs of good quality weed. The white powder are the resin glands, which is hat gets you high. Weed that has lots of visible resin glands is obviously much better then weed that doesnt. As for red hairs, I'm not exactly sure what they are in plant terms, but I can say weed with red hair is usually better.
