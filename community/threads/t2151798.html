Re: Smoking joints in the publics!!!!
Agreed, it depends on the area. Some places you can walk downtown in a decent crowd while smoking a joint. Other places this isn't such a good idea. I'd gauge your situation. As long as it's not obnoxious (blowing smoke in children's faces or something) I don't see a person getting bothered a whole lot, even if it's noticed by a few people. I'd try to keep out of smell-range, if your location is kind of iffy/questionable. If it's important you could probably find a way to mask the smell by mixing hash with tobacco, but that's a bit of work and I probably wouldn't bother with it.

And of course don't carry a bunch of illegal shit while you're doing it, encase you do get stopped. For me, my area is pretty cool and I don't worry much about police, but it just makes sense not to draw attention to yourself while holding some damnable evidence or something. Joints burn quickly and tossing the roach/filter leaves you with no evidence but smelly/sticky fingers and a (hopefully) decent blood THC content. So most times if the cops do roll by, the joint will probably be gone by then. I don't think it's likely you'll be stopped by authorities, but if someone calls the cops, and all you had is your one (now combusted) joint , they won't have much on you. Besides, how is taking a nice walk and smoking a joint a negative thing? It's not like you're raping children. It actually pretty nice and wholesome... like a picnic or something.

Where I live (Adelaide in SA), you can toke up in public, and as long as you aren't bringing attention to yourself, nobody cares. Also walked up and down a pedestrian mall toking, and nobody hassled me, and there's millions of CCTV cameras, rentacops, and a cop-shop in that mall.

On the other hand though, if you're like some people I know, and you're a dickhead about it, then they will react to it. We were watching these two horseback cops arresting these two other stoners, who had been yelling/swearing at some kids or something, all the while we we sitting on a bench (in plain view of the cops), obviously stoned, and they didn't even give us a second look.

Oddly enough, the only time I've ever had problems with cops and drugs (in general) has been at house-parties or nightclubs. But when I've just been walking around stoned/tripping/drunk (I'm underaged), not a soul has ever bothered me.
