Personally, I'm not a huge fan of rockabilly/sailor tattoos, but to each his own.

I like tattoos that come from the heart. I think tribal designs are a little played out, but I like stark, minimalist black and white compositions. They tend to age well, while hyperdetailed ones just like like a blur over time.

As far as piercings go, I'm not a huge fan of eyebrow rings. They just look flimsy, like they could be ripped out at any time. I admit I have a weakness for overtly sexual piercings like the middle of the lower lip and tongue. Genital piercings are okay as long as they're well cared for. I tend to think that less is more. I used to have 8 earrings in ear ear, and I think they look better now that I just have two large-ish guage ones each. A few carefully placed amendmants to the flesh look better than a lot placed haphazardly.

Tattoos can be very sexy if done right, because they tell you something about the bearer. If they were self-designed and chosen with a lot of care, they can be an insight into the very heart of someone. It's a form of nonverbal communication. You can tell about what they like, where they're from, their overall view on life without even speaking a word. Asking someone about their tattoo is a great conversation starter, so it makes it easier to talk to people.

And if it's a directly-off-the-wall tattoo they got while they were drunk, you'll know that they're impulsive and make bad decisions. Which can work in your favor.
