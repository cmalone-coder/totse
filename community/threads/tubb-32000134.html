Re: Tips of popping your back ?
The real reason that popping your joints is not good for you is because your joints are covered with cartilage, the exact same stuff in your ears and nose. The when you pop your joints you are actually scraping the cartilage against itself and grinding it down. The more often you do this the worst because cartilage really doesn't grow back. Your body can repair a small amount of the damage that you do, but not all. Cartilage for the most part doesn't grow back. When your cartilage gets worn down from popping it is extremely vulnerable to arthritis. So the more you pop your joints the more likely you will be old hunched over with large painful joints. I hope this helps.
