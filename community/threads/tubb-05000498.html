Sassafras Oil Separation/Purification
The following is a method I tried to separate Safrole from Sassafras oil, but when I tried to wash the oil layer with water I could not see any separation of layers, so I could not wash. Also, the recipe does not state how much water to use. Help....anyone????

Starting weight: 112 g sassafras oil

Placed 454g 28% Acetic Acid in a 2L Sep funnel. Added 112g Sassafras oil. Shook for 3 minutes, waited 15 min for layers to form. Separated layers, kept the bottom layer, threw away the top layer (acetic acid, eugenol & other solubles).

Here the recipe calls to wash the solution 2X with dH2O. No amount was stated so I used a equal amount of dH2O. I didn't get any separation. Can anyone give me some advice as to why I cannot get any separation> Or, how much water should I be washing my solution with? The following is the rest of the recipe:

Add 100g of denatured alcohol, no layer forms as the product is misible in ethanol. Add 20 grams of dH2O. This pulls the ethanol and other product into the top layer, bottom layer containing some ethanol and safrole. Separate layers, place bottom layer into a 2L beaker. Take the temp right to 234C. The ethanol and water come off <=100C... The safrole starts to boil @ 232C, then came to a full boil and maintained 234C. The product is yellow orange in color. (The chemist of this sythesis checked it with 5% NaOH solution to see if any eugenol was left behind, no preticipate formed)

Re: Sassafras Oil Separation/Purification
By the way, if anyone is wondering about the above sythesis (from Total Sythesis II)...the book made a boo-boo. When you wash the Sassafras oil with 28% Acetic Acid, instead of keeping the bottom layer as the book asks, we should be retaining the top layer of oil. That's why I couldn't see any layer forming in the next step, because water is miscible in Acetic Acid and Eugenol. But when you wash the top layer of the first step...separation is highly visible. Tried this last night (since this was my first time purifying sassafras oil, actually second including the screw up above) got through the whole synth, safrole's temp went right up to 234C and stayed...removed from heat and when the safrole cooled I hit it with a 5% NaOH solution and no participate formed.

What a shitty thing to deal with for a first timer like myself. I love the book, but you'd think that people would proof read it enough so there wouldn't be any mistakes like that!
