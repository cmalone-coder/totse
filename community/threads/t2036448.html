So now what?
Okay, so my boyfriend and I have been together for over a year now (lived together for 8 months) and we both love each other a shitload. Every single day at work I look forward to coming home to him. But that's not to say that everything is perfect, we have a tendency to bicker but when we do fight, we're able to get over it quickly and get back to loving each other.

So everything is good, and I really want to stay with him...But I don't know if I want to be with him forever. And as much as I love him, there's a little part of me that says "what's the point of staying together if you're not going to get married?" And I know it's stupid to think about that right now because we're both so young, but I can't seem to get that thought out of the back of my mind. Why am I building my life around this person if it might come crashing down a year or two from now?

I guess what I'm asking is if it's okay to stay in a relationship, even if you have doubts about the future. I mean, I guess it is...but obviously I wouldn't have posted this if I agreed with that.

I haven't really talked about this with him but I guess I should. But it's this crazy talk about marriage that scares boys off...

Argh, I'm not sure if I'm getting my point across, but if you do understand where I'm coming from can you please post your advice/experiences?

Re: So now what?
I usually take a similiar approach to relationships and almost sometimes end up thinking it's pointless to stay in one if you're not sure you want to be with that person forever.

The thing is, you love him. Right now [as far as I can tell] you feel he's the only one for you. He makes your day and shit. In the end I always say what happens happens. So maybe you won't be together forever; you're together now and right now that's all that matters. When things start to come to the point where you want out, you'll leave. Until things get that bad it's best not to make a thing of it.

Don't worry about wasting time in a relationship that's going nowhere because you're young and if you think of it, half the things you do in your life could be considered a waste of time. If something [or someone] truly 100% better for you comes along you'll know it and you'll find a way to go with it.

Sorry for the rambling. I was actually meaning to go to bed, hopefully that made sense.

Basically, if you're happy right now then just stop worrying about the future [it'll get here when it gets here] and be happy. For goodness sakes.
