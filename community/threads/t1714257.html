Watch out for K-mart
Today i went to my local K-mart to get some condoms. i hate buying those things so i was just going to steal them.

I went in the store and grabbed the condoms, now usually i just open the box and take them. Although, this time i had a weird feeling. I noticed that there were more cameras than there used to be.

So, I took the box into one of the back aisles with no cameras on it. I opened the bottom of the box and was about to get the condoms out, when i remembered about a post on TOTSE. It was one on how to tell if undercovers were after you by stashing the box and staying in the store for about 10 mins and coming back to see if the box was gone(for evidence).

So i put it in the furniture aisle and went to the electronics aisle to play some games and kill a little time. After i was done i went back to the aisle i stashed the box at. As i was walking by it i noticed a plain clothed guy messing with the box and eyeing me.

After that i knew they knew i was going to steal them. So i decided to leave. I got outside and halfway into the parking lot when someone yelled at me. I turned around and it was a guy (not the same one that picked up the box). He asked me where the condoms were. I told him i didn't have them and showed him that there was nothing in my pockets and i just left the box in an aisle. He kept trying to get me to go back in the store and show him where the box was. I said no a few times and he threatend to call the cops on me. I said go ahead I didn't steal anything. Then I left.

Morale of the story is, watch out for K-Mart. After they came out of bankruptcy they have been boosting security and i guess hiring undercovers.

Be careful shoplifers!
