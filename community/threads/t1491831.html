Re: Need some advice....Lately I'm shying away from relationships...
Haha, yeah I know, that's what the ellipsis was for.

So then what's the deal with you two? Is she attracted to you? I usually don't encourage long-distance relationships but there's nothing wrong with talking on the phone.

The more you get to know her, the more you'll be able to define what you like and what you don't. You'll be able to recognize what's soo good. Once you make these connections in your head, you'll be able to receive other girls easier. You'll recognize which features they lack and which one's they have.

If you pick it apart, it's much easier to deal with then if you look at the situation as a whole.

As far as being nostalgic, I think, by admitting to yourself that you do this, you're just helping the cycle go on and on.

To tell you the truth, it's hard to get out of a state of mind. After a while, you just wake up and that's how you think. But, if it's messing with you so much that you can't connect with other girls, than you really need to do yourself a favor and try to change it. How? I have no idea. How long it'll take.. it's up to you.

But, if you're planning on NOT have a relationship with this girl than you're better off finding someone else.

BTW, when I say "know someone" I don't mean you know how she is around you. That's just one facet of a person. Often, this is not a direct reflection of who they are as a whole. You can't judge someone on 10 days of knowing them. For instance, you don't know how she'll react in a crisis, you (most likely) don't know her parent's names and birthdays, you don't know her past and all the gamut of emotions she has developed from experience. There are so many aspects to a person that you don't see after a week, month, or year of knowing them. Stuff like that comes out with time.
