Hm, I'm pretty sure anyone that refers to a larger joint with tobacco mixed in as a spliff is european. Mostly I've heard Americans refer to any big joint as a spliff, and I'm pretty sure jamaicans say that too.

Either way. It all depends on the weed, who I'm smoking with, and the setting.

If it's really really good weed and I'm somewhere comfortable, I always want to use a bong, or at least a nice glass bowl. Just to not waste as much as possible and get the full effect.

For smoking middy to high mid to low goody weed (anywhere in that range) by myself, I'll usually roll a small or medium sized joint. I tend to be conservative with my weed when I'm by myself, because you can only really get so high, and theres no reason to waste a bunch of weed on yourself when you really just want to get a decent high going and watch aqua teen or some shit.

For smoking the above mentioned weed with some friends, it's all about the blunt. No fucking bullshit phillys or white owls, I always use either game or dutch masters brand. You gotta take the outer leaf off real nice, cut open the inner leaf, roll with the inner leaf, then re wrap it in the outer leaf. Any other blunt is false. Those flavored blunt wraps aren't bad though.

The grav bong is nice too, but I usually reserve that for special occasions when I feel like actually making one.

I also have this little metal one hitter that looks just like a cigarette. You pack the weed in one end, and you can hit that thing anywhere.
