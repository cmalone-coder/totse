Aerial Salutes being Discontinued
I've had the pleasure of finding these small single shot tubes called Sammy Shots. They are damn loud for being about the size of a small fountain, easily louder than a standard sized artillery shell. I would guess it has about as much flash powder as an M-80... When I set one off in my backyard I hear the boom reverberate for several seconds.

So, now they are pulling these things off the shelf and replacing them with imitation ones. No more boom, just some shitty sparks and whistles.

Theres one way to tell the difference though, the good ones have a red plastic base, whereas the new shitty ones have a black base.

I guess I'm lucky this thing has slipped through the cracks this long.

I'm just passing this info on as it may be helpful to some of you.

Have a safe 4th everyone. Don't need anymore reasons for the firework nazis to make more shit illegal.
