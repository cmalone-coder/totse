shadow people
What follows are actual encounters which I am trying to explain away a rationally as possible.
I am not a drug user or suffering from mental problems that could cause hallucinations (I think)
nor do I have visual or optical problems.

Recently i have been getting glimpses of humanoid figures in shadows around my house, more exactly, in my room, more exactly in the two opposite corners of my room, wherein one is the edge of my bed. These figures are always morphing from humanoid to blobs of ephemeral *is that really there*
visual disturbances.

I am not bullshitting. I have been looking around the net for other reports of experiences only to come up with stuff on sleep paralysis or alien abductions.

Now let me tell you, these figures do not seem evil, in fact, they seem to be more observant, and they are always standing still or moving very slowly until I turn towards them and they flit away.
Now when they move away, they almost completely disappear except for this weird sort of wavy, currenty movement through the air like a ripple, sometimes I even feel the air. This is felt on skin even though there are no drafts in my room and the window is firmly shut.

2 nights ago I had a freaky encounter where one persistent *entity* was messing about unclearly around my feet in bed. When I looked in its direction
I will damned if I didnt see a sort of compression in the air followed by a tiny spark that was actually audible!

Now I do get the occasional freaky moment of sleep paralysis, but this is never accompanied these shadows. Or, maybe I am in a position where I cant observe them.

Also, I would like to note that on several occasions, these things have touched me.
I dont see a hand or or something but it looks like the air where the movement is, is more.......there. I dont know how to explain....almost heavier, somehow. When they make contact with skin the sensation is barely there. But definetely felt, as if someone is blowing on your toes very gently, mixed with a slightly cold tingly numbness.

At first I was thinking that I was drugged or maybe going psychotic. Their activity was keeping me up all night to the point were I was missing work after being awake for 3 or 4 days at a time. the only time I slept was when I would be too exhausted to control my motor functions!

It has now been 3 months. Where i was seeing one, I am now sure that there are quite a few of these things around my house maybe 6 or 7 or 8.

Any goddamn explanation is appreciated, although its getting to the point where I almost dont mind them anymore, in fact I am becoming very curious as to the why, and how of their appearances.

I am even thinking of experimenting with a hallucinogen, maybe psilocybe mushrooms to see if I can make contact with these fuckers, because on one occasion where I smoked some pot (which is a rare thing) the entity or entities seemed to speed up their movements and move intentionally in circles around me. The only difference being that I seemed to perceive them not with my eyes but a different sense altogether. I got really scared and turned on the lights. Mind you, I am 22 years old and in no shape to be scared of the dark.
And also when I smoked the pot I was pretty confident that I heard there movements a sort of whirring like helicopter blades but slower.

Here is the criteria of their appearances:
Nighttime from 9 - 3 am
I am alone (usually)

This is what they look like:
1- when they are on a flat surface:
dark and vaguely humanoid from 2-5 feet tall
cannot tell if male or female.
2-when they are in the middle of the room
a) dark blobbish humanoid shapes that
disappear or move quickly when looked at.
b) if you have seen the movie Predator and saw when the alien wears the invisibility cloak,
picture a little mini one of those floating around your room, but not as definite, a little blurrier.

Sorry if this was a little long, but I have not spoke of this in while and had to get of my chest.

Reminder: I am not on hallucinogenic drugs or insane.

I believe what I am experiencing to be real.
But then again I bet the whackos say that too.
