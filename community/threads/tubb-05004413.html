Just a point, i think that your units are off Dexters. A whole bottle would not be 1.7mg/kg. Unless the liquid inside the bottle was measured in weights (kg). mg/kg is normally used to say- to get to platuea x you need to take Xmg of dxm per kilogram that you weight. From what i have learnt, through practice and research, this is a rough dxm dosage guide:

1st plat= 1.5-2.5 mg/kg
2nd plat= 2.6-6.5 mg/kg
3rd plat= 6.6-9.5 mg/kg
4th plat= 9.5+ mg/kg
*Beware, you should work your way up the plateaus across several trips, you will not be ready for plateau 2 or above on your first trip and in the same fashion, on your second you might not be ready for plat 3 or more. Also, i don't know what the lethal dose of DXM is, but on my 5th dxm trip i took 9.5mg/kg and had a really trippy forth plat style trip- i had built up a little tolerance by then though*

To work out how much you should take- weigh yourself in kg. Then multiply this amount by a measurement in mg. Ie, I weight about 95kg (yeah i know, i'm chubby), if i wanted a mid 2nd platuea trip then i would multiply 95kg by 4.5mg, this means i would take around 427mg of DXM. Get it?

(Weight in Kg) X (Milligrams for trip)= (How much you should take in Mg)
