Something like the construct from the Matrix. I simply will it, and its there, no matter what I want. If I want my family, they appear. If I want to be sitting back in my house, thats where I will be sitting. If I want a big pile of cocain to appear on my table, thats what will happen.

The would be the ultimate glory for anyone Im sure. There would be no limits and endless happiness. Hell, if you feel at all bored [could that be possible?] you simply bring on the will to be happy, and thats what will happen. You could change anything when you felt like it and have anything when you wanted it.

Essentially if you wanted to create an entire universe and all sorts of being you could. Hell, perhaps we're simply the creation of some dead guy now just waiting for our turn.

I guess in all reality it would be an eternity alone. While we could create all the people we wanted to be with us, or all the beings we wanted to control at the root we would be by ourselves. BUt the illusion would be more powerful and more real then anything so what does it matter. Every feeling we had, and every thought we imagined would still be there, and with it would exist endless oprotunities without restriction.

I don't think many people would pass up the oprotunity to exist in their own new world as a God. Seeing as how thats kinda what you would be, though I guess that wouldn't make you the God, simply a God with his own little box to play around in. Not really a God then, just an all powerful being restricted to an inescapable plane of existance.

That would kick major ass. You could live out every possible fantasy you had in life and then go on to do other things. And if you die a crippled old man, you could simply go back to being 20 again!
