How to make your own Napalm?
Simply easy, yet i am known for it around where I live, and people buy it off me.

But here is the simple recipe:

Take a jar (a good sized jar that holds about 2 liters) and pour lots of gasoline in it (to about 3/4 of the way). Then get as much styrofoam as you can find (Not the popcorn balls or the huge chunky styrofoam, they have to be the kind that come from comp. boxes and such) and stick it in the gasoline, and watch the gasoline melt it, keep melting the styrofoam repeatedly till the gasoline and melt no more. Once youve made enough, you should get a nice brown textured jam, and you have that, you've made my classic "NAPALM!"

It's hella easy, yet there is a hard way that includes boiling gas, and soap shavings, but i really prefer the easy way.

Put this on the street and make like a shape or words on it, with the napalm, and burn it at nighttime, it'll be like the movie "The Crow" where he burns the Napalm to create the shape of a crow. And Napalm also burns longer than gasoline, and when it burns in the street, it sticks to the street forever till demolished (really hard to peel off anyway.)

Also if you have an enemy you need to get rid of, if you have a friend in the welding or gunsmith business, ask him to make you a gun with the shape of a flamethrower (Napalm is the official liquid for the flamethrower) and store storage tanks of napalm and strap it on the flamethrower, and burn your enemy's house down with it or car, or him/her with it. Youll be paying serious consequences with it, but if you really hate him/her, and want to get rid of them, then thats the coolest way, watch them burn.

Thats all i can really say about Napalm, if you sell it, you can get an easy 20 bucks for selling a liter of it to a retard who thought you spent a lot of time making it, because frankly thats what i do, so far Ive made atleast 300 dollars from just selling it to people from my city. Now all i have to worry about is getting caught by the law.

Have fun! and Good Luck!
