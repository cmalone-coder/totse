Finally Accepted.. (story about my kids.. :p)
It was a rough road, but I think she's finally come around.

My daughter had not been taking too well to her new baby brother.. She's come quite a ways from when we first brought him home 10 weeks ago..

At first she would throw tantrums lasting AT LEAST 30mins.. She progessed out of the tantrums and into the, "I don't want baby near me.." stage.. She was currently in the acknowledging baby stage.. lol.. But that seemed to change today..

Before today.. She would say, "Hi, baby," when we told her to, but that was it. Today, the baby had his 2 month Well Baby Check, fully equipped with SHOTS.. The appointment was fine and everything was going well.. My daughter wasn't even as impatient as she usually is.. She sat in the stroller and watched the doctor do his thing..

Now before, in the car, we were working on the the word 'doctor.' I found it appropriate since thats where we'd be spending a portion of our day. :) She would say, "Hi. doctor! Bye, doctor! Thank-you, doctor!" So when we were in the exam room, she continued to say, "Hi, doctor!" etc.

The doctor finished his examination of my son and left for the nurse to administer the shots. I held my sons arms down to prevent too much wiggling and my daughter remained seated in the stroller, watching what was happening.. The nurse poked my son and he began to scream and cry..

My daughter was so shocked over what just happened, she gasped loudly. She had her face scrunched up like she normally does when she's angry, and she was crying and yelling, "Not nice, doctor! Not nice, doctor! Baby! Baby! Not nice, doctor!" She handled it much worse than my son did.. and he was the one getting poked..

Although, she was greatly saddened by what had just happened, I was very pleased.. I'm fairly certain that my daughter, in fact, LOVES her new baby brother..

It was an exciting day for me.. :)
