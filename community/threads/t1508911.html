I come from a household where everyone yells. We yell so much that sometimes we just make shit up to yell about for fun. However, my boyfriend comes from a nice WASPy repressive family where they never yell. First time he stayed over he heard the yelling in the morning as my mother went apeshit on my brother for doing something stupid and my boyfriend nearly crapped himself. He told me his parents never yelled at him. I couldn't believe it. I don't even know how to communicate without yelling. I mean, if someone doesn't yell at me, how am I supposed to know that they care?

Because of this he's always seemed very passionless to me, which is strange because he's an artist, and troubling because I can't see myself without someone who isn't very passionate about things. I'm very passionate about things. And in my way I do small things to try to incite him - not so much to yell - but to show any level of interest. Of course, he thinks this is histrionic but I don't know what do with him sometimes because if he had his way nothing would ever happen. We would just never do anything or go anywhere and he'd be happy to just be the way we are now forever despite how much it bores me.

I need to be in love. May be not right this second, but I need it to be a prospect. He doesn't even want the cards on the table. He hates that there are ex's of mine and various men in general you ask me out on a regular basis and he fears that I'll leave him for them, but he never does anything about it.

Then on New Years we had plans to go out with my bandmates. All he had to do was show up before midnight and he didn't even do that. My own boyfriend stood me up on New Years. And what's really sad is that I'm so used to him doing this sort of thing that I wasn't even sad. I'm so used to the chronic disapointment I don't even question it anymore. He showed up later with the excuse that he was "reflecting". I let it go because it's a holiday and I just wanted to make the best of the rest of the night. That night I went home with him and the next day he caught me crying a little, but I told him it must've been from all the cocaine we'd done the night before.

Then the other night I went out with my mates and I stopped by the bar just for a second to say 'hi'. He didn't seem very happy to see me because my exboyfriend had just showed up and he thought that I'd orchestrated it somehow (for some RETARDED reason) that way. I told him I might come back later but that we were all going to another bar and left.

Anyways, after getting completely tanked (that's an entirely different story) my brother and I took a cab home and my boyfriend called me. I was happy to hear from him but he was angry. He was mad that I hadn't come back and hadn't called him. So, while I'd been very calm and cool about the whole New Years thing I immediately became very upset and started crying because how could he get mad at me for not coming back when he didn't look that happy to see me and I only said I "might" come back when he couldn't even show up before midnight on New Years because he was "reflecting", because reflecting is much more important and much more interesting than spending New Years with me. Of course, I was hurt by that, and I didn't say anything, so how could he get all mad at me for this.

Somewhere in the night he took a cab over and crawled into bed with me. When I woke up he was there and we started talking about all of this. He asked me if I need to be madly in love right now and no, I don't, but I think I deserve to be with someone who wants to be with me on special occassions. At least someone who will kiss me at midnight on New Years...

And we didn't yell, we didn't scream. We lay in bed and discussed it calmly. I think he feels bad about what he did, but I don't have any way to tell. And I don't know if we really worked anything out because nothing seemed to happen. I just don't think my requests are that steep./rant ends

Cheers,
