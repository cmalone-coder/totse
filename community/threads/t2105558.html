Re: Giving my dog the muchies?
Just blow your smoke in the dog's ear/face, if you've never gotten the dog stoned before do it slowly. A hit every ten minutes or so ought to suffice, take big hits and blow them out slowly. If the dog turns away, or gets up and leaves it doesn't want to be stoned. It's up to you if you want to continue at that point, if the dog really has been eating that little, you may want to give it a shot, but don't get it stoned and lock it in another room or something because it gets a bit whiny, watch the animal for at least half an hour, regardless of it's initial reaction.

Animals react differently to being high, one of my cats loves it to the point it'll try to eat my stash and climbs into my lap if I'm hitting a bowl, the other'll gets wiggy, so I don't get her stoned. If you're lucky, your dog will either like it, or not really mind. As I said before, watch the animal if you've never gotten it stoned before, to do otherwise is irresponsible. It really shouldn't be much of a task, if it follows the pattern I've usually seen in dogs, it'll get tired and kind of goofy and clumsy, fall asleep, then wake up and eat everything it can.
