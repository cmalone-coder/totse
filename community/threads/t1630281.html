drinking games and explanations
self-explanitory:

Edward Forty Hands - common with frat initiations, you tape a 40 ounce bottle to each of your hands and down them quick.

Chandeleor - There are five cups lined in a row. each is assigned a number (from 1 through 5). Now you go head to head with someone and roll a dice to be assigned a cup. if you roll on a number with no beer (or drink mix) in the cup you get to pour your desired amount in and pass the dice to your opponent. If the glass is filled with any liquid, you must consume that liquid and roll again. Rolling a six will skip your turn. a variation to this game usies six cups and no "skip turns."

Century club - also known as a hundred shots, you take a hundred shots of beer.

Power Hour - a shot of beer every minute for an hour.

Tips - you arange yourselves into groups of about four. Then you each fill your plastic cups with an equal amount of beer. Once you chug your beer you put the cup on the edge of the table and flick it up with your finger so it lands upside down. Then the next person in your group goes. The first team to finish wins. i enjoy giving the losing team a punishment

Quarters - Queer game, but someone will tell you about it.
