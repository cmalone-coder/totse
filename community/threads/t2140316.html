The next day I thought it had worn off... but my heart was beating not fast but irregularly, I had a "sensitivity" of my own heart beat, and a feeling of restlessness in the left side of my chest.
All conventional stimulant amphetamines have as a major metabolite the cardiotoxin 4-hydroxyamphetamine... in this case, being meth, it would be 4-hydroxy-n-methylamphetamine. (phenylpropanolamine would be 4,b-dihydroxymethamphetamine, ephedrine would be 4,b-dihydroxy-n-methyl, etc).

Yeah. Cardiotoxins can do that. 'n as you'll notice from the above, unreacted ephedrine in your mefs, while less weight-potent a stimulant, will also metabolize into cardiotoxic 4-hydroxyamphetamines... (which, in fact, should be faintly more potent a cardiotoxin).

'n yes. Not surprisingly, the largest concentration of cardiotoxic metabolites is... after it's metabolized. I.e., when you've just come down.
