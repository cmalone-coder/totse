First Salvia trip:
Holy fucking hell it was not at all what I expected it to be, there is nothing about salvia that I would EVER consider to be anything more than a short intense mindfuck, but an amazing mind fuck at the least. It's not like any other trip I've ever had in the fact that with other tripper drugs I could at least classify some of the feelings as a high.

It was a beautiful day with clear blue skies and a gentle breeze that flopped all the leaves on the trees, me and a very close girl friend of mine planned to do
salvia today (she's already done it) and I was excited because it was one of the tripping drugs on a short list that I hadn't tried yet, I knew that salvia is unpleasant 99% of the time but I curiosity got the better of me. I was in a
completely good mood, and I expected a great trip.

We were laying out on her double lawn chair, it was white and had an interwoven pattern (this is important later on) and I had brought my bubbler for the occasion.
We had traveled out to a headshop not far and purchased 30X.
I let her load the bowl because I didn't know how much to put in, and then it began:

I hit the bubbler and laid down to hold in my hit, I held it in for no more than 15 seconds and closed my eyes.
I began to feel as if my body was disconnecting internally, almost like it was shutting off.
My vision turned completely black and I had lost ALL sense of touch, taste, smell, everything. I was so completely out of this world that I didn't even exhale the hit, just swallowed it.
Then it started; I lost ALL feel of MY body, but I could feel what I was seeing:
I became a hair, a brown cartooney hair, there were hundreds of other hairs and we were all on a conveyer belt, and it felt like the setting was that of a laboratory or supermarket.

It was very very uncomfortable, we all had eyes and mouths drawn in but It felt like I was the only one that could actually see; and I was the only one with a voice.
The conveyer belt then lifted off of the black floor that no one could see, and it began to wrap us up.
It was almost like we were individual muscle fibers, and we were being tightened into one big muscle.
There was some unseen figure that was doing this to us, and it was very uncomfortable: I thought that we were all going to die, I thought we were going to be cut or smoked like a jay.
We were being selected by something, picked out of a continuous cycle that to this hour I still can not explain what. No one was fighting it, no one was resisting their deaths but me.

I struggled and struggled to get out of the tightly woven cylinder, but I couldn't get out. All I understood was that we were going to die, and I had to get out. No matter how much I fought and held back my fear, I couldn't break loose. It was horrible, all the hairs were slipping and sliding against me as if they were slimey, and it felt disgusting.
Then it all started to disappear, and I slowly came back to reality.
I didn't understand ANYTHING, I remember the blackness opening up in a circle until I realized that I was staring at her lawnbed-thingy(the hairs were the color of the lawnbed)
Slowly, I began to understand life again, very slowly. It was as if waking up from a dream, but much much slower.

When I asked what had happened and what I had done, she told me that apparently I was lying down and she was just sitting there when suddenly I rose up, like INHUMANLY fast and she knew that something fucked up was going on.
I had fallen down and I was struggling against something, I was falling towards her pool and my foot had gotten caught between the metal of her lawn bed.
I am almost positive that had she not grabbed me and been on top of me that I would've fallen into the pool and drowned.
I didn't even recognize her as I was coming back, I didn't know who she was or anything about my past and history, or why I was where I was.

All in all, it was fucking terrifying; but it was very very fucking worth it and I feel like a better and happier person because of it.


Not pleasant AT ALL, but the amnesia and complete loss of memory that I had was AMAZINGLY powerful, and the slow re-gaining of my person felt amazing.
Almost like growing up condensed into a very short moment.
I highly recommend salvia at least just once, and I myself will venture to it a few more times.
