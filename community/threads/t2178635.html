Freedman, M.F., Leary, T.F., Ossorio, A.G., Goffey, H.S. 1951. The interpersonal dimension of personality. Journal of Personality 20: 143-161

There's one thing Leary has contributed to mankind.

I would say that calling him an idiot is kind of over-reaching. He taught at Harvard...Harvard doesn't hire idiots. I will grant that just about everything he wrote after LSD was a confused mix of Eastern mysticism and Western psychology, which is interesting considering the Cartesian split in the latter.

The grassroots movements of the '60s, which were in large part fueled by psychedelic drugs, played a key role in the civil rights and women's rights movements, at least this is the opinion of Dr. Noam Chomsky, another Harvard professor (see his recent speech about the state of contemporary democracy at http://www.democracynow.org/2008/11/..._the_elections)

The Crick/LSD example is good, but not really pertinent to the counter-culture movement. If anyone is bothered I'm sure there are lots of other examples in the literature, but that will take some time.
