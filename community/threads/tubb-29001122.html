the BBs are very light, thus reaching that
speed is possible...
but the loss in fps is very dramatic...
the BB slow down greatly at range...
the light weight retains little momentum...

i tried .20 cal (5mm) wax balls...
they travel faster, but the fps loss is greater...

so if you are around 20 metres, the BB might have slow down to the speed of a normal
spring pistol, maybe lesser due to wind...

you might not be able to accelerete the .177
fast enough if they are made of metals...

see KE = 1/2 M X V^2

the increase in mass will be decrease in velocity, thus the energy is still same...
a heavier BB can retain it's fps at range,
but it would be moving much slower than
a lighter BB...

if i want range power, i have my own rubber band gun...
it shoots a wooden dart heavier than BB
at a slower speed...
