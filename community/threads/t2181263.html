Re: selling drugs
Its not so much hard, as it is time consuming. Especially if you want enough to live off of. You've got to be ready to make a deal any time of the day for that kind of thing, or make nothing but big sells, which is super hot.

You have to be able to read people really well. If you don't want to get caught up, you have to weed out the good customers from the bad quickly.

You have to conduct yourself a certain way if you intend to succeed, too. You don't need to be a hard ass all the time, but you have to be able to tell people no. They will ask favors, and for deals a lot. It might be temping to cut $5 here, so you can make a quick sale, but if you start giving in when they ask, it starts a bad precedent.

Also, your customers ain't your friends. Even if a friend is buying off you, hes not. You'll end up losing money off this.

Also, no fucking fronts! Even if you get collateral, its just a huge fucking hassle.

There's more to it than this, but that's a start. I'd seriously just do it as supplemental income if you are going to at all.

Its really not as glamorous as you'd think, and it is a lot of work. It gets very tiring dealing with all the drama and all the many many different people. Its really a big fucking headache, man. Especially when people think you're "boys" with them. Which people always do.

Re: selling drugs
Selling drugs is something you probably don't want to get into. All it will take is one of your customers getting arrested, and the police will offer them a deal if they rat you out. I hate to say it, but I imagine at least 50% of the customers only have their self-interests in mind.

Be especially careful with college students, because if they get arrested, they usually get expelled from school, and will rat out a dealer to keep from getting arrested. That happened at my college campus numerous times.

Also, depending on your state, the penalties could be horrible. Get on Norml, and look at the sentencing guidelines for your state before you even think about it. If you are in a place like Maine, it isn't that bad if you are caught, but don't deal in any of the red (republican) states. They usually associate drugs with black people, and are racist assholes.

I got arrested in Maryland for growing, and they charged me with distribution as well. I got court in 2 days, and I am facing 11 years. I got lucky though, and I was offered a reduced sentence and my distribution was dropped completely, so I am likely to only get probation.

I learned this the hard way, even though I wasn't a dealer, I grew for my own personal use. But, I still got charged with it initially, and my bail was $75,000.

So, be very careful if you choose to sell drugs, but my advice is not to. Just use like you are now, and you won't ever get caught unless you do something stupid. Being a dealer has too many possible risks, with too little benefit.
