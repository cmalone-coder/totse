Re: What's some good experimental music?
If by experimental you mean avant guard, I highly suggest Frank Zappa. Never will be another man like him. Some songs I recommend:

Titties and Beer - not really out there, but very funny and it was the first song I heard from him.
Peaches En Regailia - Great instrumental
Andy-
This one has it all. A pretty rythmic song, for the bass especially. I believe one riff is done with only one note on the bass, but the rythm at which he picks them sounds wild.
Muffin Man - My favorite guitar solo

Another guy is Tom Waits. His stuff is just as bizzare (not so much in his early stuff though). He has a very deep, scratchy, voice. It reminds me of Howilng Wolf or Captian Beefheart. You can't go wrong with these songs:

Big In Japan - Guys from Primus are in here (Les Claypool mainly, not sure of the other two.
Tempation - His voice is fucking great.
Hill - Pretty wild here. My favorite song at the moment.
