I'm not a fan of exclusive relationships. The only reason it's so bad and hits you so hard when you break up with someone is that you're left /alone/ - if you have several relationships on the go at any given time, it's not so bad if someone decides they don't want the relationship any more.

Also, I'm willing to bet that once you've decided not to bother keeping relationships exclusive, they last longer. People break up because there's some sort of taboo about having more than one sexual partner, so when they want someone else they have to ditch the old one even if they still want to have sex with them, which is a crying shame for both parties. Also, when someone finds out you've been `cheating' they feel - well, cheated.

Think about non-exclusive relationships; I used to be violently jealous, and miserable as a result. Now that I've decided to take this course, I'm a lot more relaxed about the whole thing. Keep it above board, of course - everyone has to know what's going on.

I hope I haven't gone horribly off topic and that this post is of some use to someone - this stuff is very much on my mind at the moment for various reasons.
