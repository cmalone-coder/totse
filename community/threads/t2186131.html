That's not true. Plastic has microscopic pores through which smells can escape. For example, ziplock bags are airtight, but after a few minutes you can smell bud right through the bag. This is true for other containers, but it takes differing amounts of time for the smell to diffuse through different materials. Barry Cooper goes into detail in his Never Get Busted video.
