Re: Getting Past cameras at school
Is what you do is you get a sharp knife and you strip the insulation half off a 1 inch section, then you jam your knife between the copper and the white insulation and you bend it up so you can get a wire under it. Then you take a piece of copper wire and wrap it around the copper with a good connection, and wrap the other end around a loop you made in the copper tip of a stripped piece of coax cable. Then you electrical tape a second piece of wire to the ground wire braid on the outside of the white core, and do the same to the stripped piece of coax. You clamp a clampshell T split case around the whole assembly and fill it with silicone. Then you run the spliced piece of coax up to a personal DVR and record a couple second loop of the empty hallway. Then you hit play on that and cut the cameras coax at the same time. Then you cut a half inch section of the cameras coax lead, insulate it with electrical tape, and tape it back to the severed line.

It's fool proof. Just practice the routine a couple times standing on top off a ladder. You want to get it down to 30 seconds to be safe.
