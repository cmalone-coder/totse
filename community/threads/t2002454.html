Re: Hard drive is... dead?
That ticking sound means your hard drive is dead. If you can boot back onto it, back up what you can. Otherwise you might have to boot off a different disk and copy information out (you might not even be able to do that, depending on how bad it is). It's the sound of the read/write arm on your hard drive "head banging." For some reason your controller is unable to read the data it needs or is broken in a way that won't allow it to find the data it needs. So instead, the arm swings back and forth across your platters making a nice tick or tock every time it hits the end of its reach.

The chances of data recovery: 25%
Chances of fixing the drive: < 1%
