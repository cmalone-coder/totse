If a God does exist should he be worshiped?
The main religions believe that God placed us upon earth because he/she/it loved us, Others placed us here solely to worship him/her/it, many other religions have different reasons most include worship or love.

My argument is this. If God is omnipotent(sp?) he/she/it will know exactly what each person he/she/it creates will turn out like. He/she/it knows, even if he/she/it gives us free will, exactly what every single person will do in his or her life.

This brings up the question, Why does God create people who are less then morally perfect?

Even if he/she/it has given us free will, he/she/it still knows exactly what is going to happen, so in essence he/she/it has created us for knowing full well that the person in question will go to hell, maybe forever.

This seems wrong. Although God may be above question, he/she/it is not above contemplation. If he/she/it created a person who was going to be sent to hell, then what was the point of creating that person? Hell is not a nice place, it is eternal punishment, since God knew that that was the destination of this person why did God create this person.

This fact either means God, by our ethical moral standards, is bad, or God does not care either way, I don't feel that I should worship him because of these facts. For this, assuming God exists, I will probably be sent to hell. This again raises the question of why did he create me. I will never love or worship him, I will go to hell, why did he condemn me to this?

Why does he/she/it choose to create people who will spend an eternity in hell even though he/she/it could just as easily have never created them?

Re: If a God does exist should he be worshiped?
Your questions are based on the assumption that God is sitting in some corner of the universe thinking with a human brain. Perhaps the Higher Power doesn't express the human qualities of jealousy, emotions, ego, or contemplation?

I, personally, don't believe the Higher Power "wants" to be worshipped. Why would omnipotence have a self-confidence problem? I don't mind the idea of recognition and appreciation of the Higher Power as a way to stay connected to your spirituality. But the whole idea of being worshipped seems to be such a human ideal - not something that an omnipotent being would "need".

You asked why God would create someone knowing they would turn out "bad". If there were no bad people, how would we recognize good ones? If there were no hardships, how could we appreciate the good times? Good is relative to bad. You can't just "not make" evil. It's a consequence of having free will. I consider the Hitlers and bin Ladens of humanity to be gifts. How else would humankind be spurred towards growth? It takes a huge atrocity to get the population as a whole to communicate and change. It's rude to say, but not too far from the truth, that having Hitler on this earth has changed society for the better much more drastically than say, Mother Theresa. And I don't think Hitler is in Heaven OR Hell. I think he got an F on his report card and had to repeat the 4th grade.

Just my thoughts - but I'm a big dummy. Just ask Nox.
