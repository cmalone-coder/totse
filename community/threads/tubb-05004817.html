I don't know what the hell you guys are smoking but the term "chronic" simply means great bud, regardless of the way it was grown. When someone wants to identify how bud was grown the term "indo" , "hydro" or "outdoor", "humbolt" would be correct terms. "Chronic", "Kryptonic", "Dank" "Green"refers to the quality. "Sinsemilla" (without seed) refers to the growth characteristic."Indica", "Sativa" or "Ruderalis" refers to the species. "Afghani", "Northern Lights", "Purple Haze" refers to the genetic strain.
