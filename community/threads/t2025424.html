Re: Best home defense guns?
The most popular option seems to be the shotgun. Generally something along the lines of a Mossberg 500/590 or a Remington 870. A flashlight foregrip is a good idea, too.

There are many who choose rifles for home defense. This is definitely a good idea if you anticipate anything beyond short-range use. Light, high-velocity rifle rounds tend to rapidly lose energy after penetrating objects, and there's a good bit of statistical info to show that they don't present a risk of overpenetration any greater than a handgun round or buckshot. Compact assault-style weapons tend to offer excellent options for customization. Personally, I'd like to build a 16" integrally suppressed AR for this purpose, when funds allow.

Generally speaking, a handgun is the least viable home defense gun, as they've got less stopping power than the average long gun. Then again, a .357 revolver is about as simple as you can get, which might be to your advantage when you're half-naked and barely as conscious.

The key is to have something you've used often. Get comfortable with your weapon choice.

Re: Best home defense guns?
It depends on where you live, and what your house is made of.

A shotgun and a handgun is pretty much it - you might want to add an intermediate rifle like an AR or AK but thats personal preference.

Google some of the names, and do some homework.

Shotguns: Remington, Mossberg, Saiga, Ithica, Winchester.

Handguns: List of them, with links.

The most important thing about firearms, even more important than price, is the fit. Think of it like buying a suit, if you don't have the right fit, it feels all wrong, and you're not going to use it. The same applys to firearms. Do you have any experience shooting at all? You might want to go hit the gun ranges and gun shops and feel the firearms up (so to speak) and maybe shoot a few rounds with them.
