Re: Rock Band 2 vs Guitar Hero World Tour
and I really like the look of the Custom song creator in Guitar Hero.
:confused:

Honestly, I wouldn't let this sell you on anything. The MIDI songs created on this are literally ringtone quality, you're not really going to feel like a rockstar playing ringtone quality songs.

Also, I'd be wary of the GHWT instruments. Well, the guitar is supposed to be pretty damn good (The RB2 Strat is also awesome too), but there have been a lot of problems with the drums and the pedal isn't very good quality. Also, they're kind of a pain to use on a lot of Rock Band songs because of the raised cymbal. Doing any song in RB that has rolls that go across all four toms on the GHWT drums is much more difficult than it should be because you have to go from a Tom, to a Cymbal, to a Tom. This is incredibly awkward, and on some rolls nearly impossible.

When it comes right down to it, if you like the RB2 setlist, I'd just get that.
