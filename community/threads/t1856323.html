Congrats. SWIM wishes the thread wasn't so damn long so he could take the time to read it and help more (attention span not huge right now).

For nano batches, like your ~3g pfed, I always use at least 3/4 that weight in rp (so thats at least 2 1/4g of rp).

Now as far as I2 goes assuming it's completely dry and lab grade, use AT LEAST 1.5X the weight of pfed, SWIM often uses double, so thats 6g of I2 for you buddy.

The reason why so much I2 is because if the 2 1/4g rp is clean enough, then it is plenty to accomodate that amount of iodine. Iodine is where your HI comes from (the rp just helps make the HI), and you need several times molar excess of HI in order to get a complete reduction.

Do not SKIMP on the I2, more I2 means longer reaction times, but higher conversion.

If you are making your I2, then if your goal is a quick 1-2 hour dry run (for that 3g pfed) and you want complete conversion, then make damn sure that I2 is as dry as can be. You'd be surprised how much water a little bit of soggy I2 can provide.

However, you can do what SWIM does if your gonna do a long reflux. Dry the I2 enough to where it is like play-dough, and use it in the rxn, still adding as much water as you would as if it were going to be a dry run. That water plus whatever the I2 carries will be sufficient. 12 hours later you'll have higher than 90-95% conversion rate (thats conversion rate, not yeild). The best shit SWIM has ever had comes from the long reflux like this, m.p. so close to 172C you'd shit your pants.
