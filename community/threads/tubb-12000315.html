Talking about phones, hacking systems, technical advice, and such is completely LEGAL in the United States -- it's covered by the First Amendment. What's illegal is conspiracy to commit a crime. So if you say something like "Meet me at 2am so we can crack a can" or "I changed my cell phone's number myself" you are criminally liable. Stating "You can crack a can by doing thus-and-so" or "If you wanted to change a cell phone's number, you could do it like so" is perfectly OK. Posting passwords, calling card numbers, or credit card numbers is forbidden on this network. Posting such info will get you booted off the net.

Here's a general rule of thumb:

If you wish to know something, use a search engine. If you can't find what you're looking for on there, you may want to look on TOTSE- some of the stuff may be outdated, or downright wrong, but not all of it. Search engines are the one of the greatest tools the internet has to offer, and even if all of us we're bursting with helpful vibes and willing to show you all the things we know and all the sites you can learn from, you likely could still find something we don't know or know about by searching yourself.

If you can't find your answers that way, ask us. Part of the reason to go through that step is so you can say, "I checked some sites (names help too) and I found out this, this, and this, but I need to know that," and ask what you need to ask. It's not hard, but it helps those who can help you by doing two things- 1) it shows you did some footwork of your own, and you're not just a "feed me info" twit; and 2) it narrows down what those reading your post have to tell you by pointing directly to where the gaps in your knowledge lie.
