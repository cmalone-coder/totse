Oh my oh my, budder is amazing. I emplore each and every one of you to set out to get some for your very own. The only way i can think to describe it is to say that it's stoner crack. Whatever you call it, it's an awful lot of THC in your lungs in a 2 second lungful.
The budder I got was mainly sativa based, and gave a borderline hallucinogenic, energetic high that lasted about 4 hours when combined with a joint of NYC Diesel.
I rolled 1/3 of a gram of budder into a long, snake-like shape. This was done on a glass surface, since I don't think any other surface would suffice for this ultra-sticky stuff. I stuck the snake-like budder to a paper, then rolled a regular joint of NYC Diesel with that on the inside of the paper.
The end result was a long line of golden budder. It was so golden that it looked like it was drawn on with a golden permanent marker. When I smoked it the budder burned and sizzled slowly, and was enough to demolish my two friends and I, who all smoke a fair amount of herb.
Like I said, this stuff is a must-try for anyone who loves THC. It's stronger than any hash I've had, and even blows the best honey oils out of the water. The only problem is, just like crack, there's only just a little bit and when that starts running low you start thinking about getting more. Oh ya, and it boosts your THC tolerance to an unfavourable level, so smoke this sparingly or even the best herb will seem like brick weed.
