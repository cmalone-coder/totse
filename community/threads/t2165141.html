Re: 115.2 kbps dial up?!
There was no speed jump. That 921.6kbps is the speed from computer to modem, not over the phone line. That is all that the tweek does -- it shows you the speed computer-to-modem rather than over the line.

The only things that might have affected it would be things like phone line conditions, local weather, etc. His max speed is 52Kbps, like it always was -- so that's the highest speed he could have actually reached. If federal law allowed 56kbps, he could reach that speed, but at 57kbps his modem would be fried. Anything higher than that would also destroy the modem.
