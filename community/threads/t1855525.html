For nymph and miss--

First things first. Part of the problem is the equipment. Test tubes and light bulbs ain't gonna cut it. You've already invested enough time, money, and effort to consider using the proper equipment. There are two ways to look at this. First, at least one 500ml. round bottom flask with 24/40 ground glass joint, heating mantle, controller for heating mantle, and a allihn condenser as long as you can afford, but at least 300mm, 500ml. seperatory funnel with teflon stopcock (this is a must) and some kind of stand to hold it. Thats it. Ebay will yank about 125 bucks out of your pocket. Get a cardboard box and some foam (like the stuff in a couch cushion sittin' out on someones curb at least once a week) then make cutouts and store the box where your winter sweaters are. No sweat.

OK, fine. You don't want to spend money or find somebody to ship the ebay stuff to. You'll just have to make it yourself. If you are not creatively inclined, you'd be better off sticking to the first idea. If you can afford the precursors that are being wasted, then you can afford the right equipment. On the other hand, it really kicks ass to make the things you need from stuff in your house. Best of all, its free (labor not included).

Where the hell did I leave that pi...., oh, there it is. Hang on a minute. Well, maybe more than a minute. Damn butane.

Anyway, the boiling flask can be a clear jar, 8-16 oz (250-500ml.), short and squaty like a jelly jar or some kind of sauce. The grocery store is a wealth of great equipment and you get to eat the contents first. Be warned!!! If you use glass that is not pyrex or kimax, you run the risk of it cracking from the heat. Swigotta has used many jars that are not lab glass and has broken few. You have to consider this:

If you have a 30-40 gram cook goin for 36 hours and you know for damn sure you can't stay with it the whole time, do you really want to trust a jar when a 15 dollar flask can put your mind at ease? Shit that takes 15-20 minutes at &lt;80C, use a jar. If its the reduction, use the best equipment you can.

I'm not going to talk about individual ghetto devices, but if everything goes well tonight, time wise, there will be some threads popping up soon.

See how much you can come up with and give me an update ASAP. Until then, don't waste anymore of your hard earned precursors.

The lady replied, "Do you know how many animals I had to fuck to get this coat?!" I have no idea why that came to mind other than precursors are hard to come by, conserve and preserve them.
