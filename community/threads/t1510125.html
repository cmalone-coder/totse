Downward Spiral
The short version:

Every negative event in my life as worsened me. For the first half of my life, pre-school to sophomore year in high school, I was damaged on a social level. For whatever reason I was often picked on. It wasn't physically malicious, perhaps typical verbal torment. I wasn't good at team sports for whatever reason, so that was a constant burden, and I was perhaps borderline nerdy...though mainly I'd say I was just smart. As time went on I would spend my time trying to counter all the insults. In time I would learn to say vicious verbal things, happily hitting where it hurt.

In middle school I was basically just in limbo. Only fully accepted by the less popular people, of course, and although not disliked, not particularly part of the "cool" group. Things weren't really bad at all during middle school, and perhaps everything was rather normal.

In high school I found a lot of vicious torment. I wasn't beaten up or anything, though people had decided I was a doormat of sorts since I was rather friendly. Some people would step over the line with the playful taunting and physicality. Every day tension would build up inside me. I sometimes had to plan out how I react when someone was messing with me. Looking back, I could have easily been the star of news story involving a student killing another student.

After being mugged on the train when I was about 15, I became tougher. I essentially was hungry for some type of revenge, and thanks to a growth spurt and running track, I actually was a different person once junior year came along. At this point, I began to see things how they were, and throughout the course of the year I began to withdraw from the society of my peers and become my own person. While this was good in some respects, it developed into a strongly embedded superiority complex that I still have today. It's not simply arrogance, but a strong sense that most people are worthless. This was the culmination of about 17 years of life, and that's when I began to feel alive.

Now we segway to sexuality. That certainly didn't start when I was 17, though...it began to reach a new level then. I'd say that I began to think sexually around the age of 12. I started looking at all types of porn, masturbating (not able to ejaculate of course), and just generally getting hooked to sexuality. Conveniently, I was/am a good looking guy. I would get some flirting and such in middle school and high school, though none of my crushes led to anything. I probably had a fairly normal approach to girls, and realizing that I was at least superficially appealing, I worked on various attractive things, such as being a fiarly good dancer (latin dances).

Anyways, as fate would have it, my first real hook-up was late when I was 16. It was at a summer camp, and right after I came accross a book called "The 48 Laws of Power". Keep in mind that this was near the time that I began to emotionally withdraw from society. Anyways, there were a handful of potential hookups at this summer camp, and I actually had a nice selection. I actively used the techniques from that book, and voila. Although some other readings and such slightly influenced me, this was a major turning point in my view that women (and people in general) can and should be manipulated.

With my newfound confidence and outlook on life, humanity, and women, my final year in high school was easily my most successful. Withdrawing such as I did ironically brought me closer to everyone. There were still some awkward and obvious antisocial tendencies in my ways, but I managed.

Once I hit adulthood, university, and freedom, I had many more experiences with girls. Unforunately, they've been gradually getting less and less successful. While I could have had sex with various girls earlier in the year, now it's gotten to the point where things will stop after making out once (just making out, nothing inappropriate, etc.). Everytime that I mess something up with a girl (and ultimately, I've messed something up every single time), the emotional toll it takes on me feels more and more dull. I still feel something painful, but...it's a lot less stronger. It's just a feeling that I should move on.

I feel like I've been on one downward spiral for a long time. My personal peak back when I was 17 was at the same time a low point. I was disconnected from people and definitely had some disturbing mentalities; I still do.



Anyways, this thread is leaidng up to this:

I'm at some type of crossroads. Something is wrong with me, and the way I see it I either need to learn how to improve manipulatively, or I need to get my emotions and mind back to some type of normalcy. I'd like advice on either one.

I think throughout my life I've dulled everything down as a self-defense mechanism, but...while things don't hurt much anymore, all of my joy is perverse and shallow.
