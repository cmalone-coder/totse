Re: How to do Astral Projection
You might just want to try this, has worked for me.

Lay down on your bed, my bed is in an almost exact North-South (magnetic) alignment, my head faces north (this might be a coincidence or might be important). Start some of that transcendental meditation. At least, that's what I think it is. Get comfortable, might want a blanket as you will eventually get cold and uncomfortable.

You will have thoughts going through your mind, but ignore them, don't try and block them out, just try not to concentrate on them. Think about your breathing, in-out, so everything you breathe in, have your internal dialogue say iiiiiiiiiiin then with the out breath, ouuuuuuuuuuut. Keep doing this. You will consciously feel your brain/mind shifting and dropping in to different levels. Whether this is brain wave shifting or consciousness shifting, I have no idea. Keep this up, eventually you will feel lead like. Make sure all your muscles are totally relaxed and untensed, as you will develop pins and needles if you don't, muscles requiring blood when you aren't providing it, or something.

Eventually you will hit a point where you feel your entire body has pins and needles, but it's not pins and needles, as the longer you hold it (trying not to laugh, it is surreal, but if you do, it's easy to get back to this point quickly, like less than 5 seconds, as long as you don't break your train of thought) you still don't develop a numbness or pain. Might be REM or you might be reverbrating with the universe. Don't know. It's open to interpretation.

After a while of this, you will be able to see your hands in front of you, doing stuff. They will appear shadowy/yellow tinged/charcoal/white tinged, and you can control them, move them as you will. Eventually you can leave your body and things will either stay that weird appearance or go full colour, but everything will melt/shift in to the background/surroundings if you look at it for too long. Go flying!

From my location, in a south westerly direction, heading towards the sky, you can find this temple place where an old dude just sits there and looks at you. Packed full of books, a big library or something. You won't remember what you learnt when you come back to your body. Also, somewhere in central Australia, there is a giant rat man (I think he could be a Chinese grand master or something, no idea, he doesn't exactly look like a rat, closest comparison is that rat guy leader off Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, there's a lizard kind of dude also somewhere around) who lives in the bush and sort of underground also. You can have conversations with him and never recall anything when you return.

The vibrations are nice to just feel and absorb.

Anyway, after that, you will be able to AP by accident.

You will be laying in bed, asleep, sit up in a hurry, and get out of bed, notice your body behind you, and be like, woah, I did it, and I wasn't even trying.
