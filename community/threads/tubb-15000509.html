Widely accepted modern evolutionary theory states that man is a separate branch from present day primates. Darwins theories will, as have other pioneers of science, be disproved or refined over time with the advent of new tools such as DNA analysis. Darwin's first and foremost tool was mere observation. He used to record his data by making hand sketches and he sailed in wooden ships.

'Survival of the fittest' as applied to your snake story would be:

The snakes that are better able to hold their breath will be more likely to escape their predators or obtain food by crossing the river. Those snakes live to reproduce and their offspring carry the genes that allow them to hold their breath, i.e. large lungs, bigger muscles with which to swim the current, etc.

The giraffe with longer necks will see the hyena approach, and in turn, run off and live to reproduce offspring with large necks. The short necked ones are meals. Nature didn't intend to grow long necks, but rather provided the mechanism for automatically sustaining itself. If the trees happen to mutate in a manner that only 4' trees will grow, the giraffe will die off as others have for similar reasons.
