opium tea... can anyone explain this weird brain chemistry I'm seeing?
I order dried pods and brew this stuff pretty often (too often, but thats besides the point), and I always enjoy making some for a friend who wishes to try it, but I've noticed something weird about it, both in myself and most others.

It seems like when my body has little or none of the active chemicals, I don't get much of a "high" the first time drinking it. Occasionally I'll take 3 or 4 days off to detox a little and remind myself not to get in too deep, and the first cup back releives withdrawl feelings, but I barely get any of the euphoric buzz. The next day I'll have most of my buzz back, and the day after that it's completely restored.

Looking back, I beleive this happened when I first started drinking it as well. It probably took atleast 4 consumptions, all occuring within a two-week period, before the buzz really started hitting me.

This seems completely backward and I almost feel stupid posting it, but I'm absolutely confident that it's not some big stupid placebo that I've fallen for. A few weeks ago I took 3 days off which got me to the point of pretty bad leg cramps, moderate insomnia and the crawling skin feeling, and the day I came back I brewed a cup with 6 grams, which usually is enough to give me an awesome and solid buzz for 3+ hours with good pain releif (I have some back soreness from an injury). That time I got most of the expected pain releif, but only a light subtle buzz. The next night I brewed 4 grams and had twice the buzz.

^Nearly that exact scenario has happened 2 or 3 times, so it can't be a mistake, and other people have had the issue too. They'll be interested in trying the stuff, so I brew em a cup, and more than half of them say "It just feels like a mild pot high", and then they throw up the next morning. I'm pretty sure if they drank another cup that night they'd get the buzz I hoped to let them experience, but most people don't wanna try it again.
A few of em say the first time they had it they got an amazing buzz that hit em like a ton of bricks and have even gone and ordered a bag of dried pods themselves.



Can anyone explain this to me, and maybe even suggest how to avoid it? There's a few people who were really interested when I told em about it, but I want to be sure its gonna work before making them gag down the rather unpalletable brew. Is there some way I can sorta "prime" their system for it? Maybe taking a gel cap of the dried powder the day before?



I think its the most bewildering to me because it's changed what chasing the dragon means for me; I didn't catch the dragon the first time I did it... I caught him the 4th time, with relative ease, and have done so like 20 more times on various occasions since then. I buy a pound of dried pods for $63 after shipping, which comes to about 250g of useful material... I need about 8g to full blown catch the dragon for 4+ hours, and that's a hell of a deal IMHO.

thanks for any help,

In the past few weeks there have been tons of threads on "OMGZ, I'D IN W/D FROM OPIATES" And it's all for nothing. I make no claims, guarantees, or endorsements, but the studies done are pretty conclusive. I recently did a paper on the subject and there are MANY documented cases of tolerance inhibition.

As for a pseudoscience explanation, opiates cause a bunch of firings in sensitive parts of the brain. These firings are not related to the recreationality of opiates, but a by product of it. The sensitive parts of the brain are not meant for that level of firing, so they begin to desensitize and shut down receptor cites. They can be inhibited by NMDA antagonists.
