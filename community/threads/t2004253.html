Re: Giving out infractions in forums you dont moderate.
vBulletin's setup allows you to assign the "can give infractions" to a "user role" -- it's not tied to a particular forum.

The fact that you can hand out infractions to anyone in any forum isn't because I think that's how it should work, it's just how vBulletin was written. Just because you have the ability to hand out infractions to anyone, anywhere doesn't mean you should.

The user roles that have this ability are Administrators (me), Super-Moderators, and Moderators. At some point I may give the ability to other people as well. If I do that, then it really won't be tied to a particular forum.

I think a better solution is to get agreement on what constitutes an infraction, so if someone does give someone an infraction in "your" forum, you'll think "Yeah, I would have done the same thing" -- and you'll think that every time it happens.

Here's a tangent to think about: What if there were no titles, nothing that indicated that someone was or wasn't a moderator, but people just had additional abilities based on their totse-karma? You help out, you get more abilities and access to more tools, you post spam or crap-posts, you never get any more abilities than a newbie.
