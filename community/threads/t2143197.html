What's your KFC order?
When you go to KFC what do you usually get? I believe there are some locational variations (eg. I've read here before about KFC biscuits...but we don't have them here)

I get a zinger burger without the mayo fries and a mountain dew. I also like the nuggets and popcorn chicken except they are too expensive for what you get.

There is a deal here called the ultimate burger meal where you get a fillet or zinger burger, piece of chicken, sml potato and gravy, some chips and a drink. I generally get that as I find it to be good value for money.
