[video] Bakers Hotel, Mineral Wells TX
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L5OdR5O3ouc

"January 10th, 2009
0:00 to 2:18 shows 2 friends and I entering the Baker at around 1 AM and proceeding to the main staircase. Some of our friends had gotten to the hotel earlier that evening. As my group were walking up the stairs, we heard high pitched screaming. At first we played it off as our friends trying to mess with us, but they only had one girl with them, and I have never heard her make a noise like that. We kept making our way up the stairs and explored the floor we thought we heard the screams coming from and found nothing. We asked our friends if anyone was screaming like that after the night was over and none of them had made those screams. We showed them the video and some got chills down their spines.

2:19 to 3:49 shows us 3 in the Bakers suite on the 11th floor. We wanted to explore this room to find the hidden liquor cabinet in a bookshelf. We found it. Pretty exciting. I kicked a board laying across the bottom of it and scared everybody on accident.

3:50 to 6:01 was probably the scariest part of the night. You see the 3 of us walking towards a big white door in the Cloudroom/Ballroom on the top floor. It's hard to tell, but the door starts slowly swaying open by itself as we walk through it. I was in front and I assure you we did not touch it at all. We kept walking through the room and pass another doorway and right at 4:24 you can hear the door begin to creek and then close. We were all about 10-15 feet away from the door. It was pretty windy up there, but we were in that room for about 5 to 8 minutes and it is pretty strange that it only opened AND shut once, as we were passing through it!

6:02 to the end shows us at a door on the 5th floor, supposedly one of the most haunted. The door that my friend opens is right in front of the main staircase and we decided to check it out.As the door opens it hits the wall and scares us. At 6:35 we shut the door and a few seconds later a make an easily audible gasp. If you listen carefully (volume up) you can hear someone taking a breath inbetween the door shutting and my gasp, right around 6:37. It is really quiet. This was none of us, as one friend was in front of me and the other to my right. I heard this noise from my left right next to the door. Pretty creepy.

Every experience that night, my 2 friends and I were the only...living... people in the area. Crazy night."

That's the description.
