Re: A good long distance relationship
I've spent the last two years (just about, it's a little short of that actually) in a long distance relationship with the girl i love.

I've learned a thing or three, so listen up. if you keep it long distance, then it will start to become an on/off relationship. If you and her really do have strong feelings for eachother, then you can get through this until you're together. but if not, it will eventually end.

Also - make plans to get together, and keep to them. when you go back on your plan to see her, she'll lose faith in you whether it was your fault or not. This only creates more distance between you.

Most importantly, plan to get together permanently if you want the relationship to last. This is the most important thing you can do, so if the two of you are up for it, one (or both) of you needs to relocate. if you want it to last. I'm not saying you have to do it now, but the longer you wait, the worse your chances are.

Good luck, they're not easy, but if both people are willing to put in the effort, then you'll get through.
