Two serious questions...
what color is the blood? is it frank (bright red or obvious red blood) or is it that sort of clotted blood color? Frank Rectal bleeding is more often than not either a Hemorrhoid or anal fissure. Anal fissure is a concern mainly because of the risk of infection which can lead to systemic septicemia. Hemorrhoids are not too much of a problem unless they become painful. Both are relatively easy to treat! If the blood is a dark and clotted color it may be more serious, then you're looking at a more internal problem! Rectal fissure or some type of rectal ulcer. these are the most likely explanations. either way a visit to the doc makes good sense!
