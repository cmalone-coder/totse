Dear Totse,

Why are there arrogant pricks in "My God Can Beat the Shit Out of Your God" that believe their way is the only way, and that everyone else is wrong? Can't someone just approach it with an open mind and possibly believe in something new for a change? I believe in what I believe in because I know for a fact that it's true. Can I sit here and name reasons why I know it's true? yea, I could, but I don't feel like it. Well, I just wanted to say that I am a strong standing Christian, Christ has always been in my life, and that people who think that Christianity is a cult, or something like that is dead wrong. In the original Hebrew version of the Old Testament (The Torah), there is a "Biblical Code" that literally tells you every single MAJOR event in time. It speaks of the Gulf War, assassinations, etc etc. You would be amazed at all this information. Best of luck to you, and I just wish to say that are there others out there who PUSH their beliefs onto others? Yes, there are. I'm not that way though. I'm 15, and I'm going to be a pastor when I grow up. Am I going to go to a college and get a degree? No. Am I going to join a specific church? No. I'm going to open my own church, and preach what I believe. What I believe is that the ONLY way into Heaven, is through Jesus Christ, and by keeping God's will in your life everyday. The 4th commandment is "Honor thy father and thy mother" and part of honor is obedience. The Lord IS your Father, honor Him, and make Him happy.

Peace be with you all,
