Re: LSD for the first time.
Take two, jump in with both feet man, you only get one first time when it comes to acid, and I say make the best of it, I did it and don't regret it a bit. I was kinda like you, acid was my first drug besides weed or beer, and i dropped 2 hits and it was fantastic.

But don't be stupid and do it at like a Gwar show or something

My first time i did 1.5 hits, me and my friend bought 3 and just split it... it was intense and amazing experience, but when it was all over i wished i had taken 2 hits or more... As long as you are in a safe environment and your life is generally happy go for at least 1, preferably 2 hits...

things that help when your tripping, before the trip write yourself a message to yourself on a sheet of paper that says something along the lines of "i took acid, it is making me feel the way i am, everything will go back to normal". seeing that on a sheet of paper in your own handwriting will be more convincing than someone else telling you that its all a drug. Do it with a friend that you trust, its also good if that friend has done acid before. Its always nice to trip with someone else, because you know they are feeling how you are feeling. For your first time its not a good idea to trip by yourself.....

if your trip start to turn negative, look at what you wrote before the trip. try to distract yourself, your thoughts will be speeding through your head and you can take advantage of that by something as simple as looking at your thumb and index finger and trying to figure out how much distance is in between them... it seems like a simple question sober but when tripping conventional thought is thrown out the window and by the time you come to an answer you might not even know what was upsetting you in the first place. try to get away from whatever might be bothering you, if you feel cramped inside, go outside.

If you play any instruments, try playing something on acid. if you are the artistic type, try drawing something on acid, and if your the writing type then write. video games don't go well with acid, the second time i did acid i attempted to play gears of war, sucked horribly and then i just lost interest, sat back, and listened to pink floyd.

Eat before your trip, it suppresses appetite a good bit although things like oranges are mindblowingly delicious...

try to decide if you would rather trip in the day or the night. im a night person and like how peaceful nights are, but tripping in the day has always been nice and colorful... if you do trip at night, i would suggest a night with a clear sky. you will see why its called lucy in the sky with diamonds...

try to stay away from mirrors for your first trip. for your entire life whenever you have looked in a mirror you have seen yourself as you appear to everyone else, when your on acid what looks back at you isn't what you look like, and most of the time whats looking back at you isn't pretty. Personally i can stand in front of a mirror and seeing my face become gaunt and skeletal in appearance and it doesn't bother me, what does bother me is my eyes. they just look so hollow, black, soulless and while still looking slightly deranged....

if you smoke cigarettes, have at least a pack... it will be the best pack of cigarettes you will ever have smoked. Weed is also very nice, but will make things generally more intense.

have a nice trip.

edit: for your first time dont do it at a party... its not a good idea. i tripped at a Halloween party around a whole bunch of icp kids running around with hatchets and machetes, but i had a bit to drink to knock the acid down a bit and make things tolerable.... it wasn't a bad trip but it sucked because at 4 am in the morning when everyone had crammed into the house, it was just me and my friend still up from the acid... with everyone else passed out until the sun came up... generally though, alot of people around you while tripping is not a good idea, do it with a single friend or maybe 2 in a calm environment...
