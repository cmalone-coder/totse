I just noticed that the Email link in my signature was set up wrong. It should work now.

I have been playing with a crude BB zipgun lately and noticed something that should have been obvious from the get-go: A pen is too large a barrel for a BB. The gun will have greater range, power, and accuracy if you reduce the diameter of the pen by gluing a small brass tube inside it. These tubes are available in many sizes from your local hardware store. I am using a 3/16" tube in mine and it works very well.
