Re: 7.62x54R AR-15
I've been wanting to see this for ages, but there's some difficulties in doing so.

Ruskie 7.62 is .311, not .308 like American ammo.
You need a custom bolt face and barrel with matching throat and bore.
You might need to redesign the entire upper to accept the larger bolt and barrel.
You already mentioned getting magazines to accept the ammo.

Eh, who knows. Some rifles look gay with a thumbhole, some don't. Besides, if this is true to the AR name, then you should be able to swap out any stock on it and have it accept any typical AR stock/grip.

The only thing I would foresee this being ill fated for, is price. I know a LOT of people want a blaster for all the x54R they buy, save, and put in their Mosins. I've actually mentioned the idea before here.

Custom work is almost always ridiculously expensive, and this looks to be almost entirely custom. Even if you throw parts together that work safely, people want a AR in x54, not some mutant gun that shoots it.

Mass production lowers prices, and unless this project can be priced well from the start, it'll probably have the same fate as uppers like 6.8SPC or 6.5 Grendel. At least you have crazy cheap ammo going for you, those didn't.
