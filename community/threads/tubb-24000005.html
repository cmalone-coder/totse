Re: spoilers on rear wheel drive cars
No! They are all just for looks. Most of the spoilers out there are not designed with fluid dynamics in mind. So if they produce any downforce they are inefficient. Truth is they will actually decrease your top speed by a few mph. Not many cars on the street right now would even benefit from a proper wing anyways, you just aren't cornering that fast.
If you are using a proper rear wing on a FWD racing car:
Rear wings on front drivers won't affect them at all, unless the wing at (high)speed changes the body angle and the front aerodynamics start to create lift. If there is lots of downforce on the rear and the front stays neutral however this may reduce any oversteer conditions that existed before, as it would help pin the ass of the car to the track.

Wrong O! ?!? WTF
Honestly even the small ones are just for looks. Street cars cannot get more acceleration by putting more force down on the rear, there has to be some wheel slippage. Even then a wing doesn't work until you get going at speed. There isn't a stock car out there (excluding high end supercars)that needs it's wing.

Wings are good for really fast racecars however: A formula one car at top speed has over 8 tons of downforce on it from all of its aerodynamic features. this means it can race on an upside down racetrack as long as you can get it up there in the first place.
