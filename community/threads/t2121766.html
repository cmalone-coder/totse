Allowing the consumer side to be 'decriminalized', while keeping the rest of the market illegal is not very good for the market.

In some possible decriminalization scenarios, the penalties for illegal acts (growing, dealing) actually become harsher.

This would force the market to change. While we, the consumers are fine, demand would go up. But as the penalties for the illegal acts increase, the number of non-crime related people willing to risk those penalties goes down.

This would increase the opportunity for organized crime to take over the market, in place of mom and pop grows. This would at first decrease supply, which would raise prices for you and me, and it would also decrease quality, as organized crime will try to increase productivity with lots of commercial-quality weed.

Eventually supply and demand would reach a balance again, but the end result is more commercial-quality weed and more organized crime connected to the weed market. This is bad for everyone.

Full legalization would avoid that.

They could leave you alone and the dealers/growers alone as well.

Allowing the consumer side to be 'decriminalized', while keeping the rest of the market illegal is not very good for the market.

In some possible decriminalization scenarios, the penalties for illegal acts (growing, dealing) actually become harsher.

This would force the market to change. While we, the consumers are fine, demand would go up. But as the penalties for the illegal acts increase, the number of non-crime related people willing to risk those penalties goes down.

This would increase the opportunity for organized crime to take over the market, in place of mom and pop grows. This would at first decrease supply, which would raise prices for you and me, and it would also decrease quality, as organized crime will try to increase productivity with lots of commercial-quality weed.

Eventually supply and demand would reach a balance again, but the end result is more commercial-quality weed and more organized crime connected to the weed market. This is bad for everyone.

Full legalization would avoid that.
