an Alaskan's take on Sarah's elevation
Not since the assassination of Jack Kennedy have I been so depressed, discouraged, and disoriented by a national event. McCheney (excuse, me, McCain) must be truely desparate, as well as monumentally cynical. In one fell swoop, he trivialized the most important presidential race in decades..

Now I find myself literally lying awake at night, worrying about the future of this country if she actually gets elected. After all, she is anti-environment, and anti-human rights. Plus she would do all in her power to force her evangelical Christian beliefs on the rest of us. She has at least made no secret of this. (Cecile Richards has observed that a woman who votes for her is like a chicken who votes for Colonel Sanders.)

I was an early supporter of her when she ran for governor--had the bumper sticker, a sign on the porch of my cabin and so forth. Then I started to listen to her. In every interview, she was incaple of giving a straight answer-she just danced around, mouthing platitudes about upholding the constitution, family values, yadda yadda yadda. I voted for Andrew Halcro, who recently blew the whistle on what is now being called Troopergate, which resulted in the legislature's doing an investigation into possible criminal activity on the part of her or her appointees--one of whom she suspended WITH pay--in other words, gave the guy some extra vacation time in return for his having misused the power of his office.

When she was mayor of Wasilla, she was notable for 1) opposing funding for the local library, which is woefully understaffed and 2) firing anyone who disagreed with her.

When she ran for governor, early on she supported the "bridge to nowhere,": saying it would improve the local economy. This was a lie, and when she fessed up later, and said she really opposed the bridge, she promised the local residents she would come there to apologize. That was another lie--the locals are still waiting for her apology.

Her most recent lie was to the citizens of Alaska--never mind all the times (starting in June) she said she would not accept the VP offer.

She had made a comittment to apprear at the state fair--this is a very big deal with us--some of us take a day off work and drive hundreds of miles to get there. She broke this commitment when McCain held out the vice-presidential carrot.

for still another Alaskan's take, see
