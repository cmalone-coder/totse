In my experience, 2c-i will clump together a little bit, and the powder will have a crystally appearance and will look pure white. A little taste and you will recognize the halide flavor, similar to fire extinguisher (Type I or II) dust or some OTC medicines. You can really only rely on the word of your supplier, but it's trusted enough, do a little 5milligram bump, go ahead it's good for you. If it burns like insufflated Benadry or strong chili powder and you find yourself cursing me and my advice, it's probably 2c-i.

As far as experience, I think 2c-i is a little strange. Sure, there's euphoria, and a nice body high, but the body high is never too "streamlined" for me. It feels like I have this energy that makes me want to move my body around to consolidate, even though I'm usually very relaxed. Take it for a trippy concert and dance a little bit.

Visually, on doses around 15mg, there are faint patterns that will appear on surfaces. On higher doeses around 25-30mg, there are very pronounced, moving, constantly rearranging patterns on everything. Colors are usually a bit brighter, too.

I, personally usually have these moments where I suddenly realize how reflective a room can be. I'll notice reflections of myself and others in every reflective surface, mirrors, plastic, shiny metal, guitars, etc. It's really crazy, like going to a house of mirrors for a moment, seeing every reflection of everything around me, and reflections of reflections, then realizing I'm back.

Mentally, there's a constant feeling of realization. Like the mental feeling you get when you're looking for a pencil and you realize there's one in your hand. Even seeing someone on 2c-i you will see them stop and look around as if they just figured out something obvious. They will do this for a few hours. It's a good time in the right environment.
