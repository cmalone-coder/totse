strange change in thinking after acid trip
So I tripped for the first time on Halloween, and since then I've been what could best be described as personifying inanimate objects. I'll look at a pencil, or my speakers, or whatever else and it's like it has thoughts, or it's conscious.

Kind of like looking at a bird or a cat.

In no way is this going to noticeably affect my personality, it's just a strange and subtle afterglow.

Has anyone experienced something like this?

Edit: An example; I just look at the lamp on my desk, and I acknowledge "that lamp is aware of itself and observing me + this room".

EDIT 2:I just went to the bathroom to take a shower and noticed that it's individual body parts too, like id I look at my hand, or my foot, or my leg, or my har, ect. Luckily not my face, but it happens if I stare at PART of my face in the mirror, like an eye or my nose. Also, my body as a whole minus my head (as in neck and below).

Hopefully this won't last :( :( :( .

EDI 3: I have mirrored sliding doors on my closet, and I probably stared at myself way too much in them during the trip (while lying in bed), and when I had a mild feeling of my body being seperate from my mind, which may explain the body part thing.

Won't be doing that next time.
