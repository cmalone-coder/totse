Girlfriend bought me a new gun :) VZ-58
Well my girlfriend received some cash on a lawsuit she won, ended up buying me a brand new Czech VZ-58, got it for $799.99, came with 4 mags (plus 1 so 5 total), put about 200 rounds down range. It's a nice rifle, not quite an AK47 though it looks similar, the gas system is different, bolt is 2 pieces, striker fired, gas piston is separate from the bolt carrier which is weird but looks like reduction in the recoil, receiver is milled but yet the thing weighs less than an AR15, i'm impressed.

Accuracy is very nice as well, 3 headshots at 200 yards.
