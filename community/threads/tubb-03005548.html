Re: Can I cut down a relatively small tree with this?
Best to use a chainsaw or axe. Cut a wedge out about half way thorugh the tree, on the side of the tree in the direction you want it to fall. Then cut into the back side of the tree on the other side of the wedge until it falls.

Be careful the tree can kick back toward you. So when it starts to fall Move out of the way.
