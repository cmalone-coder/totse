Obviously you have a bias. Shonn Greene is no doubt a force to be reckoned with in the back position, but '08 Heisman? Eh, nahh. For one, it's notoriously biased towards the mvp QBs (which there isn't exactly a shortage of this season.)

You also have to realize Greene, Ringer, and a lot of the other top backs in stats have nearly twice the attempts as others. Since I'm a 'Bama/SEC fan I'll use GA's Moreno and Bama's Glen Coffee for examples. Moreno (1st of 4 in depth) has 1113 yards with an avg of 5.9 a carry and 15 TDs, with only 188 attempts. Coffee (1st of 3 in depth) has 1020 total with an avg of 6.3 a carry and 8 TDs, but only has 162 attempts. Coffee's TDs and attempts are below the others because Alabama utilizes a system that constantly rotates between their workhorses Coffee and Ingram. Also, up until the game in Death Valley, their other back Upchurch would get around the same attempts as the previously stated.

What it all really comes down to is skill. You can not say that Greene is a better back than say, Moreno, no matter what stats say.
Think about it like this: could Greene make some of Moreno's plays? Nah.
