basically, he is asking,

Can God make a rock so big, even He cannot lift it?



I would personally have to say that God can make a boulder infinatly large enough that nothing could theoretically control it (or lift it), including himself. Since he has almighty power, he has the power to make it big enough that even he cannot lift it.

Although with what is known as almighty power, God can do "anything" (with a couple exceptions), that means anything he can make, he can also lift. With him being able to lift anything that he has the power to make in the first place, that means he can lift any rock anyway.

Now, in my opinion, both seem to be very valid points and if the other isn't represented, both seem legitament (only because together, they somewhat "cancel" each other out). But since this is more or less a yes or no question (not QUITE that simplified, but to some extent it's one or the other) it cant be either way.

Still, with what Catholics call the "limitations of God", there is included something that means he cannot learn anything. With God knowing everything, you have to realise HE has to know the answer to this question. That being so, since there are both very valid and so called "legitament" points to both sides, and the question is designed to be a paradox of itself, I believe that the question itself is not made for human comprehension. The question in itself is so complex and spiritually infinitly complicated (wow) that only God himself knows, because only God really knows what he is capable of, isn't he?
