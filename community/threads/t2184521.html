George Carlin reconsidered
Right after he died, Carlin threads abounded. In order to conduse a Merry Xmas, I rented the four-DVD set of his stuff. It was quite a revelation.

For one thing, he seems older in his early stuff than in his later stuff--I attribute this to the drugs he used early on, before he cleaned up.

But what was really interesting was the light shed on societal attitudes. The set included his very first HBO special--circa 1977--and the producers were obviously scared shitless of public reaction to his "dirty" language.

The DVD started with a preface from Shana Alexander--this hoity-toity woman from PBS--comparing him to Aristophanes, Chaucer, and Shakespeare. Then towards the end--and his famous words you can't say on TV bit--they stopped it and ran a warning, saying that "you might not want to watch this."
