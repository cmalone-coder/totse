Happens to my girlfriend every once in a while. I usually sympathize with her that she's sad but don't add fuel to the fire. I'll give her a hug and try to calm her down, but be pretty honest and stern about my opinion about the topic.

It's a "Yeah that sucks, sucks that you're sad, now get over it" method. If you keep this attitude consistently (and I mean every time she acts up do it this way) soon she will start to change her ways to. I mean, she's not a test animal or anything, but its pretty much behavioral shaping. You're being kind to her, but reinforcing her getting over it quickly.
