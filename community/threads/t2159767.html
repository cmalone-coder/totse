Sure, I could go to bed earlier, but 6 hours of sleep should be plenty.

I'd really like to keep this habit up. Waking up early with out having to put any effort into is wonderful.

The next day I went to bed at 2am and woke up at 6:45am and drank half a bottle of "Fuel: 7 hour energy shot" because I figured it shouldn't require an entire bottle to wake you up. I'm not sure if it was having less sleep, the lower dose, the different brand, or doing it two days in a row, but I fell right back asleep till 10am.

tl;dr
For the next couple weeks I'm going to experiment with different energy drinks to see which one wakes you up best after you hit that snooze alarm. I am going to try to avoid "energy sodas" like redbull and rockstar, because that stuff is pretty bad for you. Suggestions for energy drinks, drugs or otherwise are welcome.

The problem with melatonin is that it was discovered long before scientists really understood what it does and how much you need. For example, in the late 80's and early 90's, we thought melatonin was a sleep hormone. Now we know it is much more complicated. In addition, tablet sizes average 3-5 mg. New evidence shows that adult males only need 150 micrograms, and the average female needs only 100 micrograms (a microgram is 1/100 th of a milligram). So the average melatonin supplement is 20 - 50 times more than we need! If you are using regular melatonin tablets, you can cut the pill into fourths, otherwise, try to find the smallest pill size available. If you are taking time-released melatonin, do not break the pill, as this will ruin the time-release.
