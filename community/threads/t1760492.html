Ok, after a little discussion with an old friend, I have decided to give it a go with dual monitors.

However, I need to go find an adapter before I can do anything.

Anyway, it sounds like you have one monitor plugged into your graphics card, and one in your Mobo? Wont work. Your GFX card should have a white port with funny squares to the right of the one your screen is in now, that port is for your second monitor. You have to go buy an adapter like I plan to tomorrow, and plug your other monitor into that.

Then from what I gather, right click on desktop and go to your "ATI Catalyst control center" and go to the "displays" tab, then detect your second screen and decide which is going to be which, and apply.

Now, lets hope it works

EDIT: Just found the adapter in a drawer, worked like a charm

Re: 2 screens
If you want more monitors, the easiest/cheapest way to do it is buy old PCI graphics cards off ebay, that's what I did.

I have an ATI 9600XT, I bought two Matrox Millenium IIs at £4 ($10) each off ebay and put them in, and now I have a triple-monitor setup.

The ATI card supports 2 monitors on its own, but the second is only via S-Video, so sometimes I plug it into my TV and have 4 monitors, but the resolution on the TV is crappy so I usually don't bother.

desktop (http://webzoom.freewebs.com/ormy/desktop.jpg)

desktop2 (http://webzoom.freewebs.com/ormy/desktop2.jpg)

EDIT: I forgot to mention, this method is only possible with win XP (maybe 2000, im not sure). And you'll notice I have a taskbar for each monitor that I can minimize things to, so all my programs don't get cramped up on the taskbar on my main monitor. You'll also notice that I have a single image spanning all 3 monitors, this is done with a neat program called "UltraMon".

To extend the Windows taskbat onto the other monitors, there's a setting in the video options of WinXP for ..."extend taskbar onto multiple monitors". I think though if you do this, then the wallpaper on the first screen extends to the other screen also however.

----------

Most people now who want dual screens jut buy a dual-screen card, but there are advantages and disadvatnages to doing it off one card, it really depends on what you want to do with the screens. It used to be that dual-screen-output videocards weren't real good for gaming, but that's not true anymore, many high-end cards have dual video outs. ,,,,,To my knowledge there's VERY few games that can use dual-screens in any way. What it really is great for is working, having a bunch of programs open.
