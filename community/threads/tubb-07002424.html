Avoid slot machines like the plague. The odds are greatly against you. There's no strategy for playing slot that will increase your odds of winning. The casino makes the majority of their money off of the machines. If they have a bunch of card counters on the black jack tables and get taken, the slot machines more than make up for it.

With black jack, do some reading. Read up on how to count cards and practice. I recomend Jerry Patterson's books. There's not really an easy way to tell you how to count cards over the internet, but I'll explain the theory.

Each card is assigned a value, then you use that to determine your strategy. You can keep track of what cards are still to be dealt and figure out whether to bet high or low or to just leave that table.
