As far as when to start doing drugs, when I was younger, I thought that 13 was a good age. I made my brother wait until he was 13 before getting him high for his first time. I'm only 2 years older.

Now, I'm 22, and I've seen a little more of the long-term effects. I have friends that started smoking and drinking at all different ages. I've noticed a definite correlation between the age that a person starts drugs and the likelihood of them heavily abusing hard drugs. I think it's just that you get over those stigmas of each drug at a younger age, and of course the older you get, the more likely you are (in most cases) to make better, more informed decisions.

Maybe you're 15 when you first try heroin and you haven't had time to really see any of your friends get bad to learn from their mistakes instead of your own. You start doing heroin, and by the time you're 19, you've got a problem that you just can't kick. But if you'd have waited until you were 19 to start, you'd probably have had a couple friends OD and would be much more respectful of the drug.

I've thrown the whole "__ age is good to start drugs" mentality out the window. Now I have to get to know the kid. Some are ready at 13, some aren't really ready until they're 33, some are never ready.
