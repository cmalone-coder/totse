Re: First time shrooms
Half an eighth is a perfect dose for first time. Most important thing to remember is stay in a positive mindset, and make sure you are completely free of any obligations or worries during the time you will be tripping. Make sure you know the people you are tripping with, because the people you trip with will have an EXTREME effect on how your trip turns out. Just go to www.erowid.com and read the mushrooms FAQ, read as much as you can about them, and read the first time trip reports with mushrooms. Also, the shroomery is by far the best place for any type of mushroom research.

Just stay positive, and stay confident, don't let fear manifest, it leads to a bad downward spiral. Just plan properly, make sure you are in a good mental state at this time in your life, and I guarantee you will have a mind opening, amazing experience.

I read today that if you take it with lemon it really heightens the effects aswell?
Yes, mushrooms absorb through the lining of your stomach. Taken from wiki - "Psilocybin is a prodrug that is converted into the pharmacologically active compound psilocin in the body by dephosphorylation.[2] This chemical reaction takes place under strongly acidic conditions or enzymatically by phosphatases in the body."
Honestly, I have done lots of shrooms, and powdering your shrooms and taking them with a cup of lime or lemon juice intensifies the trip more than you would believe. I honestly begin having pretty good visuals in ~5 mins, no fucking joke. (on a completely empty stomach)

Hope you have a good trip bud!
