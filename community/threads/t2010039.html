Health issues?
Asides from burns, is there really anything that concerns you health wise from chemicals from various synths, or dust from handiling compositions?

Although it has beenabout 4 years since I last did anything pyro related, I have a small paranoia about some particular inncedents...

From my friend who worked a lot with modelling, and various things solvent related (Hobby builders are exposed to lots of toxic crap from what I have seen, solvents, glues, puttys, aerosol spray) and he was telling me how bad Acetone was for you for skin contact. I don't know if he was reffering to as long-time exposure, but it still kinda made me think...

There was several occasions where I had bits of acetone spilled on my hands... but the worst happened when an entire bottle tipped over in my lab, coating the workdesk. I was mopping it up with paper towels, and I remember the place wasnt very well ventelated so I got woozy and a headache fast, but the worst part was for about a minute and a half I could not speak I don't know if it was my vocal cords going dead, but all that came out when I tried to talk was a kind of wheeze. It quickly went away, but that shit was scary. Havent been near the stuff since, but it kinda worries me what other long-term effects things like that might have.

I used to take apart lots of fireworks, especially the whisiling kind (Screecharoos, humeroos, screaming deamons, ect) and when the powder is broken up it forms dusty quite easily. A lot of these times were without resparators, and I think I inhaled a bit on a few occasions. I do remember getting headaches alot then.

The last one was somehing that grabbed my attention was when people here were debating weather naphaline was carcinogenic. On 2 occasions I powdered store-bought mothballs with a morter and pestel. Fortunatley, the powder was relly clumpy and didnt go airborne, but I did use it in a tube FAE. But one thing was is that I have seen some shows on special effects where these guys are making "Nap bombs" for fireballs, and ther was footage of a technitian with a barrel of "Industrial naphthaline" which looked like dry clumps of crystals, and he was breaking it down into a powder by grinding the clumps into a wire screen... so i dunno.

But the thing is, is that 2 of my grandparents died of cancer, so i might be due for it anyways
