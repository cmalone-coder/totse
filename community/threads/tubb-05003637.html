Re: Why is everyone so dry?
I'm going to guess the reason there is no drought in the states farther north has to do with the amount of shit coming into the border states from Mexico. Before the current supply shock, people around here could pick up an oz of Mexican shit for $50-$60. People could get a fat quarter of schwag for $20. A lot of people, if given the choice of spending $20 on a gram of hydro or a quarter sack of schwag, will buy the cheap shit.

So, this means that compared to a state farther north, imported Mexican weed makes up a much larger portion of the market. So, imagine 90% of weed consumed is schwag and 10% KB/Hydro (which is probably about the ratio I observed around my area when supply was good.) Suddenly, the border closes down and only a tenth of what was getting through now gets through. Can't get anything from Mexico, so now it's up to the domestic producers. They were doing okay supplying 10% of the market. Really, the shit from south of the border was so plentiful and cheap that they couldn't get that big. Now, all of the sudden, they're being asked to supply an additional 80% of the market. That would be like telling them, "For every plant you have growing now, plant 8 more." That's just crazy. So, prices go up. You've got a lot of pissed of potheads who are used to smoking a lot of cheap import being now asked to smoke a little bit of this expensive domestic. Eventually, if the border situation doesn't clear up the domestic producers will take up the slack, but with a plant that takes a while to grow and the possibility of this being transitory it will take a while.

Of course, you might ask, "But, we get Mexican weed in Virginia or Carolina or wherever. Why haven't we experienced a drought?" The simple answer would be that the proportion of cheap Mexican weed on the market in those non-border states is not nearly what it is on the border. If there is a problem with Mexican weed getting through, the domestic producers have the infastructure in place to ramp up production and take up the slack.

Well, okay. That's at least what I think is going on.
