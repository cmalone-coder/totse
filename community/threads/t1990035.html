Re: What to do with cordless phones
The 8870 chip serves as an analog decoder, outputting the tone results to the digital microcontroller. It will read tones from the land line, but it doesn't have the ability to pick up incoming calls, and the ringing voltage might even damage it.

I've been trying to redesign my decoder. I've looked into software decoding to cut it down to one chip, but it will be a while before I figure out how to get anything working.
