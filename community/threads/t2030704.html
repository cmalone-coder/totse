There's an interesting short-article in Wikipedia on what is referred to as the Ideomotor Effect.
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideomotor_effect

It is basically a scientific answer (not THE answer of) to what are typically considered supernatural devices... the ouija board, automatic writing, etc. Says that ultimately the answers are stemming from your unconcious.

If you're not ready to get in touch with your unconcious then there are potentially bad effects to this, particularly if your questions end up touching repressed issues.

Alternatively, if you have a mind that is closed to the supernatural other than surface actions like experimentation, you could find that your close-mindedness is based on a fear of the unknown and really fuck yourself up if something unexpected comes out.

Or, if you're a skeptic such as myself, one who has immersed himself in general reading about the paranormal and supernatural... I might find myself with a self-induced psychosis when I become convinced that the ouija is real... whether it is or not. Why would that happen? Because I know the warnings of interaction with the ouija, know the risks of the doors it supposedly opens, and may have forgotten a critical step necessary (or simply ignored it) to protecting myself from that which is on the other side. Whether it's there or not, I become convinced that I have unleashed something and turn into a victim of my own paranoia.

So yeah, it seems to me that there are some risks to doing something like this, and it might be a good idea to do a little introspection and decided exactly what kind of preparations you should make beforehand. After all, this experiment is dependent upon the interpretation of the board-user... whatever video you provide is going to be meaningless without the context you give us. Ultimately unless something wildly visible happens, the parameters of the experiment are ... you.

I'm being a bit long-winded here, so I hope that the point I'm making isn't lost in the message.
