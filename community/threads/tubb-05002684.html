Re: Making a good quality pipe
You don't have to have a lot of experience or equipment to make good glass pipes. They are more suited to crack or crystal than pot, but if you blow them right they work for pot too. All you do is get a propane torch, the kind that screws onto a can of propane. Then get some test tubes (must be pyrex), I get them by the dozen at a local science store. They must wonder what the hell we're doing with them all, but they probably know damn well what we're doing with them. You can also order pyrex tubing and rods online to do really slick work. But the easy way is to fire up the torch, hold the test tube by its open end (use heat proof glove) and hold its bottom end in the flame. Turn the tube back and forth between your fingers as you do this. After a while the glass will start to get soft and start distorting while you turn it. That means you've heated it up too much. Heat it to just before that point, then, while still turning the tube continually back and forth, blow gently into the open end (it shouldn't get hot) and remove the tube from the flame while still blowing. The glass will balloon out into a ball until it breaks through in some spot. While the glass is still hot use metal things to poke at it (like a screwdriver or something) and shape it any way you want. Or it can be used as is. Just enlarge the openening in the ball a bit and use as is for crystal, insert brillo for crack. To make a glass pot pipe is a bit more difficult, but with practice you can make them in a couple of minutes. Heat as before, but when blowing the glass ball don't keep blowing until a hole pops in it. Instead, let it cool a bit, then hold one side of the ball in the flame and don't turn the tube like before. When the side you are heating gets soft use your tools to push a section of the ball inward to form a bowl. Then poke a small hole or two in the bottom and you're done. Once you learn how to make these pipes, you can play around and make really cool stuff with coloured glass and hotter torches. Glass blowing is not as hard to do or get into as most people think. It's fun! I no longer use pipes like this but I still like to make one once in a while to give to one of my junkie friends for five bucks or a doobie or something. Have fun.

Why hasn't anyone written down the old-fashioned apple pipe? Also works with pears. Hollow out a tube-shaped opening in an apple in kind of an L shape, so there's one hole to suck out of and one hole to put the herb in. Enlarge the opening of that hole so it's in a bowl shape. Throw in some tinfoil (burn off the shiny side first, it really does give you cancer), poke some holes, and voila, an apple pipe! It adds flavor as well and your weed will taste very sweet.
