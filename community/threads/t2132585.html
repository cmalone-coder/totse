Revelation Chapter 11: An Interpretation
Revelation Chapter 11 (http://www.awitness.org/biblehtm/re/re11.htm): An Interpretation



11:3 I will give power to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy one thousand two hundred sixty days, clothed in sackcloth."

11:4 These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands, standing before the Lord of the earth.

11:5 If anyone desires to harm them, fire proceeds out of their mouth and devours their enemies. If anyone desires to harm them, he must be killed in this way.

(Ancient artifacts on the Earth begin to display their transdimensional characteristics, spewing forth a higher intensity vibratory æther pattern into physical frequency)

11:6 These have the power to shut up the sky, that it may not rain during the days of their prophecy. They have power over the waters, to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with every plague, as often as they desire.

(This is simply a symbolic way of describing the powerful effects these transdimensional artifacts will have over our current structure of matter)

11:7 When they have finished their testimony, the beast that comes up out of the abyss will make war with them, and overcome them, and kill them.

(Equilibrium must be restored, thus transdimensional artifacts are destroyed by backlash wave)

11:8 Their dead bodies will be in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also their Lord was crucified.

11:9 From among the peoples, tribes, languages, and nations people will look at their dead bodies for three and a half days, and will not allow their dead bodies to be laid in a tomb.

(Awestruck by awesome power that has been brought into physical frequency, unsure of what to make of this new signature that has been added to the pool)

11:10 Those who dwell on the earth rejoice over them, and they will be glad. They will give gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented those who dwell on the earth.

(Joy overcomes those of the worldly persuasion because the unknown that has brought fear into them is now gone, yet they choose to ignore this new ingredient that has been added to the brew, and celebrate trying to return to their normal mentality, thinking everything is OK, it is over)

11:11 After the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered into them, and they stood on their feet. Great fear fell on those who saw them.


(Reactivation of transdimensional antennas, higher vibratory æther waves spew forth once more. Changes in structure of matter are now more intense as everything begins to absorb this powerful energy. Fear now sweeps over those that were recently blissful as the unknown washes over them)

11:12 I heard a loud voice from heaven saying to them, "Come up here!" They went up into heaven in the cloud, and their enemies saw them.

(A signal enters the mind of those who are ready as their physical forms disintegrate. The realm at which the frequency of their specific section of æther waves were vibrating, or the fertile ground in which physicality manifest upon, was transferred into the extradimensional consciousness ships. “Look, a flying saucer!” exclaim the ignorant.)

11:13 In that day there was a great earthquake, and a tenth of the city fell. Seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake, and the rest were terrified, and gave glory to the God of heaven.

(Backlash of higher vibratory æther waves causes numerous disasters to occur, many die but do not fear for them, for it is their choice. Their signature will be reintegrated into the system and become once again on a planet with an ætheric vibratory level more suitable for them. Seeing the destruction, those whom were not yet ready, flee the cities and lead a more meditative life with less obsession of material distractions.)

11:14 The second woe is past. Behold, the third woe comes quickly.

(After destruction many entities, confused in this world of higher æther waves, begin to worship the physical antennas that draw the energy into this world rather than the energy itself)

11:15 The seventh angel sounded, and great voices in heaven followed, saying, "The kingdom of the world has become the Kingdom of our Lord, and of his Christ. He will reign forever and ever!"


(A higher dimensional being received the signal that has been in the æther ocean since the dawn of time, waiting for the proper vibration to manifest. Once integrated into the angel’s wave pattern it set the stage for the beginning of a new cycle. The trinity is now upon us!)

11:16 The twenty-four elders, who sit on their thrones before God's throne, fell on their faces and worshiped God,

11:17 saying: "We give you thanks, Lord God, the Almighty, the one who is and who was; because you have taken your great power, and reigned.

(The elders bowed down in praise of this joyous event, for the trinity, the Absolute One is in their presence. Earth belongs to you our beloved Father, and always it has but it is now that you have taken your great power to reign here on earth.)

11:18 The nations were angry, and your wrath came, as did the time for the dead to be judged, and to give your bondservants the prophets, their reward, as well as to the saints, and those who fear your name, to the small and the great; and to destroy those who destroy the earth."

(The many fools whom still worshiped these false material antennas, or those whom refused to accept the changes they’ve experienced in their very being were incinerated by this new æther cycle, cleansing the bucket of patterns of which were not ready, reintegrating them into the system to be incarnated in an area more suited to their signature level. But for those who are ready, reward is soon to come.)

11:19 God's temple that is in heaven was opened, and the ark of the Lord's covenant was seen in his temple. Lightnings, sounds, thunders, an earthquake, and great hail followed.

(The energy patterns are now becoming fully integrated into this higher ætheric cycle as the atomic structure of the earth and all it inhabits transform into this new frequency of perception and experience. Heaven is now among us!)
