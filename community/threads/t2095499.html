Re: So, what's the easiest drug to make?
What do you mean by 'make'?

If you mean synthesize, I would say arguably chloral hydrate.

Conversions, such as GBL -> GHB are also fairly simple, but I would not personally class as a synthesis, same, and to a greater extent, to cocaine -> crack.

Extractions such as salvia, DMT & mescaline extractions aren't too hard, with a lot of work and following a spoon feed guide exactly.

Growing is probably the easiest, and your plants will do all the synthesising for you - pot, shrooms, kratom, salvia, mimosa, cacti and other drug containing and entheogenic plants could all be grown with enough work.


If you do consider a synthesis or anything involving anything vaugely lab related, just remember you're going to eat it. And that heavy metals are cumulative poisons.
