The Non-MAOI PhenylEthylAmine Thread!
So I have around 100g of PEA, (Phenethylamine, phenyethylamine, phenylethylamine, etc) and after reading around google, particulary the body building forums, I have decided that this supplement should be tested alone instead of with a MAOI.

As most people already know, PEA gets broken down by mono amine oxide inhibitor enzymes (MAOI) in your body really quickly. There are quite a few reports of people taking large enough doses to bypass this. There are also 2 or 3 reports of intranasal/smoked doses.

The theory is that if you take a large amount, the majority will get inhibited but some will pass, or if you use a different ROA they will pass it more (Like DMT is inhibited by MAOI but if you smoke it, it works).

Personal background experience with phenethylamine derivitives:
Mixed amphetamine pharmaceuticals, methamphetamine, MDMA, 2c-e/b/i.

The controversy is that Shulgin said he didn't feel anything after oral 1,600mg or 50mg IV, but many many people online said they have.
