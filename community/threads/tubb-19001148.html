Re: obtaining BP
Black powder can be had from some fireworks (a servicable replacement can be found in Camelia Flower spinners, for starers) and model rocket engines. I can be bought from a gun store with ID but a license is not generally required.

Potassium nitrate (an ingredient in black powder, as it happens) can be had as stump remover as well as a fertilizer. Check the hardware store or agricultural supplier, and don't be afraid to ask them to get it for you if need be.

Potassium nitrate can also be bought/stolen from labs or laboratory suppliers, but this is rarely a feasible means of procurement.

~Zero the Inestimable
