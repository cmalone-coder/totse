Re: Getting weed into festivals
Depends how much you have on you as to how careful you want to be. The one place they usually post dogs is at train stations so if your carrying a large amount to deal then you may want somebody with a phone to scout ahead and let you know about the situation.

In terms of hiding it, use your imagination: Tape it to your balls, put it in your sock, shoe or under the sole. If your camping wrap it in your tent (They wont unwrap that cause it takes too long, iv seen them check sleeping bags though.) Use one of those baked bean stash tins, an opened can of beer in the middle of a crate (How I get spray paint in.) Up your arse or just stick it in the back of your bag.

Bear in mind that bringing weed to festivals is normal, they cant give 1000s of people a full search or confiscate it of 1000s of people. They may know you have weed but they wont make a huge effort to find it so unless its easily visible then your safe.

Like I said, just use your imagination and make it difficult for them to find.
