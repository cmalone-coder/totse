Well, D-Link is the name of the brand, not the name of the chipset. Drivers are written for the wireless chipset, so D-Link isn't a name that you really need to know. Use the utility "lspcmcia" (list pcmcia) to see what the actual chipset of the card is, and then look on the fedora wiki or forums for how to get it to work.
