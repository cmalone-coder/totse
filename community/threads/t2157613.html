I thought that I would update anyone who remembers my small house
So, what I've done so far is I've built the floor and three walls. So all I have left is the roof and one more wall. Rejoice!

Anyways, one of the walls has a 2'x4' window for some natural light, and one of the walls has a door (Duh). I plan on making an overhang when I'm done with the interior and everything else so that I can maybe make a small porch and overhang.

The house is 2 stories, but the top story is just for storage and sleeping, it's not even close to tall enough to stand in. It's enough for a small mattress and a couple of shelves for canned and dried food.

In front of the house I'm planning on having a small lawn and a garden. The space I have for the lawn is about 15x15, but that's just an estimation.
