Re: mosin nagant m44
Mosins are good guns for the price. Cheap ammo to boot. I love my 91/30. However something that should be said as you don't seem to know a whole lot about these guns is to take great care to clean it after every, I repeat every, time you shoot it. The ammo is berdan primed so it's corrosive. The best way I've found to prevent this is to take the cleaning rod and run a patch soaked in an ammonia solution trough the barell and wipe off the bolt with the same solution. In leiu of an ammonia solution, glass cleaner such as windex can be used also. This gets rid of the chemicals that cause the corrsion. Then clean it as you nomrally would. Oh and by the way dose anyone here have any issues with the bolt sticking?
