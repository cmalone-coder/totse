Ok...I searched this topic and couldn't find anything like it so if this is a repeated topic please excuse me. Ok...the bible states that God created angels to worship...and it also says that the big difference between angels and humans is that God gave man free will...and not angels. Well...Lucifer was an angel...and Lucifer decided to rebel against God and make an attempt at the throne. How can this be possible unless God created Lucifer strictly for this purpose? Unless God gave Lucifer the will to believe himself better...then he couldn't have thought that way. So God had to have created Lucifer to fall...to create the balance between good and evil. So Lucifer is a good thing...because without him we wouldn't know good...because we wouldn't know evil. This is all supposing you believe in God and the Devil...furthermore it says that when he fell Lucifer took some of the angels from the heavens with him...how? Unless God created them to fall as well.
