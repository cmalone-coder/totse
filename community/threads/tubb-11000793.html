Re: Computer-related entry into a competition
IP over audio.
Figure out a way to represent various octal or binary data sats aurally. Send it to a speaker, and stick a microphone across the room. Pump up the speaker, and turn on the mic, both of them being hooked up to a transmitting and recieving computer.

A scheme like this would allow you to store data on audio or video tape, burn it onto CD as a wav file. If you were to implement some sort of encryption it would go beyond obfuscation as a transmission scheme.

also, you could network two computers (albeit slowly) through audio cable...
