I think I'm going to die lonely...
I'm not really sure where to post this, but in the end, it seems centered around affection, so I assume this is probably where I should... Anyway... I seem to be a generally well liked person. I was voted to be president of my highschool class three years in a row. I just won "Funniest" for the superlatives in the yearbook. Really, none of that means much to me, except that it kind of shows me that people appreciate what I do for them. Really, my whole life, I've just wanted to make other people happy, even if it's at the expense of my own happiness because, in the long run, I've always thought that my generosity would be returned. I don't usually tell anyone this, but this is the internet, so it doesn't really matter, but I spend most of my money by giving it to charities. The only reason I don't tell people this is because the people who go around talking about how much money they donate are really only doing it to seem like kindhearted people. Anyway, it seems that my generosity is never returned because of something I'm missing. Girls have liked me before because I seem nice and I can make them laugh and whatever else, but for some reason, I can never keep a girlfriend. I just get way too nervous over every little thing and end up blowing it. I don't know if to call her, when to call her, does she like me, what can we do for a date, etc. It's just killing me. I'm a senior and haven't had a single girlfriend throughout highschool. I would legitimately just kill myself if it weren't for my family who I love too much to do something like that or the fact that I know, no matter how slim the chance, there's still a chance that I can improve. Just to clarify, I'm not suicidal, it's just that the only thing I'm really living for now is my family, humor, and hope. But now, I'm feeling hopeless, and it's hard to laugh at, so really all I have is my family. Can someone give me advice as to what I can do to help myself be able to be with a girl? I'm sure I'll have at least a few replies just telling me what a loser I am, but if I get at least one serious piece of advice, it'd be fine.

Do things that boost your confidence, and start to love yourself, and I mean really love yourself (I know, that sounds like a horrible, girly thing for me to say, but it's true, core confidence is one of the most attractive things in anyone). Give it a little while, see how thing pan out.

Don't think to seriously on the whole relationship fontier, at this stage in your life, you should be aimed at having fun, not bogging yourself down with issues of manogamy.
