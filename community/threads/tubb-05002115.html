Re: LSD to brain, how'd I do that?
a clarification on the mechanism of LSD...

LSD, DMT and psilocin are seratonin agonists (i.e. they bind to postsynaptic seratonin receptors). However they have been shown to exhibit inhibitory effects on signal transmission. So while stimulating postsynaptic receptors, they creating a negative feedback system which acts to inhibit presynaptic neurons. bufotenine and isoergine (LSA) also fall into this category.

The utility of this distinction is that all seratonin agonist are not the same... there are some that exhibit an excitarory effect on signal pathways, producing an entirely different effect. a-methyltrytamine falls into this category.
