So I thought I'd write a 'how to play safe on totse' guide. I'm posting it as a thread and not an announcement so people can add things I have forgotten and questions be answered.

So...

How does a E-stalker track you down?

Probably the first thing he'll do is get you to email him.

You won't believe how many people use their real name or their maiden name when they open an email account and of course it gets sent with the email!

But you didn't, cos you're a bit savvy?

Well

You won't see it normally but hidden in your email is an 'address'.

Often this address will only tell him who provides your internet access (ie Expensive Calls R Us.inc Georgia or something like that) but that at least helps him narrow down the search to an area of the country or just confirms the 'Georgia' (or where ever) that you posted in your profile.

BUT if you post from a company office, a Library, a school, a college etc then its very likely that that hidden address will tell him that.

Here an example of what I mean. This was the hidden part of an email I received from someone at a college in the UK.

MIME-Version: 1.0

Google tells me that 'ccn' means its 'City College' in Norwich. And even if it didn't then the 194.*** bit would. I simply put the number that was there (the stars are mine because we shouldn't post numbers like this on totse) into whats called an 'IP lookup' and bingo :


netname: NOR-CTY-COL01

country: GB
admin-c: MM950-RIPE


Now that college has probably several hundred computers on several different sites so that alone may not help him too much but a small public library or internet cafe and all he needs to do is be there often enough to work out who you are (he can be emailing you from his cell phone).


So how do you check?

Go to http://www.dnsstuff.com/ .At the very top of the page it says Your IP: 86.***.**.** (your number will different to mine and have no stars!)

Copy that number and paste it into the box labelled IPWHOIS Lookup (scroll down the page).

That will bring up reams of goobledegook but just scan through it to see if anything there identifies where you are.

For example, mine brings up this:

Generated by www.DNSstuff.com

Location: United Kingdom [City: London, England]

ARIN says that this IP belongs to RIPE; I'm looking it up there.

Using 0 day old cached answer (or, you can get fresh results).
Hiding E-mail address (you can get results with the E-mail address).

Which simply tells them that my internet provider is in London (which is 21/2 hours drive from where i am now, even the way I drive!)
So no danger there then.

But don't.

You see the next trick for a E-stalker is to punch your username into google. He'll find and read all those things you posted on other boards maybe 10 years ago!

Like those posts where you mention your mum's first name is Patricia or whatever or you live not far from the biggest windmill in the whole of Holland.

Never forget that for an online public records/white pages search all he needs is a surname and a town!. This includes things like your post on 'chattering_teens'dot com five years ago
that your dad is a builder and you listed your real home town in your profile.

Can you remember all the crap you've said online over the last 5 years? I can't, as someone proved to me here the other day by posting one of my 'real' first names.

If you answer that honestly then you have damn near given a would be stalker the keys to the kingdom. After a surname and a town then a date of birth and a town are the next best thing. If you want us to congratulate you on your birthday then just lie a little, we'll forgive you.
