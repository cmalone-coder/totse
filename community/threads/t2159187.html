Zenwalk will do great.
As pointed out by the previous poster, the problem most likely isn't related to the distribution itself, a dmesg would be useful in case their's faulty hardware and the previous OS didn't detect it. (very often the case when you let the system decide what hardware you want to make use of)

Try installing another WM/DE, there is a wide varity of WM that aims to be minimalistic.
Fluxbox --- very minimalistic, command line based
fvwm-crystal --- feature-richer than Fluxbox, CLI based
Xfce --- feature richer than fvwm-crystal, GUI based

My personal all time favorite (although i liked the kde 3.4.x series) is xfce and afterstep (it's dead nowdays, though).
