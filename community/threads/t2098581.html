I think my dad used to cook/use meth
I was watching Breaking Bad the other night, and in one scene the chem teacher picks up this big round bulbous flask (not sure what it's called) and insists they cook in that. My dad has some lab matierals in boxes in our garage for some reason, that as far as I remember, have never been used in my lifetime, but I remember seeing one of those big round flasks in there, maybe two. I think it sounds a lot like a methadone clinic, from what I've read up on recently. I guess when I go home for spring break, I can examine the chemistry set more closely, as I haven't seen it in a few years and now have actually had a few chem classes. And if I get a chance, I could go back to that salvation army place and see if they have/had a methadone clinic.

What other reason would someone need lab equipment at home? Even if they are a college chemistry student...I don't see how that would help you study, really. I mean he had this shit before graduate school too, I believe.

It's possible, I guess, but he could've used the flask (a round bottom/RB flask, btw) for a whole load of other chemistry.

The flasks are used generally for heating liquids. You use the RB flasks over regular Erlenmeyer flasks as the RB flask heats its contents more evenly, I believe.

He could've been distilling some alcohol or something.
