Current Low Dose 2 CT 7 Report
So this stuff is lot more gentler than I imagined. Let me say that for starters. I inhaled around 3 mg's (around 3, because of this shitty scale from ebay I got for 40 dollars, spend the money for a nice scale if you really want to know what you're taking) using the chasing the dragon method.

I didn't do it all at once, because that could potentially burn it all up and melt it into burning plasticy nasty shit. So in little clumps (because this stuff has a mind of it's own when you're trying to prepare it, it sticks to random things, randomly explodes onto the sides of the container due to static electricity (an entirely different story with this shit and the way it reacts to that, and how my scale actually came with a statically charged lid that continuously warped the internals of the scale causing the measurements to never be the same), etc.

So basically, carefully vaporizing this stuff in little piles at around 2:12. I sat for a little bit and began to get cold sweats and generally like, but not like where this was really going.

So I took a shower, which turned into a bath, which in turn felt like I was rolling on ecstasy. The cold is your enemy on this, it's like E in those regards.

The first of the visuals (I didn't get "visuals" with this low dose, just the beginnings) I barely got any affects, and definitely didn't go above a plus 1 trip, if it even got to 1) are that everything is detectably "brighter". Brighter in the sense that you kind of feel like you're dreaming, your mind feels a little like that too, unleashing everything from the surface (notice I say surface) what your imagination has been pondering about for the past 2 days.

So everything's brighter, and then, from what I think this was going towards, everything begins to vibrate and rotate about it's own axis in a spiraling fashion, within it's own section of your vision, while you yourself do this.

So everything get's pretty whirly (on the higher doses....) and I wouldn't recommend eating a lot anytime around this.

Mental giddyness is the only way to explain the mind effects. It's like I'm reduced to seeing everything in a very carefree and expressive field of vision. Nothing really matters, unless it has to, in which case I'm fully prepared to use my brain to the full extend of it's capacity (and beyond) in order to deal with the problem.

It's definitely a stimulant, definitely a noo-tropic (brain enhancer), definitely like E. it has visuals but I can't really comment on them because I didn't get to that level yet, this was a first try. Very happy with the results.

If you've read about the muscle tension and tremors like I did, they are here as well. And they're nice. They bring back the E qualities to the experience, like I'm in a good state of mind, my body's revved up to max, and I'm sweating balls and loving it.

Sweating feels good. Like I'm removing toxins.

Anyway. In order to stop rambling. I'll end this here. Not much of a report, but it's worth typing. Because it feels good. Everything feels good. I guess I just had to let BLTC know, 2 ct 7 is worth trying.

Sorry...for any confusions...typos...etc etc.
