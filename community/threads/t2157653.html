Yeah, you could. It's pretty damn easy to get a cannabis card if you want one. However your going to need California Id or be a resident. A lot of people I know go through MediCann. MediCann is an organization of integrated medical clinics committed to providing a stress-free, holistic environment where patients in need of medicinal marijuana receive a cost-effective, expert evaluation by a licensed medical professional. Qualified patients receive a physician's recommendation allowing them the legal use of Medical Marijuana under California Proposition 215, also known as the California Compassionate Use Act of 1996 Health and Safety Code Section 11362.5, and under SB420, section 11362.7.

http://tinyurl.com/5grhww

Pretty much all you need is 150-175 dollars. Although actually having something in your medical records helps quite a bit, it's not required. A valid California ID is also required but if your coming out here for college that wouldn't be a problem obviously.

Go in there with a respectful attitude and say your complaining of anxiety or back pain. It's hard to test for both of those. Which means it's hard to disprove. You can also say it's to help with the appetite if your a skinny fella. Don't say it's for insomnia or you will simply be prescribed a sleeping pill which works on everyone. Basically don't act a fool and you should pass your evaluation fine.

So great you have a card. What can you do with it? What powers does it posses? Federally none. But statewide laws you may ask?

Law SB 420 states:

" No person or designated primary caregiver in possession of a valid identification card shall be subject to arrest for possession, transportation, delivery, or cultivation of medical marijuana in an amount established pursuant to this article, unless there is reasonable cause to believe that the information contained in the card is false or falsified, the card has been obtained by means of fraud, or the person is otherwise in violation of the provisions of this article"

That's pretty nice in and of itself. But then there's also this.

SB 420, Section 11362.77 states:

“... that qualified patients and primary caregivers may. . . maintain no more than six mature or 12 immature marijuana plants . . . Counties and cities may set guidelines exceeding, but not reducing, the state limits.”

Yes, you can legally grow weed. Most counties will let you have more then six plants growing in your house. For example I believe the maximum in mine is thirty plants.

Ya can't get cocky though. It doesn't make you immune.

SB 420, section 11362.79 states that patients may not ingest medicinal marijuana:

1.In any place where smoking is prohibited by law.
2.In or within 1,000 feet of the grounds of a school, recreation center, or youth center, unless the medical use occurs within a residence.
3.On a school bus.
4.While in a motor vehicle that is being operated.
5.While operating a boat.

The cards have expirations of 6 months to a maximum of 1 year before renewal. There are several dispensaries in most county's which sell the CHRONIC of the chronic. They have mids to dank all at prices based upon their potency. Their is several different types of edibles. Brownies, Cookies, Pot tarts, etc etc.

They also usually have really hot girls working there. Which let's face it just makes them hotter. The staff is usually very nice aside from the BIG BLACK security guards( Don't fuck with them). It is not unheard of to receive a free gram or joint just because it's a new club your going to and they don't know your face. Be sure to have your card ready when you go.

Don't answer your cellphone in the dispensary or even in the fucking parking lot. That is sketch and they will get pissed. If you get caught selling marijuana via your cannabis card you will be royally fucked.

I wish you luck and hopefully you come check out the West coast.

Re: Someone explain California to me
Just getting a piece of mail and renting an apartment is not enough to establish residence. Otherwise people would be able to do that and get resident rates for college too.

No, you need to be able to show that you have lived in state for at least a year.

As for getting a card, while it is true that there are still places where anybody can get a card for enough money, most places are tightening up things a lot. But the law was written so vaguely that it is still pretty easy to come up with an at least semi-legit reason to get one.

As for the quality, it is the best the state has to offer. And when you go to a medical coop when they say you're getting Granddaddy Purple, or OG Kush, you know for damn sure that that is what you are getting.

Now here's the thing though: I don't even bother anymore. I realized that the only advantage of having the card was that I could go into the coops.

Now don't get me wrong, it is quite fun to be able to choose from so many types of high quality bud and hash, and oils and edibles, etc... But like someone already mentioned, you can buy pretty much the same stuff that the clubs have - since a lot of it comes from the same growers. And believe it or not, the prices on the street are pretty comparable with those of the coops**. So if that's worth $125 to $200 a year to you, then by all means go for it.

But if you're like me, you'll opt for still getting it on the street. Or better still, get to be introduced to someone who grows. That's what I did. I know two who grow for the coops. And I can get an ounce for $280, which is cheaper than the coops charge [and is more than the growers make from the coops, see the note at the bottom].

I keep a copy of one of my expired prescriptions. I figure if I ever do get questioned by the cops I can show that I did have one at one time. That may be enough to get a pass as many cops here - especially in northern Cali - really don't like having to waste time on small time pot shit.

But and here's the main thing for me - even if they do bust me with the full force of the law, the worst that can happen to me for having an ounce or under - as long as it's all in one container - is that I get a $100 ticket. It's an infraction [how apt...lol], not even a misdemeanor.

So it comes down to that I was paying $125 to $200 a year to keep from getting a $100 ticket. Seeing that in my entire life I have never been busted to pot, it seems that it just isn't worth it to me just to be able to go to the coops. I mean, if I really felt the need for something specific that I can't get myself or make , I just ask someone I know who does still have a card.



** Those suckers make money hand over fist and might be an endangered species. I recall recently reading something about a court decision that said that coops that operate on a for-profit basis are not legal. Personally I think that this is good. I don't think for profit coops are good, at last at this stage of things.

Right now they are about the biggest target for the anti-medical pot crowd and it really is hard to refute them. I mean, it is pretty hard to keep saying, on the one hand, that these are just oops by patients and for patients to be able to get cheap and safe access to medical pot. Well it may be safe, but it is certainly not cheap if it is the same as street prices.

People should not be making money off this yet. Money going into the cause, to other such non profit shit, fine. But asking a lot of money always pisses off people who don't have money, for one thing. Once medical pot gets to the point where it is taken for granted, [I]then will be the time to allow profits to go to people. Not to mention the time for making it legal for all.
