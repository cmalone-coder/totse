I would like to begin by saying that my A.P. English teacher is a dried up old whore who thinks that doodling while she is talking is a cardinal sin. I knew she didn't like me and she knew I didn't like her. Nevertheless, I tried to behave myself and not be an asshole to her. Then one day she gives us an assignment to write a short story about anything we want.

I wrote what was, in my opinion, a great story. It was inspired by various cool things that I was thinking about that day like The Highlander, psychic powers, Fight Club, and a lot of anime shows that I had been watching. It was told from first person P.O.V.

I turned it in expecting an A, but the next day, I was called out of Latin class and asked to come to the office. I was thinking to myself, "I haven't done anything wrong, so maybe it's just a message from my parents or something." I walk in and see the principle, a counselor, and two uniformed police officers. Now I know that I am in some deep shit. They tell me to come with them and take me out to a waiting police cruiser. I was on the verge of shitting myself.

They take me to the police station and lead me to a small room with a metal table and one chair. I sit down and one of the officers asked me if I knew why I was there. I said I had no idea. Then, he laid down a copy of the story I had written. He said that the last paragraph of the story, in which the unnamed main character imagines what he will do to his classmates the next day (yeah, it was violent), had been construed as a threat by my teacher. They also tell me that my writing bears a resemblance to Eric Harris and Dylan Klebold's writing.

I spend the next hour and a half convincing the cops that I am not a terrorist or a psychopath. I explained that the entire story was a work of fiction and that I had no intention of hurting anyone in my school.

They eventually decided that I wasn't a threat and let me off with a warning. They also said that while the subject matter was inappropriate for school, it was very good writing, which made me feel a little better. They called my parents to come and pick me up. As you can imagine, that wasn't a very pleasant ride home. My dad understood that it was a misunderstanding, but my mom (who spends all her nights watching Court TV and Dateline) is screaming at me the whole time. She said that it was my own fault because I look like a goth (I don't. She thinks that black T-shirts are goth attire.)

I go to school the next day, expecting an apology from my teacher. Instead, she tells me that I will have to write another story and that I would lose 50 points for turning it in late. I tell her in the nicest way possible that she can go fornicate herself with rusty pole. She then sends me to the office, where I find out that I will spend the next week in detention.







Yeah, my week has been just fucking grand.

-NS
