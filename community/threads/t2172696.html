I get awful throat/chest congestion from my Vyvanse (ADHD Rx)... can anyone help?
mods, if this is not suitable for BLTC you can move it. I know its more of a medical inquiry than talking about the drug, but I don't know of any other forum where people might possibly know about weird side effects like these.


I had adderall XR for quite a while, but I had started to crash on it like 6 hours after taking it, so my shrink switched me to Vyvanse. I think its dextroamphetamine thats chemically bonded to release more evenly throughout the day, so its pretty similar to the adderall. I don't remember having this problem with the adderall (maybe i did but not as bad), but more often than not when I take it I get some degree of runny congestion in my throat and it just drives me nuts. It's very rare that I'm actually coughing stuff up... I'm just constantly clearing my throat because it tickles and kinda gurgles.


I usually only take it monday-thursday or monday-friday and i have enough work done that I don't need it on the weekends. When I take it thursday or friday morning for the last time that week, the congestion is nearly all gone by afternoon the next day, and then im fine for the rest of the weekend. When I take it the following monday morning the congestion usually doesn't return until sometime tuesday.

On a given day in the middle of the week I take it at 6:30 or 7:00 in the morning, and I'm usually fine until about 11:00, then it starts. It seems like the more stressed that day is the worse it gets. By 9:00 or 10:00 at night it usually starts to subside. It doesn't go away but it gets significantly better.

When I've taken double the dose (100mg) for an all-nighter it usually gets bad enough that it's become my primary motivation to do those as infrequently as possible.

If I'm really strict about going to bed early and get like 9 hours of sleep every night then it doesn't get very bad, but its not practical for me to be in bed by 10pm every night (which i'm sure most ppl who work go to school etc. can relate to).

In short, it seems like
things that help: longer periods of time between doses, smaller doses, lots of rest, low-stress environment
things that make it worse: taking it daily, higher doses, less or lower quality sleep, stressful/busy environment.

I smoke cigarettes lightly (1/3 a pack a day at most) and I thought that would surely be a big part of the problem, but I can't actually find a link between them. If I take the Vyvanse and don't smoke any cigarettes I get the congestion the same as if I do smoke, but on the weekends when I don't take the Vyvanse i can smoke and not have any congestion at all.

I've tried quite a few OTC products and home remedies... sudafed, chloratrimaton (sp?), benadryl, zyrtec, robitussin, gargling salt water, those are the ones I can think of now. My roommate and I drink opium tea (more often than we probly ought to) and sometimes this helps a lot, but its hit 'n miss. Dicyclomine (Bentyl) 10mg seems to help "okay" ... My doc wrote me a Rx for it because the Vyvanse has given me mildly loose stools (no i wasn't running to the restroom leaving a trail of shit lol) a few times and he said I can just take this as needed. Could the loose stools and this gurgly throat congestion have any correlation?

Would you guys guess this is some kinda histamine response allergy thing, or a short lived infection that the Vyvanse makes my throat temporarily vulnerable to? Like I said I garggled a whole lot with HOT salt water, and it didnt seem to do anything.



i've googled the hell outa this and can't seem to find anything, so if anyone has some insight I'd be extremely greatful.

thanks
-Jon

That's pretty strange to me...but I think I might know what you're talking about.

I've been taking Vyvanse off and on for about a week, not by prescription though. If I had a long night and need to work early I take about 60mg and it gets me goin. Sometimes 90 and occasionally 100.

The most prominent side effect for me is dry mouth. But this could be what you're experiencing. Sometimes the dry mouth causes the mucus in your mouth to become so thick that when you try and swallow it just gets kind of stuck.

Or it really could be congestion (too much fluid in sinuses/chest). Does it feel tight in your chest like there is flem in there? Try taking a relatively high dose of guaifenesin (mucinex). If that relieves tension and you cough stuff up, then you should probably talk to a doctor about it. I'm pretty sure that amphetamines are decongestants.

I'm guessing, however, that it is the first one in which case you can go to your doctor and get a lower dose, or drink an extra few glasses of water and avoid other diuretics.
