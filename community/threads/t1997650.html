Headphone wire repair trick
Ok.. so the cat chews through the wire on your headphones. Sucks, but you dont need new ones if you don't mind a repair job.

Cut out the damaged part of the wire. On each end of the exposed wire, remove the outer housing so 1" of each individual wire is showing. Now, twist each wire together with it's other half on the opposite end, and using a lighter, burn them back together. The smoke smells pretty bad but it won't set off smoke alarms.

You do this to remove the varnish on the wires so you can make a contact between them, and probably fuse some copper together as well; I'm almost certain a lighter flame can soften copper a lot.

Either way, I just fixed an expensive pair of earbuds; sound quality in the repaired side is perfect.

Now, the proper way to do this likely involves soldering, but if you don't have one of those a lighter clearly works.
