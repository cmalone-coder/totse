I defintely advise staying sober for as long as possible. Then after a while if you feel you are in control again you can make hte experienced decision as to whether you wnat to responsibly use drugs again.

Obviously if he can't control his urge to do drugs as of this moment, it will never happen. Yes, that's how it works. If you can't use self-control now, you most likely won't ever be able to moderate yourself in the future. If you don't think that's true, you're in denial :(

so you can do two things, you can ever quit for good and never look back. Or you can learn to use in moderation now. What you have to learn how to do is enjoy your life without being fucked up. And I think most peoples problem is they either are unhappy with their situation and/or hate themselves personally (how they look, who they are, etc) so you need to come to terms with this shit. You need to understand why you turned to drug use in the first place to escape your life.

We all want to escape reality, some more than others, we have to find why specifically and personally what makes us unhappy about being sober. For some people, it's just as simple as having nothing better to do and not knowing or caring to do anything besides getting fucked up. For most however, it's self-esteem related.

I hate getting fucked up/drunk and loving life and feeling happy, whenever that used to happen I would get sad afterwards while fucked up and ask myself how come I can be this happy when i'm fucked up and not sober. It made me learn to appreciate my life when i'm sober, and now it's the same thing.

But yea, like the other guys have said, it's all about getting rid of free time and finding something productive to do with your time. Making music is productive. Swallowing pills or drinking liquids like beer and vodka are not. Fun? Yes. Contributing to your life and/or society? Not in the least. (Except as a social laxative. That does count for something. Fuck, America was founded in taverns for christ's sake...sooo)

Re: Ways to stay sober
You want my honest advice?

Stay the fuck away from this message board.

And I am dead serious when I say that.

Why? Well I would say that 9 out of 10 (If not 10 out of 10) people who try and stop using or try to take a break, actually relapse/use earlier than expected/start using again much, much sooner.

Obviously there is no scientific data to back this statement up, but I do know quite a few people from this board (Both on and off-line) that are poster-children for this theory.

It's actually common sense if you think about it. If one is trying to stay away from drugs in the first place, what the fuck is the point of coming to a board that talks about using and abusing illicit, illegal, controlled and household drugs/chemicals in an abusive/self-medicating manner?

Give up? There is no fucking point, other than to use or tease yourself, give yourself cravings, and 9.9 times out of 10, trigger a relapse.

So while you are taking a vacation from drugs and starting up your weight training/gym schedule, also remember to please take a vacation from this forum. And I don't mean just stop posting, I mean to not even come here to read anything either; it's just all pointless and anti-conducive to anyone's plan of fretting from drugs.

I would also recommend talking to a psychiatrist perhaps or someone who you can really open up to. And if you KNOW you have addiction or think you might (Then you do), I would definitely look into seeing a substance abuse counselor at least once to see what and how serious the shit you are really getting yourself in to.

On a lighter note, yoga seems to help me very much as far as clearing the mind of all stress and cravings; just relaxing. If there are any local classes (Perhaps at the gym you will be going to even), I would definitely look in to that as well.

Good luck with everything, and I would say come back and keep us updated, but if I were you, I would wait for a long, long time, until you think you are ready, if at all. And as much as I hate saying this, I would search for and become a member of some sort of drug recovery board to help aide you in your mission and also to get advice to any more questions you may have, resulting in much more positive and experienced answers/info along the way.

Take care and again... good luck!

Mike.
