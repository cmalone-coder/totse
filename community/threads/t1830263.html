The tubes from cannonfuse arent that good. I think the ones from UN are much better, and work much better with black powder.

Paper end plugs are meant for explosives that burn faster than black powder, so the paper ones are strong enough to hold before the walls of the tube rupture, which is what gives a loud noise. I prefer using epoxy or wood glue and saw dust end plugs, since they are much harder, and allow the tube to burst before blowing out. Just about any black powder will work in salutes if you know how to make them right, but the finer black powders work the best, so when buying your black powder, go for the finest powder. I like flash powder, since it works much better, and it is cheaper than buying black powder, but if you wanted to you could make your own black powder for 4 or 5 dollars a pound.

Re: Newbie, Need help with m-80
First of all, FFF black powder doesn't burn fast enough to be used in any normal paper casing. However it does work quite well with shell casing salutes, COBs, or steel pipes with endcaps. But beware of shrapnel when using any of those.

Second, tubes and plugs from cannonfuse.com are really shitty. The tubes are too big and you can't get the damn plugs in! The tubes might be ok if you made your own plugs, or used glue plugs. Try these sites for info on how to do this.

ICP's Method (http://www.geocities.com/tight_rappa2002/plugs.html)

Rat's Method (http://krimzonpyro.com/rat/casings&endplugs/endplug.html)

Even with good endplugs though, it's still recommended that you fill them with flash or something even faster burning. Black Powder can work in paper casings, but I've found it's much easier and cheaper to just make shell casing salutes. Pour some BP into an empty rifle shell casing, stick in a piece of visco fuse, and crimp the ends shut with a pair of plyers. The report is as loud if not louder than anything you could make with a paper casing, and it's realitively safe because brass doesn't appear to fragment like steel and aluminum do, so there isn't any flying shrapnel.

Cannonfuse's tubes are too big. A true M80 is 1.5 inches long and 9/16 inches in diameter. It is filled with 2,916 milligrams of flash powder. This is a little less than 3 grams of flash. Flash powder is by far more powerful than any type of black powder there is out there. Black Powder has a deflagration velocity of around 300 meters per second, whilst flash is about 2300 meters per second... You do the math! I have heard bad things about Cannonfuse's tubes, but a true M80 is made with flash powder, and NOT BP. That's simply the way it goes. Although it is possible to create decent BP firecrackers, they no where near to compare to what a little KClO4/Al 7/3 Flash powder can do...

IcEColdPlaYA
Regular
  
Re: Newbie, Need help with m-80
With thinner cases (like 1/8) it works best to fill it up 3/4 full, or all the way full, but you need room for end plugs. For very thick cases (like 1/4 inch) it is ok to pack it, as long as the end plugs are thick and tightly gripping the inside of the tube. I just fill it 3/4 since it takes less time and powder, but if you have a thick enough case, packed black powder will work better than 3/4 full.

For rpliving, look for 5 minute epoxy at wal mart. It hardens after only 5 hours, but hardens even more after only an hour, so both plugs should dry after about 1 hour and 30 minutes, since after the five minutes is up on the first plug you can go ahead and finish up the salute. It is more expensive than wood glue and saw dust, but it drys hard as a rock. You should also try 1/2 inch thick hot glue plugs.
