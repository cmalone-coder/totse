Trip Report (Sort of): Ambien
There it is, just sitting there. Ten milligrams of Zolpidem also known as Ambien. It's just sitting there and although it is inanimate I can hear it, calling me as if I were miles away. A faint echo can only be noticed, "Take me, snort me, do what you will, but get me inside of you." Who's voice is it? Mine.

So, what do I do? I ponder this question as I cough involuntarily, making sure not to make any extra noise besides the loud thumps of the keys being depressed. It's Monday morning, 3:58AM to be exact. I sooth the voice that now softly whispers, rather than an echo. "Come on, hurry up." It'll be alright. Well, the morning comes quick, so must make haste. :-)

I grab a piece of paper, which is hot pink, and slowly fold the paper over. The numbers imprinted on the tablet start to resemble a face, saying goodbye. My jaw clenched as I pulverized the little fucker. The voice is now gone, but instilled into a former form of what it once was. Milky white powder now sits in front of me. Hello You.

There it is, sitting inside me. Relieved I'd bet, victory has fallen upon my sorry nasal tubing. The flurry of words that were normally supposed to slip into my fingers has now declined in it's speed of process. I feel that if all of my cares in this world could be put into a bucket, I'd kick it over and watch it spill, just for the thrill of it. The cold air from my feet radiates through my socks, bringing in the spirits of whatever was outside. Reminiscing past experiences that have swept through my existence remind me of the one thing that always bugs me, nothing is real, not even you. You may think you are, but thats because you are thinking. If you don't think, are you real?

Tired, lazy, and a bit of déjà vu, déjà vu.

The salty substrate washes down my throat every so often. Not bad if I had to say. Man, do I need a cigarette.

They are perceived as being real. How do you know that the rock you speak of will be the same rock once you look away? The mind creates knowledge based on three non-logical principles.

1.Principle of continued existence of things. The mind must make the illogical jump of determining object permanence. How do you know that because a cat walked behind a sofa, that it isn't gone? Why should you believe it's the same cat once it comes back into sight? Logic says you shouldn't, but you know that if you go behind the sofa, and come back you're still the same, so you apply this principle to your "reality"

2. Principle of induction. We make general laws which we say will hold true in the future, but this is not known for sure because the future has yet to happen. Just because we've seen things fall down towards the ground when you drop them doesn't mean the future will follow the same pattern, yet the mind still makes that jump.

3. Principle of causality. The mind constantly links events and creates a cause and effect relationship. For example, two billiard balls hit each other, and fly off in their respective directions. All the mind should see the balls moving in some direction, and then change and move in some other direction. There is no reality based explanation for explaining the human mind creating causal relationship. It just does.

These ideas are inherited after generations, and evolve and change like anything else in nature. For reasons unknown, words have the same value to every human being. As you said, language does seem to act as a "universal visionary filter".
