Re: My Boyfriend's mother thinks i'm going to kill her cat
You know, (it's funny looking back on it, but it was really kind of scary at the time) about two years ago I was living with my "girlfriend" at the time in a down town apartment, and we often went to dark clubs and wore a lot of black and a lot of leather. So, we meet this guy who lives in the apartment: Michael.

We think he's pretty cool. We hung out on the roof a lot, smoking, drinking. We often invited up people we saw passing on the street to smoke and drink with us. Sometimes we found ourselves with full blown parties up there, and he often attended. He brought his cat up every night anyways to let it walk around. So, we talked quite a bit and we figured we were friends, right?

Well, this one day he comes up onto the roof and see's my girl and I, and we gave him a beer and he smoked us out. So, we were kind of high and he tells us

"You know, I have to go now, but I don't want to take my cat in. Can you take it in with you, girls, when you go back inside?" Of course, we're friends, so we say

"Sure!" So, Michael leaves and we play with the cat a while and talk about how nice it is that we've made such a cool friend in the apartment and then, come night fall we go back inside.

An hour or so later she and I are getting ready to leave, dressed in black leather from head to toe when he comes banging on the door in a fury. He can't find his cat and the first thing he does is accuse us of hurting his cat.

"I know all about you freaks." He remarks and then threatens to call the police on us for underage drinking and prostitution. So, of course, (mostly in defense of my girl) say

"We are NOT prostitutes! And we would NEVER hurt your cat!" The idea was utterly ridiculous.

The guy says "If I don't find my cat... If you hurt one HAIR on my cats HEAD I'll KILL you!" And he meant it. "You better FIND my CAT!" Damn. And, certainly, my girl and I feel awful. First off, we thought Michael and us were friends. We had no idea that he was actually harbouring some ideas that we were actually cat killing freak prostitutes. And on top of having this hurting our feelings, he threatened to call the cops on us, and kill us. But, more than anything we were upset that we couldn't find that cat.

But, we were sure we'd brought it in. Still, niether one of us could remember bringing it in. We had been high at the time, but it just wasn't the kind of thing we would've forgotten. So, finally, we decide 'fuck it' and we go in. I write down our numbers and go downstairs and give it to the guys girlfriend as I can hear him in the background screaming about us nuking his beloved feline.

After that we attempted to go out and have a good time, but were thwarted by our own pointless guilt. We'd done nothing wrong, yet we felt as though we did. So, finally, we just went back home and found Michael standing in the hallway in front of our apartment with his arms crossed over his chest.

We seriously thought it was the end of us. I asked

"Are you okay, Michael?" Then he suddenly smiled and embraced us both.

We flinched.

"I'm sorry, you guys." He was all happy now. "I didn't mean to flip out on you. I didn't mean any of those things I said."

"So," I start. "you find your cat?"

"She'd gotten stuck in the janitors closet." He said and then laughed.

"I'm sorry. I really am. I know you wouldn't hurt my cat, girls."

"Certainly, we'd never hurt your cat." I say.

"You're our friend, Michael." My girl says. "We'd never hurt your cat."

"Well, I was out of line." He opologizes.

"It's okay." I say, trying not to shake with fear. What a psycho! Who knew what he'd do next? "Don't think about it. I'm sure loosing your cat could be very traumatic."

"Well, I was out of line. I didn't mean those things. Forgive me?"

"Yea... sure." I say and then we go inside without even saying good-bye. My girl says to me

"He meant those things. You could tell."

We avoided him forever, after that. But, well, there's my cat killing story. Though, I'm not sure why he thought we were going to kill his cat. I don't know if he thought we were "witches" or what. Also, I don't know of any Wiccan practices that involve killing cats... do you? If I ever find his cat again, I might take advantage of it.

Cheers,
