Re: which of the hk g36 series should i buy?
No dice. HK doesn't offer the G-36 for import to US civilians. If you are a LEO you might be able to get your superior to sign off on it but you'll have to leave it behind when ya quit.

The closest you'll get to a G-36 is by buying an SL-8 (quickly: they're stopping production due to low sales). Dye it black. Get the magwell conversion so it'll take AR-15 mags. Then get the aftermarket pistol grip and stock. Do it IN THAT ORDER. If you don't, the ATF will shit on you for violating import bans. Even though its plain to see that the SL-8 has a thumbhole stock, the ATF classifies it as a pistol grip. Anyway, if you get the magwell conversion, you'll have the right US made parts count so its no longer classed as an import. You'll still be tied down to semi-auto only, unless you have a Class II manufacture license or put through the papers for a Class III weapon.
Confusing, eh?

More info can be found at www.hkpro.com in the discussion forums. Check out the Rookie Corner and Long Gun Talk.

Re: which of the hk g36 series should i buy?
FOPA of May, 1986 froze the supply of transferable MGs and conversion devices.

GCA of 1968 stopped importation of transferable MGs. However, transferable MGs that were exported can be reimported and retain their transferable status.

Individuals can no longer make and register a MG unless they are doing so to sell to an approved govt agency.

A licensed manufacturer and SOT can make all the MGs he want, but they are dealer samples that cannot be transfered to individuals.

Unless you want to get licensed, your only way to get an MG legally is to buy a transferable registered MG or registered conversion device.
