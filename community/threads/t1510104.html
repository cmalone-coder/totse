Frankly, I think you look just as good at all lengths. And while your choice of cuts are generic they're all "flattering". However, speaking as a cosmetologist longer cuts on men are popular right now. So, in a purely cosmetological point of view (without knowing you) I'd go with the longer style. May be even let it go a little longer and then have it layered so that the layers fall around your chin.

Since your face is slightly narrow and your eyes slightly close set a layer that lands at your chin would create the illusion of extra width around the eyes and strengthen a narrow chin. You could keep it professional looking by having it thinned so it's close to the head. But since your hair looks on the fine side as it is, I really think a basic layered cut would be fine.
