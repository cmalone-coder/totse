Most fun things you've done on acid/shrooms
1.) Found an office chair at the top of a steep hill and started rolling down the hill on the chair playing chicken with the cars. I can't even describe how much fun that was.

2.) Went to the theme park and went on the rollercoaster and ghosthouse and the big wheel and shit. Getting stuck in the glass maze really fucked up the experience though. Its hard enough getting outta those places sober.

3.) Found a big icy road which was also a hill so started sliding all the way from the top of the hill to the bottom on my feet on the ice.

4.) Went swimming in a canal.

5.) Went on a magical bike ride with a couple of my friends who were outta their heads on XTC. Sober people bring me down when I'm on shrooms but people on XTC are funny as fuck when your on shrooms.

6.) Starting fights and causing trouble outside nightclubs. I'd never find that fun sober but when I'm in that anarchy state of mind on shrooms can't help causing trouble. I've never actually fought on shrooms though. People are usually scared of us cuz of how crazy and weird we act on them.

7.) Wandered through a forest. I swear to god it feels like walking through the Amazon jungle or something.

Maybe it doesn't sound fun, but this was literally one of the best trips of mine to date.

It was a couple summers ago and we were sitting in one of those bug tents (the huge 10'X10' kind). But there was no wind so we were fine even though the sides of the tent were just mesh. It even stayed surprisingly warm, so we just sat in that tent and watched the rain for almost an hour straight without even talking to each other.

Other than that, the only really awesome trip I've had was exploring old abandoned houses. We were just biking along the road and spotted the house through a few fields. We decided to go check it out. After we ripped apart one of the walls, we decided to smoke a joint in the basement. Because the house was so old, the boards of the walls had separated and the sun shone through and made amazing patterns in the smoke.
