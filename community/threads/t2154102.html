Tales from the Crypt: Demon Knight.
Rewatching this one right now on encore. I saw this in theaters when it was released, and watching it now, I realize how supremely awesome this movie is.

1-You have a staring cast made almost exclusively of character actors, and damn good ones at that. Billy Zane and William Sadler are two of the most underused and underrated actors in the business. Throw in Dick Miller, Jada Pinket (Smith), and Thomas Hayden Church, you got what the refer to in the business as a winner.

2-Special effects. Keep in mind, this was 1995, 13+ years old, and the F/X still stand the test of time. The demons don't look out of place like CGI does, and they genuinely look creepy (Except for the very last one, but hey, they can't all be winners).

3-Story. Not the best, or the most original, but damnit, what it does, it does well. Gotta love that claustrophobic, stuck in here while the monsters from hell are out there type of plot.

4-It's Tales from the Crypt, which speaks for itself really.

I wholeheartedly recommend this to anybody that likes horror movies of any flavor.
