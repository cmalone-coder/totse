Re: How to Fight
I find that keeping an enemy at arm's length is the best way to deal with someone. If you are on the defensive (the other person started the fight and is attacking you) then you should attempt to stay just inside kicking range. You will notice that before a fight when someone is trying to intimidate you they will get very close and "in your face". Don't let them. This puts you at a disadvantage if you are on the defensive. If you keep the proper distance you can hit them at your leisure, dodge, or block as needed with plenty of time to react.

Wait for them to rush or lunge at you. Chest kick, head kick, sweep. Down they go, and seldom do they get back up.

Of course, you could just pull a katana on them, which is a tactic that has never failed me.
