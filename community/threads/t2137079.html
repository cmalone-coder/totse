Re: Hunter S Thompson. Doctor of Journalism. Kewl.
If you think Thompson's books are just about him doing drugs breaking the law and going wild than you don't know what you're talking about.

He provides a look into the special era where cultures were clashing and a new American ideal was being born. Out of the tightly wound conservatism of the 50's came a radical shift - sex drugs and rock and roll...and Thompson was there to witness it all. His writing touches on everything from the political realities of America during the 60's and 70's to the disappearance or death of the 'American Dream' ideal. He uses the drugs and wild carousing as a plot device to help give the reader the theme of the times - Thompson was running wild at the time as were the rest of America's children. It was people like Thompson who allow places like totse to exist in today's world and not be shut down by the 'fascist right wing nazi pigs' who hate your freedoms.

Why don't you read some of his other works instead of watching Fear and Loathing w/ Johnny Depp and then pontificating down to the rest of us folks who actually know how to read.
