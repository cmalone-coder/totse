I have heard a lot of folks complain that Salvia is some semi-pleacebo, kinda-stony herb that can get you sort of high if you smoke enough....well those folks are complete idiots.

I have done just about every drug there is for the last 15 years...and let me tell you Salvia is by far the most potent drug I've ever done. It makes pure DMT look like a cup of coffee. BUT YOU HAVE TO SMOKE IT CORRECTLY. Savinorin A (the active part) doesn't vaporize unless you ignite it a very high temperatures - so you need to pack a huge bowl in a bong (so it will cherry) and put the lighter right up to the salvia (a torch style lighter works best). Once the bowl is a giant red-flamin' cherry... you have to take a very large hit and then hold it for at least 20 seconds. Then you must do it again 2 more times.

YOU BETTER HAVE A SITTER and you better have a table next to you so that you can put the bong down in a hurry (you will have to concentrate to even remember that you need to put the bong down).

A full-on salvia trip only last about 8 minutes..but you will be in a distant land with no sense of where you are, who you are and why you are there. Savia done correctly is a train-wreck into another dimension..it makes 8 grams of shrooms look like child's play.. so I don't want to hear people complain that it's a joke because either you got bunk shit (you need at least 5x SALVINORIN enhanced leaf) or you didn't smoke it right.

Also to note: the first time you smoke salvia you need to smoke just a little bit a few hours prior to you first real trip. For some reason (probably too strong and your body rejects it or possible MAO inhibitor qualities) salvia rarely works the first time you smoke it (you usually just get really stoned). Once the salvia is in you system for a while...the real trip can begin.
