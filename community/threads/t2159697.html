Stoners...why they're so dumb [my theory]
Ok, I know the lot of you don't really like "stoners." You know...that stereotype of the lazy, dumb pot-smoking burn out. I know I certaintly don't take too fondly of being called one, not that it has happened to many times. To the point...

Yesterday the editor in chief of High Times and a retired DEA officer debated about legalizing at my university, and the public's opinion was a little startling. The majority of people there had the impression that smoking cannabis makes you slow and dumb. "The bums lost, Lebowski." Perhaps the cause and effect are mixed up here. My theory is this -- smoking marijuana is not the cause and becoming lazy is not the effect, being lazy is the cause and smoking marijuana is the effect.

I'll explain: Stoners are predisposed to be lazy. Before any weed is ever toked, they already have the personality and look. It just so happens that poking smot is perfect for this lifestyle. It makes sitting on the couch and watching hours of TV, what would be boring to most people, much more enjoyable. Of course I'm generalizing here and not everyone who smokes weed is lazy and not every lazy bum is a pot smoker. But that's just it -- a generalization, I can't catalog every single individual's personality.

I smoke a good amount of bud. At least one bowl is put into the air each day but I generally try to limit it to only a night time activity. Despite this, I'll try to make this not so biased and include some facts from the other side of the argument.

It is scientific fact that cannabis slows down response time and impairs memory. Although plenty of studies have been done, "not much is known why" is all I ever here. The only explanation I've seen was in a video I saw once in addiction class. It went on to say that cannabinoids are absorbed by the fatty tissues throughout the body and this includes the brain. Over months and years of use, the lipids in the brain become saturated with cannabiniods which hinder and slow down the neurological processes.

This is a descent idea, but a google search provided no resources, in fact all I could find where positive things about cannabinoids in the brain -- how the cannabinoid receptor system targets cancer, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, Alzheimer's or Parkinson's diseases, inflammation, pain, schizophrenia, depression, and anxiety.

Down to the meat and potatoes of this thread, I'm not trying to defend anyone. I'm just trying to defend the drug so that the next time you see a walking replica of a guy from Half Baked you don't get the wrong idea.
