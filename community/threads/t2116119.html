a few Alaskan terms
On the off chance anyone is interested, in no special order:

visqueen : a brand name for clear plastic sheeting, which became generic, since we use a lot of the stuff.

bunny boots : super heavy-duty cold-weather boots made of white rubber for the armed forces. I don't like'em--they are too heavy and clumsy for me, plus they donpt breath, so your feet get all wrinkled and mushy .

Snow-jogs : winter boots for poor people-- plastic, made in China jobs that go for $20 at Wal-mart. AKA moon boots, but no one here calls 'em that.

snow-goes : what the Natives call snowmobiles, only no one here calls'em that--most folks call tham snow machines.

tundra bunnies : what white asshole racists call female Natives.

gussicks : what natives call white asshole racists, also translated as "peckerwood cocksucker." Said to come from the word "cossack," from the Russian settlers.

Pilot bread : cracker-like things, only bigger. They keep forever. Produced Outside, mostly consumed here.

Outside : Anywhere that isn't Alaska.

The Dividend (or PFD) : Free money from the state for residents, from a $30-billion pot of oil money invested in stocks, bonds, and real estate, formally known as the Permanent Fund. This year, each of us is expected to get around $2000. Rich folks blow it on trips to Hawaii, some kids save it for college--folks like us buy groceries and pay bills with it.
