The Bible records it, and since the Bible keeps proving its historical accuracy, there is no need to question it. But if you want a more thourough explanation, ask why the Centurian stabbed Him at all. There are medical reasons. To test if someone crucified truly was dead instead of simply passing out from the pain, it was routine to stab the offender in order to check for the separation of water and blood. A steady stream of water and blood flowed from the wound, as indicated in Johns' gospel

"Then the soldiers came, and broke the legs of the first, and of the other which was crucified with him. But when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was dead already, they did not brake his legs. Instead one of the soldiers pierced Jesus' side with a spear, bringing a sudden flow of blood and water." -John 19:34

This is something that can't be avoided by tricking someone. When you die, blood plasma and the water in your body separate.

This is why the Romans stabbed Jesus. It was to see if He was actually dead.

And to add: There is nothing 'magical' about the spear that stabbed Jesus, nor is there magical properties to His graveclothes, i.e. the Shroud of Turin, or the Holy Grail. This is nothing more than people who think they need trinkets to make their faith real.
