OK, to be honest, I know the feeling. You seem like the kind of person I was at high school. My main advice to you would be to find people you can talk to for extended periods of time about things which are not always serious. Find people who can give and receive playful jibes.

I know this seems like irresponsible advice, but try taking up smoking. Contrary to common belief, smoking is a very social thing. When you go out at breaks, you have a cigarette with the other smokers and you will pick up friends from there easily. If the smokers at your school aren't the type of people you would get on with, just leave it.

As for carrying on friendships out of school, there's not much I can say to help. If you have a reputation as someone who is not fun to be with or as a bit of a geek, then it's already too late. If not, then try to throw a few parties, invite people from different cliques. Hang around with a lot of them and so forth. Invite them places and don't wait for them to invite you all the time. If they don't answer, keep pushing them until they do. You don't really have much to lose.

If all of those ideas fail, I took up the guitar when I was had very few friends. People like guitarists. Get good and you will draw friends to you like a magnet (if friends were made of metal).

As for the situation with girls, not much can be said if you feel uncomfortable around them. Basically, all you need to do is make friends with some girls. If you are shy, that may be easier said than done, but it's worth a try. If there is a night club near you which has an under 18s night, go there and sit on your own, some girl will eventually come and talk to you. I can guarantee it.
