Weed just isnt the same anymore...
I've noticed, when I first smoked pot, it was AMAZING, I felt the most intense calming sweet gravity push down on me, caressing me with warmth.

I noticed about 6 months ago that i'd start getting a bit of a headache and the most intense feeling of pressure in my head that was unbearable.

That feeling went, and i had a good run of 6 months of the same awesome high, and weed just aint the same anymore. Like, i just smoked a bit of green, enough to generally give me quite the buzz... but nearly nothing. I mean, i feel a little mellow, and generally physically relaxed, but i'm not goofing like i used to. Not literally ROFLing like I used to.

It's not that it's shit weed - this guy is the most loved dealer in about a 50km radius, and my friends and I go by train for half an hour just to buy from him.

Maybe it's because I'm addicted to nicotine, and what I was feeling was a combination of nicotine's effects with THC, because I always roll with and fill my bowls with mix...
