My ghetto version, was simply a wet piece of bread inside a mason jar. I put some butcher's paper over the top with a rubber band, nuked it for 10 - 15 minutes. Instead of punching holes in the paper, I just lifted up the edge of the paper, and shot the spores in through there. Sure, it doesnt have quite the yield of the whole grain method, but it surpassed the PF-Tek BRF my a slight margin (Explainable by environmental methods).

And nshanin, larger jars arent nesecarily better. You have to wait longer for the entire jar to be colonized, and in that time, more food and water is used. They on.y make things more complicated and the cakes also break easier (leading to certain contamination). I suggest just going to walmart and getting STRAIGHT WALLED pint jars.

Edit: For your sake, Id use popcorn as a substrate, it's foolproof, very easy to get the proper amount of water, and is one of the fastest colonizing substrates ive seen.
