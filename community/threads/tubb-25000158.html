Re: which element reacts violently to water?
Perhaps a lesson in the chemistry of how it works would help.

2 Na + H2O = Na2O + H2 + heat

The Na2O disolves into the body of water.
The H2 disperses into the air. The heat of the reaction is enough to ignite it. That is where the explosion comes from. The same formula holds true for all 1A elements. The metal itself does not explode, the explosion is a secondary effect caused by too much heat.

Just a note about this reaction: All of the alkali earth metals and and alkali metals (first two groups) react with water to form the hydroxide and one half mole of dihydrogen(or one mole in the case of Calcium etc)

Ca + 2H2O --> Ca(OH)2 + H2
