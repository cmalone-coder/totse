This is the Free Software scene.

There are only two really used free OS's. They're Gnu and BSD (you'll commonly see Gnu referred to as Linux; this is both wrong from a technical and every other logical standpoint, so say Gnu/Linux or Gnu if you want to not look like an idiot). The big choice is what distro to use.

Ubuntu is probably the best for a beginner. The latest is 8.04, I suggest you use that. The other big options that are thrown around on this board are Fedora, OpenSuSe (which might be dangerous due to Novell's involvement), and Slackware (for the noob that wants to learn by the 'crash and burn underwater somehow or swim' method).

No matter what you choose, we (and many other people in the free software community) will help you run it. You're making a good choice.
