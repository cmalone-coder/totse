Re: Growing the magic plant
If you're talking about cannabis then you have plenty of time. The plants won't be as large as they could be if you started earlier but with only 4 plants it doesn't matter anyway. They could just be a strain that doesn't require long vegetative stage, so again it doesn't matter. Just grow them...... And keep a male to pollinate the females so you'll save some of your "good"(lol) seeds. If you don't keep a male you can clone during vegetative or induce hermaphrodism to get seeds. People may tell you that hermaphrodism is an unwanted trait and that you should kill all hermaphrodites but if they tell you that they don't actually have any experience with growing/breeding. Florida is an excellent place to be for growing healthy potent plants since you're near the equator..(More UVB).... Most of the classic landrace sativa strains are minimally hermaphroditic. Which is a good thing for the plant but you can breed that out of them pretty easily if you want. You may get about 3 seeds per plant if you have a minimally hermaphroditic strain. Breeding isn't as complicated as people would have you believe either. You will not get much out of that, you'll get about an ounce at most I'm guessing. Unless you really know your stuff, and you don't. But you can learn pretty quick.
