Re: LSD Questions
LSD has no potential for physical brain damage (as opposed to something like alcohol)...

However, if you have a bad trip (which is easy to avoid) or you have some sort of family history of schizophrenia (don't even worry about that, the chances of that happening are extremely rare) you could potentially experience some form of psychosis...

But seriously, do it. You might have some sort of intense emotional experience and you may think your going crazy every now and then while on it, its nothing to fear. Its just coping with a different perception. As long as you can remember that whatever is going on is under your mental control you will be fine. If something intense or scary happens, relax and think happy thoughts (seriously), its all under your control, more or less.


Think positive, be in a relaxed interesting and SAFE place and you will be 100% fine and it will most likely be one the greatest experiences you will ever have.

Oh and colors are a must. I never understood tye dye until I took acid, it is seriously the most amazing thing to look at haha

enjoy and post back how much fun you had afterwords :)

With a test kit, and a black light, and some practice and experience it can be done fairly easily.

Shine the black light (quickly) on there and look for spots that do not glow (no LSD where it doesn't glow) and then cut a small sample of the hit and test with reagent. The deeper and more color you see after waiting about 15 minutes will indicate the level of potency in the hit.


By doing this many times on many kinds, one can get a very good feel for what the results mean.


Also, the thing about flashes in the dark mentioned above, it must be totally anhydrous for it to work, which it most certainly will not be unless you happened to pull it straight from an LSD chemists vac filter.
