hydroponichronic
Regular
  
Re: Butane Hash Oil.
make sure you get butane that does not say it has ODERANTS anywhere on the bottle because it will ruin all your weed and make your oil taste like ass. Also make sure you don't get small butane lighter refill canisters because they will rip you off. I use one of the larger cans which are about 9 inches long and cost me 2.99. I use one of these cans on an ounce of mid grade. by the way mid grade is much more economicalthan dro for making oil. I use a pop bottle with as many small holes as i can fit from a small straight pin/safety pin in the cap. I put a hole just about the size of the butane nozzle on the bottom of the pop bottle, to make an air tight seal between your pop bottle and butane canister put a a piece or electrical tape over your hole with a hole poked in it. This will prevent butane from spraying out all over the outside of your bottle and freezing your hands off. I just spray it in on a plate until the liquid butane coming out the bottom runs clear for a while. After all the liquid butane dries off and theres just hash oil with bubbles in it heat it with a blow dryer until all the bubbles are gone. (It will dry much faster if you let the plate float in in some lukewarm water and its not cold outside). blow drying is the longest part of the process and can feel like its taking forever but make sure the hash oil stops bubbling because that means the butane is gone. afterwards scrape the oil off the plate with a razor blade and put it on wax paper. I use a paperclip to pick up globs of hash off my wax paper and drop them in test tubes I bought at the American Science And Surplus for 10 cents a piece. You can use a light bulb vaporizer if you don't have test tubes. Make sure when the air is dry you discharge yourself so a static shock doesn't burn/blow you up, it will have a warning for this on you butane canister. And duh don't smoke when your making the stuff.
