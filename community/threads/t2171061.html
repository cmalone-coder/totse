The key issue with ADD isn't intelligence or discipline and motivation. If you think that people who take medication for ADD aren't as mentally strong, resilient, or intelligent as "normal" people, you are very much mistaken. It has to do with arousal and concentration and it has been shown by empirical and quantitative data from fMRI, EEG, PET scans, genetic biomarkers, that there is a biological basis for this. Simply put, people with ADD are less aroused by what is going on and therefore jump around with their thoughts because they can't focus on something that doesn't hold their interest.

I am smart as fuck (I say that with some degree of ego) and managed my way through highschool and got accepted to an Ivy League university without the aid of stimulants but I knew since I was very young that my mind seemed to work faster than most people and I understood quickly material that took a lot of effort for others and it became mundane to me, preventing me from concentrating.

This might not be the case with all people diagnosed with AD(H)D, but dopamine agonists allow me to tune into things that I would ordinarily find boring or repetitive and is thus something that helps me. I really don't care about the label of ADD, just that one must do whatever works because people have different neurochemistry and some are not conducive to the demands of society and culture.

I wish I didn't need anything to help me focus but I've tried meditation and that seems to help a little bit, but adderall works for me (not perfectly, always some unwanted effects).
