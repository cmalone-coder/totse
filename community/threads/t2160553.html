Re: RIP Toonami 1997 - 2008
Memoir:

Sometime in the 4th grade I used to watch Gundam Wing on Toonami. When I was younger, maybe the third grade or earlier, I used to watch Dragon Ball Z without really knowing it was anime. In the end of the 4th grade I was building Gundam Models. Tenchi Muyo followed after this. Outlaw Star followed. Then the Big O. I began to go to my friends house and we both o would watch his copies of DBZ. We would watch Ranma 1/2. His older sister was an Otaku. She had tons of Japanese Manga, untranslated and translated. In the 6th grade I began reading Manga. Trading Yugioh cards.

We began collecting Newtype Magazines. I had thier demo DVDs of King of Bandit Jing, Di Di Charat, and Azumanga Daioh. We met the proverbial geek, who was well ahead collecting Shonen Jump and watching CN or Animidnight on Action. Sometime between 6th grade and 7th grade Techtv began showing Betterman, and Boogybop Phantom. At some point Ruroni Kenshin snuck in Cartoon Network. Some other show ended up on Techtv after awhile. Then Adult Swim got my attention. Cowboy Bebop. Trigun. Dare I say, Inuyasha.

I was getting a normal fix of Anime morning, day, and Night. then in the 8th grade I got a girlfriend who was into Japanimation. No, not some ugly thick glasses chic. A hot out-of-my-leauge filipino girl. This sped me into my addiction. Mostly becuase she had scores of anime. Chobits, Hellsing, Love-Hina. A month before we met, and this is important to know -FLCL came on [AD]. That was my all time obsession. Following these up until now are the last few recent anime on Cn or talked about in Newtype.

I quit wathcing toonami when it started sinking, but even so, that doesn't make the sting any less worse.

-That was my early years, and they ended one minute at a time. I will never get those experiences duplicated. Toonami will never be duplicated. a chunk of me is dead.
