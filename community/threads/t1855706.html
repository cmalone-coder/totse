Re: Obtaining chemicals for meth in the UK
Right... UK info...

Pseudoephedrine HCl 60mg tablets can be bought from 'Boots'. Either Sudafed Non-drowsy (Blue packet) or Boots now do their own generic 'pseudoephedrine decongestant'

Iodine is available from 'Countrywide Stores' in the vetenary section in either 2% or 10% concentration and in 250ml or 1 litre.

IPA, Acetone and MEK are available normally in 5 or 25 litre sizes from most printing suppliers.

Glacial acetic acid is available by mail order from photographics suppliers

I think 'Evo-stick brush cleaner' is almost pure xylene, but don't quote me.

HCl 37.5% is available from smaller hardware stores as is NaOH

H2S04 used be be available as proper fuming vitriol from B&Q, but I think the've stopped that now... Wonder why?
