more sex=less aids? wtf?
I was at the library today, and I found a book with a title similar to the title of my thread.

basically, the author was arguing that we can greatly reduce the amount of people who have aids if more people who are celibate or monogamous go out and fuck other people.

here's an example of his reasoning (and the example he used in the book):
a guy, mike, and a girl, joan, work together. they've been talking, and it's pretty much decided, yet unspoken, that they're going to hook up after the next office party this weekend.

on the morning of the party, mike sees an ad promoting celibacy, so he decides not to go to the party because of the ad or something like that. as a result, joan hooks up with the (male) office slut. he gives her aids.

the author's reasoning was that if mike, who wasn't very sexually experienced and as a result had no std's, had hooked up with joan instead of frank, the office slut, hooking up with her, she wouldn't have gotten aids.

another example he used was that for every person who doesn't have aids that goes to a bar and picks someone up, that's one less person that will get aids as a result of being picked up at a bar (assuming, of course, that the person being picked up doesn't have it either). because for every person without aids that picks someone up, that's one less person for someone with aids to pick up.

basically, think of it like this: everybody who has casual sex makes up a stream. the people who have aids pollute the stream, and the people who don't have aids help clean up the stream. so the more people who don't have aids that are in the stream, the cleaner the stream is.

one last argument. i know it doesn't make sense, but it was in the book, so i'm including it: say everybody in a certain town is in a monogamous relationship. the women are happy in the relationship, so they don't cheat. the men, on the other hand, want an average of 2 partners per year. the men are forced to go to prostitutes (no word on whether or not the prostitutes are in relationships), and since every man in the town goes to the same couple of prostitutes, one of them is eventually going to give them aids, and they will in turn spread the aids to the rest of the men, who will in turn infect their innocent wives.

ok, one more thing that he said. he said that frank would be less likely to use condoms because he already has aids and doesn't have to worry about catching it, so he's more likely to spread it to other people. mike, on the other hand, will use a condom to try to prevent himself from getting aids.


i think his arguments are total bullshit, but that's just me. what do you all think?
