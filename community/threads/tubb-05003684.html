Actually many, MANY people have great success with just boiling. Infact, I bowled my jars, and didn't have a signal one get contaminated. As well, I went straight to casings and found it to be nothing short of simple. All it takes are a few disposable cooking pans, and some vermiculite. Sandwhich some crubled cakes in between two .5 inch layrs of moist vermiculite, seal the top, wait a weak, the remove the seal and put it in your terrarium.

On my first try, I yielded roughly 40 grams dried, so I can say Im happy, though I fucked up 7g's when drying, but now I've learned.

Mushroom cultivation is like 60% patients, 30% sterility, and 10% knowledge. Its not a hard concept to grasp at all, and theres really not to much to basic cultivation. As long as you follow your plan as set, and give it time, then it should all go well.
