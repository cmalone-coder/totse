Following christian teachings you would be strengthened spiritually. And your commitment to the community would be devout. A tight-knit community with a strong spirituality! That is a society! Christianity teaches a way to change the world. Why did the romans try to crush christianity? Because it was a threat to their power. A force that grows inside its host nation until it is overpowering. In Rome the official religion changed to christianity, it was because most of Rome had become christian, they had the numbers to control an empire, won without outright battle. People didn't follow the teachings, and many were only in Christianity because it was the cool thing to do. Without everyone doing their part a christian utopia wouldn't be possible. And in the end we didn't end up with one. Now people have forgotten what religion is about, it is about teaching people how to work together, it is a government that all the people share in, it is knowing for yourself how to act without laws or rules.
