All in all, the circumstances and details of everything that happened between Kristen and I hurt me almost as bad as when Katti and I broke up. Sometimes, I still read the first letter Kristen wrote me. It's sickening, but not because it's full of lovey-dovey prose. I actually find that to be rather trite. No, this letter contains just the right amount of meaning in it to make it believable. That's what makes it worse. If, as the letter claims, I'm so easily lovable and thoughts of me so easily induced, then why would the person who feels this way do something to make me feel bad and hurt me worse than I ever imagined? It was all a deceitful lie. That's why. Kristen was a self-edifying bitch who was/is guilty of the crimes she accused me of herself. Perhaps it was projection that she was doing and I never saw it until now. Because she was the one with the huge ego but no self-esteem.

Sigh. Still, though...

She was the only girl that could carry a conversation with me without solely smiling and nodding. She was the only one that would (and did) dance with me to the entire rat-pack without thinking it was weird or uncomfortable. And she was the only one to stay with me for as long as she did when I was in the hospital, even if it was false sentiments. But if this was all a lie, then why did she do it? Was it because she hadn't found herself? Was it because she was bipolar? Probably a combination of those two reasons, plus the reasons mentioned previously. She's too smart for her own good.

I just would like it to be known that I could say so much more about our situation, but there's no more blood coming out of this stone that I'm squeezing. To whoever reads this, I should also make it known that my relationship with Katti was much more meaningful and thus, it was more depressing and painful when it ended, despite the fact that I wrote much more about Kristen. But my relationship with Kristen was the most intense so far.

So where am I going with all of these personal stories about my past liaisons with the aforementioned girls? The biggest point of this entire rambling is this: before I had any kind of personal attachment, I was stable. Things weren't good, but things weren't bad, either. Given the situation that I'm left with, I'd much rather go back to how things were before it all started. Don't get me wrong: I'm not telling anyone to not care about their opposites. That's no way to live. Ever. Falling in love and all that good stuff is excellent. When it's good, it's the stuff dreams are made of. But when it's bad, it feels like the whole world is one big place of pure evil everywhere you go.

I know I don't have all the answers, and I know I'm not a relationship counselor or anything of the sort. I'm just some guy that's been there and knows what it feels like. I can only base my decisions on that fact. I know that there are people out there that have had it worse than me, but it's very hard for me to empathize with anyone in this situation, and I assume that will skew my views at least slightly. Therefore, before I say anything, I'd like to preface it with this: no matter how bad you think things are, there's someone out there that at least feels for you. You and me... we're in the same boat. So, to you, I say be sure of what you want before you really latch on to something. And make sure that whoever it is you latch on to is everything you're looking for. And then, when that all falls into place, by God, you keep it going and you keep it good. Believe me; it'll save you a lot of trouble from dealing with psychos and a broken heart.

I can honestly say that I don't know if I'll heed my own warning in the future, but I do think I'm getting better at it. Depression is a great motivator.

But then again... so is sex.
