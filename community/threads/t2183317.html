Ok.

I took two hits on blotter paper. I took the first hit around 12, didn't really feel anything, figured I was thinking about it too much..so I took the next hit about an hour later. I didn't start tripping until around 3:00 - 3:30, but it started to hit me hard when I started staring into the sun. An aura of colors flowed out and around it and I couldn't take my eyes off of it. I eventually did though, for fear that I would damage my eyesight. I was in a car, my friend was driving me and my girlfriend to my house where I planned to experience the peak of my trip. All of the plants on the side of the road started changing colors, purples, greens, yellows, reds, all so beautiful...The houses in my neighborhood started to change to a purple hue and once I got to my house, my girlfriend put on The Doors movie.

I turned my xbox on, and I could see light radiate from my xbox controller for a second when I hit the little green button, if you're following me...

Once the movie started to play...I think I was peaking. It was the most amazing experience I've ever felt. I think that movie was meant to be watched on acid, because I understood what Jim Morrison was feeling and the underlying plot behind the movie. I somehow felt connected to him as if the movie was describing how I feel and it just seemed to make so much sense to me.

I can't go into too much detail about the movie, but I'm definitely going to have to watch it again and see what it's like when I'm not tripping :p.

Ummm, my girlfriend fell asleep, and I got bored once the movie finished so I came on totse, posted a thread, then drew a sick ass picture on an index card because I didn't have anything else to draw on. I didn't need to use the eraser on my pencil, everything just flowed perfectly from my minds eye and at one point I could actually see what my drawing was going to look like before I drew it...then I drew it. It's hard to explain but it was goddamn amazing.

I stole a cigarette from my girlfriends purse, stepped outside, lit it, and watched the cherry burn the paper up. I wanted to go exploring in my neighborhood. I got my shoes and a jacket on and went into my backyard and made my way to the fence leading to my neighborhood, but I stopped myself and went back inside. I knew that if my girlfriend woke up and I was gone, she might have got scared or something.

But I eventually came down and told my friends how awesome it was and that they should take acid too. :)

I don't think I damaged my eyes from staring at the sun, except for a purple circle that followed wherever I looked..but that went away in the morning. I didn't want to sleep, but I forced myself to because my girlfriend was sick and she got me a little sick, and I think sleeping helped the symptoms. But other than that, it was a fucking awesome day/night and I plan on doing it again sometime. I might be missing a few details, but that's the jist of it.
