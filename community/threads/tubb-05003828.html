Re: someone inform me of salvia
Salvia Divinorum/salvinorin A may turn out to be one of the most exciting drug discoveries in a long time. Not only is salvinorin A the most potent naturally-occuring hallucinogen on the planet but the discovery that it's a potent K-opioid agonist has interesting consequences.

It could in theory, be used to treat herion withdrawal. Unlike Methadone, it's not at all addictive (as far as I know).

Just last night I consumed (chewed and swallowed) about 600mg regular, non-enhanced leaf...only to wake up with the "warm and fuzzies" extremely similar to an opiate. It felt like a Percodan. I've tried ingesting salvia many different ways but only when eating it does the opiate-like feeling manifest. Smoking it is obviously very different and reminds me more of a 150mg Ketamine/700mcg LSD experience.

Hallucinogenic diterpene opiate!? I believe this plant has a future.

BUT..unless Merck, Upjohn or any pharma$utical company begins a process to patent it, it'll go schedule 1 soon. It just doesn't have the cover of a 'DXM' or 'pseudoephedrine' to keep it out of DEA wrath.

The only thing we can do is discourage it's use to potentially irresponsible users (and yes, not talk about it a lot, sorry!)
