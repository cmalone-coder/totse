Re: Suboxone WD / Klonopin therapy
Use the bupe tapering methods already posted, and as for the Klonopin, regulate yourself to 1 mg PRN (as needed) for anxiety, with a limit of 3 pills a day. As in, if you have someone to help you out and who would do it for you, or if you have some self-restraint, line up 3 pills each day and put the rest away somewhere.

I'm sure 50 pills of Klonopin is enough for some slight withdrawals if they're used continuously in a short amount of time, but as long as you can keep in mind that feeling anxious about having to wash a sink full of dishes isn't a good reason to eat a Klonopin then you should be ok.

If, on the other hand, you find yourself reaching for a pill every time daily life isn't going your way, then you're better off tapering the bupe and forgetting about the Klonopin.

Re: Suboxone WD / Klonopin therapy
My main goal with the Klonopin is just to dull down the shakes/shitty sleep pattern that WD's give me - so as of right now I am good to go.

And they're great for that purpose- there are a fairly decent number of psychiatrists who believe that moderate and controlled benzodiazepine therapy is greatly beneficial to certain patients recovering from opiate addiction.

Also, when I warned a bit about the dangers of using too much etcetera, I wasn't really alluding to a belief that you would abuse them-- benzodiazepine tolerance can (and often does) result from nothing more than a normal therapeutic regimen, such as the 3 mg a day I recommended you.

Basically, on some days, you probably will end up needing all three-- on others, you may only need one. Benzodiazepines definitely affect people differently as far as dosages are concerned, so if you're one of the fortunate people who can achieve a therapeutic benefit from 0.5 mg, feel free to break a pill in half here and there if you think it's enough to tide you over.

Ruling out the factor of abuse potential which you've said isn't an issue with you, your biggest risk with the Klonopin would be using the maximum 3 mg a day every single day. Giving your body a break here and there will greatly reduce the chance of you becoming dependent on the higher dosage.
