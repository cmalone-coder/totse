Finding a Job as a Felon
Is damn hard. I have so far applied at twenty different places. Not a single one of them has called me back. I've tried calling them back, letting them know that I really am eager to know if they're hiring. I've turned in Resumes and even attached letters of why I should be hired. I figured maybe the extra mile would help but alas not a difference. I don't even want a life that is based on monetary gains but I still need to eat.

Now I'm not a felon yet seeing as how they continually postpone the trial but I will be as of October 8. I figured that I should try to get a job before I am charged seeing as that would be ridiculously easier. I am however on recognizance waiting for said trail and every job application says you must list any charges that are pending or if your on recognizance. There goes that idea, the bastards.....


Now I know that the type of felony matters quite a bit so here's some info on mine. They are MDMA distribution charges. I don't know how many there will be. There was eight but they dropped those to 5 charges but I haven't had trial yet so I don't know if more will be dropped or if those five are sticking. They did an investigation on me while I was still in high school. It evidently lasted four months. I had no idea. The head investigator is the epitome of a prick because he told me they purposefully waited till after I graduated high school to arrest me. Therefore confiscating all my graduation money as "drug" money and fucking my employment opportunities for life. Thanks Uncle Sam.

It's getting ridiculous. I need a fucking job. I need to pay my rent. I need to buy food. The last of my funds is slowly draining away and it's damn hard to find a way to replenish it. No, before the acolyte says it, I am not fucking selling drugs again. Prison is not for me.

Here is a list of some of the places I have applied.
Safeway
Starbucks
Big 5 Sporting Goods
Blockbuster
RadioShack
Juice Shacks
Raleys
R. E. I
Target
Costco
Movie Theaters
A bunch of Mom and Pop places



It seems any medium to large corporations will never take the liability to hire anyone with a dotted past. I figured my best chance was the Mom and Pop places just because they are more likely to look at it on a case by case basis. Besides you get to work with the actual owner and get to know them and well basically there is a fucking chance of getting hired at least.

When I have to check the "Do not hire me box" I attempt to explain it a little bit. Not in a sappy, take pity on me way but in a clear manner. This is what I usually write.

"I was convicted of MDMA distribution , and have fulfilled my obligations to the Court/Society/etc. I know that crime is wrong, and I also know that I have to try harder, and be better than the average person. I am ready to do this."

I figured that's the best way to word it. Anyone have any better ideas?

No, I can't ask my P.O. because I don't have one yet. So he can't help direct me to any work programs. I however still have to check the felon box because it comes up in the database if they do a background check. That's automatically not getting the job if you lied.

I want to go to college but I have no money whatsoever. I have about half a tank of gas and seven dollars to my name. I can't get a college grant because all felons are exempt from those. It honestly seems like the only way to make money is to go back to crime but I'm not going down that path.

Trade school is looking like a viable option at the moment. No one in construction seems to give a fuck about your past. Anyone have any good ideas? Anyone been in this situation before?
