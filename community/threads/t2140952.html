A man dies, goes to heaven. It is built of his own preconceived view of heaven. It looks exactly like he pictured it. He finds this a little troubling at first, but continues on. He lives in heaven a while, talking to people, experiencing life just as he did on earth. He goes through the same suffering mentally as he did on earth, yet heaven is just how he pictured it. He realizes the cyclical nature of life in heaven easier, because it is supposed to be the final goal, yet he doesn't feel like he is in heaven. He ultimately learns that heaven wasn't to be found in the things he was looking for, and finds it inside.
