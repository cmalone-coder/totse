Re: Got a quetion can you answer it?
Aluminum. Why?

1. Newspapers are easy ro re-use without recycling, and there's a better chance someone will go recycle paper, than aluminum. So in that way, there'd probably be more recycling going on anyway.

2. Aluminum is very problematic for the environment, much more-so than paper. Paper will disintegrate, and reintgrate into the environment, depending upon it's composition, and the conditions, in even 50-100 years. Aluminum takes upwards of 4000 years, but also depending upon composition and environmental conditions, so it varies a touch.

3. If you're recycling Aluminum, that means *everyone* in the neighborhood is gathering their aluminum, washing it, placing it in neat containers, and then to their curbs (or better yet, a giant neighborhood bin) in an oh-so-very-kind way, just for you. They are essentially -giving- you, an unlimited source of aluminum to do whatever you want with. Experiments galore.

4. One word - Thermite

Have lots of fun,
