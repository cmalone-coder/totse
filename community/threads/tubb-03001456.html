I'm such a trend setter..

I did this first.. but at least I did it for my own needs and reasons, felt I was getting too beat down by the morons, and getting too sarcastic and negative on a constant basis.

Since my (still fairly recent) return, I've attempted to keep the moron bashing to a minimum, and did my part to create a few "oldstyle" totse posts.

The simple fact of the matter is that you cannot recreate a community elsewhere, it becomes something new, and usually something less than the original. At least in my experience. Totse has it's flaws yes, but it also has a unique strength, it's complete lack of moderation, beyond a very simplistic sense anyway. Noone here is ever banned for their opinions, no matter how insane they are. Laudable ideas always have a downside, and the truth is that we do not always want to hear the opinions of others. Free speech is all well and good, but what we really mean by that, is the freedom for those like us to be able to speak. If we are ever, as people, to become people of integrity and worth, our need is to listen to all the voices, not just those that sound like ours. I may disagree heartily with many here, I may find many people to be stupid, or make posts without merit. But for those who do not make posts I consider worthwhile, maybe it's just time for them to learn from us, and us from them. Some of the people here are young, some are not, some are intelligent, thoughtful people, some are not. But we're all people, and totse's power, is the power of people to say what they will, without fear of retribution, other than words in kind.

No matter where you go, or what you do, this will remain unique to the temple.
