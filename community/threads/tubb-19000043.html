Re: easier detonation
Number One : Never ever ever use a car remote control unless you live in some totally deserted area. These thigns operate typically from 27-30mhz which is a very busy frequency range. Anything could set it off.

Your best bet is to go and buy a cheap watch somewhere with an alarm. Make sure that the "hourly chime" is off or <boom!> you're missing an arm. Now unscrew the back. Take a look for what looks like the little speaker, it should be about 1/4-1/2" in diamter circle usually blue or some stand out color. Now there will be 2 wires running into it, usually also color coded with black and red. Assuming you have a good detonator, plug these wires into it. Now set either an alarm so you can leave it there for hours, or set the Timer function, and let it tick down. You might want to tape the watch on to the side of Mr. Boom to ensure nothing falls apart. Hopefully this helps.
