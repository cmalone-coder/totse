Better Living Through Chemistry's Rules of Posting

By using totse.com's Better Living Through Chemistry forum you are obligated to follow certain rules set by us, the moderators.

These rules are also subject to change, and it is your responsibility to know the current rules so check in often to see if anything has changed.

These rules will be explained to you in this thread. You should read them thoroughly and carefully, making sure you understand them to the fullest because disregard of the rules can ultimately lead to your banishment from totse.com

1. Posting of Online Pharmacies, Mail-Order Marijuana Websites, Illegal Drug E-mail Sources, etc:

These websites are strictly forbidden from being posted. The URL's, the company names, e-mails, or any other information that gives away, or hints at the identity of these types of "businesses" will not be posted. In other words, do not use the boards as any means to discuss them.

http://www.undrug.org will help you for all "Is this source good?" threads.

Note: Botanical Vendors are allowed to be posted, these are the only websites that are allowed.

2. Posting Inside The Proper Thread for Your Question:

Better Living Through Chemistry's moderators have created threads that are asked so often, an "Official" thread was needed. These threads are here for a reason. There are a number of them and you should know them. Take a look at the Official Thread Index (below) for more information.

3. No Looking For Connections:

Totse.com, as well as Better Living Through Chemistry is not your neighborhood drug spot. You cannot come here to look for a drug connection, so do not ask.

4. Totse.com's General Rules:

Totse.com also has a list of rules that you should know anyway. These rules can be found here.

These rules will be enforced strictly and are essential for the smooth running and functionality of the forum. We ask that you respect these rules so that your experience at Better Living Through Chemistry be a positive one.

OFFICIAL THREADS:

The BLTC community is second only to SG in activity, and as such, can become quickly overrun by threads if not properly organized.

To this end, we use a selection of "Official Threads" to corral discussion of the most popular/active topics. This keeps the information in one place, leading to easier searching, easier browsing, and a cleaner board.

The following four Official Threads are those topics which have been deemed to benefit the most from having their own thread. If you are going to make a post that falls into one of these categories, please post them in the appropriate thread. Posting outside of the official threads is not only frowned on, it is a violation of our rules.

Official DXM Thread - 2007 Edition - Description: Everything related to DXM belongs here. Everything.

Official Pill ID Thread - Description: If you have found a pill, and need help positively identifying it, post in this thread.

Official Post Your _________ Thread 2007 - Description: All pictures of stashes, pieces, and other paraphernalia belong here.

Official Homemade Alcohol Thread - Description: Everything about home-brewing or distilling alcohol belongs here.

TGO's Official Cannabis Grow Thread - Description: All cannabis grow questions and discussions should be placed in TG

Official Drug Testing & How To Pass One Thread - Description: All drug testing questions should go in this thread.
