how did the foxy trip compare to 2c-b?
I know they're different, but id like to know if its worth spending money on.

Foxy was a lot more introspective than 2cb (as are most tryps for me compared to phenethylamines). It produced some pretty sweet visuals and coloring. Visually it might be on par with a shroom trip, but it's definately not as mentally deep as one. The reason I ranked it so low was because of the crazy body load that comes with even a mild dose. I was fucking shaking like no other during most of the two trips i had on it. It probably gave me the worst body load of any other hallucinogen i've done.

2c-b was just pure fun. I wanted to socialize and dance and explore and just have a good fucking time. The body high is good (but don't be fooled, it's no mdma), and the good vibes and energy i got from it made the trips a constant adventure. There was very little of a mental aspect to it; just a slight amount of extra depth and "thinking space". I definately had a lot of empathy for others. Visuals were less intense than 2ce, and were much more distortions rather than hallucinations.
