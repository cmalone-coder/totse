Now each side put up a few hundred grand and built the place up and got it running and it was doing well. Everything was fine until the landlord started to want to deviate from the desired plan of the pool room. He wanted to add dance floors and make it into a club. Now keep in mind that this pool room was in the middle of a hick area and there is really no way a club could survive there and it would cost much more money for both sides and just really wasn't part of the deal that was agreed upon by both parties(plus the guy I know and his dad know how to run pool rooms not dance clubs, and with dance clubs you need bouncers and shit, a lot more staff, a lot more money to run it and a lot of trouble makers can fuck shit up and the village usually hates you because cops have to be on the look out around there). So the guy I knows dad said no, and went back to running his other businesses and that one. The landlord starts trying to fuck shit up at the other businesses they owned to try and get more people at the one they jointly owned (they are all withing like a 50 mile radius) and was caught by the dad, but the dad didn't say anything. So eventually the dad got sick of his shit and just wanted nothing to do with the guy so he sold his half of the buisness to him to just get rid of him, plus the landlord was trying to steal cash from the dad too.

Time goes on and this landlord of the other place starts spreading rumors around the town saying that the dad was trying to ruin his buisness, when really it was him trying to do it to them. Now hes trying to undermine their buisness and trying to do everything to ruin them.

Well my friend and his dad are crazy SUPER rich people whose family used to be mobsters in the 30's in Chicago. This guy is trying to ruin them and they haven't done anything to him to begin with except not wanting to go along with the buisness plan the landlord said they were gonna do after the buisness opened.

Now here where I comes in. My friend and dad (who I have known for like 15 years and since grade school, they are like my second family) asked me to help my friend go to this guys house, murder his family and burn his house down to the ground. They would pay me $75,000 cash. These people are no joke either. They are buisness men who have been fucked by some guy with a big ego problem, and they gave him time to stop, and he didn't. I am pretty sure the Dad has killed people before in the past and I know for a fact that my friend's grandpa has killed people in the 30's who tried to fuck with them.

My friend has told me that the reason the dad asked me to do it is because they trust me because I have worked for them before at these billiard rooms and I have been their friends for a long time and helped out my friend once in court and lied to a prosecutor when he stole a bunch of cars and robbed to 2 ATM machines, and I helped him not go to jail. The dad paid me 3000 dollars when I did that for them.

$75,000 dollars is a lot of cash, and they said they have a flawless plan, plus I have been involved in doing some jobs for people in the past(not murder or arson, but some pretty bad things) and him and his dad know this.

Well should I do it totse? This would be a new thing for me, but hearing what this guy did (and them showing me the evidence that he did so I knew they weren't lying) he sounds like he was asking for it. He was trying to ruin their businesses and in his own mind he thought that he could take over all of their pool rooms, and he was causing them some damage at a few of their places by making shit up and getting people to leave.

Should I do it? Any extra ideas on how to execute this plan? Anyone done anything like this before, and if so what was it like and how did you feel after? Do you think 75,000 is enough money, or should I ask for more?
