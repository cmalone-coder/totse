Well, first of all, it's important to point out that if your friend is a Witness, part of his feelings of devastation are because of the fact that he feels he's committed a sin in addition to all the emotional pain of being rejected.

It's worth mentioning that there is a thread on the pros and cons of religion in relationships ... I'd encourage you to read it at your leisure:

http://www.totse.com/bbs/Forum4/HTML/006678.html

Your friend's reasoning that 'sex before marriage is immoral' and his further reasoning that it is 'inevitable' and that you would regret it later are all questionable points to make -- he's not in the situation after all; you are. He also believes that you should break off the relationship sooner rather than later because of how much harder it would be after the time had passed. Again -- questionable.

It sounds, to be honest, like your friend is trying to justify his actions to you in the hope that he can absolve himself of some of the guilt he's feeling. Since you don't necessarily share his views, I wouldn't take everything he suggests at face value.

Anyway, here are my answers to your core questions:

1) I would say that most teenagers younger than 18 are not mature enough to handle a sexual relationship. From a statistics viewpoint, the vast majority of relationships started at 17 and under are doomed to fail simply because of the lack of life experience involved. Losing one's virginity (a profound experience, to say the least) is often enough to change one's perspective on the relationship and cause problems.

2) Teenagers (again, under 18) aren't ready for serious long-term dating which will eventually end up in marriage. This is one of the reasons why, for example, it's encouraged for young teens to date as freely as they like -- it allows them to sample experiences and ideas so that when they do marry, they get it right the first time. The idea is to be happily married, not quickly.

3) Yes. It won't feel that way, but it will be worth it. Especially if you know that you treated her with love all the way through and tried to work things out, and maybe even parted ways in an amicable fashion.

4) Absolutely. Treat her well, respect her, do all the things you say you're going to do, and talk honestly about any problems you're having with her.

In summary:

Your friend was burned -- what happened to him may not necessarily happen to you.

You love this woman. Like you've stated, you plan to respect her moral/religious wishes and wait until she's ready.

If something were to happen, you believe that it would only happen with due consideration and mutual consent, and even then if you believed she was sincere.

So, all other things being equal, you're probably in for a wait of another few years before she's ready. I'd say, let her get out of high school first. Hug her, kiss her, pleasure her all you want, but perhaps the sex itself could wait for a bit. I think you'll find it'll be worth it.
