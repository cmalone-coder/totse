Re: which makes u stronger?
It really depends on just what your going for.

A soldier is not exactly the strongest but they must be prepared for anything. Their workouts are daily and consist of all sorts of basic stuffs, pushups, pullups, running, situps, etc. They actually workout so much that they never get a chance to build up much muscle. The body just can't rebuild what they teardown fast enough. The advantage of this is that you don't have all that bulky muscle getting in the way of your movement increasing reflexes, range of motion, and agility.

Body builders and Olympic class athletes are the strongest in very specific areas. Most things like benchpresses, squats, and all that enhances only a few muscles leaving many others out. Causing you to be really strong one way and weak another . Even free weights if can do this but to a smaller extent. Ever see an Olympic Bench Press champ? The are almost always kind of chunky looking, but the top halfs of ther bodies are huge. Most of these people couldn't throw a ball further than a girl in grade school.

ProWrestlers are much the same, except a bit more flashy. The little guys who do all the jumps and stuff are probably the most well rounded of that group. You may notice that a round with the big guys is pretty lame since the can't do all those fancy flips and stuff.

Then there are the true fighters like Boxers, Ultimate Fighters, Shootfighters, etc. Alot of their training focuses on taking blows and trying to remain standing.
Their endurance would also be incredibly high.

Last you have American football players, their work outs are so stupid that your are more likely to hurt yourself than anything.

Stretching also adds a curve. The more you can stretch a muscle the more improvement a muscle can actually gain.

Overall it depends on if you want to look strong or you want to be strong.
