Tick Tock
Regular
  
Re: Waking Baked
Maybe if you smoked, and blew the smoke into the person's face.

Exactly what I was thinking. I doubt smoke in the air will make you wake up high.

Besides, it's not going to be that easy to hotbox a whole room; the only rooms I can see that working for are small ones like closets or bathrooms, basically all the rooms that a group of people can't sleep in. If you do want to try with a regular room though, make sure the air conditioner/heater isn't on, that will ruin your plan right there. On top of that, you'll have to make sure there's no circulation at all, which means towels under all doorways and all windows closed.

Once you have the room airtight, you're going to have to figure out a way to fill it up with smoke. The bigger the room, the harder this will be, one joint probably won't cut it. I'm thinking a bucket bong would be useful for releasing smoke into the air without having to hit it yourself every time. The only other alternative would be to just start a fire in the middle of the room and throw weed on it, but something tells me this isn't an option for most people. So if you don't use the bucket bong, be prepared to take a ridiculous amount of hits. You'll probably be blazed before you even fill the room up, I would get sick of taking so many hits after I was already very high.

Let's not forget about the properties of smoke, since it rises, that means you're going to have to fill the room up from the ceiling down, meaning you're going to waste lots of smoke before it even gets to the level that your friends are at. God forbid they sleep on the floor, you'll be puffing all day.

Shotgunning a person will probably wake them up. Although it's not as likely to wake people as shotgunning, I think the smell of smoke will awaken people too, long before the amount of time it would take for them to get high in their sleep; although we've pretty much defined the concept of getting high like that as impossible.

So it's nearly impossible to get a contact high, nearly impossible to hotbox a large room, and definetely impossible to get someone high in their sleep before they wake up.

That being said, if you smoke some very potent cannabis product such as high grade weed or hash right before you go to sleep, you have a chance of waking up high. You would have an even greater chance of waking up high if you ate some. Those are the only two ways you'll wake already baked.
