Re: I need a new kind of cologne...
If you're 16 you don't want to smell manly, you just don't want to smell like a gym bag/Axe/Tag. I've gone through many colognes. Here are a few that would be good for you, since you're in high school. Make a list and go to your local Dillards or Saks or whatever department store you have and ask to sample them. Remember that they'll smell different on your skin than on a piece of paper (due to oils, pH level of skin, body heat, and so on), so if you really want to take the time to find the perfect cologne then you'll sample them on your skin. If they just spray it on a card, then smell one or two, walk around for a while, then come back and smell a couple more. Don't smell 10 all at once or something, because you'll lose sensitivity. For $60 you can get pretty much any cologne you want that isn't ridiculously overpriced. Anyway, here's my picks:

Nautica (in the blue bottle; it's a nice daily scent, works well for casual wear)

Jaguar "Performance" (smells good, lasts a long time, I've gotten a lot of compliments on it)

Victoria's Secret "Very Sexy 2" For him

Banana Republic "M" (smells citrusy, kinda like oranges, a little spicy, very good daily cologne, little more pricey than the others, about $55 for 3.4oz)

These are more feminine scents, but I think will still work:

Clinique "Be Happy" for men

Clinique "Chemistry" for men (Order these off the clinique homepage, because it's the cheapest price anywhere. I've looked. These are both good colognes, great for going out because you'll smell clean.)

Always remember not to spray too much. A bottle should last you a year. Most of mine last me 2+ years. Best of luck to you.
