Re: ubuntu 7.04 small issue/problem.
You didn't really describe in any sort of distinguishable manner what "login screen" it takes you into after already logging in, I expect you are talking about the Xscreensaver lock setting.

When the screensaver goes active, from the system being idle, you are required to log back in, if in fact you have the Xscreensaver lock activated.

First, describe what login screen it takes you to, and second, find your screensaver properties menu and open it, turn off Lock screen when screensaver active box, and see if it still happens.

I hate to harp on Ubuntu, but this seems to be yet another useless "feature" of thier Linux distribution.
