I've got to say it: With this woman specifically, the sex was amazing, multifaceted, passionate (whether in romance or in anger, frustration or roughness), perfect, and frequent! Of course I miss it, but I think what I miss most is when she'd go to sleep with her head on my chest, because she said listening to my heartbeat always comforted her and put her to sleep. I miss the way she'd sit on the floor in front of me and lean back against my chest; I was much bigger and longer of torso than she was, so she fit perfectly. I would always slide my arms out in front of her and up her shirt, but not to get sexual: I'd just rest them on her stomach, and we'd sit there and talk, or watch a movie, warm up in front of the fireplace, what have you. If I were laying in her lap, or something, I'd always lift her shirt and kiss her stomach, but if I didn't do it slowly, she'd get ticklish; yes, you KNOW I took advantage of that sometimes. What's my point? There isn't one, really; this is just fond reminiscing for real intimacy, and the tangible side of it that I miss. So shut up and save your bullshit, if you were planning on flaming me for "pointless" threads. Anyway:

Do you enjoy intimacy over sex? Have you found it before? Did you lose it? Do you miss it? Have you never shared it, but want to? Mm, cuddling. Haven't done it since my ex over a year ago. I don't like to think about it too much anymore because it hurts but for other's entertainment..

We were physically fit for each other. When we held hands, they perfectly fit together. No matter the position we were in, we could always find a comfortable way to lay, sit, stand together.

I remember sitting in the back of his Jeep together watching the waves. When we were driving I would lean over and put my arm around his and rest my head on his shoulder. He had a stick shift so I would switch gears for him.

We used to lay there for hours, sometimes we'd be in bed the entire day just laying with each other and watching TV. The best part was his hands. I could play with them forever. His smell, his hair, his lips.

I liked when he was sitting on the bed playing guitar. I would come from behind him and wrap my arms around him and kiss his neck He would start playing some love song (usually Led Zep or a blues song). The greatest was he'd just start playing, without saying a word. It was like communicating on a different level.

If we were sitting on the couch cuddling, I would write words on his hands. I don't know if he ever noticed. I used to draw hearts a lot. But I write invisible words all the time (it's a habit).

When we went to the mall sometimes we would just stop and stand there and hug each other. Saying goodbye could last a good hour. It was hard for us to leave each other, even if it was only for a few minutes.

Every single picture we have of us, our arms are around one another, it looks like we can't get closer.

That was one of the greatest things of our relationship. The expressing of emotion without words and facial expressions. Of course, we talked and expressed our love in words but the actions made it that more special, whether it be cuddling, playing music, doing special things (seperating the charms and cereal in Lucky Charms), being courteous (him keeping the toilet seat down, or me putting the driver seat back to where he likes it).

To me, intimacy is great as a tool for expressing how you feel. Skin to skin contact is important to babies. I guess it's something we're born with.

Come to think if it, the first thing to go when the relationship turned sour was the cuddling.
