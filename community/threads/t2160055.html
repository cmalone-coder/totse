As a previous poster mentioned, no form of heroin is going to be completely pure, unless youre getting somehting pharm grade, or fent, of course. But from my experience, and talking to a lot of people who've abused both tar and powder for long periods of time, the prevailing opinion is invariably that black tar is by far an inferior product. Here is Los Angeles, its not that bad, but then again theres really not a whole lot else available. I read a study about the communication of disease as well as other complications related to IV use of heroin, comparatively on the west ad east coasts.... the first point they made was that the prevalece of AIDS on the east coast can be largely contriuted to the kind of heroin that is available to users. Powder heroin doesnt need to be cooked and heated, so pathogens are far more likely to spread. Also, black tar tends to clog rigs more quickly than does powder, so users are less likely to clean the needle before using again or sharing. These simple acts cut the AIDs transmission rate among addicts in mre tha half.

However, black tar presents a completely different set of problems. A lot of the homeless junkis around here have large abcesses in their arms, lips, and legs- which are unsightly as well as potentially dangerous. Ive been told that black tar is a major contributor to arteriosclerosis in IV users, moreso than users of powdered heroin. When you cook a shot of tar, you can see all the things that come off it...first theres a kind of vinegar-y smell, then oftentimes there are little black chunks that dont dissolve at all, and then on top of the fix, theres a little oil slick. This leads me to believe that theres a whole lot more in this stuff than people ould like to believe. Ive heard rumors that a few dealers try to mix dirt with their tar, to make weight, and that people who didnt cook the mixture well enough, and missed a vein, came down with botulism. Of course thats only possible if one were to IM tar, as botulism cant develop in the bloodstream. However, i dont think any sane, informed user would risk IMing tar unless they couldnt hit a vein and were very, VERY desperate.

Pretty big mistake. So...not that anyone telling me so would have dettered me any...but dont do it. Its the biggest trial you'll ever go through and it SUCKS.

And try to stay away from the needle if at all possible. If you've gotta do something, smoke it. Do it the least pleasant way possible...but know that you can still get addicted.

be safe niggas.
