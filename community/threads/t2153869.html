Call of Duty 4
...Does anyone still play COD4? I imagine a lot of you do, but there's very little talk about it nowadays.

I got on today after a lengthy hiatus, and I must say, it still hasn't gotten old. And I still don't know what it is about me, or maybe its just the stupidity of other people...but I pwn hard. Going 62-20 on Showdown (Headquarters) is quite fun, but it really isn't that hard, you know? I fail to see how very few people bother to flank and/or take a non-linear route to the HQ.

Of course the game still has its apparent problems in multiplayer, like the ridiculous helicopter and the airstrikes that still manage to kill you while you're in a building. One of the most annoying and recurrent flaws is the shitty spawning system, because apparently if two teams are spawning too close to an unclaimed HQ, the server decides to fuck over one team and spawn them at the other end of the map, leaving the other team to capture the HQ with no resistance.

So if you guys still play COD4, you should definitely put &T in your clan tag. Maybe we'll meet up in some games, and kick some ass / kick each other's asses. I play HQ and the Hardcore varieties, especially the latter because they're a lot more fun.

Anyways, here's a survey.
- What's your rank/prestige level?
- Favorite weapon/perk setup?
- Favorite map/mode?
- Headquarters on Shipment. Yay or gay?

Mine:
- Prestige 6, rank 46.
- Silenced G36 (no skin), silenced M9; 3x Frag/Stun, Stopping Power, Steady Aim
- Bloc on Hardcore Team Deathmatch.
- Only if I'm on a decent team.
