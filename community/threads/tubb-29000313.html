The Armors Blast-Pack
---------------------
Purpose:
Blast-Packs purpose is to knock down barriers, and obstacles that a person cannot do with there bare hands. The bottom "hump" of the back pack is connected by 5 metal bars. The two bars to the right and to the left of the center one, have a spring around them so there is a shock reducer. The other bars have unscrewable heads that interchange, for different purposes.
Another purpose for the Blast-Pack is to provide protection agains flying debris and potentially dangerous flying objects. The way it works is that there is a layer of metal that separates the pack from the body that is on a hinge with a support to it. So when you are ramming stuff down, you can protect yourself from unwanted debris. Calling it a back pack inquires that it have space for objects and items. The Blast-Pack comes is different sizes, so holding capasity is different, and there are different versions, some of them don't have any holding capasity, just brute power and protection. Others are made for the "scout" personnel, lightly armored and more storage capasity. The Blast-Pack and the Exo-Arm are a force to be delt with. All this along with the Helipack go along with each other. This makes anarchists dream come true, being able to plant "stuff" and if s/he gets caught they can take the heat like it was nothing, and give back enough heat to cook the opposition (literally, there is a flame thrower built in the suit, lol). There is an incidiary launcher, that can lob tons of stuff at the opponents. Some of the special made Blast-Packs will be fitted with a built in power supply pack that can power the suit, any add-ons, outside sources that need the power to operate. In certian versions there is a pnuematic punching arm that latches onto the fore-arm for either knocking stuff down, knocking people out, or anything of the sort. The pack latches itself onto the armor by hooks, latches that you have to pinch down on with your two fingers then pull apart to separate (recommended), or whatever you want. For those interested in communication there is a special pack that similar to the "scouts'" pack. The creation of the generic pack which will have other other packs based upon has been started on 1-25-02.
