I can't "solve" your problems over the Internet, but there is a technique that really works if you are willing to give it a shot.

Lesson One: Arguments (fights) will happen. The frequency and intensity may differ, but conflict is part of human nature. Only if you live a fake life (appeasement) and avoid the sensitive issues, you might co-exist peacefully - but then again the frustration will build up on the inside. So bottom line, take fights into your stride. It is something you have to deal with, not run away.

Lesson Two: After the initial exchange or words, slurs, curses, punches, kicks... when the dust settles - Ask yourself: Do you WANT to solve this one or no? If the answer is "Yes", go to Lesson Three. Otherwise, screw it

Lesson Three: On a piece of paper, write down... What happened, why it was wrong from your viewpoint, how you want him to rectify it, and what you are willing to do in return.

Let your mate read it and absorb it. Give him enough time to reply to it in a similar manner. Most probably, after you both have exchanged your 'war-diaries', things will be clearer, calmer, and definitely on the positive foot.

This works between my wife and me. Although we go through it verbally, because we are so good at making up
