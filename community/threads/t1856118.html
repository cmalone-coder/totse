Litup:

Most of these people seem pretty informed, but I'm suprised they haven't explained the basics to you.

1) Vicks Vapor Inhaler used to be L-desoxyephedrine (another name for L-methamphetamine). If it is still made from L-desoxyephedrine, you COULD, theoretically, change it into d-methamphetamine.

The meth molecule is a mirror image of itself; it has a left and a right side, abreviated d- for dextro (right) and l- for levo (left). The reason you want d-meth and not l-meth, is that l-meth raises blood pressure and increases heart beat, but doesn't make you alert. D-meth does make you alert, but doesn't really increase heart rate or blood pressure.

This is what you would do to get d-meth, if Vick's Vapor Inhalers are still made from l-desoxyephedrine:

First, break open the inhalers, and soak the cotton in about a 3M solution of HCl. This will convert the L-meth to L-meth HCl (the HCl makes it a salt in smokable form). Now you have L-meth HCl + H2O. You need to somehow get out the H2O, without getting the solution so hot as to break up the H and Cl bonds, which would leave you right back with l-meth. (Also, in this solution have the H and Cl molecules already broken up? They might since cations and anions separate in H2O, meaning you would have l-meth + H+ + Cl- +H2O???)

Anyway, once you have pure l-meth HCl, you will need to change it to d-meth HCl, which can be done by "oxidizing it and reaminating it". I personally am not sure how to oxidize and reaminate this, so perhaps one of the more experienced people can explain it.

Like I said, theoretically this should work, but the oxidation and reamination might be more work than it looks like.
