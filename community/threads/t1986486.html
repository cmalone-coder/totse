Load-testing vBulletin today
Nope, this is not an April Fool's prank. I am load-testing vBulletin today. That means that I'm going to allow a limited number of people to use the new BBS system and see what kind of a load that puts on the system.

If you want to check it out, click this link:

http://www.totse.com/community/

If it gets too loaded or bogged down I'll shut it back off until I can fix whatever is causing the problem.

P.S. I re-wrote the crappy BBS conversion software that comes with vBulletin to allow me to do incremental data migrations, so new messages posted in UBB will be migrated over to vBulletin's database once per day until I finally shut UBB off. That means anything you post on UBB will eventually (probably) show up on vBulletin.
