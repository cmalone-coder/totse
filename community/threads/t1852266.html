0/0 is an indeterminate form. You can't specifically say what it would be unless it was encountered in an equation....you could use limits and limit properties to find out what it was...

example:

f(x)=(x^2-4)/(x+2)

at x = -2, f(x)takes on the indeterminate form of 0/0. However by simple algebra and limit properties we find that lim x-&gt; -2 of

f(x) = 0
