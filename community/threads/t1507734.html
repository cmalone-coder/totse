My family is full of psychological issues. My dad's side is riddled with depression and alzheimers. My mom's side... some weird form of OCD and... I guess insanity is a good word for it. Noone likes her, or her parents, or anyone related to them because they're all fucking nutjobs. I don't know what the hell my dad was thinking, but anyway.

Lately, the 2 of them have lost their minds. Seriously. I've been bugging my parents about getting a dog for a while now, and they alternate days on who wants one and who doesn't. I thought I was making progress, my mom was already planning on buying it one of those stupid sweaters. My dad gets pissed at me. Next day goes on this whole rampage about how I have no respect (no shit) and how I'm a horrible child and blah blah blah. Right, whatever. Which one of your children tried to kill you? Yeah, not me. Anyway, whenever they're "talking" to me I have to either freak out or leave. I can't have a logical conversation with these people because they bring up the most random things, claim things were said that weren't even mentioned, and so on.

And herein lies my problem. (Sorry this is so long, by the way.) There's gotta be a way to have a civil conversation with my own parents. I can do it with everyone else. I honestly don't think I'm the problem though. I don't yell for no reason. Nor do I have to write down everything because I forget it all. I do get blamed for it though.

Help please? My home life is starting to really, really suck, but I can't leave yet. I'm in school, they're still paying for it, unfortunately. And I think I'm developing an ulcer from the constant stress.

Re: How the hell do you deal with irrational foreigners?
If by school you mean college, then spend less time at home. Try distancing yourself as much as possible without actually moving out, since that won't work for you right now. You'd be amazed at how much bad blood gets forgotten once you're out on your own more, and especially when you reach a point when you can move out and have your own life.

The best thing you could do is try to talk to them one on one and have a rational conversation and be very honest and to the point about how you feel. Unfortunately, some people just aren't rational in any situation and if that is how they are, there's not much you can do. Instead of getting mad, try being absolutely calm when they start getting onto you. Instead of yelling back, simply get up quietly and remove yourself from the situation.

If you're still in high school, there's not a whole lot you can do but suck it up and take my advice from above. By getting a rise out of you, your mom may be feeding her own need for yelling/drama in the household. Don't give it to her. Take it away the instant she comes looking for it.
