I would like to share a very important thought with everyone on this forum.

I am an atheist. I do not believe in any religion or god. I cannot be sure if god exists.

There is however one thing I do know for sure...

When I die, I will find out if god exists. I he doesn't then nothing happens. BUT if he does exist and I have to face judgement,I will not be afraid. Do you know why?

The answer is simple. If there is heaven and hell I know that I will be rewarded not punished. This is because I AM a good person. I believe in the same life rules as all true christians, muslims, buddists etc. I try to savour every moment of my life. I would like to experience all wonderful things that have been given to us by destiny (or god). I try to find something new that will put a smile on my face everyday. Nothing gives me more pleasure than helping other people in need. Minor needs and Major needs alike.

The fact is that every religion- real or not, tries to show people the right path in life and all gods reward good people.

This is why I am not afraid of death. Nomatter which god is real or not. I will be rewarded. Even if there is nothing after death I am sure that I will stand on the good side of the universal energy that created us.

any comments and thoughts apreciated
