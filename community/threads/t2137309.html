Phenylalanine and tyrosine are the most variable, and depend on your existing plasma level. At the weight you gave, 75mg per 1mg/kg increase in plasma level, obviously. Common recreational dosages range from 250mg to in excess of 2 or 3g, though doing 10-20mg boosters to adequate blood levels has also been known. Apparently due to the response of phenethylamine - which is an analog of amphetamine - tyrosine usually requires higher dosages to reach a full euphoria, and is usually also gentler.

As for the cholergenic agent, well... it depends quite a lot on what you're using. No one's used huperazine successfully, but it's listed as being active in 2-400ug range... so, it would kill you in the 500mg range, which is commonly used for choline.

Basically, a good measure is to start taking small doses until the cholergenic is psychoactive or physiologically active in its own right. In the case of an AChE, this usually means drooling, pupil constriction, and the like, whereas in the case of choline, this would be a mild decrease in the threshold of muscle spasm & cramping. Try not to get to "major increase," 'cause the dude who did that said it sucked. ;)

Once you've slowly ramped up, either there or 10%-20% lower would be a good dose, depending on your tolerance of the side effects.
