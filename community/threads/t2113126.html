NSA gets basically everything. Also, all phone companies have a dedicated, unmonitored, full-access line going to one of the FBI's processing centers for CALEA compliance.

Basically what they can do is monitor EVERYTHING using supercomputers. Local PD does not have access to this as it is still used for "terrorism," but it's just a matter of time.
