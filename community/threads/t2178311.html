Re: Phenobarbital Question
Ouch, that's a tough one man. 10 mg of Xanax is going to be tough to kick.

Phenobarbital is a weak barbiturate and 64.8 mg is a low dose. It's been awhile since I've taken Phenobarbital so I'm not 100% positive, but you should take around 300mg and see how you feel.

Seriously though, you should taper off the Alprazolam instead of jumping to Phenobarbital so quick (Phenobarbital is more helpful after the worst of the withdrawals are over). If that's not an option, you should seriously see a doctor, Benzo withdrawal, especially at that high a dose is brutal and can even be fatal.

The best advice I can give you is to consult a professional.

Best of luck man.
