A few words about happiness--it is a choice, as is everything else in life. We alone are reponsible for our reality, and whether we have a "good" day or not is not about what happens to us, but about how we feel about what happens.

If you require anything--whether it be sex, drugs, money, power, pain, square dancing, shopping, whatever-- in order to be happy, you are addicted to it. Most people agree that addiction is not a desireable condition to be in.

This is also known as being outer-directed. When you can be happy just to BE, you are inner-directed, and a large step closer to full enlightenment than most.
