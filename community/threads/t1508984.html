As for destroying the need for love, I'm not sure that it is, neurochemically speaking, possible. Love, or more specifically lust, is a result of the body's addiction to dopamine (see also: cocaine, chocolate, runner's high, or pretty much anything the body finds rewarding or pleasurable.) What this means is that your body is pretty much predetermined to be inclined towards the freaky-deaky, and that all hope for completely erradicating it from your natural makeup is an unachievable aspiration.

In fact, if it's just lust that you're after, and not the easily equivocated cousin love, than every act of aquiring the lust will make the next high harder to obtain.

Love, on the contrary, requires less effort, and becomes a much higher higher, assuming that it is with the same partner.

The difference rests in the chemical used in each oft-confused big L.

Lust=dopamine (neurotransmitter, synapses, reward, hooray.)

Love=Oxytocin (hormone, everywhere, helps immune system, fights depression, etc. etc. etc.)

What makes these two so easy to confuse is that we live in a world that draws a very powerful link between sex and love, hence the forum you're currently frequenting sex & affection. Oxytocin is a hormone released at different times, child birth for example, to bond the two experiencing the hormone and allow for a better life (genetically assuming the two shall give birth some day), Oxytocin is also released during orgasm, but only in very small amounts, and is released much more thorougly during foreplay

Especially the nipples. For obvious reasons.

What's released intensely during orgasm is Dopamine, and immediately afterwards Prolactin which is a chemical the body secrets to suppress the flow of reward, reward, reward from the dopamine. Which is the reason men can't have sex immediately following orgasm.

Which is also the reason that most of us haven't fucked ourselves to death.

In increasing trials of solely seeking dopamine, the aforementioned state of needing more dopamine to obtain that same high comes in, but the same amount of prolactin keeps shooting down our high. Which is why most people bent on fucking to prove their love usually fall out of love as quickly as they fell into it.

So I guess what you could get from all of this is the closest way to kill your need for love would be to flood your system, somehow, on a constant basis with prolactin. I'm afraid you'll find this won't destroy your need for human emotional bonding and contact, it will just make you a prude.

My advice would be to, rather than trying to kill your need for love, find a different way of expressing it.
