Re: Looking to get a laptop for college
For portability, get a Dell Latitude or an IBM/Lenovo machine. Most of them are quite small, light and hard to kill by accident. My Dell D600 has fallen off tables, been stepped on and a lot more and the only signs of its misfortune is the paint coming off the metal lid.

Thinkpads are known for their strength too. Both Latitudes and IBMs have metal frames too.
