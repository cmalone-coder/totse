Well, It started about 6 months ago. There is a club that I work for. It's a great place to work. The owner is an older man that has owned a few places over the years and he's good guy. He knows what he's doing and he's a fair man. Sometimes he can be hard to work for cause he's an old marine. He's a guy that will help you if your down i.e. money or whatever he can do.
At this club he is an absentee owner. Usually the manager makes all the decisions on a regular basis. The last manager (not the current one) didn't have the heart to run the place. He was drinking on the job, stealing, and not promoting the place as he should of been. When the owner caught on it was too late. When he was about to dismiss (fire) the guy, and send a guy that has worked for him a while in his place, the manager emptied the safe and left with out any notice. The place was hurting. The assisstant mgr. a young kid who didn't really manage much but did all the errands and other stuff had to open the place. He had no idea of what happened until he came into work and realized there was no mgr and no money. Money had to be released to the assisst mgr who would normally not have acsess to the clubs bank account to open the place for buisness. The money was for change, beer, etc.
Now the new mgr gets here. He came here on short notice and drove for 24 hours to get here in order to save the buisness. When he got here I could tell he felt uncomfortable.
He didn't know anyone and had never been in an awkward situation like this before. Also, he had never even been in the state. Now the plan was that he would come down for 2 weeks stabilize the place and go home for a week to get his belongings to move to here. During the first week I felt bad for him. The last mgr had left the place in such a rutt/debt/ hole that the new guy literally fought off bill collectors, utility companies, and even the IRS on which last mgr did not pay. Just to keep the place open. He was stressing. To make matters worse, one of the promoters had loaned the last mgr money (a few thousand) with a legal agreement to be payed back in the CLUBS name for god knows what and was threating to take his night somewhere else. The 2nd week the new mgr was here the owner came down. He had just had heart surgery and the whole thing was taking a toll on him. He didn't do anything wrong to deserve this but shit happens ya know. He came down with $20G cash to pay the bills that he had not known owed and left with $40 in his pocket and not everything was payed off.
Then, the new mgr leaves for a week to go home and move here. He worked his ass off and even though he was only here for 2 weeks the club was running better internally than it had ever did. We had everything that we needed. Everything was stocked. We had flyers for the nights that didn't have a promoter. Things were getting done. He even made plans to renevate the place to give it a fresh new look. While he was gone the assisst mgr had to open the place and make sure things were working. During the week, Friday night, one of the DJ's said to every one "why don't we go to another club so we don't have to deal with the problems of this place". I said I thought that it was fucked up for him to even mention something like that, it seems like everything is getting better. Most of the other employees aggreed and no one thought it meant anything more. Came into work Saturday night to set up for my shift. NO ONE was there to open the doors. I thought, "this is weird""what the fucks going on". I and few other employees where just waiting/thinking the same thing. We called the assisst mgr and there was no answer. It came time to open and still no one was there. We waited the entire night. What was even more strange was that not that many people showed up that night. We figured that many would show and we would take there name down for a free entry and drink for next week. Only about 50 people came from a regular amount of about 500. I couldn't believe it. The DJ + the promoter for that night + the assisst mgr + another promoter/bartender had quit with out notice and didn't tell the mgr or the owner. The reason not that many people showed was that they took the mailing list and sent a mass mailing that stated the place is closed down due to the fact the owner does dirty buisness, anyone who would still work there must be dirty too, and that the reason the last mgr left was because the owner owed HIM money and would not pay.

They literally STOLE the buisness!
