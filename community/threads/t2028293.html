Re: reality
Radical skepticism might seem tempting, but it is a pit that must be avoided if we're to make any real progress in philosophy. It seems to me that we must operate under the assumption that our senses are more-or-less reliable most of the time; the fact that we can't prove or disprove the assertion that they are not seems to indicate that the proposition is not even really worth considering.
