5 Basic Rules for new stoners
Hey all. Over the years, I've noticed that among the pothead community, there are a LOT of dumbasses. It probably comes with the territory, but fuck....the things some of these people do are just retarded. People getting caught coz they don't know how to cover up the smell...paying ridiculous prices for bags of weed that are clearly pinched...the list goes on. I've compiled a list of things that hopefully should be obvious for everyone. But the thing is, often times what seems obvious is often ignored by many. While these may not be completely applicable to everyone's situation, lets just say in my experience of 6+ Years of smoking pot and never being caught, more than one of my unluckier (or retarded) smoking buddies could have saved themselves a lot of money and trouble by remembering these facts. And of course, this is completely for information purposes only.

1. Sploofs don’t work. For those of you that don’t know, a sploof is a cardboard tube, usually from a toliet paper roll or a paper towel roll, stuffed with fabric softener sheets. The idea is that if you exhale your smoke through the tube, it will mask the smell of the weed, leaving only the wonderful scent of fresh laundry. Don’t fool yourself; the room WILL smell a bit like laundry…and a lot like pot. If you must smoke inside a room and have no alternative, at least point the sploof towards a wide open window.
2. Get a scale and know your measurements. An eighth is 3.5 grams, a quarter 7 grams, etc. Don’t be afraid to bust our your scale when buying from a dealer for the first time, especially for big amounts. If he has nothing to hide, then there shouldn’t be a problem. This isn’t like a transaction at store, there is no way to get a refund or return so you’re stuck with what you pay for. If you don’t have a scale, then just ask your dealer to weigh it out in front of you. Don’t support shady dealers, if he shorts you then don’t go back. You shouldn’t expect hookups, but you should expect to get the weight you pay for.
3. A dealer is not your friend. He is your dealer. Naturally, if they were your friend first, then this shouldn’t apply, this is just referring to your general dealer you meet from a friend of a friend, etc. They’re out to hustle and make cash, not hook you up. And another thing…
4. Don’t buy into dealer speak. “This shit right here nigga, this is BOMB! Way better than the last shit! This be White Widow Purple Kush Chronic, ya hear me bro?” I would say 95% of the time, its total bullshit. Will you get good weed? Yeah, probably. Is it the fantastic brand names that your dealer gives you? Eh…probably not. It just cracks me up when I hear high school kids talking about how they get “hooked up with White Widow” from some guy they know in art class or whatever. Unless I know the guy is getting it from a club, then I doubt that it’s the brand name weed that most dealers like to stick on their product. And I certainly won’t be paying the ridiculous prices that most street dealers will be asking for it. Give me the no-name quality bud for half the price please; its probably the same shit anyway.

5. Fool-Proof Method of avoiding detection:

a. Bring an extra shirt/jacket/sweatshirt to change into when you’re done
b. Wash your hands, face, and neck with soap and water.
c. A spray or two of cologne or a cigarette will hide the rest of the smell just fine, no need to bath yourself in the stuff. Reeking of AXE or any other cheap cologne is more of a giveaway than slight weed smoke.
d. Use eyedrops and drink plenty of water.

Thats all for now. Comments, arguments, flames, whatever are appreciated. Just a few random thoughts by me trying to help out the newbies. And if you don't like it, then fuck off, I'm too busy smoking my great, cheap weed to care.
