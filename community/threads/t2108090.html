lol this a joke?


Yeah they're recreational. It's a very long lasting opiod. The onset is VERY slow, so don't redose if you don't feel anything quickly enough. In my experiences, the euphoria comes at around 2-3 hours of ingestion, and lasts up to 12 hours. I'd say if you're not experienced with it, don't redose within 12 hours, always wait whole a day. The reason for this is because even when the euphoria wears off, a large portion of the methadone is still in your system; people sometimes overdose this way. It can be tricky and take advantage of a novice, so be aware.

I'd say if you don't do opiates too often, or have a negligible tolerance, take 10mg your first time. It is ruthlessly known for causing extreme nausea when someone takes it for his first or second time, so maybe have something around for that. Take it on a light stomach to be sure (but not FULL stomach, only light). it produces a very nice euphoria, Although much more clouded than oxy or hydros.

Expect to pay around $0.50 per mg. Possibly more. If the person selling them is stupid, you can probably get a good deal.

Enjoy, and be cautious.
