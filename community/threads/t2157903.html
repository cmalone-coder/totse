Re: so i wanna get a cat...
Get a kitten. Why would you want to skip the cutest part of cat ownership?

I don't know about table scraps, but my cats thrive just fine on cheap Meijer brand food.

If you're not going to let the cat outside, consider getting two (of the same age and at the same time is probably best). They'll keep each other busy and won't fuck with as much shit.

The litter box stinks sometimes if you don't clean it often enough. Other than that, my cats never stink up anything, and the only messes they make are when she gets knocked over. Or when the toilet paper needs to be destroyed.

I used to think I was a dog person until I got a cat. I'd highly recommend it.
