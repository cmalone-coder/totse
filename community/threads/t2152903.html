I can't cry anymore
I'm 18, I've noticed in the past few years that even when I want to cry, I just can't, I mean I can force a few tears out in a vain attempt to feel better; but I can't cry.

I don't know why really, I mean I've gone through a lot of stuff but it's not like I was in the holocaust or anything... why would I feel so emotionally desensitized like this? Most of the time I just feel empty, and 95% of the emotions I feel are fleeting and before I can even register them, they're gone.

Don't get me wrong, there are times when I do feel really happy, or really sad, but they're just really fucking rare.

I want to add that I was diagnosed with clinical depression like 4 years ago, but I remember what that felt like, and I don't feel anything like that now. It doesn't feel like depression, it just feels like... nothing. Sometimes I'll be walking around and things will start to feel like a movie, like I'm not even there and I'm watching a screen or something. It's weird.

Sorry if this post seems a little scatterbrained, my question is does anyone have any experience with feeling so detached?
