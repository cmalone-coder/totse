Re: Long distance relationship
my boyfriend and i are in a long distance relationship. he lives in sydney, i live in perth. that's a 4 hour flight and $550 return airfare away.

it's not easy. you will be miserable. i see my boyfriend on uni breaks, we take turns. it's going to be ten weeks before i see him next.

i do not recommend the semi-open relationship. if either of you can consider sleeping with another person, then you're considering cheating, even if it is 'allowed'. i couldn't imagine going off with someone else, i love my boyfriend too much to want to do that.

as for advice: talk often. my boyfriend and i talk almost daily on messenger (IM or video chats) or through texting (neither of us can afford the phone calls). it's really hard to stay close to someone when you don't tell them the little things, so just make sure you do. i also like to send letters (and receive them). the internet makes contacting each other easy, but there's nothing like knowing that someone took the effort to write you a letter and post it etc.

i really suggest you rethink the open relationship idea. it will get messy, you will be hurt.

good luck.
