Re: Working in New Zealand
If you are a dual citizen it shouldn't be a problem for you.

You need a IRD# (inland revenue department) number for them to pay your tax to, thats about it.
You get free medical care here, with a max of NZ$3 per quater for each medicine.

I think the minium pay rate is about $NZ12 (US$8.50?)
Thats for any job, even pushing shopping carts back to the supermarket, which is a job advert I just saw.

There is plenty of work around, lots of jobs if you will work behind a counter or have some special job experience.

If you want to travel around NZ & work theres lots of 'fruit picking' jobs about.
They let foreigners do that job under special 'work visas' if thats a problem.
They can arrange housing & transport to work.
You get to meet plenty of other people that are doing what you do that way.

If you rent a house with, say 5 others, weekly costs will be about NZ$125.
rent each $40-$70 pw , power av $150 month (for 4 people), food $30 - $60.

I pay, per week, no rent (ha ha!) $50+ food, $20 power.

cellphone costs $40mth with 300mins off peak
sat TV = $40 mth
homephone $40 mth,
internet connection $40 mth (landline)
Bus costs normally about $2 per trip, $4 across city.
Petrol is $2.20 litre
Milk is about $2litre
Beer is $1 - 340mls can, $12+ dozen cans
Internet is $4 or cheaper, per Hr
pot is $20 tinny (1gm)

It'l cost NZ$400 for petrol to go from top to bottom of NZ, $60 side to side. $50 for the ferry between islands, Nth - Sth - Stewart island.

Days Ski lift ticket is about NZ$60, cheap skis/boots at second hand dealers.

A shitty car is worth NZ$400+
(make sure its got rego/warrant or it costs $200 fine for each & you WILL get picked up on it, DON'T drink/drive!)
or buy a car for $2000 with wof/rego & sell it a year later for $1000.
(a relistic yrs cost is - $1000 inc drop in value & wof/rego & insurance.

WOF is $25- every 6 mths +what it costs to bring it up to Wof standard
Rego is $220 + yr
Insurance is about $280 (depending on situation/car value/you can get 3rd party ins)

Use 'Trademe' site to get a idea of jobs & costs.
