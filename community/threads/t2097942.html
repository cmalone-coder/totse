Ultram: MUCH better than nothing...
I just got 10 Ultram ER (200 mg.) tablets, and i must say, they really arent too bad, i'm actually quite pleased after crushing up one pill and eating it.

As we've all heard that they are pretty weak, 45 minutes later i decided to cut another one in half and rail about 25 mg's and eat the rest to get me going. (about 300 mg. total)

I'm feeling pretty good now after about 1 1/2 hours.

The effects seem to compare somewhat to vicodin, but with less of an artificial euphoria if you know what i mean (less nodding off out of nowhere/wanting to just chill) but i am getting some of the forgetfulness of vicodin and no bad side effects!

Now i want to just watch a movie, much like i always do when taking vicodin, but slightly more awake. Which is almost the effect i was looking for. I was hoping for a little more drowsiness, but whatever, i'll work with waht i got.

All in all, i would say, if you have some, and you're bored, like i said, they are better than nothing.

I plan on taking another one, when my mom gets home so i have the patience to sit through her lame movies. That satisfies me well.
