Basically, if it's skit (little powdery bits) don't buy it unless you can very clearly see that it's little bits of bud. Generally speaking pale dry buds are better than dark wet buds. Just keep buying weed. Your stoner senses will develop over time. You'll learn how different kinds of weed look and smell, and be able to recognise what you're being given as shit you've had before (or at least something very similar to it).
