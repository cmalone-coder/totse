Freebase morphine and oxycodone will not decompose at their melting points. There is something like awww, fuck, another 100 degrees C until decomp. Basing is easy.

Add NaOH to water, add pill mass, stir a bunch, keeping it cool. Then add a good nonpolar solvent like ether, and shake it really well. You could add table salt to help push some of the base out of the lye solution. Seperate the polar and nonpolar layers, and then evaporate the nonpolar layer until you have a scent free oil like substance. Then, take that, and put it in an oil burner, aka chicken bone aka glass dick, whatever, and smoke it as you would any other freebase.

As for amounts, you want to use as little as possible for easy of evap and working with it.

For the lye solution, just keep adding it until its saturated, then add about .25 the total volume of water to it, to ensure you don't get any pure NaOH in there.

For the ratios of nonpolar to use, just check and MSDS for the freebase forms miscibility in ether, or whatever nonpolar solvent you choose to use.

I suggest against naphtha since it has higher boiling point distillates in there, and you end up with a more viscous yield, since some of those oils are left behind because of the high BP, contaminating your product.

It is more for novel use than anything. If you are fine with using IV I suggest you stick with that. If you have the extra dope, and extra chems, and extra time to fuck around and try something new, go for it, but don't have your expectations too high.

Smoking the salt form ALSO works, but not NEAR as well. A huge waste IMHO.
