I still fight with food and with myself every day. Every time I eat food, I think about the consequences and the fat that it will grow into. I try my best to ignore these feelings. I will always have an unhealthy relationship with food. I have been trying to get over hating myself and my flesh, and I think I might be improving. I don't hate myself so much anymore... I am unsure whether I caused any serious harm to my body, as I have some problems now with my period and it not showing up. How none of my family or friends noticed this whole thing I don't know.. I think they must have known... But who knows.

I don't have a scale anymore. So, that's my story I guess. It's nice to talk about it sometimes. When I think back, sometimes it doesn't even seem really real. I've never done much talking about my problems and writing it out helps to realize how big the problem was.
