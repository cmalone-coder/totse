Re: Legal "feel good" drug or supplement
Just plain old psuedo at the regular dose. It's not good to try to get high with it. ER Docs are very familiar with the people who come to them with the heart palpitations. I took a time release & had the heart palps for a good 8+ hours. It was nasty! If I take half that, my concentration is improved. I don't get jitters as with coffee & if I don't use tobacco: no heart palps.

60mg per 4 > 6+ hours. No more than 240mg/day. Probably less. I had a cold last week & 240/day just burned off. It can be hard ('specially with caffeine) to mellow out with a full 240. Not good to take it every day either.

Heart Palps: I get a flutter more in my Solar Plexus than my Heart. Simultaneously I seem to get attention fluxuations in my head. As if variations in blood pressure made it hard for my brain cells to perform smoothly. I find it analogous to Coffee Jitters, but more like what one gets from getting way too far up into the caffeine dosages. Nasty!

Probably doesn't help that I often take in lots of caffeine anyways. Adding too much psuedo & ginseng too is just too much.
