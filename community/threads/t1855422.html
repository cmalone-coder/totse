Re: ? % Isopropyl Alcohol
sparkey--

I really don't know just how that would come out as I have never tried to remove that much water from iso.

Automotive gas line dryer (one of them) is isopropyl. If its gas dryer and it says isopropyl, then its 99% unless otherwise noted.

To check for dryness, take the piece of glass from a 4X6 picture frame (or something similar). Clean it very well, then hold it close to a lightbulb for .....oh, maybe 30-45 seconds. Then, holding the glass at a very slight downward angle and still over the bulb, let a drop of your questionably dry iso roll down the glass. Watch it closely. If its dry, it should evaporate quickly, evenly, and leave nothing behind. If water is still in there, it will form micro droplets after the iso has evaporated. It doesn't require much heat for this, so don't get carried away with it.
