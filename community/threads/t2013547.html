Re: KCLO4 Cell Help
1) What would be a good power source?
There is really no way to find the ideal voltage of a cell exactly, but you can come close by using the nernst equation for your cell and modifying your reaction quotent for activity changes due to temperature. The only true way to find ideal voltage in a cell (which will change as the reaction goes towards completion) is to experimentally scan various voltages. Cell potentials, junction potentials, diffusion rates, etc make it nearly impossible to do on paper.

dD is right with amperage though, you want as much as possible. This works of the principals of kinetics, reactions are affected by the state, concentration, and temperature of the reactants. Electrolytic cells are forced redox reactions, so electrons are acting as reactants. By increasing the amperage (current) you are increasing the amount of electrons flowing into the system at a given time, forcing the reaction to go faster.

2)What is a good way to check ph without dissesembling th cell?
Probably a glass pH electrode, or simmilar model used for fish tanks. Just seal one through the top deep enough into the cell that it comes in contact with the solution.

3)What do most people here use to filter out the chlorate that never got to kclo4?
Fractional crystalization I would think...

4) Any Safety precautions with the CL?
Cl2(g) can be removed by bubbling through water. I could reccomend you add activated carbon to the outlet pipes too, and pack it well. Bring that water to a waste to a chemical disposal company.

5) I heard dd used titanium cathode, would this work?
The composition of your electrode is really only useful if you want to avoid corrosion. You could really go with something as cheap as graphite rod, you'd just have to replace them more.
