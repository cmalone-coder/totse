Similar thing happened to me a couple of months ago; we grew different and apart over the last year and were becoming too different people to make it work.

I really, really missed him, he was a big part of my life and i didn'twant to fill the void with some comforting rebound, but being close to him still was upsetting. Instead, I threw myself into every new opportunity I could, college, friends, work, anything, and I came out a generally all around better person. Spending more time with close friends you've neglected feels good, one actually said he gained a lot of respect for me because of how well i dealt with it. Minimal moping and maximum self improvement.

It's better to fill the masses of free time with things you enjoy, rather than sulking over the past. Realising you can be happier without him will help avoid pulling a Miss Havisham and sticky situations such as him crawling back after two months expecting you'll get back with him.. that just ends with a few more months/years of doubt before you go through the same break up all over again.

Work out your "shit" and live for yourself.
Being single is a selfish way to live, but damn is it good.
