Re: Best way to smoke hash? And other questions
Well first I start emptying out my bong water, shake out excess water, clean the bowl area of excess gunk, put paper towel in the bowl, and stick it in the freezer for an hour, the paper towel keeps condensation out of your bowl. Then, add a preprepared mixture of really slushy ice water, made from distilled water into the bottom of the bong, and then, depending on bong design, ice cubes, or chunks in the neck of the mouth piece, size allowing.


Then a metal screen is folded and manipulated in a way so that it sits just inside of the "bottle neck" part of the bowl. Where it gets smaller. Then, a little bit of regular pot is put on top of that, then your ground hash mixture, then a bit more pot. Or you could mix hash with pot.


Anyway, yeah, that method right there gets me fucking baked as all hell off one hit, and the flavor with the ice bong is just divine. Distilled water is used to avoid any flavors that might be added by dissolved contams or additives etc in tap water.
