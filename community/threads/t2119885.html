I would like to add that it's very obvious that benadryl and similar deleriants have a horrible reputation for a reason.

It's very easy to overdose, most people do not enjoy their experience, there are REAL risks involved, you usually have a bad hangover the next day and most people who do them do not properly research their effects.

However, some people DO enjoy the experience and when done with caution and knowledge can have a great time and I still press my theory that they can be a useful tool for self-exploration.

This is not a party drug and do not expect to have a fun time and "trip out". It can be very scary and you can be a harm to yourself (as well as others) while under their influence.

I suggest for anyone starting (I'm not suggesting that you do) to take very low doses and over a large period of time build on the dose and get yourself acquainted with the effects. If your impatient and decide to do a larger dose for your first or so times that you do indeed get a sitter who knows about the drug and preferably has done it before. Even if you've done low doses many times, you might want to consider a sitter when you decide to do a higher dose. Someone who WILL get help if you need it.
