Re: Do You Believe in God?
I can only answer that question if you define what you mean by "God."

What God? Whose God?

Is God male? Female? Genderless? Apart from his creation, part of his creation, or simply creation in its totality?

Your question assumes that you and your audience share the same definition of God. If I said to you, 'Do you believe in apples?,' you'd say 'Of course' because we both agree on what apples are and we both agree that apples exist.

But God is a bit more complicated than apples. Apples are objects. God, since no one can be empirically sure what he/she/it is, is only a concept. And everyone's vision of that concept differs greatly.

So which concept/model of God are you referring to?
