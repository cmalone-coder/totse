quote:

Why worry about RP when you can skip it and use a chem that is made with easily obtainable chems. http://www.rhodium.ws/chemistry/hypophosphorus.html


How is that easier, you still have to obtain WP.

I would think that the amount of HI theoretically needed would be a double molar excess of HI to ephedrine. This is based upon the equations given in that hypophosphorus link. Where:
The OH gets torn off and forms OH-, the positively charged carbon picks up the I- ion to become iodometh, then the H+ left reacts with the OH-.
(1)ephedrine + (1)HI----->(1)iodometh + (!)H2O
Then the HI reacts with the iodometh.
(1)Iodometh + (1)HI----->(1)meth + (1)I2
So in short:
(1)ephedrine + (2)HI----->(1)meth + (1)H2O + (1)I2

To figure out how much RP and I2 you need to supply that amount of HI:
(1)P4 + (6)I2----->(4)PI3
(1)PI3 + (3)H2O----->(3)HI + (1)H3PO3
