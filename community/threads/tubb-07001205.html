Re: Atm Security Features
I don't imagine such a thing would be that readily available.

I do know this: If a standalone ATM (such as the ones in gas stations and Kmarts) is disconnected from its line for long enough an alarm will be tripped. I know this because I found the line for one of these at a local department store, which was located directly next to the bench at booth in the food court area. Apparently the ATM machine used to reside directly in front of the jack, but it was moved away from the door (and the jack) by a few feet, faced the other way, and the empty floor space was filled with the table. It had "ATM" and a code number penciled in on it. Since I had nothing better to do I unplugged it to examine the hardware (a normal RJ11 telephone plug, but with 6 pins on it instead of 4). Satisfied with my discovery I plugged it back in and went to wander around the store. No later than ten minutes afterwards the police were there.
