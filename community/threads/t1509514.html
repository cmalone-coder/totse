I had a girl.
When we met, I was interested, but she was too young for me. It was awkward, but nonetheless enjoyable in a humorous way.

As I got to know her better, I came to realise that she was more than I gave her credit for. I started to take her seriously.

She was dumped, and I wondered how anyone could do that. I asked her out. She was elated.

We lasted a month, a very good and wondrous month, before I started to wander. There was another girl who was more like me, and that I had known for some time. I broke it off.

She wasn't very happy, but nonetheless we stayed friends. I didn't actively pursue this other girl, I thought I'd let things run their course.

She found someone else, and that was that.

I was fine with that.

The first girl had had a boyfriend in this short time, nothing serious. He pissed her off, she dumped him. I took her back.

The next day, I was out. I took it well as really, it was my fault to begin with.

She's now happily invested in someone else, while I sit here...

Point of this post: Do you know the feeling when you have somebody, one person special, who you want to spend time with? Always?

This girl is her. Yet I messed her around, and thus, I am not him to her. I'm destined to become just another of her close male friends. Something I feared from the start.

I cannot share anything on a deeper level with the only person I feel I can.

I shot myself in the foot, and there's nothing I can do about it. To inflict her with my feelings now would be to rob her of at least a little part of the amazing relationship she has right now.

It's happened before, and I know this one will be a big one for her. I don't have a hope in hell.

I just needed to tell someone.
