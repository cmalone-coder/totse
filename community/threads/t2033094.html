I suck at remembering inspiration quotations when I need them, but the gist of it was:

"If you set a low price on yourself, don't expect the world to raise that price"

Basically what I'm trying to say is: Think positively about yourself. If you don't think positively about yourself and feel confident in everything you do, then people won't feel positively about you either.

Start talking more within your group. Let your voice and opinion be heard and known, but don't dominate the situation. If you say something stupid, so what? Take every mistake confidently and give the impression that you don't care you said something stupid. Alternately, you could say "Hah, that was stupid.." In a joking manner. If you joke about your own mistakes and criticise yourself, then you're showing others that your confident in your actions and are not afraid to make a mistake.

At least that's what I do, and it's worked for me. Just do everything in moderation and, as much as I imagine you won't, don't dominate a situation. When you do that, you go from shy to just being an asshole.

Hope that helps,
Zaknafein.
