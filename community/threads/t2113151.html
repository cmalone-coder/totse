Easiest way to stop taking a drug and not miss it
The best way to stop taking any type of drugs and not miss it is to make yourself sick of it right before you stop then when you think back on taking the drug you'll remember feeling sick so you won't wanna take it.

The last time I took heroin I took shitloads of it. I was smoking the shit for 3 days straight from morning till night. First thing I'd do after I ate my breakfast was take a bit hit of the shit then I'd keep smoking it every 20-30 minutes for the rest of the day. It was great fun but by the end of it I got sick of the shit and I lost the thrill of it so now that I've stopped altogether I don't miss it at all.

The exact same thing happened with speed. The last time I took it I took so much I ended up feeling sick for a day or two so now I when I think about taking it I don't feel like it.

I saw in the movies where the parents catch their kids smoking cigarettes so they make them chain smoke 10 packs of them so they get sick. That trick works. Just like liquor too. Alot of people when they think about tequila they automatically remember how sick they got last time they drank it so they don't want to drink the stuff anymore.

I think if your physically addicted to any drug this won't work because you'll take more to ease the withdrawal symptoms no matter what. It works for psychological cravings though.
