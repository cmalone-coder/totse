Western Australia is kind of odd. We -did- have some pretty good, open minded laws, up until labor just lost power - Now the liberals have made thigns remarkably draconian, including banning the sale of bongs, pipes, or anything marijuana related, including clothing or items branded with a hemp leaf logo.

Most people here buy their weed in 'sticks', which is nominally 1.5 - 2.5g, and has a fixed price of $25. Next up will be an oz with sets you back a couple of hundred depending on where you go. In bunbury you're most likely to get a lot of bushies (home grown and non-hydroponically grown) which isn't extremely strong, but I prefer because it tends to be a bit nicer, and often cheaper. bunbury in particular I was driving through a while abck, some guy asked us if we wanted to buy some bud (it was about 2am, lol) and we went back to his, he had a few plants growing out the back and he sold us about 11g of decent bushie bud for $35.

'Kief', rather then meaning pressed trichromes, in WA means shitty shake, stem and leaf.

And likewise, 'crack' is actually just low-grade speed, also commonly known as 'gear'.

That's pretty much it to the WA drug culture, the only other concern you should have is that everything is RIDICULOUSLY EXPENSIVE, the going rate for a tab is $25, pills will be $30-50.

oh, and almost nobody has coke, ever, and when they do it's about $80/0.1g

I'd just like to put up some defence for aus, for starters wester australia (perth etc) is like 4000km away from the east coast, and only 15% of the population live there. I've never been there and don't want to from the descriptions given, but over here on the east coast drugs can be very prevailant, although since things like coke and heroin are imported in huge amounts, if the cops take down big drug rings then it can get pretty dry. Also the precursers for meth are often imported due to tough pharmaceutical laws, so prices can be high for that, and base is always shit, however crystal is usually pretty good. With weed, i've almost always come across only good stuff (either hydro or simply well grown and a good strain), however if u go to nimbin (althought it's easy to get) you'll almost always get sold outdoor shit - even if they say its hydro, and it can be nice, but you should just buy hydro somewhere else since it's the same price (320 and ounce and 3.5g for $50). I've got a feeling most of the hydro comes up the coast from victoria and nsw (biker gangs as said before) which is why it can be kinda expensive, but if you have friends who grow it you don't have to deal with these people. and yeah E is usally $35 but lsd is $15-20 and when it's easy to find you can buy heaps of it.
