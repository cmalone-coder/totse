Starcraft Single Player Challanges!
I lost my internet for a couple days and decided to install some of my old favorite games. One of the games happened to be Starcraft and I was wondering what kind of offline play besides campaign could I enjoy? The answer was simple, I needed some challanges. I called up a friend and got him to google starcraft challanges and put whatever he could find on a disk. For the next 2 days I was trying to beat these challanges and actually found it fun to have limitations and handicaps that I had to overcome. I have only made it to challange #8 so far, but I plan to beat all of them hopefully. So if you have Starcraft save the following list of challanges for a boring day and maybe you will like them as much as I do.

Waterboi[h20]'s Presents...
Single Player Starcraft BW Challanges!

Before you start a challange make sure that you have read and understood the rules of that challange. Remember to set the game mode on melee and not free for all. All challanges have been tested and beaten by me. I wish good luck to everyone who attempts to beat these challanges and watch out for my next set of challanges coming soon!

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #1
"The Turtle"

Map: Bloodbath
Your Race: Terran
Computers: 1 Zerg, 1 Protoss and 1 Terran
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Ghosts or Wraiths.
Difficulty: *

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #2
"The Lone Zergling"

Map: Lost Temple
Your Race: Zerg
Computers: 3 Protoss
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Lurkers or Scourge.
Difficulty: *

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #3
"Hellfire"

Map: Diablo
Your Race: Protoss
Computers: 2 Zerg, 1 Protoss and 1 Terran
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use High Templars or Archons.
Difficulty: **

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #4
"Clear Skies"

Map: Island Hop
Your Race: Zerg
Computers: 2 Protoss and 2 Terran
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Scourge.
Difficulty: **

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #5
"Group Assault"

Map: Hidden Shrine
Your Race: Protoss
Computers: 3 Zerg, 1 Terran and 1 Protoss
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use High Templars, Archons, Dark Templars and/or Dark Archons.
Difficulty: ***

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #6
"No Place To Hide"

Map: New Gettysburg
Your Race: Terran
Computers: 3 Protoss and 2 Zerg
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Vultures or Firebats.
Difficulty: ***

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #7
"Skinny Dipping"

Map: River War
Your Race: Protoss or Zerg
Computers: 2 Terran, 2 Protoss and 2 Zerg
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Photon Cannons, Creep Colonies, Spore Colonies and/or Sunken Colonies.
Difficulty: ****

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #8
"Afraid of the Dark?"

Map: Continental Divide
Your Race: Terran or Protoss
Computers: 3 Zerg, 2 Protoss and 1 Terran
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Bunkers, Missle Turrets, and/or Photon Cannons.
Difficulty: ****

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #9
"Infestation"

Map: Homeworld
Your Race: Protoss
Computers: 5 Zerg, 1 Protoss, 1 Terran
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Dragoons or Reavers.
Difficulty: *****

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #10
"The Horde"

Map: Killing Fields
Your Race: Zerg
Computers: 3 Protoss, 3 Terran and 1 Zerg
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Hydralisks, Lurkers and/or Queens.
Difficulty: *****

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #11
"The Hunted"

Map: The Hunters
Your Race: Terran
Computers: 4 Terran, 2 Protoss and 1 Zerg
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use Siege Tanks or Battle Cruisers.
Difficulty: ******

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #12
"Plane Crash"

Map: Char Magma
Your Race: Protoss or Zerg
Computers: 3 Terran, 2 Protoss and 2 Zerg
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use any air units besides Overlords, Shuttles and/or Observers.
Difficulty: ******

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Challange #13 (BONUS)
"One Choice"

Map: Station Unrest
Your Race: Terran, Protoss or Zerg
Computers: 5 Protoss, 1 Terran and 1 Zerg
Rules: You must win in 60 minutes. You cannot use any air units besides Overlords, Shuttles, Observers or Dropships. You also cannot use Siege Tanks, Ghosts, Firebats, Hydralisks, Lurkers, Defilers, High Templars, Archons and/or Reavers.
Difficulty: *******

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
