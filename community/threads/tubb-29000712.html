Originally posted by GHETTO_SMURF:
Has anyone been able to throw 'stars' with moderate accuracy? Or throwing spikes? I'de like to know what's possible.


Yes, I've been able to consistently throw shuriken with accuracy. The thing is you have to have real shuriken, not those crappy knock-offs they sell at malls and head shops. Real Shuriken will have a decent heft for their size and will actually have bladed tips rather than just being punch pressed from sheet metal. Throwing spikes/blades as well as 3,4,5,6, and 8 point shuriken are all accurate, but you have to learn how to throw them. No, you will not likely kill someone with a shuriken, that's Hollywood malarkey. Shuriken do hurt like hell though and are excellent deterrent attacks, as well as distractions if you are being pursued. You CAN kill someone with one, if you hit them in the jugular or something by chance, but it's highly unlikely.

Throwing axes, however....those are fun, and can easily kill...
