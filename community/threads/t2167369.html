Re: First time 'cid
Well, in all honesty, even if it's decent/good acid, half a hit isn't going to do a whole lot. It will probably have some visual effects, but don't expect a huge mindfuck. Try to get some nitrous, it'll potentiate the trip when you're on your peak (about 2 hours in). Nitrous, and weed if you can get it, will most likely intensify the trip a little.

Next time try to get 10 :)

Only advice I have to give is don't trip around people (who aren't tripping, anyway.) A nice secluded place in nature would be ideal. Stars, fire, clouds. All good stuff. If you can only do it inside, just try to do it somewhere that's a comfortable environment (somewhere where you don't have to worry about getting caught or something.)

Anyway, have fun and be safe.
