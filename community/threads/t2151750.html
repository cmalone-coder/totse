Re: Question about opiates, the "1 shot and your addicted" thing.
At lot of times, it depends on your personality. Some people get addicted to anything, be it coffee, cigarettes, or even just watching TV or playing a video game. Others can do pretty much anything, and stop at any instant.

How often have you tried quitting cigarettes? If you have, how long did it last? How were your urges to smoke while you were 'quitting'? Some argue that quitting cigarettes is harder than quitting Heroin, so if you smoking cigarettes is merely something you do, in the same way you might watch a certain show or go to a certain place, rather than an addiction (NEEDING one when you wake up, etc), the chances you have what's known as an Addictive Personality is slimmer than if you aren't that way.
