It seems to me that the only way to save the earth is to stop reproducing. All this recycling and conservation and whatnot just puts off the inevitable. I find it hilarious when people talk about recycling and etc., and then they have a kid. It's like they just undid all the recycling and conserving and did 10x as much damage to the environment. I don't recycle, I don't do any of that shit, I'm doing my part by not reproducing. There doesn't always have to be a future generation, there is no reason that humans always have to be around. So if you really want to save us, the best thing you can do is STOP HAVING KIDS.
