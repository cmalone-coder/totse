Re: 16 year olds?
there are all different types of maturity. physical, emotional, intellectual, social....just to name a few.

physical and intellectual are pretty irrelevant in this case.

emotional and social, however, are key. emotional maturity entails being able to understand and deal with complex emotional issues, both in oneself and in others. social maturity is being able to deal effectively with other people in day to day events. it involves not losing your cool in stressful situations, being able to assert yourself and get what you need (to a point) and knowing when to shut up and let others have their say. people who are socially mature are easy to pick out because they're well-liked and don't have conflicts with the people around them.

i should also add that the various forms of maturity do not necessarily coincide. one can be extremely mature socially, but still unable to deal with something as simple as breaking up with someone or consoling a friend who has suffered a loss (emotional maturity). i could go on and on but i'll stop there.
