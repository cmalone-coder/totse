Re: What makes someone 'clingy'?
It depends on the type of girl. Some like the whole clingy thing and are suited to be that sort of girlfriend. And many women aren't... some like some level of space, or they take an intellectual or dispassionate attitude towards relationships in order to preserve individuality and to not feel stifled by someone's (possibly too possessive or annoying) affections. I understand it. I need some level of space in a relationship, as well. It's pretty important, the person I'm seeing not getting that generally ends in failure, since it's just a character trait of mine, one that's not likely to change.

Different people want different things from relationships. This is pretty crucial since it tends to determine theoretical viability of a relationship between two people, ie: if it can work long term, fulfilling both peoples intentions through seeking a relationship in each other. Be more perceptive of her. Realize you're making certain demands by perhaps trying to take possession of her time, and keep in mind her perspective. People can be pretty varied and interesting. Some people are so complex they're full of tonnes of inner contradictions and dualities. A person like this may be difficult to sort out, but if you expect this relationship to fulfill your intentions (your image of a relationship/romance/whatever) then you have to fairly access where you stand, and pay increased attention on if clingyness, and perhaps dependence or possessiveness is a wanted thing for her.
