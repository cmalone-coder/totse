Re: Post your favorite stealth smoking technique.
Multi-parter but as close as you can possibly get to being 100% safe. I spend most of my time living in a very small dorm room so I have to be very cautions, everyone else that I know that has smoked in the dorms has gotten busted except for me.

First, a window fan does wonders. I have two window fans that have the option of intake or exhaust. Use the exhaust setting with the fan turned on high, and very little smoke stays in the room. Put a rolled up towel under your door too, if there is room for air to escape under the door. This will help prevent any smell from leaving the room, and combined with a window fan it will help seal off the room even more. When I have my window fans in the windows turned on exhaust with a towel under my door, I can actually feel air being sucked in from the hallway when i put my hand by the door frame.

Second: Use a spoof or doob tube. Paper towel rolls generally do the trick. Don't go easy on the fabric sheets though, stuff that shit as full as you can get it without making it difficult to exhale through (and don't pack them in too tightly). I personally use a gatorade bottle packed full of fabric sheets with small holes poked near the bottom because i think it works a little better, but both work just fine.

Third: Smoking itself: either get a vaporizer or be sure as hell to cover your bowl after every hit to trap any smoke. vaporizers are ideal in this situation because if you get a good one, they work very well and are more efficient, as well as the fact that there is MUCH less smell with a vaporizer. if you don't have the money to spend on a good vap, just make damn sure that you cover your shit up immediately after hitting it to trap the stray smoke - when hardcore stealth smoking, any smoke that doesn't pass through a doob tube is an enemy.

Four: Air freshener and/or incense during/after every smoke session. Febreeze, as well as pretty much any other air freshener work wonders. Incense also does the job very well, so light up a stick of nag champa before you toke (incense not recommended for dorm rooms).
