"I HATE AMERIKA" is starting to get damn irritating
The topic pretty much says it all. It seems that everywhere I go on the internet, and even some of my pals at school (I live in NY, by the way) are waltzing around saying "I hate america!" "I want to move!".

Everyone is welcome to their own opinion, of course, but I think if these naysayers do some research on how people in America live and how people in other countries (besides canada and western european ones) live, they'll choose America any day.

Now I'm not denying that America is a bully when it comes to international politics and economy, but I think we've earned it. It's the same argument I used for MSFT's antitrust case. They earned everything they have. So what's the problem?

While our government isn't as well run as possible, it's a lot more well run than many other governments in this world. To use one person as the icon for the country (generally Bush) is unfair and unjust. What foreigners don't seem to realize is that anyone can run for president on paper (provided they're American and fit the age). You just need to EARN the capacity to run. Which brings me to my next point.

America is home to a CAPITALIST economy. Which means that the more work you do, the more money you get. There's no real limit.

So just because America is prosperous doesn't mean other countries and people can't be just as prosperous. You just have to try. So quit your complaining and do something about it, god damn it! Run for mayor, start a business, move to America, move out of America, do anything to make YOU happy! That's what America is about. Anyone can be happy if they put forth enough effort.

If you don't like America, be my guest and live (or keep living) in another country. But just because you're jealous of our prosperity, don't blame America.
