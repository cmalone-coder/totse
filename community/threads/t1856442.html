Good grief. It was silly to try to order a List I chemical (assuming you're in the US) from a big company and have it shipped to your home address. Are you wanting to make PCP? IIRC, piperidine can be replaced by a variety of other (unwatched) amines to make PCP analogs of very similar activity.

Here's how you get List I chemicals: You make them YOURSELF. If you can't handle that you should find a different hobby/business. Of course that's not how the big clandestine labs get their chemicals, but it's how every smart small-scale chemist will work.

Still, I'd be astounded if anyone's gone to jail simply for a single botched attempt at ordering listed chemicals. I certainly wouldn't want to try ordering other chemicals - listed or not - to the same address, or using the same credit card, after a mistake like you made.
