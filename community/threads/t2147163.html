no don't make your own jewerly. thats dumb. it's not that expensive.

i suggest a septum ring. i have one. they hurt like a bitch when it's being pierced , but worth it after it heals. while it's healing, the only time it hurts is if you touch it.

if you're piercing it yourself, i recommend buying a hypodermic needle (hollow) they're not too expensive. you can buy them on ebay, for about 2 bucks a piece i think, and the piercing shops i have around here sells them.

but, if you're using a safety pin, sewing needle, etc. make sure you sanitize it first. i usually soak it in alcohol, or burn it with a lighter. (make sure the flame is not too close to the needle because it can create soot.)


as for your eyebrow. that can probably be a hard piercing to do yourself without clamps. theres not many nerve endings in the eyebrow so it's not very painful. also take into consideration the side of your face you sleep on, if you sleep on your side. and one more thing. make sure you needle is the same gauge or one gauge bigger than your jewelry. i pierced my tounge with an 18 gauge needle and shoved a 14 gauge bar through it. it was not fun.


i hope i helped :]
