Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Re: A little idea...and it works!
If you've ever filled 2-3L plastic coke bottles with isopropanol (rubbing alcohol) fumes and lit it, you'd see really how dangerous it would be if the flame managed to get inside the can. If the opening is restricted, it burns much faster and whistles because the heat causes the oxygen in use to be compressed and be more accessible to the fuel. The pressure in the can can only last so long at the certain point where the flame is continually repelled from entering, when it drops below that you're in serious trouble if you're anywhere near it.
