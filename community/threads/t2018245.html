Your thoughts on this please (giving up babies)
In Germany and some other European countries there are things called 'Babyklappe'.

http://tinyurl.com/27rqaa

These are basically holes in a wall (usually a catholic or protestant hospital) where a mother can 'deposit' her new born in total anonymity.

If she doesn't reclaim the baby then after 8 weeks its given up for adoption.

So really its the old idea of unwanted babies being left on the steps of a church or monastery.

This idea has become particularly relevant in Germany due to a recent spat of mothers killing their new borns (28 cases in the first 3 months of this year)..

As most of you know I'm violently Pro Life and while the Babyklappe worries me , on the whole I think its a good idea and I'd like to see it spread throughout the world.

I'd be interested to hear your views. Especially from those who view abortion differently than I do.
