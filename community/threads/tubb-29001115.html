5.56x45mm my bad, I must have mistaken the 5.45x39mm.

THe main drawback to the RPK as a light MG is it is fed by a magazine. Magazines add more weight to the weapon and unlike belt/linked ammo can not be easily carried as such. Not to mention the RPK can only hold so much ammo at a time where as linked ammo can be linked to another link of ammo removing the need to reload in a defensive position.

I would say the M60 is superior over the M249 in the "demoralizing affect". When a M249 opens up it doesn't sound intimidating, on the other hand when a M60 opens up all you see is assholes and elbows as everyone hits the deck. Not to mention the stopping power the 7.62 has over the 5.56.
