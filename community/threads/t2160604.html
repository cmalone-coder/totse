Re: Is your index finger longer than your ring finger?
ok, I study this at uni a fair bit, so I'm going to take a stab at explaining it a little bit better/in more detail.

What you're looking at is the 'digit ratio' - that is, the ratio between the length of your second and fourth digits (counting from your thumb to your little finger). The length of the second digit is divided by the length of your fourth digit and voila, your 2D4D ratio (second digit/fourth digit).

The thinking goes that higher levels of prenatal testosterone result in a more 'male' brain, that is, more mathematically and systematically inclined. Also resulting from higher levels of prenatal testosterone is a lowered 2D4D ratio (ie your fourth digit is longer than your second digit).

The reason this concept is about is because some psychologists suspect that autism is an extreme of the male brain, no social/emotional skills but more systematic. They've found that the 2D4D ratio follows this theory, with autistic subjects more likely to have a lower (<1) 2D4D ratio.

Me - I'm female and my 2D4D ratio is about 0.93 (ie my ring finger is longer). I'm definitely more systematic than many of my female (and male) friends BUT I am studying psychology, so I don't think my emotional skills are completely shot.

If anyone one has any questions I'd be happy to link you to a few articles about the idea.
