Im not sure about Canada, but here in California the whole medical marijuana thing is a crock of shit. No legitimate doctor will prescribe weed, and all the ones who do are complete quacks.

They operate small one man practices that dont accept any insurance, and typically charge about $100 for a consult. You go in, pay in cash and give them any bullshit excuse you can think of (some teenagers are prescribed pot for arthritis) and he writes you a prescription without doing any kind of tests or considering other treatments. The whole meeting is typically about 15 minutes.

Then you turn your prescription in to the state and they issue you a card thats valid for one year (i think). then you take the card to your local pot club where you can buy upto a half pound a day at a little above black market prices.

EDIT: Thats not to say that there arnt some people who do really need and are helped greatly by medicical marijuana. But the vast majority of people i've ever met with a card obtained it in this manner.
