Racism is a natural survival instinct in humans, one that we have yet to evolve past. What people normally refer to as 'racism' in essentially xenophobia. Xenophobia is a necessary survival trait in primitive/tribal societies. The tribal mind separates everything int he world into two basic groups, the familiar, and the unfamiliar. Those who are 'us' and those who are 'not us'. In mankind's formative years this was essential, strangers often bore disease, foreigners often came to push you out so they could hunt your lands, steal your water, and take away your most attractive and/or healthy women to breed with. Not that this is a bad thing. Racism is still an important part of every cultures natural diet. People tend to strive harder when they think that they're oppressed. It gives lazy people something to bitch about and spoiled kids something to empathize with.
