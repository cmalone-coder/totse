Depressed/Anxious Supplement Combo
Okay I am 19 years old, I am depressed, get panic attacks and I am currently getting counseling which is helping a bit. I also take about 10 gs of Omega 3 Fish Oils a day.

After reading posts by JoePedo I started to figure that maybe there is some hope for my situation. I know hardly anything about the brain and how much of each supplement is adequate.

I have a very rough idea of what may improve things. Could people please help me if they have any ideas of what to add or modifications.

Vitamin B6 500 Mg
I know vitamin B6 is very good at helping autistic people. I have very bad anxiety and trouble communicating with others so perhaps this would work for me. I know B6 helps with serotonin, couldn't tell you exactly how. Anyone know whether this dose is too high? I read on a website that 0.2-1g is safe for autistic kids. Does the fact that I am not "autistic" make this vitamin more dangerous for me?

Magnesium 200 Mg
Magnesium plays a vital role in the brain. It is usually used with vitamin B6 for treating Autism and ADD. I couldn't explain to you why. I think this dose of Magnesium should be good as it's Recommended Daily Allowance is 400 mg and many foods also contain it.

5-HTP 1 Gram
"Tryptophan" is not sold where I am living (Canada) so I will use 5-HTP (same thing, right?). I have tried this before and I have taken even more then 1 Gram before. Thus I believe that 1 Gram should turn out to be a safe and effective dose. 5-HTP is a precursor to serotonin, and the taking of B6 and Magnesium should make this more powerful.

St. John's Wort Whatever Dose is on the bottle
St. John's Wort works similar to an SSRI in that it will keep the serotonin in my system longer. I remember JoePedo saying it was effective for cutting down on the "speediness" of that L-Phenylalanine thing he created. I guess it could probably help anxiety.

Vitamin C Double the recommended dose
The dose on the bottle is usually a little too low (they can't make it dangerous for really lite girls and I weigh 190). Vitamin C seems to be a component of everything and I am sure some more of it would be beneficial.

L-Phenylalanine 1 Gram
Based on FullMetalJacket's experience I think 1 Gram should be an adequate amount. I take it that L-Phenylalanine is to Dopamine what 5htp (or Tryptophan) is to Serotonin. I read that when you smoke cigarettes dopamine is released in your system. As an ex-Smoker I can say that I'd love to have that feeling again.

Niacin 100mg
Dosage also based on FullMetalJacket's experience. Works as an anxiolytic. Helps in other respects too.

Valerian Recommended Dose
Also works as an anxiolytic.

Folic Acid 800ug
Dosage is 200ug less then the Recommended Daily Allowance. Folic Acid deficiency is the cause of some mood disorders so I think taking it could be beneficial.

Caffeine Cup of tea when needed
If more caffeine is required then I will up the amount.

Okay guys, this is all I have for now. Any thoughts about this and experiences shared are greatly appreciated. Please help me on my quest to happiness.

I will be updating this Combo upon reflection of the replies.

5HTP and B6 could possibly cause heart problems.

From - http://yarchive.net/med/5-htp.html

"5-hydroxytrytophan (5-HTP) is what's sold as a serotonin brain
precursor. It is taken up into the brain. If you could keep if from
getting converted to serotonin in the liver, you'd be in great shape.
Taking 10 mg of B6 a day, however, is going to get most of it converted
to serotonin before it has a chance to get into the brain. 50 mg a day
without carbodopa is too much, since most of it will get converted to
serotonin, and 50 mg a day of "outside the brain" serotonin or so is
about the amount associated wtih heart damage in carcinoid syndrome."


It says that levels of blood serotonin (in the range 50mg or more) can cause problems with fibrosis of the endocardium and valves in the right heart, which can cause heart failure.

Be careful with that, especially if your planning on taking a gram of 5HTP when they are saying only 50mg of 5HTP and 10mg of B6 (and your going for 500mg!) can cause heart problems.

If you dose that phenylalanine combo, don't dose the 5-HTP till hours later or they will compete for receptors, meaning less will be absorbed.

Theanine increases brain serotonin, dopamine, and GABA levels! And has affinities for NMDA receptors. Theanine also promotes alpha wave production in the brain!

"Theanine may help the body's immune response to infection by boosting the disease-fighting capacity of gamma delta T cells.
