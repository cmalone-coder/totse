blunt wraps in my experience are the easiest thing to roll a fairly presentable blunt out of, there is no worry about gutting the cigar incorrectly or it being too dry, or have issues with it sealing.... just unroll it, put weed onto wrap closer to you than the middle, bring the side closest to you up and over the weed, pull it back so it makes 1 even line across the wrap, you can repeat this until you work it fairly tight, and then bring the edge of the wrap and tuck it under, roll, lick, heat with a lighter to dry and tighten.... if any fell out while you were rolling you can tap it lightly in with the straw...

if i gut something its usually when i want something small so i use a swisher sweet cigarillo. make it wet by licking the entire thing, find the seam and gently pull off the outer leaf, save these. You should be left with a clean inner leaf, lick this to avoid cracking. cut straight down with a razor. remove all tobacco, replace tobacco with weed. work this with your hands until it is evenly distributed and compact, bring edge over, lick, and seal... then heat with a lighter to dry...
