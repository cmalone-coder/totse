Just a quick point, Wittgenstein would say that you're both having difficulty with this argument because you're not talking about the same thing. Neither of you have agreed on a definition of Nihilism. So, everything that follows from your exchange becomes defunct.

One attempt to define philosophic nihilism (and its a pretty good attempt) is to say a nihilist believes (yes, believes) that moral assertions (eg Killing is bad) have the capacity of truth or falsehood but that none of them are true.
