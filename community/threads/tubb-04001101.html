Obviously she thinks that screwing around outside the relationship is OK (or at least while she's got an excuse). So, if that's not cool with you, get some distance, bro, and find a chick who's on your level (and make sure if she talks the talk, she walks the walk!)

If you're just getting laid, and the relationship is just a convenience, then I'd say do this: have your own life and stuff, meet chicks, flirt, and one-night-stand as long as there's this pseudo-committment going on, but pay attention to her and keep up an equivalent front to hers. Honestly, from your post, it seems this one is about due for self-destruct if either of you is taking it seriously... so, depending on whether you want to let sleeping dogs lie or rock-n-roll, either dump her or start "cheating" yourself. What goes around, comes around, ya know?

Personally, I think honesty (not necessarily truthfulness) is the key component of ANY relationship more involved than business or acquaintance. Honesty is doing right by people. Truthfulness is just not lying (even by omission). Do right by your dawgs, your girl, and your family, and what goes around will come around... you just have to be patient and be ready to take care of yourself, because they won't always be there for you when you need 'em.

Hope that helps.
