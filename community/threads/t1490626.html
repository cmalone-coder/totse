if you're serious about losing the weight having nothing but junk food in your house is a shite excuse for not eating right. I don't pretend to know your mom but i'm going to go out on a limb and say that if you asked her she would probably buy you some healthy food. Fruits, veggies, crackers, what have you. Most food like that is a lot cheaper than canned ravioli and all that frozen shit anyway so she'd be saving a chunk of cash too, and i've never known anyone opposed to that.

So far as working out goes you can get an evil cardiovascular workout without an expensive gym membership or any equipment at all. Although a small set of freeweights is good to have, and usually pretty cheap at places like K-Mart/Wal-Mart. Set aside an hour each day and do a body circuit. Start out easy, do 25 upper body crunches, 25 lower body (lay on your back in the sit-up position pull your knees up till they hit your upper chest then swing them down and away and tap your heels against the floor, if it starts to hurt you're doing it right), 25 left and 25 right ab crunches (lay on your back with your legs out straight, cross right leg over your left nd put your foot next to your knee then put your right hand behind your head and touch your elbow to your knee, then do it for your left side it'll hurt if you do it right). After that do 25 push-ups. Then get a watch and a wall and do 2 minutes of wall sit (put your back up against the wall and go into a sitting position with your thighs at a 90 degree angle to your shins, don't put your hands on your shoulders or let them hang at your sides, don't use them to take weight off your legs, keep your legs parallel to the floor, you should be able to put apen on your thigh w/o it rolling off). Then do a 2 minute pushup bridge (lay on your stomach put your hands together and brace your forearms against the floor, use them to elevate yourself of the floow just enough that your back is straight, like you were doing a pushup). Run through that as many times as you can in an hour, as you get stronger increase reps/time for each exercise. You'll lose that fat and replace it with trim muscle. If you can't run i'd advise using an ergometer, or rowing machine, which should be at a local YMCA or a gym if you do get a membership. Ask someone or check the erg website, www.c2.com (http://www.c2.com) to make sure you have the proper form, a big guy like you should be able to burn more than 1000 calories an hour on an erg, while improving your cardiovasicular strength, endurance, and working all your major muscle groups.
