Solace


Amazed by all these wondrous scenes
I gaze across this field of dreams
My heart beholds the grief unseen
Deep chasms filled with want and need
The desperate souls who might have been
Alive and free and young

For creatures lurk among the trees
I feel their shadows close to me
They sing their songs of sympathy
A loathsome dead cacophony

Cruel laughter madness borne
With sobbing edged by scorn

Like dark clouds on a dark night
Chasing black stars in a black sky
With worn dreams in a worn heart
Over torn sails on a tired sea

And yet the Light keeps shining brighter
Ever brighter each new morn'
Until the One belongs the Fight
Then fear at last the coming Storm

Re: Solace
Moon Girl


Moon so bright with endless sight
I wonder what you see tonight

The kings and meek, great and lost
Sail crimson seas of dreams and hopes
Spent waves that break upon the shoal
Upon drift captives stripped of soul

And have you grief when you look down
To dim the glory of your crown
Look there does sadness plenty pour
Both great and small soon nevermore

Grey pools of wine to fill their cups
Brought forth with roses turned to brown
Grey mornings greet the bell toll sound

And have you love when you gaze up
To light your lamp a blazing white
Those hearts like jewels pure and bright
Their eyes turn fiery fill the night

I wonder if you see my face
As I look up to you in space
We share that often restless place
Beyond this land called home

Re: Solace
The Way


Caught amid the flames of youth
Trapped inside the pain of truth
Speeding on toward the proof
And stand or fall we must

A mirror formed of marble black
Within we see our shrouded face
That darkened part we know so well
Grins back with wholesome, sinless grace
Invites us come explore a place
And sleep or wake we must

Tall trees tell news the mass ignore
A world of gold now turned to rust
Few bold stand firm upon the field
Choose independence lost in trust
In deeds of love their offspring must
Grow grapes of riches sown in dust

The Storm approaches
Rain to cleanse our hearts

Re: Solace
Within and Without


The howl of the wind sounds a yearnful song
It calls to me and to you, my friend
We're caught in the tide and then led out to sea
While the doorway fades, ever closed at the end

The damned and the blind
Try to slay humankind
The Storm ever flows from the black, my friend
Wide is that path and it's filled 'round the bend
Cold waves on the seas in the hearts of men

The eye within and without
It looks this way and about
Through darkened mist of doubt there
To look but not to see

Between the tall trees
The Wind stirs the leaves
And who knows where It's going
But there in our heart lie the wage of our art
The field in which we are sowing
