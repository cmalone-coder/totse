Sustaining an Opiate/oid Habit
I was reading a little bit about pain and pain management, both physical and emotional, and it occurred to me that a life mostly on these sort of substances would be a fairly nice one - if you could defeat the twin demons of tolerance and addiction. So, my question is... how can you do this?

My idea was to:

a.) Use potentiators, to keep tolerance from rising as rapidly (since you'd be taking smaller amounts).
b.) Use tolerance-reducing drugs such as proglumide.
c.) Cycle different opioids - this one this week, another one the next.
d.) Take time off when possible.
e.) Use, when possible, drugs with similar effects (benzos?).

I'd like more ideas, and comments on the ones I've proposed. I have no idea how to actually put even those into practice. For instance, how long does it take for your tolerance to an opioid decrease? To build up? Does it differ substantially with different drugs?

The main problem I've been having is deciding when someone, if they were to do this, would take their breaks to prevent withdrawal symptoms. That is, would one week on/one week off stop someone from going through hell when stopping? From having to take ever-increasing amounts?

I'm going to go Wikipedia various opioids to see if they have anything on when physical addiction becomes problematic, but any help Totse could provide would be appreciated. :)
