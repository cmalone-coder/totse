According to John Carmack the PS3 is the weakest link
Here's a quote from him, courtesy of Kotaku:

"Kotaku: "Ps3 sounds different to develop for?"

Carmack: "It is more difficult to develop for, in basically every case that becomes the gating factor on what we wind up, uh, limiting the game in different ways"

Link: http://kotaku.com/5025229/carmack-ta...s3-programming

This is pretty damning, because Carmack is basically a programming genius, when it comes to people who know their shit, he's about at the top of the list. This is the guy who just about invented the FPS genre with Wolfenstein3D, and the guy who popularized online multiplayer, and the guy who made the Quake 3 engine in 1999, an engine so amazing it's still in use today, you may have seen a modified version of it running Call of Duty 4. Also, check out what he's up to nowadays:

http://www.gametrailers.com/player/36283.html

Pretty much the most impressive looking game graphically at E3 in my opinion.

Now, I know the PS3 is a powerful system, but I find it absolutely hilarious that there's this misconception about it being more powerful than the 360 despite the fact that it has a much less powerful GPU, and time and time again the most talented developers in the world like Gabe Newell and John Carmack are saying it's the weak link in terms of multiplatform development. Of course, the proof is in the pudding, for every one multiplatform game that looks better on the PS3, there's 10 that end up running and looking better on the 360.
