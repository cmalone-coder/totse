Re: for general use, revolver or pistol ?
If the gun's not going to be getting much attention, then a revolver is the way to go. They can sit loaded for quite a while without springs getting set or any problems like that. Magazine springs and a few others in semi autos can get weakened over time if the gun is left ready to use, whereas a revolver isn't going to have that problem.

If I was going to have 1 handgun to cover a broad range of use, then it would probably be a revolver. You prety much know they are going to go boom when you need them to.

Don't get me wrong, I love my springfield 1911 but a revolver is just a more reliable setup and usually a bit more accurate.

Leaving your mags loaded for long period of time will cause them to eventually fail. This is what we call metal-fatigue.

Loading and unloading the mag will not cause problems.

For some one who want a good general purpose gun that will serve many purposes, purchase a 4" .357

Defesively, the .357 is a fantastic round. You can take medium sized game with it.

If your worried about reloading, then you probably shouldn't be in the fight unless ur toting a mag-fed battle rifle, ie ar, galil, aug, m1a1 etc.
