With the short cook, the buzz is way more euphoric with a lot more fireworks. The buzz doesnt have the long legs like the long cook though. Now with the long cook, you have a really clean product that has the long legs....but the buzz (which is the important part in SWIAs book) is really boring. It actually feels just like desoxyn(which is should since desoxyn is script meth).... but the buzz is BORING. there is no euphoria and there is no fireworkds with the long cook.

Now there are a couple of guidelines when you are going to dream about the short cook.

1. The prescursers have to be REALLY REALLY clean. You dont have as much leeway as you do with a long cook. any form of ephedrine will work nicely. SWIA prefers freebase ephedrine but hcl works just as well.

2. Start the cook at 120F and slowly move up to 180F over the period of 1 to 1.5 hours. Idealy you should have it up to 180F about 40 to 45 minutes in to the cook though.

3. Do some extra cleaning on the post-rxn a/b.
