Ok, I have two 20 pound dumbells for home use. I do this workout once on Mondays, Wednesdays, and twice a day on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays(if I haven't gone to the gym).

20 curls
20 tricep press
20 bench press(I don't have a bench, so I just do it on the floor and let my arms hover above the floor each time they come down, see how long you can keep them down there for good endurance)
20 wrist curls
10 military press

Then repeat each set 1x

Followed by 2 more sets of whatever I feel like doing, usually another set of curls and another set of military press.

On weekends I mix it up a little by throwing in shrugs, hammer curls, cuban press etc.
