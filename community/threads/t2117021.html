Re: Is it possible ...?
A Pavlov's Dog theory related to drugs...

Yeah, I mean it is possible. I wouldn't put this toward the drugs themselves but your own ability to cope with what they do or did to you.

The mind is a whacky thing and it can recognize and remember things that we, consciously have no idea about.

For example, I don't know how many people have had this kind of thing happen to them before.

I have been watching TV, a re-run of a show that I couldn't remember and all of a sudden something random comes to my mind... like "carrots"

Then within the rest of the episode that I couldn't remember for the life of me, carrots become a main part of the plot of the show... things like this have happened to me several times.

Maybe when you were high on acid, you smoked weed, and the whole combination was so intense that your mind made a special note to remember what is happening.

The same kind of shit can happen to people that have been to war... think of yourself sitting in the cabin of a large plane with 20 other men ready to jump out of the plane, parachute to the ground and start killing or get killed, carnage, intensity, shock = Post Traumatic Stress Disorder which is pretty much what flashbacks are. Picture getting into a large plane 15 years after you left the war, with 20 other people with parachutes, ready to jump out and parachute calmly to the ground, just for fun, just for the sake of parachuting... there is a good chance that said war 'hero' would freak the fuck out and have a war flashback while in the plane.
