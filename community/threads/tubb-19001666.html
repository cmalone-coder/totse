Re: Cratermakers
Rocket engine black powder will work just fine, provided you grind it down to sand sized particles or thereabouts. I find the best way to fill a cratermaker is to drill out the end a bit to widen the hole, fit a funnel over the neck, and pour in the powder. If your powder is of the proper grain size it will just pour right in, like sand in an hourglass.

A sparkler will work for a fuse, but I recommend using proper Visco fuse for cratermakers. If you don't use waterproof fuse you deprive yourself of one of the best things to do with cratermakers - Setting them off underwater!

If you don't use a CO2 or N2O cartridge it's not really a cratermaker, but a similar effect can be had with short lengths of pipe.

~Zero
