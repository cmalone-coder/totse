Caps or Pills
SWIM is wondering if it is better to sell a 2c- in a size 1 gel cap or in pressed pills.

Swim has come up with his pros and cons for caps and pills

Caps
Pros: easy to make, can make 15 a minute, can allow consumers to razorblade their doses easily, cheap, and easier to move equipment
Cons: Sketchy, not sure about market

Pills:
Pros:a popular source in my area, smaller and easier to carry, less sketchy, can be branded in a specific way (swim would like to put a eye of horus on the pills)
Cons: difficult for users to adjust dose, larger cost, can make 1 every minute, cannot move press easily, cannot be hidden among legit prescriptions to throw off others.

tl;dr: would swiy rather pay $5 for a pill or a cap, dosage and quality aside.

Re: Caps or Pills
Cons: Sketchy, not sure about market

At raves and things of the sort, like you mentioned is your main target -- people are very used to seeing stuff in capsules. Don't sweat it. As long as you don't come off as a total sketch you'll be fine.

smaller and easier to carry

Not significantly so

less sketchy

Many vitamin supplements and things of the like are in capsules and will look just like your homemade goodies. They will be just as "unsketchy" as an obvious amateur pressed pill. However, if you have the equipment to quickly press pills that are high enough quality to press very clean looking pills, then by all means...

can be branded in a specific way (swim would like to put a eye of horus on the pills)

Pill dies are expensive and difficult to track down. Also, since you're worried about sketchy, this is the best way to raise some red flags.

make 1 every minute, cannot move press easily

Some of the cheaper presses are a huge pain in the ass and occasionally will be stubborn and take a bit longer to press a solid pill. But, those same presses are generally rather small and easy to transport. The big ones are expensive as hell, but you'd have no reason to transport it.

would swiy rather pay $5 for a pill or a cap, dosage and quality aside.

Assuming you're getting the same product, a cap. To press a pill you're going to need a significant amount of binder. Several hundred percent more than the actual drug itself. With caps, people can remove the (almost) pure drug and take it however they please.


disclaimer sticker to the bottom to avoid legal problems

Good luck. If they're branded, they're going to test the pills. It's as simple as that.

Branded and disclaimed craft bags could also work, but could not be sold at parties.

If you're traveling with a bunch of pre-weighed bags they'll most certainly try to get you on the analog act. If that doesn't say intention to distribute, I don't know what does.

as he does not want to be the one person that causes 2c-I to become illegal

Rest assured, 2c-i is on the waiting list of chemicals to be scheduled. It's not an overnight process -- they've had their eyes on many RCs pretty much as long as there's been a market for them.
