Are you completely honest with your mate?
It doesn't seem like anyone is. Take the issue of looking at porn, for example. I know there was at least one thread on it a while back where the recommendation from just about every guy was to simply not tell your girlfriend about it. So most guys would rather keep something from their girlfriend that would bother her if she knew about it (even if she is being irrational) than to be honest and hope to find a girl that actually is rational and knows that he doesn't care about her less just because he looks at porn.

If you're just dating someone for sex, I can see how it would seem justified to lie. But if you actually care about the person you're with and you want a good relationship with them, how do you justify keeping secrets from them? Is it completely naive to think that someone could actually be in a healthy relationship with someone and be completely open with them? Really, could you call any relationship healthy if either person isn't being honest?

Re: Are you completely honest with your mate?
I think a certain level of openness is definitely encouraging in order to maintain a relationship healthy, but the truth is, to be completely open to another human being isn't exactly the easiest thing to do, even when backed by a sincere desire to do so.

It's impossible to tell your significant other every single thought that goes through your mind, or even every little thing about you. Even in serious relationships, a bit of secrecy is demanded--it's practical even.

For instance, I'm not one to care if my significant other watches porn or not. The honesty and sincerity in our relationship will grant that he tell me he watches it, but maybe not tell me that he sometimes thinks about those girls instead of me, see what I'm saying?

To a certain degree, complete, naked truth is sometimes imprudent and impractical to the relationship.
