DMT: I can't exactly write an entire paragraph about it. The sheer magnitude and totality of the experience can only be expressed as a combination of :O and "whoa" and "OMG!" and "...I wasn't ready for that..."

2c-i: This one would probably be the most wonderful one I've tried. Feeling a lot like mdma, but more trippy and less emotionally outpouring. Another plus would be the incredibly small dosage, how easily such a small dose can be hidden, and how easily it can be snorted. The negative would be the fact that tolerance builds up astronomically. There is a definate burn when snorted but, because of the incredibly small dose, it doesn't cause an immediate and intense burn... instead, it goes up like air then, very gradually, the burn sets in, possibly exaggerated by the psychedelic/sensory effects.

Butylone: Remarkable ADD medication; made me talkative, energetic, got my homework done, participated in class, learned, stayed awake, got good marks, made new friends.... good substitute/addition for my morning, noon, and afternoon expresso. Also made for an interesting synergy with methylone. Not very interesting in itself, but very useful and expensive.

Methylone: Quite possibly the most gentle, yet powerful, high I've experienced outside of mdma. Burns significantly more than 2c-i when snorted, however the burning subsides rather quickly. It didn't have much of a physical euphoria, hardly even noticable. It did, however, have a strong mental component. For the first time, since I took a ritalin pill, I've had a wonderful clarity of thought and razor sharp concentration... as well as lowered inhibitions. It didn't, however, reduce anxiety like mdma would. The physical component sets in during the comedown, manifesting itself as a physical discomfort. It was most useful when used in combination with LSA, not a strong dose of the methylone, as both have vasoconstricting effects, but rather enough to elevate the emotional rollercoaster from high/low to high/really-high... I'd say a nice 20-50mg is a nice top-off to HBWR... along with a beer to keep my hands from turning purple. Also, it made a good substitute for butylone.
Personal anecdotes:
I once snorted a bunch when I discovered that I had an oral exam in German class and I came completely unprepared. I randomly started talking about Genetic engineering, the philosophy behind it, and the politics... as well as I could. I felt a strong social anxiety since I was standing in front of 20 students, completely unprepared and intoxicated. I even started to sweat a little bit. After a riveting speech, I discovered that I scored the highest out of the class on the oral exam due to my fluency and the fact that it was actually interesting as opposed to the other students' topics. I did, however, get a poorer mark on my written because it turned out I had to hand in a hard copy of my speech... which I not only handed in late, but also worked on it the next morning.
Another time I had a gram and brought it to a party with me. No one else was a druggie so I measured out about 2 or 3 doses, assuming that it would be an "all night drinking" type party, and also brought a bottle of some weak wine. I was about 3/4 down the bottle of wine and then my memory fails. Turns out I finished the whole gram in about 6 hours, as well as a few beers, some big gulps out of the vodka, Jäggermeister, rum, whiskey, and champagne... waking up in a chair, holding a beer can... about half full.... tasting it, I found that it was a 50/50 mix of beer and whiskey.... only stories have been told about the wonders I achieved that night.

Mephedrone: Not as interesting as methylone. Definitely a strong physical euphoria, but the mental side would be equivalent to being on the 17th hour of an mdma binge... "e-tarded". I've never done so many random things in one weekend... most of which I can't remember clearly enough to say anything about this drug aside from the fact that I shared it with someone in return for some heroin, hash joints, beer, and vodka. Maybe some people enjoy this drug, I personally find the jaw-grinding, dry mouth, vasoconstriction to be a little extreme. Left me with a burning mouth the next morning.

MDPV: Never in my life had I been so concentrated... socially. It was not meant to make me do my homework. It was stimulating, yet un-motivating beyond whatever motivation I already felt while sober... which is barely enough. Never in my life have I entered stimulant-induced psychosis so soon as I did when I binged on this for 2 days straight. Never have I felt so sketchy without it... never has a stimulant lasted so long for me, or been as cheap. As for that 2 day binge... I spent 48 hours jacking off with no success aside from making my dick sore/numb and building up my left arm strength.... I don't think my liver enjoyed that one either, even though I abstained from alcohol the whole time. It also left me with an amazingly sore jaw and teeth. The back of my throat was burned to shit from the sheer amount of cigarettes I smoked, ulcerations, and even burns on my lips from smoking cigarettes WAY too fast. I was a whole week before I could eat anything spicy, salty, sour, or even above room temperature. Alcohol, specifically shots of vodka, created a wonderfully soothing experience for my mouth, mind, throat, heart, and soul.

uhhh.... I guess piperazines aren't too commonly known aside from ecstasy pill adulterants so, I'll include that....

BZP: Very clean high. (Snorting burns like a motherfucker!) Absolutely no anxiety. Complete awareness, no sketchyness, full concentration.... not the greatest fine coordination, though only noticeable in my penmanship, but pretty good gross motor control, mostly noticable in my Tae Kwon Do class. Not so much of an after-sketch as with ecstasy pills (the kind that are relatively more adulterated) however, definate insomnia, the sketch becoming apparent on attempts to sleep, that can't be cured without benzos. Dreaming, however, was definately effected. An almost hilariously clear, and artistic dream followed, intertwined with real-world sensory events such as my alarm clock and someone calling me early in the morning. Next day... no emotional hell... but rather, motivational hell. I couldn't motivate myself to do ANYTHING without just a little dose to get me up and going. Reminds me of my preconceptions about meth.

BZP/TFMPP mix: Definitely FEELS like an mdma experience, complete with eye dis-coordination and visual disturbance... definite alteration of emotional response to various stimuli... though, not as emotionally/socially outgoing as a typical MDMA high, as far as I could tell as I was in a German Tae Kwon Do class at the time.
The other time would be when I went to a German comedy ...thing. I've never been so amazed.
Negative: If you smoke too much weed and/or too many cigs on this shit, without at least moderately sipping beer the whole way though, then you get one HELL OF A MIGRANE.
Snorting burns like a mother fucker.
Other Positives: See above
Negatives: Insomnia. See above.

TFMPP alone: Definate visual changes, trippy/psychedelic body sensations reminiscent of LSA/LSD... slight headache... burns the sinuses like flaming hydrochloric acid followed by too much sodium hydroxide and non-water-based flame retardant. Sneezing, congesting, as if I were sick... for a whole 10 minutes.
Other positive effects: Look at all I've written! (aka, see ALL above) ...and I'm still editing. Funny thoughts come to mind, the kind of stuff I would have when I first started smoking weed... or with my LSA/methylone experience.

Short version: I tried a few things, they were mostly interesting, I write a lot while on drugs....
