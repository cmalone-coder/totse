For a better 'bomb' YOu must get a car battery, 2 jumper cables, and two copper wires or any THICK wire that will conduct electricity. Don't use insulated wire that is in plastic. YOu also need a plastic or glass bottle WITH A CAP.

Put the two wires that are conneceted to the jumper cable INSIDE THE water bottle, but not touching. It is important that the two wires are not touching, or else the wires will overheat and your water will evaporate, or the battery will explode.

Put on some insulated electricians gloves, or be prepared to die. NOw, clip jumper cable to the positive terminal on the battery. DOn't clip the wire on yet.Put a RUBBER balloon over the bottle mouth, and wrap an elastic band around it so it is air tight, or close to air tight. Now you may connect the other jumper cable to the negative terminal on the battery. The resulting gas that will bubble is highly explosive hydrogen. FIll bottles wiht it, tie the balloon with a long fuse, light and take cover, or do anything to it.
