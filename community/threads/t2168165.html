Re: Firecrackers made simple
Quote:
Originally Posted by PirateJoe
Is microwaving it really necessary? Can't you just eat some weed + fatty food?
People do use a microwave sometimes but I personally refuse to go that route. There's just something about nuking THC that just seems...not right. As far as just eating it, no, you do need to have heat in order to release the THC.

Quote:
Originally Posted by Crusty Conn
Will cooking them make much of a smell ?
That's one of the best things about Firecrackers. The smell never leaves the confines of the oven.

If you're worried about the smell getting out or being noticed at all there are thing's you can do. First, when removing them from the foil, just take them outside or in the garage or something. There really isn't much of a smell that accompanies them. Second, if you're worried about the 'rents smelling it in the oven just spray a little oven cleaner in it and kick on the auto clean, something that pretty much all ovens have. BUT, as already stated, none of this is really necessary unless you're just wanting to put your mind at ease.

When you get a chance, let me know how it went. I love firecrackers just because they are so easily made and work just as well as anything else. The only drawback is the taste. It's sometimes hard to stomach them just because of the gritty texture and the smell that comes with them.
