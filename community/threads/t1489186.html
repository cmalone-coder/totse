Re: Stretch marks
Reject any cream that claims to eliminate stretch marks no matter how convincing the advertising and "testimonials" are.

Cocoa butter as Mojo said, or if you don't want to smell like chocolate, use shea butter or mango butter. Get the pure stuff; creams and lotions don't have enough in them. These will condition your skin.

Nothing will eliminate stretch marks that are already there, but they will fade, and you can prevent more from forming if you keep your skin well-conditioned with the aforementioned plant butters. They help your skin retain the elasticity it needs to handle stressful situations such as rapid growth (or weight gain.)

Drinking plenty of water also helps maintain healthy skin.
