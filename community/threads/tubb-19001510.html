Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Re: Exploding ammo
One of these days I'm going to find someplace that sells potassium permanganate as septic tank cleaner.

I much prefer using cratermaker mortars. The concept is simple enough - A piece of very durable metal pipe is capped at one end and loaded with a teaspoon or so of black powder. Preferably this pipe will fit a cratermaker nicely. The cratermaker is then lit and dropped into the pipe, fuse end fist. Get your fingers out of the way.

The burning fuse on the cratermaker will ignite the black powder in the pipe, sending the thing rocketing out like a bat out of hell.

Rarely does the cratermaker even reach the ground before it explodes, but it's still fun.
