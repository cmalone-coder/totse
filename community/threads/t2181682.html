a few picks that should be good, and cheap for a first car if you care about driving are:
a golf gti, miata (excellent driving cars, but little room), honda civic, 1G eclipse, and the 1st gen focus is a pretty good car, in svt form. or actually if it can be found at a decent price, the SVT contour is a damn fine compact sedan.
