There's about a thousand threads around here that go in-depth into this kind of problem.. but the quick of it is, if you don't talk to her, you have no chance (kinda like "you miss 100% of the shots you don't take"). You may not be in a situation where doing that is too easy (ie. you're never around her); in that case, maybe you should try forgetting about her as much as possible.

Yes, I realize that's not an easy thing to contemplate, but if you two are never in the same place at the same time (besides passing each other in the hall at school), it would suggest you don't run in the same circles, do the same things, or share many interests. You have this insane tension built up about trying to hook up with this girl that you're head over heels in love with, and you don't want to fuck up, but what happens if you succeed? If you don't share any interests, you'll be together for a matter of days before it's over. You'll be even more miserable after that than you are now. So, first off, figure out what you two have in common.

After you've done that, if you find something that you both enjoy doing (maybe, I don't know, there's a rock-climbing gym she likes to go to, and you want to start- it could be *anything*), wait for a decent opportunity to converse with her. If you can begin one smoothly, terrific. If not, wait for something to happen that lends itself to conversation. Then talk. Flow with it- ask her about this, that, or whatever, show an interest. You don't have to force it on for as long as possible; once it seems like the conversation is fading, break it off, smile or whatever, and go on your merry way before it gets weird. It sounds like a lot to think about, but you'll do fine with it if you just pay some fucking attention to her and the feel of how things are going, and *don't* think too much.

First and foremost, though, if you two are really that far apart and she doesn't seem to know you from Joe Shithead, don't expect too much. That'll just give you false hope now and depress you later. If you can get to a point where you're having even semi-regular conversations with her, just as an acquaintance, you're doing better than probably 95% of the guys who have walked in similar shoes, so the success rate of getting the girl is pretty damn low. It doesn't mean you can't, just that you really have to have a lot of patience and let things happen as much as possible.. and, maybe most important of all, keep an open mind towards other girls. The biggest tragedy is if you miss out on a chance with someone who likes you that you might click really well with because you're still holding a candle for this chick that won't give you the time of day.
