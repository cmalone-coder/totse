Re: New to Marijuana
If you're going for a bong, be very careful at first. You might cough into the bong, getting the weed wet and ruining it. I didn't notice anyone else post this: break up the weed and get out all the seeds & the stems if you can. You'll see the difference in seeds, stems and bud. Bud is green and sort of crumbly, stem is kind of tan and will snap when bent, and seeds are small, hard, roundish and either white if they're premature or brown with black stripes if it's good. Your bud will be connected to the side of the stem, the seeds will be in the bud. This is why we break it up - seeds taste disgusting and don't get you high. Stems aren't as bad, but they're not pleasant and they don't contain much, if any, THC.

Also, have any of you ever smoked anything? Wrong technique is why a lot of people don't get high their first time. My method for teaching n00bs is to suck the smoke into your mouth (gonna be hard with a bong, I suggest making a pipe out of, say, a soda can) then inhale a big breath of air. You won't get a huge hit, but you don't need one yet and you'll get the smoke into your lungs, not just roling out of your mouth.

And if you see any of your friends bowing out smoke that just kind of rolls up into the air, they're not inhaling. Don't talk shit, none have done this, but try to help him out. If it gets into your lungs, first off, it'll burn a little in your chest, but you'll blow out kind of a stream of smoke that's more grey than blue.

And just take 2-3 hits at first, then give it 10-15 minutes. There's no reason to get super baked and start working on a heavy tolerance now.

If you want you can make a small piece with just a pen, aluminum foil and a piece of tape. It's my macgyver quick fix pipe.

Fold a square of aluminum foil, at least a double layer, with a side length of about 1/3 the length of the pen.

Wrap aluminum foil around the end of the pen tightly, holding its cylinder form. It should extend at least an inch out from the end of the pen.

Tape the foil cylinder to the pen with just one piece around it in a circle.

Then take a pencil and put it inside the foil cylinder but not yet inside the bic pen. Squish the aluminum foil around the form of the eraser leaving only a tiny hole at the end of your bowl.

Obviously try to avoid using the lighter as much as you can too because plastic is not a good thing to get melting. Keep an eye on that.

Don't use plastic for a bowl. If you heat it and it melts, it's terrible to inhale. If you're old enough, check out a headshop. They usually will have some kind of metal pipe for $3-5. Won't be anything too nice, but it will work. A one-hitter would be great. You can get glass ones or you can get little ones that look like cigarettes. The cigarette ones usually come with a stash box and can get expensive. If you plan on smoking for a while, go ahead and get a glass pipe. They're the best kind of pipe you can get. Make sure not to mention anything about smoking pot in the headshop, too. Most of them will throw you out immediately if you do.

If you're really hard up and can't go to a headshop, I would reccomend making an all-aluminum foil pipe. Get a thick pen or a permanant marker and wrap a bunch of layers of foil around it fairly tightly, making a cylinder. Pull the pen or marker out and take one end and tilt it up just a little, maybe an inch from the end. You may have to play with it to get the hole the right size. Just think about how big your chunks of pot will be and make it a little smaller than that. You can crimp it or twist it or whatever you need to do to get the right size hole. Just take a hit through it with nothing in it first to make sure that air can come through. If so, put your pot in the bent-upwards end and light it. You don't need to keep the lighter on it the whole time. Just let it burn on its own. You'll also have to take breaks with this pipe, because it will get hot quickly. It'll be good for you to judge how high you are and if you need more, though.

Re: New to Marijuana
Don't use plastic for a bowl. If you heat it and it melts, it's terrible to inhale. If you're old enough, check out a headshop. They usually will have some kind of metal pipe for $3-5. Won't be anything too nice, but it will work. A one-hitter would be great. You can get glass ones or you can get little ones that look like cigarettes. The cigarette ones usually come with a stash box and can get expensive. If you plan on smoking for a while, go ahead and get a glass pipe. They're the best kind of pipe you can get. If you end up not liking pot, chances are one of your friends will keep smoking and you could sell the pipe to him for a few dollars less than what you paid. Make sure not to mention anything about smoking pot in the headshop, too. Most of them will throw you out immediately if you do.

If you're really hard up and can't go to a headshop, I would reccomend making an all-aluminum foil pipe. Get a thick pen or a permanant marker and wrap a bunch of layers of foil around it fairly tightly, making a cylinder. Pull the pen or marker out and take one end and tilt it up just a little, maybe an inch from the end. You may have to play with it to get the hole the right size. Just think about how big your chunks of pot will be and make it a little smaller than that. You can crimp it or twist it or whatever you need to do to get the right size hole. Just take a hit through it with nothing in it first to make sure that air can come through. If so, put your pot in the bent-upwards end and light it. You don't need to keep the lighter on it the whole time. Just let it burn on its own. You'll also have to take breaks with this pipe, because it will get hot quickly. It'll be good for you to judge how high you are and if you need more, though.
