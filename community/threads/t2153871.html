1kg = 1,000g

1000g = 1,000,000mg (That's ONE MILLION MILLIGRAMS)

1,000,000mg = 16,666, 60mg Recreational doses of Valium (Diazepam) per kilo.

Therefore, if one were to have 65kg, multiply the above numbers by 65 and VOILA! There's your answer (Sorry, you can do some work).

But if it is definitely pure Valium (Diazepam) powder, and in a very large quantity, please have a VERY, VERY, VERY ACCURATE SCALE to weight out 60mg doses.

Take no more than 60mg at once if you have some experience with benzos.

However, if you have no experience with any sort of benzo at all, then maybe start at a 40mg dose and take 10mg - 20mg every 1-2 hours until you're where you wanna be/feel.

As said, with that amount of powder, YOU MUST BE VERY, VERY CAREFUL and be VERY, VERY ACCURATE with a VERY, VERY ACCURATE SCALE!! If not, you are risking overdose very easily! 40mg-60mg of pure Valium (Diazepam) powder, is an extremely small amount that you would need an extremely accurate scale in order to measure the dose [of 40-60mg doses, as well as the 10-20mg re-doses, whether you're capping to sell, capping to use, just using yourself, or just selling as powder] correctly, without overdosing, which at that point is a very great reality!

Please be careful and be safe!
