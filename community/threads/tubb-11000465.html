Re: Help me! Can't post to TOTSE
Heres the thing.

There's no possible way for you to be unable to reply with the symptoms you describe.

all your "user area", ie member file contains is the following info.

Your username
your password
your email
number of posts
date registered

and then it contains things like your preferences for whether admins can email you, and anything you put in your profile.

That's IT.

Now, all that information is still there, so the member file isn't corrupted (happens, but only if the server starts running out of disk space).

So what does that leave? The only thing unique to you is obviously fine, as far as what resides on the server is concerned.

I suppose it's vaguely possible that enigma added a routine somewhere that would prevent you and specifically you from replying, but since that's most likely not the case, the simple answer is that it's something YOU'RE doing, either intentionally, unintentionally, whatever.

First of all, sounds stupid, but are you hitting refresh after you post and you say you can't see your reply?.. I've had people complain about that before, not realising that they're simply seeing the cached page and not the new one. This would also explain why you see your new topics, since that's a totally new url, and thus can't be cached.

Honestly, that's the only answer I can think of.. I've spent a lot of time playing around with UBB's, digging around in the code, running them, having them glitch on me, and I honestly cannot think of any other reason you would not be able to see your own replies.. the only other vague possibility I can think of is that you have some problem with your browser not processing the client server relationship in the right way, but that's extremely far fetched.
