Advice for First Time Tripping
This is for anyone who is [someday] contemplating trying a hallucinogen for the first time [i]or for someone who will be guiding person or persons on their first trip.[/i]

Out of all of my friends I have, by far and away, the most experience with hallucinogens. Somewhere along the way it became sort of a given that when one of them got a new girl friend, and if she had never dosed, they would bring her over to old Meta's to guide her through her first trip.

[I know this is going to sound sexist; I don't mean it to be: Out of all of the people that I've been with during their first trip, only a few of the males had any trouble. But the females - to a one -had at least a small amount of trouble such that it required someone [me, usually] to talk them down. Again, I know I'm going to catch hell for this and I don't mean to imply that all females have/had/or will have trouble on their first trip [keep in mind, too, that the last one I helped was over 10 years ago]. I am only reporting what I experienced and while the numbers involved do not make up a good enough statistical base, it is still a telling trend... For some

Mind you, at first I wasn't too sure about it. Sure: I had help some people though some bad trips, but I eventually got comfortable in it and had it down to a fine art.

Anyway, I got to thinking about it recently, to see if there was anything I could pass down - that wouldn't take up volumes for me to describe. There are a couple, one more important and the other a bit trivial.

I'll start with the trivial first. And I'll put it this way: Mirrors are not always the best thing to have around people who are tripping.

Even for seasoned 'travelers' you need to be pretty damn comfortable with the way you look if you are going to spend more than a passing glance at a mirror.

And if it's you first trip and if you have any reservations about the way you look, I advise stay away from mirrors: They will not only distort your face the way everything gets distorted, but any flaws that you think you have become vastly exaggerated and can be kinda disturbing.

I know many of you think I'm joking or have finally lost one brain cell too many.

But there will certainly be those out there that know what I'm talking about.

But like I said; that is kinda trivial: it's only a matter of getting someone away from a mirror and getting them to attach their focus on something else.

This next one address something a little harder to deal with.

One thing that I've noticed that most people [male and female] who carpet dive [Carpet dive: A term to describe someone who is having a bad trip. It comes from a friend from years ago. He had a bad trip and in an attempt to get away from whatever it was that was 'chasing' him, he tried to 'crawl' into the carpet. I said it looked like he was trying to 'dive� into the carpet and the term has stuck since] have [at least] one thing in common.

And that is that they start to think that they are the only one who is tripping; that everyone else is sober and playing a big trick on them. They become real paranoid, then, that the way they are feeling at the moment will never go away: that they will be as they are forever.

This, if left unchecked, soon spirals down further and further.

Trying to talk to them, to convince them that others are tripping just as hard, that it's not a joke and [most important] that they will be straight by morning is not easy to do: Getting past that paranoia that everyone is in on the joke - including you [no matter how trusted a friend you may be] is not easy and takes a lot of time and effort and persistence.

The first thing I came up with, to help me in that task, was to get a book that describes what to expect on a trip [the one I used was called - I think [it's been many years after all ;-) ] was "The Encyclopedia of Recreational Drugs" [It may have been subtitled: "Everything you ever wanted to know about anything ever taken for recreational purposes"]. It sure covered a lot of stuff and was practically my 'bible' during my years of experimentation [I don't have it anymore, though I wish I did: I think some of the information wasn't quite right. Though it was the best that was available at the time and the LSD and other hallucinogen sections were spot on].

When I was first asked to guide someone new, I loaned them the book and had them read about it. [Assuming they still wanted to go ahead with things] Before the trip started I had them read it again [most times, after they took the hit and were waiting for it to come on]. Particularly some parts that I had highlighted. One of which address the fact that many people felt like a joke was played on them; another about people thinking they would be frying forever [I just loved that book].

Then, once the trip got going, and the [almost] inevitable carpet dive began to commence, I got the book out again and had them concentrate on trying to read it [failing that I had them follow along as I read it]. I would try to get them to remember reading it before they tripped and thus [hopefully] assure them.

That usually worked somewhat, but many times the person would think I had just brought the book as a joke and that it wasn't quite what they had read before the trip started.

I finally came upon something that worked 99% of the time.

After reading the book again before the trip started I had them take a pen and some paper and write what to expect mainly that they would come down again and that they were likely to think a joke was being played on them.

Then when carpet dive started, I would make them read what they wrote.

Almost all would recognize their own writing and that would be 80% of the battle.


I know this was long, as per my usual, but I think this could really help someone or someone�s sometime.

Tripping is something that I think everyone should experience at least once in life .It only takes once for your outlook on life and the way you see everything to change forever - for the better.

I also think that it shouldn't be tried until after high school - when the body and mind are pretty much done growing: hallucinogens are some pretty potent chemicals and can interfere with the growth process.

Afterthought: A good book to read on acid [at least until you can't read anymore ]is "The Tao of Pooh".
