I'm becoming discouraged...
Hey guys, I don't really come here too often anymore but this used to be the place I hung out. I remember Captain Asshat and Barabapa's great tales of adventure and I looked up to them (Sybil too, I s'pose). Traveling is my greatest ambition; there are a lot of things I want to see and experience, so this problem is really weighing on my mind.

Lately though, I'm becoming withdrawn from my goals. I just recently got two tickets: one for a seat belt violation (100) and now just yesterday I got busted at a Bob Dylan concert. Bob was incredible, and his band was on fire; I couldn't help but get caught in the emotions of the event, but eventually security busted me and my girlfriend and now that's another 300 dollars I have to work off plus a court date. I also have another 600 dollars due in past utilities.

When I drove home that night, Dylan played his songs again for me via my ipod. I don't think I've ever felt so defeated. But, beyond missing my hero's show, I contemplated my traveling predicament. With all these bills to pay off, and I'm sure more are coming, I'm terribly confused how I will ever pull off the global trekking that I've been dreaming of since high school. How do I see the world on the budget of a young guy who's being thrown around the court systems. How do you guys experience the great things in the world without becoming stranded, broke, lost.

All I want to do is find a way to a far-off place and immerse myself, but I'm worried it's not going to happen for a long time, and by then I have no idea what kind of person I will be. So, please, if anyone has felt the way I do, been in my situation, or has some words that could help me I would really appreciate it.

I can take care of myself out there. I just need to know I'm not the only one who feels stuck. Thanks.

Re: I'm becoming discouraged...
How do you guys experience the great things in the world without becoming stranded, broke, lost.

We do. That's half the fun. I haven't traveled much at all, I've just started. But on my last trip at one point I was stuck in Queenstown with no ride, so I hitched the 500km. At one point I ran out of money so I decided to use my credit card, turned out it had a pin code for over seas purchases. So I had $40, no plane ticket home, and no credit card. I rang my bank and they said my pin will arrive in 7 business days. $40 to last 9 days. It was fucking fun man. I ended up staying with 5 Irish girls and sleeping on their floor/couch. I'd pay my way by chopping their firewood and doing chores. Every day we'd just drink wine around a fireplace till we were drunk then go to the local bar. Oh and I got 4 fines from the Department of Conservation with in 5 minutes, fishing with no license, outside of fishing season, using live bait and having a fire going in a national forest. He confiscated my fishing gear and gave me a very hefty fine. Second he left, I tore up the fine and grabbed another rod out of the car :)

Point is man....being broke, stranded, lost and in trouble is half the fun. Find jobs where ever you are, work hard, pick vegetables, milk cows, work dirty jobs for minimum wage, because at the end of the day you still get to see the world.

Anything you can recover from is a good thing. It teaches you a lesson and will make a good story to your kids one day. Suck it up and get out there!
