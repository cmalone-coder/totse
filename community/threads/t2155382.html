http://www.na.org/

Go there and find a meeting. I've never been addicted to ope's although I was once a very heavy meth user for about 4 years and I ended up going to rehab and NA for a year. Rehab was gay but it got me through the beginning part, and once I found an NA meeting that was right for me NA was actually pretty cool. No matter what method you want to try to kick your habit, it's not all up to the method. The majority of it is going to have to come from YOU, and the programs will guide you along and make it a little bit easier on you. That is, if you do it right. If you're going in sour and with a closed mind all pissed off just tapping your feet and staring at the clock waiting to get out, you're just wasting your time.

You have to LISTEN and have an OPEN MIND. Remember when you first got curious about drugs and the drug culture and even though your parents and teachers and other important people that have authority and/or love you all told you how awful drugs are, but you didn't listen, read up a little bit, talked to some people, and found out that while drugs may not be the safest, most healthy, the best route to take, and come with many serious risks that it is still very possible to live a productive and happy life as a user as long as you take the proper precautions? You had an open mind. You didn't listen to the hearsay, the rumor mill, the peanut factory, or the propaganda. You wanted to think for yourself and find out the truth and take some risks and see if drug use and drug culture was something that you would enjoy being a part of. You kept your mind wide open to all the possibilities, perceptions, beliefs, stand points, and so on.

Now you have to re-direct that mindset towards getting off habitual drug abuse, rather than towards recreational drug use. It's not THAT different, is it? Just go to some meetings. You may not like the first one, or first few, that you find and attend. But trust me, every meeting is different. There's one for every type of person. Find the one that's right for you, park your butt towards the front, shut up, and fucking LISTEN. Then what you need to do is talk to the other people there before and/or after the meeting or during the break if that particular meeting has breaks. You need to find a sponsor. If you're afraid to walk up to people, tell one of the meeting leader dudes up front that you're looking for a sponsor and maybe towards the end of the meeting they'll ask if anyone is taking sponsees. You'll be surprised at the hands raised, I'm sure. Then talk to them, get some phone numbers, and choose one to be your sponsor. He will then guide you from there. And as long as you truly want to quit and put in the effort, you'll get off the drugs and be on your way to a better life.
