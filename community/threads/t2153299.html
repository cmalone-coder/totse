Well if by ''mescaline'' you are referring to 3,4,5-trimethoxyphenethylamine you have yourself what's known as a psychedelic alkaloid of the phenethylamine class. In lamens terms it's a recreational hallucinogen. It is a derivative of the peyote cactus if I'm not mistaken, but that doesn't really matter.

Average dose : I'd say 200 mg, start lightly as it can be rather "surprisingly intense".
You can take more and still be safe, I wouldn't go higher than 500 being a noob to the stuff.

I find with mescaline as opposed to LSD the intensity of what already IS there is amplified, unlike with LSD where colors and psychedelic images are often seen when there is nothing even there. That is in MY opinion and is not neccessarily a fact for everyone.

what else..
Oh, it also affects your peripheral nervous system.

Just be safe bro! stay chilled out and if you start panicking just remind yourself you took a drug and everything will be cool. You don't have to worry about OD'ing when it comes to Mescaline, but you can have a bad trip. Try to stay one all by your lonely, or with a small group of trusted friends.

and lastly.. Have fun!;)
