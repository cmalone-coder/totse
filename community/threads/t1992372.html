Loneliness... It stings so
I feel like im unworthy of love at times. Totse is the only place i can talk about it because i'm fairly confident i'll never meet any of you. I spend a lot of time looking at social situations with a bleak outlook and come away from situations feeling like my interactions with others have little value, a waste of calories.

I just finished a long text conversation with a girl that i like, apparently she's going through some rough times. Her sister has cancer, and she can't talk to any of her close friends anymore, so she comes to me, the guy who accidentally slipped into the friend zone. I had a mad crush on this girl, and the conversation reeks of wasted thought... I have no problems consoling her, but i'm always on the other side of the fence. I want to be the guy that she wants to not only console her, but to kiss her and hold her too. However, I'm pretty confident she has no romantic interest in me.

After the convo died, i feel nothing but resentment and bitterness towards her. She only thinks of herself.. if she read between the lines, it would be easy to see that i like her, but all that she cares about is getting attention for her problems. But i supressed the feeling, and i let her know that i would always be there for her.

I view things like this all the time- I can usually find the bitter and sad line of reasoning in any situation in no time flat. Short of the random girl that briefly floats into my life from time to time, I have had no romantic attachments for quite sometime... Actually, i have never in my life had any kind of relationship that is anything close to love. (im 18 by the way)

I blame my self projected image, and my recent degradation into my world of emotional despair.

What do you think guys? Too much angst probably... I still feel better for telling somebody about my problems though. Thanks for reading if you made it this far.
