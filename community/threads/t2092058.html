I "justify" it because I know for a fact that the tar I get isn't as shitty as most that goes around, and due to the fact that I am meticulous about filtering as well as possible.

The key is to heat your tar. Believe it or not, some people actually don't do this. And no, heating it does NOT kill bacteria, despite popular belief. If you heat your dope long enough to kill bacteria then you've basically heated off most if not all of your dope too. The point behind heating tar is that the heat clumps alot of the cut together making it much easier to filter out.

If you're all about smoking then more power to you, I could never get my technique down right enough :(

The rush from shooting tar is slightly different than that of east coast powdered dope though, anyone whose done both will attest to that. Some prefer (high purity) IV tar over powder for this reason. Tar contains more active alkaloids along with diacetylmorphine, including 3-mam and 6-mam as well as acetylcodeine. Thus, the overall high from black tar usually lasts longer than that of regular east coast heroin, though the rush from straight heroin is considered to be stronger and more euphoric.
