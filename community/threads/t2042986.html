'Game threads' in SG.
According to the forum descriptions, game threads belong in HB instead of SG. That's fair enough in principle, but a lot of the game threads people in SG make that should be locked according to the rules do fit the nature of the forum and the mindset of the posters, and they don't fit the nature of HB at all.

This was the thread that prompted me to make this, but it's not the first time I've thought about it. (http://www.totse.com/community/showthread.php?t=2042974) It's not a criticism of the modding, it's about the rules for the forum themselves. SG's always had threads like these, they might not bring about discussion per se but they do bring about participation and interaction, and are entertaining for the people who post in them.

For the same reasons, I have a bit of a problem with one of the sections of XiPPiLLi's stickied 'rules' thread in the forum too, the one that reads;

" ---Threads with no valid initial content for discussion.
-"I am typing in this thread. You should try it!"
-"Look outside! Do you see what I see?!"
-"When this page gets to (X) pages, I will (Y)""

It's on the exact same grounds as with the game thread thing; participation, interaction and entertainment should be enough to let a thread stay open in my opinion, as long as it also involves some creativity and isn't something like "everybody quote this quote and make a big nested quote", which really does contribute nothing.

Basically, I think 'game threads' and the like should be allowed, since they fit the spirit of the forum, and the rules relaxed a little.

I'd particularly like to know what the other SG mods think about this.

Yeah, the forum descriptions would definitely need tweaking if this was to be changed; it's them and the rules that I see as the problem. Like I said earlier, the "making game threads" part of the HB forum description wasn't meant to imply that HB is the only place for game threads, even if it had that effect.

'Game threads' aren't just part of SG, they're part of pretty much every general discussion board on internet forums. They don't fit the spirit of HB at all (and telling users to go post them there doesn't change that; they just get hijacked or sink), and they do SG. IMO participation in a game thread would fall under the umbrella of conversation anyway, just at the looser end of the scale. If it was agreed on to change this, it'd be a simple matter of removing the 'making game threads' section of the HB forum description and replacing it with something else ('grand plans' was the first thing that popped into my head because it kinda rhymes with 'handstands', but I'll ask Euda and MS about it) and putting 'some' in before the word 'conversation' in the SG description, which would allow things like game threads to be posted while still allowing us to maintain standards and remove threads that actually lack content (which, along with the different userbases, would still keep SG and HB pretty separate).

I do think disallowing these things kills the spirit of SG a bit. I've had a few emails from regulars asking about it, and there was a big complaint thread about game threads being closed a little while back. Obviously it's ultimately down to the mods' discretion as to what belongs in the forum and what doesn't, but when longer-term regulars are unhappy with it (not just kidiots whining and jumping on a bandwagon when a few threads get rightly locked) it seems like something's wrong to me; they're the ones who made the forum what it is and should be allowed greater input on what should be allowed.

Again, this isn't anything negative about your modding, Meta, nor was it just aimed at you. You're quite right in saying you think you've mellowed out quite a bit, it's just the game threads rule I disagree with. Nothing wrong with following rules when it's what our position entails, I just have a problem with those rules.
