Re: What do dreams mean to you?
the brain picks up millions of different pulses from each of the 5 senses every minute. of course, to keep your head from exploding, your brain also filters out all but what is *most* important to you (like what youre seeing, for example). now, when you fall asleep, your subconscious mind reassembles what you picked up that day, but "filtered out". when you goto sleep, you no longer have inputs from sight, smell, taste, etc. so your mind pulls up some random stuff it picked up and "loads" it into a dream. thats why you can taste, smell, feel, etc. while youre dreaming.

in short, your dreams reflect inner thoughts, inner desires, and what-not. so, basically, when youre dreaming about fooling around with a girl, then it HAPPENS... you dreamt it because your brain picked up on it during the day, it just filtered it out from your normal thoughts.

hope that clears it up...
