The Annunaki and Nibiru (Part 1)
Now, I'd say about 99% of all the world rejects the idea of Extraterrestrials visiting earth and involing themselves with early human culture?

Why is this? Probably the stigma of being labeled a cook, but, crazy, pseudo-scientist...you know the terms.

Yet when you actually look at facts you begin to wodner why science has failed to pick up on this sound theory. They don't want to, I believe. I also believe they havn't even tried.

I mean, if they did, they would see many sound ideas and clues come forth that rival (and sometimes even surpass) current ones logicaly, and historicaly.

I'm sure many of you here at Totse are familier with Sitchen's idea of the Annunaki (You're such an intellegent bunch, no sarcasm.) I agree with the idea, though not many of his interpertaions of that idea.

I've never read any of his books. In fact I havn't read a single book on the subject (I've rad a total opf about 4 UFO books ever.), but thanks to the wonderous tool of the Internet, I don't need to.

These Annunaki, which is a Sumerian word for 'Gods of the Earth' are thought to be (by Sitchen and numerous others who's thoughts all differ slightly.) Extraterrestrial beings from the 12th(10th) Planet (Planet X, Nibiru).

This Planet is said to have a 3600 (3657) year orbit around our sun (and possibly a twin sun, which is now a black dwarf). Itself is hypothesised to be a Brown Dwarf (failed star...warm but not nuclear), and this brown dwarf passes through our Solar System every 3600 years, as far as I know, about where the Asteroid belt is located, or near there.

It's said that Many thousands of years ago, the Solar System was made up as follows-

Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth(Moon), Mars, Tiamat(*), Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Nibiru(*), and possibly Dark Sun.

This is where the term "12th Planet" comes from. Counting either the Sun or Moon as well.

As said, thousands of years ago, Nibiru crossed into the path of Tiamat and collided with it (or perhaps one of Nibiru's large moons or Asteroids, which collect to it each time it passes through the Oort Cloud). The resulting destruction tore Tiamat apart, creating the modern day Asteroid Belt.

Now I'm sure you all are thinking "But beings can't live on a star, or a planet that goes 3600 years away from the sun!".

Well I'll tell you. I view this is a major problem in the theory if not explained in some rather surprising (or maybe unbelievable) ways.

Here's Theory #1)
-Nibiru is a Brown Dwarf. It's surface is mainly water, yet it has scattered land masses. The Planet self heats itself, and it has an atmosphere that keeps the heat for the most part in, and thus, a relativly stable enviroment is created over unchanging times.

Now Theory #2)

-Nibiru's occupents are in 4th Density. It's kind of hard to explain what density is, but I'll try.
Think of it as the next level of energy in the universe. As scientists have become aware of, matter is energy at a certain speed. Think of the next density as a faster(or maybe slower) form of the energy, where what we percieve as light and other invisible forces, is actually matter in a different density.

My beliefs (and that of others), is that when you die, your soul can reincarnate. Each time your soul lives through a life and gets more knowledge, it gets larger and heavier. Eventually, when everything there is to know (or is relevent to know) is learned, the soul moves up a density. (Usually corrisponded with the urge to better the whole, and empathy).

I hope this makes it a bit more clear.
