Ground beef has alot of nasty parts in it, all perfectly safe. They take meat that isn't exactly "pretty" and grind it up. Blood meal brings in quite a bit of money for most slaughter houses and sometimes makes more of a profit than ground beef does, so the nasty entrails and brains are going to go to the right place.

The patties come preformed and are shipped to the restaraunt frozen. I've never worked there, but I've seen them pull the patties out of a box and throw them on a grill. So how exactly does anyone at the restaraunt have anything to do with what goes into the patties?

The patties come from slaughter houses and packaging plants around the country. IBP and EXcel are ones that are pretty big around here. I've known people that have worked in both(IBP in amarillo tx and excel in friona tx) and they said there is a seperate production line for most fast food companies. As part of the contracts, the fast food meat goes through more inspections with tighter criteria. Other than having a few quality control people from the coporation there, mcdonalds(bk, wendys or whatever) has no significant part in the production. Once the contract is signed they just sit back and watch.

Cows do get exposed to wuite a bit of antibiotics, but not nearly as much as humans give to themsleves. Most farmers can't afford to randomly inject their cows with anything.
