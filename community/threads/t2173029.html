Brabus B10 for E430
I've recently been inspired by a friend at my school who owns a Mercedes C240.

Brabus B9, lightening, (And not just shitty lightening, it's still balanced towards the middle), tuned, and chip'd.

It's inspired me to do the same to my car, my E430. Granted it's older, has more miles, and is much heavier. I have been working on this for quite a while, and have started saving my money to make something phenomenal. I've thought about replacing all the gaskets, get some more life out of the engine.

Brabus B10 kit, True re-done dual exhaust, and that's really about all of my rational ideas.

For my one irrational idea. A low-boost turbo. I know it seems insane due to the nature of the car, but hell, why not?
