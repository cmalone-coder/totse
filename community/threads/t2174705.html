Simple pasta dish I've been making the past couple of days:
- Fettuccine
- Can of italian tomatos
- 1/2 teaspoon of mixed herbs (thyme, rosemary, sage, majoram and savory are in the bottle I use)
- Garlic powder (depends on how garlic-y you want it)

Put the tomatos into a saucepan with the mixed herbs and garlic powder and cook until all (or most of) the juice is gone. Cook the fettuccine however you normally do it and then when it's done and strained, put it on a plate and top with the tomatos. Put some grated parmesan cheese over the top if ya want.

It's easy to make, most of the time is spent trying to reduce the fucking juice from it. Doesn't taste too bad either, not the nicest, but edible.

Synkk's Chicken de la Fond :)

Mince some onions (any type) and garlic and sautee in olive oil. Once browned, add some chicken breast fillets that have been dusted with any generic chicken seasoning. Fully cook the chicken. Once you take it out of the pan, add butter and worcestershire (or any similar sauce) sauce to the hot skillet (turn your heat on a low medium) and add some flour or corn starch to thicken your fond. (Note: you can add more butter and flour if you think you need to to change the texture and/or thickness) Allow the chicken breasts to rest for about 2 minutes in the microwave, then cut them into strips. Lay the strips on a bed of wild rice (or plain white instant rice is fine too) If the chicken's cooled beyond your liking microwave it for 30 secs or so then spoon on the fond.

I was going to post this in a thread, but it fits here. :)

Chicken breast fillets
Onions
Garlic
Olive oil
Chicken seasoning
Butter
Your favorite rice

Also: What is a fond? (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fond)

Mushroom, steak and potato sandwich.

Get some big portobello mushroms, your favourite cut of budget/average priced meat (I used cheap sirloin for this), some Kumaras (any sweet potato will do) and some good bread.

Get a frying pan real hot, put olive oil in it, coat a steak with steak spice and minced garlic and cook till desired level. Just before taking out put a dollop of butter in the frying pan, let the steak coat in it, add a bit more garlic to the pan and cook the mushrooms in the butter.

Boil the sweet potatoes, mash, add milk and butter, then mash till a smooth and creamy consistency.

Put your steak, mushroom and sweet potato between two peaces of good bread. Enjoy.

DarkSkye's turkey burgers


1 package of 97% fat free ground turkey

4 buns

1 white onion

1 package of baby bella mushrooms (sliced)

1 jar of minced garlic

soy sauce

powdered sugar

flour

butter

1 slice of white bread (crumbs)

grated parmesan

4 slices aged swiss

turkey bacon

ice

ranch dressing (optional)

take the turkey out of the package and start kneading it. add about a half cup of parmesan. Somehow (I use a magic bullet) get your bread into small crumbs. add them as well.

Take your baby bellas, and sautee them in a garlic/soy/powdered sugar sauce on med/low heat. play around with the sauce until the flavor is right.

preheat your oven to broil.

slice your onion. be sure that each of the 4 burgers has at least two decent sized rings. Pat out the burgers, and form them around the onion rings, being sure the middle of each patty is indented so the burgers dont ball up.

Heat your frying pan with either butter or oil (or both). add a small amount of soy sauce for color. Heat it on med/high heat. Add your burgers, flipping occasionally. When mostly cooked, take an ice cube and throw it in the pan. move your pan around to make sure the irritated oil covers the patties.

Once the burgers are cooked, stick them (pan and all) into the oven. take your buns and butter them, and put them on a cookie sheet along with your turkey bacon. After 2-3 minutes take out the burgers and put in the buns//bacon. Take out the buns when they are browned, and the bacon when it is crispy.

assemble, and add condiments. dip in ranch dressing if you so choose. Enjoy!
