I'm not sure what you mean by fastest, there are much faster characters than the shotos.

To a scrub, the three will feel pretty much the same.

Ken is in the top three, with Yun and Chun. He's easy to use, has a great super, very versatile, and has no major weaknesses. He doesn't dominate but he has good matchups against most of the characters. He's popular because he's so easy to learn and be good with.

Akuma is actually becoming very good. There are a ton of Jap and American pro players who are beasting with him. Akuma is faster than Ken and has excellent rushdown. Played properly, his low defence isn't much of an issue. I would rate him at least mid tier, up from low tier. I'm tempted to put him at mid-high.

Ryu is probably the worst of the three right now, at least in professional tiers. He's a low-mid at best. That said, he's still my favourite scrub-smasher . Seeing them go 'wtf??' after a Denjin combo is always fun.

Aside from the other 2 top tiers, a good Makoto or Urien will beat the shotos. Yang and Dudley have about a 50/50.
