Re: I have no interest to eat unless im high =/
This is a very common problem with Marijuana. I went through a phase where I developed severe nausea if I didn't toke up in a 48 hour period but there are ways around it.

For a medicated approach some people find that small doses of benzos cause slight hunger. Another (less ideal) choice is small doses of Seroquel. I really wouldn't recommend this because Seroquel isn't the type of drug you want to take if you absolutely don't need it.

For a more natural approach just "force" yourself to eat at least 3 very small meals each day. Small being a sausage biscuit for breakfast, a sandwich or a half sandwich and some chips for lunch and whatever for dinner. Continue doing this until it becomes a routine. After around a week it will begin to come naturally and the problem should resolve itself.

Also, when you are toking don't gorge yourself because of the munchies. Just as stated above try to keep your snacking very light. I know this can be very difficult but all it really takes is a little self control. Eating more healthy foods instead of shit like chips doesn't hurt either.
