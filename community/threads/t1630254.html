<LI>What is it?

Salvia Divinorum is a plant grown in Mexico and South America. It is popular on the drug scene for its varying halucigenic effects. It is not a marijuana replacement , it is not anything like any drugs you have tried before. Salvia has been used for years as a religious aid to natives of Mexico and South America. It is a very spiritual plant not to be taken lightly. It can also be great fun.

<LI>Is it Legal?

Salvia is legal in all countries and states except Australia and Israel.

<LI>Where can I get it?

There are many ways to get it, online and off. Online it is available from many vendors, I shall list a few here; www.IamShaman.com (http://www.IamShaman.com) is the place I buy mine. They have decent prices and great customer service. They also accept money orders and checks sent by mail. www.ShamanShop.net (http://www.ShamanShop.net) is another good place. Their prices are not as good as IAS but the quality is unsurpassed in my experience. www.BouncingBearBotanicals.com (http://www.BouncingBearBotanicals.com) is popular, although I have never ordered from them myself. Their prices are on par with IamShaman. www.Ebay.com (http://www.Ebay.com) has Salvia available from many users, but caution is advised, there are many scams on eBay.

Offline, you can go to many head shops to find it. For the uninformed, a head shop is another name for a smoke shop, but you already knew that.

<LI>Can I grow it?

Salvia almost never produces seeds (really, never), anyone claiming to have Salvia Divinorum seeds should be suspect. I have never seen Salvia Divinorum seeds available from any reputable sellers. That being said, it is quite easy to grow if you live in a warm climate. Many nurseries can special order it from greenhouses for you, so check a local nursery first. www.IamShaman.com (http://www.IamShaman.com) and www.BouncingBearBotanicals.com (http://www.BouncingBearBotanicals.com) both sell live Salvia Divinorum plants, but it is advisable to shell out the cash for next day express shipping.

Salvia loves warm temperatures and lots of humidity. Water your plants every two to three days. If you live in a cooler climate, place your potted Salvia plant into a clear plastic bag. Insert a few wooden dowels into the bag to keep the plastic off the leaves. Pour about a cup of water into the bottom of bag and use a wire twist tie to seal the top. Open the bag and replenish the water once a week.

You can harvest from your plant as soon as it is growing healthily. Never pick all of the leaves, take 2/3 at most never more. Always pick leaves from the top down, and cut the stem off where you stopped picking leaves. Place a dab of Elmers glue on the stem where you cut it. Doing this will yield a bushy plant over time instead of a tall skinny one that could break in a good wind.

<LI>What does it do?

Salvia is a powerful halucigen it can completely remove all of reality and replace it with something else. Open eye visuals are common, auditory hallucinations also take place on occasion. At lower doses Salvia is a euphoric; it produces a deep contentment similar to early stages of drunkenness, warm and fuzzy It is best to consume in a quiet room with soft lights and soft music.

At levels where it replace reality with Salvia space you will probably feel an unknown entity or entities telling you what to do. Fear not, this entity is Lady Salvia, the spirit of the drug, the lesser entities are called smelves, they serve her. You may actually see the smelves, mine are like small blue elves with wings, but a visual sighting of Lady Salvia is very rare if you do see her, count yourself extremely lucky.

<LI>How much should I take?

There is no one answer for this, some people seem to be immune to Salvias effects, I have smoked with some people who needed so much to catch a buzz it would have perma-fried me. On average two hits of a 10X extract is usually more than sufficient.

<LI>How should I take it?

Ah, the question of the ages. Some people swear you need a bong, myself, I just use a regular glass pipe. A bong is nice because it cools off the smoke, and the smoke is less harsh. Pipes have the advantage that the distance from bowl to lungs is much shorter and therefore more effective than a bong, if you dont mind the smoke. Whether you decide to use a pipe or bong, one thing is sure, A TORCH LIGHTER IS A MUST Salvia works at a much higher temperature than most other drugs.

<LI>What about extracts?

In my humble opinion extracts are the best way to go, because some people are seemingly immune to the effect of salvia, extracts provide more power in a smaller package. Extracts are also good because you are smoking less material. Salvia extracts are most commonly sold in two strengths, 5X and 10X. These are, obviously, five times and ten times stronger than raw leaves, respectively.

<LI>I am trying it for the first time, what do you recommend?

I recommend that for a first time you get a trip sitter, someone who you are comfortable with. If at any time during your trip you become freaked out, they are there to talk you through it. Trust me a sitter is mandatory.

That being said, load some 10X extract into a pipe and take a big hit, hold it in as long as possible. Once you exhale, immediately take one more. So 2 hits of 10X. Once you stop screaming OH My God, what did I just do!?!? youll start to experience the trip, and find out why its so popular. Yes, you should take 2 hits of 10X, it gives you a good reference point for all future experiments.

<LI>Anything else?

Yes, read all the experiences here on &TOTSE and those on www.Erowid.com (http://www.Erowid.com) , Erowid is you friend, and so are the smelves. Obey the smelves, they know what they are doing.</UL>
