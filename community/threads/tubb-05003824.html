Well with any season there are highs and lows for getting high.

Summer is nice weather but sometimes gets too hot or the sun gets bright/annoying. Although Summer nights are nice.

Fall is nice because the temperatures are moderate so it's not too hot or too cold (hopefully) and the leaves and the change to winter is nice to watch.

Winter brings a lot of fun with the snow and skiing/snowboarding if you're into that.
