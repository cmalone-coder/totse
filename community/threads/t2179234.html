Heh. Well, I've been registered here since '04. I just rarely post.

And OP, don't do salvia. At least not right now. It's a bad idea for a first-time drug experience, and will really skew your view of what "tripping" normally feels like. I love tripping with all my heart, but salvia is just a different ballgame. It can be as intense as DMT but with none of the good qualities that DMT comes with. I'm actually loath to even call salvia a psychedelic. It's not quite a delirient or anything, and apparently can get pretty shamanic, but it's just... different.

Now if you want to hallucinate productively with easy-to-get chemicals and can ignore the inevitable cries of "kidiot!" that will follow this suggestion... there's always DXM. And I mean a massive dose of it. Google phrases like "4th plateau" and "dxm mg/kg calculator." A few spray bottles of Zicam really is nothing to fuck with...
