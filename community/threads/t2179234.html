Re: How to get acid?
Acid is great. It's at constant war with mushrooms for the spot of "my favoritest drug."

However, you can't just walk through your high school and suddenly find an acid dealer. That ain't really how it works. In fact, it has taken me a couple of years to build up a small number of acid connections, most of whom are quite unreliable in the first place, just because demand is so sky-high and supply is so low.

While a music festival is by far your best bet for procuring acid, it's not like music festivals just happen every weekend, anyway. What you should do is start attending a local coffee shop's open mic night. Odds are, there is such a place locally, even if you don't know about it. Hippies flock to those kinds of places. And even if you don't know anyone there, which will most likely be the case from what you're saying in your first post, just develop friendships with people. The more dreadlocks they have, the more likely they will know someone who deals acid. Eventually, just ask them if they can get you acid. But don't ask about it on the phone or in a very public place, especially in a loud voice. Hippies are generally open and generous, but are also extremely paranoid of the government and all of its agents.

And as for it being something else, that's not that likely in the first place. Most drugs simply aren't active in small enough doses to cause any noticeable effects and still fit on a blotter (which is the most common way it's sold). It might wind up you get DO* (research chemicals active in very small dosage that make you trip, but... just not like acid) but those are rare enough to begin with that it's generally not a huge concern.

And do listen to whoever said "smoke pot first." It'll help mentally prepare you for what you're getting into if you experience an altered state of consciousness beforehand. Also, it'll let those paranoid hippies know that you're not a cop. Also, smoking pot makes your trip stronger, brings out the visuals, and, by my estimation, makes the whole thing a little "earthier."

And as far as E is concerned, do it, anyway. Ecstasy can be pretty mind-expanding in its own right, and goes out-fucking-standingly WITH acid! It's called candyflipping, and is made of infinite win.

Happy tripping!

Edit: Now, ecstasy. THERE'S something where you have to worry about getting fake product. On second thought, avoid ecstasy if you don't REALLY know your source.

SYNTHESIS : A solution of 6.7 g KOH in 100 mL H2O, under an inert atmosphere and magnetically stirred, was brought to 75 °C, and 10 g ergotamine tartrate (ET) added. The reaction mixture turned yellow as the ergotamine went into solution over the course of 1 h. The stirring was continued for an additional 3 h. The reaction mixture was cooled to about 10 °C with an external ice bath, and acidified to a pH of about 3.0 by the dropwise addition of 2.5 N H2SO4. White solids started to appear early in the neutralization; approximately 60 mL of sulfuric acid was required. The reaction mixture was cooled overnight, the solids removed by filtration, and the filter cake washed with 10 mL Et2O. The dry solids were transferred to a beaker, suspended in 50 mL 15 % ammonia in anhydrous ethanol, stirred for 1 h, and separated by decantation. This extraction was repeated, and the original decantation and the second extract combined and filtered to remove a few hundred milligrams of unwanted solids. The clear filtrate was stripped of solvent under vacuum, the residual solids dissolved in 50 mL of 1% aqueous ammonia, and this solution was acidified as before with 2.5 N H2SO4. The precipitated solids were removed by filtration and washed with Et2O until free of color. After drying under vacuum to a constant weight, there was obtained 3.5 g of d-lysergic acid hydrate, which should be stored in a dark, sealed container.

A suspension of 3.15 g d-lysergic acid hydrate and 7.1 g of diethylamine in 150 mL CHCl3 was brought to reflux with stirring. With the external heating removed, there was added 3.4 g POCl3 over the course of 2 min, at a rate sufficient to maintain refluxing conditions. The mixture was held at reflux for an additional 5 min, at which point everything had gone into solution. After returning to room temperature, the solution was added to 200 mL of 1 N NH4OH. The phases were separated, the organic phase dried over anhydrous MgSO4, filtered, and the solvent removed under vacuum. The residue was chromatographed over alumina with elution employing a 3:1 C6H6/CHCl3 mixture, and the collected fraction stripped of solvent under hard vacuum to a constant weight. This free-base solid can be recrystallized from benzene to give white crystals with a melting point of 87-92 °C. IR (in cm-1): 750, 776, 850, 937 and 996, with the carbonyl at 1631. The mass spectrum of the free base has a strong parent peak at mass 323, with sizable fragments at masses of 181, 196, 207 and 221.

This base was dissolved in warm, dry MeOH, using 4 mL per g of product. There was then added dry d-tartaric acid (0.232 g per g of LSD base), and the clear warm solution treated with Et2O dropwise until the cloudiness did not dispel on continued stirring. This opaqueness set to a fine crystalline suspension (this is achieved more quickly with seeding) and the solution allowed to crystallize overnight in the refrigerator. Ambient light should be severely restricted during these procedures. The product was removed by filtration, washed sparingly with cold methanol, with a cold 1:1 MeOH/Et2O mixture, and then dried to constant weight. The white crystalline product was lysergic acid diethylamide tartrate with two molecules of methanol of crystallization, with a mp of about 200 °C with decomposition, and weighed 3.11 g (66%). Repeated recrystallizations from methanol produced a product that became progressively less soluble, and eventually virtually insoluble, as the purity increased. A totally pure salt, when dry and when shaken in the dark, will emit small flashes of white light.

Ok, you're a drug virgin. You want to try LSD. Keep in mind, you probably won't enjoy a fully blown trip. Assuming what you get is mediocre strength, take a quarter of a hit first time (keep in mind 1/4 of a hit of strong stuff can be as powerful as 1 hit weak stuff), just to get the feel of what being in an altered state of mind is. If you feel comfortable with it, take a full hit, or even two, a weeks time afterwards.

The computer is a fine tripping enviroment. The fact that you're in control of everything the computer outputs is a good setting. It all depends. If you're alone, your focus will be inwards, into your mind. You'll start thinking about all these crazy things, in other words, acid seems to be more mentally powerful when all alone. If you're with friends and socializing, your focus will be focused outwards, making you notice visual and audial patterns much more. If you have a good few close friends, who you think you could talk into doing it (but by all means be honest), I'd suggest waiting on a nice sunny day, have a room where you can sit and chill, play videogames, watch movies, while retaining the abillity to go outside and explore nature if you wish. You should avoid any akward social confrontations, for example avoid at any cost any confrontations with people you don't want to know that you're on acid.

Also, acid affects people differently! Me and my brother seem to be in full control of the trip, and have no problems talking to our parents when tripping for example. Other people zone out and start screaming and falling over from it. You can't tell what will happen. And, I hate saying this, but when alone there's always a rather small chance you'll freak out and attempt suicide (often unsuccesful), you DO not want this to happen. This is why I recommend taking just a quarter hit at first, as this should not affect your behaivour, while making you somewhat comfortable with the drug.

Also I wouldn't bother trying to program. All tasks which require straight forward logic are impossible while tripping.

Getting a hold of acid is no easy task. You can't just go up to random hippies and ask them for it. You gotta get to know them first, which can be akward. There aren't any guidelines, you'll have to adapt your strategies of looking to who you know and where you live. Start out by asking people you know who do drugs, ask them for experiences and stories they've had while on drugs.
