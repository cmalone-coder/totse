Do you think you could function properly if...
Do you think you could function properly if for some reason, your thought patterns, changed to those of another?

I'm sure many of you people have seen others mention things like how we will never know if everyone sees the same colour. Like, you may see what I have labeled as RED and call it GREEN, simply because thats the label you've been tought for that colour. Well this kinda ties into that. [in a way]

We will never know if everyone thinks the same. Hell, I know that everyone thinks in different ways, but do you think it could be completely possible that everyones way of think is completely obscure when compared to someone elses?

Ill use programming as an example. Lest say you have two programmers, who only program in one language, and know no others. PG-1 knows only C++ and PG-2 knows only VB.

Now, both of them go ahead and make a program, that, for example, removes pop up windows. They make the GUI's look the same, they give it all the same options and features and everything.

The end product, and their abilities where both equal in this little example. However, if you show PG-1's code [C++] to PG-2, PG-2 won't know what the hell hes looking at. And if you told PG-2 that from this day forth he must begin programming in only C++, he'd be fucked. [of course when it comes to programming you can get a book, but you get the idea]

I'm wondering, if I was to one day take on the thought patterns of a friend, would I be able to function normally? Would I have to learn how to speak again, learn how to walk, learn everything? For all we know, my brain stores its info on walking over here, and my friend stores his info on walking over there.

Perhaps when I went to move my arm, I would yell 'covertinpapple!'. Simply because I'm used to accessing this type of info over here, but only he may use that piece to yell things. [like covertinpapple]

It's kind of hard to imagine trying to move your arm and yelling, simply because we don't have to actually think to do things like move, and speak and such. However, when we do them, or brin is actually being told to retrieve some sort of info, and then it does so, and we get the result.

So, what do you think? Am I just nuts or what?

I don't think of the brain as a place to store things. When I learn something, I feel that I am subtracted something.
