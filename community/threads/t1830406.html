Re: Altitude, rockets, parabolic motion
If your using the projectile parabolic flight trajectory you cannot achieve accurate results because rockets do not follow purely parabolic paths. Negating wind resistance and gravity flux, a projectile (which is any object whose flight is determined by a force acting not of its own accord but rather of a separate object, i.e. cannon and its projectile) will follow a parabolic path.

In other words, use an altimeter because your gonna get the most accurate results.
