Casual Drugs
What are the best drugs for taking casually (as opposed to recreational weekend drug binges). Drugs to take the odd time on weekdays/weekends, in the mornings, at work, at school or whenever and wherever the fuck you feel like it. Heres the pros and cons

Cannabis - A good one, effects everyone differently but doesn't last too long, its cheap, still leaves you sober enough to do your daily activities.

Alcohol - Can only be used up to a certain point. A few beers and your alright but a few too many and your all sloppy and can't do your daily activities properly.

Amphetamines (including meth) - Not bad if you take it in the mornings, its cheap, makes you do your daily activities even better but the downside is it cuts off your hunger, can make it hard to sleep even after it wears off and its not the healthiest drug out there.

Heroin - A real good one. Makes some people drowsy but other people it just relaxes them. Makes you take joy in all your daily tasks no matter how boring and mundane. Problem with H is its way too expensive and way too habit forming.

Miscellaneous opiates (oxycontin, dilaudid etc) - Seem to be some of the best casual drugs around. Not too expensive and not too intoxicating.

Cocaine/Crack - Not bad but too short lived and way too expensive. Only good for people with alot of money to waste.

Mushrooms/Acid/Mescaline - God no

MDMA - Not bad at all but unpredictable. One day it'll make you buzz through the whole day no matter what kinda shitty job your doing another day you won't wanna do anything.

I've only really done conventional drugs like those ones I haven't tried many of the pharmaceuticals or new drugs going around like 2C-B and that kinda shit.

The best drugs I've come across to be used casually are the opiates.
