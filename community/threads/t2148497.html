Re: coke withdrawal
Do not mix being run down from depleting yourself and withdrawal.

You don't get withdrawal from coke because it is not physically addicting.

Withdrawal is what you get from opiate addiction.

You do coke a long time and stop you only feel a bit run down for a day or two or three.

You do opiates a long time and stop, you feel like death - or that you wish you were dead. Your body aches for the drug.

Big difference.
