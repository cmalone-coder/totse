Re: MDMA price?
You'll rarely see 'pure' MDMA. Still, the only way you (or your dealer for that matter) can possibly know is if they are the chemist or know them personally.

I live in England, so this probably won't help an awful lot, but whatever. The price for crystalline MDMA has gone down recently - partly due to its prevalence in my part of the world. Generally, you should expect to pay £30 a g. £20 or £15 for a half. I'd buy at least a half if you're doing it with a buddy though - take 0.25g in three hits over the course of the night and you'll be spinning.

$90 for 0.5 seems very steep, so I probably wouldn't pay that unless I knew it was some super special stuff that genuinely did a lot more than the average shit you find on the streets. I'm talking a fine piece of chemistry here; perhaps even a perfectly balanced cocktail of various MDMA-like drugs.

In short, don't be a mug and pay through the ass for MDMA. It really shouldn't be that expensive. Heck, when compared to a drug like coke (and considering how much more for your money you get), MDMA is positively cheap!
