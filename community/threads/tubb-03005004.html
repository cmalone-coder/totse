Remember: I did warn you:

Since I first discovered that I had a talent for writing music [I should say ever since I found out that people really liked what I did I had wrote and recorded about one tape a year of new music. 1993 was the last tape I did. The last song I wrote and recorded was a serious one I wrote for my daughter [I don't hardly ever write anything serious, it usually ends up getting to corny and/or sappy, so I stayed away from serious. This one, though was well done. Amazing what you can do when it's something really heart felt

[shameless plug] You can hear it for yourself at the MP3.com page I have: Alchemy�s Song [/shameless plug]

It was right after that that my life took a crap [wife left, took kid, body cast, etc.];
Understandably I didn't feel much like doing music.

But I've been back on my feet for at least 4 to 5 years, yet I hadn't felt the spark; I had hardly even picked up my bass [though a large part of that was that my operation had left my left hand not-fully-functioning and that is the main hand for guitars. Every time I did try to play I got real frustrated because my brain still knew what to do, but my hand wasn�t being a team player].

This past X-mas, though, my friend the recording engineer all but forced me to do a X-mas song. So I did; almost as much to get off my back as any other reason.

It wasn't the best thing I had ever done - not by a long shot: It was barely passable [in my opinion].

It did though seem to break up dam, unclog the works. In the next month I wrote a dozen sets of lyrics - no music yet.

Thing was, though, they were all about my break up, and all that had happened in '95: About still being in love with my wife and wanting to get her back, and all that crap. All a bunch of real downer crap � not my norm at all. I call them Therapy Songs. Because, the thing is, that was not at all how I now felt. It was how I felt back then, yeah, but not now. It was like I had to get all of that crap out of my system before I could get back to what I was best at.

It was about then, IIRC, that I stumbled across the 44-D thread. Something just struck me then and I jumped in. [This is the connection to the post I said it was cut from....for those keeping score]


I've been steadily working on my next project, which my friend is going to mix in quad [and/or surround sound] and the cover of the CD will 3D.

I�m even going to use one or two of the Therapy Songs; too many have told me I have to do it.
