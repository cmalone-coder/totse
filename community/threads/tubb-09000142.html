It's impossible to have hundreds of dreams a night, because time moves at a "real" pace in dreams, mathematically it doesn't make sense. So, assuming 8 hours sleep, you would have to have a new dream every five minutes. It is indeed possible that you are correct, but dreams are more of a Joyce-ian stream of conciosness type deal, where everything simply flows, and it's hard to discriminate endings and beginnings (good call syphon detonator).

Now to the real subject. The time skipping around thing has close correlations to what quantum physicists call "the arrow of time". It is indeed perplexing that everything in the known universe can go up, down, back, forward, left, right, and more depending on the existence of a fourth dimension. So, why doesn't time ever move backwards? Why don't we remember things from the future? I have to debunk your VCR claim, though, because I would imagine that we are more sentient than signals from a video tape.

Almost there. In my honest opinion, the most reasonable explanation of Deja Vu is Schrodinger's quantum hussy theory. I've explained it before, but I'll go into it again for those who missed it. Schrodinger proposed that every time a decision in the universe comes up, the universe "tests the waters". All possibilities are tried out, spread across time and space. Then the universe piscks it's "favorite," thought to be the most probable end. This is called the "collapse of a wave function", also a Schrodinger baby. This is where everything gets tripped out Carl Jung style. Basically, we retain a residue of what could of happened in our brains. The people, places, and things that we would of encountered are all in there. So when we see something that occured before the collapse, a trigger goes off in the brain. Bada Bing bada boom, deja vu'. Hold on kiddies, things are about to get scarier. Since our experiences influence us, they think that the "memories" from the other experiences influence our behavior. So if we have a bad experience with tapioca in one of the realities before the "real" reality, we have an unexplainable aversion to tapioca, which is a real shame. Yowzers.

There are a few facts to support this. When an electron switches to another atom, it exists in all of the fields before it selects the one it's best suited for. Furthermore, the whole non-locality issue (which is mathematically proven though entirely inexplicable and contradictory to quantum physics) helps the second half of the argument.

Let's finish it up now. Time has to exist curios monkey. Newton says so. We can say time exists because it moves. Hence a past, now, and future. The real question is, can we control it's movements?
