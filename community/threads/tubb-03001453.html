Somewhere In Between Depression and Nostalgia
Hi,

I'm in one of those moods. You know the kind of moods you get in when you're not exactly depressed and you're not exactly nostalgic, but it's something in between those ones? Well, that kind of what I'm in. It's the kind of mood that makes me hate a lot of things. The thing is, I hate a lot of things sometimes, but I almost don't hate anything all the time. I think I'm pretty good at seeing the good in bad. But, I am 3,000 miles away from EVERYTHING (excuse the All Caps). Literally. All the streets I used to walk, all the shops, and Golden Gate Park. I really miss Golden Gate Park. I even miss riding MUNI. Never thought I'd see THAT day. But, I do. But, what I miss most of all is my friends. Because, I didn't have the kind of friends that like you for your money, or your popularity, or your looks. They didn't stab each other in the back and they were always there for you when you needed them or a place to crash. I mean, serious friends that I'd known since childhood and I just turned my back on them and ran 3,000 miles away from the only people I know who would literally die for me.

I know the kind of friends that most people have. They have the kind of friends that they do drugs and get drunk with them and go cruising around the city all night long talking about sex and drugs and all other things superficial, and even things that aren't superficial but on a strictly superficial level. They think they're all really fucking cool so they make fun of each other relentlessly to prove to the group just how cool they really are. But, my friends weren't like that. They are genuine, intelligent, funny people.

I don't know. I'm really just blathering. But, you know how when you can't get something off your mind and everything reminds you of that something you're thinking of, so you rearrange things, so that it won't remind you of it. But, once everything is rearranged and you sit down and look at it, it still reminds you of it because you know why it's rearranged. Know what I mean? Well, I've been neurotically cleaning my apartment, over and over again ever since we got here. I'm not a neat freak or anything, but every day I have the urge to rearrange all the furniture, which makes me ultimately want to clean. Finally my pop is telling me I'm doing it to avoid dealing with my thoughts. I hate it when he's right. Around here they call him The Lord, and they don't just call him that for no reason at all.

So, everyone keeps telling me that good friends make for good memories and that friends come in and out of your life. They tell me I'll make new friends as good as the last and sooner or later we'll part and make new friends, etc... etc... But, I don't even want to make new friends, because I know it's when you make the new friends that you stop talking to the old ones! And there is this girl: "my-best-friend-in-a-bag" conveniently packaged for me. He's my pops best friends niece and she just moved from Providence Rhode Island. Apparently she has a million contacts and has VIP passed to all the clubs. I've seen her a few times and she's pretty groovy. She keeps inviting me to come with her, but I can't seem to be able to pick up the phone and call her.

It's so annoying! I just wish I could be in two places at once: West Palm Beach and San Francisco. I know it's impossible. I hear that modern science is working on teleportation, though. Which would be convenient. Alter the way we look at travel. But, who cares, because when the aliens land teleporting will look as ancient as the wheel. Why the Hell do they have an "h" in wheel anyways? Why isn't it just weel? Weel isn't a word. At least not in English? Why the Hell did they have to add in an "h". O God, I'm rambling. I'm going to stop now.

Cheers,
Lost
