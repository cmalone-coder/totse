Re: What should I do
Oof. 10th grade? The best advice I can give you is that at your age, don't listen to other people's advice.

If this is the first boy who you think might be interested in a relationship with you, then just let things play out naturally. So much unnecessary drama could have been avoided when I was in high school if only people didn't think that what happened with one couple should be the standard for their relationship, vice versa, etc.

I have no idea why he's suddenly interested, hell, at that age, HE probably has no idea why he's suddenly interested. If a week from now he's still acting the same way, consider him a friend. Go from there. Don't automatically consider him a "boyfriend" if in a week or two you end up hooking up or holding hands or going on a date. This will lead to disappointment. If he truly is interested in you, after he takes enough time to get to know you and feel comfortable around you, he'll probably voice his intentions clearly.
