Re: Getting a new Desktop...
I wouldn't bother with RAID0 unless you are going to be doing lots of large sustained file transfers. Personally, I would get one 500gb HD over two 250s in RAID0, less power consumption, less noise, less heat, and more future proof because of the higher density. If you want killer hard driver performance get a raptor or RAID0 raptors if you do want RAID0. From what I have seen the onboard RAID controllers are fine for RAID0, I would not spend the requisite $200 on a RAID controller card unless reliability was paramount. Two 74gb raptors in RAID0 for OS and apps and then a 500gb storage drive for movies would be a killer setup. Only downside is the price on raptors.

Get whatever the best graphics card you can afford is. The best bang for buck currently is the 8800GTS 320mb. The 8800 series are DX10, but they are also the BEST DX9 cards out there. Whether or not you want to get a card with more than 320mb RAM depends on the resolution that you will play games at. Also, ATIs line of DX10 cards should be coming out very soon (a matter of weeks). Wait and see how those go. The 8600 is a decent card, and is great for decoding highdef videos (better than the 8800GTX!), it's just not spectacular for gaming.

New intel chipsets are coming out in a few weeks too, these will be compatible with intels next gen cpu code named penryn (you could get a cheap C2D now and then upgrade to penryn later). If you wait until august intel's C2D line will see another round of price cuts. AMD also has their barcelona line coming out soon.

The C2D line is definately better than the X2 line. Cost/performance wise they are about the same at stock speeds, but the C2Ds OC much better.
