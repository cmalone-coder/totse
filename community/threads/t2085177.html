Hardware-wise, this would probably be a very expensive project, require above average soldering skills, and basic electronics knowledge.

Your best bet would be to get a hold of a phone that lets you run home-brew software. Have it run a rogue program silently in the background, possibly recording calls, then sending them to you over the air, over WiFi, or even Bluetooth. You might even be able to get GPS coords if the phone has such a feature.

It's possible, but would take a lot of hard work, and a good reason to be spying on someone.
