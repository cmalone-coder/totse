Right click on the item in confusion of opening, and click on the "Open Containing Folder" option. It should be the second option down (assuming your uTorrent version is 1.8.1). There is what you should have downloaded. Double-click (or single-click, whatever the hell your settings for the mouse are) and it should work.
If it's ending in .rar, use something like winRAR to unzip it or something along the lines. Or, if you want to save the trouble of downloading other torrents, you could check the file types before you hit "OK" when trying to add a torrent into uTorrent. It gives you the option to uncheck anything you don't want downloaded either.
Take note of where the files are being downloaded to. It could be your "Download" folder on your C: Drive, or the "Download" folder in your "My Documents", but it helps if you know where your shit is heading beforehand if you plan on using, moving, or burning it.
