Poopeen's new really easy methcathinone synth
I did it this way.

Take a beaker. Put any amount of clean pseudo in it. Pour in three% hydrogen peroxide. Use a wee bit more than it takes to dissolve it. Cover it and put it in a dark place for forty five minutes. After 45 minutes, remove the covering and add isopropanol 99%. Add as much isopropanol as peroxide. Put it on LOW heat until it has all, or mostly, evaporated.

Now recrystalize it with your preferred method. Easy.
