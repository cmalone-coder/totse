ok - here is my list of countries visited so far:

Canada, England, Wales, Scotland, Holland, Germany, Belgium, France, Luxembourg, Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, Israel, Egypt, Thailand, Burma, India, Nepal, Colombia.

I lived in England (East Anglia) for 23 months and did stay in most places visited for at least a few weeks as I agree with the idea of achieving as much immersion into a culture as can be managed instead of the superficial, kaleidescopic and often mercenary experience many folks associate with having 'done' a place ... the word 'done' (mostly unwittingly) often being used figuratively to mean fuck, or even kill. Everyone knows the sort of traveler I am describing - and often this traveler is one of my American countrymen - who has me quietly pulling the brim of my hat down and sinking lower in my seat, as the fool bumbles/swaggers into a group of fellow travelers (say, hanging out in the hostel common room) and loudly proclaims their cluelessness ... and reveals their arrogant opinion of themselves by displaying a condescending superiority to all that surrounds them.

The hope (for me) is that more (and better!) travel and the rich and diverse cultural experiences that can be gathered from a thoughtful, open-minded approach to willful wandering is essential for the interconnectivity and cross-cultural pollination needed for the deeper level of understanding that we must necessarily cultivate in order to survive ourselves and our ongoing (self) destructive ignorance and irrational hostilty towards one another. *pauses for a deep breath*
