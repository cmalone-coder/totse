IMO...

Though it's a %100 theoretical question, I don't think it is possible...

Because Theoretically...

If you (whoever you are) are pure in intentions, a good (whatever religion this question is based on, Catholic?) and have done enough right that you are going to Heaven, then I believe the person you loved (spouse, im assuming) cannot be so bad that he/she would go to hell WHILE you still go to Heaven.

First off, keeping in mind the (now factual) statement that there IS a heaven and hell, God and Satan, etc etc, that leads us to the basic lines of religion.

God is Perfect. God knows all. He knows who everyone ends up with and how everything ends up forever on out. Because of the promises God made in the Bible stating how all those who believed would go to Heaven, He could not have you (A good person on Earth) fall in love with someone who is so bad they would be going to Hell. Here's Why:

God gave us free will. We can make our own decisions. With free will, no one can really make us do anything (no technical arguments, like paying taxes and breathing, etc..). For the most part, we control ourselves.

When you both pass from Earth (die) and go to pergatory, you (would be) going to Heaven, and your love, to Hell. Because God is Perfect and is never wrong, He cannot allow anything to happen to you to allow you the choice or opprotunity to choose your enternal fate to Hell over your loves. You still have free will and can make your own choices, but God still has the promise made that if you are a believer on Earth, you have your eternity in Heaven, which cannot be broken.

Paraphrasing...

If there is a heaven and hell,

there is a God.

If there is a God,

he is perfect.

Also is there is a God and there is a Heaven,

he made the promise that those who were good go to Heaven.

People have free will.

If you fall in love with someone who is bad enough to go to hell, but not bad enough to corrupt you,

God cannot give you the choice or opprotunity to go to hell for "that someone"

because it is always your choice and you could disprove God of his perfection by disproving his promise of an eternal in Heaven BY choosing to go to hell for someone else.
