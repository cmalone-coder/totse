An ethical quagmire.
Lets say you're out walking in the woods one day, a really nice, sunny day. There is little or no wind and the sun is at your back.

You get to the top of a small hill and you see a large meadow opening out before you for 300 yards in every direction. Short grass, not even 8 inches tall.

Looking around, you notice a rifle leaning up against a tree. Nothing fancy, just a bolt action .22, but it sports a Ciener suppressor and a run of the mill scope.

Just then your eye spots movement in the distance, you pick up the rifle, look through the scope and there, a couple hundred yards off, is your worst enemy, the guy you love to hate.

Nobody knows you are there, there is no one else around. You're already wearing gloves, your enemy doesn't see you. The rifle can't be traced to you, and there is extra ammo laying on the ground.

What do you do?
