I immediately checked my sights were set correctly, before pulling back the cocking handle and loading a round into the chamber. I could hear the shots starting around me, I looked to my sides and saw the line of us, each trying to put shots accurately down upon our opposers.

I knew this was no time to be contemplative or philosophical - I had a task to do, and do it I must. Despite the fact that up until now, mere seconds had passed - it seemed like ages. I brought my rifle to bear in my shoulder, looked through the sight picture and squeezed the trigger to first pressure - adjusted my breathing then took the last squeeze. *bang*

[b}I kept putting shots down, Until I saw the faint black outline of a man with a weapon fall.[/B]

"STTTTTTTTOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOP" It was all over, the firing had ceased and our opposers had gone. We were now presented with a clear view.. No outlines at all.

I now questioned my own actions, "How many shots had actually found their target?", "Could I have reacted better?", "What if..." My own thought process was interrupted by the sound of a radio.

My mind still kept going back to seeing the black outline of a man falling..

"UNLOAD" - We removed our magazines, checked our chambers were empty before firing off the action. We then dressed behind the firing point... before being allowed to check our targets. I looked at the 6foot sqaure wooden board with the black outline on it and saw a small group of shots just to the right of its head - "Damn, sights were off."
