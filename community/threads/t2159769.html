Take a low oral dose (20-40mg) of whatever amphetamines are availible. Taking it orally is preferable because it's less intense, lasts longer and for me at least it's a smoother experience overall compared to insufflation, and there's less irrational fear or anxiety. Yes I know how much lower the bioavailability is, but higher numbers aren't automatically better in every situation. This isn't recreational use as much as "therapeutic" use, like when they give it to kids who don't want to do schoolwork.

If you can only find meth it's about three times as potent, so reduce dosage accordingly. I don't have any direct experience with meth though, so I'm not sure if it's that simple, hopefully someone who doesn't live on the east coast can shed some light on that.

Keep in mind I'm just telling you what I would do if I was faced with this situation, you may find that something else suits you better. Before you actually go out and do this you should take it at home or in an otherwise stable, undemanding situation where you can get a feel for it's effects. Also I'm operating under the assumption that this is just something you're doing because you need some money and you're not planning on doing this for a long time. If that's not the case I hate to say it but you're going to have to learn to do it sober.
