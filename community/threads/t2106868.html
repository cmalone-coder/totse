Re: Moldy weed (what to do?!)
Heat in an oven preheated @ 150C for 5-10 minutes. The heat will kill bacteria/mould, and the moisture evaporation means you can powder the weed up and massively increase surface area.
Dump your powdered weed into high-proof alcohol (which also kills bacteria/mould), shake a lot and warm it up if you want to speed up the process. Filter the weed out, and let the alcohol evaporate. Scrape up the oil and smear it onto a rolling paper/cig.

I did this with a 1/4 of mouldy weed, and ended up with enough to smear onto 2 Rizlas. One joint got 5 of us high as fuck.

Waste not want not.
