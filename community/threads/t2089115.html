Re: Keeping MJ fresh
Don't use fruit peels of any kind unless you got some dirt weed that could use improvement in flavor. Because orange and apple - the two I've used - does leave a taste of its own. Now this taste ain't bad, and - like I said - if the weed lacks in good flavor, it can really help. But you don't want to change the flavor of good weed.

These days I use a couple of different things. I learned them from a friend of mine who grows for some of the medical pot clubs around here. The first is carrot peels. The other - and better one, IMO - is [believe it or not] pieces of flour tortilla. In a pinch I have used small pieces of iceberg lettuce. But that is very easy to over do it.

Something I also learned from my friend is that most growers - and I don't mean the 6 plant home 'hobbyist' - will actually dry out their pot on purpose to store. If they didn't they would have a lot of moldy weed if they have to store it any length of time [and for outdoor growers, that is a lot of time usually]. Then, just before they get ready to sell more, they use carrot peels or flour tortilla to rehydrate it.
