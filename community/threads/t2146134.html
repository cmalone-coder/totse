To be clear, I want everyone to discuss what they think of Totse members, not just bitch, moan, or praise people.

Average Totsean (Makes up 80% of the site)
Good Posters (Make up 10-15% of the site)
Mods (Make up 4-9% of the site)

Well, when I think of an "average Totsean", I see someone who is awe-inspiringly stupid, obnoxious, racist, unlikeable and...did I say stupid? You have people that are just utter morons (refer to Dizzie's book-burning threads in Printed Matter), and you have people that devote all of their posts to being various forms of hate-speech. Ironically...a lot of you would be described quite well by the word "nigger" you love so much. To be clear: I hate the average Totsean, and you guys are mainly just good for insulting.

These select few mainly fall in the categories of senile, batshit insane, or retarded. Occasionally there's some overlap. All in all, I love giving mods shit, mainly because they think they are above it.

Zok: Awesome. Cares about the site, doesn't have anything up his ass, and is helpful in the tech forums.

Meta: Similar to Zok fundamentally, but the oak tree up his ass has made him into a prick.

Good posters: These aren't necessarily people I always get along with, but they contribute, occasionally provide lulz (without being obnoxious), are somewhat intelligent, and/or they are just good people.
