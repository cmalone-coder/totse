and to detach the psuedo from binders.

psuedo from an ab will still come up not useuable. Crush,soak in toluene three times,

Re: Confused
Start reading about solvent polarity. This will help to understand the difference in solvents and which solvent to use for the intended application.

Some are polar(can mix with water), and some are Non-Polar(usually will not mix with water).

Since pseudo/ephedrine is polar soluble, to extract one would use a polar solvent such as alcohol. To clean the binders, one would probably use a non-polar solvent.

Learn about polarity and the acid/base extraction. Otherwise you are doomed to throwing the baby out with the bath water!!!

Re: Confused
no,

ok here, step by step what i did,

1 crush pills put in container with water about double the amount of pill powder..

shake this around really good, and let it sit,

u'll get a white mudd at the bottom, and a milky liquid on top,(this is what u want).

syphon off the liquid and try not to pick up the mudd(it plugs up your filters)

so syphon the liquid, into another container.

add more water to the mudd and then shake and syphon again to make sure you get most of the ephi from the mudd.

once you have the milky water, filter it a few times, i say about 3, then take the filtered milk, and boil it down.

you get white powder(take this)

and disolve it in the methyl alcohol(methenol) shake really well and filter 2 times, after its filtered twice, put it in the freezer, till it gets really cold then filter it once more(this removes a wax, its only removed when u do it cold)

once u do the cold filter, take the alcohol and then boil that down. not all the way, or u'll burn the shit, just till there is very little left(microwave works for this)

let the rest evaporate, and u will see the crystals form, if u watch(its neat)

this is the shit u want to put in the a/b.

take this do the a/b and you got ur pure.
