Re: Werid Adderall Experience. I'm confused too.
Alright, to start with you have nothing to worry about. More or less that can be called a normal experience. To answer your questions in order:

1)I was addicted to Dextroamphetamine (Adderal is a combo of Dextro and Levo amphetamine) for awhile and can say I NEVER crashed, ever, not once. What happened to myself and what happened to everyone I know who did Amphets regularly would be an increase in energy, combined with an early and short euphoric peak (generally 30min. to an hour) followed by around 8 or 9 hours of talkativeness and comfort. Generally I personally would stay awake 36 hours after ingestion, then go to sleep as normal the next night around 10 or 11pm.

2) Some people are effected differently. A standard, regular, good dose of amphetamine is 50mg. This is the dose that is considered normal around my group of friends for recreational purposes. at 50mg-70mg for a semi-regular or new user, the euphoria is increased as compared to 20-40mg. the amount of energy you mantain and have should stay relitivly close to either dosage level.

3)Did you drink a lot of water at the gym?

4) You probably strained your back lifting and didn't notice it until now

5)Yes, more or less. I recommend using Dexatrine brand amphets for future use if you wish to do this again.

6)It wasn't dangerous compared to what it could have been at higher doses, and I don't recommend you use weights like you did on future speed trips (if you plan on doing more).

My comments:

1: As noted, use Dexetrine. It is pure Dextroamphetamine, better euphoria.

2: Aquire Valium, or another benzo or muscle pain-reliever/any pain reliever. Railing or ingesting these 'downers' after your euphoria has died and your sitting around bored at night because youre still full of energy with nothing to do and nowhere to go will help you relax and eventually put you to sleep (I recently tried Cyclobenzaprine, a back-pain reliever, as a Valium substitute for this purpose and it worked like a dream with only one 10mg pill).

3: Don't use sleeping pills or any diphenhydramine, diphenhydrinate, dimenhydramine or dimenhydrinate (ie no dramamine or benadryl) to help you sleep. I learned the hard way that using these pills to help you sleep when coming off of amphetamines is very unpleasent; you only feel the bad side effects of either instead of 'tripping' on them like some people do.

4: If you have schoolwork or work to do that involves research or writing, its good to do it on amphetamines. you concentrate better and enjoy what you're doing, generally giving weilding better results.

most important-5: Stick to around 50mg.
The warm fluttering in your chest thats pelasent during your euphoric period becomes complete and utter HELL on higher doses (75mg+) unless youre a very regular user and have a developed tolerance. That warm fluttering is your heart racing at a faster pace and your blood pressure rising (I learned the hard way by taking 80mg of dextroamphetamine with no tolerance, I spent the entire next day with that feeling of my heat picking up speed).

Stay safe. Don't get addicted. Enjoy.
