Re: How long do you hold it in?
There's so many stupid drug myths surrounding weed that it makes my head spin. I don't know where people got the idea that most of the THC is absorbed in 3 seconds (I've heard that High Times said so, but where the fuck do you get information on that? The government doesn't fund studies like that and who would bother doing it themselves?)

I'm pretty sure that the longer you hold it the higher you get. Still though, I used to be all hardcore and I'd hold it till I coughed my ass off, but really, there's not a whole lot of difference between doing that and just holding it for 5 or 7 seconds or so.

Really I just prefer not to hurt my lungs so I let it out quicker.

If it's a small toke I'll hold it really long, but if I'm hitting some huge bong I like it smooth so I hit it huge and blow it out in a few seconds. This way the harshness of all that smoke is covered by the water. I find if I hold bong tokes for a long time they really hurt my lungs and the water doesn't help that much. But if I blow them out fairly soon, it's so smooth and nice.

I dunno though, if I was a yankee and had shitty weed that could cost me jail time, I'd be more inclined to make the most of it. As it is, living in Vancouver, I'm not too worried about getting 110% of the THC out of every bud. I'd prefer to just enjoy it and be wasteful.
