Get a pregnancy test. Then go to Planned Parenthood, get an abortion and/or get some free condoms.

Quote:
Originally Posted by TehWTF
A pregnancy test wont tell you truthfully if she's pregnant unless she's been of the pill for about a month. First, you make sure she gets off the pill and wait like 3 weeks.
No. Pregnancy tests detect the presence of Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG). The pill doesn't contain hCG, and will have no effect on a pregnancy test. Some diseases can produce elevated levels of hCG, but she would likely know if she had them.

What will make a difference is if you take the test too early. If you do there won't be sufficient levels of hCG in the urine for the test to detect.
