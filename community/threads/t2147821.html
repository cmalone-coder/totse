Re: Bought new glass color-changing pipe, but carb hole sealed (by law).How to open carb?
Strictly speaking, the entire head of the pipe is somewhat analogous to a carburetor. Vacuum from the manifold [lungs] pulls fuel [smoke] from the bowl. The hole, which would more properly be called a choke, regulates the amount of fresh air mixed with it. Just like an engine, it's necessary to close the choke until you get things going, at which point you can open it up for better performance.

Now you've got me wondering if a venturi in the stem would improve the hits...

So, while it's technically a choke, it's commonly called a carb. Get over it.
