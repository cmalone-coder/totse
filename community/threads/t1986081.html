The Problem of Cohabitation
Some of you may know what cohabitation is, while others of you may not. Cohabitation is the process where two individuals whom "love" each other live in the same apartment, house, etc. They do this because they want to bring their relationship to the next step. They do this because they think it will be good for the relationship. And all together, they are daydreaming romantics thinking living in one place is an awesome idea.

However, to say the least, people think cohabitation is a good idea.

Yet from studies done throughout the decades among various households, relationships, and more, people find many couples aren't doing well in these kind of relationships.

People cohabitate for various reasons: economic and more. Yet many people are poor, struggling to keep a decent income, and want to have an education. People with a high socio-economic status, meaning with education, money, and high status, typically don't have as many problems in cohabitation.

People have been fooling themselves by thinking cohabitation is going to work out.

Many people don't have education, money, and high status. And people don't notice the dark reality of cohabitation until it's too late.

Dureing cohabitation, you get to see a person's dark side. You get to see what makes the person awful, bad, lazy, incompetent, and more. You get to see all of these things in a larger depth than before.

So, to say the least, the next time you like a girl and she's dating a guy, get her to cohabitate with him. The relationship will eventually fuck up, and most cohabitations end in breakup.

Not many people understand the reasons for breakups. Sometimes the women sees the guy using her as an economic tool. Sometimes the girl will use the guy as an economic crutch. The reasons for these breakups vary widely, and they often lead to problems in socio-economics. Otherwise, people eventually cheat on each other, ignore each other, and try living their own lives while under the same household.

So, if you want two lovers to break up, tell them to cohabitate. Tell them you think it's an awesome idea. Agree with them.

Good luck!
