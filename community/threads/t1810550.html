I'm more or less agnostic. (I often find it difficult to label myself when it comes to religious beliefs, but I suppose this comes closest.)

The simple fact of existence (of the universe, life, everything) just fascinates the hell out of me, and I think all religions are interesting attempts to explain it.

At the deepest philosophical level, though, I have no clue what any of it is or means (or if it means anything at all). None of us *can* know for sure, so I think agnosticism is the only acceptable philosophical position. That's not really what religion is about, though - being able to "know for sure."

(Although you wouldn't know it, to hear some religious people talk.)
