Too late now...

I'll edit in a report into this post sometime tonight.

Summarized:

Dosed at 8:30pm.

Had it under my tongue for 10-12 minutes then swallowed it.

By 8:55 I was feeling it.

Some Euphoria, weird "body energy", obvious change in thinking and mild visual disturbances.

I then went out on a bike ride with my dog.

(I began to notice the whole "my head is separate from my body" thing that happened last time start to happen, so I immediately looked away from my hands/arms area (this was while on the bike) and decided that I wasn't going to look at my hands or any other part of me, or the mirror for mirror for more than a few seconds.)

This kind of coming up half-trip went on until about 10:20, when the rest of it (that I swallowed on a half full stomach) kicked in.

Then it was full blown, maybe 30 or 40% stronger than the last one.

So I looked at trippy things on my computer for 20 minutes.

Talked to the kid in my PE class who got me the stuff in the first place for maybe 30min on and off.

Then it was 11:00.

I watched some of "Planet Earth", which was mind blowing on acid.

Then it was 12:00

I took a ten minute shower, and then lay(ed?) on my bed for 40min just having what seemed like hours of introspective, epiphany like mind blowing thoughts.

From that point on, maybe once every ten minutes I would sense my thought process and emotions start to digress towards a really bad trip, so I would tell myself "you are on acid. you are getting these thoughts because you are on acid" and listen to some upbeat music on my ipod for 20 or 30 seconds. This consistently put everything back on track.

This even worked when, a few times, I sensed and "evil presence". Like it was in me, everywhere, outside, ect.

Even when I looked in the mirror (ableit for only a few seconds), my face morphed into a sinister expression.

But then I put on some of my happy pop punk rock music that I usually DEPSISE, and everything turned happy.



The weirdest part was that I felt like my subconscious and my conscious were merged.

Don't ask why.

It was just a higher plane of consciousness.

Last trip was like a dulled down type of trip.

It was really awesome sensually (sights/sounds), but my thought process was like I was kind of drunk of something.

This time, I felt a lot smarter, aware of and in tune with my environment and smarter as a whole.

Also, I could visualize things extremely well.

To test this, I sacrificed 5min to do a word problem from my math homework. It took me 5min, but it would have taken me 10 or 12 normally.

After that, I realized that I could control the trip.

If I stared at something for more than a couple seconds, I would go into full pseudo-hallucination acid mode, which I could stay in for more than a second or two.

But I could will myself out of it.

It's impossible to explain, but I could just close my eyes for a second or two and "snap out of it". When I was "snapped out of it", I just had pure euphoria. It was like everything was perfect and awesome. And an almost scary burst in energy too. It sounds kind of weak when I explain it, but it was the best high *if you'd call it that; not acid mode, happy hyper mode)) that I've ever experienced.

So I then decided to go out and go around town (1-ish in the morning).

I was initally scared, and started feeling that the "evil presence" was there again.
And then I saw one of these outside: http://image.com.com/gamespot/images/2006/234/928519_20060823_screen009.jpg (the big black one).

So yet again I popped in my earbuds and all my fears stopped.

I walked down to the local 7-11, in euphoria mode, and went and bought a gatorade.

The guy behind the counter knew I was on something, and I knew he knew, but I thought it was funny.

Then I went to the local shopping plaza and saw people power washing the sidewalk.

I thought it was just the acid at first.

There was basically a two story house sized cloud of steam.

I dunno, I thought it was cool.

Then I wandered around rite aid for a few minutes until an employee asked me to leave.

I then walked home.

Before I went back inside, I sat on the grass and stared at the sky for a little bit.

As I snuck back into the house, and was walking towards the stairs, I had this thought that I was a cat, like a cat stalking it's prey, and I felt my face morph into a cat face.

Like I felt my teeth GROW.

That went away when I got upstairs and turned the light on.

I then (maybe 3am) decided that I wanted to go back to bed.

But as soon as I turned the light off and got in bed, I heard all of these scary noises from the hallway right outside my door (screeching, banging, ect.) so I put my music on and then everything was good again.

I closed my eyes while I listened to the music.

Now that I think about it, the music sounded more--and this sounds weird--golden and metallic than before. At the time I thought that it just sounded better in every regard. This is probably an after affect, mild synthenasia (spelling?).

After listening to Dark Side of the Moon and The Best of Radiohead, I went to bed (probably around 5:30 or so), where I had n incredibly vivid WILD (Wake Induced Lucid Dream) lucid dream that seemed to last for days.

Then, around 12:30, I got up, grabbed and drank a giant glass of OJ and sat down to right this.

This is nowhere near a full account of my trip, just enough to give an idea.

I think that I liked this kind of trip better than the last.

I was still fully functional.

The last trip, I could barely talk ("Hewo da--d").

I feel that when I go on this trip this summer (week in paris, week in london), I'm gonna drop a tab and see how it goes.

To me, the reason that this trip was so much better was because I was so much more aware and in control, in every aspect.

If I notice some persistent disturbances in my senses (like the last time), I won't complain or freak out.

Last time they went away completely.

If anything happens this time, I'll just shut up and hope for the best.

believe it or not, they kicked in in around 15min. pupils wide after 10

nausea, stomach discomfort, but nothing too bad. didn't vomit(thank god)

felt like I was melting on the come up, nice visual. then I laid back and listened to pink floyd (echoes ftw) for several hours, thinking about similarities to dmt, yet also a distinct salvia edge to it, yet much more comfortable. then I went and sat next to the shrooms that were still growing, that was fucking beautiful to watch all the little pins.

also, I noticed how much brighter colors got, much like dmt again. amazing.

then my head started to clear up, posted that reply (comeing down off a mushy trip), and then I went to bed (about six hours after I took them)

today, everything seems much happier and brighter than before. interesting afterglow, yet I don't feel disconnected at all.

you get an afterglow like

this one I had?

Actually, no.

The best way I can put it is that I'm a lot more mellow and nicer.

And my head's really clear.
