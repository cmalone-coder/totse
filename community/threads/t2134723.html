Re: Think I'll try ephedrine
Look for Chinese ephedrine pills marked by the name "Ma Huang". Also you should take caffeine pills with this to enhance the effects.

That or you could buy a commercial caffeine/ephedrine stack brand of pill.

A good solid dose for a healthy man is 200mg caffeine, and 25mg ephedrine combined. Increasing the ephedrine dosage won't increase effects, but increasing the dosage of caffeine is said to help but do not exceed 400mg caffeine. Ephedrine above 50mg can get ugly and lead to being restless, "jitters" and nausia. Ephedrine dosages over 50mg usually make one feel mentally spaced-out as well so overall increasing the dosage past 40-50mg will just make the effects more uncomfortable.

Remember everyone reacts differently to drugs so start with a low 200mg caffeine/20-25mg ephedrine your first time. Drink at least a quart of water with the pill combination or the stack since both caffeine and ephedrine are water hogs. When you start to feel the effects wear off, chugging another half quart of water seems to revive the stimulation.

Just for reference, 120mg of Ma Huang is equal to 12mg ephedrine alkaloids.
