Well enogh of me rambling I was just wondering if anyone else has done an extraction like this before. Here is the extraction.


fresh pods were freezed, then ground in a blender, with 3 volume equivalents of cold water,
and allowed to stand for 1 hour.
The resulting goo was stirred, then sifted crudely, to retain the biggest pieces.
The filtrate was allowed to stand for 1 hour at room temperature, after which the fairly clear,
light brown top layer was decanted into a bottle, which was not to be filled completely.
The filter residue was extracted again with water, fractions were combined.
The closed bottle was kept still, in a freezer at -5 centigrade until 2/3 of the solution was frozen.
The freezing solution was filtrated, after which the ice was discarded.
The filtrate was put back in the bottle, and the freezing procedure was repeated.
The resulting brown solution was allowed to evaporate on a hot water bath,
yielding a brittle brown substance which was potent and tasteful enough to smoke pleasurably.


The dude who came up with this said for dried pods like I am using to let it sit in water for a while in the fridge before freezing and to do 3 extractions instead of 2 with the fresh.

The best method to get smokeable opium from dried pods, or even fresh unsliced pods would be as follows:

0. Prepare pods. Cut off crowns, break off the nuckle, take out seeds (keep em)
1. Crush to fine powder, whatever means dont create excessive heat via friction (electic grinders take 1-2 sec grinds every 10 seconds etc.)
2. First soak in warm water for 15mins, stirr up every 2-3 mins. Filter, strain the mush, squish it around etc. dont break the filter paper.
3. Resoak the mush in hot water (not boiling but scolding hot), stir around, steep for 15mins. Filter,strain,press out them juices
4. Resoak the mush in hot water, steep for 20 mins, adding a squirt of citric/ascorbic acid every 5 mins and stirring. Filter and strain (french press ftw) (this step is only good if you want to drink your tea, coz lemon/lime makes the smoke kind of nasty. So drink this and use previous 2 soaks for the smokin goo. unless you use pure form acids, dont use that for smokable product.
Compare the color the liquid from each soak&strain use that as reference for future batches. The darker and more bitter it gets, the more potent it is.
5. If you need to store it, freeze it. It will spoil and smell really sick in room temp in 2-3 days.
6. With the help of a vacuum pump/aspirator and a vacuum adapter in a boiling flask you need evaporate the water below morphine breakdown point, you can also use dessicants but they take a while on their own and that liquid goes bad pretty fast in room temps.
7. Here you got few options, if your pods were high potency from the start, your goo might be good to go at this point. However, chances are that it was not, and the taste is nothing like sliced latex opium. You should refine your product, if you cant find out/figure out how then you wont be able to do it anyways. Then you can use another batch of pod tea and a simple process (that as i found out wont work very well without a nice >$100 ph meter) you can precipitate out all the nasty sugars,fats,chlorophyl etc and keep the calcium morphenate, and then basify it. Use that to "lace" the goo from the first process to make the already good opium into the 2-hit-OD shit :D
