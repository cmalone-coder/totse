I quite like coffee and as such only drink it black and never add sugar. I've tried the kopi luwak and camocim jacu bird beans but found them lacking body and although nutty, too acidic for my palate. I don't necessarily blame the beans or processing for this but believe they were not roasted darkly enough, I have a problem with roasters who are frightened of the ominous second crack and leave the beans a medium-dark tan with few if any oils.
However there is an excellent roaster in Ireland(of all places) who sources direct from farmers and produces excellent full bodied dark roasts of India's Monsoon Malabar and Brazil's Brazilian Santos; my all time favourite being Monsoon Malabar which I drink every day made with a single cup italian quite expensive but excellent espresso kettle.
