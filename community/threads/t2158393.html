InuYasha (help)
Yeah so I used to never be able to get into anime until I sat down really high and watched this show one day. This show fucking amazes me and I love it, I ended up having to go download a bunch of episodes so I could watch it on my own schedule instead of staying up until 4:30AM to watch it on adult swim because school started up.

Why do I like this show you ask? For a cartoon the characters are so deep and the story is so well written. The setting and plot are pretty interesting and fun. I really can't think of anything I don't like about it...

c) Can you recommend me some similar animes? I can't get enough of this show.
