Screen Resolution
I'm running Linux Mint Bianca and it won't give me an option to run my screen resolution past 1024x768. I went into

Code:
 sudo gedit /etc/X11/xorg.conf
and added

Code:
 Section "Screen"

Identifier "Screen0"

Device "Videocard0"

Monitor "Monitor0"

DefaultDepth 24

Option "metamodes" "1280x1024 +0+0; 800x600 +0+0; 640x480 +0+0"

SubSection "Display"

Depth 24

Modes "1600x1200" "1280x1024" "1024x768" "800x600" "640x480"

EndSubSection
but in the options, it doesn't display anything past 1024x768.

What did I do wrong?

I'm running an NVIDIA Corporation NV43 [GeForce 6600] graphics card and my monitor is a NEC Multisync LCD 1810x.

Re: Screen Resolution
You will probably want to explicitly specify HorizSync and VertRefresh rates in your Section "Monitor" section of xorg.conf and set them according to your LCD panel.

The important parts of your log file are these:
Code:
(II) NV(0): Supported VESA Video Modes:

(II) NV(0): 720x400@70Hz

(II) NV(0): 720x400@88Hz

(II) NV(0): 640x480@60Hz

(II) NV(0): 640x480@67Hz

(II) NV(0): 640x480@72Hz

(II) NV(0): 640x480@75Hz

(II) NV(0): 800x600@56Hz

(II) NV(0): 800x600@60Hz

(II) NV(0): 800x600@72Hz

(II) NV(0): 800x600@75Hz

(II) NV(0): 832x624@75Hz

(II) NV(0): 1024x768@87Hz (interlaced)

(II) NV(0): 1024x768@60Hz

(II) NV(0): 1024x768@70Hz

(II) NV(0): 1024x768@75Hz

(II) NV(0): 1280x1024@75Hz

(II) NV(0): 1152x870@75Hz
and this:
Code:
(II) NV(0): Generic Monitor: Using hsync range of 28.00-51.00 kHz

(II) NV(0): Generic Monitor: Using vrefresh range of 43.00-60.00 Hz
As you can see the highest resolution supported at the maximum setting of 60Hz is 1024x768.

My machines do DDC probes quite well under Fedora, I can get resolutions up to something insane like 1600x1475 or something without any specification of HorizSync and VertRefresh rates..
I use 1024x768 myself though.
