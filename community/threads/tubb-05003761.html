hoffman discovered it. April 16, 1943

kesey (robert hunter was also a part of those cia tests) founded the 'acid tests' back in the mid 60s. they were crazy parties basically, where everyone got dosed. kesey and his group 'the merry pranksters' had a very before its time multimedia kinda set up. theyd have microphones in one room, but the speakers in another. so one person could be in one room yelling, and people in another room hear it. they had all kinds of taper recorders set up around the place, lots of freky lights and all that shit. the grateful dead was the house band towards the end of the parties, but the very beginning of their career. after this 2 main producers of LSD emerged. Owsley (who was also the deads soundman) and The Brotherhood of Eternal Love; famous for the 'orange sunshine' blotters. timothy leary was close to on of the BOEL chemists, Nick Sand. Sand got busted in st. louis in 73 for having some LSD labs set up, around the same time that Ronald Stark was busted with a lab in belgium. Ronald Starks name also came up recently, when the group of people who produced most of the acid we used to get was busted in Kansas with the capacity to make around 60 million doses. how or why his name came up im not sure. this is why we dont see much acid anymore.

back to leary, he discovered psychedelics in 1960, shrooms to be exact. he went to jail in 70 for distribution i think, but escaped. he was then busted in afghanistan in 73 for that. he got out in 76 and continued to the the main guru and supporter of psychedelics until he died just a few years ago.
