What kind of Oxy is it? OxyContin brand or Roxi? I've heard all sorts of myths surrounding heating oxys but I do not heat them. My reasoning is that there is a waxy material in the controlled release pills. Heating wax doesn't sound good to me.

Just make sure your water is distilled and sterile. If your unsure about sterility boil the water beforehand and then add the drug and mix while warm. I use a sterile Q-tip because it seems like a tighter rolled up cotton would filter better than a little ball of it. The solution in the rig should be CLEAR. NO CLOUDINESS AT ALL. If it is cloudy refilter it. If there is a dye in the pill like some of the roxi's then the water will be colored but CLEAR. I can't stress that enough. You still aren't getting all the fillers even if it is clear but it's alot safer.

You have a small rig. I use 1unit of water per mg of pill plus a little extra. So if I was shooting an 80 I would fill it with 1cc of water and probably only get back 60-70 units after filtering. The shit left in the spoon is stuff you don't want so don't think you aren't getting all your drug. There is some leftover but the binders aren't worth those few mgs.

Point the syringe needle up, flick it, push until a drop comes out and go to town. I usually sterilize not only my injection site but my needle as well. Remember, wipe in one direction otherwise you're spreading shit back and forth.

To prevent a skin pop, which is what causes most pill related abscesses to my understanding, I flag frequently as I shoot. I flag the first time and shoot 20-30 units, flag again, then shoot more, flag again then finish the job. Keep in mind I'm shooting 100mgs at a time or more. If it stops flagging stop shooting because you aren't in the vein.

Hopefully that helps you and anybody else out. I don't condone shooting pills or anything that isn't meant for shooting, because it is very bad for you over the long term. A few pills shouldn't do too much damage.

Edit: As far as veins go I've never actually used the one on the underside of my forearm because that seems awkward. I've had no problems shooting in the tops of my forearms, and the old standby in the crook of the arm is the easiest if you've never injected before. If you're using a fresh needle you shouldn't track too bad.

Take a belt and loop it around your arm and pull tight. The vein in the crook of your elbow should be sticking out big.
