Re: Sig Pro
I test fired one at the local shooting club some time ago. I only fired it very briefly, though, because the shells were about $2 each. Anyway, the recoil didn't strike me as very bad. It is more of a pusher than a kicker, and I've fired .357 revolvers that were worse with regards to felt recoil.

It jams a lot if you don't grip it firmly enough, and if you limpwrist just a little bit, it will either jam or eject the spent brass straight into your face. IMHO it is a good gun within its relatively small envelope, but it is not an ideal carry gun as depicted by Hollywood.
