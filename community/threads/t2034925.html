Aliens fucked with us in the past?
The first thing which I would like to point out, is that I do not personally believe in any of the following theories; personally, I believe the past to be as mysterious and as full of as many 'paths' as the future. Because of this, I do believe in the possibility of such things I am about attempt to discuss.

That out of the way, there have been many things discovered which seem to indicate humans have had interaction with intelligent alien races thousands of years ago.

Such encounters would explain many of the mysterious things written in numerous religious texts around the world. They would explain mysterious, selective advancements in comparatively 'primitive' societies.

They would help to explain the existence of now-radioactively weak deserts of glass in the middle east. Or the 'atomic wars' and 'flying machines' described in texts like the Mahabharata.

I would like to hear the opinions of other people. Especially, of course, those interested in extra-terrestrial explanations of parts of religion and myths and those with reasons why such beliefs should be considered crap.
