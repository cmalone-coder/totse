I don't know the whole story as of yet about the shooting, but christ. I don't think half the people really give a shit, they just want an excuse to loot and plunder. As far as I know they protested at the guys funeral, then all hell broke loose as more protesters decided to argue their point with civil unrest and terrorism.

People that pull that type of shit should just be shot, I don't care the color or their skin, just that attitude. A white cop did bad, (but they complain about black cops too) so I'm gonna go bust up a 7-11. Makes sense don't it?

Actually I'm pissed off because of the division of the races that is still going on. I'm not sure when it started to increase again, I think it was Clinton, with his apology to the black community for slavery. Perhaps a noble idea, but with unintended consequences. After that I remember talking to some, not many, so I can't make this a generalization, blacks who had the attitude that all whites should apologize. Perhaps that attitude, started by a white guy, caused an increase in racial tensions. Who knows. I am empathetic to their ancestors yes. But why on earth they deserve an apology for something that was never brought down on them from person that never practiced such a thing is beyond me. The president said he was sorry, so on his behalf should I? No, I don't share many, if any beliefs with that guy, not knowing him on a personal bases.

Regardless I do believe that govt workers should be held to higher standards, more strict laws, and harsher punishments. Sure they are only human, but it's too easy to abuse, and besides if govt officials were so much better than the common man (as we hope them to be), then such a thing shouldn't be a problem. If it was murder, fry the guy. If it was an accident, take him off duty, and perhaps a manslaughter charge. But like I said I don't know the exact situation. Though I intend to find out. The whole thing is just sickening. Any community doing such things just make themselves look worse. In Cincinatti, they have made it that every black person is a suspect for something and many will just assume they are guilty. Because they went about things in a very wrong and discouraging way. Terrorism only proves you are what you are protesting against. I'm done for now, but still pissed.
