Read before posting in Bad Ideas
Before you make a post on Bad Ideas it is essential that you read and comply with the following advice.



1. Search before you post.

Search the main TOTSE archives here:

http://www.totse.com/en/search/index.html

Search the TOTSE forums here:

http://www.totse.com/bin/bbs/search.cgi?action=intro

Search the web here: www.google.com (http://www.google.com) or here: www.alltheweb.com (http://www.alltheweb.com)

There are two main reasons for conducting a search: 1) Seedng if the topic was already discussed to avoid posting information that has already been shared, or if it is new information on a topic, posting it in an older pertinent thread to avoid multiple threads on the same topic. 2) If you are asking a question, you will likely find the results faster if you take a few minutes to do the search yourself, versus waiting for someone else to see your post and maybe do the same search for you.



2. Post in the appropriate forum.

Before posting, ask yourself: does this thread belong in Bad Ideas, or would it be better suited in another forum?

*If your post is about building explosives, pyrotechnics, or generally 'how to blow shit up' it belongs Backyard Ballistics ( http://www.totse.com/bin/bbs/forumdisplay.cgi?action=topics&forum=Backyard+Ballistics&number=19&DaysPrune=45&LastLogin )

*If your post is about weapons or how to fight, it belongs in Weapons & Combat ( http://www.totse.com/bin/bbs/forumdisplay.cgi?action=topics&forum=Weapons+and+Combat&number=29&DaysPrune=45&LastLogin )

*If your post is about what you might call 'hacking' (although that is most likely erroneous in the first place), your post belongs in Technophiliacs & Technophiles ( http://www.totse.com/bin/bbs/forumdisplay.cgi?action=topics&forum=Technophiliacs+|AMP|+Technophiles&number=11&DaysPrune=45&LastLogin )

*If your post is about expressing anger at a particular person/group of persons/thing it belongs in Bitch & Moan ( http://www.totse.com/bin/bbs/forumdisplay.cgi?action=topics&forum=Bitch+and+Moan&number=30&DaysPrune=45&LastLogin )

*And so forth...



3. Constructive criticism only.

If you don't have any constructive comments/compliments to make, resist the urge to flame or post derogatory comments. If you find flaws in the ideas presented by the poster, point them out in a respective way, and suggest alternative solutions.



4. Stay on topic.

If the original post is about some topic A do not shift the discussion to some unrelated topic B. However, if the topics are related you may of course discuss them in the same thread. For example, if the original post is about shoplifting do not ask things like 'where are you from?' On the other hand, if the post is about breaking and entering, a reply about overcoming attack dogs is acceptable.



5. No personal information/messages

Do not post things like phone numbers, fax numbers, private addresses, etc.

Do not make posts directed to specific members, unless the topic/question is of interest to the membership of the forum. For example, an acceptable question would be, "...could you please explain so and so in greater detail from the article you submitted to TOTSE." An unacceptable question would be, "Hey ... can you email me?"



6. Make coherent posts (use a spellchecker/dictionary)

Before posting, spell check the message that you have typed by pasting it into a word processor program such as MS Word. If you are unsure about what certain words mean, check www.dictionary.com. (http://www.dictionary.com.) Posting coherent messages that everyone can understand saves everyone a lot of time of having to ask you to rewrite the post.
