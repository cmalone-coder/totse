How will we know what happens to us in the afterlife?
How will we know? Reading books that could contain distortions? Or projecting our minds deep within our spirits? Or are our bodies recycled - mind brainwashed, when we die?

Think it like a metaphor. What would happen if a couch potato fell out of his living room and got plopped at the starting lines of the olympics? He would be at the wrong place! He is fat, can't run well, and has to... RACE???

This is what I think happens to you in the afterlife. If you aren't spiritually developed - your concsiousness gets back into the circle of life - manifests itself as a baby, and loses its experiences.

Or, if a man is aware at a point that he can get hold of this state - he might be able to travel around in the nothingness. Remember, an article on this site says that people have communicated with the dead with this radio. They said the dead said they live here - on Earth (invisible to us) - and do not know time or place. Lastly, this guy claimed to have been contacted by a ghost that said the afterlife is cold, and there is a gap between life and relife. That ghost said he is trying to help save the earth from some kind of disaster.

Or, what if we are as inanimate as a computer? What if we are computers - and our consciousness is an operating system controlled by neurons and random processes. This makes sense... but I have a feeling that operating system is controlled by a user - the soul - which contains the spirit.

Aliens could know more about death - and all that shit - but can aliens mislead us? Angels/Gods can deceive people easy - so why not aliens?

Finally, what if life is a test we have to pass so we can graduate "whatever" and go to the other side? Let us say we are a species that needs to learn EXPERIENCES and practise GOODWILL - and choose between good and evil? After this meaningless life, we awake. How meaningless is the universe? If HTML is the universe - SGML is the true reality.

Do you guys have any other ideas?
