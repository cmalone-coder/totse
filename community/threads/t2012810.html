Re: I am going to try out for rugby
Make sure you release the ball when you got to ground.(get tackled)
Make sure when you pass you look at the person your passing to BEFORE the ball leaves you hands.
If your mate gets tackled and your one of the closest to him, HELP HIM.
If a guy runs at you, dont wimp out. The harder you go the less it will hurt. + everyone loves a big hit.
Stay DEEP, the deeper you are the less pressure there is. You want to catch that ball when you already jogging, running slowly will get you snapped.

Don't lift weights, do push ups and those sorts of things, weights if for bulking up and you don't want that if your fast.
If you are dead KEEN on bulking up do light weights but high reps. Ideally you want to be strong, fit and nimble.
