Hypocratic Christian Rock
I am watching this show on CNN, and the program's name is God Warriors. They go into the lives of a couple of Christian believers.

They interview this girl, around 16 or 17 years of age. She is helping out with a Christian rock concert, called "Battlecry".

She says, quote "God is fighting for us, against the enemy. It is our duty to be soldiers in his war against hate."

Now I pause for a second. These are Christians, advocating war. Isn't the definition of a Christian as someone who "love's their enemy, and turns the other cheek" ?

I see it a lot in fact. Christians advocating war against those who don't agree with their faith. Now, this is not all, and not a majority of Christians, but isn't it a form of hypocrisy, advocating war?
