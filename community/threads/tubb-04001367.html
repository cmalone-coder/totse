Sigh. I refuse to feel an obligation to visit here more often... even if I did talk spiffy into coming back

Anyway to the topic at hand. The issue here is one of a relationship, *NOT* one of sex. At this age it would be quite normal for you to be going out 18 months or 2 years before the question of sex even comes up. In that context... If you ask her out now she'll either say yes or no. If you don't ask, she may regard you as a good friend but you'll pass outside the potential b/f sphere and into the "friend" sphere. She may start going out with someone else and... you may not get another chance to ask her.

If you do go out, let her set the pace as far as what you do goes. Let her know you're doing this. All relationships should be built on more than sex. IF you can talk openly to each other and trust each other... thats a great start. If she can do that with you, she'll tell you if she wants a kiss. The next step, deciding when to kiss her is yours. She might one day tell you she wants to cuddle. Fine. From then on, unless she tells you its uncomfortable, take the initiative and cuddle her when it feels right. This goes right the way up. When she decides she wants to try sex, if she wants to try it with you, fine. Its her choice and your choice. If she changes her mind at the last minute, thats fine too. Just support her, cuddle her and be there for her. Again this advice might not be usefull for a few years but... the same logic should apply even now. Offer to hold her hand, but don't force it. Ok, enough ranting on that. Soem other things to remember.
1) You may or may not have been ready for a relationship at 14. Think back. She may or may not be ready now. I wouldn't discount it on age though.
2) If you go out for 18 months and then decide to have sex... she'll be roughly how old you are now. At that point remember how you feel now about it. Don't patronise her. Maybe even write a note now about how you feel and if you are going out with her in 18 months... read it.
3) Protect her, but don't be over protective. She's her own person too.

Anyway good luck.
