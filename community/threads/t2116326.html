Re: Legality of "Vapor Lounges"
I'm not going to pretend to understand the intricacies of Canadian drug law, but I believe it boils down to two things. One, weed is mostly decriminalized, isn't it? I'm not sure if you guys have the same Federal/State breakdowns for drug laws, so perhaps it's only in some provinces, but whatever the case is, it brings me to point two, which is that what regulations you guys do have seem to be inconsistently enforced.

So are the lounges legal? It's probably a pretty gray area. I mean, setting up a place to smoke weed is probably technically illegal, but given the climate that seems to pervade up there, it's probably one of those things that's just overlooked. Even if someone wanted to bust them for it, I imagine the proprietors have enacted some sort of plausible deniability measures. "Oh, no, officer, this place isn't for smoking weed, it's just a club for people to hang out in. If anyone is smoking marijuana, we don't know about, nor do we encourage it... We're just in the back trying to run a business, scout's honor!"

I mean, go to a nightclub. How much fucking cocaine is going through any club on any night? A lot. But the owners aren't (generally) going to be busted because they didn't create a place to use drugs. They created a legitimate business (a night club in this case, a "social lounge" in your case), that a few bad apples happened to use as a place to do drugs. As long as they have the facade of not supporting the drug use and taking "appropriate measures" to ensure no laws are broken, business owners tend to have a certain level of protection from that.
