Getting a Valium Prescription (long)(tl;dr ver at bottom)
I get anxiety pretty badly and it makes my life very stressful. I stress out about many things in life but the thing I worry about most is school. I stress out about school so much that I literally get sick to my stomach (having IBS which is triggered by my stress doesn't help either).

Well, as a result of not being able to deal with the stress of school, upon other things, I began smoking pot everyday. I love pot. It gets rid of my anxiety, my stomach pain, and helps cope with stress.

Problem is, I got busted (last night I spent the night in jail actually) for possession and paraphernalia and have to go and do PreTrial Intervention (like rehab and community service) so that I can get the charges expunged. It's costing me $600 dollars for the PTI plus $1000 dollars in tickets. Not only that, I don't want to take a chance at getting busted again because I'll lose my federal financial aid and my state scholarships (which a requirement is not having drug charges) and will be unable to finish college.

My dilemma: do I keep smoking pot, the miracle drug I think has kept me in college (3.9GPA if you think I'm a dumb pothead college kid), and risk my college education.

OR

Is there somthing else I can do?

Then I remembered, I love Valium. I used to eat these as a self-medication when I was into pills and worked wonders. It helped me immensely and I think it is the right choice for what I want. Not only that, if I can get my doctor to prescribe it to me, I have my cake and can eat it too.

It's not as if I don't need it; I believe my anxiety is bad enough to warrant medication. I don't think I can deal with the stresses of life and not want to entertain the idea of blowing my brains out.

So, I've been doing some research into what symptoms get prescribed Valiums.

From http://www.mentalhealth.com/drug/p30-v01.html

"The short-term symptomatic management of mild to moderate degrees of anxiety in conditions dominated by tension, excitation, agitation, fear or aggressiveness, such as may occur in psychoneurosis, anxiety reactions due to stress conditions and anxiety states with somatic expression."

When I describe my own anxiety, it is similar to what I have seen in the indicators. It seems Valium is for short-term use; i.e. when someone is being overcome by anxiety, you pop one and feel better quick.

So I figure my description of my anxiety to my doctor will be something like:

I begin by worrying over some assignment or test and then begin to obsess over the work. When I get nervous like this my stomach flips, I start to get a nervous movement of my legs, and I can't study or work because I can't calm down. This stress begins to seep into other areas of my life and have generally led me into a downward spiral and I just cannot stop thinking about all the things I worry about. This makes me worry more, thus completing the vicious circle.

I think it's important to note that this doctor treated me for depression about 5 years ago with Zoloft. However if he begins to prescribe it, I will say that I didn't like how it made me feel (jittery).

So, I need any comments, tips, revisions of my plan. Personal experience also appreciated greatly.


TL;DR Version:

I need to get my doctor to prescribe me Valiums, what should I say?
