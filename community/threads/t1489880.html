Re: Shit. Major headfuck of a problem.
There are some things, even big things, that two people can willfully ignore in each other so they get on well.

This is rarely one of them.

Forget your friends for a second. We're not talking about politics here, either. We're talking about a basic belief in the nature of humanity. If she remains as racist as she is now, it's going to show, regardless of how much she tries to hide it from you. Likewise, your open-mindedness is going to show, probably a good deal more because you don't have to worry about any other people (for the most part) giving you grief. Racists often have to tone their shit down to save themselves trouble, even if it's only because they're not in the mood for it.

The best possible outcome is that, over time, your solid beliefs and freer mind will osmose into her, softening her stance against other races. It's a nice thought, and honestly, I think it'd be worth it if you knew for certain that'd be the end result. But there's a flip side- what happens if she's the stronger one and starts to influence you? Or you both influence each other towards more of a middle ground (which shouldn't even exist in terms of racism)? In the latter case, instead of one open-minded person and one hardcore racist, you've got two mildly racist people. That seems worse.

Truth be told, I don't think any of that's likely. If she didn't have those friends around, positive exposure to other races and your attitude would almost assuredly bring her around towards your way of thinking, but she does. You have your friends, too, and most of society behind you as well, so you're not likely to fall to her side.

I don't see why it can't work for awhile, but given enough time (and no other reasons for a breakup), this is probably going to cause a problem. It damn near always does. If you're one of the few who can make things work, more power to you.
