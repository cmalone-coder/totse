Truthfully there is nothing any of us can say that will make you feel better.

Be glad that you can't go and beat the shit out of the guy. I hate to be cliche, but you you are the better man.

I know that numb feeling. It's beyond crying. When a relationship ends like that, it is like your soul is gone. The relationship has been a defining thing to you for months, you have put your time, love and effort into it and now it is gone. It's almost as though the floor has been pulled from under your feet.

Don't make this an excuse to be evil. Make it an excuse to become a better person. Everyone always talks about defining moments, and this is one of them. You have to react to it. You can either get angry, bottle it up, and then bad will come about, or you can be upset about it, talk to people about it, and give it time and end up in a better place. I think you are actually making it better by talking about somewhere. Do you have someone that you feel comfortable talking about it too in person?

Remember that getting over a relationship takes time. It can take months. The numbness doesn't just disappear. Being able to love and trust again seem impossible, but as time goes on, it will get better.

What are your creative outlets? Try writing music, poems, stories, painting or drawing. You could be surprised what you can do with the emotion. It will help you to try it as long as you dont get frustrated and let things flow.

Please talk to someone about it. Talking will make it better, I promise. Just letting the feeling out will calm you down. If you ever need to talk, just let me know that you want to talk and I'll give you my info.
