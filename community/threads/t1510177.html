I can't pretend that she's particularly important to me now...I barely know her. I can set my sights on someone else today and she'll just shift to the back of my mind. Ideally I'd be hoping to "get to know her more" (that's such a vague, generic expression), and then we'd gradually end up hanging out again over the next couple weeks, and then I'd be able to have the upper hand. But where do I start with the whole "get to know each other more" bullshit? I truly need to be walked through this, sadly.
