Illegal bar.

Now my basement is not used. It has pre-historic couches and chairs that collect dust. My house is set up so that you park around the back, enter the basement, and go upstairs. My grandparents would probably be thrilled by the idea, since they love hanging out with other drunks and drinking themselves stupid. I love to drink as well
as most of my friends.

So I got the idea to renovate(?) the basement into a bar. I figured I'd purchase or rent two couches, a few sofa/lounge chairs, a pool table, a card table with four or five chairs and of course a long bar with stools.

Now the problem with my plan is the merchandise. No one can easily predict if the whole group is going to want Budweiser or Miller or Smirnoff, or a mixture of them all.
So my friend figured we would just steal the stuff. That's still not really the problem.

I want to maximize profits while avoiding police apprehension.

Here is the rundown of my future bar.

Pool table:$1.00 a game.(it's build into the table)
Beer:$1.00 a beer. ($1.75 and up at all other bars.)
Shots:$1.00.
Cigarettes:$3.00 all brands.($3.35-$5.00 here)
