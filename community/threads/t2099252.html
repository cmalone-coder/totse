because you have a brain.

but really, without any more cheap shots at christianity, here's why.

The first step to being saved is hearing the word of god, so you can realized that you're lost. Then you must believe that Jesus was the son of god... obviously, because if you didn't believe that you would go to hell and that's not salvation. Third step is to repent. You have to realize that you've sinned and you have to change your ways. But if you try too hard, and with the intention of being saved, you might die. But it's okay, if you die for Jesus, you have a get out of hell free pass. You're more than halfway there when you confess your belief. But somebody has to be there to witness it, because god is too busy doing other things to listen to you. After that you have to get baptized. By the meaning of the word, you're supposed to be fully immersed. This is where most people mess up. Raise your hand if you were nearly drowned as a baby when you were baptized. Most people just get water poured on their face. Anyway, to truly be saved, you have to follow the examples set by the bible and go completely underwater. I can't find a rule against nose plugs though. Finally, you have to remain faithful. Being saved once doesn't mean you'll be saved forever. If you stop being faithful you'll have to do it all over again.

So... to all you newly saved people out there... tell me, were you baptized? If not, you're probably still going to hell. Try reading the bible before believing it. Basically, you cannot think logically and be saved. You can't ask questions about contradictions in the bible, you can't pick and choose which parts to take literally and which to take figuratively, and you can't have your own opinion about it if it contradicts christ's teachings in any way. So yeah, you couldn't be saved because you use your thought process.
