Re: A new Official Thread?? Mods?
Optimal dosages from person to person vary so widely that any attempt to standardize them would be pointless and possibly dangerous to some. You (OP) say 40mg hydro and 30mg oxy - those might be right for you, but most would consider those pretty high for first time doses of either.

It also needs to be specified whether it's a guy or girl in question, because dosages vary hugely between guys and girls. If you give a first time male user 30mg oxy, he might have a nice time, or he might be uncomfortable, maybe feel pretty sick. If instead of a guy, it's a 110lb girl, everything is totally different. She might be VERY sick, and though she probably won't die, it's safe to say that the dosage was way high.

As for mushrooms, there really isn't any way to calibrate your dose effectively unless you've taken mushrooms from the same batch before. Potency varies from batch to batch, to the point where an eighth might be too much, or not enough. I've had amazing trips off half eighths, and unimpressing ones off eighths.

The only safe way to calibrate your dose is to start off with something that is way low, and work up from there. Since most people don't want to waste their drugs that way, they need to figure out how to estimate their own dosage based on their individual circumstances. It is impossible for somebody on the internet to determine what your (as a first time user) dose should be, because he or she has no idea how much you weigh, whether you're a guy or a girl, how low or high your natural tolerance is, etc...

I guess what I'm trying to say is, be careful giving out dose recommendations to strangers on the internet. Though well intentioned, you could do some damage if you don't know enough about the individual.
