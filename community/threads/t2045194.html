A while ago I realized that in the grand scheme of things, I don't matter. People die every day, and the world is no different because they have lived. For the longest time I didn't understand what the point of living was; you just spend your days dealing with the utter impossiblity and hopelessness of the world. Yeah, I know that's pretty emo, but I think most people with any kind of emotional complexity go through a stage that is very much like what you are feeling.

The thing that made that change for me is the realization that we are each utterly in control of our own lives. There are so many awful things in the world that cannot be changed. You can't control the world, the only thing you can control is your
self. Don't live your life based on any external standards or ideals, as cliche as that sounds, and don't hold yourself up to any standards but your own. Fashion a world for yourself, create the life you want, and become the person you want to be.

That probably sounds like whole lot of nonsense, it's such a vague concept to explain. Perhaps you should look into existentialism. If you don't kow what it is, it's basically a philosophical movement which claims that individual human beings create the meanings of their own lives. I hope I could be of some help. Good luck man.
