Re: Salvia?
All you need to use salvia is salvia smoke, that's it.

That said, bic lighters are fine, some probably like using torch for the hype.

And a bong is easier, because you get more smoke at once, which is what you need with salvia.

The only thing you really need, is to hold the smoke in for 30 seconds. If you don't hold the smoke in for a good amount of time 15-30 seconds, you won't break through, and if you don't hold the smoke in at all, you barely or won't get high.

And btw, the high is many things.

At first you notice a sensation of being pulled off to the side, they call this "salvia gravity", you literally start to lean over to one side, wondering why no else can feel this. Then your perceptions begin to narrow, not being able to remember what you're doing or why you're doing it, looking down at your shoe in this beginning state you will forget where you are and when you look back up at the room will be shocked to realize that you are actually in the middle of doing something (prepare yourself at this moment for what comes next).

Usually, the body sensations will spread from a gravity type pull, to feeling like your consciousness is actually physically (spiritually?) exiting your body through this "perception", in this time you'll usually go out.

That can happen by either "going in between" objects in your immediate vision, for instance seeing a row of chairs and entering into the space between the chairs and the space between that space etc etc, until you get into a world of objects built upon a repetitive, extremely colorful, and two-dimensionally simple, yet oddly consciously active space which seems to exist "between" or "to the side of" this world.

Or you will simply black out with your vision switching directly over, with a small blank in between, to a dream world or a world of shapes and colors.

You can't communicate with anyone at this time.

Your consciousness is light years away from the state you were in when you inhaled the smoke, and it will seem like you will return in light years from the "now" moment that you reach in the void in time and space that you go to on salvia.

Basically, for most people it's a mind-flup and you really have to know what you're getting into to be able to enjoy it and do it again.

Just make sure you take enough, usually just a little pinch of 20x or about a small bowl of 5, but don't over do it the first time. This post can't explain anything but a vague dream of what the salvia experience is really like. You might lose all 3-dimensional perception thinking you are a flat entity stuck "within" a two-dimensional slice of reality/time, seeing your entire history behind you with your future ahead of you. Knowing more than seeing./rant

It's just too much to try to explain. And it all happens in a few minutes.
