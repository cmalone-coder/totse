The Trippy Thread
Quote:
trippy
adj

trippier, trippiest
1. Said of music, film camera work, etc: producing psychedelic effects that are similar to those experienced after taking a drug such as LSD.
No, it isn't the most straightforward or all-inclusive definition, but it fits. Then again, the parameters for what defines trippy aren't especially clear set themselves, trippiness is only in the eye of the individual. But without any further rambling, here it is- the Trippy Thread, the ultimate compendium of trippiness, guaranteed to have something for even the most discerning Totseans. But remember, in order for the Trippy Thread to be kept alive, members must actively contribute to it!
