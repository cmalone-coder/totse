Re: vending machine fuck
If you own a property like a store you can arrange for Coca-Cola to put a machine in front of or inside your store. They give you a percentage I think, or maybe a monthly rate. But since you don't own a store, I'm pretty sure they don't let people set up their own Coke machines. You're using their logos and product name and all that, and most major companies keep strict control on that sort of stuff. I'm not sure how they would feel about you stocking a generic machine with Coke products for resell, though.

Along the same lines though, you can make a fuckton of money by getting snack machines and setting them up somewhere, with the property owner's permission of course. They will probably ask for a cut or a fee. One machine won't get you that much, but several throughout your area would. You just have to be willing to do all that driving around and restocking, not to mention dealing with all that change. There's a good chance your machines will be vandalized, stolen from, or broken, so be ready for that.
