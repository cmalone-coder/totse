Fishing for fish with milk jugs!
Hopefully most of you are familiar with this, if not it's an excellent lazyass way to catch a lot of good fish, especially catfishies.

Get a screw top milk jug (not the pop-top, trust me), and attach a two to six foot length of fishing line around the handle. Get your hook, tie it on with some nice, smelly bait (I recommend nearly anything dipped in tuna oil) and set in the middle of the lake. Check at least once a day.
