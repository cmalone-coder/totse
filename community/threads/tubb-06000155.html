Re: punks not dead!
Haha, op ivy quote - I love it. I listen to stuff life Good Riddance, Fury 66, Anti-Flag, Propagandhi, DKM, Dead Kennedys, D.I., Operation Ivy, both Subhumans, Sham, Out of Hand, Oi Polloi, Minor Threat, Guy Smiley, Dwarves, Descendents, Blitz, Bigwig, Adolescents, Agnostic Front, AFI, Bikini Kill, Cock Sparrer, DOA, DBS, etc etc etc. I like a lot of the pacifist/unity oriented political leftist bands that try to make a difference. It's good to see so many people agree with me that punk's not dead in light of how fasionable it is to argue to the contrary. In some ways I think modern punk is better than the 76-77 style stuff because a lot more of it then was about fashion and being an asshole. Anarchy was so poplular just because it would cause a lot of chaos and piss people off. People would die - Punk rock! People who think punk is dead aren't really looking for it. Lots of people are still running DIY labels, independently publishing fanzine, distributing, starting bands, writing, booking bands, and still generally giving a fuck. Even bands like Blink, Sum 41, Rancid, SR-71, Green Day, Bad Religion, Offspring, Pennywise, etc often serve as gateway bands to get people into the less commercial sutff. I started out listening to Nirvana and the Offspring, and it it wasn't for bands like that I wouldn't have gotten into the other bands I like more. I mean, I think the Ramones were total assholes who played mediocre music (and I'm sure I'll get shit for saying that), but if it wasn't for them, there wouldn't be a lot of the good punk bands that are around today. Uh, that's it
