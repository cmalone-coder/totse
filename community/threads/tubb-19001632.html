Re: Potassium carbonate
80g NaOH + 128g S ----> 110g Na2S2 + 22.4L SO2 + 22.4L H2S

112.2g KOH + 128g S ----> 142.2g K2S2 + 22.4L SO2 + 22.4L H2S

138.2g K2CO3 + 160g S ----> 284.4g K2S2 + 22.4L SO2 + 44.8L CO2

212g Na2CO3 + 160g S ----> 220g Na2S2 + 22.4L SO2 + 44.8L CO2

All of these reactions can be carried out by heating the two reactants dry. They can be done also by heating the two reactants in water, but that certainly is far from the best process. The best way to do it is to mix the reactants in denatured alcohol, and warm gently. Polysulfides are yellow compounds that are soluble in ethanol and water.
