Hotel theft.
So in about two weeks I am going to be traveling around a few countries staying in different hotels. I will be on holiday for about 14 days, staying in one hotel for no longer than 2 days at a time. So...thats roughly a minimum of 7 hotels.

Now as always I will be stealing as much as possible without getting caught. However how much can you steal without being billed?

I live with a few friends, so obviously I will steal towels, all the shit in the shower/bathroom, bathrobes etc. But how much do you think I could get away with without being billed. Seeing as I'm going to so many different hotels I don't need to go over the top in every one.

Last time I was in a Hilton hotel I figured it would be better to steal from the hotel store room than my room,,,,so followed the maids and found it. Stole from their carts as well. They were all Mexican so a substantial amount was probably stolen anyway by them.


What else do you think I should try and steal? I will be doing a hell of allot of pickpocketing. But I'm more interested in the hotel. On the last day I might go for a tv or something.

I could really do with some suggestions as to what to steal other than cheap little things. Ash trays/towels/robes etc

Anything is fair game in my opinion, so don't hold back.


Also....the employees of the hotels have those cards that allow entry to any room. How hard would it be to make one of them?


So basically does anyone have any experiences to share, or suggestions as to what to steal to make the most money or just the best shit available.




Finally...a tip for everyone.


Every year I meet up with my family and we go on holiday, we tend to go to the same hotel, however we have never ended up in the same room.

So...when you are at the hotel. Scan your card and make a clone. Now next time you are at the hotel, you have the room number and access key for one of the rooms.

Once you collect a few maybe you will begin to see a pattern. If you can clone the master then you're up for some serious looting.
