Re: How is this possible?
Assuming that this isn't a troll.

I went through the same thought processes once.

The feeling is like deja vu, except you can tell whats about to happen next too. Usually I would realise I was having deja vu, with that "memory" sensation, then "remember" what was about to happen too which was weird.

To be honest its probably a coincidence - millions of unremarkable things happen to us every day, which we don't notice, or forget about, because we basically don't give a shit. But when something seemingly meaningful occurs, we think "wow!". Its the coincidence/synchronicity thing - coincidences just happen. Sometimes people attach meaning to them but really its just the laws of chance - what are the odds that something really weird will happen - not much, but if you go through your daily tasks a million million times it becomes much more likely that, for example, the person you were thinking of rings your phone. You never stop and think "omg the person I WASNT thinking of just called".

This is similar to the "number 23" thing, I think. You see millions of numbers over the course of the day, but if they're not a 2 and a 3 next to each other (or some other meaningful string) then you wont notice them. It becomes ridiculously improbable that you WON'T notice the number 23 (or whatever) everywhere after thinking about it like this.
