Zipit personal IM device
Ive been poking around the anals of the internet (read: store websites) looking for some new device i could bring home, rip open and extend its functionality (or turn into some type of weapon) when i cam across the zipit z2 im something or another. It is touted as an open source alternative to using a computer for merely IMming, has a built in wifi card (no cellular bullshit ) and costs 50 bucks.

The thing runs linux and is open to dev work according to some websites. If its as open as Ive heard it is im going to get my hands on one. Of course I'll be reporting back with as much excruciating detail as possible.

Closer inspection revealed that a one year service plan is required for this thing. Further reading seems to tell me that I can bring the device home for 50 and then gets really vague on the specifics on what I can and cant do with it.

If anyone has ever held the device you can be of some help. Can you still IM without buying the contract? Do I have to pay the 110 bucks for the contract in store or can I just have 50 plus tax and thats that?

The thing looks fucking awesome and all I'd have to do is run a few (albeit creatively programmed) services on my home/office PC and I'll be set to do anything on the go so long as Im in range of an open wifi network.

Any word on if the card is good for cracking WEP? The CPU seems a bit slow for that but with a large enough storage card I could cart the data home and then proc it all there.

Thanks in advance for your response.

It's sort of a mix between a DS and a PSP and a laptop.
