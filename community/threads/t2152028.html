What's a pandora?
It's sort of a mix between a DS and a PSP and a laptop. Basically, there was this handheld system the GP32 that all these game nerds loved in the 90's, and it had lots of homebrew games and whatnot on it. It was succeeded by the GP2X, which was even more homebrew-friendly, ran Gnu/Linux, was fully free software, etc.

Sadly, the company that made them either discontinued them recently or went belly-up, and now the successor to the GP2X will be the Pandora, this gaming system on CRACK that's been developed by a few GP2X community members.

http://openpandora.org/
