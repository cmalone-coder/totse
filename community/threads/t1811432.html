Re: pact with the devil
Considering "the devil" is a character in mythological stories there is no way to make a pact with "the devil". The devil doesn't exist. The character, itself originates from the Persian demi-goddess: Angra Mainyu, though some might say Lilith.

It's incredibly debateable whether or not even the arch angel Lucifer exists(ed), since he's an angel and no one knows if angels really exist. If he does, though, and the story about him is true, then there is a particular being you could try to make a pact with.

But, even according to the bible Lucifer can't do anything without gods permission anyways. So, you'd basically be making a pact with a figment of the imagination that has absolutely no power because he's controlled by an even bigger figment of the imagination.

Cheers,
