You can't see through the domes with a camera.

They're not magic bubbles that only a lens can see through, you know. If anything, the camera has less light sensitivity than your eye and will have an even worse time of it. The domes are tinted on one side, and the only reason the cameras inside them can see out is because there is enough light outside the dome for the camera to take in. You can't see the camera because by the time the light comes in (and is dimmed by the coloring on the dome), reflects off the camera (dimmed again), and is passes back through the dome (dimmed yet again) there isn't enough of it left for you to see what's in there.

Sometimes you can shine a laser pointer through there and see if it reflects off the camera inside. Sometimes you can't. Either way, firing lasers at the security cameras is bound to annoy someone.
