Let's be honest (and I ask this of everyone).. if you knew, were just about 100% guaranteed with no hope of it being otherwise, that being honest about what you'd done would end the relationship and quite possibly cause no small amount of hurt to the other person, and you knew in your heart of hearts that despite your past screw-up, you loved that person through and through, would you still do it? Lies hurt more than apologies, but not all falsehoods are discovered, and how often does truth hurt more than lies?
