It would certainly level out the trip at hand a little bit but it's not going to help you for any longer than it's pharmacokinetic duration, which, as far as therapeutic effects are concerned, isn't more than a few hours. Even taking into account half-life of the clonazepam, you've only got about 50 hours, tops, and likely less than that.

I'm not extremely well-versed about HPPD but from what little I do know of it I'm very much inclined to believe than any exposure to hallucinogens is probably a very inadvisable thing to do. Sure, you might not come out of it worse for the wear, but if you do, you're going to be kicking yourself in the ass for coming to believe that a few hours tripping was worth the risk of permanent augmentation to a preexisting psychological condition such as yours.
