Re: bra sizes
40E (same as 40DD) is kinda hefty, actually. Maybe you misunderstood the size? Perhaps her overall bust measurement is 40", meaning she might be about a 36DD?

A quick lesson on how bras are sized: The number refers to the ribcage measurement in inches, just under the swell of the breast. So a measurement of 40" is getting on the tubby side, unless she's exceptionally tall and "big boned".

The letter refers to the difference between the ribcage measurement and the actual breast measurement at the fullest point. If the breast measurement is less than the ribcage, it's an AA. If the measurements are the same, it's an A. 1" more in the breast is a B, 2" more is a C, 3" more is a D, 4" more is a DD or E, 5" more is a DDD or F. And so on, more or less.

This might vary slightly among manufacturers and countries.
