You can get seeds. Even the strains you want. What matters here is the breeder. Any 'ol asshole can say 'hey, I just bred some [insert strain here] with [insert strain here]. Female seed are made by taking a female plant and then, with some sort of chemicals/hormones I would suppose, induce it to grow some male parts. These pollen sacks grow and then open, which the breeder then collects the pollen and places onto the pistils of the same plant. The resulting seeds are all female.

Sort of.

These are genetically hermaphrodites, which display mostly (99.95%) female characteristics. If the breeder knows what he is doing, you can hit that percentile. Most often, you get some dipshit who doesn't know what the fuck he's doing, or does it sloppy, and you get seeds that when grown out either are females that turn male, resulting in shit bud with seeds, or are plain out male.
