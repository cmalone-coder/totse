Yes and no.
There is black/dark brown sticky tar, which is mostly seen on the west coast.

There is also some brown powder heroin which is nothing more than tar that is cut with baking powder; it'll turn back into tar if you heat it/breath on it.

There is some brown/tan colored powder that is just that - powder. Quality varies I hear.

Then there is the white dope. Being white doesn't necessarily mean purer dope, as it could be cut with anything that is powder & white in color. Alot of people call this white colored dope "china" no matter whats in it.

Alot of different varieties/forms.
