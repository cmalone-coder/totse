Removing a bad taste from marihuana.
Here's what happened.
Sometime around last October I had a shitload of chron, and when I get a LOT of something, I have a habit of hiding small bits of it around my house/property so I will find it some time when I have nothing. The happiness from finding something when you have none, far exceeds the happiness from knowing for a while that you've got weed, or whatever else and just having it. At least for me.

Anyways, for the longest time it's been in my head that there's no hidden weed left anywhere, i found it all months ago and I didn't remember putting any anywhere else, until about 3 days ago when i looked in a 'Bounce' box (scented shit you put in your drier in case you don't know) and i found about 3 grams of extremely dry weed. When I found it I was like 'Oh YEAH! and I didn't happen to have any weed at the time so I thought it was great.. [i]until[/b].. I smoked it.

Oh my fucking god was it ever nasty. Inhaling wasn't so bad, but upon exhaling I tasted so much soap going through my airways I nearly vomited on-spot. I'm not thinking it's dangerous to be smoking or anything, but it's just so gross. I let it sit for a couple days and just tried again. Still. Fucking Nasty. I'm a seasoned straight tequila drinker so taste isn't much of an issue for me, but does anyone know if smoking this to desperately catch a buzz can make me sick or fuck up my lungs? I can't really think of any way to get whatever's making it taste bad out, other than trying to air it out which doesn't seem to work :S?? Anyone got any funny similar stories or anything?
