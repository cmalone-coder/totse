Re: ultram. a waste of time?
Tramadol certainly isn't a waste of time. Obviously it effects everyone differently, but for me it gives you a very very nice high. Not as potent as something like vicodin, but the high lasts longer.

I've had several back surgeries in the last few years, and have been on everything from vicodin, morphine, oxycodone, codeine, and tramadol..and tramadol works wonders, I actually love it more than any other pill. I'm not saying it is the best for killing pain, but I just love the feeling, it gives you that opiate buzz, but doesn't wear off as quickly as other pills.

Keep in mind when I got into taking it for recreational purposes I had been off the more potent opiates for a while and had just been occasionally taking codeine, so I didn't have a high opiate tolerance at the time. Still, I can verify tramadol can be very very fun.

As for people getting sick when they come down, that is a sign that you're taking too much. Even lowering your dose by half a pill can make the difference between getting sick or not. I'd say beginners need to start out at around 200mg, if you get sick adjust your dosage accordingly. Of course you'll eventually build a tolerance, but I wouldn't go past taking around 400-450mg. Also, definitely always try to eat something when you take that pills, that has been a practice of mine with any opiate for years now.
