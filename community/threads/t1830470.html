Re: Exploding Dye Packs?
I don't know everything about them, but my mom has been a banker for a while. Basically, the dye packs are a thin sheet of plastic that contains some type of die, obviously, a radio reciever, and some other chemicals. These can be placed in the middle of stacks of cash, without the robbers knowing about them because they relatively thin. When the robber walks out, the radio transmitter, usually near the entrances/exits of the bank will emit a frequency, and the reciever will read the frequency, and usually the ignitor, or whatever it is, is usually on a delay of some sort. When they go off, they release the dye and smoke, and the chemicals heat up to around 450 degrees Fahenheit. I guess this was useless because you probably wanted to know what makes them explode, in which I don't know.
