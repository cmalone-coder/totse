Re: Just drove 300ft w/ e-brake on
It would be the brakes, not the tires, and theres a good chance nothing is wrong.

Put the car on an incline where you would normally trust the e brake to hold it, and give it a burl. If it works, good.

Just don't try the e brake while moving, I assume they are drums in your case, if they have overheated, I guess they may have warped slightly (I don't think its likely), so it may cause a rough ride if you do.

An interesting thing about drum brakes is they (as far as I know all of them that aren't archaic...) have an automatic adjusting mechanism, so even as you wear out the pads, a mechanical device will move them closer to the drums to account for it. This 'device' usually 'checks' for wear if you park on a hill with the rear of the car downhill and the drums stopping the car (ie the drums stop the car from reversing). At which point they will adjust as nescessary.
