The more you open yourself to another person to allow positive things to develop, the more vulnerable you're making yourself to be hurt. Unfortunately, that's just the way it is.

The only thing you can do is try to understand the way things are a little better and keep everything in perspective.

Try to love everyone, but don't expect anything from anyone. When you find a person whose personality you are very compatible with, and you'd like to life alongside them a while, go ahead, but don't try to demand something from the interaction. It's better just to use the experience with a person of higher than average pleasantness to you as a way to stretch your ability to love.

Desire is the root of all suffering. Don't demand returns on your actions, just live the best, the most happiness-efficient (for yourself and others) life you are able.
Do that and all life's base are belong to you.
