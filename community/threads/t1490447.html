Wow a young guy on the right track, sort of.

A girlfriend is only going to last if you are good friends. Relationships that start on sex and continue like that will wane in most cases, sad but true. Most of the succesful marriages and LTR's I know are based on being best friends first. You need something to talk about with your clothes on and when you are in trying times.

Second point is that actively looking/searching for a girlfriend is not the greatest mindset to be in. You should tell yourself that you are not looking but you are receptive. Become a little more introspective and look after yourself. Develope your interests and strong points, go to the gym alot and play some sports. Get out there and do shit. If you aren't actively trying to seek out the "perfect" girlfriend she will fall right into your lap. I'm not suggesting you play hard to get or that you be someone you are not. I'm just saying that if you relax, be confident and take your time good things will happen, girls can tell when a guy is actively looking and it puts the best ones off.

This has worked for me all the time.
