Haha, PirateJoe's reply is actually kind of funny.

Now, who is to say that we have a 'purpose' or not? I don't think I have any purpose or 'meaning', nor does anyone else.

As I see it:

EITHER

God Exists

-Deist God (No objective purpose/meaning, our organic cells and atoms are as valuable as inorganic atoms.)

-Interventionist God(We may have objective purpose/meaning)

-Interventionist God obsessed with mankind(We definetly have purpose/meaning)

God Does Not Exist

-No universal Objective values(No objective purpose/meaning)

I personally am unsure of GOD's existence, so I do not presume that I have such an objective value/purpose/meaning.

Secondly, to say that GOD made us out of interest, love, fun, joy, hate, or any other emotion is to commit an awful fallacy: that GOD is animal-like, in that she feels emotions like man/animal,etc.
