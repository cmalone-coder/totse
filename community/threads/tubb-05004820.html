Re: Just tried to smoke some MJ
You are surrounded by pipes. An empty toilet paper or paper towel roll will make a superb carburetor. A carburetor pipe holds the smoke, and then mixes it with air as it is pulled into your lungs. Just make a thumb sized hole about 3/4 of the way up the tube. Wrap that area of the tube with aluminum foil, covering the hole. Push the foil a little ways into the hole to make a bowl. Tape the foil edges to the cardboard tube, to eliminate air leaks. Poke a few tiny holes in the foil (don't try to make a pipe screen, just a few tiny holes - or you will end up inhaling a bowl full of burning ganja).

Load up the bowl, keep your hand over the open end of the tube, inhale, and then inhale hard as you remove your hand from the end. Works great.

An apple or potato will also make a great disposable pipe. They do not make carburetor types, but just straight forward commonplace run-of-the-mill everyday fruit and vegetable pipes. Keep foil, a pin, and a lighter handy, and you will never be stuck for a pipe. Making them is half the fun!!!
