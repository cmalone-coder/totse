Re: losing my acid mentality
On the topic of mental illness.

Yes, there's a higher chance that you will bring something out through the use of psychedelics if what they bring out is present in a family member.

HOWEVER,

That's not to say that you actually WILL become mentally ill. You have to look at the way people are before the mental illness arises to be able to judge for yourself whether or not you have a good possibility of having an onset of mental illness. Are you a fairly happy person? Comfortable with yourself and others? Are you okay in social situations? If you answered yes to all of these things, then I don't think one trip will really affect your mental stability a whole lot.

To put things in perspective, we'll take a famous case of schizophrenia/psychosis - Syd Barrett. Everyone knew he was an odd character before the drug use, and he was one that really shouldn't have been doing ANY drugs whatsoever because he was already so mentally unstable that it was fairly obvious that doing drugs would lead to what happened to him (he became a total recluse for many years). Does this sound familiar to you? If so, then I'd stay away from drugs altogether.

Also, how old are you? The peak years of an onset of schizophrenia is late adolescence to early adulthood, so if you're past that then the chances of you getting it (technically...I mean, if you're going to get it then you're going to get it) go down.

All it takes is knowing yourself.
