Re: I need to build muscle and keep it
3 days a week should be ok, maybe 4 if you feel you need to push it more. Your workout doesn't need last any more than an hour, but try for at least 30 minutes. Don't use any weights that you can go over 15 reps with. And like the guy above said, you need to progressively increase the weight for more resistance.

Use compound exercises that work large muscle groups. Than means things like benchpress/dumbell press, deadlift, squats, lunges, pullups, dips. Curls and other small exercises should only be supplementary.

Eat alot but eat well. That means at least 3500 calories a day, maybe more like 4000 for your skinny body type. You should also try cutting out most trans fats and sugars, which means less waffles and more fruits, wheats and grains, meats, nuts, and vegetables.
