If you were giving her what she wants, you would not be in this boat. Don't ever confuse being super nice and pacifying perceived immediate desires with giving a girl what she wants.

As far as "making her work for it" goes, the relationship must be reciprocal. If her commitment is at a level 2, and yours is an 8, then it won't work. You have to match her or even go just below her level for a bit to have a shot. Think of it this way...if there was this girl that you knew would have sex with you whenever, however, no matter what; don't you think at some point you might not be as motivated to please her? The two of you have to grow the relationship together. You cannot get serious by yourself.

Getting back "what she wants". It is better to focus on creating more desire than trying to satisfy it.

Cindy is right that all games are not for everyone. I think it is important to have a working knowledge of them so you recognize them and have them at your disposal if you do need them at some point, but some people are better off just waiting for Ms Right.
