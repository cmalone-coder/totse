Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. You got 5 dollars? You can get about 50 grams of black powder for 5 dollars, they are in the model rocket engines, if you are desperate. You can get a weak black powder from bottle rockets, and a weak flash powder from the sparklers, but it must be in a very fine powder.
You can use a empty rocket engine case for a tube, or roll your own thick tube out of kraft paper. You can make really good plugs from dowels or epoxy. Everything is at walmart except for fuse. You can order fuse from cannonfuse.com, but it will not get to you until next week. I would just use the sparklers as a fuse.
