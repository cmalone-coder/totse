Dog, you're going to fry your brain........

Seriously though. E is wayyyy fun. It's like you're thrown into a world where you're walking on clouds and you're entire body is orgasming at the same time.

A few things I personally suggest:

- Grab a pack or two of fruity gum. Stride Forever Fruit or Trident Watermelon Twist have worked out best for me. You're going to be chewing on the inside of your mouth, and the gum helps. It also tastes AMAZING during your roll.

- Get a bottle of 5-htp from your local supermarket or vitamin shop. It's like $15-20/bottle, but it's an easy item to pocket. Take one a day for a week or so prior to your roll. Take two before your roll, and one before you go to sleep.

- You're going to want to be drinking water (best choice), but if you're liking the way you're feeling (you will, trust me), you're going to want to drink either lemonade, OJ, or any other drink with a high vitamin C content.

- Glowsticks, strobe lights, a pair of those glasses that give all the lights a rainbow, tracerish glow around them (if you can find them, sick. I had these things on at a rave.....)

- Download a torrent full of trance, drum & bass, hardcore, etc. etc. music. This is the best music you'll listen to during the roll. Here's a REALLY GOOD set of songs (probably better than your current selection):

http://thepiratebay.org/tor/3972549/Top_100_Trance_and_Techno_Party_Songs_of_All_Time. 2008

- Make sure you have some bud handy. That would be a way better choice than a whippet. I can smoke endless amounts when i'm rolling and it just adds to it. I don't get the normal high as strongly, it just helps the roll out. It also is really nice in the morning to help the comedown.

As to prices. It all depends on your area and the people you know really. I'm not too sure on prices in Europe, but over here it goes for $10-20/pill. You should also check your source before you pick up. I've known too many people get ripped off because they heard someone had E, jumped right on top of the deal, and ended up getting bunk pills.
Go through a reliable source to ensure you're getting what you pay for. If it's at a rave, it shouldn't be a problem at all. Prices may be more, but they'll be good pills.

Don't roll too often. Limit it to once or twice a month. I sell pills to way too many E-tards and i see how it effects people who use it often, and it's not a good thing. Kids picking up 7 pills for the night, two or three nights a week. Don't do that. You may find out that you REALLY like this drug, and prefer it, but don't abuse it; enjoy in moderation.

Be safe and happy rolling.
