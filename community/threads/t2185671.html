h0p's Method to get off of Opiate Addiction
Well I know this wont work for everyone but It worked for me and a buddy and were doing 200+mg a day everyday for a few years.

Step 1: Start to taper your dose down to as low as can. Just take when you need to not feel sick everyday and try to get it down to 30-60mg (We were using oxy)

Step 2: The day you take the pill you have buy a bunch of xanax, and instead of waking up and taking oxy take some xanax. We would usally take 4mg of xanax in the morning and 2mg every few hours when we started getting real sick. We would also take ~4mg at night to sleep along with some beers.

Step 3. After a week or so of the xanax its times to stop. This is where its get different. You need to either get some GHB or something like GHB (GBL,1,4b,ect). When You wake up take around 3g of GHB or the equal of the analogs. This will even you out and make you hungry so you can eat. Take 2-2.5g before each meal of the day. The GHB amount isnt enough to knock you out just make you hungry and feel evened out. At night take 4-5g of GHB for sleep. [WARNING: Do not drink or take anything that will slow your breathing]

Step 4. After a week of doing this its time to stop. You can either stop cold turkey from the GHB but you will feel shitty for a couple of days, I found smoking a bowl when I woke up and a few more bowls throughout the day made me feel normal.

Step 5: You can keep smoking weed if you want, but I stoped and I would wake up feeling a little shitty by not like opiate W/Ds just like depressed and bored as hell before I had no drugs.

I used this method and was clean off opiates for over 2 months and with very little W/D pain. It worked alot better than when I tried methadone and suboxone to quit opiates. Again this is just a method that worked for 2 people and worked great so I figure Id post it.

For those of you that dont know about GBL or 1,4b. GBL can be bought overseas and is a liquid were every 1ml of GBL is equal to 1.6g of GHB. 1,4b can be bought online and some stores in the US its a solvent like GBL. 1,4b is very cheap I get 4oz bottles of the liquid for a little over $30 shipped. Please test any GHB,GBL, or 1,4b alone before you do this to make sure you dont have problems with your body.

So please give me some thoughts of my method and I hope I can help just 1 person get over their addiction. Who knows it might even help with some other addiction. I bet a GHB treatment would be able to help with benzo w/d.

# If you overdose even slightly on ghb (double dose), you may fall unconscious and be unrouseable for up to 4 or 5 hours. One of the great dangers of a mild GHB overdose is being taken to a hospital and treated as though you have OD'd on a depressant. This can include stomach pumps, breathing tubes and IV's, not to mention huge hospital bills.

# GHB can be dangerous when used improperly or when mixed with other depressants. Combining a normal GHB dose with alcohol can trigger the overdose reaction of unrouseable sleep. GHB has been attributed to a few deaths in the US, but most have involved other drugs (most commonly, alcohol) or high doses. Be safe...avoid mixing GHB with alcohol.

# If you take 3 - 4 times the normal dose, you may find yourself unconscious and vomiting.

# If you take more than 3-4 times your normal dose, there is risk of death.

# If you take GHB, make sure your companions know what you're taking, how much of it you've taken and the associated risks.
