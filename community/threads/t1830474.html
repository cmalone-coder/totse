Backyard Ballistics This forum is dedicated to the safe teaching and responsible handling of high and low explosives. You will not find any information on how to make "bombs" or any such device(s) . Backyard ballistics will teach you the science and the art of pyrotechnics and how to manufacture and use explosives/pyrotechnics safely and responsibly. Backyard ballistics is also a haven for rocketry and pneumatic cannons. All information contained within this forum is for educational purposes ONLY. Re: Starter Kit
For mesh nets, look under fabric vs steel screens (the topic that is). I bought fabric printing screens with a 110 and 200 mesh count and they work awesome. Go here for them http://zim-intl.com/screen/screens-tools.htm They have 390 mesh screens with aluminum boarders, but that is as high as they go. If you want extremely fine mesh, go here http://www.twpinc.com/twp/jsp/product.jsp?type=10 They have mesh nets all the way up to 635. It is going to cost you though, $171.00 a square foot, I'd recommend not going any higher than 400 though, any higher than that and I think everything will float away when you are trying to sift it through.
