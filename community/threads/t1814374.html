I was thinking about revelations a while back and the 144,000 that will raptured. I'm not sure on the exact number of christians in the world, but it's somewhere around a billion +/-. I found it strikingly odd that such a small percentage of Christians will be saved by God. Then I started thinking, what if the Bible isn't the devine word of God, but a test put forth by God, or even a higher intelligent race in the universe. So I started thinking about different groups in the world and found a group that fits that 144,000 almost perfectly, moral atheists. Who is more richeous then the men and women that live moral, just, and caring lives without cause. These people have no rules put forth by anyone, however, they create no friction or conflict on this planet. This group of people is the smallest minority on the planet, numbering no higher than 200,000. Even in revelations it says that the anti-christ will come as christ. This is the recipricol of christ will appear as the anti-christ and will be shunned by the christian populous, and most likely won't even out himself to them at all. How can Christ be more shunned than to be an atheist.

Being a Atheist, I've concluded that there is no evidence of there being a god, absolutely none. This doesn't mean that there couldn't be one, just that there is no evidence. However, if there is a god, he created us with free will and other higher thinking abilities like logical deduction. Looking at the world from a creation standpoint, god himself was a very logical being, as the universe before us is a perfect functioning system based around some pretty heavy mathematics and logic. This makes me wonder why he'd give us a book that contradicts the functioning system, even giving us rules that will disrupt his perfect system when it comes to society. It only makes sense that he didn't want followers, he wanted a free thinking race that chooses to live a peaceful way out of logic and free will, and not through the fear of an oppressing god as mindless followers have always been the strong arm of evil men in history. Almost all policy that is causing problems around the world and destroying societies where instituted by religious majorities blinded by faith ignoring the truth about human nature. Relgions are litterally spawning a world that is rapidly resembling hell while moral atheists are pushing for more responsible, logical, and better suited policies that will bring peace. Christians and all other religions that are doing this have failed the test, and you're fucking yourselves, blinded by faith as to your mistakes and flawed thinking.

Exploring other ideas, it might not be a god at all, but rather a higher intelligent race, and we are their colony. The tribulation is the elimination of the mindless followers that are the power of evil men standing in the way of peaceful function that will signal their time for contact with us. Just keeping my mind open.
