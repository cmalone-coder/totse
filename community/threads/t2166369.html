Re: How much experience do I need before Salvia?
I've only had a couple of bad trips on Salvia; one of which was down to my friend who fucked with me while I was tripping. (He got a fist to the stomach when I had regained control)

No amount of preparation will ready you for how you will feel and what you see. My suggestion however is, don't do it sitting down (at least, when I do it sitting down, I get a bad trip because I want to move around but I can't.). I suggest putting yourself away from anything that you could harm yourself on (including roads) just a precaution, you don't know how you will react. Also as the other have said, use a bong, its much easier and the trip is a much quicker onset. Hold the smoke in your lungs and count to 20 you will of felt it by the time you get to 18.

Oh. Always use a torch lighter. Attempts with a regular clipper were futile.

The trip wont last longer than 15 minutes at the most and as a first time try I suggest 20x tops. Anything else and you're going to scare yourself shitless. Be warned; salvia will fuck with your perception of life/the world for a couple of days after.
