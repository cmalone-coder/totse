Re: psychodelics and potentially unstable people...
It's pretty much impossible to gauge whether or not a single trip on mushrooms will affect someone for better or for worse. People who say they want to trip to better themselves can have emotional, spiritual, and intellectual issues that they want to resolve. By intellectual I mean exploring questions about consciousness and how the mind is implemented by the brain. I've introduced many people to psychedelics and I would say that the people who gain most from them are smart people who need confirmation of a realm outside of their normal reality and who are willing to put real effort in gaining knowledge not just about the substance, but everything else surrounding its use and are open to the implications of what they experience.

The people you don't want to be giving psychedelics to are the ones that have a very set pattern of thinking that they will try to fit everything they experience into this paradigm that they aren't willing to let go of. Psychedelics should be a humbling experience and a lot of people with schizophrenia for instance (especially combined with OCD) drive themselves crazy by these mental patterns that produce of vicious cycle of thinking. Some of them do benefit from taking psychedelics but the ones who don't are negatively affected because they must try to reinforce their usual way of thinking at any cost.

People who are genuinely interested and open to the experience will do their own research and the best outcomes for trips in my experience come from preparation without expectation.
