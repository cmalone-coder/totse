Grow Box - Does It Make Sense?
I came up with a way to grow weed. This way, you won't have to pay money for hydroponics, or worry about dogs. I'm just wondering if you think it will work before I waste my money.

Here it is: You make a box, maybe a foot deep and 2 feet across. You fill it up with soil and put some seeds in. Then, you carry it with you as you climb up a tree. Set it on a branch about 15-20 feet up, and nail it to the trunk and branch. Then, duct tape it with that camouflage duct tape all up ons to the tree. Caulk/rubber cement it to the tree, etc. to secure it as best as possible.

Then, in theory, it would be able to get sun and rain and heat and air, without being found by police or others. Climate would be a factor in if this works.

So, do you think it will work? If not, what other clever ways are there?

There is a product like that called gellscape that you can buy at most plant nurseries. I used to use it when I used to work as a landscaper. In the long run, you can really tell the difference between plants that had the gellscape and the ones that didnt. But in a marijuana grow, it could benefit in well draining mediums like coco coir (which by the way is the best growing medium IMO). Time release nutrients only last for so long, and seedlings dont need or want any nutrients for the first 2-3 weeks. That could easily cause nute burn. They get all the nutrition they need from the first leaves that appear, the cotyledons. Until they start to die off, don't fertilize. Either way, the guy wanting to do this will be spending a lot of time up in that tree transplanting, feeding, watering, etc.

All in all, I think this method would be a giant pain in the ass. If you are forced to grow outdoor, just go out on a hike and find a spot no one will find. And there are tons and tons of ways to protect against pests.
