Re: If you had a choice between the following brands..
1. Bootlegger: I adore Bootlegger. They have a variety of different styles that are fairly affordable for me, and they have great quality denim that I can actually afford. If I need cheap basics that are good quality such as tank tops, cardigans and pants I know bootlegger will have something, and I can also spend a little more on a really unique bag or dress.

2. American Eagle Outfitters: I would never, ever, ever wear a logo tee from AE. I would feel like an absolute joke prancing about in a $40 advertisement, and their logo clothes pretty much epitomize homogeneous. However they've got good quality denim even though it's a bit out of my price range, and a lot of their blouses, skirts and shoes are really adorable and not even recognizable as AE.

3. Bluenotes: I own a few things from Bluenotes but I find their girl clothes to be extremely juvenile. Bright colors and childish prints aren't really my thing and their denim doesn't fit well, nor does it look or feel like quality stuff. Bluenotes to me is where preteens go to get their first 'teen clothes.' A lot of their guy's stuff is very nice, though, and I've been tempted to buy my boyfriend some bootcut jeans, plaid button ups and zip-up hoodies for a while now.
