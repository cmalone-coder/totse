Ended up scoring 50 dollars worth of hard, which in my opinion looked to be about a gram, totaling 5 rocks.

I had never smoked the shit, so the dope boy took me to the local 711 type shop and bought me a rose with a glass stem holder and bought some chore boy.

He set it all up put the wire mesh in the glass tube all that jazz.

I took one 'rock' and put it in the pipe and ripped it a few times.

I felt like I had just snorted some cocaine, I thought the effects were supposed to be far superior than that of powder cocaine hcl?

Was it I got bad crack? This dude had many a fiend buying from him, usually when that happens its good shit. Ive seen lines of people when buying heroin for the good shit.

I ended up smoking all the rock in like 15 minutes.

I held that shit in as long as I could and exhaled large clouds of this crack smoke.

The dope boy that sold it to me it was supposed to make me feel like i snorted grams of powder coke and my ears should be ringing, which it did not.

Was it I got bad shit, didnt smoke enough, or what?

I dont plan on doing it anytime soon again, as im a heroin type of dude.
