Re: Confused!!! (Long Post)
If you need a sugar-coated response, please don't read this.

Yes, your life might be called "shit". I've seen far worse, but it doesn't sound at all like things are the way you'd like them to be.

Why not? Why are you 20 and still living at home, not in school, and have no money? You should have had a good two years of gainful employment.

If you're healthy, mentally and physically, why are you in the situation you're in? Have you been in trouble with the law? Are you not yet capable of money management? Are your parents control freaks and have discouraged you from being independent? Those are the main reasons I've seen people unable to leave their parents' home.

You say you have a "shit job". So, you have some sort of job. Is there a reason you can't get a better one? Do you live in an economically depressed area? Do you lack of transportation? Do you lack motivation?

If you want your life to be better, you'd benefit from being more realistic about what you can handle. Learn to recognize your limitations and live life within them.

Committed relationships are a bit beyond that scope for now. You need to learn to take care of yourself first. Going from one dependency (family) to another dependency (fiancee and her family) is a classic "out of the frying pan and into the fire" situation.

It seems it would be in your best interest to move out of your parents' house and live independently for awhile.

Pondering a trip to Florida is a mix-up of priorities. I think that such an extravagant (yes, for you, it's extravagant just to come up with the spending money) is beyond your means. If you don't have at least 3 months of on-your-own living expenses in the bank, you can't afford extras like travel.

If your parents really want to be supportive, they'd cancel the trip and help you with things you'd need for your own apartment.

Sorting out your life is going to involve causing disappointment (to your family) and maybe pain (to your fiancee). That seems to be the very thing you'd like to avoid, but let me be real with you: you can't avoid it. You need to start putting yourself first, and the longer you wait, the more complicated and unsatisfying your life is going to be.

This is a lot of info at once, so I'll see how you respond before going on.
