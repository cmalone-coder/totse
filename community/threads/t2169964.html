Relationship: Goner?
Started seeing a girl a year ago and she seemed a bit different: Wouldn't get pissed off about trivial things, she didn't seem insecure, by all means she just seemed intent on having a good time.

So we've had a good time, seeing each other has been like a breath of fresh air - I just couldn't believe I'd found a girl who wasn't neurotic enough to start systematically ruining everything for both of us!

Very recently, things have just started to look like they're teetering on the edge of a nose-dive. She's getting more tetchy, those 'harmless friends' and ex's aren't brushed aside as easily as they used to be. We take a small holiday together, we don't argue, but it's just not right.

So I'm wondering what's going on, and then in the middle of a sleepy silence she says "I bottle things up". After about ten minutes trying to get the lid off this bottle, out spurts a foamy torrent of contradictions: "I sometimes think we're too young to be in a long-term relationship... But I really love being with you... It's just that it's all I know... I wonder what it'd be like to be single again... But I don't want to be on my own... Not that I'm just staying with you because I don't want to be alone... I want to be with you, it's just that the thought of being single has got stuck in the back of head... All that's bothering me is that it's there..." and eventually, after chatting away about it for a while... "I love you, I don't want anything to change.", and then the most cushy and sickly few hours you could ever see.

Thing is, I sort of agree, I really want to stay with her and we're happy together but I'm 18 (she's 17), we still both have university to come and it just seems too early to be so committed. It almost feels like we should have a hiatus and pursue solo projects and then reunite for a big tour and a comeback album. Only thing is... well that just wouldn't work.
