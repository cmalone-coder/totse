As far as MMDA goes, SWIM thinks that myristicin is 3,4-methylenedioxy-5-methoxy-allylbenzene, if not it is the propenylbenzene which would be just as suitable for precursor use to MMDA. However I don't think that your gonna directly aminate that carbon chain so easily with just ammonia. Just apply the techniques associated with production of amphetamines from any essential amine (i.e. use myristicin like safrole and get MMDA).

SWIM would suggest spending a little bit of cash at the photoshop and pick up some hydroquinone and PdCl. A simple chromic acid oxidation on the hydroquinone gives p-benzoquinone, which SWIM suggests for you to use along with the PdCl on a wacker oxidation of the myristicin, giving the corresponding ketone. Then reduction with any method of choice. Although if using something like ammonia as the amine SWIM suggests using NaBH4 reductions or something.

Another method which would be very clever in SWIMS opinoin, by making the nitroalcohol isntead of nitropropene, and reduce it to the amphetamine. Myristicin can be oxidized easiy to the aldehyde. Make bisulfite adduct, and then condensate with nitroethane, producing an amino alcohol in higher yeilds than if doing a condensation to get the nitropropene. This nitroalcohol can be reacted with something like acetic anhydride producing the acetyl ester. Reduction of this with CTH reduces not only the nitro group, but the ester as well, going straight to the amphetamine. This would give MMDA in three simple high yeilding steps.

That idea came from some patents about nitroalchols SWIM read. Originally, it was mainly a patent about making ephedrines, by condensating benzaldehyde with nitroethane in the above mentioned fasion, creating the nitroalcohol. Reducing that gave PPA. N-methylation will give pseudo. Then it was found that you could proceed straight to amphetamine in one reduction, by esterfying the alcohol first. Then it gets reduced at the same time, giving amphetamine. N-methylation gives meth.

Re: myriscticin -> MMDA
quote:

eugenol &gt; pma



I don't think so.

Eugenol is 3-hydroxy-4-methoxy-allylbenzene. This is why it is a good starting point for safrole. Demethylation on the 4 position gives 3,4-dihydroxy-allylbenzene. Methyleneation of the hydroxy's gives 3,4-methylenedioxyallylbenzene, or safrole.

PMA stands for para-methoxyamphetamine. Para means the substituents are directly opposite of each other. The aminopropane chain is on the 1 position, and directly opposite on the 4 position is the methoxy group, hence the word para. Another name is 4-MA, which is 4-methoxyamphetmine. The allylbenzene for this is present in star anise oil (4-methoxyallylbenzene).

To get PMA from eugenol, one would have to reduce the OH on the 3rd position, which is not that easy. It is more practical to start from anise.

Re: myriscticin -> MMDA
To answer the above question of what does MDEA come from, the answer is still safrole. MDEA does not have any ring or chain substituents changed. A different amine is used. Safrole is made into 3,4-MDP2P. Reductive amination with N-methylamine yeilds MDMA, but red.-amin. with N-ethylamine would yeild MDEA.

The use of nitromethane is popular in aluminum amalgum reductions of phenylacetones because the nitromethane gets reduced to methylamine insitu, so the N-methylated product is always easier and cheaper to make. However, if one had synthesized nitroethane (a class 1 precursor) to make the nitropropene from the aldehyde or something, and had a bunch of nitroethane laying around, I'm sure it could be used instead of nitromethane in the Al/Hg, giving ethylamine produced insitu, instead of methylamine. This would produce MDEA.
