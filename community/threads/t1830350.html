YES! Gun store owners are OFTEN suspicious. They'll most likely ask you "What kinda gun you gonna use this in?" And it's a good idea to know which kinds of guns various types of BP are commonly used in.

ELEPHANT BLACK POWDER -



FFg - Mainly used in shotguns and rifles over a .54 calibre.

FFFg - Used in .50 calibre rifles and below.

FFFFg - Used for priming flintlocks, probably the best for salutes or expolosives.

GOEX BLACK POWDER -

FFg - Used in .40, .45, .50, .54 and .58 calibre rifles.

FFFg - Used in .36 and .32 black powder handguns. (I told him I had a .36 calibre replica remington revolver)

FFFFg - Flintlock priming only. (Try telling them you have a Thompson Firestorm or a Austin Halleck .50 calibre flintlock rifle. If you plan on buying more than one pound of priming powder you'd better throw in a pound of Fg powder as well so your story will be consistent.)
