Damn the False Pleasures
I seem oddly driven to make this post, I haven't the slightest clue why I feel anyone should want to hear this, I presume that maybe someone who has hardly dabbled in any drugs might find this useful.

My drug interests started as very much risk free, I was interested in mind expansion, altered perception, and did not want to use drugs for an escape or for pleasure, only to see the world in a different light. This was good, I now see in retrospect. I smoked cannabis in various settings and dosages, some experiences were what one would label as "recreational" and others were quite intense, not quite pleasurable, and produced quite intense visuals in the dark. I then tripped on 600mgs of DXM and that made me rethink my definition of intense. Once again, that was in total darkness. Definitely appreciate my sanity more now. Still after just those drugs, I hadn't crossed over to the "pleasuregasm" types of drugs.

I had surgery a while ago however, and had some left over percocet. I used them a couple times in higher than medical doses and loved it, but for whatever reason, luckily, I didn't have too strong of an urge to do it again, and again. I had also used codeine, and hydrocodone, with much less euphoria. But I was still not negatively altered in my view of psycho actives.

Fucking amphetamine. God dammit. I should have known this would be what I'd enjoy just a little too much. I got it for free. I maybe should mention that I will most likely be getting a prescription, I have pretty bad ADD, and it's confirmed I'm not just a lazy fucker. So I have these four 30mg XR pills of Adderall. I crush them up as I have been told, and baggy them up for later use. (I crushed two btw.) One day before work, I decided that I was really tired and needed some pepping up. So I rail my first drug, 30mg. And parachute the other 30mg. As soon as I am back upstairs, I feel it. And it was love at first tweak. I was euphoric, talkative, alert, loving. On my ride to work (from my parents, too young for a liscence) I put on some Modest Mouse and wow, it was just as good as the euphoria from oxycodone or hydrocodone, but not drowsy, very awake! I was feeling absolutely great. At work I had long conversations with my co-worker, while working like a beast. I treated the customers like best friends, and every word that came out of my mouth was witty and clever. I loved this version of me, because I knew that others loved it as well. Hours later, I begin to comedown, I get a little tired, still in a good mood. And I go home a little lethargic.

I wake up the next morning, and after the morning routine, I immediately think "I'd like to do that again!". Problems already, this drug just fit me too well, and I'd always thought I was a downer guy. So a couple days later I repeated the same routine before school, snort and drop, thirty and thirty. I snorted much more efficiently this time, and it hits me in seconds, just more addiction potential. At school the effects are the same again, and my girlfriend loved how outgoing and talkative I was, I publicly expressed how much I loved her. I do that sometimes anyway, but I guess not as loud, haha! I got unbelievable amounts of work done, and learned a ton of material. I was asking questions, contributing to discussions, and getting shit done! I loved it, I am terrible with school work, so this was a whole 'nother euphoria for me. I eventual came down, harder than last time, mostly because I hadn't eaten a damn thing.

Now, I cannot help but realize I should never do this again. One problem, I feel I really medically need this stuff. Just on a standard ROA and much less dosage, as to get rid of the euphoria. It gave me the absent motivation to get work done, and made me realize how real my ADD is. I really can't pay attention to a mundane task when not on adderall. I have been prescribed Concerta but it did jack shit and had a terrible crash.

I can't just enjoy my perception altering drugs alone anymore, I can't just never experience that amazing mind set again, and it sucks. I wish I had never partaken in the abuse of amphetamines.

My message is as follows: If you are thoroughly enjoying psychedelics like cannabis, LSD, mushrooms, and other perception fucking drugs, do yourself a favor and just do not take part in opiates, or amphetamines and the like. Unless you just have a really strong willpower for these things. I thought I did, I really did. But Its gonna take some serious effort not to abuse my prescription when I get it.

Peace, and be safe.
