Kids, listen up(needle discussion)
I just wanted to say this real quick, in here. I know most people are smart enough to stay away from the needle; I was not. Recently, my test results have come back positive for hep C; you'd think I'd be devastated. The truth is, I was relieved, I was only nervous about the results coming back with aids/hiv or any other diseases which are just very very difficult to control. Ultimately these diseases will take your life; and I've just found out a very close family member of mine has aids. She's terminal, she doesn't have very long to live anymore. Now I feel an enormous amount of guilt, I was the one that introduced her to herron. I've personally, and I can say this in complete honesty, have NEVER been addicted to any drug; I did share a needle once, my very first time I've shot and that's how I got hep C. Sharing needles is an insane practice; in retrospect, I'd have much rather played russian roulette. At least you'll die instantly and not have your death drug on for many, very very painful years.

Basically, even though I had never gotten addicted to it, she had followed my lead and quickly went from snorting to IVing, and got very strung out. And now she's only got a few short very painful years to live. Yesterday she stopped by, and she used my needle to shoot (THANK GOD ALMIGHTY I went first and she followed, then I trashed the rig...things could be SOOOO very different for me now because I only found out about this today). It doesn't matter how fucking sick you are, how bad you need dope in your system, sharing needles is literally a much more dangerous practice than holding a gun with one bullet. So, don't fucking SHARE NEEDLES, EVER. Even if nothing bad happens to YOU personally, you never know what could come of you using drugs in such a manner, and if you do, keep it the fuck away from others, PLEASE.

The crazy thing is, I've been taking my sister to a clinic (and supporting her drug habbit by taking her to go get the shit all the time, only so I can get high when I want to), and ALL my friends that I had ever gone to school with are now at the clinic, so fucking strung out it's unbelievable. Most of them with blood diseases now. It's just not worth it. And if only one person reads this and decides to not fuck with needles, then I'd be happy :).
