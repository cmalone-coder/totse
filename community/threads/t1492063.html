What's your living situation? Do you (as in both of you) live on your own or with family?

Infrared has so little faith in humanity. He could be right, he could be wrong. There's not enough info to speculate.

The simplest explanation is that she's actually tired. A new job can do that. Plus, assuming it's summer where you are, the heat can add to the fatigue.

Relationships evolve and change. The timeline is a little different for everyone, but at some points you reach plateaus where everything seems sort of "meh". These are times when the less committed often give up.

Often, it seems one person is pulling away because they need some space. This could be due to new job demands or other stuff. If you sense this, allowing her more space than you think she needs is the best tactic. I realize it's not an easy thing to do. But she may need it so that she can reconfigure herself and become accustomed to a new schedule and new personal goals while deciding how to dole out her time and energy.

In recent weeks I've found myself, for the first time in my life, "too tired for sex" for various reasons. It certainly had nothing to do with my man, except that I felt comfortable enough with him that I knew he'd understand and not take it personally.

2. She really is tired. Women go through phases. Like this past weekend, my husband and I had sex... damn, six or seven times? It was a lot. Then leave.
