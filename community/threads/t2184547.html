Re: can a doctor actually do this?
The pharmacist has no legal authority to deny someone medication they've been prescribed. However, they do have the authority to confirm your prescription(s) with the doctor who issued them. A doctor does have this authority, however. I'm fairly certain that a doctor wouldn't revoke any of the prescriptions based off-of one query from a supermarket pharmacist, especially on a once-off basis. The doctor could consider revising the prescriptions, but if s/he's the one who originally issued them, I doubt they would - unless of course there were evident signs of negative side effects, which, from what you've told us - it doesn't sound like there are.

I wouldn't worry about it, and if you continue having problems with the pharmacist, just go elsewhere or ask to speak to their supervisor, indicating that this pharmacist has been giving you trouble - and that both you and your doctor are aware of the situation.
