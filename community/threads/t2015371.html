Getting my weed back from the cops
Yesterday, me and a few friends were about to toke up in a forest. Unluckily, there were cops in the area. After my friend lit his joint the cops came through the bushes. They saw we had a few smokes, and that my one friend had the joint. They said we were all in possession of marijuana, and searched us. I left my bag on the ground (which had 2 grams, a grinder with loose tobacco in it and a pack of zigzags). They confiscated all of this. Now the trick is, they had no grounds to search me and I gave them no permission to search my bag. The joint my friend lit up, I was unaware of and had no intention of smoking. I phoned the non-emergency line a while ago, and got the officers name and my file number. What I'm thinking of is contacting the arresting officer, and asking him questions about the incident (recording it aswell) to make the officer confirm my story. Since I live in Vancouver chances are they are not going to press charges. Could contacting the arresting officer about the case provoke him to charge me? Will I need a lawyer to do this? Will it have to go through court? And last, are there any chances I'll get my weed back?

Now when I do contact the officer, I plan on asking the following questions:
Did you see (myname) smoking a joint?
What were your grounds for searching (myname)?
Did (myname) give you verbal permission to search (mynames) bag?
Did you have a searchwarrent to search (mynames) bag?

What I'm afraid of is if he answers yes to me giving him verbal permission, which I did not. It would be my word against his, and my other friends who got caught there would certainly not be interested in testifying. Is there any other questions I can ask, or anything else to increase my chances?
