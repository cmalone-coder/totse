Re: For those of you with jobs requiring degrees
When I worked as a senior network security architect, part of my job involved hiring and firing. So let me divulge:

Unless the job you're looking for is very specific, a degree simply says "I'm trainable." You're able to learn and able to be taught. Entry level positions such as field tech that require a 4 year degree are perfect for this; sometimes even the major of the degree meant little. Looking at transcripts is something I never did nor was it something I would ever do because I cannot imagine a situation that would warrant such action. Experience counts folds more than actual grades.
