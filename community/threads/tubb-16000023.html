Do you have a specific path that you follow? Re: What kind of Pagan are you?
quote:
We are all pagans, we all came from the earth


I think this definition is a tad too broad

Generic definitions of the term vary - country dweller, a *worker* of the earth, is one form of definition. A person who is not of monotheistic faiths (Christianity, Judaism, Islam) is another, and there's bickering within those faiths over how "pagan" is further defined.

"Neo-pagan", is a term where we can be more specific since we've coined it. A Neo-pagan is a person who, in one form or another, reconizes the sanctity of life as found in all around us in nature and beyond.
