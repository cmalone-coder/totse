Re: Cratermaker questions
I use epoxy putty to seal mine. You can use anything that will hold the fuse in, really. I've used duct tape before and it worked just fine.

I'd suggest investing in some good fuse before playing with cratermakers. Also, I've found that model rocket igniters don't work so well in cratermakers because they tend to touch the cartridge and short out, rendering them useless. You'll have to improvise some sort of insulation if you want to use electrical ignition on a cratermaker.

~Zero the Inestimable
