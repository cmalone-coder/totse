(from Dinix, not 36fuckin5)
The Ruderalis strain is fairly new in the commercial world of cannabis, therefore there are a lot of unsupported theories and 'water-cooler' talk about it. As a land strain (i hate the term ditch weed) it grows mainly in Ukraine (the Crimean Mountains i believe), as a 1-2 foot tall cannabis plant. Because of its small size it isn't good for hemp production, and because of its low cannabanoid content it's not good as a medicinal plant either. The Joint Doctor brought the ruderalis genetics back with him from a trip and started cross-breeding it with an existing strain (the exact strain can be found with some research, but i'm lazy. find it yourself if you don't believe me) giving lowryder.

What makes it special is that its reproductive cycle is not triggered by light. Cannabis Sativa or Cannabis Indica have a built in code to determine when it should make babies. Summer equinox is the magic point (12 hours light, 12 hours dark). Ruderalis has a timer instead. When the plant has been alive long enough it makes babies. Cloning takes advantage of a cannabis plants light detector genes by reversing sexing back into vegitative growth with 18 hours of light or more. With the ruderalis it doesn't give two flying fucks about how much light it gets, when it's old enough it's gonna make babies.

Cloning has been deemed nearly impossible with autoflowering strains, so don't bother. Some theories state that if you grow it in a low temperature enviroment it will prolong sexing long enough to take clones, but the life cycle will be stretched so long that you might as well go with a indica or sativa. AND it hasn't been done yet, only a theory.

Playing with hermaphrodites is risky business. The genes for hermaphrodism are dominant, meaning that you run a high risk of propogating a crop of lots of hermaphrodites.

As stated above, i grew a few Diesel Ryder recently. This is a good personal smoke, easy to grow past seedling stage, but they are vulnerable when they are young. I transplanted 10 by burrying peat moss starter cups in bigger pots and they HATED me for it. half kicked the bucket. I havn't done LR#2, but from the reports i've read in the community Diesel Ryder is the best Autoflower currently available. The high is compairable to any beaster on the market (6-7/10), and the taste is NYCD all the way.

Now then, yield is pathetic. This is a strain that is kinda novelty in a way; it was meant for people living in amsterdam without balconies who could grow this strain in thier window-sill. When it grows out there will be one main cola about 3-5 inches, with 6 or so 1" buds down the side.

For that many seeds, grow one male in the closet with your ladies and plan on having VERY little smokable bud. Keep him around untill there are visible colas on the ladies then yank him. Let the pregnant ladies grow out till they start to die on thier own. Manicure and dry as normal.

My opinion- Autoflowers are the way of the future for personal growers. The black market for pot is out of control, and the most liberating thing you can do is grow your own crop. Don't sell it, don't buy it, just grow it smoke it and share it.

Seperating the best male and growing him away from the girls is optimal. Put a sheet of aluminum foil under him and save his pollen in a masson jar in the fridge. take a fine tip paintbrush and pick which buds you want to impregnant. A better guide to this is easily found on google. but 15-20 seeds at a time is all you should need for your household.

If there's anything you need me to explain in more detail, lemme know.

Re: Auto-Flowering cannibis propigation ?
...I would like to know exactly how to produce ones own seeds.

Buy a pack of seeds that you want more of. Example: get a 10 pack of NCYD (New York City Diesel-variety). You payed a pretty penny for these seeds and you only have 10, some of which are bound to be male. What to do?

Grow them out, all of 'em. Induce 12/12 flowering. Monitor closely for preflowers and separate males from females keeping 12/12 flowering cycle. Once males produce flowers (pollen sacks) and they split open, collect pollen gently with small soft artist's paint brush. Deposit into small vial for safe transport/storage.

Take your pollen to the female room. Your ladies should be well into the flower stage also. They should be showing pistils (the white hairs that shoot out of the buds everywhere). Using the artist's brush, gently dip into the pollen and brush/dab/sprinkle it onto the pistils.

Thats it.

The pistils will recede in a few days taking the pollen with them. And in a few weeks you'll have buds bursting with seeds!


IMPORTANT: After each grow you MUST wash the room top to bottom to remove any traces of pollen. You want your next grow to be "fresh" as it were.

Re: Auto-Flowering cannibis propigation ?
so what is the prime time to pick the seeds is there a set time or look i should be looking for ... of course i want a dark brown to striped seed .. but is there a seed matureing time

The stripes mean the seeds are not ripe. Those stripes will rub off, they're actually pieces of flower stuck to the seed. Bacteria and mold can feed/grow on these stripes and kill the seeds. At that point they're also not fully developed, so they're more susceptible to infection. Collect seeds when the seed pods are split and the seeds are falling out or nearly falling out, or come out easily if you shake or move them around.

This would make the bud less energetic, but possibly more potent. If it's a potent strain anyway it doesn't really matter.
