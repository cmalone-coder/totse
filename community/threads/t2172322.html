Well, how quickly you absorb chemicals is controlled by a number of factors, including the solvent (oil/DMSO) and the size of the solute (cannabinoids in this case). DMSO would surely lead to a quick effect, peaking the blood plasma concentration of THC et all whereas oil would probably be absorbed, leading to a longer, less intense effect. If one was using oil as the carrier for better peace of mind, you could supplement your use with Tagamet HB (cimetidine, 800 mg ingested probably 30ish minutes before the oil's application). Tagamet inhibits your liver from breaking down cannabinoids. The enzyme that it inhibits, cytochrome P450-3A4, also regulates many other chemical systems in the body, so be sure to look up any potential contraindications for yourself, and don't consistently use Tagamet. This enzyme inhibition would give you more bang for your buck, so to speak, by allowing what has been absorbed not be broken down as quickly.
