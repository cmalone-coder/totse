No. Sounds to me like you were subconsciously looking for a coincidence. If the guy on the bus hadn't offered you the cigarrettes, you probably would have never noticed the lighter.

One time I had a weight training class at my high school. There was this one girl with brown hair in that class that seemed to have a crush on me. I kept noticing that weird things would happen that would bring us together, as if by fate. One time I missed the regular time to start the weight training thingy (it went on a 30 minute routine) and so I went in for the second round. And she was the only one there with me. I would also meet her by accident over and over outside of class, usually walking through the hallways.

And so one day I eventually got her phone number and tried calling her, thinking I must have been fated to know her somehow. It turned out that she had a boyfriend, and although I suggested that we could talk sometime she never called back. And that was the last time I ever saw or spoke to her.

Point being, coincidences are just that -- coincidences. Try not to take them too seriously.
