It Finally Caught Up With Me *long*
You guys know, I wouldn't exactly be described as a "nice girl". Some of you - I know - think my morals are too loose and some of you - I'm sure - find my existance completely disillusioning. So, it should come as no surprise to you that it has caught up with me a bit. I figured the day may come when I'd get into an actual relationship and he'd start asking me question about my past or would hear from somebody else. Especially since my boyfriend and I have known each other for a long time.

People talk shit. People have been talking shit about me since before I was even old enough to do anything worth talking about. It's just the way I am. I inspire this in people. So, when my boyfriend tells me he's heard some bad things about me and I ask him

"Who said what?" Of course, he doesn't want to tell me who or what but I finally get out of him that it was actually one of my old bandmates. My old drummer and dj partner who loathes me ever since we have a breif fling. We were only sleeping together for about a month when he started getting all "I love you" on me talking about how we could be just like Syd and Nancy, and fuck all that shit. Especially any sort of "Let's be so very punk rock" Fuck punk rock. And may be it would've worked if the man had a punk rock bone in his body, but the only bone in his body that was work rocking was in his pants. And even that was a gamble. So, when I dumped him he claims that I had led him on to think that we were in love and we were going to be wed in punk rock bliss and shoot heroin together for the rest of our days.

Whatev. And apparently that hairdresser bloke from DiPenis Todd came into my boyfriends bar with Liza from So So Many White White Tigers (who I've had a fued with ever since I was in this punk band called Sweet Polly) and then they were talking a bunch of shit about how I'm all cracked out, and nearly died off the junk, and that I'm a big old crackwhore and the only reason my band made it anywhere was because I'm a whore and I fucked my way to the top. She's just mad that I told everyone she has herpes. Which she does.

Then one of my exboyfriends (who he just happens to work with my new boyfriend) tells him all about how I used to work for this sex club and how I was a stripper at The Gold Room and how I was Suicide Girl and that I fucked all sorts of bitches while we were together, etc...

Then we get back to his place and he had a piece of some rag cut out that had a picture of me kissing this bloke. It looked like I was kissing him on the lips, but it was just on the cheek. The problem is is that the guys cheek I was kissing was an exboyfriend of mine who is in a rather famous band now, so whenever we go out together he gets his picture taken and I, by default, was in the picture this time.

My boyfriend was less than amused by all of this and what could I do about it?

So I came up to him and I said "Listen. I don't know what everyone is saying about me and I don't need to know. People have been talking about me forever. Some of it's lies, but to tell you the truth, most of it's true. But, the point is, is that I never want to do anything bad to you. I really want to be a nice girl to you."

I hope that sounded as good as I think it does.

Cheers,
