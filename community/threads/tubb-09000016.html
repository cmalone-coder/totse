In my quest for the no mind and search for my true will I happened upon many mental anomalies. On three occasions(Is that number meaningful? Teehee) I fell into a trance and saw the exact happenings of what was to happen a few seconds later.

It would happen like this. I would drift off in a whirlwind of boredom. Always boredom. It's hard to explain but I would no longer be thinking or living; merely existing as some object does. Then I would raise my head and look around. All the time not thinking, not feeling, not understanding, just observing. It was like the processor of my mind was turned off and my memory was just logging what was happening around me. Pure memory, without the distortion of thought or expectation.

My vision was always tinted red and almost liquidy around the edges. Sort of what it's like when you tear up but add a tryptamine flavor to it. What I was hearing was always distant and echoy.

The whole thing was like a dream where you loose total control of everything including your own actions. As if you're being compelled by some unknown desire but are on the verge of waking.

As this was happening a feeling of "This is so cool" began to creep into my peripheral thought. As that feeling began to fully realize, I would hear something oddly familiar. I'd then realize that I just heard it. Then I'd hear or see something else and get the same feeling. When this happened, it'd click and I'd recite to myself exactly what would happen next. Peoples' actions as well as words.

This never happened when I had someone to tell what would happen so I've never verified that this was really a vision of the future or simply the beginnings of my instability. Oh let me tell you, that was no fun.

This never happened when I was on drugs although it did occur in the midst of my intensive stint with disasociatives. It would also correlate with smoking large amounts of pot in the mornings but these events happened in the late afternoons.
