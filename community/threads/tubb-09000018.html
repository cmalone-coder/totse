Any body have any paranormal experiences that they would share?

I have two that I consider paranormal, meaning that I cannot explain them. I cannot explain them because the first experience, I just don't know what it was. The second, I was too scared to look.

The first. Both of these experiences happened to me in a time that I was experimenting if you will. I'd play with ouija boards was playing with witchcraft, so maybe it was just in my mind. Anyhow my first experience was in my own bedroom. I was about 13 if I remember correctly. My room used to just get this eerie mood about it and I'd get very pissed off for no apparent reason. I'd just get mad. One night I got into one of these mad moods, so mad I woke up from my sleep. I sat up in bed and saw a body laying on my couch. It was gray and fat and was the shape of sleeping person. It didn't move nor make any sounds. I layed back down, rolled over, and closed my eyes shaking. About an hour later I rolled over to see it still there. I left my room and sat up in the living room all night, never saw this thing again.

My second experience was at a friends house near the same time. His mom claimed to be a witch that could control spirits. Well us kids had the house to ourselves, it was parents night out. We were laying in bed about 2am, when the dogs came running out of their kitchen and jumped on the hide-a-bed with us. Very shortly after we could hear what sounded like kitchen chairs scooting around, dishes rattling in the sink, and doors opening and closing. We just layed there quite and shaking. Several hours after the noises quit we fell asleep.

Since then I've though I may have seen or heard things, but have had an explanation for each one. I've also quit playing with things considered to be demonic. I suppose though, now, if I saw something out of the ordinary I'd approach it and try to communicate. But I really don't know if I'd be ready for it.

In the end I don't know if such things exist. I believe God exists so I do believe in demons, angels, the devil. But I can't prove beyond a doubt that whatever I have seen or heard were either of those. Ghosts are harder for me to believe it, what are they exactly? Demons, Angels? I don't believe the Bible makes mention of ghosts.
