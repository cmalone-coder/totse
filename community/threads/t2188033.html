Meth recrystallizing extreamly slow?
Tl;dr: Why are some bowls of meth taking an extremely long time to recrystallize? On the order of 2mins from liquid to pure white Crystie puddle. Kind of a "water dope" appearance; however, I don't truly know what that term means.


Details:
My knowlage in meth/speed is that it should recrystallize within 5-15seconds after the heat is removed, and will recrystallize into white semi-clear crystal like puddle.

Recently it has seemed some meth recrystallizing extremely slowly. In the area of 60 seconds to even begin to change from a pure liquid "oily/watery" appearance to a white crystal puddle. This process is very slow, and recrystallizes unevenly in a polka dot pattern. After around 2-3mins it will be fully recrystallized and completely solid white, with minimal 'veins' or 'webs' throughout.

Within about 30secons the puddle appears to be solid, however still resembling water. Not moving around like water, just looks clear. The puddle will not move when rotated.

When lit, it burns like normal meth; however, "softer" or "slower". Such as the hits look smaller as the bowl seems like it is not filling with a huge amount of rapidly spinning smoke.

Using the same gear, this has happened with 4 out of 9ish bowls, using three different pipes.

Any suggestions on how/why this is happening? My guess is the quality, most likely very cut up, or pure bunk. I do not believe it is MSM as the horrible first hit and 'glass like' appearance does not apply.

Substance X forms a clear, noncrystalline, amorphous mass - by your own description - within 30 seconds.
