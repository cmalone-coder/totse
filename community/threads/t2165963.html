Re: Harvest Fest
Most festivals the police situation is pretty lax. They know drug use is widespread, and they're not so much worried about the consumers as they are the dealers.

Don't be stupid about things. If you can, try not to buy stuff out in the open. Going to someones car or in their tent is usually the way to go. If anyone is acting the LEAST BIT sketchy, especially if you're buying acid -- don't buy their stuff. Chances are the acid is rather weak for the price, or it's not even acid at all. Actually, this applies for every drug you buy. The obvious hippie types are the goto guys for good drugs, and good prices. Especially their acid. 9 times out of 10, you'll get some kick-you-in-the-face acid from them. Look for people decked out in Grateful Dead gear

And yes, while the police in the fest itself aren't excessive, you can bet there are cops swarming like angry bees on the highways around it. That's pretty much how it goes at every fest -- too many people in the fest itself to bust, but on the road everyone is fair game. If they see a car piled with luggage with college age kids in it, they're going to try and find whatever reason they can to pull you over. Give them no reason to pull you over, and they can't. Make sure you registration is up to date, your lights all work, and you stay 5mph within the speed limit. I wouldn't even so much as throw a cigarette out the window, since littering is technically against the law in most states, and I'm sure some douchebag cop will use that as a reason to fill his drug quota for the month.

Re: Harvest Fest
I've been to over a dozen festivals and I've never been to one that had dogs. Usually the people at the gates who search are regular folks on the staff so they can get free tickets, so they don't give a fuck.

As far as cops on the road go, concealing stuff is always a good idea, but they won't call in dogs unless you give them reasonable suspicion. Remember, unless they have a legitimate reason to search, they can't unless you give them permission. If they ask something retarded like "got something to hide?", just reply with "No, I know my rights, and I'm trying to get home as soon as I can" or something along those lines.

For securing bud, I have two Rx bottles, one that's large enough for the smaller one to fit in. I thoroughly wash each of them every time I use them -- bag the bud several times being as careful as I can with any residue (even using gloves). Once the buds are bagged, I'll put them in the smaller bottle, and put that one in the large container. Even got by dogs once.

Also, another technique for fending off the dogs is pepper, preferably cayenne. It irritates the fuck out of their sinuses, so they'll very quickly lose interest in your car.

The radar detector will definitely be a nice addition, but if they're illegal in Maine be sure to keep it out of sight just in case you do get pulled over.
