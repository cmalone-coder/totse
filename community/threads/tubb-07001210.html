Re: defeating shoplifting tags
An aluminium foil wrapped bag could defeat radio frequency tags.

Generally, three types of security tags are used:
  • Radio Frequency Tags - These are a spiral shape. They can be disarmed by making a slit in them. Sometimes radio frequency tags are actually embedded in the cardboard.
  • Electromagnetic strips - These are a tiny strip of metal. They can usually be pulled off but if you want to disarm them, you can do so by magnetising them. They can be magnetised by waving a powerful magnet in front of the strip repeatedly for 20-40 seconds. A good magnet to use would be one of the two magnets inside your computer because these magnets aren't needed and they are very powerful. These magnets also have a North and South Pole on either end and either face of the magnet.
  • Acousto-magnetic tags - these tags are a centimetre in length and width. They stick out of the box about a sixteenth of an inch. There are three metal strips going through these type of tags, one of the strips actually shrinks if you go through the sensor and it sets off the alarm. You can disarm these tags by demagnetising them.
