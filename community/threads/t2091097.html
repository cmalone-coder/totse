Re: How to press pills.
dont bother with pneumatics and hydraulics unless you wanna crank out buttloads of pills.

if you have a bench vice you can make a single press with only a tube and two dowel pins (one short and one long) that fit inside. you fill the tube with the powder, insert dowel pins into each end and then crush as hard as you can (you might wanna replace the tensioning bar with something longer for more leverage). with this setup you can easily crank out 100+ pills in an hour

it's especially discreet, and won't land your ass in prison when you get busted. for an even more decorative product you can put a drill point on each end of the dowel pins to make the pills come out semispherical.
