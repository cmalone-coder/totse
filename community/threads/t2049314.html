Do you have room-mates?

I'm assuming no, since you've been drinking alone so consistently. And if I've taken anything with me in the past couple of years it's that time spent living alone is in direct proportion to the weird fucking behavior and habits you get yourself into. It's not that you're a sick, demented, pathetic, incurable fuckoff; it's just that there's nobody to say "YO! what the fuck are you doing?" We all have those guilty pleasures that weren't pleasurable enough to make up for the guilt (ie clown sex or the burning yourself thing) that we do anyway when we slide far down enough into our own self loathing where we ask "who gives a fuck" and our liquor soaked brain says "nobody! go for it" It's usually around this time that, if we have a good friend with us, they'll look over our shoulder with a shocked expression saying "JESUS GOD!" and take the shot that's in our hands while we're looking, throw a arm over our shoulder when we turn back, and walk us away from the bearded lady midget human pin cushion we were about to take for a wild ride in the porto-potty.

Ok, maybe that's not everybody, but the point is that there's a reason why misery loves company, and drinking is a social event, and other things like that as well.

Not chastising you for the simple act of drinking alone, hell, I might be up to similar shenanigans if I had the cash and I wasn't banned from the liquor store down the street, plus I kind of have a special someone in mind I'm sort of trying to better myself for. What I'm saying is that you've caught yourself on the tailspin of who-gives-a-fucks, and that's the first step, now you just have to pull yourself out of it before you do something stupid like filling the one you seek's phone with messages half of which are psychotically threatening, the other half horrifyingly masochistic, all threaded together with the kind of incoherence you'd expect from the drunken lovechild of Kurt Cobain and bjork with tourrettes, and you waking up in between two condom smeared sneeze guards at your local all-you-can-eat chinese buffet.

I usually go for some artistic way of purging all of this emotional nausea: writing, making music....I can't paint, but it would go into this category. You could do anything: carpentry, mechanics, architecture, physical training. Just anything that requires focus, and lots of energy, and preferably passion and creativity.
