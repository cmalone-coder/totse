I actually agree with this. And for this reason, a lot of people manipulate /etc/fstab and organize certain filesystem folders into their own HDD partitions.

/dev/hda1 /
/dev/hda2 /home
/dev/hda5 /var
/dev/hda6 /usr
/dev/hda7 /tmp

I think the best thing to do is have /var on a seperate partition and a backup stable OS on /dev/hda8 that can use the packages in /var incase things to to hell in /dev/hda1. That works pretty well. But I do think using clonezilla to backup/restore /dev/hda1 from/to /dev/hda8 is a much better idea.

Once the filesystem is understood, all those things make sense. But yeah, I still think the /etc folder is disorganized.
