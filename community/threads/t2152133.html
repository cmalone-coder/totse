Hippieflipping - My Report and the Days After
I ate my first batch of shrooms last sunday, three or four caps. I let it take hold, and I was feeling pretty good. Nohing real trippy, but cars looked glossy and everything was bright. I was laughing at anything, and everything. I would sit on the front handle bars of a bike, and riding down the street would seem like a thousand mile trek, only to find that we traveled half a block.

Pretty soon, we entered a shopping mall and all the fear took over. I became extremely anxious and nervous, as everyone was clearly out to get me. It didnt help that I was swaeting up a storm. We wait inside a fast food joint when my two friends take one roll each. I barely even remember them getting E, but it was all fine. I decided I needed to get out of their asap though, and this was the start of my Bad Trip.

Soon, one of the friends leaves for no reason, and now Im stuck with my original accomplise. We ride back to my house, when he makes a brave observation of getting more pills. I say sure, not point in going against the flow of others I thought. Soon enough, I find myself back in my house, but this time, somethings different. I find Im sitting in a bathroom, and its hard to get out. Its bigger than usual, surely Im trapped! I see my body, and its the body of a wormish thing. Quite disgusting! I soon hear the sounds of my friend, whom is causing a great disturbance in our peaceful house. I feel I need to stop him before any damage is done.

I rush out and find him making a mess of my room. Why? Mdma usually makes one peaceful. But never mind that, he was on a mission. And so was I for that matter. When your brain is turned to mush from those foul little devils, how can you possibly comprehend Not following your friend? We head out for accross town, in order to pick up the extra pills.

My friend is racing accross town, speeding like a raging crack fiend off for his last fix. And here I am, caught in the dust, unable to properly function. Instead of waiting up (like any mdma induced friend would do) he orders me to go faster and faster. This is strange I thought, normally one is kind on that sort of drug, what could possibly be in that pill to cause such a rage?

We finally get the pill, and my friend generously offers to pay with my money. To make matters worse, there are atleast eight on duty cop cars in the same lot as our dealer, making me more nervous than any man should ever be. We get the pills and head on our way. Our way to where? Wherever my pill laden friend goes. And this would be McDonalds...and the start of the most unpleasant night imaginable.

Without my knowing, I take the pill. Now, let me say this. I will not soley place blame on another for my drug intake. That is unfair. Did he shove the pill down my throat? No. However, we were all supposed to be on shrooms that day, but when I bought them, they backed out. They said they would make sure nothing bad happened around or to me. I felt totally assured. Here are my friends, making the best and safest possible environment for my first trip. How could I be mad?

Well, my 'friends' urged me to lend them thirty dollars when I was high. Its so hard to sya no, and they new that. But the thing that is downright horrible, while I was in the bathroom at my house, my friend asked my kind mother for money for dinner. He said he didnt have any. Being the kind mom that she is, she obliged. 20 dollars.

He kept it.

And later that night, I saw 6 twentys in his wallet.

Without going on and on, we witnessed a motorcycle crash which ended any chance of a good trip. Now, even days later, I still feel horrible.

Is it the lingering of those godawful memories? The pain of knowing Ive been conned by my 'friends'? Or is it the seretonin too afraid to show back up, in fear that my horrible judgement gets the best of us again? I dont know, but I dont feel myself. I feel like a shell, and to top everything off, my vision is nothing. Everything is a bit fuzzy, and I cant focus in on anything. In retrospect, what I did was not much, but for me, I must have pushed a limit. Hopefully these symptoms go away, and my sight will return to normal.

One thing is for sure. Im never touching those drugs again.
