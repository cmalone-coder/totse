Any decent system will have a universal escape for all wrist grabs, all chokes etc.

Anybody thats disaplined and motivated can take a shot to the balls.

There are a lot of Tae kwon do places(other arts too) that are just like Rex Kwon Do.

I can beat anybody, and anybody can beat me. Simplest way to put it.

The most effective for of self defence would probobly be having "street smarts" and avoiding a situation. Firearms, with good training are great too.

Ive never heard or a cross breed Aikido-Judo. Aikido, Jiu-jitsu and Judo are related and quite similar. Jiu-jitsu, boxing and wrestling sounds like mixed martial arts(MMA) A great sport but it is not ideal for self defence. Assuming you have a decent instructor any one is fine.

In any system you need to:

-Learn to avoid and escape confrontations

-Practice techniques with a highly aggressive, resistant opponent. Once you get the techniques down, that is.

-Avoid any rules. Brutal, dirty and no "this technique is too dangerous to spar with" crap

-Learn not to "fight" and attacker but eliminate him without letting him have giving him a chance. Fighting is for WCW and the ring.

-Avoid any systems that use highly complicated techniques, and lots of them. If you cant do it right first time, every time it wont work for you. You need something thats simple yet effective, so that it works well under stress.
