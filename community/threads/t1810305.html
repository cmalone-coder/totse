Atheist- Someone who doesnt believe in god. When an atheist is asked, "Do you believe in god?" They will reply, "No."

Strong atheism- When posed the question, "Do you believe in god?" A strong atheist will reply, "God does not exist."

Agnostic- Someone who believes that the existance of god is unknowable. When an agnostic is asked, "Do you believe in god?" They will reply, "I dont know."

Theist- Someone who believes in god. When asked, "Do you believe in god?" A theist will respond "Yes."
