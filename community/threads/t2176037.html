Re: Solvent hash
the reason butane can come out looking cleaner is because it is in contact with the bud for a shorter period of time

NOT (entirely) TRUE !

Although contact time between solvent and buds is proportional to the purity of oil, it is not the main factor.

Cannabinoids are nonpolar while chlorpohyll and most of the plant's garbage is polar, meaning that you will not be able to get SAME QUANTITY OF SAME PURE PRODUCT from same amount of bud with for ex. ethanol as you will be able to with say hexane for example. WHY?

Because butane, hexane, pentane etc. are NONPOLAR solvents while ethanol, acetone, etc. are MODERATELY POLAR.

Meaning that due to inherit nature of ethanol/acetone you will pull proportionately more "garbage" along with cannabinoids, than you would by using strictly polar solvents.

This is why quickwash with acetone/ethanol is rather wasteful, unless you do multiple washes therefore compromising the purity. Pure honey oil that matches the BHO quality can be obtained without butane by doing something called "quickwash"with an appropriate solvent and medium to high quality buds.

IMO and IME hexane and pentane are far superior to butane in this process due to the ease of adjustment of purity vs. yield ratio - you can observe the extraction and stop when the color of your batch starts getting too dark (impure) for your liking. Not to mention that they are much easier to work with than butane. Therefore:

Hexane/Pentane > Butane > Acetone/Ethanol
