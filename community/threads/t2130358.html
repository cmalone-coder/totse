My best friend takes his aggro out on me
I'm feeling so upset at the moment and I feel like I can't talk to anyone about it.
One of my best friends, when he gets stressed out, he takes it all out on me and gives me a real emotional beating. I always tell him that he does it but he swears blind he doesn't. Every time he's in a mood, I will get it in the neck.

I understand he's stressed out, but I'm having a really hard time too. Sometimes I tell him things about himself that he doesn't want to hear. People need to hear these things sometimes! It's part of being a good friend. I didn't want him to have a shock about something that was going to happen sooner or later. He absorbed this, and then the next mood he was in he threw it all back at me and raised his voice and was shouting down the phone at 3am, or something.
I asked him to please not speak over me and to not shout because I can hear him, and I was adamant that I would not let him hear me cry, but when he didn't, it all came out.
Don't get me wrong, he is the loveliest person when he's not in one of these moods. They don't happen often exactly, just too much to ignore. He is always telling me he loves me to death and would never want to hurt me, and I believe him, I love him too. When I tell him that he's hurt me he never accepts it and it always ends up being my fault.

I don't think I can deal with this anymore - I'm in a really fragile state emotionally at the moment. I've told him that if he is in a bad mood to not even speak to me, because I never seem to help and I always get a bashing. He insists that I'm the best friend anyone could ever have, but he still does this to me?

I love him but I am too delicate at the moment to deal with the other side of him. I don't know how what to do.

I used to yell, scream and swear at everyone and afterwards I had little/no recollection of it or being so bad. This is often referred to as a rage black-out. I swore until I was blue in the face that I wasn't as bad as everybody was making me out to be and refused to see a doctor. It took my then-girlfriend to video tape me of how exactly what I was like at my worst and she re-played it back to me when I was back to my normal self.

Upon viewing the footage, I almost shat myself and couldn't apologize enough to everybody who I had hurt.

I went to the doctor that day and was diagnosed with bipolar and underwent treatment.

At the same time, you are not an emotional whipping post, nor should you ever have to be. If he doesn't seek help, if you can, record one of his phone conversations to you and when he isn't manic (or whatever condition he may have), play it back to him. Dealing with mental illness' in love ones can be extremely testing. Don't give up on him. If people gave up on me I know I'd be a homeless drug addict in a dark ally shooting heroin right now. That's where I was heading.
