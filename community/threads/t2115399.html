It is not a good idea to watch tv in this period because you will be sucked into that viral thing, no joke. After that first hour to an hour and a half the aggressive hallucinations should be gone.It has been my personal preference to just listen to music during that point with my eyes closed and embrace those aggressive visuals. Now you get to toy with your new found "growth vision."

Orally:
It isn't bad, but it takes roughly an hour to come up. The nausea with this can be nasty and last up to two hours. You get your "growth vision" and then it'll eventually peak and you'll see more intense form of the "vision" and then it'll taper back to your normal "growth vision."
