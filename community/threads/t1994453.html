When we confess our sins, it is to a priest, but the priest is acting "in persona Christi capita," which means he is absolving sins in the person of Christ, Who is the Head of the Church. The Confessor acts merely as His Minister, it is still God Who forgives, and absolves. We confess, not so much to the priest, as through the priest. This is mentioned in the Bible - "Whosoevers sins you forgive are forgiven them,"
In the case of the girl in the car crash, when you are in a state of mortal sin, all that is required to remit the sins (insofar as to not be condemned to Hell upon death,) is what we call an "Act of Perfect Contrition," which simply means that, truly contrite, the sinner proclaims their repentance to God - a simple (yet sincere and utterlt heartfelt) "I'm Sorry," could suffice. Confession, however, is required before one receives Communion.

I was interested in the Catholic church for quite some time, and embarked on a serious fast in order to see if I was being called to that church.

I sincerely felt that, no - God did not want me involved in that establishment.

The following were the things I believe were revealed to me during that week.

Primarily, I couldn't find a lot of the doctrine to be biblical.
This includes but is not limited to Mary being without sin, confession, etc. (Notwithstanding, although there may be long, elaborate explanations as to why these actually are biblical that I do not know about, the *confirmed Catholics I that dealt with, from the SEVERAL churches in my area, had no understanding of them... and even went as far as to declare they were taught things that simply weren't Christian doctrine. )

(This can be elaborated on in later posts if anyone wishes).

Secondly, I had a real moment when going through the book of St. Matthew during the fast. It eventually came to the realization that, when I sincerely gave my life to Jesus Christ(the toughest test of my life), it was my Catholic peers who gave me the toughest time over it. It was also brought to my attention that these were the people who drank the most, fornicated the most, and lacked the most understanding of Christianity and having a personal relationship with Christ (To this very day, they try to persuade me to get drunk, to have sex, and to indulge. They also scold me for following Jesus)...I even knew confirmed members who didn't understand what the gospel was, or how to be saved!

Don't get me wrong, I'm not some geek just getting picked on. These were, at one time, my best of friends. They turned their back on me, however, when I found out who Jesus was and took up my cross.

Of the Catholic churches I've seen, I cannot say I have seen any good fruit - that is, with the exception of one person. Yet this one person, who truly loves Jesus even moreso than I do, reminds me that I do have Christian brothers and sisters in the Catholic church. Us protestants shouldn't go around fighting with the Catholics - we should respect each other's choices and stay in unity to the best of our understanding of the truth.

And to whoever the guy is who keeps swearing at atheists and people who disagree with him - if you are a Christian, it probably isn't a good witness to attack people when you think they're wrong (or even if you know they're wrong).

We were all terribly wrong at one time, and Jesus didn't jump down our throats when he had the chance. This is because Jesus is love - and I tell you this in love.
