There are two definitions of "sound"

one refers to physical disturbance of molecules

the other refers to sensation

the latter: Sound is the auditory sensation produced through the ear by the alteration in pressure, particle displacement, or particle velocity which is propagated in an elastic medium (gas has elastic collisions as defined in Kinetic Molecular Thoery [KMT])
