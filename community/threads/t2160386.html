tl;dr: If you are going to ask for a tl;dr version the please don't post, I believe that you can only accurately predict what my weed was laced with if you have as many details as possible.

So yesterday my father was out an i decided to smoke some weed that i had left over from my birthday... It was laced, I don't know with what but that is why am asking you totse what it is... So here is the trip report.

Smoked as normal, tasted sweet in the tongue but was harsh on the throat. Put on some music and laid down in bed. Suddenly there is a whooshing feeling and I hear static, my vision starts fading and i feel my whole body being pierced by needles or shocked, I don't know and them i see a human figure made completely of energy floating towards me...

Then i see standard geometric visuals... Then I'm in my old room in my old house (ohh god bad memories)... then I'm in back in my room but I'm hardly in control, it feels that I'm in a state between sleep and awakedness, it just keeps repeating itself, during that time my vision is like that of an old television, alternating stripes of color and black, but they are not straight lines they are, how can i describe them??? Curvy. I try as hard as possible to get up but i just keep looking back and forth across my room wondering weather what I'm seeing is just a memory projected into my vision, or me actually seeing my room.

Anyways them I get stuck in a loop in my mind and i cant get out of it, the television vision is still happening, im still in my room, but just I'm looking around and the same say, 10 seconds is constantly being rewinded and then being played back. but every few minuets a slight difference in my memory is presented, like i look a little further to the left that time, or a little bit more up, revealing a bit more of the room. It goes like this for a long time, also alternating between full visuals (me not seeing anything in my room) and me looking around my room but in television vision.

Then i gain a little bit of control and i manage to get on to my seat and vomit, then I am propelled back in to full visuals, and the vomiting is just a memory that fades. the i go back to being able to see a bit and I vomit again, good i say to myself I'm coming down. I did but when i was walking around visually I saw my hand touch the wall but I didn't have the physical sensation until about a half a second later (I was also hallucinating that i had 2 bodies and one was about a half second off the other one so when the second body touched anything THEN i felt the physical sensation (So it went; Hand touches door with first hand then the second hand touches door and i feel the physical sensation of me holding the door))

Today, i have been extremely tired, mentally and physically and a bit nauseous, still thinking a little weird....

The come up was very hard, the trip was okay (abet unexpected) and the come down was actually the funnest part other than vomiting a lot...

So totse, what was my weed laced with? I had 2 good bowls and i weight somewhere in the 115-120 pound range.

Thanks ahead for your help!
