Re: harsh smoke?
okay, why have my fellow smokers not mentioned this? If you smoke imported mexican schwagg, chances are that it is old, and was packaged in order to conceal, and save space. The tight compression, combined with lack of airflow can cause mold, bacteria, mildew, and other undesirable contaminats. These make the smoke harsh, and make the weed taste like shit. Also if you are smoking hydro, or chemicaly fertilized soil based smoke, not flushing the nutrient fertilizer out can cause harshness. I recomend spending the extra cash, and getting a bag of organic soil grown goodness.
By the way, plants with high THC content can be hard on the throat and lungs too.
