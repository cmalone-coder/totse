Advice on Opium withdrawals
Hi, it's been a real long time since I have been on totse. Most of the people in this forum are excited about getting high and exploring the drug life. However, I'm done with that and I can't begin to explain how hard it is to quit using opiates like vicodin and morphine. I began using when I thought I was justified about a year ago when I was diagnosed with am auto immune disease called Crohns. Over time ignorantly I built an addiction to opium.
From time to time without realizing what was wrong with me I would suffer withdrawals, I thought I was having anxiety and panic so I was put on more medicine than I was already taking for all the wrong reasons. About a month ago it became very clear to me what was going on, call me naive but I thought I needed the medicine. I tried to quit once and I relapsed in two weeks where I quit again for this past week.
I'm asking for any advice and for some people to please pay attention to what you are getting yourself into. Opium creates a false threshold of pain in your brain, everything is amplified if you don't have your pills. The symptoms of withdrawals are horrible, you don't want to be awake but you can't sleep, you puke and have the runs for weeks, you can't concentrate or enjoy anything. I have become overwhelmed with the guilt of what I have done to my girlfriend and family, the pain they suffer from watching me suffer, it's enough to make you feel terrible about yourself.
