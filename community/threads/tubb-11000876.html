Re: Leaving Computer on
There is the other side of the argument:

When your PC is on, the chip (among other components) is hot. When your PC is off, the chip is cold. When you power on your PC, the heat causes the chip to expand. When you turn off your PC the chip cools and contracts.

Having your chip expand and contract several times a day *could* cause it to be damaged/crack... but in reality, this does not often happen.

I personally turn my computer off when I am not home as I generally do not use it for 8-10 hours of the day until I get home, then it stays on until I go to bed.
