OK Vegas--

I went back and read the entire thread and found a few more things you may want to consider. (As if you don't have enough already.)

First, it never occured to me how problematic working with a hot plate can be. Never had to use one. Three things that are NECESSITIES, not luxuries:

heating mantle with controller (variac is fine)

condenser (preferrably allihn 6 or 7 bulb)

500ml. separatory funnel with teflon stopcock

Well...make it 4 things becauce you need a real round bottom single neck 24/40 ground glass fitted flask.

I know it has already been said, but you need to go right now and get some kind of idea what the calibration is on that hotplate. Do exactly these things

A candy thermometer and a sauce pan is ghetto as hell, but acceptable. MAKE SURE YOU MAKE BOTH OF THEM YOURS.

Whatever oil you choose, USE THE SAME KIND AND BRAND EVERY TIME.

Put the 3 or 4 inches of oil in the pan. Make a measuring stick out of an ink pen casing or something and mark the depth of the oil. YOU GUESSED IT. MAKE IT EXACTLY THE SAME DEPTH EACH TIME.

Attach the candy thermometer in a way that it is a) secure b)you can read the damn thing c)it is at least a pointer finger away from any wall or bottom of pan. MARK THE PAN AND PUT IT THERE......well, you know.

USING EVERYTHING EXACTLY THE SAME WAY EVERY TIME WILL GIVE YOU ONE CONSTANT TO WORK WITH--ACCURACY. WITH EXPERIENCE, IT WILL BECOME MORE IMPORTANT THAN PRECISION.

Now, pick an arbitrary starting point. Lets say 5. Let it sit on that for 30 minutes and don't alter anything. (door open or closed, a/c on or off, etc.) Now, start writin shit down 5 temp, 5 1/2, 30 min., temp and on and on. You want to know 85C, 90C, 95C, and 100C. If you want to join the barbeque club, then also mark 110C and 125C. Swigotta's golden numbers are 85 and 90.

Curb's mega iodine is for insurance purposes. Too much iodine will only make for a messier post rxn. Too little and no reaction.(no 57%, no reduction) Read about resubliming the iodine. It's as easy as a large pyrex custard dish with a mini pie plate on top with the edges taped with electrical tape and sittin on your computer monitor. Even resublimed iodine will fuck with your mucas membranes. (worse even)
