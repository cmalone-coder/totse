I like drugs because some of them can really open you up socially and help you to be the person you want if only for a short while.

I have an inherited tolerance for alcohol, and I have alcoholics on both sides of my family, so I'm cautious about how often I drink. Even so, alcohol would have to be my favorite drug besides methamphetamine.
