Before I get picky, I want to say I find the sphere concept interesting.

It is the sphere, however, where I disagree with a small part of your post. A point (by definition) is dimensionless. Beginning at a given point and going around the sphere will indeed bring us back to the original point. Shifting 10 degrees will create a straight line track which will only intersect the original line in one point (directly opposite the beginning point).

Now, relating this back to your post, you used the word points and implied multiple intersections of the lines.

If we apply these different tracks to different universe's we will only have one common event in both places (refering to two different universes).

Where I fully agree with you is in the multilple universe theory. The ten degree divergence, to use your example, is more akin to Robert Frosts, "The Road Not Taken". We will change course and not go back to the point where we changed course from. The non-return is why I believe that our choices dictate every aspect of our life.

I could go on, but I hope I've made the point. My point would be that we don't shift ten degrees. These trips around the sphere would cut concentric circles, not intersecting circles.

Each parallel universe would have its own unique events ... nothing in common.
