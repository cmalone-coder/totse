One Piece and Gantz are probably two of the best mangas I've ever read.

Both are still going and have reached epic proportions.

One piece is up to chapter 462 while Gantz is at 252.

When I first picked up One Piece I was kind of ... eh ... about the art style, but it really grows on you. It's actually pretty amazing. The mangaka Oda manages to fill each of his panels with beautiful and detailed art. The story has come a long way. After reading volume one, I could have never imagined they would end up declaring war on the entire World Government just for the sake of one of their nakama. Also, before Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince came along, the One Piece volumes were the highest sailing books in Japan. It's they way for reason, because it's just that fucking good.

Gantz is a Seinen manga (older). A Sci-Fi with guns, gore, aliens, and much nudity. At first it was ok and everything, until the main character became more developed. Then it got really interesting. The story, at first, was slow-paced, had no idea what was going on. But finally plot development has started and it's pretty insane. This is a really good series to pick up.

Also if you havn't already, I'd recommend Hunter X Hunter. The mangaka for this one, Togashi, is the same guy who created Yuu Yuu Hakusho. Very very interesting ideas and story. The latest villian though looks remarkably like Cell... something I wasn't too happy about. The mangaka has not put out a new chapter in over a year, and has been in sick leave for quite sometime. It's pretty gory, make sure you find the uncut versions. A lot better.

Anyways hope that helps, enjoy.
