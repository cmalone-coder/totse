the unconscious mind is a very interesting concept . I think we could say it is our new metaphysical frontier , a kind of new found territory to explore .

However to be prone ( i hope ) , people have to have a particular receptive passive like nature ( sheep ) . We may all feel the ripples but do the ripples sway us ?


The ( unconscious ) mind and how it works is always good material to think upon ^^ when we * listen * to our thoughts what do we hear ? We * hear * thoughts ( and we may feel / sense etc on another level but thats another matter ) in our mother tongue . In our language , in our Abc's in our vowels etc also habits ( which are the result of decicions and reactions ) , attitudes and biases . There are alot of factors that design the tracks that the train/s of thought run upon .

the more we become aware of why we think the way we do the more we can break down the unconscious design and construct new , more efficient means of ( purer ) thought . I believe in much the same manner the unconscious is not so determined and constructed by these factors and so has a greater degree of clarity ( in it's own unique way ) . Anyone that is interested should invest some time into understanding methods to discern unconscious content , right now even though psychologists and neuro whatevers will tell you that everything is underwarpas , it aint true . We have only taken infintely small steps in this aspect of ourselves .

Here is a good exercise to understand yourself better . Helps demonstrate to yourself over time unconscious cogs turning and that once you become aware you are more enabled to choose how those cogs should turn ( if they should be cogs , if they should turn etc ) .

generally the idea is to ask your answers questions . The most simple that comes to my mind is why do you want money ( as in beyond practical needs ) ? For which a person will respond in a variety of ways , I'll use for material goods . So naturally the person will say because these material goods produce some form of happiness or result in some form of satisfaction . Now this may sound rather basic initially which would be consistent with the point . It is one of those questions you perpetually ask , and as you find your answers you realise there are deeper questions to your nature . Very simple and very effective , but be honest with yourself . One of the costs of knowledge is the truth sometimes can be unpleasant , if you start denying aspects of your true self / existence then i think something called neurosis ensures . The flip side here is Im not preaching you accept your demons simply name them ( which means become aware of them ) .


I understand dreams play a big role here but I dont have a thorough understanding , they most certainly are deep for examples that individuals or anything can generally embody other concepts then prevcisely what the image would literally convey . It is very complex and quite vast but when we sleep yes we lose a more accute reality based consciousness ( self existence ) and tend to be absolved in our knowledge and awareness .

Someone has probbaly heard about the recent testing that has been going on between dreams and what part of the brain is used ( they used tetris ) , well this demonstrates how the mind defines relevant data when it comes to dreams .
