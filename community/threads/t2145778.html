Ok, let me rant off to you a little. The very first time I saw One Piece was the 4Kids dub on FoxKids well over three years ago.

I hated it. It had the gayest sounding voice actors I've ever came across. The art was very off the wall, I was greatly turned off to it.

But I, being an anime fan since 4 years, I knew better. I did some research on the net, heard nothing but good things despite the art and picked up episodes 1-136.

Those were some glorious moments in my life, discovering the deep story telling embedded in the story, the drama, the comedy, the character development. Everything essential to an epic story was present. It had it all. It was the greatest series I had ever seen.

I couldn't wait for the episodes to be released. (I originally got them from Kaizoku-Fansubs who were far behind on the current episodes and no other groups were subbing One Piece 3 years ago.

This is when I picked up the manga, discovered all the parts I hated in the anime were just filler. And sailed smoothly on the greatest story ever told. Now I prefer the art style over any other series, it's very unique, very distinguishable. You'll definitely grow to love it.

This series is better than Harry Potter, Star Wars, Naruto and many more all combined.
