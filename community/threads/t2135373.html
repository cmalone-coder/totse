Re: What is you're favorite horror writer
Well, Stephen King will always be at the top of my list, with Clive Barker and Joe R. Lansdale close runners-up. Richard Matheson's up there, too. Jack Ketchum's good but spotty; The Girl Next Door was excellent I thought, but She Wakes was downright awful.

Peter Straub... I've read a few, of course the ones co-written with King. Loved The Talisman, thought Black House was pretty good, but the omniscient narrator was sort of off-putting. Straub seems like a mixed bag, but maybe I just haven't read enough of his stuff. I really liked Koko. Floating Dragon was a chore to get through, didn't care for that one at all. I thought Ghost Story was pretty good, if a little over-hyped. Recently I read If You Could See Me Now, and thought the plot was way too similar to Ghost Story. I started on Julia, got about 50 pages in, thought 'Ugh, more female ghosts,' and gave up. Maybe somebody could recommend me one of his other books, cause it seems like he's the only "big" horror author I haven't read too much of.

Also, I don't see the big fuss over Richard Laymon. I've read a handful of his books, and they all seem like badly fleshed-out outlines. And after seeing his picture I couldn't keep the image out of my mind of him hunched over a keyboard fat and sweaty, jerking off while writing all of those rape scenes. Although I admit I enjoyed The Traveling Vampire Show.
