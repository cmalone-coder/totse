Barrett is the manufacturer of 50's for the US Military snipers, they have a bolt gun and a semi auto as choices to use.

The muzzle break is not a silencer, muzzle breaks make a gun much louder, any gun, whether it be a .22 rifle, shotgun, or even the 50. It however is used mainly for knocking out materials and equipment, but is not the biggest of it's kind. There is a 20mm rifle that has the same mission.

Sniper have used the .50 for taking out the enemy however and to great effect. The bullet will always pass through a two legged target, but the energy absorbed is still phenomenal and one shot stops are nearly 100%. The military uses ball ammo, so there is no removing of legs or heads with one shot. There will be a nice permanent hole however about 0.6" diamter (a little larger), than the bullet itself.

In competition with .50's sollid brass or copper rounds are used. I have also seen Speer Point Boat Tails in matches. Basically Speer increased their dimensions of their famous .30 hunting round to .50. These bullets can double their diameter on impact and in the .30's they almost always stop in the target, meaning that all the energy is absorbed by the target.

So with this bullet on the .50 we can expect to see 1" wound cavities, much larger shock cavities, and if the bullet stops the target absorbed around 13,000ftlbs of energy, compared to 4000ftlbs from good high powered hunting rifles. Maybe with this bullet combination a person would see limbs removed, and instant decapatations. But with ball ammo? No, just clean holes.

With a muzzle break on the .50 people should be be 30ft from you, the muzzle blast is that bad and kicks up a hell of a lot of dirt. I have fired 2 .50's in my life, one a Barrett and one a custom designed target rifle. The sound is unreal, the recoil, it's manageable if you hold the gun correctly, but after 5 shots or so, you are very fatigued by the recoil and it starts getting very sore.

The 50cal is a round that will probably never die as it hasn't changed much, Since John Browning invented it. But all that is about to be turned upside down. There is a project in development to increase it's effective range to over 4000m! How so? Laser guidance. A bullet is being developed that has the capability to be guided, it has a single canard and can change it's center of gravity to maneuver. A person paints the target with a laser the sniper shoots at the target with a site or fires using GPS technology and with direction from the laser designator. This puts the bullet on a pretty much straight course with the target. The laser guidance takes care of wind. The problem is that the bullet cannot change it's altitude that well so a very correcty angle of fire is needed. However I'm sure in time, that will be improved as well. When that comes, 50cal gunners too, will be able to shoot and destroy unseen targets, the ultimate in sniping.

Then again this changes things, gunners will not be as dangerous as laser designators, and the conventiol role of the enemy sniper which used to be to take out other snipers (their main goal) will in the future to be to take out anyone that packs a laser. And the ever changing battlefield continues.
