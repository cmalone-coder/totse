BLTC & Tobacco
So I don't ever see a whole lotta tobacco discussion on this board, I know technically topics about "legal smokeables" go in OI, but this is a little different.

Just wondering, do most people on bltc smoke cigarettes? It's a fun thing to do while under the influence of a select few drugs in my opinion (weed, shrooms, acid), and socially of course, but I don't know anyone else who uses tobacco alone as a moderate drug.

In my opinion, smoking alone brings out the full potential of tobacco. I usually find a nice place outside, usually some rocks on any trail in this large nature preserve very near my homestead, and just sit down to smoke and think. I don't know about you guys, but I still get a very nice high when I smoke, feels like a powerful adrenaline rush comparable to when one is surprised/scared, only...pleasant. And during these smoking sessions, if I take big hits very quickly, I honestly cannot stand up or I lose my balance completely. It leaves me in a state of giggly euphoria and bliss.

Anyway, does anyone else appreciate the psychoactive effects of nicotine?

By the way, I only smoke pipe tobacco out of a pipe or roll it into cigs, or American Spirits, the menthol variety occasionally :)
