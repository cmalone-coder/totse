I miss greasy-spoon breakfasts...
Made on a flat top in a little home-town joint, not this Denny's and Village Inn imitation crap.

In the hometown (a community of 1500 people in rural Wyoming), there was a joint that had what they jokingly called the Bottle of Cholesterol (it was listed on the menu as the Breakfast Platter). The placed closed down when I was sixteen, but if I recall, it consisted of...

*3 Eggs Any Style
*3 Sausage Links
*3 Strips of Bacon (and this was reasonably thick, none of that thin crap you get in the store)
*Hash browns or home fries
*Three slices of toast (White, wheat, sourdough, or rye)
*Add coffee for an extra $.30 (which usually was $.50 for a bottomless cup)

I never ate it, but I knew a lot of people who did. That whole thing,which would likely be a ridiculous amount of money at a chain, was $5.75 at this joint.

I was always a fan of two eggs over easy, a ham steak, and hash browns, personally. Total cost at that joint and the place I worked? $4.00.

Anyone else have similar nostalgia trips about joints that serve a legit homey breakfast without having to tell us how "homestyle" it is and screw it up by marginalizing it?
