Re: exploding a propane tank.
Solid oxygen. Lol. Unless you got some really high end scientific equipment, that's not going to happen. Also, cold won't blow the tank, all it'll do is fuel the propane combustion, but as I said above, it's impractical, the dude was joking. Fueling the propane isn't necceary, but it will make a bigger "boom." I'll explain more below.

Also, what kind of propane tank are we talking? Blowtorch size, 5 gallon, or like the fuel-a-house 500 gallon? I'm pretty sure the first two could be a thermite job, whereas the last would not be advisable under any circumstances.

As for thermite, almost all the directions you find online will work. It's a little hard to light though. What I'd recommend is make some ANAL, and have it partially mixed with a part of the thermite. Then when you light the ANAL, it'll burn right into the thermite. Then all the heat created by the thermite, when the tank pops, should ignite the propane released into the air producing a wicked cool fireball.

If you're looking for something with a little more bang, powder some AN and put a big pile of it on top of the tank, so when it pops, the propane throws powdered AN up in the air with it, which when the gas ignites will cause decomp and oxygen release. As for how much, probably 2 kg for 5 gal or .5 kg for blowtorch size.

and its not a "bomb," it's a unique pyrotechnic experience.
