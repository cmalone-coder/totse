PERPETUM MOBILE - THREE POEMS
by THE GNN/DualCrew/uXu



"ICE"

I am the iceman

I am made up of frozen water

if you look at me

I look away

If I look at you

you look away

No One can get under my skin

since I am cold and frozen

No One knows anything

about me

I used to be water

soft and warm?

a lake maybe

a small river maybe

anything you want

now I am

ice

cold hard

untouchable

ice!

a man made of ice

walks on the street

no one sees him

and he looks at nobody

talk -

no answer

words -

what?

I thought

I heard something

have you seen

a man of ice?

or

maybe you have felt

a man of ice?

Forgotten

Alone

left in a package

of frozen

water

I tell you this

a man made of ice

does not want

to speak

with you

or

anyone else

Alone

Forgotten

he like it

Isolated

inside himself

and

made out of

frozen water

that used to be

something

else

a long time

ago

He like it

They like it

She like it

Everybody like it to be as it has always been

an iceman

is always welcome

just keep him away

from the sun

he may

melt



"THE PRICE TO PAY"

Life
is a gift

Somebody gave it to
us

Now
we have to pay
the price

Pay
in agony

Do you think
you
DESERVED
this?

That
you
are special?

Ha ha ha

Welcome to
paradise
sucker

Welcome to
life
Customer

Keep on
paying
my friend

Soon
it will be
gone

Feel
the whip
beat your bones

The agony
rips your inside
apart

Keep on
paying

Pay
for
life

Pay
for
the gift

The greedy
bastard
smiles

Now
he got you

exactly

where he wants
you
to be

Alive!

Keep
on paying

Soon
you cannot
afford it
anymore

Ha ha ha

Keep on paying
Keep on paying
Keep on paying

Life - agony
life - agony
and so on

until

you
cannot afford
it
anymore



"PERPETUM MOBILE"

over and over and over and over
again and again
one more time

everything is ruled
by the machine
the Eternal machine

the machine
the perpetum mobile
does it over and over again

you cannot stop it
you cannot do a thing
to stop it

you are only a small piece
of the perpetum mobile
machinery
