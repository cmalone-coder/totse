So much for the bad news....

I am pleased to announce the relese of Project Bluebook II. For those
of you who unfamialiar with the original Bluebook, it was a text file
containing a list of phone numbers, id's, passwords etc for various
computers througout the country - sadly , it is now out of date.

Bluebook II will cover the following subjects :-

1) A list of all known Hacker bbs's

2) A list of private systems that ahve been cracked, with valid id's
and passwords etc. Probably some log/capture files as well.

3) the same as 2), but specialising in PSS, Pad, Xi-Pad etc.

4) Techniques and methods used for hacking into various systems.

5) A collection of text-files on subjects not covered by 4)

6) Phreaking ,contacts, numbers, methods, circuit diagrams etc.

7) Software, including wardiallers, reviews ,programs to gain
passwords/priveleges for systems.

8) Hardware, including reviews of modems, blue-boxes etc.

9) List of on going targets (i.e computer systems under hack-attack),
reports, methods/passwords tried.

Eventually BB II will become a database, not just a text file. It
will be an information pool for hackers to draw upon, but your help
is needed.

We need you to prepare a list of subjects you feel you can contribute
on. Please don't feel you are too inexperienced or that someone else
will duplicate your contribuition, we need everything.

Obviously the heart of Bluebook II lies in subjects 2) and 3). Any
reluctance on your part to share any hard-earned passwords etc is
understandable, but without them Bluebook will be stillborn.

Why should I contribute ?

When you contibute information, you are offering the knowledege and
experience of one hacker, in return you can use the knowledge
someone else has already been over, and more importantly:-

Why Re-invent The Wheel ?

When the project takes off,each subject will be updated at least once
a month, hopefully once a week. It will be an ongoing concern,
continuously providing fresh information for hackers everywhere.
Think about it, it could revolutionise hacking, for once we will be
organised. There are hundreds of systems that have never been hacked
because no-one has ever got round to them, Bluebook II will change that.

Why should you waste hours on a system looking for a valid id ,let alone
password when a hacker not ten miles away cracked it last week ??

I welcome all comments on this project. I would ask you to debate it
amongst yourselves, with your local sysop and spread the word, the more
who know about this, within certain circles of course, the more
information you will be able to draw upon.

Thank you for reading this. PLease leave a response, the sooner I hear
from you, the sooner you will see some results.

Thank you,
Joe Dredd (22/9/89)
