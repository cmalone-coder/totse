In this session, I will bring forth Bell's Identification Letter Codes and the meaning along with each letter code. These codes are used in addressing, basic letters, forms, order sheets, and a lot of other assorted documents. The codes are usually no more than four letters long. If they were much longer, a person couldn't really call them codes.

Bell's letter codes will be useful in many ways. For one, if a person would happen to get their hands on some good information, with some parts encoded with Bell's identification codes, those parts could be easily understood.
