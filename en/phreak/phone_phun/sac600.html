SAC 600 ASSIGNMENTS

SAC 600 (formerly 610) is an assignment in the North American Numbering
Plan used for Canadian "datalink" or ISDN services. This is a descendant
of the old 610 TWX code used for telephone company teletype service,
which since became obsolete.

courtesy Industry Canada - Canadian Numbering Administrator
as of 20 October 1994
