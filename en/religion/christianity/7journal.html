7. ELECTRONIC NEWSLETTERS/JOURNALS

Electronic Newsletters and Journals are a special type of mail- based
information sources in that they are typically one-way.

BALM IN GILEAD NEWSLETTER
Mail: walter@pro-christ.cts.com
Desc: Official newsletter of Balm in Gilead Ministries.
Sub : To subscribe send a request to Walter at the above address.

A BYTE OF TORAH
Mail: listserv@israel.nysernet.org
Desc: Weekly discussion of Old Testament passages from a Jewish
perspective. (788 subscribers)
Arch: Back issues available via gopher to nysernet.org (1/Selected
Gateways to many resources/NY - Israel project/Jews and
Judaism/Devrei Torah/commentary/bytetorah)
Sub : To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUB BYTETORAH YourFirstName YourLastName

CONTENTS
Mail: listserv@acadvm1.uottawa.ca (Internet), listserv@uottawa
(Bitnet)
Desc: Announcements of new and recent publications of relevance to
Religious Studies and related disciplines (1351 subscribers)
Note: Back issues available via gopher to gopher.cic.net
(1/e-serials/alphabetic/c/contents)
Sub : To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUB CONTENTS YourFirstName YourLastName

CHRISTIAN GROWTH NEWSLETTER
Mail: bible@olsen.ch
Desc: Intended to help Christians grow, includes testimony, articles,
book reviews and event calendars
Sub : To subscribe send a request to Dan Smith at the above address

Chuck Colson's BREAKPOINT COMMENTARIES
Desc: A daily article on timely issues of interest.
Note: The daily BreakPoint posts have been suspended, but special
BreakPoint posts are still being released and archived at these
sites. Archives available via aftp to ftp.cs.albany.edu
(/pub/ault/bp). and iclnet93.iclnet.org
(/pub/resources/text/breakpoint)

DAILY VERSE/INSPIRATION
email: daily-verse-request@cc.umanitoba.ca
Desc: Daily mailing of a bible passage for encouragement.
Sub : Send a request to the above address.

DESKTOP PROVERBS
Mail: ronm@slc.slac.stanford.edu
Desc: Daily distribution of several verses from proverbs without
commentary.
Sub : Send your name to Ron MacKenzie at the above address

FAMILY RESEARCH COUNCIL
Mail: server@family.netcentral.net
Desc: a mailing list (not explicitly sponsored by the Family Research
Council) which will distribute Family Research Council's (FRC)
monthly newsletter from president Gary Bauer and soon, FRC's
monthly publication, Washington Watch.
Sub : To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUBSCRIBE frc-list

FOCUS ON THE FAMILY
Mail: server@family.netcentral.net
Desc: a mailing list (not explicitly sponsored by Focus on the Family)
which will distribute Focus on the Family's monthly newsletter
by Dr. James Dobson and other periodic (non-copyrighted)
material from Focus.
Note: Archives available via ftp to iclnet93.iclnet.org
(pub/resources/text/Focus.on.Family).
Sub: To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUBSCRIBE fof-list

THE INTERNET PROLIFE JOURNAL (IPLJ)
Mail: server@prolife.netcentral.net
Desc: A twice-monthly newsletter which seeks to convey the pro-life
message, share information and help pro-life individuals have a
strong impact on their communities.
Sub: To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUBSCRIBE iplj-list

INTERCESSORS FOR AMERICA (IFA)
Desc: A monthly newsletter highlighting significant issues for urgent
prayer in America. Available on Conchr-l

JOHANNINE HOURS (Taize Community)
Mail: brother.roy@almac.co.uk
Desc: A monthly devotional from the Taize Community to assist young
adults in the assimilation of the Bible's message. Brief
commentaries on selected passages and questions to assist
meditation and sharing (160 subscribers).
Note: Archives accessible via ftp to iclnet93.iclnet.org
(/pub/resources/text/taize) and via web at WWW Christian
Resource List
Sub : To subscribe send a request to Brother Roy at the above address

THE LIGHTHOUSE
Mail: lighthouse-request@sabine.psu.edu
Desc: A monthly e-magazine focusing on contemporary Christian music
with a mission to encourage people to engage in a deeper
personal relationship with God through His Son Jesus Christ
using the contemporary Christian music.
Note: Archive available via gopher at etext.archive.umich.edu
(1/Zines/Lighthouse)
Sub : To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUBSCRIBE YourEmailAddress

MORNINGSTAR MAGAZINE
aFTP: ftp.lysator.liu.se (pub/religion/christianity/morning-star)
Desc: a Christian publication grounded in the deity of Christ and the
inerrancy of Scripture. Intent is to 1) give out the message of
the Gospel and 2) to provide material to help the believer grow
in the Christian walk
Note: Recent issues available in msdos and mac executable versions
only

ONE BREAD, ONE BODY
Mail: mail-server@mintir.new-orleans.la.us
Desc: Daily meditations from an evangelical catholic perspective (82
subscribers)
Sub : To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUB OBOB-L YourFirstName YourLastName

OUR DAILY BREAD
Desc: A daily devotional from Radio Bible Class ministries. Available
via web http://unicks.calvin.edu/daily-bread.html also
available to Conchr-l and Bstudy-l subscribers. An archive
available at http://neptune.corp.harris.com/mbox_bread.html

THE PROLIFE INFONET MAILING LIST
Mail: server@prolife.netcentral.net
Desc: Moderated, one-way list for distribution of information
submitted by pro-life organizations at the national, state,
regional, and local levels. This includes newsletters, event
announcements, and legislative alerts from various pro-life
watch-dog organizations.
Sub: To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUBSCRIBE infonet-list

THE PROLIFE NEWS
Mail: server@prolife.netcentral.net
Desc: A twice-monthly newsletter developed to inform, remind and
encourage people about the sanctity of life. Provides news and
resource info from around the world (300 subscribers).
Note: Extensive archive, including back issues and indices, available
by ftp to ftp.pitt.edu (/users/s/t/stfst/pln). Also accessible
via html doc at http://www.pitt.edu/~stfst/pln/AboutPLN.html
Sub: To subscribe send the following to the above address -
SUBSCRIBE plnews-list

THE SCRAWL
Mail: scrawl@vax.dickinson.edu
Desc: A Christian E-Mail newsletter for students published weekly
during the academic year.
Note: Back issues available via gopher at gopher.cc.lehigh.edu
(LUNA/GROUPS/LCF/CFSNEWS). For updates finger
scrawl@vax.dickinson.edu
Sub : To subscribe send the following to the above address
Your name and email address

TODAY
Desc: A daily devotional from the Back to God Hour. Available via web
at http://unicks.calvin.edu/today.html

WORDS OF HOPE
Desc: A daily devotional from the Reformed Church in America.
Available via web at http://unicks.calvin.edu/woh.html
