Shamanism

Shaman 101-C

The primary goal of the following exercises is to introduce you to,
and enable you to work with, the chakras that make up your spirit
body. While most people have heard of chakras, and many have had
some experience in dealing with them, relatively few know how to use
them to advantage. This is not only useful in esoteric realms, it is
helpful in daily life. A brief explanation of the chakras is in order
for those who need some "brushing up."

Beginning at the point where the feet touch the earth, and ending
above the physical head, there are a number of energy vortices,
generally known as chakras, spaced along a vertical line from the
feet up through the spinal column and out past the top of the head.
If you can see them, the surface of these chakras have the appearance
of half-dollar sized discs or wheels, each rotating at an individual
speed. They have color and vibrational and planetary correspondances,
as well, although we will only touch on that in this series. And, for
shamanic purposes, they are also tunnels through which you can travel
inward into adventure.

Although there are MANY chakras, there are seven chakras that are
worked with in these exercises. If you are interested in the Eastern
names and relationships of these (and other) chakras, there are
plenty of books available on them. For the time being, let's simply
call them by numbers for ease of identification.

Chakra 1, for our purposes, lies at the base of the spine, the
sacrum. The color of this chakra is red, which corresponds to
vibrant life. Survival is the key of this chakra, and it is here you
access ancestral memories from this and other lives. Incidents that
frighten you/threaten your survival (including financial reverses) can
cause this tunnel to open to the point where you lose valuable energy.
Without the grounding provided by this chakra, you can begin to feel
"spaced out", and become anxious and fearful. Maintaining this chakra
in a balanced and controlled state is necessary to maintain good
physical health. Additionally, this is the seat of Kundalini, the
power that can be raised through all the chakras to open blockages and
promote healing. (There are a number of books on this subject, too,
if you choose to pursue it.)

Chakra 2 is located just below the naval, above the womb in a woman.
This chakra (variously seen as pink or orange) controls raw emotion,
sexual energy and attraction and fertility functions. Blockages here
often have their roots in sexual traumas experienced in childhood or
another lifetime. (This need not be sexual abuse, but simply
frightening or unsettling situations that were never fully resolved.)
People of both sexes with chronic abdominal problems often need to
balance and unblock this tunnel.

Chakra 3 is in the solar plexus, above the naval. Generally seen as
yellow, this is a center of will and action. From this chakra,
access can be gained to leadership skills, assertiveness and the
ability to withstand extreme physical exertion. This chakra also acts
as an overall balancing system for the body's energy level. This is
the seat of Ki. or Chi, or Miwi, that many people know of, and few
learn to control. The greatest single cause of blockage in this area
is a lack of self-esteem rooted deep in the childhood or another lifetime.
Learning to control and take charge of this tunnel will enable you to
deal with oppresive people and situations without being threatened,
and to exert yourself physically (such as racing or swimming) when
necessary without undue depletion.

Chakra 4 is the heart chakra. This is probably the most talked
about, and the most improperly exercised, chakra in the modern New
Age movement. The heart chakra controls emotion, affinity for the
environment, and affectionate and intimate relationships. The chakra
itself is usually green, although pink stones are associated with
balancing this area. Most often, this chakra is closed because of
unresolved grief. At an adult level, this can happen for short
periods after arguments or breakups, but when anger and hurt are
unresolved from childhood or past incarnations, this chakra can
become so blocked that the normal harmonic/loving feelings a person
has are unable to escape. We know these people as cold, cynical, and
hard to please.

Blockages in this area MUST be resolved in order to use the three
higher chakras, which are higher vibrations of the first three. In
most people, however, this is the stopping point of chakra work, if
indeed they get this far. (I've often wondered if the large numbers
of heart attacks in people in high-pressure situations is a result of
too much Will (chakra 3) and not enough Love (chakra 4)...)

(It should be noted that there are a few people in whom the opposite
problem exists: they are TOO open in this area. This allows legions
of manipulative people to attack their self-esteem and hurt them, and
they appear helpless to stop the cycle. Control is the answer - not
just to open or close, but, with practice, to be able to control HOW
open or closed this chakra/tunnel is, and thereby control the damage.
At the same time, additional work with the third chakra (will) enables
these individuals to raise their self confidence and the manipulators
will be forced to move on if they want an easy target!)

Chakra 5, at the base of the throat, is light blue to lavender in
color. This chakra is one of the most abused in the system, and one
of most necessary for health. This chakra governs communication,
artistic expression, and the abilities of clairaudience/channelling.
Chronic ear or throat problems probably point to a blockage here
(literally swallowing your words can cause trouble). Much of this
stems from childhood memories of being ignored or ridiculed for
attempts to communicate with adults. Also, creative expression in all
forms, if stifled, will cause a blockage here. People with unpleasant
voices frequently have a blockage here, which, with work, can be
overcome.

If you think of the chakras as being on an ascending spiral, chakra 5
is the "higher" counterpart of chakra 3. Chakra 3's keynote is will
as applied to physical form; chakra 5 is will applied to creativity
on a spiritual level.

Chakra 6, located at the top of the bridge of the nose, between the
eye brows, is indigo. It represents the "third eye" or "second
sight", and is the basis of visioning work of all kinds. When open
and functioning properly, it gives you s ike x-ray vision - the
ability to see what people are trying to hide from you (especially
useful in business deals!). Beside the ability to see the spirit
bodies of others, and to see the spirits of all beings in their true
form, this will enable you to "far see" into another time/dimension or
into this world at a distance. (It is here that you may be able to
"replay" past lives like movies, and be watching them, as well as
experiencing them). Blockage here might include repressed imagination.
Frequent headaches or sinus aches after extended periods of "people
watching" might indicate a need to control this tunnel, to prevent
overload.

Chakra 6 is the higher counterpart of Chakra 2. Fertility in the
physical sense transmuted to fertility in the spiritual realm -
taking the creativity of Chakra 5, and manifesting it, through the
imagination, into spiritual vision.

Chakra 7 is the top of the head, and is in many ways a very important
area for shamanic work. This is the tunnel that your spirit animal
uses to come and go, and the one most shamans use for travelling.
(Some schools of thought use the 3rd chakra as a "doorway" instead.).
It is through this opening that knowledge from other worlds comes to
us (intuitively?), and that our light and knowledge take wing.
Frequent headaches in this area could indicate something trying to
get in (or out) that is blocked. Most blockages in this area come
from simply not recognizing the spiritual nature of this area. Most
of us simply don't take the time to notice what happens "up there."
Colors ascribed to this chakra included Violet, silver, gold and w ,
depending on the person involved.

Chakra 7 is the higher counterpart of chakra 1. This is chakra 1's
survival on a physical level transmuted to survival in all the
spiritual realms, including those which you yourself have created for
this purpose.

It is not necessary to have an encyclopedic knowledge of chakras or
energy systems within the physical and spiritual bodies in order to
do these exercises. What IS necessary is the "seed" knowledge of
what each chakra is, and an acceptance of your ability to influence
this system by your concentrated effort.

The colors, planetary correspondances and other facts about your
chakras are contained with the system, just waiting for you to
introduce yourself. Rather than worry about studying or memorizing
such information, relax! Here is the "important stuff" in a diagram
that's easy to remember:
