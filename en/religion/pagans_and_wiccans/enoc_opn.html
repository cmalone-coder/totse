In the number 21, in the grand word HIHA;

In the name HVSHHI, in the pass word I.N.R.I..

O Spirits of the Tablet of Spirit

The sign of Osiris Risen!
L.V.X., Lux. The Light of the Cross.

invoked.

duly invoked.
