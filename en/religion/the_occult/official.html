THE THELEMIC TEMPLE
AND ORDER OF THE GOLDEN DAWN
1626 N. Wilcox Ave. #418
Los Angeles, CA 90028

OFFICIAL DOCUMENT

1) No illegal substances are allowed at our religious ceremonies,
at classes or any other Temple or Order events.

2) We do not promote the taking of illegal substances nor do we
endorse becoming a "slave" to any drug legal or otherwise.

3) The signing of a document stating that you are not in the
possession of illegal substances nor will you be in the
possession of illegal substances at any Temple or Order
activities will be required.

4) Our position should be quite clear by now that doing physical
harm to individuals or to animals is abhorrent and is in no way
endorsed by the Temple.

5) It is essential that donations and support of Temple and Order
events be done in a timely manner.

6) Thelema is a Religion just like any other Religion.

7) It is our clear intention to repeat the policy that the Temple
does not endorse any political party. Of course, members may
endorse anyone they see fit.

8) We do not endorse nor is it our position to interfere with the
life style of any individual, yet in regards to the Temple these
obligations are essential if you wish to work within the Temple's
framework and structure.

9) No one is allowed to represent the Temple or Order unless they
are a member of the board of directors, or unless they have an
official document signed by the Chiefs of the Temple and Order.

10) The joys of Thelema are always at your disposal and it is our
sincere desire that you avail yourself of your deepest and most
profound life purpose as well as to share with all the peace, joy
and freedom of The Book of the Law.

Please address any questions regarding this official document to
the Temple in writing.

Christopher Hyatt
(Frater Supreme Chief)
Sir David Cherubim
(Frater Superior Chief)
