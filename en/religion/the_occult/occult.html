a) The Oxford Dictionary:
Something that is kept secret, esoteric, mysterious and
beyond the range of ordinary knowledge; involving the
supernatural, mystical or magical.

b) Christian definition:
From the Latin "occultus", hidden, secret, mysterious:
All that is supernatural and yet not of Divine origin,
(according to God's revelation in the Bible); therefore all
that is from below, from the powers of darkness.
