The New Golden Dawn Rose Cross Ritual


THE NEW GOLDEN DAWN
ROSE CROSS RITUAL

Issued by the
Thelemic Order and
Temple of the Golden Dawn

By Sir David Cherubim
(Frater Superior Chief)
Copyright © 1990 e.v.

1. Light a stick of incense. Go to the South East corner
of the Temple, and make a large cross, vibrating HADIT, and a
large circle vibrating NUIT. Then holding the point of the
incense in the centre of the cross and circle vibrate the Mighty
Name of RA-HOOR-KHUIT.

2. With arm outstretched on a level with the centre of the
cross, and holding the incense stick, go to the South West corner
and make a similar cross, repeating the name.

3. Go to the North West corner and repeat the cross and the
name.

4. Go to the North East corner and repeat the cross and the
name.

5. Complete your circle by returning to the South East
corner and bringing the point of the incense to the central point
of the first cross which you should imagine astrally there.

6. Holding the stick on high, go to the centre of the room,
walking diagonally across the room towards the North West corner.
In the centre of the room, above your head, trace the cross and
circle and vibrate the name.

7. Holding the stick on high, go to the North West and bring
the point of the stick down to the centre of the astral cross
there.

8. Turn towards the South East and retrace your steps there,
but now, holding the incense stick directed towards the floor. In
the centre of the Temple, make the cross and the circle towards
the floor, as it were, under your feet, and vibrate the name.

9. Complete this circle by returning to the South East and
bringing the point of the stick again to the centre of the cross,
then move with arm outstretched to South West corner.

10. From the centre of this cross, and, raising stick
before, walk diagnolly across the room towards the North East
corner. In the centre of the Temple, pick up again the cross
above your head previously made, vibrating the name. It is not
necessary to make another cross.

11. Bring the stick to the centre of the North East cross
and return to the South West, incense stick down, and pausing in
the centre of the room to link up with the cross under your feet.

12. Return to the South West and rest the point of the
incense a moment in the centre of the cross there. Holding the
stick, retrace your circle to the North West, link on to the
North West cross -- proceed to the North East cross and complete
your circle by returning to the South East and the centre of the
first cross.

13. Retrace the cross, but larger, vibrating HADIT, and make
a big circle, vibrating NUIT. Then holding the point of the
incense in the centre of the cross and circle, vibrate the Mighty
Name of RA-HOOR-KHUIT.

14. Return to the centre of the room, and visualize the six
crosses in a net-work round you. This ceremony can be concluded
by the analysis of the Key Word as follows.

15. In the centre, stand upright, feet together, left arm at
side, right arm across body, holding incense stick upright in the
median line, and say:

16. I.N.R.I.
Yod. Nun. Resh. Yod.
Virgo, Iacchus, Holy Father.
Scorpio, Asi, Holy Mother.
Sol, Orus, Holy Child.
Iacchus, Asi, Orus, IAO.

17. Give the Sign of Orus (also called the Sign of Osiris
Slain), and say, "The Sign of Orus."

18. Give the Sign of the Dance of Asi (also called the Sign
of the Mourning of Isis), and say, "The Sign of the Dance of
Asi."

19. Give the Sign of the Joy of Iacchus (also called the
Sign of Apophis and Typhon), and say, "The Sign of the Joy of
Iacchus."

20. Give the Sign of the Blazing Star (also called the Sign
of Osiris Risen), and say, "The Sign of the Blazing Star."

21. Extend the arms again as in 17 and cross them again as
in 20, and say, "L.V.X., Lux, the Light of the True Cross."

22. Now vibrate the four Names to equilibriate the Light:

NUIT BABALON THERION HADIT

23. Now draw down the Light over your head to your feet, and
say:

24. LET THE WHITE BRILLIANCE OF THE CROWNED CHILD DESCEND!

25. Now say the following, giving the Sign of the Enterer
at the word 'stir' and returning in the Sign of Silence at the
word 'still':

26. THE WAYS OF THE KHABS RUN THROUGH TO STIR ME OR STILL
ME. AUM! LET IT FILL ME! (All ends in the Sign of Silence.)
