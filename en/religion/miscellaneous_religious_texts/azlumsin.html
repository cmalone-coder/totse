/////////////////////////////////

I have studied many religions and cultures from all over the world. Christianity
is the most important religion as pertaining to these essays since it is the
one religion I LOVE to HATE.

In christianity you can repent any sin accept for two. You can murder, rape,
molest, steal, and torture yet still go to Heaven. If you commit suicide, there

The worst sin in the whole of Christiandom is not believing. IF you doubt, IF
you question, THEN you and and your heathen ass be going straight to Hell. Yes,
proceed straight to hell, do not pass Go, do not collect $200.

What the fuck is this??!!

We can perform atrocities a hundred times over but we can't question the Church?
what kind of just and kind religion drowns its followers in this kind of bullshit.
The kind that kills, and hurts. Unlike Azlumic bullshit which just makes the
flowers grow.

I urge you to question, talk to holymen, do what you want, ect. Being Azlumic
is living in the light of truth, not in the fear of false sins.

"But," you ask, "aren't there any Azlumic sins?"

Well, yes and no. We don't have any commandments because times change to quickly
for laws and commanding people isn't my style. However, there are a few
-guidelines- for being Azlumic. Let's take a look...

Vengeance doesn't restore life: an easily missed law of the cosmos

However, blood rituals can: hmmm. this is getting strange.

Death should not be meaningless: killing is an art and a committment, not a game.

Honor is forever: The Knyghts live with honor or they die with lots of blood
and gory things flying out of them.

Vengeance can make things better: A Knyght should take a personal matter into
their own hands and resolve it quickly and, if necessary, brutally.

Tears make the flowers grow: Life goes on and Azlum flourishes in everything
around you. Every emotion is power, never forget that.

Bullshit makes the flowers grow: Have fun, dammit!

Well, that's it for formal guidelines. As for suggestions on delicate subjects,
read on.

Sex is good, really good- erotism is power and it really feels good. Azlumites
believe in the beauty of nudity and purity of loving sex. We don't have
strict rules such as the need for marriage or monogamy, simply the need
for love along with sex. Without love, sex is meaningless; ask the whores.

Drugs are good, really good- Marijuana, exctasy, amphetamines, opium, ect. are all
condoned by Azlumites. Drugs can provide enhancement to life, prophetic
dreams, and cool trips. Just don't use harmful drugs (i.e. Heroin) and don't
rely on drugs as a complete escape from life. Otherwise, have fun.

Violence is both good and bad- Justify your actions, you are responsible.

Death Penalty- No. As I've said before, a Knyght or monk or any follower takes
his or her or its problems into its own hands. Death is inevitable, but
it shouldn't be handed out by a government which hasn't been touched by
the crime. Vengeance is personal and intimate, it should stay that way.


I hope you have enjoyed this little essay on sin and Azlum, look for many
more exciting stories from Moebius. Azlumites Unite!!!!!!!!!!
