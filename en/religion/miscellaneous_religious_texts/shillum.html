The sound of dripping water is heard. Other than that, silence. Crumpled buildings mark the landscape. Now one can hear a moan. Far off can be heard a siren wailing its sad song. Is this the Balkans? Oklahoma? Korea? Yes, but more than that, it is the whole world-one vast asylum of pain. This document is not designed to awaken anyone to the knowledge that the world is violent and disintegrating. That is not news. One would have to be blind not to know that already. The intent of this document is to explain the causes and show where these causes will lead. One need not consider what he can do to change the things that are taking place. No change is possible, no solution is offered. It is too late for that. Why is it that it is too late to change our world? There is an answer to this question. Mankind has too long resisted the Spirit which makes for unity and peace. Mankind has grieved away the only solution to its problems. One may not be able to change things, but he can be made to understand why. The author will share what that preparation was. If you have that preparation, you need not concern yourself with the outcome. If you have not prepared yourself, and no desire for preparation is present, then no preparation is now possible.

The author will write frankly and speak specifically in many instances. He invites the reader to set aside offense and simply consider whether or not these things are true. If the propositions in this book are true, then take courage or gather blackness, whichever applies, for the day of judgment has arrived.

Finally, if one lives in the land of stories, then this document may seem like fiction, but if one is willing to tell the truth and tell it at all times, the document here written will serve that person well. These days fantasy and make-believe are the cornerstones of our society. Just one moment's truth-telling sweeps away the refuge of lies. Men must now reap the results of their democracy. That is-the spiritless, lifeless, corrupt majority make their will felt throughout the world and enforce this will by their laws. God's people have often been the few who have been forced to speak against this mob.
