the long awaited file pertaining to the sorcerer:

The Wandering Soul presents... 1995
year of the moon


*******************
( The )
( Sorcerer )
*******************

This essay is written on the topic of the Azlumic apocalypse. True, we don't
believe that the world is destined to end, but we recognize that it is a
possiblity that grows closer to reality with each day.

The number of Ethereal Suns is dwindling as the sorcerer hunts and destroys
them without mercy. When the last Ethereal Sun dies the world will be forever
changed.

When the universe was young and pure there existed a great multitude of Ethereal
Suns throughout all of the physical and other planes. Then great quantities
of energy flowed through all living and dead things. They learned to use this
natural energy for many different things. Some used it to heal, others to kill,
and still others to explore.

Into this world came a man who aspired to sorcery, a common practice then. He
was one of the first men to see the Ethereal Suns as what they are, beings. This
sorcerer found that if he destroyed the suns then he would retain a portion of
their power. An idea of evil erupted in his diseased brain.

The sorcerer figured that if he killed all the Ethereal Suns then no one would
be able to use their power except for himself. He would become God and no one
could oppose him.

In a dream, I heard the dying screams of an Ethereal Sun as it sunk, flames
erupting. It screamed out the name of its killer: Abaddon.

I have found disturbing facts since that fitful night. I have discovered that
Abaddon has been on our plane of existence before and will return. I have also
found that Abaddon has masqueraded as God and given the gift of false prophecy.
The following descriptions are found in Revelations in the New Testament.

"His head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow, and his
eyes were as flame of fire. (1:14)
And his feet like unto fine brass, as if they bruned in a furnace; and
his voice as the sound of many waters. (1:15)
And he had in his right hand seven stars: and out of his mouth went a
sharp twoedged sword: and his countenance was as the sun shineth in his
strength."

Those passages give an impression of power, greed, decadence, and of worse
things. The seven stars may represent the number of suns he had destroyed
so long ago. Sorcery has often been represented by a twoedged sword. He
shone with the power of dead suns.

"I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, saith the Lord
which is, and which was, and which is to come, the almighty. (1:08)
I am he that liveth, and was dead, and, behold, I am alive for
evermore, Amen; and have the keys of hell and of death. (1:18)"

These passages reflect the mortality of a man and the eternity of his power.
The also show that he is not yet finished. The keys of hell and of death
may represent his knowledge of how to destroy the Ethereal Suns.

"He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the
churches; To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the hidden
manna, and will geive him a white stone, and in the white stone a
new name written, which no man knoweth saving he that receiveth it.
(2:17)"

This passage shows Abaddon starting to gain a following. He promises power
and rebirth to those who follow him. In many ways the white stone may be
symbolic of some sort of slavery pact with Abaddon in which you are reborn
with his power and become part of him.

The following is a quasi-prophey of things which may come if we are not
careful...

"And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book
written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals. (5:01)"

The book, as all theologists know, will unleash the end of the world through
God's will and wrath. However, it is truly the end of the world as we know
it through Abaddon's destruction of the final Ethereal Suns. The seven seals
seperating us from the end are the last seven Suns.

"And I saw, and behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had
a bow; and a crown was giveth unto him: and he went forth conquering,
and to conquer.
And there went out another horse that was red: and power was given
to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth."

These passages show the giving of power from Abaddon to others so that they
can destroy those that stand before him. Colors and symbols are very powerful
in sorcery so that this passage looks very much like a powerful sorcerer
creating disciple to do his work.

"And the third angel sounded; and there fell a great star from heaven,
burning as it were a lamp, and it fell upon the third part of the
rivers, and upon the fountains of water;
And the name of the star is called Wormwood: and the third part of the
water became Wormwood; and many men died of the waters, because they
were made bitter. (8:10-8:11)"

These two passages show Abaddon may attempt to create a new, evil, Sun which
will be called Wormwood. This sun shall fall to earth in order that he power
be established as part of the living planet. Abbadon's evil will pollute the
pure waters and his followers will feast of his blood as you will see in the
following passages pertaining to vampires.

"They shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more; neither shall
the sun light on them, nor any heat. (7:16)
For the Lamb which is in the midst of the throne shall feed them,
and shall lead them unto living fountains of waters: and God shall
wipe away all tears from their eyes. (7:17)
And the moon became as blood. (6:12)"

The Lamb is innocent blood and living fountains are arteries. If they shall
not be bathed in the light of the Ethereal Suns then they shall hide beneath
a moon which has become blood. If God wipes away all tears then God takes
away their souls for it is the soul that weeps and feels the pains which make
us who we are inside. This shows vampiric slavery.

The magical energy is dying in the universe as the Ethereal Suns dies. As more
join the ranks of believers, more energy is protected and focused. Eventually,
Abaddon will have to be destroyed and the remaining Ethereal Suns will need
to be protected. However, no matter what happens the world will not end. In
the worst-case scenario, all energy will dissipate and Abaddon will become
God and enslave untold multitudes using their own beliefs as his leashes.

In the past there were dragons, vampires (both of evil and good), and many
other creatures which are rare or non-existent now. They perished as the
energy fields ebbed. Now new creatures which are of putrid evil and have
been created by Abaddon or other corrupted sorcerers are moving into our
realms. They hide in the cities, our lack of belief their only strength.
Soon we will have to break down the walls of modern propaganda and fight
the coming hordes.

Our greatest weapon in the coming years will be tolerance, belief, love, and
unity. When the new millenium comes a new age will dawn and we will step
up to take our place among the many religions.
