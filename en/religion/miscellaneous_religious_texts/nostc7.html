Century VII by Nostradamus

This is the seventh century by Nostradamus, it only contains 42 verses
instead of the normal 100.

The special characters in the text are:
? - [dec:130, hex:82] e accent d'aigu. (e')
? - [dec:133, hex:85] a accent grave. (`a)
? - [dec:135, hex:87] c cedille. (c,)
? - [dec:138, hex:8A] e accent grave. (`e)
? - [dec:147, hex:93] o accent circonflex. (o^)

Source: Erika Cheetham, "The prophecies of Nostradamus",
1981, Berkley Books, New York.
first print in 1973.
