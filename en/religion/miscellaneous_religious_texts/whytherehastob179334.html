Why there has to be a Supernatural Being

by Absolut Genius

This article (while it takes examples from different religions) does not delve solely into a specific religion in and of itself, but rather the belief that there is a being who created the universe. There are multiple factors in which I believe prove the existence of a higher power.

Evolutionists and atheists believe we come from nothing, essentially we are created from dust. They believe everything we are, and everything we were created from was simply the result of random chance. The reason they do not believe something higher could create us is that they do not believe in the spiritual realm, a realm that is of a different dimension of our own realm. They cannot believe this because they do not believe in hauntings (at least true atheists don't). These people who call themselves atheist but do believe in hauntings are simply agnostic, they recognize there is a different realm, and therefore have a sense of spiritual religion.

While a person can claim that hauntings are the result of an EMF (electro-magnetic field) residing in a specific area acting on the brain to induce hallucinations, ask that person why the field can fluctuate in magnitude, disappear, reappear, and move. This is because these fields are "spirits" of people that have gained the ability to move between realms at will. However, because their characteristics must change to a form this physical realm will allow, they must move about in the form of the energy they possessed before they crossed from that plane of existence to this one. Since each human has a unique electro-magnetic field surrounding them, this is the "physical" energy of their "soul" in this world.

Consider Buddhism, which concerns its believers with achieving the state of nirvana, where your mind (spirit) is able to shed its mortal skin, and no longer has a need for your body, and thus can travel at will along different planes of existence upon "death".

Near-death experiences have shown us insight into the spiritual realm from first-hand accounts. People who have come back from near-death have told researchers many things, few tales from "beyond" do not include going through a tunnel of some kind, along with peace, belonging, and happiness once they arrive at their "destination". Dr. Raymond Moody is the leading researcher on near-death experiences and his insights will no doubt answer any clarifications you need.

Why this is relevant: Supernatural events tell us that the laws of physics can be defied by something that is not able to relate to the laws of this dimension. If a "human spirit" is able to traverse between planes, then others of the same dimension may surely do so as well.

Second, the only thing that lasts forever in our universe is time, which is simply the measurement of change. There cannot be a beginning to the measurement of change, it is essentially counting to infinity. Because of this, we must go back to way before Earth was created and look at the reason for "the big bang". Theorists say this is just a small speck of ultimately compacted mass. Our intellect tells us that everything must have a beginning, and everything must have an end. This is the way of things in our universe. The speck's _end_ was the "big bang", but it must have been created from something else. It CANNOT have been there for the infinite amount of time before that.

You might be asking where the matter of our universe may have come from then, well I believe it was a being of the spiritual realm who was able to reach our own realm. Once here, this being was converted to the energy it possessed. This being must have possessed enormous amounts of energy, forcing some of the energy of this being into the physical form of atoms. However these particles of matter were so compressed and so full of energy, they simply exploded out. This is probably the cause of the "big bang" they talk about. This is what led to the creation of the universe, and this is why there has to be a higher being. As I go along, I come to more and more realizations of this theory. This that we are made from God him/her/itself. And that while we may be made of the physical part of him, the energy, the lifeforce of our creator, is the guiding force in our lives, leading us unknowingly along to its ultimate plan, or possibly just deistically letting us wander through this existence until we are able to go back to our home in the spiritual plane.

In summary, this whole article is simply saying something cannot come from nothing, and EVERYTHING of this world has a BEGINNING as well as an end.
