State of Intent and Purpose

The American Encyclopedic Dictionary defines the word "testament" as: "A covenant esp. between God and man." The word "covenant" is defined as: "A solemn and binding agreement." Definitions for the word "study" are: "1) application of the mind to the acquisition of knowledge by reading, investigation or reflection; 2) to examine or investigate carefully and in detail; 3) to think deeply, reflect or consider; 4) to apply oneself to the acquisition of knowledge by reading, investigation or practice." The word "fellowship" is defined as: "The condition or relation of being a fellow. "Definitions for the word "fellow" are: "1) to make or represent as equal with another; 2) belonging to the same class or group, united by the same occupation, interests, etc; being in the same condition: fellow students, citizens, etc.; fellow sufferers.

In light of these definitions and with that portion of the Holy Spirit which dwells within us, we, the founders of this society make the following statement:

The intent and purpose of The Seven Diamonds Plus One--Testaments of Jesus Christ--Study and Fellowship Society is: To examine carefully and in detail the covenants between God and man as contained in the Testaments of Jesus Christ that are herein se forth; and to think deeply upon, to reflect and consider how we (individually and together) may fulfill the solemn and binding agreements that we have entered into with our God, and to practice--to perform all that we have covenanted to do that in so ding God might thereby bless us according to all that He has covenanted to do. In other words, to associate together as members of this society, in full fellowship and brotherly love, being equal in the temporal things of this earth that we might also be equal in the eternal things of heaven, being fellow sufferers for the testimony of Jesus Christ--together mourning, weeping, fasting, praying, rejoicing, testifying, and singing praises unto God, lifting one another up in all things and thus, "Living after the manner of happiness."

Furthermore, there rests upon you the great and eternal honor and blessing and privilege and responsibility if you are faithful to recover all of the Last Tribes of Isreal- to gather them in from their long despersion even all the children of promise even all the House of Israel. Wherefore ye sons of Abraham, Issac and Jacob and Joseph and Ephraim, do you think you can walk in less power and authority than your father walked in and accomplish this mighty work yea, this great and marvelous work? Do you think you can walk in less power and authority than Joseph Smith and Brigham Young waked in, who delivered my people with a mighty hand? For behold, I say unto you, ye sons of Ephraim that you must walk in even greater power and authority than Joseph and Brigham. Yea, I say unto you that you must walk in even greater power and authority than I did when I walked and ministered among the Jews. For behold your are my last day church in this the dispensation of the fullness of times. An you are called and chosen to represent me, even Jesus Christ, upon the earth and therefore you must walk in my last day power and authority to bring forth my great and marvelous and mighty work. Even the restortation of all things spoken of by the mouths of Adam and Enoch and Noah and Abraham and Issac and Jacob and Joseph and Moses and Elijah and Isaiah and David and John the Baptist and I Jesus Christ and John the Beloved and Nephi and Alma and Moroni and the brother of Jared and Joseph Smith. And you my some, Oh Ephraim! Must needs accomplish a greater work than all those mighty prophets of God who went before you. For you are my last day church which is called by my name even The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints and you must needs walk in my last day power and authority. For behold ye are the sons of the noble birthright! Therefore I say unto you, yea, I command you, Oh Ephraim! Awake and arise! Shake yourselves loose from the awful chains which bind you! Awake and arise and come forth out of Babylon and wash yourselves clean! And be clothed in the beautiful and holy garments of light and truth and put on the whole armor and righteousness and salvation and take hold of the sword of my spirit and the word of God and come up, ye sons of Jacob, to the light upon hill. Yea, come and lift up the ensign to the nations, yea come and follow the sign and portent in Isreal. Yea, come and partake of the waters of eternal life freely. And walk as the mighty sons of Jacob in the full power and authority of the Holy Priesthood of God. Yea, come listen to a prophets voice even the voice of him whom I have sent crying in the wilderness. Repent Ye! for the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand! Yea, come listen to my servant Immanuel David Isaiah for in him is my light and my convenant unto my people even all my servants then consider this. I even Jesus Christ, the Savior of the world, came unto mine own and mine own received me not. Yea, they counted me to be of no worth and the least among all the children of Isreal and I was taken by mine own and they crusified me upon the cross. Wherefore, ye sons of Jacob, if ye wait for another deliverer ye must needs wait in vain and ye must needs be destroyed even as the Jews for they would not receive me and they waited in vain for another and they were destroyed saith the Lord. Wherefore, ye have my Righteous Right Hand. And the Holy Ghost doth witness unto you that it is I who speaketh unto you. I am the Spotless Lamb. I am the Great Jehovah. I am Jesus Christ who am mighty to save and deliver, Therefore, listen of obey. Amen.
