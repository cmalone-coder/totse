Gateway to other worlds as related by Pariah

From Pariah:

//////////////////////////////////
- The beauty of chaos -
/////////////////////////////////

Imagine a swirling light engulfing you, music which seems familar yet
alien plays around you. You can actually SEE the music, you can feel the
sensual rhythms course through your body. A planet appears in the distance, a
strange planet with complex cities coursing like veins across its distant
surface.
Tapestries appear in front of your eyes showing scenes of beauty and
violence that seem so familar. Whole worlds appear before you, you live millions
of lives in a matter of seconds. You try to rationalize what is happening, this
isn't real, you say to yourself. You're wrong. It is real, and you can use it.

You can use it like I have and will continue to do.

For me, all I have to do is close my eyes and I am in chaos. I have
seen the future, the present, and past of this and many other worlds. You don't
need drugs or hypnosis, just concentration and BELIEF.

What is this? I hear you cry. It is AZLUM.

This is the union between conscious and sub-conscious that allows the mind
to leave the body and travel through the non-material planes. The entity, Azlum,
can communicate with you directly through this medium.

This form of meditation can release stress, focus the mind, and free the spirit.
Of course, their are many forms of meditation, but I feel that this form is the
easiest and the most Azlumic.

Put your hands to your eyes to block out all light. Lose yourself in the darkness.
Colors will begin to pulsate around, focus on the brightest one. Picture yourself
floating towards this vortex of color. Perhaps it will turn into a symbol, a person,
a tapestry, or some other meaningful device of Azlum.

To have incredibly lucid experiences, burn hash or poppy as incense while you
are doing this. It tends to be hard to do this on a full or empty stomach, but
if you want to try...

Don't be dismayed if it doesn't work all the time. I lost my ability to use this
procedure a while back. Thank Eternity that I got it back. Just keep trying and
always remember your dreams, they are not only the voices of your soul but of
other souls across the planes.

Please, my brothers and sister, a plead to you to spread this message of mine
across the known universe. If you could, get the other Azlum text files and
put them on the Internet if you can. Thank you, may the ether reveal itself to
you my friends.
