Century VIII by Nostradamus

This is the eighth century by Michel de Nostradame.

The special characters in the text are:
? - [dec:130, hex:82] e accent d'aigu. (e')
? - [dec:133, hex:85] a accent grave. (`a)
? - [dec:135, hex:87] c cedille. (c,)
? - [dec:138, hex:8A] e accent grave. (`e)
? - [dec:147, hex:93] o accent circumflex. (o^)
? - [dec:151, hex:97] u accent grave. (`u)

Source: Erika Cheetham, "The prophecies of Nostradamus",
1981, Berkley Books, New York.
first print in 1973.

Note: The French texts in the above book, are not the originals as
written down by Nostradamus, but to increase readability the
old style writing was converted to a more modern French.
