"Intellectual Laziness" Rebuttal

by Aaron A. Brown

"In/out, up/down, right/left, hot/cold, tension and the release of tension, appetite and the removal of appetite, the passage of time as moments. These are all IDEAS." - William Jeffreys

William, I submit that you are a fucktard. While the way we perceive all of them is an idea, they are realities you fucking douchebag, save for the passage of time, perhaps. And even if they weren't facts, the only thing we have to go by in life is what we perceive to be reality, and logical, rational thought based on these perceptions. The fact that you would dismiss them as false merely because their intensity is an opinion proves that you are the biggest douche in the universe.

Just because left and right, up and down, this minute and last minute may not be facts in and of themselves, if you don't act like they are, you are a worthless maggot that deserves to die.

Ignoring reality doesn't make it go away, and wishing doesn't make fantasy into fact - there is nobody looking out for you. Not even to MENTION the fact that you think using logical thought based on our senses and research is intellectually lazy... There is nothing more intellectually lazy than to make such an overt dismissal of reality.

Atheists are intellectually lazy and seek to put finality and closure to the question of God's existence so they don't have to think about it any more"

You stupid, stupid man. If you are trying to imply that the discovery of truth is a path to laziness, or that mono/polytheists actually THINK about the existence of gods at all is BLINDINGLY stupid. It is truly painful to live on the same planet with someone as stupid and ignorant as yourself.

"It's right there in front of you, but you choose to ignore it." You know, this stupid quote of yours is a VERY good argument AGAINST your position on religion. Fuck.

"Take an honest look and try to see the world (reality, not man's world) as it really is, not through the haze of your preconceptions." YOU shut the fuck up and take a look at reality, douchebag. SENSES, and LOGIC are not preconceptions. FUCK, you're so damn stupid I think I am going to have an aneurism.

"Shut your internal dialogue up long enough to take a good look." I'll shut off your external dialogue, you dumb piece of shit. You pseudo-self-enlightened hippy bastard. FUCK.
