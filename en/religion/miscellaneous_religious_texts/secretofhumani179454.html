Secret of Human Infinity

by Mojo-monkey

In desperation I write. Previously, I have witheld knowledge of this from others, as the ancient wisdom which kept it secret still applies. However, the time is soon approaching when all that is hidden will be revealed (we are already partially there).

This is knowledge that I gained on my own, and was not taught (it is difficult if not impossible to teach; one can only outline, point the way)- I paid for it with years of my life.

It is simply this: the approximation (there is only one true infinity) of infinity for each of us - is nothing. Emptyness. Nada. Void.

This is in regards to the conciousness. Not the senses. The senses are on a more basic level than the conciousness (or merely a differnt level) and do not apply to this.

In practice, simply empty your mind (conciousness).

Do not get high (any way at all)- in order to percieve the feeling of being high, one must lower oneself to recognize a higher level than themselves.

One method (propounded by some eastern thought) is to imagine a flame. And empty your mind into the flame untill there is nothing and even the flame is gone.

Another method is "prayer and fasting" (most who have discovered this have been through religeous observance- in which one submits one self to an _inconcievable infinity and self becomes nothing.

Your concept of self is flawed, as self is inconcievable.

In order to achieve the conceptions and mental 'growth' that we do, one must asociate things with self- and so the things (self) interact to give us knowledge of those interactions. Knowledge we then use in order to function in a world which action is required.

On a physiological viewpoint, there is a specific neurochemical which is our interface of conciousness. Our state of consciousness depends on where along the spinal column this neurochemical is, and in what proportion.

The range is from the bottom of the spine to (and including) the gland at the center of the brain.

This neurochemical, interacts with specific glands along the spine/brain which secrete different neurochemicals when in contact with It. Call them flavors (as good a name as any).

The important part for this document is that when all of the neurochemical (It) is in the gland at the center of the brain, we have achieved concious infinity. This is very difficult, as most have come to rely on the various flavors for their very definition of existence. DO NOT attempt to move the neurochemical up to the gland. Doesn't work like that. Refer to one of the two methods above to do so. Fasting is essential to learn the true meaning of human perfection (paradoxical as that is). But a lesser understanding can come about without fasting.
