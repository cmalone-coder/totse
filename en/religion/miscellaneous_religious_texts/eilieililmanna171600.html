Eili, Eili, l'manna sh'wik-thani!

by Eil

My postulation on the essence of existence follows. When I say God, devil, Heaven, or hell, I mean them not in the usual specific literal or cultural ways that have become vague and meaningless. I use them merely as an appeal to your individual higher spiritual senses of what the words mean, based on your life experience.

The following is not entirely an original idea. It is not a new, emerging philosophy of the strange age we live in, where ordered complexity and sophistication, exist side by side with chaotic complexity and brutality like never before. This philosophy is old. In fact, it is more than just an ancient paradigm, it is the eternal heart of all experience, and, as such, not quite "obsolete" yet. Not yet, not now, not ever. IT IS THE TRUTH. The Gate to Heaven. All of those who seek it in the dark, relax your breath, slow your pulse, and take heed of these words like never before, for I am about to open the gate and drop THE KNOWLEDGE OF GOD.

The Sacred Trinity, the equil-lateral triangle, is the perfect entity. Mathematically, spiritually, aesthetically. Its angles represent all facets of consciousness AND unconsciousness. In perfect equality lays the heart of divinity.

When mind perceives that one side of the triangle is in the position of base, meaning the triangle ( i.e., reality, science, God, divinity, perfection) is pointing up, then the top angle, though equal in degree to the two bottom angles, represents a separated God, being above. As is always the case in any perception of the configuration of the eternal, the positive aspect of this top angle is equal to the negative. This is to say, when God is perceived as omnipotent and apart from creation, then creation is extolled for being from the most precious source, and at the same time, debased for not being part of it. In this configuration, with the triangle pointing up, existence as we experience it comes to be understood as the base. Thus, if what we see in front of us as the universe that God molded for us, is the lowest rung of existence, the chaotic, dissipated mess at the bottom of the triangle, then either despair takes hold or it is seen as an opportunity to build the divine foundation. But my focus is on shedding light to those who know despair.

The illogical solution to the illogical problem of separation is that God does not exist. We cannot accept that we live in hell because that is to accept that Satan is our master. Something not even a supposed "satanist" can really do. Satan is not a master to anything because Satan is hell itself, the lowest rung. But the problem of separation is nonsense to begin with, so confrontation with Satan can be avoided in any case.

God is not separate because God does not exist as we know It, and neither does the devil. This is not the same as saying God must be separate, therefore God does not exist. The first statement is an expression of the divine trinity, the second is simply an expression of the base angles of the trinity.

The greatest ecstasy, experienced as epiphany or orgasm, is equal in magnitude to the deepest rejuvenation that can be reached in a meditative or dreaming state. Recognizing this erases the desire to be close to God, since in truth, God's divine presence cannot be escaped! Not through the scheming of rebellion nor the release of worship. As an illogical solution to the puzzle of logical consciousness, deicide is no solution at all. As Divinity cannot exist without entity, so consciousness cannot survive without sipping from the divine water. Therefore, THE ANSWER is not to actively work to destroy our perception of hell, it is to actively not seek a solution. It is to instead PERCEIVE THE SOLUTION. You are not yourself, you are GOD'S self. As am I, as are we all. The solution is in us. When connected to our true divine nature, we cannot succumb to the pitfalls of ego, for we are all equally divine. Yet joy flows nevertheless. No false pride, just bliss. This is the divine source. This is what matters.

If pure energy is that which flows freely, then it does not know obstacles or resistance, including the limitations of physics, space, time, emotion, and even perception. Energy in its purest form is empty, which means it has no form!!! This then means that all energy is pure. But mind cannot perceive emptiness, so we perceive form. Where does form come from? No where! Because perception itself is empty, one cannot perceive that one's own perception is emptiness. But I just did.

Do you see?

I hope not! This is the Divine Paradox of God, you had no need to see it for you are one with Divinity, as you always were.

-----

In this "mind" state, we can begin to perceive how effective solutions to the rampant superstition, ignorance, and cynicism of life as we naively know it emerge.
