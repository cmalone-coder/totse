The Jesus of Christianity
Vs
The Jesus of Mormonism

. The Jesus WE KNOW contrasted to the "Jesus" of Mormonism. The
one true Jesus of the BIBLE, of all eternity God the Son, who died
that we may have eternal life; who paid the price of all sins; who has
entered our lives and is walking with us in a personal relationship
day by day -- is not known in Mormonism. The Jesus of Mormonism is an
entirely different "Jesus". Let's examine what the leaders and
prophets - the foundation of the Latter Day Saints Church - have said:

. "When the time came that His First-Born, the Saviour, should come
into the world and take a tabernacle, the Father Himself came and
favored the Spirit with a tabernacle instead of letting any other MAN
do it. .... The Saviour was begotten by the Father of His spirit, by
the same being who is the Father of our spirits, and that is all the
organic difference between Jesus Christ and you and me." BRIGHAM
YOUNG, Journal of Discourses, 4:218

. "The fleshly body of Jesus required a Mother as well as a Father.
Therefore, the Father and Mother of Jesus, according to the flesh,
must have been associated together in the capacity of Husband and
Wife; hence the Virgin Mary must have been, for the time being, the
lawful wife of God the Father." ORSON PRATT, The Seer, pg 158

. "Christ was begotten by an immortal Father in the same way that
mortal men are begotten by mortal fathers --- And Christ was born into
the world as the Literal Son of this Holy Being; He was born in the
same personal, real, and literal sense that any mortal son is born of
a mortal father. There is nothing figurative about his paternity; He
was begotten, conceived and born in the normal and natural course of
events, for He is the Son of God, and that designation means what it
says." BRUCE R. McCONKIE, Mormon Doctrine, pg 547,742

. Brigham Young, to be consistent with his "Adam-God" doctrine,
believed Adam was the literal father of Jesus Christ:
. "When the Virgin Mary conceived the child Jesus, the Father had
begotten him in his own likeness. He was not begotten by the Holy
Ghost. And who is the Father? He is the first of the human family
... Jesus, our elder brother, was begotten in the flesh by the same
character that was in the Garden of Eden, and who is our Father in
Heaven." BRIGHAM YOUNG, Journal of Discourses, 1:50,51

. As is evident in these statements, there is no Virgin Birth in
Mormonism. On the other hand, the Bible clearly shows us:

. Matthew 1:18 Mary with child of the Holy Spirit
. 1:20 That which is conceived in her is of the Holy Spirit
. 1:23 Behold a virgin shall conceive and bear a son
. Luke 1:35 The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of
. the Most High will overshadow you

. Further, the "Jesus" of Mormonism was married in polygamy and had
children. This had to be so to be consistent with the doctrines of
Joseph Smith, Junior. As Joseph reports as a "revelation" given him
by God on July 12,1843: "I am the Lord thy God .. obey the
instructions I am about to give unto you .. if ye abide not that
covenant, then ye are damned; for no one can reject this covenant and
be permitted to enter my Glory .. verily I say unto you, if man marry
a wife by my word, which is my law, and by the new and everlasting
covenant .. they shall be gods .. And again, as pertaining to the law
of the priesthood if any man espouses a virgin, and desire to espouse
another, and the first give her consent, and if he espouse the second,
and they are virgins, and have vowed to no other man, then he is
justified .. and if he have ten virgins given unto him by the law, he
cannot commit adultery, for they belong to him." JOSEPH SMITH,
Doctrine & Covenants, 132:2,3,4,19,20,61,62

. "It will be borne in mind that once on a time, there was a
marriage in Cana of Galilee, and on a careful reading of that
transaction, it will be discovered that no less a person than Jesus
Christ was married on that occasion." ORSON HYDE, Journal of
Discourses, 13:259
. "How was it with Mary and Martha, and other women that followed
Him? In old times, and it is common to this day, the women, even
Sarah, called their husbands 'LORD' .. Jesus was the bridegroom at the
marriage of Cana of Galilee .. We say it was Jesus Christ who was
married, to be brought into relation whereby he could see his seed,
before He was crucified .. I shall say here, that before the Saviour
died, he looked upon His own natural children, as we look upon ours."
ORSON HYDE, Journal of Discourses, 2:81,82

. The Bible states that Jesus is God the Son -- eternally existent
with God the Father -- yet Mormon doctrine says Jesus was a spirit
offspring of the Father; that Lucifer was the second spirit offspring
(THEY WERE BROTHERS?!); followed by billions more - or each of us, for
instance. We are "brothers" and "sisters" of Jesus Christ. Jesus is
not the only begotten Son in Mormon Theology: "It makes me think of
that second person that came forth in the heavens when the voice went
forth: 'Who will redeem the earth, who will go forth and make the
sacrifice for the earth and all things it contains?'. The Eldest son
said: 'Here am I'; but he did not say 'send me'. But the second one,
which was 'Lucifer, Son of the Morning', said, 'Lord, here am I, send
me, I will redeem every son and daughter of Adam and Eve that lives on
the earth." BRIGHAM YOUNG, Journal of Discourses, 13:282
. "The appointment of Jesus to be Saviour of the world was
contested by one of the other sons of God. He was called Lucifer, Son
of the Morning .... this spirit brother of Jesus desperately tried to
become the Saviour of Mankind. MILTON R. HUNTER (First Council of 70)
The Gospel Through the Ages, pg 15 [1958]
. "Jesus Christ is also of our species .. I have heard sectarian
priests undertake to tell the character of the Son of God, and they
make him half of one species and half of another, and I could not
avoid thinking at once of a mule." BRIGHAM YOUNG, Journal of
Discourses, 4:217
. "There is no impropriety ... in speaking of Jesus Christ as the
elder brother of the rest of human kind." JAMES TALMAGE, ARTICLES OF
FAITH, pg 473

. In Mormonism, Jesus was a created being: "Jesus is man's
spiritual brother. We dwelt with Him in the spirit world as members
of that large society of our eldest brother." MILTON R. HUNTER,
Gospel Through the Ages, pg 21
. "The devil is a spirit son of God who was born in the morning of
pre-existence." BRUCE R. McCONKIE, Mormon Doctrine, "Devil", 1966
edition, pg 192
. "There are three Gods - the Father, Son and Holy Ghost." BRUCE
R. McCONKIE, Mormon Doctrine,"God", pg 317
