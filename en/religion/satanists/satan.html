I will start this file by saying that I don't believe in Satan, and the
Magick system I follow has no 'God' or 'Satan' persay! If this works for you,
than *RAD*!! Let me know! I have been present at only one 'Black Mass', and
would not like to EVER participate. I will give you two seperate methods of
doing this. The first iss traditional, and the second modern. The only major
difference is the circle.





I. The traditional.

A. You should start off by placing on the ground a circle large enough for
you and your initates to stand inside. It is advised that this is chalk, or
If you must, rope or string.

B. A blessing must me said by all. 'Oh great god of the underworld, father of
the wicked and mother of earth, bless this noble undertaking. We gathered
(Each state full name) ask thy divine protection in this circle.'
PRIEST: (after the blessing) Acta, Noba, Maladicta, Concerita, Agla!
ALL: "So Mote it Be!"

C. Proper apparrel whould be red robes (or clothes is you don't happen to have
a robe lying around the house), or dark black. The person acting as priest
should have a 13 inch wand made of pine, cherry, or willow. You'll need a
sacrafice, but not necessarily alive. A prized possesssion from each will
also work. Something that you value. You will also need 5 white or black
candles.

D. After the blessing, those not making the pact should stay as far away from
the area ass possible! The rest (or one) shall stay in the circle.

E. All hold hands and move slowly around BEING CAREFUL TO STAY IN THE CIRCLE
AT ALL TIMES.... Don't even step on the line. If you are alone, walk slowly
around. All chant "Lucifer, Satan, Evil of ages past. Come to us (me) now
so my breath shall last!" Keep chanting until you can feel the energy
building in the circle. The the priest stands in the middle. All else just
stop where you are. He/She faces the East and says (while tracing a
pentagram in the air) "Father Satan, he who rules now and below, pay heed
to our humble plea. We offer you this (these) sacrafice(s) as tokes of our
wantoness. (At this time, slay the sacrifice, or burn the possessions).
Send a servant to do your bidding and pact with us NOW!"
ALL: "So Mote it Be!"

F. Take a piece of virgin parchment, and in blood write the contract. (The
contract does not have to be in blood, just signed in blood... I'll have
a contract at the end of this file.) Each person takes the bloody quill
and says "Father Satan, this is my pact!" and signs it.

G. Burn the parchment. Arrange the five candles in a pentagram and then
travelling from east clockwise extinguish each flame saying the following.

1. This represents our (my) life (lives) up until now.
2. This represents our following of God the all-father.
3. This is all who stand in our (my) way!
4. This is all that is mine.
5. This is my unity with God & the universe!

H. The Deed is done. Priest: "Oh Father Satan, protect us as we go our
seperate ways and forgive us our sins against you."
All: "So Mote it Be..... And we love thee."
