CIA Proprietaries, CIA Infiltrated or Influenced Organizations, and CIA Contractors

Updated 1/15/97

I am often asked how I come by this information, and how I am able to do so safely. Actually, anyone can do what I have done, it is no great secret, and represents no great danger -- though I often ask in return '"'If we live in a free country and can trust our government to uphold our Constitution, why do we find it neccessary to consider that question?'"' I follow one basic rule: I include on my list chiefly only those operations which have already been exposed in other publications or records of affiliations and activities with those operations. I must acknowledge that I have not always been so careful, and this has invited some attention from the other side which was unwanted.

Jim Hougan (Spooks) estimates that in 1966 there were over 100 industrial security firms headed by fmr. FBI and/or CIA officers, all of which likely share favors with their old employers -- and this is just one small market sector into which the over 150,000 intelligence operatives move once they retire. He also points out that just about any Fortune 500 company eagerly hires retired intel types, as do many of the richest of the rich looking for specialty personal protection and other services (such as the Rockefeller, Hunt, Getty, and DuPont families, etc.), and that hundreds of college professors and even whole university departments or research institutes regularly perform tasks for CIA, including aiding in recruitment efforts. These may be borderline '"'fronts'"' according to the interests of this list, but are not included herein except where specific evidence has been presented to warrant reasonable concern -- particularly with respect to mind control projects. Since it is impossible to prove a particular entity is a front or engaged in CIA activities, short of confession, even those cited by knowledgeable sources here may be subject to critical review. No conclusion can be drawn regarding any given front's operational legality or criminality by mere inclusion on the list, but a review of the bulk of references themselves would reveal few actually seem to be taking care of '"'National Security'"' issues, instead focusing on all manner of violations of laws, civil rights, and even CIA's own charter.

CIA operations within the United States have been, and continue to remain extensive. At one point, in order to satisfy Congressional inquiry, a large wall map with pins showing locations of agency proprietaries in the United States was prepared. The map was so densely populated with pins that even CIA leadership was aghast -- whole states were obliterated by pin heads. Even so, the creation and death of proprietaries was so active that the map was never more than 90% correct at any given time. It is also important to point out that CIA no longer operates proprietaries within the borders of the United States (official position by decree of law) and has divested ownership (to retired intel types who '"'share favors'"' with CIA) -- but it is still free to operate proprietaries off-shore and on Indian reservations, where U.S. law does not apply. That being said, If anyone thinks the old fronts are not still active in CIA work, they are as much the fool as are the Congressmen who think so. It is, for the most part, business as usual -- with the added benefit of even more plausible deniability and an unheard of accountability to Congress and the President.

For the purposes of this work, any currently active American-based CIA '"'friendly'"' front (any entity actively involved in spying or other covert work within the U.S.) is likely also a Shadow friendly front, and is therefor likely an enemy of Americans and bears watching for evidence to that end. Shadow is this author's name for elements of the Intelligence community and vested-interest parties and groups exercising external and unauthorized influance and control of the intelligence community. It also includes those engaged in financial rape and other profiteering by illegal activities involving machinations of these same intel elements -- a kindred in conspiratorial activities Daniel Sheehan of the Christic Institute called the Secret Team.

For an even better understanding of how these fronts are Shadow related, Jim Hougan's Spooks and Jonathan Kwitney's The Crimes of Patriots goes into great detail about ties not only to the intelligence community, but to national and international elitist power players and news events or other intelligence community activities -- though they offer little analysis, merely reporting the facts of circumstance. These elite include the Council on Foreign Relations, the Trilateral Commission, and the Bilderbergers, a point of fact this author urges all readers to consider carefully, especially in light of the critical suspicions easily cast on the motivations of these groups -- and an undue influance on both the U.S. intelligence community, for which and argument can be made they essentially founded, and American and international politics.

With respect to the actual names on the list below, note the wide diversity and odd mix of naming conventions -- you can't (usually) tell these books by their cover. Appearance on the list is, in and of itself, not concrete proof that a given entitiy is actually a '"'bad guy'"', but is an indication that, at the least, its leadership has either in the past, or now does work within spheres of infulance of/by Agency. Some may even be counted as '"'good guys'"' for that relationship, as they may have or may be serving legitimate national security interests, and may even be doing so openly, as in the case of the contractors found here. Only those who hold CIA in disdain or who are obvious at-risk individuals or organizations as potential targets of illegal CIA activities should actually take care. As an interesting note, I have had over a dozen names submitted to me for inclusion on the list by EMPLOYEES of their OWN companies. I have NOT included any names on that basis alone, always asking for a reasonable basis or justification.
