NARR/REF A TERMINATED THE USE OF INTELLIGENCE CONTROL MARKINGS
"WARNING NOTICE-INTELLIGENCE SOURCES AND METHODS INVOLVED" (WNINTEL)
AND "NOT RELEASABLE TO CONTRACTORS/CONSULTANTS" (NOCONTRACT).  REF B

TERMINATED THE USE OF INTELLIGENCE CONTROL MARKINGS "NOT RELEASABLE TO
FOREIGN NATIONALS" (NOFORN) AND "AUTHORIZED FOR RELEASE TO" (REL AND REL
TO) AND INTRODUCED TWO NEW INTELLIGENCE CONTROL MARKINGS, "US ONLY" (UO)
AND "US AND (SPECIFY COUNTRY(-IES)) ONLY."  REF C IS NOTIFICATION OF
DCID 1/7 OF 16 APRIL 96 IMPLEMENTATION EXTENSION.

REF D INITIATED THIS ALMAR.//

RMKS/1.  PER REF A, THE INTELLIGENCE CONTROL 
MARKINGS "WNINTEL" AND

"NOCONTRACT" SHALL NO LONGER BE USED.  INTELLIGENCE THAT WAS MARKED
"NOCONTRACT" PRIOR TO 12 APRIL 1995 CONTINUES TO BE NOT RELEASABLE TO
CONTRACTORS UNTIL THE REQUIREMENTS OF PARAGRAPH 6.1 OF REF B ARE MET.

2.  PER REF B AND C, AFTER 01 AUG 1996 THE INTELLIGENCE CONTROL
MARKINGS "NOFORN," "REL," AND "REL TO" SHALL NO LONGER BE USED.
REMARKING EXISTING DOCUMENTS AND MATERIAL BEARING THE ABOVE MARKINGS
IS NOT REQUIRED.  INTELLIGENCE THAT WAS MARKED "NOFORN" PRIOR TO
01 AUG 1996 CONTINUES TO BE NOT RELEASABLE TO FOREIGN GOVERNMENTS,
FOREIGN NATIONALS, OR NON-U.S. CITIZENS.

3.  DIRECTOR, CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE (DCI) POLICY APPLIES TO THE TERMINATION
OF "NOFORN" AS AN INTELLIGENCE CONTROL MARKING, BUT NOT TO THE USE OF THE
"NOFORN" MARKING TO IDENTIFY NAVAL NUCLEAR PROPULSION INFORMATION (NNPI).
THE MARKING AND CONTROL REQUIREMENTS FOR NNPI ARE STATED IN NAVSEAINST
C5511.32B(NOTAL) AND REF E.

4.  THE "US ONLY" INTELLIGENCE CONTROL MARKING IS USED TO IDENTIFY THAT
LIMITED AMOUNT OF INTELLIGENCE WHICH AN ORIGINATOR HAS DETERMINED FALLS
UNDER THE CRITERIA OF SECTION C, REF E AND MAY NOT BE RELEASED IN ANY FORM
TO FOREIGN GOVERNMENTS, INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS, COALITION PARTNERS,
FOREIGN NATIONALS, OR IMMIGRANT ALIENS.  REF E IS BEING REVISED TO UPDATE 
INTELLIGENCE DISCLOSURE POLICY AND IDENTIFY THE LIMITED AMOUNT OF 
INTELLIGENCE THAT WILL WARRANT THE "US ONLY" MARKING.

5.  THE "US AND (SPECIFY COUNTRY(-IES)) ONLY" INTELLIGENCE CONTROL
MARKING IS USED WHEN A LIMITED EXCEPTION TO THE MARKING REQUIREMENTS
IN SECTION C, REF E MAY BE AUTHORIZED TO RELEASE INFORMATION BEYOND
U.S. RECIPIENTS.  THIS MARKING IS AUTHORIZED ONLY WHEN THE ORIGINATOR
HAS AN INTELLIGENCE SHARING ARRANGEMENT WITH A FOREIGN GOVERNMENT
APPROVED IN ACCORDANCE WITH DCI POLICIES THAT PERMIT THE RELEASE OF
THE SPECIFIC INTELLIGENCE TO THAT FOREIGN GOVERMENT, BUT TO NO OTHER
COUNTRY IN ANY FORM.

6.  THE DIRECTOR OF NAVAL INTELLIGENCE WILL DISSEMINATE NEW DETAILED
INSTRUCTIONS FOR APPLYING THE "US ONLY" AND "US AND (SPECIFY COUNTRY(-IES))
ONLY" MARKINGS.  APPROPRIATE POLICY AND PROCEDURES GOVERNING INTELLIGENCE
CONTROL MARKINGS WILL ALSO BE INCLUDED IN A FUTURE REVISION TO REF F.

7.  COMMANDS ARE REMINDED THAT ALL CLASSIFIED INFORMATION IS REGARDED
AS NOT RELEASABLE TO ANY FOREIGN NATION OR PERSON, UNTIL AN
AUTHORIZED FOREIGN DISCLOSURE OFFICIAL MAKES A POSITIVE DETERMINATION
THAT A FOREIGN RELEASE IS WARRANTED.

8.  FOR AUTOMATED INFORMATION SYSTEMS, A PHASED-IN ELIMINATION OF THE
"WNINTEL," "NOCONTRACT," "NOFORN," "REL," AND "REL TO" INTELLIGENCE
CONTROL MARKINGS SHALL BE ACCOMPLISHED AS SYSTEMS ARE UPGRADED OR
SOFTWARE IS MODIFIED, BUT NO LATER THAN 15 APRIL 1999.  NO PHASE-IN
PERIOD IS AUTHORIZED FOR ELECTRICALLY TRANSMITTED MESSAGE TRAFFIC.//
