Jihad

by Sparrowhawk

History. It is taught to us since birth. Our glorious past as a race and as a nation is presented before us whereever we look. To justify an argument, we need only reach back and cast forth an anecdote from the books. In any subject, such details are teh structural supports to the facts presented before us. There is no arguing that in factmankind is capable of genocide. One can not disagree with the idea that a strong leader is nessesary. To many, history is the infallible pillar that we may rest against in our times of need. Whereas science, philosophy and religion may be, and will forever more, be discounted few will put forth muchu effort to find fault in memories. But, as has been proven, nothing is exempt from corruption.

Stop for a moment, and consider the matter of perspective. By simply altering the word choice and details omitted, anyone could make a previously uncontected fact seem foolish. A grevious defeat or foolish mistake could be covered up, and an inhumane society seem to be heavan. Yet, while you may question some input, you do not ever ponder the whole of this subject. YOU ARE BEING DECIEVED! NOTHING IS DEFINATE, AND ANYTHING CAN BE USED AS A WEAPON AGIANST YOU! When you see on the television, a panorama of great American victories, do you stop to consider the methods by which these were achieved? We look at the standpoint for patriotism, September 11th, and balk as the slaying of a meager few thousand civilian deaths. Were you ever told of the CIA's training of Afgan guerillas during the Soviet invasion? NO! We cower in fear of the thought of the DOGS and COWARDS who may use nuclear weaponry against us! But, was it not us who did such acts in the past? We gloat in our victories over the Nazi empire, who slew countless innocents, but somehow, the ALLIED FIREBOMBING OF DRESDEN is never mentioned. Operation Gomorrah will never ring a bell to the populace.

No, history is just as subject to lies as anything else, but to point this out to the populace would mean the collapse of social structure. THE COLLAPSE OF A WORLD BASED ON LIES! But you will never be told that. To glean the truth from your history textbook would mean, in fact, an end to your slavery. And we can not have that, now can we? To end that would be to allow questioning of all that is taught. And asking a question is to simply be unpatriotic. You are not un-American, now are you? Or is it, in fact, that it is all for the best. Maybe this warping of the truth is what is needed to seperate us from the gulf of hell that is knowlege. Perhaps a lie is for the better? That is for you to ask yourself, decide your own answer, and be not swayed.

"Either you are with us or you are with the terrorists."
