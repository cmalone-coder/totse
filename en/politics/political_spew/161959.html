The New York Plane Crashes

by Mark Marsh

I am not a religious person but I do not discredit people who believe in a God of some kind, for now is the time that divine intervention is at it's most needed. So if prayer is your way of communicating and makes you feel like you are helping the cause, please do it, the human race needs as much help as possible and all is appreciated.

A few days ago for the first time since the War, the whole world stood still in dumbfounded shock as two planes, one after the other struck the World Trade Centre in New York City, while a third plane hit a side of the Pentagon. A totally heinous act against humanity with no moral justification at all, the world, whether it is officially declared or not is in a state of war, and we are the pawns, the cannon fodder, the easily expendable resource at the Government's disposal.

At the start of this crisis it was reported several planes were missing, yet nothing was reported about these planes until a mysterious fifth plane touched down safely not hijacked as first thought, now while I appreciate that in times like these information gets distorted much like a game of Chinese whispers but to miscount the number of planes unaccounted for is a silly and careless mistake.

There are more secrets obviously being held from then world's eye at this very time concerning these planes, these secrets will make it out into the public domain but only after suitable damage has been done and it will not affect any planned operations. Some reports suggested 7 planes instead of the vague several but only 5 planes out of the alleged 7 have been documented, the news now no longer even suggests more than 4 planes were reported missing. I'm sure somebody somewhere can fill in the blanks that I am struggling to fill in myself given time.

Practically every news organisation ran pictures and videos of Palestinians celebrating over the WTC and Pentagon incidents, yet not one channel or newspaper credited a source for these pictures, a deeply suspect that what we all saw was archive footage intended to be force fed into the eyes of Western citizens over and over again to fuel the already boiling anger to make it easier for the Government's to get public opinion on their side for when a state of war is declared, or simply to look like the good guys when thousands of more innocent people are killed in an Eastern country simply because the media encouraged people to be baying for blood.

The FBI has placed Osama Bin Laden on its Top 10 Most Wanted list in response to these attacks, one word: scapegoat. While on the surface this man does indeed look like the perpetrator of all this death I have my doubts. He announced to the world a few weeks before hand that America would be attacked yet as soon as it was he refused to take the blame for it, instead insisting through his associates that it was indeed an American group who orchestrated these deeds. Even Sadam Hussain has come forward and denied responsibility but like Bin Laden agrees that America deserved it and his opinion had it coming. It is easy to point the finger at Middle Eastern countries simply because Eastern people were aboard those planes, but I'm sure a person of Eastern origin is on nearly every plane taking off in this world. If this world were still anti-black I'm sure we'd all be blaming people with a darker coloured skin for this incident. The East is recognised as hugely different to the West making it an easy target as people cannot relate to every day life in those countries, the public are far more oppressed and controlled than any news channel cares to point out, they are right at the opposite end of the spectrum from American citizens and as such are seen as barbaric and forever hostile, making it easy for the Western world to not give a damn about them.

32,000 military personnel died in the Pearl Harbour raid, attacking a military target in war is the way it works, but dropping a nuclear device over a civilian city killing hundreds of thousands of innocent citizens was deemed as acceptable retaliation. That is what I fear will happen this time, the American people are even more out raged about this attack (and rightly so) but they are already screaming for Eastern blood via US bombs and with a trigger happy Bush at the controls I sadly think they will get their wish and more innocent people will die. Jumping to conclusions will not work, but it wins votes and provides a quick fix until they figure out the real truth.

If it emerges that it really was an American group who planned and carried this attack it will no doubt be kept a secret until somebody brakes under pressure and reveals it, America can carry out it's threat of retaliation on itself and Bush has no intention of admitting his beloved country is folding in on itself.

Weeks before this attack people were proclaiming how America was heading for a recession, now a chance has come along for the Government to easily get public opinion on their side to dip into numerous surplus cash deposits and spend freely on what they want. Uniting the country against a common enemy and keeps everyone happy by brushing the economic problems under the carpet.

Do not be surprised if this was seen as a few American lives = The Economic Save.

Many countries have a grudge against the USA but every major country and terrorist group has denied responsibility. Only time will tell whose theories are right and which Generals, Prime Ministers and Presidents are trigger-happy and who was scapegoated to make sure powerful people save face.

The world just keeps getting worse, yet the worse it gets the less we know.
