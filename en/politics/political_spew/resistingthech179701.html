Resisting the Che Guevara Craze

by AoxomoxoA

It's all too common. Pictures of Che Guevara are on t-shirts, stickers, watches and other crap. He's often pictured on such items as noble revolutionary, the symbol of the inner rebel inside us. Worst yet, many who wear the clothing don't even know who he is or what he stood for.

Che Guevara was no noble man. He was a ruthless, immoral gangster who butchered thousands of his fellow Cubans during Castro's bid for power. Guevara, Castro's henchman, was free to dispose of Castro's opponents and noncommunists by any means neccessary. And he did. Guevara mass executed innocent Cubans by firing sqaud on his imfamous bloodsoaked wall. Guevara enjoyed what he was doing, he was a cruel murderer with a thirst for the blood of his countrymen.

It's disgusting to think that people are hold up a man whose deeds compare to those of Hitler, Himmler and Stalin. His picture to a Cuban is what a KKK hood is to an African American.

Don't buy into the Che Guevara craze... he is a symbol of hatred, murder and bigotry.
