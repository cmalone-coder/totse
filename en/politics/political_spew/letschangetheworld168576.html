Let's Change the World!

by Steve Mathe

As for modern day slaves - who are they? You and I of course! Slaving away our guts to create a future for ourselves and for our kids, only to have 60% of our labor stolen away from us by coercion through harsh and unlawful taxation, ever crippling our efforts to get ahead. Even the sheriff of Nothingham would have Cringed as such daylight robbery!

Today, we may have the last opportunity to change the world's sytems to serve Humanity. Big Brother is already well entrenched, and World Government is just around the corner!

We can change the world by seizing all governmental power from the elite establishment, peacefully, legally, and lawfully - without violence...through the ballot box.

The goal is to shift governmental power from elite stablishment into the hands of the people where it belongs morally and lawfully! Without any hassle, or any disorder - the same as when Labor takes over from the Conservatives; the Replubicans kick the Democrats out of office, or when Labor ousts the Liberals in Australia.

The vehicle with which we accomplish this is through a Direct Democracy system in which every citizen has the facility and the power to propose legislation and policy, and enact them by 75% of the votes.

Now, there are a number of Direct Democracy groups striving for the same goals, but they are lost in academic debates which leads no where. You see, they are working with current standards of values, and with current legalist concepts.

Citizens' Rights League, sweeps the old system away and makes a new start.

In the CRL system, the people appoint the authorities over themselves, with power to hold them accountable for their actions, and also with the power to dismiss them from office.

In fact, the authorities are totally bound to administer the decisions of the electorate. Yes, even you have the power to start proceeding for dismissal of an authority if you have perceived something unlawful, or uncostitutional in the actions of the authority.
