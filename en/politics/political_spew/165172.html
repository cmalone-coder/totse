FBI / CIA: Worthless Pigs?

by A. Shemonia

Recently, the FBI released a statement to all police agencies and the American public, warning that they have information that there may be further terrorist attacks within the next several days in retaliation for the US bombings of Afghanistan. They stated there is no information as to where the attacks may be, what type of attacks will be carried out or who will be carrying out the attacks.

A full seven days before the FBI released this heart-stopping information, the al-Queda terrorist organization had stated publicly that if American forces struck Afghanistan, the American people would be subject to further terrorist activity in retaliation.

The FBI has over 4000 federal agents working with their anti-terrorism task force, which is designed to stop terrorists from carrying out their deadly plans.

My question is: If the FBI and CIA together with all their intelligence operations cannot thwart the WTC and Pentagon disasters, and the FBI’s anti-terrorism task force of over 4000 federal agents cannot do anything but pass on the information that was publicly broadcast by the terrorists themselves, then what good are they? What are they being paid to do?

In light of the recent Anthrax ‘outbreak’ in Florida and New York which has now been officially labeled a ‘criminal investigation’ the FBI is there taking the reports of the dead, sick and dying. Where were they in preventing the crime?

It seems to me that the 4000 tax-paid FBI anti-terrorism ‘experts’ could effectively be replaced by a messenger service and a few competent investigators. The FBI as well as local police agencies across America have proven they have no effect on curbing crime before it happens, as usual they were there after the crimes occurred to clean up the bodies and take pictures of the carnage, then relay messages from the terrorists.

Give us back our guns and civil liberties, let us protect ourselves and our families and keep our hard earned money as it is being given to these worthless parasites who are more interested in harassing freedom and constitution lovers than combating any type of crime or terrorism.
