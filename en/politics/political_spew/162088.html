Future

by Lyricyst

A choice you make, is a choice for you to make. Apathy is a choice we make when it dosn't serve our luxurious lifestyle to care. Obediance to the law is a choice we never make but something we are born with. Disobediance to the law is a choice we make at some point in our life. Is disobediance to the law wrong, maybe its the right choice to disobey the law? What is right and wrong? Right is that which best serves our selfish desire. Wrong is something that dosnt serve our selfish desire. Even under these circumstances (which are very true) the law isnt right. Even under proper right and wrong the law dosnt serve us justly. Shouldnt it?? Everyone should be able to live happily and in doing so, not hurt anyone else. Hurt has a perception of its own. What is hurt? Suffering, mental, physical or emotional pain. Somepeople are easily hurt, others are not. Some people are hurt by things that dont bother someone else. How can one law serve all? Impossible.

So, not only is the law not fair in the sense of what it should be (right, fair, just) but even the possiblity of a single set of laws is impossible to make fair. But then, the world isnt fair right, life's not fair. What a fucked up place we live in. The people that do best in life are those that have the most negative traits. Greed makes people rich.

Laziness makes people happy. Lust gives people hope. The people veiwed as the most succesfull all carry the traits that all people since the beginning of time have seen as negative traits. Cant you see the end to all this? Society kills itself. All that will be left is lazy, greedy people who wont have anything better to do than destroy eachother and everyone else. We try to prevent suffering for as long as possible but in doing so we make the suffering much, much worse. In only a small time, perhaps two decades give or take a few years, apathy wont even be possible anymore for 90% of people. Under the capitalist system the rich will get richer and richer and the poor will get more and more poor, as there will be more and more people on the planet that people will begin to die of starvation everyday, here in our free country. Seem inconceivable,... wake up. There will simply be too many people and not enough planet. The people most likely to survive are the rich, greedy scum that led our society into this in the first place. Luckily they cant possibly kill off everyone, you can only kill the working class, the people that follow blindly like sheep and participate daily in being part of this society. What about the people that refuse to live by the rules, what about the people who will fight and take action against this evil greed to put things right and make the world fair, the people who are willing to give up everything they were born into or worked for and will hide to tear at the heels of the rich. The people that if and when they achieve victory will be able to set up a peaceable and fair society. Modern society has made you and all of us too weak to possibly comprehend such a battle, but it will happen. The free people will always be there to fight those few who want to much for themselves. This greed that comes from a false image that some people deserve more than others. We are all born equal, what in life makes us less so. Only our willingness to sacrafice fairness in the world lets people become rich. This is why a man who plays a game for a living makes more money than someone who breaks their back to help their family and friends. When it comes down to it you have three choices.

1. Live for your own selfish reasons, sacrafice others to get what you want. Become rich, greedy and hate yourself for it.

2. Stay apathetic, remain with the perception that violence and force wont solve anything and therefore never make any progress and die.

3. Fight for what's right, help abolish unfair law and prejudice in the world. Live always with the hope and vision of equality.

No matter what I say, the choice is yours.
