The Manifesto Of A New World

by Vajlea

Again and again we find ourselves flooding our minds with garb of modern day media and falsehoods of American and world politics. Every day, we become more apathetic and more greedy than the day before. And, for those not pampered in the American Utopia, the will to fight and die for what they believe is gone – because they’re dying by the hands of others already.

In this endless cycle of increase in the rich-poor gap, we have lost our will for kindness as a whole. We have lost our compassion, some even our dignity, and we are achieving nothing through it. The common people, whether of America, Japan, Afghanistan, Panama, or anywhere else, the public gets dumber and dumber. Such harsh words, it’s true, but none other can describe those people who need instructions on how to make a pop tart, or need to know that smoking’s bad for you. Scientists conclude that then Universe is heading towards disorder – and that all the energy to keep up the necessary processes is running out.

Perhaps disorder, on a more human platform, is in order.

I understand, probably better than most, that this doesn’t apply to everyone – I know many intellectuals and many very kind people. Not everyone is greedy, not everyone is cruel. As a matter of fact, most people aren’t inherently either. But people are, as a whole, and under most circumstances, utter conformists. They also have a gradual tendency towards apathy. I have done both of these in my life, and may even slip towards them again, this I admit. But call me a hypocrite not. There is a fine line between hypocrisy and wisdom form experience, and that is genuine concern. I have, or at least I’ve fooled myself into having, this very genuine concern for the future of this world.

I am Eveonix. Eveonix is my ideology. There are three main objectives in mind now – though I would never close my mind to any more.

My Ultimate Goal is to help create a New World in which every person is educated and completely free from any boundaries – but still make mistakes and face the consequences. I believe that everything that you do effects you, and all evil you do will come back to you no matter if by the hand of another or fate, thus humans do not need to be shielded by rules and regulations called forth by would-be superior facades.

Firstly and foremost, I, (and by ‘I’ I mean myself and those whom I could not do it without), will dissemble the puzzle of government. This is absolutely necessary to me – it is our false and heartless governments, especially the US Democracy, that should be abolished for their crimes. Though I am an Anarchists, I will compromise – whatever best suites the people after the overthrow will be put in place and maintained.

Dissembling government entails many things. Currency will be eliminated. I envision a world where food and other things will be created well enough as to not create competition between a civil race. Will we run out of food that way? Not if we don’t waste it. Will functions of an industrial manner begin to cease? Perhaps. But what may be for the better is always determined at the moment of crucial juncture. This is not that time. I have already made one crucial decision, and that is to change. But I will not be the controlling will behind the spirit of this new order – I will be merely a tool of a true and educated spirit of a new type of people. An Enlightened, I would think – at least hope for.

That leads me to my second goal – one which although is less important to me than the previous, will probably happen partly before and partly after the achievement of the first. Th Reform of Education. AS I sat in my high school classes, I thought of how utterly inefficient the whole system was. No motivation, no interest, and nothing about anything that wasn’t scientifically proven. But, I found it was hardly the schools to blame – their hands are tied by the overbearing ‘sensitivity to others’ that strangles the joy out of education. Now, I’m not bashing tolerance and respect for culture; it’s something that should be taught, in my opinion. What I disagree with is the exclusion of Darwinism because it interferes with Religious beliefs. Or, the abhorrent fear of every administrator to come under a firestorm because someone thinks that a little ethical training or religious rehearsal will forever scar and poison their child. Hopefully, through better education, anything that a parent may not approve of can be dealt with by the parent in a healthy, non-punishing way. The ideal of education for me is ethical, religious, conscious, and scientific training and exploration through an individualistic yet collective form of teaching. More effort will be put into education than anything else as a long term goal. ‘Knowledge is power’ isn’t just a cute print for a child’s math folder; it’s truth, and that power can be utilized for a greater good.

The third goal is the elimination of Nationhood. Probably ready to be the most controversial of the goals, this would entail everyone renouncing their nationhood and becoming a member of one unified race. This will depend on my second objective; but also on the common good that the public is capable of. Will this mean the abandonment of culture? Perhaps for some, for others, no. Nationhood and culture provide a sense of belonging. With that belonging comes the proverbial secure feeling that we all crave, and that we all need. Many have adapted a strong sense of pride. But it is important, I think to bring all of the beauty and creativity of the people together in order create something old and new – to form new beliefs from the ashes of the old – to adapt new traditions that will become old. The idea of Eveonix lies in rebirth and creation. We need not entirely abandon all of what we have known and held dear; we musty change for the spirit of a New World and people. Change is not only a good thing, whether eventually or immediately – it is a necessary thing. Without it, we would fall before we learn to sit up. Changing involves adaptation, and changes happen in nature, whether we wish it or not. The more control we have over this change, the more likely it will be for the better.

These are what I and my colleagues represent; this is our dream of a better future through radical and unprecedented means. It is abstract thinking in a world flattening out. And we will utilize that thought to every advantage of humanity that we can. We will not fail.
