American Fascism

by Eric John

4. Supremacy of the Military: support our troops signs everywhere, automobile discounts for military personell, 396 Billion dollar budget for the military (19.8 % of federal tax money), America is the one with weapons of mass destruction

5. Rampant Sexism: Some radical conservatives want to make clitorectomy MANDATORY, gay marriage laws

8. Religion and Government are Intertwined: atheism is intertwined with the public school system

9. Corporate Power is Protected: auto insurance companies are the reason why we have a drinking age, auto insurance is required for anyone to drive, Bush Administration exempts oil companies from clean water act

12. National Police Force: CIA, DEA, FBI, Border Patrol, BATF, FCC need i go on?
