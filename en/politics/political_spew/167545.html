Liberatarian Socialism Guidelines

by Anonymous

www.racun.tk

1. We want freedom. We want self-organized communities in which the land, produce, and machinery of production will be owned by the community, and the power of the political and class state abolished.

2. We want the immediate removal of occupying police forces from our communities. The racist brutality, murder, and repression of our communities must stop now.

3. We want cooperatively organized, non hierarchal forms of education for our communities. Taught by the people, for the people.

4. We want an immediate end to the robbery of our communities by the capitalist system.

5. We want an immediate end to worker exploitation. We want revolutionary unionizing. Workers should control the means of production to insure equal distribution within the community.

6. We want housing, fit for shelter of all human beings. Not a single human being should go homeless.

7. Not a single human being should go hungry. Food is a right, not a privilege.

8. We demand an end to corporate exploitation and degradation of our planet. We want a safe and clean ecological environment for all living things to grow in.

9. We want direct democracy, the right of every individual to participate in the organization of their own community and their own lives. Representation is nothing but the denial of representation.

10. We want self-defense. We cannot rely on the system to protect us from it's own brutality, racism, murder and repression of our communities. We must rely on our communities to defend ourselves by any means necessary at any given time.
