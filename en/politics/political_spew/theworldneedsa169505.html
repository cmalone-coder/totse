The World Needs a Hero

by Sparrowhawk

Why do we fall for these lies? Why do we let ourselves be TORTURED? Since I was aware of my surroundings.. No! Since the eldest among you was born.... SINCE THE BEGINING OF TIME! Humans have let themselves be subjected to the worst of horrors. All because our society has stripped form us what we hold most sacred. The only thing seperating us from animals. Our minds. Tyranny always has, and always will be dependant upon the common, working class sacrificing his free will to the aristocracy. It has depended upon people like you and me being ignorant. It has depended upon us being nothing.

What is tyranny? Does it only exist in fantasy and history? Isn't it only present in other parts of the world? Hasn't it been abolished. NO! Wake up and look around you! Do you not see YOUR LIFE SLIPPING AWAY? Do you not realize your are being pacified? Your only sustenance is bread, water and LIES! Yes. I think you have seen it. I know you have understood it for what it is. You know what I speak of. Yet no one has stood up against it. No American has taken it upon himself to lift the veil. No human has undone the chains of the capitalistic aristocracy. Why not? If it is not becase of being BLIND to the problem, what is it? IT IS BECASUE WE DO NOT SEE THE RESULT! ALL OF US ARE AFRAID OF WHAT OUR FELLOW WILL THINK OF US. ALL OF US ARE AFRAID NO ONE WILL PROVIDE US SUPPORT!Yet, we all need someone to take that very risk. We need a hero. Someone who will not ask "What will others think of me?". We all owuld rally behind them. You know it.

If you are not this hero, hope is not to be lost. Keep fighting! Fight for your neightbor! Fight to keep what you have! But, more importantly, FIGHT FOR WHAT YOU DO NOT HAVE!

-=Sparrowhawk=-
