Standards To Live By (Before You Become Brainwashed And Forget)

by AngryFemme

I will never have any sort of "chip" implanted in me for Security or Health purposes. I will not succumb to the delusion that this chip is necessary for me to obtain public health services, participate in community banking, or have the false sense of security as a citizen. In the event of a totally cash-free society, I will rely on my tenacity and dogged determination to get by. I take great comfort in the fact that there are others like me who will provide, as a collective bunch, the means necessary to reinvent our free will society. I will take no part whatsoever in any remaining Social Security benefits - especially if it means being implanted with a "chip".

For every hour of media consumed each day (i.e.: television, radio, newspapers, magazines or interstate billboard advertisements), I will redirect my efforts in equal or greater amounts to seeking alternative means of information such as the Internet, word of mouth, or my own findings and observations. I will make it my primary objective to filter out as much propaganda, mind control and misinformation as possible. It is my sole responsibility to communicate my ideals, opinions and beliefs on interested, open-minded parties.

I will not vote again. Ever. I will discourage my peers from doing so. The only condition to wavering from this creed would be in the event of the People actually taking over our government. I don't see this happening in my lifetime without much strife and peril to go right alongside it. Should it actually appear to be happening, I will carefully scrutinize, doubt and take great pains to find out what's really going on behind the scenes. I will reflect on the fact that We Are Seeing/Hearing/Reading What They Want Us To See/Hear/Read. I will always be of the assumption that "something is up".

Taking the time to re-learn American and World History will enlighten me further on how things truly came to be for our civilization. I will forget all of the lies, coverups and misinformation I learned in grades K-12 regarding history. Independent reading will be the new frontier for my future learning experiences. It is completely up to me to stay informed.

I will focus more energies into studying different cultures and religions. This will further promote my acceptance for peoples of different race and culture. I will express my absence of religion as a personal plight and will never try to force the will of my beliefs on another human being. I will successfully and deliberately dodge any attempts at being converted to any sort of organized religion.

Should our civilization be visited by, taken over by, contacted by or stormed by beings from outer space, I will not rave like a blithering lunatic, nor will I run down the streets screaming "I told you so!" to all of the naysayers. I will not be afraid. I will not be misguided or led by my government or any other outside coalition into becoming an automated enemy. And I will.....not.....willingly.....become......a......pet. I will discourage my peers from doing the same.

Constantly reminding myself that "I am not what I own" will further distance me from the infection of consumerism and the gluttony of material possessions. I will make it my goal to simplify my overcrowded life using simple creativities such as reading, writing, art and music. I will not judge my peers for their individual choices regarding their material possessions, or lack thereof. I will help those less fortunate than me only if I deem them worthy and appreciative of my individual time and efforts. I will not have pity on those who choose to be puppets and lose themselves in the system.

I will become more enthusiastic about educating myself on Creationalism and Philosophy. I will shed all remaining traces of guilt, remorse, and self-depravity left in me from a Baptist/Catholic/Pentacostal upbringing. I will seek new answers regarding the beginning and make it a point to become more focused on the possibility of an afterlife. (where else would our souls go?) I will be careful to examine a multitude of facts and testaments before stating it as my personal "gospel".

While there is still an ounce of fight left in me, I will oppose censorship or anything even mildly resembling censorship. I will use my computer, my literacy and my ability to type 12,000 keystrokes per hour as tools to spread the "word" to interested parties. I will be as dilligent as possible at differentiating my opinion from so-called fact. I will not be intimidated, belittled or strongarmed by oppressive people or individuals. I will not leave myself open to mind control or any other deviant means of manipulation.

Regarding common civic laws, public displays of conformity and inevitably, taxes - I will always abide whenever necessary to the current government's policies and procedures, thereby rendering myself a poor candidate for the even uglier world of: Poverty, Psychiatric Institutions or The American Penal System. Also known as: Skid Row, The Nuthouse and Prison. I will, in those three circumstances only, conform when necessary. But I won't like it.

Lastly and perhaps most importantly, I will never, ever lose my sense of humor or the ability to make fun of myself or other people. I will willingly and eagerly depart from this existence when the aforementioned does happen. I will not allow myself to go out by any self-inflicted means or through careless activities. When my time here expires, I will leave something meaningful behind as proof of my existance.
