Announcement of Major Importance to all Unity Party Supporters

by L. Nelson McAlexander

Announcement of Major Importance to all Unity Party Supporters

I'm pleased to announce that the initial concepts for the Unity Party have been refined to the point that it is more inclusive than ever before with objectives that will create a win - win proposal for America, and in particular for everyone who supports National and State Unity Party Political Committees. To everyone who participated in improving the basic concepts I originally proposed through suggestions and objective criticisms - may I offer my heartfelt thanks because I think that, together, we have created a viable solution that will empower us to bring back America from the brink of destruction.

The single most important objection was the concept regarding attempting to merge current political parties into a "coalition" political party. It was the general consensus of opinion that the various current third party alternatives would not want to relinquish their agendas or platforms for the purpose of merging into a coalition party.

The solution to this problem was to create the Unity Party as a registered Political Committee, instead of the Unity Party becoming a Political Party. Accordingly, the temporary Board of Directors has elected to become an officially Chartered National and State Political Committee that will be registered with the Florida Division of Elections within the next 10 days.

One of the benefits of being an officially authorized Political Committee is that we can contribute as much as we want to any political candidate. (Unity Party Members in each state need to check with their states' election laws for filing requirements). With our refined concept we will not attempt (nor will we need to) a coalition of current third party alternatives. However we firmly believe this new concept for the Unity Party will appeal to all third party alternatives because they will want to be eligible for our financial support.

Our primary objective remains the same - to promote and financially support Minor Party Candidates, Independent Candidates, Non Political Party Candidates and Ballot Initiatives or Referendums.

Below is the first draft of the proposed Mission Statement and Charter for the Unity Party. All present members should reply with comments or suggestions as soon as possible. Approval of the UP Mission Statement and Charter will need to be completed ASAP in order to complete the UP web site. The Unity Party web site is nearly 90% complete, and it will be online as soon as the Mission Statement and Charter is approved, by those members who participate in the approval process, at the following URL address. http://www.unityparty.net

Please keep in mind that the following is a first draft and is being sent simultaneously to the current voluntary Board of Directors and all supporters who have subscribed to the Unity Party eGroup. Please reply with your comments, suggestions, and or criticisms as soon as possible.

THE UNITY PARTY POLITICAL COMMITTEE MISSION STATEMENT AND CHARTER

THE UNITY PARTY POLITICAL COMMITTEE, hereafter referred to as the Unity Party or UP, IS A COALITION OF CONCERNED CITIZENS WHO MAKE FINANCIAL CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE UNITY PARTY FOR THE PURPOSE OF PROMOTING AND SUPPORTING ANY MINOR PARTY POLITICAL CANDIDATE, ANY INDEPENDENT CANDIDATE, AND ANY NON POLITICAL AFFILIATION CANDIDATE ELECTED BY SELECTION COMMITTEE MEMBERS OF A CHARTERED UNITY PARTY POLITICAL COMMITTEE DURING PRIMARY ELECTIONS SANCTIONED BY THE NATIONAL UNITY PARTY AND EACH OFFICIALLY CHARTERED STATE UNITY PARTY. ANY BALLOT INITIATIVE OR REFERENDUM MUST FIRST BE APPROVED BY ANY STATE CHARTERED UNITY PARTY BOARD OF DIRECTORS, hereafter referred to as UPBD. TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR UNITY PARTY PROMOTION AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT A POLITICAL CANDIDATE MUST ENDORSE "THIS MISSION OBJECTIVE" OF THE UNITY PARTY AND AGREE TO PARTICIPATE IN THE PRIMARY ELECTION PROCESS OF THE UNITY PARTY IN THE STATE WHERE THERE IS A FULLY FUNCTIONING SELECTION COMMITTEE OF NO LESS THAN 100 MEMBERS.

THE UNITY PARTY DOES NOT ENDORSE OR PROMOTE POLITICAL ISSUES OR AGENDAS UNLESS THEY ARE IN THE FORM OF A BALLOT INITIATIVE OR REFERENDUM APPROVED BY UNITY PARTY SELECTION COMMITTEE MEMBERS.

THE UNITY PARTY DEVELOPS, PROMOTES, AND SUPPORTS BALLOT INITIATIVES OR REFERENDUMS FOR THE PURPOSE OF INSURING THAT THE GOVERNMENTS OF THE UNITED STATES AND EACH INDIVIDUAL STATE REMAIN UNDER THE CONTROL OF THE CITIZENS OF THESE UNITED STATES.

REGULAR UNITY PARTY MEMBERS Any American citizen is eligible to join the Unity Party through National and/or State UP eGroups that are hosted on the Internet at http://www.egroups.com. The Florida Unity Party URL address for regular members is http://www.eGroups.com/group/unitypartyFL.

SELECTION COMMITTEE UNITY PARTY MEMBERS Selection Committee (SC) Members are members who have pledged to support the Unity Party mission through a yearly contribution of no less than $90 - but no more than $500 for any two year period. Members who make a contribution will be automatically subscribed to the appropriate national or state Internet eGroup. Members who recruit a minimum of 10 verifiable UP Members will be automatically subscribed as Selection Committee Members. The Florida Unity Party Selection Committee URL address is http://www.eGroups.com/group/unitypartyFLSC

UNITY PARTY BOARD OF DIRECTORS The Unity Party Board of Directors will be elected by UP Selection Committee Members. Each UPBD will be comprised of no fewer than 12 Members and no more than 16 Members. Initially, UPBDs will be volunteers. In order to form a UPBD there must be a minimum of two UP Selection Committee Members who are willing to apply for Political Committee status in their respective states. As soon as there is a minimum of 100 UP Selection Committee Members in any state, the SC Members must hold an election to vote for UPBD Members. Thereafter, in order to hold a UPBD meeting there must be a quorum of BD Members present on the Internet in the UPBD eGroup Chat Room, in person, or through telephone conferences. Minutes of any UPBD Meetings must be posted as a message to the appropriate eGroup within seven days, and the UPBD Members present must be listed in the meeting minutes. The Florida Unity Party Board of Directors URL address is http://www.egroups.com/unitypartyFLBD

THE UNITY PARTY PRIMARY ELECTION CANDIDATE SELECTION PROCESS Each National and State UPBD must hold a UP candidate Primary Election approximately 30 days prior to the last day for qualifications as a candidate according to the election laws of each respective state. Only Selection Committee Members are eligible to vote in UP Primary Elections and each election will be conducted on the Internet at the respective National or State eGroup web site using the polling function from the eGroup menu. Primary Elections will be conducted over any 72-hour period, and that 72-hour period must be announced everyday, through posting a message to the UP eGroup, beginning 30 days prior to the Primary election. Selection Committee Members will vote on as many candidates as apply for any national or statewide public office. Only the candidate who receives a majority of the votes for any one elective office will be eligible for financial support from each respective Unity Party Political Committee. 80% of all funds contributed by UPSC Members will be used for the purpose of promoting and supporting the candidates selected through the UP Primary Election process and 10% of all funds collected will be reserved for the purpose of promoting and supporting Ballot Initiatives or Referendums. Each UPBD will be responsible for dispensing in-kind and direct contributions in support of the candidates selected through the UP Primary Election process. ALL FUNDS COLLECTED AND DISPENSED BY EACH RESPECTIVE UP POLITICAL COMMITTEE MUST BE ACCOUNTED FOR USING THE DATA BASE FUNCTION OF EACH RESPECTIVE Unity Party eGroup.

That's the basics, folks. Below are the nuts and bolts of how it works.

Each UP member can "pledge" to contribute from $90.00 per year (that's just $0.25 per day) up to $500.00 per every two years. 80% of all contributions from members will be used to promote and support Minor Party, Independent, Non Political Affiliation candidates, and 10% of all contributions will be used to promote and support Ballot Initiatives or Referendums.

Our goal is to get just 3% of the population to financially support the UP. If we achieve that goal we will have 10,000,000 members who, even if they contribute just the minimum, will be contributing $810,000,000 per year for statewide candidates. It is expected that eventually all UPBD Members will be compensated for their work.

Members do not have to make a contribution to join. But those who do become Selection Committee Members (SC) can participate in polling questions right on the Internet, and they can vote for the candidates they want to receive campaign funds. But no one is excluded because a Regular Member can also become a SC by recruiting at least 10 SC members per year. SC Members who contribute to the National Unity Party will be subscribed to the eGroup at The URL address http://www.egroups.com/group/unityparty. When the state abbreviation is added to the end of the above URL address it becomes a State UP eGroup. i.e. FL will be http://www.egroups.com/group/unitypartyFL.

Those who send in a contribution will be automatically subscribed to a national or state UP eGroup with the SC designation on the end of the eGroup URL address.

i.e. http://www.egroups.com/group/unitypartySC For the UP National Selection Committee. http://www.egroup.com/group/unitypartyFLSC. For the Florida UP Selection Committee.

Those who don't make a contribution will still be able to keep up with UP news, chat with other UP members, exchange email, and discuss issues and agendas right on the Internet and each UP eGroup, Those who make a contribution to become SC Members will be able to chat, maintain accounting records, upload and download files, maintain a calendar of events, participate in polls, and vote in UP primary elections for the purpose of electing candidates who will then receive financial support from the National UP (for presidential candidates) and the State UPs for statewide elections.

UP primary elections will occur before the State's qualification deadline. So the UP hopes to be able to pay the qualification fees for those candidates who the UPSC members elect. Only one candidate for each elective office will be selected in the UP primaries, and they will then go forward to compete in the General Elections against the Republicrat candidates. Those candidates who aren't elected by the UPSCs will still have the option of participating in the General Elections. But they will be doing so at their own expense.

If we achieve our goal of 3% we will easily be able to fully support each candidate so their full time job can be to work full time to convince the public to vote for them in the General Elections, and they will have ample funds for lots of advertising. If we achieve 3% all Minor Party, Independent, and Non Political Affiliation Candidates will be knocking down the UP door to achieve our financial support, and UP members can get to know each candidate right over the Internet.

Each National and State UP will have a Board of Directors (BD) eGroup who will be elected by SC Members only. i.e. http://www.egroups.com/group/unitypartyBD

For the UP National Board of Directors http://www.egroups.com/group/unitypartyFLBD For the Florida Board of Directors In the beginning, however, the Board of Directors will be volunteers.

Each BD will be responsible for developing Ballot Initiatives or Referendums and SC Members who support the Initiatives or Referendums will be able to send in their support petitions right over the Internet. This way if enough SC members send in petitions for a Ballot Initiative or Referendum we will be able to get it on the General Election Ballots very quickly. The first one we're going to work on is a Ballot Referendum for a "Citizens Grand Jury" for the purpose of investigating, impeaching, and jailing judges and public officials who abuse their positions of authority and/or violate their oaths of office. Citizen's Grand Jury members will be selected by lottery from a pool of the entire state's registered voters.

The National and Florida State UPs will soon be officially registered as Political Committees with the State of Florida. Although UP members may fully discuss any issues, NO UP IS ALLOWED TO ENDORSE OR SUPPORT ANY POLITICAL ISSUE. This will prevent arguing over a party platform (because there isn't one) and it will prevent subversives from undermining the mission and objectives of the Unity Party. Besides, voters do not vote on a political party's platform. They vote on the candidates who best represent the issues that are important to them.

Well, there it is folks. I hope you become as excited as the Board of Directors is over this initiative. There's no limit to the good things we can accomplish if we all put in a little effort to contact prospective members about The Unity Party. Think about it. There really is no reason not to join. Do it for a better future. Do it for your families. Do it for our children and our children's children. Do it for America. Like I said in the beginning - this is a win/win proposal for all Americans.

At a per person cost of just $0.25 per day we can return control of the government to the people, If we aren't willing to pay for the government we want, we will get the government we deserve.

THE ONLY THING IT TAKES FOR ANY GOOD PLAN TO WORK IS ENOUGH PEOPLE WHO BELIEVE THAT IT WILL.

L. Nelson "Mac" McAlexander

Founder, The Unity Party
