The Structure of Iraq's Amn Al Khass (Special Security Organization)

Offices of the SSO

Office of the Director General. The office is located in the Hai Al Tashriya district of Baghdad.

Director General: Qusay Saddam Hussein Manager of the Director General's Office: Suleiman Hajim Al Nasiri Secretary to the Director General: Moyed Sami Ahmad Al Douri Secretary to the Manager: Abbas Ayash Al Nasiri

Security Office of the SSO. Located in Hai Al Tashriya district next to the Director General's Office. This office also includes the ID department which produces all Ids for the SSO, the SSO prison and the security department.

Director of the Security Office: Ahmed Wahab Nasser Al Douri.

The SSO Prison is in Hai Al Tashriya. The officer in charge of the SSO prison is Hassan Khalid Al Tikriti. The deputy head of the prison is Abbas Mohammed Hamed Al Dulaimi.

The security department is in Hai Al Tashriya. Located across the street from the Director General's Office. Officer in charge of the security department is Haji Zuheir Hameed Al Tikriti. The deputy head is Muklif Sabah Ali Al Dulaimi

Protection Office. Located at the Agricultural Circle near the Council of Ministers building. This office provides bodyguards for senior SSO personnel. Director: Habib Nahi Semiyan Al Hadithi.

Office of Presidential Facilities. This office provides protection and security to the Presidential Offices, Council of Ministers, National Council, and the Regional and National Command of the Baath Party. Director: Nasser Sa'ad Waheeb Al Nasiri.

Security Detail. Located near Baghdad Clock. Director is Haji Yahya Zakaria Naji Al Tikriti. The deputy director and commander of the HQ Platoon is Haji Dhari Hamid Abbas Al Nuaimi. Director of the Baghdad Platoon is Hamad Abdel Karim Al Douri. Director of the Quick Reaction Platoon is Sa'ad Nofan Al Janabi. Director of the Traffic Platoon is Captain Saber Al Jishi.

Audit Office. Located in Hai Al Tashriya near Director General's Office. Director: Faris Hameed Al Tikriti.

Communications Office. Located next to Al Hayat Building. Director: Wasfi Jabbar Qasim Al Tikriti.

Public Opinion Office. This office is responsible for collecting and disseminating rumours. It is located at the Agricultural Circle. Director: Samir Abdel Aziz Al Ajili.

Administration Office. Located inside Al Hayat building. Director: Mahmoud Shaban Abbas Al Tikriti.

Security Institute: Provides training. It is located in the Hai Al Amil of the Officers' District. Director: Mahmoud Sohail Al Tikriti. Deputy Director: Ghassan Zakaria Najim Al Tikriti.

SSO Gun Club: Located near Al Masba. Contains the armoury of the SSO and Qusay's private swimming pool. Director: Haji Said Hamid Al Douri.
