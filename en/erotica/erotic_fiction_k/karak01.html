Chapter 1 - Finding the Lair

Finding the cave had not been easy and exploring the cave was
even worst. As I crawled through the narrow, twisting passage, I
probed with my extended sword to find any traps or deadly
bodyguards. In this tight space, there was no room to turn
around or to fight and this was the only passage left. My huge
muscular body would be a weapon against me in this tight
space. All of the other passages had turned into deadends.

I was fully extended and crawling as much as I could with one
hand holding my sword out and the other holding the burning
torch. Behind me, I towed several more unlit torches to light my
way as I explored the cave.

The cave widened and opened into a chamber. I smiled as I
was able to get off of my stomach and bloody knees and elbows
for the first time in over an hour. But my smile was short-lived as
I discovered that this chamber was a dead-end to my passage
also.

"Kalam", I exclaimed as I discovered another dead-end. I sat
down and ran my large dirty hand through my mane of dark
muddy hair.

As I rested, I thought about how many deadends I had come to
in the last twenty years. This cave had been my only remaining
potential access to Valdar.

Valdar, the protected kingdom of my enemy. Valdar, the land
where only women could enter. I had tried every place on the
surface to penetrate the barrier, but I could not walk through the
spell that stopped all men. I watched women enter and tried to
enter with them, but I would be stopped time and time again by
the invisible wall that protected this small kingdom.

Cak has strong medicine. Only Cak could take an entire
kingdom, granted a small kingdom compared to most of the
neighboring kingdoms, and put a protective spell around the
borders. Curse Cak and the lifetime that I, Karak of the
Cimmababas, had spent trying to find and kill Cak.

I laid back on the cold rock and rested my tired muscles. I was a
warrior and the best fighter. Ever since Cak killed my parents
and wasted the small village twenty years ago, I had been
searching for Cak, with the dedicated mission to kill Cak. Thus,
my travels had prepared me well for my task. My body was lean
and very muscular. The scars covering my body proved that I
was not afraid to fight and the fact that I was alive, proved that I
was very good at killing.

I had finally found Cak's lair. The kingdom of Valdar. No men
existed in there. Only women, who came and went as they
pleased, as they served Cak. While men could not enter, any
woman could. I had paid women to enter and report on what
they saw. When they returned, if they returned, they served
Cak. Veanak had tried to kill me in my sleep and she had been
my favorite woman before entering Valdar. She tried to act
normal, but my experience with other women made me cautious.
She told him about the beautiful land, about seeing Cak and
making love to him. She said that she only did it so that she
could find out what she needed to learn for my task. After she
told me about the castle layout, and that she discovered nothing
that could help me enter; we made love. After making love, I
pretended to sleep. I was ready when she slashed at me with
her knife and was able to deflect the blow. She was a good
fighter and would not surrender. As she lay dying, she told me
that I would never enter Valdar and that Cak did not want to
leave.

It was a strong spell that protected Valdar and turned my women
against me. When I discovered that I could not enter it from the
ground, I tried to enter it from underground and had explored
every cave and stream on all of the borders for the last two
years. This cave was the last cave and it had turned into a
dead-end also.

I exited the cave slowly. I had used my last torch and had to find

cool water wash away the dried blood and grime.

"Karak" said the nearby voice.

I jumped and turned to face the direction of the sound. Three

I looked at her and grunted "You entered Valdar to kill Cak.

realized how much good he had done for his subjects. How he

"No thanks." I said, knowing that I could not trust them enough to

"Your choice. We will be in town. All you have to do, is ask.

I watched them walk up the hill. The dark skinned one looked
