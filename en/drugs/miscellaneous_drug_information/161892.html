What is Ketamine?

Ketamine is dissociative drug that is used in on cats, dogs, (and in some cases small children) in surgery to knock them out. A tranquilizer. It comes in small bottles as a liquid of 10ml each, mostly. It is a schedule 3 (?) drug in the United States. It became illegal to possess Ketamine without a Vet license in 1999. Obtain it through your nearest dealer for 50-60 dollars a vial.

Any other names for it?

K, Special K, Vitamin K, kitty

What are the effects?

-Low dosages: loss of balance, loss of perception, distortion in vision, feeling of "weightlessness", difficulty walking, speaking... senses are dulled similar to the effects of alcohol.

-High Dosage: Unable to express thoughts, total mind/body dissociation, out of body experiences, extreme disorientation... also known as a "K" Hole.

The effects of ketamine wear off approx. 45-60 minutes after the drug is taken. It can be either snorted in powder form, or injected in liquid form.

What exactly is a "K-Hole"?

A K-hole is when you are truely having an out-of-body experience in which the real world around you has completely disappeared, and is often said to be like dying----however, you are NOT really going to die. It's only your mind which is reacting in way that manifests visions of tunnels, lights, demons, angels, places of the unknown, etc. This is what makes K so interesting, because in many ways, it's more powerful than most psychedelic drugs such as LSD and psylocybin.

How do you cook K?

Take the vial and simply dump it out on a plate, leave sitting under a desk lamp overnight and the next day you will have white, flaky crystals. Scrape off, cut up, and enjoy.

Speedy version: If you desire your K RIGHT away, put the shit in an oven to speed up the process, however, make sure you take it out before it starts to burn and turn a brownish color. I like the first method much better because the crystals always turn out nice n' white.
