I write in cluster form so everything may not come in logical order, seems like most of us here won't really mind.

First off, distinguish the difference in tests... Instant tests are EASY, GS/MS (Gas Chromatography / Mass Spectrophotometry) or lab are harder, and to be honest I have no advice/experience to offer for hair and blood tests.

As others have mentioned, drugs tests are testing for drug metabolites not the actual presence of drugs. They test for a defined level of metabolites present in your piss, your goal is to make your sample below this level.

I've smoked heavily on a Friday night and passed an instant drug screen on Monday afternoon. Dilution is key to instant drug screens. One gallon of water will work wonders for instant tests. If you have more than two days to prepare for an instant test you can easily pass using dilution.

Dilution is your enemy in lab or GS/MS tests since most labs test for dilution by checking creatine levels. Mosts places considered too low of creatine a negative test and you will be in trouble or not hired. My personal outlook if I knew I were going to probably fail a lab test would be too dilute heavily and deal with the fact creatine was below the normal level and have no drugs show up. It's your choice to make though.

Remember learn as much as you can about your drug test. Will you be watched? What type of test is it? Pay attention to standard procedures if you will going often. You can find weaknesses in their systems. Ask people who have gone their before you will be suprised what you can know. Even knowing where the person watching you will be standing can be very benificial. BE CREATIVE.

If you are not going to be watched, plan on definately substituting some sort of urine it is fail-safe and easy, why risk it when you don't have to? If it is instant test you could use hot colored watered and get away with it, I've seen over 10+ instant tests and none have any device to check for real urine. If it is a lab style test sythetic urine from a WWW site or a clean friends will work. I am not sure if a lab can detect 'stale' urine so I would recommend using something only a few days old (most centers only collect the samples and ship them to the testers, and they may sit at the test center a day or two before actually tested). You need to keep temperature in the 91F-98F range, as most places will test this Usually the sample cup has a aquarium type temperature thing on it(some centers use an infrared reader or actual thermometer, the method of reading the temperature does not really effect you just make sure you are within range). Using skiing handwarmers can be helpful in keeping the temperature of a sample high.
