Ambien is a sleeping pill for insomniacs that is very effective. The main ingredient in it is zolpidem tartrate, which does not show up on drug tests, so for those who are concerned don't worry about that. Anyway, I had heard that 20 or 30 mg of this stuff will trip you up pretty good.

The week before I did it, somebody I know had taken 50mg at school, and was so fucked up he pulled his cigarettes out in class and started countng them, cuz he didn't know he was at school. My dad had some ambien, it's a prescription drug, but it isn't too hard to get, some places sell it without prescriptions.

The feeling of an ambien trip is very different. One night at about 10 pm in my room I took 3 ambien (30 milligrams), I don't suggest blasting the shit cuz it burns your face like shit. Now, for the trip to work, you have to force yourself to stay awake. It's easy. The stuff kicks in fast too, within 12 minutes or so you feel some weird stuff.

I layed on my bed and I was listening to some music and suddenly I felt vibrations from the music in my body, my arms twitched and I felt like gravity had been reversed and the world was totally weird. Later in the trip, maybe 20 minutes afterwards I looked at my bed and coulda sworn a lion head had appeared to be coming out of the bed. Now, it's not like an acid trip where you lterally see the form of something real, on an ambien trip it's like a dream, you see it, but you don't believe it, it's hard to explain.

Then i noticed a pair of crutches leaning against my wall, and um...well I don't have any crutches so that was weird to me. About an hour after taking the pills I had no idea what the fuck was going on. I was convinced that a party was going on in my room, and that some idiot had just driven up in a Lexus. I know that makes no sense, but at the time I could have sworn it was real.

I then began to throw things over my shoulder because I thought it would have some magical efect. The next day a few of my cds were broken and scratched. I never felt sick the entire time, the feeling was great and I loved the idea of total insanity. Imagine your mind being asleep and dreaming, but you can see, feel, and you can move. In my journal where I was logging everything I saw, I wrote "1:40AM, going to sleep". The strange thing is, apparently, I DIDNT goto sleep after all. Because my dad said at 3 AM he could hear me bumping around in my room.

I loved this experience and I suggest it over any of the other legal drug trips, it doesn't beat acid but it was the closest thing in my opinion. The total thing probably lasted about 3 hours. I dont suggest driving or doing anything important on this shit, cuz it totally throws you into insanity. I comprehended things that I could never understand sober. For those of you who might be scared of this, take 10 mg your first time, it's still pretty fuckin weird(It takes over 400 mg of ambien to overdose, so don't be worried about that either). Even 20mg is very strange. The recomended dose is 5 mg for prescribed users. It doesn't make you feel sick and it is damn fun. Oh yeah, and smokin a lil bud with it helps intensify the effect. And uh, don't do it at school, cuz it puts you totaly out of it.
