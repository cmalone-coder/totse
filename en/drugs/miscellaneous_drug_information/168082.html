I am going to correct the errors in this article written by "John Doe". There are MANY errors in his text.

Ok lets get started....I only commented on the things i am nearly 100% sure of.

"Diphenhydramine Hydrochloride, will make you really sick (not high) and is a tricyclic antihistimine, also causes irregular heartbeats, coma (if you conisder that getting high) and of course the ulitimate high DEATH!!! Dramamine, used for motion sickness, fucks with your balance and middle ear, will keep you from puking, therefore if you OD on it your bodys normal defense to poison (vomiting) will not work, therefore you die.(also treats ringing of the ears)"

>Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) and Dimenhydrinate (Dramamine) are basically the same drugs. There is not one mentionable difference between them. You can get "high" off of this drug, but it is neither a "fun" or "safe" high (by most's standards). The high is very similar to the anticholinergic plants Datura and Belladonna. The high will produce very real and scary open-eye hallucinations. It will also give you the WORST cottonmouth you've ever had. It is not a fun high in any way (by most people's standards). However, these chemicals do have toxicity levels, and one can die from them (though i know of an individual who have taken up to 72 Benadryl at once). I do not recommend using these recreationally.

"Ultram, is not a narcotic and will not produce any euporia, thats why its not a controlled substance. It's just super advil."

>He is not correct here. Though Tramadol is not an opiate, per se, it does antagonize opiate receptors in the brain and the "buzz" is of similar potency to Codeine (weak, though better than nothing).

"Tylenol, liver failure and dialysis."

>Tylenol (in high doses above 4 grams a day, or mixed with alcohol) can cause liver damage and/or liver failure. Tylenol is safe to use in recommended doses, however.

"DXM, will fuck you really bad, apparently a intense high does major long term organ system damage..."

>Come on, man. Do some fucking research. DXM has never been suggested to cause "organ system damage". If anyone would like to get REAL information about this drug, please read the DXM FAQ by William White. I have used DXM quite extensively with no ill effects. Note: I am talking about pure DXM, NOT Coricidin, which can kill in high doses due to the extra antihistamine.

"Nitrous Oxide, (dental gas) Will produce a quick high that can make you pass out, but you'll most likely keep breathing and wake a few seconds later(when your blood O2 levels return to normal)with a smashing fucking headache."

>I have never known anyone to get a headache, nor have I, even after serious NO2 binges. It doesn't make you pass out either. The only risk that NO2 might have is that if you inhale too much, you might be depriving yourself of oxygen. Take breaks between balloons.

"Ritalin, In my opinion ADHD is bullshit (and you kids just need some freakin dicipline) This shit numbs your mind and WILL cause cardiac problems."

>Ritalin is a stimulant which will likely make a normal person "hyper" or "speedy" in relatively high doses. I've used Ritalin, and my mind is not numb. I'm sure excessive use can cause heart problems though.

"Benzos, very relaxing, kinda hard to OD on (well you might forget to breathe."

>Benzodiazepines are not very fun, in my opinion, and are very addicting. Withdrawal from heavy benzodiazepine use can result in death. These are very dangerous to mix with alcohol. Use with caution.

"MDMA or "X" used as a psychological therapy in the 60's and early 70's, Real Stuff "not speedballs" in low doses produced euphoria, very small "therapeutic Index" easy to OD on. Dangerous stuff to OD on, the person who is over dosed will experience cardiac abnormalities, very high spike in body temp (105 f+),liver failure, kidney failure, it just gets worse from there. (if someone is OD on X keep 'em cool (not frozen) till the medics get there)there is no drug to reverse this."

>MDMA is not very easy to "overdose" on. The LD50 in rats is nearly 100mg/kg. This would give MDMA a relatively large gap between recreation and overdose. The average recreational dose is 100mg. I'm not saying MDMA is safe, as one's body can react badly with any drug. But this guy really doesn't know what he's talking about. "DMT, fast intense high, haven't heard of it being around in a long while."

>He is partially right. DMT is a 15 minute long trip that is extremely intense, much more intense than that of LSD. However, DMT can be extracted relatively easy from phalaris grass. It's still around, just somewhat rare.

"Shrooms, indians have been doing them for centuries w/o ill effects (unless you eat the wrong ones)"

>No drug is 100% safe. One's body can be different from others, in terms of a drug.

"Heroin, bad scene, almost impossible to kick the habit. OD treated w/ narcan."

>Not quite impossible to kick the habit. Benzodiazepines are a lot harder to quit than opiates, and the withdrawal is much worse.

However, an 80mg grinded up can be an overdose for someone with no tolerance. If you insist on using Oxycontins, start out with 20mg.
