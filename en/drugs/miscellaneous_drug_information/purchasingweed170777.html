Purchasing Weed on the Street

When buying pot from a house, Always have one person with you. No more no less. A person alone is an easy target for muggings and the like. If you are in a car, the driver should remain in the car, and park with the lightsoff, while the passanger goes to the door. Never go somewhere you, or your friend havn't been to before.

How are you supoused to know where to go if neither of you have been there? Walk slowly. Walking fast attracts attention. If you walk fast past someone looking to rip you off, you'll look like a very potential target. And police will also notice someone walking fast, and stop you.

When approaching the house, if on foot, one person should stand at the front of the house, the other goes to the door. This way eyes can be kept on the street for potential cop action, and keeps the dealers less paranoied by keeping the numberof people visiting their down.

Always be polite to your dealer. You don't want to get him angry, because as a dealer, he most likely has ways of harming you at his finger tips. Also you want him to give you the most for your money.

Never ask a dealer where he gets his supplies from. Only undercover police will ask that question, save that bullet for them. On the way out, walk. I think we already know why.

If you are in a car, always park at least 3 houses away from your spot. If there seems to be any kind og police activity, do not get into your car. This could result in your car being seized.

I personally do not like to purchase from people walking the street. This is bad in a number of ways because:

Cops don't have houses to deal from.

People on foot have a much better chance of runing with your money.

It's too out in the open.

But if you're gonna purchase from people on the street, at least know what they're selling. Nothing is worse then trying to buy some weed, and getting a small baggie of baby powder. If in a car, drive past once, and gain eye contact with the dealer. give a hand signal of how many bags you want so they can have it ready before approaching your car. (it's a bit hard if you need more than 5 bags)

Try not to exit the car, rather have them come to you. If you have to, the passanger (and you'll have a passanger, because you never go to the bud spot alone.) should get out and make the deal.

Also beware of thieves who know what your business was there. Always avoid people who stare at you on the way in. It's always best to keep a knife handy. If they ask (and i say 'they' because usually these operations don't do weel with only 1 person) how big the bags are, DO NOT show them your bag. it would be easy for them to snatch, swing and run. These kids know why you were there, and playing dumb with them would only make them angry.

Just walk away. WALK away, and keep your hand firmly on your weapon. If you didn't bring one and are suddenly forced with a situation where you think you might be attacked, a branch or anything really, will make a great weapon to grab, but only if it is very apparent you're about to be attacked.

If you know a cop is going to pull your vehicle over upon leaving the area of the bud house he knows why you were there and excuses like "I took a wrong turn back there" will never work. Instead, the best thing I've ever told and officer was "Yes officer I did go to purchase potm however they sent me away because they were too paranoied of cops watching the place, and told me to come back in an hour." If the cop doesn't believe you, then be perpared to be yanked out of the car. Very often the passanger is not searched, unless police have noticed the passenger leaving the car to goto the spot.

A great technique for hiding pot is to have the passanger hold it, and the driver several ties try to look as if he's fumbeling with something under his seat. This, to the cop, will look like the driver was trying to stash the bag under his seat and the police will spend their whole time searching under the seat, ignoring the passanger.

If you know you're going to be caught, just own up. But not right away. I've gotten out of trouble a few times by telling the police where the bags were and dumping them on the ground, then stepping on the littered buds. Most police patrolling outisde bud houses just want to take the binesnuess away from the dealer and not spend their time hauling kids off to jail for buying $10 worth of pot.
