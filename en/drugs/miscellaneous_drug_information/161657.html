DATURA: I heard by a serious source, that when the seeds are white(before they dry to be brownish), they can make one irreversibly crazy. Also, it is delicate since the active substance that makes you tap in the spiritual/hallucinogenic realm varies much in concentration from one plant/fruit to the other, so you should always go for a smaller dose and check how it does(10 seeds), then if it was within reach of your physical/mental strength, renew the experience with more seeds *from the same flower*, in what you estimate will not make a drastic difference so you're always safe. These experiences can instruct you in some aspects of the higher self, if you define some inner yearning for personal development *before the intake*, say, you recite some strong mystical or philisophical motto or legend that is synchronizing in your mind at that time, or simply focus your mind for a moment on positive good, then the experience may reveal you some aspects of the given tale, that is what happened to me once (with mandrake apples). In any case the only guided datura experience I heard of, is of the Carlos Castaneda's Don Juan-guided initiation reports, and these guys were specialists in the field to begin with. So beware very much, be ready for (hopefully minor) discomfort such as dry mouth, altered vision and speech etc. I found that the intake of just a few seeds, acts also, with homeopathic-like effects, it helps to get distance from stressful situation and relativize emotional harm, but in any use of this plant, consider it as a one-time experience and be careful of what it can do to you if you abuse. There are milder alternatives, such as nutmeg, (which is said to have a clugging effect on your kidneys that is eventual long term harm to your body, repeat it not often) and the wonderfully mild morning glory that gives you an experience comparable to its name, yet I consider that using these substances for fun instead of a research in human mind consciousness fields and self-development, may be abusive and degradating. You don't want to be "wasted", but to find temporary tools for wider mind fields and eventually substance-free self-development. This is the condition that I transmitted this information.

About the datura and the MG(ipomea) I have no data on long-term toxicity/harm it can bear, I just remember having abused of Ipomea taking it several times within a few weeks (3-5 packs each) , and slightly after that was in bed for a week or so with a very stong and weird flue, without even being able to go buy food or eat anyting a few days. Maybe it was a detoxification process. Beware. But MG as a one time experience seems ok toxically. Especially it can open you to your "good genie" and induce nice magical happenings, without hard effects as daturas has. One or two packs to begin with, then a few weeks later adjust *with the same series of packs, the same batch*, (BTW some are treated with mercury for conservation and/or to prevent its psychedelic use, those make sick), double the dose and observe progressively what it does.

You can be alone in nature and develop a mystical/magical/meditative trip that may give you insight for ages as a milestone in the divining/theurgic process for the collective good. But then you develop these insights in hard life, without drugs. Unless you want to stay in the illusion and go ahead getting wasted, bad kid. This is for good kids and mature people even though some bad kids came down the road and built later on their inner space based on some reminiscences of constuctive youth trips. In any case by taking drugs like a "consumer" and laughing like a dog or just enjoy the first degree of the experience, you don't honour necessarily the spirit of the plant used for it, and it can be a drag on the long term. Sinsemillia crisis happens to people who have not enough respect and consideration for herb, and use it as a routine. If you're unable to use drug as if it were a one-time gift, get away from it because you may not be honouring it properly and it can make holes in your mind instead of broadening its scope.
