Hello, I first started taking DXM "Dextromorphine Hydrobromide" in the form of Coricidin Cough and Cold during my senior year at High School.

I had met some new friend's, and started hanging around with them more and more often. One day they told me about Coricidin Cough and Cold, since I told them I had missed acid when it started to dry up in my area.

So one nite at a friend's house named Brian, we actually got some Robotusin ES nite time cold medicine. "Robotripin" as it's called, this was my first time and I drank a entire bottle "about 250mg" of the fowlest shit I ever had tasted. But no matter what I kept my mind off of throwing up so that I could experience the trip that was told to me by my friend. We were laying on the bed at this point after playing several games of Magic listening to some music "music intensifys the trip". About 45 minute's after I took it, I got out of the bed and stood up. Emmedietly I fealt it, I was on another plain field. I said out loude holy crap dude what the hell is this? I could not believe what a good feeling and high I was having just from a small bottle of cold medicine. It fealt like I was on coke/weed/acid/beer every drug I had taken before all rolled into one.

His brother was in the room, on the top bunk bed. So I decided to look up there and see how he was doing, as soon as I looked up at the top bunk bed I was looking into a cave that stretched for mile's in. I yelled up to the cave, are you in there chris where are you? "His brother did not take any DXM" All the rest of that nite I was scared I was going to OD or somthing because I never had experience with DXM before and what a intense experience it was, for some strange reason time stand's still while on DXM minutes seem like hours.

Well that was my first trip on DXM with only 250mg and I was on maybe the 2nd plateu. I have been on the 4th plateu several time's, let me tell you of the last and the last time iv done DXM.

One nite by myself on the weekend I whent to a local grocery store and pocketed 2 pack's of Coricidin C&C. I whent home and took all 4 pack's "32 pill's 960mg of DXM I weigh 155 lb" all at once swallowing 15 pill's at a time. Be sure to have lot's of water on hand, you get awfully thirsty.

I started playing C&C Yuri's Revenge, and exactly 45min's as always it hit me. The feeling of high and a quenching feeling in my stomach, I didnt think the high was full effect yet so I kept at the game. All of a sudden I hear a voice say pick me coming from my computer screen. I scroll down a bit in the map and I heard it again, I look at the screen and see one of my unit's waving at me saying "no me i'm next to die" walking around without any command to do so. So then I think well it's here let's turn off the computer and go watch some t.v. I turn on the t.v. and layed on my bed, and all I could see was different colored ray's streaking out of the t.v. After a few minute's of that, I turned it off and put on my headphone's to listen to some music.

As soon as I did that, and started to relax I fealt myself leaving my body, or being transported out of my room, looking down i'm being pulled even further away leaving the earth the solar system. When i'm suddenly drifting in space aproaching a blue/green planet similar to earth.

I look at the planet a while studying it, when I look to my side and see another person or thing. A head with wings, and what it say's to me I can never get out of my mind to this day. It say's this planet is your's to mold and to make into your own with your own thought's. And so then it drift's away toward's other being's far in the darkness of space only barely seeing them.

All of a sudden i'm molding the planet to my liking, making hill's, mountain's, ocean's people and animals start coming alive. Then all of a sudden the thing with a head and arms come's back and say's you must leave now, but take this the meaning of life. A box start's coming toward's me from outer space untill it's a few feet away from my face. The Box's center lid's open and inside all that I can see is darkness. But all of a sudden cloud's start forming, and the darkness become's bright white, then black dark again and it keeps morphing in and out and then I wake up back in my room.

And what I get from the box "meaning of life" is that the box was a universe, where there is darkness there is light, where there is light there is darkness. One cannot be without the other, positive cannot be without negitive. The natural balance of the universe, cleansing then dirty, the universe come's to being with a explosion then implode's to anti matter to nothing repeating forever untill one day a being can leave this place escaping the nothing not by body but mind.
