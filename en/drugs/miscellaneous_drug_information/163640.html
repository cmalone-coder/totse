Dramamine: The Vomit, The Vomit...

by Noisemachine

So the first time I tried it was some night during the middle of the week about 6-7 months ago. I took 12 pills and began to feel extremely out of it but in the strangest way that I ever experienced. Keep in mind I have taken many drugs and been very intoxicated previously, but this was one of the weirdest, most purely horrific drug experiences ever. I went home and laid around with no real brain activity. I kept hallucinating the presence of people. I would feel like I was having a conversation but it was just babbling noise inside my head. Real nonsensical totally fucked up shit. The reality that I was totally wacked out on a household drug would hit me for like a millisecond then immediatly another illusion or hallucination would occur. My body felt like it was on the very edge of death. I felt the realization that I was going to die but was so fucked up that I didn't care, since I couldn't hold onto a thought for more than a few seconds. My head ached, oh and I felt the feeling that you get when you're just about to vomit...the whole time that I was on the shit. It sucked. I tried so hard just to fall asleep. I was SO incredibly tired but my entire body kept going into these full body muscle spasms. Each spasm gave me the feeling that I was flying a great distance from one side of my bed to the other. It was like I had these uncontrollable earthquakes going off inside my whole body every 10 seconds or so. It was horrible. Sure I saw my world melt and watched objects slip into the void and certainly I escaped from my normal consciousness but meaningless hallucinations are not worth the horrible feelings that I now associate with Dramamine.

Sadly I took more after I swore it off forever. A couple months later I was so depraved and desparate to get high, so I reluctantly bought a 12 pill tube and took them all.

Soon I was feeling like I was passing out or comatose but every five seconds I would jerk awake with a muscle spasm and grind my teeth trying to get a grip over my body and my mind. But no grip came. I was like a useless body for the drug to fuck with. I was watching a film about teenage drug abuse, murder, rape, etc..."Bully" was the film. Certain scenes scared the hell out of me just because this veil of darkness had overtaken my mind. I tried to sleep just like that last time but again, could not. I had a hallucination that my father was doing these strange stretching excercises in the middle of my room. I stared at the image for a while, my heartbeat was racing, I was dumbfounded. Then suddenly I realized that I was hallucinating. This is the worst way to get high (along with huffing). I recommend that one use heroin, cocaine, meth, crack, LSD, psilobin, peyote, or anything before they try this shit. It is not a good drug. I can see no way to have any good effect come from the abuse of this drug.
