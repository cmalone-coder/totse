We crushed the seeds (10 a piece..10 being the "maximum recommended dose" mind you!)into an ULTA-FINE powder, and let it steep in some hot water in a dark place for WELL over 25 minutes. We drank the stuff, it didn't taste so bad (nothing like Robitussin), but it was very obvious that our stomachs were in for a bad ride. We waited over an hour while watching Falling Down (you know, Michael Douglas at war with the world?). If any movie was going to help ignite our trip, that would have been it. Guess what? NOTHING HAPPENED. So, us being the real psychonauts that we are, decided to use the rest of the package which left us with 75 seeds each, 85 counting the previous 10. This time however, my stomach went on the fritz about an hour after drinking the shit. It was totally disgusting. I was puking like a motherfucker. Even after all that, nothing was happening. Bare in mind that my drug of choice is DXM. When you drink the mighty tussin, chances are that you throw up and start tripping ultra-hard. This was, as I have mentioned, not the case. In conclusion, if you are going to look for legal highs on the internet, JUST GET SALVIA. Don't waste your time with anything else. We have tried HBW seeds as well as Amanita Muscaria mushrooms to no avail. Please note, that we ate those in excessive amounts as well, and nothing happened. So yeah, don't fucking bother.
