I actually read about camellia sinesis on totse, i saw it though "hey that looks fun!", it is common place that i look for drugs that are legal, free, and natural so this seemed a great choice for me. I collected several tea bags that where pure green tea, to make sure i was actually getting green tea minus an additives i grabbed several different brands of tea. With my tea i went up to my room and opened the tea bag. Doing this is very complicated if you just try to rush through it, it spills and is very hard to conceal on a carpet short of just vacuming. I opened the bag by pulling the little string and cutting the bag down the middle and pouring the contents onto a paper plate, i suppose you could use any flat surface but i figure paper plate is a pot head's sterile operating surface :). I first tried rolling a joint of the tea, i rolled a perfect joint and started smoking, one thing i noticed with this is that when smoking it you get super aware to evrything that occurs around you similar to drinking something cold or something with caffine in it. I finished my joint and sat down on my bed, i realized however that my usual 9 o'clock cigarette craving was absent! I smoke full flavored newports and am serverely addicted, but this seemed to take away the craving i usually experience. I figured fuck it, if this curbs cigarette cravings maybe it will give me a smoker's high of some sort, i packed a bong of the stuff and smoked for about half an hour reloading the head several times with more tea. This didnt really do anything except make my throat sore, angry that it did nothing i took a sip of water and fell asleep.

CONCLUSION

Well in conclusion this isnt any kind of hallucanagin as promised in ealier aticles, but it is however very helpful in curbing cigarette cravings and i am happy to say with camellia sinesis i was able to quit smoking. This also smells very good when burned so it is great to smoke out of a pipe as a social thing almost like a cigar.
