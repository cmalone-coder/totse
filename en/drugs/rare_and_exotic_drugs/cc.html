FILE #1 - An Introduction to Chemical Children
By Radiation X
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What IS Chemical Children?? Bad question. But, you asked...

Unless you're extremely new to cyberspace, and the underground that exists
there, you probably have one or two files laying around on your HD titled
`Wierd Drugs' or `Legal Highs' ..... Most of these contain one or two
untested things like banana peels, and maybe something about sniffing glue.
Great. In case you hadn't figured it out from personal experience, these
files are a little less than complete.

Chemical Children originally started when I decided to write one, HUGE, meg
or so file on ALL legal highs .... About 6 to 7 pages into it, I realized it
was going to be pretty much impossible. So the idea for this zine came out.
Why not, I decided, put out a zine covering a few of our favorite legal highs
each issue?? This way, we can take our times with it, we wouldn't have to
create such a foreboding file, and since we're always learning of new stuff
to tweak out on, it'd be perfect.

The name Chemical Children, I decided, is a pretty fair representation of
what the cyberphreaks down at the SLF have in effect become to have such vast
knowledge of such things ... it hasn't been easy. It's been dangerous. But it
sure has been fun!!

So hopefully, by the time you've leeched every single issue of Chemical
Children, you will have a complete index of every single legal, usually over
the counter, unusual, obscure, harsh, dangerous, socially unacceptable drug
in existence.

Before I go, it should also be noted that I've decided to be a not-nice-guy
(refraining from harsh language here..) and write the first issue of this
alone when a lot of the credit for the stuff contained in here should go to
Terminal Junkie and the Government Cheese. Sorry guys. But I can't get ahold
of either of you. Hopefully, issue #2 will be coming out soon, and will be
co-authored by Terminal and I...

So, here's the first installment. Bye bye...

Radiation X

FILE #3 - Diet Rite, Etc.
by Radiation X
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

0k, this is a quick tutorial on diet pills. Anyone who has experimented with
methamphetamines (speed, crank, wire, etc.) will be farmilliar with the
affects of diet pills. Meth is a stimulant. A very, very, intense stimulant.
To get `wired' usually means you're not gonna be sleeping or eating for
roughly 24 hours, in exchange for talking about absoloutly nothing faster
than you ever though possible, biting your fingernails, and incessantly
tapping your feet up and down.

Sound like fun?? You bet.

Well guys, check out diet pills. Any will do. There's some major brand that
everyone knows that for some reason (heh heh) I can't remember right now.
Perhaps it's Contac. Anyway, I put Diet Rite at the header of this file
because it's the only one I can seem to remember all the sudden, I guess
because someone offered me a few recently.

You see, as I mentioned before, speed makes eating seem like an extremely
unpleasant task. So all these Diet Pills are, really, is a little bit of
SPEED. Hehehehe...

Anyway, find any over-the-counter medication in K-Mart or wherever that comes
in tablet format, listed as diet aides. These don't (at least no brand I
know of) contain methamphetamines, but a chemical that is very similliar,
with almost identical effects. Wheeee!!!!

Dosage probably depends on the brand. Different strengths come in different
packages. As a rule, check the `reccomended dosage' and then double it, at
least for your fist time.
