First off. The idea of kryptonite is not complicated and the ingredients all except for one are easy to get.

Kryptonite is a combination of crushed caffiene pills (no-doz), crushed up ritalin (methylphenidate), nutmeg, and crushed up poppy seeds. (baking soda and water are also combined.

Now i will explain those ingredients, the caffiene acts as a stimulant creates much energy, and ritalin acts also as a speedy stimulant. Nutmeg is a drug wich is a common alternative high in places like prison, and rehab. The crushed poppy seeds act as a minor relaxant but only contain a very very VERY small amount of narcotic qualities, when crushed, the juice from the poppy seeds is like a distant rendition of opium.

Kryptonite should not be smoked in a regular pipe, instead it should be smoked in tin foil, or a can.

Now here is how you make it.

INGREDIENTS

1. two and a half tablespoons of crushed up no-doz.

2. two tablespoons of crushed up ritalin.

3. one tablespoon of ground nutmeg.

4. half tablespoon of crushed up poppy seeds.

5. two teaspoons of baking soda.

6. one tablespoon of hot water (at least 150-200 degrees.)

now, mix this in a small plastic cup for about one minute. Let the mixture sit for 15 minutes.

then put on a cookiesheet on a pizza pan or any oven pan (not too thick though.) Bake the mixture for fifteen minutes on 450 degrees.

After it is baked you'll say "what the hell?"

because it will be sort of like a grey cookie and kind of crumbly. now get another tablespoon of water and mix with the already baked mixture. now get a spoon or fork and little by little, scoop little gobs of the goo into little square cut pieces of cookie sheets and wrap each of them up into little squares. You should have about ten of them. One of these is considered 'a hit' I guess.

Well...i mean i made the shit...

anyway... put the ten little squares in one ziplock bag and roll the bag up, now turn up your freezer to the coldest temp and let em' freeze for a half hour.

NOW you've gor kryptonite.

...AFTERMATH...

But after three or four hits you'll feel a rush and feel pretty dizzy you'll start shaking and laughing.

The effects are almost exactly like taking a hit of weed and a hit of crack but not as strong.

I'm not responsible for any injuries

use this responsibly and make sure you or anyone who uses it does no more than ten hits im not sure what would happen but i am sure i don't want to know.

When smoked the mixture has a tendancy to bubble and pop. This is normal.

ALSO if you have heart problems or a heart murmur experiment with this carefully.

Good luck, and enjoy this shit, Im sure it will do a boogie woogie on your brain!
