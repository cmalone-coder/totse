Only five seconds after inhaling the salvia, I began to notice a change in the world around me. First my body became very hot, and my skin became very itchy. The bed I had been instructed to sit on then began to spin clockwise at approximately 2000 km/per hour. Then in only the time of a millisecond I spun along with the bed and the floor, the room and my whole world… In all differing directions.

I suddenly appeared 100 km in the air above a red canyon. I was standing on a spinning golden disc that kept me from falling into oblivion. I was not prepared for the swift occurring experience and I became frightened. I attempted to ‘fight’ the high, and was suddenly teleported into a world of pain and agony. I felt the most horrible pain in the entire universe. I was dead. I was dying. I was sprawled out across the bed being stretched and torn into millions of pieces by millions of little needles. I could feel every muscle in my body being slashed apart. I became more terrified and tried to remove myself from the bed so as to control my high. My efforts were futile and I returned to the bed and tried to embrace the experience.

As I laid down on the blankets and rested my head on the pillows I felt myself being sucked through the mattress and into oblivion.

I became the pillow, and I melted into the sheets, then I became the pillow, the headboard and mattress and began melting into the floor below me. I returned to my natural human form only to transform into another alien form. I was changed into a puddle of ooze and I felt ever molecule of my body and every particle of my energy drip to the floor. This is where the ‘peak’ of my salvia high appeared to end. I stood up off the bed and and sat down with my friends who did not smoke any salvia. They laughed at me, and I told them that salvia was fucked. I felt a marijuana like high for the rest of the night, which may have been just because I smoked about 5 bowls before smoking one hit of Salvia Divonorum.

The experience was so much more than what I have written here. It was a very spiritual adventure that led me deep into the caverns of my mind. No human words could ever come close to explaining the river of feelings and emotions that rushed through my brain.
