An African Aphrodisiac which librates women and fortifies Men

Making love while stoned on Khij is an incredibly beautiful experience. All I can say it try it.

1) Put a few oranges in a paper bag and leave them in a dark closet for about 2 weeks. When a powdery blue-green mold covers the surface of the fruit you may begin the process

2) Sift 4 ounces of marijuana through a screen.

3) Place seeds and stems in a large pot and cover with isopropyl (rubbing) alcohol. (if you have seeds and stems from other lids and kilos or seeds left over from anywhere you can add them too).

4) Cover the pot with lid and heat on electric hot plate for 3 hours. (Caution: alcohol is inflammable. Do not use gas flame.)

5) Strain liquids and store in container labeled soultion A.

6) Repeat steps 4 and 5 with fresh alcohol.

7) repeat step 4 and 5 with water instead of alcohol. Store in container labeled solution B. (This time gas flame may be used with 1 hour boiling time.)

8) Repeat step 7

9) On an electric hot plate outside or in a well-ventilated room reduce volume of solution A by boiling.

10 On a gas flame reduce volume of solution B by boiling.

11) When both solutions are considerably reduced (but not too thick), combine the two solutions and boil them down further on hot plate. Lower temperature as mixture thickens.

12) When this combination begins to get syrupy allow to cool. When cooled it should be about the consistency of a thin syrup. If it is still very watery it may be boiled down further and cooled again.

13) Place sifted marijuana leaf into same pot with the extracted syrup. Knead it and roll it around in this until leaf is thoroughly and evenly coated with the stuff.

14) Keeping the grass in a moist, spongy condition, add about a teaspoon of mold (remeber the oranges ) per 4 oz of grass. Knead it thoroghly into the grass; use rubber gloves and when finished be sure to salvage the tars that cling to them.

15) Put the grass into a polyethylene bag. Close the bag so that it is snug to the grass, thus minimizing air in the bag. Store this in a dark place.

16) After a few weeks when the grass has developed a sharp ammonia like oder spread it in a baking tin. Preheat oven to 300 for 15 minutes. Turn off heat. Place tin in oven for 15 minutes. Repeat process until dry.
