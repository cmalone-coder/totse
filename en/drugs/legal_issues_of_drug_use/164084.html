I say, um what was that for, I thought maybe for warrants and I'm told to go to the bag searcher. Now, this is fucked. I was watching these 2 guards search ALL my bags and talking french back and forth, i asked what they were doing, one of the guys asked, do you use cocaine? I say, um not lately, and he says well there's cocaine residue all over your license; i was kinda screwed there, eh? I'm like, well that was a while back. They swab all my stuff (shampoo, make-up, clothes, etc) with these cloths that i guess detect drug residue. They got residue off all my stuff! How does cocaine get on your shampoo bottle? I also had my methadone, so now they're dealing with a heroin AND cocaine user!

I guess this was big excitment for those airport security guards, because I swear at least 10 walked by and tried eavesdropping and speaking french back and forth all fast(you know when someone's talking about you and its another language, doesn't that piss you off?) then they 20-questioned me over why i was there, who's my boyfriend, where does he work/live, totally embarassing me in front of the airport and people waiting for their bags to be checked!!

This whole time(they kept me for 2 1/2 hours,no joke!) they never searched me! Good thing! All that BS over nothing but some residue. This was the first international airport that did that to me, because ive been cutting coke w/my license for years, so check your license or ID card before you get off the plane in Ottawa!
