I begin my experiments with Morning Glory Seeds due to the lack of contacts for other materials and came across a description of the possible effects from Morning Glory Seeds.

I reveiwed plenty of resources in order to understand what type of method I would need in order to reach the level I needed.

Upon buying 1500 seeds from a online source that sold them untreated I went to a local department store and obtained a Coffee Grinder so I could Grind the seeds to a powder as suggested in the reports that I have read.

The first experiment was just your basic grinding of seeds into powder. Mixing the powder with water and drinking water and sludge all together. The amount of seeds used was 300 and it was extremely nasty unless you can deal with the nutty flavour of the seeds.

In all though the experience was as expected and other then the weighted feeling and something stuck in my throat after vomiting, I found that Morning Glory Seeds have a real Potential so I worked on another method to rid myself of the sludge.

I found that if you take about 400 seeds, grind them in a Coffee Grinder until the seeds are powder(about 2 minutes), Pour the seed powder into a glass jar 12 ounces of ice cold water, set the jar in the fridge for about 3 hours, stirring the mixture about every 30 minutes. You will have what I consider a very good cold water infusion. To use this mixture just pour the water off into a drinking glass and leave the sludge in the bottom of the glass.

Effects:

You WILL more than likely vomit within 20 - 30 minutes, and I would strongly suggest that you do because you really do not want to trip with this stuff floating around in your system.

You will experience lethergy( that funking weighted feeling like you have been working for about 12 hours and your tired as hell). You may or may not be disoriented at times, but it is a very wonderful experience, and the afterglow is well worth it.

I have tried serval methods and the Cold water extraction works very well, but some of you out there may not be able to handle what you seek. This is not for the weak and I do not suggest this substance for those seeking a thrill, but for those of us that seek enlightenment through nature.

Until then....
