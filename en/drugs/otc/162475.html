My Experiences with Coricidin(TM) or DXM

by Scranduple

Coricidin(TM) cough/cold pills contain DXM as most of you know. So one day i decided to take a short break from school and try to trip on them as ive seen a lot of my friends do. I read up a little bit on DXM and Coricidin pills to find out what i was getting myself into. the sites i went to advised me to take only 8 of the special non acetomenophin tablets. So i did just that. I had noone to be my trip-sitter but i figured i could handle it... I took them with water at about 12 pm. Before 1, i started to feel dizzy and somewhat "out of it". when i tried to walk i began to feel sick. i layed down, hoping to end this uncomfortable feeling. i started to get lost in my thoughts and the swirly things you see when you close your eyes really tight. Then a sudden urge to throw up came over me. I ran to the bathroom and puked. Only after i "tossed my cookies.." is when i started to feel the effects of the pills. Lolling my head from side to side to the beat of non-existing music was very relaxing. i stood up and walked around a bit. I felt like a chicken, my walking was very abnormal.. i collapsed on the couch and just talked to myself, trying to make sense out of my words. The phone rang. It was my sister, and she sounded like an alien... She wanted to know why i wasnt in school and if she should pick up my homework? Apparently i couldnt talk right, the words kept melting into each other. she asked me several times what i was saying. I thought all of this was funny. So she said goodbye, and when i hung up the phone i felt like i was being hung up, too. I started to sympathize with the telephones loneliness. After i was done babbling, i put in a movie. This was fun, because i felt like i was a character in the film, watching what everyone was doing and playing my roles.. When the movie was done or when i thought it was done, i went on the computer. Talking to people was fun, they were very nice and funny of course. before i knew it, 2 pm came rolling along. i got offline and just wandered around my house and attempting to sing songs. I kept bumping into things. I started to come down off my trip when it was 3 15 pm. it probably didnt last as long as i had hoped because i threw up. Anyways. my parents came home and we had a nice long talk about school. At this point i was dizzy but i had control of what i was doing. After all the effects were gone, i felt refreshed and happy. I decided that id do this again sometime, but not too soon... i didnt want this to become a habit. So there you are. Coricidin. Not everyone is alike and im probably lucky i didnt tweak out or die or something. I cant gaurantee this same result for anyone... so be careful...
