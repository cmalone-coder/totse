Well this is my story of mi go at nutmeg

Me and my mates we planning a trip to Auckland (yes, in New Zealand) about 2 and a half hours away, we were planning to go in a van and i would be sitting on the couch in the back. Well anyways, me and one of my mates went to the supermarket and bout a small container of whole nutmegs, it was $4.50 NZD and contained 10 whole nutmegs, I chose whole nutmegs as I had done alot of research and it seemed whole fresh nutmegs worked bestt, these were in a plastic container with foil on the top.

Went back home at about 11 am and began gringing the nutmegs on the cheese grater (what a mission) after about 5 minutes on each nut i was done, i only did 3 to see what happened and then i planned to do more the next time. My friend ground his ones up and we found out we had no milk to mix it with to drink it, so we drove up to the gas station and got a couple of bottles of chocolate milk.

About 12pm and we get back to mi friends place after stopping to see people we knew, got our glases that we ground our nutmeg into and went outside onto the deck to drink it, I poured my chocolate milk into my glass on top of the nutmeg and filled it to the top and stirred it with a spoon. Instead, my friend decided to try and put the nutmeg into the chocolate milk bottle, dropping most of it onto the ground and he took one sip, said it tasted like shit and spat it out, tipped the rest out (now that I think of it, i shouldv drank his stuff) anway, I knocked mine back right after stirring it, I expected the worst taste in the world as i had read on several websites, but it actually tasted quite nice besides the fact that it felt as if i was swallowing saw dust.

About 2pm we left for Auckland in the van, about half way we stopped for a smoke and a stretch, not really feeling anything at this stage and thinking it might not have worked.

Around 5pm we got to Auckland and got lost and we parked up outside Mc Donalds and had the sliding door open while cranking the music waiting for the girls we were supposed to be picking up.

9pm and we are going down the motoway and i was lying down in the back behind the couch on all the blankets with one of the girls and all i could see out the window was the lights going past the window of the van and I tell ya it looked reaally cool, it was at this point i realised i was starting to feel the nutmeg.

about 11pm, after alot of screwing round we got to where we were going, parked up in a big paddock with another 1000 or so modified cars camping out for the drags the next day, we parked up and started drinking vodka and orange juice, by this time i was really feeling the nutmeg, went to bed early hours of the morning after alot of drunken stupidness, didnt get any sleep at all.

9am next day, sitting on grass hill watching the drag racing in the baking sun for around 6 hours i started feeling real weird, before hand i was jus feeling comfortably stoned sort of didnt giv a fuck attitude from the previous night up until now, about 3pm and the mixture of the nutmeg and sitting right under the sun for so long wasnt good, I would just be sitting there and kinda fall asleep thinking im in a completley differnt place like the back of the van or something, and Id open my eyes, look at the ground and thing "wooah there wast grass on the floor of the van b4" and itd fully trip me out for a few seconds and then id realise where i was, this happened several times before we left to go back to Tauranga, i slept in the back of the van the whole way, got back to my mates place around 11pm or something (i dont remember) and i went to my car, got my blankets out of my bag and went to sleep. Woke up the next morning feeling pretty normal but very sunburnt.

Overall it was a good experience, i found drinking plenty of water and keeping comfortable was best, I will definatly do it again but next time do 5 whole nutmegs and see how that goes.
