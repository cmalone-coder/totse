After coming across another article here on getting high on some other common medication, I decided to believe everything I hear and try it. It was explained to me that a certain dosage of diphenhydramine would indeed fuck you up. I don't remember exactly what it was, but I ended up taking 12 expired Benedryls I found at my house. After taking them all, I went outside and waited for my brother cause we were goin' to go walkin' to my friends house. He just wanted to smoke, I wanted to smoke and leave my house. About halfway there, we stopped in the woods and we smoked two blunts he had. He went on and went back home and I continued up to my friends house. When I got there, I was toned and all, jus as expected from the bud. Me and her sat there watching that movie "Scarface" when I started to notice a little somethin' different.

Besides from some angel dust, this had been the closest I had come to hallucinating. As I was watching Al Pacino rob some guy of his coke, I couldn't help but notice the walls were kinda wavy. They seemed to be slowly, but surely, moving like water does. This struck me as kinda odd, but I didn't let it phase me.

Well time went on and that was all that happened... then my mom came to pick me up. She had decided to take me and my brother to see that movie "The Mothman Prophecies". I was like "hell, whatever" and went with them. When we got there, I sat down like normal and started watching the movie. I got extremely tired and fell asleep many times throughout the movie. I knew these pill were making my this tired. It was like see two seconds of a scene, black out, see some more scenes, black out, and so on. Actually, most of what I was watching wasn't that movie at all. I found myself watching a totally different movie, "Final Destination". I didn't notice this until later. Not much else happened until my brother had to take a piss and I had to stand up and let him move through the aisle. This was almost impossible for me since it had seemed I had gained an extra 50 lbs to my body weight. It took all my strength just to stand up.

When the movie finally ended, I had to struggle just to walk. If you ever saw that movie with Cuba Gooding Jr where he had to walk with that diving suit on, that's what it was like for me. On the way home my mom and my brother were talkin' about the movie. I was adding in couple of my thoughts when I realized I was talkin' about a completely different movie. It was like I was having a conversation and I was hearing them say completely different things. I remember I was like "It was cool when they were at the airport and the plane exploded". My mom and my brother were both like "What? What are you talkin' about?" I continued with "Oh, that was in my dream." "What dream?" my mom asked. "When I fell asleep". This went on the rest of the way home and I just decided to stop before my mom caught on. At home I had to crawl up the stairs like a dog with three legs. My brother said "Yo man, what the hell are you on?" He figured I had takin' a bunch of pain killers again. I insited I was on nothing and went to sleep cause, no duh, I was tired. I have to say this, this experience was different. It was kinda fun, but kinda scary too cause I didn't really know what was goin' on. I you plan on gettin' high by ODing on diphenhydramine, be careful and don't plan on doing anything important.
