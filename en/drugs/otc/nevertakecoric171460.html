A great method for taking Robitussin Max is to drink 32 oz. lemon lime Gatorade down to the top of the label and then pour the Robo in the Gatorade, they compliment eachother nicely.

O.K. my story, I went to the store near my house and purchased one box of Coricidin (16 count) containing 30mg DXM and 2mg Chlorofil Meleate. Chlorofil Meleate is the reason that Coricidin is not a good source of DXM. Anyways, I took all 16 pills at around 10:30 P.M. and took a shower. My shower must have lasted 15 min., and when I got out to dry off I noticed that I was getting a little light headed and thought I was on my way to a fun trip, boy was I wrong. I went to my room and laid down on my bed and listened to music with my headphones. I started to notice the usual effects of DXM like music enhancement and closed eye visions, very cool. Unfortunately I was unable to enjoy these effects for very long because my stomach started to feel horrible my heart strarted to beat out of my chest and I was really scared. Lucky for me I had the overpowering urge to vomit, and I managed to get most of the toxins out of my system. I felt a million times better after I vomited.

I will say that I actually felt pretty good after I threw up and was able to enjoy about an hour of visions and trippy sensations, but the effort to get the effects far outweighed any positive experience. I relize that there are 3 or 4 varietys of Coricidin pills and only one or two contain Chlorofil Meleate, but these are the ones to avoid at all costs, that wicked drug is the reson my heart went crazy and it almost makes you want to stop breathing, not good. If you or any one you know takes this drug, make them vomit and STAY AWAKE!!!, I have read too many experience reports talking about parents who have lost sons and daughters who fall asleep and forget to breathe. Just remember to study up on any drug you plan to take and don't think you can handle something even if you have taken it before. Abstinance is the best answer.
