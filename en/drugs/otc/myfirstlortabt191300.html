It was Wednesday, we had a half day meaning we were released at 12:30. My best friend and I, we'll call him Rick, went walking around town. We ate at Mcdonolds and then walked back to his house. At about 1 we started wrapping newspapers because hes a paperboy. We decided that since I had to be home at 5, I would take 2 Lortabs before we started on the route.

His brother who we'll call Stan, assured me that it would wear off by then. So we started on the route and I downed the pills. About 20 minutes into the route I began to feel euphoric, and Rick said that was about the time my speech began to get a bit slurred. I felt as if I were almost completely at peace with everything, and things that would normally bug the hell out of me just seemed insignifigant. I enjoyed the rest of the walk, and by the time we were getting back to Rick's I was feeling a bit dizzy, and my speech was even more slurred. My thoughts multiplied and were buzzing all around my head but at the same time they were fuzzy and unclear.

We hung out at his house, watched TV, walked to Walgreens. Rick says that I was very observant and I noticed things I wouldn't usually pay any attention to. My sense of time was wildly distorted, it went from seeming like seconds were hours to vice versa. At 5 my mom called and I asked if I could stay longer, I could, and its a good thing too, because I was still tripping majorly. At around 630 we had dinner and I was a bit nervous because I had to come in direct contact with Rick's older sister and parents. For the most part it was fine, but Rick said that I was scared they would notice I was high, so I told him I was going to copy everthing he said so that I would sound normal. I went home and at about 7:15 is when i think it finally wore off. Overall, it was awesome.
