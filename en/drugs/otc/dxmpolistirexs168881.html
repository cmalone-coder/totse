First of all, you're going to need a syrup that contains Dextromethorphan [DXM] Polistirex. Most syrups contain DXM Hbr. YES THERE IS A DIFFERENCE! DXM Polistirex, is DXM Hbr coated in an edible plastic, to make it time release. The only syrup Ive ever come accross that contains DXM Polistirex is Delsym brand. Ive seen it in every pharmacy Ive been in, so it shouldn't be a problem to find.

Next, you will need the following things:

  • DXM Polistirex [already mentioned, but whatever...]
  • 2L pop bottle
  • Water
  • A razor blade, or exacto knife
  • A spoon

What you do:

Pour your syrup into the 2L bottle. [note: do not use a 2L bottle for doses under 900mg. Use a smaller bottle.] Then, add water. Fill the bottle up about 2 inches from the top, and give it a shake to get the syrup mixed in there.

Next, simply leave the bottle upright for 2+ hours. I usually mix up the syrup and water 3 or 4 hours before I plan on dosing. After 2+ hours is up, you'll notice a collection of powder on the bottom of the bottle. Thats what you want. Thats the DXM.

Once the bottle has sat for 2+ hours, take it into the bathroom, and set it in the sink. Now, use your razor to cut through the bottle near the top, to let some of the fluid drain out. Then, make the cut go further down, to let more drain out, and further down, until you're about 1 inch from the piles of powder. [note: you can syphon it out, but I find this to be an easier way of getting rid of the unwanted liquid]

Once theres only about 1 inch of liquid above the powder level, cut the bottom portion of the bottle off. Then, use a spoon to get as much of the liquid as you can out, without disturbing the powder.

Now you have two options. What I usually do [simply because its easier] is then pour about a mouthfull of coke [coca-cola!] into the bottom portion with the DXM. Then I swirl it around the get the DXM unstuck from the sides of the bottle. Then, I down it all in one gulp. It should look clumpy and gross, just drink it.

OR, you can try and get as much water off as you can, and then blow dry it, and let it sit for some hours so you can compact it into pills. I find this to be too time consuming, and frankly, too much of a pain in the ass. I can simply drink it all in one gulp much easier. But the choice is yours.

How it works:

Honestly, I don't know. I got the idea to try this as I noticed that DXM Polistirex containing syrups had a gritty texture. It made sense to assume that due to its plastic coating it wouldn't disolve in the syrup. So, I figured, if I mixed it with some water, thatd give me the extra dillution I need to syphon most of the liquid off of the DXM once it settled to the bottom. As well, I figured the DXM would settle better in thin water, rather then just sitting in the thick syrup. [plus, I didnt wanna even try to syphon the syrup off the DXM] As you can guess it all worked out. I have done this countless times, and it has NEVER failed me.

WARNING!

DXM Polistirex is different from DXM Hbr. First off, DXM Polistirex takes around 3 hours to be hitting me good in most cases. And about 4.5 hours to start peaking. Yes this is a long time, but its very easy to dose 3 hours before I plan on being high. Very easy. As well, DXM Polistirex stays in your system a lot longer. Ive felt the effects, for more then 24 hours at some points. Keep in mind these where high doses, and I wasn't peaking for 24+ hours. A lot of that was a small drunken feeling, but you get the idea.

I suggest you realize the difference between DXM Polistirex and DXM Hbr before you do it. As well, DXM Polistirex poses more of a risk, when it comes to damaging your body, as its suspended in your system for a longer time. But then again, using this simple methode you can cut down on all the damage you're doing to your liver by chugging syrup, which is a big plus. Not ALL as you're still drinking some, but you're not drinking nearly as much crap as you would if you where to drink straight syrup.

In any case my friends, the choice to use DXM Polistirex is all yours. Im only here to inform you of a way to make getting that DXM into your system, a lot less shitty.
