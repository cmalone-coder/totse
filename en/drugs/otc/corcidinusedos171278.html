It all started last November when I had a cold.

I'm at my friends house, 12:AM. My nose is stuffy as hell and I can't stop coughing. So I go into my friends bathroom cabnet and look for some remedy. (Up to this point, the only drug I have used is alchohol) I find a bottle of Robutussin Cough. Being very irritated by this cold bug and being very irrisponsible, I decide to take a cup more than directed for adults, about 220mg. I had heard of kids drinking whole bottles of the shit so I figured I was in no danger of an OD. Half an hour later, I'm sitting on his cough watching some typically stupid movie on Comedy Central. I stand up for somthing or other, and immediately feel somthing entirely different than anything I have ever felt; the feeling of flying! All of a sudden, this huge sensation of euphoric, almost drunkeness hits me like a bomb. I've never had so much fun! Thus begins my expiriments with drugs.

The following summer, Myself and all of my friends have tried drinking cough syrup, when I hear of somthing new; Cough and cold pills! I thought to myself, "The feeling of DXM (The psychoactive ingredient in cough medicine) but easy to take without the bad taste of syrup!?" I had to try it.

The girl that told me about Corcidin C&C pills had said to take about 8, or half a box. But being adventurous, I decide to take 24. A box-and-a-half.In about 45 minutes, my stomach has never felt worse. I force myself through it. After an hour, i go to my bathroom, kneel over the toilet and throw up.When I stand up a few minutes later, I couldn't beleive what happened. The greatest feeling I have ever had. Stomach ach now gone entirely, I'm tripping so much ass that I don't know where I am anymore, nor do I care. I lay down on my bed and turn my radio on. The music has never sounded so great. Every song is like the best song I've ever heard and I can feel every beat pulsate through me like an earthquake of rythmic bliss. I think to myself, "I hope this is what heaven is like".

I close my eyes. The next thing I know, I feel myself be pulled from.. myself. Now I know what an out-of-body expirience is. I thought I died but didn't care because I was now in a world I have never seen before, flying through it's atmosphere looking down at it's beautiful, alien scenery. This drug is soo great that these out-of-body expiriences can be controlled. All you have to do is close your eyes while your trippin on a lot of the shit and sit their. In a few seconds, you feel yourself fly away, but knowing that your body is still sitting where it was before.

Their are 4 plateaus or highs you can get from DXM. Take Coricidin to expirience them.

Plateau 1: about 5 to 8 pills will get you here. The feeling is like the most euphoric drunkeness you have ever felt, with less lack in coordination.

Plateau 2: takes about 9 to 15 pills to get you here. The high is like being on X, (or so I've heard) and you will stumble around at this stage because your nervous system is now being paralized. You feel like your flying whenever you walk. Very euphoric.

Plateau 3: 16 to 20 pills gets you this fucked up. And beleive me, on this plateau, you are fucked up. Music becomes intesnse and euphoric, changes in conciousness are expirienced, out-of-body expiriences can occur. Thoght becomes soo bivid that you may be observed talking to yourself, as you now think you are somewhere else talking to someone or somthing . I once thought I went to heaven and was talking to jesus.

Plateau 4: 21+ pills. Now your officially trippin' balls. Basically the same as Plateau 3 but much ,much stronger. Out-of-body expiriences are occompanied by a hallucination of scenery and noises. Everything becomes a dream. You are now on the verge of subconciousness. Euphoria is replaced by a feling of well-being. You will not be able to walk straight, type, or even talk without slurring on this plateau. Here, your basically in a new world and whatever happens will be accompanied by it's feeling. (ie. you will imagine thigs so vividly that they become your reality. If somthing in this dreamlike state touches you, you feel it. Makes a noise, you hear it, etc.)

DO NOT TAKE OVER 30. DEPENDING ON BODYWEIGHT, DOSES OVER 20 CAN RAISE YOUR HEARTRATE TO NEAR-FATAL B.P.M.'S. (BEATS PER MINUTE)

Well thats it. I have now tried a shitload of different drugs and this will always be my favorite. But don't do it soo often, as you WILL build an immunity.
