Diphenhydramine HCL is a very fun drug that provides an unusual altered state when taken responsibly. The first step in having a good time is to test your tolerance to the drug, perhaps starting with 2-3 pills and dosing up as you see fit. Another almost mandatory step, in my book at least, is to smoke some marijuanna. The marijuanna enhances visuals and calms the body and mind, as many of you may know.

There are many good sources for Diphenhydramine HCL, but I find the best sources to be Benadryl Allergy Kapseals and Unisom Sleep Aid, these both provide 25-50mg Diphenhydramine a pill. As usual, please check and make sure you are only taking a product that contains the active ingredient you want to take. Avoid products containing Acitomenophen and Pseudoephedrine, they will damage your liver at higher doses.

My best trip with Diphenhyramine started at 8 pm on a friday night. I took 12 pills containing 50mg each and waited for my freind Dave to pick me up. When he arrived it had been 10 min since I took the pills, and we journeyed the short distance to the park next to my house. Within 5 mins my friends Johnny and Melissa joined us at the park. At this point we partook in the ancient herb, marijuanna dumbass, and I went flying off into never never land. I literally felt I was floating with each hit and I was seeing swirling visions ranging the whole spectrum of visible light, and possibly some not visible. I noticed within 30 min my vision was badly distorted, don't drive, and there appeared to be a grid over my field of vision, similar to a bad digital picture. I now went along for the ride to my friend Daves house. Man,I was totally out of it and I began to feel very sluggish and tired, my depth perception was horrible making it difficult or rather impossible to play foosball with my friends. They thought it was kinda funny when I would completely miss a ball and occasionally the handle. My friends also commented on my "bug eyes" as they called them, I call them extremely sensitive dialated pupils. After what seemed like hours I got a ride home and retreated to my room, narrowly avoiding a suspicious mom and an inquisitive dad. In the safety of my room I took 6 more pills totalling 18 in all and 900mg of Diphenhydramine.

O.K. let me start by saying this was the best drug experience in my short 19 year life. I was able to listen to what sounded like talk radio, you know Rush Limbaugh that sort, that sounded like it was being beamed into my head, like I was a radio. I did this for God knows how long, then I decided to look for visuals, the darkend enviornment of my room was not the best for inciting hallucinations but it sufficed. I saw shadows change into spiders, cats and even an enigmatic shadowy character that would jump up and down in the corner of my room. Many of the visions would startle me and I highly reccomend marijuanna for this reason. I was able to enjoy myself much more than on subsequent trips just because the marihuanna calmed my nerves while trippin.

Have fun with Diphenhydramine HCL, but always be as safe as possible and research, read and start slow with any new drug. Stay safe!
