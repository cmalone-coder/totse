So one day I stole 3 bottles of robitussin. (the kind with only dxm and no other active ingredients.) long story short, I got really fucked up and went to see Star Trek: Nemesis. When I walked out of the theater I thought I was going to die, but I was able to calm myself down. Somehow I made it home but I dont remember how, or anything else.

Since I didn't die or have any hallucinations I tried a larger dose. It was a few days before christmas and I had nothing to do except wait for the day I would fly home for the holidays. My room mate was going to leave the day before I did so I was planning to spend that day alone in my aparment with a brain full of DXM. I went and stole 2 bottles of Delsym, which is this new cough syrup that is supposedly 3 times more powerful than robitussin but tastes like orange juice concentrate, sort of. anyways, i drank them the day before my room mate left and tried not to act fucked up. before they hit me too hard i went and stole 2 more bottles and drank them too. so i had ingested 3 3 oz bottles and 1 5 oz bottle of this stuff thats 3x more powerful than regular cough syrup.

I can pretty much sum it all up in two words. 'holy shit.' That night I watched 'Freddy Got Fingered' and it was horrifying. Then I went to bed and dont remember much about what I did there, but I know there wasn't much sleeping. I think I just laid there staring into space. When I did doze off I would wake up about 20 minutes later and not remember who I was. The only thing I remember was when I woke up at about 5 AM and I wasn't me. It was so weird. I can't describe it any other way. I was just some creature with massively dialated pupils staring at weird black blobs on the ceiling in a room.

By the time my room mate left the next day I was somewhat sane but still totally mind fucked. I could remember my name and where I lived. But my phone number had been changed a couple days before. So I called up this girl and asked her what it was. It was really hard to talk and she immediately knew I was fucked up. Let's call her Percy, just for fun. The conversation went somthing like this:

Percy:"Hello"

me:"Hi, I have a new phone number"

Percy:"Oh, ok. What is it?"

me:"I don't know. I was hoping you could tell me."

Percy:"Are you on something?"

me:"Yeah"

Percy:"What are you on?"

me:"Cough syrup. I took it like three days ago."

Percy:"Oh, ok. uh... Bye"

It was pretty embarassing once I was able to think again and realized what I had done.

Also, I couldn't pee for about 24 hours because I was so tense. It kind of looked like I was pregnant because I had to piss so bad. When I finally did go, I almost filled up the toilet over the course of about 15 minutes. And I really wish I was exaggerating.

Oh yeah, I almost forgot, it made my dick feel like rubber. That was really scary.

So after this wonderful experience I think I can safely say that I will never get fucked up on DXM again.
