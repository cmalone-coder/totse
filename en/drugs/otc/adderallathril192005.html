I have a friend who has ADHD, my friend doesnt like to take his medicine because he has found that he can do fine without it and by hording it he can make a profit.

My friend one day comes over with his sack of horded adderall (roughly 30-80 pills). He explained to me that he and his other friends do them they get a rush and that is really it.

What he didn't include was that these friends are only taking 1 at a time and are rather skinny. My friend looks as though he has some sort of eating disorder; he is 6'4 and roughly 14 something pounds (sickly), when he informed me that he takes one to get nice in addition to his normal dose. I figured i am a much heavier person than my friend (185lbs), and with at least 20lbs of ego added in i should be able to handle 5 pills, nothing could be further from the truth!

At first there was a wave of euphoric feelings, I was giddy and my feet were tingling- as far as I was concerned things couldn't be going any better. I soon became 'super-charged', I was highly ambitious and contemplated modding my xbox controller to work on my computer (a task that I have been putting off due to PC gaming), I abandoned this idea in fear of being a bad host to my friend (normally I couldn't care less). As a result we stormed out to my trampoline at midnight, while my father slept immediately in the path of the door.

While jumping I have never felt so great or as in control as I did then. I was completely in rhythm with the mat and expected every landing and push for what seemed like minutes before.

Soon however jumping became annoying and I had the desire to listen to music, I fled to my computer in fear of my friend taking my place and thus controlling the computer for the rest of the night (totally un-founded fear).

I came inside and cranked Mudvayne only to then remember that I would be awaking my household. After a few moments of listening to music I became content with the action and called a friend of mine on the phone and began dispensing various sodas to my friend and brothers.

I noticed that I was extremely dehydrated at this point yet i noticed i was taking sips a lot smaller than the ones that i have grown accustom, this bothered me and i tried to drink more however holding the glass and attempting to drink more soda soon repulsed me and i rejected it as a gluttonous act.

I continued on the internet going on various forums that i am member and responding to threads in the oddest fashion i have ever experienced; i would answer the question then go off on a tangent, i would feel silly going off on this tangent so i began putting in all my side notes into parenthesis, soon i had flooded what should have been a simple link and explanation of codecs into a half page rant about how i dislike the non-inclusion of many formats into today's popular media players. Upon finishing this i began chugging coke furiously until i really thought about my action and soon felt as though i was going to puke. After all of this i had realized it was only 20 minutes since this ordeal had begun and i felt oddly productive yet skeptical as to the possibility of such a thing, i sat and complexly thought about the nature of this 'quantum leap' i had two working theories that i had either done everything really fucking fast, or that time had simply been going slower than usual for some unknown reason. In reality i had been working slightly faster at non-time consuming tasks, yet i was stunned at the outcome. Time passed and i soon discovered my friend had his bowl with him, we began scraping furiously in search of all potent resin, while doing so i became so intent i zoned out on nearly everything around me, completely missing the episode of Curb Your Enthusiasm that had been playing since the commencement of our scraping ritual. We went outside and smoked this bowl of resin and i got unbelievably high, it felt as though i was ultra tempremental to the wind and had their had been an increase in the breeze i would have blown away bit by bit as if i were a mountain of sand. While high i began conversing with my friend about everything in the most anal-retentive depth ever known to man, i left no detail un-described and i completely acknowledged that he was barely listening however i didn't care. The night progressed essentially in this manor until i realized that it was 5AM, we had not slept at all nor did i feel even slightly tired, we proceeded down to the local store and bought yet more Coke, i chugged a liter bottle in 2minutes flat and then began puking wildly all over the area in which we were walking, my puke contained the beads that are inside of an Adderall XR and brown acidy liquid which i later determined to be Coke, soon after the euphoria started to fade and i began to feel simply un-comfortable, commonly shivering whenever left at rest for periods of time. I lasted the whole day until roughly 4 o'clock that afternoon when i crashed and found myself not able to sit for more than a few minutes without falling asleep. I went on to later do more adderall and I've found that 3pills are enough for me to have a good trip that lasts nearly 2days and 1 night, i would definitely recommend adderall as a recreational drug in smaller doses and would highly recommend it to someone who is in need of a 'pick-me-up' to get chores done or work completed.

Stay safe, and remember sites like Totse and Erowid are your friends and should be used whenever you are in doubt about anything related to a drug nature.
