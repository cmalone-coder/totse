I had gotten home and I was really bored. I was prepared for this though--I had a container of Tone's Ground Nutmeg waiting for me. A friend, who I shall kick the ass of, told me that you can get high off nutmeg but it is very stoning. Nutmeg stoned the shit out of me!

I took the entire container of Tone's ground nutmeg which was about 6 tablespoons worth. The average dose for a nutmeg high is half that. I was like, "Fuck being conservative, I want to get high!" So I took the entire 6 tablespoons, not expecting it to work. This was at 12:30am with 825mg of caffine from tea, pills, and chocolate covered espresso beans. (There is no x in espresso.) I went to bed at 1:30am not noticing anything.

I woke up at 8:30am and my gross and fine motor movements were impared. I could still perform tasks but an observer would notice something was up. I regained full control by 9:40 am.

I went out with a friend at about 11am. This is when it started to get nasty. I was at his house taking advantage of his DSL with my 1.8" Toshiba mk2004 skg hard disk which is about as big as a credit card and stores 20GB. Really nifty. Anyhow I was getting an *.iso started downloading and I just zoned out. Thinking was dificult and I pretty much just sat there for a minute trying to get that brain capacity back. Not long after I remembered I was at my friend's house. I was having one of those "where am I?" moments. I just couldn't think, it was a haze over my head that I couldn't get through. After I got the D/L started and recovered from my minute-long lapse, we went out war driving (that's wireless hacking for those who aren't computer inclined.)

I was having real trouble with my short-term memory. It is said that you can store 7 things plus or minus two. Well at that point I could store 0 things plus or minus two. =) I would be talking about something, stop and then not having one fucking clue what I just said a literal second before. This really sucked as my friend kept asking me specific questions that were building on the conversation. I think the lack of short term memory was where my lack of brain function came from. To use a loose analogy, I had no RAM (memory); A processor (brain) is worthless without RAM.

We drove around, went to the mall, probably did a few things I can't remember and ended up at another hacker friend's house where we spend a long time. Or atleast is seemed a long time to me. We were over there from 12:50pm to 4pm pirating software. My short term memory was still shot to hell. I was starting to forget whole half minuetes. Was funny, I would be in one room then be in the bathroom with no memory of walking there. My brain was still not working too well. My IQ dropped to about 80, the average person's is 107.

I was dizzy for the most part and really thirsty. I never felt hunger but I ate a little more than I usually would. I was just like, my body needs food so I ate, I just didn't feel hunger.

At about 10pm, about a whole day after taking the nutmeg, I was working at an average level. In the morning I was fine with no noticeable symptoms.

To sum it up, nutmeg stones you without any recreational benefits. It lasts a long time so it sucks if something comes up. Plus it tastes like SHIT. Not that I know what shit tastes like. I don't reccommend it as it detracted from my day in a big way.

James
