Vaporizers! You've heard about them, mabye you know what they are, how they work, but I'm going to teach you a method of making one for less than the $200 dollar ones that you can buy online.

Vaporizers work by boiling THC, the chemical that makes you "high". THC boils at around 450 degrees Farenheit, and that temperature can be attained through candle heat. After the THC boils, it will become steam that you will see floating at the top of your vaporizer. Considering you only get 30% to 50% of the THC in the weed you smoke, I think a vaporizer would be a good deal.

Building the Vaporizer

First, we're going to need some things:

  • knife
  • duct tape or electrician's tape
  • 2 straws or glass tubes (optional)
  • 16 oz. bottle cap
  • Clear 100w. lightbulb (not frosted)

First, hold the lightbulb so that the black thing on the end you put into the socket is facing up. Take your knife and cut off about a half-inch from the black thing. you might want to sand off the metal edges. Next, you need to take the wire and glass parts just inside the lightbulb, so you have a glass container. Take your bottle cap and cut two holes in it, about the size for a straw to fit through. You can use tape around the neck of the lightbulb to make a handle, the glass can get hot. Rinse out the lightbulb, and screw/tape on the lid. Put the straws in, or a peice of tape over the holes, and you're ready to go! (I'd let it dry before using it)

How To Use Your Vaporizer

Take your weed and put it in the lightbulb. Now put your cap on, and have a lit tea-candle under the bulb. Let the candle heat up the weed, and you should see a white mist in the glass. After you think you've got enough THC out, breathe through a straw or hole. Now you're high.

Have phun whith your vaporizer!
