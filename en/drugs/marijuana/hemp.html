Though the following issue is controversial, we cannot allow the type of propaganda that gave us "Beef, food for Real People"and "Pork, the new White Meat" to mislead us from the sustainable possibilities of hemp.

Hemp has been used in everything from papermaking to food production for over 6,000 years.

Hemp can reduce the land needed to produce paper by over 75%, saving over one billion trees per year, while greatly reducing the toxicity of paper production!

Hemp can produce longer lasting denim, canvass, and linen using fewer acres and pesticides then any other plant! During the 1930s, it was estimated that over half of the linens imported into the US were made of hemp.

Hemp can produce more edible protein per acre than any other plant including soybeans!

Over 5,000 textiles can be made from hempfiber while the hurds off the same plant produce cellulose which can be used in over 25,000 products. Meanwhile the seeds and their oil can be used to make paints, lamp fuel, lubricants, and be eaten as food.

SOURCES: USDA film "Hemp for Victory" 1942, Popular Mechanics article "New Billion Dollar Crop" February 1938, USDA Agricultural Statistics
