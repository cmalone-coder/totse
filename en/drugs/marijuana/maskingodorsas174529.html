I come from a couple of uptight assholes, who unfortunately i cannot leave until i am 18. This doesnt however affect my smoking habits and the secret lies in masking the odors. No matter pot, cigarettes, or any other illegal substances, the odors are all incriminating. Here are a list of suggestions to remedy this

AXE OR BODY SPRAYS

Axe is my god! axe is great becuase it not only covers smells in any room it also leaves behind a pleasant aroma. The proper way to apply it is to spray it on walls surronding doorways, windows, and any other inlets/outlets of air. Doing so will make any air that comes in smell of axe, thus filling the room with a great smell. The alcohol in these sprays also will float into the noses of people entering the room numbing their receptors of smell.

RUBBING ALCOHOL

I keep a spray bottle filled with rubbing alcohol handy at all times becuase it is a great for cleaning CD's and it will no doubt numb the receptors of anyone who happens to be smelling your smoking area.

FANS

Though it may be hot when smoking you should always aim your fan at an open window; doing so will create a current removing all smelly air in the room and leaving normal fresh air. This also disperses smoke that may be lingering around the room.

SMOKE STACKS

No not a chimney, a smoke stack is a helpful device that will not allow smoke to smell like anything but laundry detergent. To make this take an empty paper towel roll and stuff the end with 3-6 dryer sheets, when it comes time to exhale stick your finger in one end (prevent the dryer sheets from falling out), and blow into the other. The hot smoke will heat the sheets making the room smell of fresh laundry.

WINDOWS

Pretty self explanatory, blow smoke out an open window and the smell should be greatly decreased.

So you see by following these measures you should not ever be caught due to smell. As for hiding your stuff that is up to you, if you happen to leave anything out in the open your stupid and you deserve what ever may happen to you.

Happy Smoking!
