It's a simple bong and the only things that you need are a tube of Pringles (empty) a joint or bowl and a knife (or something else sharp that will go through the tube).

You start by sawing the bottom of the tube off leaving the lid end alone, I prefer to do it at an angle cause it looks better and its easier to cover. Then just make a hole to stick your bowl or joint into, when u have done that your pretty much ready to go. All u have to do is make an air tight seal around the mouth peice, light up and start sucking untill the entire pot is filled with smoke (you will have to guess when this is) when it is filled just keep sucking and remove the lid quickly, all of the smoke that was in the tube is know in your lungs.

Although very crude this bong is usefull and needs nearly nothing to make, it wastes no smoke and you get too eat the pringles :-D so happy smoking.
