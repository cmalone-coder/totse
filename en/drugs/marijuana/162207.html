I like to use the mountain dew bottle because it has a big mouth on it for a nice sized bowl.

First, put a hole (about the size of the straw) in the middle of the dew bottle, and then make one in the 2-liter at the same height. Put a peice of duct, or electrical tape, over each hole. Poke a hole through each piece of tape, then connect the two bottles with the straw. You'll need to seal it where each bottle meets the sraw, so just put tape around it in every possible way so that no air comes in or out. Now, get your other hose, pen, or whatever you decided to use, and get ready to put it in the 2-liter. Put a hole in the big bottle about an inch or so above the label. If you have a hose around a foot long, it will make it better. But either way it should kick the shit out of you. Anyways, get this hose and stick it through the hole, leaving about half inside and half outside the bottle. Angle it so the the part outside is pointing up. Seal this, and make sure that the tape is thick enough so your hose won't fall. Now fill up both bottles with water, gaterade, whatever(powerade and all that adds flavor to the smoke; you can even use beer if you want). I like to use (cold) orange flavored gatorade, and then drop in chunks of orange popsickles. Use your imagination, or strait cold water. But make sure not to cover up the straw connecting the two bottles. Now, make a bowl out of the aluminum foil on the mountain dew mouth. Leave the lid off of the 2-liter, and use that opening as a choke.

You can put multiple hoses on it, for multi-users, but one is enough. Trust me, if yours comes out like mine have, you'll need to wait for your turn cause you'll be spending that time coughing like a bitch. Just make sure not to hit it untill both chambers are getting cloudy, then dont stop untill theres no pot in the bowl. You can give a great shotgun off of this, too. Take the bowl off after youve hit it a few times, have somebody put their mouth over the mountain dew opening, hold your hand over the choke, and blow hard thro your hose. If they hold that in then they should have tears in their eyes. Also when you use this, one guy will have to sit the bottles in the palms of his hand, and you work the choke while you hit it. Or, I guess you could sit it on a table. Anyway, have fun, get stoned, and forget what happened.
