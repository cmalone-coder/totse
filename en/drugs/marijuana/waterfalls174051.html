Step 1: Put the tinfoil over the lid-hole on the bottle and make it as air tight as possible, make a small dome shape in the tinfoil curving in towards the bottle, use the pin to make loads of tiny holes in the tinfoil to make a gause. Once the tinfoil is quite see through(the more the better) you have an ample gause.

Step 2: Burn a SMALL hole in the bottom of the bottle, smaller than a penny.

Step 3: Crumble your stash into the gausse untill you have a sufficeint amount(more than enough for a strong joint)

Step 4: Take the gausse off and set it down. Fill the bottle up with a liquid(we normally use a stream but a tap is better) remembering to keep you finger over the whole at the bottom so it dosn't leak out

Step 5: Still holding the hole place the gausse back on the top of the bottle, burn the Mary-Jane for a few seconds before letting go of the hole, keep burning untill all the water runs out.

Step 6: Take a lung fulla fun! Suck in that mystery air like a whore, removing the tinfoil from the top first of course.
