No, not Italian pasta, I mean the half way mark between the coca leaf and cocaine hydrochloride. It is possible to smoke this substance in combination with tobacco or cannabis for euphoric and stimulating effects.

What you need:

  • Coca leaves (as many as you want)
  • Salt
  • Sodium Carbonate
  • Petrol
  • Hydrochloric Acid

It's as easy as that. Get the leaves from Peru or any where else is South America. Any idiot can make this. It's not like the complex synthesis for cocaine. Sprinkle salt and sodium carbonate on the leaves and leave them to sweat in the sun for maybe five hours. If your short of time then you can leave them for three hours but five is the best.

Now pour the leaves into a 50 gallon drum and add a large amount of petrol. Leave this for a week or so. Now the liquid in the drum off into glass jars. This will be tedious. Bare in mind that this method is for mass production and could be done just as easily on a smaller scale. After the petrol has been completely separated from the drum and put into loads of glass jars, allow them to sit so a separation can take place. After 48 hours the liquid in the jars should have clear petrol at the top of the jar, a muddy petrol/alkaloid mix in the middle, and the wonderful guarapo at the bottom.

This guarapo should be filtered out using coffee filters. Then add it to the hydrochloric acid. Let the reaction take place. Filter it again. The remaining substance should be dried in the sun.

You now have some C-/B+ quality pasta. Smoke it up yourself or cut it up and deal it out!
