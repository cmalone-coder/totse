#1 Getting the condensing rig prepared

First, purchase a 2 ? GALLON VENTLESS GAS CAN. Take the cap off of the spout and dispose of (the cap not the spout). Next, using DUCT TAPE, go over the joint again. In the rear of the can, poke a small hole in can and cover with 1 small piece of tape. This is VERY important. This will prevent flask from exploding due to too much pressure. This part is now ready.

VERY IMPORTANT!!! Do not attempt to use anything except the finest quality cookware! Pyrex , Kimex, or equivalent. DO NOT USE CHEAP SHIT AS YOU WILL GET HURT! This is said from experience the hard way, as they will explode from pressure.

#3 Clear hose

Depending on which size and type of cookware you have, hose size will vary. The Preferred apparatus would be either a 1000ml or 2000ml Pyrex volumetric flask. These have plenty of room to hold on to. The Florence Boiling Flask works well also. With the volumetric flasks the hose needed is about 4 FEET OF 1"OD. CLEAR HOSE, with others measuring the top can determine the output size. A fit to the inside of the flask is preferred, however one that fits over the outside is O.K. Now, the 1"od. Hose should fit tightly over the spout on the can. TAPE WELL with both tapes as described earlier, and reduce or expand, with proper connectors, until the size to go into the flask is obtained.

#4 Use preparation

Fill gas can with 1 gal h2o. Regular tap is fine here. Hang from hook approx. 3 to 4 feet above cooking table.

#5 Heat source

A DIGITAL HOT PLATE is preferred, however a one eye electric burner will work also. A good quality COFFEE MAKER to heat and keep hot DH2O is needed also. DO NOT USE OPEN FLAME!
