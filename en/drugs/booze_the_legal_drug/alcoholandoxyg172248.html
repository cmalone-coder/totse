Impaired function caused by drinking alcohol could be restored by oxygen inhalation. In other works, oxygen can reverse the effects of alcohol on the body.

The alcohol in liquor is dissolved into acetaldehyde. If it is redissolved, it is dissolved into carbonic acid and water. So when the alcohol gets dissolved; it needs 3 molecules of oxygen to dissolve one molecule of alcohol into carbonic acid and water The more one drinks, the more oxygen one needs.

If there is insufficient oxygen after drinking, acetaldehyde also remains undissolved. As a result, one gets a headache and an attack of vomiting.

Among many other experiments proving this theory, the one that caught my attention was the experiment measuring brain waves that bear the results of the significant connection between alcohol and oxygen. Brain wave measurements focused on the wave centered on a certain frequency, so that they could understand the correlation. According to the results, a waves of 11 to 13 cycles per second appeared in a stable state, but a waves of 8.5 to 11Hz appeared after drinking. The distribution of wave movements signifies impaired functioning of the brain. This phenomenon occurs 90 minutes after drinking. However, after we get oxygen, the first a wave begins to decrease while the wave increases. That is, the brain restores itself to normal functioning ability. This normalization happens as soon as 1 to 2 minutes after inhalation of oxygen.

"Because of the close relevance between alcohol and oxygen, I can conclude that inhaling oxygen is the best way of overcoming a hangover. For example, according to the present traffic laws, if one's blood alcohol level is below 0.05ml, he is not considered in violation of the law. However, it might be better to have him inhale oxygen to help him recover instead of allowing him to remain inebriated. Additionally, forcing drunken bus and taxi drivers to inhale oxygen through an oxygen mask would prevent car accidents dramatically. In saloons and bars with impure air, inhaling oxygen would decrease the number of fainting episodes and drunkenness by supplying them with pure air."
