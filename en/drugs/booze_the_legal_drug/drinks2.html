Some More Good Drinks

Crown Royal Manhattan:
1 part Crown Royal
1/2 part Sweet Varmouth
Dash of bitters and lemon peel
Stir or Shake Lightly

Seagram's 7 Crown Seven Seas:
1 ox. Seagram's 7 Crown
Splash of Leroux Apricot Liquer
Splash of Pine-apple juice
Splace of Orange juice.

Seagrams's V.O. Gold Rush:
1 1/4 oz. Seagram's V.O.
Ginger Ale
Pour Wiskey over ice cubes
Fill with ginger ale, stir

Gin Driver:
1 1/4 oz. Seagram's Gin
3 oz. of Orange Juice
Seagram's Tonic Water to fill over crushed ice.

Chivas Regal and Soda:
1 1/4 of Chivas Regal
Seagram's Club Soda
Ice
Fill to desired level in rocks or highball glass

Myer's Rum Sharkbite:
1 1/4 oz. of Myer's Original Dark Rum
3 oz. of Orange Juice
Dash of grenadine over ice in a tumbler

The Glenlivet Classic
1 1/4 oz of the Glenlivet Single Malt Scotch over ice.

Captain Morgan Original Spiced Rum and Cola
1 1/4 oz. of Captian Morgan
4 oz. of Cola
In tall glass add ice. Stir. Squeeze in wedge of lime

All drinks are used with Seagram's products...an expert would know that
it is the best.
