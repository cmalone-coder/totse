Electromagnetic Radiation - a health problem?

Electromagnetism is not a serious problem is "There is no proof that
Electromagnetic radiation (EMR) causes any diseases in humans ". This is
literally true because no tests of humans has ever been done. We live in

where it cannot be ignored. Electromagnetic radiation is a serious problem
and may threaten man's existence on this planet. Consider the following:

- Children of mothers who slept under electric blankets
developed 250% more brain tumors, 70% more leukemias

University of North Carolina in study duplicate

ovarian cancer all lived less than 600 feet from 69,000
volt power line.
