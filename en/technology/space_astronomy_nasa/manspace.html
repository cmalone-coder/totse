On January 27, ?1967 Virgil Grissom, Ed White and Roger Chaffee died when
?testing ?their Apollo capsule. ?The capsule was scheduled ?to take off in
Mid-February as the first manned Apollo launching.

Although most people think John Glenn was the first man in space ?he was
actually the fifth. He was the first American to go into orbit. An
interesting sidelight is that of all the Americans who went ?into space he has
logged the least space flight time.

America has launched 56 ?manned space flights logging 483 days. ?The 132
astronauts have logged 1522 man days in space.

The manned space program was halted after the Challenger tragedy but is
scheduled to resume in February, 1988.
