Rocket/Missile Designations under DoD's Joint Desi

I have tried to compile a complete list of the Rocket/Missile
designations, including (theoretical) Booster, Probes and Satellites,
used under the Joint Designations System by the DoD and its
services, USAF / USN / USMC / US Army, from 1962 onwards.

Last changed January 1995 -- a new, updated version will be
available soon .



The Status Prefix letters

C - Captive
J - special tests, temporary
M - Maintenance
N - special tests, permanent
X - eXperimental (or prototype)
Y - prototype (or preserial)
Z - planned, proposed, projected

The Launch Mode or Environment letters

A - Airborne or air-launched
B - multiple
C - Coffin or Container
F - Individual or Infantry
G - runway
H - silo-stored, surface-launched (High ?)
L - silo-stored, silo-launched (Low ?)
M - Mobil
P - soft Pad
R - overwater, vessel or ship
U - Underwater or submarine

The Mission letters

D - Decoy
E - Electronic warfare or special electronic equipment
G - surface attack (Ground ?)
I - Intercept, aerial
Q - drone or target
T - Training
U - Underwater attack or submarine attack
W - Weather (I don't know any)

The Vehicle letters

B - Booster (don't know any)
M - Missile (guided)
N - probe (don't know any)
R - Rocket (unguided)
S - Satellite (don't know any)

Examples: (Status) Launch Mission Vehicle - No. Series (Name)

Y Q M - 98 A
A D M - 20 B Quail
P T M - 16 E Atlas
X M G M - 52 B Sea Lance
A I R - 2 A Genie
Z A I M - 132 A ASRAAM
