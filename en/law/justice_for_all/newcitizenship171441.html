Just for a moment, try to make believe that today is April 1986. A few months ago U.S. President Ronald Reagan was publicised all over America as promoting the international Foreign Birth Registration program to U.S. citizens if they planned to travel abroad.

"This is the height of the terrorist season...", exclaimed Reagan, referring to planes being hyjacked by individuals, both 'foreign' and 'domestic'. One such instance took place at the Los Angeles International airport in couthern California. A Continental airline passenger jet was actually 'comandeered' on the ground or as news reports indicated "hyjacked'.

What wasn't revealed was the so-called "terrorist" was a former U.S. Special Forces soldier who later became a high-level U.S. Department of Defense scientist and nuclear physicist working on super secrets.

Imagine, a ROCKWELL Senior Staff Scientist and Nuclear Physicist working for several years at U.S. National Laboratories like the Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena, California who all of a sudden goes crazy.

The "terrorist" in this case was Vytautas "Vic" Jonas Malasauskas, a native born Lithuanian and naturalized U.S. citizen who had served honorably in the U.S. Army and was previously stationed in Augsburg, West Germany.

Victor was 50-something, drove a red Corvette convertible, was an avid skier and tennis player, lived in the popular adult community known as Oakwood Garden Apartments in Marina Del Rey and in Seal Beach, California.

Vic had other favorite things too. His work.

Scholastic and work wizardry skills of Malasauskas involved electro-optics, growing crystalline materials in outer space and some very classified research and development projects for U.S. government space weapons programs.

Malasauskas was one of the great minds behind a secret technology project involving "staring plane mosaics", a 'star' navigation and telemetry guidance system used in various NASA space projects, which was being converted into the U.S. "Star Wars" program using airborne lasers and projected energy beam weapons.

Vic had just returned to the JPL lab in Pasadena from an intense Pentagon briefing, given by a group of high-level Generals of the U.S. Strategic Defense Initiative (S.D.I.). A subject where even the 'names' of security classifications were 'classified'.

Vic had just been informed by his JPL program manager that the long-haired hippy who was constantly reading the comic strips and the Sunday funny papers in the department next to him, was not just a Carl Sagan wannabe astronomer from the University of California at Berkeley, but was going to be Vic's new 'assistant' for the program the Pentagon officials had just briefed Vic on.

Vic had a serious problem with the vast majority of JPL's so-called 'rocket scientist' "specialists" who for the most part were stupid, marijuana-smoking, hippy freaks, and beat-niks. Thowbacks from the 1950s and '60s, according to Vic, would typically nap and eat lunch out on one the back lawns outside the JPL building, which Vic swore smelled like it had been fumigated with freshly smoked hemp or other.

Vic's program manager cited 'budget cut-backs' as the reason for the curious lack of truly specialized talent that would have otherwise filled those 'specialist' spots on their JPL team.

Vic was a middle-aged rocket scientist and if you didn't know him you might swear by his short blonde haircut and steel blue eyes that he was a true Aryan rocket scientist straight out of Adolf Hitler's rocket research center at Peenemunde.

Vic couldn't or wouldn't grasp what his boss seemed to understand. Hippy's could work on sensitive defense projects too. Vic's boss didn't want to hear Vic's please for mercy to be saved from having an idiot assigned to him for what the Pentagon officials had just drilled Vic about in the briefing. Vic gave those generals his personal assurance that their project would receive the highest level of talent under his direction.

One sunny day, after Vic was tired of begging for qualified talent, he stepped into a Radio Shack store in southern Califormia, bought a plastic remote-controller for a remote-control toy car, stuck a disassembled Browning 9mm automatic pistol inside his 'carry-on' ski boots, which then caused the gun to pass flawlessly right through airport security x-ray machines due to the boots reflective metal buckles that provided the much-needed cover for the concealed weapon. Vic was sentenced to twenty (20) years in federal prison, where he actually wound up serving twelve (12) years before returning to California and then Colorado.
