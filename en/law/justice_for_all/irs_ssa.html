Though I have not yet read this particular posting... I have personally
sent Revocations of both Social Security and "my" Birth Certificate. Thus
far I have not received any reply concerning "my" BC, though I am about to
send off an FOIA request to the agency concerned. The SSA is another
matter. I received a "polite" one page [form] letter from the alleged
"Chief, Correspondence Section" claiming my Asseveration was "irrelevant."
To "back" their claim they cited United States v. Lee (455 U.S. 252), a
supreme Court ruling concerning an Amish employer (Mr. Lee) who refused
Social Security payments based upon religious exemption - IRC @ 1402(g).
Summarily, the Court stated that "mandatory" participation in Social
Security did not violate Mr. Lee's First Amendment protected rights, IOW:
he didn't qualify for the exemption.

***Keep in mind, now, that a Court must only address the issue(s) brought
before it (Brushaber v. Union Pacific, a classic case). The Court, in this
case, ruled properly based upon the issue brought before it - Mr. Lee's
non-participation in Social Security based upon claim of religious
exemption (this is why it is sooooo important for all patriots to bring
their issues before the Court properly and completely, and in the pretense
upon which the Court is desired to rule -- a lot of bad case law has been
built-up as the result of poorly planned issues and poorly prepared
dockets).
Furthermore, for a citation to have a legal premise, whether Mandatory
or Persuasive, it must be applied to a case of similar issue.***

The SSA's response was/is entirely fraudulent based upon this cite: 1) the
issue brought before the Court in U.S. v. Lee is one of religious exemption
-- NOWHERE in my Asseveration to the SSA was a protestation based upon
religious preference ever raised; 2) the exemption being claimed by Mr. Lee
(the entire premise of the case) is found among a list of definitions (IRC
@ 1402) for "Tax on Self-Employment Income" (IRC Chapter 2) -- Nordby
Supply Co. notwithstanding, I am not "self-employed" and could not qualify
for exemption under 1402(g) even if my asseveration had dared to claim it
WHICH IT DID NOT(!).
