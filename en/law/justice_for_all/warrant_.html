documentation. Here is an exact replica of a "SEARCH WARRANT" needed
to enter someones house or property. The only items I didnt include
on the warrent are any numbers that the police would be able to trace
the origonal copy from. I have placed "X" in place of any numbers,
also the boxes remain empty to simulate a raw copy of the warrant.

On the origonal copy, the paper is light blue with a 3mm thick
white stripe on top of the page. The inside of the boxes are white
in colour. The print is in Dark Blue, with the exception of some
lighter blue on parts like "Street number/name".
