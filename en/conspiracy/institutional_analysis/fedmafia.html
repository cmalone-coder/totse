The Federal Mafia

by Irwin Schiff

This is Irwin Schiff's fourth book on the federal income tax, and reveals in shocking detail how every aspect of it is implemented in violation of law and the U.S. Constitution. YOU WILL DISCOVER, among other things, THAT NO LAW REQUIRES YOU TO FILE INCOME TAX RETURNS OR PAY THIS TAX.

Approximately 400,000 copies of Schiff's books are in circulation. His best seller, "How Anyone Can Stop Paying Income Taxes," published in 1982, caused the government, in 1983, to enact laws designed to frustrate the public's ability to use the information contained in that book. That book also caused the government, in 1983, to change the Privacy Act Notice, which actually informs the public that NO ONE IS REQUIRED TO FILE INCOME TAX RETURNS. However, the government still words that notice, which appears in all 1040 booklets, so the public can not figure this out.

In "The Federal Mafia," Schiff provides excerpts from IRS manuals which show how IRS agents routinely break the law. One such excerpt admits, for example, that people "may refuse" to be audited by the IRS, further revealing that those who are audited are tricked (unknown to themselves) into surrendering constitutional rights. Similarly, people surrender constitutional rights when they file income tax returns, mistakenly believing that this is what the law requires them to do.

Few Americans realize that the word "income" is not even defined in the law, and that the Supreme Court defined it as a corporate profit. This means, that since individuals are not corporations, no one, by law, can have "income" that can be subject to an "income" tax. In addition, Americans are under the false impression that the income tax is imposed pursuant to the 16th Amendment. In this book, you will discover, however, that the income tax is imposed IN VIOLATION OF THAT AMENDMENT.

Did you know, for example, that INCOME TAXES HAVE TO BE ASSESSED JUST LIKE PROPERTY TAXES? And, if you do not file an income tax return, there is no lawful way the government can assess you. Did you know that no law says that you have to pay income taxes or that you can be "liable" for such a tax, though such provisions are contained in the law in connection with *other* taxes? Would you believe that the United States Tax Court is really a bureaucratic agency that is called a "court" in order to dupe and intimidate the public?

Are you aware that there are no criminal statutes that apply to income taxes? And, there is no law giving federal courts authorization (i.e. jurisdiction) to prosecute anyone for income tax "crimes". Discover in this book, for example how Leona Helmsley's lawyers could have stopped her trial the first day it opened. The question is, why didn't they do it?

The February, 1991 issue of 'The Reader's Digest' contained an article entitled "How the IRS Abuses Taxpayers." The article reports that in 1989 "The IRS filed 904,000 liens, levied 2.3 million paychecks and bank accounts, and made 13,000 seizures of property." Chapter 7 of this book explains why EVERY ONE OF THESE SEIZURES AND LIENS WAS ILLEGAL! That article also stated that "It's time [for Americans] to fight back." THIS BOOK WILL GIVE YOU THE AMMUNITION TO DO *JUST THAT!*

This is merely part of the information contained in this amazing book. It reveals, chapter and verse, how the IRS prepares bogus documents in order to collect income taxes in violation of law. And while this book tears the mask off the IRS, it also exposes its partners in crime -- our duplicitous federal judiciary, our lawless Department of Justice, and a culpable U.S. Congress.

While this book deals with what is normally a dry subject, income taxes, it is written in the humorous and entertaining style that have made all of Irwin Schiff's books so popular with the public. If you are unfamilar with the income tax rip off -- as are most Americans -- prepare to be shocked. Sadly, if the facts in this book were not so thoroughly documented, it would be unbelievable as a work of fiction. This book will convice you that *organized crime* in America begins with the Federal Government!
