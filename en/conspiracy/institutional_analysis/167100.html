Organized Crime: Psychiatry/Child "Protective"/Judical System

by Diane Booth

My son was kidnapped, imprisoned, tortured, and brain damaged by the side effects of psychotropic drugs forced upon him by the State. He was a beautiful, normal, healthy, intelligent boy. Now he has "regressed into a primitive" condition. This is what the US government is hiding from its citizens.

TIMELINE of CASE

DIANE AND VINCENT BOOTH

JD11110

MARCH 19, 1999

Meeting with Pamela McMahn, School Psychologist Cherry Chase School, Sunnyvale, CA re. Assessment and Notification of Referal for Special Education - Discussed my assertion of rights under the Americans With Disabilities Act; I told the panel that I refuse to put my son on Ritalin.

APRIL 4, 1999

Parent conference re. Vincent's "hyperactivity". Again, I refused Ritalin for my son.

APRIL 24, 1999

Referral to Sunnyvale Police Dept. by Cherry Chase School for alleged "suspected emotional abuse" and "domestic violence" .

MAY 19, 1999

I wrote letter to Susan King, social worker, regarding an "accident" Vincent had at school and again asserting my rights under the ADA.

JUNE 30, 1999

Sunnyvale Police Dept. enters my name to the Dept. of Justice Child Abuse Central Index without any investigation.

JULY 24, 1999

Santa Clara County freezes my boyfriend's bank account causing our rent check to bounce.

JULY 29, 1999

Vincent kidnapped by social worker Christy Tran and Officer Terry Schillinger without a warrant, without an investigation and without a court order

NOVEMBER 19, 1999

Vincent prescribed Ritalin, Dexedrine and Cyclert by Daniel Katzenberg, M.D. of Santa Clara County Medical Centre

DECEMBER 8, 1999

Vincent prescribed Imipramine Heterocyclic Anti-Depressant

Side effects: constipation, difficulty in urination, disorientation, confusion

MARCH 16, 2000

Vincent started taking Ritalin 5 mg. Side effects: anxiety, agitation, tics, Tourettes Syndrome

MARCH 29, 2000

I filed lawsuit under Section 504 of the Americans with Disabilities Act, C - 00- 20357 JF/EAI

MAY 2000

Vincent hospitalized several times for severe bruising of face suffered at Eastfield Ming Quong

JUNE 2000

I filed for an Emergency Protective Order in Federal Court with pictures of Vincent's injuries as exhibit;

Judge denies

JULY 2, 2000

Escape with Vincent into Canada

SEPT. 2000

Vincent finally recovered, "tics" subsided, his emotional and physical condition stable.

SEPT 10, 2000

Apprehended by police who had $500,000 bounty for Vincent. Warrant for my arrest with NO BAIL. I file for refugee status.

SEPT. 11, 2000

Vincent returned to Santa Clara County - requires hospitalization for severe emotional distress and separation anxiety.

FEB. 5, 2002

Letter from Dept. of Social Services, Community Care Licensing Division, stating that investigation revealed Vincent was hospitalized for mouth inflammation/swelling. Also, there were several incidents concerning other clients at EMQ.

AUG. 8, 2002

Status Review Report of Franklin Kitchener states that Vincent has regressed to a primitive condition, severe, related to out of home placement
