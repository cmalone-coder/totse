9/11 Theory

by Mike Dowd

A Thought From Mike Dowd.

For those who are cold war buffs, what significance did September 11th have before 2001? What connection did the American CIA have with Afghanistan before 2001? Let me answer the last question first.

Afghanistan was a battleground for the two world superpowers for most of the 80’s. The soviet military occupied the country, the king went to exile, and the CIA took over covert control of the Afghanistan resistance forces which 20 years later is know as AL Quada. The CIA trained and supplied the rouge tribal armies with weapons, information on the Soviet operational theory and twentieth century fighting techniques. These people, including our favorite scapegoat Osama bin Laden, were trained, by the CIA, for years to fight the Soviet machine with little more than small arms and their own creativity and passion. Eventually, the tribal forces overcame, the Soviet’s left. Thus so did the CIA, no reason to be there any more. Leaving these people with no infrastructure, no government and a war ravaged homeland.

Since the early days of the cold war, the Soviets and our recent “victims”, the Americans, have been on the brink of destroying the earth hundreds of times over. Nikita Khrushchev parked a half dozen ballistic missiles 90 miles off the coast of Florida, the world nearly ended. The cold war was always fought on someone else’s land, the cold war destroyed Afghanistan, how happy would you be if your “friends”, the CIA, took off once the Soviets were done. It’s like leaving a buddy to clean up after you were the one who trashed their party. Only one word comes to mind, raped. That how they were feeling, lasted for damn near 15 years. Those guys got back at the US, yeah, remember the tragic video of the towers falling, still chills you doesn’t it? Remember how we all felt, our western society was violated, raped even?

You have to wonder if the CIA ever thought the people who they trained to fight the mighty hammer and sickle would one day be the ones to attack the western world, probably not eh? That “Arbitrary” day two years ago was chosen for a reason. Fuck the last guy that nearly brought war to the States, who was that? good ol’ Nikita, god rest his soul. Shit, I can’t remember when he died? Oh yeah, September 11th, 1971, that answers the first question. Fuck, I guess paybacks a bitch, good too see the terrorists have a sick sense of humor too.

Forward this with this moral sentiment; never create a problem to solve a problem, know your enemies better than your friends, evil is everywhere you left it, old enemies may die, but they will never go away, hate unleashes a maelstrom god himself cannot stop. Oh yeah here’s another few good ones ”Politicians are the same all over. They promise to build a bridge where there is no river. “, ”The more bombers, the less room for doves of peace.”, want to know who’s quotes those are? it’s ironic, it was Khrushchev. Just a thought, Love you all…..

Mike Dowd.
