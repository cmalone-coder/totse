Golden Cobb on the Oklahoma Bombing

by Golden Cobb

25th April 1995

Beloved ones. I am swimming across the ethers to your world and I see much that could distress me. But I know that everything which happens in these End Times is needed. Even the horrific massacre in Rwanda, that too is necessary on a global level. I cannot tell you all which is happening, but I see it and I see it is good. It is good that the evil is exposing itself, and in these Endtimes it will expose itself more and more. Do not be afraid; you are safe in the bosom of this Existence, you are safe with the healing streams of all the worlds.

It is important that you try not to be distressed by all that happens. Compassionate, yes. Distressed does not help the whole. A psychologist was saying 80% of people are suffering from post-traumatic stress syndrome simply from seeing the pictures on the TV news of Oklahoma. The fears are triggered, the anger is triggered, their terror and their panic are triggered. I would like you to be among the 20% who can remain compassionate, that are so much in their centre, they are not deeply disturbed.

There are two sets of people who are not traumatically affected. There are those who are so deeply in their centre that they know what is happening is necessary for the whole as the evil of the world exposes itself. And then there are the blockheads, who are simply dumb, insensitive human beings. Be open, but try not to be distressed - this does not help the work.

Oklahoma was created by all those who have fed hatred on the airwaves. That energy was lurking in the airwaves ready to be eaten by a group of psychopathic white supremacists. It was in the air already. It inflated the perverse and evil desires of men whose psyches are on the edge of insanity. That hate was America's hate, and look at what it has created. America is totally responsible for allowing the hate on the airwaves. So America, as a nation, is totally responsible for the Oklahoma bombing. And more will happen unless America changes. It was amusing to us that one of the most massive perpetrators of racial hatred on the airwaves was saying after the bombing "We'll get the bastards who did this, the Middle Eastern bastards, the foreign bastards." And yet this very same man was one of the people who fed the hatred on the airwaves which caused the bombing. He shares the same views as the bombers. This was amusing to us. For what now can he say? "I will get the white supremacist bastards who did this crime"? The key question is: WILL AMERICA WAKE UP? Will it be challenged enough to go beyond this narrow hatred and massive rage against those who do not deserve this rage.

I am pleased the Oklahoma bombing happened. It was essential, it was necessary, for the evil to expose itself. And as in all such situations, those who are killed and maimed, they are fulfilling a higher soul purpose. They have volunteered on a higher level for the service of saving the world. They have volunteered for the service of saving America. It may be that their death will be in vain if America does not wake up, or it may be that the victims' sacrifice of their lives in this bombing will be rewarded with a wake-up triumph.

Can America awaken? I am utterly optimistic about America. I do not care what anybody says. I feel they are simply adolescent as a national soul. Awakening is possible, but they have to grow out of their adolescence and become adults, sensitive, conscious adults.

The whole world is waiting, for the whole world always waits for America to show the lead. I am utterly optimistic because I feel the soul and pulse of America. I feel my heart beats and breaks with America's waves. They swing to one extreme then they swing, within days, to the opposite extreme. There is no stability in the quicksilver of their emotional response. They are crazy. And the quest for America, can that craziness become a constructive and positive force for good in the world?

The Root Chakra has been stacked up with immensely powerful vibrations which will be issuing forth for some time now. They are pouring and pouring everywhere. And the vibrations will ignite the hearts of Americans and the souls of Americans.

I cannot see a way out for America, yet I am utterly optimistic, because America is a pioneer nation and they will find their own way out. Yes, the evil must be exposed. It must be drummed so deeply into the psyche of America that a great shift happens, a great flame catches fire, the flame to go beyond the petty, trashy hatred...

(Golden Cobb then goes on to talk about other issues)
