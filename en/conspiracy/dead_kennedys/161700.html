JFK's Assassination

by Awesomniac

In november of 1963, the United States President was shot dead by a gunman posted behind "the grassy knoll". This gunman was in fact a gun-woman. An assassin hired from the Triads. She was not, herself, an important factor in the assassination. The question is, who hired her? That is a question that I will answer for you now. She was hired by a conspiring organisation unknown to the US Government. Their name was the StarBorn, and there were fifty-two members, including Jack Rubinstein, who killed their decoy assassin, Lee Harvey Oswald, in order to protect the secrets of the StarBorn. Their secret was a large one. Their leader was an outsider. He was not of earth origin. The being known only by his codename, Alpha-Sigma, was the one who organised Kennedy's assassination. It is believed that he was acting on orders given to him by superiors in another star system. Shortly after Kennedy's assassination, Alpha-Sigma was found and taken to the USSR by soviet agents known commonly as "The Men in Black". Before Alpha-Sigma could be questioned, he shot himself in the head and died. The StarBorn organisation shut down after that, and no more has been heard of them.
