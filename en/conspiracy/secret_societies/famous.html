UNITED STATES PRESIDENTS AND THE THIRTY-THIRD DEGREE

There has been some loose talk about Presidents of the U.S. secretly being Thirty-third degree Masons. Actually only two Presidents have received this honor - Harry S. Truman and Gerald R. Ford Jr.

Truman was coronetted in Washington,D.C. on October 19, 1945. He was Grand Master of the Grand Lodge of Missouri in 1940 and signed the Dispensation and Charter of the Missouri Lodge of Research of which he became its Master in 1950 while President.

Warren Gamaliel Harding was elected to receive the 33~ on September 22,1921 On September 20, 1921 he wrotea letter to the Sovereign Grand Commander stating that he could not be present. He was given an extenson of one year, but died before the degree was conferred. An oddity in this case is that he became an Entered Aprentice on June 28, 1901, received his Fellowcraft on August 13, 1920, & MasterMason on August 27, 1920. A short time later he became a member of a great many Masonic Bodies and visited frequently.

Ford received the 32~ in Grand Rapids, Michigan, on October 16, 1957 & the 33~ on September 26, 1962, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
