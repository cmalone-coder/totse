Waco: The Big Lie

10/17/93

This is an overview of a Christian talk show called "Cross Talk" on WVCY radio in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. It was broadcast 10/14/93 to 16 other stations across the country Via Satellite.

The program was discussing a video tape Linda Thompson got a hold of that was not totally edited by the government. The video was first fed through the satellite uplink feeds to TV stations across the country. These uplink feeds were captured by film taping services, and people with satellite dish receivers, and home video recorders.

When the Major Networks finished hacking up the video they received, the public saw an antiseptic view of what really happened.

I wanted to do an entire transcript of the conversation between Vic Aliason the host, and Linda Thompson an attorney who made a tape called "WACO THE BIG LIE", but I don't have the time. I did however give you a slight overview of the entire program.
