Waco, Texas

David Koresh and the Branch Davidians were a group of Christians in Waco, Texas, that violated the law regarding the registration of automatic weapons. In Texas it is legal to own an automatic weapon, as long as you register the weapon. Registration costs about $100. Not registering costs a lot more, as David Koresh found out.
