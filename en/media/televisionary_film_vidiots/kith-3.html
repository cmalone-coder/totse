Recurring Character List
------------------------

This section of the FAQ contains a list of characters that have appeared
in more than one episode of KITH. Each character is listed by name,
with the Kid who plays him/her in parenteses. ("Not a Kid" is listed if
the character is not played by a Kid.) A short description of the
character is listed, and an identifying quote from the character. All
of the sketches the character appears in are listed, and a season number
is listed for each sketch. The sketch names and season numbers are taken
from Part 2 of this FAQ. "Listed as" in this list refers to the name
this character is listed under in the Sketch List. If "listed as" does
not appear, the character is listed in the Sketch List with their whole
name.

This section attempts to be alphabetical. Most characters are
alphabetized under their more-commonly-known first names instead of
less-commonly known last names. Related characters (or characters that
always appear together) are listed under the name of their association.
There are tons of cross references so you shouldn't have any trouble. The
cross references also have the letter under which the real entry is
alphabetized. If you have any suggestions for alternate ways to
organize this, email davidson@mercury.interpath.net.

Some characters are listed with the spelling of their names as it appears
in the closed captioning ("CC Name"). Names from the closed-captioning
are probably not canonical, but are listed if they differ from the common
spellings.
