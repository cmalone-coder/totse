Episode/Sketch List
-------------------

NOTE: If you have a sketch list, or know of sketches that aren't in this
list, please email or post!!!

This section of the FAQ contains a list of episodes of The Kids in
the Hall, with the sketches that were on each episode. The episodes are
listed by season, and the source the episode came from is noted. The
sketches are listed as follows:

Melanie's Date (MK)(2): Melanie has a date over (Melanie)
+------------- +- + +---------------------- +------
| | | | |
+- Sketch Name | | +- Sketch Description +- List of Recurring
| +- Season Number Characters in the Sketch
+- Kids in the Sketch
B = Bruce M = Mark
S = Scott D = Dave
K = Kevin P = Paul Bellini
J = Jeff Berman (the Sixth Kid)
all = all 5 - = no Kids

Sketch descriptions also may contain an identifying quote.

If anyone has any better names (including canonical ones) for these
sketches let me know. Sketches with numbers mean that they are part
of a running bit in a show (e.g. "Hotel La Rut"). Slashes ("/") mean
that one sketch runs into the next.

Shows are listed in random order within each season.

Some of the shows may be list in the wrong season -- I think CBS used the
"Season 3" opening sequence for shows that weren't originally aired in
that season.

Shows in seasons 3 and 4 are very often edited by Comedy Central, so
they may not appear as listed here.

Some recurring sketches, for example "It's a Fact", "Whores", "Thirty Helens
Agree", and "Police Department" are not formatted as above unless it is an
unusual version of the sketch (for example, Police Department Car Chase).

* means that the sketch name is official, usually taken from the credits
of the episode or titles in the sketch itself.

(###) means the episode has been verified as being a complete episode
actually shown on the network listed.

(---) means the episode is probably an edited collection of other shows
(???) means don't know
