1) Who is Lila Feng?

Lila Feng is an attractive Asian woman, who is one of the weather
anchor-women on Canada's own "The Weather Network" cable television
station. Seen coast to coast, usually in the late evening, Lila has
attracted a cult-like following. In the words of a fan:

"I watched Lila since the day she started for the network. She was
unpolished, she was not so professional with her makeup, and she was
so nervous while giving the weathercasts that, to put it bluntly, her
saliva kept getting in the way of her words. :-) However, her shy
demeanor only added to her charm, and from that day I was smitten with
her beauty.

Since then I have watched Lila transform herself into a professional
veteran who does her job with ease. Lila is true poetry in motion.
My only regret is that she is on so late in the day. :-) "

Indeed, should The Weather Network ever decide to lay Lila off in favour
of cheaper labour, it would not surprise us if they were deluged with
hate mail demanding her immediate recall.

---===

2) What are Lila's responsibilities on The Weather Network?

At one time Lila was quite active in forecasting the weather to
viewers. However, as she is no longer seen in that role it is possible
she has been given a promotion.

Lila can now be seen in the late-hours of the day in a miriad of "desk
reporting" roles. She discusses major weather disasters, as well as
notable weather facts from previous years. One of her major
responsibilities is reporting on "Earth Watch".

In a P.R. clip shown recently, I was surprised that Lila takes an active
(major?) role in finding suitable material for "Earth Watch", actually
does the editing(!) for that segment, as well as doing her own makeup.
Indeed, everything this woman does seems to add to her fans' adolation
of her!

7) How can I increase the awareness and popularity of this group?

First of all, bear in mind that this forum is relatively new. It was
only recently that the Lila FAQ was approved for cross-posting to
news.answers. It may take some time before many sites carry this group.
However, if you wish to help increase people's awareness of this group,
perhaps one of the suggestions listed below may appeal to you:

- Word of mouth. Don't underestimate the power of word of mouth. Tell
your friends if they are fans of Lila Feng! Perhaps consider posting
something around your university or work site (if you don't mind being
labelled as being somewhat "strange"). :-)

- If you carry the alt.fan.lila-feng newsgroup on your system, perhaps
you'd consider taking part in the 'arbitron' survey compiled by Brian
Reid? Each month, the top 2840 groups are listed, including the number
of readers world-wide, the percentage of sites that carry the group, the
number of articles, etc. This information is compiled by data sent from
participating sites, purely on a volunteer basis. This is one way the
alt.fan.lila-feng group can climb higher on the ratings list, thereby
possibly gaining the notice of sites or subscribers not currently aware
of this group. I'll post this information from time to time to
alt.fan.lila-feng for your interest.

8) How old is Lila? What nationality is she? Is she single or married?

Although the answer to many of these questions is not known at this
time, I have heard from a number of people who knew Lila in the past who
have contributed the following information:

Lila is Chinese, probably 2nd generation. She was born in Nova Scotia.
Her father James, who is a lawyer, is from Shianghai and her mother Tina
is a 2nd generation Chinese Filipino from the province of Fujian. It
has been said that her mother is (was?) "a serious crab", but one might
forgive the motives of a mother with an attractive daughter. :-)

Lila is dynamic and friendly, apparently unmarried, and is about 28 years
old. It is quite likely that Lila does not know how to speak Chinese. In
secondary school Lila seemed quite talented in interpretive dance. It is
not known if she has continued with this art.

Her family name, "Feng" is the same as the Cantonese name "Fong" but her
family hails from Si Chuen province in mainland China, and her name is
in the dialect of Chinese spoken there (Mandarin?).

She grew up in a suburb of Montreal and attended elementary and secondary
school there. She attended Beaconsfield High School somewhere around
1979-1981 (give or take). From there she went on to CEGEP at Marianopolis
College, a private school, from which she graduated around 1984. She then
went on to McGill University where she received a degree in communications.

In secondary school she was in the English-only stream instead of a
billingual stream, so even though she is a "Quebecer" she may not speak
any French. However, this probably makes her even more appealing to 75%
of Canadians. :-)

Apparently, she invented a new position and got herself elected to it when
she was in Communications at McGill.

She did a lot of work producing at the local CTV affiliate, CFCF, and
may have also worked at CBMT, Canada's first television station, the local
English CBC affiliate.

There was a full-page article on Lila in the Montreal Gazette a few years
after she graduated from McGill (between 1988 and 1991). If anyone can get
access to a computerized index of Canadian Periodicals and send in the
article, perhaps it can be excerpted for inclusion in this document.

It may be possible to get a bio on Lila from The Weather Network.
Anybody got the b*lls to contact them and ask for one? ;-)

11) Are there any GIF's of Lila anywhere?

Yes, there is currently one GIF available that shows Lila in her anchor
duties, wearing a black jacket and white shirt with a floral design.

You may retrieve this file by FTP via itre.uncecs.edu. Chdir to the
/pub/satellite/images directory, and then get "lila1.gif". Make sure you
do a binary transfer.

If you're interested in what the other pictures are, use a WWW client and
this URL: http://itre.uncecs.edu/misc/images/images.html

If you choose this method, have patience, the network line there is quite
slow.

Of course, other GIF's would be appreciated, and can be posted to the
alt.fan.lila-feng newsgroup. One image that would please many would be
to see Lila in her rare (discontinued?) duty as stand-up weathercaster.
