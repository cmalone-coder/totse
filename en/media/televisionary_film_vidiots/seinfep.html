The pilot episode originally aired as "The Seinfeld Chronicles".

July 5, 1989 9:30(ET) pilot, #04-0101
Comedian Jerry Seinfeld blends his stand-up work and a sitcom format as he
plays himself, a single comedian, wondering exactly what signals he's getting
from a woman he met on the road, who wants to stay with him while she's in
town. George: Jason Alexander. Laura: Pamela Brull.
