what I hope will become a regular (monthly) production. In it, I (and those

intrest to the Hacker/Phreaker. Future issues may even include NUA's and

faciliating the the growth of the H/P community by activly particapating in
the exchange of infomation <g>. And if all that wasn't enough, you will also
have my deepest appreciation.
