REACT WANTS YOU TO KNOW HOW TO GET HELP BY C.B. RADIO
You can use CB Channel 9 for any emergency involving immediate danger to life
or roperty- accidents, fires, injuries, impaired drivers.

BROADCAST YOUR CLIP: CALL SIGN- Identify yourself and your vehicle or boat.
LOCATION- Be exact and include City and state/ province.
Injuries- Jumber and type. Is anyone trapped ?
Problem- Give details and specify what type of help is needed.

MONITORS NEED YOUR CLIP AIR IT REPEATEDLY. HERE'S HOW:

" Emergency. Any station. This is (I.D.).
I'm on (ROAD) about 4 miles east of (TOWN) in (STATE/PROVINCE). One person in
injured. Two cars have collided. Please Notify police".
Pause for a response, Then Repeat your message over and over. Monitors often
hear callers great distances away and can send help without the callers hearing
them. EVEN IF YOU CAN'T HEAR A RESPONSE, KEEP TRYING.
NOTE: On GMRS use 462/7.675MHZ.
