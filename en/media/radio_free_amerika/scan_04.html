Welcome, my friends, to the fourth issue of The Scanner's News! For
those of you who have been with us from the start: Glad to have you back.
For those of you who are new: Welcome, I hope enjoy what you find here. And
for all the readers: Please use this infomation! It is of no use to anyone
if nothing is done with it. Explore the systems, see what is out there, then
pass on what you have learned. The more YOU know, the more WE know.

What is The Scanner's News you ask? Well, grab a beer, sit back and
relax. Mad Man is going to tell you all about it. The Scanner's News is an
open forum where some of the latest scans are presented along with any info-
mation that may be of interest and/or use to the Hacker/Phreaker. Alright,
go get another beer and we'll be on our merry way.

This issue should be a pretty good one (they ALL should be pretty
good ones, if they're not, then what the fuck am I writing them for? (just
had to ask myself that)). Anyway, presented below are many, many carriers,
a large percetage of them being 800's, a few tones, an 800 exchange listing,
and a few miscellaneous numbers thrown in for good measure.

If you wish to contribute, ask questions, or learn how you too can sell
your conscience for cash, I can be contacted at the locations listed at the
end of the phile. Please note that if you do contribute, not only will your
handle be listed along with your scans, but you will have the satisfaction
of knowing that you are faciliating the the growth of the H/P community by
activly particapating in the exchange of infomation <g>.

Well, what are we waiting for? Let's get on with the scans!...
