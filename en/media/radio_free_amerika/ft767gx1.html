MARS Mod for the Yaesu FT-767GX

There is a switch about middle way of the stacked boards in the 767.
When flipped in the opposite direction of the way it comes from the
factory will give you transmitting capabilities on every freq. that
the rig receives. Pretty nifty, huh?

73 de Jerry, KK4TV @ WA4JUB.TN .

FOR 2 METERS TURN BATTERY BACK-UP OFF, (U'LL LOSE WHATS IN THERE SO WRITE
THEM DOWN) TURN RIG OFF. PRESS AND HOLD #3 KEY WHILE TURNING RIG BACK ON.
2 METERS NOW SHOULD COVER 10 MHz RANGE (140-150)

Another way of doing it...

Subject: FT-767 TRANSMIT MOD

IF YOU WOULD LIKE TO GET YOUR FT-767 TO TRANSMIT ANYWHERE THAT IT WILL RECEIVE
JUST REMOVE THE COVERS AND LOOK ON THE TOP BOARD JUST UNDER THE SHIELD
COVERING THE PC BOARD NOTICE A SMALL WHITE SWITCH IN THE CENTER OF THE RADIO.
FLIP THIS SWITCH TO THE OTHER POSITION AND IT WILL NOW TRANSMIT ANYWHERE.

ALSO IF YOU WANT THE FT-767 TO TRANSMIT OUT OF THE REGULAR 2 METERFREQUNCY
RANGE, ALL YOU HAVE TO DO IS TO REDO THE INITIALIZATION PROCESS FOR INSTALLING
THE 2 METER MODULE BUT INSTEAD OF RESETING WITH THE KEY IN THE MANUAL, POWER
UP THE RIG WHILE HOLDING THE 'OFFSET' KEY. THE RIG WILL THEN RX AND TX OUTSIDE
THE REGULAR HAM BAND ON 2.
