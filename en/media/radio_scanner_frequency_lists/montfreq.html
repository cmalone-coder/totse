Searching for wireless microphones:

The FM band is a good place to start with your scanner when
searching for wireless FM bug. An FM bug can be set to work in the dead
area between FM stations, or on the fringes.

Frequency ranges to search:

149-150MHz
165-174MHz
177-185MHz
190-199MHz
450-470MHz

Most will be in the FM mode but some are in the wide band FM mode. They
usually will only have a range of about a quarter of a mile.
