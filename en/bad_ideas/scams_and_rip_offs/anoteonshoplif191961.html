A few guidlines to help anyone out there stupid enough to rob a multi-million dollar corporation. (Ha)

Never rob from mom and pop stores, they defend their turf like they have nothing to lose, and plus, why not bring it to the man by ripping him off a few bucks?

Big stores, like Wal-Mart and K-Mart.

I know for a fact Wal-mart employees can't stop you until you leave the store, and they can't detain you over things that may or may not have happened in the bathrooms.

What's worked before: Go in with 2 people, but to anyone watching, you can't look like you know each other. One picks something up, walks around with it while browsing for about 5 minutes, goes to the bathroom. Then said person deposits it somewhere tricky in the bathroom, more or less concealed. The next person goes in, after the 1st leaves, and picks up whatever was stashed. Has worked dozens of times. If anyone suspects anything, the person stopped is the one with nothing on him. And if you remove the packaging, you can always claim it was yours.

I've talked to one of the CCTV surveilance camera people at Wal-Mart, he's my sisters friends father, and according to him, the cameras you see hanging from the roof in the store are almost all fake. He says the real ones are hidden cameras in high risk sections, monitored by 4 or 5 lazy slobs.

I know for a fact that in Walgreens, those black orb hanging cameras are fakes, only 4 are taped constantly, and no one ever watches the monitor. There are other cameras, ones even the managers don't know about, placed there by the upper echelons of management.

Most importantly: Cameras observing you will not get you caught. Employees do, they just use the cameras as proof. Number one rule, stay away from all people, even if they're wearing street clothes. Some may be people with inflated consciences, some may be undercover workers, like those used by Wal-Mart.

And please, be a smart one, and remove all of those tags with the metal wiring on them. Plastic or paper things, I first learned about them on this website, and evading security to get caught at the alarms is not worth it.

Smaller Stores, like mall shops

These are a little bit more difficult, because the employees don't always wear uniforms, and they sometimes walk around the store. I've been taught, for my service to Walgreens, that anyone who takes an unreasonably long time shopping should be watched. Don't do the "is it safe NOW...how about now..." thing. Observe your suroundings, pick a target, and go. Use a friend, let him pick up something nice that you want, and let him browse for a few minutes. Maybe even look suspicious. Again, don't let it be apparent that you know each other. Have him drop the goods in an unwatched corner, then leave. Pocket them a few minutes later, and good luck.

And another thing, running from cops is scary, I've never been caught doing anything illegal inside stores, but I've run quite a few times. Most times, if you're not an insane ninja, you're gonna get caught. Don't try running out of an already impossible situation, cuz if they catch you, they won't be leniant, and you'll face charges for sure.
