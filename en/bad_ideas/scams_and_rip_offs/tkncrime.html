I'm talking about those big machines, where you gamble your token for
tickets in those BIG machines at Pier 39. Well if you want free tickets, and
you have the guts to do this then continue to read.


1st you will need more than one person, preferably two people, one for the
left side, and one for the right side to cover you from being seen. IT CAN
BE DONE! Go to Pier 39 with your friends, and bring along a very thin piece
of plastic, or use tickets that you've recieved from the other games that
give tickets. Go to the very big machine (which is flat, and has a big arm
moving forward and reverse to move the tokens forward), the machine i'm
talking about is the one that use to be used with quarters, you stick your
quarter in and it rolls down the guide, and lands on it's surface, then the
bar pushes the coin forward. (the object of that game is to see how many
quarters you can knock down by using your quarter to take the risk of
knocking other quarters down to get more) But now they use tokens, so if you
have a thin piece of plastic, or some tickets you won from another game that
gives tickets, go to the big machine that dispenses tokens (not the changer
machine the other one, it's next to the entrance near the place where people
sing on records to get a tape of themselves, if you know what I mean.) so go
to that machine, take your thin piece of plastic or tickets, have your
friends, one standing on the left, and one standing on the right. Slide the
plastic or tickets (make sure the tickets are lined up not seperated, and
make sure it's long enough to get inside the machine and to the tokens.) on
the side of the bar where you put the tokens in. If you look closely on the
side of the bar which you move to the left, and right, notice on the side
there is enough room on the corners to slide a thin object in. The best
machine (there are four levers.) go to the one on the left, when your facing
the man where you redeem the tickets for the prize, not that stupid ball
game with those wooden balls. The one on the left has more leverage in
getting a ticket or plastic. We used the tickets, cause that's all I had
when I thought about that little gap on the side of the lever. So I slid the
tickets in, and presto, I could move my tickets in and out of the side of
the lever. I told a friend that I just figured out how to manipulate it, and
the person was exstatic about the finding, so she slid her tickets in, and
she had the skill to move the tokens off the side, and some on top to knock
them down and get more tickets, you about 5 tickets per token you knock
down. So the tickets were like rolling all over the floor, people were
watching (other students from some prom or something) and I was laughing
hesterically, cause I couldnt believe it, tokens were falling, and we were
getting tickets, all because of a flaw in workmanship.. haha I was looking
around on the machine, cause I noticed if you put anything else besides a
token in it, it will reject, so I looked around more, and looked on the side
of where you put the tokens in (the side of the bar). It has a little gap on
the side if you look closely. You would probably get a lot more with a study
piece of plastic, and slide that in. So if your into prizes at Pier 39, and
need more tickets, this one's for YOU!

P.S. If your new to Pier 39, or just confused about where this machine is,
