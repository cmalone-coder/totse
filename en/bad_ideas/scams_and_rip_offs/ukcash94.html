THEORY
------

The method that I am going to explain will only work on the type of ATM which

ATMs which pass you your money through rollers or something else are immune to
this little scam. I have found that the machines to look for are building

Once a withdrawl has been requested and authorised on one of these machines, themoney is moved into withdrawl tray, and a check is made to see if the tray

customer doesn't get a surprise bonus of the previous guy's cash. This check isdone very late in thocess, so if the tray is not empty, the cash has alreadybeen removed from the safe and has to be lcdin the closed withdrawl tray.

Once you have found a machine, you should find out the times when the immediate

Now what you have to do is open the withdrawl drawer enough to put in a foreign

paper should do it, but a Mars bar wrapper or anything should also work. To getthe drawer open you use a strong flathead screwdriver, a piece of metal or acrowbar. Some machines are tougher than tesso you will have to experiment.
Remember not to damage the drawer more than is necessary to open it.

Get your screwdriver again, and re-open the withdrawl draw. With a bit of luck
you shouldn't have too much trouble as you will have already weakened it.

Inside should now be any amount of money from 10 to 250 quid depending on how
