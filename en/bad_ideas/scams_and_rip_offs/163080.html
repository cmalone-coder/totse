How to Blackmail Someone

The difficult thing about blackmail is how to get the money in a safe way. I have found one. It works for sure.

I am not talking about big blackmails with kidnapping the child of a VIP and asking for a million bucks or so. This article is about a small "daily" blackmail with an ammount of, let's say, a few thousand bucks at maximum.

First you need a subject. There shouldn't be any problem. You know for sure a man who betrays his wife or the other way around. Your subject should have done something which would be embarrassing if people would know about it. Most of the people are gladly willing to pay you a few thousand bucks to get away with nobody knowing. If you find such a subject, the blackmail is a piece of cake.

But there is always the slight possibility that the subject doesn't care about his reputation or his marriage and goes to the police. If they are looking for you, the handing over of the money is becoming a tough task. But I have found the perfect way.

But let me tell you first that you have to work carefully when you prepare the whole thing. No finger prints on the paper and all that. Otherwise it won't work. Because if there IS some police, they will get you first – but you will get away if there is nothing which links you to the crime apart of being at the wrong place to the wrong time.

Now. Write to the subject and tell him to get the ammount in big banknotes, so that there are not many notes. Tell him to go into a music store and stuff the banknotes into the booklet of a CD. It shouldn't be a problem as we talk about 1000 or 2000 bucks. The subject has to choose a certain CD you have named him before. It has to be an unknown artist, just some crap. Now you go into the store and buy this CD. It only works in stores where the booklet is already in the CD, not the ones where you get the original booklet when you buy a CD. Of course it could be something different, let's say a book or anything where your subject could hide the money.

So what's the point about it? You think if there IS any police in the store they will get you as soon as you want to get the CD or the book?

Ok, listen to this. Let's say the police is waiting in the store. They wait for a person who buys this CD because this has to be the blackmailer. But they are wrong about that. There is no way on earth how they could ever prove that you are not just an unlucky guy who wanted to buy this certain CD. Of course everybody would know that you are the bad guy – but they have to prove it. You don't have to prove that you are innocent. At least that's the way it works in Europe.

Now, it's very likely that there is no police at all and you will get away with it comfortably. The worst thing which could happen is that there is some police and they arrest you and take you to the station. But a few hours later you will be out and there will be no prosecutor who tries to get you because the chances of a conviction are just too slim.

All the best Zapata
