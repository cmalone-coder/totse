I read a short story on how to recycle paper, so I was trying different stuff in my blender and I came up with a fairly close substitute for money, I reccomend you try out different combinations because I only did one.. before my mom came home = D.

Materials

  • Cotton balls
  • Toilet paper
  • paper, preferably white
  • talcum powder
  • water
  • tools - a blender

First, put how ever much of each ingredient you'd like, I told you to experiment, even try denim bits, because denim is in our currency.. once you have your stuff, put it in the blender, make sure its all ripped up into small pieces, and fill it with about 40% water. Next, blend on liquify or something powerful for about 10 minutes...

Next, pour onto a sheet of foil or something that it wont stick to, and flatten with a rolling pin, then use a blow dry to dry quickly, when its solid and you can toss it around without it breaking, but still moist, toss it in the dry with some talcum powder for about 3 minutes.. Take it out, and cut it into the paper you wish to print on, this solves one of the biggest problems in counterfitting, paper.

Next do your photo and copying work, and when your all done, do the baby powder in the dryer thing again, beat it up, iron it, and repeat.. after a while you should have a pretty nice bill.
