For us. For you. Or for your insurance company. Why a stateoftheart
measuring system is so important to your modern unibody car. Unibody
cars are engineered to absorb shock and transfer it away from the
passenger compartment. That's good for passengers. It saves lives. But
it means damage inflicted to one part of the car may show up in
another. For example, damage to the right front fender will sometimes
affect the suspension of the left rear wheel. and that, in turn, unless
the damage is located, can cause tire wear, handling, and even car
control problems. Unless the shop restoring your car has a
stateoftheart measuring system that can locate all the hidden damage,
you'll miss the peace of mind of knowing your car has been returned to

Collision repair was once simple. Just pull, pound, and paint.

But that was before imports and unibodies. Now, even a slight accident
can throw the whole car out of line--tracking out of sync- and steering
out of kilter.

That's because with unitized carbody construction, there's no heavy,
cross-member frame, with a body added. The body and frame are one
piece--a single, lightweight shell that can be distorted by even a
minor collision.

As a result, your unitized car has to have new, more advanced, more
sophisticated and more precise repair techniques than older model frame
cars. You simply can't afford to risk putting your damaged car into the
hands of a body shop not trained in new, high strength steels--advanced
welding techniquesstateoftheart measuring.

You can put your confidence in our skilled and experienced collision
repair technicians. They use the most up-todate equipment available.
And their skills are constantly being upgraded through ongoing, handson
training.
