At first all we were using was my computer speakers and two cordless phones, one held up to the speaker the other to my ear. At first it was kind of hard to make the calls because we were cracking up so much.

Foreigners are some of the funniest because they just get confused. Ony one or two people actually figured out it was an arnold recording.

The soundboard I downloaded was pretty limited so on saturday I collected more wavs from different websites. I began using winamp and the soundboard, so I could pick all the correct responses so it sounded real. Then I finally got a damn speakerphone from my fathers office and we could all hear it.

Then we started calling people in my brothers phone list.

We called an entire house full of drunk people and they thought it was the funniest shit. At first they thought it was someone doing the voice of arnold then they realized it was a computer. But they were still amazed that if they asked a question I had a good response. Then this dude Ryan comes over and we call his house, his dad awnsers and we go through the routine, usuall beginning phrases and we could hear him laughing and then he said he had another call and switched over, we didnt want to stick around for that so we hung up, then Ryan gave us his dads cellphone number and we called that. We could hear him laughing but then he hung up. We made some more calls and then went to sleep.

Then the cops told them that they recieved several complaints and had started a file. But the thing that I thought was wierd is I didnt talk to a single person who sounded pissed off.
