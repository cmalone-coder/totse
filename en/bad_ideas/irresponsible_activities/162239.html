First pick a car, this is simple, use common sense, looking at the car can tell you many things about it and the owner, first and foremost if you look in the window. You can check things like whether it is an automatic (very important, I can't drive manuals, so I always take automatics, unless I bring a friend(very helpful, a friend can be a lookout, a cover and an alibi all in one) who can drive manuals). If you can drive manuals, take them, because they're less likely to have an alarm. You can also check whether a car has an alarm by checking whether it has a flashing light on or near the dash, if it does have a flashing light, forget it. Alarms are too hard to bypass, and even if the car's a Ferrari, its not worth the chance of some beefy bloke running over and smashing your face in (I'm not a pussy, I'm just smart). Although I will inform you that there are some interesting devices hanging around the market, that can quickly scan through the codes it has in its database for the model of alarm you have chosen. Of course you need to know the type of alarm first, but this device can sometimes disable the alarm and even open the car in less than five minutes. You can check the type of person the car belongs to by looking at the seat, if the seat is pulled back and has a big indent where the person sits its either a fat bitches car or a big beefy blokes car. Use your common sense, like I said, and you'll be fine.

Second get in the car. This can be done a number of ways, if you don't intend to sell the car just hammer a large FLATHEAD screwdriver in the keyhole and turn HARD (this SHOULD break the pins and allow you to turn the chamber which opens the car). I don't believe this method would work with newer model cars though as they're electronic (then again I know jack shit about how cars work). You can also use a lock-out tool kit, or a Slim-Jim. Slim-Jim's are easy to use, lubricate them with Vaseline or hand crème and insert them into the weather stripping, then push and pull up, or pull and pull up. With a Slim-Jim it helps to have a lever like a piece of dowel when you have to push (try pushing and pulling because different manufacturers put their mechanisms in different places. Another way is to take a screwdriver to the quarter-glass, just put it inside the weather stripping and lever it to the side, this will either pop the window out or smash it, again its helpful to have a bit of dowel to lever on. You don't have to be Einstein to realize that removing the quarter-glass will allow you to unlock the door and in turn unlock the front door. Another way I've heard of but never discovered if it works or not is getting a spark plug and breaking the ceramics off, get a small piece of this (about the size of a pea) the throw it at a window, apparently it literally EXPLODES the window. The file I read this in said it is because the two components are oppositely charged. Again I do not know whether this works or not but I guess its worth a try. Ok I've covered Slim-Jims, forcing the lock, breaking in and miscellaneous shit. I don't think there's anything else so I'll go on to raiding.

Third in respective order is raiding, you'll want to check the glove compartment, the dash, the radio, center console, and under the seats, oh yeah, also the boot, once your in the car just pop the boot and have a look. Check quickly if you dont have much time, (or if youre stupid enough to do it during the day), wear gloves and dont accidentally honk the horn (I know someone who has been caught because of the horn).

Fourth, Start the car. For old cars look under the dash or cut into it, and near the ignition find two red wires, cut them, splice them and cross them ( a good idea is to wear gloves as you can get zapped). However this only works for old cars, newer cars have a lock mechanism that doesnt let you turn the wheel too far without the ignition switched, there's a drill bit you can get for about $150 but I have no idea where to get it from (probably Auto Collectors). If you picked an early model Holden, your in luck, these have a little wing kind of thing around the ignition, to start these all you need to do is get a shifter and shift the switch towards the dash, this starts the car. Also you can use the first method described to open the door, to turn the ignition. There are other ways like buying manufacturers keys, which are masters for a certain make and model of car, these can be purchased for about $150 upwards. Or you could use a Slide Hammer. I know this works but I cant describe it, because Ive never used one. From what I know screw the hammer into the ignition and pop it out with the slide hammer, then stick a screwdriver in there and turn the little brass triangle and it will start the car. Another way is to pop the ignition keyhole off by using a screwdriver and prying between the plastic and the metal, then stick your screwdriver in and turn. But the way i do it is I get a scrap piece of metal thin and strong enough to fit in the keyhole and stick it in bend it down and turn hard. Also a device called The Club may be installed on your target vehicle (Warning: I have never heard of this method before but I believe it may work, although it is definitely dangerous). A solution I have heard of is to buy a flask of Liquid Nitrogen and pour it onto The Club (making sure not to spill it on yourself) then hit The Club with a very heavy object (like your shifter). This should snap it in half. That is all I have on starting the car so I will go on to conversions and selling.

While you are at it re-spray it, and change the plates and VINs. I am sorry but I know nothing about VINs so I cannot give you any Info.
