Liquid Oxidizer

A liquid oxidizer for explosives, comprising nitric acid, ammonium nitrate and water.

The idea is to balance those factors and intended results so that the liquid oxidizer can be mixed with other materials to form explosive and achieve the desired results, and to provide required (good) oxidizers for explosives. These and other objects are achieved by providing solution comprising of nitric acid, ammonium nitrate, and water. The liquid oxidizers by this method are solutions of nitric acid, ammonium nitrate, and water.

This oxidizer comprises of nitric acid, ammonium nitrate, and water in the proportions give below.

The nitric acid comprises preferably from about 30 to about 95 and in case of more it can be from 50 to 81 percent of the liquid oxidizer as per use. Nitric acid used in this method can be from 90-100% concentrated; this can also be changed as per for the use of oxidizer.

The ammonium nitrate constitutes from 5 to 40 and more can be from 10 to 30 wt percent of the liquid oxidizer.

The water comprises from about 8 to about 30 by weight percent of the liquid oxidizer. The water's salt content would also effect the composition as it may contain other unwanted salts, this may be solved by using clean and fresh water and if possible the water can also be filtered and distillated.
