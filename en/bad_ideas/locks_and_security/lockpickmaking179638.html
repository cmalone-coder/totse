So everybody wants to learn how to be shifty and dangerous and be able to do what they want, and break into wherever the hell they want. But not everybody has access to a nice set of picks because dropping 30-60$ is out of the question and/or they cant find, or get them shipped to where they are. This is by far the easiest method of making high quality, very strong picks using only a few items. They are as follows:

<ul> <li>A dremel rotary tool with accessory heads: Cut off wheel (look like small brown discs, these are the most important heads) A grinding wheel, a metal polishing wheel (looks like e bunch of metal fibers in a circle) and finally a cotton/soft meterial polishing wheel. <li>A pack or two of hacksaw blades, they tend to come in packs of three (yes this is what you make your picks out of!!) <li>Electrical tape <li>Some sort of vise or clamp (hold the hacksaw blade on a workbench or other area) <li>Safety Glasses (you will want these, metal in the eyes is very uncomfotable) </ul>

Procedure:

<ol> <li>After you have obtained all of your materials find a well ventilated place, or just go outside and set up shop. <li>Cut one of the blades in half with the dremel and vise it to something or just secure it down in any manner (but make sure you have it held down VERY hard) <li>Put on your cutting head on the dremel and begin by cutting a rough shape like the below picture, note how there is a chunk at the end, note that you should try to round the inside edgeof the pick to make it smoother. You may want a lock near you so you can see how thin you want the neck of the pick to be. <pre> {{{{{{{{{\ { } | \ { }--chunk at end | ------------------ } | ) |---------------------------------- </pre> <li>Use the grinding wheel to smooth the inside ede of the pick and the outside handle area. <li>Now you are going to make the actual pick end and you should put some thought as to how you want it. Do you want a rake? Single pick? You can just freehand if you want to also. Here are some ideas. <a href="http://www.lock-picks.com/images/16big.jpg">http://www.lock-picks.com/images/16big.jpg</a> <li>Once you get your design in you just use the rough metal wheel to smoothe the larger burrs and then the cotton/clothy one so make it VERY smooth, you want it very smooth or when you go to practice, you'll destroy the inside of the lock. <li>To finish off wrap the pick in electrical tape to get a better grip or your hands will hurt like hell after a few minutes and when they start sweating. <li>ENJOY!!!!! </ol>

NOTE: I did not include how to make a torque tool but one can be fashioned from myriad sources, like allen/hex wrenches and very small screwdrivers. But you need to figure that out on your own.
