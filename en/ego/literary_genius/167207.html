Life Is Not A Struggle But A Blessing

by Adeel Ansari

Many Nights I've prayed with no prove, anyone could fulfill
Still my hopes kept dragging me, but never could fulfill
But now I'm standing here, and there's no one that can make me frail
For I am a feral Swan that quickly flew away



There ain’t no peers in my way, unlike the pathos that protrude my way
How I quench to stray away to quickly join my way
And when that time will come
Those peers shall be taciturn
As they can never quite fulfill, the zeal that you have proved
Still my heart kept fluttering away as if a pigeon descended from above



But it is time now for you to decide
Which path shall make your way
And when you seek that path
For struggle and sweat in the way
Take my blessings that you won’t fail
For your struggle had quite prevail



And as one day it will be
The waves will turn towards your way
Still that hope to be the one that you quite never predict



And it will come to you, as sworn
That it be a symbol of Faith
Let it be forsworn to you that it won’t come over your faith
For it is like a wilted rose that somehow attained Life for now



But now you look at it above
That this was not just a game
For only in the Earthly world, that your patience will not be apprehended,



Don’t take Life as a gore
As it will consume you whole
With your faith and tolerance
No one shall dare to cast you away
For God shall never abandon you 
Don’t quit your mission half way
For do it with your might



And one day it shall be proved that you’re not that unworthy I thought you were,
For opportunity doesn’t come to you, have to snatch it for you to say
"I will do it, Who are you to crush me in the way"
