Ramblings underlining the basic phillosophies of M

Waxen Philosophic With Michael


People these days are so arrogant.
Everything has to be centered around reality.
Everyone must subscribe to this religion called reality.
Along with science and only tightly defined fact through
experimentation which we pay an insignificant factor of along with
an unfathomably large faction of semi-individuals who pay
insignificant fraction composing the whole.
Anything is nothing if it is undeniably true.
Everyone must know the truth.
No one is worthful unless they are educated and enlightened to the
truths of the people who percieve the majority of people who don't
know their truth.
You wanna know the truth?
The truth is that you are insignificant.
The truth is no one cares if you live or die.
The truth is you are everybody else.
The truth is it's futile to be otherwise.
The truth is it's wrong to live in your own little world to escape
the excessive lack of autonomy which strains every day of your
frayed consciousness to push you in it.
The truth is that right now you are under a lot of stress.
The truth is you either don't understand or don't care and I don't
care if you don't care and I don't know why I don't care or you
don't care or I don't know but i'm fine with that because I don't
really care.
The truth is there is no truth and the correlation coefficient
won't equal one no matter how hard you try because there will
always be that one point that's just way off the line.
The truth is that every time you dig sand from a whole, more sand
falls in.
The truth is you are lied to and fed mass-produced prepackaged mush
to keep you under control twenty four hours a day, 80 years a
lifetime.
The truth is that there is no spirituality.
The truth often times contradicts itself and there's nothing you
can do about it because you are an insignificant statistic just
like everyone else.
The truth is you're fooling yourself.
The truth is that we need a revolution.
The truth lies in the lies of my religion.
The truth is that in my religion everyone gets into Heaven their
own way and in my religion God is a play, not to control, but to be
acted out because in my religion you are God and I am God because
in my religion the search for our hidden omnipotence is immortal
and in my religion people are judged by their minds and experience
and in my religion everything is okay and that in that way
everything is doable and must be done.
In my religion there is truth and meaning in everything and
everyone and all you percieve which defines your undefinable
imagination which in and of itself is nothing more than another
incomprehensible imaginary fragment of your infinitely vast
imaginary universe hiding many other universes to compile an entity
called you and called existence and called perception and called
everything and it is your very own personal everything that you
unwittingly created from a void of nothingness which is really just
a place holder holding a generic reality which simply exists.
In my religion everything is real and everything is true and you
are wrong and in my religion the only religious scriptures are the
ones you create and hold in your mind and in my religion the only
sin is sin itself and no one can make you anything while you still
have wisdom you utilize your restless contentment to the point of
deity.
Look beyond reality and see the bubbles of floating energy and see
and the love and hate all swirly in a sea of gray and hear all the
voices in your head and listen to them and talk back to them and
create and live and die and fear nothing and do everything and be
significant because the most efficient way to be is in the place
you call dream and some of the phychodelic fairy tales may not
agree but keep on asking and questioning and breaking through your
reality into the next and then to the next and then to the next and
find a home in each and open the doors of perception, dirty or not,
while everything you don't want turns into shades of gray.
The truth is you are an insignificant statistic, and can do
anything.

-Michal D. Merrick
