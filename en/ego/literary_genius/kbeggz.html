---[4]--

How should we begin a discussion as deep as this? And without
firm ground to stand upon, how would one excite oneself to action?
There is no answer to the sparrow's question! Then we must conspire to
limit our questions.
As a manual on interrogation and brainwashing techniques 'Green
eggs and ham' is very informative. One of the 1st things one notices is
the incredible amount ov time that the interrogator spends in trying to
produce the desired effect, which seems to indicate that the appropriate
amount ov time is '_as_much_time_as_is_needed_' to produce the desired
effect. The amount ov time ov which i speak is indicated by the symbols
ov the car, train and boat. These speak ov a journey or more indirectly
ov a great period ov time that a journey would take. Three forms of
transportation are included as symbols, which seems to suggest not only
a long time but more so, a _long_ time.
Let the reader observe that the main character, which is not
named (Here let the reader note that most serial killers as well as
interrogators do not want or like to know the victims name.), is very
decided about not liking green eggs and ham! He knows this. Yet the
interrogator (aka. Sam-I-Am) assumes that he has never tried green eggs
and ham, and ignores the victims pleas for a pardon from this discussion.
The interrogator takes on many guises...as a mouse, the interrogator
takes on the face ov weakness and harmlessness, as a fox the interrogator
has taken on the face ov slyness and cunning, and then as a goat the face
ov pure evil to break all men. And round the corner the main character is
broken....proudly and happily announces that he loves green eggs and
ham!?! This is a complete 180 degree turn! In other words if you keep
working on someone long enough constantly running them in circles and
harassing them, keeping them awake for days then anyone would confess
to anything.

As a warning on impending alien invasion green eggs and ham is a
very urgent warning for anyone listening...they are here and they are inside
us! These are parasitic creatures who spread from host to host by way ov
cattle mutilations, infecting chickens who lay green eggs and infecting
pigs with green blood (which makes there meat green). These aliens have been
breeding these chicken and pig hybrids for years. First they infect the pigs
and chickens, then they breed them over the mutilated cattle. And the
parasitic alien is introduced into the host by way ov the host eating
the green eggs and ham.
They are watching us and know our secret numbers and names. Their
agents are among us. Watching thorough the cable-TV box and barcodes in the
grocery store...mapping every move. We are food to them, nothing more than
cattle. We are being harvested! Wake up!
We sit and chew our cud unaware of _their_ intentions...like the
cattle we are. The message is clear _sleep_consume_reproduce_! And why do
you think that Christianity is _the_ biggest most widely accepted religion?
Because it says to have blind faith (SLEEP). It says that homosexuality is
wrong and abortion is wrong and birth control is wrong (REPRODUCE). Two out
ov three isn't bad. Capitalism, 'fair trade & competition', takes care ov
the rest (CONSUME). They watch us and...tagged we are cataloged. They
want us to sleep and consume...fattening us up, we are nothing but
sleeping cattle!
We are sleeping-cattle and slave labor! The time has come to
awaken is the message of 'Green eggs and ham'! AWAKEN!

There is only a small bit ov evidence concerning the formations
of crop circles, and their origins. Aliens? The Illuminati? As it
turns out the mathematics ov the Egyptian pyramids and the mathematics ov
crop circles are very similar in nature. Most can be decoded into a
strange symbolic alphabet. Yet beyond that not much is know.
This crop circle was a very large one. Asymmetrical with many
lines and only three circles. Many small geometrical shapes run
the circumference ov the largest circle, three large lines connect
the largest circle to the smallest circle. As most are circles it an
interesting to note that the middle sized circle was really a square
with 12 small circles on the interior. The square was the only form not
connected to the whole design, yet two lines ran perpendicular from the
center ov the square ending outside the square.
The only thing ov any importance concerning the relationship
ov this crop circle to 'Green eggs and ham' is one letter addressed to
the author's wife and one photograph ov the crop circle found among his
papers after he died.
