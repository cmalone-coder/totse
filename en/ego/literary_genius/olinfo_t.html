Oldenburg Info

Oldenburg can be found in the North-West of Germany, West
Germany. It is located about 150 km west of Hamburg and about 45
km west of Bremen.
At the border of Ostfriesland there is a little nice
town with about 150.000 inhabitants. Oldenburg is the
capital of Weser Ems County. Thus we have a
regional government and some other official bueraus here.
The university of Oldenburg is one of the youngest univer-
sities in Germany. It exists since 1971. There are also
a lot of new buildings, and a lot of very good computers.
We have a System Cadmus from PCS Germany, build
on the 680x0 processor, a HP 9000 system, both running under
UNIX operating system, a Siemens 7890c (Fuji IBM 3081K remake), running
under IBM VM/CMS and a lot of PCs, ATs, Macs ... Oldenburg itself
is a very big city, because you can find mostly buildings
for one or two families.
Oldenburg is located via mail by D-2900, calling by
telephon needs the numbers +49 441 , the university can
be called by
+49 441 798-0

The address is Universitaet Oldenburg
Ammerlaender Heerstr. 112-118
D-2900 Oldenburg
