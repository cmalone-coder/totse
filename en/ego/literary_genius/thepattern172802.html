The Pattern

by Mojo Hojo

A few months ago I watched some random things happen and thought about how they are related which got me thinking. This is my pattern theory and the billard effect I noticed.

I will begin by telling where it started and then explain what I mean and how it works.

It started with the theorized "Big Bang" which would be an explosion in the void releasing many particles in all directions smashing and bouncing against each other at high speed generating heat, radiation, and combining to make larger molecules which also will be smashing and bouncing.

This goes on for a long time causing all that stuff you learned in science class leading to us sitting on our blue rock. All the smashing and bouncing is still going on but it now smashes and bounces on different scales.

The explanation.

This is the billard effect which is like the game itself where if one ball hits another ball at a certain speed it will send the other ball moving in a different direction at a certain speed. The only rules are physics. Such is with the paricles and everything that came after the "Big Bang".

(I am unsure how to continue so I will just say it with an explanation)

This theory affects people and EVERYTHING they do, think, and plan on doing. An example:

Mr.Joe is sitting in swivel chair. There is a lack of nutrients in his body. His brain releases chemicals that come with hunger. His neurons fire off and the stored memory of kitchen=fridge=sandwich=nutrients relays to the neurons that he must go to the kitchen in which his neurons fire to make his nerves control his legs (As the stored memory of becoming bipedal during childhood dictates) which in turn move his body mass to the kitchen to take out the sandwhich, eat, and swallon all based on the lack of food. Run on sentace but hopefully shows what I'm trying to say.

Mr.Joe was affected by the pattern since his father was. It's was all predetermined before the big bag that his father would go through life controlled by physics which lead to Mr.Joe's mother (who was also controlled by the rules of physics) crossing paths with the father which lead to them screwing and Mr.Joe being born and the chemicals in the father and mother telling them to raise their son which set the smashing and bouncing of particles in Mr.Joe's head which lead him to have memories, habits, and a dictated thinking process. In the end all that lead to him having the sandwich. With the sandwich's nutrients in him the chemicals may signal him to interact with somebody else who will also be contantly affected by the pattern.

This is all hard to comprehend at once and I spent a day swearing about how damn annoying it is. This theory takes away the theory of free will because time is contant in movement and otherwise is only an interpretation of time speeding up or slowing down because of the particle movement speed in you which are governed by unchangable physics. You go forward in time but that will be because your particles slow down while the world around you continues to move forward. Going back in time is a bit trickier or impossible because of the many paradoxes (though the universe splitting is possible although inpractical).

The events that lead to you logging on to Totse (where I wrote this) and reading this articles were all predetermined and based on your predetermined thought process what you will do will be part of the pattern. I freaked out and spend my day thinking about just how deep this goes.

Free will is just the more advanced and complex chemical reaction in our main control center (brain) which gives us self awareness (or the illusion of it) while animals have simpler reactions in their brains. Physics is like a huge sheet of paper that has all the information and rules on it and the things we don't understand (The paranormal) are like black areas (like the fog of war in an unexplored map in a stratagy game) which can and should be solved in a algebraic type way based on the things around them (if there is smoke there must be a fire (Though not literaly)).

During a fight (hell something happened that lead to the fight and something happened that lead to the people that are fighting to exist) Person one has the thought or reaction to do a right hook, now person two will either dodge it if his brain can process and fire the signals fast enough or get hit which will quickly depress his tissue sending the sensation of pain. In the end the victor (though insignificant on the larger scale) will be the one who had the "right" events happen to him/her which where all used in the last nanosecond of the fight and that fight will be stored in the neurons and will affect him in many (though subtle) ways during his life and his life will affect others lives.
