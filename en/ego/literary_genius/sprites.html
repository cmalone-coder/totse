Artificial Sprites

I am surrounded by artificial sprites.
They love me like no one will.
They take care of me when the Earth falls down, and I know I can
eat them when it's over.
Sunlight will do make them die.
I'll miss the artificial sprites.
I saw one chewing on a thing in the sea of peas.
The fairy blue crushes me like a window being crushed by someone
too week to crush a window.
Delusioned idiocy is happy.
I love the artificial sprites.
My artificial sprites will never eat me or call me names, no matter
if I cry.
I cry sometimes.
I see things that are there.
They see that I am looking at them sometimes.
They don't look though.
They let me see things.
I watch my artificial sprites sometimes.
I stepped on an artificial sprite once.
He went smoosh.
I don't like grapefruit like that.
I like artificial grapefruit;
The kind that goes crunchy candy.
Boom.
Pop goes the artificial sprite.
I like when they do that.
A pilgrim ate the artificial sprite once.
I killed him.
His bloody body was funny nutty.
Not like a sprite though.
He was red.
I don't like when bloody body is red.
Scary things try to eat me sometimes.
My artificial sprites don't let me see things sometimes.
They don't let me do anything.
I hate your artificial sprites.
They can make cow corn.
They have magic.
I don't know why they never talk ever.
They talk to me sometimes though.
We talk about things except when bad people come.
I don't like bad people.
They make my artificial sprites go away.
Have you seen a big scary monster?
I do sometimes.
I ate a picture pill again.
My artificial sprites let me have special candy sometimes.
It's crunch candy sometimes.
It makes me fly sometime.
I'm not supposed to eat not good food, but they let me sometimes.
I love my artificial sprites.
