Slasher book bibliography

I came up with this while learning to use a database program.
It's a bibliography of what are euphemistically called "true
crime" books--that is books about serial killers, mass murderers
and so on. I've read most of these books and I've added some
comments about them.

TED BUNDY

If not the top serial killer, Ted is one of the best known
and best loved ones.

?; The Phantom Prince
She lived with Bundy.

Larsen, Richard W.; Bundy: The Deliberate Stranger

Michaud, Steven and Aynesworth, Hugh; The Only Living Witness

Rule, Anne; The Stranger Beside Me
She knew Bundy.

Winn, Steven and Merrill, David; The Killer Next Door

JACK THE RIPPER

What can you say about Jack. A legend in his own, and
any other, time.

Begg, Paul; Jack the Ripper--The Uncensored Facts
Not exciting but more accurate than others.

Harrison, Michael; Clarence
A theory. Ho hum.

Knight, Stephen; Jack the Ripper--The Final Solution
Blather.

Rumbelow, Donald; The Complete Jack the Ripper

Spiering, Frank; Prince Jack
Another theory. Ho ho hum hum.

MANSON FAMILY

Charlie gets a lot more credit than he deserves, so he
must have something going for him.

Atkins, Susan; Child of Satan, Child of God
Not very trustworthy. She's much more interesting as a child
of Satan. More of the book is about that.

Bishop, George Vincent; Witness to Evil
A waste of time.

Bugliosi, Vincent and Gentry, Curt; Helter Skelter

Emmons, Nuel; Manson in His Own Words
The Holy writings.

Gilmore, John and Kenner, Ron; The Garbage People
Has some stuff on Manson's background that others don't have.

Livsey, Clara; The Manson Women
The author is a terminal normal. Too bad, it could have been
an interesting book.

Sanders, Ed; The Family
The best Manson book.

Schreck, Nikolas; The Manson File

Watson, Charles (Tex); Will You Die For Me?
Probably fairly reliable, even though Watson became a Christer.

Zaehner, R. C.; Our Savage God
Only partly about Manson. Dumb.

CULTS

Humes, Edward; Buried Secrets
About the Matamoros killings.

Kahaner, Larry; Cults That Kill
Credulous dork.

Linedecker, Clifford; Hell Ranch
More Matamoros stuff.

Raschke, Carl; Paint it Black
This guy is a jerk.

Terry, Maury; Ultimate Evil
Maybe you believe it. He ties Son of Sam with Manson and a
ring of Satanists from North Dakota.

SERIAL KILLERS

Baldwin, James; The Evidence of Things Not Seen
A well-written but pointless essay about the Atlanta child-
killer.

Biondi, Ray and Hecox, Walter; All His Father's Sins

Blackburn, Daniel J.; Human Harvest

Burn, Gordon; Somebody's Husband, Somebody's Son
The Ripper tradition lives on in the Yorkshire Ripper

Cahill, Tim; Buried Dreams
About John Wayne Gacy.

Cameron, Deborah and Frazer, Elizabeth; Lust to Kill
Basically feminist bullshit but some is okay.

Caputi, Jane; The Age of Sex Crime
Feminist bullshit.

Carpozi, George Jr.; Son of Sam: The .44 Caliber Killer

Cook, Thomas H.; Early Graves

Dettlinger, Chet; The List

Egginton, Joyce; From Cradle to Grave
Dead babies.

Elkind, Peter; Death Shift
More dead babies.

Gaddis, Thomas E. and Long, James O.; Killer: A Journal of Murder

Graysmith, Robert; The Sleeping Lady
Not as good as his book about Zodiac.

Graysmith, Robert; Zodiac
The one and only.

Howard, Clark; Zebra
Black Muslims get crazy.

Kolarik, Gera-Lind and Klatt, Wayne; Freed to Kill

Levin, Jack and Fox, James Alan; Mass Murder: America's Growing Menace
Light-duty

Leyton, Elliot; Hunting Humans
I don't buy his theory.

Linedecker, Clifford; The Man Who Killed Boys
Gacy again.

McDougal, Dennis; Angel of Darkness
Randy Kraft--possibly the record body count.

Norris, Joel; Serial Killers
I don't buy his theory.

O'Brien, Darcy; Two of a Kind
Hot stuff! The Hillside Stranglers.

Reed, Dan and Moore, Kelly; Deadly Medicine
Yet more dead babies.

Reynolds, Richard B.; Cry For War
This pair is truly nuts.

Rule, Anne (Andy Stack); Lust Killer

Rule, Anne (Andy Stack); The I-5 Killer

Rule, Anne (Andy Stack); The Want-Ad Killer

Sullivan, Terry and Maiken, Peter T.; Killer Clown
They try to excuse it taking so long to catch Gacy

Van Hoffman, Eric; A Venom in the Blood

Wambaugh, Joseph; The Blooding
This one's great

MISCELLANEOUS

Alexander, Shana; Nutcracker
One messed up family.

Bledsoe, Jerry; Bitter Blood
What a pair!

Bradlee, Ben Jr. and Van Atta, Dale; Prophet of Blood
Ervil Le Baron--the Mormon Manson.

Carpenter, Teresa; Missing Beauty

Irving, Clifford; Daddy's Girl
I don't buy Irving's explanation.

James, P. D. and Critchley, T. A.; The Maul and the Pear Tree

Levy, Steven; The Unicorn's Secret
Live with a corpse for a year?

Lindsey, Robert; A Gathering of Saints

McGinniss, Joe; Blind Faith
Apt title.

McGinniss, Joe; Fatal Vision

McKernan, Maureen; The Amazing Crime Trial of Leopold and Loeb
The two and only.

Naifeh, Steven and Smith, Gregory White; The Mormon Murders

Olsen, Jack; Cold Kill

Olsen, Jack; Predator

Olsen, Jack; Son: A Psychopath and His Victims
Mom got into the act too.

Rule, Anne; Small Sacrifices

Schreiber, Flora Rheta; Shoemaker: The Anatomy of a Psychotic

Schwartz-Nobel, Loretta; Engaged to Murder
She shows the appeal of the psychopath.

Sillitoe, Linda and Roberts, Allen D.; Salamander

St. Clair, David; Say You Love Satan
White punks on dope murder. Big deal.

Thernstrom, Melanie; The Dead Girl
Over-sensitive, uninformative dreck.

Wambaugh, Joseph; Echoes in the Darkness
Good!

Wolfe, Linda; Wasted
The Preppie Killing.
