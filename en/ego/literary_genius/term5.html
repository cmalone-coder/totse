"The Terminator: Rewritten V \ Judgment Day"

Written by David Minter.

Finished 8-15-95.

Based on the movies "The Terminator" @1983 by Carloco
Pictures, and "Terminator 2: Judgment Day" @1991 by
Carloco Pictures, the concept of the Book and Record Set
@1984 by Buena Vista Records, and material from the
collected storylines of "Gremlins: Rewritten," "Critters:
Rewritten," "Gremlins 2: Rewritten," "Night of the Living
Dead: Rewritten," "Bionic Commando: Rewritten," "Star
Wars: Rewritten," and "Return of the Living Dead:
Rewritten," "The Terminator: Rewritten \ Dry Run," "The
Terminator: Rewritten II \ The Raising of Lazarus," "The
Terminator: Rewritten III \ Children of the Damned," and
"The Terminator: Rewritten IV \ Double Edged Sword" all
written by David Minter @1991, 1992, 1993, 1994, and 1995
by David Minter.


This is the story of "The Terminator: Rewritten V \
Judgment Day." You can read along with me in your book.
You will know it is time to turn the page when you hear
ARNOLD shout, "I'LL BE BACK!" Let's begin... now!
Remember to turn the page every time you hear the sound.
I'LL BE BRAINS!



The Terminator, having taken Billy's and Kate's son,
Jason, hostage from the Chump Towers' Day Care Center,
holed itself and its captive up down in the Research and
Development wing. With them in the basement, was Rand,
since it was his laboratory that the Terminator had
seized! But, Rand managed to get a distress call across
the building's computer network. With a small farce of
security guards, Billy and Kate went down to face the
Terminator, as per it's plan. After a short battle, Billy

the laboratory safe. As Billy and Kate celebrated their

victory was a hollow one as the mechanical portion of the
Terminator, now free of the damaged tissue that concealed
it, arose from the fires to begin the battle anew! I'LL
BE BACK!

hands, it was as if the robot had been slapped together

NOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Billy was genuinely worried as well,
