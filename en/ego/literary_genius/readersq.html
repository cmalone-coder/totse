Quotable Quotes from Reader's Digest

This is a compilation of various 'Quotable Quotes' from Reader's Digest.
I'm sure you will find most, if not all, of these interesting, if not
funnny. Typed by Ethan Filner, SysOp of *The Coliseum BBS* -838 0820-

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
"Be bold in what you stand for and careful what you fall for."
RUTH BOORSTIN in The Wall Street Journal

"We are tomorrow's past."
MARY WEBB, Precious Bane

"Do not let what you cannot do interfere with what you can do."
JOHN WOODEN, They Call Me Coach

"An economist's guess is liable to be just as good as anybody else's."
WILL ROGERS

"The love of truth lies at the root of much humor."

"Discipline is remembering what you want."
