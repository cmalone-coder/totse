Bookbinding Materials Sources

From: ANAND JAYANTILAL BARIYA

Bookbinding Supply Source

Many of these sorces have catalogs and samples available:

1. Binding tapes and end sheets:

Arcon Coating Mills
3067 New Street
Oceanside, NY 11572

2. Bookbinding tools and supplies (they have almost everything a
beginner needs)

Basic Crafts Co.
1201 Broadway
New York, NY 10001

3. Table cutter for cardboard and leather

Dick Blick
P.O. Box 1267
Galesburg, IL 61401

4. Tools, cloth, paper, threads, pastes, how to books

Bookmaking Needs
665 Third St
San Francisco, CA 94107

(415) 546-4168

Klaus, the owner, is very good. He helps people to locate old equipment,
tools
etc.

5. Binder's board

The Davey Company
164 Laidlaw Ave
P.O. Box 8128 - Five Corners Station
Jersey City, NJ 07306

Davey board, of course, is almost synonymous with binder's board. I think
they
must have a large minumum order, I have never ordered from them.

6. Fine papers

McManus and Morgan Inc.
2506 W. 7th St
Los Angeles, CA 90057
(213) 387-2717

They have beautiful papers, handmade, marbled, etc. But their sample book

costs $ 40 - I hear their papers are great.

7. Leather tools and supplies

The Tandy Leather Company
P.O. Box 791
Fort Worth , TX 76101

They have 184 stores throughtout the country.
