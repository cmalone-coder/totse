You & the Credit Bureaus - Knowledge is Power!


INSIDER'S Report - " You & the Credit Bureaus-Knowledge is Power"

Credit CORRECTION - PART 1

You're concerned about your Credit Report, and for GOOD reason.

You WILL get Credit if it's GOOD.
You WILL NOT get Credit if it's BAD.

NOT SO SIMPLE.

Getting Credit, yes or no, is ONLY ONE reason to "Repair" your Credit and not the most significant.ou can still get Credit if it's BAD. BAD Credit means you'll pay a lot more for the same loan. Itculd
cost you many hundreds of dollars to have a BAD Report.

What you may not be aware of is that your Credit Report can be right or wrong,
AND whether it's WRONG and gives you BAD Credit or it's RIGHT and gives you BAD Credit, there is
little difference.

BAD Credit - can, and USUALLY DOES:

1/Increase the cost of your Credit, (Set-up/points/cost of documents)

2/Require a larger down payment

3/Result in a higher rate of interest (same loan)

4/Lower the required debt-ratio. Debt ratio is debt vs income. (See Chapter 2- "What You Should AsBefore Applying" for a detailed explanation) "A" would get the loan if paying out 50% of his income "B"
could be turned down.

AND, you'd never know the difference since no one will tell you. They'd just say you owe too much mey.
They'd be right. Your Credit Report tells them you can't handle your money.

OR you may have to live with a poor loan rather than the best available because of an incorrect entrfor
years! If your Credit is not "A" because of a derogatory entry, a mortgage lender will oftentimesharge
you 1% more interest on your 30 year loan. Not to mention extra "points". If you borrow $100,000 r 30
years 1% can mean a bigger or a smaller monthly payment EVERY MONTH for 30 years!

Your payment at 8% is $733.76 a month, and at 9% $804.62 but at 7% $665.30 a month.
EVERY MONTH for 30 years!

And it doesn't end there. Your ability to rent/lease a place to live could be, and usually is, affeed.
Many employers "run" your Credit before they offer you a job. Especially in Management and positionof
trust. You cannot get on the Los Angeles Police force with BAD Credit.

You can't get a Merchant Account for a new business to take Credit Cards with BAD Credit.

There's probably many more times that BAD Credit Reporting can change the quality of your life but
these are the first that come to mind.

It's interesting to note here that BAD Credit stays in the Bureaus longer than GOOD Credit for the
most-part. What's chilling is that it doesn't even have to be right !

AND even more disturbing is that with the exception of the Police Force and possibly a Merchant bankyou won't be told WHY you were turned down or WHY the Creditor offers Credit that costs so much.
They don't tell you that there are many levels of Credit. You just think that's the way it is.

Since there's no one to ask, and no one will offer the information, you can go on like that for yea!

The Credit Reporting done by the Bureaus is your Credit "reputation",- a Reporting of the claimed fas as
they say were Reported to them. We say "claimed" since there are no guarantees that your Credit rerds
Reported to them are correct.

Many times items are incorrect due to Reporting errors. Clerical errors occur at the Bureaus in posng
or at the Reporting agency when Reporting to the Bureau, or at the Reporting agency.
OR, ALL OF THE ABOVE!

So there are several stages at which your Credit history can be posted incorrectly. In some cases ts is
compounded even further. The major Credit Bureaus Report BAD Credit from small Credit Bureaus who
give only the information that you have BAD Credit, not the source of their Reporting.

Also included is public record. Judgements, bankruptcy filings, liens, wage garnishments, Ch 13- Wa
Earner Plans, taken off by low paid clerks who are usually over-worked and are likely to make errors

WHAT CAN YOU DO ABOUT IT?

It may come as a shock to you that no one will do it for you under $1,000 (that's the latest figures know
of) per individual or couple. You just can't holler "foul" if your Credit Report is incorrect. Notng will
be done if you don't do the "steps". Further, if you ask improperly the Bureaus use a clause in theair
Credit Reporting Act and call your request "frivolous".

There's something you can do about that but we'll deal with it LATER in the chapter:
"WHEN ALL ELSE FAILS".

In a nut-shell: Creditors and Credit Bureaus can say ANYTHING about your payment history
RIGHT OR WRONG and it's UP TO YOU TO "POLICE" them. Sad, but true.

It is your responsibility to be sure that the Credit history being Reported about you is accurate. ince no one doing the posting and Reporting really cares if you have GOOD Credit or BAD, it is up t
you to check your Credit at LEAST once a year. Every six months is preferable.

It's TOO LATE to find out your Credit Report is inaccurate AFTER you are turned down for Credit or
miss that GOOD job or fail to get that apartment or house!

There are several things you must always keep in mind regarding Credit and Credit Correction the mosimportant of which is that YOU ARE NOT THE CLIENT. YOU ARE THE COMMODITY!
(or strictly speaking, your "track record" is the commodity)

Money exchanges hands based on Reports made about you, right or wrong. But, there is a Federal Lawhat can protect you from unfair Reporting. It is up to you to use this law to protect yourself!

The abuse of the Credit Reporting Agencies was so extensive that the Federal Government created the
FAIR CREDIT REPORTING ACT. This is the first chapter in a course book to help you use the act to
your advantage and empower you with the Credit Bureaus.

You and Uncle Sam make a formidable adversary. The trick is to conduct yourself properly and requesinvestigation and/or correction in the proper way. IF YOU DO IT IMPROPERLY you'll find that the
Bureaus just "brush you off".

I suppose they have their problems but the general feeling about the Credit Bureaus is that they aremean spirited" and uncooperative. They have the audacity to hold themselves as if they were a
Government Agency when in truth their business is no different from any other business. Except for e
structure of their business they must answer to their clients the SAME AS ANY BUSINESS such as the
shoe repair man or the cleaners answers to you. EXCEPT, in this case, YOU ARE NOT THE CLIENT!

The Bureaus' Clients for the most part are AUTOMATIC SUBSCRIBERS: Major Banks, Travel &
Entertainment Cards, Major Savings & Loans, Major Department Stores, Finance/ Loan Companies,
Collection Agencies. They Report automatically monthly to the Bureaus who act as a "Clearing Housefor information. In turn, when they want to determine if you are a GOOD Credit risk, they request aredit Report on you. Since they are Automatic Reporters and they contribute to the information
(COMMODITY) monthly, the Bureaus in turn give them Special Consideration: Service and Cost.

It must be noted here that it takes a LOT to get an automatic Reporting removed and many times a
Creditor who wants to collect from you will promise to remove the entry from the Bureau but in truthill
only mark it "paid". What you are left with is a derogatory entry that is paid. VERY slightly beer than
an unpaid one when you apply for Credit. VERY slightly.

GIVE THEM WRITTEN PERMISSION.
