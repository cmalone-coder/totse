Here's what to do:

Step 1 - First, get a copy of the "Daily Racing Form" (it
comes out the day before the race - the Advanced
Edition).

Step 2 - Read the "Capsule Explanation of Past Performance"
which explains how to read the racing form.

Step 3 - Turn to the First Race of the Day.

You must first underline with a pen up to 3 past
races that are the same distance as today's race. For example: if
today's first race is 6 Furlongs then underline the most recent 3
past races that are 6 Furlongs. Underline up to 3 past races for
each horse in the race. - See BOOM3.PCX

If there are only 2 past races of the same distance
as today's race then underline 2. - See BOOM2.PCX

If there is only 1 past race of the same distance
as today's race then underline only 1. - See BOOM1.PCX

(Note: You might come across a horse that doesn't
have a distance the same as today's race...Example: If today's race
is 6 Furlongs and the horse has no past race with 6 Furlongs in it
then you use something close to it. If you see 6 1/2 Furlongs then
use that. Always use the next longest distance. Also, if there are
horses in a race without any past historys then don't play that day.)

Step 4 - When you log onto our BBS go to the Door Menu and
Door 1 will ask you questions. Answer All Questions.
