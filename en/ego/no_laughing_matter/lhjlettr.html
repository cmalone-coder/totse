Funny letter to Ladies Home Journal ragging on fem

Fred D. Fagbag
P.O. Box 42
Ft Myers, FL 33908

LHJ
100 Park Ave.
New York, NY 10017

As a law enforcement officer, I must disagree with some
of the statements made by Carol Mithers in her October 89
article "The War Against Women". She underplayed the fact
that rape is a crime of violence and usually has very little
to do with sex. I'm not sure if she is aware of it, but the
violent crime rate in America is on the rise (much of this
due to the situation with crack cocaine) and, as such,
violent crimes to women are also going to increase due to
this. Her statements concerning sexual harassment go both
ways. Myself and many fellow officers have had 'passes' made
at us while on-duty in methods ranging from the very subtle
to out and out rude. Just because I am a man does not mean
that it is any more acceptable for me than it is for a woman.
I am a recent graduate of the academy, and am proud of the
fact that police officers are better trained now than they
have been in any other time in history. I am considered by
many people to be an intelligent, honest and liberal person.
However, I just can't help but feel that Ms. Mithers did men
a great injustice by insinuating that we are simple
creatures, with minds easily twisted into sadistic, sexist
rapists by the literature we might read. If Mrs. Mithers
would care to study some theories on deviance and social
control by Durkheim, Merton & Hirschi then perhaps her views
of men as socially conditioned chauvinist prisoners might
change to be more accurate. There are women I trust with my
life every day when I call on them for backup. The fact that
they are women makes no difference to me at all, they are
human beings. I suggest Ms. Mithers start looking at men the
same way.

Sincerely,


Fred D. Fagbag

P.S. Ms. Mithers is kind of cute and if she were to let the
boys down at the station fuck her God-Damned feminist
lesbian slut asshole then we might let her slide on
the ticket she got on the way to work last night.
