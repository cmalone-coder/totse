In response to this and another post requesting real names, I'll re-post
my list of the current WWF and WCW rosters below. It's slightly updated
with Nailz (Kevin Wacholz) and Max Moon (Paul Boric) being removed from
the WWF side.

Besides an error in Nailz' name (you've got a previous wrestling identity),
and slight errors in Perfect's and Skinner's name, the only error in your
list is Brutus Beefcake's name. His real name is Ed Leslie. He wrestled
as Dizzy Hogan in New Japan and the NWA Mid-Atlantic area (and elsewhere?)
in the earlier 80s, with the gimmick being that he was Hulk Hogan's brother.
