Glossary:
(This will explain some terms that you may need to know in th FAQ)
commercial - the full version of a game. Usually bought from a store or
catalog. It is illegal to copy this for other people. See
registered.
demo - some authors make demos of their software which is 1 or 2 levels.
It may or may not be legal to copy this. See shareware.
flag - added to the name of a file. Looks like FILENAME FLAG.
press - press these keys or buttons at the same time.
registered - the full version of shareware software. May or may not be legal
to copy. See commercial.
shareware - similar to a demo, but is usually a larger sampling or full
product. After a fair amount of time, you are required to either
delete it or purchase the full product. It is totally legal to
copy this software. See demo
type - type these keys in order.
+ - at the same time as the letters on the side(s) of it.
, - in order. In example, C,H,E,A,T would mean you type C, then H,
then E, then A, then T.

Note:Some games here are shareware and the codes will only work in the
registered version. Codes also may not work if you are using an interactive
demo, the wrong version, or sometimes have too little memory.

the quotes to the command line to get the ctrl-del destroy command, but not
invincibility.
