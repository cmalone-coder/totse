BABYLON 5
LIMITED EDITION SOUNDTRACK ALBUM

The Babylon 5 Soundtrack Limited Edition Compact Disc will be available at
Loscon 21, November 25-27, 1994, in Burbank, California USA. You can
still pre-register for Loscon at the rate of $35; same as the at-door
price, but your badge is pre-made and waiting for you when you arrive.
Send to Loscon 21, c/o LASFS, 11513 Burbank Blvd., N. Hollywood, CA 91601.
For more information on Loscon, write to hazel-chaz@netcom.com.

More about the Limited Edition B-5 CD:
* 44 minutes running time
* Will have different packaging and art than the commercial release
* Will be a numbered edition, signed by the artist (Chris Franke)
* Is authorized by Babylonian Productions, PTEN, Warner Bros., JMS
himself, etc.
* Will cost under $20
* Almost but not quite identical with the February '95 commercial release
(they'll be adding a few more tracks as soon as they finish them up)
* Is available via mail-order.

To order the CD by mail, send a check for $16.50 to
Sonic Images
P.O. Box 691626
West Hollywood CA 90069

For shipment to Canada: send US$17.50 to the above address.

For shipment to UK: write or call
Compact Disc Services
The Bryans
89 Dundee Road
West Ferry
Dundee DD5 3LZ
Scotland
Phone: 0382-76595
