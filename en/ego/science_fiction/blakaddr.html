The Black Adder Episode Guide

The Black Adder (1983)

Edmund Blackadder is prince to King Richard IV (1485 - 1498)

1. The Foretelling
2. The Queen of Spain's Beard
3. The Archbishop
4. Born To Be King
5. Witchsmeller Pursuivant
6. The Black Seal

Black Adder II (1986)

Lord Blackadder in Elizabethan times

1. Bells ( Jan 9, 1986)
2. Head ( Jan 16, 1986)
3. Potato ( Jan 23, 1986)
4. Money ( Feb 6, 1986)
5. Beer ( Feb 13, 1986)
6. Chains ( Feb 20, 1986)


Black Adder : The Cavalier Years (airdate?)

15 minute episode set in 1648 that aired during a BBC telethon

Black Adder the Third (1987)

Blackadder as butler to Prince George (1760 - 1815)

1. Dish and Dishonesty ( Sep 17, 1987)
2. Ink and Incapability ( Sep 24, 1987)
3. Nob and Nobility ( Oct 1, 1987)
4. Sense and Senility ( Oct 8, 1987)
5. Amy and Amiability ( Oct 15, 1987)
6. Duel and Duality ( Oct 22, 1987)

Blackadder Christmas Carol (December 23, 1988)

Edmund Blackadder as the proprietor of a mustache shop in Victorian
England (with scenes of Blackadder II and Third)


Blackadder Goes Forth (1989)

Edmund Blackadder as a Captain in the trenches of World War I (1917)

1. Captain Cook ( Sep 28, 1989)
2. Corporal Punishment ( Oct 5, 1989)
3. Major Star ( Oct 12, 1989)
4. Private Plane ( Oct 19, 1989)
5. General Hospital ( Oct 26, 1989)
6. Goodbyeee ( Nov 2, 1989)
