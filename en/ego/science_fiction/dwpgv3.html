Doctor Who Program Guide 1963- 1989

DOCTOR WHO PROGRAMME GUIDE REVISION THREE
1963-1989
DWPGV3:Released 2/5/89 ©1988-1989 Joe Siegler
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chronology Of Programme Guide:

June 1, 1988:Version 1 Released with Preliminary Information on Season 25
Jan 7, 1989:Version 2 Released with Season 25 Information Corrected and
Preliminary Information on Season 26 Added
Feb 5, 1989:Version 3 Released with Season 25 Information Finalized and
Some corrected information on Season 26 added
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The format for this programme guide will be as follows. Each story will
contain this information line----

CODE TITLE WRITER DIRECTOR DESIGNER
---- ----- ------ -------- --------

And then underneath that line further information will be presented...

episode number & companions orig air date
individual episode name ---------- -------------
(if warranted)
-----------------------

Notes:If an episode does not exist in the BBC archives, there will be an
asterisk (*) directly preceeding the episode number.

Also, the producer and Script editor will be mentioned at the beginning of each
season, and also if the post changed during the middle of the season.

===============================================================================

Doctor #1:William Hartnell (1963-1966)
There were 29 stories made, with a total of 134 episodes. Today, only 17
stories exist competely with 87 episodes still existing. Of those 87, 76
comprise the 17 complete stories. This leaves 11 that exist from various
incomplete stories. This leaves a total of 47 that still do not exist in the
BBC archives. (Some are rumoured to exist, but as of now, no BBC confirmation
of the recovery of these episodes has been given.)

DOCTOR #3:Jon Pertwee (1970-1974)
There were 24 stories made, with 128 episodes total. All 128 episodes do exist
in one form or another, but not all are in syndication and all are not in
color. The serials BBB,CCC,EEE,JJJ are in syndication in b/w, but the color
versions do exist. The serial FFF does not exist in color at all. And episode
1 of serial WWW and episode 3 of serial SSS are not in syndication but do exist
in black and white copies.

DOCTOR 4:Tom Baker (1974-1981)
There were a total of 42 stories made with a total of 178 episodes. All but
one have been broadcast. Serial 5M was never completed due to a strike at the
BBC at the time. The total of stories/episodes (42/178) is including Shada,
becuase serial 5M was started, and footage from all 6 episodes DOES exist, and
the BBC has put together a tape that a few have been lucky enough to see which
has the shot footage along with computer generated text to replace the non-shot
scenes.

THE FUTURE OF DOCTOR WHO:
Well, Dr. Who seems to have shaken the past of Michael Grade. The show seems
to be healthy again, although running at a smaller pace then before.
Sylvester McCoy does 1/3 the number of episodes that William Hartnell did, and
1/2 the number of episodes Tom Baker did in his last season. Still, this
reduced amount of Doctor Who is better than no Doctor Who at all. 14 episodes
is what I consider to be the bare minimum to run the show at all. If they
ever decide to go lower than 14, they might as well kill it. I don't think
we'll ever get to see another 6 parter unless the number of episodes goes up.
Anwyays, there are some interesting things going on with the show's future.
Scripts for Season 26 have been commissioned, so that is a reality, also there
is some word from England that Season 27 (and beyond) will be done by an
independent production company. (Much like shows here. Ex:ST:TNG by
Paramount, ALF by Alien Productions) The front runner for this coveted
position seems to be JNT's production company, for which he will leave after
season 26 to run. I've also heard that old-time Dr. Who person Victor
Pemberton will be putting in a bid to make the show. There does seem to be
a future! Season 26 will be the end of a few things. End of JNT. Andrew
Cartmel is also leaving. Sylvester & Sophie's contracts will be up, but both
have expressed interest in returning. The future looks bright for the show.
We just passed the Silver Anniversary, let's hope it gets to the GOLD anniver-
sary!
