Tribble Flambe'
---------------
3 TBLS butter Chives
2 TBLS oil Parsley
1 medium Tribble (1lb) 4 TBLS Romulan Ale
Salt Few grains of sugar
Pepper 1 jigger brandy (your choice)
Tarragon 1 TBLS lemon juice
