TARDIS Landing Log

THE TARDIS LOG VOLUME III

This is a list of all the landing the Doctor's TARDIS has made in every
story that has been broadcast. It is a variant of the list that
originally appeared in Dr. Who Magazine (formerly Dr. Who Monthly), and
a variant on Steve W. Hill's version. Thanks to both, and no
copywright infringement is meant.
