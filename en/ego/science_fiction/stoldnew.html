Category: Star Trek (tm) parodies
Title(s): (no title) (ST Old and New)
Author(s): Jay Smith

First date published: 1990 07 04 00:20:59
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Kirk: (entering the bridge) Amazing. What did they do to my
bridge?

Picard: YOU'RE bridge, Admiral?

Kirk: (contempt oozing) Sorry, Captain. Its a nice ship...who's
the white stiff?

Picard: This is Lt. Commander Data.

McCoy: Lord help us, they've made a better Vulcan.

Data: Hardly, sir. I am an android.

McCoy: Where's Spock? He's gotta see this.

Riker: Admiral Kirk! It is an honor to meet you, sir...I've studied
your career since I was a cadet...

Kirk: Thank you, Commander.

Riker: I particularly like the supplemental logs they published
after your death...the guide to babes in the Federation. I live
by it.

Kirk: Thank you, I think.

Picard: Number One...

Riker: And the Kobyashi Maru solution...fantastic. I tried it a
little different, though. I seduced the programmer.

Picard: NUMBER ONE...

Kirk: Put a cork in it, Baldy.

Picard: Er, um... (blushes pragmatically)

Scotty: (entering bridge) Admiral! Didya see wat they've dun ta
the berns? I canna believe it! An there's a BLIND man runnin' the
whole operation!

Picard: That would be Lt. LaForge.

Spock: Facinating.

Kirk: Mr Spock?

Spock: I'm just having a look at this android, Admiral.

Data: To be more precise: you are doing something I think is caled
"goosing" Please refrain from further ...//ooch//

Spock: "Facinating.

Kirk: Picard, we've got to talk...the Klingon?

Picard: Lt. Worf. A fine Officer.

Kirk: Yeah. Not exactly a bantu female, but...listen. I can't
help but be alarmed...

Picard: By?

Kirk: A blind man as Chief Engineer? A Klingon head of security?
Who's the brat at the helm?

Picard: Acting Ensign Wesley Crusher. (Crusher stands to rigid attention)

Kirk: Interesting crew assortment, Captain. Who's your Chief
Medical Officer? A Romulan Executioner? (on cue, Beverly Crusher
enters the bridge)
Kirk: Hellllooooo! (Hormones kick in)

Picard: This is Dr. Beverly Crusher.

Kirk: 'Crusher'?

Picard: Wesley's mother, yes.

Kirk: FIne boy, that Wesley...

S.O.V.
