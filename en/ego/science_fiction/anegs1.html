Alien Nation Episode Guide - Season One

Matthew Sikes Gary Graham
George Francisco Eric Pierpoint
Susan, George's wife Michelle Scarabelli
Buck, George's son Sean Six
Emily, George's daughter Lauren Woodland
Cathy Frankel, Matt's neighbor Terri Treas

1) Premiere Movie 9/18/89 1:35:27
Written by Kenneth Johnson
Directed by Kenneth Johnson
George Francisco and his family are among the 300,000 alien slaves who've
been stranded on Earth. In the opener, the Franciscos adjust to a new
neighborhood--and vice versa--while George, an LAPD detective, and his
partner Matthew Sikes investigate the disappearance of a vagrant's corpse,
and a mysterious file kept by Sikes' late former partner. Buck shoots a
rival-gang member.

2) Fountain of Youth (6W03) 9/25/89 46:50
Written by Diane Frolov
Directed by John McPherson
Surgeon uses dead Newcomer glands to slow human aging via implants.

3) Little Lost Lamb (6W02) 10/02/89 46:51
Written by Diane Frolov
Directed by Kevin Hooks
Runaway Newcomer prostitute murdered.

4) Fifteen With Wanda (6W04) 10/09/89 46:47
Written by Steve Mitchell & Craig Van Sickle
Directed by Rob Bowman
Matt & George protect a mob witness; Sikes' daughter introduced.

5) Takeover (6W05) 10/16/89 46:48
Written by Tom Chehak
Directed by Steve Dubin
Terrorists raid unmanned Police HQ; kidnap Susan; steal docility gas.

6) The First Cigar (6W01) 10/23/89 46:50
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by John McPherson
Newcomer businesswoman supplies information for drug bust

7) The Night of the Screams (6W06) 10/30/89 46:50
Written by Tom Chehak
Directed by Gwen Arner
Emily & Susan mutilate a Halloween pumpkin; Overseers are murdered by an
old Newcomer man.

8) Contact (6W07) 11/06/89 46:46
Written by Joe Menosky
Directed by John McPherson
Possible Tenctonese space probe detected; Sikes inherits a
trunk from his uncle

9) Three to Tango (6W08) 11/13/89 46:46
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by Stan Lathan
Binaums (Newcomer 3rd sex) being murdered

10) The Game (6W09) 11/20/89 46:39
Written by Steve Mitchell & Craig Van Sickle
Directed by David Carson
Celebrating the Day of Descent; Newcomer Russian-roulette game.

11) Chains of Love (6W10) 11/27/89 46:39
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by Harry Longstreet
Newcomer love-drug Sardynak; a Newcomer male falls for Matt.

12) The Red Room (6W11) 12/18/89 46:40
Written by Steve Mitchell & Craig Van Sickle
Directed by Chuck Bowman
US govt plan to train Newcomers as assassins revealed.

13) Spirit of '95 (6W12) 1/15/90 46:40
Written by Tom Chehak
Directed by Harry Longstreet
Susan and Buck work for Newcomer right-to-vote.

14) Generation to Generation (6W13) 1/29/90 46:41
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by John McPherson
Newcomer artifact found; vision of Tencton; Moodrie dies.

15) Eyewitness News (6W14) 2/05/90 46:10
Written by Steve Mitchell & Craig Van Sickle
Directed by Lyndon Chubbuck
Matt & George investigate TV-porn murders.

16) Partners (6W15) 2/12/90 46:40
Written by David Garber & Bruce Kalish
Directed by Stan Lathan
George is framed for drug theft; Susan's pod is transferred to George.

17) Real Men (6W16) 2/19/90 46:38
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by John McPherson
Bodybuilders use a Newcomer "steroid"; Matt helps George deliver
prematurely.

18) Crossing the Line (6W17) 2/26/90 46:41
Written by Steve Mitchell & Craig Van Sickle
Directed by Gwen Arner
Sikes tracks a serial killer he's crossed paths with before.

19) Rebirth (6W18) 3/12/90 46:41
Written and Directed by Tom Chehak
Sikes dies during a hold-up, brought back to life by his Newcomer
assailant.

20) Gimme, Gimme (6W19) 4/02/90 46:41
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by David Carson
George and Susan invest in Nu-Knit, the inventor is murdered

21) The Touch (6W20) 4/30/90
Written by Steve Mitchell & Craig Van Sickle
Directed by Harry Longstreet
Cathy works with an abused Newcomer child; new character Lorraine, a
social worker, is introduced

22) Green Eyes (6W21) 5/07/90
Written by Andrew Schneider & Diane Frolov
Directed by Tom Chehak
Season finale; prominent Newcomers are dying unexpectedly
