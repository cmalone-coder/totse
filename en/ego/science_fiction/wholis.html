A list of Doctors and Companions

This is a list of runs of companions and Doctors in Doctor Who.

First off, this list makes a couple of assumptions. When totaling number of
episodes, I didn't count re-appearances of companions, ala Jamie in the Two
Doctors, and especially The Five Doctors. The only exception to this is
Turlough and Teegan who I DID count for the Five Doctors because they were the
regular companions at the time. Also, that story counted as only 1 episode
becuase that's the way it was originally transmitted. Plus Mission to the
Unknown was not counted because there was no Doctor or any companions in it..
And- Shada was not counted because it was never transmitted.

And for season 22, In the number of episodes for Doctor #6 and for Peri, I
counted them as they were "officially" classified. That's 2 episodes for
Vengeance on Varos, 3 for Two Doctors, etc. However, I also included the
comparisons in parenthesis of what they would have been if the were 25-minute
episodes.

Also Resurrection of the Daleks is counted as 4 episodes because that's the way
it was originally inteded to be broadcast. It was cut down to 2 becasue of the
Olympics at the time.

As for Katarina and Sara, the actual number of episodes is unknown (to me
anyway) because I don't know exactly in what episodes they die in.

Without any further adieu-------
