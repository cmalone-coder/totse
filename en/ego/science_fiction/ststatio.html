TV Stations carrying Star Trek: The Next Generatio


The following is a list of tv stations that carry TNG across
North America and Hawaii. They are alphabetized by state or
province, and city within that state or province. The broadcast
times are local time, and subject to change.
I am grateful to participants of the Relaynet Star Trek echo,
as well as the CompuServe Star Trek conference of the SciFi forum.
Please send any additions or corrections to Robert Sartain via Relay-
net at Channel One (->CHANNEL), or on CIS at 72451,612, or via
Internet/Bitnet at 72451.612@COMPUSERVE.COM.
If the station you watch TNG on is improperly listed, please
let me know so that I can correct future lists. Thank you!
