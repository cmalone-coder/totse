Leprosy

RIB Productions
Proudly bring to you
"Leprosy"
A Deep Space Nine Parody of the Original
Two Hour Pilot, "Emissary"
Double Length For your reading enjoyment
By Robert I. Brayer
Author's Note:
Regarding the regular writing of Star Trek: Deep Space
Nine parodies, the matter is up in the air, and is subject to the
amount of time I have. I would like to do it; however it is hard
enough writing TNG weekly. Believe it or not.. It could get very
difficult the first time they both air in a week. If you see a
parody of Past Prologue, it's a good sign. But after watching the
excellent episode, I could not *RESIST* writing one of at least the
pilot :) Hope you enjoy...

Robert I. Brayer
1/8/93
-=-
SCENE I
(We see words scrolling by on our screens)
