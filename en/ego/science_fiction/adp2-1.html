[Inside Paradise Loop train]

Girl
W-what do you want?

Carol
How much?

Girl
It differs from day to day. Some people give me cards, some give me 80,000 yen or so. ($533: $1 = 50 yen)

Carol
How old?

Girl
Fourteen. What about it? No!!!!!!!!!!!!

---------------------------------
[A Paradise Loop entrance inside a slum]

Police woman (PW for short)
Geez, it's cold. Is something wrong with your eyes?

Iris
A bit, recently.

PW
Riding on Paradise Loop means [looking for] prostitution and drugs.

Iris
That's what I meant. So why do they want to come to a dangerous place like this?

PW
You truly are an old-fashioned one.

Iris
The subway, called Paradise Loop, is a belt line which was shut down when the super-conductor metro pened to traffic. However, even after the shut-down, the electricity supply wasn't stopped and so tere are always a few
cars running. It became kind of an outlaw area.

---------------------------------------
[Inside the train]

PW
External injuries?

Police man
The abdomen's been completely cut out. But it's more like a heart attack than excessive bleeding [tat caused her to die].

PW
Her identity?

Police man
A woman who's been doing business here recently.

------------------------------------------
[Area near Paradise Loop entrance]

PW
Hey, you! What is this mountain? Is it Booma's work?

Leon
Finding that out is also our job. How many women have been
killed in Paradise Loop including this one?

PW
Well, that's....

ADP officer
We're saying that we'll investigate for you normal police, who excuse your ineffectiveness by sayingyou don't have enough men.

Iris
Wait a minute. Aren't the AD Police only supposed to deal with armed terrorists and Booma? You kno how much damage will be inflicted on the citizens if the AD Police meddle in an ordinary crime, dont you?

Leon
Are you in favor of disarmament?

Iris
I've never ridden on Paradise Loop. I can't understand the thought of wanting to ride it.

--------------------------------------------
[Tokyo City Police HQ]

Chief Commissioner(?)
They were killed by having their abdomens cut out and they're all prostitutes of Paradise Loop. Thoe are the only common points among the six victims. According to Identification Division, the crimeswere committed by an imprinted A.I. or equivalent. It is highly possible that they were done by a Boma.

Man
A Booma's killing prostitutes?

Chief
Yes, something like that. Possibly a Booma who was abused as a useless machine by women. As normalpolice, as long as there's a possibility that a Booma's the killer, I'd like to let AD Police handlethis case formally. Iris Keran?

Iris
I think the killer is a human, and a female. I have a basis for this belief. I feel this act of alays cutting out only the abdomens is emotional. I think it was caused by highly complex hate or angr, which Booma do not possess.

Chief
Does anyone else share this opinion?

PW
I agree. I believe it's more likely that the killer is a human.

chief
Hm? Alright. I permit you to form an investigating group. But it'll be in cooperation with the ADPolice.

Iris
Alright!

----------------------------------------
[Some operating room in TCP]

Iris
Thanks a lot for your support.

PW
Never mind. I wanted to have a break anyway. You don't seriously think you're going to investigatethis case serious, do you?

Iris
Ah!

PW
Does it still hurt?

Iris
Only a little.

PW
Go to the bank. It won't take even 5 minutes to do an exchange operation. This is cybernetic, but i doesn't look like it, does it? You're so old-fashioned. No one appreciates that these days.

--------------------------------------
[Town]

Leon
Geena.

Geena
I'm fine.

Leon
Shit. hm? Cybernetic Bank of Tokyo...

----------------------------------------
[Cybernetic Bank of Tokyo]

Geena
You don't have to come with me.

Leon
This might be my fault.

Geena
Don't worry about it. This time it was unavoidable.

Leon
Sorry..I've got to...

Geena
What is it?

Leon
Just a moment.

Iris
Leon!?

Leon
Why are you in a place like this? Do you know what this place is? This is a bunch of freak body buchers who cut out human organs and exchange them for cybernetics, then store the organs and sell the to rich people who want
organ transplants.

Iris
My eye hurts. If I exchange it for an artificial eyeball...

Leon
So there's a little pain? Even if you only change a part of your body, you're making yourself inhumn!

Iris
You're old-fashioned. Hating artificial organs doesn't make you live long.

-----------------------------------------

Doctor 1
The pain might get worse or it might not. However, if it bothers you even a little, I recommend theoperation. This is not the 20th century. Everyone can live just the way they want.

Iris
I see.

Carol
Why can't you do it? I'll pay you whatever you wish, doctor!

Doctor 2
But...that was the operation you desired, and now you want to make your body back into flesh... It' not even broken. Also, it has more than enough functions as far as a woman goes...

------------------------------------------
[On the road]

Iris
At this speed, you'll lose her!

Leon
What're you talking about, hopping into my car without being asked!

Iris
L-look, it turned.

Geena
So what's this all about?

Iris
It's Normal Police business. That lady....

--------------------------------
[Recollection of Iris]

Iris
Ooops! I'm sorry.

Carol
I'm ashamed. Did you hear everything?

Iris
No.

Carol
So. See you.

---------------------------------
[Riverside]

Iris
Why did she come to a place like this?

Leon
This is a slum in the process of demolition. We can't find her once she gets mixed in.

Geena
What nice legs!

Iris
What was that?

Geena
Paradise Loop is running under here.

Iris
Really?

-----------------------------------------
[TCP computer room]

PW
Caroline Evers. She's the president of Green.

Iris
Green?

PW
In the sky, in the sea, Green cereal is delicious everywhere.

She's a very capable woman. She married half a year ago, but her husband doesn't work. Her yearly ncome is 12 hundred million yen [$800,000 : $1=150]! She had the operation five years ago. She tookup the president's office three years ago, so her progress after the operation must have been fine. She took out and altered her uterus, her ovaries, and also her kidneys. Looks like she exchanged allher abdomen. But wait, she's still capable of having sex. Other than that, she looks like just a prfect office lady to me.

Iris
I don't know, but I have a feeling.

PW
Iris.

Iris
Hm?

PW
Are you a lesbian? Anyway, is it okay not to inform AD Police about her?

Iris
[It's okay because] We don't have definite proof.

PW
I'm sorry. The sale finishes today. I'll be back in 15 minutes.

Iris
That's alright.

PW
Okay, I'll leave it to you then.

Iris
Bye.

------------------------------
[Office]

Carol
No, no..... No, don't, I can't....

--------------------------------
[Paradise Loop]

Iris
I wonder where this leads to.

Carol
To Paradise Loop. We meet again. Do you have something you want to do with me? Well, well, what apretty detective you are.

Iris
As I thought, you're the killer?

Carol
Yes, I am.

Iris
Why did you [kill] so many?

Carol
I wonder why, myself. I competed with a man for an office. In any aspect, my ability and intellectexceeded that of his, but he got the job. He made a graph of my menstrual cycle and my productivity,then said to the president,
"Obviously, her productivity varies widely just before and after her period." It's a lie. But everyne believed it. So I took out all my female organs, and replaced them with cybernetics. After that my work became smooth. Before long, I owned the company. Then last year, the man who had taken awa my office visited me, asking for a job. I realized I loved the man, so I made him my husband. I gve him everything. Even the sex must've been better than
anyone else. But he betrayed me. With a prostitute of Paradise Loop. After he did it, he said to m, "I like human women better."

Iris
N-no! You killed your husband, too, didn't you? Why did you kill the prostitutes so indiscriminatey?

Carol
How should I know? But, sometimes it aches. The period pains, which should've been gone a long tim ago, come back and torment me. When I came to, I always held a knife in my hand. The transfiguraton of a body brings about a reformation of a mind. Please tell me! Am I a human? Or am I a Boomer

Iris
You're a human. Only humans commit murder with nothing but hate!

Carol
Really? I'm a human? Then if murdering people is wrong, kill the woman inside me. Another me...

Iris
Leon? How come you're here?

AD Police officer
It's proof that the AD Police are also doing their job, young lady.

Leon
Where is it?

Geena
It ran away. But Paradise Loop is a belt line. It can't get away.

ADP officer
Miss, from now on, it's our job. I'll let someone escort you back.

Iris
Please wait a second. The Normal Police will catch her. She's a human.

ADP Officer
Well.

Iris
She is a human, not Booma.

Leon
She's already a Boomaroid. She has been declared as a Booma that is very close to a human. The AD olice have been ordered to shoot her dead.

Iris
But...

Leon
A human with seventy percent of his or her body replaced with cybernetics will be disposed of as a Bomaroid. It's a test case for the Booma Law which will be amended next month.

AD Police
We've found her. Gather at the corridor to the east.

Iris
Seventy percent? What's the difference between seventy percent and sixty-nine percent? Even though she's still a human!

---------------------------------------
[Inside the train]

Carol
I'm Booma, I'm a human. You can have me. Come on.

---------------------------------------
[The terminal]

Geena
She died as a woman....must be satisfied.

Leon
In the end, being Booma or human doesn't matter in this town.

----------------------------------------
[Cybernetic Bank of Tokyo operation room]

Doctor
Are you really sure?

Iris
Yes. ...and I cast away a piece of my humanity.
