1/D/BTL 14/2/91 Camille.

Brief summary

The same old,old story.

Droid meets Droid,
Droid becomes Chameleon
Droid loses Chameleon
Chameleon truns into Blob
Droid gets Blob
Blob meets Blob
Blob goes off with Blob
Droid loses Blob
Droid loses Droid,Chameleon and Blob.

The story starts with Lister trying to get Kryten to lie,cheat and
insult by getting kryten to lie about the identity of fruit. Kryten eventually
succeeds until he tries it out on Cat.
Kryten is then asked to acompany Rimmer on an asteroid spotting
trip. Whlist in flight Holly(now with much longer hair and still 'Hilly')
gets a distress call from an escape pod on a planet that is about to explode
Kryten disobeys orders and lands starbug to check for survivors.
Kryten explores the ship but falls off a gantry only to be rescued
by a female mechanoid who calls herself 'Camille'.
Camille is a 4000 series GTi with realistic toes and a flip top
sunroof head and Kryten falls instantly in love. However when Camille is
brought back to Starbug Rimmer sees a Hologram Girl instead and he too
falls totally in love with Camille.
On their return to Red Dwarf Lister sees Camille as a scouse
girl dressed in kind to Lister. However when Camille talks to Rimmer and
Lister ,Lister notices that Rimmer hears something different to what he hears
and questions Camille on this. Camille then spills the beans.
Camille is in fact a Pleasure-Gelf, A genetically engineered being
whose sole purpose is to give pleasure by being who you desire most. In other
word you see what you want to see.
The cat wishes to see who he desires most and is surprised and then
delighted to see a mirror image of himself, the cat of course falls instantly
in love with himself.
Camille is persuaded to show her true form and she is in fact a
green blob with a protruding Eye stalk. Kryten is however still in love and
says he does'nt care , he then proceeds to wine and dine Camille.
They are both watching Casablanca when Holly interupts and says that
Camilles 'Husband',Hector has arrived and wants her back.
Kryten persuades her to go with Hector and she leaves with him.
Kryten then says he was lying all along as not to hurt her feelings and
promptly blames Lister by insulting him,much to Listers delight

2/D 21/2/91 D.N.A

Summary : The crew finds an abandoned space craft which contains
a machine that can transform any organic matter into any living
thing. This has it's problems when one of Listers curries turns
psycotic.

The crew detects a UFO and is in the hectic task of tracking
it and bringing it in. They just reach a crucial stage when the
cat pulls out the plug and switches on his hairdrier. After much
persuasion the cat agrees to turn it off.
The crew board the ship and walk through gunk covered walls
and they find a mysterious three headed creature . Cat comes
across a funny looking console and presses a few buttons. Lister
is trapped inside a forcefield and the cat plays around with a
few buttons which results in the 'transmogrification' of Lister
into a chicken. The rest of the crew try and get him back but
only succeed in turning Lister into a hamster and trapping Kryten
in the same forcefield.
Eventually Lister is returned to normal and Kryten is turned
into a human(apparently his brain is organic and so he can be
transmogrified). The machine can alter any organic substance
into any other thing just by altering it's DNA.
Kryten tries to get to grips with being human but mostly
fails(what no Zoom capacity?) and especially with the sexual
parts of the male anatomy.
Kryten goes off the visit his spare parts for advice and to
say goodbye but only succeeds in hurting their feelings. Kryten
regrets his new found humanity and wants to be a mechanoid again.
Holly says 'she' as just about sussed how the machine works
but the crew wants to try it out first. The crew need some
organic material so Listers lamb vindaloo curry is chosen , the
experiment goes wrong and the curry turns into the mutton
vindaloo beast, half man, half extra hot curry. Resembling a man
with a sheeps skull covered with goey curry
Memories of the polymorph come flooding back (incidentally
the adventure take place a year exactly to the day that the
polymorph was encountered). 'How can the same smeg happen to the
same guy twice'(from Die Hard 2 I think?).

Last time it was hors d'ouvre(sp?) this time it's lunch' (from
Aliens). Both Lister and cat fire their Bazookoids but to no
effect. They retreat to the DNA machine Lab where Lister has an
idea, turn into a superman. It sort of works when Lister is
turned into half robocop ,half Lister but only in miniature. The
creature finds them and the rest of the crew scarper leaving
robo-lister to fight the creature.
Robo-Lister hides behind some cans of lager knocks one of
them over and it spills over the floor. When the creature steps on
the lager it recoils in pain. 'Of course Lager the only thing
that can kill a vindaloo'. Lister then throws a can into the
creatures mouth and fire at it with his miniature Bazookoid, thus
killing the creature(Jaws).
The creature killed and the crew safe Lister enjoys the
reamins of his curry.

3/D 28/2/91 Justice.
Summary

Red Dwarf meets the Borg in a fight to the death on board a
prison ship,Rimmer is imprisoned for the death of the Red Dwarf
Crew.

Lister has got space mumps which has disfigured his head to
resemble the elephant man. Meanwhile Rimmer has been on a
walking holiday in the ships diesel decks with two scutters and
Kryten mistakenly reveals that Cat has been investigating an
escape pod that supposedly contains a Barbara Bellini. Lister
gets up to investigate and goes to investigate. The cat is
drooling over the contents of the pod and after telling Lister to
get lost starts up the de-frost sequence.
After a short while Rimmer walks in and reveals that all is
not as it seems. Apparently the Pod was launched from a prison
ship in which all but two people were killed in a convicts
revolt.
One was a warden, Ms Bellini and one was a simulant, a
highly advanced and lethal killing machine. It is decided to go
to the prison ship and intern the pod until it opens(the thought
being that the prison will have the facilities in which to hold
the simulant). They set off in starbug and while on the trip
Listers mumps burst spreading pus everywhere but especially over
the cat who wants to be shot as a result.
They arrive at the prison and are welcomed by escort boots
which when worn lock onto the feet and force the wearer to go
where the boots are directed. The crew are subjected to a mind
probe which scans the mind in search of crimes committed which
have'nt been punished or for which the subject feels guilty of
doing.
Lister is worried as his past is littered with petty
crimes however it is Rimmer who is singled out for committing
1167 counts of 2nd degree murder. Each counts carries a sentence
of 8 years and so because of his hologrammatic nature the
sentences are to be carried out consecutively bringing the total
to 9328 Years.
The crimes for which he was sentenced was the wilful
negligence of failing to re-seal the drive plates which resulted
in the death of the entire crew of Red Dwarf.
Rimmer is led away into the Justice Zone in which it is
impossible to commit any crime. In order to demonstrate this
'justice field' Lister is encouraged to try and set light to
Rimmers bed sheets. Instead of them catching alight Listers
Jacket catches on fire. In short any thing you do wrong happens
to you instead.
The Justice computer has allowed an appeal and the entire
case rests on Kryten proving Rimmers incompetence in all things.
At the hearing Kryten continually insults Rimmer and calling upon
Lister as a witness proves the Rimmers only crime and punishment
is being Arnold Judas Rimmer. The Justice computer grants the
appeal as Rimmer is far too incompetent even be near a drive
plate let alone fix one. What the mind probe sensed was just
Rimmers guilt over the death of the crew.
The crew walk back to Starbug and find the pod open and a
smell of perfume. Unfortunately it is'nt Babs but the
Simulant(it bears more than a passing resemblance to a Borg.
another spoof?). The crew run with the Simulant in pursuit
firing away at everything.
Both Crew and simulant enter the justice zone where the
simulant discards it's weapon and demands to talk. Lister
ignores Rimmers suggestion to shoot it in the back and goes to
talk to the simulant.
The simulant lied when it says it was unarmed and pulls out
a knife, Lister has also lied and pulls out a metal bar.
However the Simulant has lied twice and pulls out a pistol/knife
combo. The simulant fires at Lister but instead of killing Lister
it hits the simulant(because of the justice field). The simulant
then throws his knife at Listers chest but it embeds its self in
It's chest instead. After removing the knife it throws it again
at Listers head and the knife sticks in the simulants head.
Lister by this time has sussed it's the justice field that has
caused this and persuades the simulant to hit him over the head
with a variety of objects, all having the desired result of
weakening the Simulant. Finally the simulant gets a stranglehold
on Lister and strangles it's self to death. The cat runs to
rescue and against Listers warning hits the Simulant over the
head with a spade. Of course it does nothing to the simulant but
totally flattens the cat.
Back on board Red Dwarf Lister is philosophically
contemplating natural justice. why do bad things happen to good
people and vice versa. Fortunately Lister is so concerned with
his revelations that he fails to miss an open man hole cover and
falls right down it much to every ones relief.

Summary
'The light that burns twice as bright burns half as long and

to wax world where Rimmer has his chance to lead an army against
the greatest generals of all time. He succeeds and fails in his
mission to bring peace to waxworld.

Rimmer is boring the crew solid by telling them his RISK
game stories . He cannot be told to shut up but is when Kryten
suddenly appears from nowhere holding a black cross shaped object
with a sphere on each side point of the cross.
Kryten explains that he has found and repaired a matter
transporter in the science labs on Z floor. It works by
digitally recording the molecular structure of the holder/s and
transmitting sub space to anywhere in the universe.
Kryten directs them to the science labs. Rimmer and Cat have
to work but Kryten and Lister try to teleport there but end up in
the shower. They too walk to the labs.
Holly also says that it can be made to seek out planets with
a breathable atmosphere within a 5000 Light year radius. Kryten
suggests that Rimmer accompanies him on the first trip so that is
the planet is not suitable no harm will be done. Rimmer objects

very phony looking dinosaurs chase after them and both run away
pretty quickly.
Cat and Lister appear a little way away in a meeting between
Hitler and his minions. They try frantically to move elsewhere
but only succeed in teleporting to the table and finally the
chimney. They are captured,the paddle confiscated and thrown in
jail.
Meanwhile Kryten and Rimmer have escaped the dinosaurs but
are captured by Elvis and Pope Gregory touting submachine guns.
While this is going on Lister witnesses the execution by firing
squad of winne the pooh and the construction of some gallows.
