"History of the Vessel Enterprise"
by Ronald M. Roden, Jr.
Copyright 1989

"Space...the final frontier, these are the voyages of the starship,
Enterprise."
This is the charter for the Heavy Cruiser Enterprise, from her first
building in 2252 to her sixth incarnation in 2365. It was said on the launching
of her missions, no matter what length and recited by her gallant Captains, from
Robert April, who took her from Earth's spacedock to Captain Jean-Luc Picard of
the new advanced Galaxy Class Starship. This line though, serves for all
vessels named Enterprise throughout history.
From 1616, when men set out to discover "strange new worlds" in their tiny
fragile-looking wooden vessels to find routes to the Orient, to fighting
schooners and frigates. All these being wind powered vessels of the oceans of
Earth. These ships all served great purposes. Back to 1616, the HMS Enterprise
was sent out by the royal crown to find a better and faster route to the
Orient,to better the trade routes, and to bypass land travel, where hordes of
bandits lurked in every shadow. The vessel did find the fastest route, and
started to return to England, but she was met by a fierce hurricane near the
sub-continent of India and was destroyed with all hands.
Many vessels followed, from sailing vessels to the new iron and steel ships.
Fighting in wars and carrying cargo, from freight to people.
The next great vessel was an Aircraft Carrier, made by the United States.
She was built at the Newport News Dockyards and was 806 feet in Length, and
could carry 80 to 100 aircraft in her huge hangar bay belly. On November 25,
1941, she left Pearl Harbor to take troops to another Pacific Island, and on her
return of December 5, 1941, she encountered a storm and was forced to take
another route. And of course, December 7, 1941, Japan attacked Pearl Harbor, and
the Enterprise and her sister vessels were lucky to have missed, since carriers
were the main target. In her service, she engaged in 18 of the 22 battles at
sea, and was damaged 15 times. The last one sent her to drydock till the end of
the war. She was then used at the Bikini Island Atomic Bomb test, and then
dismantled in 1960, and sold as scrap to Japan in 1961. Some of her famous
commanders were Admiral W.F. Halsey, Captain R.A. Spruance and Admiral
M.A.Mitscher.
Britain again named a vessel Enterprise, but this time it was a research
vessel with highly sophisticated radar and undersea mapping equipment. She was
launched in 1957 and in 1986, she was one of the first vessels to find the White
Star Ocean Liner, RMS Titanic, which sank on 14 April 1912.
In 1958, the U.S. Congress gave the go ahead for a nuclear aircraft carrier,
and that same year the keel was laid at Newport News Naval Yards. In 1961 she
was launched, and tried to navigate through the Panama Canal, but she was too
wide, so she was the first vessel not able to traverse the locks. In 1981 she
was updated and retired in 1999.
In the early 70's, President Richard Nixon gave NASA a go ahead and funding
to build a reusable space system, called a space shuttle. A fleet of 5 were to
be made. The first was to be called Columbia, but a petition by over 100,000
people protested she should be called Enterprise, due to a famous TV show of the
late sixties, called Star Trek, which was a science fiction show. NASA changed
the name and in 1976 she was rolled out of her Palmdale, California Hangar. She
journeyed through the streets of Lancaster, CA and was put atop a modified
Boeing 747 Jumbo Jet. She flew mock landings, did engine tests, and was the
model for Vandenberg AFB shuttle launch facility in CA. In 1986, she was given
to the Smithsonian Institute for an exhibit, after a world wide tour to promote
the shuttle.
The Goodyear Company named their fifth blimp Enterprise in 1980. She went
to sports showings, store openings and just advertisements. One of her famous
stopping points was Lancaster, PA.
The country of Barbados acquired a patrol vessel from England, and they too
named the little cruiser, Enterprise, sometime in 1981.
Soon the 1990's came, and the Eugenics Wars started and the Earth was in
turmoil. It wasn't till the mid 21st century till we ventured beyond Earth.
Then after 169 years and the mid 22nd century, a Declaration Class Starliner was
called Enterprise. She had second generation warp drive. For 40 years she
transported 950 people at per trip to Alpha Centuri and other outlands. Then in
2229 she was retired and later scrapped.
Third generation Warp Drive was achieved, and a cruiser called the Horizon
Class was developed. The first 10 ships were financed by the young Federation,
and the eighth was to be called Enterprise, but she was canceled prior to
construction in 2234.
The next great exploration vessels were made, the Constitution Class Heavy
Cruiser, able to achieve speeds of Warp 5/7. The second built from the keel up
was named Enterprise, the most famous starship of the 23rd century. Her first
captain was Robert April, who took her for 5 years into deep space. The
Enterprise was uprated to a new class of Heavy Cruisers, called the Bon Homme
Richard Class at Starbase 12. The captain this time was Christopher Pike. He
had her for 13 years, and the most famous incident was the visit to Talos IV
(read 2259 in the following section).
Then in 2271, the most famous Captain of all took her for a five year
mission, James Tiberius Kirk. From 2271 to 2273, he captained the vessel and
430 crew members. All the bridge crew had gained high fame along with missions
that led the way to the cornerstone of exploration. In early 2273, she was
again uprated to a newer class, the Achernar class.
Finally, in 2275 (SD6987.31), the ship returned to Earth. She was 21 years
old. Kirk rose to the rank of Commodore and to Admiral in less than 2 short
years. He gave the vessel to Commander Montgomery Scott and Commander Decker to
refit her to the new Linear Warp systems and a total new design of Heavy
Cruisers. In 2279 she was finished and rushed into service by Admiral Nogura
and Admiral Kirk, during the V'Ger Incident. Kirk saved the Earth, and got
command of the Enterprise again for a five year mission. Upon her return in
2283, she was awarded to Captain Spock of Vulcan as a training vessel, thanks to
pull by Admiral Kirk. The vessel was uprated that same year, and in 2286, Kirk
was on a training cruise, and did battle with an old foe named Khan Noonian
Singh, from 20th Century Earth (see SD3141.9 & 2286 in the following section).
The ship returned, badly battered by the Avenger Class heavy frigate, Reliant
(NCC-1864). She was to be dismantled, and was only 32 years old. Kirk in a
desperate attempt to save Spock and his kartra, stole the Enterprise, and took
her to Genesis. After a small exchange of torpedoe fire, Kirk lost her due to
self destruction to kill a Klingon boarding party.
In 2286, a company named the Solaris Corporation had developed a design
close to the Excelsior Class Control Cruiser, that would have 5th Generation
Warp Drive, but lack of funds ended the proposed NX-1701-A project. But
instead, in 2285, the new Levant Class Heavy Cruiser, Ti-Ho, was launched and
successfully tested the transwarp nacelles that resembled the original
Enterprise Class ships, and was rechristened, Enterprise, NCC-1701-A. She
served for 18 years, and was destroyed in 2306, but the actual account is still
under highly classified seals.
In 2308, that same year, the Excelsior Class Enterprise [NCC-1701-B] was
launched, with a second generation TransWarp Drive. More details on this vessel
are still classified.
Starfleet wanted another vessel to be named to replace the Excelsior Class
ship, so the Alaska Class Battleship project was mentioned, but the Federation
Council wanted an exploration cruiser. So the newer Ambassador Class got the
Enterprise [NCC-1701-C] in 2337. After 8 long years of deliberation, the vessel
only lasted 7 years until she was destroyed by 4 Romulan Warbirds while trying
to save a Klingon Base in 2344.
In 2362, the Galaxy Class Exploration Cruisers were being developed. State
of the art vessels where 70% of the ships operations are done by the computer.
The U.S.S.Galaxy was built and trial ran, but she was rechristened Enterprise
[NCC-1701-D], since she was to explore for 10 years. She was given to the
aforementioned Captain Jean-Luc Picard, and a well seasoned crew. Already
exploring the galaxy, she has gotten notariety, with discovering new life and
new civilizations. Her future voyages can only prove better!
