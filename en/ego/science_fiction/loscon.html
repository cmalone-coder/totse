<*> BABYLON 5 PARTY <*>
at Loscon 21
=======================

Saturday, November 26, 1994
at the
Burbank Airport Hilton
Burbank, California
USA, Earth, etc.

It will start about 10:00 pm, or
whenever the Saturday Show
(Masquerade+Cabaret) at Loscon lets out.

Food, Babylon 5 episodes, and fellow fans

Attention Babylon 5 Fans: A group of net.babbylers will be
throwing a B-5 party at Loscon 21,

For more information about the Babylon 5 party, write to Dianne Hackborn
<hackbod@mail.cs.orst.edu> (This party is not sponsored by Loscon or by
Babylonian Productions/Warner/PTEN; it's being put together by a bunch of
fans.)

=======================
<*> Be seeing you <*>
=======================

Babylon 5 Soundtrack Album:
===========================
Sonic Images (Chris Franke's record label) will have a Babylon 5
Soundtrack Album in a limited edition (a 1000-compact disc pressing,
numbered and signed by Chris Franke) at the convention. This is *Three
Months Early.* (The regular release isn't until February '95.) You can
hear the soundtrack album, and buy your very own copy. (It'll be less than
$20. If you can't make it to Loscon, you may be able to mail-order a copy
from Sonic Images; send email to hazel@netcom.com for info.) If Chris
Franke is free from professional commitments that weekend, he'll probably
show up at the convention; we can't promise it at this early date, though.
(Stay tuned.)

Babylon 5 Programming
=====================
There will be a Babylon 5 Fans get-together as part of the daytime
programming during Saturday, and J. Michael Straczynski will be doing a
B-5 presentation on Sunday afternoon. We don't know who he'll be bringing
with him (because they're in the middle of filming the new season), but he
usually brings a videotape to show. There will probably be more B-5 and
media programming during the 3-day convention; stay tuned for more
details.

The Early Birds Party
=====================
The Early Birds Party will be held in the Burbank Airport Hilton, on
Thursday, Thanksgiving day, starting at 7:00 pm. Look for the discreet
"Early Birds" signs. If you're flying in from out of town, come as you
are, all are welcome. If you're driving in locally, bring some finger food
or some drinks. Mark your dishes if they're not expendable -- we still
have a bowl and a corkscrew leftover from a previous party. If you decide
you want to bring hot food and/or home-cooked food, please call first to
coordinate it. (We can't cook it on-site.) Contact C.S.F. Baden, P.O. Box
1792, Redondo Beach CA 90278, (310) 542-5849, email: hazel-chaz@netcom.com.

Loscon memberships
==================
Loscon memberships are just $35 at the door. However, you still can send
in your memberships, and have a pre-made badge waiting for you when you
arrive at the convention. Send it to LOSCON 21, c/o The Los Angeles
Science Fantasy Society, 11513 Burbank Blvd., N. Hollywood, CA 91601.

Room Rates
==========
The Burbank Airport Hilton has special rates for Loscon of $72
Single/Double, $79 Triple/Quad. You must make your reservations by
*November 8* to get the Loscon rate. Write to Burbank Airport Hilton, 2500
Hollywood Way, Burbank CA 91505, or call (818) 843-6000, or (800)
643-7400. (This is their direct- line 800 number; don't call the Worldwide
Reservations number.)

More Information
================
For more information about Loscon 21, write to hazel-chaz@netcom.com and
ask for the Loscon 21 flyer to be mailed back to you.
