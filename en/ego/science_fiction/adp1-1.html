Police Officer 1: We think this is Buma's work.

Buma's owner: I'm counting on you....

Leon: "Buma, the artificial humanoid made by proteins, artificial intelligence, and artificial organ. These few years, the cases like this, the Buma goes crazy, have occurred not only one or two time."

[Geena's room]

Geena: What is it?

Leon: Well, that miss was caused by my fault. In any case, I thought I should apologize to you.

Insurance Agent: I have a talk concerning Mr. Aruth who died on duty today.

[Inside of a restaurant]

Geena: Why can't you pay Aruth's insurance money?

Agent: Well, take a look at this data. We investigated the Buma that was used in today's case, andwe found nothing particularly wrong in the Buma's circuit. Except, that shop was using the Buma for24 hours full-time while it has to rest every 12 hours.

Geena: Hey, rookie, aren't you going to eat?

Leon: No, not after what happened.

Geena: Then, I'll take it.

Agent: And to tell you the truth, Mr Aruth also insured himself for 7 billion yen besides the insurnce for dying on duty.

Geena: Yes, he said something about having a huge sum of debts...

Agent: And we have testimonies that the chinese restaurant where the incident occurred today had ben used by My. Aruth a several time, and Mr. Aruth had had contacts with that Buma.

Geena: Wait a minute! You don't think Aruth made the Buma crazy and died on duty to get the insurace money!

Agency: I have no comment at this time.

Geena: Aruth is not like that.

Leon: If some troubles happen in the mental side of the same type of Buma, we can infer that Aruths death at Chinese restaurant was a pure accident.

Agent: I see, that's an interesting theory.

Geena: Then we will investigate for you. If something abnormal is happening among the same type Bua circulating in the market or not. If that is the case, pay up the Aruth's insurance money.

Agent: That's fine. But it must be proven within 6 hours.

Jerk: My arm.....

[Inside Geena's car]

Leon: Ouch. Do you have any clue?

Geena: None.

Leon: This is not our job. We should leave this to the Investigation Division......

Geena: Shut up. You idiot, killed by a mere Buma...
Rookie, why did you resist me?

Leon: Nothing. When I was still a normal police officer, I was attacked by a crazy female Buma. I was a very high quality one. It had vocal cord organs and even a body odors.

Geena: How come something like that....

Leon: According to the findings, the destruction in the program by the unusual emotions caused durig sex had occurred, but I just can't accept that.

Geena: Even she is just a Buma, she is still a woman. The emotions come first when she was made a ove to by a man. I was, too, a little while ago.

Leon: Wait a second. About that Buma I mentioned now, it seemed an non-licensed junk dealer regeneated it.

Geena: Illegal regeneration... I've heard the rumor. You want to try it?

[Underground compartment. Somewhere in Akihabara(?)]

Geena: Yo, this is Geena. Are you here?

Leon: What is this place?

Geek(lori-con): Hi, Geena, havn't seen you for a long time. Can I help you?

Geena: Can you identify those who regenerate Buma illegally?

Geek: That'll cost you a little bit more.

Geena: Do it! We don't have a time.

Geek: Just a second. 'Cause that Buma is pretty old type, even in this Mega-Tokyo, there remains oly a handful of them. Anyway, among the makers, it is coordinated to be able to handle all the womn's occupations in the software modification, however among the some Junk dealers, they disassemble roken Buma and sell them through illegal channels.

Geena: Why?

Geek: Isn't it obvious? You can sell many times more expensive for the ones that have sex function than the ones that can't do it. Therefore, they remodels again and again. even with used parts.

Geena: Is that why the mental side disorder happens?

Geek: No, not really. It depends mostly on the regeneration technology level, but it is said that he abnormality in the mental side occurs when the hormone signals rush into the normal biochip durin sex, in the case of the Buma who is merged with used parts. Ok, this is the list. Some of the guy in this list are rather dangerous, so be careful.

[a various Buma factories]

Leon: We tried a several Buma regeneration factories, but we couldn't find any clue about the Buma ho killed Aruth. The time is rushing toward the due and Geena and I were desperate.

[At the traffic light]

Geena: Leon, look at this.

Leon: What? Looks like it's indicating the some of the places. Is it from the Head Quarters?

Geena: No, it can't be. (It's a break in.) <--I couldn't find English word for it.

Leon: A fake info?

Geena: We don't have much time. We have to try it.

Leon: Wanna investigate separately?

[A Buma inside the car. A shopping mall(?)]

Buma: At last, you are all alone. You must have forgotten about me, but I won't forget. Do you knw what happened to me after that incident? My body was carried to the Police Department. I was disasembled to the parts, and then examined. But, still my memory is.....

[A Buma regeneration factory]

Geena: I'd like to see the B-class Buma Regeneration Data.

Junk Dealer: What are you talking about? We are only licensed to regenerate D-class Buma license.

Geena: To only regenerate D-classes, you spent a lot of money on these equipment, didn't you?

Dealer: Yes, that's so.

Geena: Don't play the innocent. We have a proof.

Dealer: Ehhh, oh, I remember.

Geena: Hurry up!

Dealer: Alright, alright.

Geena: Depending on the circumstance, you might have to suspend the business. Don't try a foolish rick on me!

[The shopping mall]

Leon: Sorry, but try another. I don't have money tonight, anyway. Sorry, but I think you are mistaen.

Buma: I can't speak. Please remember. Please break my body into the fragments like that time. Qickly, I beg you. Shoot the bullets into my breast. Kill me quickly. If you don't, I......

[The Buma regeneration Factory]

Dealer: Alright, I'll show you now. I admit we have regenerated the B-class Buma. The Buma who comitted climes even once are forbidden, nevertheless the demands for the female Buma are unending.

Geena: Wait, bring it back. Is this Buma, too?

Dealer: Yes, it is. It's an A-class, too. Our best product. No other factories can regenerate soething like this.

Geena: Where is it now? Hey, I'm asking you what happened to it.

Dealer: It's not here now. We did regenerate it, however a particular image refused to be deletedby any means from its memory file....

[The shopping mall]

Leon: Shit!

Buma: Go on, shoot me, shoot more, sever my skin, tear my flesh off, spread my organs about. If yo don't, I must....

Leon: You bitch.

Buma: To you, to the one who killed me for the first time.....

Leon: It has proven that the Buma who killed Aruth was an illegal product, and therefore Aruth's inurance money was paid. When I was almost getting killed by the Buma, I was thinking, "Buma, just a achine doll, matches better than human in this crazy city....." and such.
