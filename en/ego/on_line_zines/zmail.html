The FACTSHEET FIVE Electronic Mail address list ::: Release 4.0, 9/23/90

This is a list of people in some way involved with fanzines and other
small press and independent media activities who are reachable via
electronic mail. It is in alphabetical order by the person's name, unless
no name is available, in which case it is alphabetical by the publication
name.

Most of the people listed here are available via the Internet by one
set of contortions or another. I know how to do this for people on Fido,
MCI Mail, and Compuserve. If there are direct Internet gateways to MCI Mail,
GEnie, Delphi, Connect, America Online, or Prodigy, I would appreciate details.

Additions and corrections may be sent to Mike Gunderloy at either
ffmike@well.sf.ca.us or Compuserve 72771,275.

Zine names are in ALL CAPS.

Changes in this version:
- ^ indicates a new entry
- # indicates an amended entry

Hints for Net Navigators:
------------------------
If you would like an Internet address of your own, I recommend signing on to
the WELL, the system run by Whole Earth Review, where this listing is also
available for downloading. Call 415-332-6106 (1200 baud) or 415-332-7190 (2400
baud) and enter "newuser" as your login to register.

From Internet to Compuserve address nnnnn,rrr the address to use is
nnnnn.rrr@compuserve.com [NOTE that the comma is changed to a period]

From Internet to MCI Mail address nnn-nnnn the address to use is
nnn-nnnn@mcimail.com. Or if you know the MCI Mail account name you can send it
to account@mcimail.com.

From Internet to Fidonet user Joe Smith at a:bbb/ccc.0 the address to use is
joe.smith@fccc.nbbb.za.fidonet.org.

For more information the best source I know of is Donnalyn Frey & Rick Adams,
!%@:: A DIRECTORY OF ELECTRONIC MAIL ADDRESSING AND NETWORKS, $26.95 from
O'Reilly & Associates, 632 Petaluma Ave., Sebastopol, CA 95472.

"FF BBS" in the Email address section means that the person is a fairly
regular caller to the FACTSHEET FIVE Bulletin Board (518-479-3707,
3/12/2400 baud, 24 hours), which is also the home system of this list.
