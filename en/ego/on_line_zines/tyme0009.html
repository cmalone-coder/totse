T.Y.M.E. #9 Anarchy and Video Gamez


===============================================================================
|| T.Y.M.E. - Twisted Young Minds Expand || # 1, by Talen Kardde 07/13/94 ||
===============================================================================

For those of you who don't know too bad for those who do I hope these
files shed some light on my personality...

This TYME its about Anarchy Files and Video Games

===============================================================================
|| Anarchy Files ||
===============================================================================

I would like to consider myself an expert on anarchy files i have about 110
megs worth of um on my computer. Most of them i've tried even tho the warning
labels say "DO NOT TRY FOR ENTERTAINMENT PURPOSES ONLY", what a joke do they
really think people aren't going to try them? Why make them if no one uses
them?

Most of them suck real bad (Nitephun.txt, Kmartphun.txt) but there are some
awesome ones out there (anything with the Jolly Roger's name on it). I've
tried
lots of them and i must admit some of them are much more dangerous then you'd
think. Thermite is gr8 stuff, burns through just about anything. I've tried
all the recipes for nitroglyc but none of them worked. My personal fav to play
with is napalm!!!! sticks to anything and burns for ever!!!

I wonder who writes these things and why? I mean sure they're cool but not
cool enough to spend hours writing. The only ones i can see being useful are
anything haveing to do with carding, hacking, and phreaking. And those are the
ones that no matter what if you get caught your going to jail (but hell the
piggies are so stupid they have a hard time chasing a Mexican down a back alley
let alone chasing down an illegal phone call...

===============================================================================
|| Video Games ||
===============================================================================

Is their a kid in America who can say they've never played a video game? MKII
Virtua Fighters and Super SFII Turbo are cool games but how could a person
spend gobs of money on them as you see some of these people doing? The only
time i can ever get into them is when I'm stoned and then i can't even hit the
enemy let alone beat him... I have spent lots on them but i can stop when i
want to (ya right). Who is tired of all these different SFII's why not just
bring out a SFIII? I don't know a bout you but i got real tired of all these
new versions and new moves with every version. Just as i learned all the moves
on Super Street Fighter 2 Super Turbo comes out with something like 6 new
moves per player not counting super moves. Now MKII that game kicks ass!!! i
am a god at it and I know every move and trick in the game (drop me email on
any BBS for the txt file i wrote on it) Fatalities kick butt and without blood
the game would suck dick. Can you imagine how bad it's going to suck on snes.
Virtua Fighters is the GAME tho that game is so trippy to watch even when your
not fucked up. Cube people kick ass whats up with the name couldn't they have
come up with a better one like maybe Polygon Warriors? Ok so it's not as cool
as virtua fighters but it describes the game better.
