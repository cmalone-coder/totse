A few months ago, I wrote a TANJfile titled "On the Origins of Derf". Now
in the interests of fairness, I will admit that I told my side of the
story, as I saw it. However, there is to every story another side. So, I
am going to give my loyal readership a view INSIDE the insidious Angel's
Lair, where the mighty Derf himself has seen fit to regale his users
with his version of the events leading up to various things. Once his
say is through, I will give a quick synopsis of the actual calendar of
events, and y'all can decide for yourselves which of us is the holder of
the sacred truth.

I have stripped away such nonsense as post headers, user I.D.'s and post
dates. Anyone interested in such trivia is offered the opportunity to
call Angel's Lair and see for themselves. I have left all of Derf's
numerous & copious spelling errors intact, so that you might get the
"flavor" of Derf in all its glory.
