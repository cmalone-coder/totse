Weisenheimer and Compressionism. (Cont from ATI 65)
THE PARTICLE POETS
A similar phenomenon was pressing on in the field of poetry between the late 1990's and now. Compresionism took its shape in poetry much the same way it did in painting. Words were collected up from tme immemorial then mooshed down into compressed "pulp" phrases that seemed to say almost nothing, atfirst read. Further study would show that they took at least a little thought to build.
In the example "Ode to Odes" by the still famous Franco-American poet Carello Sands Burgero, at firt it seems to make no sense whatsoever:

Ode to ode an ode an.
I Splendid how odes of ode.
Ode odic an what oh, and odes to ode, my.
Ago centuries 21 it received I
Is this "my ode" ode odious;

Further study shows that if you read Burgero's "Ode" backwards it makes a little more sense.
Hence:

This is my ode to odes.
Odious ode my ode, this is.
I received it 21 centuries ago,
My ode to odes, and oh, what an odic ode.
Ode of odes, how splendid is
An ode to an ode to odes.

Particle Comprssionism came to the fore in the work of such poets as Burgero as well as Neil P. Eugne (1990-2007) J. Edgar Oem (1988-2025) and Allen Kernsberg (1941-pres.) Kernsberg, Oem and Burgero ere all trained at the Virginese schools, but all felt it only fair that they do with words what othrs were doing with brushes. Like paintings, some of these poems found some unusual things on the pags right from the poets' heads. In "The Winged Creature" by J. Edgar Oem legend has it that Oem's manscript contains actual illustrations on every other page using decoupaged composted vegetables rightout of his kitchen. Modern technology kept the texture and the smells in each print but managed to kep the bacteria from traveling book to book. By the turn of the century, programmers had gotten realy good at debugging such virus'. No one has seen Oem's original manuscript after publication of the opies distributed. Rumor has it that the entire book of galley sheets is under his wife's Corsican mnt patch in their herb garden.
A little must be said about Neil P. Eugene who in 1990 was born in a cottage in New Japan, Connectiut. That would suffice.
COMPRESSIONISM IN MUSIC
Music was very important to all the Compressionists, not just the recomposers and song splicers. Paiting was done to fully orchestrated music; poetry was read aloud to such musical instruments as the lack & Decker drill, Norelco's Dial-A-Bean, and of course La Machine.
Car stereos became obsolete at the turn of the century along with cellular phones, as people began earing both on their wrist in a multi-purpose device called the Sony "Hand Stand Man." It allowed pople to fax, phone and groove to old Grateful Dead songs while doing light calisthenics: all the necssities of the modern american workforce worker.
Compressionism reflected this in its popular music in the '00's.
"What Do You Want Me To Do, A Hand Stand, Man?" was the most popular song in 2002 and it managed toremain anonymous until 2007 when style sleuths were able to detect that the rhyme scheme was similarto another by Marc Weisenheimer. He was charged with 1 count of e-mailing a song on the internet wihout a Handel, and let go on grounds that the song was necessary with or without credit whether it lcks prurient interest or not.
Even though the song wasn't made in Virgin, it got him $4000 and is still popular as today's' most mportant work anthem, second only to The Who's "We're Not Gonna Take It."
Other composers true to the Compressionist genre were Warm Milk (1998-pres) Gangsta M.C. (short forMozart Christ) (1990-2005) Spinning Light (2000-pres) and L.M.N.P. Cool Musician (1975-2005)
It can be said Compressionist music comes from many styles but mostly from a cross between Gregoria Harmonies and Baroque MegaPolyphony. Cool Musician was called to do a Miller Light beer commercial ast year and poked harmless fun at Compresssionism when he was heard saying "Sounds Baroque; Tastes ike chicken."
A little more ought be said about Marc Weisenheimer who was born 1964 in Rotten Villa, England; the moved to Virgin, Virginia in 1003 to see the beginning of Compressionism. That's enough.

SCHOOL OF THE AMERICAS by Shelly Stern. 6feb97
School of the Americas. When I first heard the phrase I tried to imagine what this place could be abut. Was it an institution of higher education that initiates and encourages dialogue between our norhern and southern neighbors? Was it a non-profit organization that taught the true history of our cotinents? I was pretty optimistic with the fairly inclusive title. This did not leave me fully prepard for the hidden truths I was soon to discover. All I had to hear was the title of the video "Schoolof the Assassins" and I knew I was in for some grim news. Once again I learned how our government isundoubtedly responsible for the deaths of thousands of people in Latin America. Our tax money is goig to support torture, rape and murder.
The school of the Americas (SOA) has been in existence for about 50 years now; first in Panama to utimately "combat" communism and today in Fort Benning, Georgia to essentially crush socialist though. It wasn't some ignorant soul who one day decided that the "School of the Assassins" was a better nme for the place, but rather a passionate reaction to the discovery that graduates from the school wre committing disgusting acts of violence on their own people. SOA students aren't taking Non-violen Conflict Resolution Skills: 101 or even a mediocre leadership course like I might have hoped. Thesepeople are taught "how-can-I-get-away-with-torturing-raping-killing-entire-villages-of-peasants-who-re-becoming-empowered-by-an-honest-liberation-theology-driven-church-worker-and-get-away-with-it: 10." Let's face it, with former panamanian dictator, Manuel Noriega as an "honored" graduate, what mor do we need to concur that something is just a little bit corrupt with the activities of SOA?
