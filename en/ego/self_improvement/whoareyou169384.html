Who Are You?

by Sparrowhawk

Reguardless of age, sex, or race, all of us are living a lie. We are taught to be independant, defend freedom and fight injustice. Yes, right before us atrocities against our moral code are ignored. We are being controlled by the media, yet we are happy. Your opinion has no relevence. Your actions are inconsequential.

We each wake up every morning, not to live our life free as we are promised, but to fuel our 'economy'. When was the last time you were honestly content with your life? "True, I am not content with my life, but hell, life isn't fair, deal with it." Who is to say that we can't be happy? Why CAN'T we go out and be ourselves rather then a mindless drone? Becuase it is unproductive. Now, you might ask yourself "who is this evil person that dictates my life?". The president? Senate? No, although our government does play a large role in my general discontent, there are more powerful monstrocities then this. Airline industries. Oil companies. These capitalistic groups that dictate our every movement are the source of our pain. Propeganda makes us content with what we have. Aren't you the least bit curious of what you could attain given proper oportunities? That, my friend is the thought pattern you must avoid! OPPORTUNITIES!? What is that supposed to mean? We have unlimited potential, but to achieve more then our megar existance, we need the figurative green light from our controllers.

"If life is really this bad, then why don't we rise up against it?" Slaves like us can't be trusted, so we are administed tranquilizers, telling us that we are happy. Instead of "CIVILIAN/SLAVE CALMING PROPAGANDA" we call this "media". IT SERVES NO PURPOSE THEN TO STOP YOU FROM BEING FREE.

Honestly, you are fucking nothing but a slave. A slave to those more powerful, without someone to force them to wiait for an opportunity. Look at your government! Elected to represent the people?! I know few people who are on their economic level. I know few who are as unrestricted in their opportunity to dominate. I know none else who abuse their power so flagrantly. No, they are not ment to REPRESENT, but to give you the false idea that what you think matters. Why does anyone want your opinion? They are perfectly happy in their lives, and no thoughts of helping those with less freedom crosses their minds!

With the money spent on cosmetics directed towards more practical matters, a state of near world peace is not too far fetched! Why do we care so much about our outward apearance? I THOUGHT we were supposed to look at people for inner beauty? No one wants to appear shallow. But looks mean success. Inner beauty means about as much as my opinion in the great scheme of things. "But I'm a different person! I don't care what people think of me. I don't care about my outer looks!" Shut the fuck up. If you didn't care what others thought, then why are you saying this?

So, what are we to do about this? Nothing. Thats the beuaty of the system. Unless the majority of the slaves revolt, nothing cna be done. And media tranquilizers quell thoughts like these. Banding togehter in an uber-slave revolt is too far fetched: we would be cut down withing 2 days of forming our ideas. No, we must strike in one decesive action, with no time to quell the rebellion, and no chance to defend against the dark tide of change.

No, my opinion matters little. So does yours. You are nothing. Anything that comes from you is nothing. Why do you bother to live? Simply to feed those above you, clinging on to our media addiction. Give up, it isn't worth it.

-=Sparrowhawk=-
