Thinking in a Completely Different Way

by Joey

I write this as an attempt to change the thinking methods of the general public, in order to benefit the present and future well-being of all mankind. This is the truth, and hopefully the strong will have enough sense to stop believing in the bullshit brain-washing their minds.

I hate to say this, because I myself have relied on this figure many years of my life, but there is no God. God is just a way that people cope with all of the stress going on in their lives. They believe in him because they're too stupid to think about the truth. Sure, perhaps Jesus was a real person who performed what others considered miracles, but he did it without the help of some supernatural being watching over us all. When people sit in church to pray to God, all that they are really doing is limiting their knowledge of the universe by praising the sky, like some unrealistic cartoon.

People need to learn to use more than a small portion of their brain, and take their thinking to a higher level, observing and analyzing every possible detail. Virtually everyone goes about their life limiting what they can do, because others tell them that it's not possible. People only tell them that it's not possible because they themselves are scared of being surpassed. They go about their lives believing in a deity that doesn't exist and working on the same job every day just to make a living to try to make their children have better lives than they had. What's the point? Continuing to go this way does not end up in advancing anywhere in the long run. Children are going to have the same difficulties that their parents had because of being mouth-fed their whole lives, sheltered from the so-called "real world" and beyond. In order to better all of man-kind, the life forms of the human race must realize their full potential. They all must be enlightened, and this can only be done by thinking at the higher level that is not contemplated on by most. Ignorance must end, because there is no absolute truth. All truth are relative, all opinions. The red apple may be a red apple to most, but those blind of color it really is a grey apple. To those with mental illness, the red apple really can be a green orange, and there is nothing that anyone else can do to stop that person from believing their relative truth. In order for world peace to ensure, people must see the other points of view, and must realize that everyone thinks that what they're doing is right. The opposing sides must meet at a compromise point for any hope of love among all.

The only evil that exists is when a person goes against what they themselves think is right, when they are not innocent. People need to stop being negative and have a positive outlook on life. By doing this they will enjoy living, because attaining pleasure and helping others attain pleasure is the meaning of life. Laughing is one of the most satisfying means of pleasure, and three effective aspects to make yourself and others laugh are being random, over-stereotyping and lying obviously. By being in a constant state of euphoric positive pleasure at non-mournful times, people can enjoy life more than they ever would mentally living in their "real world" with stocks and lawsuits. People can even use tools such as music or mind-altering substances, such as alcohol, to help them realize the fullness to the truth that living emotionally free is better than being trapped in your own mind. Living emotionally free allows us to feel and manipulate the energy that connects all living things, the energy that allows us to push ourselves beyond the points of what most believe as limits, into the infinite.

Being pessimistic and depressed gets one no where, because of the simple fact that they have no incentive for moving life along at a fast pace. The faster life moves, the more fun there is to experience, but one must take break every often and catch up on responsibilities. This allows them time to think and evaluate their attitudes and behaviors, changing the attitudes if needed. Behavior should not be based on these realized attitudes though, but rather one should behave in life as being reserved and devious to their true attitudes to strangers, people that don't understand the real them. That way, they can reveal their real attitudes based on behavior when needed. While being reserved and devious to strangers, people should at the same time be comfortable to meet strangers and act positively around them. It is wise for people to always act devious to some extent, never letting anyone but themselves know their absolute thoughts, because it is never known who is watching, and who can be trusted.

The secrets of living successfully can never be completely understood unless experienced. There are too many fucking idiots in the world to keep track of who really knows what is going on. Naive hypocrites dominate the population of all societies, trying to say that what they think is right. All that they really care about is the well-being of them selves. People should fuck it all, and not care about what others think. They should stop living their pointless lives, controlled by work and fear, and have enough courage to try to do what really matters in the big picture of it all. They should try to eliminate pain in others, for it is in dealing pain to others that people are blinded from seeing other people's relative truths. People should take their jobs way less seriously, which actually results in improved efficiency. Focusing too hard on simple work tasks fogs the mind, which in turn declines the speed and quality of work performed. Living each second of every day as if it is the last is the best way to go, because that day very well may be Armageddon. Each day should include time to take in the glories of nature, both ugly and beautiful, and be thankful for what is already acquired.

Observation is the key to predicting. The more that behavior is observed, the higher the chance is of an accurate analysis of their attitudes. The enlightened move just as fast as those who rush through life without inspecting anything around them, and at the same time observe just as much as the people who go too slow to get anywhere. It is only worthwhile for people to prove their abilities to themselves, not to others. When they prove to themselves, they gain more confidence, courage and skill. When they prove to other people, they lose the knowledge that can protect them. It is very tempting to share proof of ability and other secrets though, because of the satisfaction it gives of being positively reinforced. Only by resisting the temptation of becoming famous can a person actually become more knowledgeable about the universe and its secrets. Other temptations, such as those of the sexual nature and of the eating nature, are the strongest that can be felt, and the hardest to control, but they must be tamed at certain times for a person to completely be in control of themselves. There are many other things that are difficult to tempt, and an example of this, so I have heard from numerous sources, is marijuana. It is a very tempting substance because of the feelings that it gives the user, but in the long run it limits the ability to take action, and also provides less vivid dreams. When making a decision of action, one must consider to use the heart and the brain equally. The heart does what the emotions of love and hate tell it to, and the brain does what is the best decision without any emotions. It is only by using a balanced combination can the most accurate decisions be made that adhere to that person's point of view, without any regret.

A few people do realize what is really going on around them, and this can cause for an odd, yet useful, effect. When people realize that almost every action around them is pointless, they begin to feel like they are living reality as a dream. Environments begin to feel fake for them, and every around them seems stupid in comparison to them. This feeling provides for an expanded observation and analysis of all, an expanded state of mind, useful for breaking physical, mental, and spiritual barriers. It is only in breaking these barriers that the world can become a better place. Even though it all feels fake, it is important for those people to remember not to perform any actions that could hurt any lives, both their own and those of others. By hurting lives, the fun of dreaming actuality would be spoiled, and they would be forced into a non-pleasing reality, and the mind would return to its formal state of thinking.

To conclude, I ask of all readers of this to change their lives, after the moment of taking this all in. I ask of them to observe and analyze as much as possible, while in the process seeing things from all view points, keeping a completely open mind. I ask of them to allow emotions to go unsuppressed and attempt to break all physical, mental, and spiritual barriers that others have placed on them. I ask of them to view life through a positive outlook, aiming to negate all pain around them, placing others before themselves.. I ask of them to make efficient decisions that they think themselves are really the right decisions to make, balanced with the heart and mind, leaving room for compromise with others. I ask of them to see the big picture of all, and feel the energy in all. I ask of them to free themselves of limits, and know what is really possible. I ask them to live life at a neck-breaking speed, while at the same time taking in the view around them, and taking breaks to contemplate and help others out. By everyone doing all of this, I promise that the future of humans will begin to better at an infinite rate, improving the well-being of the present and future universe in which we live.
