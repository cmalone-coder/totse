It was full of light brown nuts - but I don't remember eating any corn.

were green streaks along the sides.

That was the part that was floating. It had broken in half and the sunken
end wasn't oblong at all. The sunken end was more like a lumpy muddy ping pong
ball.

I wiped my ass and there was blood.

My asshole tingled as I noticed the larva wiggling in the stool. Their
wiggling sent the chunk of defecation on a slight counterclockwise spin.

I wiped my ass again and there was less blood.

I pressed the lever and watched my prize go spinning away until it finally
vanished in the sinking whirlpool.
