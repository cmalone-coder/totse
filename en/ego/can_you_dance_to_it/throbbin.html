Throbbing Gristle Discography

Subject: TG Discography

::::::::::::::
Throbbing Gristle Discography
::::::::::::::

On Industrial Records

IR0002 "Second Annual Report", LP
IR0003 "United / Zyklon B Zombie" (IMO, one of their best songs), single
IR0004 "D.O.A. The Third and Final Report", LP
IR0008 "Twenty Jazz Funk Greats", LP
IR0009 "Heathen Earth", LP
IR0013 "SUBHUMAN/Something Came Over Me", single
IR0015 "ADRENALIN/Distant Dreams (Part Two)", single

On Fetish

FR2001 "Second Annual Report", LP re-release
FR2001 "Second Annual Report", LP re-re-release
FR2001 "Second Annual Report", LP re-re-re-release
FET006 "DISCIPLINE", 45 rpm 12"

"A Boxed Set" incl. "Second Annual Report", "D.O.A", "Twenty Jazz Funk
Greats", "Heathen Earth" and "Mission Of Dead Souls", a live LP of the last
Performance.

On Sordide Sentimental

SS45001 "We Hate You (Little Girls)/Five Knuckle Shuffle, single

On Nice Records - "You Don't Know/Damaru Sunrise", single

On Rough Trade - ROUGH US 23 "Throbbing Gristles Greatest Hits", LP

On Zensor Records - Zensor01 "Funeral In Berlin", LP

On Mental Decay Records - "Special Treatment", LP (very good!)

On American Phonography - "Fuehrer der Menscheit", 10" LP

On Expanded Music

"Rafters - TGs Psychic Rally", LP
"Mission is Terminated", double LP

On Casual Abandon - "Once Upon A Time...", LP

On Karnage Records - "The Psychic Sacrifice", double LP (excellent!)

On Illuminated Records

"Editions.. Frankfurt... Berlin...", LP
"The Industrial Records Story", compilation LP or IR singles

On Spurt Records - "The First Annual Report of TG", LP

On Mute Records

Re-releases of all LPs released on IR + "Missions Of Dead Souls"
"CD1", compact disk

Unknown labels (at least I don't know)

"Live at Death Factory, May '79", Picture Disk LP with flash logo/naked girl

"Grief", LP

A cassette tape of every live performance they did was available by mail
order from IR. Some very nice material among them.

In addition there were/are some live videos from IR and Target Video. I've
got "Heathen Earth", "Live at Oundle School", "Mission Of Dead Souls" and
"Destiny".

Most of the still available disk releases are bootlegs of live performances,
hi-quantity/low-quality, play it loud!!

I know there's more of everything. I stopped collecting...
