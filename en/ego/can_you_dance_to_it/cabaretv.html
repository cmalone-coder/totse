+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Cabaret Voltaire was formed in 1972/73 by Kirk, Mallinder & Watson. Watson
left after 2 X 45 to form Hafler Trio.

Cabaret Voltaire's career can roughly be divided in three parts:
experimental electronic (1974 - 1982)
industrial/electro funk (1983 - 1987)
techno/house (1989 - present)

OTHER INFO:

Cabaret Voltaire recorded a session for BBC Radio One's John Peel, broadcast
25th June 1981. Tracks were 'Walls of Jericho', 'Greensboro', 'Jazz the Glass'
and 'Black Mask'.

Chakk 12" Out of the Flesh, Doublevision DVR 6 1984, says "Special thanks to
Richard H. Kirk and Steven Mallinder".

Workforce 12" Skin Scraped Back, Doublevision DVR 13 1985, says "Discreet
edit and thanks: Richard H. Kirk"

Workforce 12" Back in the Good Books, Rorschach Testing Product ROR5 1986,
says "special thanks and a bottle of bourbon to Richard H. Kirk, Stephen
Mallinder"

Mute/Grey Area released CABS 15CD, MAL 1CD, and KIRK 1CD as a boxed set. It's
just the three regular CD's in a "Grey Area" sleeve/box.
