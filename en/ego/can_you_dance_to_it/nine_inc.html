Nine Inch Nails Discography (off the net)

NINE INCH NAILS DISCOGRAPHY
By: paul@ais.org (141.211.206.17)
last update: December 10, 1992

This list needs vinyl, cassette, other countries, and whatever
other format input! And don't forget bootlegs too.

Does anyone know if the Down In It CDS was released twice?
I have it in a full size jewel box and my other singles
are in slimlines, that's why I ask.

All track times taken from CD. If not available
from the CD, I took the times off of my CD player.

CD = Compact Disc CS = Cassette
CDS = Compact Disc Single 12" = 12 inch record
CD5 = Compact Disc E.P.
