Broken Movie:
Trent had this movie produced with Peter 'Sleazy' Christoherson of COIL.
supposedly, it was not released because of gratutitous violence.
well, we now know it does in fact exist, and was coming out of
a certain LA record store for awhile, which here will remain unnamed.
It contained a synthesized snuff, likely created by Sleazy. In the midst
of this movie was all of the videos legally released from Broken, in the
order of the album. Also in here, was Help Me I Am In Hell, the duration
of the song with a black screen, and a video for Gave Up, with snuff film
rising to a climax. A certain individual posted to alt.music.nin,
claiming he had a letterboxed version which included videos for Last,
and an actual video for help me. . .. This person was angered by folks
on amnin, and said he was sending all of the orders back, so I suppose
we shall never know.

Reznor performs Suck with Pigface members on the PIGFACE video Glitch

Trent's group called The Problems appears in the movie _Light_of_Day_.

A live video will be released from the TDS tours, with heavy film-shooting
done at several shows(Dallas, Omaha, and New Orleans being a few I think.
Corrections appreciated). Considerations are being put forth of doing
a limited theater release before selling the video.
The director is one Simon Maxwell, who I am told has previous
dealings with NIN. They originally intended to be done 3 months after the
tour was completed, but as most things NIN go, are late.
