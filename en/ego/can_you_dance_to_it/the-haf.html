ABOUT THE HAFLER TRIO
=====================

The Hafler Trio was formed in 1981 by Dr Edward Moolenbeek, Christopher R
Watson (formerly a member of Cabaret Voltaire) and Andrew M McKenzie.
It would seem that, some years later (probably between 1987 and 1989,
but that's only a guess), Moolenbeek and Watson ceased any form of
active collaboration. Now the Hafler 'Trio' is a 'collective enchanted
loom' centered around Mr McKenzie and receiving inspiration by many
different people, among whom are Zbigniew Karkowski, John Duncan, Annie
Sprinkle, Adi Newton (T.A.G.C., CLOCKDVA) and Genesis P-Orridge (Throbbing
Gristle, Psychic TV).

"My definition of music is just organised sound" -- Andrew M McKenzie, 1987

COMPILATION / COLLABORATIONS / SOLO WORKS
=========================================

* NURSE WITH WOUND & THE HAFLER TRIO
[Untitled], C45, d.u., Staaltape {ST 00M}
Original recordings by Nurse With Wound (with the Murray Fontana Orchestra)
mixed, processed and added to by the Hafler Trio.

* VARIOUS ARTISTS
"Devastate to Liberate", LP, d.u., Yangki Records {YANGKI1}
This LP (a benefit compilation to raise funds for the Animal Liberation
Front) includes a short track by the Hafler Trio, "In the Cradle" [1:15].
It also features Annie Anxiety, Coil, Crass, Current 93, Legendary Pink
Dots, Nurse With Wound and others.

* VARIOUS ARTISTS
"The Fight Is On", LP, 1986, L.A.Y.L.A.H. {lay 10}
This compilation includes "Blanket Level Approach" [4:22] by the Hafler Trio,
plus tracks by Coil, Current 93, Nurse With Wound, Organum, Lustmord and
Robert Haigh.

* ANDREW M McKENZIE
"Protection", LP, 1987, Touch {T33:6}

* THE HAFLER TRIO, "Ben, Ruach, Ab, Shaloshethem Yechad Thaubodo"
LUCIANO DARI, "Idrogeni Superiori"
LP + booklet, 1987, Musica Maxima Magnetica {eee 01}
Limited edition of 1,428 copies.
These are actually two different titles that happen to share the same vinyl.

* VARIOUS ARTISTS
"Q.E.D.", 2LP + 7", 1988, NL-Centrum / Play It Again, Sam! {NLC 001}
A short track by the Hafler Trio ("Restimulation") appears on this
compilation.

* THE HAFLER TRIO & THEE TEMPLE OV PSYCHICK YOUTH
"Dreamachine", LP/CD + booklet + cardboard dreamachine, 1989,
KK Records {KK 015}
Limited edition, dedicated to the memorial of Brion Gysin and his Dreamachine.
Included music should only be used to enhance the effect of the dreamachine.
The CD format includes a track not present on the LP.
Also released in 1990 a video [VideoPhile (?)] of the dreamachine
installation performed at Aorta (a squatted newspaper building),
Amsterdam, on February 13, 1988.

* MOTHER TONGUE
"Open in Obscurity", LP, 1990, Touch {TO:10}
Mother Tongue is Z'ev, Andrew M McKenzie and Dorothea Franck.
A track by Mother Tongue appears on the compilation
"A Bead to a Small Mouth" [LP/CD, Barooni {BAR001}].

* JOHN DUNCAN & ANDREW M McKENZIE
"Contact", CD, 1990, Touch {TO:17}

* GENESIS P-ORRIDGE & THE HAFLER TRIO
"Alaura / Slave Priest", miniCD, 1990, Nuovi Equilibri {CD 170}
Included with the book "Psychic TV / Genesis P-Orridge -- A Coumprehensive
Collection Ov Lyrics 1981-90", Stampa Alternativa / Nuovi Equilibri

* VARIOUS ARTISTS
"The Tyranny of the Beat", CD, 1991, Mute Records / Grey Area
This compilation includes 2 cuts by the Hafler Trio ("A Thirsty Fish",
"The Dirty Fire"), plus tracks by Cabaret Voltaire, Throbbing Gristle,
SPK and others.

* WILLEM DE RIDDER & THE HAFLER TRIO
"Snuff", CD + 32pp booklet, 1992, Touch {SPL:2}
Volume two of the Spiral audio-magazine.
Volume one in the Spoken Word series.
This CD contains two oral pieces by Willem de Ridder, plus a musical
interlude by the Hafler Trio, "Projection".
