This is my current list. I have listed everything that I own, regardless
of how bad it is. So, if you are looking for that unlistenable Cure show
from the L.A. forum in 1987, I have it. I currently have a few things
that I have not mastered and these are noted in the list. However, they
are still available for trade. When you see an "A" next to source, that
means that there is appreciable crowd noise. Source means the manner
in which I first received the material. Length, when listed usually refers
to the tape length on which material resides. Sound ratings as follows:
EX - Excellent (soundboard, or excellent audience tape),
VG - Very Good ( genrerally an audience tape, low crowd noise),
F - Fair (a lot of crowd noise or average instrument definition),
P - Poor (Self explanatory), R - Radio (Taped off of radio).
Sources are as follows: T - Tape, C - CD, V - LP.

All requests, along with tapes should be sent to:
