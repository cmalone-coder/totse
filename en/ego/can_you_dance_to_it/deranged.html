Quisen Creations Sep 1, 1988 12:45 Pm

Well,Once When I Was A Younger Lass... I Went On The Meditererrean Cruise With
My Rich Aunt And Uncle,She Looked Like Mrs Cunningham From Happy Days And He
Much Resembled James Dean,Only A Little Pudgier,,, I Fell In Love With Several
Men On This Cruise Even Though I Myself Was Only Approaching 16,The Ship Had
Very Odd Toilets And After Using One I Just Couldnt Figure Out How To Flush
It,Much To My Dismay,And Even More To My Embarrassment Was The Fact That The
Platform Over The Facilitywas Actually A Stage On Which Bruce Willis Performed
Nightly,Well Anyway...My Aunt Needed One Of The Several Men That Adored Me To
Marry Me,So,Picking The Richest Man,She Sent Victor Over To My Estate That Was
Situated On The North End Of This Very Large Ship.Before Victor Could
Arrive,One Of My Other Handsome Suitors Was Trying To Bribe One Of The Guards
At My Gate To Let Him,The Poor Fellow Was So Eager To See Me,That In His
Anxiousness Beat The Guard Over The Head With A Small Stick,Stunning Him Only
Momentarily But Long Enough To Slip In...

He Was Beautiful,He Had Long Wavy Blond Hair,Like Those Men In The Gap
Commercials,As I Too Had Very Long Hair,Which Flew Over My Shoulders When He
Wisked Me Up Intp His Arms In A Long Embrace..

I Decided That He Was The One For Me,Not That Victor ,Who Was Taking An Awful
Long Time To Show Up So I Led My Love To The Long Halls Of My Palace,Situated
On The Meditererrean Cruise Ship...

We Both Gazed In Wonderment At Some Of Monet'S Finest Paintings ,Which Are A
Part Of My Vast Personal Art Collection,However As His Eyes Lay Transfixed On
The Portrait,I Felt Myself Drawn To The End Of The Hall,And For Every Step I
Took,My Hair Became Shorter And Shorter,And Blacker And Blacker,My Eyes Became
Wild And Hungry,I Came To A Man Who Was Also Dark Ang Hungry,And We Consumed
Each Other,Blood And Bone Spewing Everywhere,I Wiped The Corners Of My Mouth
With A Fine Cloth Napkin And Enjoyed The Remainder Of My Cruise
