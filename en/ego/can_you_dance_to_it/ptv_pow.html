:::A NOTE ON CATALOG NUMBERS:::
Quite a few of the catalog numbers, particularly those issued by Temple
Records, appear to be incredibly out of order. From what I understand, this
is because Temple Records frequently has (or has had) to push back/shuffle
release dates on many releases, but has opted to retain the original catalog
designations. So, if TOPY 051 comes before TOPY 043, it's because TOPY 043
was scheduled to come out before 051, but was pushed back. It's a bit
confusing when you look though the list, but keep that in mind and you should
be okay. :)
