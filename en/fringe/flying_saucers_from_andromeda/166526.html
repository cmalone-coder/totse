Australian Encounters

by Simon

Some of the dates I cannot fully remember as being the typical teenager, I forget to write things down...

All this occured in 2002 Early July (6th?) on a friday night I had my first hardcore sighting of a UFO.

I live in Toowoomba of Australia

I would like to stress that I was under the influence of no drugs including alcohol.

I was coming home one night from a mate's place in Toowoomba. It was around 1am in the morning and I was driving through my neighbourhood. As I came over this hill that leads to my home I saw a brihgt light above and beyond the house at the end of the road(the road ends as a T intersection and I would turn left). At first I looked at it and thought "wow that is a bright star" but I realised stars aren't that big, as it seemed too close for a star. Suddenly the warning from a mate of mine came to mind: "The greys want to use us, just be careful of what happens to you"

My mind filled with fear of what it could be, then suddenly the light dropped out of sight, straight down.

Frightened, I turned at the corner and made towards my driveway and turned right into my driveway. I stopped in my driveway and looked towards the right... north east from me i could see the light had risen back up.

Quickly I drove into the garage and went to bed to forget what i saw...

The next night I forgot about it and went to work, i got home before midnight...

The next night was a sunday night, 2 days after the first on this weekend, and I was at the some mate's home on the other side of town. My house is just outside of town...

I was cruisng home at 1am I new what the time was because I had stopped to roll a cigarrette on my way home...

To continue, I was coming up over the hill and I saw the light again. I slowed right down and thought "Holy crap". The light then moved at what i figure to be quite fast as it kept its distance and cirled the car counter clockwise all the way to my driveway... As I pulled into the driveway it stayed to the south of me(stayed behind me).

(As you can read I don't have full details of driving down the hill as I do not actually remember driving down the hill after I saw the light)

The next thing I did was called stupid by everyone a told the story to except for my friend from Brisbane who has had some hardcore encounters.

I got out of the car and turned to look at this light (I figure if they wanted me they could hold be still so getting out of the car is no different to satyng in it). I watched it for a moment and without warning it flew at amazing speed and acceleration south. It was gone.

I'd like to mention here that 30km somewhere south of toowoomba there is a secret US conrtolled base of some sort. I know this because some army guys i know don't keep quiet when drunk, and have found the place...

(As I went to my room I read the clock said 2:30am)

Anyway, I went to bed and was scared out of my wits, but I eventually fell asleep.

About 2-3 weeks later I was on MSN talking to my mate in Brisbane about my experiences and he was scared. He told me of some things I could do etc. In the middle of my explainign what happened the power dimmed and as i tried quickly to tpye the powers gonna go it blacked out. This was around midnight.

(Our neighbourhood's power system is dodgey and does often power down, but you'll read wh ythis was different outage)

I sat in the dark for about five minutes saying shit and waited for my eyes to focus. I walked out of the computer room and looked out the window to the street. To my surprise the street lights were on, as well as some of the neighbours houses. At this I freaked and went to bed. I sat for a while unable to sleep wondering what the hell is going on. Then I noticed a sound I don't normally hear at night. It was a weird low hum. I blacked out...

I woke up the next day at about 12:30pm(lunchtime) and felt wrecked, like you do with a hangover. I went to the bathroom and dry wretched for a few minutes...

That is my series of strange encounters this year.

My grandmother is in her late 60's

My grandma told me a month back when I talked to her about them. SHe told me of an encounter I estimated happened close to when I had the blackout.

As she went out to the kitchen for a drink in the middle of the night she came out and poured her drink of water. She noticed light coming in the window and looked out. Hovering over the house of neighbour behind her was a great ball of light. It floated between that neighbours house and the one to my grandma's left. She watched for about 5 mintues and went to get her camera. Next thing she knows it was morning and she was in her bed.

My mate in Brisbane has experienced many encounters in his life from UFO sightings to abductions to mental messages, etc... He is quite knowledgable on this area at the age of 18. He believes everythign he says, so do i. He has even proven on several occasions that he can read minds (emotion thoughts).

If you can help me with further understanding my encounter or would like to know more about other experiences of my mate's please email me.
