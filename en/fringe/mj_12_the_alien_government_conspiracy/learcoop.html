Michael:

As you know, I had posted a general notice on ParaNet Alpha to the effect that
anyone knowingly posting false or misleading information regarding government
involvement in UFO investigation, or any other paranormal field, would be given
the boot. I've done it before, and I'll continue to do it. I didn't want anyone
to get the impression that I was singling out Cooper. I ixnayed a guy named
Lemming for the same thing over a year ago.

Before he began his public posting of controversial files, Cooper had been
privately sending me files containing much the same information -- names of
black projects and their stated goals. Some of those project names we had heard
before: MJ-12, Aquarius, Sigma, Snowbird, etc. Some were new: Plato, Excalibur,
etc. I was told to forward the information to researcher Stan Friedman for
verification, which I did. I also made it available to several other sources
whom I knew to have very high security clearances within the intelligence
community, and who counted themselves UFO believers. I believed Cooper's
information was 90% regurgitation of what was known publicly, and 10%
embellishment, some of which was inspired by Lear's information, some inspired
by information from another bogus "insider" named Jeff Felix. Stan Friedman was
unable to substantiate any of Cooper's claims, and my intelligence sources
actually had a good laugh over it. These sources will remain anonymous, despite
any protests from Cooper. He has accused the government in complicity with an
alien takeover plot, and refuses to name his sources; two can play that game.
<contd>

The reason I did not take the same action with John Lear is this: John has
continually stated that his information is nothing more than an hypothesis,
based on information from "confidential informants" and other sources such as
"UFO CRASH AT AZTEC." Wild as his story is, none of it can be directly
contradicted by the public record because there IS no public record. Cooper, on
the other hand, strongly implied that he had direct knowledge that his
information was correct, based on his memory of actually having seen government
documents. He further implied that he was getting much of his info from an
MJ-12 insider, and stated several times that his life was imperiled by passing
it on. His info was full of internal inconsistencies; some of it could be
directly contradicted by known intelligence procedures; and the inability of
Stan Friedman, who is known to be in contact with people claiming to be MJ-12
insiders, to verify Cooper's info, all these factors led to my decision.

I've worked for two and a half years to build ParaNet's credibility as a source
of paranormal information, and I saw that work crumbling under a mountain of
self-aggrandizing messages and cloak-and-dagger files, and most dangerous of
all, many people were coming to associate ParaNet EXCLUSIVELY with this sort of
activity. While we pride ourselves on having the inside story, brother, that
ain't it.

Hope this makes it clearer.

Jim
