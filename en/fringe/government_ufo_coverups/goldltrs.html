The subject of UFOs is one that has interested me for some time.
About ten or twelve years ago I made an effort to find out what was in

is stored that has been collected by the Air Force, and I was
understandably denied this request.

there is a plan under way to release some, if not all, of this
material in the near future. I'm just as anxious to see this material
as you are, and I hope we will not have to wait too much longer.

Sincerely.
