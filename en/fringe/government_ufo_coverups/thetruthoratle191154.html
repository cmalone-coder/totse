The Truth (or at least part of it)

by Paul Dedrick

My father, a retired cargo load master for the U.S. Air Force, had revealed to me that he held a classification rank two levels above top secret. After he told me this we got talking on his experiences in the air force, cause naturally i was intrigued to know what he did before i was born. What we discussed was more mind boggling for me than anything i had ever heard.

While talking we got into where he went and what he did. I asked him if area 51 was real and if he was ever there, of course, he said yes it was real and no ufo is there right now. what area 51 was, was a base working on a top secret aircraft, the stealth aircraft/bomber, at the time of the incident. that seemed true since stealth came out only less than ten years later. We laughed and then he told me that he was at the base where the alien craft was. While telling me, he expressed that he was in more fear of his life at this base than he had ever been in his life. They were to drop off supplies picked up at an andrews air base in germany and transport them to an undisclosed location, no one knew where they were going. He said they travelled at least 3 hours and when descending he saw that they were out in a desert-like area, no signs of any buildings or cities or even life. Their runway was a long, smooth, strip of desert land that stretched to a mountain. once they landed and drove the plane towards the mountain, he could see the rocks moving to the side and opening up. I asked if it was'nt just doors painted to resemble a mountain side rock formation. he told me no it was the actual rocks from the mountain that were moving. the plane was driven inside as soon as the plane was in side the doors closed and men in long black coats quickly surrounded the plane in a circle formation. A man dressed in civilian clothing went on board and told my dad that they were to stand in a designated area right beside the plane. That designated area happened to be a circle no bigger than two suvs, where the pilot and the my dad and his team were to stand in while they unloaded the supplies. When they were in the circle the man told them, with no expression or sympathy in his voice, that if they step over the line they will be shot. That is when i asked if he meant all of them or just the one person that stepped out of line would be shot, he didnt know because he said they were all kinda fearing for their lives once he said that to them. I then wanted to know how he knew for a fact that that was the place where the alien ship was. He told me that when they were unloading the supplies from their cargo plane. they took it into a a huge room right next to the "hangar" that they were in. He was only able to see a little inside the place but he knows for a fact that he saw the tip of an aircraft he believed was a wing, he knew it wasnt any he had ever seen nor has ever seen still to this day because the metal and the look was of something out of a movie it looke too rough and thick to be anything out their today. once they were done moving all of the supplies out they were told to immediately get back on the plane and leave. That was just one conversation with my dad. There were plenty more after this one.
