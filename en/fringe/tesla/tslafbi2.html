76- ***..Jan/30/1950 | FBI Memo L.B. Nichols | Mr. Tolson |
Summary->These next three pages are too important to be summarized,

XXXXXXX called yesterday and said he wanted to see me.
I asked him (?) if there was anything special he wanted to discuss.
He said yes two things.
1. The case of Nikola Tesla and Abraham N. Spanel,XXXXXXXXXXXX
Our files reflect that Nicola Tesla was one of the world's
outstanding scientist and in fact designed the generators installed
at Niagara Falls. He died in New York on January 7, 1943, and is
supposed to have left details and plans for a so-called death ray.
XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX.
Our files also reflect that Colonel Erskine of Military Intelligence
called us on Jan. 9,1943, advising that Tesla had died, that A. Spanel
had communicated with the War Department regarding this death, that
Tesla had a nephew named Sava Kosanovich who had taken possession of
Tesla's papers and Spanel thought the papers might be used against our
government.
We made an immediate inquiry in New York and the first report was
that Kosanovich and others entered Tesla's room with the aid of a
locksmith, broke into a safe containing some of Tesla's valuable
papers and formula.
Coincident with this, on Jan. 8, L.M.C. Smith called Mr. Tamm
regarding the death of Tesla and Smith stated he was talking to
the Alien Property Custodian about seizing these items.
We interviewed Spanel who expressed concern over Tesla's effects
and Spanel stated that Kosanovich had turned over the effects of
Tesla to the Alien Property Custodian. Spanel further stated the
day DEFORE Tesla died, he tried to get in touch with the War
Department to make available certain patents.
77-***.....Cont.-76 In 1945, we talked to a Private Bloyce Fitzgerald,

It was very clear we had no responsibility for Tesla's effects,
that the Alien Property Custodian seized them and we learned
later that Naval authorities made microfilms of all his papers.
Kosanovich communicated with the Bureau on March 29, 1950, and
under date of April 3, 1950, in response to his request for the
microfilms of the papers of Tesla, who was a relative of
Kosanovich, we advised Kosanovich that this Bureau had never
been in possession of Tesla's papers.
Senator XXXXXXX furnished the Bureau five communications
