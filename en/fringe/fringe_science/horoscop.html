Your Horoscope



QUESTIONS
For every planet, sign, and house in our horoscope there are unanswered
questions. We go to astrologers to find out how transits and progressions will
effect our lives in the future. We go outside ourselves looking for answers.
Perhaps by going WITHIN and asking OURSELVES some profound questions we will
become more assured of our future, and our present.
Read the questions & assess as honestly as possible what the answers might be.
This is you, in deep and meaningful conversation with yourself. If any question
makes you feel uncomfortable emotionally, go back to it and meditate on why.
There are probably subconscious memories being brought to the surface, or old
patterns which you have surpressed being stimulated. This can be a clensing and
renewing experience. A personal inventory which can help create a new you!
As you read for the various planets, consider also how these questions might
apply to the house it rules. Of particular importance are planets or houses
receiving heavy transits or progressions at this time.
Here is a list of which planet rules which sign to assist you:
Sun rules Leo
Moon rules Cancer
Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo
Venus rules Taurus and Libra
Mars rules Aries
Jupiter rules Sagittarius
Saturn rules Capricorn
Uranus rules Aquarius
Neptune rules Pisces
Pluto rules Scorpio
I send you Love and Light on your journey.
SUN
1. How centered are you?
2. How able are you to set and achieve goals?
3. How able are you to touch and demonstrate the child within yourself?
4. How self aware are you?
5. What is your self esteem level?
6. Are you able to love self enough to love another? (use your Venus properly)
7. What are your feelings about your own creativity?
8. Can you relate to the father within?
9. Are you commanding and determined? Or overpowering and dominating?
MOON
1. Are you in touch with your feelings?
2. Can you demonstrate emotions easily?
3. Do emotional displays embarrass you or make you fearful?
4. How do you deal with your past and childhood?
5. How do you nurture others?
6. How do you nurture yourself?
7. Are you sensitive or thin-skinned?
8. What (or who) makes you secure emotionally?
MERCURY
1. How do you communicate?
2. Are you as able to communicate "feelings" as "thoughts"?
3. What motivates you intellectually?
4. How easily do you assimilate new ideas?
5. Do you use words as symbols of power? (as in positive affirmations) Or as a
means to demean yourself and others through criticism?
6. Are you discriminating or finicky? (Virgo)
7. Are you versatile and interactive or nervous and flighty? (Gemini)
VENUS
1. How much do you value yourself? (and thus are able to value others?)
2. How easy is it for you to share? (on any level?)
3. What sort of relationships do you attract? (and thus feel you deserve?)
4. What gives you satisfaction?
5. Do you look for beauty only in an "outer" sense? Or can you appreciate your
own inner beauty and that of others?
6. Can you tune into and be comfortable with your physical and sexual needs?
7. Do you value money for its' own sake (as a power symbol) or for the beauty
and quality of the life experience it can give you and others?
8. Is love to you a thing to "get" or a thing to "give"?
MARS
1. How do you handle anger and aggression?
2. How able are you to "act" on ideas?
3. Do you take offence easily?
4. Is your self esteem strong enough that another can disagree with you without
your becoming angry and upset?
5. Are you impatient and impulsive or direct and spontaneous?
6. How able are you to be self directed, self motivated, and self responsibile?
7. Do you blame others for the consequences of your actions?
JUPITER
1. How strong is your faith in yourself?
2. Are religious teachings a springboard to broadened horizons or a set of self
limiting concepts?
3. Are you able to love all mankind, or only those persons who conform to your
cultural, political, and religious beliefs?
4. Do your acitvities and goals have real meaning for you?
5. How able are you to look to the future with optimism?
SATURN
1. Where do you feel the most insecure?
2. What did your father and your relationship with him leave you believing
about yourself?
3. How able are you to be self disciplined?
4. How ambitious are you?
5. How do you handle authority and power? (Your own or that of others)
6. Does the physical world and its challenges leave you depressed and feeling
limited?
7. Are you able to deal with life's realities and see them as spiritual lessons
with value?
NEPTUNE
1. Are you in tune with your subconscious?
2. How able are you to tap into your Higher Self? Your Higher Power?
3. Are you spiritual and inspired or confused and spacy?
4. Can you express your inner feelings through writing, poetry, music, or
dance? Doing OR enjoying?
5. Can you meditate for inspiration?
6. Are you willing to look within for answers, or complain and whine that
"others" are using you?
7. How able are you to forgive self and others for experiences in the past?
8. Are you motivated by guilt?
PLUTO
1. Can you eliminate from your life attitudes, behaviors, or experiences which
are no longer necessary to you & replace them with those which are?
2. How able are you to release and "let go" of persons in our life that must
more on?
3. What is your attitude toward death?(on all levels)
4. How able are you to forge deep, intimate, and powerful emotional ties to
persons in your life?
5. Do you use the power you have over loved ones to manipulate their lives and
behavior?
6. Do you manipulate your own behavior to gain concessions from others?
7. Are you deep feeling and intense or possessive and demanding?
