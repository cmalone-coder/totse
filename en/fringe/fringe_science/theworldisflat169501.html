The World is Flat

by Fuck

"Dreams are real when they are happening. Can we say anymore about life?"

*note* Atheists, skeptics, Christians, Catholics, etc... I wouldn't recommend reading this article if you're any of those. If you have an open mind and you choose not to reject things according to set beliefs, and you like hearing about paranormal things in the world, then I urge you to read on

I remember waking up the other morning from a dream where I could fly, I could turn invisible. I could do virtually anything that I wanted to, simply by thought. My mindpower alone would control how high I flew, how opaque my body would appear to others. This feeling of power was never abused in my dreams. I'd wish to fly an inch above the clouds on a starry moonlit night, and it was so. I don't let my dreams simply be forgotten upon awakening, like many people do. I dwell upon them, trying to find their meanings. Many say dreams have hidden meanings for our subconscious mind to interpret. Many have mastered the skill. Many have found so many truths and secrets in life, that they could never be accepted among the society of our modern world.

Why is it that these things are laughed at or thought of as "crazy?". Because people are so mentally attached to their corrupt system in which they live, that anything that would shatter it is ignored. People try to rely on scientific evidence to show them what is real and what is just imaginary. What happens when this evidence turns on them? True scientists don't assume that if something cannot be proven, it does not exist. All knowledge of science starts from experience and ends with it.

What can science say about the hundreds of people who have had near death experiences, with their brain and body completely dead? You cannot hallucinate when your brain is dead. Science can't disprove that people saw a light they called God, and learned many lessons, came back and changed their lives around. What can science say about the scientific tests that allowed someone to leave their physical body, go into the room next to them, look at an object, come back and report it in detail? The skeptical can think these people are all in on some big universal conspiracy, but it's harder to hide the truth when there are these kinds of events taking place every single day around us, and we simply cannot see it. We don't hear about it, and why? Because we're afraid of it, that's why. We're afraid of knowing more than we know. We're afraid of what we really are. You can't rely on science alone anymore, because there is just too much out there that can't be proven, yet it's still there.

Years ago, according to science, the world was flat.

We know science cannot always explain everything, we should start exploring the weird happenings around the world. Farmers report two balls of light in the sky at night over their crop fields. They move in zig zag circular patterns for six seconds. In the morning, they find incredibly detailed designs in their fields.

The crop circle phenomenom has been around for years now. Nobody really understands how they got there or who, or what put them there. Yes, there have been pranksters who have made few of them after the world started noticing them. It was easy to tell though. It was easy to tell which ones could be made by people overnight, and which ones would be virtually impossible for people to make overnight. The genuine ones had no trace, no footsteps anywhere near them. No evidence of people walking or using machinery to create the images. The corn stalks were laid down in a manner that they would still grow and live. Also, there have been reports of high electromagnetic fields in the area after the design was created.

Out of body experiences. Reported by too many people to be false, the out of body experience phenomenom also attracts a lot of attention these days. Skeptics call the people crazy, but I decided to see for myself. I have told few of my friends about the out of body experience I had about 2 years ago, but I think now would be a good time to say it in perfect detail.

I became interested in out of body experiences one day while I was surfing totse.com. I read articles in the fringe science section about lucid dreaming (having the ability to know when you're dreaming). I immediately became completely obsessed with the subject, and started keeping a dream journal next to my bed in which I recorded all my dreams upon awakening. I couldn't get the hang of lucid dreaming, but it did lead me to start reading up on out of body experiences. I remember exactly how I induced it... I simply asked for it to happen. I never really felt myself drawn to any specific religion, but I relied on faith that night alone to give me what I wanted. Here is how it happened.

I was laying on my right side, with my covers partially covering my face. I was facing the wall, my mattress was low to the floor. Immediately I had a sense of awareness, as if I knew everything that was going on all at once. I knew that this is what I had asked for. The light was coming in from the other end of my room, and reflecting off the wall I was facing. It was a very very overwhelming experience, I thought I'd go blind. In fact, it almost became frightening, but I knew I should face it since this is what I had asked for. Immediately I was up... not really standing, just..up. This is when the heightened awareness hit me really hard. My vision became waaaay more accurate than it could ever be. I could probably spot a needle in a haystack from a mile away, that's how clear it was. My whole state of consciousness was altered. I remembered looking at my computer, and wondering "why is the light coming from my computer?"... then I was immediately at my computer screen, looking at it closely. It felt so weird, not really good or bad, just overwhelming. It felt like all 5 of my senses were increased by 1,000,000%. I then saw the image on my computer screen. A bunch of rainbow-colored triangles, small and large, with eyes, spinning slowly in a clockwise circular motion. It was talking to me, I heard it with my mind though, not my ears (once again, this is all hard to percieve unless this kind of shit has happened to you)... I don't remember what it said, all I remember is that it looked fucking awesome. That was the end of it. I knew after this happened that I would never be the same, I would always be in a state of mind where I'm searching for answers, searching for the truth. I didn't know what this design meant, but something caught my eye the other day when I started researching crop circles. I came across a site with the image that I saw. It scared the shit out of me.

If you search the internet for a picture of the "sri yantra", you'll see a circle with triangles in it, surrounded by a square kind of border. This totally freaked me out the other day, because that is exactly what I saw, if my memory serves me well. Except it had all different sorts of colors in it, with eyes in the triangles, and I'm pretty sure the border was gold. The reason this scared the shit out of me is because before the out of body experience, I never saw this design before. I never knew what it meant, or what it was, and I have yet to do a lot of research on this design, but I plan on doing some soon.

I don't understand all this right now. Like I said, the only true science is through experience. You don't have to believe anything that I'm saying right now, and I understand. For those that are interested though, I don't know what to say anymore. My life started taking turns after the out of body experience, and I never really got the time to focus on trying again (though I can't get it out of my head now.). How did I know it wasn't just some sort of dream? Because it felt more real than reality. There was a sense of timelessness, and the fact that I saw an image (which is said to be used by ancient religions to somehow help induce out of body experiences) that I had never seen before, just proves to me that it was no dream. I was seeing something that other people who travelled out of their body saw, before ever hearing about it. I just found out the name of this thing the other day, and the experience happened almost 2 years ago!

I realize I may sound a little crazy...but people really need to keep an open mind, and start looking up articles of things like these. We can't always trust the articles though, just like you may think this one is complete bullshit. You may think I made the whole thing up, but it's alright because if you ever had it happen to you, you would know that you don't need to prove it to people. The pressure is more on the skeptics to prove these things don't happen, than it is on the people who experience it to prove that they do. That is more true than you realize.

So in conclusion, save me the bullshit about how God cannot exist because you can't see him, or 'it', and save me the bullshit about being good according to "God's laws" or having a big red monster you call satan poke me with a hot fork. I've read into near-death experiences for a long time, and according to the accounts, they say that priests are just as misguided as atheists. Near-death experiences might just be the only good source of truth for those who are open-minded. I have learned a lot from them, and it has made me take life less seriously, and seek more knowledge of existence, rather than follow along with the crowd in this system of living. I apologize to the christians and athiests who read this article, to you I'm either crazy or influenced by satan. To me, I am just a very curious soul.

Telekinesis, telepathy, remote viewing, astral projection... these are areas of life that need to be explored by scientists further. So stop worrying about the end of your life, and the end of the world.

How long do you think this system will last? NDErs had visions of the terrorist attacks of new York and Washington, DC...In fact one of these people wrote about it in their book which was published 6 months before the WTC attacks. Following the attacks, they saw war breaking out between the US and Middle East. Then the world's economy is supposed to completely collapse, followed by wars and terrorism involving Italy. Then Chinese armies will march all over the world as Russia and China are involved in a major world war. The US government will fall, and there will be one world government and the return of concentration camps. In the end, they predict 2/3 of the human population will be dead, and there will be almost no technology at all. After this, we will have accomplished world peace among ourselves for 1000 years. They saw these events taking place from 1992-2012. (They also say that Jesus is already in the world reincarnated as a child, right now.) Some of these things have happened as predicted. It would be scary to see the checklist completed. (Of course, NDErs were also told the point of prophecy is to prevent things from happening if people can change. They say that major wars have actually not happened because of the way people changed during the years. Wars are a lesson to us, this is the truth. When we learn from this lesson, we will realize we do not need war.)

What are we to make of all these things? It's a fucking head trip. Some people go insane just wondering about it. It's easy to point the finger and call these people crazy, to call telepathy and telekinesis fantasies, and nothing more. There have been scientific tests. Telepathy is real. Telekinesis is real. These people really did leave their bodies and really did meet the same light, "love", "God", whatever you want to call it. These crop circles are really there, and they weren't made by people. Our society raises us to believe these things are false. While your tv floods you with images of hollywood and sitcoms of the ideal social life, hundreds of civillians in Iraq are being killed. Millions of people are seeing the direction this world is turning. Prophetic visions or not, this world isn't headed in a good direction until the "kill or be killed" state of mind in human beings is completely abolished and we reach a new era in evolution.

There comes a point in everyone's life where they are out of their normal routine, even for a split second. They question things, and realize just how much they don't know. They snap out of the thinking that they were raised to have, and they jump off the bandwagon. There comes a time when something is just a fantasy, but there comes a time when the pressure is on the skeptics to prove these things don't happen, rather than the open-minded to prove that they do. There are just too many stories, too many truths, frightening as they may be, to why life is the way it is. Once you search for the truth, it becomes addictive like a drug, you just need to know more, you can't stop your thirst for knowledge. If you rely on proven theories to limit your knowledge of the universe, you will never expand your mind.

Just remember: The world is flat.

Revolution, people. Revolution.
