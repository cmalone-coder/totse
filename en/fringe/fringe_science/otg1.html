I have realized magazines are most certainly an example of our
disposable society.It is easy for me to buy a magazine and receive
quick information. It is unfortunate that this is so superficial, it
causes me to wonder how intelligent the intellectual oriented
publications are. It is depressing that advertisements are becoming so
underhanded in there quest to sell sell sell. It is good that it is
becoming possible for anyone to produce there own publications. The
"desktop" age, is a benefit to those who before were unable to express
themselves in media they before could not afford. There is the
arguement that the desktop age is a scam just utilizing techknowledgy
that exists and giving people a reason to buy something. And there is
the arguement that having such a masive amount of people buying into a
trend that is so anti environment/progress is a detriment, but I
figure Jon Q is in a better position whenever he is permitted another
conduit to communicate by.

If something is done simply for monetary reasons it is oviosly not
best for mankind. Is consumerism good for mankind? It gives one a goal
but it simplifies the world to haves and have nots. Have nots will
always want and it simply will not be possible because the more the
have nots do to consume the more the haves benefit anyway. And the gap
will always exist and as times get tougher by bad decisions on every
level life gets tougher for the have nots. At this low point it may be
better for the have nots to be consumers or they may realize the
futility of life etc. etc. and give up, thus halting the system. The
masses are not very intelligent, an intelligent person never would
take something at face value and would understand the complexities of
what was happening. GO ON FOR PAGE AFTER PAGE OF STUFF THAT MAKES ONE
INTELLIGENT AND THEN SUGGEST: or is the average masses that all though
not versed in every angle of every happening are much more satisfied
with it all. At any rate you can see I value objectivity as
intellegence and when it comes to the big questions I dont have an
answer. But life has been the best in history so I figure either we
all blow up or its utopia in another two hundred years or so. You no
they set back the nuclear clock a quarter second or something. I think
thats very unrealistic seeing as how were all still making nuclear
weapons and the solviet union is crumbling loosining acess to there
secrets and arsenel. I personaly dont believe there will be a nuclear
war but I believe there will be more nuclear explosions on this planet
soon. If anyone knows alot or much about Myoelectrics I welcome any
data.

An ouvious tech book is BUILD YOUR OWN LASER,PHASER,ION RAY GUN &
OTHER WORKING SPACE AGE PROJECTS by ROBERT E. IANNINI (ISBN 0-8306-
0604-1 ,$17.95). The title sayes it all, it has descriptions of how to
construct 24 projects of various applications. It has plenty of
illustrtions but requires moderate knowledge of electronics. I'm not
sure of it's conection to INFORMATION UNLIMITED (has adds in lots of
magazines selling kits to build high-tech projects) but all the
projects transpond to some of INFORMATION's kits so parts aren't tough
to get.

For those of you daunted by the complexities of electronics but realy
would like to learn you can go to RADIO SHACK and get GETTING STARTED
IN ELECTRONICS(cat. 276-5003). Its 128 pages of the basics in the most
basic terms and 100 circuit diagrams fully illustrated and very cheap
as well as references to one of americas easiest sources of
components.

STAR TREK THE NEXT GENERATION TECHNICAL MANUAL by RICK STERNBACH and
MICHAEL OKUDA (ISBN 0-671-70427-3 ,$13.00) is an in depth look at the
theories behind the TV show's techknowledgy. It is the highest quality
book of its type and recomended to anyone who wants to know how the
warp coils or phasers work. It has depthy explinations and
illustrations defining how everything works in the "next generation".
Based on scientific principles the authors atempt to explain the
fanasies from the last twenty five years of STAR TREK and make it all
seem believable. Fans will be thrilled sceptics will be impressed.


ALIEN III by William Gibson although not official in print Gibson's
reputaion has made this rejected script a popular item in the cyber-
cicuit. It really dosen't measure up to the first to films its more a
composite of the two and completely different from the final film. It
reminds me a bit of STAR TREK 6 which may have something to do with
its demise, it draws a strong paralell to the US's citizens
relationship with the USSR's citizens and there underlying humanities.
Although for the most part I would call it a shoot-em-up in a space
station filled with mutant ALIENS.


Syd Mead is one of if not the most knowen futurist in the film
buisness if not the world. After his work on BLADE RUNNER and TRON I
think he took part in inspiring my generation. His uterly intelegent
and his geometric perfections are a delight to study and think about
and now he has releaced another collection of his painting in the "'92
OBLAGON CALENDAR". "The 9"x12" full color calendar contains twelve
fantasy illustations plus a specially commisioned 12"x18" centerfold".
With concept work for TOYOTA MOTORS to INTERVIEW MAGAZINE. Avalible
from OBLAGON 1716 North Gardner Los Angeles,CA 90046.


Another futurist/cartoonist I think of after SYD MEAD (and RON COBB)
is MAKOTO KOBYASHI who occationaly blatintly "borrows" MEADS designs
but alwase creates fantasticly intresting work. A member or Xmember of
ART MIC (BUBBLE GUM CRISIS, MADOX 01, MEGA ZONE 23) KOBYASHI has had
numerous books devoted to his ideas including CITIES IN LABRINTH,
PSYBER COMICS #3, HYPER WEAPON And mulitudes of DRAGONS HEAVEN books.
He has had series in B-CLUB and HOBBY JAPAN which has been parcialy
compiled in TWO FACTORY (ISBN4-89189-337-0 c0076 JAPANESE 1800yen).
TWO FACTORY is devoted half to MAKOTO KOBAYASHI and half to KOW
YOKOYAMA and highlights both there careers. KOW YOKOYAMA is another
great Japanese talent and has done SF3D models and HOBBY JAPAN and B-
CLUB series as well as multitudes of models and illustrations for such
clients as LOG-IN(japanese computer magazine) and POCARI SWEET
(japanese soft drink). Both of these artists have had many models and
garage kits releaced legaly and illegaly based on there designs. I
will do bios on both these artists eventualy.



ITS GRIM UP NORTH:JAMS

I was a little displeased with this twelve inch. After BUILD A FIRE
and THE MAN I expected a nice eerie/blarny piece to welcome the
winter. Instead we get a insturmental with an ocational list of towns
in northern england. I guess it could have been expected after
repeated statements that there next album THE BLACK ROOM would be
techno metal. And from the sound of this single techno metal is a
cross of industrial and anolog sounds FX'ed into a dance hit. Not to
say this will be the smash the previous four KLF/TIMELORDS singles
were. I think that budgeting is causing problems in pressing and
distribution. My copy is low quality and the UK promo (releaced in
spring(grey vinal one side)) is only a bit better. The JAMS have a
history of "questionable" releaces, short supply and multiple formats
have only caused this band to rise to legendary status among
collectors. But after all of this for those who have followed the JAMS
will not regret for a moment taking the next step on BILL&JIMMY's
legacy.

Look out for lots more JAMS text later.
